  1. 预印本Preprint2026-10-31
    紧群上 p-liquid 系数的正次数上同调：一般系数的反例Positive-degree cohomology of compact groups with p-liquid coefficients: a counterexample for general coefficients

    对 G = SU(2) 与 Vp = coker(∂₄) 的平凡作用，证明三阶上同调非零， 从而在「任意 p-liquid 系数」的量词下否定 Question 0.10.4。 p-Banach 情形仍开，归约为一个显式标量下确界。For G = SU(2) acting trivially on Vp = coker(∂₄), we prove that the third cohomology group is non-zero — so Question 0.10.4 fails once the coefficients range over all p-liquid spaces. The p-Banach case stays open, reduced to one explicit scalar infimum.

  2. 预印本Preprint2026-11-16
    紧群上平凡 p-liquid 系数的低次上同调Low-degree cohomology of compact groups with trivial p-liquid coefficients

    对任意紧群证明 H¹(G, V) = 0，对 G = SU(2) 证明 H²(G, V) = 0，V 取任意 p-liquid 实系数。 核心是一个替代 Haar 平均的算子 2 − f*，不经过局部凸性，也不要求系数来自 Banach 空间。For any compact group we prove H¹(G, V) = 0, and for G = SU(2) also H²(G, V) = 0, with V any p-liquid real coefficients. The heart of it is an operator 2 − f* that replaces averaging over the group: no local convexity is used, and the coefficients need not come from a Banach space.
