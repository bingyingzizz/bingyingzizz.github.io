## Statement · **draft**
A **(set-valued) presheaf** on a category $\mathcal{C}$ is a functor

$$T : \mathcal{C}^{\mathrm{op}} \longrightarrow \mathbf{Set}$$

A **morphism** of presheaves is a natural transformation between them. Notation: for a morphism $f : Y \to X$, write $f^{-1} = T(f) : T(X) \to T(Y)$; for $s \in T(X)$, write $s|_Y = f^{-1}(s)$.
