# Generalized Eigenvector　`def.generalized-eigenvector`

[in graph](../#n=def.generalized-eigenvector)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./def.generalized-eigenvector.md)

**Core facts**: $\dim G_{\lambda} = $ the algebraic multiplicity, and $V = \bigoplus_{\lambda} G_{\lambda}$ (the root-space decomposition of thm.primary-decomposition).

## Notes · **draft**
**Why this makes up the dimension**: $T - \lambda I$ is zero on $V_{\lambda}$, but on $G_{\lambda}$ it is **nilpotent** (def.nilpotent).

Nilpotent operators have a very simple structure (a chain), so restricting $T$ to $G_{\lambda}$ gives "$\lambda I$ + a nilpotent operator" — this is precisely a Jordan block.
