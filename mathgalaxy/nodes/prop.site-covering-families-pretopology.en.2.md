## Statement · **draft**
Let $\mathcal{C}$ be a site with fiber products. Then the family of all "covering sieves generating $X$", namely $(X_{i} \to X)_{i \in I}$, forms a **pretopology** on $\mathcal{C}$, and it **generates the original topology of $\mathcal{C}$** — this pretopology is called the **maximal pretopology**.
