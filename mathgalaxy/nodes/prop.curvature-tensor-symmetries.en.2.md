## Statement · **draft**
**What this does**: it takes stock of how many independent components the curvature tensor really has — four symmetries cut $n^{4}$ down to $n^{2}(n^{2}-1)/12$. This is the arithmetic source of "curvature in dimension two is one number, in dimension four it is two tensors".
