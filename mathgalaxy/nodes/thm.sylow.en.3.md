# Sylow theorems　`thm.sylow`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=thm.sylow)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./thm.sylow.md)

⚠ Galois theory **does not require** Sylow's theorem (the Galois correspondence uses normality rather than counting).

It is marked optional in the plan.

It is included as a direct application of group actions and a first step toward the classification of finite groups.

⭐ Standard application (showing that groups are nonisomorphic or not simple): $|G| = 15$.

$n_3 \mid 5$ and $n_3 \equiv 1 \pmod 3$ $\implies$ $n_3 = 1$.

$n_5 \mid 3$ and $n_5 \equiv 1 \pmod 5$ $\implies$ $n_5 = 1$.
