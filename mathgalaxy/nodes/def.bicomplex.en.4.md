# bicomplex　`def.bicomplex`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=def.bicomplex)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./def.bicomplex.md)

**Typical source**: resolving a complex by injectives naturally produces a bicomplex (in $I^{p,q}$, $p$ is the degree of the original complex and $q$ is the degree of the resolution). The **Grothendieck spectral sequence** comes from it.
