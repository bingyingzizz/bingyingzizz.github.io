## Notes · **draft**
⭐ **Its meaning: why chasing elements is legitimate.** A statement in a small abelian category can first be embedded along a fully faithful exact functor into $A\text{-}\mathbf{Mod}$, proved there (where elements make sense), and carried back. "Chasing elements in a concrete category" is therefore a legitimate method of proof.
