def.quasi-separated

Machine-drafted and pending author review. The Chinese text is authoritative.

Statement

In a topos T\mathcal{T}T, an object XXX is quasi-separated if for all Y→XY \to XY→X and Z→XZ \to XZ→X (with Y,ZY, ZY,Z quasi-compact) the fibre product

Y×XZY \times_{X} ZY×X​Z

is again quasi-compact.

Notes

Properties: closed under subobjects (a subobject of a quasi-separated object is quasi-separated); closed under coproducts; closed under filtered colimits along injections.

"Quasi-separated" adds one requirement between two quasi-compact objects: the overlap must also be small. This is exactly parallel to "compact plus compact overlaps" in topology — together, quasi-compact and quasi-separated single out the "small and well-behaved" objects.
