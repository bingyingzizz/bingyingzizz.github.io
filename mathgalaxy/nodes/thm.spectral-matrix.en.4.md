Thus **"diagonalization" = "splitting the operator into a bunch of mutually perpendicular directions, each multiplied by a number"**.

⭐ Connection with functional calculus: once the spectral decomposition is available, any function $f$ can be applied: $f(A) := \sum_i f(\lambda_i)P_i$. The operators $\sqrt{A}$, $e^{tA}$, $A^{1/2}$ are all special cases — this is why "a positive definite matrix has a square root" needs no separate construction.
