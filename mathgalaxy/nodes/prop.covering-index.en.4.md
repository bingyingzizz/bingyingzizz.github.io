# index of a covering　`prop.covering-index`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=prop.covering-index)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./prop.covering-index.md)

⭐ **When the covering is universal** ($E$ simply connected, so $p_{*}\pi_{1}(E) = 1$), the conclusion simplifies to "fibre $\leftrightarrow \pi_{1}(B)$ as sets". The map $p : \mathbb{R} \to S^{1}$ is exactly this case.

**Geometric meaning of injectivity**: something nonzero in $\pi_{1}(E)$ stays nonzero in $B$ — a covering never pretends to "undo" an already trivial loop; it only makes a loop "tighter".

💡 This proposition is the entry point to the classification of coverings: connected coverings of $B$ up to homeomorphism correspond to conjugacy classes of subgroups of $\pi_{1}(B)$.
