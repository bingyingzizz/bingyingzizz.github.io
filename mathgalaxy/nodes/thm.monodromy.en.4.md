# monodromy theorem　`thm.monodromy`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=thm.monodromy)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./thm.monodromy.md)

⭐ **Its use**: it upgrades “continuation along paths” to “a single-valued function on the domain”, so the statement in `prop.single-valued-branch` that “on a simply connected $\implies$ the logarithm has a single-valued branch” becomes a special case. Conversely, it explains why the “branches” of a multivalued function can go wrong only on domains that are **not simply connected**.

⚠️ The hypothesis “can be continued along every curve” cannot be dropped: $\sqrt{z}$ can of course be continued on a domain with a slit removed, but once a curve hits the singularity the chain breaks.
