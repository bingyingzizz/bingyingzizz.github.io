# six functor formalism　`def.six-functors`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=def.six-functors)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./def.six-functors.md)

A **6-functor formalism** consists of:

- for every scheme $X$ a closed symmetric monoidal triangulated category $D(X)$ (the tensor product $- \otimes_X -$ has a right adjoint $\operatorname{Hom}_X$);
- for every map $f : X \to Y$ a **pullback** $f^* : D(Y) \to D(X)$, with right adjoint $f_*$ (pushforward);
- for every map $f : X \to Y$ (satisfying suitable conditions) a **proper-support pushforward** $f_! : D(X) \to D(Y)$ (keeping only the part "whose support lies in the compact fibres of $f$", analogous to integration being meaningful only on compact regions), with right adjoint $f^!$ (duality).
