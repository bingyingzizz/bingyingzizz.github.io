# Implications among the AB Axioms　`prop.ab5-implications`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=prop.ab5-implications)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./prop.ab5-implications.md)

A **cocomplete** category with a **small cogenerating set** and **co-well-powered** (each object has only a small set of quotients) is **complete**.

For a Grothendieck category $\mathcal{A}$: cocomplete ✓ (AB5);

co-well-poweredness and a small cogenerating set both come from a **generator** $U$ — a quotient is determined by its "points over $U$", so the quotients of $U$ form a set, and their product is the cogenerator.

SAFT then gives that all limits exist ✓.
- **Item 2**: this is a classical result of **Roos**.
