# Lie Bracket　`def.lie-bracket`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=def.lie-bracket)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./def.lie-bracket.md)

**Jacobi is the composition of three things**: writing it as $[X,[Y,Z]] = [[X,Y],Z] + [Y,[X,Z]]$, the reading is "$[X,\cdot]$ is a derivation". It is not an arbitrary axiom but a necessary consequence of "the bracket comes from first-order differential operators" — in the expansion six terms cancel pairwise, using the product rule.
