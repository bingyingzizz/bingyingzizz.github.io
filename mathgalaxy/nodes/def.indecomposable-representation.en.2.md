## Notes · **draft**
Every irreducible representation is indecomposable. The converse fails: over $F[x]$ the matrix $T=\begin{pmatrix}1&1\\0&1\end{pmatrix}$ is indecomposable but has the invariant line $F e_1$, hence is reducible. The Krull–Schmidt theorem gives uniqueness of decompositions into indecomposables. Indecomposable is weaker than irreducible.
