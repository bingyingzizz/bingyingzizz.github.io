# existence and uniqueness of the determinant　`thm.det-exists-unique`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=thm.det-exists-unique)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./thm.det-exists-unique.md)

⭐ Construction for "existence": expand $(v_1, \ldots, v_n)$ in the basis, obtaining the coefficient matrix $(a_{ij})$ of size $n \times n$, then take the Leibniz formula $\sum_{\sigma} \operatorname{sgn}(\sigma) \prod_i a_{i,\sigma(i)}$ as the definition — then verify the three axioms one by one (see the proof section).

⭐ Relation to "universal property": this is not a universal property in the usual sense (there is no loose adjoint), but it is **the same technique** — pinning an object down by a universal condition (the three axioms).
