cor.k-acyclic-acyclic · Banach

Machine-drafted and pending author review. The Chinese text is authoritative.

Statement

If a complex of Banach abelian groups M∙M^{\bullet}M∙ is KKK-bounded acyclic (for some KKK), then it is acyclic in the usual sense.

Notes

Immediate corollary: Apply the preceding proposition degree by degree. Every pair of adjacent degrees satisfies the boundedness condition with bound KKK, so the complex is exact in every degree.

⭐ This is the conversion step used throughout the chapter: The acyclicity proofs first establish bounded acyclicity on Stone or compact pieces, then use this corollary to obtain ordinary acyclicity and hence cohomological vanishing.
