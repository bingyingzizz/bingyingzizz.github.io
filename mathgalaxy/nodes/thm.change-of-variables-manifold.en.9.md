# Change of Variables on Manifolds　`thm.change-of-variables-manifold`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=thm.change-of-variables-manifold)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./thm.change-of-variables-manifold.md)

**⚠️ Choosing orientations**: if $M$ and $N$ each carry an orientation, $\varepsilon$ is decided by whether $f$ preserves these two orientations; reversing the orientation of $N$ changes both $\varepsilon$ and the right-hand side, and the identity survives. So "orientation-preserving" must be read relative to two fixed orientations.
