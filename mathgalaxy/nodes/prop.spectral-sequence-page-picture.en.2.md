## Statement · **draft**
Each page $E_{r}$ of a spectral sequence can be drawn as a **family of lattice points** in the $(p, q)$ plane (with an object $E^{p,q}_{r}$ at each lattice point), and the differential $d_{r}$ is a **directed arrow** from $(p,q)$ to $(p+r,\, q-r+1)$. Thus $E_{0}, E_{1}, E_{2}$ are different “vector fields” on the same diagram.
