# Subcategories Inherit Additivity and Abelianness　`prop.subcat-additive`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=prop.subcat-additive)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./prop.subcat-additive.md)

## Statement · **draft**
Let $F : \mathcal{C} \hookrightarrow \mathcal{D}$ be a **fully faithful** functor, with $\mathcal{D}$ additive (resp. abelian). If $F$ has an **adjoint or a coadjoint** (resp. and $F$ is exact), then $\mathcal{C}$ is also an **additive** (resp. **abelian**) category.

## Notes · **draft**
**Proof.**

**(i) Biproducts.** An adjoint (left or right) is additive: a right adjoint preserves products, a left adjoint preserves coproducts ✓ (see "adjoint functors are additive").
