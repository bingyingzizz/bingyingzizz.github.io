# orthogonality　`def.orthogonality`

[in graph](../#n=def.orthogonality)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./def.orthogonality.md)

⚠️ **Two pairs of words that are easily confused**: an **orthogonal set** (pairwise orthogonal, arbitrary lengths) versus an **orthonormal set** (additionally unit length); the **orthogonal complement** $S^{\perp}$ is "the vectors orthogonal to all of $S$"; it is a **subspace** and $S \cap S^{\perp} = \{0\}$ (for a positive definite inner product), but for a general bilinear form this can fail (def.isotropic-subspace).
