## Statement · **draft**
Let $U \subseteq V$ be a subspace and $\pi : V \to V/U$ the quotient map. Then for every linear map $T : V \to W$ with $U \subseteq \ker T$ there is a **unique** linear map $\overline{T} : V/U \to W$ making the diagram commute:

$$T = \overline{T} \circ \pi$$

(that is: $U \subseteq \ker T$ $\iff$ $T$ factors through the quotient map).
