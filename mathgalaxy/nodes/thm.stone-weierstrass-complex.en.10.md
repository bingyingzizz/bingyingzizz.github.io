# Stone-Weierstrass theorem, complex case　`thm.stone-weierstrass-complex`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=thm.stone-weierstrass-complex)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./thm.stone-weierstrass-complex.md)

⭐ **Link with representation theory**: for a compact group $G$, the matrix coefficients $e_{ij}$ generate $C(G)$ (because $\{e_{ij}\}$ is closed under conjugation), and $\Delta : C(G) \to C(G) \otimes C(G)$, $e_{ij} \mapsto \sum_k e_{ik} \otimes e_{kj}$ — the algebraic basis of Peter–Weyl.
