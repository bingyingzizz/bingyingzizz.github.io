## Statement · **draft**
Let $f$ be integrable on $[a, b]$, and define

$$F(x) := \int_a^x f, \qquad x \in [a, b]$$

Then:

**(a)** $F$ is continuous on $[a, b]$; in fact, if $|f| \le M$ then $|F(y) - F(x)| \le M|y - x|$ (that is, $F$ is Lipschitz).
