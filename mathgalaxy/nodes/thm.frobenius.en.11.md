# Frobenius theorem　`thm.frobenius`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=thm.frobenius)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./thm.frobenius.md)

**III. (3)$\implies$(1):** If $X,Y$ are tangent at $p$ to an integral manifold $N$, then $[X,Y]_{p}$ is also tangent to $N$ (by naturality of the bracket, $[X,Y]$ depends only on first-order information about $X,Y$, and after extending $X,Y$ to vector fields tangent to $N$, their bracket still lies in $TN$, see `def.lie-bracket`). Thus $\mathcal{D}$ is involutive. $\square$
