## Statement · **draft**
Let $G$ be a group.

**(a) Subgroups**: if $G$ is solvable and $H \le G$, then $H$ is solvable.

**(b) Quotients**: if $G$ is solvable and $N \trianglelefteq G$, then $G/N$ is solvable.
