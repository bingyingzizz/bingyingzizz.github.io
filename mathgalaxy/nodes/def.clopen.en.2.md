## Notes · **draft**
Clopen sets are a **rare species** in a general topological space: in connected spaces such as $\mathbb{R}$ or $[0,1]$ there are only $\emptyset$ and the whole space. **The more clopen sets, the more fragmented the space** — totally disconnected means "every connected component is a single point"; "clopen sets separate any two points" (= zero-dimensional) is equivalent to that only under **compact Hausdorff**; and in a profinite space "the clopen sets form a base".
