# effective equivalence relation　`def.effective-equivalence`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=def.effective-equivalence)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./def.effective-equivalence.md)

$$R \;\cong\; X \times_{\overline{X}} X, \qquad \overline{X} := \operatorname{coker}(R \rightrightarrows X)$$

that is, $R$ is exactly the kernel pair of the quotient map.

## Notes · **draft**
"Effective" says the **quotient glues nothing extra**: take the equivalence relation, then the quotient, then look at the kernel pair of the quotient map, and one returns to the original equivalence relation.
