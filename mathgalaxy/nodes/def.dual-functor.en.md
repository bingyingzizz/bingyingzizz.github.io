## Statement · **draft**
**The dual functor** $(-)^{\ast}:\mathbf{Vect}_K^{\mathrm{op}}\to\mathbf{Vect}_K$ is defined as follows:

- on objects, $V\mapsto V^{\ast}=\operatorname{Hom}_K(V,K)$;
- on morphisms, it sends $T:V\to W$ to $T^{\ast}:W^{\ast}\to V^{\ast}$.
