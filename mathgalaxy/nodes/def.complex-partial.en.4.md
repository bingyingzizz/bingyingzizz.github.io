# Complex Partial Derivatives　`def.complex-partial`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=def.complex-partial)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./def.complex-partial.md)

⭐ Mnemonic (a footnote in textbooks): substitute $x = (z + \bar z)/2$, $y = -i(z - \bar z)/2$ into $f(x, y)$, and **formally** treat $z$ and $\bar z$ as independent variables, differentiating by the chain rule — this yields the two formulas above.

⚠ This only aids memory — the rigorous definition is the combination on the right, because $z$ and $\bar z$ are not independent as two coordinates on $\mathbb{R}^2$.

⭐ What is genuinely useful is its **criterion** property: $f$ is complex differentiable $\iff$ $\partial f/\partial \bar z(z_0) = 0$.
