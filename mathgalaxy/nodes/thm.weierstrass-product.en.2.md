# Weierstrass product theorem　`thm.weierstrass-product`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=thm.weierstrass-product)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./thm.weierstrass-product.md)

$$f(z) = z^{m} e^{g(z)} \prod_{n=1}^{\infty} E_{p_{n}}\left(\frac{z}{a_{n}}\right),$$

Here $m$ is the multiplicity of $0$ as a zero, and $p_{n}$ are suitably chosen nonnegative integers that ensure uniform convergence of the product on compact subsets.

Thus **the zeros of an entire function, together with their multiplicities, can be prescribed arbitrarily**. The only remaining freedom is a zero-free entire factor $e^{g}$.
