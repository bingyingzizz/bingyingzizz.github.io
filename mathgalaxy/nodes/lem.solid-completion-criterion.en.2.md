# criterion for solid completeness　`lem.solid-completion-criterion`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=lem.solid-completion-criterion)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./lem.solid-completion-criterion.md)

Let $\mathcal{A}$ be an abelian category with arbitrary colimits and $\mathcal{A}_0 \subset \mathcal{A}$ a family of compact projective generators (= a generating family in which every object is both **compact** and **projective**; this is the abstract version of "finite free modules" in module categories).
