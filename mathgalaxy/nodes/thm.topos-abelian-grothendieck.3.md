## English abstract · **draft** · machine-drafted, pending author review; Chinese is authoritative（续）
For a topos $\mathcal{T}$, the category $\mathcal{T}(\mathbf{Ab})$ of abelian group objects in sheaves is a Grothendieck category: it has a small set of generators, all colimits, and exact filtered colimits. Generators are the objects $\mathbb{Z}\cdot X$ for $X$ ranging over a generating set of $\mathcal{T}$.
