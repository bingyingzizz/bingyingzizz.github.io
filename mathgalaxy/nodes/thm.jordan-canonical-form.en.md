## Statement · **draft**
Let $K$ be algebraically closed, $T : V \to V$, $\dim V < \infty$. Then there is a basis of $V$ in which the matrix of $T$ is **block diagonal**

$$J = \begin{pmatrix} J_{1} & & \\ & \ddots & \\ & & J_{s} \end{pmatrix}, \qquad J_{k} = \begin{pmatrix} \lambda_{k} & 1 & & \\ & \ddots & \ddots & \\ & & \ddots & 1 \\ & & & \lambda_{k} \end{pmatrix}$$
