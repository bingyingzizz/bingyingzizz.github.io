## Statement · **draft**
A sequence $\{x_n\} \subseteq \mathbb{R}$ **converges to** $x$ (written $x_n \to x$ or $\lim_{n \to \infty} x_n = x$), iff

$$\forall \varepsilon > 0, \exists N, \forall n \ge N : |x_n - x| < \varepsilon$$

That is: no matter how small $\varepsilon$ is given, from some index on all terms lie in the $x$-neighbourhood of $\varepsilon$.

A sequence that does not converge is called **divergent**.
