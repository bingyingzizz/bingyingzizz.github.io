# long exact sequence of a pair　`thm.long-exact-pair`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=thm.long-exact-pair)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./thm.long-exact-pair.md)

**Why this is simpler in homology than in homotopy.** The long exact sequence of **relative** homotopy groups holds for every pair $(X, A)$ and requires no fibration. The delicate step is replacing the relative groups by the absolute groups of the quotient space $X/A$; it requires a good pair hypothesis (NDR pair or cofibration) on $(X, A)$. The homology sequence applies whenever $A \subseteq X$ is a subspace. This is one of the main reasons chain complexes are easier to work with than homotopy groups.
