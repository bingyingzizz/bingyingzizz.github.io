## Notes · **draft**
**Relation to fine sheaves**: fine sheaves (sheaves admitting a partition-of-unity action) are always soft; on a paracompact Hausdorff space, sheaves of $C^{\infty}$-modules (such as the sheaf of differential forms) are fine and hence soft. Constant sheaves are in general not soft.

**Why it matters**: on a paracompact Hausdorff space soft sheaves have no higher cohomology (Godement's theorem), so soft resolutions can be used to compute sheaf cohomology; see @@thm.soft-sheaf-acyclic@@ (skeleton: steps 0–2 complete, step 3 conditional on "flabby sheaves are acyclic").
