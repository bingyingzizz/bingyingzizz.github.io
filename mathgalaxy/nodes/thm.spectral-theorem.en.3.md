# spectral theorem for real symmetric operators　`thm.spectral-theorem`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=thm.spectral-theorem)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./thm.spectral-theorem.md)

⭐ The two steps of the proof: (1) there is at least one (real) eigenvalue (by the extreme value theorem: $|Tv|$ or $\langle Tv, v\rangle$ attains an extremum on the **compact** unit sphere);

(2) induct on the orthogonal complement of the eigenspace (invariance comes from self-adjointness: $T(U) \subseteq U \implies T(U^{\perp}) \subseteq U^{\perp}$).
