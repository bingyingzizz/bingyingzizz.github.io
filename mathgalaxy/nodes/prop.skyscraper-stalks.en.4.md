# Stalks of a Skyscraper Sheaf　`prop.skyscraper-stalks`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=prop.skyscraper-stalks)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./prop.skyscraper-stalks.md)

**Corollary (the $X$ case)**: $T_{1}$ is $\iff$ $\iff$ $\overline{\{x\}} = \{x\}$, so the stalk of a skyscraper sheaf is non-zero only at $x$ — then "$A$ supported at one point" is literal.

⚠ On a general topological space (e.g. a spectral space with a specialization order), the **support** of a skyscraper sheaf is the closure $\overline{\{x\}}$, not $\{x\}$. This step must be re-examined when skyscraper sheaves are transported to schemes or adic spaces.
