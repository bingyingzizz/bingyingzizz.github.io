# Net　`def.net`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=def.net)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./def.net.md)

**Net**: a map $x : \Lambda \to X$, written $(x_{\lambda})_{\lambda \in \Lambda}$.

**Convergence**: a net $x_{\lambda}$ **converges to** $x$ (written $x_{\lambda} \to x$) if for every neighbourhood $U$ of $x$ there exists $\lambda_{0}$ such that

$$\lambda \ge \lambda_{0} \implies x_{\lambda} \in U .$$

**Two characterisation theorems**:

- $f : X \to Y$ is continuous $\iff$ $f$ sends every convergent net to a convergent net (`def.continuous-map`);
- $X$ is compact $\iff$ every net in $X$ has a convergent **subnet**.
