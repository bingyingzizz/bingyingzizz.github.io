# Equivalence Relations in Condensed Sets Are Effective　`prop.cond-equivalence-effective`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=prop.cond-equivalence-effective)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./prop.cond-equivalence-effective.md)

**Why it matters**: only effective equivalence relations allow "quotient and pull back to recover", that is, the operation of "taking quotients" **loses no information**. Specialized to $\mathrm{Cond}$, it guarantees: gluing two compact Hausdorff pieces along an equivalence relation, the quotient object "is" that equivalence relation in the sheaf-theoretic sense.
