# Radon-Nikodym theorem　`thm.lebesgue-radon-nikodym`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=thm.lebesgue-radon-nikodym)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./thm.lebesgue-radon-nikodym.md)

and $\rho$ can be written as an integral: there is a generalized $\mu$-integrable function $f$ with

$$d\rho = f d\mu$$

Any two such $f$ agree $\mu -\text{a.e.}$.

## Notes · **draft**
⭐ This is the **structure theorem** of the whole theory of signed measures: every $\nu$ splits uniquely into "a part singular with respect to $\mu$" plus "a part absolutely continuous with respect to $\mu$".

In particular, **when $\nu \ll \mu$**, $\lambda = 0$, hence $d\nu = f d\mu$ — this is what is usually called the **Radon–Nikodym theorem**.
