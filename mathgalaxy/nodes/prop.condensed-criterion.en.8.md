ndition $\iff$ the condition for surjections: use that compact Hausdorff surjections are effective quotients and that quotients by closed equivalence relations are again compact Hausdorff, with the cost paid in @@def.chaus-pretopos@@.
- Descent for a single surjection is not enough: the constant two-element-set presheaf satisfies the kernel–coequalizer condition for every single surjection, yet does not send the empty coproduct to a point and does not preserve binary coproducts, so the finite-coproduct condition cannot be dropped.
