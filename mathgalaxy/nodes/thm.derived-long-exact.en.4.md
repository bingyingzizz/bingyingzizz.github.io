Sending them to $K^{+}(\mathcal{A}')$ still gives a triangle ($F$ is additive, and a mapping-cone triangle remains a mapping-cone triangle termwise). But $H^{n} : \mathbf{K}(\mathcal{A}') \to \mathcal{A}'$ is a **cohomological functor**: applying homology to a triangle gives a long exact sequence. On injective complexes $R^{n}F = H^{n}F$, hence
$$\cdots \to H^{n}FK^{\bullet} \to H^{n}FL^{\bullet} \to H^{n}FM^{\bullet} \to H^{n+1}FK^{\bullet} \to \cdots ,$$
which is what we wanted. ∎
