Statement

Let (G,⋅)(G, \cdot)(G,⋅) be a group and H⊆GH \subseteq GH⊆G. We say that HHH is a subgroup** of GGG (written H≤GH \le GH≤G) if HHH is itself a group under the restricted operation** — that is:

  • a,b∈H  ⟹  ab∈Ha, b \in H \implies ab \in Ha,b∈H⟹ab∈H (closed under the operation);
  • a∈H  ⟹  a−1∈Ha \in H \implies a^{-1} \in Ha∈H⟹a−1∈H (closed under inverses).

(Associativity is inherited from GGG and need not be checked again.)
