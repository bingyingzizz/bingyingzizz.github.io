## Notes · **draft**
⚠️ Note that "homotopy equivalence" is **not** "termwise isomorphism": $1 \sim 0$ means that between two adjacent layers of $K^{\bullet}$ there is a family of maps $s^{n}$ contracting the identity; such a complex (for example $0 \to M \xrightarrow{1} M \to 0$) has every layer nonzero yet is homotopy trivial.
