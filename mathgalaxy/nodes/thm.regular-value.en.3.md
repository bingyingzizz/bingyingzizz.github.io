## Notes · **draft**
⭐ "The preimage of a regular value is a manifold" is the gateway to the whole theory of smooth manifolds — it turns **solving equations** into **describing geometric objects**.

⭐ Standard example: for $f(x) = \|x\|^{2}$ ($\mathbb{R}^{n} \to \mathbb{R}$) the gradient $\nabla f(x) = 2x$ vanishes only at the origin, so every $c > 0$ is a regular value and $f^{-1}(c)$ is an $(n-1)$-dimensional submanifold — this is where "the sphere is a manifold" comes from.
