# the integrally closed subring is determined by the spectrum　`prop.huber-subring-recovery`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=prop.huber-subring-recovery)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./prop.huber-subring-recovery.md)

⭐ Why it is **smallest**: an integrally closed subring always contains $\mathbb{Z}\cdot 1$, hence contains $\widetilde{\mathbb{Z}}$ (integral elements are closed under integrally closed subrings); and an integral element satisfies $|a| \le 1$ under **every** valuation — if $|a| > 1$, then the left side of $a^{n} + c_{n-1}a^{n-1} + \cdots + c_{0} = 0$ ($c_{i} \in \mathbb{Z}$, so $|c_{i}| \le 1$) is dominated by $a^{n}$ and cannot equal $0$. Hence it lies in every unit ball, and choosing it filters out the fewest points.
