# Stokes' Theorem on Chains　`thm.stokes-chain`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=thm.stokes-chain)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./thm.stokes-chain.md)

Let $c$ be a chain in $M$ of dimension $k$ ($k\ge 1$), and let $\omega$ be defined on a neighborhood of the image of $c$ and be a $(k-1)$-form. Then

$$\int_{\partial c}\omega = \int_{c}\mathrm{d}\omega .$$

**Relation to the manifold version** (`thm.stokes`): The manifold version requires $M$ to be compact and orientable with boundary, and $\omega$ to be defined on $M$. The chain version does not require $M$ to be orientable or compact, or $\omega$ to have compact support. It uses a chain in place of a manifold.
