Statement

Let U⊆VU \subseteq VU⊆V be a subspace and π:V→V/U\pi : V \to V/Uπ:V→V/U the quotient map. Then for every linear map T:V→WT : V \to WT:V→W with U⊆ker⁡TU \subseteq \ker TU⊆kerT there is a unique linear map T‾:V/U→W\overline{T} : V/U \to WT:V/U→W making the diagram commute:

T=T‾∘πT = \overline{T} \circ \piT=T∘π

(that is: U⊆ker⁡TU \subseteq \ker TU⊆kerT   ⟺  \iff⟺ TTT factors through the quotient map).

Notes

⭐ “Universal property” = characterizing the quotient as “the most general target that kills UUU”: every map vanishing on UUU factors uniquely through V/UV/UV/U.

Existence comes from the definition, uniqueness from π\piπ being surjective.

⭐ This is the general recipe for the family “cokernel / quotient”; in category theory it is the universal property of a coequalizer.

⚠ The hypothesis is U⊆ker⁡TU \subseteq \ker TU⊆kerT (TTT vanishes on UUU), not U=ker⁡TU = \ker TU=kerT — one is allowed to kill a little more.
