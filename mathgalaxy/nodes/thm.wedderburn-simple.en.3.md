## Notes · **stale**
Take a minimal nonzero left ideal $V$; it is a simple $A$-module, so $V$ is semisimple, and by Schur's lemma $D=\operatorname{End}_A(V)$ is a division algebra. The natural map $A\to\operatorname{End}_D(V)$ is injective because $A$ is simple: if $aV=0$, then $\operatorname{Ann}(V)$ is a nonzero two-sided ideal, hence equals $A$, contradicting $AV=V$. Burnside's theorem makes it surjective, so $A\cong\operatorname{End}_D(V)\cong M_n(D^{\mathrm{op}})$. If the field is algebraically closed, Schur's lemma gives $D=F$.
