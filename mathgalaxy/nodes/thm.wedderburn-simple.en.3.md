# Wedderburn Structure Theorem for Simple Algebras　`thm.wedderburn-simple`

## Notes · **stale**
Take a minimal nonzero left ideal $V$; $V$ is a simple $A$-module, and Schur's lemma makes $D=\operatorname{End}_A(V)$ a division algebra. The natural map $A\to\operatorname{End}_D(V)$ is injective because $A$ is simple, and Burnside's theorem makes it surjective; hence $A\cong\operatorname{End}_D(V)\cong M_n(D^{\mathrm{op}})$. Over an algebraically closed field Schur's lemma gives $D=F$. Note the direction: $A$ is a matrix algebra over $D^{\mathrm{op}}$.
