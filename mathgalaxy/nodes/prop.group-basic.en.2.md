## Notes · **draft**
⭐ Cancellation is the watershed between groups and monoids: $(\mathbb{N}, \cdot)$ is a monoid, but $0 \cdot 2 = 0 \cdot 3$ cannot cancel $0$ — what is missing is precisely inverses.

⚠ In a nonabelian group the order in $(ab)^{-1} = b^{-1}a^{-1}$ **must** be reversed: $(ab)(b^{-1}a^{-1}) = a(bb^{-1})a^{-1} = aa^{-1} = e$.

Writing $a^{-1}b^{-1}$ is generally wrong (test with two transpositions in $S_3$).

⭐ (iii) is the group version of `def.group-power`;

the proof is again "induct on nonnegative integers, then pass to negative exponents using inverses".
