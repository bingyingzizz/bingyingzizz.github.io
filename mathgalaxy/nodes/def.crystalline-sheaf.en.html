Statement

On Top/X\mathbf{Top}_{/X}Top/X​, giving a sheaf F\mathcal{F}F is equivalent to giving all FY\mathcal{F}_{Y}FY​ (Y→XY \to XY→X) together with compatible morphisms

αf:f−1FY⟶FZ(f:Z→Y).\alpha_{f} : f^{-1}\mathcal{F}_{Y} \longrightarrow \mathcal{F}_{Z} \qquad (f : Z \to Y).αf​:f−1FY​⟶FZ​(f:Z→Y).

If all αf\alpha_{f}αf​ are isomorphisms, F\mathcal{F}F is called a crystalline sheaf.

Notes

Reading. A sheaf on Top/X\mathbf{Top}_{/X}Top/X​ is: for XXX, on each space YYY, the datum FY\mathcal{F}_{Y}FY​, glued compatibly under pullback ✓; crystalline means precisely "pullback is the same thing" — the data depend only on the homotopy/homeomorphism type of Y→XY \to XY→X, not on the choice of YYY ✓.
