Statement

A first-quadrant spectral sequence E2p,q  ⟹  Hp+qE^{p,q}_{2} \implies H^{p+q}E2p,q​⟹Hp+q gives the five-term exact sequence

0⟶E21,0⟶H1⟶E20,1→ d2 E22,0⟶H2.0 \longrightarrow E^{1,0}_{2} \longrightarrow H^{1} \longrightarrow E^{0,1}_{2} \xrightarrow{\ d_{2}\ } E^{2,0}_{2} \longrightarrow H^{2}.0⟶E21,0​⟶H1⟶E20,1​ d2​ ​E22,0​⟶H2.

Notes

Proof (checking page by page). In the first quadrant d2:E20,1→E22,0d_{2} : E^{0,1}_{2} \to E^{2,0}_{2}d2​:E20,1​→E22,0​ is the only possible nonzero differential between these two terms ✓; E∞1,0=E21,0E^{1,0}_{\infty} = E^{1,0}_{2}E∞1,0​=E21,0​, E∞0,1=ker⁡d2E^{0,1}_{\infty} = \ker d_{2}E∞0,1​=kerd2​, E∞2,0=coker⁡d2E^{2,0}_{\infty} = \operatorname{coker} d_{2}E∞2,0​=cokerd2​ (d2:E20,1→E22,0d_{2} : E^{0,1}_{2} \to E^{2,0}_{2}d2​:E20,1​→E22,0​ is the only possible nonzero differential, and it kills part of E0,1E^{0,1}E0,1) ✓.

Assembling E1,0∞=Gr⁡1H1E^{\infty}_{1,0} = \operatorname{Gr}^{1}H^{1}E1,0∞​=Gr1H1, E0,1∞=Gr⁡0H1E^{\infty}_{0,1} = \operatorname{Gr}^{0}H^{1}E0,1∞​=Gr0H1, together with the filtration E2,0∞=Gr⁡2H2E^{\infty}_{2,0} = \operatorname{Gr}^{2}H^{2}E2,0∞​=Gr2H2 term by term gives the displayed chain ✓.

∎

⭐ Use: this is the standard tool by which "a spectral sequence directly yields a low-degree exact sequence"; the low-degree terms of H1H^{1}H1 and H2H^{2}H2 are read off from it.
