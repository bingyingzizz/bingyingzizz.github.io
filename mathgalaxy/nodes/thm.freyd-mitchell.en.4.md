# Freyd–Mitchell Embedding Theorem　`thm.freyd-mitchell`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=thm.freyd-mitchell)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./thm.freyd-mitchell.md)

⚠️ **Dual version**: the same argument gives a version embedding into $\mathbf{Mod}\text{-}A$ (right modules); $A$ is not unique and the construction depends on the chosen generator.

**Compared with Giraud's theorem**: Giraud describes a **topos** as "an exact reflective subcategory of a presheaf category", Freyd–Mitchell describes a **small abelian category** as "a fully faithful exact subcategory of a module category" — the two theorems are the same idea ("find a concrete large category to house oneself in") for different structures.
