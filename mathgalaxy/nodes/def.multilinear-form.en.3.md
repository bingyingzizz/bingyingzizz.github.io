# multilinear form　`def.multilinear-form`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=def.multilinear-form)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./def.multilinear-form.md)

**Multilinear $\ne$ linear, and both directions need care**: $f(x,y) = xy$ is multilinear but not a linear map; conversely $g(x,y) = x + y$ is linear as a whole, yet after fixing $y = 1$, $g(x,1) = x + 1$ is not a linear function of $x$.

⭐ It is the source of the "tensor product": an $n$-linear form is a linear functional on $V^{\otimes n}$ (see "universal property of the tensor product").

⭐ The determinant is the most special multilinear form in the case $n = \dim V$ (with the two extra conditions of alternation and normalization).
