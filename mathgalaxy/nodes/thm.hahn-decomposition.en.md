## Statement · **draft**
Let $\nu$ be a signed measure on $(X, \mathcal{M})$. Then there exist $P, N \in \mathcal{M}$ such that

$$P \cup N = X, \quad  P \cap N = \emptyset, $$

where **$P$ is positive and $N$ is negative**.

If $P', N'$ is another such pair, then $P \triangle  P' = N \triangle  N'$ is a $\nu$-null set.
