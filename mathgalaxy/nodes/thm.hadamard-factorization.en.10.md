**Step 4 (continued). The good-radius lemma.** Take $M:=\lceil\rho\rceil+3$ and $\delta:=r^{-M}$. The number of zeros in $[r/2,4r]$ is $N\le n(4r)\le C\,r^{\rho+\varepsilon}$. Each such zero excludes an interval of radii of length $2\delta$. Since $2\delta N\le2C\,r^{\rho+\varepsilon-M}<r$ for large $r$, there exists $R\in[r,2r]$ such that every $|a_{n}|\in[r/2,4r]$ satisfies $\bigl||a_{n}|-R\bigr|\ge\delta$. Fix such an $R$.

For $|z|=R$, split the terms according to $|a_{n}|\le4r$ and $|a_{n}|>4r$ in $\log|\mathcal{P}(z)|=\sum\log|E_{p}(z/a_{n})|$.
