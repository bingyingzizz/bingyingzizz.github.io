Statement

A sheaf on a topological space XXX (for the standard topology) is quasi-compact   ⟺  \iff⟺ the corresponding espace étalé is a compact space.

Notes

Proof. Quasi-compactness means "an epimorphism out of a coproduct admits a finite subfamily that is still epimorphic" ✓; in the concrete world of sheaves on XXX, "∐iFi→G\coprod_{i} \mathcal{F}_{i} \to \mathcal{G}∐i​Fi​→G is epimorphic" is exactly the map of espace étalé being surjective on each fiber, so "a finite subfamily is already epimorphic"   ⟺  \iff⟺ "the corresponding compact space is covered by finitely many pieces" ✓ — both sides hold simultaneously ✓.

∎

⭐ Reading: a "quasi-compact sheaf" on a topological space is exactly that "compact étale space" of geometry.
