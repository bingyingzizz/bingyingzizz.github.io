# abelian group　`def.abelian-group`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=def.abelian-group)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./def.abelian-group.md)

**Homomorphism groups**: for any group $G$, the set $\hom(G,A)$ (with $A$ abelian) is again an abelian group under pointwise operations; this is the simplest form of the statement that abelian groups are the enriched base of $\mathbf{Grp}$.
