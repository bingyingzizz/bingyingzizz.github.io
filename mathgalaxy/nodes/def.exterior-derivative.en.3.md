# Exterior Derivative: Coordinate Formula on Euclidean Open Sets　`def.exterior-derivative`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=def.exterior-derivative)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./def.exterior-derivative.md)

1. $\mathbb{R}$-linearity: $\mathrm{d}(a\omega+b\eta) = a\,\mathrm{d}\omega+b\,\mathrm{d}\eta$;
2. **Graded Leibniz rule**: when $\omega$ is a $k$-form, $\mathrm{d}(\omega\wedge\eta) = \mathrm{d}\omega\wedge\eta+(-1)^{k}\omega\wedge\mathrm{d}\eta$;
3. **$\mathrm{d}^{2} = 0$**: a direct consequence of the equality of mixed partial derivatives;
4. it is independent of the choice of coordinates (equivalently, it commutes with pullback; see `prop.pullback-forms`).
