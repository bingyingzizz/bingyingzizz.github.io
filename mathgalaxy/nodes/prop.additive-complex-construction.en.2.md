# Additive Functors Commute with the Complex Construction　`prop.additive-complex-construction`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=prop.additive-complex-construction)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./prop.additive-complex-construction.md)

$$C(F) : C(\mathcal{A}) \longrightarrow C(\mathcal{A}'), \qquad (K^{n}, d^{n}) \longmapsto \bigl(FK^{n},\ Fd^{n}\bigr),$$

that is, $F(C(\mathcal{A})) \cong C(F(\mathcal{A}))$: **taking the category of complexes first and then applying $F$ is the same as applying $F$ first and then taking the category of complexes**.
