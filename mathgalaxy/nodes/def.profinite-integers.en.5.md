# profinite integers　`def.profinite-integers`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=def.profinite-integers)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./def.profinite-integers.md)

📌 **Why it matters in condensed mathematics**: $\hat{\mathbb{Z}}$ is the "prototype" of all profinite spaces, and the projectivity of $\mathbb{Z}[\hat{\mathbb{Z}}]$ (the branch of @@thm.gleason@@ / @@lem.stonean-projective@@) is seen from here.

⚠ **$\hat{\mathbb{Z}} \ne \mathbb{Z}$**: $\hat{\mathbb{Z}}$ is an uncountable compact space and $\mathbb{Z}$ is a countable discrete group. ⛔ Do not write "they are the same after completion" — it is a **new** object, containing compatible information in the "infinite" direction (for example $\prod_{p}(1 - 1/p)$ and the like).
