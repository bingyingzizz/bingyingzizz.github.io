## Statement · **draft**
Let $\dim E < \infty$. Then any two norms on $E$ are equivalent; equivalently, for any basis $e_1, \ldots, e_n$ there exist $\alpha, \beta > 0$ such that

$$\alpha\,\|x\|_\infty \le \|x\| \le \beta\,\|x\|_\infty , \qquad \|x\|_\infty := \max_{1 \le k \le n} |x_k| .$$
