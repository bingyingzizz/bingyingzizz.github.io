Statement

If F≅F′F \cong F'F≅F′, then FFF is faithful (resp. full, fully faithful, essentially surjective, an equivalence)   ⟺  \iff⟺ F′F'F′ is as well.

Notes

Proof. A natural isomorphism gives a family of bijections on each pair of Hom-sets, compatible with composition ✓, so "is Hom⁡(X,Y)→Hom⁡(FX,FY)\operatorname{Hom}(X,Y) \to \operatorname{Hom}(FX, FY)Hom(X,Y)→Hom(FX,FY) a bijection" holds on both sides simultaneously ✓; essential surjectivity and equivalence are similar (isomorphisms translate objects) ✓. ∎
