# Affine Connection　`def.affine-connection`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=def.affine-connection)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./def.affine-connection.md)

$$\nabla_{X}Y = \sum_{k}\Bigl(X^{j}\partial_{j}Y^{k}+\Gamma^{k}_{ij}X^{i}Y^{j}\Bigr)\partial_{k}.$$

On a Riemannian manifold there is a unique connection that is **torsion-free** ($\nabla_{X}Y-\nabla_{Y}X = [X,Y]$) and **metric** ($X\,g(Y,Z) = g(\nabla_{X}Y,Z)+g(Y,\nabla_{X}Z)$), called the **Levi-Civita connection**; its $\Gamma$ are the Christoffel symbols.
