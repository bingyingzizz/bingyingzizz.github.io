## Statement · **draft**
Given any two sets, the collection of all ordered pairs is again a set:

$$\forall A \forall B \exists C \forall z [ z \in C \leftrightarrow  \exists a \in A, \exists b \in B, z = (a, b) ]$$

It is written $A \times B = \{ (a, b) : a \in A, b \in B \}$.

## Notes · **draft**
It is the prerequisite for defining relations and functions as sets: without $A \times B$ there is no such thing as $R \subseteq A \times B$.
