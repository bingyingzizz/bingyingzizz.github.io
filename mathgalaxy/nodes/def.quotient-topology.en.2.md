## Notes · **draft**
Intuition: **the open sets of the quotient are those whose preimage is open**. So properties of the quotient must be read "from above": whether two points in $Y$ can be separated by open sets asks whether their preimages can be separated by open sets of $X$.

The quotient and subspace topologies are opposite directions: one takes "the coarsest obtained by cutting", the other "the finest obtained by gluing back".

📌 The "quotient space" of topology is this one; the quotient of a **vector space** is `def.quotient-vs`.
