# internal hom　`def.internal-hom`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=def.internal-hom)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./def.internal-hom.md)

The internal Hom computed in the presheaf category agrees with that in the sheaf category: $\operatorname{Hom}_{\widehat{\mathcal{C}}(\mathbf{Ab})}(M,N) = \operatorname{Hom}_{\widetilde{\mathcal{C}}(\mathbf{Ab})}(M,N)$.
