在对象上 U↦Hom⁡K(V,U)U\mapsto\operatorname{Hom}_K(V,U)U↦HomK​(V,U)，在态射上把 T:U1→U2T:U_1\to U_2T:U1​→U2​ 送到前复合 T∘(−):Hom⁡K(V,U1)→Hom⁡K(V,U2)T\circ(-):\operatorname{Hom}_K(V,U_1)\to\operatorname{Hom}_K(V,U_2)T∘(−):HomK​(V,U1​)→HomK​(V,U2​)。

固定 WWW。反变 Hom 函子 Hom⁡K(−,W):VectKop→VectK\operatorname{Hom}_K(-,W):\mathbf{Vect}_K^{\mathrm{op}}\to\mathbf{Vect}_KHomK​(−,W):VectKop​→VectK​ 在对象上 U↦Hom⁡K(U,W)U\mapsto\operatorname{Hom}_K(U,W)U↦HomK​(U,W)，在态射上把 T:U1→U2T:U_1\to U_2T:U1​→U2​ 送到后复合 (−)∘T:Hom⁡K(U2,W)→Hom⁡K(U1,W)(-)\circ T:\operatorname{Hom}_K(U_2,W)\to\operatorname{Hom}_K(U_1,W)(−)∘T:HomK​(U2​,W)→HomK​(U1​,W)。

两个 Hom 集都按逐点和、逐点数乘成为 KKK-向量空间，且函子的态射部分线性，所以靶范畴是 VectK\mathbf{Vect}_KVectK​，不只是 Set\mathbf{Set}Set。

说明

函子性：Hom⁡(V,−)\operatorname{Hom}(V,-)Hom(V,−) 保恒等与复合；Hom⁡(−,W)\operatorname{Hom}(-,W)Hom(−,W) 反变，把复合顺序倒过来。

与对偶的关系：取 W=KW=KW=K，Hom⁡K(−,K)=(−)∗\operatorname{Hom}_K(-,K)=(-)^{\ast}HomK​(−,K)=(−)∗ 就是对偶函子；对偶空间是 Hom 函子在 KKK 处的取值。

内 Hom：在闭结构里 Hom 函子由内 Hom 对象表示；本图的内 Hom 是拓扑斯里的版本，表示关系是「内 Hom 表示 Hom 函子」。

Statement draft

Fix V∈VectKV\in\mathbf{Vect}_KV∈VectK​. The covariant Hom functor

Hom⁡K(V,−):VectK→VectK\operatorname{Hom}_K(V,-):\mathbf{Vect}_K\to\mathbf{Vect}_KHomK​(V,−):VectK​→VectK​

sends an object to U↦Hom⁡K(V,U)U\mapsto\operatorname{Hom}_K(V,U)U↦HomK​(V,U) and a morphism T:U1→U2T:U_1\to U_2T:U1​→U2​ to precomposition T∘(−):Hom⁡K(V,U1)→Hom⁡K(V,U2)T\circ(-):\operatorname{Hom}_K(V,U_1)\to\operatorname{Hom}_K(V,U_2)T∘(−):HomK​(V,U1​)→HomK​(V,U2​).

Fix WWW. The contravariant Hom functor Hom⁡K(−,W):VectKop→VectK\operatorname{Hom}_K(-,W):\mathbf{Vect}_K^{\mathrm{op}}\to\mathbf{Vect}_KHomK​(−,W):VectKop​→VectK​ sends an object to U↦Hom⁡K(U,W)U\mapsto\operatorname{Hom}_K(U,W)U↦HomK​(U,W) and a morphism T:U1→U2T:U_1\to U_2T:U1​→U2​ to postcomposition (−)∘T:Hom⁡K(U2,W)→Hom⁡K(U1,W)(-)\circ T:\operatorname{Hom}_K(U_2,W)\to\operatorname{Hom}_K(U_1,W)(−)∘T:HomK​(U2​,W)→HomK​(U1​,W).

Both Hom-sets become KKK-vector spaces under pointwise addition and pointwise scalar multiplication, and the morphism part of the functor is linear, so the target category is VectK\mathbf{Vect}_KVectK​, not merely Set\mathbf{Set}Set.

Notes draft

Functoriality: Hom⁡(V,−)\operatorname{Hom}(V,-)Hom(V,−) preserves identities and composition; Hom⁡(−,W)\operatorname{Hom}(-,W)Hom(−,W) is contravariant and reverses the order of composition.

Relation to duality: taking W=KW=KW=K, Hom⁡K(−,K)=(−)∗\operatorname{Hom}_K(-,K)=(-)^{\ast}HomK​(−,K)=(−)∗ is the dual functor; the dual space is the value of the Hom functor at KKK.

Internal Hom: in a closed structure the Hom functor is represented by an internal Hom object; the version used here lives in a topos, and the representing relation is "the internal Hom represents the Hom functor".
