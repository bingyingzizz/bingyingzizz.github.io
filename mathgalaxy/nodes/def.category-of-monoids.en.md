## Statement · **draft**
**The category of monoids** $\mathbf{Mon}$: objects are monoids and morphisms are monoid homomorphisms. The terminal object is the trivial monoid $\{e\}$; products are constructed pointwise; equalizers are submonoids; pullbacks are fiber products.
