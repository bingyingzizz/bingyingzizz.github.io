def.compactly-generated

Machine-drafted and pending author review. The Chinese text is authoritative.

Statement

A topological space XXX is called compactly generated (also a kkk-space) if it is a colimit of compact Hausdorff spaces.

Equivalently: the topology of XXX is completely determined by "continuous maps from compact Hausdorff spaces" — Y⊆XY \subseteq XY⊆X is open (closed)   ⟺  \iff⟺ for every compact Hausdorff space SSS and every continuous map f:S→Xf : S \to Xf:S→X, f−1(Y)f^{-1}(Y)f−1(Y) is open (closed) in SSS.

Notes

"The topology is determined by maps from compact Hausdorff spaces" means: a subset is open as soon as its preimage under every such map is open.

General topological spaces do not satisfy this — so compact generation excludes the spaces with "too few tests", and the remaining ones behave well under products and function spaces.

This is the entry point of the coreflection: kkk-ification (see "kkk-open, kkk-closed and the kkk-topology") turns any space into a compactly generated one, with a bijection between maps to other compactly generated spaces before and after.
