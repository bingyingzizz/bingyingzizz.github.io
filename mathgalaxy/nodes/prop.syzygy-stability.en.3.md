## Notes · **draft**
**Proof (induct on $n$, applying Schanuel's lemma at each step).**
For $n = 0$, we have $M \cong M$ and take $F = F_{1} = 0$ ✓.
For $n = 1$, the two precovers $0 \to \Omega M \to P_{0} \to M \to 0$ and $0 \to \Omega_{1}M \to Q_{0} \to M \to 0$ both have projective middle terms, so Schanuel's lemma gives $P_{0}\oplus\Omega_{1}M \cong Q_{0}\oplus\Omega M$ directly ✓.
For the inductive step, suppose $\Omega^{n}M \oplus F \cong \Omega_{1}^{n}M \oplus F_{1}$.
