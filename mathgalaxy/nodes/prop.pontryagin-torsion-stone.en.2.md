# torsion Stonean　`prop.pontryagin-torsion-stone`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=prop.pontryagin-torsion-stone)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./prop.pontryagin-torsion-stone.md)

- $M$ is **torsion** (every element has finite order) $\iff$ $\widehat{M}$ is a **Stonean** space (an extremally disconnected compact Hausdorff space);
- $M$ is **torsion-free** $\iff$ $\widehat{M}$ is **connected**.

More generally, the torsion subgroup of $M$, namely $M_{\mathrm{tors}}$, corresponds to the connected component of the dual, while $M/M_{\mathrm{tors}}$ corresponds to the Stonean part of $\widehat{M}$.
