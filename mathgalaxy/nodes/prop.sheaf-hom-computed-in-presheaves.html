Hom⁡C~(T#, F)  =  Hom⁡C^(T, F).\operatorname{Hom}_{\widetilde{\mathcal{C}}}\bigl(T^{\#},\ F\bigr) \;=\; \operatorname{Hom}_{\widehat{\mathcal{C}}}\bigl(T,\ F\bigr).HomC​(T#, F)=HomC​(T, F).

说明

证明。 层化 (−)#(-)^{\#}(−)# 与含入互为一对伴随 ✓，于是

Hom⁡C~(T#,F)=Hom⁡C^(T, F)✓\operatorname{Hom}_{\widetilde{\mathcal{C}}}\bigl(T^{\#}, F\bigr) = \operatorname{Hom}_{\widehat{\mathcal{C}}}\bigl(T,\ F\bigr) ✓HomC​(T#,F)=HomC​(T, F)✓
