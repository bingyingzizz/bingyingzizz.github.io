## Notes · **draft**
Note: $K \to L$ quasi-isomorphism $\iff$ $L \to K$ quasi-isomorphism ("quasi-isomorphism" is a symmetric phrase, although the map has only one direction); $K$ acyclic $\iff$ $[0] \to K$ quasi-isomorphic $\iff$ $K \to [0]$ quasi-isomorphic.

Two propositions: (1) if $K \to L \to M \to$ is a distinguished triangle, then $f$ is a quasi-isomorphism $\iff$ $M$ is acyclic; (2) for a morphism of triangles $(u, v, w)$, $u, v$ is a quasi-isomorphism $\iff$ $w$ is a quasi-isomorphism.
