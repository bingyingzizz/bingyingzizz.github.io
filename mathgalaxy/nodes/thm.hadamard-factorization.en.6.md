**Step 3. The quotient is zero-free.** Set $\Phi(z):=f(z)\big/\bigl(z^{m}\mathcal{P}(z)\bigr)$. At each $a_{n}$, $E_{p}(z/a_{n})$ has a simple zero that cancels a zero of $f$. At all other points, $f\ne0$ and the product is nonzero. Thus $\Phi$ is entire and nowhere zero.
