# Nilpotent Operator　`def.nilpotent`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=def.nilpotent)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./def.nilpotent.md)

**Equivalent conditions (often used as tests)**:

1. all eigenvalues of $T$ are $0$;
2. the characteristic polynomial is $x^{n}$;
3. $T^{n} = 0$ ($n = \dim V$ suffices — Cayley–Hamilton).

**Structure**: a nilpotent operator can always be brought to a direct sum of Jordan blocks $J_{k}(0)$; the order of the largest block equals the **nilpotency index** of $T$ (the least exponent at which $T^{k} = 0$ first holds, namely $k$).
