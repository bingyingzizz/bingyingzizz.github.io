For a field KKK, on the set K[x]K[x]K[x] of all univariate polynomials over it define

(f+g)i:=ai+bi,(fg)i:=∑j=0iaj bi−j(f + g)_i := a_i + b_i, \qquad (fg)_i := \sum_{j=0}^{i} a_j\, b_{i-j}(f+g)i​:=ai​+bi​,(fg)i​:=j=0∑i​aj​bi−j​

(addition termwise, multiplication convolution). Then K[x]K[x]K[x] is a commutative ring with unity, called the polynomial ring over KKK, and

deg⁡(fg)=deg⁡f+deg⁡g(f,g≠0)\deg(fg) = \deg f + \deg g \qquad (f, g \ne 0)deg(fg)=degf+degg(f,g=0)

Hence K[x]K[x]K[x] is an integral domain (no zero divisors): fg=0  ⟹  f=0fg = 0 \implies f = 0fg=0⟹f=0 or g=0g = 0g=0.

Notes draft

⭐ K[x]K[x]K[x] is the value of the “univariate polynomial functor on KKK”: K↦K[x]K \mapsto K[x]K↦K[x] is left adjoint to “sending KKK to KKK-algebras” (the free KKK-algebra).
