## Notes · **draft**
In other words: **a pretopos is determined uniquely by its topos**. On one side are "small objects", on the other "the small and good sheaves inside the topos"; the two are two names for the same things.

This rounds out the structure of this section: pretopos $\to$ with precanonical topology $\to$ site $\to$ sheaf category (topos) $\to$ recovers the "small and good sheaves" from the topos and returns to the pretopos.
