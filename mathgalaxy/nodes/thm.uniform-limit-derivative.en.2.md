# interchange of derivative and uniform limit　`thm.uniform-limit-derivative`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=thm.uniform-limit-derivative)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./thm.uniform-limit-derivative.md)

that is, differentiation and taking limits commute: $\bigl( \lim_n f_n \bigr)' = \lim_n f_n'$.

## Notes · **draft**
⭐ The key difference from the integral case: **differentiation amplifies errors**, so what must converge uniformly is not $f_n$ but $f_n'$.

One also needs the extra hypothesis that the sequence converges at one point; otherwise $f_n$ could differ by constants ($f_n + c_n$ has just as good derivative convergence).
