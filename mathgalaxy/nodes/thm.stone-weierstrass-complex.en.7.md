# Stone-Weierstrass theorem, complex case　`thm.stone-weierstrass-complex`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=thm.stone-weierstrass-complex)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./thm.stone-weierstrass-complex.md)

$$\lVert f - (g_n + i h_n)\rVert_\infty \le \lVert \operatorname{Re} f - g_n\rVert_\infty + \lVert \operatorname{Im} f - h_n\rVert_\infty \to 0 .$$

Hence $f \in \overline A$, i.e. $\overline A = C(K, \mathbb{C})$. ∎
