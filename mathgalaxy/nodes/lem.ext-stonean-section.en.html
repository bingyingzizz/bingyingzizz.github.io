Statement

Let M,NM, NM,N be condensed abelian groups and SSS a Stonean space. Write M⋅S:=M⊗ZZ[S]M \cdot S := M \otimes_{\mathbb{Z}} \mathbb{Z}[S]M⋅S:=M⊗Z​Z[S]. Then for all n≥0n \ge 0n≥0

Ext⁡Zn(M,N)(S)≅Ext⁡Zn(M⋅S, N).\operatorname{Ext}^{n}_{\mathbb{Z}}(M, N)(S) \cong \operatorname{Ext}^{n}_{\mathbb{Z}}(M \cdot S,\ N).ExtZn​(M,N)(S)≅ExtZn​(M⋅S, N).

Notes

What it does. The left side is "the sections of the Ext sheaf over SSS", the right side is "an ordinary Ext computed out". It trades the sheaf Ext for a single Ext — so "computing an Ext sheaf" becomes "computing one concrete derived Hom".

Why Stonean is enough. Over a Stonean space SSS, the group Z[S]\mathbb{Z}[S]Z[S] is projective, hence Hom⁡Z(Z[S],−)\operatorname{Hom}_{\mathbb{Z}}(\mathbb{Z}[S], -)HomZ​(Z[S],−) is exact. Apply this exact functor to both sides of the natural isomorphism

Hom⁡Z(M⋅S, N)≅Hom⁡Z(Z[S], Hom⁡Z(M,N))\operatorname{Hom}_{\mathbb{Z}}(M \cdot S,\ N) \cong \operatorname{Hom}_{\mathbb{Z}}\bigl(\mathbb{Z}[S],\ \operatorname{Hom}_{\mathbb{Z}}(M, N)\bigr)HomZ​(M⋅S, N)≅HomZ​(Z[S], HomZ​(M,N))

and the conclusion follows: the right side is "sections of an internal Hom", the left side is "tensor first, then Hom".

⚠️ The key hypothesis is Stonean (extremally disconnected), not merely compact Hausdorff — extremal disconnectedness is exactly the source of projectivity of Z[S]\mathbb{Z}[S]Z[S].

⭐ This is the connecting port for the spectral sequence below: the Breen–Deligne decomposition expands Ext into free objects, and the Ext of each term is brought back from a sheaf computation to an ordinary one by this lemma.

Proof. SSS Stonean   ⟹  \implies⟹ Z[S]\mathbb{Z}[S]Z[S] is projective, hence Hom⁡Z(Z[S],−)\operatorname{Hom}_{\mathbb{Z}}(\mathbb{Z}[S], -)HomZ​(Z[S],−) is exact.

For the natural isomorphism

Hom⁡Z(M⋅S, N)≅Hom⁡Z(Z[S], Hom⁡Z(M,N))\operatorname{Hom}_{\mathbb{Z}}(M \cdot S,\ N) \cong \operatorname{Hom}_{\mathbb{Z}}\bigl(\mathbb{Z}[S],\ \operatorname{Hom}_{\mathbb{Z}}(M, N)\bigr)HomZ​(M⋅S, N)≅HomZ​(Z[S], HomZ​(M,N))

(the adjoint form of M⋅S=M⊗ZZ[S]M \cdot S = M \otimes_{\mathbb{Z}} \mathbb{Z}[S]M⋅S=M⊗Z​Z[S]), take right derived functors on both sides: the left gives Ext⁡Zn(M⋅S,N)\operatorname{Ext}^{n}_{\mathbb{Z}}(M\cdot S, N)ExtZn​(M⋅S,N);

the right, since Hom⁡(Z[S],−)\operatorname{Hom}(\mathbb{Z}[S],-)Hom(Z[S],−) is exact, passes through the derived functor and gives Hom⁡Z(Z[S],Ext⁡Zn(M,N))\operatorname{Hom}_{\mathbb{Z}}\bigl(\mathbb{Z}[S], \operatorname{Ext}^{n}_{\mathbb{Z}}(M,N)\bigr)HomZ​(Z[S],ExtZn​(M,N)), which by definition is Ext⁡Zn(M,N)(S)\operatorname{Ext}^{n}_{\mathbb{Z}}(M,N)(S)ExtZn​(M,N)(S).

Compare the two sides.
