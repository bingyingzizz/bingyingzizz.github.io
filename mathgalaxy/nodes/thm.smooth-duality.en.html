Statement

In words: in the smooth case f!Rf^!Rf!R is a "twisted line bundle" given by the determinant of differential forms. This is the dualizing bundle ωX/Y\omega_{X/Y}ωX/Y​ (canonical bundle) of classical geometry.

Here ΩA/R1\Omega^1_{A/R}ΩA/R1​ is the relative Kähler module of differentials: formally generated by da\mathrm{d}ada (a∈Aa \in Aa∈A), modulo the Leibniz rule d(ab)=a db+b da\mathrm{d}(ab) = a\,\mathrm{d}b + b\,\mathrm{d}ad(ab)=adb+bda and dr=0\mathrm{d}r = 0dr=0 (r∈Rr \in Rr∈R) — that is, all "differential forms of AAA relative to RRR" (it is an AAA-module, and det⁡\detdet means its top exterior power).

ddd is the relative dimension, and [d][d][d] in the formula means the whole complex shifted to homological degree ddd.

Let f:R→Af : R \to Af:R→A be a smooth ring map of dimension ddd. Then there is a natural isomorphism

f!R  ≅  det⁡A(ΩA/R1)[d].f^!R \;\cong\; \det\nolimits_A\bigl(\Omega^1_{A/R}\bigr)[d].f!R≅detA​(ΩA/R1​)[d].

Since the isomorphism is natural and everything is defined locally, it globalizes to any smooth map f:X→Yf : X \to Yf:X→Y.

Notes

⭐ This is the dualizing complex: in the smooth case f!1Yf^!\mathbf{1}_Yf!1Y​ is an invertible sheaf (a line bundle) shifted to degree ddd, given by the determinant of the Kähler differentials ΩA/R1\Omega^1_{A/R}ΩA/R1​.

⭐ The proof uses the diagonal: g:A⊗RA→Ag : A \otimes_R A \to Ag:A⊗R​A→A is the regular closed immersion of codimension ddd (the diagonal Δf\Delta_fΔf​); split f!Rf^!Rf!R using g!g^!g! and the two projections A⊗RAA \otimes_R AA⊗R​A, then apply the formula for regular closed immersions to get

f!R⊗f!R⊗g!(A⊗RA)  ≅  f!R,f^!R \otimes f^!R \otimes g^!(A \otimes_R A) \;\cong\; f^!R,f!R⊗f!R⊗g!(A⊗R​A)≅f!R,

and comparing the two sides gives det⁡(Ω)[d]\det(\Omega)[d]det(Ω)[d].

⚠ This is the precise source of "coherent duality in the smooth case is Poincaré duality": ωX/Y=det⁡(ΩX/Y1)[d]\omega_{X/Y} = \det(\Omega^1_{X/Y})[d]ωX/Y​=det(ΩX/Y1​)[d].
