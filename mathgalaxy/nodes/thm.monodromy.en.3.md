# monodromy theorem　`thm.monodromy`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=thm.monodromy)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./thm.monodromy.md)

By continuity of continuation, $\varphi(s)$ is continuous; and “two elements are equal at the endpoint” is a closed condition (equality is preserved by limits), so $\{\, s : \varphi(s) = \varphi(0) \,\}$ is both open and closed and nonempty, hence all of it — that is, $\varphi(1) = \varphi(0)$.

∎

**Why simple connectivity is necessary**: for $\Omega = \mathbb{C} \setminus \{0\}$, going once around the origin and not going around are not homotopic, and the continuations of $\log$ differ exactly by $2\pi i$ — single-valuedness breaks precisely here.
