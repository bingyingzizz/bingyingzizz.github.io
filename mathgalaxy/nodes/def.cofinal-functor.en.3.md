# cofinal functor　`def.cofinal-functor`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=def.cofinal-functor)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./def.cofinal-functor.md)

**Examples**: $J \to \mathbf{1}$ is cofinal in $\iff$ $J$ connected; the inclusion of a directed set along $\le$, $\mathbb{N} \to \mathbb{Z}$, is cofinal; the inclusion of $\mathcal{C}$ into its reflective subcategory is in general **not** cofinal (reversing the direction gives coinitial).
