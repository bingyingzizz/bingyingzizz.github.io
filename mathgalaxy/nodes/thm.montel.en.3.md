# Montel's theorem　`thm.montel`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=thm.montel)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./thm.montel.md)

**The limit is holomorphic**: by `thm.weierstrass-convergence`. ∎

⭐ **Relation to compactness**: Montel says "$\mathcal{F}$ is relatively compact", so every **continuous** functional (e.g. $f \mapsto |f'(z_{0})|$) attains its maximum on a bounded closed family. The whole strength of the Riemann mapping theorem comes from this one sentence: among the family with "univalent + $f(z_{0}) = 0$ + $f'(z_{0}) > 0$", take the one maximising $f'(z_{0})$, then prove it must map onto the disc.
