# determinant　`def.determinant`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=def.determinant)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./def.determinant.md)

Basic properties: $\det I_n = 1$; swapping two columns changes the sign; equal columns give $0$; $\det A^{\mathsf{T}} = \det A$.

## Notes · **draft**
⭐ Geometric reading ($K = \mathbb{R}$): $|\det A|$ is the **volume** of the parallelepiped obtained by applying the linear map $x \mapsto Ax$ to the unit cube, and the sign is the **orientation**.

Hence $\det = 0 \iff$ the map flattens space $\iff$ not invertible.

⭐ This geometric meaning reappears in the change-of-variables formula for integrals: $\int f\,dx$ becomes $\int f\,|\det|\,dy$ — there $|\det|$ is the volume scaling factor.
