## Notes · **draft**
⭐ The proof is two sentences: **cosets form a partition** ($g_1 H \cap g_2 H \ne \emptyset \implies g_1 H = g_2 H$, using that $H$ is closed under multiplication) + **translation is a bijection** ($h \mapsto gh$ is a bijection $H \to gH$).

The full proof is on the edge.

⭐ Three immediate corollaries:
- the order of every element $g$ divides $|G|$ (take $\langle g\rangle$ as $H$);

- a group of prime order is cyclic (take any non-identity element; its order is either $1$ or $p$);

- $g^{|G|} = e$ holds for every $g$ — so Euler's theorem $a^{\varphi(n)} \equiv 1 \pmod n$ is a special case (take $G = (\mathbb{Z}/n)^{\times}$).
