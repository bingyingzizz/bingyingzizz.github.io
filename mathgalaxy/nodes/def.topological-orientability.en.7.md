**3. Homological characterization.** Let $M$ be a closed connected $n$-manifold. For any $x$, $H_{n}(M,M\setminus\{x\};\mathbb{Z})\cong\mathbb{Z}$. The image of the natural map $H_{n}(M;\mathbb{Z})\to H_{n}(M,M\setminus\{x\};\mathbb{Z})$ can only be $0$ or $\mathbb{Z}$ (Hatcher §3.3). $M$ is orientable $\iff$ this image is $\mathbb{Z}$ (equivalently, there is a **fundamental class** $[M]\in H_{n}(M;\mathbb{Z})$ giving a local orientation at each $x$).
