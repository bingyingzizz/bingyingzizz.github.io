## Notes · **draft**
**Proof.** By "the associated sheaf of a higher cohomology presheaf is zero", $H^{n}(M)$ has zero sheafification ✓; and Čech cohomology depends only on the **sheafification** of the presheaf (the Čech functor factors as two passes of $(-)^{+}$) ✓ — and the zero sheaf certainly has no Čech cohomology ✓. ∎
