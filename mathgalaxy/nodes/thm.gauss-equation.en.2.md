# Gauss Equation　`thm.gauss-equation`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=thm.gauss-equation)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./thm.gauss-equation.md)

Equivalent index-free form (the component on the surface of the fact that $\mathbb{R}^{3}$ is flat, with zero curvature):

$$\langle R(X,Y)Z, W\rangle = II(Y,Z)II(X,W)-II(X,Z)II(Y,W).$$

## Notes · **draft**
⭐ **The hinge of this packet**: not a single partial derivative is expanded. The curvature form on $\mathbb{R}^{3}$ vanishes identically, and after splitting, the surviving term $\omega^{1}_{\ 3}\wedge\omega^{2}_{\ 3}$ is the curvature — curvature equals the wedge of two extrinsic forms.
