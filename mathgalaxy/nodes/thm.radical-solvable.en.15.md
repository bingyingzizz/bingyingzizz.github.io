# solvable by radicals　`thm.radical-solvable`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=thm.radical-solvable)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./thm.radical-solvable.md)

**Solvable series**: Since $M_{i}/M_{i-1}$ is Galois, $\operatorname{Gal}(M/M_{i})\trianglelefteq\operatorname{Gal}(M/M_{i-1})$, and the quotient is isomorphic to $\operatorname{Gal}(M_{i}/M_{i-1})$, which is abelian. Thus $\operatorname{Gal}(M/F^{\prime})\ \trianglerighteq\ \operatorname{Gal}(M/M_{1})\ \trianglerighteq\ \cdots\ \trianglerighteq\ \operatorname{Gal}(M/M_{m})=1$ is a normal series with abelian quotients, so $\operatorname{Gal}(M/F^{\prime})$ is solvable.
