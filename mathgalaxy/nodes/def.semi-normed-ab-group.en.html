Statement

A seminormed abelian group is an abelian group MMM equipped with a function ∥⋅∥:M→R≥0\|\cdot\| : M \to \mathbb{R}_{\ge 0}∥⋅∥:M→R≥0​ satisfying

  • ∥0∥=0\|0\| = 0∥0∥=0;
  • ∥s+t∥≤∥s∥+∥t∥\|s + t\| \le \|s\| + \|t\|∥s+t∥≤∥s∥+∥t∥ (triangle inequality);
  • ∥−s∥=∥s∥\|-s\| = \|s\|∥−s∥=∥s∥ (symmetry).

Notes

⚠️ There is no requirement that ∥s∥=0  ⟹  s=0\|s\| = 0 \implies s = 0∥s∥=0⟹s=0. This is exactly what the prefix “semi” means. The elements of norm 000 form a subgroup M0={ s:∥s∥=0 }M_{0} = \{\, s : \|s\| = 0 \,\}M0​={s:∥s∥=0}.

Why start with seminorms. Completion starts from a seminormed space. Before completing it, one first quotients out M0M_{0}M0​. The definition of KKK-bounded exactness is most natural for seminormed groups, while the Banach setting gives the cleanest formulation. This is why both notions are retained.
