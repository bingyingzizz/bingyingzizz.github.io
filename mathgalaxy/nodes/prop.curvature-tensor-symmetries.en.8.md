**Why count components**: for $n = 2$ only $R_{1212}$ remains, so two-dimensional curvature **is one number** (the Gauss curvature); for $n = 3$ all $6$ components are determined by Ricci (the Weyl tensor vanishes); for $n = 4$, $20 = 10+10$, the second half being the Weyl tensor — that is where gravitational waves live.

⚠ **Sign convention**: here $R_{ijk\ell} = \langle R(\partial_{i},\partial_{j})\partial_{k},\partial_{\ell}\rangle$, so the unit sphere gives $R_{1221} = +1$ and $R_{1212} = -1$.
