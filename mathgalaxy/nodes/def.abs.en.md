## Statement · **draft**
Let $K$ be an **ordered field** (both $\mathbb{Q}$ and $\mathbb{R}$ are), and let $x \in K$. Define

$$|x| := \begin{cases} x, & x \ge 0 \\ -x, & x < 0 \end{cases} \qquad\text{i.e.}\qquad |x| = \max\{x, -x\}$$

Then $|x| \ge 0$ always holds, and $|x| = 0 \iff x = 0$.

## Notes · **draft**
⭐ Reading: $|x|$ is the **distance to the origin**, independent of sign.
