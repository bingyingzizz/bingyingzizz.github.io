Notes

⭐ The exterior algebra is a "polynomial algebra with signs": together with the symmetric algebra it forms the two extremes — the symmetric algebra allows swapping, the exterior algebra forces anticommutation.

Both come from the same construction: quotient V⊗kV^{\otimes k}V⊗k by the action of the symmetric group, or take invariants.

⭐ List of uses: differential forms (⋀k(Rn)∗\bigwedge^{k}(\mathbb{R}^{n})^{\ast}⋀k(Rn)∗ is the space of kkk-forms), volume and determinants, Clifford algebras (replace ∧\wedge∧ by v2=−∥v∥2v^{2} = -\|v\|^{2}v2=−∥v∥2), the de Rham complex.

⚠ It is noncommutative (odd part anticommutes), but graded-commutative: this is the easiest place to make a sign error when writing symbols (swapping two odd elements introduces a minus sign).
