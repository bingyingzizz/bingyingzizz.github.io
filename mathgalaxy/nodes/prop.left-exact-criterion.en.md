## Statement · **draft**
Let $\mathcal{C}, \mathcal{D}$ be abelian categories and $F : \mathcal{C} \to \mathcal{D}$ a functor. Then

$F$ **is left exact** $\iff$ $F$ **is additive and sends left exact sequences to left exact sequences**.
