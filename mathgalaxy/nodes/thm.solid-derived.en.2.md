# solid complexes and the derived category　`thm.solid-derived`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=thm.solid-derived)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./thm.solid-derived.md)

$$C \longmapsto C^{L\blacksquare} : D(\operatorname{Cond}(\mathbf{Ab})) \longrightarrow D(\mathbf{Solid}),$$

which is the left derived functor of $M \mapsto M^{\blacksquare}$.

## Notes · **draft**
⭐ This extends solidity from objects to complexes. It suffices to check cohomology in place of checking each term.

⚠ Together with (i), this is the full statement of Theorem 5.8. It is presented in two nodes because (i) is used for constructions and (ii) for computations.
