# CGWH Is a Reflective Subcategory of CG　`thm.cgwh-reflective`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=thm.cgwh-reflective)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./thm.cgwh-reflective.md)

Hence $R \subseteq R_{f}$ ($R$ is the intersection), so $f$ is constant on each class of $R$, and therefore factors **uniquely** through the quotient: $\exists!\, \bar f : X/R \to Y$ with $\bar f\circ\eta_{X} = f$ ✓.
- **$\mathrm{CGWH}$ sits fully faithfully in $\mathrm{CG}$**: when $Y$ is itself weak Hausdorff, $\eta_{Y} : Y \to Y/R_{Y}$ is a homeomorphism ($R_{Y}$ is $\Delta_{Y}$ itself), so the component of the reflector at $\mathrm{CGWH}$ is the identity ✓.
Together these say "$\mathrm{CGWH}$ is a reflective subcategory of $\mathrm{CG}$, with reflector $X\mapsto X/R$". ∎
