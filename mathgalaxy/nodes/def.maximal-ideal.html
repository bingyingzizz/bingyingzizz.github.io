又称极大理想·maximal ideal of a ring

陈述

设 AAA 是含幺交换环，M⊂AM \subset AM⊂A 是真理想。称 MMM 是极大理想，若不存在理想 JJJ 使 M⊂J⊂A.M \subset J \subset A.M⊂J⊂A. 等价地，商环 A/MA/MA/M 是域（见 商环）。每个真理想都含于某个极大理想（用 Zorn 引理）。

说明

⭐ 极大   ⟹  \implies⟹ 素：域一定是整环，故 A/MA/MA/M 是整环。反之不然：Z[x]\mathbb{Z}[x]Z[x] 中 (x)(x)(x) 素但不极大（(x)⊂(2,x)(x) \subset (2, x)(x)⊂(2,x)）。

⭐ 在 PID 中，非零素理想都极大：K[x]K[x]K[x] 中 (f)(f)(f) 极大   ⟺  \iff⟺ fff 不可约。

⭐ 例：Z\mathbb{Z}Z 中 (p)(p)(p) 极大；K[x1,…,xn]K[x_1,\ldots,x_n]K[x1​,…,xn​] 中 (x1,…,xn)(x_1,\ldots,x_n)(x1​,…,xn​) 极大。

Statement draft

Let AAA be a commutative ring with identity and M⊂AM \subset AM⊂A a proper ideal. Then MMM is a maximal ideal if there is no ideal JJJ with

M⊂J⊂A.M \subset J \subset A.M⊂J⊂A.

Equivalently, the quotient ring A/MA/MA/M is a field (see 商环). Every proper ideal is contained in some maximal ideal (by Zorn's lemma).

Notes draft

⭐ Maximal   ⟹  \implies⟹ prime: a field is a domain, so A/MA/MA/M is a domain. The converse fails: in Z[x]\mathbb{Z}[x]Z[x], (x)(x)(x) is prime but not maximal ((x)⊂(2,x)(x) \subset (2, x)(x)⊂(2,x)).

⭐ In a PID, every nonzero prime ideal is maximal: in K[x]K[x]K[x], (f)(f)(f) is maximal   ⟺  \iff⟺ fff is irreducible.

⭐ Examples: in Z\mathbb{Z}Z, (p)(p)(p) is maximal; in the polynomial ring in n variables, the ideal generated by the variables is maximal.
