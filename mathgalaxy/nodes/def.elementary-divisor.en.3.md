## Notes · **draft**
⚠ Disambiguation: the elementary divisors here are a matrix/module-theoretic notion, **not** the Weierstrass elementary factors $E_p(z)=(1-z)\exp(z+\cdots+z^p/p)$ (@@def.elementary-factor@@, entire functions in complex analysis). The two share only their Chinese name.

Relation to invariant factors: $d_i$ is the product of the elementary divisors grouped along the divisibility chain; conversely, the elementary divisors determine all invariant factors uniquely.
