## Statement · **reviewed**
**(a)** If $E \in \mathcal{M} \otimes  \mathcal{N}$, then for every $x \in X$ we have $E_x \in \mathcal{N}$, and for every $y \in Y$ we have $E^y \in \mathcal{M}$.

**(b)** If $f$ is $\mathcal{M} \otimes  \mathcal{N}$-measurable, then for every $x$ the function $f_x$ is $\mathcal{N}$-measurable, and for every $y$ the function $f^y$ is $\mathcal{M}$-measurable.
