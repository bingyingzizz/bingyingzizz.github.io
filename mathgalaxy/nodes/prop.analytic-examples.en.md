## Statement · **draft**
The following pre-analytic rings are analytic rings:

**(i)** $\mathbb{Z}_{p,\blacksquare}$ (the $p$-adic solid ring);

**(ii)** for any discrete ring $A$, $(A, \mathbb{Z})_\blacksquare$, whose free module is

$$(A,\mathbb{Z})_\blacksquare[S] = Z[S]^{\blacksquare} \otimes_{\mathbb{Z}} A.$$
