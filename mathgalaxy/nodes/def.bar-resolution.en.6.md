# Bar Resolution　`def.bar-resolution`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=def.bar-resolution)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./def.bar-resolution.md)

⭐ **Why "bar"**: the vertical bars in $[g_{0}|g_{1}|\cdots|g_{n}]$ were printed as horizontal bars in the old literature, hence the name. It is the most naive way of "pulling the product structure of the group apart one factor at a time" — no choices, purely combinatorial.
