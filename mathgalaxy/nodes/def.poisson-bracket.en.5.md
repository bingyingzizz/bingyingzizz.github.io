# Poisson Bracket　`def.poisson-bracket`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=def.poisson-bracket)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./def.poisson-bracket.md)

⚠️ **Relation to quantum mechanics**: Dirac's correspondence rule is $\{f,g\} \mapsto \frac{1}{i\hbar}[\hat{f}, \hat{g}]$. There is no unique quantization, so this is a rule rather than a theorem. It explains why the Jacobi identity must hold, since it corresponds to the Jacobi identity for commutators.
