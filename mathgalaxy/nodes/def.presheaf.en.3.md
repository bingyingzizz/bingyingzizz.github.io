# presheaf　`def.presheaf`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=def.presheaf)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./def.presheaf.md)

The presheaf of continuous functions $C^{0}$, the presheaf of smooth functions $C^{\infty}$, the constant presheaf $\underline{E}$ ($\underline{E}(u) = E$, with restrictions the identity), and the presheaf of constant functions are all examples.

- Fix $X \in \mathcal{C}$: $h_{X} : Y \mapsto \operatorname{Hom}_{\mathcal{C}}(Y, X)$ is a presheaf, called a **representable presheaf**.
