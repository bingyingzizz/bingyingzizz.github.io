# Vector Bundle　`def.vector-bundle`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=def.vector-bundle)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./def.vector-bundle.md)

Let $M$ be a smooth manifold. A **smooth vector bundle of rank $k$** is a map $\pi : E \to M$ (**smoothness of $\pi$ is not assumed**: it follows from a local trivialization $\operatorname{pr}_{1}\circ\Phi = \pi$ and smoothness of $\operatorname{pr}_{1}$), together with:

1. on each **fibre** $E_{p} := \pi^{-1}(p)$ a structure of $k$-dimensional real vector space;
2. **local trivializations**: every point has an open neighbourhood $U$ and a diffeomorphism $\Phi : \pi^{-1}(U) \to U \times \mathbb{R}^{k}$ such that $\Phi$ commutes with the projections, i.e. $\operatorname{pr}_{1} \circ \Phi = \pi$ and linear on each fibre.
