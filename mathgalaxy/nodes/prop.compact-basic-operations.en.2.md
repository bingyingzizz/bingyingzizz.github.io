# basic operations on compact spaces　`prop.compact-basic-operations`

## Notes · **draft**
**Proof.** (1) Given a closed subset $F \subseteq X$ (with $X$ compact) and an open cover, add $X \setminus F$ to obtain an open cover of $X$, take a finite subcover and delete that term ✓. (2) Given $f : X \to Y$ (with $X$ compact) and an open cover in $Y$, pull it back to an open cover of $X$, take a finite subcover and push it forward ✓.

(3) Given disjoint closed sets $A, B$: for each $a \in A$, use the Hausdorff property to produce an open neighbourhood $U_a$ of $a$ with $\overline{U_a} \cap B = \emptyset$ ✓;
