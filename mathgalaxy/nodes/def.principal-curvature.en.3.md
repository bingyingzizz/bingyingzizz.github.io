# Principal Curvatures　`def.principal-curvature`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=def.principal-curvature)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./def.principal-curvature.md)

**Principal directions are orthogonal**: eigenvectors of a real symmetric matrix belonging to distinct eigenvalues are orthogonal (spectral theorem). The same conclusion follows from the Rayleigh quotient together with variations; the treatment here uses the spectral theorem directly.

**Two characteristic equations**: $\det(W-kI) = 0$ and $\det(h-kg) = 0$ agree in an orthonormal frame; in a parameter net $\{r_{u}, r_{v}\}$ one must carry $g = \begin{pmatrix} E & F \\ F & G \end{pmatrix}$, so $g$ cannot be omitted.
