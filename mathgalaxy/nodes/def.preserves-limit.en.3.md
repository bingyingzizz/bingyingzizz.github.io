# preserves limits　`def.preserves-limit`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=def.preserves-limit)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./def.preserves-limit.md)

**Weaker versions**: $F$ preserves **finite** limits (= **left exact**), **filtered** colimits (the AB5 condition), **products**, **equalizers**, ... — each restricts "every diagram" above to a class of diagrams.

⚠️ Not to be confused with "$F$ is continuous": a continuous functor here **preserves limits**, with no relation to continuity in topology.
