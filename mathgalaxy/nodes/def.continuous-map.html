又称连续映射·continuous map·连续性

陈述

设 XXX、YYY 是拓扑空间。映射 f:X→Yf : X \to Yf:X→Y 连续，当且仅当每个开集的原像是开集：

V∈TY  ⟹  f−1(V)∈TXV \in \mathcal{T}_Y \implies f^{-1}(V) \in \mathcal{T}_XV∈TY​⟹f−1(V)∈TX​

只需对某一组基（或子基）验证即可。

连续的复合仍连续：fff、ggg 连续   ⟹  g∘f\implies g \circ f⟹g∘f 连续。

说明

⭐ 这条定义是 ε\varepsilonε-δ\deltaδ 的抽象化：在距离空间里，「f−1(V)f^{-1}(V)f−1(V) 开」与「ε\varepsilonε 给出 δ\deltaδ」逐字等价 —— 因为开球就是 ε\varepsilonε 邻域。

所以「连续」是拓扑性质，跟具体距离无关。

⚠ 用原像而不是像：原像对并、交、补全部保持，像没有这些性质。

这与可测函数的定义（f−1(E)∈Mf^{-1}(E) \in \mathcal{M}f−1(E)∈M）是同一个套路。

Statement draft

Let XXX and YYY be topological spaces. A map f:X→Yf : X \to Yf:X→Y is continuous iff the preimage of every open set is open:

V∈TY  ⟹  f−1(V)∈TXV \in \mathcal{T}_Y \implies f^{-1}(V) \in \mathcal{T}_XV∈TY​⟹f−1(V)∈TX​

it suffices to check this on a basis (or subbasis).

Composites of continuous maps are continuous: fff and ggg continuous   ⟹  g∘f\implies g \circ f⟹g∘f continuous.

Notes draft

⭐ This definition is the abstraction of ε\varepsilonε-δ\deltaδ: in a metric space, "f−1(V)f^{-1}(V)f−1(V) is open" and "ε\varepsilonε gives δ\deltaδ" are literally equivalent — because open balls are ε\varepsilonε-neighbourhoods.

So continuity is a topological property, independent of any particular metric.

⚠ Use preimages, not images: preimages preserve unions, intersections and complements; images do not.

This is the same pattern as the definition of a measurable function (f−1(E)∈Mf^{-1}(E) \in \mathcal{M}f−1(E)∈M).
