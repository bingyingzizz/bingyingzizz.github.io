# the adic spectrum　`def.adic-spectrum`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=def.adic-spectrum)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./def.adic-spectrum.md)

⚠ $\operatorname{Spa}(A,A^+)$ is in general **not** Hausdorff; it is spectral (compact, **sober**, **compact open sets** form a basis, and compact open sets are closed under finite intersections).

So metric-space intuition does not apply.
