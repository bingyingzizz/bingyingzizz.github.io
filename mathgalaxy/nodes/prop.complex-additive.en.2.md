# The Category of Complexes Is Additive　`prop.complex-additive`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=prop.complex-additive)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./prop.complex-additive.md)

- biproducts are taken **termwise**: $(K \oplus L)^{n} := K^{n} \oplus L^{n}$, $d_{K \oplus L} := d_{K} \oplus d_{L}$;
- kernels and cokernels are also **termwise**: $(\ker f)^{n} = \ker f^{n}$, $d_{\ker f} := d_{K}|_{\ker f^{n}}$;
- **limits and colimits in $\mathcal{C}(\mathcal{C})$ are computed termwise**.
