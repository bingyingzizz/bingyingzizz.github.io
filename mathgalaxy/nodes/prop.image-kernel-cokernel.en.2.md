# The Image Is the Kernel of the Cokernel　`prop.image-kernel-cokernel`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=prop.image-kernel-cokernel)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./prop.image-kernel-cokernel.md)

In particular the canonical morphism $\operatorname{coim} f \to \operatorname{im} f$ is an isomorphism — **every morphism in an abelian category is strict**.

## Notes · **draft**
⭐ This replaces the universal-property definition of "image" (the smallest intermediate object) by a **computable formula**: $\ker(\operatorname{coker} f)$. Everywhere in homological algebra where one "takes the image by hand", this is the formula used.
