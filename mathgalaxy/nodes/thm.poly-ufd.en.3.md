# K[x] is a unique factorisation domain　`thm.poly-ufd`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=thm.poly-ufd)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./thm.poly-ufd.md)

⭐ Uniqueness relies on "irreducible $\implies$ prime": if $p$ is irreducible and $p \mid gh$, then $p \mid g$ or $p \mid h$.

This step uses the **Bézout identity** (a product of the Euclidean algorithm), and is precisely the only place where the division algorithm is really used.

⭐ Uses: factorization of integer-coefficient polynomials in $\mathbb{Q}[x]$, the field structure of $K[x]/(f)$, and the relation between characteristic and minimal polynomials (next batch) all rest on it.
