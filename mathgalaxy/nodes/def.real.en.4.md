- Operations and order are **written using representatives**, so one must verify that they are **independent of the choice of representative** (well-defined) — the step most easily forgotten in a quotient construction.

⭐ The **generalization of this device is "completion of a metric space"**: replace $\mathbb{Q}$ by an arbitrary metric space $(X, d)$, replace $|x_m - x_n|$ by $d(x_m, x_n)$, and "take Cauchy sequences, modulo null sequences" carries over verbatim — the construction of $\mathbb{R}$ is the prototype.
