# surfaces of constant Gauss curvature　`thm.constant-gauss-curvature`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=thm.constant-gauss-curvature)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./thm.constant-gauss-curvature.md)

**Where the three solutions come from**: the characteristic roots of the constant-coefficient equation $f'' + Kf = 0$ are $\pm\sqrt{-K}$, and the sign of $K$ decides whether the solutions are trigonometric (oscillating), linear, or hyperbolic (exponentially growing). The initial conditions $f(0) = 0$, $f'(0) = 1$ are the geometric requirements of geodesic polar coordinates (circumference zero at $r = 0$, and $r$ is arc length).
