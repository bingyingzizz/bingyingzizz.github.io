## Statement · **draft**
Let $\{f_j\} \subseteq L^1$ satisfy

$$\sum_{j=1}^{\infty} \int |f_j| d\mu < \infty$$

Then $\sum_j f_j$ converges almost everywhere to a function in $L^1$, and the series may be integrated term by term:

$$\int \sum_{j=1}^{\infty} f_j = \sum_{j=1}^{\infty} \int f_j$$
