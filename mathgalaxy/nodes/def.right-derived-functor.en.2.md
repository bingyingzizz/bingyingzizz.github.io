# right derived functor　`def.right-derived-functor`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=def.right-derived-functor)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./def.right-derived-functor.md)

$$\begin{array}{ccc} K^{+}(\mathcal{A}) & \xrightarrow{\;F\;} & K^{+}(\mathcal{A}') \\[2pt] \big\downarrow & & \\[2pt] K^{+}(\mathcal{I}) & \xrightarrow[\;RF\;]{} & \end{array}$$
in which the vertical arrow means replacing an object by an injective complex (taking an injective resolution).

## Notes · **draft**
**How to read it**: $F$ itself is not exact (so it cannot be sent directly to the derived category), but first replacing the complex by an injective one, then applying $F$, and finally returning to the derived category makes this step exact — that composite is $RF$.
