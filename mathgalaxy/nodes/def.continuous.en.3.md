## Notes · **draft**
⭐ Replacing $|x - x_0| < \delta$ and $|f(x) - f(x_0)| < \varepsilon$ by a distance $d$ carries this definition over to metric spaces verbatim — which is exactly why the definition of a metric space looks the way it does.

⚠ **Continuous $\ne$ uniformly continuous**: $f(x) = x^{2}$ is continuous on $\mathbb{R}$ but not uniformly continuous (the larger $x$ is, the smaller $\delta$ must be).

The two notions coincide only on compact intervals.
