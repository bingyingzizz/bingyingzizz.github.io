Thus $\Phi$ flattens the piece of the bundle over $U$ into the **trivial product** $U\times F$: each fiber $E_{b} := \pi^{-1}(b)$ is sent to $\{b\}\times F$, and is therefore homeomorphic to $F$. If every point of $B$ has such an open neighbourhood with a local trivialization, $\pi$ is called **locally trivial**.

**Smooth case**: when $E, B, F$ are smooth manifolds and $\pi$ is smooth, one replaces "homeomorphism" by "**diffeomorphism**" in the definition; that is the local trivialization of a smooth bundle — without it even the tangent bundle would not be a smooth bundle.
