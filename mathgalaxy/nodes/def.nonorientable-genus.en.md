## Statement · **draft**
Let $M$ be a closed nonorientable surface. Its **nonorientable genus** $k(M)$ is defined by requiring that $M$ be homeomorphic to the connected sum of $k$ projective planes and taking the least such integer $k\ge 1$. Equivalently, $k(M)$ is the number of crosscaps in standard form, and the Euler characteristic satisfies $\chi(M)=2-k(M)$.
