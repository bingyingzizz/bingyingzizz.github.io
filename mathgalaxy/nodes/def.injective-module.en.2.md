# Injective Module　`def.injective-module`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=def.injective-module)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./def.injective-module.md)

$$\begin{array}{ccc} A & \subseteq & B \\[2pt] {\scriptstyle f}\downarrow & & \downarrow{\scriptstyle \exists\, \widetilde{f}} \\[2pt] I & = & I \end{array}$$

Equivalent formulation (dual to projective objects): the functor $\operatorname{Hom}_{R}(-, I)$ sends injections to **surjections**, that is, it is exact.
