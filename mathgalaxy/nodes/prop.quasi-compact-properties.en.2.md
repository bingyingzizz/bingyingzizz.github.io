## Statement · **draft**
1. In a site $\mathcal{C}$, $X$ is quasi-compact $\iff$ $X$ is quasi-compact in the standard topology;
2. If $X$ has a **finite** cover by **quasi-compact** objects, then $X$ is quasi-compact;
3. On a pretopos $\mathcal{C}$ (with the precanonical topology), a sheaf $F$ is quasi-compact $\iff$ there is a surjection $X \to F$ with $X \in \mathcal{C}$.
