## Notes · **draft**
**This is the most important theorem in number theory** (Gauss gave eight proofs in his lifetime, the first in 1796 at age 19). It turns the difficult question of whether $p$ is a quadratic residue modulo $q$ into the easier question of whether $q$ is a quadratic residue modulo $p$, since one can recursively reduce $p$ to $< q$.
