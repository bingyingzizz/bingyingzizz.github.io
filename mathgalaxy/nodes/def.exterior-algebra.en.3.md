# exterior algebra　`def.exterior-algebra`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=def.exterior-algebra)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./def.exterior-algebra.md)

⭐ List of uses: differential forms ($\bigwedge^{k}(\mathbb{R}^{n})^{\ast}$ is the space of $k$-forms), volume and determinants, Clifford algebras (replace $\wedge$ by $v^{2} = -\|v\|^{2}$), the de Rham complex.

⚠ It is **noncommutative** (odd part anticommutes), but **graded-commutative**: this is the easiest place to make a sign error when writing symbols (swapping two odd elements introduces a minus sign).
