Statement

Theorem (the ε\varepsilonε-δ\deltaδ criterion): the condition below is not a definition but a criterion for continuity in metric terms; for the definition see 度量空间的连续, and for the metric on R\mathbb{R}R see 实数的度量. f:R→Rf : \mathbb{R} \to \mathbb{R}f:R→R is continuous at x0x_0x0​ iff

fff is continuous (continuous on R\mathbb{R}R) iff it is continuous at every point.

Uniform continuity is a stronger requirement: δ\deltaδ depends only on ε\varepsilonε, not on x0x_0x0​.
