## Notes · **draft**
⭐ Convention: the zero polynomial has **no degree** — if one sets $\deg 0 := -\infty$, then $\deg(fg) = \deg f + \deg g$ and $\deg(f + g) \le \max\{\deg f, \deg g\}$ also make sense for it, which is the least troublesome bookkeeping.

⚠ **Polynomial $\ne$ polynomial function**: over $\mathbb{F}_2$, $x^{2} + x$ and $0$ agree everywhere as functions (substituting $0, 1$ gives $0$ in both), but they differ as polynomials (different coefficients).

In this graph “polynomial” always means the **formal object** (its coefficient sequence).
