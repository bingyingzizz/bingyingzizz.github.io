⚠️ **Structure of the proof (important)**: `prop.covering-index` gives only that $\pi_{1}(S^{1})$ and $\mathbb{Z}$ are in bijection, because $\pi_{1}(\mathbb{R}) = 1$ and the fibre is $\mathbb{Z}$. To obtain an **isomorphism** one must additionally prove that $\deg$ is a homomorphism — this uses "the lift of a concatenation is the translate of the lift" (uniqueness of lifts). Both halves are needed; see the proof on the edge.
