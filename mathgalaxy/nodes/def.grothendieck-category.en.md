## Statement · **draft**
A **Grothendieck category** is an **AB5** category with a **generator** (AB5 means: it has all colimits and filtered colimits are exact).

## Notes · **draft**
Examples: $A\text{-}\mathbf{Mod}$ is a Grothendieck category; for an abelian category $\mathcal{C}$, its $\operatorname{Ind}(\mathcal{C})$ (completed with respect to filtered colimits) is too;
