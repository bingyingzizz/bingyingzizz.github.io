## Notes · **draft**
⭐ A valuation satisfies the **strong triangle inequality** $|x+y| \le \max(|x|,|y|)$, the hallmark of non-archimedean geometry; replacing it by $\le |x| + |y|$ gives the archimedean side (complex geometry), a different theory.

⭐ Rational open sets are the regions defined by "$|g_i| \le |f|$" — intuitively the locus where "$g_i/f$ is bounded".

They are both open and compact (quasicompact), which is exactly what makes adic spaces handier than schemes: affine open sets carry their own compactness.
