# Vector Bundle　`def.vector-bundle`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=def.vector-bundle)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./def.vector-bundle.md)

**Examples**: $TM$ has rank $n$ (`def.tangent-bundle`); $M \times \mathbb{R}^{k}$ is the **trivial bundle**; the cotangent bundle $T^{\ast}M$ and the $(r,s)$ tensor bundles (`def.tensor-field`);

the Möbius band over $S^{1}$ is a non-trivial rank $1$ bundle, while $S^{1} \times \mathbb{R}$ is trivial — locally the same, globally different.

**Changing the structure group means changing the bundle**: replacing $\operatorname{GL}(k,\mathbb{R})$ by $\operatorname{O}(k)$ gives Euclidean bundles (with a fibre metric), by $\operatorname{GL}(k,\mathbb{C})$ complex vector bundles, by $\operatorname{GL}(k,\mathbb{Z})$ flat bundles.
