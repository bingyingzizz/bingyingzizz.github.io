# The Breen–Deligne Spectral Sequence on Stonean Spaces　`prop.breen-deligne-ss-stonean`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=prop.breen-deligne-ss-stonean)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./prop.breen-deligne-ss-stonean.md)

$$E^{p,q}_{1} = \bigoplus_{i=1}^{r_{p}} H^{q}\bigl(M^{s_{p,i}} \times S,\ N\bigr) \implies \operatorname{Ext}^{p+q}_{\mathbb{Z}}(M, N)(S).$$

## Notes · **draft**
**Construction.**

Base-change the Breen–Deligne decomposition $F(M)^{\bullet}$ along $S$ (tensor with $\mathbb{Z}[S]$).

Since $\mathbb{Z}[S]$ is flat, $F(M)^{\bullet} \cdot S \to M \cdot S$ is still a quasi-isomorphism;

then apply the section formula over Stonean spaces to replace $\operatorname{Ext}(M \cdot S, N)$ by $\operatorname{Ext}(M, N)(S)$.
