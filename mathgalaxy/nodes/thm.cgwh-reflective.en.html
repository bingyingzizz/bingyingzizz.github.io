Statement

The category CGWH\mathrm{CGWH}CGWH of compactly generated weak Hausdorff spaces is a reflective subcategory of CG\mathrm{CG}CG (compactly generated spaces).

The reflector is

h:X⟼X/R,h : X \longmapsto X/R,h:X⟼X/R,

where RRR is the smallest closed equivalence relation on XXX.

Notes

Why the "smallest closed equivalence relation". For the quotient X/RX/RX/R to be weak Hausdorff, RRR must be kkk-closed (previous item); and to quotient out as little as possible, so that the reflector is the "most economical" repair, one takes the smallest such closed equivalence relation. (R↦X/RR \mapsto X/RR↦X/R the larger the relation, the smaller the quotient — do not get the direction backwards.)

⭐ This gives the complete chain for "how to repair a space": kkk-ification (X↦kXX \mapsto kXX↦kX, producing CG\mathrm{CG}CG) ↦\mapsto↦ quotienting by the smallest closed equivalence relation (kX↦kX/RkX \mapsto kX/RkX↦kX/R, producing CGWH\mathrm{CGWH}CGWH).

Both steps are functorial, so isomorphisms, limits, and colimits in CGWH\mathrm{CGWH}CGWH can be transported from Top\mathbf{Top}Top.

⭐ This is why modern algebraic topology works in CGWH\mathrm{CGWH}CGWH by default: CGWH\mathrm{CGWH}CGWH is closed under finite products, the function space kC(X,Y)kC(X,Y)kC(X,Y) stays inside it, and it is cartesian closed — whereas Top\mathbf{Top}Top satisfies none of these.

Together these say "CGWH\mathrm{CGWH}CGWH is a reflective subcategory of CG\mathrm{CG}CG, with reflector X↦X/RX\mapsto X/RX↦X/R". ∎ ⚠️ Direction: the larger the equivalence relation, the more is quotiented out and the coarser the space. So the "smallest" closed equivalence relation gives the "most economical" weak Hausdorffification — which is why the reflector first kkk-ifies (X⇝kXX\rightsquigarrow kXX⇝kX), then quotients by the smallest closed equivalence relation, both steps functorial.
