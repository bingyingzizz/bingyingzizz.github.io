## Statement · **draft**
In plain words: the "best-behaved" objects in the solid category (compact and projective) are neither more nor fewer than the large products $\prod_I \mathbb{Z}$ — infinite versions of finite free modules. Moreover $D(\mathbf{Solid})$ is generated by them, so doing homological algebra there is like doing it in $D^b(\mathbb{Z})$.
