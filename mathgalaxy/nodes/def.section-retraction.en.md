## Statement · **draft**
Let $f : X \to Y$ be a morphism in a category.

- A **section** of $f$ is a morphism $g : Y \to X$ such that

$$f \circ g = 1_Y$$

- A **retraction** of $f$ is a morphism $g : Y \to X$ such that

$$g \circ f = 1_X$$
