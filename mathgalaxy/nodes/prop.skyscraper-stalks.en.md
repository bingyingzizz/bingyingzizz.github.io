## Statement · **draft**
Let $X$ be a topological space, $x \in X$, and $A$ an abelian group. Then the stalk of the skyscraper sheaf $i_{x,*}A$ at $y \in X$ is

$$(i_{x,*}A)_{y} \;=\; \begin{cases} A, & y \in \overline{\{x\}}, \\ 0, & y \notin \overline{\{x\}} . \end{cases}$$
