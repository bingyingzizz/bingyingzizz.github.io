## Notes · **draft**
⭐ This is the "affine model" of Grothendieck's coherent duality: $f_!$ is **cohomology with compact support**, $f^!$ is the **dualising complex**, and both the projection formula and Serre duality follow formally from the adjunction.

⭐ Difference from the classical approach: in classical coherent duality, $f_!$ in the affine case can only be manufactured artificially out of "formal functions";

here $f_!$ is a direct consequence of the adjunction pair ($j_! \dashv j^*$), and it **does not preserve discrete objects** — exactly the gain of the condensed method.
