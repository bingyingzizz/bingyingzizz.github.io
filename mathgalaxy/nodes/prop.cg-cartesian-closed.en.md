## Statement · **draft**
Let $X, Y, Z$ be compactly generated spaces. Then there is a homeomorphism

$$kC\bigl(k(X \times Y),\ kZ\bigr) \;\cong\; kC\bigl(kX,\ kC(kY, kZ)\bigr).$$
