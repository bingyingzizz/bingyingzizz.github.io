**Lemma 2 (sufficiently fine subdivision)**: $\{\operatorname{st}_{L}(w)\}_{w \in \operatorname{Vert}(L)}$ is an open cover of $|L|$. By continuity of $f$, $\{f^{-1}(\operatorname{st}_{L}(w))\}$ is an open cover of $|K|$. Since $|K|$ is compact, choose a Lebesgue number $\delta > 0$ (`def.lebesgue-number`). Every subset of $|K|$ with diameter $< \delta$ lies in some $f^{-1}(\operatorname{st}_{L}(w))$, so its image lies in some $\operatorname{st}_{L}(w)$.
