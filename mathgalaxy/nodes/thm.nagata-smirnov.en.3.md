**Proof sketch**
- Arrange the $\sigma$-locally finite base in layers. Each layer is a locally finite family, and regularity allows these to be turned into **locally finite open covers**.
- The key step (Nagata's systematic neighborhoods) is to construct a countable sequence of locally finite covers $\mathcal{G}_{n}$ ($n \ge 1$) such that for every open set $U$ and every $x \in U$, some member of one cover contains $x$ in its interior and is contained in $U$.
