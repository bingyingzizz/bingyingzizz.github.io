Compactness. PPP is a product of compact spaces (each [−∥x∥,∥x∥][-\|x\|,\|x\|][−∥x∥,∥x∥] is compact), hence compact by Tychonoff 定理; the closed subset Φ(BX∗)\Phi(B_{X^{*}})Φ(BX∗​) is compact; Φ\PhiΦ is a homeomorphism onto its image (continuous bijection, compact →\to→ Hausdorff), so BX∗B_{X^{*}}BX∗​ is weak* compact. ■\blacksquare■
