# Abelian Sheaves Have Enough Injectives　`thm.pushforward-enough-injectives`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=thm.pushforward-enough-injectives)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./thm.pushforward-enough-injectives.md)

1. Every sheaf has an injective resolution, and one can take it **bounded below**;
2. The right derived functors of the section functor $\Gamma(X, -)$, and of the pushforward along any continuous map $f$ — that is, $f_{*}$ — namely $R^{q}\Gamma$ and $R^{q}f_{*}$, are defined on bounded-below complexes and are independent of the choice of injective resolution;
3. $f_{*}$ sends injective sheaves to injective sheaves ($f^{-1}$ is exact and $f^{-1} \dashv f_{*}$), so the injectives "on the $f_{*}$ side" also suffice to compute $Rf_{*}$.
