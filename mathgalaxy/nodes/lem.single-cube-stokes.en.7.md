The two sides agree term by term, so $\int_{I^{k}}\mathrm{d}\eta = \int_{\partial I^{k}}\eta$. When $k = 1$, we have $\eta = f$ and $\mathrm{d}\eta = f^{\prime}\mathrm{d}x$. The two sides give $\int_{0}^{1} f^{\prime} = f(1) - f(0)$ and $f(1) - f(0)$, respectively, so the sign conventions agree. ∎
