⚠ "A limit, if it exists, is unique" and "convergent implies **locally** bounded" also come from the same definition (the function version only guarantees boundedness on some punctured neighbourhood of $x_0$: for $f(x) = x$ with $x_0 = 0$, the limit exists, yet $f$ is unbounded on its whole domain).
