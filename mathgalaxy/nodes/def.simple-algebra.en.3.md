# Simple Algebra　`def.simple-algebra`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=def.simple-algebra)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./def.simple-algebra.md)

Terminology: this map calls an algebra with no nontrivial two-sided ideals simple; the lecture calls this property quasi-simple and reserves simple for semisimple algebras with a unique isomorphism class of simple left ideals. In finite dimension the two notions agree (see thm.wedderburn-simple).
