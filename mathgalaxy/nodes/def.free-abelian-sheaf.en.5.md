# Free Abelian Sheaf　`def.free-abelian-sheaf`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=def.free-abelian-sheaf)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./def.free-abelian-sheaf.md)

sheaf cohomology is $H^{n}(X, M) = \operatorname{Ext}^{n}_{\mathbb{Z}}(\mathbb{Z}\cdot X, M)$ ✓.

⚠️ **It is not computed pointwise in general**: sheafification alters values, and "free pointwise, then sheafify" agrees with "free" only when the sheaf condition does not interfere.

📌 Example: $\mathbb{Z}\cdot \mathbf{1} \cong \mathbb{Z}$ (the constant sheaf). ⚠️ But it is **not** a projective object: in a sheaf category $\operatorname{Hom}(\mathbb{Z}\cdot X, -) \cong M(X)$ is only left exact, and "free $\implies$ projective" holds only in the **presheaf** category (@@prop.presheaf-free-projective@@).
