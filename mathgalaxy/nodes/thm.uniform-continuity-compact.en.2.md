## Notes · **draft**
⭐ The only price for upgrading "continuous" to "uniformly continuous" is **compactness** — it lets $\delta$ be chosen "all at once" instead of "point by point", and this is the template for every elementary proof that "takes a finite subcover and then the minimum $\delta$".
