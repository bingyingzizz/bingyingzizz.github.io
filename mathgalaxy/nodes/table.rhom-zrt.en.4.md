# Table of Derived Hom for Z, R and T　`table.rhom-zrt`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=table.rhom-zrt)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./table.rhom-zrt.md)

$$\cdots \to \operatorname{Ext}^{n}(M, \mathbb{R}) \to \operatorname{Ext}^{n}(M, T) \to \operatorname{Ext}^{n+1}(M, \mathbb{Z}) \to \operatorname{Ext}^{n+1}(M, \mathbb{R}) \to \cdots$$

Since the surrounding $\mathbb{R}$ terms vanish for $n \ge 1$, exactness determines the middle term:

$$\operatorname{Ext}^{n}(M, T) \;\cong\; \operatorname{Ext}^{n+1}(M, \mathbb{Z}) \qquad (n \ge 1),$$

and $0 \to \operatorname{Hom}(M,\mathbb{Z}) \to \operatorname{Hom}(M,\mathbb{R}) \to \operatorname{Hom}(M,T) \to \operatorname{Ext}^{1}(M,\mathbb{Z}) \to 0$.
