## Notes · **draft**
**Reading.** A sheaf on $\mathbf{Top}_{/X}$ is: for $X$, on each space $Y$, the datum $\mathcal{F}_{Y}$, glued compatibly under pullback ✓; crystalline means precisely "pullback is the same thing" — the data depend only on the **homotopy/homeomorphism type** of $Y \to X$, not on the choice of $Y$ ✓.

📌 This is one half of the definition "crystalline sheaf $\simeq$ sheaf on $X$" below.
