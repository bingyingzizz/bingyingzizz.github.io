Statement

For any X,Y∈TopX, Y \in \mathbf{Top}X,Y∈Top,

k(kX×Y)  =  k(X×Y).k(kX \times Y) \;=\; k(X \times Y).k(kX×Y)=k(X×Y).

If YYY is also locally compact Hausdorff, then the stronger statement holds:

kX×Y  =  k(X×Y).kX \times Y \;=\; k(X \times Y).kX×Y=k(X×Y).

Notes

The difference between the two formulas is exactly the two levels of the coreflective subcategory: in general kkk is not closed under products, and one must apply kkk once more to the product; when YYY is locally compact Hausdorff, (by the theorem on quotient maps and products) kkk commutes with "multiplying by YYY". This is why products in kTopk\mathbf{Top}kTop must be taken as k(X×Y)k(X \times Y)k(X×Y) rather than X×YX \times YX×Y.
