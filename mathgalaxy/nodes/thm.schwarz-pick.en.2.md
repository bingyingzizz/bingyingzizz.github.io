# Schwarz–Pick theorem　`thm.schwarz-pick`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=thm.schwarz-pick)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./thm.schwarz-pick.md)

$$\left| \frac{f(z) - f(z_0)}{1 - \overline{f(z_0)}\, f(z)} \right| \le \left| \frac{z - z_0}{1 - \overline{z_0}\, z} \right|,$$

In differential form (contractivity of the hyperbolic metric):

$$\frac{|f'(z_0)|}{1 - |f(z_0)|^2} \le \frac{1}{1 - |z_0|^2}.$$

Equality in either place (for some $z \ne z_0$, or for the derivative) holds iff $f$ is a fractional linear automorphism of $\mathbb{D}$.
