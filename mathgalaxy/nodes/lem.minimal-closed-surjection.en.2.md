## Notes · **draft**
The proof uses Zorn's lemma: order by **reverse inclusion** the family of closed subsets on which $f$ is surjective, and take intersections for upper bounds of chains — compactness ensures the intersection is still surjective (if it were not, the remaining compact set would be exhausted by a decreasing sequence of closed sets, contradicting the finite intersection property). Hence there is a maximal element, which is minimal in the original order.
