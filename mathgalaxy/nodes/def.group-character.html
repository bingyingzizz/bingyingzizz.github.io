Statement draft

Let (V,ρ)(V,\rho)(V,ρ) be a finite-dimensional representation of a finite group GGG. Its character is the function χρ:G→F\chi_\rho:G\to Fχρ​:G→F, χρ(g)=tr⁡ρ(g)\chi_\rho(g)=\operatorname{tr}\rho(g)χρ​(g)=trρ(g). It satisfies χρ(1)=dim⁡V\chi_\rho(1)=\dim Vχρ​(1)=dimV and is constant on conjugacy classes.

Notes draft

Conjugacy invariance follows from the cyclicity of the trace: χ(hgh−1)=tr⁡(ρ(h)ρ(g)ρ(h)−1)=χ(g)\chi(hgh^{-1})=\operatorname{tr}(\rho(h)\rho(g)\rho(h)^{-1})=\chi(g)χ(hgh−1)=tr(ρ(h)ρ(g)ρ(h)−1)=χ(g). Isomorphic representations have the same character. Characters add on direct sums and multiply on tensor products (see the node on character operations). A character is not a group homomorphism G→F×G\to F^\timesG→F×.
