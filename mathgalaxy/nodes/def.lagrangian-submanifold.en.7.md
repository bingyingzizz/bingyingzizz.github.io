English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=def.lagrangian-submanifold)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./def.lagrangian-submanifold.md)

is a Lagrangian submanifold ($L$ is immersed, and $\omega|_L = 0$ and $\dim = n$ hold). The direction $(\Leftarrow)$ is immediate pointwise. The direction $(\implies)$ requires that $L$ have constant dimension, which the immersion guarantees.
