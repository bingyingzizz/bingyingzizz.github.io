# Completely Regular Space　`def.completely-regular-space`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=def.completely-regular-space)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./def.completely-regular-space.md)

**Position in the chain**: normal space $\implies$ completely regular space $\implies$ regular space $\implies T_{2}$. The only non-trivial step is "normal $\implies$ completely regular" (Urysohn's lemma, `lem.urysohn`).

## Notes · **draft**
⚠️ **Naming convention**: the T-number denotes the bare property, and the named property denotes the bare property plus $T_{1}$.

Thus $T_{3.5}$ means that a point and a closed set not containing it can be separated by a continuous function (`def.t35-space`, without $T_{1}$), while complete regularity is $T_{3.5} + T_{1}$.
