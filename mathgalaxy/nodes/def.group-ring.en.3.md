# Group Ring　`def.group-ring`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=def.group-ring)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./def.group-ring.md)

**Basic properties**:
- when $G = \{1\}$, $R[G] = R$; when $G$ is finite, $R[G]$ is a free $R$-module **with basis $G$**, i.e. $R[G] \cong R^{\oplus |G|}$.
- $R[G]$ is **in general not commutative**: when $gh \ne hg$, $g$ and $h$ are distinct basis elements, and $g h$ and $h g$ differ accordingly. So $R[G]\text{-}\mathbf{Mod}$ is in general not a symmetric monoidal category.
- $g \mapsto g^{-1}$ gives $R[G]^{\mathrm{op}} \cong R[G]$. Thus "left modules" and "right modules" differ only by taking inverses — in group cohomology one need not worry much about left versus right.
