# law of quadratic reciprocity　`thm.quadratic-reciprocity`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=thm.quadratic-reciprocity)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./thm.quadratic-reciprocity.md)

$$\sum_{x=1}^{P} \left\lfloor \frac{qx}{p} \right\rfloor + \sum_{y=1}^{Q} \left\lfloor \frac{py}{q} \right\rfloor = PQ .$$

**Step 4. Combining the results.** Apply Step 2 in both directions, $(p, q)$ and $(q, p)$, and substitute the result of Step 3:

$$\left(\frac{q}{p}\right)\left(\frac{p}{q}\right) = (-1)^{PQ} = (-1)^{\frac{p-1}{2}\cdot\frac{q-1}{2}} ,$$

This is the desired quadratic reciprocity law ✓. ∎
