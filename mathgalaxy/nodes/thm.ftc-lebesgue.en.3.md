# fundamental theorem of calculus　`thm.ftc-lebesgue`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=thm.ftc-lebesgue)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./thm.ftc-lebesgue.md)

The three conditions split the work: (a) is the function-side condition (expressed via partitions of the interval), (b) is the integral side, and (c) is the derivative side. The theorem says they are the same thing.

⚠ Without absolute continuity the formula **fails**: the Cantor function satisfies the a.e. differentiability and integrability in (c), $F' \in L^{1}$, but $\int _{0}^{1} F' = 0 \ne F(1) - F(0) = 1$.
