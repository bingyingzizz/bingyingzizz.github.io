# Hurwitz theorem　`thm.hurwitz`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=thm.hurwitz)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./thm.hurwitz.md)

📌 Complementary to Morera's theorem: Morera handles "the limit is holomorphic", Hurwitz handles "where the zeros of the limit come from". Together they show that some **discrete** properties of holomorphic functions are closed under uniform limits.
