## Notes · **draft**
⭐ One-line reading: $g$ is just an arrow between categories, but once we pass to presheaf categories it automatically grows adjoints **on both sides** — $\hat{g}_{!}$ on the left and $\hat{g}_{*}$ on the right. Thus "functor" upgrades to "topos morphism" at the presheaf level.

⚠️ The direction is easy to reverse: $g : \mathcal{C} \to \mathcal{C}'$ while $\hat{g} : \widehat{\mathcal{C}} \to \widehat{\mathcal{C}'}$; **pushforward** $\hat{g}_{*}$ goes along $g$, while **pullback** $\hat{g}^{-1}$ goes the other way.
