Statement

Cat\mathbf{Cat}Cat has a terminal object (the one-object category 1\mathbf{1}1), an initial object (the empty category 0\mathbf{0}0), and finite products (product categories C×C′\mathcal{C} \times \mathcal{C}'C×C′);

and Op⁡(C)\operatorname{Op}(\mathcal{C})Op(C) is the kernel of the domain–codomain functors Mor⁡(C)⇉C\operatorname{Mor}(\mathcal{C}) \rightrightarrows \mathcal{C}Mor(C)⇉C.
