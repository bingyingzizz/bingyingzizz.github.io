# Flatness and Torsion-Freeness over a Domain　`prop.flat-domain-torsionfree`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=prop.flat-domain-torsionfree)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./prop.flat-domain-torsionfree.md)

$I$ is an ideal of $R$, and visibly torsion-free as an $R$-module; but it is not flat — $x, y$ is a regular sequence, and exactness of the Koszul complex gives $x \otimes y - y \otimes x \ne 0$ (in $I \otimes_{R} I$), while its image under $I \otimes I \to I$ is $xy - yx = 0$, so the multiplication map is not injective.

📌 In one sentence: **"torsion-free" is a divisibility property tested ideal by ideal, while "flat" demands it for ideals all at once**. Over a domain the latter is stronger, and over a PID the two coincide.
