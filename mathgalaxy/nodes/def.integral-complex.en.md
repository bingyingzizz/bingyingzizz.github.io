## Statement · **draft**
Let $f : X \to \overline{\mathbb{R}}$ be measurable, and write the positive and negative parts

$$f^{+} = \max\{f, 0\}, \qquad f^{-} = \max\{-f, 0\}$$

(both belong to $L^{+}$, and $f = f^+ - f^-$, $|f| = f^+ + f^-$). If **at least one** of $\int f^+$ and $\int f^-$ is finite, define

$$\int f := \int f^+ - \int f^-$$
