## Statement · **draft**
Let $f$ be continuous on $[a, b]$, and let $c$ be **any** real number between $f(a)$ and $f(b)$. Then there exists $\xi \in [a, b]$ with

$$f(\xi) = c$$

## Notes · **draft**
⭐ Geometric content: in passing from $f(a)$ to $f(b)$, the graph of a continuous function **cannot skip any intermediate value** — a continuous function maps an interval to an interval.
