**Step 4. Dominated convergence.** By Steps 2 and 3 and the dominated convergence theorem, $\int (\chi_{B(x_k,r_k)} - \chi_{B(x_0,r_0)}) f \to 0$. The coefficient $1/m(B(x_k,r_k))$ is bounded and tends to $1/m(B(x_0,r_0))$, so the first term tends to $0$. Adding the second term gives $A_{r_k} f(x_k) \to A_{r_0} f(x_0)$. ∎
