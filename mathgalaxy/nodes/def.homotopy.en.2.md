## Notes · **draft**
Null-homotopic morphisms form a **subgroup** of $\operatorname{Hom}_{\mathcal{C}(\mathcal{C})}(K, L)$; moreover homotopy is an equivalence relation **compatible with composition on both sides** ($f \sim g$ implies $h \circ f \circ k \sim h \circ g \circ k$).

Only the two facts together allow one to quotient by "homotopy".
