**Lemma 2 (convergence of the product).** If $u_{n}$ are holomorphic on a domain and $\sum_{n}|u_{n}|$ converges uniformly on every compact subset, then $\prod_{n}(1+u_{n})$ converges uniformly on every compact subset to a holomorphic function $F$, and the zeros of $F$ are exactly the zeros of the factors $1+u_{n}$, with multiplicities added. The partial products are uniformly Cauchy by the telescoping estimate $|\prod_{n\le N}-\prod_{n\le M}| \le \prod(1+|u_{n}|)\bigl(e^{\sum_{n>\min}|u_{n}|}-1\bigr)$, and the limit is holomorphic by `thm.weierstrass-convergence`.
