## Statement · **draft**
Let $\mathcal{C}$ be a site, $T \in \widehat{\mathcal{C}}$, $s \in T(X)$. Then on $s$, a sieve $R$ is a **covering** in $\mathcal{C}/T$ $\iff$ $\hat j_{T!}R$ is the **covering sieve** of $X$ in $\mathcal{C}$.
