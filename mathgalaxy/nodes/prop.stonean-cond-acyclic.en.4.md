**Proof.** The section functor is $\Gamma(S, -) = \operatorname{Hom}_{\mathbb{Z}}(\mathbb{Z}[S], -)$.
- **$\mathbb{Z}[S]$ is a projective object when $S$ is Stonean**: extremal disconnectedness gives projectivity over the lattice of clopen sets of $S$ (when $S = \beta I$, $\mathbb{Z}[S] = \mathbb{Z}[\beta I]$ is free, and free objects are projective);
- hence $\operatorname{Hom}_{\mathbb{Z}}(\mathbb{Z}[S], -)$ sends epimorphisms to epimorphisms — that is, it is **exact**;
