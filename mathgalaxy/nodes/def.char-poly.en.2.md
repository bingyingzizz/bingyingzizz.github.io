# characteristic polynomial　`def.char-poly`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=def.char-poly)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./def.char-poly.md)

Its roots (in $K$, or in an extension field) are exactly the eigenvalues of $T$, and their multiplicities are called **algebraic multiplicities**. Expanded,

$$\chi_T(x) = x^{n} - (\operatorname{tr} T)\,x^{n-1} + \cdots + (-1)^{n} \det T$$

## Notes · **draft**
⭐ The characteristic polynomial turns the **geometric** problem "eigenvalues" into the **algebraic** problem "roots of a polynomial" — so the fundamental theorem of algebra guarantees $n$ eigenvalues over $\mathbb{C}$ (counted with multiplicity).

⭐ Relation of coefficients to invariants: the coefficient of $x^{n-1}$ is $-\operatorname{tr} T$, and the constant term is $(-1)^{n}\det T$.
