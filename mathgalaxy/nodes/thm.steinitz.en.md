## Statement · **draft**
Let $V$ be a $K$-vector space, $S = (v_1, \ldots, v_n)$ linearly independent, $T = (w_1, \ldots, w_m)$ generating $V$. Then

- $n \le m$;
- one can **delete** $n$ vectors from $T$ and insert all vectors of $S$, and the resulting $m$ vectors still generate $V$. In particular, $V$ has a basis whose elements are taken from $T \cup S$ and which contains $S$.
