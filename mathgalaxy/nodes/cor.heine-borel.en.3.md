# closed and bounded iff compact in Rn　`cor.heine-borel`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=cor.heine-borel)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./cor.heine-borel.md)

⭐ An immediately usable consequence: the closed interval $[a, b]$ is compact — hence a continuous function attains its extrema on it, is uniformly continuous, and is integrable.

The three existence theorems of elementary calculus all rest on this.
