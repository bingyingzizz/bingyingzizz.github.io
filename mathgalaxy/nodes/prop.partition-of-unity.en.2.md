## Statement · **stale**
Let $V_1,\ldots,V_s$ be pairwise non-isomorphic finite-dimensional irreducible representations of $A$. Then there exist $a_i\in A$ such that $\theta_j(a_i)=\delta_{ij}\operatorname{id}_{V_j}$ for all $i,j$.
