# Bounded-Below Complexes of Injectives Model the Derived Category　`thm.k-plus-injectives`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=thm.k-plus-injectives)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./thm.k-plus-injectives.md)

The resolution is a quasi-isomorphism, hence an isomorphism in $D^{+}(\mathcal{A})$ — $K^{\bullet}$ is isomorphic to $I^{\bullet}$.

⭐ **Practical value**: this is the source of legitimacy for "computing right derived functors by injective resolutions" — the definition of the right derived functor $RF$ already requires replacing complexes by injective ones, and this theorem guarantees that the category on that side **is** the derived category itself, so nothing is lost.
