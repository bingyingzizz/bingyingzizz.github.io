## Notes · **draft**
**Proof.** The constant sheaf on $X$ takes as values the "**locally constant** $E$-valued maps" ✓ (sheafification turns the constant presheaf into locally constant maps), while the sheaf given by the discrete space $E$ takes as values the continuous maps $X \to E^{\mathrm{disc}}$ — which are exactly the locally constant maps ✓, so the two agree ✓. And "every locally constant map on $X$ is constant" $\iff$ $X$ connected ✓. ∎

⭐ This connects "constant" with "discrete": **the condensed set of a discrete space is the constant sheaf**.
