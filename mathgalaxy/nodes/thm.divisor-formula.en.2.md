## Notes · **draft**
**Why a product**: every divisor of $n$, call it $d$, has the form $d = p_{1}^{f_{1}} \cdots p_{r}^{f_{r}}$ with $0 \le f_{i} \le e_{i}$ — and by unique factorization this representation is unique. So "choosing a divisor" reduces to "choosing each exponent independently", giving $\prod (e_{i}+1)$ choices.

The same for $\sigma$, except that each column is a geometric sum: $\sum_{f=0}^{e_{i}} p_{i}^{f} = \dfrac{p_{i}^{e_{i}+1}-1}{p_{i}-1}$.
