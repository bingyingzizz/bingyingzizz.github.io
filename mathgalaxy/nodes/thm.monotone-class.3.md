## English abstract · **stale** (the Chinese wording changed since this translation) · machine-drafted, pending author review; Chinese is authoritative（续）
For $\mathcal{E} \subseteq \mathcal{P}(X)$, the $\sigma$-algebra generated by $\mathcal{E}$ equals the monotone class generated by $\mathcal{E}$ if complements of members of $\mathcal{E}$ and unions of two members of $\mathcal{E}$ lie in that monotone class. With "$\mathcal{E}$ is an algebra" this is the standard monotone class theorem; the form above weakens that hypothesis to the two closure conditions, and the proof is the same two-step argument with the monotone class as the running object.
