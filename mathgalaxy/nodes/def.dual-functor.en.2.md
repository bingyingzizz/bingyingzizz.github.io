# dual functor　`def.dual-functor`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=def.dual-functor)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./def.dual-functor.md)

Since $(S\circ T)^{\ast}=T^{\ast}\circ S^{\ast}$ and $(\operatorname{id})^{\ast}=\operatorname{id}$, it is a **covariant** functor from $\mathbf{Vect}_K^{\mathrm{op}}$ — equivalently, a **contravariant** functor on $\mathbf{Vect}_K$.

The natural transformation $\eta_V:V\to V^{\ast\ast}$, $\eta_V(v)(\varphi)=\varphi(v)$, gives $1_{\mathbf{Vect}_K}\implies(-)^{\ast\ast}$; in finite dimension $\eta$ is an isomorphism, so duality gives an equivalence between $\mathbf{Vect}_K^{\mathrm{fd}}$ and its opposite category.
