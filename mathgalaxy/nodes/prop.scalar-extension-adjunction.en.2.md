# Extension–Restriction Adjunction　`prop.scalar-extension-adjunction`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=prop.scalar-extension-adjunction)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./prop.scalar-extension-adjunction.md)

$$\operatorname{Hom}_{S}\bigl(S \otimes_{R} M,\ P\bigr) \;\cong\; \operatorname{Hom}_{R}\bigl(M,\ f_{\ast} P\bigr), \qquad g \longmapsto \bigl(m \mapsto g(1 \otimes m)\bigr).$$

The unit is $\eta_{M} : M \to f_{\ast}(S \otimes_{R} M)$, $m \mapsto 1 \otimes m$; the counit is $\varepsilon_{P} : S \otimes_{R} f_{\ast} P \to P$, $s \otimes p \mapsto s p$.
