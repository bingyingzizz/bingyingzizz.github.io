# Banach–Alaoglu　`thm.banach-alaoglu`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=thm.banach-alaoglu)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./thm.banach-alaoglu.md)

**Compactness.** $P$ is a product of compact spaces (each $[-\|x\|,\|x\|]$ is compact), hence compact by `thm.tykhonov`; the closed subset $\Phi(B_{X^{*}})$ is compact; $\Phi$ is a homeomorphism onto its image (continuous bijection, compact $\to$ Hausdorff), so $B_{X^{*}}$ is weak* compact. $\blacksquare$

⚠ The weak* topology is not the norm topology: $B_{X^{*}}$ is in general not compact in the norm topology (when $X$ is infinite-dimensional). The compactness here holds only in the weak* sense.
