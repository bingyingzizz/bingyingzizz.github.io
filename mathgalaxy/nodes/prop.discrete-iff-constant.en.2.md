# Discrete, Constant and Quotients of Compact Hausdorff Spaces　`prop.discrete-iff-constant`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=prop.discrete-iff-constant)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./prop.discrete-iff-constant.md)

1. $X$ is **discrete**: there is a discrete topological space $E$ with $X \cong \underline{E}$;
2. $X$ is **constant**: there is a set $E$ with $X \cong \widetilde{E}$ (a constant sheaf);
3. for every compact Hausdorff space $S$,
$$X(S) \;\cong\; \varprojlim_{S \twoheadrightarrow S'} X(S'),$$
where $S'$ runs over the **discrete quotients** of $S$.
