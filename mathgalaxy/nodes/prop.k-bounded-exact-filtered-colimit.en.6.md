# K-Bounded Exactness Is Stable under Filtered Colimits　`prop.k-bounded-exact-filtered-colimit`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=prop.k-bounded-exact-filtered-colimit)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./prop.k-bounded-exact-filtered-colimit.md)

⭐ Application: $K$-bounded exactness is a quantitative analytic property that persists when **passing to a larger complex by filtered colimit**. One can therefore check the condition on finite or simple complexes and then take the colimit.

⚠️ The **isometry** hypothesis is essential. If the transition maps are merely continuous and can decrease norms, the colimit seminorm becomes the infimum of the norms of the representatives, and $\|d s\| = \|d s_{k}\|$ above no longer holds.
