## Notes · **draft**
By thm.schur-lemma, $D=\operatorname{End}_A(V)$ is a finite-dimensional $F$-division algebra. For $a\in D$, $F(a)$ is a finite extension of $F$; since $F$ is algebraically closed, $F(a)=F$, hence $a\in F$.
Alternatively, $a$ has an eigenvalue $\lambda$, and $a-\lambda\operatorname{id}$ is not invertible, hence zero.
This is the source of "endomorphisms are scalars" in character theory.
