Thus "compute cohomology with the Čech complex" has a foundation — $\check{C}^{\bullet}(X, M) = \operatorname{Hom}_{\mathbb{Z}}(\mathbb{Z}\cdot X_{\bullet}, M)$ really is computing $\operatorname{RHom}$ from a projective resolution ✓ (see "Čech cohomology is the colimit over covering sieves").

⚠️ Note that "cover" here means the **whole family** $(X_{i} \to X)$: acyclicity is asserted for the whole Čech nerve, not just for one $X_{i}$.
