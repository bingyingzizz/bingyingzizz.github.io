## Notes · **draft**
**Proof of (a)**: The norming condition gives $\|x\| = \sup_{\|y\| \le 1}|\langle x, y\rangle| = \|T x\|$, where $T x := \langle x, \cdot\rangle$. Nondegeneracy makes $T$ injective, so $T$ is an isometric embedding. The argument for $y$ is analogous. $\blacksquare$
