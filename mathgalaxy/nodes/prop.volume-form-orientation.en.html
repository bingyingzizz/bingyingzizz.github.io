Statement

What this does: it gives orientability a criterion that needs no atlas — it is enough to write down an everywhere nonzero nnn-form on the whole manifold.

Let MMM be an nnn-dimensional smooth manifold. Then MMM is orientable if and only if there is an everywhere nonzero nnn-form

ω∈Ωn(M),ωp≠0  (∀p∈M).\omega \in \Omega^{n}(M),\qquad \omega_{p} \ne 0\ \ \bigl(\forall p \in M\bigr).ω∈Ωn(M),ωp​=0  (∀p∈M).

More precisely: two everywhere nonzero nnn-forms ω,ω1\omega, \omega_{1}ω,ω1​ necessarily satisfy ω1=f ω\omega_{1} = f\,\omegaω1​=fω with fff an everywhere nonzero smooth function; they give the same orientation if and only if f>0f > 0f>0 everywhere.

Hence on a connected manifold, "everywhere nonzero nnn-forms modulo positive smooth functions" is exactly "orientation", corresponding to the maximal oriented atlas of 可定向流形.

Notes

Two halves of the proof: (  ⟹  )(\implies)(⟹) on each orientation-preserving chart take dx1∧⋯∧dxn\mathrm{d}x^{1}\wedge\cdots\wedge\mathrm{d}x^{n}dx1∧⋯∧dxn; it changes by a positive smooth function under a change of chart, so a partition of unity sums it into a global ω\omegaω, and the positive coefficients keep it nonzero;

(⟸)(\Longleftarrow)(⟸) given an everywhere nonzero ω\omegaω, on a chart it equals f dx1∧⋯∧dxnf\,\mathrm{d}x^{1}\wedge\cdots\wedge\mathrm{d}x^{n}fdx1∧⋯∧dxn with fff everywhere nonzero; replace every chart with f<0f < 0f<0 by reversed coordinates — then all transition Jacobians are positive, giving an oriented atlas.

Details on the edge.

Why a partition of unity is unavoidable here: locally built objects have only the freedom of "multiplication by positive functions", with no cancellation mechanism — summing with positive coefficients guarantees no zeros. So this is one of the standard uses of 光滑单位分解.

Line-bundle language: ⋀nT∗M\bigwedge^{n}T^{\ast}M⋀nT∗M is a line bundle of rank 111 (the orientation bundle), and ω\omegaω is an everywhere nonzero section of it. Thus "orientable" and "orientation bundle trivial" are two ways of saying the same thing — the same criterion as "an everywhere nonzero section is equivalent to a trivial bundle" in 丛的截面.

"Everywhere nonzero" cannot be weakened to "almost everywhere nonzero": at a zero one cannot choose an orientation. Any nnn-form on a nonorientable manifold must have a zero — this is the precise form of "a volume form cannot be chosen globally on RP2\mathbb{RP}^{2}RP2".

Where connectedness is used: only in "f>0f > 0f>0 iff the same orientation". On a disconnected manifold fff may have different signs on different components, so two everywhere nonzero nnn-forms may agree only on some components — the sign issue must then be discussed componentwise.
