## Statement · **draft**
Let $R$ be a commutative ring with $1$ and $G$ a group. The elements of the **group ring** $R[G]$ are **formal finite sums**

$$\sum_{g \in G} a_{g}\, g ,$$

where $a_{g} \in R$ and $a_{g} = 0$ for all but finitely many indices $g$ of the $g$. Addition is componentwise, and multiplication is expanded bilinearly:

$$\Bigl(\sum_{g} a_{g} g\Bigr)\Bigl(\sum_{h} b_{h} h\Bigr) := \sum_{g,h}\, a_{g} b_{h}\, (gh) .$$
