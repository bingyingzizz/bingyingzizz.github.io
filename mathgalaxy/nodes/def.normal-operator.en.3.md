# normal operator　`def.normal-operator`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=def.normal-operator)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./def.normal-operator.md)

⭐ A self-adjoint operator has real eigenvalues, and eigenvectors for distinct eigenvalues are orthogonal — together these two facts are the content of the spectral theorem.

⚠ "Normal" unifies unitary and self-adjoint only in the complex case: real symmetric (self-adjoint) and real orthogonal (unitary) are special cases of real normal operators, but in the real case they **need not be orthogonally diagonalizable** (rotation by $90^\circ$ is orthogonal and normal, yet has no real eigenvalue) — the spectral theorem in the real case requires **symmetry**.
