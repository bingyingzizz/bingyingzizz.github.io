# basic properties of extremally disconnected spaces　`prop.stonean-basic`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=prop.stonean-basic)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./prop.stonean-basic.md)

∎
(Equivalent phrasing: extremally disconnected $\iff$ the closures of disjoint open sets are disjoint.)
**(b) made precise**: by (a), the separating open sets $U\ni x$, $V\ni y$ given by Hausdorffness satisfy $\overline U\cap\overline V = \emptyset$;

and $\overline U$, $\overline V$ are both **clopen** (extremal disconnectedness), so they form an open-closed separation between $x$ and $y$: the connected component of $x$ lies in $\overline U$, hence does not contain $y$ ✓.

Thus every connected component is a point, i.e. the space is totally disconnected.
