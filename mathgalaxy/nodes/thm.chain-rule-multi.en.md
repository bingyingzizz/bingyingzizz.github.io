## Statement · **draft**
Let $U \subseteq \mathbb{R}^{n}$ and $V \subseteq \mathbb{R}^{m}$ be open, $f : U \to V$ differentiable at $a \in U$, and $g : V \to \mathbb{R}^{p}$ differentiable at $b = f(a)$. Then $g \circ f$ is differentiable at $a$, and

$$D(g \circ f)(a) = Dg\bigl(f(a)\bigr) \circ Df(a)$$
