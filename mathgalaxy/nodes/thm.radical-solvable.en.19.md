# solvable by radicals　`thm.radical-solvable`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=thm.radical-solvable)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./thm.radical-solvable.md)

📌 **This theorem is the entry point to the quintic problem**: It turns the question of whether a radical formula exists into a purely group-theoretic question ($S_{n}$ is solvable $\iff n\le4$, see the solvability of symmetric groups). When applying it in reverse, distinguish the absence of a radical solution from the absence of any solution: $\operatorname{Gal}(f)$ is not solvable $\implies$ has no solution by radicals.
