# Embedding a Category into Its Complexes　`prop.category-into-complexes`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=prop.category-into-complexes)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./prop.category-into-complexes.md)

$$\mathcal{C} \longrightarrow \mathcal{C}(\mathcal{C}), \qquad M \longmapsto [M], \qquad [M]^{n} := \begin{cases} M, & n = 0 \\ 0, & n \ne 0\end{cases}$$

(all differentials zero). Then:

1. this functor is **fully faithful**;
2. it **preserves all limits and all colimits**.
