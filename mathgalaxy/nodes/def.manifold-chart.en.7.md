# Chart and Transition Maps　`def.manifold-chart`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=def.manifold-chart)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./def.manifold-chart.md)

**Coordinates are not part of the manifold**: one chart admits infinitely many descriptions (any diffeomorphism of $\mathbb{R}^{n}$ gives new coordinates); what belongs to the manifold is the existence of a pairwise compatible family. Distinguishing these two facts motivates the maximal atlas.

**Dimension is pinned by charts**: if two charts on $M$ have overlapping domains of dimensions $n$ and $m$, then open subsets of $\mathbb{R}^{n}$ and $\mathbb{R}^{m}$ are homeomorphic, so Brouwer's invariance of domain gives $n = m$. Hence "$n$-dimensional" is not an extra choice.
