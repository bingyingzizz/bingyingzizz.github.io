## English abstract · reviewed by bingyingzizz (2026-10-16) · Chinese is authoritative（续）
The category $\mathbf{CHaus}$ is a reflective subcategory of $\mathbf{Top}$, the reflection being the Stone–Čech compactification: $\operatorname{Hom}_{\mathbf{CHaus}}(\beta X, K) \cong \operatorname{Hom}_{\mathbf{Top}}(X, K)$ for every compact Hausdorff $K$. Construction: embed $X$ into the Tychonoff cube $[0,1]^{C(X,[0,1])}$ and take the closure, which is compact Hausdorff as a closed subset of a compact Hausdorff space. The embedding is injective iff $X$ is Tychonoff. Corollary: any limit of compact Hausdorff spaces is again compact Hausdorff.
