# Linearity and Additivity of the Integral　`prop.integral-linear`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=prop.integral-linear)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./prop.integral-linear.md)

⚠ The **product** of two integrable functions is integrable (amplitude estimate: $\omega(fg) \le M\omega(g) + N\omega(f)$ on each subinterval), but the **quotient** need not be: one must first ensure the denominator stays away from zero.

⭐ Integrability of sums and of products both rest on the amplitude criterion: substitute $\omega(f + g) \le \omega(f) + \omega(g)$.
