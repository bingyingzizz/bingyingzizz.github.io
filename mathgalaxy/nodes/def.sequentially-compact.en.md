## Statement · **draft**
A metric space $(X, d)$ is **sequentially compact** iff every sequence in $X$ has a subsequence converging in $X$:

$$\forall\{x_n\} \subseteq X,\ \exists\{x_{n_k}\},\ \exists x \in X : x_{n_k} \to x$$
