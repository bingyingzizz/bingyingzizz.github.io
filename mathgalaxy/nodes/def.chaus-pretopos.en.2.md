## Notes · **draft**
Item 1 is read off directly from "$\mathbf{CHaus}$ is a reflective subcategory of $\mathbf{Top}$":

$$\varprojlim{}^{\mathbf{CHaus}} D \;\cong\; \varprojlim{}^{\mathbf{Top}} D, \qquad \varinjlim{}^{\mathbf{CHaus}} D \;\cong\; \beta\Bigl(\varinjlim{}^{\mathbf{Top}} D\Bigr)$$

**Limits are computed as in $\mathbf{Top}$** (a reflective subcategory is closed under limits), while **colimits must first be computed in $\mathbf{Top}$ and then compactified back by $\beta$** (a left adjoint preserves colimits).
