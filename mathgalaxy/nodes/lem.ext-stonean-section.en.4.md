⚠️ The key hypothesis is **Stonean** (extremally disconnected), not merely compact Hausdorff — extremal disconnectedness is exactly the source of projectivity of $\mathbb{Z}[S]$.

⭐ This is the **connecting port** for the spectral sequence below: the Breen–Deligne decomposition expands Ext into free objects, and the Ext of each term is brought back from a sheaf computation to an ordinary one by this lemma.

**Proof.** $S$ Stonean $\implies$ $\mathbb{Z}[S]$ is **projective**, hence $\operatorname{Hom}_{\mathbb{Z}}(\mathbb{Z}[S], -)$ is **exact**.
