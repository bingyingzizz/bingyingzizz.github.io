**Proof.** $S$ Stonean $\implies$ $\mathbb{Z}[S]$ is **projective**, hence $\operatorname{Hom}_{\mathbb{Z}}(\mathbb{Z}[S], -)$ is **exact**.

For the natural isomorphism

$$\operatorname{Hom}_{\mathbb{Z}}(M \cdot S,\ N) \cong \operatorname{Hom}_{\mathbb{Z}}\bigl(\mathbb{Z}[S],\ \operatorname{Hom}_{\mathbb{Z}}(M, N)\bigr)$$
