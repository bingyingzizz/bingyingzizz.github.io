# Stone–Čech compactification　`prop.chaus-reflective`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=prop.chaus-reflective)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./prop.chaus-reflective.md)

Corollary: **any limit of compact Hausdorff spaces is again compact Hausdorff**. This is what the general statement "a reflective subcategory is closed under limits" looks like here — from now on, taking limits in $\mathbf{CHaus}$ no longer requires re-checking compactness and Hausdorffness.
