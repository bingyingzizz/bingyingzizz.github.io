Statement

Let GGG be a finite group. A representation of GGG over FFF is a pair (V,ρ)(V,\rho)(V,ρ), where VVV is a finite-dimensional FFF-vector space and ρ:G→GL(V)\rho:G\to GL(V)ρ:G→GL(V) is a group homomorphism. It is equivalent to a unital left F[G]F[G]F[G]-module structure on VVV; hence representations of GGG are the same as unital representations of the group algebra F[G]F[G]F[G].
