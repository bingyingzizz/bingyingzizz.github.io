# Frobenius theorem　`thm.frobenius`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=thm.frobenius)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./thm.frobenius.md)

In particular, involutivity holds **if and only if** there are always local coordinates that straighten $\mathcal{D}$ into coordinate slices. When $k = n-1$ and $\mathcal{D} = \ker\omega$ (with $\omega$ nowhere zero), involutivity is equivalent to

$$\omega \wedge \mathrm{d}\omega = 0,$$

This is the classical Frobenius condition. Its proof is the explicit calculation for hyperplane distributions corresponding to the flow-box argument for (1)$\iff$(2) below.
