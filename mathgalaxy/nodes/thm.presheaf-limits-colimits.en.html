Statement

In a presheaf category C^\widehat{\mathcal{C}}C all small limits and small colimits exist, and they are computed pointwise: for every X∈CX \in \mathcal{C}X∈C,

(lim→⁡iTi)(X)  ≅  lim→⁡iTi(X),(lim←⁡iTi)(X)  ≅  lim←⁡iTi(X).\Bigl(\varinjlim_{i} T_{i}\Bigr)(X) \;\cong\; \varinjlim_{i} T_{i}(X), \qquad \Bigl(\varprojlim_{i} T_{i}\Bigr)(X) \;\cong\; \varprojlim_{i} T_{i}(X).(ilim​​Ti​)(X)≅ilim​​Ti​(X),(ilim​​Ti​)(X)≅ilim​​Ti​(X).

Equivalently: for every XXX, the evaluation functor C^→Set\widehat{\mathcal{C}} \to \mathbf{Set}C→Set, T↦T(X)T \mapsto T(X)T↦T(X), preserves all limits and all colimits.

Notes

Proof. Two steps.

(i) The pointwise definition really gives limits (colimits). In Set\mathbf{Set}Set, for each X∈CX \in \mathcal{C}X∈C take the limit (or colimit) of that family of sets; for a morphism f:Y→Xf : Y \to Xf:Y→X, the restriction map (lim←⁡Ti)(X)→(lim←⁡Ti)(Y)\bigl(\varprojlim T_i\bigr)(X) \to \bigl(\varprojlim T_i\bigr)(Y)(lim​Ti​)(X)→(lim​Ti​)(Y) is given by the universal property of the limit (on the colimit side by that of the colimit) ✓.

The resulting presheaf, together with the pointwise cone (cocone), satisfies the universal property: a map from a cone to lim←⁡Ti\varprojlim T_ilim​Ti​ is uniquely determined at each point XXX, and the universal property is stated pointwise, so it holds at every point and hence globally ✓.

(ii) Set\mathbf{Set}Set is complete and cocomplete.

  • Small limits exist: small products exist (the axiom of choice provides a set of choice functions); equalizers exist ({x:f(x)=g(x)}\{x : f(x) = g(x)\}{x:f(x)=g(x)} is a subset); and limits are built from products and equalizers, so all small limits exist ✓.
  • Small colimits exist: small coproducts are disjoint unions; coequalizers are quotient sets; dually all small colimits exist ✓.

The two steps combine: taking the limit (colimit) pointwise for each XXX in Set\mathbf{Set}Set gives the limit (colimit) in C^\widehat{\mathcal{C}}C ✓, and "the evaluation functor preserves limits and colimits" is another way of saying "computed pointwise" ✓. ∎

⭐ This is the foundation of almost every later construction: the advantage of a presheaf category is precisely that "limits and colimits come for free", so one can perform all sorts of formal constructions inside it and then sheafify the result.

⚠️ "Computed pointwise" holds only for limits and colimits, not for general constructions — sheafification, internal Hom, and sheaf cohomology are not pointwise.
