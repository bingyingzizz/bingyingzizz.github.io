# chain homotopy　`def.chain-homotopy`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=def.chain-homotopy)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./def.chain-homotopy.md)

⭐ **It is the algebraic translation of "homotopy"**: a homotopy in topology is "a continuous deformation between two maps"; a chain complex has no space to deform, so the deformation is turned into a homomorphism $P$ **raising degree by one**. The combination $\partial P + P\partial$ is exactly what "homotopy of chain complexes" should look like: it **commutes** with $\partial$ ($\partial T = T\partial$, so $T$ is a chain map) and therefore sends cycles to boundaries; the anticommuting relation is the termwise relation between $P$ and $\partial$ ($\partial P = T - P\partial$).
