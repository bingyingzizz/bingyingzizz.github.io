Statement

The last axiom in the definition of a Grothendieck topology may be replaced by: for all R⊆R′⊆hXR \subseteq R' \subseteq h_{X}R⊆R′⊆hX​,

R∈J(X)  ⟹  R′∈J(X),(R′∈J(X) ∧ ∀f∈R′(Y), f−1(R)∈J(Y))  ⟹  R∈J(X).R \in J(X) \implies R' \in J(X), \qquad \bigl(R' \in J(X) \ \wedge \ \forall f \in R'(Y),\ f^{-1}(R) \in J(Y)\bigr) \implies R \in J(X).R∈J(X)⟹R′∈J(X),(R′∈J(X) ∧ ∀f∈R′(Y), f−1(R)∈J(Y))⟹R∈J(X).

Notes

Proof. The two conditions are exactly the "sieve versions" of axioms (T2) and (T3): the first says coverings are closed under enlargement (the original axiom was "pullback along itself is still a covering", and the two are equivalent) ✓; the second says if something is locally a covering then it is a covering (the original was "a covering refined by a covering") ✓. Comparing item by item gives the result ✓. ∎

⭐ Reading: these two sentences describe "covering" as a family of sieves satisfying filter + locality, which is easier to write and use in practice than the original axioms.
