# Real Banach Spaces Are Acyclic on Compact Hausdorff Spaces　`thm.real-banach-chaus-acyclic`

## Statement · **draft**
Let $V$ be a **real Banach space** and let $S$ be a **compact Hausdorff** space. Then for every $n > 0$

$$H^{n}_{\mathrm{cond}}(S,\ V) = 0.$$

## Notes · **stale**
⭐ **This is the main theorem of §8.2** and the central result of the chapter. It extends acyclicity from Stone spaces to general compact Hausdorff spaces, with coefficients in a real Banach space.
