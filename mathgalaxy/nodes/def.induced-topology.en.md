## Statement · **draft**
Let $\mathcal{C}'$ be a site and $f^{-1} : \mathcal{C} \to \mathcal{C}'$ any functor. The **induced topology** on $\mathcal{C}$ is the finest topology making $f^{-1}$ **continuous**.

## Notes · **draft**
**What it says**: given **any** functor $f^{-1} : \mathcal{C} \to \mathcal{C}'$, take on $\mathcal{C}$ "the finest Grothendieck topology making $f^{-1}$ continuous".
