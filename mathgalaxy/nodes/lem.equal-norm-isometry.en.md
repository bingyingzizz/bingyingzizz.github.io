## Statement · **draft**
Let $N$ be a finite-dimensional nondegenerate symmetric bilinear space with $\operatorname{char}K\ne2$, and let $q$ be its quadratic form. If $x,y\in N$ satisfy $q(x)=q(y)\ne0$, then there is an isometry $\rho$ of $N$ with $\rho(x)=y$.
