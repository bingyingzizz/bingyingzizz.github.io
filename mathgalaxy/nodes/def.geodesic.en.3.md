## Notes · **draft**
**Division of labour among the three characterizations**: $\nabla_{\dot\gamma}\dot\gamma = 0$ is the invariant formulation (recognized by every textbook), the geodesic equation is the computational tool (a system of two second-order ODEs), and shortestness is the original geometric motivation (variational). The equivalence of the first two is a pure expansion; for the third see the discussion around Gauss's lemma.

**The parameter must be affine**: $s\mapsto as+b$ is still a geodesic parameter; after changing to a general parameter $t$ the equation acquires an extra term $\frac{\mathrm{d}^{2}s}{\mathrm{d}t^{2}}\big/\frac{\mathrm{d}s}{\mathrm{d}t}$.
