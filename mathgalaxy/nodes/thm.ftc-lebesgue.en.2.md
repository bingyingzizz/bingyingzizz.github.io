**(c)** $F$ is **differentiable a.e.**, $F' \in L^1([a, b])$, and

$$F(x) - F(a) = \int_a^x F'(t) dt$$

## Notes · **draft**
⭐ This is the **complete form of the Newton–Leibniz formula for the Lebesgue integral**. Compare Riemann integration: there the condition that $F'$ be integrable and the formula hold requires $F'$ to be continuous (or at least Riemann integrable), whereas here only absolute continuity of $F$ is needed.
