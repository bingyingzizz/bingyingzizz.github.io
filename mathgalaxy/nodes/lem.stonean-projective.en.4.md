# Stonean spaces give finitely presented projective objects　`lem.stonean-projective`

- **Preservation of filtered colimits**: This also follows from preservation of all colimits ✓.
The standard criteria are: $P$ is **projective** $\iff$ $\operatorname{Hom}(P,-)$ preserves epimorphisms, and $P$ is **finitely presented** $\iff$ $\operatorname{Hom}(P,-)$ preserves filtered colimits. Together, these give that $\mathbb{Z}\cdot F$ is finitely presented and projective. ∎
