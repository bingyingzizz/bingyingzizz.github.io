# Schur Triangularization　`thm.schur-triangularization`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=thm.schur-triangularization)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./thm.schur-triangularization.md)

**Extend to an orthonormal basis**: By **Gram–Schmidt**, extend $x$ to an orthonormal basis of $\mathbb{C}^{n}$, denoted by $u_{1} = x, u_{2}, \ldots, u_{n}$. Set $U_{1} := (u_{1}, \ldots, u_{n})$ (with these basis vectors as columns). Then $U_{1}$ is unitary and $U_{1}^{*}u_{j} = e_{j}$. Thus the first column of $U_{1}^{*}AU_{1}$ is $U_{1}^{*}Ax = \lambda U_{1}^{*}x = \lambda e_{1}$. The matrix has $\lambda$ in the upper left, all $0$ in the lower left, a lower-right block of order $(n-1)$ denoted by $A_{1}$, and a row vector $w^{*}$ in the upper right.
