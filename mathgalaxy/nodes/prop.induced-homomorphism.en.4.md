# induced homomorphism　`prop.induced-homomorphism`

[in graph](../#n=prop.induced-homomorphism)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./prop.induced-homomorphism.md)

⚠ Item (4) requires **relative to the basepoint**: a general homotopy $f \simeq g$ only guarantees that $g_{*}$ and $f_{*}$ are "conjugate", not equal. This is exactly what `prop.pi1-basepoint` handles.

💡 Use: to prove "if $\pi_{1}$ differ then the spaces are not homeomorphic", it suffices to find something continuous maps cannot do (e.g. no isomorphism). For proving "cannot", functoriality is the cheapest tool — that is how `thm.no-retraction-disk` in this pack argues.
