## Statement · **draft**
Let $(H, \langle\cdot, \cdot\rangle)$ be an inner product space over $\mathbb{K}$ (**linear in the first variable, conjugate-linear in the second**), with norm $\|x\| := \sqrt{\langle x, x\rangle}$.

If $(H, \|\cdot\|)$ is complete, it is called a **Hilbert space**.
