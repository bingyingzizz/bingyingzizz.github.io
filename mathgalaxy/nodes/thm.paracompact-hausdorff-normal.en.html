Statement

Let XXX be a paracompact Hausdorff space. Then XXX is normal. The proof first establishes the stronger intermediate conclusion that XXX is already regular.

⭐ This result places paracompactness in the hierarchy of separation axioms: compact Hausdorff   ⟹  \implies⟹ paracompact Hausdorff   ⟹  \implies⟹ normal   ⟹  \implies⟹ regular   ⟹  \implies⟹ Hausdorff.

This supplies the normality used with Urysohn's lemma in Pack 40. Compact Hausdorff spaces ([0,1][0,1][0,1], βI\beta IβI) are paracompact.

⚠️ Hausdorffness is essential: The two-point Sierpiński space is paracompact (its open covers are finite and hence locally finite), but it fails to be T1T_{1}T1​ and therefore is not normal.

⚠️ The converse also fails: A normal space need not be paracompact ([0,ω1)[0, \omega_{1})[0,ω1​)). Thus paracompactness is a separate condition from normality.
