Statement

A spectral sequence consists of two parts:

  1. a family of complexes Erp,qE^{p,q}_{r}Erp,q​ (p,q,r∈Zp, q, r \in \mathbb{Z}p,q,r∈Z, r≥r0r \ge r_{0}r≥r0​) with differentials
drp,q:Erp,q⟶Erp+r, q−r+1d^{p,q}_{r} : E^{p,q}_{r} \longrightarrow E^{p+r,\, q-r+1}_{r}drp,q​:Erp,q​⟶Erp+r,q−r+1​

such that Er+1p,q≅Hp,q(Er)=ker⁡drp,q/im⁡drp−r, q+r−1E^{p,q}_{r+1} \cong H^{p,q}(E_{r}) = \ker d^{p,q}_{r} / \operatorname{im} d^{p-r,\, q+r-1}_{r}Er+1p,q​≅Hp,q(Er​)=kerdrp,q​/imdrp−r,q+r−1​;

  1. a family of filtered objects HnH^{n}Hn (n∈Zn \in \mathbb{Z}n∈Z) such that for every p,qp, qp,q and all sufficiently large rrr,
Gr⁡pHp+q  ≅  Erp,q\operatorname{Gr}^{p} H^{p+q} \;\cong\; E^{p,q}_{r}GrpHp+q≅Erp,q​

One in which p,q≥0p, q \ge 0p,q≥0 is necessary for Erp,qE^{p,q}_{r}Erp,q​ to be nonzero is called a first-quadrant spectral sequence.

Notes

Where it comes from: give a complex KKK a filtration FpKF^{p}KFpK; the filtration on homology is inherited from

FpHn:=im⁡(Hn(FpK)→Hn(K))F^{p}H^{n} := \operatorname{im}\bigl(H^{n}(F^{p}K) \to H^{n}(K)\bigr)FpHn:=im(Hn(FpK)→Hn(K))

Hence on each HnH^{n}Hn there is a chain Hn⊇⋯⊇FpHn⊇Fp+1Hn⊇⋯⊇0H^{n} \supseteq \cdots \supseteq F^{p}H^{n} \supseteq F^{p+1}H^{n} \supseteq \cdots \supseteq 0Hn⊇⋯⊇FpHn⊇Fp+1Hn⊇⋯⊇0.

A spectral sequence says: for sufficiently large rrr, the page ErE_{r}Er​ stabilizes and equals exactly the associated graded pieces of this filtration. So it is a tool for "approximating homology layer by layer from a filtered complex" — when computing HnH^{n}Hn directly is too hard, break HnH^{n}Hn into easily computable pieces.
