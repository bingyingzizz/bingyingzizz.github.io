## Statement · **draft**
Let $K$ be compact Hausdorff, and let $A \subseteq C(K, \mathbb{C})$ be a subalgebra containing the unit, separating points, and **closed under conjugation** ($f \in A \implies \bar f \in A$). Then $\overline A = C(K, \mathbb{C})$.
