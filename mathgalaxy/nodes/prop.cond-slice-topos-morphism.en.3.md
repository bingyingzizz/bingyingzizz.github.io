## Notes · **draft**
**What this is doing.** $\mathrm{Cond}/X$ is the world of "doing condensed mathematics with $X$ as basepoint"; $\mathrm{Open}(X)$ is the world of "the open-set lattice of $X$". This morphism connects the two: **cohomology computed in the two worlds can be converted into one another**.

**How to read the shape of the inverse image**: $\operatorname{Hom}_{/X}(\underline{U}, Y)$ is "over $X$, a map from $U$ to $Y$". The smaller $U$ is, the more maps it can give — this is exactly the "restriction" flavour of sheaves.
