## Statement · **draft**
Let $f_{1}, \ldots, f_{r} \in k[t_{1}, \ldots, t_{n}]$.

The functor sending a commutative $k$-algebra $A$ to the **solution set** $S(A) = \{(a_{1}, \ldots, a_{n}) \in A^{n} : f_{1}(a) = \cdots = f_{r}(a) = 0\}$ is **representable**.
