# Limits and Colimits of Condensed Sets　`prop.cond-limits-colimits`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=prop.cond-limits-colimits)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./prop.cond-limits-colimits.md)

**Colimits cannot be computed this way** — one first takes the colimit pointwise in presheaves and then **sheafifies**:

$$\Bigl(\varinjlim_{i} X_{i}\Bigr)(S) = \Bigl(\operatorname{sh}\bigl(S^{\prime} \mapsto \varinjlim_{i} X_{i}(S^{\prime})\bigr)\Bigr)(S).$$
