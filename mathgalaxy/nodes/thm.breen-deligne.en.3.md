# Breen-Deligne　`thm.breen-deligne`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=thm.breen-deligne)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./thm.breen-deligne.md)

**Why it is called the Breen–Deligne decomposition**: the construction was proposed by Deligne but lacked a complete proof for a long time; Scholze completed the proof in an appendix to the fourth lecture of his condensed mathematics course. So it is a **modern** theorem.

⭐ **It is the engine of all Ext computations in $\mathrm{CondAb}$**: with this termwise free resolution, any $\operatorname{Ext}^{n}(M, N)$ can be expanded into Exts of free terms, which can be computed (see the spectral sequence below).
