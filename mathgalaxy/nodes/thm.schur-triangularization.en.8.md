# Schur Triangularization　`thm.schur-triangularization`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=thm.schur-triangularization)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./thm.schur-triangularization.md)

**Prescribing the order**: At each step, choose the first eigenvalue in the prescribed order that has yet to be placed, $\lambda$ (it occurs in $\operatorname{spec}(A)$ and hence has an eigenvector). When applying induction to $A_{1}$, use the order induced on $A_{1}$ by the prescribed order. Thus the diagonal order can be specified in advance ✓.
