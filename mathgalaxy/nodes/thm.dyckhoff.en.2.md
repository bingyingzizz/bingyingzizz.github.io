## Notes · **draft**
⭐ **This is the bridge joining the two cohomology theories.** Condensed cohomology is computed from "sheaves on $X$"; sheaf cohomology is computed from "sheaves on the open sets of $X$". This says: as long as $X$ is locally compact and the coefficients are discrete, the two sides **agree**.

**How to prove it.**

Using the topos morphism $c_{X} : \mathrm{Cond}/X \rightleftarrows \mathrm{Open}(X)$ and its derived functors gives a spectral sequence whose $E_{2}$ terms are $R^{q}c_{*}$ applied to $M$.
