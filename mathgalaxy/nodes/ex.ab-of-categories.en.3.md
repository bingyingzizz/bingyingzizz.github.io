## Notes · **draft**
⭐ The most useful thing about this table is **reading it backwards**: to study topological abelian groups, take $\mathcal{C}$ to be $\mathbf{Top}$; to study condensed abelian groups, take $\mathcal{C}$ to be $\mathrm{Cond}$.

**One definition, one set of theorems (kernels, cokernels, exact sequences, ...) transfers directly.**

⚠️ The identity $\mathrm{Ab}(\mathbf{Grp}) = \mathbf{Ab}$ is the one that gives pause: a group object in the category of groups is **exactly** an abelian group (Eckmann–Hilton argument) — adding the structure "group" on top of the category "groups" forces commutativity.
