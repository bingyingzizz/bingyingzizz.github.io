## Statement · **draft**
There exists a **smallest inductive set** $\omega$: it contains $\emptyset$, is closed under $x \mapsto x \cup \{x\}$, and is contained in every inductive set:

$$\omega = \{ \emptyset, \{\emptyset\}, \{\emptyset, \{\emptyset\}\}, \{\emptyset, \{\emptyset\}, \{\emptyset, \{\emptyset\}\}\}, \ldots  \}$$

Reading $\in$ as $<$, $\langle \omega , \in \rangle$ is the natural number system $\langle \mathbb{N}, <\rangle$.
