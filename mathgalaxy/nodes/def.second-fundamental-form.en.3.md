## Notes · **draft**
**One minus sign, three ways of writing it**: $\langle \mathrm{d}r, -\mathrm{d}n\rangle = -\sum_{i}\omega^{i}\omega^{i}_{\ 3} = \sum_{i,j}h_{ij}\omega^{i}\omega^{j}$.

Putting the minus sign into the coefficients ($h_{ij} = -c_{ij}$) has the advantage that $h$ is exactly the matrix of $W$; then "principal curvature $=$ eigenvalue of $h$" needs no extra sign.

⭐ **Symmetry need not be computed**: it follows from $\mathrm{d}\omega^{3} = 0$ (`lem.second-form-symmetric`); one does not need equality of the mixed partials $r_{uu}$ and $r_{uv}$.
