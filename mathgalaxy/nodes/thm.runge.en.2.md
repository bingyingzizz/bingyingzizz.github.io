# Runge approximation theorem　`thm.runge`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=thm.runge)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./thm.runge.md)

In the general case (when $\mathbb{C}\setminus K$ has holes), $f$ can be approximated by **rational functions** whose poles are restricted to one point chosen in advance in each connected component of $\mathbb{C}\setminus K$.

## Notes · **draft**
**Proof**: There are four steps. ① Express $f$ as a Cauchy integral over a grid boundary outside $K$. ② Discretize the integral to obtain rational functions with poles outside $K$. ③ Move the poles along paths in $\mathbb{C}\setminus K$ to the prescribed locations. ④ If there are no holes, move them out to infinity and expand to obtain polynomials.
