说明

⭐ 这是实际计算里用得最多的收敛定理。三个条件缺一不可：

⋅a.e.\cdot \text{a.e.}⋅a.e. 收敛 —— 不能去掉（单点概率空间上 fn=(−1)nf_{n} = (-1)^{n}fn​=(−1)n：被 g≡1g \equiv 1g≡1 控制，但函数与积分都不收敛）；

⋅\cdot⋅ 控制函数 g∈L1g \in L^{1}g∈L1 —— 不能去掉（nχ[0,1/n]n\chi_{[0,1/n]}nχ[0,1/n]​ 在 [0,1][0,1][0,1] 上几乎处处趋于 000（x=0x = 0x=0 处不收敛），积分却恒为 111；另一个例子 χ[n,n+1]\chi_{[n,n+1]}χ[n,n+1]​ 处处收敛于 000，积分恒为 111 却不趋于 000，坏在没有可积控制函数 —— 任何控制函数都得 ≥1\ge 1≥1 于整个 R\mathbb{R}R）；

⋅\cdot⋅ 必须是可积的 ggg，光是「有界」不够（在无穷测度空间上）。

为什么叫「控制」：ggg 把所有 fnf_{n}fn​ 关在一间可积的笼子里，所以质量无处可逃，极限过程不会漏掉面积。

📌 「积分与极限交换」在这里指控制收敛（逐点收敛 + 可积控制函数）；一致收敛下的交换是 积分与极限交换。

Statement draft

Let {fn}⊆L1\{f_n\} \subseteq L^1{fn​}⊆L1, let fff be measurable, let fn→ff_n \to ffn​→f a.e., and suppose there is a dominating function g∈L1g \in L^1g∈L1 such that

∣fn∣≤ga.e.∀n|f_n| \le g\quad \text{a.e.}\quad \forall n∣fn​∣≤ga.e.∀n

∫f=lim⁡n→∞∫fn\int f = \lim_{n\to\infty} \int f_n∫f=n→∞lim​∫fn​

Notes draft

⭐ This is the convergence theorem used most often in actual computation. None of the three hypotheses can be dropped:

⋅a.e.\cdot \text{a.e.}⋅a.e. convergence — cannot be removed (on a one-point probability space fn=(−1)nf_{n} = (-1)^{n}fn​=(−1)n: dominated by g≡1g \equiv 1g≡1, yet neither the functions nor the integrals converge);

⋅\cdot⋅ the dominating function g∈L1g \in L^{1}g∈L1 — cannot be removed (nχ[0,1/n]n\chi_{[0,1/n]}nχ[0,1/n]​ tends to 000 almost everywhere on [0,1][0,1][0,1] (it fails to converge at x=0x = 0x=0), yet its integral is constantly 111; another example is χ[n,n+1]\chi_{[n,n+1]}χ[n,n+1]​, which converges to 000 everywhere while its integral is constantly 111 and does not tend to 000 — the defect is the absence of an integrable dominating function, since any dominating function would have to be ≥1\ge 1≥1 on all of R\mathbb{R}R);

⋅\cdot⋅ ggg must be integrable; mere boundedness is not enough (on a space of infinite measure).

Why "dominating": ggg keeps all the fnf_{n}fn​ inside one integrable cage, so the mass has nowhere to escape and the limiting process cannot lose area.

📌 "Exchanging integral and limit" here means dominated convergence (pointwise convergence plus an integrable dominating function); the exchange under uniform convergence is 积分与极限交换.
