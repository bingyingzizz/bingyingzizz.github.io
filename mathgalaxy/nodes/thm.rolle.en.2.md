# Rolle's theorem　`thm.rolle`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=thm.rolle)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./thm.rolle.md)

It is the **only** member of the mean value theorem family that needs compactness (the extreme value theorem locates an interior extremum); the later members are the same statement in different clothing.

⚠ Neither hypothesis can be dropped: continuity on $[a, b]$ guarantees that the extrema exist (compactness), and differentiability on $(a, b)$ guarantees that the extremum is an interior point, where Fermat's lemma applies.

Replacing $f(a) = f(b)$ by $f(a) \ne f(b)$ gives the Lagrange mean value theorem.
