# symmetric algebra　`def.symmetric-algebra`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=def.symmetric-algebra)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./def.symmetric-algebra.md)

$$\operatorname{Sym}^{\bullet} V := \bigoplus_{k \ge 0} \operatorname{Sym}^{k} V$$

It is a **commutative** graded algebra, and

$$\dim \operatorname{Sym}^{k} V = \binom{n + k - 1}{k}, \qquad \operatorname{Sym}^{\bullet} V \cong K[x_1, \ldots, x_n] \ (n = \dim V)$$
