Statement

Let VVV be a vector space over a field KKK. Then

VVV has a basis;

  • more precisely: every linearly independent subset of VVV can be extended to a basis of VVV, and every spanning set contains a basis.

Notes

In ZF this statement is equivalent to AC — without AC one can construct a vector space with no basis.
