# Poincaré Lemma　`lem.poincare-lemma`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=lem.poincare-lemma)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./lem.poincare-lemma.md)

if $\omega = \mathrm{d}\eta$, Stokes' theorem gives $\int_{S^{1}}\mathrm{d}\eta = \int_{\partial S^{1}}\eta = 0$, a contradiction.

So $H^{1}(\mathbb{R}^{2}\setminus\{0\}) \ne 0$ — one hole destroys the lemma.

**$k \ge 1$ cannot be relaxed**: for $k = 0$ "closed" means locally constant and "exact" means zero (there are no $-1$-forms), so the two are far apart; hence $H^{0} = \mathbb{R} \ne 0$. The statement must exclude $k = 0$.
