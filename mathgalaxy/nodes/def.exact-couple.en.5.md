# exact couple　`def.exact-couple`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=def.exact-couple)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./def.exact-couple.md)

After one derivation the bidegree of $j$ becomes $(1, -1)$ — because $j^{\prime}$ acts on $i(x)$ but reads off $j(x)$, and $i$ has already shifted the degree by one step.

$i$ and $k$ are unchanged by derivation.

Inductively, the bidegree of $j_{r}$ is $(r - 1,\ 1 - r)$, so the bidegree of $d_{r} = j_{r} k_{r}$ is $(r,\ 1 - r)$.

⭐ **Why it is needed**: constructing a spectral sequence directly requires a **filtration** first. The exact couple removes this dependence — a single exact triangle suffices to produce a spectral sequence. Spectral sequences of Bockstein, homotopy groups and Gysin type have no other route.
