Statement

What is being done: in rank 111 the whole machine degenerates into a computable formula — this is the only case in which Chern classes can be "computed by hand", and the case to which the splitting principle reduces everything.

Let L→BL \to BL→B be a complex line bundle, ∇\nabla∇ a connection, and Ω\OmegaΩ its curvature 222-form (the curvature of a line bundle is just dθ\mathrm{d}\thetadθ, since End⁡L≅C\operatorname{End}L \cong \mathbb{C}EndL≅C is commutative). Then

c1(L)=[ i2π Ω ]∈H2(B;Z),cj(L)=0 (j≥2).c_{1}(L) = \Bigl[\, \frac{i}{2\pi}\,\Omega \,\Bigr] \in H^{2}(B; \mathbb{Z}) , \qquad c_{j}(L) = 0 \ (j \ge 2) .c1​(L)=[2πi​Ω]∈H2(B;Z),cj​(L)=0 (j≥2).

∫Σc1(L)=i2π∫ΣΩ  ∈  Z.\int_{\Sigma} c_{1}(L) = \frac{i}{2\pi}\int_{\Sigma}\Omega \;\in\; \mathbb{Z} .∫Σ​c1​(L)=2πi​∫Σ​Ω∈Z.

Notes

Why rank 111 is especially simple: gl(1,C)=C\mathfrak{gl}(1,\mathbb{C}) = \mathbb{C}gl(1,C)=C is commutative, so the "conjugation-invariance" condition is automatic — every polynomial is invariant, nothing to choose. Hence a line bundle has only one characteristic class (c1c_{1}c1​), and all the rest vanish.

What this integer is: i2π∫ΣΩ\frac{i}{2\pi}\int_{\Sigma}\Omega2πi​∫Σ​Ω is the winding number / first Chern number, called "quantization of magnetic flux" in physics. On Σ=S2\Sigma = S^{2}Σ=S2 it runs over all of Z\mathbb{Z}Z — this is the analytic root of the fact that complex line bundles on S2S^{2}S2 are classified by Z\mathbb{Z}Z (the Hopf bundle is a generator, as noted in the notes to @@thm.bundle-classification@@).

⚠️ One cannot drop cj(L)=0c_{j}(L)=0cj​(L)=0 (j≥2j\ge2j≥2): it guarantees that the characteristic class of a line bundle has only one degree of freedom. ⚠️ But do not wrongly infer "c1=0c_1=0c1​=0 implies trivial": c1=0c_1=0c1​=0 only says the bundle is invisible at the level of H2H^2H2; the flat line bundle is an example (c1=0c_1=0c1​=0 yet possibly non-trivial).

⚠️ Sign convention for the curvature: both Ω=dθ\Omega = \mathrm{d}\thetaΩ=dθ and Ω=−dθ\Omega = -\mathrm{d}\thetaΩ=−dθ are in use, differing by an overall sign; the sign of c1c_{1}c1​ changes accordingly. This map takes Ω=dθ\Omega = \mathrm{d}\thetaΩ=dθ (consistent with @@def.connection-form@@).
