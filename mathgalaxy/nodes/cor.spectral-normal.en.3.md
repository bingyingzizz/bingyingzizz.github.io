## Notes · **draft**
⭐ These three "property of $T$ $\iff$ property of eigenvalues" statements are the most useful by-product of the spectral theorem: they translate a problem about an **operator** into a problem about a set of **numbers**.

⭐ Complex normality is the common generalization of real symmetry and real orthogonality: over the complex field both can be diagonalized; over the reals only the symmetric ones can.

⚠ The projection clause holds only for **self-adjoint** operators: a general idempotent (a projection) need not be unitarily diagonalizable.
