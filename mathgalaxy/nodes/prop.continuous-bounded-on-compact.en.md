## Statement · **draft**
Let $X$ be a compact metric space (for example the closed interval $[a, b]$ in $\mathbb{R}$), and let $f : X \to \mathbb{R}$ be continuous. Then $f$ is bounded on $X$: there exists $M$ such that $|f(x)| \le M$ for all $x \in X$.
