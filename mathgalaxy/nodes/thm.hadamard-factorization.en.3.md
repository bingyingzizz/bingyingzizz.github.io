## Notes · **draft**
**Notation.** Let $f$ be entire of finite order $\rho$, with zeros $a_{n}\ne 0$ counted with multiplicity. The zero at $z=0$ has multiplicity $m$. Let $n(r)$ count the zeros with modulus $\le r$, and let $M(r)=\max_{|z|=r}|f(z)|$ and $E_{p}(w)=(1-w)e^{w+w^{2}/2+\cdots+w^{p}/p}$. Take $p=\lfloor\rho\rfloor$ (when $\rho<1$, we have $p=0$), so $p+1>\rho$. Fix $\varepsilon>0$ such that $p+1>\rho+\varepsilon$.
