# dualizing complex of a regular closed immersion　`prop.regular-immersion-dualizing`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=prop.regular-immersion-dualizing)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./prop.regular-immersion-dualizing.md)

$$f^!R = \operatorname{RHom}_R(A, R) \;\cong\; \det\nolimits_A(I/I^2)^*[-c].$$

## Notes · **draft**
⭐ Locally $A = R/(f_1, \ldots, f_c)$ (a regular sequence), and the Koszul complex computes directly that $\operatorname{RHom}_R(A,R) \cong A[-c]$;

$\det(I/I^2)$ has a basis given by $f_1 \wedge \cdots \wedge f_c$.

The global statement must be checked to be independent of the choice of generators.
