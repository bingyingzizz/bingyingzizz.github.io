## Notes · **draft**
Once kernels and cokernels exist one can speak of "exact sequences"; but pre-abelian does **not** guarantee that "coimage $\to$ image" is an isomorphism — this is exactly the dividing line between pre-abelian and abelian.

Examples: $\mathbf{PreAb}$ (the category of pre-abelian groups), the category of filtered vector spaces are pre-abelian but not abelian.
