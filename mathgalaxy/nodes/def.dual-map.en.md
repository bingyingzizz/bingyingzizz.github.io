## Statement · **draft**
Let $T:V\to W$ be a $K$-linear map. Its **dual map** (also called the transpose map) $T^{\ast}:W^{\ast}\to V^{\ast}$ is defined by

$$\langle T^{\ast}\varphi,\,v\rangle=\langle\varphi,\,Tv\rangle,\qquad v\in V,\ \varphi\in W^{\ast}$$

Equivalently, $T^{\ast}\varphi=\varphi\circ T$.
