# topos　`def.topos`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=def.topos)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./def.topos.md)

**Together with pretopos**: topos $=$ pretopos $+$ "small generator $+$ arbitrary colimits". So a "pretopos" is "a topos lacking smallness and large colimits"; many constructions (internal equivalence relations, effective epimorphisms, kernel pairs) already work at that level.

⚠️ **The four items cannot be dropped at will**: drop ①, and $\operatorname{Sh}(\mathcal{C})$ (with $\mathcal{C}$ large) still satisfies ②③④ but is not a topos (no small generator); keep only "coproducts exist" from ③ without "disjoint universal", and things like $\mathbf{Grp}^{\mathrm{op}}$ creep in; drop ④, and "quotient = equivalence relation" fails, so the "set" semantics of the internal language collapses.
