陈述

紧生成空间构成的范畴 kTopk\mathbf{Top}kTop 是 Top\mathbf{Top}Top 的余反射子范畴：含入函子 i:kTop↪Topi : k\mathbf{Top} \hookrightarrow \mathbf{Top}i:kTop↪Top 有右伴随

k:Top⟶kTop,X↦kXk : \mathbf{Top} \longrightarrow k\mathbf{Top}, \qquad X \mapsto kXk:Top⟶kTop,X↦kX

即 i⊣ki \dashv ki⊣k。kXkXkX 与 XXX 有同一个集合，拓扑换成 kkk-拓扑。

说明

要证的是：对每个紧生成空间 XXX 与每个拓扑空间 YYY，

Hom⁡Top(X, Y)  ≅  Hom⁡kTop(X, kY).\operatorname{Hom}_{\mathbf{Top}}(X,\ Y) \;\cong\; \operatorname{Hom}_{k\mathbf{Top}}\bigl(X,\ kY\bigr).HomTop​(X, Y)≅HomkTop​(X, kY).

证明。 余单位是 εY:kY→Y\varepsilon_{Y} : kY \to YεY​:kY→Y（恒等映射，拓扑从细到粗，连续）。

  • 先证 kYkYkY 确实紧生成：把每个连续映射 S→YS \to YS→Y（SSS 紧 Hausdorff）复合 εY\varepsilon_{Y}εY​ 抬成 S→kYS \to kYS→kY，再用 kYkYkY 的定义（kkk-开集的取法）验证「由这些映射决定拓扑」。
  • 于是任意 f:X→Yf : X \to Yf:X→Y 连续（XXX 紧生成）时，f=εY∘f′:X→kYf = \varepsilon_{Y} \circ f': X \to kYf=εY​∘f′:X→kY 给出一个到 kYkYkY 的连续映射 f′f'f′：设 U⊆kYU \subseteq kYU⊆kY 开，要证 f′−1(U)f'^{-1}(U)f′−1(U) 在 XXX 中开。由 XXX 紧生成，只需对每个紧 L⊆XL \subseteq XL⊆X 验证 f′−1(U)∩Lf'^{-1}(U) \cap Lf′−1(U)∩L 在 LLL 中开。而 f∣L:L→Yf|_{L} : L \to Yf∣L​:L→Y 连续、LLL 紧，故 f(L)f(L)f(L) 紧；U∩f(L)U \cap f(L)U∩f(L) 在 f(L)f(L)f(L) 中开，于是 (f∣L)−1(U∩f(L))=f′−1(U)∩L(f|_{L})^{-1}\bigl(U \cap f(L)\bigr) = f'^{-1}(U) \cap L(f∣L​)−1(U∩f(L))=f′−1(U)∩L 在 LLL 中开。∎
  • 反向由 εY\varepsilon_{Y}εY​ 连续直接得到。这样两个方向互逆，就是那个同构。

⭐ 与紧 Hausdorff 对照着记：CHaus\mathbf{CHaus}CHaus 是 Top\mathbf{Top}Top 的反射子范畴（含入函子有左伴随 β\betaβ），而 kTopk\mathbf{Top}kTop 是余反射的（含入函子有右伴随 kkk）。一个把空间「收紧」，一个把空间「松开」—— 所以前者对极限封闭，后者对余极限封闭。

Abstract reviewed

The category kTopk\mathbf{Top}kTop of compactly generated spaces is a coreflective subcategory of Top\mathbf{Top}Top: the inclusion iii has a right adjoint kkk that keeps the underlying set and replaces the topology by the kkk-topology, i⊣ki \dashv ki⊣k, i.e. Hom⁡Top(X,Y)≅Hom⁡kTop(X,kY)\operatorname{Hom}_{\mathbf{Top}}(X, Y) \cong \operatorname{Hom}_{k\mathbf{Top}}(X, kY)HomTop​(X,Y)≅HomkTop​(X,kY) for compactly generated XXX. The counit kY→YkY \to YkY→Y is the identity map, continuous because the kkk-topology is the finer one.

Statement reviewed

The category kTopk\mathbf{Top}kTop of compactly generated spaces is a coreflective subcategory of Top\mathbf{Top}Top: the inclusion functor i:kTop↪Topi : k\mathbf{Top} \hookrightarrow \mathbf{Top}i:kTop↪Top has a right adjoint

k:Top⟶kTop,X↦kXk : \mathbf{Top} \longrightarrow k\mathbf{Top}, \qquad X \mapsto kXk:Top⟶kTop,X↦kX

that is, i⊣ki \dashv ki⊣k. The space kXkXkX has the same underlying set as XXX, with the topology replaced by the kkk-topology.

Notes reviewed

What has to be shown: for every compactly generated space XXX and every topological space YYY,

Hom⁡Top(X, Y)  ≅  Hom⁡kTop(X, kY).\operatorname{Hom}_{\mathbf{Top}}(X,\ Y) \;\cong\; \operatorname{Hom}_{k\mathbf{Top}}\bigl(X,\ kY\bigr).HomTop​(X, Y)≅HomkTop​(X, kY).

Proof. The counit is εY:kY→Y\varepsilon_{Y} : kY \to YεY​:kY→Y (the identity map, topology from finer to coarser, continuous).

  • First, kYkYkY really is compactly generated: compose every continuous map S→YS \to YS→Y (SSS compact Hausdorff) with εY\varepsilon_{Y}εY​ to lift it to S→kYS \to kYS→kY, then use the definition of kYkYkY (how kkk-open sets are taken) to verify that "the topology is determined by these maps".
  • So whenever f:X→Yf : X \to Yf:X→Y is continuous (with XXX compactly generated), f=εY∘f′:X→kYf = \varepsilon_{Y} \circ f': X \to kYf=εY​∘f′:X→kY yields a continuous map f′f'f′ into kYkYkY: let U⊆kYU \subseteq kYU⊆kY be open; we must show that f′−1(U)f'^{-1}(U)f′−1(U) is open in XXX. Since XXX is compactly generated, it suffices to check for every compact L⊆XL \subseteq XL⊆X that f′−1(U)∩Lf'^{-1}(U) \cap Lf′−1(U)∩L is open in LLL. But f∣L:L→Yf|_{L} : L \to Yf∣L​:L→Y is continuous and LLL is compact, so f(L)f(L)f(L) is compact; U∩f(L)U \cap f(L)U∩f(L) is open in f(L)f(L)f(L), hence (f∣L)−1(U∩f(L))=f′−1(U)∩L(f|_{L})^{-1}\bigl(U \cap f(L)\bigr) = f'^{-1}(U) \cap L(f∣L​)−1(U∩f(L))=f′−1(U)∩L is open in LLL. ∎
  • The converse direction follows directly from continuity of εY\varepsilon_{Y}εY​. The two directions are mutually inverse, which is the isomorphism.

⭐ Keep this side by side with compact Hausdorff: CHaus\mathbf{CHaus}CHaus is a reflective subcategory of Top\mathbf{Top}Top (the inclusion has a left adjoint β\betaβ), whereas kTopk\mathbf{Top}kTop is coreflective (the inclusion has a right adjoint kkk). One "tightens" a space, the other "loosens" it — hence the former is closed under limits and the latter under colimits.

    Top→Cond\mathbf{Top} \to \mathrm{Cond}Top→Cond 忠实、限制到 kTopk\mathbf{Top}kTop 全忠实

    X‾\underline{X}X​ 是凝聚态集。 对任一点集 SSS，X‾(S)=C(S,X)\underline{X}(S) = C(S, X)X​(S)=C(S,X)；C(−,X)C(-, X)C(−,X) 把 CHaus\mathbf{CHaus}CHaus 里的有限不交并变成有限积、把商变成核（连续映射在无交并上与商上都是逐块决定的）。由凝聚态集的两条判据，X‾\underline{X}X​ 是凝聚态集。∎

    伴随。 函子 X↦X‾X \mapsto \underline{X}X↦X​ 与 Z↦Z(⋅)Z \mapsto Z(\cdot)Z↦Z(⋅) 之间要给出

    Hom⁡Cond(X‾,Z)  ≅  Hom⁡Top(X,Z(⋅))\operatorname{Hom}_{\mathrm{Cond}}(\underline{X}, Z) \;\cong\; \operatorname{Hom}_{\mathbf{Top}}\bigl(X, Z(\cdot)\bigr)HomCond​(X​,Z)≅HomTop​(X,Z(⋅))

    而 Hom⁡Cond(X‾,Z)=Nat⁡(C(−,X),Z)\operatorname{Hom}_{\mathrm{Cond}}(\underline{X}, Z) = \operatorname{Nat}(C(-,X), Z)HomCond​(X​,Z)=Nat(C(−,X),Z)，按米田式的计算，它正是「在 S=∗S = \astS=∗ 处的取值」加上自然性 —— 也就是 Z(⋅)=Z(∗)Z(\cdot) = Z(\ast)Z(⋅)=Z(∗) 上的一个元素，并且与所有 X‾(S)=C(S,X)\underline{X}(S) = C(S,X)X​(S)=C(S,X) 相容。这恰恰是「从 XXX 出发的连续映射」，两边一一对应。∎

    忠实。 由上面的伴随式取 Z=Y‾Z = \underline{Y}Z=Y​：

    Hom⁡Cond(X‾,Y‾)  ≅  Hom⁡Top(X,Y‾(⋅))\operatorname{Hom}_{\mathrm{Cond}}(\underline{X}, \underline{Y}) \;\cong\; \operatorname{Hom}_{\mathbf{Top}}\bigl(X, \underline{Y}(\cdot)\bigr)HomCond​(X​,Y​)≅HomTop​(X,Y​(⋅))

    而关键的一步是：Y‾(⋅)≅kY\underline{Y}(\cdot) \cong kYY​(⋅)≅kY（把 YYY 换成它的 kkk-化，拓扑可能变细）。于是

    Hom⁡Cond(X‾,Y‾)  ≅  Hom⁡Top(X,kY)  ≅  Hom⁡kTop(kX,kY)\operatorname{Hom}_{\mathrm{Cond}}(\underline{X}, \underline{Y}) \;\cong\; \operatorname{Hom}_{\mathbf{Top}}(X, kY) \;\cong\; \operatorname{Hom}_{k\mathbf{Top}}(kX, kY)HomCond​(X​,Y​)≅HomTop​(X,kY)≅HomkTop​(kX,kY)

    （最后一个同构因为 kTopk\mathbf{Top}kTop 是余反射子范畴，kkk 是含入的右伴随：kXkXkX 处的映射等同于一切从 XXX 出发射入紧生成空间的映射。）

    当 XXX 本身紧生成时 kX=XkX = XkX=X，上式就是 Hom⁡Cond(X‾,Y‾)≅Hom⁡kTop(X,kY)\operatorname{Hom}_{\mathrm{Cond}}(\underline{X}, \underline{Y}) \cong \operatorname{Hom}_{k\mathbf{Top}}(X, kY)HomCond​(X​,Y​)≅HomkTop​(X,kY)，逐对 X,YX, YX,Y 都是双射 —— 限制到 kTopk\mathbf{Top}kTop 上全忠实。对一般 XXX，X→kXX \to kXX→kX 是同一集合上的恒等映射，所以函子在态射层仍是单射 —— 在 Top\mathbf{Top}Top 上忠实。∎

    一句话记住：「拓扑空间 →\to→ 凝聚态集」这件事丢掉的东西，正好就是 kkk-化丢掉的东西。 所以在 kTopk\mathbf{Top}kTop 上它不丢信息。
