Notes

⭐ Finite is deliberate: the Riemann integral only cuts finitely many times.

Only on the Lebesgue side are countably many pieces allowed — that is one source of the difference between the two.

⚠ The xix_ixi​ in the notation are partition points, unrelated to the variable of the function;

Δxi\Delta x_iΔxi​ is a length, not a difference of function values.

⭐ The role of refinements: the finer the partition, the smaller the upper sum and the larger the lower sum (see the notes on “upper and lower sums”) — they are monotone, hence one can take suprema and infima.
