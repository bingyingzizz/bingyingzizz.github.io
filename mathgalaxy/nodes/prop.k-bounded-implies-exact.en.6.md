# K-Bounded Exactness Implies Exactness　`prop.k-bounded-implies-exact`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=prop.k-bounded-implies-exact)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./prop.k-bounded-implies-exact.md)

📌 To find a **specific preimage with a bound**, use the open mapping theorem. Since $\operatorname{im} d^{n+1}$ is closed and $M^{n+1}$ is Banach, $d^{n+1} : M^{n+1} \twoheadrightarrow \operatorname{im} d^{n+1}$ is open. Thus there exists $c$ such that every $z \in \operatorname{im} d^{n+1}$ has a preimage $w$ satisfying $\|w\| \le c\|z\|$. This gives the required bound on the preimage.
