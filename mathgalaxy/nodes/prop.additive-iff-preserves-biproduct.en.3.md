# Additive Functors Preserve Zero Objects and Biproducts　`prop.additive-iff-preserves-biproduct`

- **Additive** $\iff$ **preserves the zero object and all direct sums**: $(\implies)$ an additive functor preserves the biproduct structure (given by the kernel–cokernel duality); $(\Leftarrow)$ one must **use the projection/inclusion morphisms of the direct sum** to assemble $F(f+g) = F(f)+F(g)$ — this is the only step of the four that requires work.
