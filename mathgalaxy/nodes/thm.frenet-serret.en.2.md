# Frenet–Serret Formulas　`thm.frenet-serret`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=thm.frenet-serret)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./thm.frenet-serret.md)

The coefficient matrix is antisymmetric — this is precisely the infinitesimal expression of "the frame is always orthonormal".

## Notes · **draft**
⭐ **Why only two functions**: a $3\times3$ antisymmetric matrix has $3$ degrees of freedom, but the direction of $T'$ is forced by the definition of $N$ ($\langle T',T\rangle = \langle T',B\rangle = 0$ are identities), leaving only $\kappa$ and $\tau$.

**It is the same thing as the structure equations**: expanding $\mathrm{d}T,\mathrm{d}N,\mathrm{d}B$ in the frame, the coefficients are $\kappa\,\mathrm{d}s$ and $\tau\,\mathrm{d}s$.
