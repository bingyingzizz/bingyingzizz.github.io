# Properties of the Čech Functor　`prop.cech-properties`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=prop.cech-properties)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./prop.cech-properties.md)

**Hypotheses used in each direction** (the plus construction for set-valued presheaves):
- Separated $\iff$ the canonical map is monic: unfold the definition of local equality, then use that covers have common refinements; this does not hold for arbitrary colimits.
- Sheaf $\implies$ the canonical map is an isomorphism: compatible families have a unique gluing, which is a direct unfolding of the sheaf condition; conversely use that the plus construction is always separated, deduce separatedness of the original presheaf from the isomorphism, and recover gluing.
- Separated $\implies$ becomes a sheaf after one plus: needs pullbacks
