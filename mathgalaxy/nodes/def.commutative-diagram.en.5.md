# diagram and commutative diagram　`def.commutative-diagram`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=def.commutative-diagram)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./def.commutative-diagram.md)

Precomposing with a functor $\lambda : I \to J$ gives **reindexing** $\lambda^{*} : \mathcal{C}^{J} \to \mathcal{C}^{I}$: it cuts a $J$-shaped diagram down to a $I$-shaped one.

In particular, the unique functor $I \to \mathbf{1}$ to the terminal category induces the **constant-diagram functor** $\Delta : \mathcal{C} \longrightarrow \mathcal{C}^{I}$, which sends an object $X$ to the constant diagram “$X$ at every position and $1_X$ on every arrow”.
