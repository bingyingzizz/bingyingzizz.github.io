## Statement · **draft**
Every integer $n > 1$ can be written uniquely as

$$n = p_{1}^{e_{1}} p_{2}^{e_{2}} \cdots p_{r}^{e_{r}},$$

where $p_{1} < p_{2} < \cdots < p_{r}$ are primes and $e_{i} \in \mathbb{Z}_{\ge 1}$.
