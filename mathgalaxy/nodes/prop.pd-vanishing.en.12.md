# Projective Dimension and the Vanishing of Ext　`prop.pd-vanishing`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=prop.pd-vanishing)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./prop.pd-vanishing.md)

⭐ **Practical form**: To test the dimension, one generally computes just **one** group for each coefficient module. It suffices that $\operatorname{Ext}^{n+1}_{R}(M, N)$ vanish for every $N$, without checking every degree. Conversely, to determine how large $\operatorname{pd}$ is, find a witness for which $\operatorname{Ext}^{n}$ is nonzero, namely some $N$.

⚠️ **Keep the two directions straight**: $\operatorname{pd}_{R} M$ concerns $\operatorname{Ext}(M, -)$ (the **first** variable), and $\operatorname{id}_{R} N$ concerns $\operatorname{Ext}(-, N)$ (the **second** variable).
