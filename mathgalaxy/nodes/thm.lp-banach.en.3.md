# Banach　`thm.lp-banach`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=thm.lp-banach)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./thm.lp-banach.md)

A further application of the DCT gives norm convergence: $\left| F - \sum_{k \le n} f_k \right|^p \le (2G)^p \in L^1$, hence

$$\left\| F - \sum_{k \le n} f_k \right\|_p^p = \int \left| F - \sum_{k \le n} f_k \right|^p \to 0$$

∎
