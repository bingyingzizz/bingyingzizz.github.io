# rotation　`prop.triangle-rotation`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=prop.triangle-rotation)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./prop.triangle-rotation.md)

⚠ **"Non-unique" does not mean "choose arbitrarily"**: the difference between extensions is controlled (a homotopy, or some degree of freedom), but there is **no canonical choice**. That is why one cannot work "constructively" in a triangulated category as in a derived category — extra enhanced structure (dg categories, stable $\infty$-categories) is needed to turn "choice" into "property".
