**Step 3. Counting lattice points in a rectangle.** In the rectangle $R = \{(x, y) \in \mathbb{Z}^{2} : 1 \le x \le P,\ 1 \le y \le Q\}$ (with $PQ$ lattice points in total), the line $qx = py$ contains no lattice points. Indeed, $p \mid qx$ and $p \nmid q$ imply $p \mid x$, but $1 \le x \le P < p$, a contradiction. Thus $R$ splits into two parts $R_{-} = \{qx - py > 0\}$ and $R_{+} = \{qx - py < 0\}$, with $\#R = \#R_{-} + \#R_{+} = PQ$.
