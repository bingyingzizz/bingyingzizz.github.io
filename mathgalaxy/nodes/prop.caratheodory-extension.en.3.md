# the Carathéodory extension agrees with the premeasure　`prop.caratheodory-extension`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=prop.caratheodory-extension)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./prop.caratheodory-extension.md)

The two directions of 1: $\mu^*(A) \le \mu_0(A)$ is direct ($A$ is covered by $A$ itself); the reverse uses countable additivity of $\mu _{0}$.

2 is the technical core: verify Carathéodory's splitting condition on $\mathfrak{A}$.
