## Statement · **stale**
Let $G$ be a finite group. A representation of $G$ over $F$ is a pair $(V,\rho)$, where $V$ is an $F$-vector space and $\rho:G\to GL(V)$ is a group homomorphism. It is equivalent to a unital left $F[G]$-module structure on $V$; hence representations of $G$ are the same as unital representations of the group algebra $F[G]$.
