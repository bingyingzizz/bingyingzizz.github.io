## Notes · **draft**
⭐ It is **the chain rule written backwards**: $\frac{d}{dt}F(\varphi(t)) = F'(\varphi(t))\varphi'(t)$, and Newton–Leibniz on both sides does it.

So the substitution formula has no independent content; it merely transports the derivative of a composition.

⚠ $\varphi$ need not be monotone: the limits in the formula take the values $\varphi(\alpha)$, $\varphi(\beta)$, and if $\varphi(\beta) < \varphi(\alpha)$ the integral naturally flips sign (this is precisely the use of the convention $\int_b^a = -\int_a^b$).
