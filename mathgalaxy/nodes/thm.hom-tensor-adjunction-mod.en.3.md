# Hom-Tensor Adjunction　`thm.hom-tensor-adjunction-mod`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=thm.hom-tensor-adjunction-mod)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./thm.hom-tensor-adjunction-mod.md)

The inverse is $\Psi(g) := \bigl(m \otimes n \mapsto g(m)(n)\bigr)$: since $(m, n) \mapsto g(m)(n)$ is balanced and biadditive, the universal property of `def.tensor-module` gives $\Psi(g)$ uniquely.

$\Phi \Psi$ and $\Psi \Phi$ are the identity on pure tensors (or on generators of $M$), and the generators span the whole module, so the two maps are mutually inverse.

Naturality: the formula for $\Phi$ involves no choices, only $\otimes$ and composition.

∎

⭐ This is **currying**: something in two variables is the same as something in one variable taking values in a function space.
