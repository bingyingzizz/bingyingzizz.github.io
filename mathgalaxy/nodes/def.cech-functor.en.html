Statement

On a site C\mathcal{C}C, the Čech functor H^\widehat{H}H is defined on presheaves by

H^(T)(X)  :=  lim→⁡R∈J(X)Hom⁡(R,T)\widehat{H}(T)(X) \;:=\; \varinjlim_{R \in J(X)} \operatorname{Hom}(R, T)H(T)(X):=R∈J(X)lim​​Hom(R,T)

(since J(X)J(X)J(X) is a filter, this is a filtered colimit). From hX∈J(X)h_{X} \in J(X)hX​∈J(X) and Hom⁡(hX,T)≅T(X)\operatorname{Hom}(h_{X}, T) \cong T(X)Hom(hX​,T)≅T(X) one obtains a natural map T→H^(T)T \to \widehat{H}(T)T→H(T).

Notes

"Taking the limit along larger and larger covering sieves" turns into a colimit on the TTT side, because Hom⁡(−,T)\operatorname{Hom}(-, T)Hom(−,T) is contravariant, and a contravariant functor takes limits along a filter to filtered colimits of the covariant functor. H^\widehat{H}H is the first step in pushing a presheaf toward a sheaf.
