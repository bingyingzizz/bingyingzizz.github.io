## Statement · **draft**
Let $A$ be a unital associative $F$-algebra. A linear representation of $A$ is a pair $(M,\theta)$, where $M$ is an $F$-vector space and $\theta:A\to\operatorname{End}_F(M)$ is a unital algebra homomorphism. Setting $a\cdot m=\theta(a)(m)$ makes $M$ a left $A$-module; this construction gives an equivalence between unital representations and left $A$-modules.
