## Statement · **draft**
Let $A$ be a unital associative $F$-algebra. A linear representation of $A$ is a pair $(M,\theta)$, where $M$ is an $F$-vector space and $\theta:A\to\operatorname{End}_F(M)$ is an algebra homomorphism (not necessarily unital; the condition $\theta(1_A)=\operatorname{id}_M$ is optional). If in addition $\theta(1_A)=\operatorname{id}_M$, it is called unital; then $a\cdot m=\theta(a)(m)$ makes it a left $A$-module, and unital representations correspond bijectively to left modules.
