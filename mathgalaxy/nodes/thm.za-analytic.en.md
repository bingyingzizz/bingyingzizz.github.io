## Statement · **draft**
Let $A$ be a finitely generated $\mathbb{Z}$-algebra and $A_\blacksquare$ the pre-analytic ring whose free module is

$$A_\blacksquare[S] := \varprojlim_i A[S_i] \qquad (S = \varprojlim_i S_i)$$

(that is, the $A$-valued measure $M(S,A)$). Then $A_\blacksquare$ is an analytic ring, and the forgetful functor

$$j^* : D(A_\blacksquare) \hookrightarrow D((A,\mathbb{Z})_\blacksquare)$$
