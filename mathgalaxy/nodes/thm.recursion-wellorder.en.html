Notes

⭐⭐ This is the licence to construct on the infinite. Before it, the only tools for building objects were finitely many steps (pairing, union, power set, separation, replacement); to build a whole family of mutually dependent objects one needs exactly this. It is also the real purpose of the word "well-ordered" — well-ordering guarantees that the part before each step has a beginning and an end, so the recursion can terminate.

⭐ Why the "well-ordered set" version rather than the "ordinal" version: recursion on ω\omegaω and transfinite recursion on ordinals are both special cases, so only the most general statement is recorded. (Exception: mathematical induction on N\mathbb{N}N has a more direct route — it comes from "ω\omegaω is the smallest inductive set" and does not need the general theory of well-orderings, so that node is kept.)

⚠ The right-hand side of the recursion gives the function F↾W<wF \upharpoonright W_{< w}F↾W<w​ of all preceding values, not a sentence. Written as a sentence, it would fall into the trap of "defining something by itself using something undefined".
