# homology of spheres　`thm.sphere-homology`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=thm.sphere-homology)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./thm.sphere-homology.md)

**The intersection deformation retracts onto the equator.** We have $U \cap V = S^{n} \setminus \{N, S\}$. Write a point as $(x, t)$, where $x \in \mathbb{R}^{n}$, $t \in (-1, 1)$, and $\lVert x \rVert^{2} + t^{2} = 1$. Since $t \ne \pm 1$, we have $x \ne 0$. Define

$$H_{s}(x, t) := \frac{(x,\, s t)}{\sqrt{\lVert x \rVert^{2} + s^{2} t^{2}}}\qquad (s \in [0,1]).$$
