**$\mathrm{d}^{2}=0$ has the same origin as the structure equations**: the structure equations of a surface are also a reading of $\mathrm{d}^{2}=0$ (`thm.structure-equations`). The advantage of the language of forms is that "mixed partials commute" and "the frame is integrable" become the same identity.
