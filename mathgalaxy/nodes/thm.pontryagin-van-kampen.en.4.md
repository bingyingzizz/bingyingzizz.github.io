# Pontryagin-van Kampen　`thm.pontryagin-van-kampen`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=thm.pontryagin-van-kampen)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./thm.pontryagin-van-kampen.md)

⭐ **This is the prototype for the theory of condensed abelian groups**. Replacing $\mathbb{T}$ with other test objects and local compactness with other hypotheses gives various dualities (Tannaka, Gelfand, and others). Condensed mathematics moves $\mathbb{T}$ to sheaves on CHaus.

**Proof (combining three cases).**
- **$M$ discrete**: Classical Pontryagin duality.

First take a surjection $\mathbb{Z}^{(I)} \twoheadrightarrow M$. Dualizing gives a closed embedding $\widehat M \hookrightarrow \mathbb{T}^{I}$, so $\widehat M$ is compact.
