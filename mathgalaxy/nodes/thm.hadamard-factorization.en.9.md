# Hadamard factorization theorem　`thm.hadamard-factorization`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=thm.hadamard-factorization)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./thm.hadamard-factorization.md)

**(i) Two elementary estimates for $E_{p}$.** For $|w|\le1/2$, we have $\log|E_{p}(w)|=\operatorname{Re}\bigl(-\sum_{k>p}w^{k}/k\bigr)$, hence $|\log|E_{p}(w)||\le2|w|^{p+1}$. For $|w|\ge1/2$, we have $\log|E_{p}(w)|=\log|1-w|+\operatorname{Re}\sum_{j\le p}w^{j}/j\ge\log|1-w|-C_{p}|w|^{p}$.
