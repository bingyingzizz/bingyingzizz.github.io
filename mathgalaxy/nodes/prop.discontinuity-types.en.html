Proof (the classification is exhaustive).

At a discontinuity x0x_0x0​, each one-sided limit has only two states: exists and is finite, or does not exist / is infinite.

Thus “both one-sided limits are finite” and “at least one fails” are mutually exclusive and exhaustive, and the former splits according to whether the two limits are equal into removable and jump — the three cases are mutually exclusive and all possibilities are listed.
