# field extension　`def.field-extension`

[in graph](../#n=def.field-extension)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./def.field-extension.md)

⭐ **Tower formula** (not a node in this graph): a finite extension $F \subseteq K \subseteq L$ satisfies

$$[L:F] = [L:K]\,[K:F],$$

because $L$ is a free $K$-module and $K$ is a free $F$-module; multiply bases. Every later “degree divides” statement relies on it.

⚠ **Finite degree does not mean computable**: $[\mathbb{Q}(\alpha):\mathbb{Q}]$ can be huge while $\alpha$ is a root of a concrete polynomial — “writable down” and “finite” are two different things (the theme of the quintic story).
