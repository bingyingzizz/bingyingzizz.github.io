## Statement · **draft**
Let $\mathcal{C}$ be a **preadditive category** and $M \in \mathcal{C}$. Then $M$ **is a zero object** $\iff$ $M$ **is a terminal object** $\iff$ $M$ **is an initial object**.

## Notes · **draft**
⭐ Thus a "zero object" has two equivalent formulations: $\operatorname{End}(M) = 1$ ($1_{M} = 0_{M}$), and "both terminal and initial" — in a preadditive category they are completely equivalent, so one may use whichever is convenient.
