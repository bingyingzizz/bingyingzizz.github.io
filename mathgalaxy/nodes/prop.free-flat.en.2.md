## Notes · **draft**
**Proof**: by the "unit" and "commutes with direct sums" properties in `prop.tensor-functor-properties`,

$$(-) \otimes_{R} R^{(I)} \;\cong\; \bigoplus_{i \in I} (-),$$

the right-hand side is "taking the $I$-fold direct sum", which is exact (a direct sum of functors is exact, since injectivity is detected componentwise) ✓.

**Projective modules are flat**: a projective module is a direct summand of a free module (the remark after `def.projective-object`), and direct summands inherit flatness (`prop.flat-summand`) ✓. ∎
