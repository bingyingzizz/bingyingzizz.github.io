## Statement · **draft**
In a real inner product space:

$$\langle x, y\rangle = \tfrac{1}{4}\bigl( \|x + y\|^2 - \|x - y\|^2 \bigr) .$$

In a complex inner product space:

$$\langle x, y\rangle = \tfrac{1}{4}\sum_{k=0}^{3} i^k \,\| x + i^k y \|^2 .$$
