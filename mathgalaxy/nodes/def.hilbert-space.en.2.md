## Notes · **draft**
Prototype: $L^2(\Omega, \mu)$ (completeness from Riesz–Fischer), and $\ell^2(I)$. Finite-dimensional inner product spaces are automatically complete, so they are degenerate Hilbert spaces.

⚠️ Convention clash: mathematical literature usually takes "linear in the first variable", physics (Dirac) takes "linear in the second". The difference is one conjugation, but **changing the convention also changes the conjugate-linearity in the Riesz representation** (whether $y \mapsto \langle \cdot , y\rangle$ is conjugate-linear or linear), so a single document must be consistent.
