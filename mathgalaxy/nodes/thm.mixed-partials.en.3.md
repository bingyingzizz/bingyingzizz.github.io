# Equality of Mixed Partial Derivatives　`thm.mixed-partials`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=thm.mixed-partials)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./thm.mixed-partials.md)

Continuity guarantees not "that the order can be swapped" but "that swapping produces no jump".

⭐ The proof strategy (full text on the edge): compute one **second difference** of $f$: $\Delta = f(a + he_i + ke_j) - f(a + he_i) - f(a + ke_j) + f(a)$ in two orders using the one-variable mean value theorem, obtaining two expressions "a second partial derivative at some intermediate point", then let $h, k \to 0$ and pass to the limit by continuity.
