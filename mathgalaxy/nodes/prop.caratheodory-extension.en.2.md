# the Carathéodory extension agrees with the premeasure　`prop.caratheodory-extension`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=prop.caratheodory-extension)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./prop.caratheodory-extension.md)

1. $\mu^*|_\mathfrak{A} = \mu_0$
2. **every set in $\mathfrak{A}$ is $\mu^{*}$-measurable**, hence

$$\mathfrak{A} \subseteq \mathcal{M}(\mathfrak{A}) \subseteq \mathcal{M}$$

($\mathcal{M}$ is the family of all $\mu^{*}$-measurable sets), so $\mu^{*}|_\mathcal{M}$ is an extension of $\mu _{0}$ to the $\sigma$-algebra $\mathcal{M}(\mathfrak{A})$.

## Notes · **draft**
This is the finale of the three-step "premeasure $\to$ outer measure $\to$ measure": **the premeasure is fully recovered on the extension to the $\sigma$-algebra**.
