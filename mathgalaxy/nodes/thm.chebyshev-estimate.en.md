## Statement · **draft**
There exist constants $0 < c_{1} < c_{2}$ such that for every $x \ge 2$,

$$c_{1}\,\frac{x}{\log x} < \pi(x) < c_{2}\,\frac{x}{\log x} .$$

($\pi(x) \asymp x/\log x$. Examples of constants from the elementary proof are $0.28$ and $6$.)
