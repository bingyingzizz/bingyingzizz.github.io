## Notes · **draft**
⭐ This corollary translates "the free solid group" into "a large product" — every later computation relies on it.

In particular, the compact projective objects of solid theory are not finitely generated free modules but **arbitrary products** $\prod_I \mathbb{Z}$.

⚠ $I$ is usually uncountable: for example, when $S = \mathbb{Z}_p$, the free rank of $C(S,\mathbb{Z})$ is countable, while $M(S,\mathbb{Z}) = \prod_{\mathbb{N}} \mathbb{Z}$ is uncountable.
