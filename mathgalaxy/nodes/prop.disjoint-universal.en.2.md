# Disjoint Universal Coproducts and Subcanonical Topologies　`prop.disjoint-universal`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=prop.disjoint-universal)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./prop.disjoint-universal.md)

## Statement · **draft**
Let $\mathcal{C}$ have finite limits. Then $X = \coprod_{i \in I} X_{i}$ is a **disjoint universal coproduct** in $\mathcal{C}$ $\iff$ for any (equivalently: for some) **subcanonical topology**, it is still a disjoint universal coproduct in the category of sheaves.

## Notes · **draft**
One direction: subcanonicity ensures that $X = h_{X}$, $X_{i} = h_{X_{i}}$ are both in the category of sheaves, and $\coprod_{i} h_{X_{i}}$ is already disjoint, hence automatically a sheaf.
