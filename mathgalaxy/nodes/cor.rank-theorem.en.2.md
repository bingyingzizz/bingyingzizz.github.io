# Constant Rank Theorem　`cor.rank-theorem`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=cor.rank-theorem)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./cor.rank-theorem.md)

Then for every $a \in U$ there are a **$C^{1}$ change of coordinates** $\varphi$ near $a$ and a **$C^{1}$ change of coordinates** $\psi$ near $f(a)$ such that (⚠ the smoothness of the coordinates follows that of $f$: $f \in C^{1}$ gives only $C^{1}$ coordinates; $f \in C^{k}$ gives $C^{k}$, and only $C^{\infty}$ gives smooth coordinates)

$$\psi \circ f \circ \varphi^{-1} (x_1, \ldots, x_n) = (x_1, \ldots, x_r, 0, \ldots, 0)$$

## Notes · **draft**
⭐ The rank theorem says "**a constant-rank map has only one local shape**": every map of constant rank $r$ looks the same locally, and the only invariant is the rank $r$.
