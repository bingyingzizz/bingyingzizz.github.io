Notes

⭐ In one sentence: "separated" = "equality in every stalk implies equality globally". The extra step that a sheaf has beyond a separated presheaf (the gluing of compatible families) is invisible in stalks — precisely the difference between "separated" and "sheaf".

Among the three, pullback = stalk is a direct consequence of "colimit", while the separatedness characterization is the criterion:

  • ① pullback is the stalk: x^∗(T)=lim→⁡U∋xT(U)\hat x^{*}(T) = \varinjlim_{U \ni x} T(U)x^∗(T)=lim​U∋x​T(U). This step uses only the definition "pullback = colimit along the continuous map" (the standard construction for pulling back sheaves along a continuous map).
  • ② pushforward and skyscraper floor: x^∗(E)(U)=E\hat x_{*}(E)(U) = Ex^∗​(E)(U)=E (x∈Ux \in Ux∈U) or empty — the usual shape of a skyscraper; x^!\hat x_{!}x^!​ is its left adjoint. ⚠️ The three form an adjunction chain x^!⊣x^∗⊣x^∗\hat x_{!} \dashv \hat x^{*} \dashv \hat x_{*}x^!​⊣x^∗⊣x^∗​, and the existence of this chain needs no hypothesis, but "x^∗\hat x^{*}x^∗ = stalk" holds only in the category of sheaves.
  • ③ separatedness: a presheaf is separated   ⟺  \iff⟺ T(U)→∏x∈UTxT(U) \to \prod_{x \in U}T_{x}T(U)→∏x∈U​Tx​ is injective. (  ⟹  )(\implies)(⟹) uses "two sections with the same germ at every point   ⟹  \implies⟹ are equal" (intersect the covers); (⇐)(\Leftarrow)(⇐) is checked pointwise. ⚠️ Separatedness is only half of the sheaf condition (the other half is "gluing", i.e. surjectivity).

⚠️ Remember the counterexample to gluing: the presheaf on X={0,1}X = \{0,1\}X={0,1} sending XXX to Z\mathbb{Z}Z and a point to 000 — it is separated but does not glue. So "stalks determine sections" does not mean "one can glue".

Hypotheses used in each direction (testing separatedness by stalks does not require separation axioms on the space):

  • A separated presheaf   ⟹  \implies⟹ the map from sections into the product of stalks is injective: equality of germs is local equality by the filtered colimit over neighbourhoods, after which separatedness applies; taking all witnessing neighbourhoods avoids pointwise choice.
  • Injectivity of the stalk maps on all open sets   ⟹  \implies⟹ separated: equality on a cover directly implies equality of germs at every point, with no Hausdorffness or compactness needed; the check on the empty open set must be included.
  • The stalk criterion does not imply gluing: the constant presheaf on the discrete two-point space taking a set with more than one element, and the point on the empty set, is separated but cannot glue two different local constants.
  • The pushforward formula in the original text needs correction: the pushforward of a set-valued sheaf along a point inclusion should take the one-point terminal object on open sets not containing the point, not the empty set; the left adjoint of the stalk functor also does not in general exist automatically, and this step of the original adjunction chain is unverified, recorded as a gap.
