## Notes · **draft**
By lem.endomorphism-local, $\operatorname{End}_A(V)$ is a local ring whose maximal ideal consists of the nilpotent endomorphisms; a maximal ideal is closed under addition, so $r+\eta$ is nilpotent.
Indecomposability cannot be dropped: for $V=F^2$ with $r=E_{12}$ and $\eta=E_{21}$ both are nilpotent but $r+\eta$ is invertible.
No commutativity of $r,\eta$ is needed.
