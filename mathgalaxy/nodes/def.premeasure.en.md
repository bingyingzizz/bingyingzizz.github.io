## Statement · **draft**
If $\mathcal{M}$ is only a **ring** (not necessarily a $\sigma$-algebra), while $\mu : \mathcal{M} \to [0, +\infty]$ still satisfies the two axioms of a measure:

$$\mu(\emptyset) = 0, \quad E_j \cap E_k = \emptyset\ (j \ne k),\ \bigcup_j E_j \in \mathcal{M} \implies \mu\Bigl(\bigcup_j E_j\Bigr) = \sum_j \mu(E_j)$$
