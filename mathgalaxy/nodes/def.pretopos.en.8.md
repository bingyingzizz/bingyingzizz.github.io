⚠️ **The most common comparison**: $\mathbf{Set}$ is a topos; $\mathbf{Grp}$, $\mathbf{Ring}$, $\mathbf{Cat}$ are all **not** pretopoi (coproducts are not universal). To decide whether a concrete category is a pretopos, **check ② first** — it is the item that fails most easily.
