# open mapping theorem　`thm.open-mapping`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=thm.open-mapping)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./thm.open-mapping.md)

By `prop.zero-isolated`, $z_{0}$ is an isolated zero of $f$ of order $m \ge 1$. Thus in some neighborhood of $z_{0}$, the only zero of $f$ is $z_{0}$. Choose a small circle $|z - z_{0}| = \rho$ on which $f$ does not vanish, and set $\delta := \min_{|z - z_{0}| = \rho}|f(z)| > 0$.
