## Notes · **draft**
⭐ Integrality is **proved**, not seen: as $t$ runs along $\gamma$ from $a$ to $b$, set

$$g(t) := \frac{\gamma(t) - a_0}{\exp\left( \int_a^t \frac{\gamma'(s)}{\gamma(s) - a_0}\,\mathrm{d}s \right)}.$$

Differentiating shows that the derivatives of numerator and denominator cancel, so $g$ is constant; at $t = b$ one gets $\exp\left( \int_a^b \cdot \right) = 1$, hence the integral is an integer multiple of $2\pi i$.
