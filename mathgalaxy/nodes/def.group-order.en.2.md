## Notes · **draft**
⭐ **The two “orders” are linked by Lagrange's theorem**: when $G$ is **finite**, $\operatorname{ord}(x)$ divides $|G|$ (for $G = \mathbb{Z}$ and $x = 1$ the order is infinite, so neither side makes sense), and $\operatorname{ord}(x)$ is exactly the order of the cyclic subgroup $\langle x \rangle$. This is the most used bridge in elementary group theory.
