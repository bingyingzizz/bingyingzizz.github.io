## Notes · **draft**
The crux is the **fiber product** $M \times_{s,t} M$: composition is not an operation on $M \times M$, but is defined only on some pairs of morphisms. Composition in a category is thus a **partial operation**, and "whether $f \circ g$ is defined" is itself something to check.

Examples: $\mathbf{Set}$ (sets and functions), $\mathbf{Mon}$ (monoids), $\mathbf{Grp}$ (groups), $\mathbf{Ab}$ (abelian groups), $\mathbf{Rng}$ (rings), $G\text{-}\mathbf{Set}$ ($G$-sets).

**The simplex category**: $\mathcal{O} = \mathbb{N}$,

$$M = \{ (m, n, f) : m, n \in \mathbb{N},\ f : [m] \to [n] \text{increasing} \}, \qquad [m] = \{ 1, \ldots, m \}$$
