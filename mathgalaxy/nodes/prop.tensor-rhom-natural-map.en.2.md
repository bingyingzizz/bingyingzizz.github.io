# Pairing of Tensor with Derived Hom　`prop.tensor-rhom-natural-map`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=prop.tensor-rhom-natural-map)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./prop.tensor-rhom-natural-map.md)

$$M \otimes^{\mathbf{L}}_{\mathbb{Z}} \operatorname{RHom}_{\mathbb{Z}}(P,\ N) \longrightarrow \operatorname{RHom}_{\mathbb{Z}}\bigl(\operatorname{RHom}_{\mathbb{Z}}(M,\ P),\ N\bigr).$$

## Notes · **draft**
⚠️ **In general it is not an isomorphism** — making it one requires extra finiteness/duality conditions. What is given here is a **natural map**, whose use is to replace "the RHom of the dual" by "the RHom of a tensor product", so that free resolutions can be used to compute.
