# solvable by radicals　`thm.radical-solvable`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=thm.radical-solvable)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./thm.radical-solvable.md)

**Returning to $K$**: The extension $K^{\prime}/F$ is Galois (as a compositum of two normal extensions). The restriction map $\operatorname{Gal}(M/F^{\prime})\to\operatorname{Gal}(K^{\prime}/F^{\prime})$ is surjective, so $\operatorname{Gal}(K^{\prime}/F^{\prime})$ is solvable. We also have the exact sequence $1\to\operatorname{Gal}(K^{\prime}/F^{\prime})\to\operatorname{Gal}(K^{\prime}/F)\to\operatorname{Gal}(F^{\prime}/F)\to1$ (the cyclotomic extension is Galois and restriction is surjective). Both ends are solvable and $\operatorname{Gal}(F^{\prime}/F)$ is abelian, so $\operatorname{Gal}(K^{\prime}/F)$ is solvable.
