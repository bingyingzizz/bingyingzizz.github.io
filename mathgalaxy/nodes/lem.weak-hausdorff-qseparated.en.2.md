## Notes · **draft**
**Intuition**: quasi-separatedness asks that "the intersection of two compact pieces is still a compact piece". Weak Hausdorffness guarantees exactly this — it says images of compact pieces are closed, so the pullback of two compact pieces along $X$ lands in a compact piece.

⚠️ Here the compactness of $X_{1} \times_{X} X_{2}$ is a direct application of the "fiber product of compact Hausdorff along weakly Hausdorff" item in the $\mathrm{CGWH}$ family.
