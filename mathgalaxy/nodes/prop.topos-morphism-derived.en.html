Notes

⭐ How to read it: to compute the derived functor of a morphism of topoi, first compute "the cohomology at the image of the other side" pointwise, then sheafify the result back. This is the same as the definition of sheaf cohomology (the right derived functor of the section functor), only "sections at a point" are replaced by "pullback along a morphism of topoi".
