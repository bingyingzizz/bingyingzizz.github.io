[in graph](../#n=def.submodule)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./def.submodule.md)

⭐ The submodule lattice: arbitrary intersections are submodules, but unions usually are not; the submodule generated by $S$ is the smallest submodule containing $S$. Quotient modules are constructed as $M/N$ (see `def.quotient-module`, a later node).
