## Statement · **draft**
**(Merged form)** A nonempty subset $U \subseteq V$ is a subspace if and only if for all $u, v \in U$ and $c \in K$ one has $cu + v \in U$.

**(Ascending chain form)** If $U_1 \subseteq U_2 \subseteq U_3 \subseteq \cdots$ is a sequence of subspaces (an increasing chain), then $\bigcup_n U_n$ is also a subspace.
