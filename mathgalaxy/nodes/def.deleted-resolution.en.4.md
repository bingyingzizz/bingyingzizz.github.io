[in graph](../#n=def.deleted-resolution)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./def.deleted-resolution.md)

② Some books retain $M$ in degree $-1$ and call the result the **augmented complex**. The two complexes differ by a single term, so specify which one is used in a formula.

⚠️ **Notation trap**: $\operatorname{Hom}(P_{\bullet}, N)$ is the Hom complex obtained by viewing $N$ as a complex concentrated in degree $0$. Its degrees run in the **opposite direction** from the subscripts of $P_{\bullet}$ (without dualizing, one obtains a chain complex). Thus use $H^{n}$ for Ext and $H_{n}$ for Tor.
