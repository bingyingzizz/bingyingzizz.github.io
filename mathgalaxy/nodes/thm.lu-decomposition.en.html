Statement

What this does: Expresses Gaussian elimination as multiplication by a sequence of elementary matrices. Denote the product of those elementary matrices by LLL and the result of elimination by UUU to obtain A=LUA = LUA=LU.

Suppose A∈Mn(K)A \in M_{n}(K)A∈Mn​(K) and all its nnn leading principal minors are nonzero. There is then a unique factorization

A=LU,A = LU ,A=LU,

where LLL is unit lower triangular (every diagonal entry is 111) and UUU is upper triangular.

Permutation version (always exists): For every invertible AAA, there is a permutation matrix PPP such that PA=LUPA = LUPA=LU (row swaps ensure that the pivots are nonzero).

Use: Solving Ax=bAx = bAx=b reduces to solving Ly=bLy = bLy=b by forward substitution and Ux=yUx = yUx=y by back substitution, both of which are O(n2)O(n^{2})O(n2). When AAA stays fixed, L,UL, UL,U can be reused.

Notes

Why LULULU holds: Each step of Gaussian elimination is left multiplication by an elementary matrix. Composing the elimination steps gives L−1L^{-1}L−1. Nonzero leading principal minors ensure that no row swaps are needed. See the edge imp.lu-decomposition-proof for the full proof, including Schur complement induction and uniqueness. The permutation version uses partial pivoting, as shown in the final paragraph of that proof.
