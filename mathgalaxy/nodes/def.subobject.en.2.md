## Notes · **draft**
Replacing "subset" by "monomorphism" has the advantage of no longer depending on elements — so this language transfers verbatim to categories without elements. Both "intersection" and "preimage" are pullbacks (fiber products): $Y \cap Z$ solves "the part that is both in $Y$ and in $Z$", and $f^{-1}(Y)$ solves "the part where $y = f(x)$ has a solution"; for details see @@def.intersection-subobject@@ and @@def.preimage-subobject@@.
