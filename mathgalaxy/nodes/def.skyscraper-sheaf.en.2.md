# Skyscraper Sheaf　`def.skyscraper-sheaf`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=def.skyscraper-sheaf)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./def.skyscraper-sheaf.md)

The restriction maps: for $x \in U \subseteq V$ it is $\mathrm{id}_{A}$, and in all other cases the zero map. Equivalently, $i_{x,*}A$ is the sheaf that **stands** $A$ **on the single point** $x$ and keeps nothing anywhere else.

## Notes · **draft**
⭐ **Shape**: a group perched on top of a column — hence "skyscraper". It is the simplest **non-trivial** sheaf: only one layer has content.
