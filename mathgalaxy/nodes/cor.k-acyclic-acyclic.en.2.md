## Notes · **draft**
**Immediate corollary**: Apply the preceding proposition degree by degree. Every pair of adjacent degrees satisfies the boundedness condition with bound $K$, so the complex is exact in every degree.

⭐ **This is the conversion step used throughout the chapter**: The acyclicity proofs first establish **bounded** acyclicity on Stone or compact pieces, then use this corollary to obtain ordinary acyclicity and hence cohomological vanishing.
