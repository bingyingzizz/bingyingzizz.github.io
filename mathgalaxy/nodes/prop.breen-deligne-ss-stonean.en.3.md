# The Breen–Deligne Spectral Sequence on Stonean Spaces　`prop.breen-deligne-ss-stonean`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=prop.breen-deligne-ss-stonean)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./prop.breen-deligne-ss-stonean.md)

Take the standard spectral sequence of the "degree filtration on the decomposition"; its $E_{1}$ terms are the Ext of the individual free objects.

**How to read the shape of $E_{1}$**: $M^{s_{p,i}}$ is "the product of $s_{p,i}$ copies of $M$", $M^{s_{p,i}} \times S$ pairs it with the test space $S$; $H^{q}(-, N)$ is condensed cohomology on that space.

That is: **convert Ext into the cohomology of a batch of spaces**, and the cohomology of spaces can be killed by the acyclicity theorems of §8.1–8.2.
