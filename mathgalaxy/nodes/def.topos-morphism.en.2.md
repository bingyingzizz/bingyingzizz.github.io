## Notes · **draft**
The motivation comes from topological spaces: a continuous map $\varphi : X \to Y$ gives a pair of functors between categories of sheaves — pull sheaves back along $\varphi$ ($f^{*}$), then push them forward ($f_{*}$).

This pair satisfies $f^{*} \dashv f_{*}$, and $f^{*}$ is exact.

So a "map" between topoi is defined on this pattern: not a functor, but a pair of adjoint functors, with the direction opposite to geometric intuition (the left one is the pullback).
