[in graph](../#n=def.field)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./def.field.md)

- $\mathbb{R}$ (reals): after constructing it from Cauchy sequences, verify that it satisfies these identities (see "$\mathbb{R}$ is a complete ordered field");

- $\mathbb{Z}$ is **not** a field: $2$ has no multiplicative inverse in it;

$\mathbb{N}$ is not either: $1 - 2$ is meaningless.

⚠ "field" here is not the same as the "ring" of measure theory: there a ring is a **ring of sets**, meaning a **family of sets** closed under finite unions and differences — same name, different object.
