## Statement · **draft**
Let $g : \mathcal{C}' \to \mathcal{C}$ be a functor between two sites. It is called **cocontinuous** if the functor $g^{\sharp}$ (pushing presheaves forward along $g$ and then sheafifying) **sends sheaves to sheaves**.

## Notes · **draft**
**What it says**: $g$ is **cocontinuous** $\iff$ after pushing a presheaf forward along $g$ and sheafifying (that is, $g^{\sharp}$) it is **still a sheaf**.
