# Stonean acyclic　`prop.stonean-cond-acyclic`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=prop.stonean-cond-acyclic)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./prop.stonean-cond-acyclic.md)

⭐ **This is the starting point of the whole chapter**: every later step of "acyclicity" reduces a general space to a Stonean one (first Stoneify, then use Čech complexity to measure the deviation), and this Stonean cell is already zero.

⚠️ Note that this holds for **any** condensed abelian group $M$; it does not require $M$ to come from a topological group.
