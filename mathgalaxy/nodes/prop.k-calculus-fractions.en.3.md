# Calculus of Fractions in the Homotopy Category　`prop.k-calculus-fractions`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=prop.k-calculus-fractions)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./prop.k-calculus-fractions.md)

Write $\theta := f - w : K^{\bullet} \oplus L'^{\bullet} \to L^{\bullet}$ and take its **shifted cone** $N^{\bullet} := M(\theta)[-1]$ — the cone triangle gives two morphisms, and the square commutes

$$N^{\bullet} \xrightarrow{\;g\;} K^{\bullet}, \qquad N^{\bullet} \xrightarrow{\;t\;} L'^{\bullet}, \qquad f \circ g = w \circ t .$$
