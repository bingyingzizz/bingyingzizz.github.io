## Notes · **draft**
The set $\operatorname{Ann}(V)$ is the kernel of the structure map, hence an ideal. Faithfulness is equivalent to injectivity of $A\to\operatorname{End}_F(V)$. The annihilator of a simple module is a primitive ideal; in the finite-dimensional semisimple case these are exactly the maximal two-sided ideals. The annihilator of a direct sum is the intersection of the annihilators.
