Statement draft

Let FFF be algebraically closed and let VVV be a finite-dimensional irreducible representation of AAA. Then End⁡A(V)=F⋅id⁡V\operatorname{End}_A(V)=F\cdot\operatorname{id}_VEndA​(V)=F⋅idV​; every AAA-endomorphism is scalar.

Notes draft

By thm.schur-lemma, D=End⁡A(V)D=\operatorname{End}_A(V)D=EndA​(V) is a finite-dimensional FFF-division algebra. For a∈Da\in Da∈D, F(a)F(a)F(a) is a finite extension of FFF; since FFF is algebraically closed, F(a)=FF(a)=FF(a)=F, hence a∈Fa\in Fa∈F. Alternatively, aaa has an eigenvalue λ\lambdaλ, and a−λid⁡a-\lambda\operatorname{id}a−λid is not invertible, hence zero. This is the source of "endomorphisms are scalars" in character theory.
