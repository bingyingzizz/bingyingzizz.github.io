## Notes · **draft**
⭐ The point is to treat "taking exact sequences" as an **object that can be manipulated**: two exact sequences can be added, and kernels and cokernels taken, with the result staying inside — it is this closure that makes the spectral-sequence machinery (filtered complexes, double complexes) writable.
