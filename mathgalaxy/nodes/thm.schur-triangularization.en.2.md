# Schur Triangularization　`thm.schur-triangularization`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=thm.schur-triangularization)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./thm.schur-triangularization.md)

where $T$ is **upper triangular** and the diagonal entries of $T$ are exactly the eigenvalues of $A$ (in any prescribed order).

Real case: Every real square matrix is orthogonally similar to a **real Schur form**, which is block upper triangular with diagonal blocks of size $1\times 1$ or $2\times 2$ (the latter corresponding to conjugate pairs of complex eigenvalues).

**Special case**: When $A$ is normal, $T$ must be diagonal. This is the spectral theorem (`thm.spectral-matrix`).
