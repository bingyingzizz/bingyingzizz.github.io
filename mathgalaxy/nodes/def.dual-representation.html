设 (V,θ)(V,\theta)(V,θ) 是 AAA 的表示。对偶空间 V∗=Hom⁡F(V,F)V^*=\operatorname{Hom}_F(V,F)V∗=HomF​(V,F) 上取 θ∗(a)ℓ=ℓ∘θ(a)\theta^*(a)\ell=\ell\circ\theta(a)θ∗(a)ℓ=ℓ∘θ(a)。由于 θ∗(ab)=θ∗(b)θ∗(a)\theta^*(ab)=\theta^*(b)\theta^*(a)θ∗(ab)=θ∗(b)θ∗(a)，V∗V^*V∗ 是反代数 AopA^{\mathrm{op}}Aop 的表示，一般不是 AAA 的表示。对群表示，ρ∗(g)=ρ(g−1)T\rho^*(g)=\rho(g^{-1})^Tρ∗(g)=ρ(g−1)T 使 V∗V^*V∗ 成为 GGG 的表示。

Statement draft

Let (V,θ)(V,\theta)(V,θ) be a representation of AAA. On the dual space V∗=Hom⁡F(V,F)V^*=\operatorname{Hom}_F(V,F)V∗=HomF​(V,F) put θ∗(a)ℓ=ℓ∘θ(a)\theta^*(a)\ell=\ell\circ\theta(a)θ∗(a)ℓ=ℓ∘θ(a). Since θ∗(ab)=θ∗(b)θ∗(a)\theta^*(ab)=\theta^*(b)\theta^*(a)θ∗(ab)=θ∗(b)θ∗(a), the space V∗V^*V∗ is a representation of the opposite algebra AopA^{\mathrm{op}}Aop, not generally of AAA. For a group representation, ρ∗(g)=ρ(g−1)T\rho^*(g)=\rho(g^{-1})^Tρ∗(g)=ρ(g−1)T makes V∗V^*V∗ a representation of GGG.

Notes draft

The transpose convention is forced by contravariance: θ∗\theta^*θ∗ is an anti-homomorphism. In the group case the inverse restores a genuine action. In a basis, the matrix of ρ∗(g)\rho^*(g)ρ∗(g) is the inverse transpose of that of ρ(g)\rho(g)ρ(g). In finite dimension the double dual is naturally isomorphic to VVV.
