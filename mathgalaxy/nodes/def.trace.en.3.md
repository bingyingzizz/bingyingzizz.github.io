# trace　`def.trace`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=def.trace)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./def.trace.md)

⭐ Its deeper meaning: $\operatorname{tr}$ is the **unique (up to a scalar) central linear functional** on the matrix algebra, and also the "trace form" from the $K^{n}$-algebra to $K$ — it reappears in Galois theory and characteristic classes.

⚠ "Similarity invariance" is weaker than "cyclic invariance": $\operatorname{tr}(P^{-1}AP) = \operatorname{tr}(A P^{-1} P) = \operatorname{tr}A$ is only a special case of the latter.

Conversely, equal trace does not imply similarity ($\begin{pmatrix} 0 & 1 \\ 0 & 0 \end{pmatrix}$ and the zero matrix both have trace $0$ but are not similar).
