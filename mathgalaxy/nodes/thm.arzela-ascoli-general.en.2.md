## Notes · **draft**
"1 $\implies$ 2": total boundedness gives a finite $\varepsilon$-net $f_1, \ldots, f_n$, and then $\mathcal{H} \cup \{f_1,\ldots,f_n\}$ is still equicontinuous (finitely many continuous functions plus one equicontinuous family).

⭐ This is the same theorem as `thm.arzela-ascoli` in the graph ($\mathbb{R}$-valued, compact metric space, uniformly bounded), in a second form: here $E$ may be any metric space, and "uniformly bounded" is refined to "pointwise totally bounded".
