# Goursat's Triangle Lemma　`lem.goursat-triangle`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=lem.goursat-triangle)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./lem.goursat-triangle.md)

**Step 3 (estimate)**: let $\varepsilon_n = \sup_{z \in T^{(n)}} |\eta(z)| \to 0$; then $|I(T^{(n)})| \le \varepsilon_n \cdot (L/2^n) \cdot (L/2^n)$, hence $|I| \le 4^n \cdot \varepsilon_n \cdot L^2/4^n = \varepsilon_n L^2 \to 0$.

⚠ Holomorphy is used only twice here: $f$ is continuous (to obtain $z^*$), and the difference quotient has a limit as $z \to z^*$ (that is, $\eta \to 0$).

For this reason the conclusion is much stronger than the Green's theorem route.
