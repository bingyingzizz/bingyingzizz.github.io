## Notes · **draft**
Equivalent description: $Y \subseteq X(\cdot)$ is open $\iff$ for every $S \in \mathbf{CHaus}$ and every $f \in X(S)$, $f^{-1}(Y)$ is open in $S$.

So "the underlying space of $X$" records all the topological information that $X$ can see, but **in general it discards the finer structure of $X$ itself** — unless $X$ comes from a compactly generated space.
