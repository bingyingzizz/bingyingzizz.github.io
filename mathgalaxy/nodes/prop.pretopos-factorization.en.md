## Statement · **draft**
Let $\mathcal{C}$ be a pretopos. Then

1. $\mathcal{C}$ is **balanced**: a morphism that is both monic and epic is an isomorphism;
2. every morphism is **strict**: $\operatorname{im} f \cong \operatorname{coim} f$;
3. every morphism has a **unique** epi-mono factorization

$$f : X \twoheadrightarrow \overline{X} \rightarrowtail Y, \qquad \overline{X} := X / R,\quad R := X \times_{Y} X$$
