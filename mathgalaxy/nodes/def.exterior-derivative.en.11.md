# Exterior Derivative: Coordinate Formula on Euclidean Open Sets　`def.exterior-derivative`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=def.exterior-derivative)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./def.exterior-derivative.md)

⚠️ **Three equivalent definitions, at different costs**: the coordinate formula (this section; most concrete but coordinate-dependent), the axiomatic one (Leibniz + $\mathrm{d}^{2}=0$ + agreement on functions; one must prove existence and uniqueness), and the coordinate-free form $[\omega](X_{0},\ldots,X_{k}) = \sum(-1)^{i}X_{i}\omega(\ldots)$ (most geometric, it displays intrinsicness directly). They are equivalent, but **only the last one shows that $\mathrm{d}$ is independent of coordinates**.
