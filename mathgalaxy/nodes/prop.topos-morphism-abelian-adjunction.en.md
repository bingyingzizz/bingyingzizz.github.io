## Statement · **draft**
Let $f : \mathcal{T} \to \mathcal{T}'$ be a morphism of topoi. Then the pullback $f^{-1} : \mathcal{T}'(\mathbf{Ab}) \to \mathcal{T}(\mathbf{Ab})$ has a **right adjoint** $f_{*}$, and $f^{-1}$ is **exact**.
