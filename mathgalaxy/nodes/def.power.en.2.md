## Notes · **draft**
⭐ A power is just a compressed notation for "multiplication written many times"; it has no new content — but it turns "$n$ times" into an **object one can induct on**: `thm.binomial`, `thm.bernoulli`, geometric series and power series all induct on $n$.

⚠ Only **integer** exponents are defined here.

$a^{1/2}$ must wait for `thm.nth-root` (n-th roots), and $a^{\sqrt{2}}$ for `def.real-power` — the order cannot be reversed, or "taking roots" would be an undefined notion.
