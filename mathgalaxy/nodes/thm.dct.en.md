## Statement · **draft**
Let $\{f_n\} \subseteq L^1$, let $f$ be **measurable**, let $f_n \to f$ a.e., and suppose there is a **dominating function** $g \in L^1$ such that

$$|f_n| \le g\quad  \text{a.e.}\quad  \forall n$$

Then $f \in L^1$ and

$$\int f = \lim_{n\to\infty} \int f_n$$
