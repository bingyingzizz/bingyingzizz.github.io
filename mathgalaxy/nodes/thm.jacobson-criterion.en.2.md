## Notes · **draft**
The proof is on the edge `imp.jacobson`: first $J(A)$ annihilates every irreducible module; then it is a two-sided ideal; finally the description as a finite intersection of maximal left ideals shows that $J(A)=0$ is equivalent to semisimplicity of the regular module.
