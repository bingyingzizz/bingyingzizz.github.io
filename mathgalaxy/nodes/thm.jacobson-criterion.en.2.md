## Notes · **draft**
If $A$ is semisimple, the regular module is a direct sum of simple modules; indeed $J(A)$ is the intersection of the maximal left ideals, and $J(A)$ annihilates every simple module, so $J(A)\subseteq\operatorname{Ann}(A)=0$. Conversely, if $J(A)=0$, then $A/J(A)=A$ is semisimple; more directly, the regular module has a simple submodule, and the radical property splits it off. Finite dimension is used for termination.
