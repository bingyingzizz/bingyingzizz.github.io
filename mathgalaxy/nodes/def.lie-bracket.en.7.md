# Lie Bracket　`def.lie-bracket`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=def.lie-bracket)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./def.lie-bracket.md)

**The boundary with connections**: $\nabla_{X}Y$ and $[X,Y]$ look alike but behave differently — a connection is extra structure (`def.affine-connection`), while the bracket is intrinsic to the manifold. The torsion-free condition $\nabla_{X}Y - \nabla_{Y}X = [X,Y]$ is precisely the use of this free bracket to constrain the connection.
📌 Example: on a matrix group the bracket is the **commutator** $[A,B] = AB - BA$. Taking $E,F,H$ as the standard basis of $\mathfrak{sl}_{2}$, one has $[E,F] = H$, $[H,E] = 2E$, $[H,F] = -2F$ (see `ex-sl2-commutator`).
