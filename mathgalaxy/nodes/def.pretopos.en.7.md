**Pretopos vs topos: the missing item**. A topos = a pretopos **+** (effective epimorphisms + local representability, or equivalently Giraud's axioms). So the whole meaning of the four letters "pretopos" is: **it already has all the apparatus for quotients, fibred products, and equivalence relations, but not yet the layer that makes "sheaves"**. This is also why many constructions (internal equivalence relations, stability of effective epimorphisms) already work for pretopoi.
