# orthogonal complement　`def.orthogonal-complement`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=def.orthogonal-complement)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./def.orthogonal-complement.md)

- $U \cap U^{\perp} = \{0\}$;
- $U \subseteq (U^{\perp})^{\perp}$;
- **in finite dimension**, $V = U \oplus U^{\perp}$, and moreover $(U^{\perp})^{\perp} = U$ and $\dim U^{\perp} = \dim V - \dim U$.

## Notes · **draft**
⭐ «$V = U \oplus U^{\perp}$» is what an inner product space has **beyond** a general vector space: a general subspace has complements, but they are not unique (`def.direct-sum`), whereas the orthogonal complement is the **unique** complement.
