# absolute value　`def.abs`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=def.abs)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./def.abs.md)

The distance between two points is $|x - y|$ — the prototype of the distance in a **metric space**: discard the difference, keep only the notion of distance itself.

Two identities follow at once (both by cases from the definition):
- $|-x| = |x|$;
- $|xy| = |x|\,|y|$.

⚠ **Estimates with inequalities are not part of the definition**: $|x + y| \le |x| + |y|$ is a **theorem** (the triangle inequality) — the definition has only cases, no inequality.
