# Möbius transformations preserve circles　`prop.mobius-circle`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=prop.mobius-circle)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./prop.mobius-circle.md)

⚠️ Circles are preserved but **not their interiors**: whether a disk maps to a disk or to its exterior depends on orientation. A real-coefficient Möbius transformation (real coefficients, $ad - bc$ ≠ 0) sends the upper half-plane to the upper or lower half-plane; to decide the direction, look at the image of one test point.
