# criterion for solid completeness　`lem.solid-completion-criterion`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=lem.solid-completion-criterion)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./lem.solid-completion-criterion.md)

`thm.solid-abelian` is its instance ($\mathcal{A} = \operatorname{Cond}(\mathbf{Ab})$, $F(\mathbb{Z}[S]) = Z[S]^{\blacksquare}$).

⚠ The condition requires an isomorphism only for **kernels**, not for cokernels — but dually, writing it for cokernels works the same (see Remark 5.11 of the source).

📌 The proof is standard homological algebra: first show that $\mathcal{A}_F$ is closed under kernels and limits, then bring cokernels in using "maps between two direct sums", and finally reduce complexes to a single point by Postnikov truncation.
