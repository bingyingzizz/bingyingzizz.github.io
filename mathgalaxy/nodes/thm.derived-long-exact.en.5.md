# Long Exact Sequence of Derived Functors　`thm.derived-long-exact`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=thm.derived-long-exact)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./thm.derived-long-exact.md)

⭐ **This is the entire payoff of deriving**: originally $F$ gave only half a sequence; $RF$ supplies the connecting homomorphism $\delta$ and the long exact sequence comes alive.

⚠️ **None of the three hypotheses can be dropped**: $R^{n}F$ is defined by taking injective resolutions in $C^{+}(\mathcal{A})$ and then homology — without "enough injectives" every $R^{n}F$ in this chain is **undefined**; without $F$ additive and left exact, neither $R^{0}F = F$ nor the long exact sequence holds.
