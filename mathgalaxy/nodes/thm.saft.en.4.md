# special adjoint functor theorem　`thm.saft`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=thm.saft)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./thm.saft.md)

The meaning of the construction: all ways of mapping $X$ into the image of $G$ form a category $X \downarrow G$; a limit in it — $F(X)$ — is the object of $\mathcal{D}$ "closest" to $X$. Every $X \to G(Y)$ can be organised into $X \to G(F(X)) \to G(Y)$.

The verification is term by term from the universal property of representing objects.
