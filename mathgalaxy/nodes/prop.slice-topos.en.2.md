# Slice Topos　`prop.slice-topos`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=prop.slice-topos)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./prop.slice-topos.md)

1. $j_{T}$ is **cocontinuous**, hence gives a topos morphism $j_{T} : \widetilde{\mathcal{C}/T} \to \widetilde{\mathcal{C}}$;
2. $\widetilde{\mathcal{C}/T} \;\simeq\; \widetilde{\mathcal{C}}/\widetilde{T}$;
3. under this equivalence, $j_{T!}$ corresponds to the forgetful functor $\widetilde{\mathcal{C}}/\widetilde{T} \to \widetilde{\mathcal{C}}$, and $j_{T}^{-1}$ to $\mathcal{F} \mapsto (\mathcal{F} \times \widetilde{T} \to \widetilde{T})$.
