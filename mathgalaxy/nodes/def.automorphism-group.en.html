Statement

Let GGG be a group. An isomorphism from GGG to itself, σ:G→G\sigma : G \to Gσ:G→G, is called an automorphism; all automorphisms form a group under composition,

Aut⁡(G)≤Sym⁡(G),\operatorname{Aut}(G) \le \operatorname{Sym}(G),Aut(G)≤Sym(G),

the automorphism group of GGG.

For g∈Gg \in Gg∈G, the map cg(x):=gxg−1c_g(x) := gxg^{-1}cg​(x):=gxg−1 is an automorphism, an inner automorphism; all inner automorphisms

Inn⁡(G):={ cg:g∈G }⊴Aut⁡(G)\operatorname{Inn}(G) := \{\, c_g : g \in G \,\} \trianglelefteq \operatorname{Aut}(G)Inn(G):={cg​:g∈G}⊴Aut(G)

form a normal subgroup, and Inn⁡(G)≅G/Z(G)\operatorname{Inn}(G) \cong G/Z(G)Inn(G)≅G/Z(G). The quotient Out⁡(G):=Aut⁡(G)/Inn⁡(G)\operatorname{Out}(G) := \operatorname{Aut}(G)/\operatorname{Inn}(G)Out(G):=Aut(G)/Inn(G) is the outer automorphism group.
