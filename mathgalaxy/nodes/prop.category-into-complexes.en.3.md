## Notes · **draft**
⭐ Use: **an "object" may be regarded as a complex concentrated in one term**. So constructions in $\mathcal{C}$ and constructions in $\mathcal{C}(\mathcal{C})$ may be moved back and forth freely, and a short exact sequence $0 \to M \to N \to P \to 0$ no longer needs to be distinguished from an exact sequence of two-term complexes.
