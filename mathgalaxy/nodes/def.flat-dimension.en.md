## Statement · **draft**
Let $M$ be an $R$-module. The **flat dimension** of $M$, denoted $\operatorname{fd}_{R}M$, is found by asking whether $M$ has a flat resolution of length $n$ (an exact sequence $0\to F_{n}\to\cdots\to F_{0}\to M\to 0$ with each $F_{i}$ flat) and taking the least such integer $n$. If no such integer exists, set $\operatorname{fd}_{R}M=\infty$.
