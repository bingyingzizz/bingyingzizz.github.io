## Notes · **draft**
**The presence or absence of the absolute value is the presence or absence of orientation**: without orientation only densities integrate, and the change of variables carries $|\det|$ (`prop.volume-density-well-defined`); with orientation the $n$-form carries its own sign, and the change of variables carries $\det$. Two bookkeepings of one fact.
