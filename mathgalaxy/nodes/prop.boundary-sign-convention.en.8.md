# Sign Convention for Boundaries　`prop.boundary-sign-convention`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=prop.boundary-sign-convention)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./prop.boundary-sign-convention.md)

**⚠ The direction that is easy to copy wrong**: the proof compares, in $\mathbb{R}^{n}$, "the determinant of the face's basis together with $N$", not "the direction of the cross product of the face's basis with $N$". The former is pure algebra and can be checked term by term; the latter is only intuitive in three dimensions. Intuition below dimension three helps one remember, but is not a proof.
