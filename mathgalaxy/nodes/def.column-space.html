说明

列空间是「AAA 能到达哪里」；零空间（齐次解空间）是「哪些方向被 AAA 压成 000」。两者维数相加为 nnn。

行空间与列空间的维数相等（都等于秩），但它们是不同空间里的子空间；联系来自转置：Col⁡(A)=Row⁡(AT)\operatorname{Col}(A) = \operatorname{Row}(A^{\mathsf T})Col(A)=Row(AT)。

秩–零化度：dim⁡Col⁡(A)+dim⁡ker⁡A=n\dim \operatorname{Col}(A) + \dim \ker A = ndimCol(A)+dimkerA=n。

Solvability of systems: b∈Col⁡(A)b \in \operatorname{Col}(A)b∈Col(A) if and only if Ax=bAx = bAx=b has a solution. After reducing AAA to reduced row echelon form, the pivot columns — that is, the pivot columns of AAA — give a basis of Col⁡(A)\operatorname{Col}(A)Col(A).

Notes draft

The column space is "where AAA can reach"; the null space (solution space of the homogeneous system) is "which directions AAA crushes to 000". Their dimensions add up to nnn.

Row and column spaces have the same dimension (both equal the rank), but they are subspaces of different spaces; the link comes from transposition: Col⁡(A)=Row⁡(AT)\operatorname{Col}(A) = \operatorname{Row}(A^{\mathsf T})Col(A)=Row(AT).

Rank–nullity: dim⁡Col⁡(A)+dim⁡ker⁡A=n\dim \operatorname{Col}(A) + \dim \ker A = ndimCol(A)+dimkerA=n.
