## Notes · **reviewed**
In one sentence: **all the data of $F$ is generated by one element $s$ of $X$**. Given any other $t$, there is a unique $f$ pushing $s$ across to it — so elements elsewhere do not come from nowhere; they are pushed out of $s$.

An equivalent phrasing: $(X, s)$ is universal $\iff$ $(X, s)$ is an initial object of the category of elements $\operatorname{el}(F)$ — the word "universal" is used in the same way on this level and on the level below.
