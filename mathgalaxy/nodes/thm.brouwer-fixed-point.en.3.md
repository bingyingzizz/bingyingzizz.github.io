**$r$ is continuous**: $f$ is continuous, and $a, b$ is computed from inner products and norms and is therefore continuous as well. Since $b(z) \ge |z - f(z)|^{2}$ is everywhere $> 0$, taking the square root and dividing are both well-defined.
