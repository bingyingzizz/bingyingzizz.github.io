**Step 4: Normalize**. Since $\{V_{\alpha}\}$ covers $M$, every point lies in at least one $V_{\alpha}$, so $\Psi = \sum_{\alpha} \psi_{\alpha} > 0$ holds everywhere. The family of supports $\{\operatorname{supp}\psi_{\alpha}\}$ is locally finite because it refines the locally finite family $\{V_{\alpha}\}$. Thus $\Psi$ is a locally finite sum and is smooth. Set $\rho_{\alpha} = \psi_{\alpha}/\Psi$.
