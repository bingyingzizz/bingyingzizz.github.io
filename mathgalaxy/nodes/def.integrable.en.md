## Statement · **draft**
A real-valued measurable $f$ is **integrable** iff $\int f^+ < \infty$ and $\int f^- < \infty$. Equivalently, $f$ is integrable iff the integral of $|f|$ is finite:

$f$ $\iff \int |f| d\mu < \infty$

A complex-valued measurable $f$ is integrable iff $\int |f| d\mu < \infty$. The set of all integrable functions is denoted $L^1$.
