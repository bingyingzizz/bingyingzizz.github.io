**VIII. Analytic formulation and smoothness.** Frobenius gives the compatibility condition for local solvability of the system of first-order linear partial differential equations $\partial_{i}f=\sum_{j}a^{j}_{i}\partial_{j}f$ ($i>k$). Compatibility is precisely involutivity, which is also how to read $\mathrm{d}^{2}=0$. Step 1 uses Picard–Lindelöf to solve the ODE, so $\mathcal{D}$ must be smooth (at least $C^{2}$ to discuss brackets). Below $C^{1}$, there are counterexamples that are nowhere involutive yet have integral surfaces everywhere.
