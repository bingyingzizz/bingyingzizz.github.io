## Statement · **draft**
Let $M$ be an $R$-module. We say that $\operatorname{id}_{R} M \le n$ if $M$ has an **injective resolution of length $n$**, meaning that there is an exact sequence

$$0 \to M \to I^{0} \to I^{1} \to \cdots \to I^{n} \to 0$$
