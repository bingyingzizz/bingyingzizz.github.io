**A direct computation also works**: $g$ surjective implies $g \otimes 1$ surjective (every $m_{2} \otimes n$ has a preimage $m \otimes n$); for the kernel: from $\ker g = \operatorname{im} f$, if $g(m) = 0$, then $m$ can be written $f(m_{1})$, so a pure tensor is $m \otimes n = (f \otimes 1)(m_{1} \otimes n)$, and a general element is a sum of these, giving $\ker(g \otimes 1) \subseteq \operatorname{im}(f \otimes 1)$;

the reverse inclusion follows from $g f = 0$ ✓.
