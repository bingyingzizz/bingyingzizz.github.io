# the closed unit ball is compact iff finite dimensional　`thm.riesz-ball-compact`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=thm.riesz-ball-compact)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./thm.riesz-ball-compact.md)

the coordinate ball $\overline{B}^{\infty}(0,1)$ is bounded and closed (in the real case it is $[-1,1]^{n}$; in the complex case it is the unit polydisc, regarded as a closed bounded subset of $\mathbb{R}^{2n}$), and is compact by Heine–Borel (`cor.heine-borel`).

∎

"$\implies$": if $\dim E = \infty$, use Riesz's lemma (`lem.almost-orthogonal`) to construct recursively unit vectors with $\|e_n - e_m\| \ge 1/2$ ($n \ne m$); such a sequence has no convergent subsequence, so the closed unit ball is not sequentially compact and hence not compact.
