## Notes · **draft**
**How to compute.** $M \otimes_{\mathbb{Z}} N$ is the **sheafification** of the presheaf $X \mapsto M(X) \otimes_{\mathbb{Z}} N(X)$: first tensor pointwise, then sheafify.

(For the proof it suffices to consider the case $\mathcal{T} = \widehat{\mathcal{C}}$ and then sheafify;

finally reduce to the same-named statement for ordinary abelian groups.)
