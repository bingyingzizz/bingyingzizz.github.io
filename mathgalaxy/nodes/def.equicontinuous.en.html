Notes

⚠️ The point is "for all fff simultaneously". Drop this condition and one only has "each fff is continuous on its own" — that is the definition of H⊆C(K,E)\mathcal{H} \subseteq C(K,E)H⊆C(K,E) and says nothing new.

When H={f}\mathcal{H} = \{f\}H={f}, equicontinuity   ⟺  \iff⟺ fff being continuous.

⭐ This is the only non-trivial hypothesis in compactness (Arzelà–Ascoli): pointwise total boundedness merely says "does not diverge", while equicontinuity says "does not oscillate ever more wildly".

📌 When KKK is a metric space there is also a stronger uniform version, see 一致等度连续; when KKK is compact the two are equivalent (等度连续与一致等度连续).
