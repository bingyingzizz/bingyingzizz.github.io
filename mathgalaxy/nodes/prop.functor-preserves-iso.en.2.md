# functors preserve isomorphisms　`prop.functor-preserves-iso`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=prop.functor-preserves-iso)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./prop.functor-preserves-iso.md)

It uses exactly the two requirements on a functor: **preserve composition, preserve identities**.

⚠ The converse **fails**: $F(f)$ being an isomorphism does not imply that $f$ is one (a forgetful functor identifies linear isomorphisms with invertible linear maps, but also sends non-invertible square matrices to maps).

To go backwards one needs $F$ **fully faithful**, which is the content of propositions such as `prop.adjoint-full-faithful`.
