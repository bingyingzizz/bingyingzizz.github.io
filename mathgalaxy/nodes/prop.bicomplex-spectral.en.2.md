## Notes · **draft**
⭐ **The next page comes from computing cohomology row by row and then column by column**. Taking cohomology in the vertical direction gives

$$E^{1}_{p,q} \;=\; H^{q}\bigl(K^{p,\bullet}\bigr),$$

and then taking cohomology in the horizontal direction gives the $E^{2}$ page. The spectral sequence continues from this page and converges to the cohomology of the total complex $H^{p+q}(K^{\bullet})$ ✓.
