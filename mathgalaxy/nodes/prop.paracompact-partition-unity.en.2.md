## Abstract · **draft**
For a topological space $X$: $X$ is paracompact Hausdorff exactly when every open cover has a subordinate partition of unity, and $X$ is normal exactly when every locally finite open cover has one. In one direction the supports of the partition give a locally finite open refinement of the cover; in the other, normality produces the functions one at a time. This is the standard bridge from local data to global objects, for instance from local metrics to a Riemannian metric.
