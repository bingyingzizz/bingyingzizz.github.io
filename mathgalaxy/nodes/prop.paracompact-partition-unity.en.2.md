## Abstract · **draft**
For a topological space $X$: if $X$ is paracompact Hausdorff then every open cover has a subordinate partition of unity, and if $X$ is normal then every locally finite open cover has one. Neither converse holds without a separation hypothesis: on the trivial topology with at least two points the constant function $1$ is a partition of unity subordinate to every cover, yet the space is neither Hausdorff nor $T_1$.
