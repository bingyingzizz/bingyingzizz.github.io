# left-invariant form　`def.left-invariant-form`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=def.left-invariant-form)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./def.left-invariant-form.md)

**The Haar-measure half**: $\omega^{1}\wedge\cdots\wedge\omega^{n}$ is a left-invariant volume form, and its absolute value gives a left-invariant measure (Haar measure). On a compact group left- and right-invariant measures differ by a constant (the modular function); in the noncompact case they need not — here only the form side is used.

⚠ Left-invariant **forms** and left-invariant **vector fields** are different families of objects, both indexed by $g$; do not confuse them: the component matrix of the former is the inverse transpose of the Jacobian, that of the latter is the Jacobian itself.
