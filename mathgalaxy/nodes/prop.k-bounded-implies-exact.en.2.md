# K-Bounded Exactness Implies Exactness　`prop.k-bounded-implies-exact`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=prop.k-bounded-implies-exact)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./prop.k-bounded-implies-exact.md)

## Statement · **draft**
Let $M^{\bullet}$ be a complex of **Banach** abelian groups. If it is boundedly exact at both $M^{n-1}$ and $M^{n}$ with bound $K$, **and $\operatorname{im} d^{n+1}$ is a closed subspace of $M^{n}$** (that is, $d^{n+1}$ has closed range), then it is **exact** at $M^{n}$ in the usual sense.

## Notes · **draft**
**This is the central step from quantitative $\implies$ qualitative, and it requires a closedness hypothesis.** Given a cycle $s \in M^{n}$ (that is, $d^{n}s = 0$), we must show $s \in \operatorname{im} d^{n+1}$.
