## Notes · **draft**
⭐ This is the third appearance of the same template on the graph: the set version $A/\!\sim$, the group version $G/N \cong \operatorname{im}\varphi$, and the vector-space version $V/\ker T \cong \operatorname{im} T$.

The proof is verbatim the same in all three — **kill the kernel, and what remains is the image**.

⭐ One particularly useful branch: if $T$ is surjective, then $W \cong V / \ker T$;

if $T$ is injective, then $V \cong \operatorname{im} T$.
