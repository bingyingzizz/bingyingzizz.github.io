⚠️ **Relation to `imp.lch-cr`**: that implication proves "locally compact Hausdorff spaces are completely regular" using exactly "$X^{*}$ is compact Hausdorff $\implies$ normal, $\implies$ a Urysohn function can be built on $X^{*}$ and pulled back". The one-point compactification is the standard interface for **transferring the good properties of compact spaces to $X$**.
