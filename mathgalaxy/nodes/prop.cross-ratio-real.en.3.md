# real cross ratio iff concyclic　`prop.cross-ratio-real`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=prop.cross-ratio-real)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./prop.cross-ratio-real.md)

⭐ **It is the complex version of the concyclicity criterion**: elementary geometry requires measuring angles, whereas here one only checks whether a complex number is real. In applications, deciding whether four points are concyclic, or where a fractional linear transformation sends the real axis, is exactly this statement.
