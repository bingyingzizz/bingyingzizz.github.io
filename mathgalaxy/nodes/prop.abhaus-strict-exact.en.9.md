# kernels and cokernels in Hausdorff topological abelian groups　`prop.abhaus-strict-exact`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=prop.abhaus-strict-exact)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./prop.abhaus-strict-exact.md)

⭐ **Strictness matters**: In topological abelian groups, exactness and strict exactness are genuinely different. The category of topological abelian groups **fails to be abelian** because morphisms need not be characterized by their kernels and cokernels. Condensed mathematics addresses this by embedding topological groups into $\mathrm{CondAb}$.
