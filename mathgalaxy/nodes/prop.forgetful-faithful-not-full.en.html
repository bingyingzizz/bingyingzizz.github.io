Statement

The forgetful functors Top→Set\mathbf{Top} \to \mathbf{Set}Top→Set and Ab→Set\mathbf{Ab} \to \mathbf{Set}Ab→Set are faithful but not full.

Notes

Proof. The underlying sets of maps are unchanged, so the functors are faithful ✓; but a continuous map between two topological spaces is only some of all maps, and a map between two groups is required to preserve the operation ✓ — so neither is full ✓. ∎
