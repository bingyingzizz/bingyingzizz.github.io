Statement

The category of groups Grp\mathbf{Grp}Grp: objects are groups, morphisms are group homomorphisms, composition is composition of maps, and identities are identity homomorphisms.

It has a forgetful functor Grp→Set\mathbf{Grp}\to\mathbf{Set}Grp→Set; abelian groups form the full subcategory Ab\mathbf{Ab}Ab, and finite groups form the full subcategory FinGrp\mathbf{FinGrp}FinGrp.

Notes

Not an abelian category: hom⁡Grp(G,H)\hom_{\mathbf{Grp}}(G,H)homGrp​(G,H) generally has no group structure (one cannot add pointwise), so one cannot do homological algebra as in A-ModA\text{-}\mathbf{Mod}A-Mod.

Monomorphisms = injective homomorphisms and epimorphisms = surjective homomorphisms. Products are direct products and coproducts are free products; the only zero object is the trivial group, so Grp\mathbf{Grp}Grp is not additive.

The Cartesian product gives a symmetric monoidal structure with the trivial group as unit; but it is not Cartesian closed (hom⁡(G,H)\hom(G,H)hom(G,H) generally cannot be made into a group naturally).
