# Operations on Ideals and Quotient Rings　`prop.ideal-basic`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=prop.ideal-basic)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./prop.ideal-basic.md)

$$IJ := \left\{ \sum_{k=1}^{n} x_k y_k : x_k \in I,\ y_k \in J,\ n \ge 0 \right\}$$

is an ideal, and $IJ \subseteq I \cap J$;

**(iii) universal property of the quotient ring**: if $\varphi : A \to B$ is a ring homomorphism with $\varphi(I) = 0$, then there is a **unique** ring homomorphism $\bar{\varphi} : A/I \to B$ such that $\bar{\varphi} \circ \pi = \varphi$, where $\pi : A \to A/I$ is the quotient map.

## Notes · **draft**
⭐ Compare groups: to quotient a group into a group, one quotients by a **normal subgroup**; to quotient a ring into a ring, one quotients by an **ideal**.
