Statement

What it does: it replaces "the kkk-form written in coordinates" by a chart-free description. Once that is done, a form really is an object on the manifold, and not "an expression inside some chart".

Let MMM be a smooth nnn-manifold. A differential kkk-form ω\omegaω on MMM assigns to each point p∈Mp\in Mp∈M an alternating kkk-linear form

ωp:TpM×⋯×TpM⟶R,\omega_{p} : T_{p}M\times\cdots\times T_{p}M\longrightarrow\mathbb{R},ωp​:Tp​M×⋯×Tp​M⟶R,

and it depends smoothly: for every chart (U,x)(U,x)(U,x) the function p↦ωp(∂i1∣p,…,∂ik∣p)p\mapsto\omega_{p}\bigl(\partial_{i_{1}}\big|_{p},\ldots,\partial_{i_{k}}\big|_{p}\bigr)p↦ωp​(∂i1​​​p​,…,∂ik​​​p​) is smooth on UUU. The set of all kkk-forms on MMM is denoted Ωk(M)\Omega^{k}(M)Ωk(M).

Smoothness is equivalent to smooth coefficients: on (U,x)(U,x)(U,x) the form ω\omegaω is uniquely written as

ω∣U=∑IaI dxI,dxI:=dxi1∧⋯∧dxik,\omega\big|_{U} = \sum_{I}a_{I}\,\mathrm{d}x^{I},\qquad \mathrm{d}x^{I} := \mathrm{d}x^{i_{1}}\wedge\cdots\wedge\mathrm{d}x^{i_{k}},ω​U​=I∑​aI​dxI,dxI:=dxi1​∧⋯∧dxik​,

On the overlap of two charts the two sets of coefficients are converted into one another by the kkk-minors of the Jacobian matrix — and minors are smooth, so "the coefficients are smooth" does not depend on the chart chosen. This step is what makes the definition work at all: it welds a local expression into a global object.
