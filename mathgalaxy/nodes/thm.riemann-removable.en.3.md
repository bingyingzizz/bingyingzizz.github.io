## Notes · **draft**
⭐ Proof: for $z \in B(a, r) \setminus \{a\}$ and small enough $\varepsilon < |z - a|$, write $f$ as the difference of two Cauchy-type integrals $f(z) = I_r(z) - I_\varepsilon(z)$, where $I_{\rho}$ is the integral along $|\zeta - a| = \rho$ (**take $\rho < r$** — the circle $|\zeta - a| = r$ itself is not in the domain; $I_{\rho}$ is holomorphic on $B(a, \rho)$), and $I_\varepsilon$ is the integral along $|\zeta - a| = \varepsilon$.
