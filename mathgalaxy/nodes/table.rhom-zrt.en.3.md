2. **$\mathbb{R}$ is divisible and hence $\mathbb{Z}$-injective**: $\operatorname{Ext}^{n}_{\mathbb{Z}}(M, \mathbb{R}) = 0$ for all $M$ and $n \ge 1$.

Thus the entries with target $\mathbb{R}$ have only degree $0$: $\operatorname{RHom}(M, \mathbb{R}) = \operatorname{Hom}_{\mathbb{Z}}(M, \mathbb{R})$.

**Replace the $T$ column by $\mathbb{Z}$ and shift up one degree**: Take the short exact sequence $0 \to \mathbb{Z} \to \mathbb{R} \to T \to 0$ ($T = \mathbb{R}/\mathbb{Z}$).
