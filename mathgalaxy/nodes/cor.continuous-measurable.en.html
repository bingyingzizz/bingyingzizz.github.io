Statement

Let X,YX, YX,Y be topological spaces, each equipped with its Borel σ\sigmaσ-algebra BX,BY\mathfrak{B}_X, \mathfrak{B}_YBX​,BY​. Then every continuous map is automatically Borel measurable:

f:X→Yf : X \to Yf:X→Y continuous   ⟹  f\implies f⟹f is (BX,BY)(\mathfrak{B}_X, \mathfrak{B}_Y)(BX​,BY​)-measurable

Notes

Continuity is exactly “preimages of open sets are open”; open sets generate BY\mathfrak{B}_YBY​, so the criterion (2) from the previous item gives the result immediately.
