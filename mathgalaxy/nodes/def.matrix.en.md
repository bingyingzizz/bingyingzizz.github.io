## Statement · **draft**
Let $K$ be a field and $m, n \ge 1$. An $m \times n$ **matrix** is a rectangular array of elements of $K$,

$$A = (a_{ij}) = \begin{pmatrix} a_{11} & \cdots & a_{1n} \\ \vdots & & \vdots \\ a_{m1} & \cdots & a_{mn} \end{pmatrix}, \qquad a_{ij} \in K$$

($i$ is the row index and $j$ the column index, both starting at $1$.) The set of all $m \times n$ matrices is denoted $M_{m \times n}(K)$, and the square case by $M_n(K) := M_{n \times n}(K)$.
