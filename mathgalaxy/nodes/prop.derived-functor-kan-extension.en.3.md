## Notes · **draft**
**Proof.** Localization (turning quasi-isomorphisms into isomorphisms) is left adjoint to precomposition ✓, and a left exact functor behaves well on quasi-isomorphisms, so the composite functor "first take $F$, then localize" has a left Kan extension along the localization, and it is precisely $RF$ ✓ — this is the universal characterization of the derived functor: it is the **best extension** of $F$ in the derived category ✓. ∎
