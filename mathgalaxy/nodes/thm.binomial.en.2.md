## Notes · **draft**
⭐ This theorem is used in two places on the graph: the functional equation $\exp(z+w) = \exp z\,\exp w$ of `def.exp-function` (expand $(z+w)^{n}$ and rearrange), and the amplification of $(b+h)^{n}$ in `thm.nth-root`.

⭐ The proof inducts only on $n$: expand $(a+b)^{n+1} = (a+b)^{n}(a+b)$, and use Pascal to merge the two groups of coefficients.

⚠ It fails in noncommutative rings ($ab \ne ba$), hence the hypothesis says commutative ring.
