## Notes · **draft**
⚠️ The point is "**for all $f$ simultaneously**". Drop this condition and one only has "each $f$ is continuous on its own" — that is the definition of $\mathcal{H} \subseteq C(K,E)$ and says nothing new.

When $\mathcal{H} = \{f\}$, equicontinuity $\iff$ $f$ being continuous.

⭐ This is the **only non-trivial** hypothesis in compactness (Arzelà–Ascoli): pointwise total boundedness merely says "does not diverge", while equicontinuity says "does not oscillate ever more wildly".
