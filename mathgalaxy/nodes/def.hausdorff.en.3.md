**Convention.** The $T$-number names the bare property; the adjective adds $T_{1}$: "regular" = $T_{3} + T_{1}$ (`def.regular-space`), "completely regular" = $T_{3.5} + T_{1}$ (`def.completely-regular-space`).

Bare $T_{3}$ does not imply $T_{1}$.
