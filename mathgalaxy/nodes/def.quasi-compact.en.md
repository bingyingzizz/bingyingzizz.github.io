## Statement · **draft**
In a site $\mathcal{C}$, an object $X$ is **quasi-compact** if for every family $(X_{i} \to X)_{i \in I}$ generating a covering sieve there is a **finite** $J \subseteq I$ such that the sieve generated by $(X_{i} \to X)_{i \in J}$ is **also** a covering sieve.
