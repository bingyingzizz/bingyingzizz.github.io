**(ii)** $M$ is called **maximal** if there is no ideal $J$ with $M \subset J \subset A$. Equivalently: $A/M$ is a **field**.

**(iii)** Maximal $\implies$ prime ($A/M$ a field $\implies$ an integral domain).

## Notes · **draft**
⭐ A prime ideal is the abstraction of "divisibility": in $\mathbb{Z}$ ($p \ge 2$), $(p)$ is prime $\iff$ $p$ is prime ($(0)$ is also prime, but $0$ is not prime); in $k[x]$ ($k$ a field, $f \ne 0$), $(f)$ is prime $\iff$ $f$ is irreducible ($(0)$ is also prime, while $0$ is irreducible).
