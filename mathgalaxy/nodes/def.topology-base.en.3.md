# base and subbase　`def.topology-base`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=def.topology-base)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./def.topology-base.md)

1. $\bigcup \mathcal{B} = X$;
2. for $B_1, B_2 \in \mathcal{B}$ and $x \in B_1 \cap B_2$, there exists $B_3 \in \mathcal{B}$ with $x \in B_3 \subseteq B_1 \cap B_2$.

A family $\mathcal{S} \subseteq \mathcal{P}(X)$ is a **subbase** if and only if all its **finite intersections** form a base.

## Notes · **draft**
⭐ Why bases are useful: **a condition checked on a base holds automatically for all open sets.**
