# Hausdorff maximal principle　`thm.hausdorff`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=thm.hausdorff)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./thm.hausdorff.md)

## Statement · **reviewed**
Let $(P, \preceq )$ be a poset and $C_{0} \subseteq P$ a chain. Then there is a **maximal chain** $M$ of $P$ with $C_{0} \subseteq M$.

Here "maximal chain" means a chain that is maximal under inclusion $\subseteq$: if $M \subseteq M' \subseteq P$ and $M'$ is again a chain, then $M' = M$.

Taking $C_{0} = \emptyset$ gives: **every non-empty poset has a maximal chain.**

## Notes · **reviewed**
In form it predates Zorn's lemma (Hausdorff, 1914), and it is equivalent to it.

The existence of a maximal chain is exactly the rigorous version of the intuition "keep adding elements from below until you can add no more".
