# orthogonal projection　`def.orthogonal-projection`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=def.orthogonal-projection)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./def.orthogonal-projection.md)

- $P_U$ is linear, $P_U^{2} = P_U$ (**idempotent**), $\ker P_U = U^{\perp}$, $\operatorname{im} P_U = U$;
- $P_U$ is **self-adjoint**: $\langle P_U u, v \rangle = \langle u, P_U v \rangle$ (see "adjoint operator");
- $v - P_U v \perp U$.

## Notes · **draft**
⭐ In one sentence: **orthogonal projection = idempotent + self-adjoint**.

Conversely, every idempotent self-adjoint operator is the orthogonal projection onto some subspace — this "operator-language" characterization later becomes, in the spectral theorem, "a self-adjoint operator whose eigenvalues are only $0, 1$".
