# Symplectic Manifold　`def.symplectic-manifold`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=def.symplectic-manifold)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./def.symplectic-manifold.md)

⭐ **Why physics needs it**: $\omega$ gives rise to Hamiltonian flows (`def.hamiltonian-vector-field`), and $d\omega = 0$ ensures that these flows **preserve $\omega$** (the Lie derivative $\mathcal{L}_{X_{H}}\omega = 0$). This is the basis of Liouville's theorem on conservation of phase volume.

⚠️ A symplectic manifold **is necessarily orientable** ($\omega^{n}$ is a nowhere-vanishing volume form), but **need not** be Kähler. A Kähler structure also requires a complex structure and a compatible metric. Symplectic geometry is more general than Kähler geometry.
