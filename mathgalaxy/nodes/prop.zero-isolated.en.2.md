# isolated zeros and their order　`prop.zero-isolated`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=prop.zero-isolated)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./prop.zero-isolated.md)

$m$ is called the **order** of the zero at $z_{0}$. Consequently $z_{0}$ is an isolated zero of $f$, and the zero set of a nonzero holomorphic function has **no accumulation point** in $\Omega$.

## Notes · **draft**
**Proof**: expand $f$ in a Taylor series at $z_{0}$ (`thm.taylor-complex`): $f(z) = \sum_{n \ge 1} a_{n}(z - z_{0})^{n}$.
