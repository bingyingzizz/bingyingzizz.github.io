## Notes · **draft**
**Proof.** The stalk is the **filtered colimit** over the open neighbourhoods of $x$ ✓, and sheafification preserves filtered colimits (it is a left adjoint, hence colimit-preserving) ✓; both sides reduce to the values of $T$ on the same family of neighbourhoods ✓. ∎
