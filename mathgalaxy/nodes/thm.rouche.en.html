Statement

In plain words: if on the boundary of a closed curve the modulus of a "perturbation term" is strictly smaller than the modulus of a "main term", then adding the perturbation does not change the number of zeros inside the curve.

Let γ\gammaγ be a positively oriented, simple piecewise smooth closed curve (counterclockwise, no self-intersection), let f,gf, gf,g be holomorphic on γ\gammaγ and its interior, and suppose that everywhere on γ\gammaγ

∣g(z)∣<∣f(z)∣.|g(z)| < |f(z)|.∣g(z)∣<∣f(z)∣.

Then fff and f+gf + gf+g have the same number of zeros inside γ\gammaγ (both counted with multiplicity).
