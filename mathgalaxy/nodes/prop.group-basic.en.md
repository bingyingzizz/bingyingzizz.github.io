## Statement · **draft**
Let $G$ be a group and $a, b, c \in G$. Then:

**(i) Cancellation**: $ab = ac \implies b = c$, $ba = ca \implies b = c$;

**(ii) Inverse formulas**: $(ab)^{-1} = b^{-1}a^{-1}$, $(a^{-1})^{-1} = a$, $e^{-1} = e$;

**(iii)** For $m, n \in \mathbb{Z}$: $a^{m}a^{n} = a^{m+n}$, $(a^{m})^{n} = a^{mn}$, $a^{0} = e$.
