## Abstract · **reviewed**
If $q : X \to Y$ is a quotient map and $Z$ is locally compact Hausdorff, then $q \times \mathrm{id}_Z : X \times Z \to Y \times Z$ is again a quotient map. For a point $(x,z)$ of the preimage of an open set one picks a basic open $V \times K \subseteq U$ with $K$ a compact neighbourhood of $z$; then $q^{-1}(V) \times K$ is an open neighbourhood of $(x,z)$ inside that preimage. Local compactness cannot be dropped. This is the engine behind "compactly generated spaces are closed under products".
