Statement

Let MMM be an RRR-module. We say that id⁡RM≤n\operatorname{id}_{R} M \le nidR​M≤n if MMM has an injective resolution of length nnn, meaning that there is an exact sequence

0→M→I0→I1→⋯→In→00 \to M \to I^{0} \to I^{1} \to \cdots \to I^{n} \to 00→M→I0→I1→⋯→In→0

in which each IkI^{k}Ik is injective. The injective dimension of MMM, denoted id⁡RM\operatorname{id}_{R} MidR​M, is the least nnn for which this holds (or ∞\infty∞ if no such integer exists).

Duality (note the precise formula): id⁡RM=fd⁡RopHom⁡Z(M, Q/Z)\operatorname{id}_{R} M = \operatorname{fd}_{R^{\mathrm{op}}} \operatorname{Hom}_{\mathbb{Z}}(M,\, \mathbb{Q}/\mathbb{Z})idR​M=fdRop​HomZ​(M,Q/Z). This is the flat dimension on the corresponding side after taking the character dual (for fd, see flat dimension).

⚠ The formula id⁡RM=pd⁡RopM\operatorname{id}_{R} M = \operatorname{pd}_{R^{\mathrm{op}}} MidR​M=pdRop​M is incorrect. Take R=ZR = \mathbb{Z}R=Z and M=QM = \mathbb{Q}M=Q. Then Q\mathbb{Q}Q is divisible   ⟹  \implies⟹ id⁡=0\operatorname{id} = 0id=0, whereas pd⁡ZQ=1\operatorname{pd}_{\mathbb{Z}}\mathbb{Q} = 1pdZ​Q=1 (since Q\mathbb{Q}Q is not projective). The two dimensions differ.

Notes

This is exactly dual to projective dimension, with the arrows reversed. The resolution extends to the right because injective objects are the ones into which the maps go. The corresponding characterizations are:

  • id⁡RM=0  ⟺  M\operatorname{id}_{R} M = 0 \iff MidR​M=0⟺M is injective.
  • id⁡RM≤n  ⟺  Ext⁡Rk(N,M)=0\operatorname{id}_{R} M \le n \iff \operatorname{Ext}^{k}_{R}(N, M) = 0idR​M≤n⟺ExtRk​(N,M)=0 holds for every NNN and every k>nk > nk>n (note the second variable).
  • id⁡RM\operatorname{id}_{R} MidR​M is the largest degree for which Ext⁡Rn(−,M)\operatorname{Ext}^{n}_{R}(-, M)ExtRn​(−,M) is nonzero on some module, with that degree denoted by nnn.

Examples (over Z\mathbb{Z}Z): Q\mathbb{Q}Q is injective because it is a divisible group, so id⁡ZQ=0\operatorname{id}_{\mathbb{Z}}\mathbb{Q} = 0idZ​Q=0.

Z\mathbb{Z}Z is not injective, and 0→Z→Q→Q/Z→00 \to \mathbb{Z} \to \mathbb{Q} \to \mathbb{Q}/\mathbb{Z} \to 00→Z→Q→Q/Z→0 is an injective resolution of length 111, so id⁡ZZ=1\operatorname{id}_{\mathbb{Z}}\mathbb{Z} = 1idZ​Z=1 ✓.

⭐ Why both sides matter: To compute Ext⁡n(M,N)\operatorname{Ext}^{n}(M, N)Extn(M,N), use either a projective resolution of MMM (left-hand side) or an injective resolution of NNN (right-hand side). The difficulty on the two sides is governed by pd⁡M\operatorname{pd} MpdM and id⁡N\operatorname{id} NidN, respectively, and both vanishing statements hold: Ext⁡n(M,−)=0\operatorname{Ext}^{n}(M, -) = 0Extn(M,−)=0 (n>pd⁡Mn > \operatorname{pd} Mn>pdM) and Ext⁡n(−,N)=0\operatorname{Ext}^{n}(-, N) = 0Extn(−,N)=0 (n>id⁡Nn > \operatorname{id} Nn>idN).
