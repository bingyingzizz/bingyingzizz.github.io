**Which assumptions each direction uses** (the definition of an ordinal needs no Choice; the existence of a representative for each order type is a separate construction):
- being a von Neumann ordinal $\iff$ transitive and well-ordered by membership: both directions are unfolding the definition, with no compactness, no Choice and no metric; the convention $\alpha<\beta\iff\alpha\in\beta$ is also a notational definition.
- elements of an ordinal are ordinals: inherit the well-order and use transitivity of membership on the ambient set to show the element is transitive; a general transitive set does not by itself have transitive elements.
