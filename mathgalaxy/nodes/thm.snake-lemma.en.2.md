Then there is a **connecting homomorphism** $\delta : \ker c \to \operatorname{coker} a$ making the sequence

$$\ker a \to \ker b \to \ker c \xrightarrow{\;\delta\;} \operatorname{coker} a \to \operatorname{coker} b \to \operatorname{coker} c$$

exact.

## Notes · **draft**
**Why "snake"**: draw this long exact sequence as a loop; the six groups/modules string together into the shape of a snake — $\delta$ is the turn at its head.
