## Statement · **draft**
Let $(X, \mathcal{M}, \mu)$ be a measure space. A proposition $P(x)$ about a point $x$ holds **almost everywhere** (written a.e.) if and only if

$$\exists E \in \mathcal{M}:\ \mu(E) = 0\ \wedge \ \forall x \in X \setminus E,\ P(x) .$$
