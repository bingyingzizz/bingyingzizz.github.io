## Statement · **draft**
Let $E$ be a **discrete** topological space and $N$ a condensed abelian group. Then

$$\operatorname{RHom}_{\mathbb{Z}}\bigl(\mathbb{Z}[E],\ N\bigr) \;\cong\; N^{E} \;=\; \prod_{e \in E} N$$

(again understood as the complex concentrated in degree $0$).
