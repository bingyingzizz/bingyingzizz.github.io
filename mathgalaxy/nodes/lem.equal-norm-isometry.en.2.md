## Notes · **draft**
The reflection $s_v(z)=z-\frac{2\beta(z,v)}{q(v)}v$ ($q(v)\ne0$) is an isometry and sends $v$ to $-v$; the lemma says that one reflection (composed with the central inversion $z\mapsto-z$ if necessary) exchanges equal-length vectors. This is the most basic block of the Cartan–Dieudonné decomposition.

For the proof see the edge `imp.equal-norm-isometry`.
