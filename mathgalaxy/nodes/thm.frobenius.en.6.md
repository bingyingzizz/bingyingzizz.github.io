# Frobenius theorem　`thm.frobenius`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=thm.frobenius)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./thm.frobenius.md)

1. By `thm.flow-local`, straighten $X_{1}$ along its local flow. There are coordinates $(y^{1},\ldots,y^{n})$ such that $X_{1}=\partial_{1}$ (the flow-box theorem).
2. Write the remaining generators as $X_{i}=a_{i}\,\partial_{1}+Y_{i}$ ($i\ge2$), where $Y_{i}$ has only $\partial_{2},\ldots,\partial_{n}$ components. Since $\mathrm{d}y^{1}$ is a closed form,

$$\mathrm{d}y^{1}\bigl([\partial_{1},Y_{i}]\bigr)=\partial_{1}\bigl(\mathrm{d}y^{1}(Y_{i})\bigr)-Y_{i}\bigl(\mathrm{d}y^{1}(\partial_{1})\bigr)=0,$$
