## Notes · **draft**
**Idea of proof.** Both directions are "**evaluate pointwise**":

- $\to$: an abelian group object $M$ in the topos evaluated on $X \in \mathcal{C}$ uses that **global sections preserve products** (the group operations, $0$ and inverses are read off pointwise) ✓, so $X \mapsto M(X)$ gives an abelian presheaf, and it is a sheaf ✓;
