## Statement · **stale**
**The category of vector spaces** $\mathbf{Vect}_K$: its objects are the vector spaces over a field $K$, its morphisms are the $K$-linear maps, composition is composition of maps, and the identity is the identity map.

The finite-dimensional vector spaces form the **full subcategory** $\mathbf{Vect}_K^{\mathrm{fd}}$: the objects are only the finite-dimensional spaces, while the morphisms are still all linear maps.
