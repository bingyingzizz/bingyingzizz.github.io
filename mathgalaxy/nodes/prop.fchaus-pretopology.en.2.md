# Pretopology on Finite Compact Hausdorff Spaces　`prop.fchaus-pretopology`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=prop.fchaus-pretopology)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./prop.fchaus-pretopology.md)

Every object of $\mathbf{FCHaus}$ is a **projective object** of $\mathrm{Cond}$, and every surjection $S \to S'$ has a section — hence **finite disjoint unions give a pretopology on $\mathbf{FCHaus}$**.

## Notes · **draft**
Why the sheaf condition "holds automatically": over free compact Hausdorff spaces every surjection has a section, so "locally has a preimage" can always be patched globally (separatedness follows directly from the existence of sections). **Having sections** is the most convenient point here.
