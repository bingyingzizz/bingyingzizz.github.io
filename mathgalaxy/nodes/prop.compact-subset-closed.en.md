## Statement · **draft**
Let $A$ be a subset of a metric space $X$ (with the subspace metric). If $A$ is **compact**, then $A$ is **closed** in $X$.

## Notes · **draft**
In fact it suffices that $X$ be Hausdorff: compact subsets are then closed, and metric spaces are Hausdorff.
