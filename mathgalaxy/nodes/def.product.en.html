Notes

In one sentence: a product is "one map = a family of maps". Maps into the product correspond bijectively to families of maps into each factor, and the projection pip_ipi​ extracts the iii-th component.

Examples: in Set\mathbf{Set}Set the product is the Cartesian product and the coproduct the disjoint union; in Top\mathbf{Top}Top the product carries the product topology (the coarsest topology making it a product) and the coproduct the disjoint-union topology (the finest one); in Ab\mathbf{Ab}Ab the product is the direct product ∏iAi\prod_i A_i∏i​Ai​ and the coproduct the direct sum ⨁iAi\bigoplus_i A_i⨁i​Ai​.
