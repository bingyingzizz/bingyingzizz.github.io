prop.image-kernel-cokernel

Machine-drafted and pending author review. The Chinese text is authoritative.

Statement

Let C\mathcal{C}C be an abelian category and f:M→Nf : M \to Nf:M→N. Then

im⁡f  =  ker⁡(N⟶coker⁡f),coim⁡f  =  coker⁡(ker⁡f⟶M).\operatorname{im} f \;=\; \ker\bigl(N \longrightarrow \operatorname{coker} f\bigr), \qquad \operatorname{coim} f \;=\; \operatorname{coker}\bigl(\ker f \longrightarrow M\bigr).imf=ker(N⟶cokerf),coimf=coker(kerf⟶M).

In particular the canonical morphism coim⁡f→im⁡f\operatorname{coim} f \to \operatorname{im} fcoimf→imf is an isomorphism — every morphism in an abelian category is strict.

Notes

⭐ This replaces the universal-property definition of "image" (the smallest intermediate object) by a computable formula: ker⁡(coker⁡f)\ker(\operatorname{coker} f)ker(cokerf). Everywhere in homological algebra where one "takes the image by hand", this is the formula used.

⚠️ The hypothesis abelian cannot be dropped: in a general category coim⁡f→im⁡f\operatorname{coim} f \to \operatorname{im} fcoimf→imf need not be an isomorphism (such morphisms are then simply not called strict).
