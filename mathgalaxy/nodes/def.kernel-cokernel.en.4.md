# Kernel and Cokernel　`def.kernel-cokernel`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=def.kernel-cokernel)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./def.kernel-cokernel.md)

**In an additive category kernels and cokernels behave regularly**: $\ker f \to M$ is always a **monomorphism** and $M \to \operatorname{coker} f$ always an **epimorphism** (equalizers are monic, coequalizers epic; no abelian-category hypothesis is needed for this).

⚠️ "Every morphism has a kernel and a cokernel" is **not enough**: in a preabelian category $\operatorname{coim} f \to \operatorname{im} f$ is in general not an isomorphism, and abelian categories are exactly the condition that fills this gap.
