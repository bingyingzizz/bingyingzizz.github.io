## Notes · **draft**
"Universal" = **still there after pullback**: no matter along which map the whole diagram is pulled back, the property is preserved. These three are the template for translating "the obviously good properties in sets" into category language — they cause no trouble in $\mathbf{Set}$, but need not hold in another category, which is why they must be written down.
