## Notes · **draft**
**So $H_{0}$ is "how many pieces $X$ has"**. This also explains why $\tilde{H}_{0}(S^{0}) = \mathbb{Z}$ (two points; rank $2$ divided by the diagonal $\mathbb{Z}$ leaves rank $1$), so the formula for sphere homology is uniform at $p = 0$ as well.

⚠ **Path connected vs connected**: $H_{0}$ sees only **path** components, not connected components. On usual spaces (locally path connected) the two agree, but not in general — for instance the topologist's sine curve is connected but not path connected, and its $H_{0}$ reflects the path components.
