# Riesz representation via perfect pairings　`thm.riesz-perfect-pairing`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=thm.riesz-perfect-pairing)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./thm.riesz-perfect-pairing.md)

(b) If $T : X \to Y^*$ is surjective and $Y$ is reflexive, then $S : Y \to X^*$ is also surjective. Symmetrically, if $S$ is surjective and $X$ is reflexive, then $T$ is surjective. In particular, when $X,Y$ are both reflexive, $T$ is surjective if and only if $S$ is surjective, and then $X \cong Y^*$ and $Y \cong X^*$ are isometric isomorphisms.

(c) The pairings for Hilbert spaces $H$ and for $L^p$ ($1 < p < \infty$, $1/p + 1/q = 1$) are surjective (by the projection theorem in the Hilbert case and Radon–Nikodym in the $L^p$ case). Thus $H^* \cong H$ and $(L^p)^* \cong L^q$.
