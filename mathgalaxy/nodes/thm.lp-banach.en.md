## Statement · **draft**
Let $1 \le p < \infty$. Then $L^p(X, \mathcal{M}, \mu)$ is **complete** with respect to the norm $\|\cdot\|_p$, that is, it is a **Banach space**.

## Notes · **draft**
The criterion used in the proof is: **a normed linear space is complete $\iff$ every absolutely convergent infinite series converges**.
