## Statement · **draft**
Let $M^{\bullet}$ be a complex of **seminormed abelian groups**, and let $K \in \mathbb{R}$. The complex $M^{\bullet}$ is **boundedly exact at $M^{n}$ with bound $K$** if

$$\forall s \in M^{n},\ \forall \varepsilon > 0,\ \exists s' \in M^{n+1} :\quad \|s - d^{n+1}s'\| \le K\,\|d^{n}s\| + \varepsilon.$$
