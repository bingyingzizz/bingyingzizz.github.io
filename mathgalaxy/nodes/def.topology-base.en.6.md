- The two conditions listed in the node do not by themselves imply being a base for the given $\mathcal T$: one also needs $\mathcal T$ to equal the generated topology. For example, on the discrete two-point space take $\mathcal B=\{X\}$; it satisfies both conditions yet does not generate the singleton open sets.
- A subbase $\iff$ the family of all finite intersections is a base: by convention this is definition unpacking, and finite intersections should include the empty intersection $X$; for the usual topology on the reals the basic elements must be usual open sets, while half-open intervals generate another topology.
