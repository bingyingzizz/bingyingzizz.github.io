## Statement · **draft**
The **free compact Hausdorff space** on a discrete space $I$ is its Stone–Čech compactification $F \cong \beta I$. One calls $I$ a set of **generators** of $F$: in this case

$$\operatorname{Hom}_{\mathbf{CHaus}}(F, S) \;\cong\; \operatorname{Hom}_{\mathbf{Top}}(I, S) \;=\; S^{I}$$
