## Notes · **draft**
Intuition: the equaliser is the part of $X$ “sent to the same place by $f$ and $g$”; the coequaliser is the quotient of $Y$ that “glues $f(x)$ and $g(x)$ together”.

Example: in $\mathbf{Set}$, $\ker(f,g) = \{x \in X : f(x) = g(x)\}$, and $\operatorname{coker}(f,g) = Y/\!\sim$ ($\sim$ generated by $f(x) \sim g(x)$).

In $\mathbf{Top}$ one adds the subspace topology / the quotient topology.
