## Statement · **draft**
Let $(K, \delta)$ be a compact metric space, $(E, d)$ a metric space, and $\mathcal{H} \subseteq C(K, E)$ equicontinuous. Then $\mathcal{H}$ is **uniformly equicontinuous**.
