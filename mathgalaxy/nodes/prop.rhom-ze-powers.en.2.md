**Another route**: by "acyclicity on discrete spaces" and the section formula, $\operatorname{Ext}^{n}(\mathbb{Z}\cdot E, N)(S) \cong H^{n}(E \times S, N) = 0$ (for $n>0$, $S$ Stonean), showing that the sheaf $N^{E}$ has no higher terms; comparing degree $0$ pointwise gives the result — the two routes say the same thing.
