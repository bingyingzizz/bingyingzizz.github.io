# criterion for solid completeness　`lem.solid-completion-criterion`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=lem.solid-completion-criterion)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./lem.solid-completion-criterion.md)

**(ii)** $D(\mathcal{A}_F) \to D(\mathcal{A})$ is fully faithful, with image exactly the complexes satisfying $\operatorname{RHom}(F(X), C) \cong \operatorname{RHom}(X, C)$;

$C \in D(\mathcal{A}_F)$ if and only if all $H^i(C) \in \mathcal{A}_F$;

the inclusion has a left adjoint (the left derived functor of $L$).

## Notes · **draft**
⭐ This is a **purely category-theoretic** lemma: it compresses "prove that the completed subcategory behaves well" into one Ext-vanishing condition.
