## Notes · **draft**
**This is Gram–Schmidt**: Orthogonalize the columns of $A$, denoted by $a_{1}, \ldots, a_{n}$, to obtain $q_{1}, \ldots, q_{n}$ (`thm.gram-schmidt`). Then $a_{k} = \sum_{i \le k} r_{ik}q_{i}$, and the coefficients assemble into the upper triangular matrix $R$.

The only difference is that QR usually requires a positive diagonal for $R$ (fixing the signs of $q_k$).
