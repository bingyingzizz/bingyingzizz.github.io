陈述

设 T:V→WT:V\to WT:V→W 是 KKK-线性映射。它的对偶映射（也叫转置映射）T∗:W∗→V∗T^{\ast}:W^{\ast}\to V^{\ast}T∗:W∗→V∗ 由

⟨T∗φ, v⟩=⟨φ, Tv⟩,v∈V, φ∈W∗\langle T^{\ast}\varphi,\,v\rangle=\langle\varphi,\,Tv\rangle,\qquad v\in V,\ \varphi\in W^{\ast}⟨T∗φ,v⟩=⟨φ,Tv⟩,v∈V, φ∈W∗

定义；等价地 T∗φ=φ∘TT^{\ast}\varphi=\varphi\circ TT∗φ=φ∘T。

性质：T∗T^{\ast}T∗ 线性；(S∘T)∗=T∗∘S∗(S\circ T)^{\ast}=T^{\ast}\circ S^{\ast}(S∘T)∗=T∗∘S∗；(id⁡V)∗=id⁡V∗(\operatorname{id}_V)^{\ast}=\operatorname{id}_{V^{\ast}}(idV​)∗=idV∗​。因此 (−)∗(-)^{\ast}(−)∗ 是反变函子（见对偶函子节点）。

有限维、取定基时，T∗T^{\ast}T∗ 的矩阵是 TTT 的矩阵的转置：[T∗]B∗C∗=([T]CB)T[T^{\ast}]_{\mathcal{B}^{\ast}}^{\mathcal{C}^{\ast}}=([T]_{\mathcal{C}}^{\mathcal{B}})^{\mathsf T}[T∗]B∗C∗​=([T]CB​)T。

说明

方向：T:V→WT:V\to WT:V→W 诱导 W∗→V∗W^{\ast}\to V^{\ast}W∗→V∗，箭头反向；这是「对偶」的核心。

与转置矩阵：转置矩阵是 T∗T^{\ast}T∗ 在对偶基下的矩阵；两者不是同一层面的对象（映射 vs 矩阵）。

复情形：内积空间里对应的是共轭转置（伴随），见共轭转置节点；差别在于对偶用 KKK-线性泛函，而内积对第二变元共轭线性。

Statement draft

Let T:V→WT:V\to WT:V→W be a KKK-linear map. Its dual map (also called the transpose map) T∗:W∗→V∗T^{\ast}:W^{\ast}\to V^{\ast}T∗:W∗→V∗ is defined by

⟨T∗φ, v⟩=⟨φ, Tv⟩,v∈V, φ∈W∗\langle T^{\ast}\varphi,\,v\rangle=\langle\varphi,\,Tv\rangle,\qquad v\in V,\ \varphi\in W^{\ast}⟨T∗φ,v⟩=⟨φ,Tv⟩,v∈V, φ∈W∗

Equivalently, T∗φ=φ∘TT^{\ast}\varphi=\varphi\circ TT∗φ=φ∘T.

Properties: T∗T^{\ast}T∗ is linear; (S∘T)∗=T∗∘S∗(S\circ T)^{\ast}=T^{\ast}\circ S^{\ast}(S∘T)∗=T∗∘S∗; (id⁡V)∗=id⁡V∗(\operatorname{id}_V)^{\ast}=\operatorname{id}_{V^{\ast}}(idV​)∗=idV∗​. Hence (−)∗(-)^{\ast}(−)∗ is a contravariant functor (see 对偶函子).

In finite dimension, after bases are fixed, the matrix of T∗T^{\ast}T∗ is the transpose of the matrix of TTT: [T∗]B∗C∗=([T]CB)T[T^{\ast}]_{\mathcal{B}^{\ast}}^{\mathcal{C}^{\ast}}=([T]_{\mathcal{C}}^{\mathcal{B}})^{\mathsf T}[T∗]B∗C∗​=([T]CB​)T.

Notes draft

Direction: T:V→WT:V\to WT:V→W induces W∗→V∗W^{\ast}\to V^{\ast}W∗→V∗, so the arrow reverses; this is the heart of "duality".

Relation to the transpose matrix: the transpose matrix is the matrix of T∗T^{\ast}T∗ in the dual bases; the two are objects at different levels (a map versus a matrix).

Complex case: in an inner-product space the corresponding notion is the conjugate transpose (adjoint), see 伴随算子; the difference is that duality uses KKK-linear functionals, while the inner product is conjugate-linear in the second variable.
