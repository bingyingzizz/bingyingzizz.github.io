## Statement · **draft**
Let $H$ be a Hilbert space. Then $H$ is **uniformly convex**: for every $\varepsilon \in (0, 2]$, set

$$\delta = 1 - \sqrt{1 - \varepsilon^{2}/4} > 0 ,$$

Then whenever $\|x\| = \|y\| = 1$ and $\|x - y\| \ge \varepsilon$, one has $\left\|\frac{x + y}{2}\right\| \le 1 - \delta$.
