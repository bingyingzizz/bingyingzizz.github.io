## Notes · **draft**
⭐ The correspondence "left multiplication = row operation" turns **operations** into **objects**: thus "solving a system by elimination" becomes "multiplying on the left by a string of elementary matrices", and can be written as the identity $E_k \cdots E_1 A = R$ ($R$ is the reduced row-echelon form).

⭐ The most important consequence: $A$ **is invertible if and only if it is a product of finitely many elementary matrices** (elimination reduces $A$ to $I_n$, and recording the string of operations gives an explicit factorization of $A^{-1}$).
