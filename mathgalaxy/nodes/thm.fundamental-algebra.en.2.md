# fundamental theorem of algebra　`thm.fundamental-algebra`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=thm.fundamental-algebra)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./thm.fundamental-algebra.md)

$$p(z) = c\,(z - z_1)^{e_1} \cdots (z - z_m)^{e_m}, \qquad c \in \mathbb{C}^{\times}$$

## Notes · **draft**
⭐ It is called the "fundamental theorem of algebra", yet it is **not an algebraic theorem** — every proof uses the **completeness** of $\mathbb{R}$ (because the algebraic closedness of $\mathbb{C}$ is essentially a compactness + continuity fact).

That is why the graph tags its `regions` as `analysis` + `algebra`.
