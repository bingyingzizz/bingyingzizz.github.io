## Notes · **draft**
**Why it is needed.** $\mathrm{Cond}$ is a topos, so the internal Hom is **automatic** from the topos structure — but that is abstract. This item gives its **concrete realization**: "the internal Hom is the space of continuous functions". With it, the internal Hom becomes something computable and transportable to $\mathbf{Top}$.
