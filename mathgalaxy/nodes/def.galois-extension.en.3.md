# Galois extension　`def.galois-extension`

[in graph](../#n=def.galois-extension)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./def.galois-extension.md)

⚠ **The most common misunderstanding**: taking “splitting field = Galois extension”. What is missing is **separability**: in characteristic $p$ the splitting field of $x^{p} - t$ over $\mathbb{F}_{p}(t)$ is inseparable, hence not Galois. In characteristic 0 the distinction disappears — which is why it is invisible at first.
