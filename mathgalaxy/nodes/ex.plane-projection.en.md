## Statement · **draft**
In $\mathbb{R}^{3}$ (with the standard inner product) take the subspace $W = \{\, (x, y, 0) \,\}$. The orthogonal projection onto $W$ is

$$P = \begin{pmatrix} 1 & 0 & 0 \\ 0 & 1 & 0 \\ 0 & 0 & 0 \end{pmatrix}.$$
