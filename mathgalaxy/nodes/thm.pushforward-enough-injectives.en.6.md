# Abelian Sheaves Have Enough Injectives　`thm.pushforward-enough-injectives`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=thm.pushforward-enough-injectives)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./thm.pushforward-enough-injectives.md)

$$\operatorname{Hom}_{\mathbf{Ab}(X)}\bigl(\mathcal{F},\ i_{x,*}I_{x}\bigr) \;\cong\; \operatorname{Hom}_{\mathbf{Ab}}\bigl(\mathcal{F}_{x},\ I_{x}\bigr),$$

and $\mathcal{F} \mapsto \mathcal{F}_{x}$ is exact while $I_{x}$ is injective, so the right-hand side is exact in $\mathcal{F}$ — each $i_{x,*}I_{x}$ is injective, and a direct product of injectives is injective (the pointwise argument carries over to the product verbatim). ∎
