## Statement · **draft**
For a category $\mathcal{T}$, the following four conditions are equivalent:

1. $\mathcal{T}$ is a topos;
2. every sheaf on $\mathcal{T}$ for the **standard topology** is **representable**, and a small set of generators exists;
3. there is a site $\mathcal{C}$ such that $\mathcal{T} \simeq \widehat{\mathcal{C}}$;
4. there is a category such that $\mathcal{T}$ is a **reflective subcategory** of it, and the reflection is **exact** (preserves finite limits).
