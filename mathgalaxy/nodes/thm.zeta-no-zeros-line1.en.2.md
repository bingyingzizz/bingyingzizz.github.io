## Notes · **draft**
Use the logarithm of the Euler product together with $3+4\cos\theta+\cos2\theta=2(1+\cos\theta)^{2}\ge0$. There is a pole at $t=0$, so that point is excluded from the zeros. (Weighted logarithmic estimate.)
