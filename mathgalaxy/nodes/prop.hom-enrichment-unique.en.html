Statement

Let C\mathcal{C}C be an additive category. Then the abelian group structure on Hom⁡C(M,N)\operatorname{Hom}_{\mathcal{C}}(M,N)HomC​(M,N) that makes composition bilinear and is compatible with the direct sum structure is unique.

Notes

⭐ In one sentence: the "addition" in an additive category is not extra data; it is forced by the biproduct structure. This is also why an "additive functor" may be required merely to "preserve biproducts" — see "characterization of additive functors".
