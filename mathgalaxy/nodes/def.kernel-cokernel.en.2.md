# Kernel and Cokernel　`def.kernel-cokernel`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=def.kernel-cokernel)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./def.kernel-cokernel.md)

- The **kernel** of $f$, written $\ker f := \ker(f, 0)$, is the **equalizer** of $f$ and the **zero morphism** $0_{M,N}$ (if it exists);
- the **cokernel** of $f$, written $\operatorname{coker} f$, is the kernel of $f$ in $\mathcal{C}^{\mathrm{op}}$ (if it exists), i.e. the **coequalizer** of $f$ and the zero morphism;
- the sequence $0 \to M' \xrightarrow{f} M \xrightarrow{g} M''$ is called **left exact** if $f$ realizes $\ker g$ (that is, $M' \cong \ker g$);

dually $M \xrightarrow{g} M'' \to 0$ is called **right exact** if $g$ realizes $\operatorname{coker} g$;
