# Lie Bracket　`def.lie-bracket`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=def.lie-bracket)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./def.lie-bracket.md)

**Geometric meaning**: $[X,Y]$ measures the difference between "walk one step along $X$ then one along $Y$" and "walk in the reverse order", divided by the square of the step. Thus $[X,Y] = 0$ means that the two families of integral curves fit into a coordinate net — **provided $X, Y$ are linearly independent at that point** (when $X = Y = \partial_{x}$ the bracket vanishes, but the two families coincide and cannot serve as two coordinate directions). This is the starting point of Frobenius integrability, which is not developed here.
