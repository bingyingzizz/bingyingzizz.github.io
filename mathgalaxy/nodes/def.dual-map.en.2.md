# dual (transpose) map　`def.dual-map`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=def.dual-map)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./def.dual-map.md)

Properties: $T^{\ast}$ is linear; $(S\circ T)^{\ast}=T^{\ast}\circ S^{\ast}$; $(\operatorname{id}_V)^{\ast}=\operatorname{id}_{V^{\ast}}$. Hence $(-)^{\ast}$ is a contravariant functor (see `def.dual-functor`).

In finite dimension, after bases are fixed, the matrix of $T^{\ast}$ is the transpose of the matrix of $T$: $[T^{\ast}]_{\mathcal{B}^{\ast}}^{\mathcal{C}^{\ast}}=([T]_{\mathcal{C}}^{\mathcal{B}})^{\mathsf T}$.

## Notes · **draft**
**Direction**: $T:V\to W$ induces $W^{\ast}\to V^{\ast}$, so the arrow reverses; this is the heart of "duality".
