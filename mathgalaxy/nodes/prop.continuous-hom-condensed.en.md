## Statement · **draft**
If $M, N$ are two topological abelian groups and $M$ is **compactly generated**, then there is a natural isomorphism of condensed abelian groups $C_{\mathbb{Z}}(M, N) \cong \operatorname{Hom}_{\mathbb{Z}}(M, N)$.
