## Notes · **stale**
⭐ This classification is not just terminology in the graph: **the discontinuities of a monotone function are all jumps, and there are at most countably many of them** — that is the starting point of the BV and measure-theory cluster (the atoms of $\mu_F$, $F' = 0$ a.e.).

⚠ The point of “removable” is that $f$ is **defined at $x_0$ but does not equal the limit**: for example $f(x) = \sin x / x$ becomes continuous after filling in $f(0) = 1$.

When $x_0$ is not in the domain at all (e.g. $1/x$ at $0$), it does not count as a discontinuity with domain $0$.
