# Vector Bundle　`def.vector-bundle`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=def.vector-bundle)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./def.vector-bundle.md)

$E$ is the **total space**, $M$ the **base**, and $\dim E = \dim M + k$. Two trivializations $\Phi_{\alpha}, \Phi_{\beta}$ give on overlaps the **transition function**

$$g_{\alpha\beta} : U_{\alpha} \cap U_{\beta} \longrightarrow \operatorname{GL}(k,\mathbb{R}),\qquad g_{\alpha\beta}(p) := \Phi_{\alpha,p} \circ \Phi_{\beta,p}^{-1} ,$$

which is smooth and satisfies the **cocycle condition**

$$g_{\alpha\alpha} = 1,\qquad g_{\alpha\beta}\,g_{\beta\gamma}\,g_{\gamma\alpha} = 1 ,$$
