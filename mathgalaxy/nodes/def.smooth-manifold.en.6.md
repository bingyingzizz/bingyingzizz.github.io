# Smooth Manifold　`def.smooth-manifold`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=def.smooth-manifold)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./def.smooth-manifold.md)

**Division of labour among the three topological conditions**: Hausdorff rules out "two points glued together and inseparable" (otherwise limits are not unique); second countability gives Lindelöf and paracompactness, hence **partitions of unity** (the `def.paracompact` line) — only with them can local metrics and local forms be glued into global ones.
