# open mapping theorem for Banach spaces　`thm.open-mapping-banach`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=thm.open-mapping-banach)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./thm.open-mapping-banach.md)

**Step 1: $\overline{u(B_E)}$ contains a neighbourhood of $0$.** Since $E = \bigcup_{k \ge 1} k\,B_E$, applying $u$ gives
$$F = u(E) = \bigcup_{k \ge 1} u(k B_E) \subseteq \bigcup_{k \ge 1} \overline{u(k B_E)} .$$
If every $\overline{u(k B_E)}$ were nowhere dense, $F$ would be a countable union of nowhere dense sets, contradicting "$F$ complete $\implies$ second category". Hence for some $k$, $\overline{u(k B_E)}$ has interior points: there exist $y_0$ and $\delta > 0$ with $B_F(y_0, \delta) \subseteq \overline{u(k B_E)}$.
