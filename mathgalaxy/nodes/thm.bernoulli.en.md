## Statement · **draft**
Let $x \ge -1$, $n \in \mathbb{N}$. Then

$$(1 + x)^{n} \ge 1 + n x.$$

If $x \ne 0$ and $n \ge 2$, the inequality is **strict**.

## Notes · **draft**
⭐ This is the smallest estimate that compresses "multiplying the same $1+x$ repeatedly" into a **linear** bound, and $x$ can be arbitrarily small.
