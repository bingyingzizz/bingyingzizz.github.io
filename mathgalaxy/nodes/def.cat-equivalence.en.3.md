# equivalence of categories　`def.cat-equivalence`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=def.cat-equivalence)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./def.cat-equivalence.md)

Under an equivalence, the question "are two categories the same" is replaced by "can they be translated into each other without losing structure" — every later statement in category theory that "$\mathcal{C}$ and $\mathcal{C}'$ are the same" means this.
