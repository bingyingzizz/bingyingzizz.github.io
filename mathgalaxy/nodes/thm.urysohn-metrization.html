设 XXX 是正则（含 T1T_{1}T1​）的第二可数空间。则 XXX 是可度量化的：存在与 XXX 的拓扑相容的距离。

说明

证明（嵌入可分度量空间）

  1. 第二可数   ⟹  \implies⟹ Lindelöf：每个开覆盖有可数子覆盖（对基元做筛子）。
  2. 正则 +++ Lindelöf   ⟹  \implies⟹ 正规：T1T_{1}T1​ 与「点与闭集可分开」配上 Lindelöf 的逐点归纳，给出两个不交闭集可分开。于是 Urysohn 引理可用。
  3. 挑可数多个分离函数：取可数基 { Bn }\{\, B_{n} \,\}{Bn​}。对每一对 (m,n)(m, n)(m,n) 满足 Bm‾⊆Bn\overline{B_{m}} \subseteq B_{n}Bm​​⊆Bn​，由 Urysohn 引理取连续函数
fm,n:X→[0,1],fm,n∣Bm‾≡0,fm,n∣X∖Bn≡1.f_{m,n} : X \to [0, 1],\qquad f_{m,n}|_{\overline{B_{m}}} \equiv 0,\qquad f_{m,n}|_{X \setminus B_{n}} \equiv 1.fm,n​:X→[0,1],fm,n​∣Bm​​​≡0,fm,n​∣X∖Bn​​≡1.

这样的函数只有可数多个，把它们按可数集编号成 fkf_{k}fk​（k≥1k \ge 1k≥1）。

  1. 嵌入：令 F:X→[0,1]NF : X \to [0, 1]^{\mathbb{N}}F:X→[0,1]N，F(x):=( fk(x)/k )k≥1F(x) := (\, f_{k}(x)/k \,)_{k \ge 1}F(x):=(fk​(x)/k)k≥1​。

FFF 连续（每个分量连续，且级数一致收敛）；

单射：x≠yx \ne yx=y 时正则性给出基元 BnB_{n}Bn​ 使 x∈Bn⊆Bn‾⊆X∖{ y }x \in B_{n} \subseteq \overline{B_{n}} \subseteq X \setminus \{\, y \,\}x∈Bn​⊆Bn​​⊆X∖{y}，于是某一对 (m,n)(m, n)(m,n) 的 fm,nf_{m,n}fm,n​ 把两者分开；

逆连续：同样用基对，把「fkf_{k}fk​ 很接近」翻译回「落在同一个基元里」。

所以 FFF 是到像上的同胚。

  1. 像空间可度量化：ρ(a,b):=∑k≥12−k∣ak−bk∣\rho(a, b) := \sum_{k \ge 1} 2^{-k} |a_{k} - b_{k}|ρ(a,b):=∑k≥1​2−k∣ak​−bk​∣ 是 [0,1]N[0, 1]^{\mathbb{N}}[0,1]N 上与积拓扑一致的度量；在像上取诱导度量即可。

因此 XXX 同胚于可分度量空间的子空间，即可度量化。∎

⚠️ 反向不成立：可度量化不必第二可数 —— 不可数离散空间是可度量化的（离散度量），却不第二可数。所以这条定理是充分条件，不是刻画。

⭐ 要把「充分」变「充要」，就得把「第二可数」换成更合适的基条件：Nagata–Smirnov（σ\sigmaσ-局部有限基）与 Bing（σ\sigmaσ-离散基）就是干这件事的。

Abstract reviewed

A regular T1T_{1}T1​ space with a countable base is metrisable. The proof embeds XXX into a countable product of copies of [0,1][0,1][0,1]: second countability makes XXX Lindelöf, regularity together with Lindelöf makes it normal, and Urysohn functions attached to pairs cl⁡Bm⊆Bn\operatorname{cl}B_{m}\subseteq B_{n}clBm​⊆Bn​ of basic open sets separate points from closed sets. The embedding is a homeomorphism onto its image, and the metric of the product pulls back to a metric inducing the topology of XXX.

Statement draft

Let XXX be a regular (T1T_{1}T1​) second countable space. Then XXX is metrizable: there is a metric compatible with the topology of XXX.

Notes draft

Proof (embedding into a separable metric space)

  1. Second countable   ⟹  \implies⟹ Lindelöf: every open cover has a countable subcover (sieve the basic open sets).
  2. Regular +++ Lindelöf   ⟹  \implies⟹ normal: T1T_{1}T1​ together with "a point and a closed set can be separated", plus a pointwise induction over the Lindelöf cover, separates two disjoint closed sets. Hence Urysohn's lemma is available.
  3. Choose countably many separating functions: take a countable base { Bn }\{\, B_{n} \,\}{Bn​}. For every pair (m,n)(m, n)(m,n) with Bm‾⊆Bn\overline{B_{m}} \subseteq B_{n}Bm​​⊆Bn​, Urysohn's lemma gives a continuous function
fm,n:X→[0,1],fm,n∣Bm‾≡0,fm,n∣X∖Bn≡1.f_{m,n} : X \to [0, 1],\qquad f_{m,n}|_{\overline{B_{m}}} \equiv 0,\qquad f_{m,n}|_{X \setminus B_{n}} \equiv 1.fm,n​:X→[0,1],fm,n​∣Bm​​​≡0,fm,n​∣X∖Bn​​≡1.

There are only countably many such functions; enumerate them as fkf_{k}fk​ (k≥1k \ge 1k≥1).

  1. Embedding: put F:X→[0,1]NF : X \to [0, 1]^{\mathbb{N}}F:X→[0,1]N, F(x):=( fk(x)/k )k≥1F(x) := (\, f_{k}(x)/k \,)_{k \ge 1}F(x):=(fk​(x)/k)k≥1​.

FFF is continuous (each component is continuous, and the series converges uniformly);

injective: if x≠yx \ne yx=y, regularity gives a basic open set BnB_{n}Bn​ with x∈Bn⊆Bn‾⊆X∖{ y }x \in B_{n} \subseteq \overline{B_{n}} \subseteq X \setminus \{\, y \,\}x∈Bn​⊆Bn​​⊆X∖{y}, so some pair (m,n)(m, n)(m,n) has fm,nf_{m,n}fm,n​ separating the two points;

inverse continuous: again using basic pairs, translate "fkf_{k}fk​ is close" back into "both points lie in a common basic open set".

Hence FFF is a homeomorphism onto its image.

  1. The image is metrizable: ρ(a,b):=∑k≥12−k∣ak−bk∣\rho(a, b) := \sum_{k \ge 1} 2^{-k} |a_{k} - b_{k}|ρ(a,b):=∑k≥1​2−k∣ak​−bk​∣ is a metric on [0,1]N[0, 1]^{\mathbb{N}}[0,1]N inducing the product topology; restrict it to the image.

Therefore XXX is homeomorphic to a subspace of a separable metric space, hence metrizable. ∎

⚠️ The converse fails: metrizable does not imply second countable — an uncountable discrete space is metrizable (discrete metric) but not second countable. So this theorem is a sufficient condition, not a characterisation.

⭐ To turn "sufficient" into "necessary" one replaces second countability by a more suitable base condition: Nagata–Smirnov (σ\sigmaσ-locally finite base) and Bing (σ\sigmaσ-discrete base) do exactly this.
