## Notes · **draft**
⭐ The definition is plain: **first push out as an open immersion, then proper pushforward along the relative adic space**.

Everything rests on the existence of $j_!$ (`prop.open-immersion-shriek`).

⭐ When $f$ is proper, $j$ is an isomorphism and $f_! = (f^{\mathrm{ad}/Y})_*$, agreeing with $R\Gamma$ — this is the source of "for proper $f$, compact-support cohomology agrees with global sections" in `thm.poincare-solid`.
