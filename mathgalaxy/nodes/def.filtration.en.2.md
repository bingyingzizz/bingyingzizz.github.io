# filtration　`def.filtration`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=def.filtration)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./def.filtration.md)

We always assume that the filtration is **exhaustive** ($\bigcup_{n} F^{n}M = M$) and **separated** ($\bigcap_{n} F^{n}M = 0$). It is called **finite** if for $n \ll 0$ we have $F^{n}M = M$ and for $n \gg 0$ we have $F^{n}M = 0$.

Filtered objects form the category $\mathbf{F}(\mathcal{A})$, and the **associated graded** is denoted by $\operatorname{Gr}^{n}M := F^{n}M / F^{n+1}M$.
