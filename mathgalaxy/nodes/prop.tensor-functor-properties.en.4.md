## Notes · **draft**
⭐ From 2 and 3 it follows at once that $(-) \otimes_{R} N$ **preserves colimits** (direct sums, cokernels, filtered colimits). This explains both "right exact" (preserves cokernels) and "preserves direct sums", and is the common root of `prop.tensor-right-exact` and `prop.free-flat`.

⚠ In general it **does not preserve** limits: $\otimes$ does not preserve kernels.
