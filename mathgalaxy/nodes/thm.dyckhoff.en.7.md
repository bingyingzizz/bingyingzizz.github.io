# Dyckhoff　`thm.dyckhoff`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=thm.dyckhoff)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./thm.dyckhoff.md)

**Products and subspaces of Stone spaces are again Stone**, so all $S_{n}$ are Stone spaces; hence, by "Čech = sheaf cohomology on compact Hausdorff spaces" and the previous item "constant coefficients on Stone spaces are acyclic",
$$H^{n}_{\mathrm{cond}}(S, M) = \check{H}^{n}(S_{\bullet}, M).$$
- Filtered colimits are exact, so the colimit in the stalk reduces to the case $S = \{x\}$; and the Čech complex of a point is zero directly from "having a section $\implies$ is acyclic".

Hence $R^{q}c_{X*}M = 0$ ($q>0$), the spectral sequence degenerates, and $H^{n}_{\mathrm{cond}}(X,M) \cong H^{n}_{\mathrm{sheaf}}(X,M)$.

∎
