# homotopy category　`def.homotopy-category`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=def.homotopy-category)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./def.homotopy-category.md)

any additive functor $F : \mathcal{C} \to \mathcal{C}'$ induces $\mathbf{K}(\mathcal{C}) \to \mathbf{K}(\mathcal{C}')$.

Why work in the homotopy category: homology functors are invariant under homotopy, so **quotienting out homotopy first leaves exactly the information homology can see**.
