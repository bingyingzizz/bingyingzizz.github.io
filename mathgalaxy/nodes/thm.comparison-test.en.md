## Statement · **draft**
Let $0 \le a_n \le b_n$ hold for all sufficiently large $n$. Then

- $\sum b_n$ converges $\implies$ $\sum a_n$ converges;
- $\sum a_n$ diverges $\implies$ $\sum b_n$ diverges.

**Limit form**: if $a_n, b_n > 0$ and $\dfrac{a_n}{b_n} \to L$, then for $L \in (0, +\infty)$ the two **converge or diverge together**; for $L = 0$, convergence of $\sum b_n$ implies convergence of $\sum a_n$.
