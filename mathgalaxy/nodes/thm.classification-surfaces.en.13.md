# classification of closed surfaces　`thm.classification-surfaces`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=thm.classification-surfaces)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./thm.classification-surfaces.md)

**Why this theorem cannot take the same route as `prop.eulerchar-genus`.** That proposition only needs $\chi=2-2g$ and can compute $H_{2}$ directly using Lemma D, without classification. This theorem needs the **structure theorem** that every triangulation reduces to standard form. That is the classification itself, and numerical homological invariants cannot establish it. Bypassing this step would amount to assuming the classification theorem.
