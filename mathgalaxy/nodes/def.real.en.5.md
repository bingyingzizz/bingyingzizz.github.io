📌 **Three groups of properties are not in the definition**: the field axioms, the order axioms, and the least-upper-bound principle are all **theorems** about this construction (see "$\mathbb{R}$ is a complete ordered field"). Existence is given by the construction and needs no axioms.

📌 Another route is **Dedekind cuts** (taking subsets of $\mathbb{Q}$ that are non-empty, bounded above, and have no greatest element): the routes differ, but the objects constructed are isomorphic — see "uniqueness of the complete ordered field".
