## Statement · **draft**
A **condensed abelian group** is an **abelian sheaf** on $\mathrm{Cond}$. Four descriptions give the same category:

$$\mathrm{Ab}(\mathrm{Cond}) \;\simeq\; \mathrm{Cond}(\mathrm{Ab}) \;\simeq\; \widehat{\mathbf{CHaus}}(\mathrm{Ab}) \;\simeq\; \widehat{\mathbf{FCHaus}}(\mathrm{Ab})$$

Write this category as $\mathrm{CondAb}$.
