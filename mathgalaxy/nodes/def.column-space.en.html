Notes

The column space is "where AAA can reach"; the null space (solution space of the homogeneous system) is "which directions AAA crushes to 000". Their dimensions add up to nnn.

Row and column spaces have the same dimension (both equal the rank), but they are subspaces of different spaces; the link comes from transposition: Col⁡(A)=Row⁡(AT)\operatorname{Col}(A) = \operatorname{Row}(A^{\mathsf T})Col(A)=Row(AT).
