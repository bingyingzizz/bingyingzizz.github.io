prop.compact-open-discrete

Machine-drafted and pending author review. The Chinese text is authoritative.

Statement

If XXX is a discrete space, then C(X,Y)≅YXC(X, Y) \cong Y^{X}C(X,Y)≅YX (the right-hand side carries the product topology).

Notes

⭐ This is exactly "the exponential law holds trivially for a discrete exponent": the product topology on YXY^{X}YX is the compact-open topology. This also shows the information content of the compact-open topology: on a non-discrete XXX the product topology is too fine (it is the "pointwise convergence" topology); the compact-open topology is the right one.
