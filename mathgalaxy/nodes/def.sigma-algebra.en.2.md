# sigma-algebra　`def.sigma-algebra`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=def.sigma-algebra)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./def.sigma-algebra.md)

1. **Contains the whole set**: $X \in \mathfrak{A}$
2. **Closed under complements**: $A \in \mathfrak{A} \implies A^c := X \setminus A \in \mathfrak{A}$
3. **Closed under countable unions**: $A_1, A_2, \ldots \in \mathfrak{A} \implies \bigcup_{n=1}^{\infty} A_n \in \mathfrak{A}$

The pair $(X, \mathfrak{A})$ is called a **measurable space**, and the elements of $\mathfrak{A}$ are called **measurable sets**.

## Notes · **draft**
The three conditions come as a package and none can be dropped: keeping only (2) and (3) gives a **$\sigma$-ring**; weakening (3) to "finite unions" gives an **algebra**.
