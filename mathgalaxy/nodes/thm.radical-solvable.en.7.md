# solvable by radicals　`thm.radical-solvable`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=thm.radical-solvable)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./thm.radical-solvable.md)

**$\Leftarrow$ (solvable group $\implies$ solvable by radicals)**: Suppose $G=\operatorname{Gal}(K/F)$ is solvable. First assume that $F$ contains a primitive $n$th root of unity, with $n=|G|$. Induct on $|G|$. When $|G|=1$, we have $K=F$ and there is nothing to prove. Suppose $|G|>1$. The solvable group has a maximal proper normal subgroup $H$, so $G/H$ is simple and abelian, hence cyclic of prime order $p$. Set $E=K^{H}$. The Galois correspondence gives $[E:F]=p$, with $\operatorname{Gal}(E/F)=G/H$ cyclic. Since $F$ contains $\zeta_{p}$, the Kummer step gives $E=F(\beta)$ and $\beta^{p}\in F$. Also, $K/E$ is Galois with group $H$
