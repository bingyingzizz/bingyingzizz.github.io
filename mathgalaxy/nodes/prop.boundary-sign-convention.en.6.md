# Sign Convention for Boundaries　`prop.boundary-sign-convention`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=prop.boundary-sign-convention)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./prop.boundary-sign-convention.md)

**Check for $n = 1$**: $I^{1} = [0,1]$ and $\partial c = -c_{(1,0)}+c_{(1,1)} = c(1)-c(0)$. In the induced orientation of dimension $0$, the end $x = 1$ (outward normal $+e_{1}$) is positive and the end $x = 0$ is negative. Term by term, they agree.

**Check for $n = 2$**: $\partial c = -c_{(1,0)}+c_{(1,1)}+c_{(2,0)}-c_{(2,1)}$.
