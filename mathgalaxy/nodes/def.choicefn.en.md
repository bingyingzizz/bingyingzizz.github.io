## Statement · **draft**
Let $F$ be a family of sets with $\emptyset \notin F$.

A function $f$ is a **choice function** for $F$ iff **both** of the following hold:

(i) $f$ is a function and $\operatorname{dom} f = F$;
(ii) $\forall X \in F$, $f(X) \in X$.

That is, $f$ picks one element from each member of $F$.

The Axiom of Choice can therefore be stated concisely as: **every family $F$ with $\emptyset \notin F$ has a choice function**.
