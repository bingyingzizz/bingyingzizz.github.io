## Statement · **draft**
Let $F : [a, b] \to \mathbb{R}$ be differentiable and $F'$ integrable on $[a, b]$. Then

$$\int_a^b F'(x)\,dx = F(b) - F(a)$$

($F$ is called an **antiderivative** of $F'$; another way to write the formula is $\int_a^b f = F\bigr|_a^b$.)

## Notes · **draft**
⭐ This is the main theorem of elementary calculus: **computing areas becomes finding antiderivatives**.
