# Christoffel Symbols　`def.christoffel-symbol`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=def.christoffel-symbol)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./def.christoffel-symbol.md)

$$\nabla_{\partial_{i}}\partial_{j} = \Gamma^{k}_{ij}\partial_{k},\qquad \Gamma^{k}_{ij} = \Gamma^{k}_{ji}.$$

(Christoffel symbols. The first engine of the whole of intrinsic geometry.)

## Notes · **draft**
**Their only source is the Koszul formula**. The two requirements "zero torsion" and "metric preserving" together give the unique solution

$$2\langle \nabla_{X}Y,Z\rangle = X\langle Y,Z\rangle+Y\langle Z,X\rangle-Z\langle X,Y\rangle+\langle [X,Y],Z\rangle-\langle [Y,Z],X\rangle+\langle [Z,X],Y\rangle.$$
