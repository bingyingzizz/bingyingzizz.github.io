## Notes · **draft**
**Why $\operatorname{Re}s > 1$ suffices**: $|n^{-s}| = n^{-\operatorname{Re}s}$, and $\sum n^{-\sigma}$ converges for $\sigma > 1$ (the $p$-test). Hence the Weierstrass test gives local uniform convergence $\implies$ termwise differentiation is legitimate $\implies$ holomorphic.
