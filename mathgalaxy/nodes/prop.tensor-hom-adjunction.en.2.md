## Notes · **draft**
⭐ This is the **tensor–hom adjunction**, the second paradigm besides “free–forgetful”: it matches “linear maps of two variables” with “linear maps valued in a function space” (**currying**).

⭐ Its categorical status: “$\otimes$ is closed” (a symmetric monoidal closed category) is exactly the existence of this adjunction — the material around `def.adjoint` applies directly.

⚠ The word “natural” cannot be dropped: the isomorphism itself is easy to write, but only naturality in $W, U$ makes it an adjunction.
