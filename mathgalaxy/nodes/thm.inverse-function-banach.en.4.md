# Inverse Function Theorem in Banach Spaces　`thm.inverse-function-banach`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=thm.inverse-function-banach)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./thm.inverse-function-banach.md)

**In infinite dimensions, boundedness of the inverse must be stated as a hypothesis**.

A topological linear isomorphism is a linear bijection with continuous inverse. Both conditions are essential.

⭐ The proof adds two steps specific to Banach spaces:
- **Neumann series**: When $\|S\| < 1$, we have $(I - S)^{-1} = \sum_{k \ge 0} S^{k}$ (the series converges in the operator norm by completeness of $\mathcal{L}(E, F)$).

This inverts an operator near the identity and is central to the reduction to $Df(a) = I$.
