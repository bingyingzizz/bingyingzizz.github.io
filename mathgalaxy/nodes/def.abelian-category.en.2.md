## Abstract · **draft**
A preabelian category (one in which kernels and cokernels exist) is abelian when any one of four equivalent conditions holds: every monomorphism is regular; $N \cong \ker(M \twoheadrightarrow \operatorname{coker} i)$ for $i : N \rightarrowtail M$; every morphism factors uniquely up to isomorphism as an epimorphism followed by a monomorphism; and $\ker(N \to \operatorname{coker} f) \cong \operatorname{coker}(\ker f \to M)$ for every $f : M \to N$.
