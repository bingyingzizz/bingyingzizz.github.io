# a polynomial has at most as many roots as its degree　`prop.root-bound`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=prop.root-bound)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./prop.root-bound.md)

⚠ It holds only over an **integral domain**: over $\mathbb{Z}/8$, $x^{2} - 1$ has four roots $1, 3, 5, 7$ (because $\mathbb{Z}/8$ has zero divisors).

$K[x]$ being an integral domain is the key.
