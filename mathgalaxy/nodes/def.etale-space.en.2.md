# Étale Space　`def.etale-space`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=def.etale-space)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./def.etale-space.md)

- **Fibres are discrete**: $p$ a local homeomorphism $\implies$ every $p^{-1}(x)$ is discrete in $E$ (and also closed).
- **Open immersions are étale**: take $V = E$.
- **Continuity of sections is automatic**: when $p$ is a local homeomorphism, if $p \circ s = \mathrm{id}_{U}$, then a map $s : U \to E$ is **automatically continuous**, so a "section over $U$" is just a set-theoretic $s$.

⭐ **Equivalence with sheaves**: sheaves on $X$ and étale spaces over $X$ form an equivalence of categories, the functor being given by

$$\mathcal{F} \longmapsto \Bigl(\coprod_{x \in X} \mathcal{F}_{x} \longrightarrow X\Bigr)$$
