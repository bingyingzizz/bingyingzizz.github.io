Notes

This is the core estimate of the Vitali-type covering lemma; the maximal theorem depends entirely on it.

How the proof chooses: first take a ball B1B_1B1​ of maximal radius; then take a ball B2B_2B2​ of maximal radius disjoint from B1B_1B1​; and so on.

Why this choice suffices: if some Ai∈EA_i \in \mathcal{E}Ai​∈E is not chosen, then some BjB_jBj​ meets it — take the one BjB_jBj​ with smallest index.

By the selection rule, the radius of AiA_iAi​ does not exceed that of BjB_jBj​ (otherwise AiA_iAi​ would have been chosen when BjB_jBj​ was).

Enlarging the radius of BjB_jBj​ by a factor of 3 gives Ai⊆3BjA_i \subseteq 3B_jAi​⊆3Bj​, hence K⊆⋃j3BjK \subseteq \bigcup_j 3B_jK⊆⋃j​3Bj​.
