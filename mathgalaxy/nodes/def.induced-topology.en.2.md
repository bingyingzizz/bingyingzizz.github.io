# induced topology　`def.induced-topology`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=def.induced-topology)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./def.induced-topology.md)

⭐ Use: upgrade an arbitrary pullback at the presheaf level to a **morphism of sites** (the standard SGA4 construction); the price is that coverings must be recomputed, and the resulting topology is generally finer than the original.

⛔ **Same name, different objects**: the "induced topology" of topological spaces (subspace topology, @@def.subspace-topology@@) has nothing to do with the topology on a **site** discussed here; they merely share the Chinese name.
