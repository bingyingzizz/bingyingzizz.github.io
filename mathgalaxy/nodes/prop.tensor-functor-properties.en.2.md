# Functoriality of the Tensor Product　`prop.tensor-functor-properties`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=prop.tensor-functor-properties)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./prop.tensor-functor-properties.md)

1. **Additivity**: for $f : M \to M_{1}$ and $g : N \to N_{1}$ there is $f \otimes g$; fixing one variable gives an additive functor.
2. **Commutes with direct sums**: $(\bigoplus_{i} M_{i}) \otimes_{R} N \cong \bigoplus_{i} (M_{i} \otimes_{R} N)$, $M \otimes_{R} (\bigoplus_{j} N_{j}) \cong \bigoplus_{j} (M \otimes_{R} N_{j})$, naturally.
3. **Unit**: $R \otimes_{R} N \cong N$ ($r \otimes n \mapsto r n$); more generally $R^{(I)} \otimes_{R} N \cong N^{(I)}$.
