# Stone-Weierstrass theorem　`thm.stone-weierstrass`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=thm.stone-weierstrass)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./thm.stone-weierstrass.md)

Since $\varepsilon$ is arbitrary, $f \in \overline{A}$, hence $\overline{A} = C(K)$.

∎

⚠️ None of the three hypotheses can be dropped: $A = \{f : f(0) = 0\}$ lacks the unit; $A = $ the constant functions lacks separation of points.

⭐ This is the model of trading **algebraic** properties for **approximation** power: no smoothness, no countability, no special kernel is needed. The classical Weierstrass theorem (polynomial approximation) is the case $K = [a,b]$ and $A = $ the polynomials.
