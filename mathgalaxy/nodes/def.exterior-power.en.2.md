## Notes · **draft**
⭐ Geometric meaning: when $\dim V = n$, $\bigwedge^{n} V$ is **one-dimensional**, and $v_1 \wedge \cdots \wedge v_n$ is exactly "the oriented volume of the parallelepiped spanned by these $n$ vectors" — its coefficient is the determinant.

⭐ Alternation is achieved by "quotienting": $v \wedge v = 0$ (send $v \otimes v$ into the quotient), which is the "zero when two arguments agree" from `def.alternating-form`.
