Statement

The Klein bottle KKK is RP2#RP2\mathbb{RP}^{2} \# \mathbb{RP}^{2}RP2#RP2 (the connected sum of two projective planes). It is also the quotient of a square obtained by gluing one pair of opposite sides in the same direction and the other pair in opposite directions. It is a closed nonorientable surface with nonorientable genus k=2k = 2k=2 and χ(K)=0\chi(K) = 0χ(K)=0.

Notes

⭐ Two equivalent descriptions: (1) Glue one pair of opposite sides of a square in the same direction (as in T2T^{2}T2) and the other pair in opposite directions. (2) Glue two Möbius bands along their boundaries. Both constructions give the same surface (the equivalence is stated here without proof).

⭐ χ=0\chi = 0χ=0: 2−k=2−2=02 - k = 2 - 2 = 02−k=2−2=0. The torus has the same value, so the information χ=0\chi = 0χ=0 alone does not distinguish the Klein bottle. Orientability is also needed. The two surfaces are standard examples of the same χ\chiχ with different topological types.

⚠ It still cannot embed in R3\mathbb{R}^{3}R3. No compact nonorientable surface without boundary embeds in R3\mathbb{R}^{3}R3. The familiar glass model whose neck passes through the body is an immersion with self-intersection.
