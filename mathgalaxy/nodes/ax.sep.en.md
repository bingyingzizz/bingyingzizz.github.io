## Statement · **reviewed**
For every formula $\varphi (x, p)$ (in which $B$ does not occur), the following is an axiom:

$$\forall p \forall A \exists B \forall x [ x \in B \leftrightarrow  ( x \in A \wedge  \varphi(x, p) ) ]$$

By extensionality such a $B$ is unique, and one writes $B = \{ x \in A : \varphi (x, p) \}$. It is commonly called the "subset axiom".
