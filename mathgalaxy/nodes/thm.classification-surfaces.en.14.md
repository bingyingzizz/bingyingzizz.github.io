# classification of closed surfaces　`thm.classification-surfaces`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=thm.classification-surfaces)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./thm.classification-surfaces.md)

⭐ Criterion (assuming Lemma K): Two closed surfaces are homeomorphic $\iff$ they have the same orientability and the same $\chi$ (equivalently, both are $S^{2}$, both are connected sums of $g$ tori with the same $g$, or both are connected sums of $k$ copies of $\mathbb{RP}^{2}$ with the same $k$). For $S^{2}$ and $\mathbb{RP}^{2}$, the values of $\chi$ are $2$ and $1$, respectively, so $\chi$ alone is insufficient and must be used together with orientability.
