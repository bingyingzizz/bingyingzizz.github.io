## Notes · **draft**
⚠ **Note where this statement sits**: it is a **theorem**, not a definition — first comes the construction ("the real number system $\mathbb{R}$"), then the proof that what was constructed has these three groups of properties.

⭐ **Item (iii) is the engine of analysis**: without it $\mathbb{Q}$ also satisfies the first two items, but $\{ x \in \mathbb{Q} : x^2 < 2 \}$ has no least upper bound in $\mathbb{Q}$.

($\mathbb{Q}$ is therefore not a complete ordered field — this is not a defect, but precisely the reason to complete $\mathbb{Q}$ into $\mathbb{R}$.)
