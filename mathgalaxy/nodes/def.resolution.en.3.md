# resolution　`def.resolution`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=def.resolution)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./def.resolution.md)

⚠️ The name refers to **which side the complex extends toward** (a right resolution extends toward higher degrees), **not** the direction of the arrow: the arrow is always $K^{\bullet} \to L^{\bullet}$. The reversed arrow gives a left resolution.

**Existence**: with enough injectives every bounded-below complex has an injective resolution (embed the kernels into injective objects step by step) ✓; dually, with enough projectives every bounded-above complex has a projective resolution ✓.
