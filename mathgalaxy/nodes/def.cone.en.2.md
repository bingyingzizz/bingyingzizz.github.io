## Notes · **draft**
Intuition: $X$ stands outside the diagram and casts a beam toward each position of $D$, with the requirement that the projections be compatible (projecting first and then following an arrow in the diagram gives the same result as projecting directly to the target).

When $X$ is fixed, the cones over $D$ are exactly the set of natural transformations $\operatorname{Nat}(\Delta X, D)$, so a cone is nothing new — just a natural transformation under another name.
