Statement

Let RRR be a unital ring and III a left RRR-module. Then III is injective iff: for every left ideal J⊆RJ \subseteq RJ⊆R and every RRR-linear map f:J→If : J \to If:J→I, there is x∈Ix \in Ix∈I such that

f(a)=ax(∀a∈J),f(a) = a x \qquad (\forall a \in J),f(a)=ax(∀a∈J),

that is, fff always extends to R→IR \to IR→I with 1↦x1 \mapsto x1↦x.

In other words: to test injectivity, the case "A↣BA \rightarrowtail BA↣B" may be restricted to "the inclusion of an ideal J↣RJ \rightarrowtail RJ↣R".

⭐ Meaning: it compresses "test against all injections" into "test against ideals of RRR" — and ideals are subsets of RRR, usually far fewer. This step is the key to translating a categorical condition into a condition on the ring; 内射等价于可除 depends entirely on it.

⚠ The "ideal" in the criterion cannot be replaced by "finitely generated ideal": flatness has Lambek's theorem allowing only finitely generated ideals to be checked, but injectivity has no such dual weakening (unless the ring is Noetherian).
