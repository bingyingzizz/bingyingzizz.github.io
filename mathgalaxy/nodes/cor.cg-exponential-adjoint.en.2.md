## Notes · **draft**
**Two immediate corollaries**: a left adjoint **preserves colimits**, so $X \mapsto k(X \times Y)$ preserves colimits of compactly generated spaces; a right adjoint **preserves limits**, so $Z \mapsto kC(kY,Z)$ preserves limits.
