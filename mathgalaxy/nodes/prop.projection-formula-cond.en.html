prop.projection-formula-cond

Machine-drafted and pending author review. The Chinese text is authoritative.

Statement

Let XXX be a condensed set and SSS a locally compact Hausdorff topological space. Then

S×X(⋅)  ≅  (S×X)(⋅).S \times X(\cdot) \;\cong\; (S \times X)(\cdot).S×X(⋅)≅(S×X)(⋅).

(the left side is the topological product of the underlying space X(⋅)X(\cdot)X(⋅) with SSS; when SSS is compact Hausdorff this says "bringing SSS in does not affect kkk-ification".)

Notes

⭐ Reading: the underlying-space functor X↦X(⋅)X \mapsto X(\cdot)X↦X(⋅) commutes with "multiplying by a locally compact space". Thus operations such as "multiplying by a compact piece" can be performed first in Cond\mathrm{Cond}Cond and then brought back to topological spaces, or in the reverse order.

📌 Together with "the underlying space preserves finite products" this forms two steps in one chain: that one gives the formula after kkk-ification, and this one removes the kkk-ification.
