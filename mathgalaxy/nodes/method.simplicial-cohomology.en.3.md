## Notes · **draft**
**What is being said.** Sheaf cohomology is defined as "take an injective resolution, take sections, take cohomology", and an injective resolution is **not computable** (injective objects are too large). This gives a **computable substitute**: replacing the injective resolution by a resolution by free / representable objects, taking sections levelwise produces a simplicial abelian group (or chain complex) whose homotopy groups are $H^{n}$.
