# Z is a commutative integral domain　`thm.int-ring`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=thm.int-ring)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./thm.int-ring.md)

⭐ **No zero divisors** is the most interesting item: let $x = [a,b]$ and $y = [c,d]$ both be positive, that is, $a > b$ and $c > d$, and write $a = b + p$ and $c = d + q$ ($p, q \ge 1$). Then $xy = [ac + bd,\ ad + bc]$, and

$$(ac + bd) - (ad + bc) = pq > 0,$$

so $xy > 0$, and it cannot be $0$. When the signs are opposite the product is negative, by the same argument.
