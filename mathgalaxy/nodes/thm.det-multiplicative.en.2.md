## Notes · **draft**
⭐ **Multiplicativity** is the most important property of the determinant: it translates "matrix multiplication (composition)" into "multiplication of scalars", so $\det$ is a **multiplicative homomorphism** from the ring $M_n(K)$ to the field $K$ (sending $\operatorname{GL}_n(K)$ to $K^{\times}$).

⭐ The trick of the proof: fix $B$ and look at $A \mapsto \det(AB)$ — it is **alternating multilinear** in the columns of $A$ (because the columns of $AB$ are linear combinations of the columns of $A$), so by existence and uniqueness it equals a constant multiple of $\det A$;
