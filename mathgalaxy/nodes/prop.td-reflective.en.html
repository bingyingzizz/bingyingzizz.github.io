Statement

The category of totally disconnected spaces is a reflective subcategory of Top\mathbf{Top}Top, with reflector

π0:Top⟶Toptd,X↦π0(X)\pi_{0} : \mathbf{Top} \longrightarrow \mathbf{Top}_{\mathrm{td}}, \qquad X \mapsto \pi_{0}(X)π0​:Top⟶Toptd​,X↦π0​(X)

and X→π0(X)X \to \pi_{0}(X)X→π0​(X) is an isomorphism   ⟺  \iff⟺ XXX is totally disconnected.

Notes

Corollary: any limit of totally disconnected spaces is again totally disconnected — because π0\pi_{0}π0​, being a reflector (a left adjoint), preserves colimits, while the inclusion preserves limits. Combined with "CHaus\mathbf{CHaus}CHaus is closed under limits", this says Stone spaces and Stonean spaces are each closed under limits.

Proof. Write Toptd\mathbf{Top}_{\mathrm{td}}Toptd​ for the category of totally disconnected spaces and i:Toptd↪Topi : \mathbf{Top}_{\mathrm{td}}\hookrightarrow\mathbf{Top}i:Toptd​↪Top for the inclusion. We must show that iii has a left adjoint π0\pi_{0}π0​.

  • Construction: take π0(X)\pi_{0}(X)π0​(X) to be the set of connected components of XXX with the quotient topology (X→π0(X)X\to\pi_{0}(X)X→π0​(X) sends each point to its component).

Under the quotient topology π0(X)\pi_{0}(X)π0​(X) is totally disconnected (preimages of components are ... components are points in the quotient, and whether a point is clopen depends on whether its preimage is; in general π0\pi_{0}π0​ is only totally disconnected and not necessarily discrete, which is what is required);

  • Universal property: given f:X→Yf : X \to Yf:X→Y (YYY totally disconnected). A continuous map sends connected sets to connected sets, and the only connected subsets of YYY are points, so fff is constant on each connected component, hence fff factors uniquely through X→π0(X)X\to\pi_{0}(X)X→π0​(X) ✓.

The two directions are mutually inverse, so π0⊣i\pi_{0}\dashv iπ0​⊣i ✓, i.e. Toptd\mathbf{Top}_{\mathrm{td}}Toptd​ is a reflective subcategory. "X→π0(X)X\to\pi_{0}(X)X→π0​(X) is an isomorphism"   ⟺  \iff⟺ each component is a point   ⟺  \iff⟺ XXX totally disconnected ✓. ∎ ⭐ Reading of the corollary: a reflective subcategory is equivalent to "the inclusion has a left adjoint", and a left adjoint preserves colimits — but here what is used is that the inclusion preserves limits (a right adjoint preserves limits), so limits of totally disconnected spaces (computed in Top\mathbf{Top}Top) land back in totally disconnected ✓.
