## Statement · **draft**
Even when $\mu$ and $\nu$ are both **complete**, $\mu \times \nu$ is **often incomplete**: there is $E \in \mathcal{M} \otimes  \mathcal{N}$ with $\mu \times \nu(E) = 0$, yet $E$ has a subset not in $\mathcal{M} \otimes  \mathcal{N}$.
