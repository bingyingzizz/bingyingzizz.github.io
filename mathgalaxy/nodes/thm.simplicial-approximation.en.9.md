# simplicial approximation　`thm.simplicial-approximation`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=thm.simplicial-approximation)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./thm.simplicial-approximation.md)

Repeated barycentric subdivision makes the diameters tend to zero. If $\dim K = n$, then $\operatorname{mesh}(K^{(m)}) \le \big(\tfrac{n}{n+1}\big)^{m}\,\operatorname{mesh}(K)$ (every subdivided simplex lies in an original simplex and has diameter at most $n/(n+1)$ times the original diameter). Choose $m$ such that $\operatorname{mesh}(K^{(m)}) < \delta/2$.
