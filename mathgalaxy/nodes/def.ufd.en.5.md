# unique factorisation domain　`def.ufd`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=def.ufd)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./def.ufd.md)

The hierarchy (`imp.ring-euclid-pid` / `imp.ring-pid-ufd`): Euclidean domain $\implies$ PID $\implies$ UFD $\implies$ GCD domain, and both arrows are irreversible ($\mathbb{Z}[\sqrt{-5}]$ does not even have gcds; there exist UFDs that are not PIDs, such as $K[x, y]$).

> Proofs of the edges are still Chinese only: `proofs/`; the Chinese page remains authoritative.
