# Associated Bundle　`def.associated-bundle`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=def.associated-bundle)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./def.associated-bundle.md)

Let $P \to M$ be a principal $G$-bundle (@@def.principal-bundle@@) and $F$ a manifold with a left $G$-action. On the product $P \times F$ let $G$ act on the right by

$$(u, y)\cdot g := (u \cdot g,\ g^{-1}\cdot y) ,$$

and write its orbit space as

$$P \times_{G} F := (P \times F)\big/ G ,$$

The projection $[u, y] \mapsto \pi(u)$ makes it a fibre bundle with fibre $F$, called the **associated bundle**.

**The most important case**: taking $F = \mathbb{R}^{k}$ with the standard action of $G = \mathrm{GL}(k,\mathbb{R})$ gives

$$\mathrm{Fr}(E) \times_{\mathrm{GL}(k)} \mathbb{R}^{k} \;\cong\; E ,$$
