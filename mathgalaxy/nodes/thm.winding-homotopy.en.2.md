# homotopy invariance of the winding number　`thm.winding-homotopy`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=thm.winding-homotopy)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./thm.winding-homotopy.md)

Corollary (the homotopy form of Cauchy's theorem): if $f$ is holomorphic on a region $\Omega$, and $\gamma_{0}, \gamma_{1}$ are homotopic in $\Omega$, then $\displaystyle\oint_{\gamma_{0}} f\,\mathrm{d}z = \oint_{\gamma_{1}} f\,\mathrm{d}z$.
