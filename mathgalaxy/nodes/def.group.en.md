## Statement · **draft**
Let $G$ be a **set** and $\cdot$ a **binary operation** on $G$ (that is, a function $G \times G \to G$, $(a, b) \mapsto a \cdot b$). Then $(G, \cdot)$ is a **group** if and only if:

- **associativity**: $(a b) c = a (b c)$ for all $a, b, c \in G$;
- **identity**: there exists $e \in G$ such that $e a = a e = a$ for all $a$;
- **inverses**: for every $a \in G$ there exists $a^{-1} \in G$ such that $a a^{-1} = a^{-1} a = e$.
