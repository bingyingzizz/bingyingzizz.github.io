Statement

Let MMM be a compact Hausdorff abelian group and NNN a real Banach space. Then

RHom⁡Z(M, N)=0.\operatorname{RHom}_{\mathbb{Z}}(M,\ N) = 0.RHomZ​(M, N)=0.

Notes

Dual to the previous item: the previous says "real vector space →\to→ discrete" gives all zeros; this says "compact Hausdorff group →\to→ real Banach" gives all zeros. Together they are exactly the two scissors used later to cut off both ends in the "three-case reduction".
