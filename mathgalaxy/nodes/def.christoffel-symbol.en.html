Statement

In plain words: moving a vector along a curve on a surface needs a rule, and that rule is called a connection; once local coordinates are chosen, the connection degenerates into 232^{3}23 functions Γijk\Gamma^{k}_{ij}Γijk​, which say "how much of a ∂k\partial_{k}∂k​ component appears when ∂j\partial_{j}∂j​ is transported a little in the ∂i\partial_{i}∂i​ direction".

Everything uses only the metric coefficients gijg_{ij}gij​ and their first derivatives — so this is the first intrinsic object to appear.

Let (x1,x2)(x^{1},x^{2})(x1,x2) be local coordinates on a surface SSS, let gij=⟨∂i,∂j⟩g_{ij} = \langle \partial_{i},\partial_{j}\ranglegij​=⟨∂i​,∂j​⟩, and let (gij)(g^{ij})(gij) be the inverse matrix of (gij)(g_{ij})(gij​). The Christoffel symbols are defined by

Γijk=12gkℓ(∂igjℓ+∂jgiℓ−∂ℓgij).\Gamma^{k}_{ij} = \frac{1}{2}g^{k\ell}\left(\partial_{i}g_{j\ell}+\partial_{j}g_{i\ell}-\partial_{\ell}g_{ij}\right).Γijk​=21​gkℓ(∂i​gjℓ​+∂j​giℓ​−∂ℓ​gij​).

They are the coefficients of the Levi-Civita connection in the coordinate frame:

∇∂i∂j=Γijk∂k,Γijk=Γjik.\nabla_{\partial_{i}}\partial_{j} = \Gamma^{k}_{ij}\partial_{k},\qquad \Gamma^{k}_{ij} = \Gamma^{k}_{ji}.∇∂i​​∂j​=Γijk​∂k​,Γijk​=Γjik​.

(Christoffel symbols. The first engine of the whole of intrinsic geometry.)

Notes

Their only source is the Koszul formula. The two requirements "zero torsion" and "metric preserving" together give the unique solution

2⟨∇XY,Z⟩=X⟨Y,Z⟩+Y⟨Z,X⟩−Z⟨X,Y⟩+⟨[X,Y],Z⟩−⟨[Y,Z],X⟩+⟨[Z,X],Y⟩.2\langle \nabla_{X}Y,Z\rangle = X\langle Y,Z\rangle+Y\langle Z,X\rangle-Z\langle X,Y\rangle+\langle [X,Y],Z\rangle-\langle [Y,Z],X\rangle+\langle [Z,X],Y\rangle.2⟨∇X​Y,Z⟩=X⟨Y,Z⟩+Y⟨Z,X⟩−Z⟨X,Y⟩+⟨[X,Y],Z⟩−⟨[Y,Z],X⟩+⟨[Z,X],Y⟩.

For a coordinate frame [∂i,∂j]=0[\partial_{i},\partial_{j}] = 0[∂i​,∂j​]=0, the right-hand side retains only two derivative terms and the left-hand side is 2Γijℓgℓk2\Gamma^{\ell}_{ij}g_{\ell k}2Γijℓ​gℓk​; solving gives the formula above. The derivation uses only gijg_{ij}gij​ and its first derivatives — this is the first intrinsic quantity.

⚠️ Γijk\Gamma^{k}_{ij}Γijk​ is not a tensor: under a change of coordinates a non-homogeneous second-derivative term appears, so "Γ\GammaΓ equals some number at ppp" depends on the coordinates. But "there exist coordinates with Γ(p)=0\Gamma(p) = 0Γ(p)=0" is a coordinate-independent fact (normal coordinates); the difference between the two is "a numerical value" versus "existence".

Why it determines geodesics: the only external datum in the geodesic equation is Γ\GammaΓ, so "which curves are geodesics" is entirely a matter of III — already the first step of intrinsic geometry.

⚠️ In two dimensions there are only 23=82^{3} = 823=8 symbols, but symmetry reduces the independent components to 666 (kkk fixed, i≤ji \le ji≤j); in flat coordinates they all vanish.
