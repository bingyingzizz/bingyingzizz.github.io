## Statement · **draft**
Let $H, K$ be Hilbert spaces and $u \in B(H, K)$. Then there is a **unique** $u^* \in B(K, H)$ such that

$$\langle u^*(x), y\rangle_H = \langle x, \, u(y)\rangle_K \qquad (x \in K,\ y \in H) ,$$

and $\|u^*\| = \|u\|$.
