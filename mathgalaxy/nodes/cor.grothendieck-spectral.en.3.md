# Grothendieck spectral sequence　`cor.grothendieck-spectral`

$$E^{p,q}_{2} = R^{p}G\bigl(R^{q}F(M)\bigr) \;\Longrightarrow\; R^{p+q}(G \circ F)(M).$$

## Notes · **draft**
⭐ **What it is for**: to compute the derived functors $R^{n}(G \circ F)$ of a composite, one may **first compute $R^{q}F$, then $R^{p}G$**, stitching the two layers together with a spectral sequence. This is the most basic "two-step" tool in homological algebra.

**Proof (double complex + two spectral sequences).** Take an injective resolution of $M$, say $I^{\bullet}$, and put $A^{\bullet} := F I^{\bullet}$.
