# Weierstrass elementary factors　`def.elementary-factor`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=def.elementary-factor)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./def.elementary-factor.md)

Proof: $1 - E_{p}(z) = (1-z)\left[1 - e^{z + \cdots + z^{p}/p}\right]$, and the difference in the brackets is controlled by $|z|^{p+1}$ (expand the exponential and cancel the leading terms). This estimate is the entire basis for choosing $p$ so that $\sum |a_{n}|^{-(p+1)}$ converges.

⚠️ $p$ cannot be taken uniformly small: the faster the zeros grow (for example $a_{n} = n$), the larger the required $p$. This freedom of "assigning a $p_{n}$ to each zero" is exactly the flexibility behind the Weierstrass product theorem.
