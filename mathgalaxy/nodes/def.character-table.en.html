Notes

The rows are the irreducible characters; char⁡F=0\operatorname{char}F=0charF=0 guarantees that χi=χj\chi_i=\chi_jχi​=χj​ iff Vi≅VjV_i\cong V_jVi​≅Vj​ (thm.character-determines-semisimple).
