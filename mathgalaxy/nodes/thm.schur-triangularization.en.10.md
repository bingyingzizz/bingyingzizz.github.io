# Schur Triangularization　`thm.schur-triangularization`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=thm.schur-triangularization)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./thm.schur-triangularization.md)

⭐ **Why it is used more often than Jordan normal form**: Jordan form uses arbitrary similarity transformations ($P^{-1}AP$). It is useful in theory, but $P$ may be too ill-conditioned for computation. Schur form uses unitary similarity ($U^{*}AU$), whose condition number is always 1. Thus **numerical linear algebra uses Schur form instead of Jordan form**.
