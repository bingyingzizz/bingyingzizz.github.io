# Lifting Maps to Injective Resolutions　`prop.injective-resolution-lift`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=prop.injective-resolution-lift)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./prop.injective-resolution-lift.md)

⭐ Together the two say that **injective resolutions are unique up to homotopy**: a morphism in $\operatorname{Hom}(M,N)$ lifts to a morphism of complexes, uniquely up to homotopy. So "computing right derived functors by injective resolutions" is independent of the choice of resolution — this is the entire basis for the well-definedness of $R^{n}F$.

📌 The "comparison theorem" here means the comparison of **injective-resolution lifts**; the comparison theorem of homological algebra is @@thm.comparison-theorem@@.
