## Notes · **draft**
Parts (1) and (2) follow from additivity of the trace and $\operatorname{tr}(A\otimes B)=\operatorname{tr}A\operatorname{tr}B$.
Part (3) uses the inverse transpose in the dual representation and cyclicity of the trace.
For (4) and (5), triangularize $\rho(g)$ over an algebraic closure with eigenvalues $\lambda_i$; the eigenvalues on $\operatorname{Sym}^2$ are $\lambda_i\lambda_j$ with $i\le j$, and on $\operatorname{Alt}^2$ they are $\lambda_i\lambda_j$ with $i<j$, using $\sum_{i<j}\lambda_i\lambda_j=\frac12((\sum\lambda_i)^2-\sum\lambda_i^2)$.
