## Notes · **draft**
(1)-(3) hold in any characteristic; (4)(5) use the $\pm1$ decomposition of the swap and require $\operatorname{char}F\ne2$. In (3), $V^*$ is an $A^{\mathrm{op}}$-representation, i.e. $g\mapsto\rho(g^{-1})^T$ for groups. When $\operatorname{char}F\ne2$, the characters of Sym²/Alt² follow from the trace decomposition of $V^{\otimes2}$.
