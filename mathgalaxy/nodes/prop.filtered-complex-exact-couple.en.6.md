# a filtered complex gives an exact couple　`prop.filtered-complex-exact-couple`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=prop.filtered-complex-exact-couple)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./prop.filtered-complex-exact-couple.md)

This is the connecting homomorphism followed by the map to the associated graded. It is precisely the differential $\operatorname{Gr}^{p} \to \operatorname{Gr}^{p+1}$ in the direct construction. Its target bidegree $(p+1, q)$ agrees with the shift of $d_{r}$ by $(r, 1-r)$ when $r = 1$.
