## Statement · **draft**
Let $A$ be a commutative ring with unity and $A \ne \{0\}$. If $ab = 0$ forces $a = 0$ or $b = 0$, then $A$ is called an **integral domain** ($A$ has no zero divisors).

Equivalent form (cancellation): for $a \ne 0$, $ab = ac$ $\implies$ $b = c$.
