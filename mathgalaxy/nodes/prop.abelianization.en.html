Statement

The abelianization functor

(−)ab:Grp⟶Ab,G⟼Gab:=G/[G,G](-)^{\mathrm{ab}} : \mathbf{Grp} \longrightarrow \mathbf{Ab}, \qquad G \longmapsto G^{\mathrm{ab}} := G / [G, G](−)ab:Grp⟶Ab,G⟼Gab:=G/[G,G]

is left adjoint to the inclusion functor Ab↪Grp\mathbf{Ab} \hookrightarrow \mathbf{Grp}Ab↪Grp.

Notes

⭐ This is exactly what "abelianization is left adjoint" means: it is the most economical functor that "kills non-commutativity" — any map to an abelian group can only see GabG^{\mathrm{ab}}Gab.
