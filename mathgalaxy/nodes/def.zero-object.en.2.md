## Notes · **draft**
**The zero object gives the zero morphism**: any $X \to Y$ factors through $X \to 0 \to Y$, and the resulting morphism is written $0_{X,Y}$. Every statement of the form “$f = 0$” or “$f \ne 0$” relies on it.

**Why the biproduct coincides**: in a **preadditive** category, product and coproduct automatically agree whenever they exist (the two universal properties satisfy each other), so one may speak of just one. An element of $M \oplus N$ is formally written $f + g$ (one summand from $M$ and one from $N$).
