2. a family of **filtered** objects $H^{n}$ ($n \in \mathbb{Z}$) such that for every $p, q$ and all sufficiently large $r$,

$$\operatorname{Gr}^{p} H^{p+q} \;\cong\; E^{p,q}_{r}$$

One in which $p, q \ge 0$ is necessary for $E^{p,q}_{r}$ to be nonzero is called a **first-quadrant** spectral sequence.

## Notes · **draft**
Where it comes from: give a complex $K$ a filtration $F^{p}K$; the filtration on homology is inherited from

$$F^{p}H^{n} := \operatorname{im}\bigl(H^{n}(F^{p}K) \to H^{n}(K)\bigr)$$
