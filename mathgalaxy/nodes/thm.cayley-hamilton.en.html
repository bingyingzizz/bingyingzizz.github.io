Statement

Let T:V→VT : V \to VT:V→V be a linear map on a finite-dimensional KKK-vector space, and χT\chi_TχT​ its characteristic polynomial. Replace xxx appearing in χT\chi_TχT​ by TTT (multiplying the constant term by III), obtaining the operator χT(T)\chi_T(T)χT​(T). Then

χT(T)=0\chi_T(T) = 0χT​(T)=0

(the right-hand side is the zero operator; in matrix form, the zero matrix.)
