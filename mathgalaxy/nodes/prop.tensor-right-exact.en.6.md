⭐ So "right exact" is free, and **left exactness is the property** — and its name is flatness. The lost left end does not vanish into nothing: it is the **image** of the connecting map $\operatorname{Tor}_{1}^{R}(M_{2}, N) \to M_{1} \otimes N$ (one cannot identify the "lost kernel" with $\operatorname{Tor}_{1}$ directly: the split sequence $0 \to \mathbb{Z}/2 \to (\mathbb{Z}/2)^{2} \to \mathbb{Z}/2 \to 0$ stays split after tensoring, with zero kernel, while $\operatorname{Tor}_{1}(\mathbb{Z}/2, \mathbb{Z}/2) = \mathbb{Z}/2$).
