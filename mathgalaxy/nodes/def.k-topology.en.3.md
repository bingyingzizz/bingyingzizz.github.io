# k-Open, k-Closed and the k-Topology　`def.k-topology`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=def.k-topology)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./def.k-topology.md)

In general the identity map from $kX$ to $X$, namely $kX \to X$, is continuous (the topology got finer), but the reverse is not — this "downward" map is exactly the counit $\varepsilon_{X}$ of the coreflection.
