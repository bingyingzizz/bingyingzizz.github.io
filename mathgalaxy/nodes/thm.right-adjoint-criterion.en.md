## Statement · **draft**
A functor $F : \mathcal{C} \to \mathcal{D}$ has a right adjoint $\iff$ for every $Y \in \mathcal{D}$ the functor

$$\operatorname{Hom}_{\mathcal{D}}\bigl(F(-),\ Y\bigr) : \mathcal{C}^{\mathrm{op}} \to \mathbf{Set}$$

is representable.
