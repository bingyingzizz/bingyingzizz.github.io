又称Wedderburn–Artin 定理·半单代数结构定理·Wedderburn-Artin

陈述

设 AAA 是有限维半单 FFF-代数。则 A≅∏i=1sMni(Di)A\cong\prod_{i=1}^s M_{n_i}(D_i)A≅∏i=1s​Mni​​(Di​)，其中 DiD_iDi​ 是除法代数；等价地，每个有限维 AAA-模都是半单的。

说明

因 AAA 半单，左正则模分解为 A=⨁iSi⊕niA=\bigoplus_i S_i^{\oplus n_i}A=⨁i​Si⊕ni​​，SiS_iSi​ 两两不同构单左模。 右乘给出 Aop≅End⁡A(A)A^{\mathrm{op}}\cong\operatorname{End}_A(A)Aop≅EndA​(A)：a↦Raa\mapsto R_aa↦Ra​，Ra(x)=xaR_a(x)=xaRa​(x)=xa；逆由 f↦f(1)f\mapsto f(1)f↦f(1) 给出。 由 Schur 引理，Hom⁡A(Si,Sj)=0\operatorname{Hom}_A(S_i,S_j)=0HomA​(Si​,Sj​)=0（i≠ji\ne ji=j），故 End⁡A(A)≅∏iEnd⁡A(Si⊕ni)≅∏iMni(Di)\operatorname{End}_A(A)\cong\prod_i\operatorname{End}_A(S_i^{\oplus n_i})\cong\prod_i M_{n_i}(D_i)EndA​(A)≅∏i​EndA​(Si⊕ni​​)≅∏i​Mni​​(Di​)，其中 Di=End⁡A(Si)D_i=\operatorname{End}_A(S_i)Di​=EndA​(Si​) 是除法代数。 取相反代数得 A≅∏iMni(Diop)A\cong\prod_i M_{n_i}(D_i^{\mathrm{op}})A≅∏i​Mni​​(Diop​)。 反向：每个矩阵代数 Mni(Di)M_{n_i}(D_i)Mni​​(Di​) 半单，乘积半单；有限维半单代数的模都半单（子模有补）。■\blacksquare■

Statement draft

Let AAA be a finite-dimensional semisimple FFF-algebra. Then A≅∏i=1sMni(Di)A\cong\prod_{i=1}^s M_{n_i}(D_i)A≅∏i=1s​Mni​​(Di​) with division algebras DiD_iDi​; equivalently, every finite-dimensional AAA-module is semisimple.

Notes draft

Since AAA is semisimple, the left regular module decomposes as A=⨁iSi⊕niA=\bigoplus_i S_i^{\oplus n_i}A=⨁i​Si⊕ni​​, with the SiS_iSi​ pairwise non-isomorphic simple left modules. Right multiplication gives Aop≅End⁡A(A)A^{\mathrm{op}}\cong\operatorname{End}_A(A)Aop≅EndA​(A): a↦Raa\mapsto R_aa↦Ra​, Ra(x)=xaR_a(x)=xaRa​(x)=xa; the inverse is f↦f(1)f\mapsto f(1)f↦f(1). By Schur's lemma, Hom⁡A(Si,Sj)=0\operatorname{Hom}_A(S_i,S_j)=0HomA​(Si​,Sj​)=0 for i≠ji\ne ji=j, hence End⁡A(A)≅∏iEnd⁡A(Si⊕ni)≅∏iMni(Di)\operatorname{End}_A(A)\cong\prod_i\operatorname{End}_A(S_i^{\oplus n_i})\cong\prod_i M_{n_i}(D_i)EndA​(A)≅∏i​EndA​(Si⊕ni​​)≅∏i​Mni​​(Di​), where Di=End⁡A(Si)D_i=\operatorname{End}_A(S_i)Di​=EndA​(Si​) is a division algebra. Passing to opposite algebras gives A≅∏iMni(Diop)A\cong\prod_i M_{n_i}(D_i^{\mathrm{op}})A≅∏i​Mni​​(Diop​). Conversely, each matrix algebra Mni(Di)M_{n_i}(D_i)Mni​​(Di​) is semisimple and a product of semisimple algebras is semisimple; modules over a finite-dimensional semisimple algebra are semisimple (submodules have complements). ■\blacksquare■
