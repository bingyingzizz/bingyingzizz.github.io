陈述

设 AAA 是有限维半单 FFF-代数。则 A≅∏i=1sMni(Di)A\cong\prod_{i=1}^s M_{n_i}(D_i)A≅∏i=1s​Mni​​(Di​)，其中 DiD_iDi​ 是除法代数；等价地，每个有限维 AAA-模都是半单的。

说明

把正则模 AAA 分解为单左理想，按同构类分组得到双边理想 AiA_iAi​，且 A=∏AiA=\prod A_iA=∏Ai​。 每个 AiA_iAi​ 是单代数，用单代数的 Wedderburn 定理。 反向：矩阵代数的乘积半单。 nin_ini​ 与 DiD_iDi​ 在同构与置换意义下唯一。 证明经由 thm.wedderburn-simple，而后者依赖 Burnside 满射性（本图 skeleton），故本条仍为 skeleton。

Statement draft

Let AAA be a finite-dimensional semisimple FFF-algebra. Then A≅∏i=1sMni(Di)A\cong\prod_{i=1}^s M_{n_i}(D_i)A≅∏i=1s​Mni​​(Di​) with division algebras DiD_iDi​; equivalently, every finite-dimensional AAA-module is semisimple.

Notes stale

Decompose the regular module AAA into simple left ideals and group them by isomorphism class to obtain two-sided ideals AiA_iAi​ with A=∏AiA=\prod A_iA=∏Ai​. Each AiA_iAi​ is simple, so the Wedderburn theorem for simple algebras applies. Conversely, a product of matrix algebras is semisimple. The nin_ini​ and DiD_iDi​ are unique up to isomorphism and permutation.
