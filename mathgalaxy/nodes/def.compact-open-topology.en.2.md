# compact-open topology　`def.compact-open-topology`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=def.compact-open-topology)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./def.compact-open-topology.md)

The **compact-open topology** on $C(X, Y)$ (that is, on $\operatorname{Hom}_{\mathbf{Top}}(X, Y)$) is the topology generated by all $W_{S,V}$.

## Notes · **draft**
Reading: $W_{S,V}$ consists of those maps "that land in $V$ on every compact piece coming in from the source". $g$ controls, via the "test piece" $S$, the space $X$ locally.

⭐ This makes $\operatorname{Hom}(X,Y)$ itself a topological space — hence one can speak of "mapping spaces", and only then of homotopy, path spaces, loop spaces. It is really useful in $\mathrm{CGWH}$ (see "function spaces are weakly Hausdorff").
