# Criterion for a Presheaf to Be Condensed　`prop.condensed-criterion`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=prop.condensed-criterion)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./prop.condensed-criterion.md)

**Proof.** It suffices to check the sheaf condition on the **generators** of the prestandard topology — a general cover is a composite of "finite disjoint union" and "surjection", and these two suffice:
- **Finite disjoint union**: the cover is $(S_{i} \hookrightarrow S)_{i \in I}$ ($I$ finite, $\coprod_{i} S_{i} \to S$ a bijection).

The sheaf condition requires $X(S) \to \prod_{i} X(S_{i}) \rightrightarrows \prod_{i,j} X(S_{i} \times_{S} S_{j})$ to be exact;
