## Notes · **draft**
The definition itself does not require $\operatorname{char}F\ne2$; when $\operatorname{char}F\ne2$ it coincides with the $-1$-eigenspace of the swap, and $\{e_i\wedge e_j:i<j\}$ is a basis of dimension $n(n-1)/2$. If $\operatorname{char}F=2$, that set is not a basis (the dimension of $\bigwedge^2V$ is $n(n+1)/2$). The exterior square is also denoted $\bigwedge^2V$.
