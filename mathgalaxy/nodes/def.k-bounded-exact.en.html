Statement

Let M∙M^{\bullet}M∙ be a complex of seminormed abelian groups, and let K∈RK \in \mathbb{R}K∈R. The complex M∙M^{\bullet}M∙ is boundedly exact at MnM^{n}Mn with bound KKK if

If M∙M^{\bullet}M∙ is boundedly exact at every MnM^{n}Mn with bound KKK, it is called KKK-bounded acyclic.

Notes

How this differs from ordinary exactness. Ordinary exactness says that every cycle is a boundary (an existence statement). In KKK-bounded exactness, the preimage s′s's′ must exist and satisfy a bound. The error is at most K∥ds∥K\|ds\|K∥ds∥ plus an arbitrary ε\varepsilonε.

Why this stronger condition is needed. In Banach and seminormed settings, knowing that a cycle is a boundary is insufficient because errors can accumulate when taking limits. The KKK-bounded exactness condition gives control over countable constructions, allowing later constructions involving geometrically decaying Cauchy sequences to converge.

What the ε\varepsilonε term does: The norm comparisons allow a margin so that ≤\le≤ still holds after taking limits. In practice, ε\varepsilonε can be arbitrarily small.
