Statement

In plain words: f=u+ivf = u + ivf=u+iv is complex differentiable if and only if it is real differentiable and its two partial derivatives satisfy a very specific pair of equations — the "two-dimensional limit condition" is completely replaced by two one-dimensional equations.

⚠ Checking the CR equations alone is not enough; one also needs fff to be real differentiable.

This also shows that "partial derivatives exist" is far weaker than "real differentiable".
