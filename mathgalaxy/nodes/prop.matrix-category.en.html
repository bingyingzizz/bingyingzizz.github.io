Statement

When kkk is commutative, Mat⁡(k)\operatorname{Mat}(k)Mat(k) (objects N\mathbb{N}N, Hom⁡(m,n)=Mn×m(k)\operatorname{Hom}(m,n) = M_{n \times m}(k)Hom(m,n)=Mn×m​(k), composition matrix multiplication) is a small category;

when kkk is a field, Mat⁡(k)≃\operatorname{Mat}(k) \simeqMat(k)≃ the category of finite-dimensional kkk-vector spaces (equivalent but not isomorphic).

Notes

Proof. Matrix multiplication is associative and has identities ✓; choosing a basis gives a functor to finite-dimensional spaces, which is fully faithful and essentially surjective ✓, but there is no isomorphism — the left side is small, the right side large ✓. ∎
