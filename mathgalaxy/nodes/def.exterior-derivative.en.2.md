# Exterior Derivative: Coordinate Formula on Euclidean Open Sets　`def.exterior-derivative`

Let $\omega = \sum_{I}a_{I}\,\mathrm{d}x^{I}$ be a $k$-form on $U$, where $\mathrm{d}x^{I} := \mathrm{d}x^{i_{1}}\wedge\cdots\wedge\mathrm{d}x^{i_{k}}$. The **exterior derivative** is defined by

$$\mathrm{d}\omega := \sum_{I}\mathrm{d}a_{I}\wedge\mathrm{d}x^{I} = \sum_{I}\sum_{j=1}^{n}\frac{\partial a_{I}}{\partial x^{j}}\,\mathrm{d}x^{j}\wedge\mathrm{d}x^{I}.$$
