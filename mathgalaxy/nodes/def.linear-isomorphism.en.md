## Statement · **draft**
A linear map $T : V \to W$ is a **linear isomorphism** iff $T$ is a **bijection**. One then writes $V \cong W$ and says $V$ and $W$ are isomorphic.

**Basic fact**: when $\dim_K V = n < \infty$, $V \cong K^{n}$ (choose a basis; $v \mapsto [v]_{\mathcal{B}}$ is the isomorphism). More generally,

$$V \cong W \iff \dim V = \dim W$$
