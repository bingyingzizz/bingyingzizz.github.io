陈述

在做什么：Jordan 标准形要求「特征多项式在域里分裂」（特征值都在域里）。有理标准形不需要这个条件 —— 只要 KKK 是域，任何矩阵都能化成它。

与 Jordan 形的分工：KKK 上特征多项式分裂时，有理标准形与 Jordan 形都是完整不变量；不分裂时只有有理标准形（在 KKK 上）可用 —— Jordan 形要到扩域上才存在。

Statement draft

What this does: the Jordan canonical form requires the characteristic polynomial to split over the field (all eigenvalues in the field). The rational canonical form does not need this — as long as KKK is a field, every matrix can be put into it.

Let KKK be a field and A∈Mn(K)A \in M_{n}(K)A∈Mn​(K). Then AAA is similar in Mn(K)M_{n}(K)Mn​(K) to a unique block-diagonal matrix

diag⁡(Cd1,Cd2,…,Cds),\operatorname{diag}\bigl(C_{d_{1}}, C_{d_{2}}, \ldots, C_{d_{s}}\bigr) ,diag(Cd1​​,Cd2​​,…,Cds​​),

where each CdC_{d}Cd​ is a companion matrix: d(x)=xm+am−1xm−1+⋯+a0d(x) = x^{m} + a_{m-1}x^{m-1} + \cdots + a_{0}d(x)=xm+am−1​xm−1+⋯+a0​ is monic,

Cd:=(0−a010−a1⋱⋱⋮10−am−21−am−1),C_{d} := \begin{pmatrix} 0 & & & & -a_{0} \\ 1 & 0 & & & -a_{1} \\ & \ddots & \ddots & & \vdots \\ & & 1 & 0 & -a_{m-2} \\ & & & 1 & -a_{m-1} \end{pmatrix} ,Cd​:=​01​0⋱​⋱1​01​−a0​−a1​⋮−am−2​−am−1​​​,

and d1∣d2∣⋯∣dsd_{1} \mid d_{2} \mid \cdots \mid d_{s}d1​∣d2​∣⋯∣ds​ (each divides the next), where each did_{i}di​ is a nonunit monic polynomial (deg⁡di≥1\deg d_{i}\ge1degdi​≥1); if there are no nonunit factors (e.g. V=0V=0V=0), we set s=0s=0s=0 and the block-diagonal matrix is empty. The did_{i}di​ are called the invariant factors.

Division of labor with the Jordan form: when the characteristic polynomial splits over KKK, both the rational canonical form and the Jordan form are complete invariants; when it does not split, only the rational canonical form (over KKK) is available — the Jordan form exists only after passing to an extension field.

Notes draft

⭐ Why "rational": historically it refers to the case K=QK = \mathbb{Q}K=Q — exactly the typical situation in which the characteristic polynomial does not split over Q\mathbb{Q}Q. The name stuck, and it now means "valid over an arbitrary field".

Sketch of proof (full proof on the edge @@imp.rational-canonical-form-proof@@): view V=KnV=K^nV=Kn as a K[x]K[x]K[x]-module (x⋅v=Avx\cdot v=Avx⋅v=Av). Cayley–Hamilton gives χA(A)=0\chi_A(A)=0χA​(A)=0, so VVV is a finitely generated torsion module. By the structure theorem for finitely generated modules over a PID (@@thm.pid-fg-module-structure@@), V≅⨁iK[x]/(di)V\cong\bigoplus_i K[x]/(d_i)V≅⨁i​K[x]/(di​) with d1∣⋯∣dsd_1\mid\cdots\mid d_sd1​∣⋯∣ds​ monic non-units. For each cyclic summand take the basis 1,x,…,xm−11,x,\ldots,x^{m-1}1,x,…,xm−1; the matrix of multiplication by xxx is exactly the companion matrix CdiC_{d_i}Cdi​​ (@@def.companion-matrix@@; for cyclic subspaces see @@def.cyclic-subspace@@). Uniqueness follows from the Smith invariant factors of xI−AxI-AxI−A (@@def.invariant-factor@@, @@thm.smith-normal-form@@).

The chain: characteristic polynomial χA\chi_AχA​ (@@def.char-poly@@) →\to→ Cayley–Hamilton (@@thm.cayley-hamilton@@) →\to→ torsion module →\to→ structure theorem →\to→ invariant factors (@@def.invariant-factor@@) →\to→ companion blocks. The characteristic polynomial is the product of the blocks d1⋯dsd_1\cdots d_sd1​⋯ds​, and the minimal polynomial is dsd_sds​; these two facts also explain the origin of "dsd_sds​ is the minimal polynomial".
