# Low-Degree Exact Sequence　`prop.low-degree-exact`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=prop.low-degree-exact)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./prop.low-degree-exact.md)

**The second line is the first few terms of the long exact sequence**: applying the right derived functor to $0 \to A \to B \to C \to 0$ (namely $R^{0} = (-)^{G}$, $R^{1} = H^{1}$) gives the long exact sequence, and this segment is exactly that by definition; the connecting map $\partial$ sends "fixed points of $C$" to "one-dimensional cohomology of $A$".
