# solidification turns hypercovers into exact sequences　`prop.solid-hypercover`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=prop.solid-hypercover)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./prop.solid-hypercover.md)

$$\cdots \longrightarrow Z[S_1]^{\blacksquare} \longrightarrow Z[S_0]^{\blacksquare} \longrightarrow Z[S]^{\blacksquare} \longrightarrow 0$$

is exact.

## Notes · **draft**
⭐ This shows that replacing "profinite sets" by "extremally disconnected sets" in the definition does not change solid groups: the two are approximated by hypercovers.

⭐ The proof uses two facts: $H^i(S,\mathbb{Z}) = 0$ (for $i > 0$, constant-sheaf cohomology on profinite sets), and `thm.csz-free` to write each $C(S_i,\mathbb{Z})$ as a free group and then dualize.
