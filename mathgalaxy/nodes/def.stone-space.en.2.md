## Notes · **draft**
Write $\pi_{0}(X) := \{\, C(x) : x \in X \,\}$ for the set of connected components, equipped with the **quotient topology**; $x \mapsto C(x)$ gives a continuous map $X \to \pi_{0}(X)$.

Two conditions that look superficially alike sit at opposite ends: **total disconnectedness** says "shattered until every component is a single point", while **extreme disconnectedness** says "the closure of an open set does not grow". The latter is stronger — an extremally disconnected $T_{1}$ space is necessarily totally disconnected.
