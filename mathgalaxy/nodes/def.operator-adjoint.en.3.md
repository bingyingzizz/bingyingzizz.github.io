# adjoint operator　`def.operator-adjoint`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=def.operator-adjoint)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./def.operator-adjoint.md)

This is the finite-dimensional inner-product version of "defining an object by a universal property".

⭐ In the complex case the conjugate in $(cT)^{\ast} = \bar c T^{\ast}$ **cannot be dropped** — it comes from the conjugate-linearity of the inner product in its second argument.
