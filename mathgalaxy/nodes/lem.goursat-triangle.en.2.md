# Goursat's Triangle Lemma　`lem.goursat-triangle`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=lem.goursat-triangle)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./lem.goursat-triangle.md)

$$\int_{\partial T} f(z)\,\mathrm{d}z = 0.$$

## Notes · **draft**
⭐ The method is **bisection plus shrinking the triangle to a point**, with no Green's theorem and no continuity of $f'$.

**Step 1 (quartering)**: join the midpoints of the three sides to split $T$ into four small triangles.

The integral over the whole boundary equals the sum of the four small boundaries (the three interior edges are each traversed twice in opposite directions and cancel), so at least one satisfies $|I| \le 4\,|I(T_1)|$.
