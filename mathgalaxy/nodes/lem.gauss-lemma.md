设 $p \in S$、$v \in T_{p}S$、$w \in T_{v}(T_{p}S)$。则

$$\langle (\mathrm{d}\exp_{p})_{v}(v),\,(\mathrm{d}\exp_{p})_{v}(w)\rangle = \langle v,w\rangle .$$
