# orthogonal projection onto a closed subspace　`thm.projection-subspace`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=thm.projection-subspace)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./thm.projection-subspace.md)

⚠️ The word "closed" in "$E$ is closed" cannot be dropped: for $E = $ the finitely supported sequences are dense in $\ell^2$ but not closed; the distance is zero but not attained, so **no** nearest point in $E$ exists, and the orthogonal decomposition fails as well.

⭐ This is one of the biggest differences between Hilbert spaces and general Banach spaces: in a general Banach space a closed subspace need not be complemented ($c_0 \subset \ell^\infty$ is not), so the word "projection" cannot be used loosely in Banach spaces.
