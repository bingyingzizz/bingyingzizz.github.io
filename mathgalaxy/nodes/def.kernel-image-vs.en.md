## Statement · **draft**
Let $T : V \to W$ be linear. Define

$$\ker T := \{\, v \in V : T(v) = 0 \,\} \subseteq V, \qquad \operatorname{im} T := \{\, T(v) : v \in V \,\} \subseteq W$$

called the **kernel** and the **image** of $T$. Both are subspaces, and

$T$ is **injective** iff

$$\ker T = \{0\}$$

$T$ is **surjective** iff $\operatorname{im} T = W$.
