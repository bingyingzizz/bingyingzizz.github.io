Statement

Let C\mathcal{C}C be a site. An abelian sheaf MMM on it is acyclic   ⟺  Hˇn(M)=0\iff \check{H}^{n}(M) = 0⟺Hˇn(M)=0 for all n≠0n \ne 0n=0.

Notes

⭐ How to read it: Čech cohomology completely detects acyclicity — although in general it only "approximates" true cohomology, the question "does it vanish" agrees exactly with true cohomology.
