## Notes · **draft**
**Proof.** $S$ is compact and $d$ continuous, so $s \mapsto d(f(s), g(s))$ is continuous and hence **attains its supremum** ✓; the formula is finite-valued and defines a (semi)metric; it induces exactly the compact-open topology (equicontinuity/uniform convergence is what the compact-open topology looks like on compact spaces) ✓.

Completeness: given a Cauchy sequence, take the pointwise limit $f$ by uniform convergence; uniform convergence ensures $f$ is continuous ✓, and it converges in the same metric ✓.

∎
