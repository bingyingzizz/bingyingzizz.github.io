## English abstract · reviewed by bingyingzizz (2026-10-16) · Chinese is authoritative（续）
Sections of measurable sets are measurable: if $E \in \mathcal{M} \otimes \mathcal{N}$ then $E_x \in \mathcal{N}$ for every $x$ and $E^y \in \mathcal{M}$ for every $y$; likewise the sections of an $\mathcal{M} \otimes \mathcal{N}$-measurable $f$ are measurable, because $f_x^{-1}(B) = (f^{-1}(B))_x$ and dually. The proof is the standard one: the class of sets all of whose sections are measurable contains every rectangle and is a $\sigma$-algebra.
