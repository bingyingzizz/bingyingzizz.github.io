# pre-abelian　`prop.lc-ab-preabelian`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=prop.lc-ab-preabelian)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./prop.lc-ab-preabelian.md)

**Why having kernels and cokernels is insufficient.** In an abelian category, every monomorphism is the kernel of some morphism. Equivalently, it is isomorphic to the kernel of its cokernel, or **strict**.

$\mathbb{R}^{\mathrm{disc}} \to \mathbb{R}$ is a monomorphism (it is isomorphic to its image), but its cokernel is $0$. The entire image is quotiented out in the cokernel.

The kernel of the cokernel is therefore different from the original object, so strictness fails.
