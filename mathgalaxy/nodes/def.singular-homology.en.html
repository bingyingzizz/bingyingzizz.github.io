Statement

Let XXX be an arbitrary topological space. A singular ppp-simplex is an arbitrary continuous map

σ:Δp⟶X,\sigma : \Delta^{p} \longrightarrow X,σ:Δp⟶X,

it need not be injective, need not be an embedding, may self-intersect, and may collapse all of Δp\Delta^{p}Δp to a point. The free abelian group on all singular ppp-simplices is denoted Sp(X)S_{p}(X)Sp​(X) (the singular chain group). The boundary operator is given by the face maps:

∂pσ:=∑i=0p(−1)i σ∘di,\partial_{p}\sigma := \sum_{i=0}^{p} (-1)^{i}\,\sigma \circ d_{i},∂p​σ:=i=0∑p​(−1)iσ∘di​,

and again satisfies ∂2=0\partial^{2} = 0∂2=0 (because djdi=didj−1d_{j}d_{i} = d_{i}d_{j-1}dj​di​=di​dj−1​ is literally the same identity as in the simplicial case). Hence

Hp(X):=ker⁡∂p / im⁡∂p+1H_{p}(X) := \ker\partial_{p}\, \big/\, \operatorname{im}\partial_{p+1}Hp​(X):=ker∂p​/im∂p+1​

is called the ppp-th singular homology group of XXX.

Notes

Relation to simplicial homology: every ppp-simplex [v0,…,vp][v_{0}, \ldots, v_{p}][v0​,…,vp​] of KKK can be viewed as a singular ppp-simplex (map the vertices viv_{i}vi​ of Δp\Delta^{p}Δp linearly to the viv_{i}vi​ of KKK), which gives a subcomplex C∗(K)⊆S∗(∣K∣)C_{*}(K) \subseteq S_{*}(|K|)C∗​(K)⊆S∗​(∣K∣). 单纯逼近 says that this inclusion induces an isomorphism Hp(K)≅Hp(∣K∣)H_{p}(K) \cong H_{p}(|K|)Hp​(K)≅Hp​(∣K∣).

In other words: singular homology is "homology without choosing a triangulation", and the two agree on triangulable spaces.

⭐ Why allow such a flood of singular simplices: this way every continuous map can act, so no triangulation and no niceness hypothesis is needed, and the theory is defined for every topological space (indeed every pair of spaces).

The cost is that Sp(X)S_{p}(X)Sp​(X) is usually enormous (countability requires extra hypotheses), so direct computation is almost impossible — practical computations rely on long exact sequences (对的长正合列), Mayer–Vietoris (Mayer–Vietoris（同调）), and "singular === simplicial" (单纯逼近).

⚠ This is not the same as 奇异链 (cubical chains on a manifold): there the basis consists of maps c:[0,1]k→Mc : [0, 1]^{k} \to Mc:[0,1]k→M (cubes), here of maps σ:Δp→X\sigma : \Delta^{p} \to Xσ:Δp→X (simplices). The two models give isomorphic homology groups, but the boundary formulas differ, and the two ∂\partial∂'s must not be mixed.

💡 Intuition: a singular ppp-simplex is "a (possibly collapsed) ppp-dimensional triangle drawn on XXX"; a singular chain is "a signed stack of such triangles".
