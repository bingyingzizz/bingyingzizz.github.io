Statement

There is a faithful functor that preserves all limits

AbTop⟶AbCond,M⟼M‾.\mathbf{AbTop} \longrightarrow \mathrm{AbCond}, \qquad M \longmapsto \underline{M}.AbTop⟶AbCond,M⟼M​.

and is fully faithful on compactly generated abelian groups.

Notes

Construction: A topological abelian group MMM gives the condensed abelian group

M‾:S⟼C(S,M),\underline{M} : S \longmapsto C(S, M),M​:S⟼C(S,M),

namely the set of continuous maps from a compact Hausdorff space SSS to MMM, with pointwise addition. Since MMM is a topological abelian group, C(S,M)C(S, M)C(S,M) is an abelian group under pointwise addition. This defines an abelian-group-valued presheaf. The sheaf condition is gluing continuous maps, which holds.

Preservation of all limits follows from the theorem that Top→Cond\mathbf{Top} \to \mathrm{Cond}Top→Cond preserves finite products. Limits of condensed objects are computed pointwise, and computing limits of continuous maps in Top\mathbf{Top}Top agrees with computing them pointwise.

⭐ Application: Transfer a topological problem to condensed abelian groups, compute there using homological algebra (long exact sequences and derived functors), and transfer the result back. Some information is lost. The functor is faithful and need not be full, so isomorphism as condensed objects is weaker than topological isomorphism.
