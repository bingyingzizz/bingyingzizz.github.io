## Notes · **draft**
The dimension of $\operatorname{Sym}^2V$ is $n(n+1)/2$.
It is the $+1$ eigenspace of the swap, while $\operatorname{Alt}^2V$ is the $-1$ eigenspace.
The character formula is in the character-operations node.
When $\operatorname{char}F=2$ the symmetric and exterior powers are no longer complementary; this cluster only treats $\operatorname{char}F\ne2$.
