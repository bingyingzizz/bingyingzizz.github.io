## Notes · **stale**
The definition does not require $\operatorname{char}F\ne2$; the subspace generated by $v\otimes w-w\otimes v$ need not be complemented. When $\operatorname{char}F\ne2$, $\operatorname{Sym}^2V$ coincides with the $+1$-eigenspace of the swap and has dimension $n(n+1)/2$. For group representations the action of $g$ is well defined; for the character formula see prop.character-operations.
