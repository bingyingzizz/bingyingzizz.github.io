def.fixed-field

Statement

Let L/KL/KL/K be a field extension and H≤Aut⁡(L/K)H \le \operatorname{Aut}(L/K)H≤Aut(L/K) a subgroup of the group of KKK-automorphisms. The set

LH:={ x∈L:σ(x)=x for all σ∈H }L^{H} := \{\, x \in L : \sigma(x) = x \ \text{for all } \sigma \in H \,\}LH:={x∈L:σ(x)=x for all σ∈H}

is the fixed field of HHH. It is a subfield of LLL, and K⊆LH⊆LK \subseteq L^{H} \subseteq LK⊆LH⊆L.

In the Galois correspondence for a finite Galois extension, H↦LHH \mapsto L^{H}H↦LH and the intermediate field M↦Gal⁡(L/M)M \mapsto \operatorname{Gal}(L/M)M↦Gal(L/M) are mutually inverse (see fundamental theorem of Galois theory).

Notes

⭐ Reverse inclusion: H1⊆H2  ⟹  LH2⊆LH1H_1 \subseteq H_2 \implies L^{H_2} \subseteq L^{H_1}H1​⊆H2​⟹LH2​⊆LH1​ — the larger the group, the fewer objects it fixes.

⭐ Examples: the conjugation automorphism of C/R\mathbb{C}/\mathbb{R}C/R generates a group of order 222, whose fixed field is R\mathbb{R}R; for the finite field Fpn/Fp\mathbb{F}_{p^n}/\mathbb{F}_pFpn​/Fp​, the fixed field of the group generated by Frobenius is Fp\mathbb{F}_pFp​.

⚠ A fixed field is in general not equal to some elementary construction of "the field fixing every element": it looks at all elements of HHH together, not at fixed points element by element.
