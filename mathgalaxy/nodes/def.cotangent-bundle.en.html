Statement

What it does: the tangent space carries "where can I go", the cotangent space carries "how do I measure". Stacking the dual spaces over all points gives the home of 1-forms — a differential form is not a formula out of nowhere, it is a section of this bundle.

Let MMM be a smooth nnn-manifold. The cotangent space at ppp is the dual space

Tp∗M:=(TpM)∗=Hom⁡R(TpM,R),T_{p}^{\ast}M := (T_{p}M)^{\ast} = \operatorname{Hom}_{\mathbb{R}}\bigl(T_{p}M,\mathbb{R}\bigr),Tp∗​M:=(Tp​M)∗=HomR​(Tp​M,R),

whose elements are called covectors, or 1-forms at ppp (also Pfaffian forms). The cotangent bundle stacks them over the base points:

T∗M:=⨆p∈MTp∗M,T^{\ast}M := \bigsqcup_{p\in M}T_{p}^{\ast}M,T∗M:=p∈M⨆​Tp∗​M,

with projection π:T∗M→M\pi : T^{\ast}M\to Mπ:T∗M→M sending α∈Tp∗M\alpha\in T_{p}^{\ast}Mα∈Tp∗​M to π(α):=p\pi(\alpha) := pπ(α):=p.
