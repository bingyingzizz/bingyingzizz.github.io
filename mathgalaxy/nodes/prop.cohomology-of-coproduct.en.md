## Statement · **draft**
Let $\mathcal{T}$ be a topos, $(X_{i})_{i \in I}$ a family of objects, and $M$ an abelian group in $\mathcal{T}$. Then for all $n \ge 0$

$$H^{n}\Bigl(\coprod_{i \in I} X_{i},\ M\Bigr) \;\cong\; \prod_{i \in I} H^{n}(X_{i},\ M).$$
