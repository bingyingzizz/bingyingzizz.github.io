# Properties of Sheaf Categories over a Site　`prop.site-properties`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=prop.site-properties)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./prop.site-properties.md)

**Hypotheses used in each direction** (these are theorems for set-valued sheaf categories, not five equivalent definitions):
- The five properties of the sheaf category $\implies$: one must use a small site, or a site small enough in a fixed universe, to guarantee the relevant sheafifications and small colimits exist.
- Presheaf limits are computed pointwise for free; sheaf colimits must first take the presheaf colimit and then sheafify, at the cost that sheafification is a left exact left adjoint, so one cannot say sheaf colimits are all computed pointwise.
- Filtered colimits are exact: use that filtered colimits in se
