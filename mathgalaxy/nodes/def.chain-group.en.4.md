# chain group　`def.chain-group`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=def.chain-group)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./def.chain-group.md)

**Other coefficient rings support the same theory**: coefficients in $\mathbb{Q}$ or $\mathbb{R}$ give Betti numbers (torsion is invisible), while $\mathbb{Z}/2$ makes all signs $(-1)^{i}$ disappear (no orientation worries). This region fixes coefficients in $\mathbb{Z}$.

💡 Intuition: $C_{p}(K)$ is the "integer ledger of $p$-dimensional pieces". Homology asks: **which of these entries are genuine holes and which are merely shadows of something higher-dimensional** (see `def.simplicial-homology`).
