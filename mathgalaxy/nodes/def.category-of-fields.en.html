Statement

The category of fields Field\mathbf{Field}Field: objects are fields and morphisms are field homomorphisms (automatically injective). It has no terminal object (the zero ring is not a field), no initial object (no field admits homomorphisms to both Q\mathbb QQ and Fp\mathbb F_pFp​), and no binary products (the product of Q\mathbb QQ and Fp\mathbb F_pFp​ does not exist). Hence Field\mathbf{Field}Field is neither complete nor cocomplete.

Notes

Equalizers exist: the equalizer of two field homomorphisms is a fixed subfield; but this does not supply a terminal object or products.

Use: it is the standard counterexample to “looks like an algebraic category but lacks even finite products”, a reminder not to assume that “algebraic structure + homomorphisms” is automatically complete.

Relation to field: fields themselves are defined in field; this entry only adds limit and colimit information at the level of categories.
