## Statement · **draft**
Let $K$ be a complete ordered field (e.g. $\mathbb{R}$).

**(i)** For every $\varepsilon > 0$ there exists $n \ge 1$ with $1/n < \varepsilon$; equivalently, the infimum of those $1/n$ is zero:

$$\frac{1}{n} < \varepsilon; \qquad \inf\left\{ \frac{1}{n} : n \ge 1 \right\} = 0.$$
