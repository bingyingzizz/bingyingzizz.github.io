# Connection Form　`def.connection-form`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=def.connection-form)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./def.connection-form.md)

**Relation to the Christoffel symbols**: expanding it in the **non-orthonormal** frame $\{r_{u}, r_{v}, n\}$, the coefficients are a mixture of $\Gamma^{k}_{\ ij}$ and $h_{ij}$ (compare `def.second-fundamental-form` and `def.christoffel-symbol`);

only in an orthonormal frame does it become a clean skew-symmetric matrix.
