Statement

CondAb\mathrm{CondAb}CondAb is a Grothendieck category satisfying AB6 and AB4*.

Notes

Consequently, CondAb\mathrm{CondAb}CondAb is an abelian category and (1) has a generator, (2) has all limits and colimits, (3) has exact products and coproducts, and (4) has exact filtered colimits that commute with products.

The Grothendieck category assertion holds for every topos (see “abelian groups in a topos form a Grothendieck category”). The additional properties here are AB6 and AB4*, which need not both hold for abelian groups in a general topos.

As a result, the homological algebra of CondAb\mathrm{CondAb}CondAb works as it does in Ab\mathbf{Ab}Ab, including derived functors, long exact sequences, and derived categories.

Proof (reduce each assertion to Ab\mathbf{Ab}Ab). The key tool is the preceding result: each Γ(F,−)\Gamma(F,-)Γ(F,−) (with FFF free) preserves all limits and colimits. Thus any property expressed using finite limits and colimits can be checked objectwise, where it reduces to a familiar fact in Ab\mathbf{Ab}Ab.
