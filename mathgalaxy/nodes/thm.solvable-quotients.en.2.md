# solvability passes to subgroups and quotients　`thm.solvable-quotients`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=thm.solvable-quotients)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./thm.solvable-quotients.md)

((a) and (b) say that solvability descends; (c) says that solvability ascends along normal extensions.)

## Notes · **draft**
⭐ (c) is the most useful of the three: to show that $G$ is solvable it suffices to find a **normal subgroup** with $G$ sandwiched in between and both ends solvable — the standard way of breaking a big group into small pieces.

⚠ The converses of (a) and (b) both **fail**, with tiny counterexamples:
- a solvable subgroup **does not** imply that the group is solvable: $\mathbb{Z}/2 \le A_5$ is solvable, $A_5$ is not;
