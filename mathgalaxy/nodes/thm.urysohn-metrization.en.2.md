## Abstract · **reviewed**
A regular $T_{1}$ space with a countable base is metrisable. The proof embeds $X$ into a countable product of copies of $[0,1]$: second countability makes $X$ Lindelöf, regularity together with Lindelöf makes it normal, and Urysohn functions attached to pairs $\operatorname{cl}B_{m}\subseteq B_{n}$ of basic open sets separate points from closed sets. The embedding is a homeomorphism onto its image, and the metric of the product pulls back to a metric inducing the topology of $X$.
