## Notes · **draft**
This translates Gleason's theorem into one sentence of "concrete topology": projectivity = retract. It is completely parallel to "projective module = direct summand of a free module" — both say "projective = cut a piece off a free object".

**Proof (combining Gleason with "every object of $\mathbf{CHaus}$ is a quotient of a free one").**
- **$(\implies)$**: let $A$ be Stonean.

Take a free compact Hausdorff space $F$ and a surjection $p : F\twoheadrightarrow A$ ("every object of $\mathbf{CHaus}$ is a quotient of a free one").
