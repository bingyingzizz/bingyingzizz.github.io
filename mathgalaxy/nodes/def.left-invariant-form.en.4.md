## Notes · **draft**
**Why the evaluation is an isomorphism**: setting $h = e$ in left-invariance gives $\omega_{g}(\mathrm{d}(L_{g})_{e}w) = \omega_{e}(w)$, hence $\omega_{g} = \mathrm{d}(L_{g^{-1}})^{\ast}\omega_{e}$, i.e. $\omega$ is determined by $\omega_{e}$ (injectivity).
