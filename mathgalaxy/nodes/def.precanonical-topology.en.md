## Statement · **draft**
The **precanonical pretopology** on a pretopos $\mathcal{C}$ consists of all **finite** families $(X_{i} \to X)_{i \in I}$ such that

$$\coprod_{i \in I} X_{i} \longrightarrow X$$

is an epimorphism. The Grothendieck topology it generates is called the **precanonical topology**, and $\mathcal{C}$ equipped with it is a site.
