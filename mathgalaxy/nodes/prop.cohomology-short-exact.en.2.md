# Short Exact Sequences Computing Cohomology　`prop.cohomology-short-exact`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=prop.cohomology-short-exact)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./prop.cohomology-short-exact.md)

Then for every $k$ there is a pointwise exact sequence

$$0 \longrightarrow H^{k} \longrightarrow \operatorname{coker}\bigl(d^{k-1}\bigr) \longrightarrow Z^{k+1} \longrightarrow H^{k+1} \longrightarrow 0.$$

## Notes · **draft**
⭐ This sequence is often written as two short exact sequences spliced together:
$$0 \to H^{k} \to K^{k}/B^{k} \to B^{k+1} \to 0, \qquad 0 \to B^{k+1} \to Z^{k+1} \to H^{k+1} \to 0.$$
these two are exactly what is used later when constructing spectral sequences.
