Statement

A division algebra over FFF is a unital FFF-algebra DDD (not assumed commutative) in which every nonzero element is invertible; equivalently, DDD is a division ring that is an FFF-algebra.

Notes

The field FFF itself is a division algebra; the quaternions are a noncommutative example over R\mathbb{R}R. For an irreducible representation VVV, Schur's lemma shows that End⁡A(V)\operatorname{End}_A(V)EndA​(V) is a division algebra. The division algebra DDD in Wedderburn's theorem is End⁡A(V)\operatorname{End}_A(V)EndA​(V) up to passing to the opposite algebra. Over an algebraically closed field with dim⁡V<∞\dim V<\inftydimV<∞, one has D=FD=FD=F.
