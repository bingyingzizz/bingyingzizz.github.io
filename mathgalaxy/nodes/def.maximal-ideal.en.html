def.maximal-ideal

Machine-drafted and pending author review. The Chinese text is authoritative.

Statement

Let AAA be a commutative ring with identity and M⊂AM \subset AM⊂A a proper ideal. Then MMM is a maximal ideal if there is no ideal JJJ with

M⊂J⊂A.M \subset J \subset A.M⊂J⊂A.

Equivalently, the quotient ring A/MA/MA/M is a field (see quotient ring). Every proper ideal is contained in some maximal ideal (by Zorn's lemma).

Notes

⭐ Maximal   ⟹  \implies⟹ prime: a field is a domain, so A/MA/MA/M is a domain. The converse fails: in Z[x]\mathbb{Z}[x]Z[x], (x)(x)(x) is prime but not maximal ((x)⊂(2,x)(x) \subset (2, x)(x)⊂(2,x)).

⭐ In a PID, every nonzero prime ideal is maximal: in K[x]K[x]K[x], (f)(f)(f) is maximal   ⟺  \iff⟺ fff is irreducible.

⭐ Examples: in Z\mathbb{Z}Z, (p)(p)(p) is maximal; in the polynomial ring in n variables, the ideal generated by the variables is maximal.
