# mean value property of harmonic functions　`thm.harmonic-mean-value`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=thm.harmonic-mean-value)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./thm.harmonic-mean-value.md)

⭐ The converse also holds: if $u$ is continuous and has the circle mean property, then $u$ is harmonic. So the "mean property" can serve as another definition of harmonic function — many books do exactly this, to avoid second-order differentiability.

⚠ The mean must be taken over the **whole circle**, not just the few points straight above; nor over an arbitrary closed curve — that is precisely what the Poisson formula answers.

⚠ $u$ need only be harmonic in the **interior** of $B(a, r)$; it is not required to be harmonic on the closed disc.
