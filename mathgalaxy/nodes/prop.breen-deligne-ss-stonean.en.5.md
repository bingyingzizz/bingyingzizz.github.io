# The Breen–Deligne Spectral Sequence on Stonean Spaces　`prop.breen-deligne-ss-stonean`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=prop.breen-deligne-ss-stonean)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./prop.breen-deligne-ss-stonean.md)

**Proof.** Take a Breen–Deligne decomposition of $M$, $F(M)^{\bullet} \twoheadrightarrow M$, base-change along the Stonean space $S$ (tensor with $\mathbb{Z}[S]$), and apply to the functor $\operatorname{Hom}_{\mathbb{Z}}(-, N)$ the standard spectral sequence of "resolve first, then evaluate on the test space":

$$E^{p,q}_{1} = \operatorname{Ext}^{q}_{\mathbb{Z}}\bigl(F(M)^{p}\cdot S,\ N\bigr) \;\Longrightarrow\; \operatorname{Ext}^{p+q}_{\mathbb{Z}}\bigl(F(M)^{\bullet}\cdot S,\ N\bigr).$$
