def.cartesian-closed-category · 范畴论 · 笛卡尔闭 · 指数对象

又称笛卡尔闭范畴·CC category

陈述

范畴 C\mathcal CC 称为笛卡尔闭，若它有有限积（终对象与二元积），且对每个 XXX，函子 (−)×X:C→C(-)\times X:\mathcal C\to\mathcal C(−)×X:C→C 有右伴随 (−)X(-)^X(−)X。等价地，存在求值态射 ev:YX×X→Y\mathrm{ev}:Y^X\times X\to Yev:YX×X→Y 与自然双射 hom⁡C(Z×X,Y)≅hom⁡C(Z,YX)\hom_{\mathcal C}(Z\times X,Y)\cong\hom_{\mathcal C}(Z,Y^X)homC​(Z×X,Y)≅homC​(Z,YX)，称为 curry 化。

说明

例：Set\mathbf{Set}Set 中 YX=hom⁡(X,Y)Y^X=\hom(X,Y)YX=hom(X,Y)；G-SetG\text{-}\mathbf{Set}G-Set 中 YXY^XYX 取共轭作用 (g⋅f)(x)=gf(g−1x)(g\cdot f)(x)=g f(g^{-1}x)(g⋅f)(x)=gf(g−1x)。

反例：Top\mathbf{Top}Top 一般不是笛卡尔闭（指数拓扑使求值连续的条件太强）；紧生成弱 Hausdorff 空间 CGWH\mathbf{CGWH}CGWH 才是，见 紧生成空间是笛卡尔闭的。

与充实范畴的 internal hom 分开：内 Hom 是充实范畴里的 hom 对象；本条的 YXY^XYX 是普通范畴中的指数对象。

Statement draft

A category C\mathcal CC is called cartesian closed if it has finite products (a terminal object and binary products) and, for every XXX, the functor (−)×X:C→C(-)\times X:\mathcal C\to\mathcal C(−)×X:C→C has a right adjoint (−)X(-)^X(−)X. Equivalently, there is an evaluation morphism ev:YX×X→Y\mathrm{ev}:Y^X\times X\to Yev:YX×X→Y and a natural bijection hom⁡C(Z×X,Y)≅hom⁡C(Z,YX)\hom_{\mathcal C}(Z\times X,Y)\cong\hom_{\mathcal C}(Z,Y^X)homC​(Z×X,Y)≅homC​(Z,YX), called currying.

Notes draft

Examples: in Set\mathbf{Set}Set, YX=hom⁡(X,Y)Y^X=\hom(X,Y)YX=hom(X,Y); in G-SetG\text{-}\mathbf{Set}G-Set, YXY^XYX with the conjugation action (g⋅f)(x)=gf(g−1x)(g\cdot f)(x)=g f(g^{-1}x)(g⋅f)(x)=gf(g−1x).

Counterexample: Top\mathbf{Top}Top is not cartesian closed in general (the compact-open topology makes continuity of evaluation too strong); the category CGWH\mathbf{CGWH}CGWH of compactly generated weak Hausdorff spaces is, see @@prop.cg-cartesian-closed@@.

Kept separate from internal hom in enriched categories: @@def.internal-hom@@ is the hom object in an enriched category; YXY^XYX here is an exponential object in an ordinary category.
