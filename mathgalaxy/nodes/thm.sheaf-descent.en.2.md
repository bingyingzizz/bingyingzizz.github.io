# sheaf condition　`thm.sheaf-descent`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=thm.sheaf-descent)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./thm.sheaf-descent.md)

$$\alpha\bigl((s_{i})_{i}\bigr) = \bigl(s_{i}|_{X_{i} \times_{X} X_{j}}\bigr)_{i,j}, \qquad \beta\bigl((s_{i})_{i}\bigr) = \bigl(s_{j}|_{X_{i} \times_{X} X_{j}}\bigr)_{i,j}$$

## Notes · **draft**
These are the two textbook conditions: **monic** $F(X) \hookrightarrow \prod_{i} F(X_{i})$ is the **separatedness axiom**, **equal to the kernel** is the **gluing axiom** (compatible families are exactly the image of global sections).
