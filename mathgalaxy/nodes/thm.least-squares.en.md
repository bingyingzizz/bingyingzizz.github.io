## Statement · **draft**
**What this does**: The system $Ax = b$ may have no solution ($b$ lies outside the column space). Least squares instead finds an $x$ for which $Ax$ is closest to $b$.

Suppose $A \in M_{m\times n}(\mathbb{R})$ has full column rank and let $b \in \mathbb{R}^{m}$. The **least-squares solution** is the minimizer of

$$\lVert Ax - b\rVert_{2}$$
