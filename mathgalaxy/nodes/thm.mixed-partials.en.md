## Statement · **draft**
Let $U \subseteq \mathbb{R}^{n}$ be open and let $f : U \to \mathbb{R}$ have second partial derivatives $\partial_i \partial_j f$ and $\partial_j \partial_i f$ both defined in a neighbourhood of $a$ and continuous at $a$. Then

$$\partial_i \partial_j f(a) = \partial_j \partial_i f(a)$$
