## Statement · **draft**
Let $\mathcal{C}$ be an abelian category with coproducts and $G \in \mathcal{C}$. Then $G$ **is a generator** $\iff$ for every $M \in \mathcal{C}$ there exist an index set $I$ and an epimorphism

$$G^{(I)} \twoheadrightarrow M.$$
