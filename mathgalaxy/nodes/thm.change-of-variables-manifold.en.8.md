# Change of Variables on Manifolds　`thm.change-of-variables-manifold`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=thm.change-of-variables-manifold)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./thm.change-of-variables-manifold.md)

**⚠️ A diffeomorphism cannot be weakened to a smooth surjection**: that would require degrees and multiplicities ($\int_{M}f^{\ast}\omega = \deg(f)\int_{N}\omega$), and $\varepsilon$ would become an integer rather than $\pm1$. Only the diffeomorphism case is stated here.
