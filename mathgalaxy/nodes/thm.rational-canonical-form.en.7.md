# Rational Canonical Form　`thm.rational-canonical-form`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=thm.rational-canonical-form)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./thm.rational-canonical-form.md)

**The chain**: characteristic polynomial $\chi_A$ (@@def.char-poly@@) $\to$ Cayley–Hamilton (@@thm.cayley-hamilton@@) $\to$ torsion module $\to$ structure theorem $\to$ invariant factors (@@def.invariant-factor@@) $\to$ companion blocks. The characteristic polynomial is the product of the blocks $d_1\cdots d_s$, and the minimal polynomial is $d_s$; these two facts also explain the origin of "$d_s$ is the minimal polynomial".
