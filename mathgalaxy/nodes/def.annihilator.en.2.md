## Notes · **draft**
⭐ The annihilator consists of “the functionals that vanish on $U$”, so $\dim U^0 = \dim V - \dim U$ is the dual form of the rank–nullity theorem: dualizing $\pi : V \to V/U$ gives $U^{0} \cong (V/U)^{\ast}$.

⭐ $(U^{0})^{0} = U$ says that **subspaces and annihilators are dual to each other** (in finite dimension a two-way dictionary: a larger $U$ corresponds to a smaller $U^0$, order-reversing).

⚠ In infinite dimension $(U^0)^0 \supseteq U$ can be strictly larger, and the dictionary breaks.
