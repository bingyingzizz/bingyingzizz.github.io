# Injective Module　`def.injective-module`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=def.injective-module)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./def.injective-module.md)

**Examples**: over a field every module is injective (vector spaces are free, free implies projective, and over a field projective and injective coincide); $\mathbb{Z}$ is **not** injective: the inclusion $\mathbb{Z} \rightarrowtail \mathbb{Q}$ has no retraction, because $\operatorname{Hom}_{\mathbb{Z}}(\mathbb{Q}, \mathbb{Z}) = 0$.

📌 Reduction route for the criterion: it suffices to test "$A$ is an ideal of $B$" (`thm.baer-criterion`); for $\mathbb{Z}$-modules this further reduces to "divisible" (`thm.pid-injective-divisible`).
