⭐ **Hub**: from it follow infinite differentiability, Cauchy's inequalities, Liouville, the maximum modulus principle, Taylor/Laurent expansions, and the residue theorem — almost every result in this galaxy grows from here.

⚠ The integrand $f(\zeta)/(\zeta - z)$ is **not** holomorphic at $\zeta = z$, which is exactly why "punching a hole" is not a trick but a necessity.
