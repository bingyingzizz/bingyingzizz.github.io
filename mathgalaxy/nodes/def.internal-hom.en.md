## Statement · **draft**
Let $\mathcal{T}$ be a topos, $X \in \mathcal{T}$, $M \in \mathcal{T}(\mathbf{Ab})$. Then

$$\operatorname{Hom}(X, M) : Y \longmapsto \operatorname{Hom}(X \times Y,\ M) \;\cong\; \operatorname{Hom}_{\mathbb{Z}}\bigl(\mathbb{Z}\cdot(X \times Y),\ M\bigr)$$

is an abelian sheaf on $\mathcal{T}$, called the **internal Hom** of $M$ at $X$.
