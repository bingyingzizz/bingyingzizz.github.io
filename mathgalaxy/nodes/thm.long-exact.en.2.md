## Notes · **draft**
**What it says**: a short exact sequence of complexes $0 \to K \to L \to M \to 0$ yields a **long exact sequence** in cohomology, and the **connecting homomorphism** $\delta : H^{n}(M) \to H^{n+1}(K)$ in the middle raises the degree by one.

⭐ How $\delta$ is built: take a representative of $m \in H^{n}(M)$, lift it along the surjection $L^{n} \to M^{n}$, then apply $d_{L}$ and land in $K^{n+1}$ — one must show the result is independent of the choice of lift (two lifts differ by a coboundary), and this step is exactly **diagram chasing**.
