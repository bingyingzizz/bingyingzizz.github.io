# simplicial approximation　`thm.simplicial-approximation`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=thm.simplicial-approximation)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./thm.simplicial-approximation.md)

- The barycentric subdivision chain maps $\operatorname{sd}^{m} : C_{*}(K) \to C_{*}(K^{(m)})$ and $\iota_{m}$ satisfy $\iota_{m}\,\operatorname{sd}^{m} = \iota$ (subdivision leaves the geometric realization unchanged and subdivides the chains), and $\operatorname{sd}^{m}$ induces a homology isomorphism (for the reverse chain homotopy, each subdivided simplex lies in an original simplex, so the cone construction applies).
