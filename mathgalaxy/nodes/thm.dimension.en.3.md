# dimension is well defined　`thm.dimension`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=thm.dimension)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./thm.dimension.md)

⭐ Basic properties of dimension: $\dim(U + W) + \dim(U \cap W) = \dim U + \dim W$;

when $V$ is **finite-dimensional**, $\dim(V/U) = \dim V - \dim U$; in infinite dimension subtraction is meaningless (cardinals do not subtract), and only cardinal addition $\dim V = \dim U + \dim(V/U)$ remains.
