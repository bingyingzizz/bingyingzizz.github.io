# Goursat's Triangle Lemma　`lem.goursat-triangle`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=lem.goursat-triangle)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./lem.goursat-triangle.md)

Iterating gives $T \supset T^{(1)} \supset T^{(2)} \supset \cdots$, the perimeter of the $n$-th is $L/2^n$, the nested closed sets theorem gives a common point $z^*$, and $|I| \le 4^n |I(T^{(n)})|$.

**Step 2 (replace $f$ by its linear part)**: near $z^*$, use the definition of complex differentiability: $f(z) = f(z^*) + f'(z^*)(z - z^*) + \eta(z)\,(z - z^*)$, where $\eta(z) \to 0$ ($z \to z^*$).

The first two terms are polynomials, and their integral over any closed triangle is $0$ (a direct computation).
