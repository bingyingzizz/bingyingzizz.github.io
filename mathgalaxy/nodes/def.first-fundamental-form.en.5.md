# First Fundamental Form　`def.first-fundamental-form`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=def.first-fundamental-form)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./def.first-fundamental-form.md)

along the surface $\omega^{3} = 0$, only two terms remain.

**Matrix**: $(g_{ij}) = \begin{pmatrix} E & F \\ F & G \end{pmatrix}$, $\det g = EG-F^{2} > 0$. Everything later about "eigenvalues of $h$ relative to $g$" uses this matrix.

⚠️ Changing the parametrization changes the expressions for $E,F,G$, but $I$ as a tensor is unchanged. An orthogonal parametrization ($F = 0$) can halve the length of the formulas — the string of $\Gamma$ symbols is written out only in that case.
