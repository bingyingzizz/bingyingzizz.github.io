# Schwarz reflection principle　`thm.schwarz-reflection`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=thm.schwarz-reflection)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./thm.schwarz-reflection.md)

The lower half-plane $F$ is holomorphic (conjugate, then conjugate again — the two anti-holomorphic steps cancel). It remains to show that $F$ is holomorphic across the real axis: use **Morera** (`thm.morera`) — take a closed triangle crossing the axis, use $f$ on the upper part and the definition on the lower part; the integral along the real-axis piece tends to zero by continuity ($f$ is real-valued on the axis, so the two sides cancel), hence the integral over the whole boundary is zero. ∎
