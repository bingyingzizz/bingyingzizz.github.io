## Statement · **draft**
Let $(V,\theta)$ and $(W,\varphi)$ be representations of $A$. An $A$-linear map (an intertwiner) is an $F$-linear map $h:V\to W$ such that $h(\theta(a)v)=\varphi(a)h(v)$ for all $a\in A$ and $v\in V$. These morphisms form the category $\mathrm{Rep}_A$.
