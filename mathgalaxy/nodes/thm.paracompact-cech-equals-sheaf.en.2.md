# Čech Cohomology Equals Sheaf Cohomology on Paracompact Spaces　`thm.paracompact-cech-equals-sheaf`

## Statement · **draft**
Let $X$ be a **paracompact Hausdorff** space (see `def.paracompact` in the topology cluster for the definition and equivalent characterizations of paracompactness). Then for every sheaf of abelian groups $\mathcal{M}$ and every $n \ge 0$,

$$\check{H}^{n}(X, \mathcal{M}) \;\cong\; H^{n}(X, \mathcal{M}) .$$
