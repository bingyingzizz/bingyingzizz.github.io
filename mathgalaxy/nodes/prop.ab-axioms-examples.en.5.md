# Examples of AB Axioms　`prop.ab-axioms-examples`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=prop.ab-axioms-examples)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./prop.ab-axioms-examples.md)

- $\mathbf{AbTop}$ only reaches AB1 — it does not even satisfy AB2. The reason is that "morphisms need not be strict" (there are monomorphisms that are not kernels), so it is not even an abelian category and cannot do homological algebra. This is exactly why topological groups must be moved to the condensed side. - $\mathbf{Ab}(\mathcal{T})$ stops at AB5, while $\mathrm{CondAb}$ reaches AB6 — what is gained is precisely "filtered colimits commute with products" and "products are exact". This is what condensed mathematics obtains over a general topos.
