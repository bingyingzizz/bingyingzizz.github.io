# Stokes' Theorem　`thm.stokes`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=thm.stokes)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./thm.stokes.md)

For $i<n$ the components have compact support in the corresponding coordinate direction, so their integrals vanish;

only the $i = n$ component remains, and the one-variable fundamental theorem of calculus integrates it to $f_{n}(x^{1},\ldots,x^{n-1},0)$ — which is precisely the integral of $\omega$ over the boundary.
