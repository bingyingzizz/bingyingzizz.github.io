# Stokes' Theorem　`thm.stokes`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=thm.stokes)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./thm.stokes.md)

**Relation to Gauss–Bonnet**: on a surface $K\,\mathrm{d}A = \mathrm{d}\omega^{1}_{\ 2}$ (Gauss's equation, `thm.gauss-equation`), so the global Gauss–Bonnet formula can be seen as Stokes plus the boundary term $\int k_{g}\,\mathrm{d}s$ — the two theorems share the same root at this level.

⚠️ **Boundary orientation is the easiest place to err**: the convention is "outward normal first", i.e. the orientation of $\partial M$ is given by "outward normal $\wedge$ orientation of $M$". A sign error changes the whole formula by a minus sign, while every special case still "looks right".
