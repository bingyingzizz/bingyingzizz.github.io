**Hypotheses used in each direction** (testing separatedness by stalks does not require separation axioms on the space):
- A separated presheaf $\implies$ the map from sections into the product of stalks is injective: equality of germs is local equality by the filtered colimit over neighbourhoods, after which separatedness applies; taking all witnessing neighbourhoods avoids pointwise choice.
- Injectivity of the stalk maps on all open sets $\implies$ separated: equality on a cover directly implies equality of germs at every point, with no Hausdorffness or compactness needed; the check on the empty open set must be included.
