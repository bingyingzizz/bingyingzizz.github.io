# Cycles and Cocycles　`def.cycle`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=def.cycle)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./def.cycle.md)

⚠️ **$Z^{n}$ is a subobject of $K^{n}$** (a kernel), but it is **not** a subcomplex: restricting $d$ to $Z^{n}$ gives the zero map, so $Z$ carries a complex structure that is "zero everywhere". Be careful whether you mean the subobject or the subcomplex.

⭐ **Homology measures "how much of the cycles is not a boundary"**: $H^{n} = Z^{n}/B^{n}$ (@@def.cohomology@@). Hence $H^{n} = 0 \iff$ every cycle is a boundary $\iff$ the complex is exact in degree $n$. This is the only bridge translating "homology vanishes" into "closed = boundary".
