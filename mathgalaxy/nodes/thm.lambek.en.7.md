# Lambek's Criterion　`thm.lambek`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=thm.lambek)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./thm.lambek.md)

⚠️ Testing injectivity only on principal ideals $(a)$ gives **torsion-freeness** over an **integral domain**. This characterization holds only over integral domains: when $R = \mathbb{Z}/4$ and $M = R/(2)$, non-zero-divisors act injectively, but $(2) \otimes M \to M$ is the zero map. Torsion-freeness is weaker than flatness. See `prop.flat-domain-torsionfree` for the counterexample $(x, y) \subseteq k[x, y]$.

⭐ **Practical use**: To test whether a given $R$-module is flat, it suffices to do one division computation for each finitely generated ideal. Over a commutative Noetherian ring, every ideal is finitely generated, so the criterion simply applies to all ideals.
