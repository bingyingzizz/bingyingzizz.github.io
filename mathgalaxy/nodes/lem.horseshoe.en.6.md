# Horseshoe Lemma　`lem.horseshoe`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=lem.horseshoe)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./lem.horseshoe.md)

To obtain $\varepsilon_{R}d_{0} = 0$, it suffices to impose one additional condition on $\chi_{0} : Q_{0} \to P_{-1} = M_{1}$. By $p\sigma e_{0} = \eta e_{0} = 0$, the image of $\sigma e_{0}$ lies in $\ker p = \operatorname{im} i$, so the map can be written as $i\theta$.

Now use surjectivity of $\varepsilon$ and projectivity of $Q_{0}$ to lift $\theta : Q_{0} \to M_{1}$ to $\chi_{0}$ with the condition $\varepsilon\chi_{0} = -\theta$ ✓.
