# Smooth Partition of Unity　`thm.smooth-partition-unity`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=thm.smooth-partition-unity)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./thm.smooth-partition-unity.md)

**Why local finiteness is essential**: Without it, $\sum\rho_{\alpha}$ may have infinitely many nonzero terms near a point, and the sum need not converge or be smooth. Local finiteness gives only finitely many terms near each point, so smoothness, integrability, and linearity carry over from finite sums.
