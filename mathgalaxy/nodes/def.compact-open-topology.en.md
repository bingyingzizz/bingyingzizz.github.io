## Statement · **draft**
Let $X, Y \in \mathbf{Top}$. For every continuous map $g : S \to X$ (with $S$ compact Hausdorff) and every open set $V \subseteq Y$, set

$$W_{S,V} := \{\, f : X \to Y \;\mid\; \operatorname{Im}(f \circ g) \subseteq V \,\}.$$
