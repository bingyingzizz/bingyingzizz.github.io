# intersection of subobjects　`def.intersection-subobject`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=def.intersection-subobject)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./def.intersection-subobject.md)

**Relation to set-theoretic intersection**: in $\mathbf{Set}$, $Y \times_{X} Z = \{(y,z) : y = z\}$, the map $t \mapsto (t,t)$ gives an isomorphism $Y \cap Z \to Y \times_{X} Z$; so the intersection is the set-theoretic one. In concrete categories such as $\mathbf{Grp}$, $\mathbf{Top}$, subobjects are determined by injections, and the underlying set of the intersection is likewise the set-theoretic intersection.
