## Statement · **draft**
A topological space $X$ is **disconnected** if it can be written as the union of two disjoint non-empty open sets, $X = U \sqcup V$; otherwise it is **connected**.

For a fixed $x \in X$, the **connected component** $C(x)$ of $x$ is the union of all connected subsets containing $x$ — it is itself connected, and it is maximal.
