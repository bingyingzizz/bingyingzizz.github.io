# law of quadratic reciprocity　`thm.quadratic-reciprocity`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=thm.quadratic-reciprocity)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./thm.quadratic-reciprocity.md)

⭐ A mainstream approach today uses **point counting on curves over finite fields**. $\left(\frac{p}{q}\right)$ is related to the number of solutions of $x^{2} \equiv p \pmod q$, and these solutions can be counted in stages using elliptic curves or multiplicative groups over $\mathbb{F}_{p}$ (Zolotarev's lemma and the signs of permutations). This is **one modern approach**. Elementary proofs also exist, including Gauss's third proof and proofs using Gauss's lemma or Zolotarev's lemma, all based on elementary counting.
