## Statement · **draft**
The limit of a diagram of shape $X_1 \xrightarrow{f_1} X_0 \xleftarrow{f_2} X_2$ is called the **fibred product**, written $X_1 \times_{X_0} X_2$, also called the **pullback** of $f_1$ along $f_2$. It completes to a commutative square

$$\begin{array}{ccc} X_1 \times_{X_0} X_2 & \longrightarrow & X_2 \\ \downarrow & & \downarrow f_2 \\ X_1 & \xrightarrow{f_1} & X_0 \end{array}$$
