## Statement · **draft**
Let $f \in K[x]$ be a nonzero polynomial, $\deg f = n$. Then $f$ has at most $n$ roots in $K$ (distinct roots).

More precisely (using **multiplicity**): there exist $a_1, \ldots, a_m \in K$ (distinct) and positive integers $e_1, \ldots, e_m$ such that

$$f = (x - a_1)^{e_1} \cdots (x - a_m)^{e_m}\, h, \qquad h(a_i) \ne 0 \ \text{for all } i$$
