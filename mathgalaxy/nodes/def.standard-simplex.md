**标准 $p$-单纯形**是

$$\Delta^{p} := \left\{ (t_{0}, \ldots, t_{p}) \in \mathbb{R}^{p+1} : \sum_{i} t_{i} = 1,\ t_{i} \ge 0 \right\}.$$
