## Statement · **draft**
Let $p$ be an odd prime with $p \nmid a$. Consider $a, 2a, \cdots, \frac{p-1}{2}a$; replace each by its least positive residue modulo $p$ (lying in $1, \cdots, p-1$), and suppose $\mu$ of them are greater than $\frac{p}{2}$. Then

$$\left(\frac{a}{p}\right) = (-1)^{\mu} .$$
