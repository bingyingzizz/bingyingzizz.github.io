## Notes · **draft**
For the general definition, see “abelian sheaf.” Condensed abelian groups are the case of $\mathcal{T} = \mathrm{Cond}$.

In the compact Hausdorff setting, the sheaf condition simplifies: **$M$ is a condensed abelian group $\iff$ it preserves finite products** (a general topos has no such simple criterion).

Notation: $\operatorname{Hom}_{\mathbb{Z}}(M, N) := \operatorname{Hom}_{\mathrm{CondAb}}(M, N)$.
