## Statement · **draft**
**What is being made precise**: the numerator in the homology formula $H^{n} = Z^{n}/B^{n}$. A cycle is an element **killed by the differential** — all of homological algebra asks "of what is killed, how much really came from somewhere, and how much went the long way round".

Let $K$ be a complex in an additive category $\mathcal{C}$, with differential $d^{n} : K^{n} \to K^{n+1}$ (@@def.cochain-complex@@).
