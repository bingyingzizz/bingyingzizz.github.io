# p-series　`thm.p-series`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=thm.p-series)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./thm.p-series.md)

⭐ The proof is the most beautiful application of `thm.cauchy-condensation` (Cauchy's condensation test): when $a_n = n^{-p}$ is monotonically decreasing, the original series converges $\iff$ $\sum 2^{k} a_{2^{k}} = \sum (2^{1-p})^{k}$ converges, and the latter is a geometric series with ratio $2^{1-p}$, which converges $\iff$ $2^{1-p} < 1 \iff p > 1$.
