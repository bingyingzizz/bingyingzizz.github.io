# First Ext Classifies Extensions　`thm.ext1-extensions`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=thm.ext1-extensions)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./thm.ext1-extensions.md)

Two extensions are **equivalent** if there is an isomorphism between them making both squares commute and inducing the identity on $N$ and $M$. The equivalence classes are in **bijection** with $\operatorname{Ext}^{1}_{R}(M, N)$. The **Baer sum** makes these classes into an abelian group, and the bijection is a group isomorphism.

The **split extension** ($E \cong N\oplus M$) corresponds precisely to the zero element.
