# Hadamard factorization theorem　`thm.hadamard-factorization`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=thm.hadamard-factorization)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./thm.hadamard-factorization.md)

• For $|a_{n}|>4r$, we have $|z/a_{n}|<1/2$, and by (i) each term satisfies $\ge-C(R/|a_{n}|)^{p+1}$. Summation by parts and $n(t)\le C t^{\rho+\varepsilon}$ give $\sum_{|a_{n}|>4r}(R/|a_{n}|)^{p+1}\le C\,r^{\rho+\varepsilon}$.
