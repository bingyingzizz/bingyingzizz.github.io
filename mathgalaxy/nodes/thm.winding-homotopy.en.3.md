## Notes · **draft**
**Proof for the winding number**: for the homotopy parameter $s$ define $N(s) := n(H(s, \cdot), a)$. By the integral representation $N(s) = \frac{1}{2\pi i}\oint_{H(s,\cdot)} \mathrm{d}z/(z-a)$, both the integrand and the domain depend continuously on $s$, so $N$ is continuous; but $N$ takes only integer values, and continuous plus integer-valued $\implies$ constant. Hence $N(0) = N(1)$.
