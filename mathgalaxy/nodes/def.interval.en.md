## Statement · **draft**
An **interval** of $\mathbb{R}$ is a subset $I$ satisfying

$$a, b \in I,\ a \le x \le b \implies x \in I$$

i.e. it has "no gaps". The usual bounded intervals are written

$$(a, b), \quad [a, b], \quad [a, b), \quad (a, b]$$

distinguished by whether the endpoints are included; there are also the unbounded intervals $(-\infty, b)$, $(a, +\infty)$, $\mathbb{R}$, and the singleton $[a, a]$.
