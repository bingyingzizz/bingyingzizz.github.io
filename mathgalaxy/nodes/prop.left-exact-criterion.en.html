Statement

Let C,D\mathcal{C}, \mathcal{D}C,D be abelian categories and F:C→DF : \mathcal{C} \to \mathcal{D}F:C→D a functor. Then

FFF is left exact   ⟺  \iff⟺ FFF is additive and sends left exact sequences to left exact sequences.

Dually: FFF is right exact   ⟺  \iff⟺ FFF is additive and sends right exact sequences to right exact sequences.

Notes

⭐ This splits "left exact" into two termwise checkable facts: additivity (look at biproducts) and preservation of left exact sequences (look at the actual sequences). These are the two rulers used in practice to verify that a functor is left exact.
