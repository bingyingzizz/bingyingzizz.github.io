# submodule　`def.submodule`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=def.submodule)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./def.submodule.md)

Kernels and images of module homomorphisms are submodules; a left ideal of $A$ is exactly a submodule of the left $A$-module $A$.

## Notes · **draft**
⭐ Three levels of comparison: subgroup (group) $\subset$ submodule (module) $\subset$ subspace (module over a field). A submodule is required to be closed under the **ring action**, one condition more than an additive subgroup.

⭐ Examples: for $A = \mathbb{Z}$ a submodule is just a subgroup $n\mathbb{Z}$; for $A = K[x]$ a submodule is a $K[x]$-subspace.
