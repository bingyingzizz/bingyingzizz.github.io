# totally disconnected spaces are a reflective subcategory　`prop.td-reflective`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=prop.td-reflective)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./prop.td-reflective.md)

**Proof.** Write $\mathbf{Top}_{\mathrm{td}}$ for the category of totally disconnected spaces and $i : \mathbf{Top}_{\mathrm{td}}\hookrightarrow\mathbf{Top}$ for the inclusion. We must show that $i$ has a left adjoint $\pi_{0}$.
- **Construction**: take $\pi_{0}(X)$ to be the set of connected components of $X$ with the **quotient topology** ($X\to\pi_{0}(X)$ sends each point to its component).
