# inner product　`def.inner-product`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=def.inner-product)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./def.inner-product.md)

⚠ Positive definiteness is the watershed between inner products and general bilinear forms: only positive definiteness gives a notion of length, hence Cauchy–Schwarz and orthogonal projection. Dropping positive definiteness (keeping a symmetric bilinear form) leads to **quadratic forms** (see `def.quadratic-form`); dropping symmetry leads to **symplectic forms**.
