## Statement · **draft**
Let $\varphi : [\alpha, \beta] \to [a, b]$ have continuous derivative, and $f$ be continuous on $[a, b]$. Then

$$\int_{\alpha}^{\beta} f\bigl(\varphi(t)\bigr)\,\varphi'(t)\,dt = \int_{\varphi(\alpha)}^{\varphi(\beta)} f(x)\,dx$$
