## Statement · **draft**
**What it does**: writes $\lVert u\rVert^{2}\lVert w\rVert^{2} - \langle u,w\rangle^{2}$ as “the square of an area” — this is the source of equality in the Cauchy–Schwarz inequality, and the two-dimensional case of the Gram determinant.
