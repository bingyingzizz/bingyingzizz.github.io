## Notes · **draft**
⭐ **$\mathbb{C}$ is algebraically closed** — this is the fundamental theorem of algebra: $\mathbb{C} = \mathbb{R}(i)$ is the algebraic closure of $\mathbb{R}$. By contrast $\overline{\mathbb{Q}}$ (the algebraic closure of $\mathbb{Q}$ inside $\mathbb{C}$) is the standard example of an extension that is **algebraic but infinite**: this is exactly where “algebraic $\ne$ finite” comes from.
