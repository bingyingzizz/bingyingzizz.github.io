# provability　`def.provability`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=def.provability)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./def.provability.md)

- $\varphi_{k}$ is a **logical axiom**;
- $\varphi_{k} \in \Gamma$;
- $\varphi_{k}$ is obtained from the two preceding formulas $\varphi_{i}$, $\varphi_{j}$ (where $\varphi_{j} = \varphi_{i} \to \varphi_{k}$) by **modus ponens**;
- $\varphi_{k} = \forall x\,\psi$, and $\psi$ occurs earlier (**generalization rule**).

Such a sequence is a **proof** of $\varphi$. $\Gamma$ is **inconsistent** if there is $\varphi$ with $\Gamma \vdash \varphi$ and $\Gamma \vdash \neg\varphi$.
