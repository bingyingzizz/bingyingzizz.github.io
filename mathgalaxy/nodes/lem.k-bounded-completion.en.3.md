# k-bounded completion　`lem.k-bounded-completion`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=lem.k-bounded-completion)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./lem.k-bounded-completion.md)

Completion embeds $M^{n}$ as a **dense** subspace. The condition of $K$-bounded exactness involves only norm inequalities and an arbitrary choice of $\varepsilon > 0$. Such conditions are **preserved in both directions** when passing to a dense subspace. Density gives sufficiently good approximations in $M^{n}$, and limits in the complete space can lie back in $\widehat{M}^{\bullet}$.
