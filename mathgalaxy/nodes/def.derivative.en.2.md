# derivative　`def.derivative`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=def.derivative)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./def.derivative.md)

If the limit is not required to exist and $\pm\infty$ is allowed, one obtains the **generalized derivative**, which is needed in the theory of derivatives of monotone functions.

## Notes · **draft**
⭐ Differentiation theory cares about **pointwise** behaviour, so the domain is often shrunk to $L^1_{loc}$ rather than $L^1$.

Key points (the BV / AC chain relies on all of them):
- differentiability $\implies$ continuity, but not conversely ($|x|$ at $0$).

- A monotone **increasing** function is **differentiable almost everywhere**, and $F' \ge 0$ a.e. (for decreasing functions, $F' \le 0$).
