## Statement · **draft**
In $\mathrm{Cond}$, every epimorphism $f : X \to Y$ is **regular**: it is the coequalizer of its own pair of projections,

$$Y \cong \operatorname{coeq}\bigl(X \times_{Y} X \rightrightarrows X\bigr).$$
