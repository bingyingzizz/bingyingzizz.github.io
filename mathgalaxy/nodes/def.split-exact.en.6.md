# split short exact sequence　`def.split-exact`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=def.split-exact)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./def.split-exact.md)

⭐ **This is the splitting lemma**: In an additive category, splitting of a short exact sequence, existence of a section, and a compatible direct-sum decomposition are equivalent. It **reduces questions about sequences to questions about direct sums**, a standard simplification in homological algebra.

⚠️ Short exact sequences **need not** split ($0 \to \mathbb{Z} \xrightarrow{2} \mathbb{Z} \to \mathbb{Z}/2 \to 0$ is one that does not). Splitting is a special case, and $\operatorname{Ext}^{1}$ addresses whether a given sequence splits.
