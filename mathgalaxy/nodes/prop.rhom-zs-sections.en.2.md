## Notes · **draft**
**Proof.** When $S$ is Stonean, $\mathbb{Z}[S]$ is a **projective** object: extremal disconnectedness gives "section-type" projectivity on clopen sets, and $\mathbb{Z}[S]$ is the colimit of the $\mathbb{Z}[C]$ over clopen $C$, with projectivity preserved under colimits ✓. Hence

$$\operatorname{Hom}_{\mathbb{Z}}\bigl(\mathbb{Z}[S],\ -\bigr) = \Gamma(S, -) = \bigl(N \mapsto N(S)\bigr)$$
