**Why item 2 is called a "generalization of constant"**: when $Y \to X$ is finite étale, $Y$ looks locally (for the étale topology) like $n$ copies of $X$, so $\mathcal{G}_{Y}$ is locally isomorphic to a constant sheaf. The analogy is the topological fact "an $n$-sheeted covering $E \to X$ gives a local system".

**Intuition for the two examples in item 4**: $\operatorname{Spec}\mathbb{C} \to \operatorname{Spec}\mathbb{R}$ is "$2$ points covering $1$ points", the prototype of a Galois covering;
