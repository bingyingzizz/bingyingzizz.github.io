# Alternating Sum of a Simplicial Object　`prop.simplicial-to-chain`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=prop.simplicial-to-chain)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./prop.simplicial-to-chain.md)

For a topological space $X$, its singular simplicial set $S_{\bullet}(X) = h_{X} \circ \Delta^{\bullet}$ together with $\mathbb{Z}\cdot(-)$ gives $C_{\bullet}(X) := \mathbb{Z}\cdot S_{\bullet}(X)$ as the standard example — its homology is **singular homology**.

⚠️ Only **semi**simplicial structure is needed here (only face maps); degeneracies do not enter the differential (this is the "normalized" convention).
