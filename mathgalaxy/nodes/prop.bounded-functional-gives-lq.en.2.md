# bounded functionals on Lp land in Lq　`prop.bounded-functional-gives-lq`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=prop.bounded-functional-gives-lq)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./prop.bounded-functional-gives-lq.md)

$$M(g) := \sup \left\{ \, \left| \int fg \right| : f \in \Sigma,\ \|f\|_p = 1 \,\right\}$$

($\Sigma$ $=$ the set of all simple functions of finite support.) If $M(g) < \infty$ and

- $\{x : g(x) \ne 0\}$ is **$\sigma$-finite**, **or**
- $\mu$ is **semifinite**,

then $g \in L^q$, and $M(g) = \|g\|_q$.

## Notes · **draft**
This is the technical preparation for the next big theorem ($(L^p)^* \cong L^q$): it says that "the functional is bounded" by itself already forces $g$ into $L^q$.
