## Statement · **draft**
Let $f : [a, b] \to \mathbb{C}$ be Lebesgue measurable. Then for every $\varepsilon > 0$ there is a **compact set** $E \subseteq [a, b]$ such that

$$\mu(E^c) < \varepsilon$$
and $f|_E$ is **continuous**.
