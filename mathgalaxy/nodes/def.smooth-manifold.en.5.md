# Smooth Manifold　`def.smooth-manifold`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=def.smooth-manifold)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./def.smooth-manifold.md)

**Examples**: $\mathbb{R}^{n}$ (one chart); $S^{n}\subseteq\mathbb{R}^{n+1}$ (stereographic projection gives two charts); $\mathbb{RP}^{n}$ ($n+1$ affine charts); any open subset; products of manifolds.

## Notes · **draft**
**Why one must pass to the maximal atlas**: if one only says "there is a family of compatible charts", "$f$ is smooth" depends on the choice of that family; after maximalization, every chart compatible with the atlas is automatically in it, so smoothness becomes a property of the manifold rather than of one chart.
