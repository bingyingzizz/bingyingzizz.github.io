# Stalks of a Skyscraper Sheaf　`prop.skyscraper-stalks`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=prop.skyscraper-stalks)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./prop.skyscraper-stalks.md)

In particular $(i_{x,*}A)_{x} = A$; if $X$ is a $T_{1}$ space (Hausdorff suffices), then for $y \ne x$ one has $(i_{x,*}A)_{y} = 0$.

## Notes · **draft**
**Proof.** The stalk is the filtered colimit over open neighbourhoods of $y$:

$$(i_{x,*}A)_{y} = \varinjlim_{U \ni y}\ (i_{x,*}A)(U) .$$
