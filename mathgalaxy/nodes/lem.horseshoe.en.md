## Statement · **draft**
Let $0 \to M_{1} \xrightarrow{\ i\ } M \xrightarrow{\ p\ } M_{2} \to 0$ be a short exact sequence of $R$-modules, with projective resolutions of the two end terms given by

$$\cdots \to P_{1} \xrightarrow{\ d_{1}\ } P_{0} \xrightarrow{\ \varepsilon\ } M_{1} \to 0, \qquad \cdots \to Q_{1} \xrightarrow{\ e_{1}\ } Q_{0} \xrightarrow{\ \eta\ } M_{2} \to 0 .$$
