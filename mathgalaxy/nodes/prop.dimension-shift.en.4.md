# Dimension Shift　`prop.dimension-shift`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=prop.dimension-shift)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./prop.dimension-shift.md)

**Tor side (dual: tensor the short exact sequence with $N$).** $\otimes$ is right exact, so after tensoring $P\otimes N \to M\otimes N$ is still surjective; the injectivity at the left end may fail, which is exactly where $\operatorname{Tor}$ appears. Take the long exact sequence

$$\cdots \to \operatorname{Tor}_{n+1}(P, N) \to \operatorname{Tor}_{n+1}(M, N) \to \operatorname{Tor}_{n}(K, N) \to \operatorname{Tor}_{n}(P, N) \to \cdots$$

$P$ projective $\implies$ flat $\implies$ $\operatorname{Tor}_{k}(P, N) = 0$ for $k \ge 1$, hence $\operatorname{Tor}_{n+1}(M, N) \cong \operatorname{Tor}_{n}(K, N)$ ✓. ∎
