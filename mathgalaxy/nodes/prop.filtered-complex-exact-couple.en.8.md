# a filtered complex gives an exact couple　`prop.filtered-complex-exact-couple`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=prop.filtered-complex-exact-couple)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./prop.filtered-complex-exact-couple.md)

⭐ **Practical use.** When computing a spectral sequence, choose whichever construction is easier to work with. For a **double complex**, the direct construction is usually convenient. Each filtration direction gives a spectral sequence, and it suffices to compare them at the $E_{2}$ page. See `prop.bicomplex-spectral`. For a **general exact couple**, such as one arising from homotopy groups or from a Bockstein or Gysin sequence, the exact couple is the only construction available, since there is no complex to filter.
