# Fiber Bundle　`def.fiber-bundle`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=def.fiber-bundle)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./def.fiber-bundle.md)

Let $E, B, F$ be smooth manifolds and $\pi : E\to B$ a smooth surjection. One calls $\pi$ (together with $E$) a **fiber bundle with fiber $F$ and base $B$** if every point $b\in B$ has an open neighbourhood $U$ and a diffeomorphism

$$\Phi : \pi^{-1}(U)\longrightarrow U\times F,\qquad \operatorname{pr}_{1}\circ\Phi = \pi ,$$

that is, $\Phi$ commutes with the projection. Such a $\Phi$ is called a **local trivialization**, $E_{b} := \pi^{-1}(b)$ the **fiber** over $b$, and $E$ the **total space**.

**Transition functions**: two trivializations over the same open cover necessarily differ over their overlap by a fiberwise self-homeomorphism
