## Statement · **draft**
Let $(K, \delta)$ be a metric space, $(E, d)$ a metric space, and $\mathcal{H} \subseteq C(K, E)$. Call $\mathcal{H}$ **uniformly equicontinuous** if

$$\forall \varepsilon > 0\ \exists \eta > 0:\quad \delta(x, y) < \eta \implies d(f(x), f(y)) < \varepsilon \quad (\forall f \in \mathcal{H},\ \forall x, y \in K).$$
