# comma category　`def.comma-category`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=def.comma-category)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./def.comma-category.md)

- $X \downarrow \mathrm{id}_{\mathcal{C}}$ is the **slice** $\mathcal{C}/X$;
- the **category of elements** $\int F$ of a presheaf is $\mathbf{1} \downarrow F$ (viewing the presheaf as a functor $\mathcal{C}^{\mathrm{op}} \to \mathbf{Set}$);
- the **unit** of an adjunction is defined as the initial object of $\mathrm{id} \downarrow G$ (or $F \downarrow \mathrm{id}$).

To recognize a comma category, check whether "objects are arrows and morphisms are commutative squares".
