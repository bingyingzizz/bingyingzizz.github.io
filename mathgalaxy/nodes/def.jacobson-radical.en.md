## Statement · **draft**
The Jacobson radical of a ring $A$ is $J(A)=\bigcap_{V}\operatorname{Ann}(V)$, where $V$ runs over simple left modules; equivalently, it is the intersection of all maximal left ideals. It is a two-sided ideal.
