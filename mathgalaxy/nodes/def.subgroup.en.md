## Statement · **draft**
Let $(G, \cdot)$ be a group and $H \subseteq G$. We say that $H$ is a **subgroup** of $G$ (written $H \le G$) if $H$ is itself a group under the **restricted operation** — that is:

- $e \in H$;

- $a, b \in H \implies ab \in H$ (closed under the operation);

- $a \in H \implies a^{-1} \in H$ (closed under inverses).

(Associativity is inherited from $G$ and need not be checked again.)
