# Rouché's theorem　`thm.rouche`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=thm.rouche)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./thm.rouche.md)

⚠ The inequality must be **strict** and must hold on the **whole boundary**. Equality is exactly where things can break: $f = z$, $g = -z$ on $|z| = 1$ satisfy $|g| = |f|$, yet $f$ and $f + g = 0$ have completely different numbers of zeros.

📌 How to choose the "main term": take the term with the largest modulus on the boundary. This is the same fact as "the behaviour of a polynomial at infinity is governed by its leading term".
