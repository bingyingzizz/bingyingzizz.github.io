# slice categories are pullbacks　`prop.slice-cartesian`

is **Cartesian**. The top arrow is the **embedding of the slice category into the category of presheaves**, the bottom arrow is the **Yoneda embedding of the category $\mathcal{C}$ into the category of presheaves** $h$ ($h(X) = h_{X} = \operatorname{Hom}(-, X)$), and the two vertical arrows are the forgetful functors.

## Notes · **draft**
In other words, **the slice category $\mathcal{C}/T$ is the pullback of the forgetful functor $\widehat{\mathcal{C}}/T \to \widehat{\mathcal{C}}$ along the Yoneda embedding**.
