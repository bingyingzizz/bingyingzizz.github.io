Statement draft

Let GGG be a group and g∈Gg \in Gg∈G. The map

cg:G→G,cg(x):=gxg−1c_g : G \to G, \qquad c_g(x) := g x g^{-1}cg​:G→G,cg​(x):=gxg−1

is an automorphism of GGG, the inner automorphism determined by ggg. All inner automorphisms

Inn⁡(G):={ cg:g∈G }\operatorname{Inn}(G) := \{\, c_g : g \in G \,\}Inn(G):={cg​:g∈G}

form a group under composition, and Inn⁡(G)≅G/Z(G)\operatorname{Inn}(G) \cong G/Z(G)Inn(G)≅G/Z(G), Inn⁡(G)⊴Aut⁡(G)\operatorname{Inn}(G) \trianglelefteq \operatorname{Aut}(G)Inn(G)⊴Aut(G).

Notes draft

⭐ Inner automorphisms are exactly the image of the conjugation action of GGG on itself: cgc_gcg​ is the map x↦g⋅xx \mapsto g \cdot xx↦g⋅x (conjugation).

⭐ The inner automorphism group is trivial   ⟺  \iff⟺ GGG is abelian; then Inn⁡(G)={id}\operatorname{Inn}(G) = \{\mathrm{id}\}Inn(G)={id} and Out⁡(G)=Aut⁡(G)\operatorname{Out}(G) = \operatorname{Aut}(G)Out(G)=Aut(G).

⭐ Examples: the inner automorphisms of SnS_nSn​ are exactly the conjugations preserving cycle type; the inner automorphisms of GLn(K)GL_n(K)GLn​(K) are A↦PAP−1A \mapsto P A P^{-1}A↦PAP−1.
