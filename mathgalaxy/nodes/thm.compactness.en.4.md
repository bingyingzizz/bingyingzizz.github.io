# compactness theorem　`thm.compactness`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=thm.compactness)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./thm.compactness.md)

**The resemblance to topological compactness (`def.compact`) is only in the name**: that one says "every open cover has a finite subcover", this one says "finitely satisfiable $\implies$ satisfiable". The two are connected only by an **analogy edge**, not by a dependence of theorems.

> Proofs of the edges are still Chinese only: `proofs/`; the Chinese page remains authoritative.
