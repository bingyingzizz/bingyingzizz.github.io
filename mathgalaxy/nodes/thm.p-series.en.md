## Statement · **draft**
Let $p \in \mathbb{R}$. Then the series

$$\sum_{n=1}^{\infty} \frac{1}{n^{p}}$$

converges **if and only if** $p > 1$.

## Notes · **draft**
⭐ $p = 1$ is the harmonic series (divergent), $p = 2$ is $\sum 1/n^{2} = \pi^{2}/6$ (convergent) — the two are standard examples where "tests fail on the boundary": the root test gives $\alpha = 1$ for both.
