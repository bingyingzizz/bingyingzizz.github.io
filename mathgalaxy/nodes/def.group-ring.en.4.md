# Group Ring　`def.group-ring`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=def.group-ring)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./def.group-ring.md)

**Why it must be built**: the group $G$ itself is not a ring, so modules, injectives, and resolutions make no sense. After embedding $G$ into $R[G]$, "the action of $G$ on some abelian group" becomes "a module over $R[G]$", and the whole of homological algebra applies at once.
