# Möbius band　`def.mobius-band`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=def.mobius-band)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./def.mobius-band.md)

**The boundary is one circle**: $\partial M \cong S^{1}$ (**one** circle, whereas the cylinder has two). The subspace $\partial M$ is connected, which also gives an intuitive view of nonorientability.

⚠ **It cannot embed in the plane**. There is no Möbius band in $\mathbb{R}^{2}$. It can embed **without self-intersection** in $\mathbb{R}^{3}$, as a strip of paper given a half twist and glued. The picture that requires self-intersection is an immersion of $\mathbb{RP}^{2}$. The realization in $\mathbb{R}^{3}$ is an example of a **nonorientable surface** (compare `def.orientable-surface`).
