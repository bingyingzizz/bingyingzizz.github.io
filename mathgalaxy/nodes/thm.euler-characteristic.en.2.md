## Notes · **draft**
**Two examples**:

- The tetrahedral subdivision of $S^{2}$: $\alpha = (4, 6, 4, 0)$, $\chi = 4 - 6 + 4 - 0 = 2$; $\beta = (1, 0, 1)$, $\sum = 1 - 0 + 1 = 2$ ✓
- The minimal subdivision of the real projective plane $\mathbb{RP}^{2}$ (6 vertices / 15 edges / 10 faces): $\chi = 6 - 15 + 10 = 1$; while $H_{1}(\mathbb{RP}^{2}) = \mathbb{Z}/2$ **has rank zero**, so $\sum(-1)^{p}\beta_{p} = 1 - 0 + 0 = 1$ ✓
