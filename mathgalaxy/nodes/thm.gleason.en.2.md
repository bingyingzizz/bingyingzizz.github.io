# Gleason　`thm.gleason`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=thm.gleason)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./thm.gleason.md)

⭐ **Why it matters**: in condensed mathematics, "$\mathbb{Z}[S]$ is projective $\iff$ $S$ is Stonean" is a direct consequence — a topological property (extreme disconnectedness) is translated into a categorical one (projectivity).

⚠ Keep the two separation properties apart: **Stonean** (extremally disconnected: the closure of an open set is open) is strictly stronger than **Stone** (totally disconnected); they are different nodes here (`def.stone-space` treats the latter).
