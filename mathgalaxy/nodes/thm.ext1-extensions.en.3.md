# First Ext Classifies Extensions　`thm.ext1-extensions`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=thm.ext1-extensions)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./thm.ext1-extensions.md)

By dimension shifting, $\operatorname{Ext}^{1}_{R}(M, N) \cong \operatorname{coker}\bigl(\operatorname{Hom}(P, N) \to \operatorname{Hom}(K, N)\bigr)$ ✓.
**From an extension to a class.** Given an extension $0 \to N \to E \to M \to 0$, pull it back along $p$ to get $0 \to N \to E_{0} \to P \to 0$, where $E_{0} = \{\, (e, x) : \pi(e) = p(x) \,\}$.

Since $P$ is projective, the sequence **splits**. Choose a splitting $\sigma : P \to E_{0}$.

The composite $K \hookrightarrow P \xrightarrow{\sigma} E_{0} \to N$ gives $\theta \in \operatorname{Hom}(K, N)$ ✓.
