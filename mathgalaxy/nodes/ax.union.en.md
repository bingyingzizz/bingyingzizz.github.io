## Statement · **reviewed**
The elements of all members of a family of sets again form a set:

$$\forall F \exists A \forall x [ x \in A \leftrightarrow  \exists Y ( Y \in F \wedge  x \in Y ) ]$$

It is written $\bigcup F$. Together with the pairing axiom it yields the binary union $a \cup b = \bigcup \{ a, b \}$.
