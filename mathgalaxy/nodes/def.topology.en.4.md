**Which assumptions each direction uses** (a topology is defined by axioms and imposes no metric or separation requirement):
- topology $\iff$ the three listed axioms: both directions unfold the definition and need no compactness, Choice or metric.
- closure under arbitrary unions $\implies\emptyset$ is open requires allowing the empty index family; closure under binary intersections $\implies$ closure under non-empty finite intersections follows by induction, while the empty intersection being $X$ still needs the convention that $X$ is open.
- the open-set axioms $\iff$ the family of complements satisfies arbitrary intersections and finite unions: use De Morgan's identities; no extra topo
