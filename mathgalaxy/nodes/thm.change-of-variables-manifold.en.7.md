# Change of Variables on Manifolds　`thm.change-of-variables-manifold`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=thm.change-of-variables-manifold)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./thm.change-of-variables-manifold.md)

**The two pieces of the proof**: first, "the integral is invariant under an orientation-preserving diffeomorphism" (well-definedness of `def.integral-form`, i.e. the matching in $\mathbb{R}^{n}$ of $\det$ with the change-of-variables formula); second, a partition of unity (`prop.paracompact-partition-unity`) to split the whole into charts. Both pieces are proved elsewhere, so the theorem itself is an assembly.
