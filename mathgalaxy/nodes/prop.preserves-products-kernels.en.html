prop.preserves-products-kernels

Machine-drafted and pending author review. The Chinese text is authoritative.

Statement

A functor FFF that preserves all finite products and all kernels (equalizers) preserves all finite limits (i.e. FFF preserves all finite limits; FFF is left exact).

Notes

⚠️ Preserving "products" alone is not enough: kernels (equalizers) must also be preserved, otherwise the second step above breaks.
