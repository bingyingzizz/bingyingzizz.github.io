# Divisible Group　`def.divisible-group`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=def.divisible-group)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./def.divisible-group.md)

⭐ Over $\mathbb{Z}$: "divisible" and "injective" are the same thing (`thm.pid-injective-divisible`). Intuitively "divide by $n$" is exactly "extend along $n\mathbb{Z} \rightarrowtail \mathbb{Z}$".

📌 Quotients: a quotient of a divisible group is divisible (`prop.divisible-quotient`); **subgroups** need not be ($\mathbb{Z} \subseteq \mathbb{Q}$).
