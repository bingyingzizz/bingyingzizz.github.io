Statement

Let XXX be a presheaf of sets on FCHaus\mathbf{FCHaus}FCHaus. Then XXX is a sheaf   ⟺  \iff⟺ XXX preserves finite products.

Notes

⭐ Use: this replaces "a sheaf in Cond\mathrm{Cond}Cond" by a purely algebraic phrase — "a functor preserving finite products". So to check that a property defines a sheaf, it suffices to check that it preserves finite products.
