**Proof.**
- **Torsion $\iff$ Stonean**: $M$ is torsion $\iff$ $M = \varinjlim_{i} M_{i}$ (with $M_{i}$ **finite**) $\iff$ $\widehat M = \varprojlim_{i} \widehat{M_{i}}$ (each $\widehat{M_{i}}$ is again finite, and duality turns colimits into inverse limits) $\iff$ $\widehat M$ is a **profinite** space, that is, a Stone space (compact, Hausdorff, and totally disconnected);
