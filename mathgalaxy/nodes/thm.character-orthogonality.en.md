## Statement · **draft**
Let $\operatorname{char}F\nmid|G|$, let $F$ be algebraically closed, and let $V_1,V_2$ be finite-dimensional irreducible representations. Then $\langle\chi_{V_1},\chi_{V_2}\rangle=1$ if $V_1\cong V_2$, and $0$ otherwise.
