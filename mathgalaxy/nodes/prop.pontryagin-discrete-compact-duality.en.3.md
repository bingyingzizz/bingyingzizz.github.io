## Notes · **draft**
**Why this holds.** $\mathbb{T} = \mathbb{R}/\mathbb{Z}$ is compact: the continuous homomorphisms from the **discrete** group $M$ to the compact group $\mathbb{T}$ form a compact space in the compact-open topology (the product $\mathbb{T}^{M}$ is compact, and closed subsets of compact spaces are compact);

conversely, when $\widehat{M}$ is compact, $M = \widehat{\widehat{M}}$ is discrete.
