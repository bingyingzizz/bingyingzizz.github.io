## Statement · **stale**
Let $V$ be a finite-dimensional semisimple $A$-module, $A'=\operatorname{End}_A(V)$, and $A''=\operatorname{End}_{A'}(V)$. The natural map $\Phi:A\to A''$, $a\mapsto(v\mapsto av)$, is surjective. Equivalently, for every $f\in A''$ and every finite family $v_1,\ldots,v_n\in V$ there is $a\in A$ with $av_i=f(v_i)$ for all $i$.
