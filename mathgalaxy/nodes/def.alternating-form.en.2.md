# alternating form　`def.alternating-form`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=def.alternating-form)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./def.alternating-form.md)

- $f$ is called **symmetric** if and only if for every permutation $\sigma$ one has $f(v_{\sigma(1)}, \ldots, v_{\sigma(n)}) = f(v_1, \ldots, v_n)$;
- $f$ is called **alternating** if and only if $f = 0$ whenever two of its arguments are equal;
- $f$ is called **skew-symmetric** if and only if $f(v_{\sigma(1)}, \ldots) = \operatorname{sgn}(\sigma)\, f(v_1, \ldots)$.

Over a field of characteristic $\ne 2$, **alternating $\iff$ skew-symmetric**.
