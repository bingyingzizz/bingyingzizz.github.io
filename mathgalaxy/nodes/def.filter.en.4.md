⚠️ **The neighbourhood system of the filter $\ne$**: $\mathcal{N}(x)$ itself is a filter (the neighbourhood filter), whereas a general filter may **fail to converge**. Convergence depends on whether it contains some $\mathcal{N}(x)$.

⚠️ **Name clash with "filter" in category theory**: `prop.covering-sieves-filter` concerns the filteredness of sieves in a Grothendieck topology, and is **unrelated** to the set-theoretic filter here.
