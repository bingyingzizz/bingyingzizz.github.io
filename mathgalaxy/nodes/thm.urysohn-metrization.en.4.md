**Proof (embedding into a separable metric space)**
1. **Second countable $\implies$ Lindelöf**: every open cover has a countable subcover (sieve the basic open sets).
2. **Regular $+$ Lindelöf $\implies$ normal**: $T_{1}$ together with "a point and a closed set can be separated", plus a pointwise induction over the Lindelöf cover, separates two disjoint closed sets. Hence **Urysohn's lemma is available**.
