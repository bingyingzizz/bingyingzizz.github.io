## Statement · **draft**
**In plain words**: the modulus of a holomorphic function cannot attain a maximum in the interior of a region — unless the function is constant. The maximum can only occur on the boundary.

Let $\Omega$ be a plane region (a connected open set) and $f$ holomorphic on $\Omega$. Then either $f$ is constant, or

$$|f(z)| < \|f\|_{\Omega} := \sup_{\zeta \in \Omega} |f(\zeta)|, \qquad \forall z \in \Omega.$$
