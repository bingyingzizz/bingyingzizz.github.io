Statement

A linear representation of AAA is a pair (M,θ)(M,\theta)(M,θ), where MMM is an FFF-vector space and θ:A→End⁡F(M)\theta:A\to\operatorname{End}_F(M)θ:A→EndF​(M) is an algebra homomorphism (not necessarily unital; the condition θ(1A)=id⁡M\theta(1_A)=\operatorname{id}_Mθ(1A​)=idM​ is optional).
