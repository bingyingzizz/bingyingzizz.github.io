## Notes · **draft**
⭐ **What the notation "$\varphi (x, p)$" says**: it says that $\varphi$ is a formula and that its free variables all lie in $\{ x, p \}$ (not necessarily all used).

The phrase "for every formula $\varphi$ ($B$ does not occur)" in the separation scheme is exactly this convention — **formulas are metamathematical objects, not objects of ZFC**, so "does not occur" is a statement about strings of symbols.
