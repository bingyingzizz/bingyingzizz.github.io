# Taylor Formula in Several Variables　`thm.taylor-multi`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=thm.taylor-multi)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./thm.taylor-multi.md)

⭐ Thus multivariable Taylor meets linear algebra directly: $h^{\mathsf{T}} H h$ is a **quadratic form**, and positive definiteness of quadratic forms has a ready criterion (`thm.sylvester-criterion`) — the second derivative test (`thm.second-derivative-test`) is this formula plus that criterion.

⚠ Pitfall in the proof: applying one-variable Taylor to $t \mapsto f(a + th)$ gives an expansion **only along one line**, and the $\theta$ in the remainder depends on $h$.
