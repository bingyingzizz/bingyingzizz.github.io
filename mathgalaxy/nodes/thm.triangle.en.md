## Statement · **draft**
Let $K$ be an ordered field (such as $\mathbb{Q}$ or $\mathbb{R}$) and $x, y \in K$. Then

$$|x + y| \le |x| + |y|$$

and hence two frequently used variants:

$$\big| |x| - |y| \big| \le |x - y|, \qquad |x_1 + x_2 + \cdots + x_n| \le |x_1| + |x_2| + \cdots + |x_n|$$

(the latter follows from the former by induction on $n$).
