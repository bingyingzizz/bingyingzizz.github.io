## Notes · **draft**
**Proof.** A natural isomorphism gives a family of bijections on each pair of Hom-sets, compatible with composition ✓, so "is $\operatorname{Hom}(X,Y) \to \operatorname{Hom}(FX, FY)$ a bijection" holds on both sides simultaneously ✓; essential surjectivity and equivalence are similar (isomorphisms translate objects) ✓. ∎
