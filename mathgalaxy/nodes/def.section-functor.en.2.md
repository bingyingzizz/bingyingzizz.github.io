## Notes · **draft**
**It is the Hom functor of $h_{X}$**: $\Gamma(X, F) = \operatorname{Hom}_{\widetilde{\mathcal{C}}}(h_{X}, F) = F(X)$ (Yoneda's lemma) — so the section functor is nothing new; it is simply "probing $F$ with a representable presheaf".
