# limits and colimits in presheaf categories　`thm.presheaf-limits-colimits`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=thm.presheaf-limits-colimits)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./thm.presheaf-limits-colimits.md)

**(ii) $\mathbf{Set}$ is complete and cocomplete.**
- **Small limits exist**: small products exist (the axiom of choice provides a set of choice functions); equalizers exist ($\{x : f(x) = g(x)\}$ is a subset); and **limits are built from products and equalizers**, so all small limits exist ✓.
- **Small colimits exist**: small coproducts are disjoint unions; coequalizers are quotient sets; dually all small colimits exist ✓.
