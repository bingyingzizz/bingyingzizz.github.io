⚠️ **Comparison with projectivity**: Projective $\iff$ $\operatorname{Ext}^{n}(M,-) = 0$ for all $n \ge 1$. Flat $\iff$ $\operatorname{Tor}_{n}(-,N) = 0$ for all $n \ge 1$. The criteria are parallel in form, but **flatness is weaker than projectivity** (projective $\implies$ flat, and the converse fails. For example, $\mathbb{Q}$ is flat over $\mathbb{Z}$ but is not projective).
