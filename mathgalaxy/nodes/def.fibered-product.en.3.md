# pullback and pushout　`def.fibered-product`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=def.fibered-product)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./def.fibered-product.md)

where $\sim$ is generated by $f_1(x_0) \sim f_2(x_0)$ ($x_0 \in X_0$). In $\mathbf{Top}$, the pullback carries the subspace topology and the pushout the quotient topology.

In $\mathbf{Ab}$ both reduce to kernels and cokernels: combine $f: X \to S$ and $g: Y \to S$ into $\varphi : X \oplus Y \to S$, $(x,y) \mapsto f(x) - g(y)$, then the pushout is $= \operatorname{coker} \varphi$ and the pullback is $= \ker \varphi$ — first take the direct sum, then quotient out the differences of $f, g$.
