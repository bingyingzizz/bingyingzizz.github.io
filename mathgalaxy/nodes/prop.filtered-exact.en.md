## Statement · **draft**
In $\mathbf{Set}$, **filtered colimits are exact**: when $I$ is a filtered category, the colimit functor

$$\varinjlim : \mathbf{Set}^{I} \longrightarrow \mathbf{Set}$$

preserves all finite limits.
