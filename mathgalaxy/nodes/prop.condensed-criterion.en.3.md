## Notes · **draft**
$\mathbf{CHaus}$ is a pretopos, so the prestandard topology on it is generated by exactly two kinds of covers: **finite disjoint unions** and **surjections**. Items 1 and 2 above are the sheaf conditions for these two kinds — "finite coproducts become finite products" and "quotients become kernels".

Another way to put it: $X$ is a condensed set $\iff$ it turns finite coproducts into finite products and coequalizers into equalizers. Every later construction uses this sentence.
