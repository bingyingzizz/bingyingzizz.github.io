# transfer of exact sequences of topological abelian groups　`prop.exact-sequence-topo-ab`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=prop.exact-sequence-topo-ab)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./prop.exact-sequence-topo-ab.md)

**(Compact lifting condition)** For every compact Hausdorff $K'' \subseteq M''$, there exists a compact Hausdorff $K \subseteq M$ such that $K'' \subseteq v(K)$.

Then the sequence of condensed abelian groups

$$0 \longrightarrow \underline{M'} \xrightarrow{\;\underline{u}\;} \underline{M} \xrightarrow{\;\underline{v}\;} \underline{M''} \longrightarrow 0$$

is also exact.
