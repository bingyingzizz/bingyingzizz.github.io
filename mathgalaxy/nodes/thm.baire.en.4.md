# Baire category theorem　`thm.baire`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=thm.baire)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./thm.baire.md)

⚠️ Completeness cannot be dropped: in $\mathbb{Q}$ every singleton is closed and nowhere dense, and $\mathbb{Q}$ is countable, so $\mathbb{Q}$ is of the first category — it is not a Baire space.
