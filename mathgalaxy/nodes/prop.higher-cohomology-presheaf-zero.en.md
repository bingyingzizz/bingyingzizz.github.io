## Statement · **draft**
Let $M$ be an abelian sheaf on a site $\mathcal{C}$ and $n \ne 0$. Then the presheaf

$$U \longmapsto H^{n}(U,\ M)$$

has **zero associated sheaf**: its sheafification is the zero sheaf.
