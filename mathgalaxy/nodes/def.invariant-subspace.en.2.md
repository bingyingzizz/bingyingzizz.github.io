## Notes · **draft**
⭐ "Invariance" is the prerequisite for "computing block by block": if $V = U_1 \oplus \cdots \oplus U_k$ and each $U_i$ is invariant, then $T$ is **block diagonal** with respect to this decomposition — so eigenvalues, invertibility and powers can all be computed block by block.

⭐ The whole idea of Jordan decomposition is "split $V$ into a direct sum of invariant subspaces as small (as fine) as possible", and the finest such decomposition is exactly the one given by Jordan blocks.
