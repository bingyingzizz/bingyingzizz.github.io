**Relation to the closed graph theorem.** The removal of the closure in Step 2 is the real technical core; the closed graph theorem uses it in reverse (first prove $\pi_1^{-1}$ bounded, then deduce that $u$ is bounded). Thus the two are not independent: **open mapping $\implies$ inverse mapping $\implies$ closed graph**.
