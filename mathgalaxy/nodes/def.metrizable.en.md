## Statement · **draft**
A topological space $(X, \mathcal{T})$ is called **metrizable** if there is a distance $d$ on $X$ such that the topology induced by $d$ is exactly $\mathcal{T}$:

$$\mathcal{T}_{d} = \mathcal{T},\qquad \mathcal{T}_{d} := \{\, U \subseteq X : \forall x \in U\ \exists r > 0,\ B_{d}(x, r) \subseteq U \,\}.$$

Equivalently, $X$ is **homeomorphic** to some metric space, i.e. there is a metric compatible with $\mathcal{T}$.
