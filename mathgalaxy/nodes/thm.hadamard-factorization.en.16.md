# Hadamard factorization theorem　`thm.hadamard-factorization`

[in graph](../#n=thm.hadamard-factorization)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./thm.hadamard-factorization.md)

**Step 7. $h$ is a polynomial.** By Step 4, on $|z|\le R$ we have $\operatorname{Re}(h-h(0))\le C_{1}R^{\rho+\varepsilon_{1}}+|\operatorname{Re}h(0)|=:A$. Apply Step 6 to $g=h-h(0)$ with $r=R/2$ to obtain $|h(z)-h(0)|\le2A$ ($|z|\le R/2$). Cauchy's estimates (`thm.cauchy-inequality`) then give, for $k>\rho+\varepsilon_{1}$, the bound $|h^{(k)}(0)|\le k!\,2A/(R/2)^{k}\to0$ ($R\to\infty$). Hence $h^{(k)}(0)=0$, so $h$ is a polynomial of degree $\le\lfloor\rho\rfloor$.
