## Statement · **draft**
An object of a category $\mathcal{C}$, say $0$, is a **zero object** if it is **both terminal and initial**.

The **biproduct** of a family $(M_{i})_{i \in I}$ is the object where product and coproduct coincide, written $\bigoplus_{i \in I} M_{i}$; in algebra it is called the **direct sum**.
