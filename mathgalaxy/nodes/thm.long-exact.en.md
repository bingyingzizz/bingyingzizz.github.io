## Statement · **draft**
Let $\mathcal{A}$ be an abelian category and $0 \to K \to L \to M \to 0$ a short exact sequence of complexes. Then there is a **long exact sequence**

$$\cdots \to H^{n}(K) \to H^{n}(L) \to H^{n}(M) \xrightarrow{\ \delta\ } H^{n+1}(K) \to \cdots$$

where $\delta$ is called the **connecting homomorphism**.

Corollary: $H^{n} : \mathbf{K}(\mathcal{A}) \to \mathcal{A}$ is a **cohomology functor**.
