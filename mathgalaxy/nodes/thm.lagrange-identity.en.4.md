## Notes · **draft**
**Geometric reading**: the left side is the determinant of the Gram matrix of the two vectors, i.e. the square of the area of the parallelogram they span; the right side is that area squared in coordinates. The full expansion is on the edge `imp.lagrange-identity-proof`. ⚠ The coordinate expression holds only in an orthonormal basis; in a general basis one corrects it by the Gram matrix.
