## Statement · **draft**
$\zeta$ extends to a meromorphic function on $\operatorname{Re}s>0$, whose only pole is a simple pole at $s=1$ with residue $1$; on $\operatorname{Re}s>0$ one has $\zeta(s)=\frac{s}{s-1}-s\int_{1}^{\infty}\{x\}\,x^{-s-1}\,\mathrm{d}x$.
