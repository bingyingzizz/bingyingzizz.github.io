## Statement · **draft**
For $x\ge1$, $\psi(x)=\sum_{k\ge1}\theta\bigl(x^{1/k}\bigr)$. The sum is finite: $p^{k}\le x$ forces $k\le\log_{2}x$.

## Notes · **draft**
This is the first bridge between $\psi$ and $\theta$: group the terms $n=p^{k}$ by $k$ (i.e. by the exponent of the prime power).
