## Statement · **draft**
Let $\mathcal{C}$ be a category and $X \in \mathcal{C}$. Then $\mathbb{Z}\cdot h_{X}$ is a **projective object** in the presheaf category $\widehat{\mathcal{C}}(\mathbf{Ab})$; more generally, $\mathbb{Z}\cdot T$ (with $T$ any presheaf) is also projective.
