## Notes · **draft**
**What "effective" means.** Given an equivalence relation, first take the quotient $Y = X/R$ (always possible), then pull back $Y$ along the quotient map to $X \times X$. In a general category the pullback can be **larger than $R$** (it glues extra things). Effective equivalence relations are exactly those that pull back to **$R$ itself**.
