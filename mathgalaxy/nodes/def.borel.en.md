## Statement · **draft**
Let $X$ be a topological space. The **Borel $\sigma$-algebra** on $X$ is the $\sigma$-algebra generated by all open sets:

$\mathfrak{B}_X := \mathcal{M}(\{ U \subseteq X : U$ is open $\})$

Sets in $\mathfrak{B}_X$ are called **Borel sets**: open sets, closed sets, countable intersections of open sets ($G_\delta$), countable unions of closed sets ($F_\sigma$), and so on all belong to $\mathfrak{B}_X$.
