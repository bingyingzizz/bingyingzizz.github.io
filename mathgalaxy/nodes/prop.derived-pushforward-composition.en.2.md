## Notes · **draft**
**Proof.** This is another special case of the Grothendieck spectral sequence: $f_{*}$ sends injective sheaves to $f'_{*}$-acyclic objects ✓, so applying the Grothendieck spectral sequence at the level $\Gamma\bigl(X'', \cdot\bigr)$ gives the result ✓; when $f_{*}$ preserves injectives strictly, the spectral sequence degenerates to the equality above ✓. ∎
