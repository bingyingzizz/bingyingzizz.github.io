# Fiber Metric on a Vector Bundle　`prop.bundle-metric`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=prop.bundle-metric)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./prop.bundle-metric.md)

**Corollary: the structure group reduces to $\operatorname{O}(n)$**: with a fibre metric, the transition functions of $TM$ can be pointwise Gram–Schmidt orthogonalized, reducing the structure group from $\operatorname{GL}(n,\mathbb{R})$ to $\operatorname{O}(n)$. This is the technical prerequisite for doing Riemannian geometry on an arbitrary manifold.

**Dual and tensor bundles work the same way**: the metrics on $E^{\ast}$, $\bigwedge^{k}E$ and $E \otimes F$ are induced pointwise from those on $E, F$ (the inner product of a tensor product). Hence "every tensor bundle carries a fibre metric" is a corollary.
