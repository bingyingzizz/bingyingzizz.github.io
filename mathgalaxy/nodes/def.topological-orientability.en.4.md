If $V$ is connected, preserving orientation at one point implies preserving it everywhere (the sign on local homology varies continuously and can take only two values). For $n=1$ this is the usual notion of being increasing, and for $n=2$ it is the usual notion of an orientation-preserving homeomorphism.
