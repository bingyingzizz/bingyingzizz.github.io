# subcategory　`def.subcategory`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=def.subcategory)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./def.subcategory.md)

**Examples**: $\mathbf{Ab} \hookrightarrow \mathbf{Grp}$ is a full subcategory (homomorphisms of abelian groups are exactly their homomorphisms as groups); $\mathbf{CHaus} \hookrightarrow \mathbf{Top}$ is a full subcategory;

$\mathbf{Grp} \to \mathbf{Set}$ is not a subcategory (not even the objects are the same sets).

⭐ “Reflective” and “coreflective” subcategories add one more condition to a full subcategory: the inclusion functor has an adjoint.
