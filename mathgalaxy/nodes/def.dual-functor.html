陈述

对偶函子 (−)∗:VectKop→VectK(-)^{\ast}:\mathbf{Vect}_K^{\mathrm{op}}\to\mathbf{Vect}_K(−)∗:VectKop​→VectK​ 定义如下：

  • 对象上 V↦V∗=Hom⁡K(V,K)V\mapsto V^{\ast}=\operatorname{Hom}_K(V,K)V↦V∗=HomK​(V,K)；
  • 态射上把 T:V→WT:V\to WT:V→W 送到 T∗:W∗→V∗T^{\ast}:W^{\ast}\to V^{\ast}T∗:W∗→V∗。

由于 (S∘T)∗=T∗∘S∗(S\circ T)^{\ast}=T^{\ast}\circ S^{\ast}(S∘T)∗=T∗∘S∗ 且 (id⁡)∗=id⁡(\operatorname{id})^{\ast}=\operatorname{id}(id)∗=id，它是从 VectKop\mathbf{Vect}_K^{\mathrm{op}}VectKop​ 出发的协变函子——等价地说，是 VectK\mathbf{Vect}_KVectK​ 上的反变函子。

自然变换 ηV:V→V∗∗\eta_V:V\to V^{\ast\ast}ηV​:V→V∗∗，ηV(v)(φ)=φ(v)\eta_V(v)(\varphi)=\varphi(v)ηV​(v)(φ)=φ(v)，给出 1VectK  ⟹  (−)∗∗1_{\mathbf{Vect}_K}\implies(-)^{\ast\ast}1VectK​​⟹(−)∗∗；有限维时 η\etaη 是同构，对偶于是给出 VectKfd\mathbf{Vect}_K^{\mathrm{fd}}VectKfd​ 与自身对偶范畴的等价。

说明

反变不等于从对偶范畴出发：这只是把反变函子写成 VectKop\mathbf{Vect}_K^{\mathrm{op}}VectKop​ 上的协变函子，复合顺序因此要倒过来。

无限维：ηV\eta_VηV​ 单射但一般不满，V→V∗∗V\to V^{\ast\ast}V→V∗∗ 不是同构；对偶不给出整个 VectK\mathbf{Vect}_KVectK​ 的自对偶。

与 Hom 函子：(−)∗=Hom⁡K(−,K)(-)^{\ast}=\operatorname{Hom}_K(-,K)(−)∗=HomK​(−,K) 是反变 Hom 函子在 W=KW=KW=K 的特例（见 Hom 函子节点）。

Statement draft

The dual functor (−)∗:VectKop→VectK(-)^{\ast}:\mathbf{Vect}_K^{\mathrm{op}}\to\mathbf{Vect}_K(−)∗:VectKop​→VectK​ is defined as follows:

  • on objects, V↦V∗=Hom⁡K(V,K)V\mapsto V^{\ast}=\operatorname{Hom}_K(V,K)V↦V∗=HomK​(V,K);
  • on morphisms, it sends T:V→WT:V\to WT:V→W to T∗:W∗→V∗T^{\ast}:W^{\ast}\to V^{\ast}T∗:W∗→V∗.

Since (S∘T)∗=T∗∘S∗(S\circ T)^{\ast}=T^{\ast}\circ S^{\ast}(S∘T)∗=T∗∘S∗ and (id⁡)∗=id⁡(\operatorname{id})^{\ast}=\operatorname{id}(id)∗=id, it is a covariant functor from VectKop\mathbf{Vect}_K^{\mathrm{op}}VectKop​ — equivalently, a contravariant functor on VectK\mathbf{Vect}_KVectK​.

The natural transformation ηV:V→V∗∗\eta_V:V\to V^{\ast\ast}ηV​:V→V∗∗, ηV(v)(φ)=φ(v)\eta_V(v)(\varphi)=\varphi(v)ηV​(v)(φ)=φ(v), gives 1VectK  ⟹  (−)∗∗1_{\mathbf{Vect}_K}\implies(-)^{\ast\ast}1VectK​​⟹(−)∗∗; in finite dimension η\etaη is an isomorphism, so duality gives an equivalence between VectKfd\mathbf{Vect}_K^{\mathrm{fd}}VectKfd​ and its opposite category.

Notes draft

Contravariant is not the same as starting from the opposite category: this is only a way of writing a contravariant functor as a covariant functor on VectKop\mathbf{Vect}_K^{\mathrm{op}}VectKop​, which is why the order of composition is reversed.

Infinite dimension: ηV\eta_VηV​ is injective but generally not surjective, so V→V∗∗V\to V^{\ast\ast}V→V∗∗ is not an isomorphism; duality does not make all of VectK\mathbf{Vect}_KVectK​ self-dual.

Relation to the Hom functor: (−)∗=Hom⁡K(−,K)(-)^{\ast}=\operatorname{Hom}_K(-,K)(−)∗=HomK​(−,K) is the special case W=KW=KW=K of the contravariant Hom functor (see the Hom functor node).
