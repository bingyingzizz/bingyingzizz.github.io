Statement

Let EEE be a set. Then the constant sheaf on Top\mathbf{Top}Top is the sheaf given by the discrete space EEE:

E~Top  ≅  Edisc‾,\widetilde{E}_{\mathbf{Top}} \;\cong\; \underline{E^{\mathrm{disc}}},ETop​≅Edisc​,

and E~Top(X)=E\widetilde{E}_{\mathbf{Top}}(X) = EETop​(X)=E   ⟺  \iff⟺ XXX is connected.

Notes

Proof. The constant sheaf on XXX takes as values the "locally constant EEE-valued maps" ✓ (sheafification turns the constant presheaf into locally constant maps), while the sheaf given by the discrete space EEE takes as values the continuous maps X→EdiscX \to E^{\mathrm{disc}}X→Edisc — which are exactly the locally constant maps ✓, so the two agree ✓. And "every locally constant map on XXX is constant"   ⟺  \iff⟺ XXX connected ✓. ∎

⭐ This connects "constant" with "discrete": the condensed set of a discrete space is the constant sheaf.
