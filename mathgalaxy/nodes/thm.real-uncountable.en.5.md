# uncountability of the reals　`thm.real-uncountable`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=thm.real-uncountable)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./thm.real-uncountable.md)

⚠ The whole proof uses **only the completeness of $\mathbb{R}$** (the least-upper-bound principle); cardinal theory is not needed. The half saying $|\mathbb{R}| = 2^{\aleph_0}$ matches binary expansions against $\mathcal{P}(\mathbb{N})$ and then quotes Cantor's theorem.
