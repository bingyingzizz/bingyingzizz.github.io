# Balancedness of Ext　`thm.ext-balanced`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=thm.ext-balanced)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./thm.ext-balanced.md)

📌 **General formulation**: $\operatorname{Hom}$ is a bifunctor balanced with respect to projectives and injectives, so its derived functors can be computed using a resolution in either variable. The same reasoning applies to $\otimes$, which explains why the two computations of Tor agree over a commutative ring.

⚠️ The equality assumes that **both resolutions exist** (there are enough projectives and enough injectives). If this assumption fails, only the Hom in the derived category is available.
