# Codazzi–Mainardi Equations　`thm.codazzi-mainardi`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=thm.codazzi-mainardi)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./thm.codazzi-mainardi.md)

⭐ **Not an assumption but a necessary condition**: $\mathbb{R}^{3}$ is flat, so every surface that embeds in it automatically satisfies Codazzi. Together with the Gauss equation it forms the complete compatibility condition for "being realizable as a surface in $\mathbb{R}^{3}$" (`thm.fundamental-theorem-surfaces`).

**Coordinate version**: an equivalent form is $n_{ij} = n_{ji}$; the symmetric way of writing it is $\nabla_{i}h_{kj} = \nabla_{j}h_{ki}$ (with $h$ symmetric the two agree).
