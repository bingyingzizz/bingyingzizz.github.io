# Tietze　`thm.tietze-banach`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=thm.tietze-banach)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./thm.tietze-banach.md)

⭐ **Why condensed mathematics uses this.** The main theorem of §8.2, acyclicity of real Banach spaces on compact Hausdorff spaces, covers a general compact Hausdorff space $S$ by a Stonean space $S_{0}$ and then eliminates the discrepancy;

the discrepancy term lies in a function space $C(K, V)$, and **eliminating it requires extension**, supplied by this Tietze theorem.

⚠️ **General topology also has a real-valued version** (the Tietze extension theorem). Here **the target can be any real Banach space**.
