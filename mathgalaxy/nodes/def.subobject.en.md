## Statement · **draft**
Regard a monomorphism $Y \rightarrowtail X$ as a **subobject** of $X$, written $Y \subseteq X$. The **intersection** of subobjects and the **preimage** along a morphism are defined in @@def.intersection-subobject@@ and @@def.preimage-subobject@@ respectively; both constructions are written out below as an overview, with the details deferred to those two nodes.

$$Y \cap Z := Y \times_{X} Z, \qquad f^{-1}(Y) := Y \times_{X} X'$$
