## Notes · **draft**
By the definition of convexity, for any $x_{0} \in C$ and $y \in C$ the whole segment $(1-t)x_{0} + t y$ ($t \in [0,1]$) lies in $C$, that is, $C$ is star-shaped with respect to $x_{0}$; the proof is on the edge @@imp.convex-star-shaped-every-point@@.
