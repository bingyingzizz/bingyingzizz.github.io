# Monomorphisms and Epimorphisms under the Precanonical Topology　`prop.precanonical-mono-epi`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=prop.precanonical-mono-epi)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./prop.precanonical-mono-epi.md)

$f$ **is a monomorphism (epimorphism)** $\iff X^{\sharp} \to Y^{\sharp}$ **is a monomorphism (epimorphism)**.

## Notes · **draft**
So "which maps are monic and which are epic" gives the same answer in $\mathcal{C}$ as in the category of sheaves. This matters later: in Giraud's theorem, when $\mathcal{C}$ is replaced by $\mathcal{T}$ with the standard topology, the mono/epi relations do not distort.
