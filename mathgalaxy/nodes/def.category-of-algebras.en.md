## Statement · **draft**
Fix a commutative unital ring $R$. **The category of $R$-algebras** $R\text{-}\mathbf{Alg}$: objects are unital associative $R$-algebras (multiplication need not be commutative) and morphisms preserve $1$ and are homomorphisms of $R$-algebras.
