# Contour Integral　`def.contour-integral`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=def.contour-integral)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./def.contour-integral.md)

$$\int_\gamma f\,\mathrm{d}z = \int_\gamma (u\,\mathrm{d}x - v\,\mathrm{d}y) + i\int_\gamma (v\,\mathrm{d}x + u\,\mathrm{d}y).$$

When $\gamma$ is a closed curve we write $\oint_\gamma$; on the reversed curve $\gamma^{-1}$ the integral changes sign: $\int_{\gamma^{-1}} f\,\mathrm{d}z = -\int_\gamma f\,\mathrm{d}z$.

## Notes · **draft**
⭐ That real integral on the right is the whole definition: **a complex contour integral is a complex-valued Riemann integral over the real parameter $t$**.
