Statement

The functor F:C→SetF : \mathcal{C} \to \mathbf{Set}F:C→Set is represented by X∈CX \in \mathcal{C}X∈C   ⟺  \iff⟺ hX≅Fh^{X} \cong FhX≅F.

In other words, “there exists a universal element (X,s)(X, s)(X,s)” and “FFF is isomorphic to Hom⁡C(X,−)\operatorname{Hom}_{\mathcal{C}}(X, -)HomC​(X,−)” are equivalent.

Notes

This connects two ways of describing representability. A universal element describes it at the level of elements, and hX≅Fh^{X} \cong FhX≅F describes it at the level of functors. To verify representability, one often uses the first description by finding the element sss. To use representability, one often uses the second description to transport calculations directly along the isomorphism.
