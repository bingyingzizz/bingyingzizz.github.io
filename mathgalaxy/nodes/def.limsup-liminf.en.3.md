## Notes · **draft**
⭐ The upper limit is "the largest of the limits of all convergent subsequences" (counting $\pm \infty$ as well) — hence **every** sequence of real numbers has upper and lower limits, whereas only convergent sequences have a limit: they are a **relaxed version** of the limit.
