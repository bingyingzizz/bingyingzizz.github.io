## Notes · **draft**
**Examples**: in $\mathbf{Set}$, $Y^X=\hom(X,Y)$; in $G\text{-}\mathbf{Set}$, $Y^X$ with the conjugation action $(g\cdot f)(x)=g f(g^{-1}x)$.

**Counterexample**: $\mathbf{Top}$ is not cartesian closed in general (the compact-open topology makes continuity of evaluation too strong); the category $\mathbf{CGWH}$ of compactly generated weak Hausdorff spaces is, see @@prop.cg-cartesian-closed@@.
