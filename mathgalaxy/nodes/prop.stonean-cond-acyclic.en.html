Statement

Let SSS be a Stonean space (an extremally disconnected compact Hausdorff space) and MMM a condensed abelian group. Then for all n>0n > 0n>0,

Hcondn(S, M)=0.H^{n}_{\mathrm{cond}}(S,\ M) = 0.Hcondn​(S, M)=0.

Notes

In one sentence: a Stonean space is invisible to condensed cohomology — as long as the coefficients are a condensed abelian group, all higher cohomology vanishes.

Why.

The section functor Γ(S,−)=Hom⁡(Z[S],−)\Gamma(S, -) = \operatorname{Hom}(\mathbb{Z}[S], -)Γ(S,−)=Hom(Z[S],−) is exact on a Stonean space: extremal disconnectedness makes Z[S]\mathbb{Z}[S]Z[S] projective, so Hom⁡(Z[S],−)\operatorname{Hom}(\mathbb{Z}[S], -)Hom(Z[S],−) preserves epimorphisms, and the right derived functor measures exactly "whether epimorphisms are preserved" at n>0n>0n>0.

⭐ This is the starting point of the whole chapter: every later step of "acyclicity" reduces a general space to a Stonean one (first Stoneify, then use Čech complexity to measure the deviation), and this Stonean cell is already zero.

⚠️ Note that this holds for any condensed abelian group MMM; it does not require MMM to come from a topological group.

Proof. The section functor is Γ(S,−)=Hom⁡Z(Z[S],−)\Gamma(S, -) = \operatorname{Hom}_{\mathbb{Z}}(\mathbb{Z}[S], -)Γ(S,−)=HomZ​(Z[S],−).

  • Z[S]\mathbb{Z}[S]Z[S] is a projective object when SSS is Stonean: extremal disconnectedness gives projectivity over the lattice of clopen sets of SSS (when S=βIS = \beta IS=βI, Z[S]=Z[βI]\mathbb{Z}[S] = \mathbb{Z}[\beta I]Z[S]=Z[βI] is free, and free objects are projective);
  • hence Hom⁡Z(Z[S],−)\operatorname{Hom}_{\mathbb{Z}}(\mathbb{Z}[S], -)HomZ​(Z[S],−) sends epimorphisms to epimorphisms — that is, it is exact;
  • the right derived functor RnΓ(S,−)R^{n}\Gamma(S,-)RnΓ(S,−) measures exactly "how far this functor fails to be exact at step nnn"; the functor itself is exact, so all vanish: 000 (n>0n>0n>0). ∎
