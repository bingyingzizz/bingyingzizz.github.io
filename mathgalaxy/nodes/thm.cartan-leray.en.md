## Statement · **draft**
If $\mathfrak{X}$ is a **cover** of $X$ and $M$ is an abelian sheaf, then there is a spectral sequence

$$E^{p,q}_{2} := \check{H}^{p}\bigl(\mathfrak{X},\ H^{q}(M)\bigr) \;\Longrightarrow\; H^{p+q}(X, M).$$
