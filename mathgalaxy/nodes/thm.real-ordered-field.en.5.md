📌 The route to (iii) has two steps (see the proof on the edge): first prove that **every Cauchy sequence in $\mathbb{R}$ converges** (extract a rational Cauchy sequence from the representatives by a diagonal argument), then derive the least upper bound principle from Cauchy completeness (bisection produces an increasing sequence and a decreasing sequence of upper bounds, squeezed to the same limit).
