# Global Dimension of a Principal Ideal Domain　`cor.gldim-pid`

[in graph](../#n=cor.gldim-pid)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./cor.gldim-pid.md)

⭐ **This is equivalent to the result on Ext for abelian groups.** Expressing $\operatorname{gl.dim}\mathbb{Z} = 1$ in terms of $\operatorname{Ext}$ gives “$\operatorname{Ext}^{n}_{\mathbb{Z}}(M, N) = 0$ for every $n \ge 2$,” precisely the proposition on Ext for abelian groups.

The two propositions express the same fact in different ways.

⚠️ **Why a PID?** The proof uses the ring hypothesis only to ensure that submodules of free modules are free. This fails over $k[x,y]$, and over $k[x,y]$ there is indeed a module with $\operatorname{pd} = 2$ (namely $k$ itself). The global dimension rises to $2$.
