where $P$ is a polynomial of degree $\le \rho$ and $p \le \rho$ is a **single** fixed integer used for all factors (the Weierstrass construction chooses it separately for each factor).

In particular, when $\rho < 1$, we have $p = 0$, hence $\sum |a_{n}|^{-1} < \infty$ (there may still be infinitely many zeros. For example, $\prod_{n}(1 - z/n^{2})$ has order $\frac{1}{2}$ and infinitely many zeros).
