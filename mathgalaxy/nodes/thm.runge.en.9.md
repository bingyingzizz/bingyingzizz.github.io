# Runge approximation theorem　`thm.runge`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=thm.runge)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./thm.runge.md)

. Truncating at $k\le N_{i}$ uniformly approximates $1/(b_{i+1}-z)$ to arbitrary accuracy by a rational function with poles only at $b_{i}$. Repeating this step shows that $1/(b-z)$ can be uniformly approximated by rational functions with poles only at $b^{\prime}$. Taking linear combinations of these basic terms gives uniform approximation of any rational function on $K$, moving its poles one at a time.
