# Projective and Injective Objects　`def.projective-object`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=def.projective-object)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./def.projective-object.md)

Expanded as a lifting condition: for every epimorphism $A \twoheadrightarrow B$ and every $P \to B$ there exists a lift $P \to A$ making the triangle commute:

$$\begin{array}{ccc} & P & \\ \swarrow & \downarrow & \searrow \\ A & \twoheadrightarrow & B \end{array}$$

Dually, injectivity of $I$ says that every $A \rightarrowtail B$ and every $A \to I$ extend to $B \to I$.
