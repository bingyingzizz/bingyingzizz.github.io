Notes

⭐ The geometric meaning of "best approximation": PUvP_U vPU​v is the point of UUU closest to vvv, and "closest" is well defined by the norm coming from the inner product.

This is the common source of least squares and of Fourier series (truncating to the first nnn terms is optimal).

⭐ The explicit formula shows the power of an orthonormal basis: the projection need not solve a linear system, only compute kkk inner products.

⚠ "UUU finite-dimensional" or "UUU closed" is necessary: in infinite dimension, when the subspace is not closed, the closest point may fail to exist (only the infimum exists).
