# dual (transpose) map　`def.dual-map`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=def.dual-map)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./def.dual-map.md)

**Relation to the transpose matrix**: the transpose matrix is the matrix of $T^{\ast}$ in the **dual bases**; the two are objects at different levels (a map versus a matrix).

**Complex case**: in an inner-product space the corresponding notion is the conjugate transpose (adjoint), see `def.operator-adjoint`; the difference is that duality uses $K$-linear functionals, while the inner product is conjugate-linear in the second variable.
