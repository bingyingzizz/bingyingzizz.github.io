# monomorphism, epimorphism and isomorphism　`def.mono`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=def.mono)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./def.mono.md)

**Isomorphism** is another notion: $f : X \to Y$ is an **isomorphism** if there is $g : Y \to X$ with

$$g \circ f = 1_{X}, \qquad f \circ g = 1_{Y}$$

then $g$ is the **inverse** of $f$, written $f^{-1}$, and is uniquely determined by $f$. A category in which every morphism that is both mono and epi is an isomorphism is called **balanced**; $\mathbf{Set}$ and $\mathbf{Ab}$ are balanced, $\mathbf{Ring}$ is not.
