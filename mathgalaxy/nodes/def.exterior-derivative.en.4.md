# Exterior Derivative: Coordinate Formula on Euclidean Open Sets　`def.exterior-derivative`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=def.exterior-derivative)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./def.exterior-derivative.md)

A form with $\mathrm{d}\omega = 0$ is called **closed**, and a form $\omega$ with $\omega = \mathrm{d}\eta$ is called **exact**.

By $\mathrm{d}^{2}=0$, every exact form is closed;

the converse fails ($\frac{x\,\mathrm{d}y-y\,\mathrm{d}x}{x^{2}+y^{2}}$ on $\mathbb{R}^{2}\setminus\{0\}$ is closed but not exact).
