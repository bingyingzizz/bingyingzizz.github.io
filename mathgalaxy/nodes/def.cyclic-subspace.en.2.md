# Cyclic Subspace and Cyclic Vector　`def.cyclic-subspace`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=def.cyclic-subspace)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./def.cyclic-subspace.md)

is the **cyclic subspace generated by** $v$; if $K[T]v=V$, then $v$ is a **cyclic vector** for $T$. A space $V$ that has a cyclic vector is called **cyclic**.

## Notes · **draft**
$K[T]v$ is the smallest invariant subspace containing $v$ that is stable under $T$; conversely, every invariant subspace containing $v$ and stable under $T$ contains all $T^kv$.

Let $\mu_v$ be the monic polynomial with $p(T)v=0$ (it exists and is unique because $K[x]$ is a principal ideal domain); then $\dim_K K[T]v=\deg\mu_v$, and as a $K[x]$-module, $K[T]v\cong K[x]/(\mu_v)$.
