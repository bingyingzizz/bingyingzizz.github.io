# Étale Sheaf　`def.etale-sheaf`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=def.etale-sheaf)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./def.etale-sheaf.md)

To do étale cohomology one further needs: $\mathbf{Sh}(X_{\text{et}}, \mathbf{Ab})$ is a Grothendieck category, it has enough injectives (again via "enough points", with points taken to be geometric points), and compatibility with the long exact sequences of $f_{*}$, $R^{n}f_{*}$ — that is another whole apparatus.
