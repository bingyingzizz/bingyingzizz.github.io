# singular homology groups　`def.singular-homology`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=def.singular-homology)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./def.singular-homology.md)

⚠ **This is not the same as `def.singular-chain` (cubical chains on a manifold)**: there the basis consists of maps $c : [0, 1]^{k} \to M$ (cubes), here of maps $\sigma : \Delta^{p} \to X$ (simplices). The two models give isomorphic homology groups, but the **boundary formulas differ**, and the two $\partial$'s must not be mixed.

💡 Intuition: a singular $p$-simplex is "a (possibly collapsed) $p$-dimensional triangle drawn on $X$"; a singular chain is "a signed stack of such triangles".
