## Notes · **draft**
**It comes from integrability conditions.**

Finding coordinates that flatten $g_{ij}$ to $\delta_{ij}$ is equivalent to solving $g_{ij} = \delta_{\alpha\beta}\frac{\partial y^{\alpha}}{\partial x^{i}}\frac{\partial y^{\beta}}{\partial x^{j}}$;
