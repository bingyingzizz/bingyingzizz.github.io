## Notes · **draft**
⭐ (i) and (ii) in fact share one proof: in both cases one has

$$\mathcal{A}[S] = \mathbb{Z}_\blacksquare[S] \otimes^{L}_{\mathbb{Z}_\blacksquare} \mathcal{A},$$

and $C$ is, in $D(\mathbf{Solid})$, an $\mathcal{A}$-module, so

$$\operatorname{RHom}(\mathbb{Z}_\blacksquare[S], C) \cong \operatorname{RHom}_{\mathcal{A}}(\mathbb{Z}_\blacksquare[S] \otimes^{L}_{\mathbb{Z}_\blacksquare} \mathcal{A},\ C),$$

The left-hand side is $\operatorname{RHom}(\mathbb{Z}[S], C)$ ($C$ solid), and the right-hand side is $\operatorname{RHom}_{\mathcal{A}}(\mathcal{A}[S], C)$.
