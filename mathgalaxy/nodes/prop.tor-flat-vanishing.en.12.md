# Flatness and the Vanishing of Tor　`prop.tor-flat-vanishing`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=prop.tor-flat-vanishing)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./prop.tor-flat-vanishing.md)

⚠️ Here $\operatorname{Tor}_{n}^{R}(F, -)$ treats $F$ as a **right** module. Over a noncommutative ring, right flatness of $F$ and left flatness of $F$ (that is, $\operatorname{Tor}_{n}^{R}(-, F) = 0$) are **two distinct conditions**. This result concerns right flatness. Over a commutative ring the two conditions coincide ✓.
