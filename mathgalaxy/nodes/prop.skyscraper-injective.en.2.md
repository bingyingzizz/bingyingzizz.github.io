# Skyscraper Sheaves Are Injective　`prop.skyscraper-injective`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=prop.skyscraper-injective)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./prop.skyscraper-injective.md)

Conversely, if $i_{x,*}A$ is injective in $\mathbf{Ab}(X)$, then $A$ is injective in $\mathbf{Ab}$.

## Notes · **draft**
**Proof (moving injectivity to the stalk).** The key is a natural isomorphism: for every sheaf $\mathcal{F}$,

$$\operatorname{Hom}_{\mathbf{Ab}(X)}\bigl(\mathcal{F},\ i_{x,*}A\bigr) \;\cong\; \operatorname{Hom}_{\mathbf{Ab}}\bigl(\mathcal{F}_{x},\ A\bigr),$$

coming from the adjunction $i_{x}^{-1} \dashv i_{x,*}$ and "$i_{x}^{-1}\mathcal{F} = \mathcal{F}_{x}$".
