Notes

Why a "chain" rather than "one function": continuation is done step by step — each step moves the disk a little, and on overlaps the uniqueness theorem guarantees the two elements are restrictions of the same function. The chain is what makes this continuous.

⭐ **The carrier is the power series**: every element can be expanded at its centre into a convergent power series (全纯函数的 Taylor 展开), so continuation can also be seen as "changing the expansion point along the curve". The radius of convergence is determined by the distance to the nearest singularity — hitting a singularity breaks the continuation.

⚠️ **The result of continuation depends on the path**. The standard example: log⁡z\log zlogz starting from 111 and going once counterclockwise around the unit circle returns to 111 with the value 2πi2\pi i2πi. When is it path-independent? That is exactly what 单值性定理 answers.
