# Derived Hom and Tensor of Discrete Groups Are Constant　`prop.discrete-cond-rhom-constant`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=prop.discrete-cond-rhom-constant)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./prop.discrete-cond-rhom-constant.md)

$$\operatorname{RHom}^{\mathrm{cond}}_{\mathbb{Z}}(M,\ N) \;\cong\; \mathrm{c}\bigl(\operatorname{RHom}^{\mathrm{abs}}_{\mathbb{Z}}(M,\ N)\bigr), \qquad M \otimes^{\mathbf{L},\mathrm{cond}}_{\mathbb{Z}} N \;\cong\; \mathrm{c}\bigl(M \otimes^{\mathbf{L},\mathrm{abs}}_{\mathbb{Z}} N\bigr),$$

where the superscript $\mathrm{cond}$ means "computed in condensed abelian groups", $\mathrm{abs}$ means "computed in abstract abelian groups", and $\mathrm{c}(A)$ denotes the **constant condensed group**.
