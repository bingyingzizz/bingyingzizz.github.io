**Lemma (one Kummer step)**: Suppose $F$ contains a primitive $m$th root of unity $\zeta$ and $E/F$ is a cyclic extension of degree $m$. Then $E=F(\beta)$ and $\beta^{m}\in F$. To prove this, choose a generator $\sigma$ and regard $\sigma$ on $E$ as a $F$-linear transformation. We have $\sigma^{m}=1$, and $x^{m}-1$ splits over $F$ with no repeated roots, so $\sigma$ is diagonalizable and all its eigenvalues are $m$th roots of unity. **The key is that the minimal polynomial of $\sigma$ is exactly $x^{m}-1$**.
