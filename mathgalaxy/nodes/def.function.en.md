## Statement · **reviewed**
Let $f$ be a relation from $A$ to $B$. Then $f$ is a **map** (written $f : A \to B$) if and only if every input has exactly one output:

$$\forall a \in A, \exists! b \in B, (a, b) \in f$$

Uniqueness is what makes the notation $f(a) = b$ unambiguous; in that case $\operatorname{dom} f = A$, and the **image** of $f$ is $f(A) = \{ f(a) : a \in A \}$.
