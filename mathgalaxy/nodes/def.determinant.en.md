## Statement · **draft**
For $A = (a_{ij}) \in M_n(K)$, define the **determinant**

$$\det A := \sum_{\sigma \in S_n} \operatorname{sgn}(\sigma)\ \prod_{i=1}^{n} a_{i,\sigma(i)}$$

($S_n$ is the symmetric group on $n$ letters and $\operatorname{sgn}$ is the sign of a permutation; there are $n!$ terms.)

Viewing the columns of $A$ as vectors in $V = K^{n}$, the map $\det$ is the unique $D$ from the existence and uniqueness theorem for determinants.
