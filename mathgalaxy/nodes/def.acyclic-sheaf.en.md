## Statement · **draft**
Let $\mathcal{C}$ be a site. An abelian sheaf $M$ on it is called **acyclic** if $H^{n}(M) = 0$ for all $n \ne 0$.

## Notes · **draft**
⚠️ A special warning: **this definition depends on the site $\mathcal{C}$, not only on the topos $\widetilde{\mathcal{C}}$** — changing the site (with the same topos) may change acyclicity. This contrasts with "the notion of a sheaf depends only on the topos".
