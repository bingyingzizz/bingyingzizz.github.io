**Conclusion.** $f=z^{m}e^{h}\prod_{n}E_{p}(z/a_{n})=z^{m}e^{P}\prod_{n}E_{p}(z/a_{n})$ gives the Hadamard factorization. ∎ Steps 1–3 establish convergence and cancel the zeros. Step 4 uses the good-radius lemma, elementary estimates, and Borel–Carathéodory. Steps 5–7 express the zero-free quotient as the exponential of a polynomial.
