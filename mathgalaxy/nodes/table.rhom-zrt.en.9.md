# Table of Derived Hom for Z, R and T　`table.rhom-zrt`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=table.rhom-zrt)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./table.rhom-zrt.md)

- Third row, second column $0$: **Compact Hausdorff $\to$ real Banach** vanishes in every degree.
- Third row, first column $\mathbb{Z}[-1]$: By $0 \to \mathbb{Z} \to \mathbb{R} \to T \to 0$, the middle term is $0$, leaving only $\mathbb{Z}$ shifted down by one degree.
- Bottom right $\mathbb{Z}$: This follows similarly from $0 \to \mathbb{Z} \to \mathbb{R} \to T \to 0$ and $\operatorname{RHom}(T, \mathbb{R}) = 0$.
