# Chart and Transition Maps　`def.manifold-chart`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=def.manifold-chart)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./def.manifold-chart.md)

A **coordinate chart** on $M$, of dimension $n$, is a pair $(U,\varphi)$, where $U \subseteq M$ is open and $\varphi : U \to \varphi(U) \subseteq \mathbb{R}^{n}$ is a homeomorphism (with $\varphi(U)$ open in $\mathbb{R}^{n}$).

$U$ is the **coordinate neighbourhood** and $\varphi$ the **coordinate map**; the components $x^{i} := \varphi^{i}$ are the **local coordinates**, i.e. $p \mapsto \bigl(x^{1}(p),\ldots,x^{n}(p)\bigr)$.

When $n = 0$, $\mathbb{R}^{0}$ is a single point, so a chart only records whether the point lies in $U$.
