# Short Exact Sequences Give Distinguished Triangles　`prop.exact-to-triangle`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=prop.exact-to-triangle)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./prop.exact-to-triangle.md)

$$K^{\bullet} \xrightarrow{\;f\;} L^{\bullet} \xrightarrow{\;g\;} M^{\bullet} \longrightarrow K^{\bullet}[1].$$

## Notes · **draft**
Essentially it says that this short exact sequence "is" a certain mapping-cone triangle (isomorphic in $D(\mathcal{A})$ to $K^{\bullet} \xrightarrow{f} L^{\bullet} \to M(f) \to K^{\bullet}[1]$).

⭐ **This is where long exact sequences come from**: given the triangle, applying the homology functor (a triangle $\implies$ long exact homology sequence) immediately yields the long exact sequence — see "long exact sequence of derived functors".
