## Statement · **draft**
Let $K \subseteq \mathbb{R}^{n}$, regarded as a metric space with the Euclidean distance. Then $K$ is compact if and only if $K$ is bounded and closed.

("Compact" means "every open cover has a finite subcover", or equivalently "every sequence has a subsequence converging to a point of $K$".)
