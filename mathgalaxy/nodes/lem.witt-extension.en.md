## Statement · **draft**
Let $N$ be a finite-dimensional non-degenerate symmetric bilinear space with $\operatorname{char}K\ne2$, and let $A\subseteq N$ be a non-degenerate subspace. If $\sigma:A\to N$ is an isometry onto $\sigma(A)$, then $\sigma$ extends to an isometry of $N$.
