Statement

If M,NM, NM,N are two topological abelian groups and MMM is compactly generated, then there is a natural isomorphism of condensed abelian groups CZ(M,N)≅Hom⁡Z(M,N)C_{\mathbb{Z}}(M, N) \cong \operatorname{Hom}_{\mathbb{Z}}(M, N)CZ​(M,N)≅HomZ​(M,N).

Notes

⭐ The role of the hypothesis "MMM is compactly generated": only then does Hom⁡Z(M‾,−)\operatorname{Hom}_{\mathbb{Z}}(\underline{M}, -)HomZ​(M​,−) really measure continuous homomorphisms; on a general topological abelian group the condensed Hom is larger than the continuous homs (one can glue a map piecewise that is not continuous globally).
