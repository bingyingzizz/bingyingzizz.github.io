# a filtered complex gives a spectral sequence　`thm.filtered-complex-spectral`

⭐ **How to read it**. First split the filtered complex into layers by taking the associated graded $\operatorname{Gr}^{p}$, and compute the cohomology of each layer to obtain the first page. The differentials then assemble these pieces page by page, converging to the **original** cohomology.

⚠️ The **boundedness on both sides** is essential — the condition for $p \ll 0$ alone is insufficient. Stabilization of $Z^{p,q}_{r}$ also requires that for $p \gg 0$, one has $F^{p}K^{n} = 0$ (otherwise $Z_{r}$ can keep growing. Some spectral sequences fail to converge).
