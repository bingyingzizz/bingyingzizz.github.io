## Notes · **draft**
Uniform equicontinuity $\implies$ pointwise equicontinuity (take $y = x_0$ to recover the pointwise definition), see `imp.fa-uniform-eq-pointwise`.

The converse holds when $K$ is compact: `thm.equicontinuous-uniform` (the proof uses a finite cover or a Lebesgue number).
