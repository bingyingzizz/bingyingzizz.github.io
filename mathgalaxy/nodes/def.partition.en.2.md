# Partition of an Interval　`def.partition`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=def.partition)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./def.partition.md)

If $P \subseteq P'$ (all points of $P$ are put into $P'$, possibly with new points added), then $P'$ is called a **refinement** of $P$. Any two partitions $P, Q$ have a common refinement $P \cup Q$.

## Notes · **draft**
⭐ **Finite** is deliberate: the Riemann integral only cuts finitely many times.

Only on the Lebesgue side are countably many pieces allowed — that is one source of the difference between the two.

⚠ The $x_i$ in the notation are partition points, unrelated to the variable of the function;

$\Delta x_i$ is a length, not a difference of function values.
