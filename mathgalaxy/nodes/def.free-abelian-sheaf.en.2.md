# Free Abelian Sheaf　`def.free-abelian-sheaf`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=def.free-abelian-sheaf)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./def.free-abelian-sheaf.md)

It is characterized by the **universal property**: for $X \in \mathcal{T}$ and $M \in \mathcal{T}(\mathbf{Ab})$,

$$\operatorname{Hom}_{\mathbb{Z}}\bigl(\mathbb{Z}\cdot X,\ M\bigr) \;\cong\; \operatorname{Hom}_{\mathcal{T}}(X,\ M) = M(X).$$

## Notes · **draft**
**How to construct it.** First free pointwise, then sheafify: regard $X$ as a presheaf, take the presheaf $U \mapsto \mathbb{Z}\cdot X(U)$ (free abelian group pointwise), then sheafify:

$$\mathbb{Z}\cdot X \;:=\; \bigl(U \mapsto \mathbb{Z}\cdot X(U)\bigr)^{\#}.$$
