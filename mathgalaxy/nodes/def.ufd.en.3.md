# unique factorisation domain　`def.ufd`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=def.ufd)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./def.ufd.md)

The criterion for UFDs: **UFD $\iff$ (factorization exists) and (irreducible = prime)**. The three properties often listed side by side are not individually equivalent to UFD: ① "irreducible = prime" (`def.irreducible-element`) is enough only when **existence of factorization** already holds (drop existence and it is weaker — the ring of all algebraic integers has no irreducible elements, the condition is vacuously true, and yet it is not a UFD); ② a $\gcd$ exists for any two elements in a **GCD domain** (`def.gcd-domain`), strictly weaker than UFD (the graph only records UFD $\implies$ GCD domain, i.e. `imp.ring-ufd-gcd`; the converse fails, e.g. for non-discrete valuation rings); ③
