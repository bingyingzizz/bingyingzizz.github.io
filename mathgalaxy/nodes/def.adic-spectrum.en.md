## Statement · **draft**
In plain words: **collect all ways of "measuring the size" of elements of a ring $A$, and you get the adic spectrum**.

Each point is an "absolute value" (valuation), and $A^+$ serves to filter out those points that measure some bounded element as $>1$.

This is the "with sizes" version of $\operatorname{Spec}$ — $\operatorname{Spec}$ records only "which ideals have elements equal to zero", while $\operatorname{Spa}$ also records "how small".
