# Balancedness of Ext　`thm.ext-balanced`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=thm.ext-balanced)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./thm.ext-balanced.md)

⭐ **Practical value**: Choose whichever side is easier to compute. When $M$ has a short (possibly two-term) projective resolution, use the left-hand side. This occurs over PIDs, over regular local rings, and in Koszul examples. When $N$ has a short injective resolution, use the right-hand side. Examples include divisible groups such as $\mathbb{Q}/\mathbb{Z}$. Spectral sequences are needed only when both resolutions are long.
