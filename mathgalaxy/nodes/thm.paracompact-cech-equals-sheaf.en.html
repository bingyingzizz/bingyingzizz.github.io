Statement

Let XXX be a paracompact Hausdorff space (see Paracompact Space in the topology cluster for the definition and equivalent characterizations of paracompactness). Then for every sheaf of abelian groups M\mathcal{M}M and every n≥0n \ge 0n≥0,

⭐ Relation to the compact Hausdorff result: Every compact Hausdorff space is paracompact, so this result includes Čech Cohomology on Compact Hausdorff Spaces. The proofs use different arguments. The compactness proof uses the cofinality of finite covers in the filtered system and the control of finite products by representable objects. The paracompactness proof uses local finiteness and the softness obtained from partitions of unity. The two arguments establish the same conclusion independently.

⚠️ Paracompactness cannot be dropped. On general spaces, even compactly generated ones, Čech cohomology and sheaf cohomology can differ. The claim that Čech cohomology equals sheaf cohomology is often quoted without qualification in topology and sheaf theory. Include the hypotheses on the space when citing it.
