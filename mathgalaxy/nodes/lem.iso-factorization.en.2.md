## Notes · **draft**
If $\alpha\beta$ is an automorphism, then $\beta$ is injective and $\alpha$ is surjective. Write $W=\operatorname{im}\beta\oplus\ker\alpha$; both summands are subrepresentations, and indecomposability forces $\ker\alpha=0$. Thus $\alpha$ is injective and surjective, hence an isomorphism, and $\beta$ is an isomorphism as well. This is the cancellation step in Krull–Schmidt.
