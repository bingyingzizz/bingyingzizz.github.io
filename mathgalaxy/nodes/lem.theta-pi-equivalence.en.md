## Statement · **draft**
$\theta(x)\sim x$ if and only if $\pi(x)\sim x/\log x$ ($x\to\infty$).

## Notes · **draft**
By Abel summation and $\int_{2}^{x}\mathrm{d}t/\log^{2}t=o(x/\log x)$ (Abel summation and integral estimates).
