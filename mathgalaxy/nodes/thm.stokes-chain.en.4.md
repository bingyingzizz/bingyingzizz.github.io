## Notes · **draft**
**The proof takes only three lines**: $\int_{c}\mathrm{d}\omega = \int_{I^{k}}c^{\ast}\mathrm{d}\omega = \int_{I^{k}}\mathrm{d}(c^{\ast}\omega) = \int_{\partial I^{k}}c^{\ast}\omega = \int_{\partial c}\omega$.

The four steps use, respectively, the definition of integration over a chain, commutation of $c^{\ast}$ with $\mathrm{d}$, `lem.single-cube-stokes`, and the definition and linearity of $\partial$.

The lemma contains the entire difficulty. Here we simply express it in chain language.
