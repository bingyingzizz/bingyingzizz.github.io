# great Picard theorem　`thm.picard-great`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=thm.picard-great)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./thm.picard-great.md)

⭐ **It completes the picture of “three types of singularities”**: near a removable singularity the function is bounded; near a pole it tends to infinity and the image lies almost in one direction; an essential singularity **fills the whole plane (omitting at most one point)**.

⚠️ The “at most one” in the conclusion is **optimal**: $e^{1/z}$ near $0$ takes every value in $\mathbb{C}\setminus\{0\}$, while $0$ is not attained — exactly one is missed.
