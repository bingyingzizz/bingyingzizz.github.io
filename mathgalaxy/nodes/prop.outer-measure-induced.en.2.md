# outer measure induced by a premeasure　`prop.outer-measure-induced`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=prop.outer-measure-induced)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./prop.outer-measure-induced.md)

$$\mu^*(A) := \inf \{ \sum_{j=1}^{\infty} \rho(E_j) : E_j \in \mathcal{E}, A \subseteq \bigcup_{j=1}^{\infty} E_j \}$$

(covering $A$ by countably many sets from $\mathcal{E}$ and taking the infimum.) Then $\mu^{*}$ is an **outer measure** on $X$.

## Notes · **draft**
This formula is just "cover $A$ with bricks of known area and take the cheapest covering" — the Lebesgue outer measure is exactly the special case ($\mathcal{E}$ open intervals, $\rho$ length).
