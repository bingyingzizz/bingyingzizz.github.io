## Statement · **draft**
**What this does**: the Jordan canonical form requires the characteristic polynomial to split over the field (all eigenvalues in the field). The rational canonical form does **not** need this — as long as $K$ is a field, every matrix can be put into it.

Let $K$ be a field and $A \in M_{n}(K)$. Then $A$ is similar in $M_{n}(K)$ to a **unique** block-diagonal matrix
