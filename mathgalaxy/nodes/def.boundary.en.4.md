# Boundaries and Coboundaries　`def.boundary`

⚠️ **Notation**: $B^{n}$ (boundary), $\operatorname{im} d^{n-1}$ and $d(K^{n-1})$ are all in use.

When $B$ and $Z$ appear together, mind the shift: $B^{n} = \operatorname{im} d^{n-1}$ is the differential **one degree lower** than $Z^{n} = \ker d^{n}$; this "misalignment" is the source of every sign error in homological algebra.

⭐ **de Rham version**: forms with $d\omega = 0$ are closed (= cycles), forms with $\omega = d\eta$ are **exact** (= boundaries). So "closed implies exact" is the part with $\iff$ $H^{1} = 0$ — exactly the content of the Poincaré lemma.
