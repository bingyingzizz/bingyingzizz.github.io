# Stonean spaces give finitely presented projective objects　`lem.stonean-projective`

[in graph](../#n=lem.stonean-projective)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./lem.stonean-projective.md)

**Proof.** By the tensor–Hom adjunction, $\operatorname{Hom}_{\mathbb{Z}}(\mathbb{Z}\cdot F, M) \cong M(F) = \Gamma(F, M)$ ✓, so it suffices to consider $\Gamma(F,-)$.
- **Preservation of epimorphisms**: The preceding result says that $\Gamma(F,-)$ preserves **all** colimits, in particular cokernels. $v$ is an epimorphism $\iff$ $\operatorname{coker} v = 0$ $\implies$ $\operatorname{coker}\Gamma(v) = 0$ $\implies$ $\Gamma(v)$ is an epimorphism ✓;
