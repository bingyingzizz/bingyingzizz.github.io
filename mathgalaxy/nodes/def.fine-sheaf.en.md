## Statement · **draft**
Let $X$ be a paracompact Hausdorff space and $\mathcal{F}$ a sheaf of abelian groups on $X$. Call $\mathcal{F}$ a **fine sheaf** if $\mathcal{F}$ admits partitions of unity: for every locally finite open cover of $X$, say $\{U_{i}\}$, there exist sheaf endomorphisms $\eta_{i}:\mathcal{F}\to\mathcal{F}$ with $\operatorname{supp}\eta_{i}\subseteq U_{i}$ and locally finite sum $\sum_{i}\eta_{i}=\operatorname{id}_{\mathcal{F}}$.
