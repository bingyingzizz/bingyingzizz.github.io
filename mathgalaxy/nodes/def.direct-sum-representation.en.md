## Statement · **draft**
Given representations $V_1,\ldots,V_n$ of $A$, the direct sum $V_1\oplus\cdots\oplus V_n$ carries the componentwise action $a(v_1,\ldots,v_n)=(av_1,\ldots,av_n)$; it is a biproduct in $\mathrm{Rep}_A$.
