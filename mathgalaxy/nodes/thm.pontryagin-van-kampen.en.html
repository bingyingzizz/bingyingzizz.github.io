thm.pontryagin-van-kampen · Pontryagin

Statement

The functor

M⟼M^:=CZ(M,T),M \longmapsto \widehat{M} := C_{\mathbb{Z}}(M, \mathbb{T}),M⟼M:=CZ​(M,T),

is an autoequivalence of the category of locally compact Hausdorff abelian groups and is a self-adjoint equivalence.

In particular, M^\widehat{M}M is again locally compact Hausdorff, and taking the dual twice recovers the original group: M^^=M\widehat{\widehat{M}} = MM=M.

Notes

What self-adjoint equivalence means: M↦M^M \mapsto \widehat{M}M↦M is both left and right adjoint to M↦M^M \mapsto \widehat{M}M↦M (duality is adjoint to itself, with the pairing given by the evaluation map M×M^→TM \times \widehat{M} \to \mathbb{T}M×M→T).

Duality carries over entire exact sequences: If 0→M′→M→M′′→00 \to M' \to M \to M'' \to 00→M′→M→M′′→0 is a (strict) exact sequence of locally compact abelian groups, then its dual 0→M′′^→M^→M′^→00 \to \widehat{M''} \to \widehat{M} \to \widehat{M'} \to 00→M′′→M→M′→0 is still exact, with all arrows reversed.

The equivalence preserves structure, so one can solve a group-theoretic problem on the dual side and translate the result back.

Some standard correspondences: R^≅R\widehat{\mathbb{R}} \cong \mathbb{R}R≅R (Fourier transform), and Z^≅T\widehat{\mathbb{Z}} \cong \mathbb{T}Z≅T and T^≅Z\widehat{\mathbb{T}} \cong \mathbb{Z}T≅Z (interchanged).

For finite-dimensional real Banach spaces, the dual reduces to the usual linear dual (a real Banach space is locally compact   ⟺  \iff⟺ it is finite-dimensional).

⭐ This is the prototype for the theory of condensed abelian groups. Replacing T\mathbb{T}T with other test objects and local compactness with other hypotheses gives various dualities (Tannaka, Gelfand, and others). Condensed mathematics moves T\mathbb{T}T to sheaves on CHaus.

Proof (combining three cases).

  • MMM discrete: Classical Pontryagin duality.

First take a surjection Z(I)↠M\mathbb{Z}^{(I)} \twoheadrightarrow MZ(I)↠M. Dualizing gives a closed embedding M^↪TI\widehat M \hookrightarrow \mathbb{T}^{I}M↪TI, so M^\widehat MM is compact.

Verify the evaluation map M→M^^M \to \widehat{\widehat M}M→M by elementary computation for finite and finitely generated groups. Then use that MMM is the filtered colimit of its finitely generated subgroups, duality turns colimits into inverse limits, and both sides are exact, to obtain the isomorphism by passing to the limit ✓.

  • MMM compact: M^\widehat MM is discrete (see the interchange of discrete and compact Hausdorff groups). For compact groups, characters separate points (Pontryagin's classical argument uses Peter–Weyl or finite-dimensional representation theory to give a separating family of characters of a compact abelian group). Thus the evaluation map M→M^^M \to \widehat{\widehat M}M→M is injective.

Then use the preceding case and the fact that the dual of the compact group MMM is discrete to complete the verification of injectivity and surjectivity ✓.

  • MMM locally compact in general: By the structure theorem, MMM contains an open subgroup V⊕KV\oplus KV⊕K (with VVV a finite-dimensional real vector space and KKK compact), and the quotient DDD is discrete. The duality equivalence is known for VVV, KKK, and DDD individually. The three are related by an exact sequence, and duality gives an exact sequence with the arrows reversed. The five lemma makes the middle evaluation map an isomorphism ✓.
  • Self-adjointness: The pairing M×M^→TM\times\widehat M \to \mathbb{T}M×M→T (evaluation) gives the adjunction Hom⁡(M^,N)≅Hom⁡(M,N^)\operatorname{Hom}(\widehat M, N) \cong \operatorname{Hom}(M, \widehat N)Hom(M,N)≅Hom(M,N). Replace MMM with M^\widehat MM and apply the equivalence twice ✓.

∎ Standard reference: Morris, Pontryagin Duality and the Structure of Locally Compact Abelian Groups.
