# Integration by Parts　`prop.integration-by-parts`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=prop.integration-by-parts)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./prop.integration-by-parts.md)

⚠ A common error when checking the boundary terms: writing $F(b)G(b) - F(a)G(a)$ as $(FG)(b - a)$ — that means "the value of $FG$ at the point $b - a$" (or $(b-a)$ times $FG$), which is neither "the difference of the product at the endpoints" nor "the integral of the product".

⭐ The most valuable form of integration by parts is "moving a term": $\int F G' = FG\bigr|_a^b - \int F'G$ moves the hard-to-integrate side to the other side.

On the Lebesgue side it corresponds to the whole package of "weak derivatives / differentiation in the distributional sense".
