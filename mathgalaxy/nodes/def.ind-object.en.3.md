# ind-object　`def.ind-object`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=def.ind-object)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./def.ind-object.md)

**Why the formula looks like this**: to give a map between two formal colimits, first fix $i$ and give, compatibly with the variation of $i$, a family $X_{i} \to Y_{j}$ (take the filtered colimit over $j$, which is $\varinjlim_{j}$), then take the limit over $i$ (as $X_{i}$ grows, maps given later can "fill in" earlier ones).

**Examples**: $\operatorname{Ind}(\mathbf{FinSet})$ is "all sets" (every set is the filtered colimit of its finite subsets); the completion of vector bundles under direct sums and colimits in $K$-theory also uses it.
