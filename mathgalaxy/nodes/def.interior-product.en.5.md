# interior product　`def.interior-product`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=def.interior-product)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./def.interior-product.md)

the Lie derivative differentiates along the flow, is defined only on vector fields, and is linear in $X$ only over $\mathbb{R}$.

The two are exactly complementary in Cartan's formula: $\mathrm{d}\iota_{X}$ is "contract first, then differentiate", $\iota_{X}\mathrm{d}$ is "differentiate first, then contract".

**Why $\iota_{X}^{2} = 0$**: contracting twice puts two vectors into the same slot, and forms are alternating, so exchanging the two contractions produces a sign $-$; hence $(\iota_{X}^{2}\omega)(v_{3},\ldots) = \omega(X,X,v_{3},\ldots) = 0$ (alternation plus the fact that a vector paired with itself gives zero).
