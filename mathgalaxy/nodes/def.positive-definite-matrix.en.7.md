- (definition) $\iff$ (1): **uses the spectral theorem** (Hermitian matrices are orthogonally diagonalizable, thm.spectral-matrix). After diagonalization $x^{*}Ax = \sum \lambda_{i}\lvert y_{i}\rvert^{2}$, so "always positive" $\iff$ "every $\lambda_{i} > 0$". **This holds only for Hermitian (real symmetric) matrices**: a nonsymmetric matrix can have positive eigenvalues yet fail to be positive definite (the $x^{T}Ax$ contains a mixed antisymmetric part).
