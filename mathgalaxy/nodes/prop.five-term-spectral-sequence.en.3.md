# five-term exact sequence of a spectral sequence　`prop.five-term-spectral-sequence`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=prop.five-term-spectral-sequence)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./prop.five-term-spectral-sequence.md)

Assembling $E^{\infty}_{1,0} = \operatorname{Gr}^{1}H^{1}$, $E^{\infty}_{0,1} = \operatorname{Gr}^{0}H^{1}$, together with the filtration $E^{\infty}_{2,0} = \operatorname{Gr}^{2}H^{2}$ term by term gives the displayed chain ✓.

∎

⭐ Use: this is the standard tool by which "a spectral sequence directly yields a low-degree exact sequence"; the low-degree terms of $H^{1}$ and $H^{2}$ are read off from it.
