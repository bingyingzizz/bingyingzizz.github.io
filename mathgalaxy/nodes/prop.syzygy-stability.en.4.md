# Stability of Syzygies　`prop.syzygy-stability`

The two resolutions give two precovers in degree $n+1$ with projective middle terms $P_{n}$ and $Q_{n}$. Their targets are $\Omega^{n}M$ and $\Omega_{1}^{n}M$, respectively. Since the targets are different modules, Schanuel's lemma cannot be applied directly.

To obtain a common target, incorporate the direct summands.

Take
