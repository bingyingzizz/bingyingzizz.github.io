It is also the least bound for which “$\operatorname{Ext}^{n}_{R}(-,-)$ vanishes for all $n > d$” holds, with the bound denoted by $d$.

There is a corresponding **right global dimension** $\operatorname{gl.dim} R^{\mathrm{op}}$. The two can differ.

## Notes · **draft**
**Why the three descriptions agree**: By the vanishing characterizations of dimension, “$\operatorname{pd}_{R} M \le d$ for all $M$,” “$\operatorname{id}_{R} N \le d$ for all $N$,” and “$\operatorname{Ext}^{n}_{R}(-,-)$ vanishes for all $n > d$” are **equivalent**. Each says that this entire family of groups vanishes in degrees $n > d$ ✓.
