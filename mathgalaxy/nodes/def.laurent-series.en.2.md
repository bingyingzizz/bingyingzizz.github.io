# Laurent Series　`def.laurent-series`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=def.laurent-series)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./def.laurent-series.md)

$$\sum_{n=-\infty}^{\infty} c_n (z - a)^n = \sum_{n=0}^{\infty} c_n (z - a)^n + \sum_{n=1}^{\infty} c_{-n}\,(z - a)^{-n}$$

that converges in $A_{r,R}(a)$ (on every compact subset) is called a **Laurent series centred at $a$**. The first block on the right is called the **regular part**, the second (the sum of negative powers) the **principal part**.
