Statement

In plain words: these two equations govern how the frame moves. The first (zero torsion) says "the change of the coframe is completely determined by the connection forms, with no extra twist"; the second (R3\mathbb{R}^{3}R3 is flat) says "rotating once in each of two directions gives the same result regardless of order". Together they express d2=0\mathrm{d}^{2} = 0d2=0 in a frame.

Let ωi\omega^{i}ωi be the dual coframe (dr=∑iωiei\mathrm{d}r = \sum_{i}\omega^{i}e_{i}dr=∑i​ωiei​) and ω ji\omega^{i}_{\ j}ω ji​ the connection forms. Then

First structure equation (zero torsion)

dωi=∑jωj∧ω ji;\mathrm{d}\omega^{i} = \sum_{j}\omega^{j}\wedge\omega^{i}_{\ j} ;dωi=j∑​ωj∧ω ji​;

Second structure equation (R3\mathbb{R}^{3}R3 flat)

dω ji+∑kω ki∧ω jk=0,dω ji=∑kω jk∧ω ki.\mathrm{d}\omega^{i}_{\ j}+\sum_{k}\omega^{i}_{\ k}\wedge\omega^{k}_{\ j} = 0 ,\qquad \mathrm{d}\omega^{i}_{\ j} = \sum_{k}\omega^{k}_{\ j}\wedge\omega^{i}_{\ k}.dω ji​+k∑​ω ki​∧ω jk​=0,dω ji​=k∑​ω jk​∧ω ki​.

Together they express d2=0\mathrm{d}^{2}=0d2=0 on the frame: applying the exterior derivative to dr\mathrm{d}rdr gives the first equation, and to dei\mathrm{d}e_{i}dei​ gives the second.

Notes

Why "structure": these two equations determine all constraints on "how the frame moves". Given the coframe and the connection, the shape of the surface together with its position in R3\mathbb{R}^{3}R3 is determined; conversely the only compatibility to check is d2=0\mathrm{d}^{2}=0d2=0. The fundamental theorem of surface theory turns this sentence into a theorem.

Curvature form: on a general manifold the right-hand side of the second equation is nonzero; that term is called the curvature form Ω ji:=dω ji+∑kω ki∧ω jk\Omega^{i}_{\ j} := \mathrm{d}\omega^{i}_{\ j}+\sum_{k}\omega^{i}_{\ k}\wedge\omega^{k}_{\ j}Ω ji​:=dω ji​+∑k​ω ki​∧ω jk​;

on R3\mathbb{R}^{3}R3, Ω≡0\Omega \equiv 0Ω≡0.

Restricted to a surface it becomes Ω ji=ω 3i∧ω 3j\Omega^{i}_{\ j} = \omega^{i}_{\ 3}\wedge\omega^{j}_{\ 3}Ω ji​=ω 3i​∧ω 3j​ — this is the Gauss equation.

The same thing can be written as "the mixed partial derivatives of rij=Γ ijkrk+hijnr_{ij} = \Gamma^{k}_{\ ij}r_{k}+h_{ij}nrij​=Γ ijk​rk​+hij​n and ni=−h ijrjn_{i} = -h^{j}_{\ i}r_{j}ni​=−h ij​rj​ commute (integrability condition)".
