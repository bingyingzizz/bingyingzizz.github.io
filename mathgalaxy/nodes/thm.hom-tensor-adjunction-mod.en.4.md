# Hom-Tensor Adjunction　`thm.hom-tensor-adjunction-mod`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=thm.hom-tensor-adjunction-mod)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./thm.hom-tensor-adjunction-mod.md)

**Two corollaries are immediate**: $(-) \otimes_{R} N$ is a left adjoint, hence **preserves colimits** (right exact, preserves direct sums and filtered colimits); $\operatorname{Hom}_{R}(N, -)$ is a right adjoint, hence **preserves limits** (left exact, preserves products).

📌 Non-commutative version: for $M$ a right $R$-module, $N$ a left $R$-module and $P$ an abelian group, the same isomorphism reads

$$\operatorname{Hom}_{\mathbb{Z}}(M \otimes_{R} N,\ P) \;\cong\; \operatorname{Hom}_{R}\bigl(M,\ \operatorname{Hom}_{\mathbb{Z}}(N, P)\bigr),$$
