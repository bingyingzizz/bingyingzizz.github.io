[in graph](../#n=def.linear-isomorphism)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./def.linear-isomorphism.md)

⚠ In infinite dimension both directions hold (equipotent bases give an isomorphism, and an isomorphism gives equipotent bases); one only has to read “dimension” as the cardinality of a basis.

⭐ In category language: $V \mapsto \dim V$ matches the finite-dimensional objects of $K\text{-}\mathbf{Vect}$ with cardinals, so the structure of the additive category reduces to concrete linear algebra.
