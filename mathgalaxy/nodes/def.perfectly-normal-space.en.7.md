# Perfectly Normal Space　`def.perfectly-normal-space`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=def.perfectly-normal-space)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./def.perfectly-normal-space.md)

 Urysohn's lemma to separate $F$ from $X\setminus U_n$, then weight by $2^{-n}$ to obtain a uniformly convergent series; the converse follows directly by taking preimages of a continuous function.
- Normality cannot be replaced by "every closed set is $G_\delta$": the two-point Sierpiński space has every closed set open, yet fails the $T_1$ required here; nor is normality automatically perfect — the singleton of the last point of $[0,\omega_1]$ is not a $G_\delta$ set.
