⭐ (i) is the standard form in analysis of "take nnn sufficiently large".

The Archimedean property originally says "N\mathbb{N}N is unbounded", but in actual proofs one almost always uses (i): given ε\varepsilonε, take n>1/εn > 1/\varepsilonn>1/ε.
