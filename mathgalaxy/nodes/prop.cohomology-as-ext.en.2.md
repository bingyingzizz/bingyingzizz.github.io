## Notes · **draft**
⭐ This is the **bridge between two languages**: on one side the geometric "sections" ($\Gamma(X,-)$), on the other the algebraic "derived Hom". With it, every computation in sheaf cohomology translates into an Ext computation in the derived category — and conversely the geometric meaning of Ext becomes clear: **it is cohomology on some space**.
