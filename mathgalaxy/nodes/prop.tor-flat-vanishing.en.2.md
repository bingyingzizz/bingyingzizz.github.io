Thus the flat modules are precisely those for which all higher Tor groups vanish. This is exactly parallel to the characterization of projective modules by the vanishing of all higher $\operatorname{Ext}$ groups.

## Notes · **draft**
**Proof.**
$(1) \implies (3)$: For $F$ take a projective resolution $P_{\bullet} \to F$.

When $F$ is flat, $\operatorname{Tor}_{n}$ can also be computed using a resolution of $N$. Take a projective resolution of $N$, denoted by $Q_{\bullet}$. The resolution on the $N$ side gives $\operatorname{Tor}_{n}(F, N) = H_{n}(F\otimes Q_{\bullet})$.
