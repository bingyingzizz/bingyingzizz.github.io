⚠️ Munkres uses the opposite convention (he takes "regular" without $T_{1}$ and $T_{3}$ with $T_{1}$) — when reading another book, fix the convention first; otherwise a statement such as "$T_{3} \implies T_{2}$" is sometimes true and sometimes false.

**One-line proof that regular $\implies$ $T_{2}$**: given $x \ne y$, $T_{1}$ makes $\{y\}$ closed and $x \notin \{y\}$; by $T_{3}$ choose disjoint open sets $U \ni x$ and $V \supseteq \{y\}$. This is exactly $T_{2}$.
