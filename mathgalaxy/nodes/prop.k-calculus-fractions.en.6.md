**Cancellation condition.** Let $f \circ s \simeq g \circ s$ (with $s$ a quasi-isomorphism) and write $h := f - g$.

Then $h s \simeq 0$; by the universal property of the mapping cone, $h$ factors as $L^{\bullet} \xrightarrow{\;i\;} M(s)^{\bullet} \xrightarrow{\;h'\;} M^{\bullet}$.
