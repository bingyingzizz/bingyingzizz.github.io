## Notes · **draft**
**Example 1. A topological space gives a condensed set.** Any topological space $X$ gives a condensed set $\underline{X}(S) := C(S, X) = \operatorname{Hom}_{\mathbf{Top}}(S, X)$ — all continuous maps from $S$ to $X$.
