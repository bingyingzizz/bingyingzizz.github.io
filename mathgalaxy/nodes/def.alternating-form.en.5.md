**Which hypothesis each direction uses** (alternating implies skew-symmetric without dividing by two; the converse needs it):
- Alternating $\iff$ the value is zero when two arguments coincide; this is just unfolding the definition and holds in any characteristic.
- Alternating $\implies$ swapping two arguments changes the sign: substitute $u+v$ into the two slots at once and expand by multilinearity; then decompose the permutation to get skew-symmetry, with no assumption on $\operatorname{char}K\ne2$.
- Skew-symmetric $\implies$ alternating: swapping two equal arguments gives $2f=0$, and one needs $\operatorname{char}K\ne2$ to cancel $2$;
