## Notes · **draft**
⭐ **"Strict" is the watershed between abelian and general categories**: in an abelian category **every morphism is strict**; in $\mathbf{Top}$ it is not.

**Why the counterexample appears in topology**: $\mathbb{R}^{\mathrm{disc}} \to \mathbb{R}$ is a monomorphism (isomorphic to its image), but its **coimage** is trivial (because the image is quotiented away in the cokernel), so the step coimage $\to$ image is not an isomorphism.

**This is exactly why "topological abelian groups do not form an abelian category" over $\mathbf{Top}$.**
