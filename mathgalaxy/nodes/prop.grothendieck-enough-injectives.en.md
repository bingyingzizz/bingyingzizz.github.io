## Statement · **draft**
Every **Grothendieck category** (an AB5 category with a generator) has **enough injective objects**:

every object $M$ embeds into an injective object; equivalently, every object has an injective resolution

$$0 \longrightarrow M \longrightarrow I^{0} \longrightarrow I^{1} \longrightarrow \cdots$$
