Statement

Let F:A→CF : \mathcal{A} \to \mathcal{C}F:A→C and G:B→CG : \mathcal{B} \to \mathcal{C}G:B→C be two functors with the same target. The comma category F↓GF \downarrow GF↓G has as objects

{(A,B,f):A∈A, B∈B, f:F(A)→G(B)}\bigl\{(A, B, f) : A \in \mathcal{A},\ B \in \mathcal{B},\ f : F(A) \to G(B)\bigr\}{(A,B,f):A∈A, B∈B, f:F(A)→G(B)}

and a morphism from (A,B,f)(A, B, f)(A,B,f) to (A′,B′,f′)(A', B', f')(A′,B′,f′) is a pair (a:A→A′, b:B→B′)(a : A \to A',\ b : B \to B')(a:A→A′, b:B→B′) making the square commute:

F(A)→  f  G(B)F(a)↓↓G(b)F(A′)→  f′  G(B′)\begin{array}{ccc} F(A) & \xrightarrow{\;f\;} & G(B) \\[2pt] {\scriptstyle F(a)}\big\downarrow & & \big\downarrow{\scriptstyle G(b)} \\[2pt] F(A') & \xrightarrow[\;f'\;]{} & G(B') \end{array}F(A)F(a)↓⏐​F(A′)​f​f′​​G(B)↓⏐​G(b)G(B′)​

Two special cases: taking A=1\mathcal{A} = \mathbf{1}A=1 (the one-object category), FFF is just "selecting an object of C\mathcal{C}C, say XXX", and F↓GF \downarrow GF↓G is written X↓GX \downarrow GX↓G; taking B=1\mathcal{B} = \mathbf{1}B=1 gives F↓YF \downarrow YF↓Y.

Notes

Reading: F↓GF \downarrow GF↓G organizes all ways of "stretching an arrow from the world of FFF to the world of GGG" into a category. The objects are the arrows themselves (together with their sources at both ends), and the morphisms are the pair of morphisms that let the arrows be compared.

Notation: the symbol in F↓GF \downarrow GF↓G is a "comma", hence the name; some texts write (F↓G)(F \downarrow G)(F↓G) or (F/G)(F/G)(F/G).

⭐ Why it appears everywhere: universal properties are often "the terminal/initial object in some comma category".

  • X↓idCX \downarrow \mathrm{id}_{\mathcal{C}}X↓idC​ is the slice C/X\mathcal{C}/XC/X;
  • the category of elements ∫F\int F∫F of a presheaf is 1↓F\mathbf{1} \downarrow F1↓F (viewing the presheaf as a functor Cop→Set\mathcal{C}^{\mathrm{op}} \to \mathbf{Set}Cop→Set);
  • the unit of an adjunction is defined as the initial object of id↓G\mathrm{id} \downarrow Gid↓G (or F↓idF \downarrow \mathrm{id}F↓id).

To recognize a comma category, check whether "objects are arrows and morphisms are commutative squares".
