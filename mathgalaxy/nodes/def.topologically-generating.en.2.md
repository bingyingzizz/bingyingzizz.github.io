## Notes · **draft**
**What it says**: $G$ is a **topological** generating set means every object $X$ admits a covering family all of whose objects **lie in $G$**.

⭐ Use: the sheaf condition need only be checked on $G$ (coverings generated by $G$); equivalently, "$\{\, h_{G} \,\}_{G \in G}$ forms a generating family".

⚠ Easily confused with the **generator** of category theory (the one requiring "Hom is faithful", in the two category-theory clusters): what is meant here is only **coverings of a site**, not faithfulness of representable functors.
