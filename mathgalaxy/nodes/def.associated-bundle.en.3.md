# Associated Bundle　`def.associated-bundle`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=def.associated-bundle)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./def.associated-bundle.md)

i.e. **every vector bundle is the associated bundle of its frame bundle** — combined with @@def.frame-bundle@@ this is the one-to-one correspondence "vector bundle $\leftrightarrow$ principal bundle of rank $k$ with structure group $\mathrm{GL}(k)$" (classification is meant up to isomorphism).

## Notes · **draft**
⭐ **$g^{-1}$ is not a typo**: for the right action to really be an action one must use $g$ on one side and $g^{-1}$ on the other (otherwise $(u,y)\cdot g\cdot h$ would become $(u\cdot gh, h^{-1}g^{-1}y)$, inconsistent with $(u,y)\cdot(gh)$). This is the notation most easily gotten wrong in associated bundles.
