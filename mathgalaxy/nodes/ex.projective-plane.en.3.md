# projective plane　`ex.projective-plane`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=ex.projective-plane)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./ex.projective-plane.md)

⭐ **Why it is nonorientable**: After going once around a line in $\mathbb{RP}^{2}$, the front becomes the back. It is a **capped Möbius band**: glue a disk to the boundary circle of a Möbius band to obtain $\mathbb{RP}^{2}$.

⚠ **$\mathbb{RP}^{2}$ cannot embed in $\mathbb{R}^{3}$** (no compact nonorientable surface without boundary embeds in $\mathbb{R}^{3}$; an embedding requires going to $\mathbb{R}^{4}$). This is a topological obstruction. The familiar crosscap picture shows an **immersion**.
