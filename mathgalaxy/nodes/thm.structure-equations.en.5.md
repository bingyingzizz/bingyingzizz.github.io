The same thing can be written as "the mixed partial derivatives of $r_{ij} = \Gamma^{k}_{\ ij}r_{k}+h_{ij}n$ and $n_{i} = -h^{j}_{\ i}r_{j}$ commute (integrability condition)". Here one only replaces "mixed partial derivatives" by $\mathrm{d}^{2}=0$ and $\Gamma$ by $\omega$: the same compatibility, in shorter notation.

⚠️ **Sign convention**: this pack takes $\omega^{j}_{\ i} = \langle \mathrm{d}e_{i}, e_{j}\rangle$, i.e. $\mathrm{d}e_{i} = \sum_{j}\omega^{j}_{\ i}e_{j}$. Moving an index up or down flips the sign of the whole second equation; align this before quoting.
