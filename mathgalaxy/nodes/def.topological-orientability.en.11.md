# topological orientability　`def.topological-orientability`

[in graph](../#n=def.topological-orientability)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./def.topological-orientability.md)

**5. Examples.** $S^{2}$, $T^{2}$, and all orientable closed surfaces are orientable. $\mathbb{RP}^{2}$, the Klein bottle, and the Möbius band are nonorientable. The orientation double cover of $\mathbb{RP}^{2}$ is $S^{2}$ (with the antipodal action), the orientation double cover of the Klein bottle is $T^{2}$, and that of the Möbius band is an annulus. These examples show that in the nonorientable case, local orientations exist but cannot be chosen consistently over the whole space.
