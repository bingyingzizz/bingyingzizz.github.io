## Statement · **draft**
Let $\{x_n\} \subseteq \mathbb{R}$.

- If $x_n$ is increasing ($x_1 \le x_2 \le \cdots$) and bounded above, then $\{x_n\}$ converges and

$$\lim_{n \to \infty} x_n = \sup_{n} x_n$$

- If $x_n$ is decreasing and bounded below, then $\{x_n\}$ converges and $\lim_{n \to \infty} x_n = \inf_{n} x_n$.
