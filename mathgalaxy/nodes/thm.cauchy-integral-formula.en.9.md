# Cauchy Integral Formula　`thm.cauchy-integral-formula`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=thm.cauchy-integral-formula)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./thm.cauchy-integral-formula.md)

**General closed curves**: if $\gamma$ does not enclose $z$, Cauchy's theorem on the region inside $\gamma$ gives $\oint_{\gamma} = 0$; if $\gamma$ encloses $z$, the same argument on the annulus between the outer boundary and the inner circle gives the form with the winding number $\operatorname{Ind}(\gamma, z)\,f(z) = \frac{1}{2\pi i}\oint_{\gamma}\frac{f(\zeta)}{\zeta - z}\,\mathrm{d}\zeta$.
