## Notes · **draft**
⭐ This is the **master switch** of differential calculus: whenever a theorem deduces global information from pointwise derivative data, the first step is to use the mean value theorem to rewrite a difference as a derivative.

Monotonicity criteria, the constant-function criterion, L'Hôpital's rule, Taylor's formula and convexity criteria are all corollaries.

⚠ **$\xi$ is uncontrollable**: the theorem asserts existence but not location — which is why the mean value theorem cannot **compute** $f(b) - f(a)$, only prove inequalities and structural properties.
