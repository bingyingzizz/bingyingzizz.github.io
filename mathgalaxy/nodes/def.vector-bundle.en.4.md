## Notes · **draft**
**Why "locally trivial" is not enough, and the cocycle is needed**: local triviality only says "each piece looks like a product"; what determines the bundle is **how the pieces are glued**, and the gluing is recorded in $g_{\alpha\beta}$.

The cocycle condition is necessary and sufficient for consistent gluing, and ties bundles to cohomology classes of 1-cocycles, $H^{1}(M;\operatorname{GL}(k,\mathbb{R}))$ — mentioned here without proof.
