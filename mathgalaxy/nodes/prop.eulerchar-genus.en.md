## Statement · **draft**
Let $S$ be a **closed orientable surface** of genus $g = \tfrac{1}{2}\operatorname{rank} H_{1}(S;\mathbb{Z})$ (`def.genus`). Then

$$\chi(S) = 2 - 2g .$$

Thus $\chi$ and $g$ determine each other: $g = (2 - \chi)/2$.
