# properties of the integral of simple functions　`prop.simple-integral-props`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=prop.simple-integral-props)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./prop.simple-integral-props.md)

- **(a)** $c > 0 \implies \int c\varphi = c\int \varphi$
- **(b)** $\int (\varphi + \psi) = \int \varphi + \int \psi$
- **(c)** $\varphi \le \psi \implies \int \varphi \le \int \psi$
- **(d)** $A \mapsto \int_A \varphi d\mu$ is a **measure** on $\mathcal{M}$.

## Notes · **draft**
(d) is the most useful item of the group: it turns “integration of simple functions” into “a measure”, so countable additivity and monotone continuity (in $A$) come for free.
