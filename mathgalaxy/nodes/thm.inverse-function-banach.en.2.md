## Statement · **draft**
Let $E, F$ be **Banach spaces**, let $U \subseteq E$ be open, and suppose $f : U \to F$ is $C^{1}$ (in the Fréchet sense: the total derivative $Df(x) \in \mathcal{L}(E, F)$ exists and is continuous), $a \in U$, and $Df(a)$ **is a topological linear isomorphism** (bounded with bounded inverse):

$$Df(a) : E \to F \quad \text{topological linear isomorphism}$$
