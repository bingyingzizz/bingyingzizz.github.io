## Statement · **draft**
Let $V$ be a real vector space and $S \subseteq V$. One calls $S$ a **star-shaped set** if there exists $x_{0} \in S$ such that for every $x \in S$ and $t \in [0,1]$,

$$(1-t)x_{0} + t x \in S.$$

Such an $x_{0}$ is called a **star centre** of $S$.
