陈述

设 RRR 是主理想整环，A∈Mm×n(R)A \in M_{m \times n}(R)A∈Mm×n​(R)。则存在可逆矩阵 P∈GL⁡m(R)P \in \operatorname{GL}_{m}(R)P∈GLm​(R)、Q∈GL⁡n(R)Q \in \operatorname{GL}_{n}(R)Q∈GLn​(R)，使

PAQ=diag⁡(d1,d2,…,dr,0,…,0),P A Q = \operatorname{diag}(d_1, d_2, \ldots, d_r, 0, \ldots, 0),PAQ=diag(d1​,d2​,…,dr​,0,…,0),

其中 di≠0d_i \ne 0di​=0，且 d1∣d2∣⋯∣drd_1 \mid d_2 \mid \cdots \mid d_rd1​∣d2​∣⋯∣dr​。这些 did_idi​ 在相差单位的意义下由 AAA 唯一确定，称为 AAA 的不变因子（invariant factors）。

等价地，d1⋯dkd_1 \cdots d_kd1​⋯dk​ 等于 AAA 的全部 kkk 阶子式的最大公因子（行列式因子），这给出唯一性的具体算法。

说明

与域上相抵的关系：KKK 是域时 KKK 是 PID（除环），Smith 标准形退化为 diag⁡(Ir,0)\operatorname{diag}(I_r, 0)diag(Ir​,0)，即域上相抵只由秩分类。

证明思路：在 AAA 的等价类中取 (1,1)(1,1)(1,1) 元素生成的理想极大的矩阵，证明它整除所有元素（否则用 Bézout 把 gcd 放到 (1,1)(1,1)(1,1) 得到更大的理想），清空第一行第一列后对余块归纳。唯一性用行列式因子。

典型例子：R=ZR = \mathbb{Z}R=Z 时 diag⁡(2,6)\operatorname{diag}(2, 6)diag(2,6) 满足 2∣62 \mid 62∣6；R=K[x]R = K[x]R=K[x] 时对 xI−AxI - AxI−A 作 Smith 标准形，得到的非常数不变因子就是线性算子 AAA 的不变因子，见有理标准形。

Statement draft

Let RRR be a principal ideal domain and A∈Mm×n(R)A \in M_{m \times n}(R)A∈Mm×n​(R). Then there exist invertible matrices P∈GL⁡m(R)P \in \operatorname{GL}_{m}(R)P∈GLm​(R) and Q∈GL⁡n(R)Q \in \operatorname{GL}_{n}(R)Q∈GLn​(R) such that

PAQ=diag⁡(d1,d2,…,dr,0,…,0),P A Q = \operatorname{diag}(d_1, d_2, \ldots, d_r, 0, \ldots, 0),PAQ=diag(d1​,d2​,…,dr​,0,…,0),

where di≠0d_i \ne 0di​=0 and d1∣d2∣⋯∣drd_1 \mid d_2 \mid \cdots \mid d_rd1​∣d2​∣⋯∣dr​. The elements did_idi​ are determined by AAA up to units and are called the invariant factors of AAA.

Equivalently, d1⋯dkd_1 \cdots d_kd1​⋯dk​ is the greatest common divisor of all minors of AAA of size kkk (the determinantal divisors), which gives a concrete algorithm for uniqueness.

Notes draft

Relation to equivalence over a field: when KKK is a field, KKK is a PID, and the Smith normal form degenerates to diag⁡(Ir,0)\operatorname{diag}(I_r, 0)diag(Ir​,0), so over a field equivalence is classified by rank alone.

Proof idea: in the equivalence class of AAA, choose a matrix whose (1,1)(1,1)(1,1) entry generates a maximal ideal, and show that it divides every entry (otherwise Bézout puts the gcd into position (1,1)(1,1)(1,1), producing a larger ideal); then clear the first row and column and induct on the remaining block. Uniqueness uses the determinantal divisors.

Typical examples: for R=ZR = \mathbb{Z}R=Z, diag⁡(2,6)\operatorname{diag}(2, 6)diag(2,6) satisfies 2∣62 \mid 62∣6; for R=K[x]R = K[x]R=K[x], applying the Smith normal form to xI−AxI - AxI−A yields the nonconstant invariant factors of the linear operator AAA (see rational canonical form).
