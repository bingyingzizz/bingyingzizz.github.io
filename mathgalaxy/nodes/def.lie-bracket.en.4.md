# Lie Bracket　`def.lie-bracket`

[in graph](../#n=def.lie-bracket)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./def.lie-bracket.md)

when subtracting, the coefficients of $\partial_{j}\partial_{i}f$ are $X^{j}Y^{i}$ and $Y^{j}X^{i}$ respectively, and since it is symmetric in $i,j$, these terms cancel one by one.

The second-order terms disappear, and what remains is a legitimate derivation.

**Item 4 shows it is not a tensor**: $[X,Y]$ is not $C^{\infty}(M)$-linear in $Y$ — the extra term $f(Xg)Y$ is precisely the trace of "differentiating twice". Hence "the bracket of two vector fields cannot be defined pointwise", which is the fundamental difference from every tensor operation (`def.tensor-field`).
