Statement draft

The Jacobson radical of a ring AAA is J(A)=⋂VAnn⁡(V)J(A)=\bigcap_{V}\operatorname{Ann}(V)J(A)=⋂V​Ann(V), where VVV runs over simple left modules; equivalently, it is the intersection of all maximal left ideals. It is a two-sided ideal.

Notes draft

Also J(A)J(A)J(A) is the intersection of all maximal right ideals, and it annihilates every irreducible left module. In finite dimension J(A)=0J(A)=0J(A)=0 is equivalent to AAA being semisimple (Jacobson criterion). The quotient A/J(A)A/J(A)A/J(A) is semisimple.
