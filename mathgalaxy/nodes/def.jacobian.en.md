## Statement · **draft**
Let $U \subseteq \mathbb{R}^{n}$ be open and let $f = (f_1, \ldots, f_m) : U \to \mathbb{R}^{m}$ have all partial derivatives at $a$. The **Jacobian matrix** of $f$ at $a$ is

$$J_f(a) := \Bigl( \frac{\partial f_i}{\partial x_j}(a) \Bigr)_{\substack{1 \le i \le m \\ 1 \le j \le n}} \in M_{m \times n}(\mathbb{R})$$
