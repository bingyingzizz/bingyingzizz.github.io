# Rado theorem: every compact surface is triangulable　`thm.rado`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=thm.rado)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./thm.rado.md)

**Two distinctions.** (a) Radó's existence theorem and the claim that any two triangulations are combinatorially equivalent are distinct. The latter is the Hauptvermutung (false in $\dim\ge 4$, true in $\dim=2$) and is the work of step (2) of `thm.classification-surfaces`. (b) Existence of a complex structure and existence of a smooth structure are equivalent in $\dim=2$ (uniformization) and inequivalent in $\dim\ge 4$.
