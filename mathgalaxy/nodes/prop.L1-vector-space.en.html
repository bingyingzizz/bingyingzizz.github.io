Statement

The integrable (real-valued) functions on XXX form a real vector space, and

∫(af+bg)=a∫f+b∫g∀a,b∈R\int (af + bg) = a\int f + b\int g\quad \forall a, b \in \mathbb{R}∫(af+bg)=a∫f+b∫g∀a,b∈R

i.e. f↦∫ff \mapsto \int ff↦∫f is linear.

Notes

"Being a vector space" is understood in the a.e. sense (two integrable functions equal a.e. are regarded as the same element).

Linearity follows by decomposing into positive and negative parts and checking (a)(b) for simple functions item by item — this is not deep in itself, but it is the first step in making L1L^{1}L1 a normed space; the whole theory of Lp spaces starts here.
