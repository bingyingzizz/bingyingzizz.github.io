Statement draft

Let GGG be a finite group and let FFF be a field with char⁡F∤∣G∣\operatorname{char}F\nmid|G|charF∤∣G∣. Then the group algebra F[G]F[G]F[G] is semisimple; equivalently, every finite-dimensional representation of GGG is semisimple (completely reducible).

Notes draft

Given a subrepresentation W⊆VW\subseteq VW⊆V, take any linear projection p:V→Wp:V\to Wp:V→W and average over GGG: pˉ(v)=1∣G∣∑gg p(g−1v)\bar p(v)=\frac1{|G|}\sum_g g\,p(g^{-1}v)pˉ​(v)=∣G∣1​∑g​gp(g−1v). The condition char⁡F∤∣G∣\operatorname{char}F\nmid|G|charF∤∣G∣ makes 1∣G∣\frac1{|G|}∣G∣1​ meaningful; pˉ\bar ppˉ​ is a GGG-equivariant projection, so V=W⊕ker⁡pˉV=W\oplus\ker\bar pV=W⊕kerpˉ​. Splitting every subrepresentation this way gives complete reducibility; the group-algebra version follows from the equivalence between representations and F[G]F[G]F[G]-modules.
