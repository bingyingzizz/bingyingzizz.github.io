Statement

In C^\widehat{\mathcal{C}}C, every presheaf TTT is a colimit of representable presheaves, as expressed by

T  ≅  lim→⁡(X,s)∈CThXT \;\cong\; \varinjlim_{(X, s) \in \mathcal{C}_{T}} h_{X}T≅(X,s)∈CT​lim​​hX​

Notes

The colimit is indexed by the slice category CT\mathcal{C}_{T}CT​. For TTT, each element (X,s)(X, s)(X,s) contributes a representable presheaf hXh_{X}hX​, and TTT is their colimit.

Thus C^\widehat{\mathcal{C}}C is “freely generated by the representable presheaves” — the representable presheaves are its building blocks. This is what is meant later by saying that the presheaf category is the closure of all representable presheaves under colimits.
