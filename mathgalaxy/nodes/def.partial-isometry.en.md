## Statement · **draft**
Let $u \in B(H)$. One says $u$ is a **partial isometry** if $u^*u$ is an orthogonal projection (write $P = u^*u$).

In that case $u$ is an isometry on $(\ker u)^\perp$, and $u\,u^*$ is also an orthogonal projection.
