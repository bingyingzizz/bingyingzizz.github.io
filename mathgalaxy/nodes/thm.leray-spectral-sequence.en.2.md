## Notes · **draft**
**Proof.** The key is the section isomorphism coming from the adjunction

$$\Gamma\bigl(X',\ f_{*}M\bigr) = \Gamma\bigl(f^{-1}(X'),\ M\bigr) ✓$$

Write $\Gamma\bigl(f^{-1}(X'), \cdot\bigr)$ as $\Gamma(X', \cdot) \circ f_{*}$, then apply the Grothendieck spectral sequence ($f_{*}$ sends injectives to $f_{*}$-acyclic objects — injective objects are themselves acyclic) ✓, and the result follows ✓. ∎
