# adjoint operator on Hilbert spaces　`thm.adjoint-hilbert`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=thm.adjoint-hilbert)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./thm.adjoint-hilbert.md)

⭐ This explains why Banach spaces have no "adjoint" but only "transpose/conjugate": the transpose lives between **duals** (${}^t\!: F^* \to E^*$), whereas in a Hilbert space $H^* \cong H$ lets the transpose be moved back onto the space.
