## Notes · **draft**
⭐ In one sentence: **a sufficiently differentiable function is locally a polynomial**, and the error is controlled by that one remainder term.

This is the only theorem in elementary calculus that recovers a function from finite information, and it is the gateway to the whole line of power series and analytic functions.

⭐ The shape of the remainder has the same origin as the mean value theorem: when $n = 0$, Taylor's formula **is** the Lagrange mean value theorem.

⚠ The remainder only asserts the existence of $\xi$, not its location;
