## Notes · **draft**
**Why it can only be defined this way**: $\omega$ itself is built recursively, and the only tool for "building something out of $\omega$" is recursive definition. Addition, multiplication, powers and order all come from this one principle.

⚠️ These two clauses are only an assertion of **existence and uniqueness** — existence is precisely the content of the **recursion theorem**, so they are **definitions**, not axioms, and require no additional axiom.
