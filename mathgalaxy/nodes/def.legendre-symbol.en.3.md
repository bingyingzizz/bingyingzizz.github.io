So $\left(\frac{\cdot}{p}\right) : \mathbb{F}_{p}^{\times} \to \{\pm 1\}$ is a group homomorphism whose kernel is the subgroup of quadratic residues — the Legendre symbol is the isomorphism table of the quotient group $\mathbb{F}_{p}^{\times}/(\mathbb{F}_{p}^{\times})^{2} \cong \{\pm 1\}$.
