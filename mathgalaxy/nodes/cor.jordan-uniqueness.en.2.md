## Statement · **draft**
Assume that the **characteristic polynomial of $A$ splits over $K$** (otherwise not even a Jordan form exists: over $\mathbb{R}$ the rotation $\begin{pmatrix}0 & -1\cr 1 & 0\end{pmatrix}$ is a counterexample). Under this hypothesis the Jordan normal form is **unique** up to "the order of the Jordan blocks", that is, $A \sim B$ if and only if $A$ and $B$ have the same Jordan type (blocks disregarded as to order):

$$A \sim B \iff \mu_A(\lambda) = \mu_B(\lambda) \ \ (\forall \lambda)$$
