Statement

Let C\mathcal{C}C be a site with fiber products, X∈CX \in \mathcal{C}X∈C, and MMM an abelian sheaf. Then

Hˇ1(X, M)  ≅  H1(X, M).\check{H}^{1}(X,\ M) \;\cong\; H^{1}(X,\ M).Hˇ1(X, M)≅H1(X, M).

Notes

Proof. Use the Čech-to-cohomology spectral sequence

E2p,q=Hˇp(X, Hq(M))  ⟹  Hp+q(X,M)✓E^{p,q}_{2} = \check{H}^{p}\bigl(X,\ H^{q}(M)\bigr) \implies H^{p+q}(X, M) ✓E2p,q​=Hˇp(X, Hq(M))⟹Hp+q(X,M)✓

By "higher cohomology presheaves have no Čech cohomology", the whole block with q≠0q \ne 0q=0 vanishes ✓, in particular E20,1=0E^{0,1}_{2} = 0E20,1​=0.

Then the five-term exact sequence of the first-quadrant spectral sequence gives

0→E21,0→H1→E20,1→⋯0 \to E^{1,0}_{2} \to H^{1} \to E^{0,1}_{2} \to \cdots0→E21,0​→H1→E20,1​→⋯

the last term is zero, so E21,0≅H1E^{1,0}_{2} \cong H^{1}E21,0​≅H1 ✓; and E21,0=Hˇ1(X,H0(M))=Hˇ1(X,M)E^{1,0}_{2} = \check{H}^{1}(X, H^{0}(M)) = \check{H}^{1}(X, M)E21,0​=Hˇ1(X,H0(M))=Hˇ1(X,M) ✓ (H0(M)=MH^{0}(M) = MH0(M)=M). ∎

⭐ Hence first Čech cohomology always equals first sheaf cohomology; only higher degrees can differ (and the spectral sequence measures by how much).
