## Statement · **draft**
Let $E, F$ be Banach spaces and $u : E \to F$ linear. If the graph of $u$,

$$G(u) := \{(x, u(x)) : x \in E\}$$

is **closed** in the product space $E \times F$, then $u$ is bounded (hence continuous).

Unwinding convergence in the product topology, the criterion is one sentence: for every sequence $x_n \to x$, if $u(x_n) \to y$, then necessarily $y = u(x)$.
