# Hausdorff　`def.hausdorff`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=def.hausdorff)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./def.hausdorff.md)

A stronger separation property is **normal** ($T_{4}$): any two disjoint closed sets can be separated by open sets. Every compact Hausdorff space is normal (the proof uses compactness twice, building open covers and passing to finite subcovers).

$T_{3}$ and $T_{3.5}$ sit in between: $T_{3}$ says "a point and a closed set not containing it can be separated by open sets", and $T_{3.5}$ upgrades this to separation by **continuous functions** (the statement that "there are enough continuous functions").

The injectivity of the Stone–Čech compactification requires exactly $T_{3.5}$.
