## Notes · **draft**
**It is the matrix of the adjoint operator**: if matrices are taken in an **orthonormal basis**, then for the linear map $T$ the adjoint operator $T^{*}$ (def.operator-adjoint) has as matrix the conjugate transpose of the matrix of $T$. ⚠️ In a non-orthonormal basis these two are no longer equal — that relation is a privilege of orthonormal bases, not a coordinate-free identity.
