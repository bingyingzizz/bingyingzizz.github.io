# Möbius transformations form a group　`prop.mobius-group`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=prop.mobius-group)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./prop.mobius-group.md)

⭐ **Three kinds of subgroups correspond to three geometries**: the real fractional linear transformations preserving the upper half-plane, $\cong \mathrm{PGL}(2, \mathbb{R}) \cong \mathrm{PSL}(2, \mathbb{R})$, form the isometry group of the hyperbolic plane; those preserving the unit disc are conjugate to them. Schwarz–Pick (`thm.schwarz-pick`) says precisely that "a holomorphic self-map does not increase the hyperbolic distance", and equality holds exactly for elements of this group.
