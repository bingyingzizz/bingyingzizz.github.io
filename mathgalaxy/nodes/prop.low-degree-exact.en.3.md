# Low-Degree Exact Sequence　`prop.low-degree-exact`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=prop.low-degree-exact)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./prop.low-degree-exact.md)

$$0 \to \ker\delta^{0} \to M \to \ker\delta^{1} \to \ker\delta^{1}\big/\operatorname{im}\delta^{0} \to 0$$

gives it — each of the three exact pieces uses only the definition.

**The "trivial action" clause**: $\delta^{0} m$ is the function $g \mapsto g m - m$ (**not a constant function**: for $G = C_{2}$ acting by signs and $m = 1$, it takes $1$ at $0$ and $-2$ at the generator); it vanishes identically (that is, equals $0$) only when the action is trivial, so then $B^{1} = 0$ and $Z^{1} = \operatorname{Hom}(G, M)$.
