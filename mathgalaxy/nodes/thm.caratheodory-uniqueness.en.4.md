# uniqueness of the Carathéodory extension　`thm.caratheodory-uniqueness`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=thm.caratheodory-uniqueness)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./thm.caratheodory-uniqueness.md)

⚠ Without $\sigma$-finiteness uniqueness **fails** (Lebesgue length on $\mathbb{R}$ is not $\sigma$-finite, and this cannot be fixed): take $\mathfrak{A} =$ the half-open intervals with finite disjoint unions, $\mu _{0}((a,b]) = +\infty$ ($a < b$); it is not $\sigma$-finite (each piece is $\infty$). Two extensions: $\mu _{1}(E) = +\infty$ ($E \ne \emptyset$), $\mu _{1}(\emptyset) = 0$; another $\mu _{2}(E) = 0$ ($E$ is a Lebesgue null set), and $+\infty$ otherwise. They agree on $\mathfrak{A}$ (both take $\infty$) yet part ways on singletons.
