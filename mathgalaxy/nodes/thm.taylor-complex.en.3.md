the coefficients obtained by term-by-term integration are exactly $f^{(n)}(a)/n!$ (the higher-derivative formula).

⭐ This links "differentiation" and "series": **holomorphic** $\iff$ **locally a power series** $\iff$ **analytic**.

In real analysis the two are **not equivalent** — $e^{-1/x^2}$ is infinitely differentiable at $0$, but its Taylor series is identically $0$, and equals the original function in no neighbourhood of $0$.

⚠ The radius of convergence is at least the distance from $a$ to the nearest singularity — exactly the topic of "where are the singularities" (Chapter 12 of the book).
