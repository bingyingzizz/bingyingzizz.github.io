# Jordan decomposition of a function of bounded variation　`thm.bv-jordan`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=thm.bv-jordan)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./thm.bv-jordan.md)

$F \in BV \iff F = G - H$, where $G, H$ **are both bounded increasing functions**.

## Notes · **draft**
The concrete choice (the function version of the measure-theoretic Jordan decomposition):

$$F = (1/2)(T_F + F) - (1/2)(T_F - F)$$

The first term is called the **positive variation** of $F$, the second the **negative variation** — the names correspond exactly to $\nu ^{+}$ and $\nu ^{-}$ for signed measures.

By the lemma, $T_F \pm  F$ is increasing; boundedness comes from $F \in BV$.
