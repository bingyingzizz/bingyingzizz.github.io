# Laurent Expansion Theorem　`thm.laurent`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=thm.laurent)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./thm.laurent.md)

$$c_n := \frac{1}{2\pi i}\oint_{|\zeta - a| = \rho} \frac{f(\zeta)}{(\zeta - a)^{n+1}}\,\mathrm{d}\zeta,$$

this value is independent of the choice of $\rho$, and $f(z) = \displaystyle\sum_{n=-\infty}^{\infty} c_n (z - a)^n$ holds on $A_{r,R}(a)$ (uniformly on every compact subset).
