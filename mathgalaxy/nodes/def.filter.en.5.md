# Filter and Ultrafilter　`def.filter`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=def.filter)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./def.filter.md)

- $\emptyset \notin \mathcal{F}$ and $X \in \mathcal{F}$: **normalization**. Requiring the family to be nonempty and to omit the empty set, together with upward closure, automatically gives $X$.
- **Upward closure**: part of the definition; dropping it leaves a "filter base", for which one must additionally require that any two members have a smaller member.
- **Closure under finite intersections**: **this is the real condition**, and the reason filters can be translated into nets (`eq.net-filter`).
