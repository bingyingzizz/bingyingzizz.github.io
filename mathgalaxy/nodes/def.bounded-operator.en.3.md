# bounded operator and operator norm　`def.bounded-operator`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=def.bounded-operator)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./def.bounded-operator.md)

⭐ When $E$ is infinite-dimensional and $F \ne \{0\}$, one has $L(E, F) \ne B(E, F)$: discontinuous linear operators really do exist (constructed using the axiom of choice). This is why the graph separates "linear" and "bounded" into two nodes: $L(E,F)$ is the **algebraic** Hom, and $B(E,F)$ is the continuous part of it.
