# Witt Cancellation Theorem　`thm.witt-cancellation`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=thm.witt-cancellation)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./thm.witt-cancellation.md)

**Reading and proof.** $U'\perp W$ denotes the orthogonal summand inside the isometric image ($U'\subseteq V$ is not required). The full proof is on the edge: first extend $f|_W$ to an isometry $V_2$ of the whole space, then use $F^{-1}\circ f$ to fix $W$, and finally, from the uniqueness of $W^{\perp}$, conclude $U\cong U'$.
