## Notes · **draft**
$K$ is called **bounded below** ($n \ll 0$, with $K^{n} = 0$), **bounded above**, or **bounded** (both).

The category of complexes is written $\mathcal{C}(\mathcal{C})$ (see @@def.category-of-complexes@@); its full subcategories of bounded-below, bounded-above and bounded complexes are written $\mathcal{C}^{+}(\mathcal{C})$, $\mathcal{C}^{-}(\mathcal{C})$, $\mathcal{C}^{b}(\mathcal{C})$;

the homotopy category receives the same decorations $+$, $-$, $b$.
