## Notes · **draft**
The quotient group is the **set-theoretic quotient** with one more layer: first quotient $G$ by the equivalence relation “differ by an element of $N$”, then check that an operation survives on the quotient set.

**Universal property** (the real use of quotient groups): for any group homomorphism $\varphi : G \to G'$, if $N \subseteq \ker\varphi$, then $\varphi$ factors uniquely through $\pi$: there exists a unique $\overline{\varphi} : G/N \to G'$ with $\overline{\varphi} \circ \pi = \varphi$.
