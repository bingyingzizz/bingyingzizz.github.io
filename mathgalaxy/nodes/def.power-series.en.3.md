[in graph](../#n=def.power-series)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./def.power-series.md)

⚠ The domain of convergence need not be open: the endpoints require separate discussion (they may converge, diverge, or converge conditionally) — that is exactly what `thm.abel-endpoint` handles.

⭐ A striking phenomenon: **inside its domain of convergence a power series has no "wild" behaviour at all** (it is infinitely differentiable, see "term-by-term differentiation"), in sharp contrast with general series of functions.
