# Cohomology of a Filtered Limit　`prop.filtered-limit-cohomology`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=prop.filtered-limit-cohomology)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./prop.filtered-limit-cohomology.md)

⚠️ Note the direction: the **limit** $X = \varprojlim X_{i}$ is on the topological side, while its **cohomology** becomes a **colimit** — cohomology turns the direction around. This is the source of legitimacy for "approximate the whole by finer and finer finite pieces, then take cohomology".
