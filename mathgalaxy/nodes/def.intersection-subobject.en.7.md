# intersection of subobjects　`def.intersection-subobject`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=def.intersection-subobject)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./def.intersection-subobject.md)

**Relation to union**: the union is the image of the coproduct $Y \cup Z := \operatorname{im}(Y \coprod Z \to X)$, not a fibred coproduct. In a pretopos the subobjects form a bounded lattice: meet = fibred product (greatest lower bound), join = image (least upper bound), see @@prop.subobject-lattice@@.

**Warning**: in a general category $Y \cap Z$ need not exist; even when it does, do not confuse it with the product $Y \times Z$ or the coproduct $Y \coprod Z$. The intersection is "the fibred product along $X$", not "the product of two objects".
