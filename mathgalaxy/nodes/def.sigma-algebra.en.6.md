- **Contains the whole set $\iff$ contains the empty set**: immediate from closure under complements ($\emptyset = X^{c}$). So writing "contains $\emptyset$" or "contains $X$" is equivalent, **provided closure under complements is also present**.
- **Closed under countable unions $\iff$ closed under countable intersections**: **use De Morgan twice**: $\bigcap_{n} A_{n} = \bigl(\bigcup_{n} A_{n}^{c}\bigr)^{c}$. So of "countable unions" and "countable intersections" only one needs to be assumed — **but it must be paired with closure under complements**; without that neither follows.
