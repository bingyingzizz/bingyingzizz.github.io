## Notes · **stale**
Equivalently, we must show that the functor $M \mapsto \operatorname{Hom}_{\mathbb{Z}}(\mathbb{Z}\cdot F, M) \cong \Gamma(F, M)$ preserves epimorphisms and filtered colimits.

The first assertion follows from the projectivity of free compact Hausdorff spaces in $\mathrm{Cond}$. The second follows from the construction of the section functor, which in fact **preserves all colimits**.
