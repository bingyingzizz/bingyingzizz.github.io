## Statement · **draft**
1. A colimit $X = \varinjlim_{i} X_{i}$ is called **universal** if for every $X \to Y$ and $Y' \to Y$,

$$X \times_{Y} Y' \;\cong\; \varinjlim_{i} \bigl(X_{i} \times_{Y} Y'\bigr)$$
