**The proof is on the outgoing edge**: the full proof is in the corollary edge "spectral theorem $\Longrightarrow$ polar decomposition" of this cluster — from the spectral decomposition of $A^{*}A$ construct $\sqrt{A^{*}A}$ and prove uniqueness; when $U=AP^{-1}$ is invertible it is unitary, and in the singular case one first defines an isometry on $\operatorname{im}P$ and extends it to a unitary.
