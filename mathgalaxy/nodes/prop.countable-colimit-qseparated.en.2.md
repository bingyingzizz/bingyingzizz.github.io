# Countable Increasing Colimits Are Quasi-Separated　`prop.countable-colimit-qseparated`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=prop.countable-colimit-qseparated)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./prop.countable-colimit-qseparated.md)

1. $X$ is **quasi-separated** as a condensed set;
2. this colimit **is the same when computed in $\mathrm{Cond}$**: $X \cong \varinjlim_{n} S_{n}$.

## Notes · **draft**
**Proof.** By "quasi-separated $\iff$ filtered colimit with injective transition maps", a filtered colimit with injective transitions is always quasi-separated ✓ — the countable case is a special case.
