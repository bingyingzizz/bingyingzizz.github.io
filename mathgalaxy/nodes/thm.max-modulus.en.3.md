# Maximum Modulus Principle　`thm.max-modulus`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=thm.max-modulus)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./thm.max-modulus.md)

⭐ By connectedness: the set $\{ z : |f(z)| = M \}$ is both open (a whole circle around every point) and closed ($|f|$ is continuous), hence is all of $\Omega$;

so $|f|$ is constant, and adding the holomorphic $f$ gives that $f$ is constant.

⭐ Corollary for bounded regions: if $\Omega$ is bounded, $f$ is continuous on $\overline{\Omega}$ and holomorphic on $\Omega$, then $\|f\|_{\overline{\Omega}} = \|f\|_{\partial\Omega}$ — the maximum is attained on the boundary.

This is the strongest form of "control the interior by boundary values".
