# little Picard theorem　`thm.picard-little`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=thm.picard-little)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./thm.picard-little.md)

📌 Relation to `thm.casorati-weierstrass`: Casorati says the image is **dense** near an essential singularity; Picard strengthens this to “omits at most one point”. The little theorem is its version for “entire functions + singularity at infinity”.
