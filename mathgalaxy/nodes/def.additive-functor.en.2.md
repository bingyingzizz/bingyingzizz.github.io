# additive functor　`def.additive-functor`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=def.additive-functor)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./def.additive-functor.md)

is an **abelian group homomorphism** (that is, preserves addition and $0$).

## Notes · **draft**
**Equivalent description**: an additive functor automatically preserves **finite direct sums** ($F(M \oplus N) \cong F(M) \oplus F(N)$), and $F(0) = 0$. Conversely, a functor preserving finite direct sums (between preadditive categories) is additive.
