# Functoriality of the Tensor Product　`prop.tensor-functor-properties`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=prop.tensor-functor-properties)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./prop.tensor-functor-properties.md)

$0 \to \mathbb{Z} \xrightarrow{\;2\;} \mathbb{Z}$ is exact; after tensoring with $N = \mathbb{Q}$ it stays exact (multiplication by $2$ is still injective on $\mathbb{Q}$), but after tensoring with $N = \mathbb{Z}/2$ multiplication by $2$ becomes the zero map and a kernel appears (see `prop.tensor-right-exact`).

Nor does it preserve infinite products.

📌 On a commutative ring, associativity 4 reads $(M \otimes_{R} N) \otimes_{R} P \cong M \otimes_{R} N \otimes_{R} P$, so multiple tensor products can be written without parentheses.
