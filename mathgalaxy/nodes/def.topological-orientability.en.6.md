# topological orientability　`def.topological-orientability`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=def.topological-orientability)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./def.topological-orientability.md)

is orientable $\iff$ $p$ is trivial $\iff$ $p$ has a continuous section $\iff$ $\widehat{M}$ has exactly two connected components (when $M$ is connected). In the nonorientable case, $\widehat{M}$ is connected and $\mathbb{Z}/2$ acts freely on it, without fixed points.
