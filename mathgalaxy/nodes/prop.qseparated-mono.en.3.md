# Quasi-Separated Condensed Sets Embed into Their Underlying Space　`prop.qseparated-mono`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=prop.qseparated-mono)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./prop.qseparated-mono.md)

Take $f, g \in X(S)$ mapping to the same continuous map: both come from $i$, say through $\tilde f, \tilde g : S \to S_{i}$, and $S_{i} \hookrightarrow X(\cdot)$ is an inclusion, in particular injective ✓, so $\tilde f = \tilde g$ and hence $f = g$ ✓.

∎

⭐ Reading: **a quasi-separated condensed set loses no information** — it can be read back entirely from its underlying space. The opposite is that the underlying space of a general condensed set erases fine structure.
