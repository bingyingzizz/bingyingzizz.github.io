## Statement · **draft**
A **2-dimensional topological manifold** $S$ (see `def.topological-manifold`) is called a **closed surface** if

- $S$ is **compact**;
- $S$ is **connected**;
- $S$ **has no boundary** (each point has a neighborhood modeled on an open subset of $\mathbb{R}^{2}$, with no points of half-plane type).
