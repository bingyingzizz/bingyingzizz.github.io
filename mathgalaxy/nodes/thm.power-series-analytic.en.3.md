## Notes · **draft**
⭐ The most noteworthy point is that **the radius of convergence does not change**: after term-by-term differentiation the coefficients become $n a_n$, and $\sqrt[n]{n} \to 1$, so the radius does not move at all.

Hence one may differentiate repeatedly — **a power series is $C^{\infty}$ on its interval of convergence**, and every derivative is determined by the coefficients: $a_n = f^{(n)}(x_0)/n!$.
