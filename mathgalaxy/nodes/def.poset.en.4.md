**Assumptions used in each direction** (axiomatic definition and passage to the strict order):
- A relation is a partial order $\iff$ it satisfies reflexivity, antisymmetry and transitivity simultaneously, and a relation is a total order $\iff$ it is a partial order plus comparability; both are unfoldings of the definition and need no axiom of choice.
- **Partial order** $\implies$ **strict order**: let $x\prec y$ mean $x\preceq y$ and $x\ne y$; irreflexivity and transitivity follow; conversely, taking the reflexive closure of an irreflexive transitive relation yields a partial order, a finite-step logical deduction.
