## Notes · **stale**
**Proof**. First make precise what it means to be solvable by radicals: There is a tower of fields $F=F_{0}\subseteq F_{1}\subseteq\cdots\subseteq F_{m}$ with $F_{i}=F_{i-1}(\alpha_{i})$ and $\alpha_{i}^{n_{i}}\in F_{i-1}$ ($1\le i\le m$), and $K\subseteq F_{m}$. In characteristic zero, $K/F$ is Galois (a separable splitting field). We prove the two directions separately.
