## Notes · **draft**
⭐ The two blocks have their own domains of convergence: the regular part converges in $|z - a| < R$, the principal part in $|z - a| > r$, and their intersection is exactly the **annulus** — this is why the natural stage for Laurent series is an annulus rather than a disc.
