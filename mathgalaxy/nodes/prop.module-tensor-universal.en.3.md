# Universal Property of the Tensor Product　`prop.module-tensor-universal`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=prop.module-tensor-universal)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./prop.module-tensor-universal.md)

A general module is a quotient of a free one (take a presentation) ✓, and the tensor product over the quotient is the quotient ✓. Then $M \times N \to M \otimes N$ is bilinear ✓, and any bilinear map $M \times N \to P$ factors uniquely through it ✓ — that is exactly the universal property ✓. ∎
