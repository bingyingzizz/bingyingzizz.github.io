⭐ The second compatibility axiom permits "multiplying both sides" only when 0≤c0 \le c0≤c — multiplying by a negative number reverses the inequality (a≤b, c≤0  ⟹  ac≥bca \le b,\ c \le 0 \implies ac \ge bca≤b, c≤0⟹ac≥bc, derived from the first two axioms).

All the usual rules for inequalities over the rationals and the reals ultimately come down to this one axiom.
