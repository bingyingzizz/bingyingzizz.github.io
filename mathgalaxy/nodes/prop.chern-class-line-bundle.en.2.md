# Chern class of a line bundle　`prop.chern-class-line-bundle`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=prop.chern-class-line-bundle)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./prop.chern-class-line-bundle.md)

Let $L \to B$ be a complex line bundle, $\nabla$ a connection, and $\Omega$ its curvature $2$-form (the curvature of a line bundle is just $\mathrm{d}\theta$, since $\operatorname{End}L \cong \mathbb{C}$ is commutative). Then

$$c_{1}(L) = \Bigl[\, \frac{i}{2\pi}\,\Omega \,\Bigr] \in H^{2}(B; \mathbb{Z}) , \qquad c_{j}(L) = 0 \ (j \ge 2) .$$

**Corollary (Chern number)**: if $B$ is a closed orientable surface $\Sigma$, then

$$\int_{\Sigma} c_{1}(L) = \frac{i}{2\pi}\int_{\Sigma}\Omega \;\in\; \mathbb{Z} .$$
