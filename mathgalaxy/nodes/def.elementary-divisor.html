Statement draft

Let RRR be a principal ideal domain and let the nonzero invariant factors of A∈Mm×n(R)A\in M_{m\times n}(R)A∈Mm×n​(R) be d1∣⋯∣drd_1\mid\cdots\mid d_rd1​∣⋯∣dr​. Notes draft

⚠ Disambiguation: the elementary divisors here are a matrix/module-theoretic notion, not the Weierstrass elementary factors Ep(z)=(1−z)exp⁡(z+⋯+zp/p)E_p(z)=(1-z)\exp(z+\cdots+z^p/p)Ep​(z)=(1−z)exp(z+⋯+zp/p) (@@def.elementary-factor@@, entire functions in complex analysis). The two share only their Chinese name.

Relation to invariant factors: did_idi​ is the product of the elementary divisors grouped along the divisibility chain; conversely, the elementary divisors determine all invariant factors uniquely.

Example: if the invariant factors are x−1x-1x−1 and (x−1)(x−2)(x-1)(x-2)(x−1)(x−2), the elementary divisors are x−1, x−1, x−2x-1,\ x-1,\ x-2x−1, x−1, x−2. When KKK is algebraically closed, the elementary divisors of xI−AxI-AxI−A are exactly (x−λ)s(x-\lambda)^s(x−λ)s, each corresponding to a Jordan block of size sss (@@thm.jordan-canonical-form@@).
