# characterisation of compact subsets　`prop.subset-compact-equiv`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=prop.subset-compact-equiv)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./prop.subset-compact-equiv.md)

Total boundedness: given $\varepsilon > 0$, the family $\{B(a,\varepsilon)\cap A\}_{a\in A}$ is an open cover of $A$ (with the subspace metric); compactness gives a finite subcover, and those finitely many centers form an $\varepsilon$-net ✓.
- **$\Longleftarrow$**: suppose $A$ is closed and totally bounded.

First **completeness**: $X$ is complete and $A$ is closed, so a Cauchy sequence in $A$ converges in $X$ to some point, and the limit lies in the closed set $A$; hence $A$ is complete as a subspace ✓.
