# Bessel inequality and Parseval identity　`thm.bessel-parseval`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=thm.bessel-parseval)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./thm.bessel-parseval.md)

1. $\{e_i\}$ is an orthonormal basis;
2. equality holds for every $x$ (**Parseval**): $\|x\|^2 = \sum_i |\langle x, e_i\rangle|^2$;
3. for every $x$: $x = \sum_{i \in I} \langle x, e_i\rangle\, e_i$ (convergence in norm).

## Notes · **draft**
Proof of Bessel: for finite $J$, $0 \le \|x - \sum_{j \in J}\langle x, e_j\rangle e_j\|^2 = \|x\|^2 - \sum_{j \in J}|\langle x,e_j\rangle|^2$, then take the supremum over $J$.
