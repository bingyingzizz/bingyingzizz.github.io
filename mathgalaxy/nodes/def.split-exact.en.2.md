## Notes · **draft**
**Equivalent criteria** (in an abelian category, the three are equivalent):

- $i_{2}$ has a **section** $s : M_{2} \to M$ ($i_{2} \circ s = \mathrm{id}$).
- $i_{1}$ has a **retraction** $r : M \to M_{1}$ ($r \circ i_{1} = \mathrm{id}$).
- $M \cong M_{1} \oplus M_{2}$ is compatible with the sequence.
