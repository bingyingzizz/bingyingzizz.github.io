# totally disconnected spaces are a reflective subcategory　`prop.td-reflective`

## Notes · **draft**
Corollary: **any limit of totally disconnected spaces is again totally disconnected** — because $\pi_{0}$, being a reflector (a left adjoint), preserves colimits, while the inclusion preserves limits. Combined with "$\mathbf{CHaus}$ is closed under limits", this says Stone spaces and Stonean spaces are each closed under limits.
