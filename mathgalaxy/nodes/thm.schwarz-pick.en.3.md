## Notes · **draft**
⭐ Both formulas come from "moving the base point to the origin": $\varphi_a(z) = (z - a)/(1 - \overline{a} z)$ is an automorphism of the disk sending $a$ to $0$; form $\varphi_{f(z_0)} \circ f \circ \varphi_{z_0}^{-1}$, which fixes the origin, apply Schwarz's lemma, then transform back.

⭐ Reading the hyperbolic metric: $\dfrac{|\mathrm{d}w|}{1 - |w|^2}$ is the hyperbolic length element on the disk, and the inequality says that a holomorphic self-map **never enlarges** it. When equality holds, the map is called an isometry.
