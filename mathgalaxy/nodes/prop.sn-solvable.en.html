Statement

The symmetric group SnS_nSn​ is solvable (可解群) if and only if n≤4n \le 4n≤4.

Equivalently: S5S_5S5​ and all SnS_nSn​ with n≥5n \ge 5n≥5 are not solvable, while S1,S2,S3,S4S_1, S_2, S_3, S_4S1​,S2​,S3​,S4​ are solvable.

Notes

⭐ This proposition is the group-theoretic half of the Abel–Ruffini theorem: the reason a general equation of degree five or more cannot be solved in radicals is here — the generic Galois group of the equation is SnS_nSn​, and SnS_nSn​ for n≥5n \ge 5n≥5 is not solvable.

⭐ The dividing line falls between A4A_4A4​ and A5A_5A5​, and this is not an asymptotic phenomenon but a hard jump: A4A_4A4​ has the normal subgroup V4V_4V4​ (so S4S_4S4​ can be split), while A5A_5A5​ has no nontrivial normal subgroup, and A5A_5A5​ is non-abelian, hence not solvable.

⭐ A splitting of S4S_4S4​: {e}⊴V4⊴A4⊴S4\{e\} \trianglelefteq V_4 \trianglelefteq A_4 \trianglelefteq S_4{e}⊴V4​⊴A4​⊴S4​, with quotients Z/2×Z/2\mathbb{Z}/2 \times \mathbb{Z}/2Z/2×Z/2, Z/3\mathbb{Z}/3Z/3, Z/2\mathbb{Z}/2Z/2, all abelian ✓.

For S3S_3S3​ use {e}⊴A3⊴S3\{e\} \trianglelefteq A_3 \trianglelefteq S_3{e}⊴A3​⊴S3​, with quotients Z/3,Z/2\mathbb{Z}/3, \mathbb{Z}/2Z/3,Z/2 ✓.

⭐ Simplicity of A5A_5A5​ can be proved completely elementarily: the conjugacy classes of A5A_5A5​ have sizes 1,15,20,12,121, 15, 20, 12, 121,15,20,12,12; a normal subgroup is a union of classes and its order divides 606060 — checking the possibilities leaves only 111 and 606060.
