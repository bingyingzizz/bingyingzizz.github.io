# tensor product　`def.tensor-product-vs`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=def.tensor-product-vs)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./def.tensor-product-vs.md)

$$(v + v') \otimes w = v \otimes w + v' \otimes w, \qquad v \otimes (w + w') = v \otimes w + v \otimes w', \qquad (cv) \otimes w = v \otimes (cw)$$

Then $\dim(V \otimes W) = (\dim V)(\dim W)$, and the construction **does not depend on the choice of bases** (see the universal property).

## Notes · **draft**
⭐ Elements fall into two classes: **decomposable tensors** $v \otimes w$ and their **finite sums**.

Only the latter fill $V \otimes W$ — “a general tensor is not a single $v \otimes w$” is the most common beginner's trap.
