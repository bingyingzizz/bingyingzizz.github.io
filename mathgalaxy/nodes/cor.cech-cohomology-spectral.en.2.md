## Notes · **draft**
⭐ **Use**: this is the general justification for "computing sheaf cohomology with Čech cohomology". To make the two directly **equal** (rather than merely linked by a spectral sequence), one additionally requires the higher Čech groups to degenerate on the $E_{2}$ page — exactly the step used in the proof of "acyclic $\iff$ Čech cohomology vanishes" ✓.
