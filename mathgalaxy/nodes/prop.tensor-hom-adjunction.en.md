## Statement · **draft**
Fix $V$. Then for all $K$-vector spaces $W, U$ there is a natural isomorphism

$$\operatorname{Hom}_K(W \otimes V,\ U) \;\cong\; \operatorname{Hom}_K\bigl( W,\ \operatorname{Hom}_K(V, U) \bigr)$$

that is, the functor $(-) \otimes V$ is **left adjoint** to $\operatorname{Hom}(V, -)$:

$$(-) \otimes_K V \;\dashv\; \operatorname{Hom}_K(V, -)$$
