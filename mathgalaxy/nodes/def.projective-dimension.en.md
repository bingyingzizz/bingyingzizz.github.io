## Statement · **draft**
Let $M$ be an $R$-module. We say $\operatorname{pd}_{R} M \le n$ if $M$ has a **projective resolution of length $n$**, that is, if there is an exact sequence

$$0 \to P_{n} \to \cdots \to P_{1} \to P_{0} \to M \to 0$$
