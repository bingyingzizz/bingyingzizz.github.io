Statement draft

Let VVV be a finite-dimensional semisimple AAA-module, A′=End⁡A(V)A'=\operatorname{End}_A(V)A′=EndA​(V), and A′′=End⁡A′(V)A''=\operatorname{End}_{A'}(V)A′′=EndA′​(V). The natural map Φ:A→A′′\Phi:A\to A''Φ:A→A′′, a↦(v↦av)a\mapsto(v\mapsto av)a↦(v↦av), is surjective. Equivalently, for every f∈A′′f\in A''f∈A′′ and every finite family v1,…,vn∈Vv_1,\ldots,v_n\in Vv1​,…,vn​∈V there is a∈Aa\in Aa∈A with avi=f(vi)av_i=f(v_i)avi​=f(vi​) for all iii.

Notes draft

Single vector: since VVV is semisimple, V=Av⊕WV=Av\oplus WV=Av⊕W; the projection p:V→Avp:V\to Avp:V→Av is AAA-linear, so p∈A′p\in A'p∈A′. For f∈A′′f\in A''f∈A′′, f(v)=f(pv)=pf(v)∈Avf(v)=f(pv)=p f(v)\in Avf(v)=f(pv)=pf(v)∈Av, hence there is a∈Aa\in Aa∈A with av=f(v)av=f(v)av=f(v). Finite tuple: apply the same argument to the diagonal action on VnV^nVn to find one aaa matching any finite tuple viv_ivi​. In finite dimension VVV is finitely generated over A′A'A′: take generators v1,…,vnv_1,\ldots,v_nv1​,…,vn​ and choose aaa with avi=f(vi)av_i=f(v_i)avi​=f(vi​) for every index; any v=∑ai′viv=\sum a'_iv_iv=∑ai′​vi​ then satisfies f(v)=∑ai′f(vi)=avf(v)=\sum a'_i f(v_i)=avf(v)=∑ai′​f(vi​)=av, so Φ(a)=f\Phi(a)=fΦ(a)=f. This is the elementary form of the double centralizer theorem and does not need Jacobson density.
