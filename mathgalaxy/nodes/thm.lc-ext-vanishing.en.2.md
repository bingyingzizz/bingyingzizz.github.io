## Notes · **draft**
⭐ **This is the endpoint of §8.3**: Ext between locally compact Hausdorff abelian groups can be nonzero only in degrees $0$ and $1$, and vanishes above. This is the final conclusion that condensed mathematics gives for locally compact abelian groups.
