# rank-nullity theorem　`thm.rank-nullity`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=thm.rank-nullity)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./thm.rank-nullity.md)

⭐ Corollaries (the three most used):
- $T$ is injective $\iff \operatorname{rank} T = \dim V$;

$T$ is surjective $\iff \operatorname{rank} T = \dim W$;

- when $\dim V = \dim W < \infty$: $T$ injective $\iff$ $T$ surjective $\iff$ $T$ invertible (**finite dimension only**; it fails in infinite dimension: the shift operator $T(x_1, x_2, \ldots) = (0, x_1, x_2, \ldots)$ is injective but not surjective);

- the solution space of an $n$-variable homogeneous linear system has dimension $= n - \operatorname{rank} A$.

⚠ The clean identity holds only when $\dim V < \infty$;
