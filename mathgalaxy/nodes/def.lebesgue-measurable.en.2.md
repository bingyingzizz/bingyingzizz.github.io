## Notes · **draft**
⚠ **There is a trap here: the composition of two Lebesgue measurable functions need not be Lebesgue measurable.**

The reason: making the composition work requires the intermediate $\sigma$-algebra to cooperate, and $\mathcal{L}$ is strictly larger than $\mathfrak{B}_\mathbb{R}$.
