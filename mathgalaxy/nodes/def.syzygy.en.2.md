## Notes · **draft**
**Origin of the name**
When Hilbert solved systems of linear equations, $\Omega M$ described the linear relations among the remaining unknowns (the Greek word behind “syzygy” originally meant “pairing”).

In the case $M = R/(a_{1}, \ldots, a_{m})$, $\Omega M$ is the submodule generated by the relations $\sum a_{i}e_{i} = 0$, the **first syzygy of the system of equations**.
