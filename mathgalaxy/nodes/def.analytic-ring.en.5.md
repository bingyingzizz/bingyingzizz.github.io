# analytic ring　`def.analytic-ring`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=def.analytic-ring)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./def.analytic-ring.md)

$$\operatorname{RHom}_{\mathcal{A}}(\mathcal{A}[S], C) \cong \operatorname{RHom}_A(\mathcal{A}[S], C) \cong \operatorname{RHom}_A(A[S], C),$$

Both isomorphisms hold term by term ($\operatorname{Hom}$ takes $C^{S}$ term by term), hence also at the derived level. Thus the comparison map in the definition is the map induced by $\iota_S$, namely $\operatorname{RHom}_A(\mathcal{A}[S], C) \to \operatorname{RHom}_A(A[S], C)$ — **the whole definition uses only the underlying $A$-modules and never the subcategory of complete modules being defined**, so there is no circularity.
