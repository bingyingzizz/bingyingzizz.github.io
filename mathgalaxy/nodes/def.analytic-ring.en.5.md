# analytic ring　`def.analytic-ring`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=def.analytic-ring)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./def.analytic-ring.md)

$$\operatorname{RHom}_{\mathcal{A}}(\mathcal{A}[S], C) \cong \operatorname{RHom}_A(\mathcal{A}[S], C),$$

This is the isomorphism supplied by the adjunction, not "the same set". For a general $A$-module complex $C$, the left-hand side has not even been defined — the "comparison map" is exactly the object to be checked in the definition; identifying the two directly at the derived level would first require a derived category of complete modules, which is not proved here (see the gap at the end of the notes).
