# Sections of Ext on Stonean Spaces　`lem.ext-stonean-section`

[in graph](../#n=lem.ext-stonean-section)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./lem.ext-stonean-section.md)

(the adjoint form of $M \cdot S = M \otimes_{\mathbb{Z}} \mathbb{Z}[S]$), take right derived functors on both sides: the left gives $\operatorname{Ext}^{n}_{\mathbb{Z}}(M\cdot S, N)$;

the right, since $\operatorname{Hom}(\mathbb{Z}[S],-)$ is exact, passes through the derived functor and gives $\operatorname{Hom}_{\mathbb{Z}}\bigl(\mathbb{Z}[S], \operatorname{Ext}^{n}_{\mathbb{Z}}(M,N)\bigr)$, which by definition is $\operatorname{Ext}^{n}_{\mathbb{Z}}(M,N)(S)$.

Compare the two sides.
