## Notes · **draft**
**Why "local homeomorphism + discrete fibre" rather than "locally trivial fibre bundle"**: a covering is **stiffer** than a fibre bundle — each sheet $V_{j}$ is required to be homeomorphic to $U$, not merely fibre-homotopy equivalent. Hence the fibres of a covering are necessarily discrete. This stiffness is exactly what makes the lifting theorem work.
