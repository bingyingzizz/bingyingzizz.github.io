# uniformly convex space　`def.uniformly-convex`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=def.uniformly-convex)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./def.uniformly-convex.md)

## Notes · **draft**
**Examples**: Hilbert spaces (the parallelogram law gives $\|(x+y)/2\|^2 = 1 - \|x-y\|^2/4$, so one may take $\delta = 1 - \sqrt{1 - \varepsilon^2/4}$); $L^p$ with $1 < p < \infty$ (Clarkson's inequality); $\ell^p$ with $1 < p < \infty$.

**Counterexamples**: $\ell^1$, $\ell^\infty$, $c_0$, $C[0,1]$ are not uniformly convex (in $\ell^1$ the midpoint of $e_1, e_2$ still has norm $1$; in $\ell^\infty$ piecewise constant sequences such as $(1,0,\ldots)$ and $(1,1,0,\ldots)$ give a counterexample).
