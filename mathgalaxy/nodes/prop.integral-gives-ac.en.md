## Statement · **draft**
Let $\mu$ be a measure and $f$ a generalized $\mu$-integrable function; define

$$\nu(E) := \int_E f d\mu$$

Then $\nu$ is a signed measure and $\nu \ll  \mu$. Moreover

$\nu$ is **finite** $\iff f \in L^1(\mu)$
