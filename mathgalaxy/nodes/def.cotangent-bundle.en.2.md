# Cotangent Bundle　`def.cotangent-bundle`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=def.cotangent-bundle)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./def.cotangent-bundle.md)

$$T_{p}^{\ast}M := (T_{p}M)^{\ast} = \operatorname{Hom}_{\mathbb{R}}\bigl(T_{p}M,\mathbb{R}\bigr),$$

whose elements are called **covectors**, or **1-forms at $p$** (also Pfaffian forms). The **cotangent bundle** stacks them over the base points:

$$T^{\ast}M := \bigsqcup_{p\in M}T_{p}^{\ast}M,$$

with projection $\pi : T^{\ast}M\to M$ sending $\alpha\in T_{p}^{\ast}M$ to $\pi(\alpha) := p$.
