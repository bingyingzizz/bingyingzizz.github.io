## Statement · **draft**
Let $K/F$ be a field extension. For $K$, a **$F$-automorphism** means a field isomorphism $\sigma : K \to K$ with $\sigma|_{F} = \mathrm{id}_{F}$. They form a group under composition, written

$$\operatorname{Gal}(K/F) := \operatorname{Aut}_{F}(K).$$

For a polynomial $f \in F[x]$ one often writes $\operatorname{Gal}(f) := \operatorname{Gal}(K/F)$, where $K$ is the splitting field of $f$.
