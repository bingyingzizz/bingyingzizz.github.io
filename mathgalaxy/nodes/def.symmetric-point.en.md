## Statement · **draft**
Let the circle $C$ have centre $a$ and radius $R$. One says that $z$ and $z^{*}$ are **symmetric with respect to** $C$ if

$$(z - a)(\overline{z^{*}} - \bar a) = R^{2},$$

and $\arg(z - a) = \arg(z^{*} - a)$ (that is, $a, z, z^{*}$ are collinear and point in the same direction).
