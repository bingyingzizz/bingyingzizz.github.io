# Inverse Image Is Compatible with Hom and Tensor　`prop.inverse-image-tensor`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=prop.inverse-image-tensor)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./prop.inverse-image-tensor.md)

$$f_{*}\operatorname{Hom}_{\mathbb{Z}}\bigl(f^{-1}M,\ N\bigr) = \operatorname{Hom}_{\mathbb{Z}}\bigl(M,\ f_{*}N\bigr), \qquad f^{-1}\bigl(M \otimes_{\mathbb{Z}} N\bigr) \cong f^{-1}M \otimes_{\mathbb{Z}} f^{-1}N.$$

## Notes · **draft**
**Proof.** The first identity strings together the adjunction $f^{-1} \dashv f_{*}$ and the adjunction of internal Hom ✓ (the natural isomorphisms they give on test objects are the same).
