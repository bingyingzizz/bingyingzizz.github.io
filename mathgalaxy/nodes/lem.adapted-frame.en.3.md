# Adapted Frame　`lem.adapted-frame`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=lem.adapted-frame)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./lem.adapted-frame.md)

⭐ This is exactly the entry point for "skipping the complicated computation": the quantities $r_{uu}, r_{uv}$ that would require computing the partial derivatives $h_{ij}$ in coordinates are here just the coefficients of $\omega^{i}_{\ 3}$.

⚠️ $\omega^{3} = 0$ holds **along the surface**, not for the frame itself: on an open set of $\mathbb{R}^{3}$, $\omega^{3}$ is generally nonzero, and that is precisely what corresponds to "the surface bending out in the normal direction".
