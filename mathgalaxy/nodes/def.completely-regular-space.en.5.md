# Completely Regular Space　`def.completely-regular-space`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=def.completely-regular-space)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./def.completely-regular-space.md)

⭐ **Why "embeds in a cube" is the same statement**: if $X$ is completely regular, assemble all continuous $f : X \to [0,1]$ into a map $X \to [0,1]^{I}$; $T_{1}$ (points closed) together with $T_{3.5}$ (points and closed sets separable by functions) is exactly what makes it an embedding. Conversely, an embedding into a cube obviously supplies enough functions. This is the other half of Tychonoff's theorem.
