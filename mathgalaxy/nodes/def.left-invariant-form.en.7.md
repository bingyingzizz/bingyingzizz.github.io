# left-invariant form　`def.left-invariant-form`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=def.left-invariant-form)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./def.left-invariant-form.md)

**Orientability**: for $k = n$, $\bigwedge^{n}\mathfrak{g}^{\ast}$ is one-dimensional; translating a nonzero element to the left gives an everywhere nonzero $n$-form, so `prop.lie-group-orientable` (a left-invariant top form is everywhere nonzero) shows **every Lie group is orientable** ($S^{1}$, $\operatorname{SO}(3)$ and $\operatorname{GL}(n,\mathbb{R})$ are, while a general manifold need not be).
