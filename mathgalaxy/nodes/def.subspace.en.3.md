Thus the lattice of subspaces is "closed under intersections, generally not under unions" — to combine subspaces one uses the **sum** $U + W$.

⚠ The empty set is not a subspace;

$\{0\}$ and $V$ are the two smallest subspaces (called **trivial subspaces**).

⭐ Do not confuse this with the topological notion of subspace (`def.subspace-topology`, the subspace topology): the two notions share a name but mean completely different things.
