# Balancedness of Ext　`thm.ext-balanced`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=thm.ext-balanced)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./thm.ext-balanced.md)

$$H^{n}\bigl(\operatorname{Hom}_{R}(P_{\bullet}, N)\bigr) \;\cong\; \operatorname{Ext}^{n}_{R}(M, N) \;\cong\; H^{n}\bigl(\operatorname{Hom}_{R}(M, I^{\bullet})\bigr).$$

In other words, $\operatorname{Ext}$ can be computed using either a projective resolution of the **first variable** (left-hand side) or an injective resolution of the **second variable** (right-hand side). Both computations give the same group.
