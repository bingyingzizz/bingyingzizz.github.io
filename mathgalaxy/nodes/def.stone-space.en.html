Statement

  • XXX is totally disconnected if every connected component is a single point;
  • XXX is extremally disconnected if the closure of every open set is again clopen;
  • a Stone space = a totally disconnected compact Hausdorff space;
  • a Stonean space = an extremally disconnected compact Hausdorff space.

Two conditions that look superficially alike sit at opposite ends: total disconnectedness says "shattered until every component is a single point", while extreme disconnectedness says "the closure of an open set does not grow". The latter is stronger — an extremally disconnected T1T_{1}T1​ space is necessarily totally disconnected.
