Statement draft

Let GGG be a finite group with char⁡F∤∣G∣\operatorname{char}F\nmid|G|charF∤∣G∣. For functions f1,f2:G→Ff_1,f_2:G\to Ff1​,f2​:G→F define ⟨f1,f2⟩=1∣G∣∑g∈Gf1(g)f2(g−1)\langle f_1,f_2\rangle=\frac{1}{|G|}\sum_{g\in G}f_1(g)f_2(g^{-1})⟨f1​,f2​⟩=∣G∣1​∑g∈G​f1​(g)f2​(g−1). This is an FFF-bilinear form; on the space of class functions it gives the pairing used in character theory.

Notes draft

Bilinearity is immediate; symmetry follows from the substitution g↦g−1g\mapsto g^{-1}g↦g−1.\nOn class functions ⟨f,f⟩=1∣G∣∑gf(g)f(g−1)\langle f,f\rangle=\frac1{|G|}\sum_g f(g)f(g^{-1})⟨f,f⟩=∣G∣1​∑g​f(g)f(g−1); for complex representations (F⊆CF\subseteq\mathbb CF⊆C) with fff complex conjugation, it is a positive definite Hermitian form when restricted to the space of characters; over a general field only nondegeneracy is required.\nThe pairing writes the orthogonality of irreducible characters as ⟨χi,χj⟩=δij\langle\chi_i,\chi_j\rangle=\delta_{ij}⟨χi​,χj​⟩=δij​.
