# Orientation and Volume Forms　`prop.volume-form-orientation`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=prop.volume-form-orientation)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./prop.volume-form-orientation.md)

**"Everywhere nonzero" cannot be weakened to "almost everywhere nonzero"**: at a zero one cannot choose an orientation. Any $n$-form on a nonorientable manifold must have a zero — this is the precise form of "a volume form cannot be chosen globally on $\mathbb{RP}^{2}$".

**Where connectedness is used**: only in "$f > 0$ iff the same orientation". On a disconnected manifold $f$ may have different signs on different components, so two everywhere nonzero $n$-forms may agree only on some components — the sign issue must then be discussed componentwise.
