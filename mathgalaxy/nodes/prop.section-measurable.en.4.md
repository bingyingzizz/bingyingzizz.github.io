# sections of measurable sets and functions　`prop.section-measurable`

$$\mathcal{R} = \{E \subseteq X \times Y : E_x \in \mathcal{N} \ \wedge\ E^y \in \mathcal{M}\quad \forall(x, y)\}$$

Then $\mathcal{R}$ contains all rectangles, and **$\mathcal{R}$ is a $\sigma$-algebra** (the section operations preserve unions, intersections and complements). Hence $\mathcal{R} \supseteq \mathcal{M} \otimes \mathcal{N}$.

(b) follows from (a) and two identities:

$$f_x^{-1}(B) = (f^{-1}(B))_x, \quad  f^{y-1}(B) = (f^{-1}(B))^y$$
