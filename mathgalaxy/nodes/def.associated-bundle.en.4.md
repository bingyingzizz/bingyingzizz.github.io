# Associated Bundle　`def.associated-bundle`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=def.associated-bundle)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./def.associated-bundle.md)

**Why use $G$ rather than "some arbitrary isomorphism"**: in the definition of the associated bundle, the $G$-action is the **only gluing data**. Thus "associated bundle" writes "a fibre bundle together with its structure group living in $G$" as one concrete construction — two ways of saying the same thing as "the structure group is $G$" in @@def.fiber-bundle@@.

⚠️ **Sections versus equivariant maps**: sections $M \to P\times_{G}F$ of the associated bundle correspond one-to-one to "$G$-equivariant maps $P \to F$".
