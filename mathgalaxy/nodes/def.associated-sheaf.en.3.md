# Associated Sheaf　`def.associated-sheaf`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=def.associated-sheaf)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./def.associated-sheaf.md)

⭐ **Use**: it gives the canonical way to "regard an object of $\mathcal{C}$ as a sheaf", namely $X \mapsto X^{\sharp}$. When the topology is **subcanonical**, $h_{X}$ is already a sheaf, so $X^{\sharp} = h_{X}$, and the Yoneda embedding factors directly through the sheaf category ✓ (this is also the most common equivalent form of "subcanonical").

⚠️ This functor is **left exact** (the Yoneda embedding preserves limits and sheafification is left exact), but in general **not faithful**, and it does not preserve colimits or infinite limits: sheafification glues together "different presheaf descriptions of the same object".
