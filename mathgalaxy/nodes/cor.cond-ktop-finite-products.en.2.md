## Notes · **draft**
⭐ **Why it matters**: it says the functor "condensed set $\mapsto$ underlying topological space" **does not break finite products**, so a product computed in $\mathrm{Cond}$ is still that product on spaces (after $k$-ification). This is the most-used step when translating algebraic constructions in $\mathrm{Cond}$ back to topological ones.
