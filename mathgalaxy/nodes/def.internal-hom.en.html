Statement

Let T\mathcal{T}T be a topos, X∈TX \in \mathcal{T}X∈T, M∈T(Ab)M \in \mathcal{T}(\mathbf{Ab})M∈T(Ab). Then

Hom⁡(X,M):Y⟼Hom⁡(X×Y, M)  ≅  Hom⁡Z(Z⋅(X×Y), M)\operatorname{Hom}(X, M) : Y \longmapsto \operatorname{Hom}(X \times Y,\ M) \;\cong\; \operatorname{Hom}_{\mathbb{Z}}\bigl(\mathbb{Z}\cdot(X \times Y),\ M\bigr)Hom(X,M):Y⟼Hom(X×Y, M)≅HomZ​(Z⋅(X×Y), M)

is an abelian sheaf on T\mathcal{T}T, called the internal Hom of MMM at XXX.

Also, for M,N∈T(Ab)M, N \in \mathcal{T}(\mathbf{Ab})M,N∈T(Ab), the presheaf X↦Hom⁡Z(M, Hom⁡(X,N))X \mapsto \operatorname{Hom}_{\mathbb{Z}}\bigl(M,\ \operatorname{Hom}(X, N)\bigr)X↦HomZ​(M, Hom(X,N)) is representable;

the representing object is denoted Hom⁡Z(M,N)∈T(Ab)\operatorname{Hom}_{\mathbb{Z}}(M, N) \in \mathcal{T}(\mathbf{Ab})HomZ​(M,N)∈T(Ab).

Notes

Thus there is a natural isomorphism

Hom⁡Z(M, Hom⁡(X,N))  ≅  Hom⁡(X, Hom⁡Z(M,N))  ≅  Hom⁡Z(M,N)(X)\operatorname{Hom}_{\mathbb{Z}}\bigl(M,\ \operatorname{Hom}(X, N)\bigr) \;\cong\; \operatorname{Hom}\bigl(X,\ \operatorname{Hom}_{\mathbb{Z}}(M, N)\bigr) \;\cong\; \operatorname{Hom}_{\mathbb{Z}}(M, N)(X)HomZ​(M, Hom(X,N))≅Hom(X, HomZ​(M,N))≅HomZ​(M,N)(X)

The "representable" step uses only one fact: that presheaf preserves all limits (hence, by the criterion for representable functors, it has a representing object).

Two corollaries: Hom⁡Z(M,N)=Hom⁡Z(M,N)(1)\operatorname{Hom}_{\mathbb{Z}}(M, N) = \operatorname{Hom}_{\mathbb{Z}}(M, N)(\mathbf{1})HomZ​(M,N)=HomZ​(M,N)(1) — in other words T(Ab)\mathcal{T}(\mathbf{Ab})T(Ab) is enriched over itself;

and Hom⁡Z(Z⋅X,M)≅Hom⁡(X,M)\operatorname{Hom}_{\mathbb{Z}}(\mathbb{Z}\cdot X, M) \cong \operatorname{Hom}(X, M)HomZ​(Z⋅X,M)≅Hom(X,M).

The internal Hom computed in the presheaf category agrees with that in the sheaf category: Hom⁡C^(Ab)(M,N)=Hom⁡C~(Ab)(M,N)\operatorname{Hom}_{\widehat{\mathcal{C}}(\mathbf{Ab})}(M,N) = \operatorname{Hom}_{\widetilde{\mathcal{C}}(\mathbf{Ab})}(M,N)HomC(Ab)​(M,N)=HomC(Ab)​(M,N).
