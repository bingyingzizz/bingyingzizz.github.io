## Notes · **draft**
Translations among the four: (2)$\iff$(3) take $\mathcal{C} = \mathcal{T}$ with the standard topology; (3)$\iff$(4) is trivial (sheafification from presheaves to sheaves is an exact reflection); (4)$\implies$(1) uses "the presheaf category is a topos" together with the reflection to transport the structure down;

(1)$\implies$(2) also equips $\mathcal{T}$ with the standard topology.

Item 4 is the most useful: **to build a topos, it suffices to build a presheaf category together with an exact reflection**. Condensed sets arise exactly this way — as sheaves on compact Hausdorff spaces.
