## Notes · **draft**
**Difference from the Stonean item.** Stonean requires **extremally** disconnected (the closure of an open set is open); Stone only requires **totally** disconnected (no nontrivial connected component). Total disconnectedness is weaker than extreme disconnectedness, so this cannot be deduced directly from the previous item and needs a separate proof — but the conclusion has the same shape: acyclicity.
