that is: **each step depends only on the values already taken**.

**Corollary (transfinite induction)**: if $S \subseteq W$ and $\forall w \in W\; ( W_{< w} \subseteq S \to w \in S )$, then $S = W$.

Taking $W = \omega$ gives **recursive definition** and **mathematical induction** on $\mathbb{N}$; taking $W$ arbitrary gives **transfinite recursion** and **transfinite induction**.
