## Statement · **draft**
Let $f$ be holomorphic on $|z| \le R$ with $f(0) \ne 0$, and let its zeros be $z_{1}, \ldots, z_{n}$ (counted with multiplicity, $0 < |z_{j}| \le R$). Then

$$\log |f(0)| + \sum_{j=1}^{n} \log \frac{R}{|z_{j}|} = \frac{1}{2\pi}\int_{0}^{2\pi} \log |f(R e^{i\theta})|\,\mathrm{d}\theta.$$
