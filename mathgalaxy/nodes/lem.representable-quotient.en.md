## Statement · **draft**
Let $\mathcal{T}$ be a topos (with the standard topology). If in an epimorphism between sheaves

$$\coprod_{i \in I} F_{i} \longrightarrow F$$

every $F_{i}$ and every $F_{i} \times_{F} F_{j}$ is **representable**, then $F$ is also representable.
