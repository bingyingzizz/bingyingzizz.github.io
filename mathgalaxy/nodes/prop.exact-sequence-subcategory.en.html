Statement

Let C\mathcal{C}C be an abelian category and C3\mathcal{C}^{3}C3 the category of composable pairs of morphisms (objects M′→M→M′′M' \to M \to M''M′→M→M′′, morphisms the compatible comparison maps of the three components). Then left exact, right exact and short exact sequences each form an additive subcategory of C3\mathcal{C}^{3}C3.

Notes

⭐ The point is to treat "taking exact sequences" as an object that can be manipulated: two exact sequences can be added, and kernels and cokernels taken, with the result staying inside — it is this closure that makes the spectral-sequence machinery (filtered complexes, double complexes) writable.
