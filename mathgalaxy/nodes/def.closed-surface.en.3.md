# closed surface　`def.closed-surface`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=def.closed-surface)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./def.closed-surface.md)

- Dropping the requirement of no boundary, $\implies$ becomes a compact surface with boundary. Capping the boundary components with disks returns to the closed case.

⭐ **Distinctions from three other notions of surface** (the reason for this node):

`def.regular-surface` and `def.orientable-surface` concern subsets of $\mathbb{R}^{3}$ covered by parametrizations (**extrinsic**, with a metric and curvature). A `def.riemann-surface` is a one-dimensional **complex** manifold. This node concerns **topology alone**, with no metric, complex structure, or presumed embedding in Euclidean space.
