# quotient vector space　`def.quotient-vs`

[in graph](../#n=def.quotient-vs)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./def.quotient-vs.md)

These two operations **do not depend on the choice of representatives** (because $U$ is a subspace), so $V / U$ becomes a $K$-vector space, called the **quotient space**.

The quotient map $\pi : V \to V/U$, $v \mapsto v + U$ is a surjective linear map, and $\ker \pi = U$.

## Notes · **draft**
⭐ This is the quotient set `def.quotient-set` **plus structure**: first compress $V$ by $U$ into a set, then check that the operations are well defined.

The well-definedness step is the whole content — because the operations are written on representatives, and there are infinitely many ways to choose them.
