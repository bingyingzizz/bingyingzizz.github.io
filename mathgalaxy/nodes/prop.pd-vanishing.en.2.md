# Projective Dimension and the Vanishing of Ext　`prop.pd-vanishing`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=prop.pd-vanishing)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./prop.pd-vanishing.md)

1. $\operatorname{pd}_{R} M \le n$.
2. The $n$th syzygy $\Omega^{n}M$ is **projective** (equivalently, $M$ has a projective resolution of length $n$).
3. For every $R$-module $N$ and every $k > n$, we have $\operatorname{Ext}^{k}_{R}(M, N) = 0$.
4. For every $R$-module $N$, we have $\operatorname{Ext}^{n+1}_{R}(M, N) = 0$.

Dually, $\operatorname{id}_{R} M \le n$ if and only if $\operatorname{Ext}^{k}_{R}(N, M) = 0$ holds for every $N$ and every $k > n$.
