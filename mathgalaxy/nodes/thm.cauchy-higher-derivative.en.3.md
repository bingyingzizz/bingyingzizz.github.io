# Cauchy Integral Formula for Derivatives　`thm.cauchy-higher-derivative`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=thm.cauchy-higher-derivative)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./thm.cauchy-higher-derivative.md)

When $n = 1$, the difference between the difference quotient and the "formal derivative" is

$$\frac{1}{2\pi i}\oint f(\zeta)\left[ \frac{1}{(\zeta - z - h)(\zeta - z)} - \frac{1}{(\zeta - z)^2} \right]\mathrm{d}\zeta = \frac{h}{2\pi i}\oint \frac{f(\zeta)\,\mathrm{d}\zeta}{(\zeta - z - h)(\zeta - z)^2}.$$
