Statement

Let RRR be a commutative ring with identity. An RRR-algebra is an RRR-module AAA together with an RRR-bilinear multiplication A×A→AA\times A\to AA×A→A, (x,y)↦xy(x,y)\mapsto xy(x,y)↦xy.
