# Grothendieck's AB Axioms　`def.ab-axioms`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=def.ab-axioms)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./def.ab-axioms.md)

⭐ **There are also implications between these axioms** (see "the relation of AB5 to AB3*/AB4*"): a Grothendieck category automatically satisfies AB3*; AB5 and AB5* are mutually exclusive (apart from the zero category they cannot both hold);

on a topos $\mathbf{Ab}(\mathcal{T})$ stops at AB5 + AB3*, while $\mathrm{CondAb}$ reaches AB6 + AB4*.

⭐ **Which categories sit at which level** is a separate matter, see "the table of examples for the AB axioms" — that table ranges over $\mathbf{Ab}$, $\mathbf{AbTop}$, $\mathbf{AbCHaus}$, presheaf categories, abelian groups on a topos, and $\mathrm{CondAb}$.
