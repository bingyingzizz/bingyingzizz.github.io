# spectral theorem for complex normal operators　`cor.spectral-normal`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=cor.spectral-normal)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./cor.spectral-normal.md)

s.
- Orthogonal projection $\implies$ self-adjoint with spectrum in $\{0,1\}$, by idempotence; conversely, the self-adjoint spectral decomposition gives an idempotent, hence an orthogonal projection. Once normality is assumed in this node, spectrum in $\{0,1\}$ already suffices.
- A non-normal Jordan block $\left(\begin{smallmatrix}1&1\\0&1\end{smallmatrix}\right)$ has spectrum entirely real of modulus $1$, yet is neither self-adjoint nor unitary; a nontrivial rotation of the real plane has no real eigenbasis, showing that the complex hypothesis matters.
