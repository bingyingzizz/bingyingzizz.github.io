# Crystalline Sheaves Are Sheaves on X　`prop.crystalline-sheaf-equiv`

## Statement · **draft**
1. **Realization** $\mathcal{F} \mapsto \mathcal{F}_{X}$ gives an equivalence between the **crystalline sheaves** on $\mathbf{Top}_{/X}$ and the **sheaves** on $X$;
2. Hence $\mathbf{Et}(X)$ (the category of étale spaces over $X$) $\simeq$ crystalline sheaves $\simeq$ sheaves on $X$ — in particular $\mathbf{Et}(X)$ is a topos, because $\mathbf{Et}(X) \simeq \widetilde{\mathbf{Open}(X)}$.
