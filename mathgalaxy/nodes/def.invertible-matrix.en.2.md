## Notes · **draft**
⭐ One table of equivalences linking three clusters: for $A \in M_n(K)$, the following are equivalent —
- $A$ is invertible;
- the linear map $x \mapsto Ax$ is a bijection (that is, an isomorphism);
- $\operatorname{rank} A = n$ (full rank);
- $\ker A = \{0\}$ (nullity $0$);
- the columns of $A$ are linearly independent (hence a basis);
- $\det A \ne 0$;
- $A$ is a product of finitely many elementary matrices.

⚠ **Finite dimension is essential**: only when $\dim V < \infty$ do "injective $\iff$ surjective $\iff$ invertible" hold (is the injective shift operator invertible in infinite dimension? No).
