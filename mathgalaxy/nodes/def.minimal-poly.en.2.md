# minimal polynomial　`def.minimal-poly`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=def.minimal-poly)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./def.minimal-poly.md)

$\mu_T$ is called the **minimal polynomial** of $T$. It is the monic polynomial of **lowest degree** that annihilates $T$ (and it is unique).

⚠️ Distinguish it from the "minimal polynomial of an algebraic element" in field theory (`def.algebraic-element`, in this atlas): one annihilates an operator, the other an algebraic element; formally the same thing, but the names must be kept apart.
