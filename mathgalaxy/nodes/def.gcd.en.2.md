## Notes · **draft**
Why the two descriptions agree: on one hand $\gcd$ divides every element of $ua + vb$, hence it is $\le$ the smallest positive element; on the other hand the smallest positive element divides $a$ and $b$ (division with remainder writes the remainder in the smaller form $a - q(ua+vb)$, which must be zero), so it is the greatest common divisor.
