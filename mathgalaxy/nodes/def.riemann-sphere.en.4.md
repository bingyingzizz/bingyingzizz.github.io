# Riemann sphere　`def.riemann-sphere`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=def.riemann-sphere)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./def.riemann-sphere.md)

**Where the formula comes from**: parametrize the line through $N$ and a point $(x,y,w)$ of the sphere as $N + t((x,y,w) - N)$, set the third coordinate equal to $0$, solve for $t$ to get $2(x,y)/(1-w)$; then combine $|z|^{2}$ with $w$ and use $x^{2}+y^{2}+w^{2}=1$ to simplify to the clean form above.

⚠️ The $\infty$ added here is **one point**, not two points $+\infty$ and $-\infty$ (that is the one-dimensional real convention, see `def.extended-real`). Complex numbers have no "sign", so there is only one point at infinity.
