## Statement · **draft**
Let $M = \prod_I \mathbb{Z}$ and $N = \prod_J \mathbb{Z}$. Then

$$M \otimes^{L\blacksquare} N \cong \prod_{I \times J} \mathbb{Z},$$

in particular $M \otimes^{L\blacksquare} N$ is concentrated in homological degree $0$.
