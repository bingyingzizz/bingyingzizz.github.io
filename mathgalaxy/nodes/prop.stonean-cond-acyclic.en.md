## Statement · **draft**
Let $S$ be a **Stonean space** (an extremally disconnected compact Hausdorff space) and $M$ a condensed abelian group. Then for all $n > 0$,

$$H^{n}_{\mathrm{cond}}(S,\ M) = 0.$$
