**The proof is on the outgoing edge**: the full proof is the corollary edge "Schur triangularization $\Longrightarrow$ spectral theorem (matrix version)" in this cluster — normality is preserved under unitary similarity, and an upper triangular normal matrix must be diagonal; the real symmetric case then follows by induction using "eigenvalues are real and eigenvectors can be chosen real".
