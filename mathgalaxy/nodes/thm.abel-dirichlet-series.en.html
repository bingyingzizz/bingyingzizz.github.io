Notes

⭐ These two tests deal with series that are, in general, not absolutely convergent — the criteria for positive-term series (comparison, Kummer, root, integral) are all useless here.

⭐ The Leibniz alternating-series test is a special case of Dirichlet: take an=(−1)na_n = (-1)^{n}an​=(−1)n (whose partial sums take only the two values 0,10, 10,1, hence are bounded) and let bnb_nbn​ decrease monotonically to 000; this gives "the alternating series converges".

So Leibniz's test is not an independent tool but the special case just described.
