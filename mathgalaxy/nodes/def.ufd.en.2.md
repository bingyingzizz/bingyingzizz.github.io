## Notes · **draft**
$\mathbb{Z}$ and $K[x]$ are both UFDs (the latter by `thm.poly-ufd`), while $\mathbb{Z}[\sqrt{-5}]$ is not:

$$6 = 2 \cdot 3 = (1+\sqrt{-5})(1-\sqrt{-5}),$$

the four factors are all irreducible and pairwise non-associate — so factorization is not unique.
