## Statement · **draft**
Let $M$ be an abelian sheaf on a site $\mathcal{C}$ and let $X \in \mathcal{C}$. Take a **hypercover** of $M$ (that is, a complex $F_{\bullet} \to M$ in which each $F_{n}$ is a direct sum of representable sheaves and which is exact everywhere after augmentation). Then the cohomology of $M$ equals the homotopy groups of the **simplicial abelian group** obtained by taking sections levelwise:

$$H^{n}(X,\ M) \cong \pi^{n}\bigl(\Gamma(X,\ F_{\bullet})\bigr).$$
