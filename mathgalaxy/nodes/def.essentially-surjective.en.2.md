## Notes · **draft**
Note that this is $\cong$, not $=$: the requirement is that the image of $F$ be **isomorphic to** every object, not that the image of $F$ **be** all objects. This single isomorphism is exactly where equivalence is laxer than isomorphism — and precisely what makes it useful, since most interesting equivalences are not isomorphisms.
