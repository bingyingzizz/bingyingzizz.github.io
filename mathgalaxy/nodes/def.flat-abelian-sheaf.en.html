def.flat-abelian-sheaf

Machine-drafted and pending author review. The Chinese text is authoritative.

Statement

Let T\mathcal{T}T be a topos. An abelian group PPP in it is called flat if the functor

M⟼P⊗ZMM \longmapsto P \otimes_{\mathbb{Z}} MM⟼P⊗Z​M

is exact.

Notes

Example: ordinary abelian groups are flat   ⟺  \iff⟺ torsion-free. It follows immediately that Z⋅X\mathbb{Z}\cdot XZ⋅X is always flat — reduce first to presheaves, then to ordinary abelian groups, and free abelian groups are torsion-free.
