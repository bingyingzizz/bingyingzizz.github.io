# Completely Normal Space　`def.completely-normal-space`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=def.completely-normal-space)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./def.completely-normal-space.md)

**Equivalent criterion (Urysohn's separation theorem; the most usable form)**: $X$ is completely normal $\iff$ for any two **separated sets** $A, B$ (that is, $\overline{A} \cap B = A \cap \overline{B} = \emptyset$) there exist disjoint open sets $U \supseteq A$ and $V \supseteq B$.

**Position in the chain**: $T_{6} \implies T_{5} \implies T_{4}$.

⚠️ One does **not** need to add "plus $T_{1}$": the definition of a normal space already contains $T_{1}$, and a subspace inherits $T_{1}$ from the ambient space, so complete normality automatically gives $T_{1}$.
