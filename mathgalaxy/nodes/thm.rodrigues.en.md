## Statement · **draft**
Let $v \in T_{p}U$, $v \ne 0$, and set $k := k_{n}(v)$. Then the following three conditions are equivalent:

1. $\mathrm{d}r(v)$ is a **principal direction**, with principal curvature $k$;
2. $\mathrm{d}n(v) = -k\,\mathrm{d}r(v)$, i.e. $W(\mathrm{d}r(v)) = k\,\mathrm{d}r(v)$;
3. $k_{n}$ attains a stationary value in the direction $v$.
