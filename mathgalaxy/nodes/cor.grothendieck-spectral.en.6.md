the other spectral sequence (horizontal then vertical) degenerates at its $E_{2}$ page because every column is an acyclic resolution, and both converge to the same thing.

∎

The details are in the first two references: existence of the Cartan–Eilenberg resolution, and convergence of the double-complex spectral sequence together with the identification of the $E_{2}$ page.

**Typical applications**: sheaf cohomology — sheafify then take sections, Čech cohomology to de Rham cohomology, the Lyndon–Hochschild–Serre spectral sequence in group homology — all are special cases.
