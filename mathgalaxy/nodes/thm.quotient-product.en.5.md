⚠ **The locally compact hypothesis cannot be dropped.** In general "a quotient map times the identity" is not a quotient map: take $q$ to be some badly behaved quotient and $Z$ a space that is neither locally compact nor compactly generated; the product topology is then strictly finer than the topology the quotient space should have.

Terminology: some write this as "$Z$ locally compact Hausdorff $\implies$ the functor $- \times Z$ preserves quotient maps".
