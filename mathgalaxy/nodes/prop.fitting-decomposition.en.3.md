## Notes · **draft**
The chains $\operatorname{Im}(f^n)$ and $\operatorname{Ker}(f^n)$ stabilize in finite dimension. On the intersection $\operatorname{Im}(f^\infty)$ the map $f$ is both surjective and injective, hence an automorphism; on the union $\operatorname{Ker}(f^\infty)$ some power of $f$ vanishes. The two subspaces intersect in $0$ and their dimensions sum to $\dim V$. Both are $f$-invariant, and they are subrepresentations when $f$ is $A$-linear.
