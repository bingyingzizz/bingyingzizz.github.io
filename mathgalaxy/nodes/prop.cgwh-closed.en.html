Statement

  1. Open subspaces and closed subspaces of compactly generated weak Hausdorff spaces are still compactly generated and weak Hausdorff.
  2. If X=lim→⁡XiX = \varinjlim X_{i}X=lim​Xi​ is a filtered colimit of compactly generated weak Hausdorff spaces along closed inclusions, then XXX is also compactly generated weak Hausdorff, and every XiX_{i}Xi​ is closed in XXX.

Notes

Item 2 is exactly the one repeatedly needed in condensed mathematics: assembling "objects built from finitely many pieces" into a larger one along a filtered colimit, without losing the property CGWH\mathrm{CGWH}CGWH.

Proof.

  • Open subspaces: U⊆XU \subseteq XU⊆X open.

For a compact Hausdorff g:S→Ug : S\to Ug:S→U, its image g(S)g(S)g(S) is closed in XXX, hence in the subspace topology of UUU g(S)=g(S)∩Ug(S) = g(S)\cap Ug(S)=g(S)∩U is closed   ⟹  \implies⟹ UUU is weak Hausdorff ✓;

kkk-ification commutes with "taking open subspaces" (the kkk topology is characterized by pullback along compact Hausdorff pieces, and whether a piece lands in UUU is a local condition)   ⟹  \implies⟹ UUU is compactly generated ✓.

  • Closed subspaces: A⊆XA \subseteq XA⊆X closed. The image of a compact Hausdorff g:S→Ag : S\to Ag:S→A is closed in XXX and contained in AAA, hence closed in AAA   ⟹  \implies⟹ weak Hausdorff ✓; AAA compactly generated follows likewise from "kkk-ification commutes with taking closed subspaces" ✓.

  • XXX is weak Hausdorff: take a compact Hausdorff g:S→Xg : S \to Xg:S→X.

By the lemma above and compactness of SSS, g(S)g(S)g(S) lands in some single XiX_{i}Xi​ (the standard "a compact set lands in one piece" lemma for filtered colimits);

  • XXX is compactly generated: from the description of closed sets of the colimit (decided piecewise) and the compact generation of each piece, checking piecewise gives it ✓. ∎
