# uncountability of the reals　`thm.real-uncountable`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=thm.real-uncountable)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./thm.real-uncountable.md)

**The most counter-intuitive point of this theorem**: $\mathbb{Q}$ and $\mathbb{R}$ are both dense and each can be approximated by the other, yet $\mathbb{Q}$ is countable (it can be arranged as a sequence) while $\mathbb{R}$ is not. **"Dense" and "equally many" are two different things** — and this is precisely why analysis has to be done over the reals rather than over the rationals.
