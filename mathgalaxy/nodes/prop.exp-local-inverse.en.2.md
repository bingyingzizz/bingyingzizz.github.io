# local inverse of the exponential　`prop.exp-local-inverse`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=prop.exp-local-inverse)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./prop.exp-local-inverse.md)

Equivalent formulation: $\exp$ is locally biholomorphic (a local isomorphism), and $\exp$ is a covering map $\mathbb{C} \to \mathbb{C} \setminus \{0\}$ with fibre $2\pi i\mathbb{Z}$.
