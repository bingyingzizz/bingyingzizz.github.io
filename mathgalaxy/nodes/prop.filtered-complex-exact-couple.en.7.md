③ **Induction on the page.** Write $D_{r}$ and $E_{r}$ explicitly in terms of $Z_{r}/B_{r}$ ($D_{r}^{p,q}$ consists of the classes in $F^{p}$ for which $d$ raises the filtration index by at least $r$ steps and whose differentials still lie in $F^{p+r-1}$). Substituting into the three formulas for the derived couple gives $Z_{r}^{p,q}$ and $B_{r}^{p,q}$ above.

The induction step uses only $d^{2} = 0$ and the universal properties of kernels and cokernels.
