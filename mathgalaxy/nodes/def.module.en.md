## Statement · **draft**
Let $A$ be a unital ring and $M$ an **abelian group**. We say that $M$ is a **$A$-module** if there is a map

$$A \times M \longrightarrow M, \qquad (a, m) \longmapsto a \cdot m,$$

satisfying $a(bm) = (ab)m$, $(a + b)m = am + bm$, $a(m + n) = am + an$, $1 \cdot m = m$.
