## Statement · **draft**
**Example 1** A locally compact Hausdorff space is compactly generated.

**Example 2** A sequential space is compactly generated: $A \subseteq X$ is closed $\iff$ whenever $x_{n} \to x$ and $x_{n} \in A$, one has $x \in A$.
