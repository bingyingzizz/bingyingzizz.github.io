Statement

Let SSS be a closed surface. Remove two open disks from SSS to obtain a surface with two boundary circles. Glue the two ends of the cylinder S1×[0,1]S^{1}\times[0,1]S1×[0,1] to the respective boundary circles. The resulting closed surface is obtained by adding a handle to SSS. Equivalently, adding a handle means taking the connected sum with a torus: S↦S#T2S\mapsto S\#T^{2}S↦S#T2.

Notes

For orientable surfaces: Adding a handle increases the genus ggg by 111 and decreases the Euler characteristic by 222. Closed orientable surfaces are exactly those obtained by adding ggg handles to a sphere (classification theorem).

For nonorientable surfaces: Adding a handle preserves nonorientability but increases the genus of the orientable part. The standard form for nonorientable surfaces uses crosscaps instead of handles.

⚠ A handle removes two disks and attaches a cylinder, preserving orientability. A crosscap removes one disk and attaches a Möbius band, producing a nonorientable surface.
