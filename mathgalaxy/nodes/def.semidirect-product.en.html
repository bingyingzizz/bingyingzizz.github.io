Statement

Let HHH, GGG be groups and φ:G→Aut⁡(H)\varphi : G \to \operatorname{Aut}(H)φ:G→Aut(H) a group homomorphism. The underlying set of the semidirect product H⋊φGH \rtimes_{\varphi} GH⋊φ​G is H×GH \times GH×G, with multiplication

(h1,g1)(h2,g2):=(h1 φ(g1)(h2), g1g2).(h_{1}, g_{1})(h_{2}, g_{2}) := \bigl(h_{1}\, \varphi(g_{1})(h_{2}),\ g_{1} g_{2}\bigr).(h1​,g1​)(h2​,g2​):=(h1​φ(g1​)(h2​), g1​g2​).

Notes

Why this multiplication: for H×GH \times GH×G to be a group while both H↪H⋊φGH \hookrightarrow H \rtimes_{\varphi} GH↪H⋊φ​G and G↪H⋊φGG \hookrightarrow H \rtimes_{\varphi} GG↪H⋊φ​G are group homomorphisms, one must let GGG, when passing through HHH, twist HHH once — that twist is φ(g1)\varphi(g_{1})φ(g1​).

Three basic properties:

  • the identity is (1H,1G)(1_{H}, 1_{G})(1H​,1G​) and inverses are (h,g)−1=(φ(g−1)(h−1), g−1)(h, g)^{-1} = \bigl(\varphi(g^{-1})(h^{-1}),\ g^{-1}\bigr)(h,g)−1=(φ(g−1)(h−1), g−1);
  • there is a split short exact sequence 1→H→H⋊φG→ π G→11 \to H \to H \rtimes_{\varphi} G \xrightarrow{\ \pi\ } G \to 11→H→H⋊φ​G π ​G→1, whose section g↦(1H,g)g \mapsto (1_{H}, g)g↦(1H​,g) is a group homomorphism;
  • when φ\varphiφ is trivial, H⋊φG=H×GH \rtimes_{\varphi} G = H \times GH⋊φ​G=H×G (direct product).

⚠️ Careful in the converse direction: a short exact sequence 1→H→E→πG→11 \to H \to E \xrightarrow{\pi} G \to 11→H→Eπ​G→1 with a homomorphic section only tells you that EEE is a semidirect product of HHH and GGG (when HHH is normal in EEE one has E≅H⋊φGE \cong H \rtimes_{\varphi} GE≅H⋊φ​G, with φ\varphiφ given by conjugation);

but once the GGG-module structure is given, the split extension is unique up to isomorphism.

H1H^{1}H1 classifies the choice of section, not the extension itself — see the warning in "one-dimensional cohomology and split extensions".
