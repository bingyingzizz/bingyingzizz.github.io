## Statement · **draft**
**What it does**: it turns "at each point eat $k$ tangent vectors and spit out a number, changing sign when two arguments are swapped" into a smooth object — a differential form is the kind of quantity on a manifold that can be pulled back and integrated.

Let $U\subseteq\mathbb{R}^{n}$ be open. A **$k$-form** on $U$ is a map $\omega$ assigning to each point $p\in U$ an **alternating** $k$-linear form

$$\omega_{p} : (\mathbb{R}^{n})^{k}\longrightarrow\mathbb{R},$$

whose coefficients in the standard coordinates are smooth:
