**2. The connected sum formula.** Let $M,N$ be triangulated closed surfaces. Choose a $2$-simplex in each and remove its interior, leaving a surface with triangular boundary. Glue the two triangular boundaries by matching vertices to obtain a triangulation of $M\#N$. The counts are $V=V_{M}+V_{N}-3$, $E=E_{M}+E_{N}-3$, and $F=(F_{M}-1)+(F_{N}-1)=F_{M}+F_{N}-2$, so $\chi(M\#N)=\chi(M)+\chi(N)-2$.
