⭐ **How to read it**: "computing page by page" in the language of exact couples is just "deriving the couple once". $D$ is "what is already known", $E$ is "the quotient on this page", $i$ is "forget one layer", $j$ is "take the quotient", $k$ is "the connecting homomorphism".

⚠ **Bidegrees are not unique**: the filtration-complex convention $(-1,1)/(0,0)/(1,0)$ is fixed here. In homological notation it is $(1,-1)/(0,0)/(1,0)$; the differential lands one slot differently, and conversion is just an index shift.
