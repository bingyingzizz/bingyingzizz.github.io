# open subsets of Baire spaces are Baire　`thm.baire-open-subset`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=thm.baire-open-subset)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./thm.baire-open-subset.md)

Then $O_n' \cup (X \setminus \overline{\Omega})$ is **dense open** in $X$ (what is adjoined is the open set $X \setminus \overline{\Omega}$; the complement $\Omega^{c}$ need not be open — for $\Omega = (0,1)$ and $O_n' = (0,1) \setminus \{1/m\}$, the complement $\Omega^{c}$ would make the union lose a neighbourhood of $0$);

hence $\bigcap_n(O_n' \cup (X \setminus \overline{\Omega}))$ is dense in $X$, and intersecting with $\Omega$ gives that $\bigcap_n O_n$ is dense in $\Omega$.
