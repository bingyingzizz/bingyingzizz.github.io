## Statement · **draft**
An object $X \in \mathcal{C}$ is called **finitely presented** (also called **compact**) if the representable functor

$$h^{X} = \operatorname{Hom}_{\mathcal{C}}(X, -)$$

**preserves filtered colimits**. That is, for every filtered diagram $D$, we have $\operatorname{Hom}(X, \varinjlim D) \cong \varinjlim \operatorname{Hom}(X, D(-))$.
