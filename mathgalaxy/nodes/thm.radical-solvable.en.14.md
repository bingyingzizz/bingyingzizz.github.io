nsion. Any $\tau\in\operatorname{Gal}(M_{i}/M_{i-1})$ sends each generator $\beta$ to $\zeta_{n_{i}}^{k_{\beta}}\beta$, and $\tau\mapsto(k_{\beta})_{\beta}$ is an injective homomorphism into $(\mathbb{Z}/n_{i})^{r}$. Hence $\operatorname{Gal}(M_{i}/M_{i-1})$ is abelian.
