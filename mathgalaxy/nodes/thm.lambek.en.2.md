# Lambek's Criterion　`thm.lambek`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=thm.lambek)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./thm.lambek.md)

$$I \otimes_{R} M \longrightarrow M, \qquad a \otimes m \longmapsto a m$$

is injective. Equivalently, $\operatorname{Tor}_{1}^{R}(R/I,\ M) = 0$ holds for every finitely generated right ideal $I$.

(Over a commutative ring, there is no need to distinguish left and right ideals.)

## Notes · **draft**
**Why this is a strengthening**: The definition of flatness requires testing **all** injections. Item 4 of `prop.flat-equivalences` already reduces the test to ideals. Lambek reduces it further to **finitely generated** ideals, which often form a countable family and can be checked individually.
