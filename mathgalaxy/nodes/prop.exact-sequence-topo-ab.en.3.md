## Notes · **draft**
Left exactness is automatic because the functor in the previous proposition preserves all limits. At the right end, $\underline{v}$ must be an epimorphism. Epimorphisms of condensed objects are detected by pointwise surjectivity on free compact Hausdorff spaces, so this reduces to the compact lifting condition.

📌 **Local compactness ensures the compact lifting condition**. Thus an entire exact sequence of locally compact abelian groups can be transferred.
