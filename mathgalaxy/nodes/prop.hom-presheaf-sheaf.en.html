Statement

Let XXX be a topological space and Y→XY \to XY→X a morphism of condensed sets (that is, Y∈Cond/XY \in \mathrm{Cond}/XY∈Cond/X). Then the presheaf

U  ⟼  Y(U):=Hom⁡/X(U‾, Y)U \;\longmapsto\; Y(U) := \operatorname{Hom}_{/X}\bigl(\underline{U},\ Y\bigr)U⟼Y(U):=Hom/X​(U​, Y)

where U⊆XU \subseteq XU⊆X ranges over open sets,

is a sheaf on XXX.

Notes

Proof. By "an open cover gives a cover", an open cover U=⋃iUiU = \bigcup_i U_{i}U=⋃i​Ui​ gives a cover in Cond/X\mathrm{Cond}/XCond/X, namely ∐iUi‾→U‾\coprod_i \underline{U_{i}} \to \underline{U}∐i​Ui​​→U​ ✓.

And YYY is an object of the topos Cond/X\mathrm{Cond}/XCond/X; objects of a topos satisfy descent for every covering sieve in the standard topology — the sheaf condition is precisely "Hom⁡(−,Y)\operatorname{Hom}(-, Y)Hom(−,Y) turns covering sieves into equalizers" ✓.

Applying this to the cover above: Y(U)→∏iY(Ui)⇉∏i,jY(Ui∩Uj)Y(U) \to \prod_i Y(U_{i}) \rightrightarrows \prod_{i,j} Y(U_{i} \cap U_{j})Y(U)→∏i​Y(Ui​)⇉∏i,j​Y(Ui​∩Uj​) is an equalizer, which is exactly the sheaf condition for U↦Y(U)U \mapsto Y(U)U↦Y(U) ✓. ∎

⭐ In one sentence: "sections over the open sets of XXX" form a sheaf, because open covers are already covers in Cond/X\mathrm{Cond}/XCond/X.
