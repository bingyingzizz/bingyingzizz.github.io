then $(F, +, \cdot, \le)$ is called an **ordered field**. Carrying over the language from $\mathbb{Q}$: $a > 0$ is called a **positive element**, $a < 0$ a **negative element**; the two combine into the absolute value

$$|a| := \begin{cases} a, & a \ge 0 \\ -a, & a < 0 \end{cases}$$

## Notes · **draft**
⭐ The second compatibility axiom permits "multiplying both sides" only when $0 \le c$ — multiplying by a **negative** number **reverses** the inequality ($a \le b,\ c \le 0 \implies ac \ge bc$, derived from the first two axioms).
