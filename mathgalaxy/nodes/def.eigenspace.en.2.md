# Eigenspace　`def.eigenspace`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=def.eigenspace)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./def.eigenspace.md)

It is a $T$-invariant subspace; its dimension $\dim V_{\lambda}$ is called the **geometric multiplicity** of $\lambda$, while the multiplicity of $\lambda$ in the characteristic polynomial is called the **algebraic multiplicity**.

**One always has**: $1 \le$ geometric multiplicity $\le$ algebraic multiplicity.

**Diagonalizability criterion**: $T$ is diagonalizable $\iff$ $\sum_{\lambda} \dim V_{\lambda} = \dim V$ $\iff$ the two multiplicities of every eigenvalue are equal.
