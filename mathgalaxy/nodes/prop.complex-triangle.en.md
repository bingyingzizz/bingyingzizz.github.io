## Statement · **draft**
**In plain words**: the modulus of a sum does not exceed the sum of the moduli; equality holds only when these complex numbers “point in the same direction”.

For arbitrary $z_1, \cdots, z_n \in \mathbb{C}$,

$$\left| \sum_{k=1}^{n} z_k \right| \le \sum_{k=1}^{n} |z_k|.$$
