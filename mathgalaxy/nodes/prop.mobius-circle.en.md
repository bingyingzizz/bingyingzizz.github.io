## Statement · **draft**
A Möbius transformation sends **circles and lines** (collectively "circles") in $\widehat{\mathbb{C}}$ to circles or lines. Equivalently: the image of $\mathbb{R} \cup \{\infty\}$ is always a circle or a line.
