$F$ is continuous (each component is continuous, and the series converges uniformly);

**injective**: if $x \ne y$, regularity gives a basic open set $B_{n}$ with $x \in B_{n} \subseteq \overline{B_{n}} \subseteq X \setminus \{\, y \,\}$, so some pair $(m, n)$ has $f_{m,n}$ separating the two points;
