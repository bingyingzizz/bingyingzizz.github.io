# Spaces Built from Compact Hausdorff Blocks along Closed Inclusions　`prop.closed-inclusion-filtered-limit`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=prop.closed-inclusion-filtered-limit)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./prop.closed-inclusion-filtered-limit.md)

1. $X$ is compactly generated and weak Hausdorff;
2. every $X_{i}$ is **closed** in $X$;
3. the topology of $X$ is the **colimit topology** of these compact pieces: $U \subseteq X$ is open $\iff$ every $U \cap X_{i}$ is open in $X_{i}$.

Conversely, every compactly generated weak Hausdorff space is the filtered colimit of its own compact Hausdorff subspaces along closed inclusions.
