# First Fundamental Form　`def.first-fundamental-form`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=def.first-fundamental-form)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./def.first-fundamental-form.md)

It gives lengths and angles of tangent vectors, arc lengths of curves, and the area element: for an orthonormal unit coframe $(\omega^{1},\omega^{2})$ **compatible** with the parameter orientation one has $\mathrm{d}A = \omega^{1}\wedge\omega^{2} = \sqrt{EG-F^{2}}\,\mathrm{d}u\,\mathrm{d}v$; when the coframe is **incompatible** with the parameter orientation there is a sign difference, and the area density must be written $\lvert\omega^{1}\wedge\omega^{2}\rvert$.
