prop.cg-wh-iff-qseparated

Machine-drafted and pending author review. The Chinese text is authoritative.

Statement

Let XXX be a compactly generated space. Then XXX is weakly Hausdorff   ⟺  \iff⟺ XXX (regarded as a condensed set) is quasi-separated.

Notes

Where the two directions come from: "weakly Hausdorff   ⟹  \implies⟹ quasi-separated" is the fiber-product lemma; "quasi-separated   ⟹  \implies⟹ weakly Hausdorff" is the underlying-space lemma. Compact generation is what aligns the notion of "underlying space" with XXX itself.

⭐ What they add up to: within the compactly generated range, the topological condition "weakly Hausdorff" and the sheaf-theoretic condition "quasi-separated" say the same thing. So CGWH\mathrm{CGWH}CGWH, the habitat of this whole homotopy theory, acquires a purely sheaf-theoretic name in the condensed world.
