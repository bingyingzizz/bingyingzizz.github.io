# The Category of Complexes Is Additive　`prop.complex-additive`

[in graph](../#n=prop.complex-additive)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./prop.complex-additive.md)

## Notes · **draft**
"Termwise" is a general fact about functor categories: $\mathcal{C}(\mathcal{C})$ is a full subcategory of $\operatorname{Hom}((\mathbb{Z},\le), \mathcal{C})$, every construction is performed on each $n$ separately, and then one checks that the differentials are compatible. This is why all later limits and colimits of complexes need no separate proof.
