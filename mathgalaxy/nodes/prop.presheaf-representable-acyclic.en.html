Statement

Let C\mathcal{C}C be any category, C^\widehat{\mathcal{C}}C equipped with the standard topology, and X∈CX \in \mathcal{C}X∈C, MMM abelian presheaves on C^\widehat{\mathcal{C}}C. Then

Hn(hX, M)=0(n>0).H^{n}\bigl(h_{X},\ M\bigr) = 0 \qquad (n > 0).Hn(hX​, M)=0(n>0).

Notes

⭐ Why this deserves its own statement: because it is the best explanation of why sheafification is necessary — at the presheaf level, representable objects have no higher cohomology at all (everything is exact); once one sheafifies, Z⋅X\mathbb{Z}\cdot XZ⋅X is no longer the "evaluation" functor, and higher terms appear. The nontriviality of sheaf cohomology comes entirely from the fact that exactness is broken in the category of sheaves.
