**Proof.**
**1.** A discrete space is a disjoint union of points: $E = \coprod_{e \in E}\{e\}$. And cohomology turns disjoint unions into **products**:
$$H^{n}\Bigl(\coprod_{i} X_{i},\ M\Bigr) \cong \prod_{i} H^{n}(X_{i},\ M) ✓$$
