## Notes · **draft**
⭐ The role of this criterion is **to turn a series problem into a sequence problem**: it is just the Cauchy criterion for $\mathbb{R}$ applied to the sequence of partial sums $\{s_N\}$ (with interval additivity writing $s_n - s_m$ as a tail).

⚠ "$a_n \to 0$" is only a **necessary** condition: the harmonic series $\sum 1/n$ has terms tending to zero yet diverges.

To prove divergence it is very useful (if the terms do not tend to zero, the series diverges at once); to prove convergence it says nothing.
