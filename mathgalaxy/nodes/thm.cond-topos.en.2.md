## Notes · **draft**
**What this sentence costs**: once $\mathrm{Cond}$ is a topos, three things come for free — **internal Hom** ($\mathrm{Cond}$ is cartesian closed), **sheaf cohomology** (one can always take cohomology of abelian groups on a topos), and **taking quotients** (equivalence relations can always be quotiented out). Everything later in condensed mathematics is built on these three tools.
