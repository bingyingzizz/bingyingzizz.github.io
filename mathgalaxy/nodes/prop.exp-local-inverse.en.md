## Statement · **draft**
For every $z_{0} \in \mathbb{C}$ there are a neighbourhood $U$ of $z_{0}$ and an open set $V$ such that $\exp : U \to V$ is biholomorphic, and its inverse

$$w \longmapsto \log w := \log|w| + i\arg_{U} w$$

is holomorphic, where $\arg_{U} w$ is chosen in the interval $(\operatorname{Im} z_{0} - \pi,\ \operatorname{Im} z_{0} + \pi)$.
