# Čech Cohomology Equals Sheaf Cohomology on Paracompact Spaces　`thm.paracompact-cech-equals-sheaf`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=thm.paracompact-cech-equals-sheaf)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./thm.paracompact-cech-equals-sheaf.md)

⚠️ **Paracompactness cannot be dropped**. On general spaces, even compactly generated ones, Čech cohomology and sheaf cohomology can differ. The claim that Čech cohomology equals sheaf cohomology is often quoted without qualification in topology and sheaf theory. Include the hypotheses on the space when citing it.
