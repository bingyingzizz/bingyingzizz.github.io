又称半单表示的等价刻画·半单判据·完全可约判据

陈述

对 AAA 的有限维表示 VVV，下列条件等价：(1) VVV 半单；(2) VVV 是不可约子表示的和；(3) 每个子表示 W⊆VW\subseteq VW⊆V 都有补 W′W'W′ 使 V=W⊕W′V=W\oplus W'V=W⊕W′。

说明

证明见边 imp.ss-tfae：三条等价用「取极大直和项」与「极大真子表示取补」两个有限维论证；(3) 推出 (2) 时先用 (3) 把极大真子表示的商补成子表示。

Statement draft

For a finite-dimensional representation VVV of AAA, the following are equivalent: (1) VVV is semisimple; (2) VVV is a sum of irreducible subrepresentations; (3) every subrepresentation W⊆VW\subseteq VW⊆V has a complement W′W'W′ with V=W⊕W′V=W\oplus W'V=W⊕W′.

Notes draft

The proof is on the edge imp.ss-tfae: the three equivalences use two finite-dimensional arguments — taking a maximal direct summand, and taking a complement of a maximal proper subrepresentation; for (3) implies (2), first use (3) to split the quotient by a maximal proper subrepresentation into a subrepresentation.
