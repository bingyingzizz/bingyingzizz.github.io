又称半单表示的等价刻画·半单判据·完全可约判据

陈述

对 AAA 的有限维表示 VVV，下列条件等价：(1) VVV 半单；(2) VVV 是不可约子表示的和；(3) 每个子表示 W⊆VW\subseteq VW⊆V 都有补 W′W'W′ 使 V=W⊕W′V=W\oplus W'V=W⊕W′。

说明

(1) 推出 (2) 显然；(2) 推出 (3) 用有限维归纳把不可约直和项分裂出来；(3) 推出 (1) 反复取不可约子表示与补。 有限维性用于保证过程终止。 这就是通常的「完全可约」判据。

Statement draft

For a finite-dimensional representation VVV of AAA, the following are equivalent: (1) VVV is semisimple; (2) VVV is a sum of irreducible subrepresentations; (3) every subrepresentation W⊆VW\subseteq VW⊆V has a complement W′W'W′ with V=W⊕W′V=W\oplus W'V=W⊕W′.

Notes draft

(1) implies (2) is clear; (2) implies (3) splits off irreducible summands by induction on dimension; (3) implies (1) by repeatedly taking an irreducible subrepresentation and a complement. Finite dimension guarantees that the process terminates. This is the usual criterion for complete reducibility.
