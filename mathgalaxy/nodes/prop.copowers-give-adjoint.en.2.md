## Notes · **draft**
**Proof.** The universal property of the copower gives $\operatorname{Hom}_{\mathcal{C}}\bigl(X^{(I)}, Y\bigr) \cong \operatorname{Hom}_{\mathbf{Set}}\bigl(I, \operatorname{Hom}_{\mathcal{C}}(X, Y)\bigr) = \mathbf{Set}\bigl(I, h_{X}(Y)\bigr)$ ✓, which is exactly the definition of an adjunction ✓.

∎
