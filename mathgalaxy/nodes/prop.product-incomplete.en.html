Statement

Even when μ\muμ and ν\nuν are both complete, μ×ν\mu \times \nuμ×ν is often incomplete: there is E∈M⊗NE \in \mathcal{M} \otimes \mathcal{N}E∈M⊗N with μ×ν(E)=0\mu \times \nu(E) = 0μ×ν(E)=0, yet EEE has a subset not in M⊗N\mathcal{M} \otimes \mathcal{N}M⊗N.

Notes

This is why one needs a separate "FFF–TTT" for the complete case: in practice (for instance Lebesgue measure on R2\mathbb{R}^{2}R2) the measure at hand is usually the completion of μ×ν\mu \times \nuμ×ν, not the raw product.
