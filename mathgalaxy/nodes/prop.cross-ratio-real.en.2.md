# real cross ratio iff concyclic　`prop.cross-ratio-real`

## Notes · **draft**
**Proof**: take a fractional linear transformation $T$ sending $z_{1}, z_{2}, z_{3}$ to $0, 1, \infty$ respectively (it exists and is unique, see `prop.mobius-three-points`). By invariance of the cross-ratio,

$$(z_{1}, z_{2}; z_{3}, z_{4}) = (0, 1; \infty, Tz_{4}) = Tz_{4}.$$

Thus "the cross-ratio is real" $\iff$ "$Tz_{4}$ lies on the real axis". And $T^{-1}(\mathbb{R})$ is a line or a circle (the real axis is the "circle" through $\infty$, see `prop.mobius-circle`), and it passes through $z_{1}, z_{2}, z_{3}$. ∎
