# Biproducts Are Products and Coproducts　`prop.biproduct-is-product`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=prop.biproduct-is-product)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./prop.biproduct-is-product.md)

where "direct sum" means there exist $p_{k} : M \to M_{k}$, $i_{k} : M_{k} \to M$ such that

$$p_{1}i_{1} = 1_{M_{1}}, \qquad p_{2}i_{2} = 1_{M_{2}}, \qquad i_{1}p_{1} + i_{2}p_{2} = 1_{M}.$$

## Notes · **draft**
⭐ This is where the name **biproduct** comes from: product and coproduct coincide in a preadditive category, so the direct sum needs only one object.
