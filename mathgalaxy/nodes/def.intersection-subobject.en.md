## Statement · **draft**
Let $Y \rightarrowtail X$, $Z \rightarrowtail X$ be two **subobjects** of $X$ (isomorphism classes of monomorphisms, see @@def.subobject@@). Their **intersection** is the fibred product

$$Y \cap Z := Y \times_{X} Z \;\longrightarrow\; X ,$$
