[in graph](../#n=thm.runge)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./thm.runge.md)

⚠️ Connectedness of $\mathbb{C}\setminus K$ is essential. When $K = $ is the unit circle, $1/z$ is holomorphic near the circle, but cannot be uniformly approximated by polynomials on $K$. Otherwise the maximum modulus principle would imply that $1/z$ extends holomorphically to $\{|z|\le 1\}$, contradicting the singularity at $z=0$.
