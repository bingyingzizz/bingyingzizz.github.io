# the Archimedean property in epsilon form　`cor.archimedean-epsilon`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=cor.archimedean-epsilon)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./cor.archimedean-epsilon.md)

⭐ (ii) "sandwiches a real number between two adjacent integers" — the proof of density of the rationals (part (ii) of `lem.archimedean`) uses it.

It relies on the **discreteness** of $\mathbb{Z}$ in $K$ (there is no other integer between adjacent ones), not merely on unboundedness.

⚠ $\mathbb{Q}$ also satisfies (i) (it is Archimedean), but $\mathbb{Q}$ does not satisfy the least-upper-bound property — so (i) itself does **not** need completeness, only the Archimedean property;

writing "complete ordered field" here is only to align with `lem.archimedean`.
