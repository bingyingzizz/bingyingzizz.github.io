说明

⭐ 为什么必须 char 0：两个方向都用了「xm−1x^{m}-1xm−1 无重根」（从而 σ\sigmaσ 可对角化）与可分性。char ppp 时纯开方扩张要换成 Artin–Schreier 扩张 xp−x−ax^{p}-x-axp−x−a，结论要改写。

In characteristic ppp, pure radical extensions must be replaced by Artin–Schreier extensions xp−x−ax^{p}-x-axp−x−a, and the conclusion must be reformulated.

📌 This theorem is the entry point to the quintic problem: It turns the question of whether a radical formula exists into a purely group-theoretic question (SnS_{n}Sn​ is solvable   ⟺  n≤4\iff n\le4⟺n≤4, see the solvability of symmetric groups). When applying it in reverse, distinguish the absence of a radical solution from the absence of any solution: Gal⁡(f)\operatorname{Gal}(f)Gal(f) is not solvable   ⟹  \implies⟹ has no solution by radicals.
