# Flat Module　`def.flat-module`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=def.flat-module)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./def.flat-module.md)

**Which modules are flat**: free modules, projective modules (a projective is a direct summand of a free module, and direct summands preserve flatness), localizations $S^{-1}R$ (tensoring commutes with localization, and $\otimes$ is exact over $R$). Over an integral domain a flat module is necessarily **torsion-free**; over a PID (more generally a Bézout domain) the converse also holds, so there "flat = torsion-free" ✓.
