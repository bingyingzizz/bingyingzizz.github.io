# Witt Cancellation Theorem　`thm.witt-cancellation`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=thm.witt-cancellation)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./thm.witt-cancellation.md)

⚠ $\operatorname{char} K \ne 2$ cannot be dropped: in characteristic $2$ “symmetric” and “alternating” coincide, $\frac{1}{2}$ does not exist, and orthogonalization and Witt theory must be rewritten (that is another subject). It is not expanded here.

⭐ A stronger version is the **Witt extension lemma**: an isometry between nondegenerate subspaces always extends to an isometry of the whole space. Cancellation follows directly; the proof is on the edge `imp.witt-cancellation`.
