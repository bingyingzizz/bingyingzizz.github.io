# Injective Dimension　`def.injective-dimension`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=def.injective-dimension)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./def.injective-dimension.md)

⭐ **Why both sides matter**: To compute $\operatorname{Ext}^{n}(M, N)$, use either a **projective** resolution of $M$ (left-hand side) or an **injective** resolution of $N$ (right-hand side). The difficulty on the two sides is governed by $\operatorname{pd} M$ and $\operatorname{id} N$, respectively, and both vanishing statements hold: $\operatorname{Ext}^{n}(M, -) = 0$ ($n > \operatorname{pd} M$) and $\operatorname{Ext}^{n}(-, N) = 0$ ($n > \operatorname{id} N$).
