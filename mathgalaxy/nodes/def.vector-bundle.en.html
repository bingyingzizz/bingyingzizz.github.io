Statement

What it says: it axiomatizes "attach a vector space to every point, smoothly" — the tangent, cotangent and tensor bundles are examples. The criterion is "locally it looks like U×RkU \times \mathbb{R}^{k}U×Rk".

Let MMM be a smooth manifold. A smooth vector bundle of rank kkk is a map π:E→M\pi : E \to Mπ:E→M (smoothness of π\piπ is not assumed: it follows from a local trivialization pr⁡1∘Φ=π\operatorname{pr}_{1}\circ\Phi = \pipr1​∘Φ=π and smoothness of pr⁡1\operatorname{pr}_{1}pr1​), together with:

  1. on each fibre Ep:=π−1(p)E_{p} := \pi^{-1}(p)Ep​:=π−1(p) a structure of kkk-dimensional real vector space;
  2. local trivializations: every point has an open neighbourhood UUU and a diffeomorphism Φ:π−1(U)→U×Rk\Phi : \pi^{-1}(U) \to U \times \mathbb{R}^{k}Φ:π−1(U)→U×Rk such that Φ\PhiΦ commutes with the projections, i.e. pr⁡1∘Φ=π\operatorname{pr}_{1} \circ \Phi = \pipr1​∘Φ=π and linear on each fibre.

EEE is the total space, MMM the base, and dim⁡E=dim⁡M+k\dim E = \dim M + kdimE=dimM+k. Two trivializations Φα,Φβ\Phi_{\alpha}, \Phi_{\beta}Φα​,Φβ​ give on overlaps the transition function

gαβ:Uα∩Uβ⟶GL⁡(k,R),gαβ(p):=Φα,p∘Φβ,p−1,g_{\alpha\beta} : U_{\alpha} \cap U_{\beta} \longrightarrow \operatorname{GL}(k,\mathbb{R}),\qquad g_{\alpha\beta}(p) := \Phi_{\alpha,p} \circ \Phi_{\beta,p}^{-1} ,gαβ​:Uα​∩Uβ​⟶GL(k,R),gαβ​(p):=Φα,p​∘Φβ,p−1​,

which is smooth and satisfies the cocycle condition

gαα=1,gαβ gβγ gγα=1,g_{\alpha\alpha} = 1,\qquad g_{\alpha\beta}\,g_{\beta\gamma}\,g_{\gamma\alpha} = 1 ,gαα​=1,gαβ​gβγ​gγα​=1,

the latter on Uα∩Uβ∩UγU_{\alpha} \cap U_{\beta} \cap U_{\gamma}Uα​∩Uβ​∩Uγ​. Conversely, an open cover with smooth gαβg_{\alpha\beta}gαβ​ satisfying the cocycle condition glues to a vector bundle.

Notes

Why "locally trivial" is not enough, and the cocycle is needed: local triviality only says "each piece looks like a product"; what determines the bundle is how the pieces are glued, and the gluing is recorded in gαβg_{\alpha\beta}gαβ​.

The cocycle condition is necessary and sufficient for consistent gluing, and ties bundles to cohomology classes of 1-cocycles, H1(M;GL⁡(k,R))H^{1}(M;\operatorname{GL}(k,\mathbb{R}))H1(M;GL(k,R)) — mentioned here without proof.

Examples: TMTMTM has rank nnn (切丛); M×RkM \times \mathbb{R}^{k}M×Rk is the trivial bundle; the cotangent bundle T∗MT^{\ast}MT∗M and the (r,s)(r,s)(r,s) tensor bundles (张量场);

the Möbius band over S1S^{1}S1 is a non-trivial rank 111 bundle, while S1×RS^{1} \times \mathbb{R}S1×R is trivial — locally the same, globally different.

Changing the structure group means changing the bundle: replacing GL⁡(k,R)\operatorname{GL}(k,\mathbb{R})GL(k,R) by O⁡(k)\operatorname{O}(k)O(k) gives Euclidean bundles (with a fibre metric), by GL⁡(k,C)\operatorname{GL}(k,\mathbb{C})GL(k,C) complex vector bundles, by GL⁡(k,Z)\operatorname{GL}(k,\mathbb{Z})GL(k,Z) flat bundles.

The structure group of TMTMTM reduces to O⁡(n)\operatorname{O}(n)O(n) thanks to "a Riemannian metric always exists" (纤维度量) — not automatic.

Rank 111 is a line bundle: the Möbius line bundle over S1S^{1}S1, the tautological line bundle over RP1\mathbb{RP}^{1}RP1, and the Hopf line bundle over S2S^{2}S2 are the three standard examples. Isomorphism classes of real line bundles are detected by w1∈H1(M;Z/2)w_{1} \in H^{1}(M; \mathbb{Z}/2)w1​∈H1(M;Z/2) (this separates the trivial and Möbius bundles over S1S^{1}S1, while H2(S1;Z)=0H^{2}(S^{1}; \mathbb{Z}) = 0H2(S1;Z)=0 cannot); complex line bundles use the first Chern class c1∈H2c_{1} \in H^{2}c1​∈H2. This is the entry point of topology into geometry.

Notation: π\piπ denotes both a projection and the circle constant; in this graph π:E→M\pi : E \to Mπ:E→M is always a projection; pr⁡1\operatorname{pr}_{1}pr1​ is the projection of a product onto the first factor; Φα,p\Phi_{\alpha,p}Φα,p​ is Φα\Phi_{\alpha}Φα​ restricted to the fibre EpE_{p}Ep​.
