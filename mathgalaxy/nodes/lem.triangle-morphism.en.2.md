# Morphisms of Distinguished Triangles　`lem.triangle-morphism`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=lem.triangle-morphism)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./lem.triangle-morphism.md)

1. if it is a morphism **from a triangle to itself** and **its first two steps vanish** ($u = v = 0$), then $w$ of the third step satisfies $w^{2} = 0$;
2. if $u, v$ are both **homotopy equivalences**, then so is $w$.

## Notes · **draft**
**What item 1 says**: a morphism between triangles is tightly controlled by its first two steps — once they vanish, the third is forced to have zero square and is squeezed between two exact sequences. This is the source of the least obvious axiom of triangulated categories.
