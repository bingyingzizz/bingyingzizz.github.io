📌 Example: for the cylinder of radius $R$ parametrized by $r(u,v) = (R\cos u, R\sin u, v)$, one has $\mathrm{I} = R^{2}\mathrm{d}u^{2} + \mathrm{d}v^{2}$ ($E = R^{2}$, $F = 0$, $G = 1$), hence $K = 0$ (see `ex.cylinder-first-form`).
