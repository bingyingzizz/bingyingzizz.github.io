# product topology　`def.product-topology`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=def.product-topology)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./def.product-topology.md)

— that is, the **coarsest** topology making every projection $\pi_{j} : \prod_{i} X_{i} \to X_{j}$ continuous.

## Notes · **draft**
⭐ **"Coarsest" is the key word**: for the projections to be continuous it suffices that each $\pi_{j}^{-1}(U_{j})$ be open; taking finite intersections of these as a basis gives the product topology. Demanding a finer topology would lose limits — and the equivalence "$x_{n} \to x$ iff it converges in every coordinate" is exactly what this coarsest choice buys.
