## Statement · **draft**
Let $X$ be a scheme.

1. **Open immersions**: an open immersion $j : U \to X$ is étale, hence $U$ is an object of $X_{\text{et}}$; every open cover is an étale cover, so **every étale sheaf is a Zariski sheaf**. The converse fails.
