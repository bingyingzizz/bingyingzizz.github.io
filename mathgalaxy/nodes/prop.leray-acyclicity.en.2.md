## Notes · **draft**
**Corollary (common form)**: if $M \to K^{\bullet}$ is a **right resolution** and every $K^{n}$ is $F$-acyclic, then $RFM = FK^{\bullet}$, hence $R^{n}FM = H^{n}(FK^{\bullet})$ — this is why computing $RF$ need not use injective resolutions.
