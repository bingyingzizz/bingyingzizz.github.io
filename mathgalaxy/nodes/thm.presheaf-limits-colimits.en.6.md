# limits and colimits in presheaf categories　`thm.presheaf-limits-colimits`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=thm.presheaf-limits-colimits)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./thm.presheaf-limits-colimits.md)

The two steps combine: taking the limit (colimit) pointwise for each $X$ in $\mathbf{Set}$ gives the limit (colimit) in $\widehat{\mathcal{C}}$ ✓, and "the evaluation functor preserves limits and colimits" is another way of saying "computed pointwise" ✓. ∎

⭐ This is the **foundation** of almost every later construction: the advantage of a presheaf category is precisely that "limits and colimits come for free", so one can perform all sorts of formal constructions inside it and then sheafify the result.
