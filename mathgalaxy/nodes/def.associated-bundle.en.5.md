# Associated Bundle　`def.associated-bundle`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=def.associated-bundle)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./def.associated-bundle.md)

This correspondence is the mathematical form of "gauge fields versus equivariant functions on a principal bundle" in physics, and the source of "sections of a vector bundle = $\mathrm{GL}(k)$-equivariant $\mathbb{R}^{k}$-valued functions on the frame bundle".

⚠️ The associated bundle **does not need** $G$ discrete, nor $F$ linear. Taking $F = G/H$ gives "homogeneous space bundles" (for example $F = S^{2} = \mathrm{SO}(3)/\mathrm{SO}(2)$ gives "sphere bundles"); taking $F = G$ with the action given by left/right multiplication produces associated principal bundles — the latter being the kind of construction "twistors of $G$-bundles".
