**Proof (transfinite iteration; the categorical version of Baer's criterion).** Let $\mathcal{A}$ be an AB5 category with generator $U$.
- **Criterion**: $I$ is injective $\iff$ for every monomorphism $A\rightarrowtail B$ and every $A\to I$, the extension $B\to I$ exists. Since $U$ is a generator, it suffices to check **subobjects** $A\rightarrowtail U$ (a general $B$ is spread by a family of $U\to B$, extended piecewise, and pasted).
