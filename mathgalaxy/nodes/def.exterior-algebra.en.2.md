## Notes · **draft**
⭐ The exterior algebra is a "**polynomial algebra with signs**": together with the symmetric algebra it forms the two extremes — the symmetric algebra allows swapping, the exterior algebra forces anticommutation.

Both come from the same construction: quotient $V^{\otimes k}$ by the action of the symmetric group, or take invariants.
