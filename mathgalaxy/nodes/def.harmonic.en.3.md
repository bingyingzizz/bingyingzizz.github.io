# harmonic function　`def.harmonic`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=def.harmonic)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./def.harmonic.md)

⭐ Examples: $\operatorname{Re} z^n$, $x^2 - y^2$, $\ln|z|$ (on $\mathbb{C}^{\times}$), $e^{x}\cos y$.

⚠ Counterexamples: $|z|^2 = x^2 + y^2$ is not harmonic, $\Delta(x^2 + y^2) = 4$; nor is $x^3$, since $\Delta x^3 = 6x$.

⚠ The definition only asks for $C^2$, but harmonic functions are automatically $C^{\infty}$ and real analytic — a direct consequence of "being the real part of a holomorphic function".

📌 Harmonic functions lack the rigidity of holomorphic ones: the product of two harmonic functions is in general neither harmonic nor holomorphic; only $u + v$ and $cu$ are.
