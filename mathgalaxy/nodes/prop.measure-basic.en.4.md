(d): **"$\mu (E_{1}) < \infty$" cannot be omitted.** Counterexample: $X = \mathbb{N}$, $\mu =$ counting measure, $E_{j} = \{j, j+1$ …}; then every $\mu (E_{j}) = \infty$ but the intersection is $\emptyset$ and $\mu (\emptyset ) = 0$.

(c) and (d) together say: a measure is continuous under **monotone limits** of sets — which is exactly what the term "monotone class" echoes.
