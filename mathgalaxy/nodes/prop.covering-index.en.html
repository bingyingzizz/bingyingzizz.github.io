Statement

Let p:(E,e0)→(B,b0)p : (E, e_{0}) \to (B, b_{0})p:(E,e0​)→(B,b0​) be a covering map with E,BE, BE,B path-connected and locally path-connected. Then

Notes

⚠️⚠️ This is a bijection of sets, not a group isomorphism. The coset set π1(B)/p∗π1(E)\pi_{1}(B)/p_{*}\pi_{1}(E)π1​(B)/p∗​π1​(E) carries no group structure in general; from this alone one cannot get the group information π1(S1)≅Z\pi_{1}(S^{1}) \cong \mathbb{Z}π1​(S1)≅Z — only that π1(S1)\pi_{1}(S^{1})π1​(S1) and Z\mathbb{Z}Z have the same cardinality. ⭐ When the covering is universal (EEE simply connected, so p∗π1(E)=1p_{*}\pi_{1}(E) = 1p∗​π1​(E)=1), the conclusion simplifies to "fibre ↔π1(B)\leftrightarrow \pi_{1}(B)↔π1​(B) as sets". The map p:R→S1p : \mathbb{R} \to S^{1}p:R→S1 is exactly this case.

Geometric meaning of injectivity: something nonzero in π1(E)\pi_{1}(E)π1​(E) stays nonzero in BBB — a covering never pretends to "undo" an already trivial loop; it only makes a loop "tighter".
