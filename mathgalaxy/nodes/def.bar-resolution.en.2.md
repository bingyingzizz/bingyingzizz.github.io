# Bar Resolution　`def.bar-resolution`

[in graph](../#n=def.bar-resolution)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./def.bar-resolution.md)

where $B_{n}$ is the free $R$-module with $R$-basis the **symbols** $[g_{0}|g_{1}|\cdots|g_{n}]$ ($g_{i} \in G$), with $R[G]$ acting through the leftmost component:

$$g \cdot [g_{0}|g_{1}|\cdots|g_{n}] := [g g_{0}|g_{1}|\cdots|g_{n}] .$$

The differential and augmentation are

$$d_{n}[g_{0}|\cdots|g_{n}] := \sum_{i=0}^{n} (-1)^{i}\,[g_{0}|\cdots|\widehat{g_{i}}|\cdots|g_{n}], \qquad \varepsilon[g_{0}] := 1 ,$$

where $\widehat{g_{i}}$ means that term is omitted.
