Statement

Notes

What it says: the rate of change of the bending is symmetric in the tangent directions — the component along YYY of the change of IIIIII while moving along XXX equals the component along XXX while moving along YYY. Equivalently: the mixed components R 3jkiR^{i}_{\ 3jk}R 3jki​ of the curvature of R3\mathbb{R}^{3}R3 must vanish.

⭐ Not an assumption but a necessary condition: R3\mathbb{R}^{3}R3 is flat, so every surface that embeds in it automatically satisfies Codazzi. Together with the Gauss equation it forms the complete compatibility condition for "being realizable as a surface in R3\mathbb{R}^{3}R3" (曲面论基本定理).

Coordinate version: an equivalent form is nij=njin_{ij} = n_{ji}nij​=nji​; the symmetric way of writing it is ∇ihkj=∇jhki\nabla_{i}h_{kj} = \nabla_{j}h_{ki}∇i​hkj​=∇j​hki​ (with hhh symmetric the two agree).

In the frame version one only reads the ω1∧ω2\omega^{1}\wedge\omega^{2}ω1∧ω2 coefficient of dω 3i\mathrm{d}\omega^{i}_{\ 3}dω 3i​, without touching Christoffel symbols.

⚠️ This simple shape holds only in codimension 111 (surfaces in R3\mathbb{R}^{3}R3); in higher codimension the right-hand side acquires an extra normal-bundle curvature term. Only the codimension-one case inside R3\mathbb{R}^{3}R3 is used here.
