Statement

Free compact Hausdorff spaces (regarded as condensed sets) are all projective objects; moreover Cond\mathrm{Cond}Cond has enough projectives.

Notes

⭐ Why this matters so much for condensed mathematics: Cond\mathrm{Cond}Cond has enough projective objects (unlike a general topos, which only has enough injectives), so "left derived functors" in Cond\mathrm{Cond}Cond can be computed directly using free compact Hausdorff pieces — this is half of the basis for doing derived algebra in CondAb\mathrm{CondAb}CondAb.
