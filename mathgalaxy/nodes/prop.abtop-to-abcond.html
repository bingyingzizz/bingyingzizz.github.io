陈述

存在保所有极限的忠实函子

AbTop⟶AbCond,M⟼M‾.\mathbf{AbTop} \longrightarrow \mathrm{AbCond}, \qquad M \longmapsto \underline{M}.AbTop⟶AbCond,M⟼M​.

它在紧生成阿贝尔群这一部分上是全忠实的。

说明

构造：一个拓扑阿贝尔群 MMM 给出凝聚态阿贝尔群

M‾:S⟼C(S,M),\underline{M} : S \longmapsto C(S, M),M​:S⟼C(S,M),

即「从紧 Hausdorff 空间 SSS 到 MMM 的连续映射集」（逐点加法）。因为 MMM 是拓扑阿贝尔群、C(S,M)C(S, M)C(S,M) 按逐点加法是阿贝尔群，这是阿贝尔群值预层；层条件是「粘合连续映射」，成立。

保所有极限来自定理「Top→Cond\mathbf{Top} \to \mathrm{Cond}Top→Cond 保有限积」：凝聚态里的极限逐点算，而连续映射的极限在 Top\mathbf{Top}Top 里算和逐点算一致。

⭐ 这一条的实际用途：把拓扑问题搬进凝聚态这一侧，用那里的同调代数（长正合列、求导函子）去算，再搬回来。代价是丢失信息 —— 忠实但不一定满，所以「凝聚态地相等」比「拓扑地相等」更弱。

证明（照书：形式上来自「拓扑空间到凝聚态集」那条定理）。 对拓扑阿贝尔群 MMM，记 M‾(S):=C(S,M)\underline{M}(S) := C(S, M)M​(S):=C(S,M)，逐点取加法，得到 CondAb\mathrm{CondAb}CondAb 的对象 ✓（层条件是「连续映射的粘合」，逐点成立）。

  • 忠实：f≠g:M→Nf \ne g : M \to Nf=g:M→N 时取一点 mmm 使 f(m)≠g(m)f(m)\ne g(m)f(m)=g(m)，则在 M‾(∙)\underline{M}(\bullet)M​(∙) 上 f,gf,gf,g 已经不同 ✓（用测试对象 ∙\bullet∙ 这一个点就够）。
  • 保所有极限：凝聚态侧的极限逐项计算，而 C(S,lim←⁡iMi)=lim←⁡iC(S,Mi)C\bigl(S, \varprojlim_{i} M_{i}\bigr) = \varprojlim_{i} C(S, M_{i})C(S,lim​i​Mi​)=lim​i​C(S,Mi​) —— 拓扑阿贝尔群的极限（积 / 核 / 等化子）里「连续映射由各分量连续决定」✓。

逐项取极限得到的仍是层，故它就是 CondAb\mathrm{CondAb}CondAb 里的极限 ✓。

  • 紧生成那一部分全忠实：由 Hom⁡Cond(X‾,Y‾)=C(X,Y)\operatorname{Hom}_{\mathrm{Cond}}(\underline{X}, \underline{Y}) = C(X, Y)HomCond​(X​,Y​)=C(X,Y)（紧生成情形，见「内部 Hom 的函数空间刻画」），把 X,YX, YX,Y 取成紧生成的拓扑阿贝尔群、并把同态条件一并带上即得。∎

⚠️ 反向没有伴随：函子 X↦X(∙)X \mapsto X(\bullet)X↦X(∙) 不保积（CGWH\mathrm{CGWH}CGWH 不是对任意积封闭），所以这个嵌入没有现成的伴随 —— 这也是「凝聚态地相等更弱」的技术原因。

Statement draft

There is a faithful functor that preserves all limits

AbTop⟶AbCond,M⟼M‾.\mathbf{AbTop} \longrightarrow \mathrm{AbCond}, \qquad M \longmapsto \underline{M}.AbTop⟶AbCond,M⟼M​.

and is fully faithful on compactly generated abelian groups.

Notes draft

Construction: A topological abelian group MMM gives the condensed abelian group

M‾:S⟼C(S,M),\underline{M} : S \longmapsto C(S, M),M​:S⟼C(S,M),

namely the set of continuous maps from a compact Hausdorff space SSS to MMM, with pointwise addition. Since MMM is a topological abelian group, C(S,M)C(S, M)C(S,M) is an abelian group under pointwise addition. This defines an abelian-group-valued presheaf. The sheaf condition is gluing continuous maps, which holds.

Preservation of all limits follows from the theorem that Top→Cond\mathbf{Top} \to \mathrm{Cond}Top→Cond preserves finite products. Limits of condensed objects are computed pointwise, and computing limits of continuous maps in Top\mathbf{Top}Top agrees with computing them pointwise.

⭐ Application: Transfer a topological problem to condensed abelian groups, compute there using homological algebra (long exact sequences and derived functors), and transfer the result back. Some information is lost. The functor is faithful and need not be full, so isomorphism as condensed objects is weaker than topological isomorphism.

Proof (following the text, formally from the theorem on topological spaces and condensed sets). For a topological abelian group MMM, write M‾(S):=C(S,M)\underline{M}(S) := C(S, M)M​(S):=C(S,M) and use pointwise addition to obtain an object of CondAb\mathrm{CondAb}CondAb ✓ (the sheaf condition is gluing continuous maps and holds pointwise).

  • Faithfulness: If f≠g:M→Nf \ne g : M \to Nf=g:M→N, choose a point mmm such that f(m)≠g(m)f(m)\ne g(m)f(m)=g(m). Then on M‾(∙)\underline{M}(\bullet)M​(∙), the maps f,gf,gf,g already differ ✓ (the one-point test object ∙\bullet∙ suffices).
  • Preservation of all limits: Limits on the condensed side are computed objectwise, and C(S,lim←⁡iMi)=lim←⁡iC(S,Mi)C\bigl(S, \varprojlim_{i} M_{i}\bigr) = \varprojlim_{i} C(S, M_{i})C(S,lim​i​Mi​)=lim​i​C(S,Mi​). For limits of topological abelian groups (products, kernels, and equalizers), continuity of a map into the limit is determined by continuity of its components ✓.

The objectwise limit is still a sheaf, hence is the limit in CondAb\mathrm{CondAb}CondAb ✓.

  • Full faithfulness on compactly generated groups: Apply Hom⁡Cond(X‾,Y‾)=C(X,Y)\operatorname{Hom}_{\mathrm{Cond}}(\underline{X}, \underline{Y}) = C(X, Y)HomCond​(X​,Y​)=C(X,Y) in the compactly generated case (see the function-space characterization of internal Hom), take X,YX, YX,Y to be compactly generated topological abelian groups, and impose the homomorphism condition. ∎

⚠️ No adjoint in the reverse direction: The functor X↦X(∙)X \mapsto X(\bullet)X↦X(∙) does not preserve products (CGWH\mathrm{CGWH}CGWH is not closed under arbitrary products), so there is no readily available adjoint to this embedding. This is also the technical reason that isomorphism as condensed objects is weaker.
