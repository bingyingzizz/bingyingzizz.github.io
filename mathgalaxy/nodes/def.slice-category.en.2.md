# slice category　`def.slice-category`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=def.slice-category)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./def.slice-category.md)

It carries an evident **forgetful functor** $j_{T} : \mathcal{C}/T \to \mathcal{C}$. In the language of comma categories:

$$\mathcal{C}/T \;\cong\; \bigl(\mathcal{C} \hookrightarrow \widehat{\mathcal{C}}\bigr) \Big\downarrow \bigl(\mathbf{1} \xrightarrow{\ T\ } \widehat{\mathcal{C}}\bigr)$$

## Notes · **draft**
This is exactly the "category of elements" transferred to presheaves: elements of $T(X)$ are regarded as elements, and the forgetful functor $j_{T}$ sends $(X, s)$ back to $X$.
