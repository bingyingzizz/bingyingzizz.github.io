## Statement · **draft**
$$S^{2} := \{\, x \in \mathbb{R}^{3} : \lVert x \rVert = 1 \,\}$$

With the subspace topology, this is a **closed surface** (`def.closed-surface`). Its genus is $g = 0$ and its Euler characteristic is $\chi(S^{2}) = 2$.

## Notes · **draft**
⭐ **It is the first standard form in the classification theorem** and the identity for connected sum: $S^{2} \# M \cong M$ (see the notes in `def.connected-sum`).
