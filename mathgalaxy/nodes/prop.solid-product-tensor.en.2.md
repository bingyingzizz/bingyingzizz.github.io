## Notes · **draft**
⭐ This says that the solid tensor product on **compact projective objects** is just "multiplying the index sets" — exactly as for finite free modules, only infinite products are allowed.

⭐ Proof: write the free abelian group as a retract of $C(S,\mathbb{Z})$, reduce to $C(S,\mathbb{Z}) \otimes C(T,\mathbb{Z}) = C(S \times T, \mathbb{Z})$, and use the monoidality of $\otimes^{L\blacksquare}$.
