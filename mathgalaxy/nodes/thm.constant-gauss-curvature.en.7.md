# surfaces of constant Gauss curvature　`thm.constant-gauss-curvature`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=thm.constant-gauss-curvature)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./thm.constant-gauss-curvature.md)

⚠ **For $K > 0$, $r$ is bounded**: $f$ returns to zero at $\sqrt K\,r = \pi$, so geodesics meet again at a finite distance (antipodal points); for $K \le 0$, $f$ stays positive and geodesics never meet again.
