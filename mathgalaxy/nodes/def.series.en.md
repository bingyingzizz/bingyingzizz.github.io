## Statement · **draft**
Given a sequence $\{a_n\}$, let $s_N := \sum_{n=1}^{N} a_n$ be its **partial sums**.

The series $\sum_{n=1}^{\infty} a_n$ is said to **converge** if and only if the sequence of partial sums $\{s_N\}$ converges; in that case write

$$\sum_{n=1}^{\infty} a_n := \lim_{N \to \infty} s_N$$
