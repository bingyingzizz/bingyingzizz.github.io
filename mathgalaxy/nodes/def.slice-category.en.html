Statement

Let C\mathcal{C}C be a category and TTT a presheaf on C\mathcal{C}C. The slice category C/T\mathcal{C}/TC/T (also written CT\mathcal{C}_{T}CT​) is defined as follows:

  1. objects are: an object of C\mathcal{C}C, say XXX, together with a section s∈T(X)s \in T(X)s∈T(X), i.e. pairs (X,s)(X, s)(X,s);
  2. a morphism from (X,s)(X, s)(X,s) to (X′,s′)(X', s')(X′,s′) is determined by the condition T(f)(s′)=sT(f)(s') = sT(f)(s′)=s, namely f:X→X′f : X \to X'f:X→X′.

It carries an evident forgetful functor jT:C/T→Cj_{T} : \mathcal{C}/T \to \mathcal{C}jT​:C/T→C. In the language of comma categories:

C/T  ≅  (C↪C^)↓(1→ T C^)\mathcal{C}/T \;\cong\; \bigl(\mathcal{C} \hookrightarrow \widehat{\mathcal{C}}\bigr) \Big\downarrow \bigl(\mathbf{1} \xrightarrow{\ T\ } \widehat{\mathcal{C}}\bigr)C/T≅(C↪C)↓⏐​(1 T ​C)

Notes

This is exactly the "category of elements" transferred to presheaves: elements of T(X)T(X)T(X) are regarded as elements, and the forgetful functor jTj_{T}jT​ sends (X,s)(X, s)(X,s) back to XXX.

⭐ The comma-category description makes its identity clear: it is the category obtained by pulling the presheaf TTT back along the Yoneda embedding — on one side C↪C^\mathcal{C} \hookrightarrow \widehat{\mathcal{C}}C↪C, on the other 1→TC^\mathbf{1} \xrightarrow{T} \widehat{\mathcal{C}}1T​C.

It also explains why, when X∈CX \in \mathcal{C}X∈C, C/X≃C^/hX\mathcal{C}/X \simeq \widehat{\mathcal{C}}/h_{X}C/X≃C/hX​ holds.

Its use is to give a presheaf an index category: when speaking of "which points glue this presheaf together", the indices run over C/T\mathcal{C}/TC/T (the density theorem is used this way).
