## Statement · **draft**
Let $n\ge 2$, and let $P$ be a regular polygon in the plane with $2n$ sides, whose boundary edges are paired by a fixed-point-free involution $\tau$. Glue each pair of edges according to $x\sim\tau(x)$. The quotient space $S=P/{\sim}$ is called a **polygonal representation**. If every pair of edges is glued with opposite orientations, then $S$ is orientable. If some pair is glued with the same orientation, then $S$ is nonorientable.
