## Notes · **draft**
⭐ **Three equivalent descriptions**: (1) A sphere with antipodal points identified. (2) A disk with antipodal boundary points identified. (3) A square with opposite sides glued in **reverse orientation** ($T^{2}$ has same-orientation gluing, so reversing orientation makes the difference).

⭐ **Computing $\chi = 1$**: Start with a disk ($\chi = 1$). Identifying antipodal boundary points amounts to attaching a half-twisted band, which eliminates the boundary. The full circle of $V$ becomes one point, and $E$ becomes one closed curve. Thus $\chi = 1 - 1 + 1 = 1$, in agreement with $2 - k = 1$.
