## Notes · **stale**
**Faithful but usually not full**: having the same underlying map does not guarantee preserving structure; $\mathbf{Top}\to\mathbf{Set}$ and $\mathbf{Ab}\to\mathbf{Set}$ are both faithful and not full.

**Left adjoints are free constructions**: free groups, free vector spaces, and the discrete topology are the left adjoints of the corresponding forgetful functors.

**Preservation of limits**: forgetful functors to $\mathbf{Set}$ usually preserve all limits (constructed pointwise), but in general they do not preserve colimits.
