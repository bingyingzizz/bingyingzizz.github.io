# Splitting Principle　`thm.splitting-principle`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=thm.splitting-principle)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./thm.splitting-principle.md)

⚠️ **With $\mathbb{Z}$ coefficients there is a torsion problem**: injectivity is cleanest with $\mathbb{Q}$ coefficients; with integral coefficients one must be careful about $2$-torsion (which is why Stiefel–Whitney classes use $\mathbb{Z}/2$ coefficients).

**Typical use**: from $c(E) = \prod(1+x_{j})$ one immediately gets $c_{1}(E) = \sum x_{j} = c_{1}(\det E)$ and $c_{k}(E) = \prod x_{j} = e(E)$ (top Chern class = Euler class). These two formulas are obtained at once in general rank via the splitting principle.
