## Statement · **draft**
Let $\mathcal{A}$ be an abelian category and $K$ a cochain complex in $\mathcal{A}$. The **homology** of $K$ in degree $n$ is

$$H^{n}(K) \;:=\; Z^{n}(K) \big/ B^{n}(K) \;=\; \ker d^{n} \big/ \operatorname{im} d^{n-1}$$
