# Cauchy Integral Formula　`thm.cauchy-integral-formula`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=thm.cauchy-integral-formula)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./thm.cauchy-integral-formula.md)

Let $\Omega$ be a bounded region whose boundary $\partial\Omega$ is a piecewise smooth simple closed curve, oriented positively, and $f$ holomorphic on an open set containing $\overline{\Omega}$. Then for every $z \in \Omega$,

$$f(z) = \frac{1}{2\pi i}\oint_{\partial\Omega} \frac{f(\zeta)}{\zeta - z}\,\mathrm{d}\zeta.$$
