# Fermat's lemma on interior extrema　`thm.fermat`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=thm.fermat)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./thm.fermat.md)

⚠ None of the three hypotheses can be dropped: differentiability ($|x|$ has an interior minimum at $0$ but is not differentiable there), interiority ($f(x) = x$ on $[0, 1]$ attains its minimum at the endpoint $0$, where $f'(0) = 1 \ne 0$), and extremality.

⚠ **The converse fails**: $f'(x_0) = 0$ does not guarantee an extremum ($f(x) = x^{3}$ at $0$).

Points with $f' = 0$ are called **stationary points**; they must be checked alongside non-differentiable points and endpoints.
