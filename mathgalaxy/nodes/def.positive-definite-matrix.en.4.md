# Positive Definite Matrix　`def.positive-definite-matrix`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=def.positive-definite-matrix)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./def.positive-definite-matrix.md)

**Why leading principal minors suffice**: Sylvester's criterion — in the LDL decomposition the $k$-th pivot equals the ratio of $k$-order leading principal minors, and all pivots are positive exactly when the matrix is positive definite. ⚠️ Only the **leading** principal minors are checked, not all principal minors (all principal minors $>0$ is a stronger condition, equivalent only in special situations).
