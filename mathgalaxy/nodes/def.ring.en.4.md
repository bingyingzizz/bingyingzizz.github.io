**Which hypothesis each direction uses** (here the definition is axiomatic; commutativity and identity are extra requirements):
- ring $\iff$ additive abelian group, associative multiplication, left and right distributivity: unwinding the definition adopted in this node; no axiom of choice is needed, nor is a field or PID required.
- commutative ring $\implies$ ring, ring with identity $\implies$ ring: just forget extra structure; neither converse is free — $M_2(\mathbb{R})$ is not commutative, and $2\mathbb{Z}$ has no identity under the usual multiplication.
- adding commutative multiplication $\iff$ commutative ring; adding an identity together with the convention $1\ne0$ of this library $\iff$ ring with id
