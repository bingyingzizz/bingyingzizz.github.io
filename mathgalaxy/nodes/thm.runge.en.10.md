# Runge approximation theorem　`thm.runge`

[in graph](../#n=thm.runge)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./thm.runge.md)

**Step 3: Obtaining polynomials when there are no holes.** If $\mathbb{C}\setminus K$ is connected, it is an unbounded connected open set. Choose $b^{\prime}$ such that $|b^{\prime}| > 2\max_{z\in K}|z|$. For $z\in K$, we have $|z/b^{\prime}| \le 1/2$, so $\frac{1}{b^{\prime} - z} = \frac{1}{b^{\prime}}\sum_{k=0}^{\infty}\Bigl(\frac{z}{b^{\prime}}\Bigr)^{k}$ converges uniformly on $K$, and its truncations are polynomials. Thus $f$ is uniformly approximated on $K$ by polynomials to within $\varepsilon$.
