**5. $\beta_{2},\beta_{0},\beta_{1}$ (complete).** By `thm.simplicial-approximation`, $H_{2}(S;\mathbb{Z})\cong H_{2}(K';\mathbb{Z})\cong\mathbb{Z}$, so $\beta_{2}(S)=1$. Since $S$ is connected and is a manifold (hence locally path-connected), it is path-connected. By `prop.h0-components`, $H_{0}(S)\cong\mathbb{Z}$, $\beta_{0}=1$. By the definition in `def.genus`, $\beta_{1}=\operatorname{rank}H_{1}=2g$.

**6. Conclusion.** $\chi(S)=\beta_{0}-\beta_{1}+\beta_{2}=1-2g+1=2-2g$. ∎ (Using Radó's theorem from Section 1 and the definition of orientability above.)
