## Notes · **draft**
**Proof.** Check the pretopology axioms one by one ✓: identity isomorphisms are coverings ✓; coverings pull back along arbitrary morphisms to coverings (using (T2) and fiber products) ✓; a common refinement of two coverings is a covering (using (T3)) ✓. ∎
