# Frobenius theorem　`thm.frobenius`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=thm.frobenius)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./thm.frobenius.md)

1. $\mathcal{D}$ is involutive (`def.involutive-distribution`).
2. **Existence of flat coordinates**: Each point $p$ has a neighborhood $U$ with coordinates $(x^{1},\ldots,x^{n})$ in which $\mathcal{D}|_{U}$ is spanned by $\partial_{1},\ldots,\partial_{k}$.
3. **Local integrability**: Each point $p$ lies in an $k$-dimensional integral manifold (`def.integral-manifold`).
4. **Existence of a foliation**: $M$ is covered by a family of pairwise disjoint maximal connected integral manifolds (leaves), each of which is an immersed submanifold.
