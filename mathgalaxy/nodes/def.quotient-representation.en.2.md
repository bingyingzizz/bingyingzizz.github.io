## Notes · **draft**
Well-definedness uses $\theta(a)W\subseteq W$. The dimension of the quotient is $\dim V-\dim W$. For group representations the action is $\rho(g)(v+W)=\rho(g)v+W$. Quotients of semisimple representations are again semisimple; see the proposition on subrepresentations and quotients.
