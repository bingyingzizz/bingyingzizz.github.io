# long exact sequence of a pair　`thm.long-exact-pair`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=thm.long-exact-pair)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./thm.long-exact-pair.md)

$$S_{p}(X, A) := S_{p}(X) \big/ S_{p}(A),$$
$$H_{p}(X, A) := \ker\bar\partial \big/ \operatorname{im}\bar\partial.$$

Then there is a **long exact sequence** (at each $\cdot$, the exactness condition is $\operatorname{im} = \ker$)

$$\cdots \to H_{p}(A) \to H_{p}(X) \to H_{p}(X, A)$$
$$\xrightarrow{\ \partial_{*}\ } H_{p-1}(A) \to \cdots$$

where $\partial_{*}$ is called the **connecting homomorphism**. The sequence holds for every pair of spaces. It **requires no assumption** that $A$ is closed, open, or otherwise well behaved, and no cofibration hypothesis is needed.
