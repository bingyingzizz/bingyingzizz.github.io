# Pullback: Between Open Sets　`prop.pullback-forms`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=prop.pullback-forms)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./prop.pullback-forms.md)

Let $F : U\to V$ be a $C^{\infty}$ map between open sets, and let $\omega$ be a $k$-form on $V$. The **pullback** $F^{\ast}\omega$ is the $k$-form on $U$ defined by

$$(F^{\ast}\omega)_{p}(v_{1},\ldots,v_{k}) := \omega_{F(p)}\bigl(\mathrm{d}F_{p}(v_{1}),\ldots,\mathrm{d}F_{p}(v_{k})\bigr).$$
