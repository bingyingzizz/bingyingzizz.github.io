# Theorema Egregium　`thm.theorema-egregium`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=thm.theorema-egregium)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./thm.theorema-egregium.md)

**An operational reading**: an inhabitant of the surface who can only measure lengths can still determine $K$. Measure the circumference $\rho$ of a small geodesic circle of radius $L(\rho)$; then

$$K(p) = \frac{3}{\pi}\lim_{\rho\to0}\frac{2\pi\rho-L(\rho)}{\rho^{3}}.$$

This turns the remarkable theorem into an experiment: curvature is the third-order rate at which the circumference falls short of $2\pi\rho$.
