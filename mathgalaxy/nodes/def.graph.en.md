## Statement · **draft**
A **graph** is a quadruple

$$( V, E, s, t )$$

where $V$ and $E$ are sets and $s, t : E \to V$ are functions — called the **source map** and the **target map**. Elements of $V$ are **vertices** and elements of $E$ are **edges**.
