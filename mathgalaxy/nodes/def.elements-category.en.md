## Statement · **draft**
For a functor $F : \mathcal{C} \to \mathbf{Set}$, its **category of elements** $\operatorname{el}(F)$ has as objects

$$\bigl\{(Y, t) : Y \in \mathcal{C},\ t \in F(Y)\bigr\}$$

and as morphisms from $(Y, t)$ to $(Z, u)$ those arrows such that $F(f)(t) = u$, with $f : Y \to Z$ the arrow itself.
