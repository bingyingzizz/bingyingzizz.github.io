$$M \longmapsto \widehat{M/M_{0}},\qquad M_{0} = \{\, s : \|s\| = 0 \,\}.$$

## Notes · **draft**
**What this looks like in condensed mathematics.** For a Banach abelian group $M$, the sections $\operatorname{Hom}(\mathbb{Z}[S], M)$ carry a supremum seminorm, so $M$ carries norm information as a condensed abelian group. All quantitative statements in §8.2 use this seminorm.

⚠️ The norm here is on an **abelian group**. Every real Banach space is a Banach abelian group after forgetting scalar multiplication, and the converse fails. Many theorems in Chapter 8 use only the group structure.
