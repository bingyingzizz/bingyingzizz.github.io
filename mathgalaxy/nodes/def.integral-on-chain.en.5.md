# Integration over a Chain　`def.integral-on-chain`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=def.integral-on-chain)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./def.integral-on-chain.md)

**Division of labour with `def.integral-form`**: there, an $n$-form is integrated over an **open set**, and partitions of unity are needed to glue to the manifold; here, a $k$-form is integrated along **one cube**, one multiple integral from start to finish. The global meaning is carried by chains (summation), not by partitions of unity — this is the most economical feature of this bookkeeping.
