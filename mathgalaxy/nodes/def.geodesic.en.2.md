# Geodesic　`def.geodesic`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=def.geodesic)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./def.geodesic.md)

$$\frac{\mathrm{d}^{2}x^{k}}{\mathrm{d}s^{2}}+\Gamma^{k}_{ij}(\gamma(s))\frac{\mathrm{d}x^{i}}{\mathrm{d}s}\frac{\mathrm{d}x^{j}}{\mathrm{d}s} = 0,\qquad k = 1,2.$$

A third, equivalent face: any sufficiently short arc of $\gamma$ is the **shortest** curve joining its endpoints.
