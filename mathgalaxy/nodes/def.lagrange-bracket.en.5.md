# Lagrange Bracket　`def.lagrange-bracket`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=def.lagrange-bracket)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./def.lagrange-bracket.md)

⭐ **Geometric meaning**: The Lagrange bracket measures the area enclosed in phase space by the families of level curves of $u$ and $v$. The Lagrange bracket thus describes areas, while the Poisson bracket describes flows. This is why the canonical transformation condition is more intuitive in terms of Lagrange brackets: it directly expresses area preservation.

⚠️ **$L = -P^{-1}$ holds only when the comparison is made in the same coordinate system**: Both matrices must be written using the **same** $(u, v)$ as a basis to make the comparison meaningful. This is the easiest point to misremember.
