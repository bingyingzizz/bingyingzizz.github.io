# Étale Sheaf　`def.etale-sheaf`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=def.etale-sheaf)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./def.etale-sheaf.md)

**Why the topology must change**: the Zariski topology is too coarse — on $\operatorname{Spec} k(t)$ it does not see a Galois covering with group $k$. After passing to the étale topology, coefficients such as $\mathbb{Z}/n$ can see geometry (Galois groups, Kummer sequences and the whole Weil-conjecture story become meaningful only after this step).

⭐ **Zariski coverings are étale coverings** (open immersions are étale, and open coverings are of course jointly surjective). Thus the topology "refines" and sheaves "become fewer":

$$\mathbf{Sh}(X_{\text{et}}, \mathbf{Ab}) \longrightarrow \mathbf{Sh}(X_{\mathrm{Zar}}, \mathbf{Ab})$$
