# the internal hom as a function space　`prop.cond-internal-hom`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=prop.cond-internal-hom)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./prop.cond-internal-hom.md)

**What the two sides say.** $X(\bullet)$ is the **underlying space** of $X$ (feed $X$ to compact Hausdorff test objects $S$ to get a set, then equip it with the topology determined by test maps); $\operatorname{Hom}(X, \underline{Y})$ is the **object** "maps from $X$ to $Y$".

The equality says: taking the underlying space and then the function space is the same as taking the internal Hom and then the underlying space.
