Statement

In plain words: on a simply connected region every harmonic function is the real part of some holomorphic function — the imaginary part is called its "harmonic conjugate" and is unique up to a constant.

Let Ω\OmegaΩ be a simply connected region and let uuu be harmonic on Ω\OmegaΩ. Then there exists a holomorphic function Ω\OmegaΩ on fff such that

Re⁡f=u.\operatorname{Re} f = u.Ref=u.

Moreover vvv (the harmonic conjugate, satisfying vx=−uyv_x = -u_yvx​=−uy​ and vy=uxv_y = u_xvy​=ux​) is unique up to an additive real constant — equivalently, f=u+ivf = u + ivf=u+iv is unique up to an additive purely imaginary constant.
