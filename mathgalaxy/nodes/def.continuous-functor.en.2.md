# continuous functor　`def.continuous-functor`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=def.continuous-functor)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./def.continuous-functor.md)

$$g^{*} : \widetilde{\mathcal{C}^{\prime}} \longrightarrow \widetilde{\mathcal{C}}, \qquad F \longmapsto F \circ g$$
(it sends sheaves to sheaves, so it restricts to the sheaf categories.)

## Notes · **draft**
**Reading**: the direction of $g$ is $\mathcal{C} \to \mathcal{C}'$, but sheaves are **contravariant**, so $g$ pulls sheaves on $\mathcal{C}'$ **back** to $\mathcal{C}$. This "twist of direction" is the source of all awkwardness with morphisms of sites.
