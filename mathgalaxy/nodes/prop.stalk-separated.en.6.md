# stalk　`prop.stalk-separated`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=prop.stalk-separated)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./prop.stalk-separated.md)

s**: a presheaf is separated $\iff$ $T(U) \to \prod_{x \in U}T_{x}$ is injective. $(\implies)$ uses "two sections with the same germ at every point $\implies$ are equal" (intersect the covers); $(\Leftarrow)$ is checked pointwise. ⚠️ **Separatedness is only half of the sheaf condition** (the other half is "gluing", i.e. surjectivity).
