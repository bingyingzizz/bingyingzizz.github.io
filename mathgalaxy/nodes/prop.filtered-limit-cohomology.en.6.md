# Cohomology of a Filtered Limit　`prop.filtered-limit-cohomology`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=prop.filtered-limit-cohomology)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./prop.filtered-limit-cohomology.md)

⭐ **Use**: reduce the cohomology of a large (possibly infinite-dimensional, non-compact) object to a filtered colimit of cohomologies of compact Hausdorff pieces. This "compute piecewise, then take the colimit" technique recurs in the Breen–Deligne spectral sequence of §8.3.
