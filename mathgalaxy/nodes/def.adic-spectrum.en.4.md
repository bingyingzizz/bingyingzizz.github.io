# the adic spectrum　`def.adic-spectrum`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=def.adic-spectrum)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./def.adic-spectrum.md)

is the set of equivalence classes of valuations on $A$ that are bounded on $A^+$ — that is, satisfy $\le 1$ (every element of $A^+$ is bounded). The topology is generated by **rational open sets**: for $f, g_1, \ldots, g_n \in A$,

$$U\!\left(\tfrac{g_1, \ldots, g_n}{f}\right) := \bigl\{\, x : |g_i(x)| \le |f(x)| \ne 0\ \forall i\, \bigr\}.$$

These rational open sets form a basis, and $\operatorname{Spa}(A,A^+)$ is a **spectral space** (homeomorphic to the prime spectrum of some ring; equivalently: compact, **sober**, and compact open sets form a basis).
