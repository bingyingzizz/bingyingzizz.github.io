## Statement · **draft**
Let $K \subset \mathbb{C}$ be compact, and let $f$ be holomorphic on a neighborhood of $K$. If $\mathbb{C} \setminus K$ is connected (that is, $K$ has no holes), then for each $\varepsilon > 0$ there is a **polynomial** $p$ such that

$$\sup_{z \in K} |f(z) - p(z)| < \varepsilon.$$
