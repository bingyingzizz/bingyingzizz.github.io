## Notes · **draft**
**Intuition**: $\mathbb{R}$ is **divisible** ($\mathbb{R}$'s elements all admit roots of every order, and are divisible by every $n$), while a discrete group has no such divisibility. **Divisibility collapses completely in the direction into a discrete group** — hence all higher Hom groups (and the zeroth) out of a real vector space vanish.
