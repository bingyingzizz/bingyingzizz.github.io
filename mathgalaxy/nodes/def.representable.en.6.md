# representable functor　`def.representable`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=def.representable)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./def.representable.md)

  $$F : \mathbf{Top}^{\mathrm{op}} \to \mathbf{Set}, \qquad Z \mapsto \{\, f \in \mathcal{C}(Z, X) \ \mid\ f(Z) \subseteq Y \,\}$$

  (here $\mathcal{C}(Z, X)$ denotes the set of continuous maps $Z \to X$). It is represented by the natural element $i : Y \rightarrowtail X$ of $F(Y)$.
