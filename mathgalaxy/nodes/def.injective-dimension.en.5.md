# Injective Dimension　`def.injective-dimension`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=def.injective-dimension)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./def.injective-dimension.md)

$\mathbb{Z}$ is not injective, and $0 \to \mathbb{Z} \to \mathbb{Q} \to \mathbb{Q}/\mathbb{Z} \to 0$ is an injective resolution of length $1$, so $\operatorname{id}_{\mathbb{Z}}\mathbb{Z} = 1$ ✓.
