[in graph](../#n=def.substitution)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./def.substitution.md)

⭐ **Substitution is not a side note on the map**: the whole content of the **replacement axiom schema** is "using one formula $\varphi (x, y, p)$, collect the images in $A$ of every $x$", and what happens there is exactly substitution.

⚠ The standard way to avoid capture is first to **rename** the conflicting bound variable ($\alpha$-renaming), then substitute. This is the most verbose but unavoidable step in logic textbooks.
