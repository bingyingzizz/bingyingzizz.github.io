## Notes · **draft**
**Proof** (Moser's trick). The goal is to pull the form $p$ back, near $\omega$, to the constant-coefficient form $\omega_{0} = \sum_{i=1}^{n} dq_{i} \wedge dp_{i}$. There are five steps, all carried out in a sufficiently small chart.
