## Statement · **draft**
Let $X$ be a topological space and $p : X \to \mathbf{1}$ the map to a point. Then the topos morphism it gives satisfies

$$p_{*}\mathcal{F} = \Gamma(X, \mathcal{F}), \qquad p^{-1}E = \widetilde{E}_{X};$$
