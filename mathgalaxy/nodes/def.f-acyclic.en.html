Notes

Why it matters: FFF-acyclic objects may be used for resolutions — when computing RFRFRF one need not use only injective objects; acyclic objects are legitimate (this is what the next Leray acyclicity statement says).

Examples: injective objects are of course acyclic (RFRFRF is defined using them); flat modules are acyclic for M⊗A−M \otimes_{A} -M⊗A​−; condensed abelian groups on a Stonean space are acyclic for the section functor — that is exactly the beginning of §8.
