Statement

Let VVV be a real vector space and x1,…,xn∈Vx_{1}, \ldots, x_{n} \in Vx1​,…,xn​∈V. If λi≥0\lambda_{i} \ge 0λi​≥0 and ∑i=1nλi=1\sum_{i=1}^{n} \lambda_{i} = 1∑i=1n​λi​=1, then

∑i=1nλixi\sum_{i=1}^{n} \lambda_{i} x_{i}∑i=1n​λi​xi​

is called a convex combination of these points. Equivalently, a convex combination is a point obtained by "first taking segments, then repeatedly taking segments"; when n=2n=2n=2 it is the segment between two points.

Notes

⭐ Characterization of a convex set CCC: CCC is convex   ⟺  \iff⟺ CCC is closed under arbitrary finite convex combinations; this is the standard way of extending convexity from two points to finitely many points. ⚠ Only finitely many points are allowed here; infinite convex combinations generally need topology/convergence and are not part of the elementary definition.
