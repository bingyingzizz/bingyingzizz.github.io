## Notes · **draft**
**Proof.** By "an open cover gives a cover", an open cover $U = \bigcup_i U_{i}$ gives a cover in $\mathrm{Cond}/X$, namely $\coprod_i \underline{U_{i}} \to \underline{U}$ ✓.

And $Y$ is an object of the topos $\mathrm{Cond}/X$; objects of a topos satisfy descent for every covering sieve in the **standard topology** — the sheaf condition is precisely "$\operatorname{Hom}(-, Y)$ turns covering sieves into equalizers" ✓.
