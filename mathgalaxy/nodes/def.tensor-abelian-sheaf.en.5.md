⭐ **Condensed abelian groups $\mathrm{CondAb}$ are exactly the special case here** ($\mathcal{T} = \mathrm{Cond}$): every item above carries over verbatim, with no need to write it out separately. In particular $\mathrm{CondAb}$ is a closed symmetric monoidal category, hence enriched over itself, and one can speak of tensor products, duality, and commutative algebras.
