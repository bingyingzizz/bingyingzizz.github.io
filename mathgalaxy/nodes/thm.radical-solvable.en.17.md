Finally, $\operatorname{Gal}(K/F)$ is a quotient of $\operatorname{Gal}(K^{\prime}/F)$. The extension $K^{\prime}/F$ is normal, and every automorphism of $K$ over $F$ extends to $K^{\prime}$. A quotient of a solvable group is solvable, so $\operatorname{Gal}(K/F)$ is solvable. ∎
