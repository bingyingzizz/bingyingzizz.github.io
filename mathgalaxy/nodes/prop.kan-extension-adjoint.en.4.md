# Kan Extensions and the Existence of Adjoints　`prop.kan-extension-adjoint`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=prop.kan-extension-adjoint)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./prop.kan-extension-adjoint.md)

The data on the right is precisely the defining universal property of "the left Kan extension of $F$ along $p$" ✓: the **unit** of the adjunction $\eta_{F} : F \implies p_{!}F \circ p$ is the $\alpha$ in the definition, and the isomorphism itself gives the universal property of $\alpha$ ✓.
So "every $F$ has a left Kan extension" and "$p^{-1}$ has a left adjoint" are the same statement; dually, reversing the arrows gives the right Kan extension clause ✓. ∎
