# Gauss Equation　`thm.gauss-equation`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=thm.gauss-equation)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./thm.gauss-equation.md)

**Classification of points**: $K > 0$ elliptic, $K = 0$ with not all principal curvatures zero parabolic, $K < 0$ hyperbolic; $k_{1} = k_{2}$ gives an umbilic point.

⭐ **Corollary read off (theorema egregium)**: $K = \mathrm{d}\omega^{1}_{\ 2}/(\omega^{1}\wedge\omega^{2})$ involves only $\omega^{1},\omega^{2}$;

and $\omega^{1}_{\ 2}$ is uniquely solved from $\mathrm{d}\omega^{1},\mathrm{d}\omega^{2}$ together with zero torsion (write $\omega^{1}_{\ 2} = a_{1}\omega^{1}+a_{2}\omega^{2}$; two equations give the two coefficients), so $K$ depends only on $I$ — the Gauss curvature is **intrinsic**.
