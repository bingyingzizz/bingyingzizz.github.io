## Statement · **draft**
A **bialgebra** is a **monoid** in the opposite of the category of $k$-algebras. $k[t]$ with $t \mapsto t \otimes 1 + 1 \otimes t$, and $k[t, 1/t]$ with $t \mapsto t \otimes t$, are both bialgebras.
