# derived category　`def.derived-category`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=def.derived-category)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./def.derived-category.md)

**What a "roof" is**: localization cannot in general be done by "one step to the right", because quasi-isomorphisms do not always admit a common denominator; hence a morphism is written as "invert one quasi-isomorphism, then take one step". This is also why the Hom of $D(\mathcal{A})$ **need not be a set** — it is under control only when $\mathcal{A}$ has enough injectives (or projectives).

📌 The bounded-above, bounded-below and bounded versions are written $D^{+}$, $D^{-}$, $D^{b}$; the injective-complex version is written $K^{+}(\mathcal{I})$ and so on.
