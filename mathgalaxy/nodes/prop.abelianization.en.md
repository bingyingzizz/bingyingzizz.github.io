## Statement · **draft**
The **abelianization** functor

$$(-)^{\mathrm{ab}} : \mathbf{Grp} \longrightarrow \mathbf{Ab}, \qquad G \longmapsto G^{\mathrm{ab}} := G / [G, G]$$

is **left adjoint** to the inclusion functor $\mathbf{Ab} \hookrightarrow \mathbf{Grp}$.
