# Tangent Space　`def.tangent-space-manifold`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=def.tangent-space-manifold)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./def.tangent-space-manifold.md)

Take the class of the curve $t\mapsto\varphi^{-1}\bigl(\varphi(p)+t\,e_{i}\bigr)$, denoted $\partial_{i}(p)$.

These $n$ tangent vectors are pairwise distinct and linearly independent, forming a basis of $T_{p}M$, hence $\dim T_{p}M = n$.

**Derivation characterization** (equivalent definition): $T_{p}M$ is in bijection with the linear functionals $D : C^{\infty}(M)\to\mathbb{R}$ satisfying the Leibniz rule, i.e. $D(fg) = f(p)\,Dg+g(p)\,Df$, the correspondence being $v = [\gamma]\mapsto\bigl(f\mapsto (f\circ\gamma)'(0)\bigr)$.
