## Statement · **draft**
Let $V$ be a real vector space and $x_{1}, \ldots, x_{n} \in V$. If $\lambda_{i} \ge 0$ and $\sum_{i=1}^{n} \lambda_{i} = 1$, then

$\sum_{i=1}^{n} \lambda_{i} x_{i}$

is called a **convex combination** of these points. Equivalently, a convex combination is a point obtained by "first taking segments, then repeatedly taking segments"; when $n=2$ it is the segment between two points.
