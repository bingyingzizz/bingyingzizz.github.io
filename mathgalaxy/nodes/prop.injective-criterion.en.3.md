## Notes · **draft**
**A Zorn-dependent supplement (crucial)**: in the category of $R$-modules, the Bolzano–Weierstrass-style argument — "$I$ is injective $\iff$ for every ideal $J \subseteq R$, every $J \to I$ extends to $R \to I$" (Baer's criterion) — uses **Zorn's lemma** (take a maximal extendable submodule). So the convenience "injectivity can be tested on ideals" **costs the axiom of choice**; the corresponding statement in a general Grothendieck category tests on subobjects and needs no AC.
