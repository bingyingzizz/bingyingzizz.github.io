# Global Dimension　`def.global-dimension`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=def.global-dimension)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./def.global-dimension.md)

⭐ **It measures how many steps are needed to compute $\operatorname{Ext}$**: The smaller the global dimension, the shorter the resolutions needed to compute derived functors. Dimension $0$ means there is nothing to compute, and dimension $1$ means it suffices to relate two exact sequences. This is also why the homological algebra of abelian groups is so simple.

⚠️ **The distinction between left and right matters**: Over a noncommutative ring, the left and right global dimensions can differ. Over a commutative ring they agree, so a single notation suffices.
