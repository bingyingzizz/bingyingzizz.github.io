# topology　`def.topology`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=def.topology)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./def.topology.md)

1. **Both ends**: $\emptyset \in \mathcal{T}$ and $X \in \mathcal{T}$;
2. **Closed under arbitrary unions**: $\{ U_i \}_{i \in I} \subseteq \mathcal{T} \implies \bigcup_{i \in I} U_i \in \mathcal{T}$ ($I$ is arbitrary, possibly uncountable);
3. **Closed under finite intersections**: $U, V \in \mathcal{T} \implies U \cap V \in \mathcal{T}$.

The members of $\mathcal{T}$ are called **open sets**, and their complements **closed sets**.
