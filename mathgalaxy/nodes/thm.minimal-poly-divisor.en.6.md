# the minimal polynomial divides the characteristic polynomial　`thm.minimal-poly-divisor`

[in graph](../#n=thm.minimal-poly-divisor)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./thm.minimal-poly-divisor.md)

agonalization must be discussed over the algebraic closure; the real plane rotation with $x^2+1$ has no repeated roots yet is not diagonalizable over the reals. Nor can "no repeated roots" be dropped: the minimal polynomial of a nonzero 2×2 nilpotent Jordan block is $x^2$.
