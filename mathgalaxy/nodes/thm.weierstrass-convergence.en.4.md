[in graph](../#n=thm.weierstrass-convergence)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./thm.weierstrass-convergence.md)

⭐ **Why this is the foundation of the whole chapter**: it says that holomorphy is **closed** under uniform convergence, so one may safely pass to limits — normal families (`thm.montel`), the Riemann mapping theorem, and Hurwitz's theorem all rely on it to justify that "the limit function is still holomorphic".
