# Abelian Sheaves Have Enough Injectives　`thm.pushforward-enough-injectives`

[in graph](../#n=thm.pushforward-enough-injectives)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./thm.pushforward-enough-injectives.md)

⭐ **Why this makes the definition of sheaf cohomology legitimate**: defining $H^{n}(X, \mathcal{F}) := R^{n}\Gamma(X, \mathcal{F})$ presupposes an injective resolution.

Without enough injectives, resolutions do not exist at all, and $R^{n}\Gamma$ cannot be defined.

With the two facts above, every $\mathcal{F}$ has an injective resolution;
