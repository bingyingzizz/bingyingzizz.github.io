## Statement · **draft**
Let $f_n : X \to \mathbb{R}$ all be continuous and suppose $f_n \rightrightarrows f$. Then $f$ is continuous.

In particular: **uniform convergence makes $C^{0}(X)$ closed** (in this sense $(C^{0}, \|\cdot\|_{\infty})$ is complete).
