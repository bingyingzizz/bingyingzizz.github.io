## Notes · **draft**
**Proof.** There are five steps. ① Take the chains whose simplices each lie entirely in $U$ or $V$ to obtain a subcomplex. ② Use barycentric subdivision to prove that the inclusion of this subcomplex into the full complex induces an isomorphism in homology (this is the main difficulty). ③ Express the subcomplex as the sum of the chains in $U$ and the chains in $V$. ④ Obtain a long exact sequence from the short exact sequence. ⑤ Substitute the isomorphism from step ②.
