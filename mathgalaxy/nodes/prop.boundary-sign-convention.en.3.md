# Sign Convention for Boundaries　`prop.boundary-sign-convention`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=prop.boundary-sign-convention)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./prop.boundary-sign-convention.md)

Let $M$ be an oriented $n$-dimensional manifold with boundary and $c : I^{n}\to M$ an orientation-preserving diffeomorphism onto its image — $I^{n}$ is mapped onto that image, which is a region with boundary and is $n$-dimensional, with $I^{n}$ carrying the standard orientation, **and suppose the boundary of the cube lands on $\partial M$**: $c(\partial I^{n}) \subseteq \partial M$. (⚠ This cannot be dropped: take $M = \mathbb{R}$ and $c(t) = t$; then $\partial M = \emptyset$, the "image of $\partial M$" below is empty, and the whole proposition collapses.)
