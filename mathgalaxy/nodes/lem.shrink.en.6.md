# Shrinking Lemma　`lem.shrink`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=lem.shrink)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./lem.shrink.md)

**Why local finiteness is essential**: The limit ordinal step uses the fact that only finitely many members contain $x$. A general open cover need not have this property and may admit no shrinking. This explains why normality does not imply paracompactness.

⭐ There are two uses: turning a locally finite open refinement from paracompactness into a **locally finite closed refinement** by taking closures, and turning a locally finite open refinement into a **partition of unity** by using Urysohn's lemma between $A_{i}$ and $X \setminus U_{i}$ and then normalizing.
