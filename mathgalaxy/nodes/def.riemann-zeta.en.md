## Statement · **draft**
For $\operatorname{Re} s > 1$ define

$$\zeta(s) := \sum_{n=1}^{\infty} \frac{1}{n^{s}} = \sum_{n=1}^{\infty} e^{-s\log n}.$$

This series is **absolutely convergent and locally uniformly convergent** on that half-plane, so $\zeta$ is holomorphic on $\{\operatorname{Re} s > 1\}$.
