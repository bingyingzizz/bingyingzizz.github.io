## Statement · **draft**
Let $N \trianglelefteq G$. The set of all left cosets $\{\, gN : g \in G \,\}$ is denoted $G/N$; define on it

$$(g N) \cdot (g' N) := (g g') N.$$

Normality guarantees that this definition is independent of the choice of representatives, so $G/N$ becomes a group, called the **quotient group**.

The natural map $\pi : G \to G/N$, $g \mapsto gN$, is a surjective homomorphism.
