- (ii) is also the licence for "approximating reals by rationals": every real number is a supremum of rationals.

⚠ Completeness $\implies$ the Archimedean property, but **not conversely**: $\mathbb{Q}$ is Archimedean yet not complete.

⚠ The $q$ squeezed in (ii) must lie **strictly** between the two numbers — this is precisely where "dense" is stronger than "there are rationals arbitrarily close".
