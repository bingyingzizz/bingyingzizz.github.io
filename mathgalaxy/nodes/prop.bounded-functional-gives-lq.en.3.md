# bounded functionals on Lp land in Lq　`prop.bounded-functional-gives-lq`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=prop.bounded-functional-gives-lq)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./prop.bounded-functional-gives-lq.md)

The proof has three steps (see the arrows on the right): Step 1 first shows that $|\int fg| \le M(g)$ also holds when $f$ is measurable with finite support; Step 2 handles $q < \infty$; Step 3 handles $q = \infty$.

⚠ The two hypotheses ($\sigma$-finite / semifinite) are each used once, in Steps 2 and 3, and **cannot be dropped**.
