## Notes · **draft**
⭐ The most common special case: when $f$ is **holomorphic** inside, $P = 0$ and hence $\oint_{\gamma} f'/f = 2\pi i Z$ — "the number of zeros" becomes an integral.

⭐ Geometric reading: $f \circ \gamma$ is a closed curve around $0$, and $Z - P$ is the number of times it winds around $0$. Zeros twist the image curve one turn outward, poles one turn inward.

⚠ There must be no zeros or poles on the boundary — otherwise $f'/f$ is undefined on $\gamma$ and the integral does not exist at all.
