**(ii)** Conversely, if $f$ is real differentiable at $z_0$ and conformal (orientation-preserving) at $z_0$, then $f$ satisfies the Cauchy–Riemann equations at $z_0$, hence is complex differentiable.

⭐ (i) needs one line: $f(z_0 + h) - f(z_0) \approx f'(z_0)\,h$.

Multiplication by the nonzero complex number $f'(z_0) = |f'|e^{i\theta}$ is "rotation by $\theta$ plus dilation $|f'|$" — a similarity, so angles and ratios of lengths are unchanged.
