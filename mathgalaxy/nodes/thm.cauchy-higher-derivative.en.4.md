# Cauchy Integral Formula for Derivatives　`thm.cauchy-higher-derivative`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=thm.cauchy-higher-derivative)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./thm.cauchy-higher-derivative.md)

Taking $|h| \le \delta/2$, where $\delta = \operatorname{dist}(z, \partial\Omega) > 0$, the denominator has a positive lower bound independent of $\zeta$ and $h$, so the modulus of the whole integral is $\le C|h| \to 0$ — the limit exists and equals the formal derivative.

This expression is continuous in $z$, so $f'$ is continuous and one may differentiate again;

for $n \ge 2$ one argues by induction.
