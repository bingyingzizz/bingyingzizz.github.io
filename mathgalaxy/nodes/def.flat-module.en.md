## Statement · **draft**
Let $R$ be a ring and $F$ a **right** $R$-module. $F$ is **flat** if the functor

$$F \otimes_{R} - : R\text{-}\mathbf{Mod} \longrightarrow \mathbf{Ab}$$

is **exact**. Concretely: for every short exact sequence $0 \to M_{1} \to M \to M_{2} \to 0$, after tensoring

$$0 \to F\otimes_{R} M_{1} \to F\otimes_{R} M \to F\otimes_{R} M_{2} \to 0$$
