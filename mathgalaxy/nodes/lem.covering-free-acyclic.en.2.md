# The Free Complex of a Cover Is Acyclic　`lem.covering-free-acyclic`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=lem.covering-free-acyclic)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./lem.covering-free-acyclic.md)

$$X_{n} \;:=\; \coprod_{i_{0}, \ldots, i_{n}} X_{i_{0}} \times_{X} \cdots \times_{X} X_{i_{n}},$$

Then the augmented complex $\mathbb{Z}\cdot X_{\bullet} \to \mathbb{Z}\cdot X$ is **acyclic**:

$$H^{n}\bigl(\mathbb{Z}\cdot X_{\bullet}\bigr) = 0 \qquad (n \ge 0).$$

Here $\mathbb{Z}\cdot X$ is the **free abelian sheaf** generated by $X$.

## Notes · **draft**
⭐ **Use**: it says that $\mathbb{Z}\cdot X_{\bullet} \to \mathbb{Z}\cdot X$ is a **free (hence projective) resolution**.
