Statement

Let XXX be compactly generated and RRR an equivalence relation on XXX (regarded as R⊆X×XR \subseteq X \times XR⊆X×X). Then RRR is kkk-closed   ⟺  \iff⟺ the quotient space X/RX/RX/R is weakly Hausdorff.

Notes

⭐ How to read it: this translates the topological condition "the quotient space is weakly Hausdorff" into the condition "the equivalence relation is kkk-closed", a condition on subsets of X×XX\times XX×X — the latter can be checked on compact pieces.
