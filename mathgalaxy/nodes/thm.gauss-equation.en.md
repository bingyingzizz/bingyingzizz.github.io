## Statement · **draft**
In an adapted frame $e_{3} = n$, split the second structure equation according to $k = 1,2,3$; the $k = 3$ term that remains is the curvature of the surface:

$$\mathrm{d}\omega^{1}_{\ 2} = \omega^{1}_{\ 3}\wedge\omega^{2}_{\ 3}.$$

Define the **Gauss curvature** $K$ by $\mathrm{d}\omega^{1}_{\ 2} = K\,\omega^{1}\wedge\omega^{2}$; then

$$K = \det(c_{ij}) = \det(h_{ij}) = \frac{LN-M^{2}}{EG-F^{2}} .$$
