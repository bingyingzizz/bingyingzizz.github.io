# Goldstine theorem　`thm.goldstine`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=thm.goldstine)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./thm.goldstine.md)

**Separation lemma (the geometric form of Hahn–Banach, derived from `thm.hahn-banach`).** Let $K$ be a closed convex set in a locally convex space and $\xi_0 \notin K$. Then there are a continuous linear functional $\ell$ and a scalar $\alpha$ with $\ell(\xi_0) > \alpha \ge \sup_K \ell$. Take $K - \xi_0$ and its Minkowski functional $p$; on $K - \xi_0$ it is $\le 1$. On the line $\mathbb{R}(\xi_0 - k_0)$, define $\ell_0(t(\xi_0 - k_0)) = t$ (with $k_0 \in K$ arbitrary), check $\ell_0 \le p$, extend to $\ell \le p$ by `thm.hahn-banach`, and take absolute values over $\pm$.
