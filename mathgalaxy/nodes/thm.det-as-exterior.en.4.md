# the determinant is the action on the top exterior power　`thm.det-as-exterior`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=thm.det-as-exterior)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./thm.det-as-exterior.md)

⭐ It also shows that $\det$ is **independent of the basis** (because $\bigwedge^{n}T$ does not depend on a basis), and $\det(AB) = \det A \det B$ (functoriality: $\bigwedge^{n}(AB) = \bigwedge^{n}A \circ \bigwedge^{n}B$, and composition of maps on a one-dimensional space is multiplication) — so multiplicativity of the determinant is a corollary.
