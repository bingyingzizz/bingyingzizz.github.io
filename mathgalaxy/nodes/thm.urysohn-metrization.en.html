Statement

Let XXX be a regular (T1T_{1}T1​) second countable space. Then XXX is metrizable: there is a metric compatible with the topology of XXX.

⚠️ The converse fails: metrizable does not imply second countable — an uncountable discrete space is metrizable (discrete metric) but not second countable. So this theorem is a sufficient condition, not a characterisation.
