Statement

Let VVV be a KKK-vector space, S=(v1,…,vn)S = (v_1, \ldots, v_n)S=(v1​,…,vn​) linearly independent, T=(w1,…,wm)T = (w_1, \ldots, w_m)T=(w1​,…,wm​) generating VVV. Then

  • n≤mn \le mn≤m;
  • one can delete nnn vectors from TTT and insert all vectors of SSS, and the resulting mmm vectors still generate VVV. In particular, VVV has a basis whose elements are taken from T∪ST \cup ST∪S and which contains SSS.

Notes

⭐ In one sentence: "independent" is never larger than "generating".

It is the entire content of the well-definedness of dimension — the two statements (n≤mn \le mn≤m and exchangeability) are proved together by one induction.

⭐ The proof inducts on nnn: express v1v_1v1​ in terms of TTT (TTT generates), and one of the coefficients must be nonzero (otherwise v1=0v_1 = 0v1​=0 contradicts independence); replace it by v1v_1v1​ and the set still generates;

then repeat for the rest.

⚠ Finiteness is a hypothesis: this only covers finite independent and generating sets.

The infinite case needs Zorn's lemma (that is the statement "every vector space has a basis").
