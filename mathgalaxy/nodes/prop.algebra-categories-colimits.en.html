Statement

Let C∈{Mon,Grp,Rng,G-Set,R-Mod,R-Alg}\mathcal C\in\{\mathbf{Mon},\mathbf{Grp},\mathbf{Rng},G\text{-}\mathbf{Set},R\text{-}\mathbf{Mod},R\text{-}\mathbf{Alg}\}C∈{Mon,Grp,Rng,G-Set,R-Mod,R-Alg} (each category with the convention of its own entry). Then C\mathcal CC is cocomplete: the coproducts are respectively free products (Mon,Grp,Rng,R-Alg\mathbf{Mon},\mathbf{Grp},\mathbf{Rng},R\text{-}\mathbf{Alg}Mon,Grp,Rng,R-Alg), disjoint unions (G-SetG\text{-}\mathbf{Set}G-Set) and direct sums (R-ModR\text{-}\mathbf{Mod}R-Mod); the coequalizers are respectively quotient monoids, quotient groups, quotient rings, quotient GGG-sets, quotient modules and quotient algebras (taking the generated congruence or two-sided ideal).

Notes

Proof on the edge @@imp.algebra-categories-colimits@@: write out the universal property of the free construction, then check that the quotient object satisfies the universal property of the coequalizer.

Colimits in Set\mathbf{Set}Set and Top\mathbf{Top}Top are analogous (disjoint unions, quotient topology); this entry lists only algebraic structures, to avoid duplicating @@prop.algebra-categories-limits@@.

The coproduct of commutative RRR-algebras is ⊗R\otimes_R⊗R​; the zero-algebra case follows the convention of @@def.category-of-algebras@@.
