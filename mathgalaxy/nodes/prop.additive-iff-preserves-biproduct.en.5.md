# Additive Functors Preserve Zero Objects and Biproducts　`prop.additive-iff-preserves-biproduct`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=prop.additive-iff-preserves-biproduct)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./prop.additive-iff-preserves-biproduct.md)

⚠️ **The hypothesis "additive category" cannot be removed**: in $\mathbf{Set}$ a functor preserving finite products (such as $X \mapsto X \times X$) need not preserve coproducts, let alone be additive.
⚠️ **"Preserves direct sums" and "preserves coproducts" agree only in an additive category**: so the precise reading of this proposition is "between additive categories, preserving products = preserving coproducts = additive".
