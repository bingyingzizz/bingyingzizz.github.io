## Statement · **draft**
Let $G$ be finite, $\operatorname{char}F=0$, and $F$ algebraically closed. Then $F[G]\cong\prod_{i=1}^s M_{n_i}(F)$ and, as a left $F[G]$-module, $F[G]\cong\bigoplus_{i=1}^s n_iV_i$, where the $V_i$ are pairwise non-isomorphic irreducible representations and $n_i=\dim V_i$.
