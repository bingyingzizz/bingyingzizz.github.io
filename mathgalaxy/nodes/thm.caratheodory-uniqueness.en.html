Key point: the Carathéodory extension is the "largest" one.

The reason is hidden in the definition of μ∗\mu^{*}μ∗ — it is built by "taking infima over coverings", and any extension ν\nuν must be subadditive, so ν(A)≤∑ν(Ej)=∑μ0(Ej)\nu (A) \le \sum \nu (E_{j}) = \sum \mu _{0}(E_{j})ν(A)≤∑ν(Ej​)=∑μ0​(Ej​); taking the infimum over all coverings gives ν≤μ∗\nu \le \mu^{*}ν≤μ∗.
