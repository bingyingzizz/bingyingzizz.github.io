## 陈述（续）
$$c(g_{1}, g_{2}) = g_{1} \cdot u(g_{2}) - u(g_{1} g_{2}) + u(g_{1}) .$$

记全体 $2$-上圈 / $2$-上边界为 $Z^{2}(G, M) / B^{2}(G, M)$，则 $B^{2} \subseteq Z^{2}$，且

$$H^{2}(G, M) = Z^{2}(G, M) \big/ B^{2}(G, M) .$$
