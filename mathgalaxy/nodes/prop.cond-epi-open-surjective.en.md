## Statement · **draft**
Let $f : X \to Y$ be a continuous map. If $Y$ is **weak Hausdorff** and every compact Hausdorff subset $K \subseteq Y$ is contained in some $f(S)$ ($S$ compact Hausdorff), then $f$ is an **epimorphism** in $\mathrm{Cond}$.
