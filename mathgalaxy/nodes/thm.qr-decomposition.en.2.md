where $Q \in M_{m\times n}$ has **orthonormal** columns ($Q^{T}Q = I_{n}$) and $R \in M_{n}(\mathbb{R})$ is **upper triangular** with positive diagonal entries.

**Complex case**: $A = QR$, $Q^{*}Q = I$, and $R$ is upper triangular (with positive real diagonal entries).

**Square case**: When $A$ is invertible, $Q$ is orthogonal (unitary). This gives the factorization of $A$ into ‘orthogonal $\times$ upper triangular’ form, corresponding to the ‘lower triangular $\times$ upper triangular’ factorization in LU.
