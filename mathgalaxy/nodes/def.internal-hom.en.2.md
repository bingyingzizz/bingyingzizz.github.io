Also, for $M, N \in \mathcal{T}(\mathbf{Ab})$, the presheaf $X \mapsto \operatorname{Hom}_{\mathbb{Z}}\bigl(M,\ \operatorname{Hom}(X, N)\bigr)$ is **representable**;

the representing object is denoted $\operatorname{Hom}_{\mathbb{Z}}(M, N) \in \mathcal{T}(\mathbf{Ab})$.

## Notes · **draft**
Thus there is a natural isomorphism

$$\operatorname{Hom}_{\mathbb{Z}}\bigl(M,\ \operatorname{Hom}(X, N)\bigr) \;\cong\; \operatorname{Hom}\bigl(X,\ \operatorname{Hom}_{\mathbb{Z}}(M, N)\bigr) \;\cong\; \operatorname{Hom}_{\mathbb{Z}}(M, N)(X)$$
