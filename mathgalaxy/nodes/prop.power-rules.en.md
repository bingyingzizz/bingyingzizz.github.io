## Statement · **draft**
Let $A$ be a commutative ring, $a, b \in A$, $m, n \in \mathbb{Z}$ (when negative exponents occur, $a, b$ is required to be invertible). Then

$$a^{m+n} = a^{m}\,a^{n}, \qquad (a^{m})^{n} = a^{mn}, \qquad (ab)^{n} = a^{n}\,b^{n}.$$

If $K$ is an ordered field and $a > 1$, then for $m, n \in \mathbb{Z}$:

$$m < n \iff a^{m} < a^{n};$$
