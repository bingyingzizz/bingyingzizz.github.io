## Statement · **draft**
Let $f$ be bounded on $[a, b]$.

- $f$ **continuous** $\implies$ $f$ integrable;
- $f$ **monotone** $\implies$ $f$ integrable;
- $f$ bounded with only **finitely many** discontinuities $\implies$ $f$ integrable.
