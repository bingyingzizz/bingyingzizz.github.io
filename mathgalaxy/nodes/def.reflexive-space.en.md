## Statement · **draft**
Let $X$ be a Banach space and $J_X : X \to X^{**}$ the natural embedding $J_X(x)(\varphi) = \varphi(x)$. Call $X$ **reflexive** if $J_X$ is surjective (hence an isometric isomorphism).
