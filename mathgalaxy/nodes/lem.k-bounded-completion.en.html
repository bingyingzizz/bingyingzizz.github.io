Statement

Let M∙M^{\bullet}M∙ be a complex of seminormed abelian groups, and let M^∙\widehat{M}^{\bullet}M∙ be its Banach completion (the completion after taking the Hausdorff quotient).

Then M∙M^{\bullet}M∙ is boundedly exact at MnM^{n}Mn with bound KKK   ⟺  \iff⟺ M^∙\widehat{M}^{\bullet}M∙ is boundedly exact at M^n\widehat{M}^{n}Mn with bound KKK.

Notes

Purpose: This reduces the general seminormed case to the Banach case. Proving subsequent theorems for Banach groups therefore suffices for the general case.

Why it holds.

Completion embeds MnM^{n}Mn as a dense subspace. The condition of KKK-bounded exactness involves only norm inequalities and an arbitrary choice of ε>0\varepsilon > 0ε>0. Such conditions are preserved in both directions when passing to a dense subspace. Density gives sufficiently good approximations in MnM^{n}Mn, and limits in the complete space can lie back in M^∙\widehat{M}^{\bullet}M∙.
