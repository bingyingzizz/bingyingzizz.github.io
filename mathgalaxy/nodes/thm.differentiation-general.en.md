## Statement · **draft**
Let $f \in L^1_{loc}$ and $x \in L_f$. Then for **every** family $\{E_r\}_{r>0}$ shrinking nicely to $x$:

$$\lim_{r\to0} \frac{1}{m(E_r)} \int_{E_r} |f(y) - f(x)| dy = 0, $$
$$\lim_{r\to0} \frac{1}{m(E_r)} \int_{E_r} f(y) dy = f(x)$$
