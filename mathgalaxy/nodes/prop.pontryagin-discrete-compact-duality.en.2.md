# duality between discrete and compact Hausdorff groups　`prop.pontryagin-discrete-compact-duality`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=prop.pontryagin-discrete-compact-duality)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./prop.pontryagin-discrete-compact-duality.md)

- $M$ is **discrete** $\iff$ $\widehat{M}$ is **compact Hausdorff**;
- $M$ is **compact Hausdorff** $\iff$ $\widehat{M}$ is **discrete**.

Thus the duality functor **exchanges** the categories of discrete abelian groups and compact Hausdorff abelian groups.
