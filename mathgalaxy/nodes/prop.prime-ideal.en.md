## Statement · **draft**
Let $A$ be a commutative ring with unit, $P, M \subset A$ a **proper ideal** (proper; if $P = A$ were allowed, "$A/P$ is an integral domain" would fail; if $M = A$ were allowed, "maximal" would fail).

**(i)** $P$ is called **prime** if $ab \in P \implies a \in P$ or $b \in P$. Equivalently: $A/P$ is an **integral domain**.
