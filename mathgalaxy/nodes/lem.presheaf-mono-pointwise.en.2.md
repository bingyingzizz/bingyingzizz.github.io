## Notes · **draft**
In short, **presheaf properties are checked pointwise**. Everything in $\widehat{\mathcal{C}}$ is defined pointwise, including monomorphisms and epimorphisms.

The ($\Longleftarrow$) direction is immediate because a pointwise injective family is left-cancellable. The ($\Longrightarrow$) direction uses the Yoneda lemma. See the proof on the edge.
