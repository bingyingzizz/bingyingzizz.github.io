# compactness theorem　`thm.compactness`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=thm.compactness)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./thm.compactness.md)

It is **the reason first-order logic cannot express finiteness**: let $\Gamma$ consist of infinitely many sentences saying "field + characteristic 0 + finite". Every finite subset is satisfiable in some finite field, so the whole of $\Gamma$ has a model — an **infinite** "finite field". Likewise the standard model of the natural numbers cannot be characterised by first-order axioms: non-standard analysis is a direct product of this theorem.
