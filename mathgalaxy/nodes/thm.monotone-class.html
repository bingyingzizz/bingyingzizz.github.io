又称单调类定理·monotone class theorem

陈述

设 E⊆P(X)\mathcal{E} \subseteq \mathcal{P}(X)E⊆P(X)。则

M(E)=m(E)  ⟺  ∀A∈E:Ac∈m(E) ∧ ∀A,B∈E:A∪B∈m(E)\mathcal{M}(\mathcal{E}) = \mathfrak{m}(\mathcal{E}) \iff \forall A \in \mathcal{E} : A^c \in \mathfrak{m}(\mathcal{E})\ \wedge\ \forall A, B \in \mathcal{E} : A \cup B \in \mathfrak{m}(\mathcal{E})M(E)=m(E)⟺∀A∈E:Ac∈m(E) ∧ ∀A,B∈E:A∪B∈m(E)

说明

这一条是「标准版 + 加强版」合起来的那一个版本。

标准版（Halmos §6 / Folland 2.37）：A\mathcal{A}A 是代数   ⟹  m(A)=M(A)\implies \mathfrak{m}(\mathcal{A}) = \mathcal{M}(\mathcal{A})⟹m(A)=M(A)。

上面陈述的是加强版：把「E\mathcal{E}E 是代数」换成两条更弱的条件——E\mathcal{E}E 中集合的补与并落进 m(E)\mathfrak{m}(\mathcal{E})m(E) 就行。证明仍是单调类定理那三步：

① 令 K1={A∈m:Ac∈m}\mathcal{K}_{1} = \{A \in \mathfrak{m} : A^{c} \in \mathfrak{m}\}K1​={A∈m:Ac∈m}。它含 E\mathcal{E}E（第一条条件），且本身是单调类（m\mathfrak{m}m 单调   ⟹  \implies⟹ 极限仍在 m\mathfrak{m}m；补用一次递减极限）  ⟹  K1=m\implies \mathcal{K}_{1} = \mathfrak{m}⟹K1​=m，即 m\mathfrak{m}m 对补封闭。

② 固定 A∈EA \in \mathcal{E}A∈E，令 K2={B∈m:A∪B∈m}\mathcal{K}_{2} = \{B \in \mathfrak{m} : A \cup B \in \mathfrak{m}\}K2​={B∈m:A∪B∈m}。由第二条条件 K2⊇E  ⟹  K2=m\mathcal{K}_{2} \supseteq \mathcal{E} \implies \mathcal{K}_{2} = \mathfrak{m}K2​⊇E⟹K2​=m。

③ 固定 B∈mB \in \mathfrak{m}B∈m，令 K3={A∈m:A∪B∈m}\mathcal{K}_{3} = \{A \in \mathfrak{m} : A \cup B \in \mathfrak{m}\}K3​={A∈m:A∪B∈m}。由 ② 知 K3⊇E  ⟹  K3=m\mathcal{K}_{3} \supseteq \mathcal{E} \implies \mathcal{K}_{3} = \mathfrak{m}K3​⊇E⟹K3​=m，即 m\mathfrak{m}m 对并封闭。

于是 m\mathfrak{m}m 对 ∪\cup∪ 与补封闭、又是单调类   ⟹  m\implies \mathfrak{m}⟹m 是一个 σ\sigmaσ代数   ⟹  M(E)⊆m(E)\implies \mathcal{M}(\mathcal{E}) \subseteq \mathfrak{m}(\mathcal{E})⟹M(E)⊆m(E)；反向恒成立，两边相等。（需 E≠∅\mathcal{E} \ne \emptysetE=∅，否则 m(E)={∅}\mathfrak{m}(\mathcal{E}) = \{\emptyset \}m(E)={∅}。）

⚠ 把 ∪\cup∪ 换成 ∩\cap∩ 或 ∖\setminus∖ 结论同样成立 —— 有了补封闭，几种封闭性互相推得出来，所以这一处的符号并不影响定理对不对。

用法（不论哪个版本都一样）：要证某个性质对 M(E)\mathcal{M}(\mathcal{E})M(E) 中所有集合成立，只需（iii）证明它对 E\mathcal{E}E 成立，（ii）证明「成立」这件事被递增并、递减交保持 —— 于是成立的集合构成一个含 E\mathcal{E}E 的单调类，它 ⊇m(E)=M(E)\supseteq \mathfrak{m}(\mathcal{E}) = \mathcal{M}(\mathcal{E})⊇m(E)=M(E)。测度扩张的唯一性、乘积测度的截口公式、可测函数的逼近，用的都是这个模式。

⚠ 与「环 →σ\to \sigma→σ环 的判据」是姊妹结果：那条说环 +++ 单调   ⟹  σ\implies \sigma⟹σ环，这条说代数   ⟹  \implies⟹ 单调类与 σ\sigmaσ代数重合。

Abstract reviewed

For E⊆P(X)\mathcal{E} \subseteq \mathcal{P}(X)E⊆P(X), the σ\sigmaσ-algebra generated by E\mathcal{E}E equals the monotone class generated by E\mathcal{E}E if complements of members of E\mathcal{E}E and unions of two members of E\mathcal{E}E lie in that monotone class. With "E\mathcal{E}E is an algebra" this is the standard monotone class theorem; the form above weakens that hypothesis to the two closure conditions, and the proof is the same two-step argument with the monotone class as the running object.

Statement reviewed

Let E⊆P(X)\mathcal{E} \subseteq \mathcal{P}(X)E⊆P(X). Then

M(E)=m(E)  ⟺  ∀A∈E:Ac∈m(E) ∧ ∀A,B∈E:A∪B∈m(E)\mathcal{M}(\mathcal{E}) = \mathfrak{m}(\mathcal{E}) \iff \forall A \in \mathcal{E} : A^c \in \mathfrak{m}(\mathcal{E})\ \wedge\ \forall A, B \in \mathcal{E} : A \cup B \in \mathfrak{m}(\mathcal{E})M(E)=m(E)⟺∀A∈E:Ac∈m(E) ∧ ∀A,B∈E:A∪B∈m(E)

Notes reviewed

This is the combined "standard + strengthened" version.

Standard version (Halmos §6 / Folland 2.37): if A\mathcal{A}A is an algebra then m(A)=M(A)\mathfrak{m}(\mathcal{A}) = \mathcal{M}(\mathcal{A})m(A)=M(A).

What is stated above is the strengthened version: "mathcalEmathcal{E}mathcalE is an algebra" is replaced by two weaker conditions — the complements and the unions of sets in E\mathcal{E}E only have to land in m(E)\mathfrak{m}(\mathcal{E})m(E). The proof is still the usual three steps of the monotone class theorem:

① Let K1={A∈m:Ac∈m}\mathcal{K}_{1} = \{A \in \mathfrak{m} : A^{c} \in \mathfrak{m}\}K1​={A∈m:Ac∈m}. It contains E\mathcal{E}E (first condition), and is itself a monotone class (m\mathfrak{m}m is monotone   ⟹  \implies⟹ limits stay in m\mathfrak{m}m; complements use one decreasing limit)   ⟹  K1=m\implies \mathcal{K}_{1} = \mathfrak{m}⟹K1​=m, i.e. m\mathfrak{m}m is closed under complements.

② Fix A∈EA \in \mathcal{E}A∈E and let K2={B∈m:A∪B∈m}\mathcal{K}_{2} = \{B \in \mathfrak{m} : A \cup B \in \mathfrak{m}\}K2​={B∈m:A∪B∈m}. By the second condition K2⊇E  ⟹  K2=m\mathcal{K}_{2} \supseteq \mathcal{E} \implies \mathcal{K}_{2} = \mathfrak{m}K2​⊇E⟹K2​=m.

③ Fix B∈mB \in \mathfrak{m}B∈m and let K3={A∈m:A∪B∈m}\mathcal{K}_{3} = \{A \in \mathfrak{m} : A \cup B \in \mathfrak{m}\}K3​={A∈m:A∪B∈m}. By ②, K3⊇E  ⟹  K3=m\mathcal{K}_{3} \supseteq \mathcal{E} \implies \mathcal{K}_{3} = \mathfrak{m}K3​⊇E⟹K3​=m, i.e. m\mathfrak{m}m is closed under unions.

Hence m\mathfrak{m}m is closed under ∪\cup∪ and complements, and is a monotone class   ⟹  \implies⟹ m\mathfrak{m}m is a σ\sigmaσ-algebra   ⟹  M(E)⊆m(E)\implies \mathcal{M}(\mathcal{E}) \subseteq \mathfrak{m}(\mathcal{E})⟹M(E)⊆m(E); the reverse inclusion always holds, so the two sides are equal. (One needs E≠∅\mathcal{E} \ne \emptysetE=∅, otherwise m(E)={∅}\mathfrak{m}(\mathcal{E}) = \{\emptyset \}m(E)={∅}.)

⚠ Replacing ∪\cup∪ by ∩\cap∩ or ∖\setminus∖ gives the same conclusion — once complements are available, the various closure properties imply each other, so the symbol used here does not affect whether the theorem is true.

How it is used (the same for either version): to prove that a property holds for all sets in M(E)\mathcal{M}(\mathcal{E})M(E), it suffices to (iii) prove it for E\mathcal{E}E and (ii) prove that "it holds" is preserved by increasing unions and decreasing intersections — then the sets for which it holds form a monotone class containing E\mathcal{E}E, hence one containing m(E)=M(E)\mathfrak{m}(\mathcal{E}) = \mathcal{M}(\mathcal{E})m(E)=M(E). Uniqueness of the measure extension, the section formula for product measures and the approximation of measurable functions all follow this pattern.

⚠ It is a sister result of "the criterion for a ring to be a σ\sigmaσ-ring": that one says ring +++ monotone   ⟹  \implies⟹ σ\sigmaσ-ring, this one says algebra   ⟹  \implies⟹ monotone class and σ\sigmaσ-algebra coincide.
