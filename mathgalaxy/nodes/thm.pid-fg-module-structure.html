Let RRR be a principal ideal domain and MMM a finitely generated RRR-module. Then there exist r≥0r\ge0r≥0, k≥0k\ge0k≥0, and nonzero non-units d1,…,dk∈Rd_1,\ldots,d_k\in Rd1​,…,dk​∈R satisfying d1∣d2∣⋯∣dkd_1\mid d_2\mid\cdots\mid d_kd1​∣d2​∣⋯∣dk​, such that

Notes draft

Proof: see the edge @@imp.pid-fg-module-structure@@.

When R=K[x]R=K[x]R=K[x] and MMM comes from a linear operator AAA, the free part is zero, did_idi​ are exactly the invariant factors of xI−AxI-AxI−A, and the blocks give the rational canonical form (@@thm.rational-canonical-form@@).

In K[x]K[x]K[x], requiring did_idi​ to be monic makes them unique; in a general PID they are unique only up to units.
