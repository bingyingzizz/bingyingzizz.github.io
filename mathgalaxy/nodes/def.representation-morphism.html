def.representation-morphism · 表示论 · 抽象代数

又称表示同态·intertwiner·缠结算子·A-线性映射

陈述

设 (V,θ)(V,\theta)(V,θ)、(W,φ)(W,\varphi)(W,φ) 是 AAA 的表示。AAA-线性映射（ intertwiner）是 FFF-线性映射 h:V→Wh:V\to Wh:V→W，满足对所有 a∈Aa\in Aa∈A、v∈Vv\in Vv∈V 有 h(θ(a)v)=φ(a)h(v)h(\theta(a)v)=\varphi(a)h(v)h(θ(a)v)=φ(a)h(v)。以这些态射构成范畴 RepA\mathrm{Rep}_ARepA​。

说明

同构恰是双射 intertwiner，其逆仍 AAA-线性。 intertwiner 的核与像都是子表示。 End⁡A(V)=Hom⁡A(V,V)\operatorname{End}_A(V)=\operatorname{Hom}_A(V,V)EndA​(V)=HomA​(V,V) 是 FFF-代数；Schur 引理将对不可约 VVV 描述它。 对 A=F[G]A=F[G]A=F[G]，intertwiner 恰是 GGG-等变映射。

Statement draft

Let (V,θ)(V,\theta)(V,θ) and (W,φ)(W,\varphi)(W,φ) be representations of AAA. An AAA-linear map (an intertwiner) is an FFF-linear map h:V→Wh:V\to Wh:V→W such that h(θ(a)v)=φ(a)h(v)h(\theta(a)v)=\varphi(a)h(v)h(θ(a)v)=φ(a)h(v) for all a∈Aa\in Aa∈A and v∈Vv\in Vv∈V. These morphisms form the category RepA\mathrm{Rep}_ARepA​.

Notes draft

Isomorphisms are exactly bijective intertwiners, and their inverses are again AAA-linear. The kernel and image of an intertwiner are subrepresentations. The space End⁡A(V)=Hom⁡A(V,V)\operatorname{End}_A(V)=\operatorname{Hom}_A(V,V)EndA​(V)=HomA​(V,V) is an FFF-algebra; Schur's lemma describes it for irreducible VVV. For A=F[G]A=F[G]A=F[G], intertwiners are exactly GGG-equivariant maps.
