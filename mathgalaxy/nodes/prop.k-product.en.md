## Statement · **draft**
For any $X, Y \in \mathbf{Top}$,

$$k(kX \times Y) \;=\; k(X \times Y).$$

If $Y$ is also **locally compact Hausdorff**, then the stronger statement holds:

$$kX \times Y \;=\; k(X \times Y).$$
