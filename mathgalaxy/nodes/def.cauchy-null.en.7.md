# Cauchy sequences and null sequences　`def.cauchy-null`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=def.cauchy-null)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./def.cauchy-null.md)

Three facts needed later:
- a Cauchy sequence is **bounded**;
- **sum, difference and product of Cauchy sequences are again Cauchy**;
- **a Cauchy sequence that is not a null sequence satisfies $|x_n| \ge \delta$ from some index on** (for some positive rational $\delta$) — hence its reciprocal, after discarding finitely many terms, is again Cauchy. The third fact is the entire basis for "multiplication has inverses".
