# sieve　`def.sieve`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=def.sieve)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./def.sieve.md)

$$S = \{\, g : Y \to X \ \mid\ \exists i,\ \exists h : Y \to X_{i},\ g = f_{i} \circ h \,\}$$

## Notes · **draft**
Intuition: **a family of "allowed maps" above $X$, and anything that can be composed further on is included too**. So a sieve is not an arbitrary family of arrows: it is **closed under precomposition** — exactly what a subfunctor should be.
