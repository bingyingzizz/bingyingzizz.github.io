## Notes · **draft**
**Standard forms**: A closed orientable surface has a polygonal representation with $4g$ sides, $a_{1}b_{1}a_{1}^{-1}b_{1}^{-1}\cdots a_{g}b_{g}a_{g}^{-1}b_{g}^{-1}$. A closed nonorientable surface has a polygonal representation with $2k$ sides, $c_{1}c_{1}\cdots c_{k}c_{k}$.

**Examples**: The torus is represented by a polygon with $4$ sides, $aba^{-1}b^{-1}$. The projective plane is represented by a polygon with $2$ sides, $aa$. The Klein bottle is represented by a polygon with $4$ sides, $abab^{-1}$.
