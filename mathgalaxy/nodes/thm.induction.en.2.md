## Notes · **draft**
⭐ **The induction principle governs proofs, recursive definition governs constructions** — two sides of the same fact, both coming from "$\omega$ is the smallest inductive set".

Addition, multiplication and order on $\mathbb{N}$, and later the operations on $\mathbb{Z}$ and $\mathbb{Q}$, are all defined recursively and their properties verified by induction.

⚠ **The induction principle holds only for $\omega$**;

for a general well-ordered set one needs **transfinite induction / transfinite recursion**.
