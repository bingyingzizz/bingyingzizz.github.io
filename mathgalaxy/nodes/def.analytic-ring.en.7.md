⚠ Why require **all** such complexes and not just a single module? Because the conclusion is needed at the level of derived categories (`thm.analytic-modules` (ii)); an isomorphism at a single point is not enough — it must hold for complexes as well.

⭐ Deciding "is this an analytic ring" is usually done via concrete theorems such as `prop.analytic-examples` and `thm.za-analytic`, not by checking the definition item by item.
