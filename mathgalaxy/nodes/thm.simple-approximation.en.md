## Statement · **draft**
**(a)** If $f : X \to [0, +\infty]$ is measurable, then there exists a sequence of simple functions $\{\varphi_n\}$ that approaches $f$ **increasingly at every point**:

$$0 \le \varphi_1 \le \varphi_2 \le \cdots \le f, \quad  \varphi_n \to f$$
