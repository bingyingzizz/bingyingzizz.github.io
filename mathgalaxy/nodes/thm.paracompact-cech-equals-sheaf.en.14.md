# Čech Cohomology Equals Sheaf Cohomology on Paracompact Spaces　`thm.paracompact-cech-equals-sheaf`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=thm.paracompact-cech-equals-sheaf)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./thm.paracompact-cech-equals-sheaf.md)

**② Gap: acyclicity of soft sheaves (Godement).** On a paracompact Hausdorff space, a soft sheaf $\mathcal{M}$ satisfies $H^{n}(X,\mathcal{M})=0$ ($n \ge 1$). This does not follow directly from ①. That step only proves that the Čech complex is a resolution, and Čech cohomology for a fixed cover can differ from derived functor cohomology (see the counterexample in ④). There are two standard approaches. For $n=1$, use the fact that softness makes the global sections of $0 \to \mathcal{M} \to \mathcal{G} \to \mathcal{H} \to 0$ exact
