## Notes · **draft**
"Separates points" is a necessary condition for Stone–Weierstrass: if two points are not separated, glue them into one, and $A$ cannot generate the functions on the glued space, $C$.

⚠️ The example of self-adjoint matrices (from the lectures): self-adjoint matrices ordered by $A \ge B \iff A - B$ being positive semidefinite do not in general form a **lattice** (the pointwise min of two self-adjoint matrices need not be self-adjoint). So "lattice" is not automatic; it must be bought by "closed + contains the unit".
