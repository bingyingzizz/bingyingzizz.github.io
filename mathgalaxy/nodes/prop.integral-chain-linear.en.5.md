**⚠️ Coefficient field**: the coefficients of a chain lie in $\mathbb{Z}$ (`def.singular-chain`), those of a form lie in $\mathbb{R}$. Here $a,b$ are integers in $\mathbb{Z}$ (an integral linear combination of chains is still a chain); if real coefficients are needed, one must first write the chain group as $C_{k}(M;\mathbb{R})$.
