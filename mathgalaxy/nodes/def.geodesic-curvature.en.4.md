# Geodesic Curvature　`def.geodesic-curvature`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=def.geodesic-curvature)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./def.geodesic-curvature.md)

**On the plane**: $\nabla$ is the ordinary derivative, $\vec\kappa_{g} = \ddot\gamma$, and $k_{g}$ reduces to the signed curvature $\kappa$ (for a circle of radius $r$: $k_{g} = \pm 1/r$). On a surface, $k_{g}$ is the curvature seen after "flattening the surface along the curve".

**Relation to parallel transport**: $\vec\kappa_{g}$ measures "how fast the tangent vector deviates from parallel transport"; $\vec\kappa_{g}\equiv0$ means "the tangent vector is parallel to itself", that is, a geodesic.
