## Notes · **draft**
⭐ **It guarantees that projective resolutions exist**: starting from $P_{0} \twoheadrightarrow X$, cover the kernel $\ker(P_{0} \to X)$ by another projective object, and so on recursively, obtaining a **projective resolution**

$$\cdots \to P_{2} \to P_{1} \to P_{0} \to X \to 0,$$

Then one can take left derived functors ($\operatorname{Tor}$, $L^{n}F$). Dually, enough injectives gives **injective resolutions** and right derived functors ($\operatorname{Ext}^{n}$, $R^{n}F$).
