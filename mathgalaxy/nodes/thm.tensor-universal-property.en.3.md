## Notes · **draft**
⭐ In one sentence: **the tensor product is the linearization device for "multivariable linearity"** — everything bilinear, once written as a map on $V \otimes W$, automatically becomes linear, and the whole machinery of linear algebra applies.

⭐ "Uniqueness" is the core of a universal property: it ensures $V \otimes W$ is determined **up to unique isomorphism** (existence gives the construction, uniqueness gives "independence of the construction").
