# Flatness and the Vanishing of Tor　`prop.tor-flat-vanishing`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=prop.tor-flat-vanishing)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./prop.tor-flat-vanishing.md)

⭐ **This gives a condition for flatness that can be checked one case at a time**. Instead of examining all injections, compute, for each $N$, the group $\operatorname{Tor}_{1}$. In practice this is often used with Lambek's criterion, which requires testing only finitely generated ideals.

📌 **Corollaries**: **Direct summands** of flat modules are flat ($\operatorname{Tor}$ commutes with direct sums), and direct sums of flat modules are **flat**. Projective modules are flat (vanishing in higher degrees on the $\operatorname{Ext}$ side is the dual manifestation of the same phenomenon).
