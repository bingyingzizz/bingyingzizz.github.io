## Notes · **stale**
Decompose the regular module $A$ into simple left ideals and group them by isomorphism class to obtain two-sided ideals $A_i$ with $A=\prod A_i$. Each $A_i$ is simple, so the Wedderburn theorem for simple algebras applies. Conversely, a product of matrix algebras is semisimple. The $n_i$ and $D_i$ are unique up to isomorphism and permutation.
