Since $A$ is semisimple, the left regular module decomposes as $A=\bigoplus_i S_i^{\oplus n_i}$, with the $S_i$ pairwise non-isomorphic simple left modules.
Right multiplication gives $A^{\mathrm{op}}\cong\operatorname{End}_A(A)$: $a\mapsto R_a$, $R_a(x)=xa$; the inverse is $f\mapsto f(1)$.
