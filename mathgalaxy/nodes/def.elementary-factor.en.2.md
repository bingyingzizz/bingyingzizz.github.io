## Notes · **draft**
**Why $E_{p}$ is needed**: one wants to build zeros $a_{n}$ from a product $\prod (1 - z/a_{n})$, but an infinite product need not converge. Replacing each factor by $E_{p}(z/a_{n})$ adds an exponential factor that "cancels" the divergence — and an exponential factor is never zero, so the zero set is unchanged.

⭐ **Quantitative estimate (the one formula to remember)**: for $|z| \le 1$ and $p \ge 1$,

$$|1 - E_{p}(z)| \le |z|^{p+1}.$$
