$$E^{p,q}_{1} = \bigoplus_{i=1}^{r_{p}} H^{q}\bigl(M^{s_{p,i}},\ N\bigr) \implies \operatorname{Ext}^{p+q}_{\mathbb{Z}}(M, N).$$
