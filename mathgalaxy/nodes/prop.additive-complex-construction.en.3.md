## Notes · **draft**
**Proof.** The data of a complex consist only of "a family of objects + a family of morphisms + $d \circ d = 0$" ✓, all of it in the language of additive categories: an additive functor preserves zero morphisms, composites and addition, hence $F(d^{n+1}) \circ F(d^{n}) = F(d^{n+1} \circ d^{n}) = 0$ ✓, and $C(F)$ is a functor ($F$ preserves commutative diagrams) ✓. ∎
