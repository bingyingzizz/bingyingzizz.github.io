Notes

The crux is the fiber product M×s,tMM \times_{s,t} MM×s,t​M: composition is not an operation on M×MM \times MM×M, but is defined only on some pairs of morphisms. Composition in a category is thus a partial operation, and "whether f∘gf \circ gf∘g is defined" is itself something to check.
