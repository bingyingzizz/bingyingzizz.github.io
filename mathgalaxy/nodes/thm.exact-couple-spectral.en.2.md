2. if for every $(p, q)$ the terms $D_{r}^{p,q}$ and $E_{r}^{p,q}$ **stabilize** for $r \gg 0$ (automatic when the filtration is bounded), then, writing $D_{\infty}^{p,q} := \bigcap_{r} D_{r}^{p,q}$,

$$E_{\infty}^{p,q} \;\cong\; D_{\infty}^{p,q} \big/ D_{\infty}^{p+1,\, q-1} ,$$

that is, "$E_{\infty}$ is the associated graded of the limiting couple $D_{\infty}$".
