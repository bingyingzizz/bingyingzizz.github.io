# condensed abelian groups satisfy AB6 and AB4*　`thm.condab-ab`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=thm.condab-ab)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./thm.condab-ab.md)

The Grothendieck category assertion holds for **every** topos (see “abelian groups in a topos form a Grothendieck category”). The additional properties here are **AB6 and AB4\***, which need not both hold for abelian groups in a general topos.

As a result, the homological algebra of $\mathrm{CondAb}$ works as it does in $\mathbf{Ab}$, including derived functors, long exact sequences, and derived categories.
