# Pontryagin-van Kampen　`thm.pontryagin-van-kampen`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=thm.pontryagin-van-kampen)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./thm.pontryagin-van-kampen.md)

The equivalence preserves structure, so one can solve a group-theoretic problem on the dual side and translate the result back.

**Some standard correspondences**: $\widehat{\mathbb{R}} \cong \mathbb{R}$ (Fourier transform), and $\widehat{\mathbb{Z}} \cong \mathbb{T}$ and $\widehat{\mathbb{T}} \cong \mathbb{Z}$ (interchanged).

For finite-dimensional real Banach spaces, the dual reduces to the usual linear dual (a real Banach space is locally compact $\iff$ it is finite-dimensional).
