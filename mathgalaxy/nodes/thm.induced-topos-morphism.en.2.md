# Every Functor Induces a Topos Morphism　`thm.induced-topos-morphism`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=thm.induced-topos-morphism)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./thm.induced-topos-morphism.md)

**has both a left adjoint $\hat{g}_{!}$ and a right adjoint $\hat{g}_{*}$**. In particular $\hat{g}^{-1}$ is exact, so

$$\hat{g} : \widehat{\mathcal{C}} \longrightarrow \widehat{\mathcal{C}'}, \qquad \hat{g}^{*} := \hat{g}^{-1} \ \dashv\ \hat{g}_{*}$$

is a morphism of toposes. That is, **any functor induces a morphism between presheaf categories**, in the direction opposite to $g$.
