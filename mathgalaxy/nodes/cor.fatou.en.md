## Statement · **draft**
Let $\{f_n\} \subseteq L^+$, $f \in L^+$, and $f_n \to f$ a.e. Then

$$\int f \le \liminf_{n\to\infty} \int f_n$$

## Notes · **draft**
When convergence holds a.e., $\liminf f_n = f$, and Fatou's lemma applies directly.
