Statement

Let AAA be a subset of a metric space XXX (with the subspace metric). If AAA is compact, then AAA is closed in XXX.

Notes

In fact it suffices that XXX be Hausdorff: compact subsets are then closed, and metric spaces are Hausdorff.

Putting this together with “XXX is complete” prepares the subset characterisation of compactness below: that result uses “AAA compact   ⟹  A\implies A⟹A closed” and “AAA closed XXX complete   ⟹  A\implies A⟹A complete”.
