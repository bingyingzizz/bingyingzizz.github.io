# separable Hilbert spaces are l2　`thm.separable-hilbert-is-l2`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=thm.separable-hilbert-is-l2)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./thm.separable-hilbert-is-l2.md)

By Bessel it lands in $\ell^2$, and by Parseval it preserves the norm;

surjectivity: if $(\lambda_i) \in \ell^2$, then the partial sums $\sum_{i \in J}\lambda_i e_i$ are Cauchy ($\|\sum_{J \setminus J'}\lambda_i e_i\|^2 = \sum_{J \setminus J'}|\lambda_i|^2$), and the limit has coordinates $\lambda_i$.
