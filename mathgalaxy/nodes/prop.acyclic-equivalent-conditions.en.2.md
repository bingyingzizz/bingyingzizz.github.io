# Equivalent Conditions for Acyclicity　`prop.acyclic-equivalent-conditions`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=prop.acyclic-equivalent-conditions)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./prop.acyclic-equivalent-conditions.md)

1. $M$ is **acyclic**: $H^{n}(M) = 0$ ($n \ne 0$);
2. for every $X \in \mathcal{C}$: $H^{n}(X, M) = 0$ ($n \ne 0$);
3. for every cover family $\mathfrak{X}$: $\check{H}^{n}(\mathfrak{X}, M) = 0$ ($n \ne 0$);
4. for every $X \in \mathcal{C}$: $\check{H}^{n}(X, M) = 0$ ($n \ne 0$).
