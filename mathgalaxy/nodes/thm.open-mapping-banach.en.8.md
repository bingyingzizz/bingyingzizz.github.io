**Step 3: finish.** Take any open set $\Omega \subseteq E$ and $x \in \Omega$; choose $\rho > 0$ with $B(x, \rho) \subseteq \Omega$. By homogeneity $u(B(x,\rho)) = u(x) + \rho\, u(B_E) \supseteq u(x) + \rho r B_F$ is a neighbourhood of $u(x)$ contained in $u(\Omega)$. Hence $u(\Omega)$ is open. $\square$
