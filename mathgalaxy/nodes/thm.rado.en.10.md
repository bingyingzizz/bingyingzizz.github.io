# Rado theorem: every compact surface is triangulable　`thm.rado`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=thm.rado)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./thm.rado.md)

**Why this theorem concerns dimension 2.** $\dim\le 2$: Radó (1925). $\dim=3$: Moise (1952). $\dim=4$: Freedman (1982, topological manifolds admit triangulations, with $E_{8}$ counterexamples in the smooth/PL case in the same dimension). $\dim\ge 5$: Kirby–Siebenmann (1969), who showed that some compact topological manifolds are not triangulable. Thus triangulability of all compact manifolds holds only in low dimensions, and the surface theorem does not follow from a general result of this form.
