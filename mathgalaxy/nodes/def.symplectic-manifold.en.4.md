## Notes · **draft**
⚠️ **Closedness is essential**: Nondegenerate 2-forms exist pointwise (the cotangent bundle provides an example), whereas closedness is a condition on the form **as a whole**. Dropping it gives an almost symplectic manifold, which carries a nondegenerate 2-form with no closedness requirement (a different theory). ⚠ Integrability is a property of an almost complex structure. It requires an additional almost complex structure and plays no role in the definition of an almost symplectic manifold. $S^{6}$ is a standard example of an almost symplectic manifold that is not symplectic.
