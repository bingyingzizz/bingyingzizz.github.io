## Notes · **draft**
Proof: by the projection onto a closed convex set. For (1), the step "$\implies$" replaces $z$ by $\pm z$, kills the real part, and gives $\operatorname{Re}\langle x - y, z\rangle \le 0$ for all $z \in E$, i.e. $x - y \perp E$;

the step "$\Longleftarrow$" uses $\|x - z\|^2 = \|x - y\|^2 + \|y - z\|^2 \ge \|x - y\|^2$.

(2) uses $\alpha_1 x_1 + \alpha_2 x_2 - \alpha_1 P x_1 - \alpha_2 P x_2 \perp E$.

(3) is Pythagoras.
