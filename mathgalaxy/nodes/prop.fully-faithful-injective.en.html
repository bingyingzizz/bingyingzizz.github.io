Statement

If F:C→DF : \mathcal{C} \to \mathcal{D}F:C→D is fully faithful, then it is essentially injective:

F(X)≅F(Y)  ⟹  X≅Y.F(X) \cong F(Y) \implies X \cong Y.F(X)≅F(Y)⟹X≅Y.
