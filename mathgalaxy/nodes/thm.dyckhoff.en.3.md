# Dyckhoff　`thm.dyckhoff`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=thm.dyckhoff)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./thm.dyckhoff.md)

The key computation is $R^{q}c_{*}\underline{M} = 0$ ($q > 0$), given by a computation on **stalks**: after passing to stalks, $M$ is discrete and carries no higher information.

The spectral sequence degenerates and the two sides are isomorphic.

**Why "coefficients discrete" is required.** Once the coefficients carry a topology (say a real Banach space), $R^{q}c_{*}$ no longer vanishes in general and the two sides fail to agree — this is precisely the situation §8.2 has to handle.
