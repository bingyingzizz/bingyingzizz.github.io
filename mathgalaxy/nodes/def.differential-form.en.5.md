# Differential Form: Coordinate Model on Euclidean Open Sets　`def.differential-form`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=def.differential-form)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./def.differential-form.md)

**Difference from a general multilinear form**: a general $k$-linear form has $n^{k}$ components; after imposing alternation only $\binom{n}{k}$ remain — exactly why it can be integrated (`def.integral-form`).

**The everyday identity of low-degree forms** ($n = 3$): a $0$-form is a function; a $1$-form $P\,\mathrm{d}x+Q\,\mathrm{d}y+R\,\mathrm{d}z$ is work and circulation; a $2$-form is flux; a $3$-form is density. Gradient, curl, and divergence are the same $\mathrm{d}$ at different degrees.
