# spectral theorem for complex normal operators　`cor.spectral-normal`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=cor.spectral-normal)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./cor.spectral-normal.md)

- $T$ is self-adjoint $\iff$ all eigenvalues are **real**;
- $T$ is unitary $\iff$ all eigenvalues have **modulus 1**;
- $T$ is an orthogonal projection $\iff$ the eigenvalues all lie in $\{0, 1\}$ (under the hypothesis that $T$ is normal one does **not** need to require $T$ self-adjoint again: a normal operator whose spectrum lies in $\mathbb{R}$ is automatically self-adjoint).
