# Cauchy Integral Formula　`thm.cauchy-integral-formula`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=thm.cauchy-integral-formula)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./thm.cauchy-integral-formula.md)

**The second term vanishes as $r \to 0$**: $f$ is holomorphic at $z$, so $\lim_{\zeta \to z}\frac{f(\zeta) - f(z)}{\zeta - z} = f'(z)$; in particular there are $M$ and $\rho > 0$ such that for $|\zeta - z| < \rho$ the quotient is bounded by $\le M$. Hence for $0 < r < \min(\rho, d)$, the ML inequality gives $\left|\oint_{|\zeta - z| = r} \frac{f(\zeta) - f(z)}{\zeta - z}\,\mathrm{d}\zeta\right| \le M\cdot 2\pi r \longrightarrow 0.$.
