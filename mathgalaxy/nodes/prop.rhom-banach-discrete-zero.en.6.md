# Derived Hom from Finite-Dimensional Banach Spaces to Discrete Groups Vanishes　`prop.rhom-banach-discrete-zero`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=prop.rhom-banach-discrete-zero)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./prop.rhom-banach-discrete-zero.md)

- $M^{s_{p,i}}$ is a (possibly large) power of a finite-dimensional real vector space, **convex $\implies$ contractible**;
- and $M^{s_{p,i}}\times S \to S$ is a homotopy equivalence, with $M^{s_{p,i}}\times S$ **locally compact Hausdorff** and $N$ **discrete**, so Dyckhoff (locally compact with discrete coefficients: condensed = sheaf cohomology) and "sheaf cohomology is a homotopy invariant" give

$$H^{q}\bigl(M^{s_{p,i}}\times S,\ N\bigr) \cong H^{q}(S,\ N).$$

- The right side **involves no $M$ at all**.
