# surfaces of constant Gauss curvature　`thm.constant-gauss-curvature`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=thm.constant-gauss-curvature)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./thm.constant-gauss-curvature.md)

- $K = 0$: $f = r$, i.e. $g = \mathrm{d}r^{2} + r^{2}\mathrm{d}\theta^{2}$ (**Euclidean metric**);
- $K > 0$: $f = \frac{1}{\sqrt K}\sin(\sqrt K\, r)$ (**spherical metric**);
- $K < 0$: $f = \frac{1}{\sqrt{-K}}\sinh(\sqrt{-K}\, r)$ (**hyperbolic metric**).

**Theorem**: a surface of constant Gauss curvature is **locally** isometric to one of the plane, the sphere or the hyperbolic plane.

**Corollary**: if it is simply connected and complete, only the plane, the sphere and the hyperbolic plane occur.
