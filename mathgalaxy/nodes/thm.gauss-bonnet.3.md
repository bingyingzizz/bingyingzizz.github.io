## English abstract · **draft** · machine-drafted, pending author review; Chinese is authoritative（续）
For a compact orientable surface $M\subseteq\mathbb{R}^{3}$, possibly with piecewise smooth boundary, $\int_{M}K\,\mathrm{d}A + \int_{\partial M}k_{g}\,\mathrm{d}s = 2\pi\chi(M)$, where $K$ is the Gaussian curvature, $k_{g}$ the geodesic curvature and $\chi$ the Euler characteristic. In particular a closed surface satisfies $\int_{M}K\,\mathrm{d}A = 2\pi\chi(M)$, so total curvature is a topological invariant: $4\pi$ for the sphere, $0$ for the torus and $4\pi(1-g)$ in genus $g$.
