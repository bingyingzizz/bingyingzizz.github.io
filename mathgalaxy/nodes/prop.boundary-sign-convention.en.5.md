## Notes · **draft**
**Why this matters**: the manifold version of Stokes' theorem uses the geometric boundary (induced orientation), while the chain version (`thm.stokes-chain`) uses the algebraic boundary ($\partial c$). That both can be written in one formula is entirely due to this proposition. It is also the true source of the "outward normal + induced orientation" sign in the divergence theorem.
