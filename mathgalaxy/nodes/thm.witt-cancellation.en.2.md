# Witt Cancellation Theorem　`thm.witt-cancellation`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=thm.witt-cancellation)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./thm.witt-cancellation.md)

Let $V$ be a **finite-dimensional** vector space, $\beta$ a **nondegenerate** symmetric bilinear form over a field $K$ ($\operatorname{char} K \ne 2$), and $U, W \subseteq V$ a **nondegenerate** subspace. If there is an isometry (a linear isomorphism preserving $\beta$)

$$U \perp W \ \cong\ U' \perp W ,$$

then $U \cong U'$ (as spaces with forms).

**Corollary (unique decomposition)**: a nondegenerate form always splits orthogonally into several hyperbolic planes plus one **anisotropic** piece, and both the number of hyperbolic planes (the Witt index) and the anisotropic part are uniquely determined.
