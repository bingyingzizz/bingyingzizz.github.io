## Statement · **draft**
Let $I$ be a **small category** (its objects and morphisms form a small set). A **diagram in $\mathcal{C}$ indexed by $I$** is a functor

$$D : I \longrightarrow \mathcal{C}$$

All such diagrams, with natural transformations as morphisms, form the category

$$\mathcal{C}^{I} := \operatorname{Hom}(I, \mathcal{C})$$
