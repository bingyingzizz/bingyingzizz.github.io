陈述

若 DDD 是可除群、H⊆DH \subseteq DH⊆D 是子群，则 D/HD/HD/H 可除。

（同一条论证对任意环上的可除模成立；在 Z\mathbb{Z}Z 上「可除」即「内射」，所以这同时说明 Z\mathbb{Z}Z-模里内射对象的商仍内射。）

说明

证明：记 π:D→D/H\pi : D \to D/Hπ:D→D/H。

给定 xˉ∈D/H\bar{x} \in D/Hxˉ∈D/H 与 n≠0n \ne 0n=0，取代表 xxx；由 DDD 可除取 yyy 使 ny=xn y = xny=x；则 nyˉ=ny‾=xˉn \bar{y} = \overline{n y} = \bar{x}nyˉ​=ny​=xˉ ✓。

所以 n⋅n \cdotn⋅ 在 D/HD/HD/H 上满，D/HD/HD/H 可除 ✓。

∎

⚠️ 只对商成立：可除群的子群不必可除（Z⊆Q\mathbb{Z} \subseteq \mathbb{Q}Z⊆Q：Q\mathbb{Q}Q 可除，Z\mathbb{Z}Z 不可除）。

⭐ 两条对照：在 Z\mathbb{Z}Z-模里，内射对象的商仍内射（本条），而它的子模不必（Z⊆Q\mathbb{Z} \subseteq \mathbb{Q}Z⊆Q）。

投射模则两头都不省心：它的商（Z/2\mathbb{Z}/2Z/2 之于 Z\mathbb{Z}Z）与子模（理想 (x,y)⊆k[x,y](x, y) \subseteq k[x, y](x,y)⊆k[x,y]）都可以不投射。

📌 一般环上要小心：可除性对商总是封闭的（逐元素论证），但「内射」对商封闭只在 Z\mathbb{Z}Z 这类主理想整环上由「可除即内射」推得，不能当成普遍事实。

Statement draft

If DDD is a divisible group and H⊆DH \subseteq DH⊆D is a subgroup, then D/HD/HD/H is divisible.

(The same argument works for divisible modules over any ring; over Z\mathbb{Z}Z, "divisible" is "injective", so this also shows that a quotient of an injective Z\mathbb{Z}Z-module is still injective.)

Notes draft

Proof: write π:D→D/H\pi : D \to D/Hπ:D→D/H.

Given xˉ∈D/H\bar{x} \in D/Hxˉ∈D/H and n≠0n \ne 0n=0, choose a representative xxx; since DDD is divisible there is yyy with ny=xn y = xny=x; then nyˉ=ny‾=xˉn \bar{y} = \overline{n y} = \bar{x}nyˉ​=ny​=xˉ ✓.

Hence multiplication by n⋅n \cdotn⋅ is surjective on D/HD/HD/H, so D/HD/HD/H is divisible ✓.

∎

⚠️ This holds only for quotients: a subgroup of a divisible group need not be divisible (Z⊆Q\mathbb{Z} \subseteq \mathbb{Q}Z⊆Q: Q\mathbb{Q}Q is divisible, Z\mathbb{Z}Z is not).

⭐ Two contrasts: among Z\mathbb{Z}Z-modules the quotient of an injective object is still injective (this item), while its submodule need not be (Z⊆Q\mathbb{Z} \subseteq \mathbb{Q}Z⊆Q).

Projective modules behave badly on both sides: both its quotient (Z/2\mathbb{Z}/2Z/2 of Z\mathbb{Z}Z) and its submodule (the ideal (x,y)⊆k[x,y](x, y) \subseteq k[x, y](x,y)⊆k[x,y]) can fail to be projective.

📌 Over a general ring be careful: divisibility is always closed under quotients (the elementwise argument), but "injective is closed under quotients" follows from "divisible = injective" only over a PID such as Z\mathbb{Z}Z, and must not be taken as a general fact.
