# entire and meromorphic functions　`def.entire-meromorphic`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=def.entire-meromorphic)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./def.entire-meromorphic.md)

📌 The Laurent expansion at a pole has only finitely many negative-power terms; near a pole a meromorphic function is "a blow-up of finite order". Entire functions are the special case $P = \emptyset$.
