## Notes · **draft**
The reason is that sections of a sheaf at $F$ are computed pointwise, and in $\mathbf{Ab}$ finite products agree with finite coproducts and filtered colimits are exact.

This is **how properties of $\mathrm{CondAb}$ are reduced pointwise to properties of $\mathbf{Ab}$**. The result on the AB axioms below uses this reduction.
