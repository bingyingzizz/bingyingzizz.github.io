# Weingarten Map (Shape Operator)　`def.weingarten-map`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=def.weingarten-map)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./def.weingarten-map.md)

**Where self-adjointness comes from**: the coordinate route uses $r_{uv} = r_{vu}$ (equality of mixed partial derivatives); the frame route uses $\mathrm{d}\omega^{3} = 0$ (`lem.second-form-symmetric` and the incoming edge). The latter is cheaper and does not depend on a special choice of parameter net.

⚠ **Sign**: taking $W := -\mathrm{d}n$ is chosen so that the principal curvatures of the sphere with the $+$ outward normal are negative (consistent with do Carmo).

If one takes $W := \mathrm{d}n$, then $II$ must change sign as well, otherwise "principal curvature $=$ eigenvalue of $W$" fails.
