## Notes · **draft**
⭐ **It is the common name for one concept across the branches** (previously only the three-in-one `def.mono` of category theory existed):

- sets / $\mathbf{Set}$: isomorphism = **bijection**;

- groups, rings, modules: isomorphism = bijective homomorphism;

- topology / $\mathbf{Top}$: isomorphism = **homeomorphism** (`def.homeomorphism`);

- vector spaces: isomorphism = **linear isomorphism** (`def.linear-isomorphism`);

- posets: isomorphism = **order isomorphism** (`def.order-iso`);
