# Alternating Sum of a Simplicial Object　`prop.simplicial-to-chain`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=prop.simplicial-to-chain)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./prop.simplicial-to-chain.md)

$$d_{n} := \sum_{i=0}^{n} (-1)^{i} d^{n}_{i} : K_{n} \longrightarrow K_{n-1}.$$

Then $d_{n-1} \circ d_{n} = 0$, so $K_{\bullet}$ is a **chain complex**.

## Notes · **draft**
⭐ This is the entry point of the **simplicial method**: turning a (semi)simplicial object into a complex by the alternating sum lets one compute its invariants with homological algebra.
