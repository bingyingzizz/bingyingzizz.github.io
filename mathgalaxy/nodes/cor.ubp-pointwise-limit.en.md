## Statement · **draft**
Let $E$ be a Banach space, $F$ a normed space, $u_n \in B(E, F)$, and suppose that for every $x \in E$ we have $u_n x \to u x$. Then $u \in B(E, F)$, and

$$\|u\| \le \liminf_{n \to \infty} \|u_n\| .$$
