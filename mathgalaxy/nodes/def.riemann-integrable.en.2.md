# Riemann Integrable Function　`def.riemann-integrable`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=def.riemann-integrable)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./def.riemann-integrable.md)

holds for **every** partition $P$ and **every** choice of tags $\xi$. Then $I$ is unique and is written

$$I = \int_a^b f = \int_a^b f(x)\,dx$$

with the conventions $\int_b^a f := -\int_a^b f$ and $\int_a^a f := 0$.

## Notes · **draft**
⭐ The two words most easily missed in the definition: **every** and **norm**.

It is not “there is a sequence of partitions whose sums converge”, but “**all** partitions of sufficiently small norm, with **all** tags, land near $I$”.

⭐ Equivalent formulation (Darboux form, see “Darboux criterion”): $\sup_P L(f, P) = \inf_P U(f, P)$.
