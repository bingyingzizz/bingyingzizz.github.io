# topological orientability　`def.topological-orientability`

[in graph](../#n=def.topological-orientability)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./def.topological-orientability.md)

⚠ **Two common points of confusion.** (1) Nonorientable surfaces can be triangulable. Radó's theorem gives triangulations of all compact surfaces, and $\mathbb{RP}^{2}$ has one obtained by identifying antipodal points of a regular icosahedron. (2) The orientation double cover is a free two-sheeted covering without branch points. The local model $z\mapsto z^{2}$ with branching belongs to the branched covering associated with a meromorphic function (the complex-analytic approach in `thm.rado`).
