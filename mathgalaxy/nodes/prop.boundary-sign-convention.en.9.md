# Sign Convention for Boundaries　`prop.boundary-sign-convention`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=prop.boundary-sign-convention)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./prop.boundary-sign-convention.md)

**⚠ Orientation preservation cannot be dropped**: if $c$ reverses orientation, every face gains an extra minus sign and the conclusion flips sign. Hence the hypothesis is "$c$ orientation-preserving", not "$c$ is a diffeomorphism".
