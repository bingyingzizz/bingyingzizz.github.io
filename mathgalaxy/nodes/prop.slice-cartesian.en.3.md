# slice categories are pullbacks　`prop.slice-cartesian`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=prop.slice-cartesian)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./prop.slice-cartesian.md)

A special case illustrates this. When $T = h_{X}$ (the representable case), the square gives $\mathcal{C}/X \simeq \widehat{\mathcal{C}}/h_{X}$ — taking the slice in $\mathcal{C}$ over $X$ is the same as taking the slice in the category of presheaves over $h_{X}$.
