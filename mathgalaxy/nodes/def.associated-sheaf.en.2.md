## Notes · **draft**
**Where this isomorphism comes from.** Sheafification $(-)^{\#}$ and inclusion $\widehat{\mathcal{C}}^{\#} \hookrightarrow \widehat{\mathcal{C}}$ are adjoint, so

$$\operatorname{Hom}\bigl(h_{X}^{\#},\ F\bigr) \cong \operatorname{Hom}(h_{X}, F) \cong F(X),$$

the last step being Yoneda's lemma ✓.
