## Notes · **draft**
The supremum is attained (compactness plus continuity). This metric is the metric of "uniform convergence", not of "pointwise convergence" (the same convention as `def.uniform-convergence`).

⭐ The notation allows an arbitrary metric space $E$, so the results in this cluster (completeness, Arzelà–Ascoli) are broader than the classical $\mathbb{R}$-valued versions.
