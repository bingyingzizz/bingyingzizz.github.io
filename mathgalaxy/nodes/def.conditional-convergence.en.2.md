## Notes · **draft**
⭐ A conditionally convergent series is **fragile**: Riemann's rearrangement theorem says its terms can be reordered to converge to **any** prescribed value (even to diverge to $\pm\infty$).

So any "interchange of order" must first be justified.

⚠ The alternating harmonic series converges to $\ln 2$, but after the rearrangement "one positive term, two negative terms" it converges to $\frac{1}{2}\ln 2$ — the same series, the same terms, a different sum.
