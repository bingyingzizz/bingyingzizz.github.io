## Statement · **draft**
A category $\mathcal C$ is called **cartesian closed** if it has finite products (a terminal object and binary products) and, for every $X$, the functor $(-)\times X:\mathcal C\to\mathcal C$ has a right adjoint $(-)^X$. Equivalently, there is an evaluation morphism $\mathrm{ev}:Y^X\times X\to Y$ and a natural bijection $\hom_{\mathcal C}(Z\times X,Y)\cong\hom_{\mathcal C}(Z,Y^X)$, called currying.
