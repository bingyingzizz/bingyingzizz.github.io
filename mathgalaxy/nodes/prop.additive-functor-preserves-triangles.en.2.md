# Additive Functors Preserve Distinguished Triangles　`prop.additive-functor-preserves-triangles`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=prop.additive-functor-preserves-triangles)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./prop.additive-functor-preserves-triangles.md)

$$K^{\bullet} \longrightarrow L^{\bullet} \longrightarrow M^{\bullet} \longrightarrow K^{\bullet}[1]$$

is a **derived triangle** in $\mathbf{K}(\mathcal{C})$, then

$$FK^{\bullet} \longrightarrow FL^{\bullet} \longrightarrow FM^{\bullet} \longrightarrow FK^{\bullet}[1]$$

is a derived triangle in $\mathbf{K}(\mathcal{C}')$. ($F$ acts termwise on complexes.)
