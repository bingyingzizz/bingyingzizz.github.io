⭐ **It can also be formalized inside ZFC**: encode symbols as sets and formulas as finite sequences; "$\varphi$ is a formula" then becomes a property in ZFC. That is further metamathematical work, not developed here. (This direction is exactly the entrance to Gödel's incompleteness theorems.)

⚠ **Keep language and structure apart**: $\mathcal{L} = \{ \in \}$ specifies only which symbols exist, not yet where variables range — that is what the **satisfaction relation** has to say.
