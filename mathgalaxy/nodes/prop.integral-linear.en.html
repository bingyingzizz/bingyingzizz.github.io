Notes

⭐ "Additivity over intervals" is the most practical property of the Riemann integral: it cuts a problem on a large interval into two halves — and it is also the basis for defining improper integrals later.

⚠ The product of two integrable functions is integrable (amplitude estimate: ω(fg)≤Mω(g)+Nω(f)\omega(fg) \le M\omega(g) + N\omega(f)ω(fg)≤Mω(g)+Nω(f) on each subinterval), but the quotient need not be: one must first ensure the denominator stays away from zero.
