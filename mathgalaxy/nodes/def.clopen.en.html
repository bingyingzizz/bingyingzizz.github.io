Statement

A subset KKK of XXX is clopen if it is both open and closed. Equivalently, KKK and its complement KcK^{c}Kc are both open, i.e. X=K⊔KcX = K \sqcup K^{c}X=K⊔Kc is a union of disjoint non-empty open sets (when KKK is non-trivial).

Notes

Clopen sets are a rare species in a general topological space: in connected spaces such as R\mathbb{R}R or [0,1][0,1][0,1] there are only ∅\emptyset∅ and the whole space. The more clopen sets, the more fragmented the space — totally disconnected means "every connected component is a single point"; "clopen sets separate any two points" (= zero-dimensional) is equivalent to that only under compact Hausdorff; and in a profinite space "the clopen sets form a base".
