# the minimal polynomial divides the characteristic polynomial　`thm.minimal-poly-divisor`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=thm.minimal-poly-divisor)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./thm.minimal-poly-divisor.md)

- $\mu_T \mid \chi_T$ (the minimal polynomial divides the characteristic polynomial); in particular $\deg \mu_T \le n$;
- $\mu_T$ and $\chi_T$ have **exactly the same roots** in the algebraic closure of $K$ (that is, the same set of eigenvalues);
- hence $T$ is diagonalizable (viewed over the algebraic closure) $\iff$ $\mu_T$ has no repeated roots.

## Notes · **draft**
⭐ The first of the three is a direct consequence of Cayley–Hamilton plus the ideal property: $\chi_T \in I_T = (\mu_T)$.
