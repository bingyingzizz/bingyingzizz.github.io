## Statement · **draft**
If $M \in \mathcal{A}$ and $K^{\bullet} \in C^{+}(\mathcal{A})$, then $R^{n}\operatorname{Hom}(M, K^{\bullet}) = \operatorname{Ext}^{n}(M, K^{\bullet})$ (dually, $R^{n}\operatorname{Hom}(-, N)$ gives $\operatorname{Ext}$ on the other side).
