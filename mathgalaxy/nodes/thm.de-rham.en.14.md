# de Rham theorem　`thm.de-rham`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=thm.de-rham)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./thm.de-rham.md)

the number of independent classes of closed forms modulo exact forms is purely topological, given by the Betti numbers.
**Relation to the three de Rham nodes in pack 131**: $H^{0}$ counts connected components (`thm.de-rham-h0`), cohomology vanishes locally (`lem.poincare-lemma`), and combining open sets gives a long exact sequence (`thm.mayer-vietoris`). These are the local versions of Steps 2 and 5.
