# Lagrangian Submanifold　`def.lagrangian-submanifold`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=def.lagrangian-submanifold)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./def.lagrangian-submanifold.md)

Let $(M^{2n}, \omega)$ be a symplectic manifold. An immersed submanifold $L \subseteq M$ is called a **Lagrangian submanifold** if

1. $\dim L = n$ (half the dimension of $\dim M$).
2. $\omega|_{L} = 0$ (on $L$ the form $\omega$ vanishes identically, so $L$ is isotropic).

Equivalently, for every $p \in L$, $T_{p}L$ is a Lagrangian subspace of $T_{p}M$.
