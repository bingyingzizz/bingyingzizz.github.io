**⚠️ Technical detail**: The proof uses two standard constructions, the chain map induced by a simplicial map and the prism construction for chain homotopies (see `def.chain-homotopy` and `thm.homotopy-invariance`). If $C_{*}$ is taken to be an **ordered** chain group, $g$ may collapse a simplex in degenerate cases, and the signs in the chain map must respect the vertex order. Homology here is defined using the quotient of ordered chain groups by degenerate chains. The sign details are omitted.
