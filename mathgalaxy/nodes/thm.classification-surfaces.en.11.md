**Step (3) (complete).** For the standard $4g$-gon, the fan triangulation from the center gives $V=2$ (the center and one boundary vertex class), $E=6g$, and $F=4g$, so $\chi=2-2g$. For the $2k$-gon, the counts are $V=2$, $E=3k$, and $F=2k$, so $\chi=2-k$. The two families have distinct homeomorphism types because one is orientable and the other is nonorientable. Within either family, distinct values of $\chi$ give nonhomeomorphic surfaces by the topological invariance of $\chi$ (`thm.euler-characteristic`).
