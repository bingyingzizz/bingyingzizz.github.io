又称Krull–Schmidt 定理·克鲁尔-施密特定理·Krull-Schmidt

陈述

每个有限维表示都可分解为有限个不可分解表示的直和，且该分解在置换与同构意义下唯一。

说明

存在性对维数归纳：若 VVV 不可分解则完成，否则分裂后归纳。 唯一性比较两个分解，用同构因子引理逐个匹配不可分解直和项；幂零和引理处理自同态记账。 除有限维外不需对域作假设。 因此它特别适用于有限群的有限维表示。

Statement draft

Every finite-dimensional representation decomposes as a direct sum of finitely many indecomposable representations, and this decomposition is unique up to permutation and isomorphism.

Notes draft

Existence is by induction on dimension: if VVV is indecomposable the decomposition is complete, otherwise split and induct. Uniqueness compares two decompositions and matches indecomposable summands one by one using the isomorphism factorization lemma; the nilpotent-sum lemma handles the bookkeeping of endomorphisms. No assumption on the field is needed beyond finite dimension. Hence it applies in particular to finite-dimensional representations of finite groups.
