陈述

阿贝尔群范畴 Ab\mathbf{Ab}Ab：对象是阿贝尔群，态射是群同态；它是 Grp\mathbf{Grp}Grp 的全子范畴。

遗忘函子 Ab→Set\mathbf{Ab}\to\mathbf{Set}Ab→Set 把阿贝尔群送到其底层集合；Z-Mod\mathbb Z\text{-}\mathbf{Mod}Z-Mod 与 Ab\mathbf{Ab}Ab 等价。

说明

阿贝尔范畴的原型：有核、余核，单/满态射都严格；hom⁡(A,B)\hom(A,B)hom(A,B) 逐点加法构成阿贝尔群。

足够投射/内射对象：自由阿贝尔群投射；Q/Z\mathbb Q/\mathbb ZQ/Z 给出内射余生成元，每个阿贝尔群都能嵌入内射群。

张量积给出对称幺半结构，单位元是 Z\mathbb ZZ；hom⁡(A,B)\hom(A,B)hom(A,B) 又是阿贝尔群，所以 Ab\mathbf{Ab}Ab 还是对称幺半闭的。

与 A-ModA\text{-}\mathbf{Mod}A-Mod 的关系：Z-Mod≃Ab\mathbb Z\text{-}\mathbf{Mod}\simeq\mathbf{Ab}Z-Mod≃Ab，这是「模」统一「阿贝尔群」的最基本例子。

Statement draft

The category of abelian groups Ab\mathbf{Ab}Ab: objects are abelian groups, morphisms are group homomorphisms; it is a full subcategory of Grp\mathbf{Grp}Grp.

The forgetful functor Ab→Set\mathbf{Ab}\to\mathbf{Set}Ab→Set sends an abelian group to its underlying set; Z-Mod\mathbb Z\text{-}\mathbf{Mod}Z-Mod and Ab\mathbf{Ab}Ab are equivalent.

Notes draft

The prototype of an abelian category: it has kernels and cokernels, monomorphisms and epimorphisms are strict; hom⁡(A,B)\hom(A,B)hom(A,B) with pointwise addition is an abelian group.

Enough projective/injective objects: free abelian groups are projective; Q/Z\mathbb Q/\mathbb ZQ/Z gives an injective cogenerator, and every abelian group embeds into an injective group.

The tensor product gives a symmetric monoidal structure with unit Z\mathbb ZZ; hom⁡(A,B)\hom(A,B)hom(A,B) is again an abelian group, so Ab\mathbf{Ab}Ab is even symmetric monoidal closed.

Relation to A-ModA\text{-}\mathbf{Mod}A-Mod: Z-Mod≃Ab\mathbb Z\text{-}\mathbf{Mod}\simeq\mathbf{Ab}Z-Mod≃Ab, the most basic example of "modules" unifying "abelian groups".
