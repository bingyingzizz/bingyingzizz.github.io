# interior product　`def.interior-product`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=def.interior-product)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./def.interior-product.md)

**Interaction with the wedge product**: the combination $\mathrm{d}\iota_{X} + \iota_{X}\mathrm{d}$ is natural because $\mathrm{d}$ is an antiderivation of degree $+1$ and $\iota_{X}$ is an antiderivation of degree $-1$, and the composite of two antiderivations is a "derivation of degree $0$" — the same type as the Lie derivative.

The proof of Cartan's formula compares exactly this type.
