Statement

A preadditive category (also a category enriched over Ab\mathbf{Ab}Ab) is a category C\mathcal{C}C such that for every pair of objects M,NM, NM,N, the hom-set Hom⁡C(M,N)\operatorname{Hom}_{\mathcal{C}}(M, N)HomC​(M,N) carries an abelian group structure, and the composition map

Hom⁡(M,N)×Hom⁡(N,P)⟶Hom⁡(M,P)\operatorname{Hom}(M, N) \times \operatorname{Hom}(N, P) \longrightarrow \operatorname{Hom}(M, P)Hom(M,N)×Hom(N,P)⟶Hom(M,P)

is bilinear (additive in one variable when the other is fixed).

Notes

Equivalent description: the functor Hom⁡\operatorname{Hom}Hom, with signature Cop×C→Ab\mathcal{C}^{\mathrm{op}} \times \mathcal{C} \to \mathbf{Ab}Cop×C→Ab, exists, and after the forgetful functor Ab→Set\mathbf{Ab} \to \mathbf{Set}Ab→Set it is the usual Hom functor.

Hence every Hom⁡(M,N)\operatorname{Hom}(M, N)Hom(M,N) has a zero element, namely the zero morphism 0:M→N0 : M \to N0:M→N; composing the zero morphism with anything gives the zero morphism.

Examples: A-ModA\text{-}\mathbf{Mod}A-Mod, Ab\mathbf{Ab}Ab, K-VectK\text{-}\mathbf{Vect}K-Vect are all preadditive. Counterexample: the hom-sets of Set\mathbf{Set}Set usually have no group structure.

⚠️ Preadditivity does not require a zero object or direct sums; adding those two gives an additive category (see @@def.additive-category@@).
