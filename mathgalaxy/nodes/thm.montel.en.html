Statement

Let F\mathcal{F}F be a family of holomorphic functions on a domain Ω\OmegaΩ that is locally uniformly bounded: every point has a neighbourhood on which the family is uniformly bounded (equivalently: uniformly bounded on every compact set K⊂ΩK \subset \OmegaK⊂Ω). Then F\mathcal{F}F is a normal family: every sequence fn∈Ff_{n} \in \mathcal{F}fn​∈F has a subsequence converging uniformly on compact subsets (with holomorphic limit).
