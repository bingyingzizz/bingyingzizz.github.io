Notes

⭐ The role of this criterion is to turn a series problem into a sequence problem: it is just the Cauchy criterion for R\mathbb{R}R applied to the sequence of partial sums {sN}\{s_N\}{sN​} (with interval additivity writing sn−sms_n - s_msn​−sm​ as a tail).

⚠ "an→0a_n \to 0an​→0" is only a necessary condition: the harmonic series ∑1/n\sum 1/n∑1/n has terms tending to zero yet diverges.

To prove divergence it is very useful (if the terms do not tend to zero, the series diverges at once); to prove convergence it says nothing.

⭐ Every criterion in this pack descends from it: the comparison test uses "a tail dominated by a larger tail", Abel–Dirichlet uses "a tail dominated by the boundedness of partial sums".
