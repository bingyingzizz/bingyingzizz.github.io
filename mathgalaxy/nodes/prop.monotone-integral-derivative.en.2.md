## Notes · **draft**
⚠ **This is in general not an equality**: the Cantor function is the classical counterexample — it is increasing and continuous, with derivative a.e. 0, so the left side is $= 0$ while the right side is $= 1$. This discrepancy is precisely what the notion of absolute continuity supplies.
