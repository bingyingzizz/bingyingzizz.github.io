## Statement · **draft**
Let $p : E \to B$ be continuous and surjective. An open set $U \subseteq B$ is **evenly covered** if $p^{-1}(U)$ is a union of a family $\{V_{j}\}_{j \in J}$ of pairwise disjoint open sets in $E$, and for every $j$ the restriction

$$p\big|_{V_{j}} : V_{j} \longrightarrow U$$
