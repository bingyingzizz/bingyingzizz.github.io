# dualizing complex of a smooth map　`thm.smooth-duality`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=thm.smooth-duality)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./thm.smooth-duality.md)

⭐ The proof uses the diagonal: $g : A \otimes_R A \to A$ is the regular closed immersion of codimension $d$ (the diagonal $\Delta_f$); split $f^!R$ using $g^!$ and the two projections $A \otimes_R A$, then apply the formula for regular closed immersions to get

$$f^!R \otimes f^!R \otimes g^!(A \otimes_R A) \;\cong\; f^!R,$$

and comparing the two sides gives $\det(\Omega)[d]$.

⚠ This is the precise source of "coherent duality in the smooth case is Poincaré duality": $\omega_{X/Y} = \det(\Omega^1_{X/Y})[d]$.
