# polynomials in one variable　`def.polynomial`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=def.polynomial)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./def.polynomial.md)

If the top nonzero coefficient is $a_n \ne 0$, the **degree** of $f$ is $n$, written $\deg f := n$. The zero polynomial has no degree, and a nonzero constant polynomial has degree $0$. $a_n$ is called the **leading coefficient**; when $a_n = 1$, $f$ is called **monic**.

Two polynomials are **equal** iff all coefficients agree — “polynomial” and “polynomial function” are two different things (see the notes).
