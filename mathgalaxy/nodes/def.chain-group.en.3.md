## Notes · **draft**
**Why free abelian**: we want to add the simplices of a chain with signs — adding $\sigma$ three times gives $3\sigma$, cancelling it against its reverse gives $0$, and **no other relations** are allowed. The free abelian group is exactly the minimal structure that permits arbitrary addition on a basis with no extra relations.

⚠️ **Exactly enough freedom**: a group homomorphism is determined by its values on a **basis**, so to define the boundary operator later it suffices to write a formula on a single simplex and extend linearly — no compatibility check is required. That is the entire use of the word "free".
