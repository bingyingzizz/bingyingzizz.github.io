# Syzygy　`def.syzygy`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=def.syzygy)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./def.syzygy.md)

📌 Hilbert's syzygy theorem is the best-known result along these lines. Over $R = k[x_{1}, \ldots, x_{n}]$, the syzygy of order $n$ of every finitely generated module is free, so $\operatorname{gl.dim} R = n$ (for a polynomial ring, the Koszul complex gives an explicit resolution of length $n$).
