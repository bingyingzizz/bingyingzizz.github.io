# dominated convergence　`thm.dct`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=thm.dct)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./thm.dct.md)

$\cdot$ $g$ must be **integrable**; mere boundedness is not enough (on a space of infinite measure).

Why "dominating": $g$ keeps all the $f_{n}$ inside one integrable cage, so the mass has nowhere to escape and the limiting process cannot lose area.

📌 "Exchanging integral and limit" here means **dominated convergence** (pointwise convergence plus an integrable dominating function); the exchange under uniform convergence is `thm.uniform-limit-integral`.
