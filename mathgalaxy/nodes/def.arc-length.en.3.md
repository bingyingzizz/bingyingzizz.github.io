## Notes · **draft**
**Arc length is independent of the parametrization**: $L(\gamma\circ h) = L(\gamma)$ (with $h$ monotone $C^{1}$), a one-line change of variables. This is the formal statement of "length belongs to the curve, not to the parametrization".

**The arc-length parameter is unique up to a constant and an orientation**: two arc-length parameters differ by a constant; traversing the curve backwards gives $s\mapsto -s$. Hence in formulas written in the arc-length parameter, every constant that appears has no geometric meaning, and every derivative that appears does.
