# basic properties of extremally disconnected spaces　`prop.stonean-basic`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=prop.stonean-basic)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./prop.stonean-basic.md)

**Making (a) precise.** Let $U, V$ be disjoint open sets. Put $W := X\setminus \overline{V}$ — it is open, and $U\subseteq W$.
Since $U\subseteq W = W^{\circ}$, taking closures gives $\overline U\subseteq\overline{W^{\circ}}$; and the **equivalent characterization of extremal disconnectedness** is "the closure of every open set is open", i.e. $\overline{W^{\circ}}$ is open, so $\overline{W^{\circ}} = W^{\circ}$, hence $\overline U \subseteq W^{\circ} = W = X\setminus\overline V$, i.e. $\overline U\cap\overline V = \emptyset$ ✓.
