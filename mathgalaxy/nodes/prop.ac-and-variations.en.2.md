## Notes · **draft**
The second half, "singular and absolutely continuous $\implies$ equals zero", is the **starting point** of the structure theorem: what Radon–Nikodym says is precisely that in general $\nu$ is the sum of these two parts.

Proof: if $\nu \perp \mu$, choose $E$ ($\mu$-null) and $F$ ($\nu$-null) partitioning the space; from $\nu \ll \mu$ get $\nu (E) = 0$; and $\nu (F) = 0$, so $\nu$ is 0 on all of $X$.
