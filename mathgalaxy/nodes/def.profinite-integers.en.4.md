⭐ **CRT splits it into prime directions**: $\hat{\mathbb{Z}} \cong \prod_{p} \mathbb{Z}_{p}$ ($\mathbb{Z}_{p}$ being the $p$-adic integers). So "prime factorization in number theory" becomes, on the topological side, "decomposing a compact totally disconnected space into indecomposable factors" — this is the bridge that **really exists** between number theory and topology.
