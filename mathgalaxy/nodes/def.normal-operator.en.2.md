# normal operator　`def.normal-operator`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=def.normal-operator)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./def.normal-operator.md)

Hierarchy: $\{$unitary$\} \subset \{$ normal$\}$, $\{$self-adjoint$\} \subset \{$ normal$\}$, and the intersection of the two is "self-adjoint and unitary" (that is, a symmetry with $T^{2} = I$).

## Notes · **draft**
⭐ Why these three words: **the spectral theorem holds for normal operators** (they can be diagonalized unitarily), and self-adjoint operators are the most important class of normal operators (all eigenvalues real).

General operators cannot do this (Jordan blocks have no orthonormal basis).
