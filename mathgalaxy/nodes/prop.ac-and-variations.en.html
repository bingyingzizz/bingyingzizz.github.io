Statement

Let μ\muμ be a measure and ν\nuν a signed measure. Then

ν≪μ  ⟺  ∣ν∣≪μ  ⟺  ν+≪μ ∧ ν−≪μ\nu \ll \mu \iff |\nu| \ll \mu \iff \nu^+ \ll \mu\ \wedge \ \nu^- \ll \muν≪μ⟺∣ν∣≪μ⟺ν+≪μ ∧ ν−≪μ

Moreover: if both ν⊥μ\nu \perp \muν⊥μ and ν≪μ\nu \ll \muν≪μ hold, then ν=0\nu = 0ν=0.

Notes

The second half, "singular and absolutely continuous   ⟹  \implies⟹ equals zero", is the **starting point** of the structure theorem: what Radon–Nikodym says is precisely that in general ν\nuν is the sum of these two parts.

Proof: if ν⊥μ\nu \perp \muν⊥μ, choose EEE (μ\muμ-null) and FFF (ν\nuν-null) partitioning the space; from ν≪μ\nu \ll \muν≪μ get ν(E)=0\nu (E) = 0ν(E)=0; and ν(F)=0\nu (F) = 0ν(F)=0, so ν\nuν is 0 on all of XXX.
