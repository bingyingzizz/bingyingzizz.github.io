# Free and Projective Modules Are Flat　`prop.free-flat`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=prop.free-flat)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./prop.free-flat.md)

⭐ **Use**: this guarantees that "projective resolutions are flat resolutions", so $\operatorname{Tor}$ can be computed either by projective or by flat resolutions; the latter are often much shorter (for example the flat resolution given by localization).

⚠️ The converse fails: flat does not imply projective (`ex.flat-not-projective`).
