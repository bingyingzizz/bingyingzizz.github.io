Statement

The following pre-analytic rings are analytic rings:

(i) Zp,■\mathbb{Z}_{p,\blacksquare}Zp,■​ (the ppp-adic solid ring);

(ii) for any discrete ring AAA, (A,Z)■(A, \mathbb{Z})_\blacksquare(A,Z)■​, whose free module is

(A,Z)■[S]=Z[S]■⊗ZA.(A,\mathbb{Z})_\blacksquare[S] = Z[S]^{\blacksquare} \otimes_{\mathbb{Z}} A.(A,Z)■​[S]=Z[S]■⊗Z​A.

Notes

⭐ (i) and (ii) in fact share one proof: in both cases one has

A[S]=Z■[S]⊗Z■LA,\mathcal{A}[S] = \mathbb{Z}_\blacksquare[S] \otimes^{L}_{\mathbb{Z}_\blacksquare} \mathcal{A},A[S]=Z■​[S]⊗Z■​L​A,

and CCC is, in D(Solid)D(\mathbf{Solid})D(Solid), an A\mathcal{A}A-module, so

RHom⁡(Z■[S],C)≅RHom⁡A(Z■[S]⊗Z■LA, C),\operatorname{RHom}(\mathbb{Z}_\blacksquare[S], C) \cong \operatorname{RHom}_{\mathcal{A}}(\mathbb{Z}_\blacksquare[S] \otimes^{L}_{\mathbb{Z}_\blacksquare} \mathcal{A},\ C),RHom(Z■​[S],C)≅RHomA​(Z■​[S]⊗Z■​L​A, C),

The left-hand side is RHom⁡(Z[S],C)\operatorname{RHom}(\mathbb{Z}[S], C)RHom(Z[S],C) (CCC solid), and the right-hand side is RHom⁡A(A[S],C)\operatorname{RHom}_{\mathcal{A}}(\mathcal{A}[S], C)RHomA​(A[S],C).
