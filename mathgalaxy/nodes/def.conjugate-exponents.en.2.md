# conjugate exponents　`def.conjugate-exponents`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=def.conjugate-exponents)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./def.conjugate-exponents.md)

For $p = 2$ one has $q = 2$: **$L^2$ is self-conjugate** — this is the starting point of Hilbert space theory.

⭐ The meaning of conjugate exponents: Hölder's inequality says that between $L^p$ and $L^q$ there is a “pairing” $\int fg$; and the duality theorem below says this pairing is **exactly the whole dual space** of $L^p$.
