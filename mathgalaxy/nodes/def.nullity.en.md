## Statement · **draft**
Let $T : V \to W$ be linear. The **nullity** of $T$ is defined as the dimension of its kernel:

$$\operatorname{null} T := \dim \ker T$$

(also called the **defect**; English nullity or defect.)

## Notes · **draft**
⭐ Nullity measures "how many dimensions of information $T$ throws away". It pairs with the rank: the rank–nullity theorem says the two add up to the dimension of the domain.
