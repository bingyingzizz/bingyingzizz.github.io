**Proof.**
**1.** The kernel is the equalizer of $f$ and $0$. In topological abelian groups this equalizer is $\{x : f(x) = 0\}$ with the **subspace topology** ($f$ is continuous and $N$ is Hausdorff, so this set is closed) ✓.
