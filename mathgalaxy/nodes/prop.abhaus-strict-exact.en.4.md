## Notes · **draft**
⭐ "Strict" is not decoration: for topological abelian groups "exact" and "strictly exact" are genuinely different — topological abelian groups are not an abelian category, and what is missing is exactly "every morphism is determined by its kernel and cokernel". Condensed mathematics repairs this layer by embedding topological groups into $\mathrm{CondAb}$.
