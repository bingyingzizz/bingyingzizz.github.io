prop.generator-coproduct-epi · AB3

Machine-drafted and pending author review. The Chinese text is authoritative.

Statement

Let C\mathcal{C}C be an abelian category with coproducts and G∈CG \in \mathcal{C}G∈C. Then GGG is a generator   ⟺  \iff⟺ for every M∈CM \in \mathcal{C}M∈C there exist an index set III and an epimorphism

G(I)↠M.G^{(I)} \twoheadrightarrow M.G(I)↠M.

Notes

⚠️ This criterion in fact uses only coproducts (AB3); filtered colimits do not appear (the AB5 part of the hypothesis is not needed).
