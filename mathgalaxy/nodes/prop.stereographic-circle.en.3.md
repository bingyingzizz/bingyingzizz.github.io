# stereographic projection　`prop.stereographic-circle`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=prop.stereographic-circle)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./prop.stereographic-circle.md)

⭐ **The meaning of conformality**: it shows that “doing complex analysis on the sphere” and “doing it on the plane” are the same subject, only with a different metric. This is also why the conformal structure on $\widehat{\mathbb{C}}$ can be defined as “$z \mapsto 1/z$ is also holomorphic” — $1/z$ is precisely what “rotation by 180° about the north pole” looks like on the plane.
