## Statement · **draft**
Let $G$ be a finite group. The regular representation is the left multiplication action of $G$ on $F[G]$, $\rho_{\mathrm{reg}}(g)(h)=gh$, viewed as a permutation representation on $F^G$. Its character is $\chi_{\mathrm{reg}}(1)=|G|$ and $\chi_{\mathrm{reg}}(g)=0$ for $g\ne1$.
