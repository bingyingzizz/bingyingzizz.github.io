## Notes · **draft**
⭐ Statement 2 is the precise version of "there are enough discrete-valued functions": on a Stone space every continuous $M$-valued function can be uniformly approximated by locally constant functions. It is a key step in the proof of acyclicity for Banach coefficients (8.2.6 / 8.2.8).
