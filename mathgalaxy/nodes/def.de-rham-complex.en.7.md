# de Rham Complex and Cohomology　`def.de-rham-complex`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=def.de-rham-complex)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./def.de-rham-complex.md)

**It is not "homology" in the sense of a homotopy invariant**: $H^{k}$ is **co**homology (forms and pullback), dual to homology with chains and boundary. It is called cohomology because pullback is contravariant; the two are proved isomorphic in the de Rham theorem.
