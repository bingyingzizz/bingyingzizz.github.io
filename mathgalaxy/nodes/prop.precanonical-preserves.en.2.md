# Coproducts and Quotients under the Precanonical Topology　`prop.precanonical-preserves`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=prop.precanonical-preserves)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./prop.precanonical-preserves.md)

1. when $I$ is **finite**, $\coprod_{i \in I} X_{i}$ is still that coproduct in the sheaf category;
2. if $R$ is an equivalence relation on $X$, then the quotient $X/R$ is still that quotient in the sheaf category.

In other words: between $\mathcal{C}$ and $\mathcal{C}$ — that is, for $X \mapsto h_{X}$ — coproducts and quotients **come out the same on both sides**.
