## Notes · **draft**
⭐ The first five follow directly from the **algebra of limits** (`prop.limit-arithmetic`): continuity means "the limit equals the value", so any operation limits support, continuity supports too.

⚠ The composite case **cannot** be copied blindly: $\lim_{x \to x_0} f(x) = f(x_0)$ and $\lim_{y \to y_0} g(y) = g(y_0)$ must be combined into $\lim_{x \to x_0} g(f(x)) = g(f(x_0))$, with an $\varepsilon$-$\delta$ transfer in between.

⭐ Corollary: polynomials are continuous everywhere;
