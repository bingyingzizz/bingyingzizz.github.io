## Notes · **draft**
**Relation to simplicial homology**: every $p$-simplex $[v_{0}, \ldots, v_{p}]$ of $K$ can be viewed as a singular $p$-simplex (map the vertices $v_{i}$ of $\Delta^{p}$ linearly to the $v_{i}$ of $K$), which gives a subcomplex $C_{*}(K) \subseteq S_{*}(|K|)$. `thm.simplicial-approximation` says that this inclusion induces an **isomorphism** $H_{p}(K) \cong H_{p}(|K|)$.

In other words: singular homology is "homology without choosing a triangulation", and the two agree on triangulable spaces.
