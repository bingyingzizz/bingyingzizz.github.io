# Newman's Tauberian lemma　`lem.newman-tauberian`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=lem.newman-tauberian)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./lem.newman-tauberian.md)

**Remark 1 (why the kernel contains $1+z^{2}/R^{2}$)**: $F(0)=1$ keeps the residue at $z=0$ equal to $g(0)-g_{T}(0)$; while on $|z|=R$ one has $1+z^{2}/R^{2}=2z\operatorname{Re}z/R^{2}$, which exactly cancels the bad factor $1/\operatorname{Re}z$, making the estimates on the right half-plane and the left semicircle uniform in $R$.
