## Notes · **draft**
"Taking the limit along larger and larger covering sieves" turns into a colimit on the $T$ side, because $\operatorname{Hom}(-, T)$ is **contravariant**, and a contravariant functor takes limits along a filter to filtered colimits of the covariant functor. $\widehat{H}$ is the first step in pushing a presheaf toward a sheaf.
