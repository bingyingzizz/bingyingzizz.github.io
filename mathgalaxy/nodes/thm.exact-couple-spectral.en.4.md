# exact couples give spectral sequences　`thm.exact-couple-spectral`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=thm.exact-couple-spectral)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./thm.exact-couple-spectral.md)

- **Limiting term**: $D_{r+1} = i_{r}(D_{r}) \subseteq D_{r}$ is **decreasing**, and its intersection $D_{\infty}$ is the "limiting term"; the image of $i$ has been used up and the image of $k$ removed, leaving exactly the quotient of the $j$ step, so $E_{\infty}^{p,q} = D_{\infty}^{p,q} / D_{\infty}^{p+1, q-1}$.
