又称环范畴·Rng

陈述

环范畴 Rng\mathbf{Rng}Rng：对象是环（按 环 的约定：不要求幺元、不要求交换），态射是环同态。零环是零对象（既是始对象又是终对象）；积逐点构造；等化子是子环；拉回是纤维积。

余积是环的自由积；余等化子是商环（取生成的双边理想的闭包）；推出是 amalgamated free product。

说明

完备且余完备；非加性（hom⁡(R,S)\hom(R,S)hom(R,S) 一般没有加法群结构）。

为什么不含幺：本图 环 明确不要求幺元；含幺环范畴的始对象是 Z\mathbb ZZ，与零环作为终对象不冲突，但会与 环 的约定分叉，故本图不单列含幺环范畴。

Z\mathbb ZZ 不是始对象：非含幺同态不必保 111，hom⁡(Z,R)\hom(\mathbb Z,R)hom(Z,R) 对应 RRR 的幂等元，不唯一。

Statement draft

The category of rings Rng\mathbf{Rng}Rng: objects are rings (in the convention of @@def.ring@@: not necessarily unital, not necessarily commutative) and morphisms are ring homomorphisms. The zero ring is a zero object (both initial and terminal); products are constructed pointwise; equalizers are subrings; pullbacks are fiber products.

Coproducts are free products of rings; coequalizers are quotient rings (taking the closure of the generated two-sided ideal); pushouts are amalgamated free products.

Notes draft

Complete and cocomplete; not additive (hom⁡(R,S)\hom(R,S)hom(R,S) has no abelian group structure in general).

Why no unit is required: @@def.ring@@ explicitly does not require a unit. In the category of unital rings the initial object is Z\mathbb ZZ; that does not conflict with the zero ring being terminal, but it would diverge from the convention of @@def.ring@@, so the category of unital rings is not given a separate entry.

Z\mathbb ZZ is not initial: a non-unital homomorphism need not preserve 111, and hom⁡(Z,R)\hom(\mathbb Z,R)hom(Z,R) corresponds to an idempotent of RRR, which is not unique.
