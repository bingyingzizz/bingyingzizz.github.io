# Flatness and Torsion-Freeness over a Domain　`prop.flat-domain-torsionfree`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=prop.flat-domain-torsionfree)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./prop.flat-domain-torsionfree.md)

Conversely, if $R$ is a **PID**, then torsion-free $\iff$ flat. Over a general domain "torsion-free" is strictly weaker than "flat".

## Notes · **draft**
**Proof (forward direction)**: take $0 \ne r \in R$.

Since $R$ is a domain, $r$ is not a zero divisor, so $0 \to R \xrightarrow{\;\cdot r\;} R$ is exact (zero kernel).

Since $M$ is flat, tensoring this sequence gives the exact sequence $0 \to M \xrightarrow{\;\cdot r\;} M$ — that is, multiplication by $r$ is injective ✓.
