that is, the left adjoint of the functor $(-) \otimes_{R} N$ (valued in $\mathbf{Ab}$) is $\operatorname{Hom}_{\mathbb{Z}}(N, -)$ (with its $R$-action); the $R$-linear version below requires $N$ to be an $(R,R)$-bimodule:

$$(-) \otimes_{R} N \;\dashv\; \operatorname{Hom}_{R}(N, -).$$
