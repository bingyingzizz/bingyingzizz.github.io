# analytic rings from finitely generated algebras　`thm.za-analytic`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=thm.za-analytic)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./thm.za-analytic.md)

① Set $A_\infty:=\mathbb{Z}((T^{-1}))$ and view it as "functions on $\operatorname{Spec} A$ at infinity"; there is a short exact sequence

$$0\to \mathbb{Z}[[U]]\otimes_{\mathbb{Z}} A \xrightarrow{1-\mathrm{shift}\otimes T} \mathbb{Z}[[U]]\otimes_{\mathbb{Z}} A \to A_\infty \to 0 .$$

② By ①, $A_\infty$ is compact projective (the first two terms are of the form $\prod_I \mathbb{Z}\otimes A$), and idempotent: $A_\infty\otimes^{L}_{A}A_\infty\cong A_\infty$.

③ By ②, $\operatorname{RHom}_A(A_\infty,C)=0$, where $C$ is built from direct sums of $\prod_I A$.
