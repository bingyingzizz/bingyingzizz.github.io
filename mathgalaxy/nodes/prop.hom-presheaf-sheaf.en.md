## Statement · **draft**
Let $X$ be a topological space and $Y \to X$ a morphism of condensed sets (that is, $Y \in \mathrm{Cond}/X$). Then the presheaf

$$U \;\longmapsto\; Y(U) := \operatorname{Hom}_{/X}\bigl(\underline{U},\ Y\bigr)$$
where $U \subseteq X$ ranges over **open sets**,

is a **sheaf** on $X$.
