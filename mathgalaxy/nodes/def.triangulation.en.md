## Statement · **draft**
Let $X$ be a topological space. A **triangulation** of $X$ is a pair $(K, h)$:

- $K$ is a **simplicial complex** (`def.simplicial-complex`), and $|K|$ is its geometric realization;
- $h : |K| \to X$ is a **homeomorphism**.
