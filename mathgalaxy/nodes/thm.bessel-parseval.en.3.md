# Bessel inequality and Parseval identity　`thm.bessel-parseval`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=thm.bessel-parseval)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./thm.bessel-parseval.md)

⭐ Reading: Bessel says "coordinate projection does not increase length" (the shadow of $x$ on a subspace is no longer than $x$); Parseval says "no direction is lost". Hence **completeness $\iff$ equality** — this is turning the geometric fact "is a basis" into a **numerical identity**.

⚠ For uncountable $I$, $\sum_{i \in I}$ must be understood as "at most countably many nonzero terms" (see the note on $\ell^2(I)$), otherwise the sum is undefined.
