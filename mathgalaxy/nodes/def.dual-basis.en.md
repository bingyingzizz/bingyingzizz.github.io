## Statement · **draft**
Let $\mathcal{B} = (v_1, \ldots, v_n)$ be an ordered basis of $V$. For each $i$ define $v_i^{\ast} : V \to K$ by

$$v_i^{\ast}(v_j) := \delta_{ij} = \begin{cases} 1, & i = j \\ 0, & i \ne j \end{cases}$$

(extended linearly to all of $V$). Then $(v_1^{\ast}, \ldots, v_n^{\ast})$ is a basis of $V^{\ast}$, called the **dual basis** of $\mathcal{B}$. Hence in finite dimension $\dim V^{\ast} = \dim V$:

$$\dim V^{\ast} = \dim V$$
