# disjoint unions are coproducts of compact Hausdorff spaces　`prop.coproduct-chaus`

## Notes · **draft**
**Proof.** A continuous map $S \sqcup T \to Z$ is determined by its restrictions to the two halves ✓; conversely, given two continuous maps, gluing them remains continuous (the topology of the disjoint union is the sum of the two topologies) ✓. Hence the natural bijection above holds, i.e. the universal property of the coproduct ✓. Compact Hausdorff spaces are closed under finite disjoint unions (both pieces compact, both Hausdorff) ✓. ∎
