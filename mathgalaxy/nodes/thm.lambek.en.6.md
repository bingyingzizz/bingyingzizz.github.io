# Lambek's Criterion　`thm.lambek`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=thm.lambek)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./thm.lambek.md)

$$0 \to K_{1} \to K \to K_{2} \to 0, \qquad 0 \to R^{n-1} \to R^{n} \to R \to 0 .$$

After tensoring, we obtain a $3 \times 3$ diagram. The vertical maps in the left and right columns are injective by the induction hypothesis and the case $n = 1$, so the vertical map in the middle column is injective by the snake lemma ✓. ∎
