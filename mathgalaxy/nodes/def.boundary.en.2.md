# Boundaries and Coboundaries　`def.boundary`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=def.boundary)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./def.boundary.md)

$$B^{n}(K) := \operatorname{im} d^{n-1} = \{\, d^{n-1}x : x \in K^{n-1} \,\} ,$$

its elements are called **coboundaries**.

**Chain case**: the $n$-th **group of boundaries** is

$$B_{n}(K) := \operatorname{im} d_{n+1} ,$$

its elements are called **boundaries**.

**Why $B \subseteq Z$**: by the complex axiom $d^{n} \circ d^{n-1} = 0$, for $x = d^{n-1}y$ we have $d^{n}x = d^{n}d^{n-1}y = 0$, so every coboundary is a cocycle. Hence the quotient $Z^{n}/B^{n}$ makes sense.
