Statement

Let μ\muμ be a measure and fff a generalized μ\muμ-integrable function; define

ν(E):=∫Efdμ\nu(E) := \int_E f d\muν(E):=∫E​fdμ

Then ν\nuν is a signed measure and ν≪μ\nu \ll \muν≪μ. Moreover

ν\nuν is finite   ⟺  f∈L1(μ)\iff f \in L^1(\mu)⟺f∈L1(μ)

Notes

This is the standard source of absolutely continuous measures, and exactly what the Radon–Nikodym theorem proves in reverse: every absolutely continuous ν\nuν has this form.

In notation one often writes the differential form:

ν(E)=∫Efdμ  ⟺  dν=fdμ\nu(E) = \int_E f d\mu\quad \iff\quad d\nu = f d\muν(E)=∫E​fdμ⟺dν=fdμ
