## Statement · **draft**
If $R$ is an equivalence relation on a presheaf $T$, then after sheafification $R^{\sharp}$ is an equivalence relation on $T^{\sharp}$, and

$$\bigl(T/R\bigr)^{\sharp} \;\cong\; T^{\sharp} / R^{\sharp}$$
