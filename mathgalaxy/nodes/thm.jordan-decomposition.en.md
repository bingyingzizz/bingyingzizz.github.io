## Statement · **draft**
Let $\nu$ be a signed measure. Then there exist **measures** $\mu_1, \mu_2$ such that

$$\nu = \mu_1 - \mu_2, \quad  \mu_1 \perp  \mu_2, $$

and **at least one of them is finite**. Such a decomposition is unique.
