## Statement · **draft**
Let $U \subseteq V$ be a subspace. The **annihilator** of $U$ is defined as

$$U^{0} := \{\, \varphi \in V^{\ast} : \varphi(u) = 0 \ \text{whenever}\ u \in U \,\} \subseteq V^{\ast}$$

It is a subspace of $V^{\ast}$ and (when $\dim V < \infty$)

$$\dim U^{0} = \dim V - \dim U, \qquad (U^{0})^{0} = U$$

(the second equality views $U$ as a subset of $V^{\ast\ast}$ via the natural isomorphism $V \cong V^{\ast\ast}$).
