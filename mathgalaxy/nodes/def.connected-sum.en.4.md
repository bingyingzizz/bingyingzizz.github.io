# connected sum　`def.connected-sum`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=def.connected-sum)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./def.connected-sum.md)

⭐ The sphere is an **identity element**: $S^{n} \# M \cong M$ (remove a ball from $M$ and a ball from $S^{n}$, so that gluing fills the hole and restores the original manifold). Thus the $n$-dimensional closed manifolds form a **commutative monoid** under connected sum. The operation is commutative and has an identity, but **inverses need not exist**, which is part of what makes classification interesting.

⚠ **Connected sums must be taken in the same dimension**, and the manifolds must be connected. Otherwise the balls could be removed from different components, making the result depend on the choice.
