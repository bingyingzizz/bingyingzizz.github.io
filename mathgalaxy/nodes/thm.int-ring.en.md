## Statement · **draft**
On $\mathbb{Z} = (\mathbb{N} \times \mathbb{N})/{\sim}$, take the operations and order defined in `def.int`. Then:

**(i)** the two operations are **well defined** (independent of the choice of representatives);

**(ii)** $(\mathbb{Z}, +, \cdot)$ is a **commutative ring with unity**;

**(iii)** $\mathbb{Z}$ has **no zero divisors** (it is an integral domain): $xy = 0 \implies x = 0$ or $y = 0$;
