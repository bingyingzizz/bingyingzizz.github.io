# Refinement of the Embedding Theorem　`prop.mitchell-exactness`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=prop.mitchell-exactness)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./prop.mitchell-exactness.md)

📌 Division of labour with Giraud's theorem: Giraud embeds a topos as an exact reflective subcategory of a presheaf category; Freyd–Mitchell embeds a small abelian category as a fully faithful exact subcategory of a module category. Both are "find a concrete large category that contains you", and this item supplies "after embedding, which properties can be transported back".
