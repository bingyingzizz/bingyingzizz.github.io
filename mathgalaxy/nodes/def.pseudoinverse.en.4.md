## Notes · **draft**
**Meaning of the four conditions**: The first two say that $AA^{+}$ and $A^{+}A$ are idempotent (projections, `def.idempotent`). The last two say that the projections are **self-adjoint** (orthogonal projections). Thus $A^{+}$ acts by **orthogonally projecting $b$ onto the column space and setting the kernel component to zero**, giving the unique minimum-norm least-squares solution.
