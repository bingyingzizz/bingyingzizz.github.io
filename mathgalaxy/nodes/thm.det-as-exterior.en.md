## Statement · **draft**
Let $\dim_K V = n < \infty$ and $T : V \to V$ be linear. Since $\dim \bigwedge^{n} V = 1$, $T$ induces a linear map of the one-dimensional space

$$\bigl( \textstyle\bigwedge^{n} T \bigr) : \bigwedge^{n} V \to \bigwedge^{n} V, \qquad v_1 \wedge \cdots \wedge v_n \mapsto T(v_1) \wedge \cdots \wedge T(v_n)$$
