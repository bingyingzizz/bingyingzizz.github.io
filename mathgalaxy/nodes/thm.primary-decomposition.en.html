Notes

⭐ This step is the first half of the Jordan decomposition: first split VVV into rrr blocks by eigenvalue, so that only one eigenvalue remains on each block.

The second half splits, within each block, the "nilpotency degree of T−λiIT - \lambda_i IT−λi​I" into small blocks (those are the Jordan blocks).

⭐ Its algebraic version: VVV is a K[x]K[x]K[x]-module, and χT\chi_TχT​ is the "annihilating polynomial" on it;

by the unique factorization in K[x]K[x]K[x], the module splits along the irreducible factors of χT\chi_TχT​ (Chinese remainder theorem) — this is where the name "primary decomposition" comes from.

⚠ KKK is required to be algebraically closed: otherwise χT\chi_TχT​ does not split into linear factors, and the blocks are not indexed by eigenvalues (one must treat "irreducible factors" as the blocks).
