# classification of closed surfaces　`thm.classification-surfaces`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=thm.classification-surfaces)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./thm.classification-surfaces.md)

- The **sphere** $S^{2}$;
- The connected sum of $g$ **tori**, denoted $T^{2}\#\cdots\# T^{2}$ ($g \ge 1$), an **orientable** closed surface of genus $g$;
- The connected sum of $k$ **projective planes**, denoted $\mathbb{RP}^{2}\#\cdots\# \mathbb{RP}^{2}$ ($k \ge 1$), a **nonorientable** closed surface of nonorientable genus $k$.

The two families are disjoint: orientable and nonorientable closed surfaces (apart from the sphere) are never **homeomorphic**.
