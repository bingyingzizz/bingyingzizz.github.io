## Statement · **draft**
Let $V$ be a $K$-vector space. $V$'s **$k$-th exterior power** $\bigwedge^{k} V$ is the quotient of $V^{\otimes k}$ by the subspace spanned by

$$v_1 \otimes \cdots \otimes v_k \quad \text{with}\ v_i = v_j\ (i \ne j)$$

and the image is written $v_1 \wedge \cdots \wedge v_k$. Thus

$$v \wedge v = 0, \qquad v \wedge w = -\, w \wedge v$$
