# Skyscraper Sheaf　`def.skyscraper-sheaf`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=def.skyscraper-sheaf)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./def.skyscraper-sheaf.md)

⚠️ Do not confuse this with "a sheaf with constant stalks": the stalk of the skyscraper sheaf at $x$ is $A$, but at other points it is in general **not** $A$ — on a $T_{1}$ space it is $0$ away from $x$, and on a more general space its support is the closure $\overline{\{x\}}$. See "stalks of a skyscraper sheaf".
