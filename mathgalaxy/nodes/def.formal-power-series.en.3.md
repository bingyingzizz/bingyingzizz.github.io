## Notes · **draft**
⭐ **Difference from analytic power series**: `def.power-series` is concerned with radius of convergence, term-by-term differentiation and analytic functions; formal power series retain only coefficients and algebraic operations, so they can be defined over any commutative ring (including finite fields). Once convergence is dropped, generating functions in combinatorial enumeration become cleaner.
