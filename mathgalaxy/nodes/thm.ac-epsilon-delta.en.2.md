## Notes · **draft**
It upgrades "zero on null sets" to the quantitative "small values on sets of small measure" — this is precisely where the word "continuous" in absolute continuity comes from.

⚠ "$\nu$ finite" cannot be omitted: otherwise $\delta$ cannot be controlled (e.g. the $\mu = Lebesgue$ measure, or $\nu(E) = \int_E 1/x$).

(The $\Longleftarrow$ direction is straightforward: when $\mu (E) = 0$, for every $\varepsilon$ one has $|\nu (E)| \le \varepsilon$, hence $\nu (E) = 0$.)
