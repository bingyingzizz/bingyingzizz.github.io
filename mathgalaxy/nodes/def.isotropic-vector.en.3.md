## Notes · **draft**
**Geometric picture**: think of $q(x) = 1$ as the "unit sphere". A positive definite form gives an ellipsoid (anisotropic); an indefinite form gives a hyperboloid, and the hyperboloid has **two lines through the origin** as asymptotes; those two lines are exactly the **null cone** $q = 0$ (not on the hyperboloid), and the nonzero vectors on it are the isotropic ones. So "isotropic" = "light-cone direction".
