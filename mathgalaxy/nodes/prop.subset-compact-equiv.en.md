## Statement · **draft**
Let $(X, d)$ be a **complete** metric space and $A \subseteq X$. Then

$A$ is **compact** $\iff$ $A$ is closed **and** $A$ is **totally bounded**.

(Totally boundedness of $A$ is understood with respect to the subspace metric $d|_A$.)
