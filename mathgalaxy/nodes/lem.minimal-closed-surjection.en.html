Statement

Let f:S→Tf : S \to Tf:S→T be a continuous surjection between compact Hausdorff spaces. Then there is a minimal closed subset S′⊆SS' \subseteq SS′⊆S such that the restriction f∣S′f|_{S'}f∣S′​ is still surjective.

Notes

The proof uses Zorn's lemma: order by reverse inclusion the family of closed subsets on which fff is surjective, and take intersections for upper bounds of chains — compactness ensures the intersection is still surjective (if it were not, the remaining compact set would be exhausted by a decreasing sequence of closed sets, contradicting the finite intersection property). Hence there is a maximal element, which is minimal in the original order.
