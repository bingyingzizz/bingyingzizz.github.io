## Statement · **draft**
If $f : \mathcal{T} \to \mathcal{T}'$ is a **morphism of topoi**, then $R^{n}f_{*}M$ is the sheaf obtained by **sheafifying** the presheaf $X' \mapsto H^{n}\bigl(f^{-1}(X'),\ M\bigr)$.
