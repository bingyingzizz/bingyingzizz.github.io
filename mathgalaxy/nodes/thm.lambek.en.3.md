# Lambek's Criterion　`thm.lambek`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=thm.lambek)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./thm.lambek.md)

**Step 1 (reduce the** objects being tested **to finitely generated ones)**: Keep the module $M$ being tested **fixed**. The fact that $M$ is a filtered colimit of its finitely generated submodules does not let us reduce $M$ to the finitely generated case. Finite generation does not pass to submodules ($R = k[x,y]$ is flat, but the ideal $(x,y)$ is not). The reduction concerns the maps being tested. It suffices to verify that for every **finitely generated** submodule $K \subseteq R^{n}$, the map $K \otimes M \to R^{n} \otimes M$ is injective.
