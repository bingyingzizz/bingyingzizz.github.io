# homeomorphism　`def.homeomorphism`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=def.homeomorphism)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./def.homeomorphism.md)

⚠️ A homeomorphism is an **isomorphism** in the category of topological spaces: it preserves every topological property (connectedness, compactness, countability, separation), which is exactly its meaning as an "equivalence relation".
