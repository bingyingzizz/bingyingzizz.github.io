# equicontinuous implies uniformly equicontinuous on compacta　`thm.equicontinuous-uniform`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=thm.equicontinuous-uniform)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./thm.equicontinuous-uniform.md)

⭐ The lecture notes use a **Lebesgue number** $\lambda$ instead ($\delta(x,y) < \lambda$ puts a pair inside some member of the cover). The two formulations are equivalent; the advantage of the Lebesgue-number version is that $\lambda$ depends only on the cover.
