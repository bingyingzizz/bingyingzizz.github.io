## Notes · **draft**
**Item 3 is the substance of this proposition.** When taking a colimit of topological spaces, the topology may be coarser or finer than the "piecewise" topology; this item says that in this situation the two **coincide exactly** — a set is open iff its trace on every piece is open. Hence "continuous functions on $X$" = "a compatible family of functions continuous on each piece", and one can work piece by piece.
