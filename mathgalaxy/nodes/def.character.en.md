## Statement · **draft**
Let $(V,\theta)$ be a finite-dimensional representation of $A$. Its character is the function $\chi_\theta:A\to F$, $\chi_\theta(a)=\operatorname{tr}\theta(a)$. It depends only on the isomorphism class of $V$; moreover $\chi_{V\oplus W}=\chi_V+\chi_W$ and $\chi_V(1_A)=\dim V$.
