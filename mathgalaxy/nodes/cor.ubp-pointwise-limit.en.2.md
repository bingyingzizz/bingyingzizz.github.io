## Notes · **draft**
**Proof.** First note that $u$ is linear: for $x, y \in E$ and $\alpha, \beta \in \mathbb{K}$, linearity of $u_n$ and of limits give

$$u(\alpha x + \beta y) = \lim_n u_n(\alpha x + \beta y) = \alpha \lim_n u_n x + \beta \lim_n u_n y = \alpha u x + \beta u y .$$

**Pointwise boundedness.** For each $x$, the convergent sequence $(u_n x)$ is bounded, so $M(x) := \sup_n \|u_n x\| < \infty$.
