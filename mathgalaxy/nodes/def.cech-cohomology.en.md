## Statement · **draft**
Let $M^{\bullet}$ be an **abelian presheaf complex** on a site $\mathcal{C}$, and let $X \in \mathcal{C}$. Its $n$-th **Čech cohomology group** is

$$\check{H}^{n}(X, M^{\bullet}) := \Gamma\bigl(X,\ \check{H}^{n}(M^{\bullet})\bigr).$$
