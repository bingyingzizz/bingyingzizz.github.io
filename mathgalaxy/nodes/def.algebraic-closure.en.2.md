# algebraic closure　`def.algebraic-closure`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=def.algebraic-closure)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./def.algebraic-closure.md)

By Zorn's lemma every field has an algebraic closure; and any two algebraic closures are $K$-isomorphic (unique up to isomorphism).

## Notes · **draft**
⭐ Examples: $\mathbb{C}$ is the algebraic closure of $\mathbb{R}$; $\overline{\mathbb{Q}}$ (all algebraic numbers over $\mathbb{Q}$) is the algebraic closure of $\mathbb{Q}$; the algebraic closure of $\mathbb{F}_p$ is $\bigcup_{n} \mathbb{F}_{p^n}$.

⚠ The algebraic closure is not "a unique set" but "unique up to isomorphism"; different constructions give $K$-isomorphic fields.
