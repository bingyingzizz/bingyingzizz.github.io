## Notes · **draft**
**Proof: injectivity.** A map of sheaves is injective $\iff$ it is injective on sections over every open set.

Take $U$ open and $s \in \mathcal{F}(U)$; its image in the $x$-th component (when $x \in U$) is the stalk of $s$, namely $s_{x} \in \mathcal{F}_{x} \rightarrowtail I_{x}$ (when $x \notin U$ the component lands in $0$).

If $s \ne 0$, then there is $x \in U$ with $s_{x} \ne 0$ (**a sheaf is determined by all its stalks**), so some component is nonzero.
