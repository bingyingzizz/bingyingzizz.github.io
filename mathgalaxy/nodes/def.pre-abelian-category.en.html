def.pre-abelian-category

Machine-drafted and pending author review. The Chinese text is authoritative.

Statement

A pre-abelian category is an additive category in which every morphism has a kernel and a cokernel (for the definitions see @@def.kernel-cokernel@@).

Equivalently: a pre-abelian category is an additive category in which all finite limits and all finite colimits exist.

Notes

Once kernels and cokernels exist one can speak of "exact sequences"; but pre-abelian does not guarantee that "coimage →\to→ image" is an isomorphism — this is exactly the dividing line between pre-abelian and abelian.

Examples: PreAb\mathbf{PreAb}PreAb (the category of pre-abelian groups), the category of filtered vector spaces are pre-abelian but not abelian.

Adding any one of the four equivalent conditions (every monomorphism is regular, images decompose uniquely, ker⁡(coker⁡f)≅coker⁡(ker⁡f)\ker(\operatorname{coker} f) \cong \operatorname{coker}(\ker f)ker(cokerf)≅coker(kerf), etc.) gives an abelian category, see @@def.abelian-category@@.
