陈述

设 {(Xα,Mα)}α∈A\{ (X_\alpha , \mathcal{M}_\alpha ) \}_{\alpha \in A}{(Xα​,Mα​)}α∈A​ 是一族可测空间，X=∏α∈AXαX = \prod_{\alpha \in A} X_\alphaX=∏α∈A​Xα​，投影 πα:X→Xα\pi _\alpha : X \to X_\alphaπα​:X→Xα​。

积 σ\sigmaσ代数定义为

⊗α∈AMα:=M({πα−1(Eα):Eα∈Mα,α∈A})\otimes _{\alpha \in A} \mathcal{M}_\alpha := \mathcal{M}(\{ \pi _\alpha^{-1}(E_\alpha) : E_\alpha \in \mathcal{M}_\alpha, \alpha \in A \})⊗α∈A​Mα​:=M({πα−1​(Eα​):Eα​∈Mα​,α∈A})

说明

即由全体柱集生成的 σ\sigmaσ代数。柱集 πα−1(Eα)\pi _\alpha ^{-1}(E_\alpha )πα−1​(Eα​) 就是「只对第 α\alphaα 个坐标提要求」的集合。

⚠ 一般不能定义成「∏Eα\prod E_\alpha∏Eα​ 的全体生成的 σ\sigmaσ代数」—— 那只在可数指标集时等价（见下一条）。

这样定义的原因：投影 πα\pi _\alphaπα​ 应当是可测的，而 σ\sigmaσ代数要对逆像封闭 —— 取最小的那一个就够了。

Let {(Xα,Mα)}α∈A\{ (X_\alpha , \mathcal{M}_\alpha ) \}_{\alpha \in A}{(Xα​,Mα​)}α∈A​ be a family of measurable spaces, X=∏α∈AXαX = \prod_{\alpha \in A} X_\alphaX=∏α∈A​Xα​, and πα:X→Xα\pi _\alpha : X \to X_\alphaπα​:X→Xα​ the projections.

The product σ\sigmaσ-algebra is defined as

⊗α∈AMα:=M({πα−1(Eα):Eα∈Mα,α∈A})\otimes _{\alpha \in A} \mathcal{M}_\alpha := \mathcal{M}(\{ \pi _\alpha^{-1}(E_\alpha) : E_\alpha \in \mathcal{M}_\alpha, \alpha \in A \})⊗α∈A​Mα​:=M({πα−1​(Eα​):Eα​∈Mα​,α∈A})

Notes draft

That is, the σ\sigmaσ-algebra generated by all cylinder sets. A cylinder set πα−1(Eα)\pi _\alpha ^{-1}(E_\alpha )πα−1​(Eα​) is a set that “imposes conditions only on the α\alphaα-th coordinate”.

⚠ In general it cannot be defined as “take all ∏Eα\prod E_\alpha∏Eα​ and then the σ\sigmaσ-algebra they generate” — that is equivalent only when the index set is countable (see the next item).

The reason for this definition: the projections πα\pi _\alphaπα​ should be measurable, and a σ\sigmaσ-algebra must be closed under preimages — so take the smallest one.
