## Statement · **draft**
For $1$, an integer $p$ greater than it is called **prime** if its only positive divisors are $1$ and $p$ itself. An integer greater than $1$ that is not prime is called **composite**.

## Notes · **draft**
The property actually used in proofs is not "has only two divisors" but **Euclid's lemma**: $p \mid ab \implies p \mid a$ or $p \mid b$.
