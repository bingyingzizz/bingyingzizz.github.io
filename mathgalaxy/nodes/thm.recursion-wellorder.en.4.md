⭐ **Why the "well-ordered set" version rather than the "ordinal" version**: recursion on $\omega$ and transfinite recursion on ordinals are both special cases, so only the most general statement is recorded. (Exception: mathematical induction on $\mathbb{N}$ has a more direct route — it comes from "$\omega$ is the smallest inductive set" and does not need the general theory of well-orderings, so that node is kept.)
