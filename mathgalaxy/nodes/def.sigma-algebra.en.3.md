From (2) and (3), De Morgan gives closure under **countable intersections** at once: $\Big( \bigcap_n A_n \Big)^c = \bigcup_n A_n^c \in \mathfrak{A}$. Hence finite unions, finite intersections, differences and symmetric differences are all available — the only thing that genuinely goes beyond an algebra is **countability**.

⚠ The direction cannot be reversed: every $\sigma$-algebra is an algebra, but an algebra need not be a $\sigma$-algebra.
