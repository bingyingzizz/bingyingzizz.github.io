Statement

A profinite space is a topological space obtained as the limit of finite discrete spaces along a directed system:

X  =  lim←⁡i∈IXi,X \;=\; \varprojlim_{i \in I} X_{i},X=i∈Ilim​​Xi​,

where each XiX_{i}Xi​ is a finite discrete space and III is a directed set.

Notes

"Pro-finite" = projective limit of finite objects. Intuitively it is built from "increasingly fine finite resolutions": XiX_{i}Xi​ is what the space looks like at resolution level iii, and III being directed guarantees that any two levels can be refined simultaneously.
