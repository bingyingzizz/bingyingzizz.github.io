# Group Cohomology　`def.group-cohomology`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=def.group-cohomology)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./def.group-cohomology.md)

**Why it equals $\operatorname{Ext}$**: $(-)^{G} = \operatorname{Hom}_{R[G]}(R, -)$, and computing $R^{n}\operatorname{Hom}_{R[G]}(R, -)$ with an injective resolution of $R$ is exactly $\operatorname{Ext}^{n}_{R[G]}(R, -)$.

The same right derived functor under another name.

$H^{n} = 0$ ($n < 0$) is likewise a direct consequence of "right derived functor".
