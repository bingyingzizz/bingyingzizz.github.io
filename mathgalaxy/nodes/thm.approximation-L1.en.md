## Statement · **draft**
Let $f \in L^1(\mu)$. Then for every $\varepsilon > 0$ there is a simple function $\varphi = \sum_j a_j \chi_{E_j}$ such that

$$\int |f - \varphi| d\mu < \varepsilon$$
