## Statement · **draft**
Let $f$ be differentiable on the interval $I$. Then $f$ is convex iff $f'$ is increasing on $I$.

If $f$ is twice differentiable, then $f$ is convex $\iff f''(x) \ge 0$ for all $x \in I$.
