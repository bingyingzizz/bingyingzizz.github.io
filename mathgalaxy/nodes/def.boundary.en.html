Statement

What is being made precise: the denominator in the homology formula Hn=Zn/BnH^{n} = Z^{n}/B^{n}Hn=Zn/Bn. A boundary is an element coming from one level up — a "trivial cycle", and homology is about dividing them out.

Let KKK be a complex in an additive category C\mathcal{C}C, with differential dn:Kn→Kn+1d^{n} : K^{n} \to K^{n+1}dn:Kn→Kn+1.

Cochain case: the nnn-th group of coboundaries is

Bn(K):=im⁡dn−1={ dn−1x:x∈Kn−1 },B^{n}(K) := \operatorname{im} d^{n-1} = \{\, d^{n-1}x : x \in K^{n-1} \,\} ,Bn(K):=imdn−1={dn−1x:x∈Kn−1},

its elements are called coboundaries.

Chain case: the nnn-th group of boundaries is

Bn(K):=im⁡dn+1,B_{n}(K) := \operatorname{im} d_{n+1} ,Bn​(K):=imdn+1​,

its elements are called boundaries.

Why B⊆ZB \subseteq ZB⊆Z: by the complex axiom dn∘dn−1=0d^{n} \circ d^{n-1} = 0dn∘dn−1=0, for x=dn−1yx = d^{n-1}yx=dn−1y we have dnx=dndn−1y=0d^{n}x = d^{n}d^{n-1}y = 0dnx=dndn−1y=0, so every coboundary is a cocycle. Hence the quotient Zn/BnZ^{n}/B^{n}Zn/Bn makes sense.

Notes

⭐ All of d2=0d^{2} = 0d2=0 is this single statement: Bn⊆ZnB^{n} \subseteq Z^{n}Bn⊆Zn. The axiom d∘d=0d\circ d = 0d∘d=0 has no other use — its only purpose is to make the quotient "cycles / boundaries" exist. So a complex is "a device that lets homology be defined".

⚠️ "Boundary" collides with the topological boundary: the boundary of a manifold ∂M\partial M∂M and the boundary operator of a simplicial complex ∂n\partial_{n}∂n​ are, in the language of complexes, the boundary here (∂n\partial_{n}∂n​ is dnd_{n}dn​).

But a general category has no notion of "point-set boundary", only images.

Always read "boundary" as im⁡d\operatorname{im} dimd.

⚠️ Notation: BnB^{n}Bn (boundary), im⁡dn−1\operatorname{im} d^{n-1}imdn−1 and d(Kn−1)d(K^{n-1})d(Kn−1) are all in use.

When BBB and ZZZ appear together, mind the shift: Bn=im⁡dn−1B^{n} = \operatorname{im} d^{n-1}Bn=imdn−1 is the differential one degree lower than Zn=ker⁡dnZ^{n} = \ker d^{n}Zn=kerdn; this "misalignment" is the source of every sign error in homological algebra.

⭐ de Rham version: forms with dω=0d\omega = 0dω=0 are closed (= cycles), forms with ω=dη\omega = d\etaω=dη are exact (= boundaries). So "closed implies exact" is the part with   ⟺  \iff⟺ H1=0H^{1} = 0H1=0 — exactly the content of the Poincaré lemma.
