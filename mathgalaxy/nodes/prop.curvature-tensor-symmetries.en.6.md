**The fourth comes from torsion-freeness**: $R(X,Y)Z+R(Y,Z)X+R(Z,X)Y = 0$.

An elegant equivalent proof: when torsion vanishes, $\nabla^{2}Z(X,Y) := \nabla_{X}\nabla_{Y}Z-\nabla_{\nabla_{X}Y}Z$ is symmetric in $X,Y$, and $R(X,Y)Z = \nabla^{2}Z(X,Y)-\nabla^{2}Z(Y,X)$;

summing cyclically over the three arguments, the two symmetric terms trade places and cancel term by term.
