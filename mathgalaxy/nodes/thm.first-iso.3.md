## English abstract · **draft** · machine-drafted, pending author review; Chinese is authoritative（续）
For a group homomorphism $\varphi : G \to G'$, the map $g\ker\varphi \mapsto \varphi(g)$ is an isomorphism $G/\ker\varphi \cong \operatorname{im}\varphi$; in particular a surjective homomorphism has $G/\ker\varphi \cong G'$. The construction is not special to groups: the same proof gives $A/\ker f \cong \operatorname{im} f$ for any "set + operations + axioms" structure, while at the level of bare sets every map induces a bijection $A/{\sim} \to \operatorname{im} f$ with $a \sim a' \iff f(a) = f(a')$, and only the final step — that the quotient by the kernel is again a group — is new.
