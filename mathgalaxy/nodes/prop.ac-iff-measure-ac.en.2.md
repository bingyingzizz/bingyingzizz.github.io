## Notes · **draft**
**($(\Longleftarrow )$)** This follows from the ε–δ characterization of absolute continuity of measures: $\varepsilon$ and $\delta$ with $\mu_F \ll  m$ give "$m(E) < \delta \implies |\mu_F(E)| < \varepsilon$". Taking $E$ to be a finite disjoint union of intervals yields absolute continuity of the function directly.

**($(\implies )$)** Let $E \in \mathfrak{B}_\mathbb{R}$ with $m(E) = 0$.

We must show $\mu_F(E) = 0$.

Take a decreasing sequence of open sets $U_1 \supseteq U_2 \supseteq \cdots \supseteq E$ with $m(U_k) < \delta$ (by regularity).
