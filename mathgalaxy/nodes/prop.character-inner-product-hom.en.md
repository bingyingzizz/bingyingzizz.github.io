## Statement · **draft**
Let $\operatorname{char}F\nmid|G|$ and let $V_1,V_2$ be finite-dimensional representations. Then $\langle\chi_{V_1},\chi_{V_2}\rangle=\dim_F\operatorname{Hom}_G(V_1,V_2)$.
