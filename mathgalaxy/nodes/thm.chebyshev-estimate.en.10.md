**Why these bounds suffice**: The constants are only used for the qualitative conclusion that $\pi(x)$ and $x/\log x$ have the same order of growth. Improving the constants to arbitrary precision is the work of Chebyshev's original paper, while sharpening the upper bound to $\pi(x) \sim x/\log x$ requires complex analysis (the prime number theorem, see `thm.prime-number-theorem`).
