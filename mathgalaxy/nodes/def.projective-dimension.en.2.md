and every term $P_{k}$ is projective. The **projective dimension** of $M$, denoted $\operatorname{pd}_{R} M \in \mathbb{N} \cup \{\infty\}$, is the smallest $n$ for which this holds. If no such $n$ exists, write $\operatorname{pd}_{R} M = \infty$.

## Notes · **draft**
**How to read it**
The dimension measures **how short a resolution can be**. Every $R$-module has arbitrarily long projective resolutions. The question is whether a resolution can terminate at a finite stage.
