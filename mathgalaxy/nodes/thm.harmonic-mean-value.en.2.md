# mean value property of harmonic functions　`thm.harmonic-mean-value`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=thm.harmonic-mean-value)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./thm.harmonic-mean-value.md)

$$u(a) = \frac{1}{\pi r^2}\iint_{B(a, r)} u(x, y)\,\mathrm{d}x\,\mathrm{d}y.$$

## Notes · **draft**
⭐ The proof has only two steps: write $u$ as the real part of a holomorphic function $f$ (the disc is simply connected), then take real parts on both sides of the Cauchy integral formula $f(a) = \frac{1}{2\pi}\int_0^{2\pi} f(a + r e^{i\theta})\,\mathrm{d}\theta$.
