# Lagrange Bracket　`def.lagrange-bracket`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=def.lagrange-bracket)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./def.lagrange-bracket.md)

Let $(q, p)$ be Darboux coordinates, and let $u = (u_{1}, \ldots, u_{2n})$ and $v = (v_{1}, \ldots, v_{2n})$ be two sets of coordinate functions. The **Lagrange bracket** is defined by

$$[u, v]_{q,p} := \sum_{i=1}^{n}\Bigl(\frac{\partial q_{i}}{\partial u}\frac{\partial p_{i}}{\partial v} - \frac{\partial p_{i}}{\partial u}\frac{\partial q_{i}}{\partial v}\Bigr) .$$
