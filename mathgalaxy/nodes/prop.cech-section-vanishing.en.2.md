# Covers with Sections Have Acyclic Čech Complexes　`prop.cech-section-vanishing`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=prop.cech-section-vanishing)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./prop.cech-section-vanishing.md)

Then for any abelian group in $\mathcal{T}$, say $M$, the augmented Čech complex $\check{C}^{\bullet}\bigl((X_{0} \to X)^{+}, M\bigr)$ is **homotopy trivial**; in particular

$$\check{H}^{n}\bigl((X_{0} \to X),\ M\bigr) = 0 \qquad (n > 0).$$

The homotopy has the shape $f \mapsto f \circ s^{n}$, where $s^{n} : X_{n} \to X_{n+1}$.
