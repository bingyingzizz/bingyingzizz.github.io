# stalk　`prop.stalk-separated`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=prop.stalk-separated)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./prop.stalk-separated.md)

- ① **pullback is the stalk**: $\hat x^{*}(T) = \varinjlim_{U \ni x} T(U)$. **This step uses only the definition "pullback = colimit along the continuous map"** (the standard construction for pulling back sheaves along a continuous map).
- ② **pushforward and skyscraper floor**: $\hat x_{*}(E)(U) = E$ ($x \in U$) or empty — the usual shape of a **skyscraper**; $\hat x_{!}$ is its left adjoint. ⚠️ The three form an adjunction chain $\hat x_{!} \dashv \hat x^{*} \dashv \hat x_{*}$, and **the existence of this chain needs no hypothesis**, but "$\hat x^{*}$ = stalk" holds only in the category of sheaves.
