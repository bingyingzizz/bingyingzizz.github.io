# Lie Group　`def.lie-group`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=def.lie-group)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./def.lie-group.md)

$\operatorname{SL}(n,\mathbb{R})$, $\operatorname{O}(n)$, $\operatorname{SO}(n)$, $\operatorname{U}(n)$ (cut out by equations, see `thm.regular-level-set`);

$(\mathbb{R}^{n},+)$;

$S^{1} \subseteq \mathbb{C}^{\times}$;

any **countable** discrete group (dimension $0$; ⚠ an uncountable discrete group such as the discrete $( \mathbb{R},+ )$ is **not second countable**, so in the sense of `def.smooth-manifold` it is not a smooth manifold).
