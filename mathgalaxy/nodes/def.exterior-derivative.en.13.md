- coordinate formula $\implies$ total differential on functions and the graded Leibniz rule: uses the product rule for partial derivatives and the sign rule for the wedge product.
- coordinate formula $\implies$ $\mathrm d^2=0$: requires equality of mixed partials; coefficients in $C^2$ suffice, while a single exterior derivative itself only needs $C^1$.
