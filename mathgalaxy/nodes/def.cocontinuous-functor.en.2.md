# cocontinuous　`def.cocontinuous-functor`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=def.cocontinuous-functor)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./def.cocontinuous-functor.md)

⭐ Why needed: a morphism of sites requires a pair of functors ($f^{-1} \dashv f_{*}$), and the existence of the **direct image** $f_{*}$ relies on exactly this "pushing forward stays in sheaves".

⚠ Different direction and different condition from a **continuous functor** (@@def.continuous-functor@@: $f^{-1}$ pulls sheaves back to sheaves) — one handles pullback, the other pushforward, and a morphism of sites needs both; ⛔ do not treat the two as dual to one statement.
