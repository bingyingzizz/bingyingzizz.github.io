又称保极限·preserves limits·保余极限·连续函子·Definition 1.4.4·Def 1.4.4

陈述

设 D:I→CD : I \to \mathcal{C}D:I→C 是图、F:C→DF : \mathcal{C} \to \mathcal{D}F:C→D 是函子。FFF 诱导出锥之间的比较映射

F(lim⁡D)⟶lim⁡(F∘D).F(\lim D) \longrightarrow \lim (F \circ D).F(limD)⟶lim(F∘D).

若这个比较映射对每个（相应型别的）图 DDD 都是同构，就说 FFF 保极限（记 lim⁡D≅lim⁡F∘D\lim D \cong \lim F \circ DlimD≅limF∘D）。对偶地：保余极限。

说明

比较映射是怎么来的：lim⁡D\lim DlimD 是 DDD 的泛锥，FFF 作用之后给出 F∘DF \circ DF∘D 的一个锥，于是由 lim⁡(F∘D)\lim(F\circ D)lim(F∘D) 的泛性质唯一地得到 F(lim⁡D)→lim⁡(F∘D)F(\lim D) \to \lim(F\circ D)F(limD)→lim(F∘D)。所以「保极限」永远是这个由泛性质自动存在的映射恰好可逆。

⭐ 最常用的一条：右伴随保所有极限（对偶地左伴随保所有余极限）。证明只用伴随同构与极限的泛性质，不需要任何具体构造。

弱一点的说法：FFF 保有限极限（= left exact）、保滤过余极限（AB5 那一条）、保积、保等化子……都是把上面的「每个图」限制到某一类图。

⚠️ 与「FFF 连续」区分：这里说的连续函子（continuous functor）是保极限，与拓扑里的连续无关。

Statement draft

Let D:I→CD : I \to \mathcal{C}D:I→C be a diagram and F:C→DF : \mathcal{C} \to \mathcal{D}F:C→D a functor. FFF induces a comparison map between cones

F(lim⁡D)⟶lim⁡(F∘D).F(\lim D) \longrightarrow \lim (F \circ D).F(limD)⟶lim(F∘D).

If this comparison map is an isomorphism for every diagram DDD (of the relevant type), one says that FFF preserves limits (written lim⁡D≅lim⁡F∘D\lim D \cong \lim F \circ DlimD≅limF∘D). Dually: preserves colimits.

Notes draft

Where the comparison map comes from: lim⁡D\lim DlimD is the universal cone of DDD; applying FFF gives a cone over F∘DF \circ DF∘D, so the universal property of lim⁡(F∘D)\lim(F\circ D)lim(F∘D) yields a unique map F(lim⁡D)→lim⁡(F∘D)F(\lim D) \to \lim(F\circ D)F(limD)→lim(F∘D). Thus "preserves limits" always means that this map, which exists automatically by the universal property, happens to be invertible.

⭐ The most used case: right adjoints preserve all limits (dually left adjoints preserve all colimits). The proof uses only the adjunction isomorphism and the universal property of limits, with no concrete construction.

Weaker versions: FFF preserves finite limits (= left exact), filtered colimits (the AB5 condition), products, equalizers, ... — each restricts "every diagram" above to a class of diagrams.

⚠️ Not to be confused with "FFF is continuous": a continuous functor here preserves limits, with no relation to continuity in topology.
