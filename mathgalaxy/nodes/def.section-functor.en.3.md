# sections functor　`def.section-functor`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=def.section-functor)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./def.section-functor.md)

- **Preserves all limits**: limits in a sheaf category are computed pointwise in the presheaf category, and a pointwise limit certainly is still that limit at $X$.
- **In general does not preserve colimits**: colimits must first be computed in presheaves and then **sheafified**, and after sheafification the value at $X$ may be strictly larger than the colimit. **This is the entire content of "the section functor is not exact".**

**When it does preserve colimits**: when $X$ is **projective** in the sheaf category ($\operatorname{Hom}(h_{X}, -) = \Gamma(X,-)$ preserves epimorphisms), it preserves colimits.
