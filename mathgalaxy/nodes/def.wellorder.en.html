Notes

⭐ Well-ordering has one use: it makes "the next step" definable. The part before each step has a first and a last element, so one can do transfinite induction** (proof) and transfinite recursion** (construction) — see "the recursion theorem on well-ordered sets".

⭐ Every well-ordered set is **order-isomorphic to a unique ordinal** (see "the order type of a well-ordered set"), and that ordinal is its "length".

Thus "which of two well-ordered sets is longer" becomes "which of two ordinals is larger", and the order on ordinals is ∈\in∈.
