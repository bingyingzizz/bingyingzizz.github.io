## Notes · **draft**
The rows are the irreducible characters; $\operatorname{char}F=0$ guarantees that $\chi_i=\chi_j$ iff $V_i\cong V_j$ (thm.character-determines-semisimple).
The rows are orthonormal for the character inner product (thm.character-orthogonality).
Characters are class functions, so the columns depend only on the conjugacy class; $s\le r$, with equality requiring the additional fact that the characters form a basis of the class-function space.
