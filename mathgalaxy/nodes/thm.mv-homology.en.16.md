# Mayer–Vietoris sequence in homology　`thm.mv-homology`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=thm.mv-homology)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./thm.mv-homology.md)

**Why no good pair hypothesis is needed.** The argument uses only the open cover and Lebesgue numbers. It requires no neighborhood deformation retraction of $A$ in $X$. This is one way singular homology is easier to work with than homotopy groups.

⭐ **The main difficulty** is passing from simplices that lie locally in $U$ or $V$ to a global isomorphism in homology. The chain homotopy in Step 4 does this, and the exact sequence then follows by algebra. The signs are all in the cone construction, $\operatorname{sd}_{p} := [b_{p}, \operatorname{sd}_{p-1}(\partial\Delta^{p})]$ and $t_{p} := [b_{p}, c_{p}]$.
