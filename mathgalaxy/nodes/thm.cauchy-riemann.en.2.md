# Cauchy–Riemann Equations　`thm.cauchy-riemann`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=thm.cauchy-riemann)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./thm.cauchy-riemann.md)

**(i)** $f$ is complex differentiable at $z_0$;

**(ii)** $f$ is real differentiable at $z_0$, and

$$\frac{\partial u}{\partial x} = \frac{\partial v}{\partial y}, \qquad \frac{\partial u}{\partial y} = -\frac{\partial v}{\partial x};$$

**(iii)** $f$ is real differentiable at $z_0$, and $\dfrac{\partial f}{\partial \bar z}(z_0) = 0$.

When they hold, $f'(z_0) = \dfrac{\partial f}{\partial z}(z_0) = \dfrac{\partial u}{\partial x}(z_0) + i\,\dfrac{\partial v}{\partial x}(z_0)$.
