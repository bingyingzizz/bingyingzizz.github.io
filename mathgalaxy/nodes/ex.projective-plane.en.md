## Statement · **draft**
$$\mathbb{RP}^{2} := S^{2} \big/ (x \sim -x) ,$$

With the quotient topology from antipodal identification, this is a **closed nonorientable surface** of nonorientable genus $k = 1$ and Euler characteristic $\chi(\mathbb{RP}^{2}) = 1$.
