rated by $\{h_{F}\}$ (with $F$ free compact Hausdorff) $\implies$ $\mathrm{CondAb}$ is generated by $\{\mathbb{Z}\cdot F\}$ ✓;
- **AB4\* (exactness of products)**: Products are computed objectwise, and products are exact in $\mathbf{Ab}$ ✓;
- **AB6 (filtered colimits commute with products)**: Objectwise computation reduces this to AB6 in $\mathbf{Ab}$ ✓;
- **Abelian**: Kernels and cokernels are computed objectwise as kernels and cokernels in $\mathbf{Ab}$. The epi-mono factorization and $\operatorname{im}\cong\operatorname{coim}$ both hold objectwise ✓. ∎
