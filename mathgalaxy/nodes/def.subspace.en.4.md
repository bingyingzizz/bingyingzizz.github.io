**Assumptions used in each direction** (the vector-space criterion after restricting the operations):
- Subspace $\implies$ contains zero and is closed under addition and scalar multiplication: this is a free unfolding of the definition, needing neither finite dimension nor the axiom of choice.
- The three closure conditions $\implies$ the restricted operations form a vector space: the algebraic identities are inherited from $V$, negatives come from multiplication by $-1$, and no existence theorem for bases is needed.
