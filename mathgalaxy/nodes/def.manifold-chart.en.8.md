# Chart and Transition Maps　`def.manifold-chart`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=def.manifold-chart)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./def.manifold-chart.md)

**Interface with surface theory**: the inverse of a regular parametrization $r : U \to S$ is a chart, and a change of parametrization is precisely the transition map — "smoothness is independent of the parametrization" and "**$C^{\infty}$**-compatible" are the same sentence (`def.orientable-surface`).
