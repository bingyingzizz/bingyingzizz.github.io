## Statement · **draft**
If $D$ is a divisible group and $H \subseteq D$ is a subgroup, then $D/H$ is divisible.

(The same argument works for divisible modules over any ring; over $\mathbb{Z}$, "divisible" is "injective", so this also shows that a quotient of an injective $\mathbb{Z}$-module is still injective.)
