## Statement · **draft**
**What it does**: it gives a curve a ruler — accumulate the speed along the curve to get its length, then use length as the parameter to rewrite the curve at constant speed.

Let $\gamma : [a,b]\to\mathbb{R}^{3}$ be a $C^{1}$ curve. Its **arc length** is

$$L(\gamma) := \int_{a}^{b}\lVert\gamma'(t)\rVert\,\mathrm{d}t ,\qquad \lVert\gamma'(t)\rVert = \sqrt{x'(t)^{2}+y'(t)^{2}+z'(t)^{2}} .$$
