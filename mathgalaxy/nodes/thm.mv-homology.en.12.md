# Mayer–Vietoris sequence in homology　`thm.mv-homology`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=thm.mv-homology)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./thm.mv-homology.md)

**Injectivity.** Suppose $z \in S^{\mathcal{U}}_{p}(X)$ is a cycle and $z = \partial b$ ($b \in S_{p+1}(X)$). Choose $M$ so that, for every simplex in $z$, $T z$, and $b$, the $M$-fold subdivision is small. Then $z = \operatorname{Sd}^{M} z - \partial T_{M} z$, with both $\operatorname{Sd}^{M} z$ and $T_{M} z$ in the small-chain complex. Also, $\operatorname{Sd}^{M} z = \operatorname{Sd}^{M}\partial b = \partial(\operatorname{Sd}^{M} b)$, and $\operatorname{Sd}^{M} b$ is small as well. Thus in $H_{p}(S^{\mathcal{U}}_{*}(X))$ we have $[z] = [\operatorname{Sd}^{M} z] = 0$.
