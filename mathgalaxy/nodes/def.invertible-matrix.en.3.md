# invertible matrix　`def.invertible-matrix`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=def.invertible-matrix)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./def.invertible-matrix.md)

⭐ $\operatorname{GL}_n(K)$ is the **unit group** of the matrix ring $M_n(K)$, and the prototype of an algebraic group: it is the **principal open set** $\det A \ne 0$ in $K^{n^{2}}$ (not a closed algebraic set — $A^{-1}$ is not a polynomial coordinate on $K^{n^{2}}$); the affine-algebraic remedy is to **add one coordinate** $t$ and cut out $\det A \cdot t = 1$ — this connects with algebraic geometry and Lie groups.
