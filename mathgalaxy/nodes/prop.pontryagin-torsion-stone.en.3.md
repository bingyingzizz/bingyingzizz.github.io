## Notes · **draft**
**Why torsion corresponds to extremal disconnectedness.** An element of order $n$, denoted by $x$, corresponds to an evaluation on $\widehat{M}$ satisfying $n\chi(x) = 0$. Its values lie in a finite subgroup $\mathbb{Z}/n \subseteq \mathbb{T}$.

Finite discrete spaces are **extremally disconnected**. Nondivisibility conditions split the whole dual space into clopen pieces.
