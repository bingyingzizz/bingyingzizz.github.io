陈述

设 AAA 是含幺环，MMM 是 AAA-模。加法子群 N⊆MN \subseteq MN⊆M 若满足 a⋅n∈Nfor all a∈A, n∈N,a \cdot n \in N \qquad \text{for all } a \in A,\ n \in N,a⋅n∈Nfor all a∈A, n∈N, 则称 NNN 是 MMM 的 AAA-子模。等价地，NNN 在限制作用下本身是 AAA-模，且包含映射 N↪MN \hookrightarrow MN↪M 是模同态。

模同态的核与像都是子模；AAA 的左理想正是左 AAA-模 AAA 的子模。

说明

⭐ 三层对照：子群（群）⊂\subset⊂ 子模（模）⊂\subset⊂ 子空间（域上的模）。子模要求对环作用封闭，比「加法子群」多一条。

⭐ 例：A=ZA = \mathbb{Z}A=Z 时子模就是子群 nZn\mathbb{Z}nZ；A=K[x]A = K[x]A=K[x] 时子模是 K[x]K[x]K[x]-子空间。

⭐ 子模格：任意交仍是子模，但并一般不是；由 SSS 生成的子模是含 SSS 的最小子模。商模按 M/NM/NM/N 构造（见 商模，后续节点）。

Statement draft

Let AAA be a ring with identity and MMM an AAA-module. If an additive subgroup N⊆MN \subseteq MN⊆M satisfies

a⋅n∈Nfor all a∈A, n∈N,a \cdot n \in N \qquad \text{for all } a \in A,\ n \in N,a⋅n∈Nfor all a∈A, n∈N,

then NNN is a submodule of MMM, namely an AAA-submodule. Equivalently, NNN is itself an AAA-module under the restricted action, and the inclusion N↪MN \hookrightarrow MN↪M is a module homomorphism.

Kernels and images of module homomorphisms are submodules; a left ideal of AAA is exactly a submodule of the left AAA-module AAA.

Notes draft

⭐ Three levels of comparison: subgroup (group) ⊂\subset⊂ submodule (module) ⊂\subset⊂ subspace (module over a field). A submodule is required to be closed under the ring action, one condition more than an additive subgroup.

⭐ Examples: for A=ZA = \mathbb{Z}A=Z a submodule is just a subgroup nZn\mathbb{Z}nZ; for A=K[x]A = K[x]A=K[x] a submodule is a K[x]K[x]K[x]-subspace.

⭐ The submodule lattice: arbitrary intersections are submodules, but unions usually are not; the submodule generated by SSS is the smallest submodule containing SSS. Quotient modules are constructed as M/NM/NM/N (see 商模, a later node).
