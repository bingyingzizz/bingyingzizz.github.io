**Curvature form**: on a general manifold the right-hand side of the second equation is nonzero; that term is called the curvature form $\Omega^{i}_{\ j} := \mathrm{d}\omega^{i}_{\ j}+\sum_{k}\omega^{i}_{\ k}\wedge\omega^{k}_{\ j}$;

on $\mathbb{R}^{3}$, $\Omega \equiv 0$.

Restricted to a surface it becomes $\Omega^{i}_{\ j} = \omega^{i}_{\ 3}\wedge\omega^{j}_{\ 3}$ — this is the Gauss equation.
