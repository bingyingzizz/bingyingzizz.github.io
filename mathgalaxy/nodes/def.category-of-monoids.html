又称幺半群范畴·Mon

陈述

幺半群范畴 Mon\mathbf{Mon}Mon：对象是幺半群，态射是幺半群同态。终对象是平凡幺半群 {e}\{e\}{e}；积逐点构造；等化子是子幺半群；拉回是纤维积。

余积是自由积（把字并置后取商使单位与运算相容）；始对象仍是平凡幺半群（与终对象同构）；余等化子是商幺半群（生成同余的闭包）；推出是 amalgamated free product。

说明

完备且余完备：上述构造逐项给出小极限与小余极限；遗忘函子 Mon→Set\mathbf{Mon}\to\mathbf{Set}Mon→Set 保极限。

与 Grp\mathbf{Grp}Grp：群是全子范畴（每个元素可逆的幺半群），包含函子有左伴随「群化」。

非加性：hom⁡(M,N)\hom(M,N)hom(M,N) 一般没有幺半群结构；自由幺半群就是字幺半群。笛卡尔闭性本图不展开。

Statement draft

The category of monoids Mon\mathbf{Mon}Mon: objects are monoids and morphisms are monoid homomorphisms. The terminal object is the trivial monoid {e}\{e\}{e}; products are constructed pointwise; equalizers are submonoids; pullbacks are fiber products.

Coproducts are free products (juxtapose words, then quotient so that the identity and the operation are respected); the initial object is again the trivial monoid (isomorphic to the terminal object); coequalizers are quotient monoids (the closure of the generated congruence); pushouts are amalgamated free products.

Notes draft

Complete and cocomplete: the constructions above give small limits and small colimits term by term; the forgetful functor Mon→Set\mathbf{Mon}\to\mathbf{Set}Mon→Set preserves limits.

Relation to Grp\mathbf{Grp}Grp: groups form a full subcategory (monoids in which every element is invertible), and the inclusion has a left adjoint, “groupification”.

Not additive: hom⁡(M,N)\hom(M,N)hom(M,N) has no monoid structure in general; the free monoid is the word monoid. Cartesian closedness is not developed here.
