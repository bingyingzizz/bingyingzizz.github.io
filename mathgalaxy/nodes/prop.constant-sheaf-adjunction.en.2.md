# Adjunction for Constant Sheaves　`prop.constant-sheaf-adjunction`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=prop.constant-sheaf-adjunction)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./prop.constant-sheaf-adjunction.md)

$$\operatorname{Hom}\bigl(\widetilde{E}_{\mathcal{C}},\ F\bigr) \;\cong\; \operatorname{Hom}_{\mathbf{Set}}\bigl(E,\ \operatorname{Hom}(\mathbf{1}_{\mathcal{C}}, F)\bigr) \;\cong\; \operatorname{Hom}(\mathbf{1}_{\mathcal{C}}, F)^{E},$$

where $\mathbf{1}_{\mathcal{C}}$ is the terminal object.
