## Statement · **reviewed**
Let $E \subseteq X \times Y$. For $x \in X$ and $y \in Y$ define

$$E_x := \{y \in Y : (x, y) \in E\}$$

$$E^y := \{x \in X : (x, y) \in E\}$$

called respectively the **vertical section** of $E$ at $x$ and the **horizontal section** at $y$.

Similarly, for a function $f : X \times Y \to \mathbb{C}$ define

$$f_x(y) := f^y(x) := f(x, y)$$

## Notes · **reviewed**
Intuition: cut a plane figure with a vertical (or horizontal) line; the segment you cut out is the section.
