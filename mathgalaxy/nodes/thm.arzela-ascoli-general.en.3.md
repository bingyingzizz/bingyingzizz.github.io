⚠️ "Pointwise totally bounded" cannot be replaced by "pointwise bounded": when $E$ is incomplete, relative compactness needs "coverable", whereas boundedness only says "does not diverge".

Together with "$C(K,E)$ is complete" this is the standard form: **totally bounded + complete $=$ relatively compact**.
