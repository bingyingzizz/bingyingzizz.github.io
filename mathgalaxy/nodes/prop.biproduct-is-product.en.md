## Statement · **draft**
Let $\mathcal{C}$ be a preadditive category and $M, M_{1}, M_{2} \in \mathcal{C}$.

$M$ **is the direct sum of $M_{1}, M_{2}$** $\iff$ $(M, p_{1}, p_{2})$ **is the product of $M_{1}, M_{2}$**
