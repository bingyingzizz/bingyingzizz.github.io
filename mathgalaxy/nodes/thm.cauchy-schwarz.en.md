## Statement · **draft**
Let $V$ be an inner product space and $u, v \in V$. Then

$$|\langle u, v \rangle| \le \|u\|\,\|v\|$$

with equality if and only if $u, v$ are **linearly dependent**.

## Notes · **draft**
⭐ This is the only step on the road from the inner product to $\implies$ the norm that needs proof (the triangle inequality is derived from it).
