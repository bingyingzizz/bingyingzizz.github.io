# One-Point Compactification　`def.one-point-compactification`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=def.one-point-compactification)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./def.one-point-compactification.md)

⚠️ **Why local compactness is indispensable**: when $X$ is not locally compact, $X^{*}$ is not Hausdorff — intuitively, "a neighbourhood of $\infty$" is too large and always meets the neighbourhood of some point. So existence and Hausdorffness of the one-point compactification are **two separate matters**: $X^{*}$ is always compact, but making it Hausdorff costs local compactness.
