# law of quadratic reciprocity　`thm.quadratic-reciprocity`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=thm.quadratic-reciprocity)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./thm.quadratic-reciprocity.md)

$$\sum_{x} x \equiv \sum_{x} x + \sum_{x} m_{x} + \mu \pmod 2 ,$$

Canceling $\sum_{x} x$ gives $\sum_{x} m_{x} \equiv \mu \pmod 2$. Hence

$$\left(\frac{q}{p}\right) = (-1)^{\mu} = (-1)^{\sum_{x=1}^{P} \lfloor qx/p \rfloor} .$$
