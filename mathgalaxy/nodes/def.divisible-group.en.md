## Statement · **draft**
An abelian group $D$ is **divisible** if for every $d \in D$ and every $0 \ne n \in \mathbb{Z}$ the equation

$$n x = d$$

has a solution in $D$; equivalently, the multiplication map $n \cdot : D \to D$ is **surjective** for every $n \ne 0$.
