# Filter and Ultrafilter　`def.filter`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=def.filter)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./def.filter.md)

**Two ways of writing convergence**: $\mathcal{F} \to x$ $\iff$ $\mathcal{N}(x) \subseteq \mathcal{F}$ $\iff$ $\mathcal{F}$, which is **finer** than the neighbourhood filter. ⚠️ The direction is easy to invert: **the larger (finer) the filter, the easier it converges**.

**The ultrafilter lemma (every filter lies in an ultrafilter) needs Zorn's lemma** — the only dependence on AC in this chain. It also makes the characterization "compact $\iff$ every ultrafilter converges" **non-constructive**.

⚠️ Unrelated to "filteredness" in category theory (`def.filtered-category`); the two share only the word "filter".
