# 同调的短正合列　`prop.cohomology-exact-sequence`

设 $K$ 是复形。则对每个 $k$ 有正合列

$$0 \longrightarrow H^{k}(K) \longrightarrow \operatorname{coker} d^{k-1} \longrightarrow \ker d^{k+1} \longrightarrow H^{k+1}(K) \longrightarrow 0$$
