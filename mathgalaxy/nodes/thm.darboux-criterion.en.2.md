## Notes · **draft**
⭐ This replaces "the limit exists" by "the two suprema coincide": the former requires discussing the choice of sample points, the latter **does not mention sample points at all** — that is the whole reason the Darboux form is convenient.

⭐ Monotonicity of the two bounds: if $P'$ is a refinement of $P$, then $L(f, P) \le L(f, P') \le U(f, P') \le U(f, P)$ (on each subinterval $m_i$ can only increase and $M_i$ can only decrease).

Hence for **any** two partitions $P, Q$ one has $L(f, P) \le U(f, Q)$: take the common refinement $P \cup Q$.
