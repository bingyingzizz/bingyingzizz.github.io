# Frenet–Serret Formulas　`thm.frenet-serret`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=thm.frenet-serret)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./thm.frenet-serret.md)

The surface quantities $\omega^{i}_{\ j}$ (`def.connection-form`) degenerate in the curve case to these three numbers times $\mathrm{d}s$.

**Where antisymmetry comes from**: $\langle T,T\rangle = \langle N,N\rangle = \langle B,B\rangle = 1$ and $\langle T,N\rangle = \langle T,B\rangle = \langle N,B\rangle = 0$ are all constants; differentiating gives the antisymmetry of the matrix — literally the same proof as `prop.connection-antisymmetric`.

⚠ It holds only where $\kappa>0$. At an isolated point with $\kappa = 0$, $N$ does not exist and the formula is meaningless (although $\kappa$ itself is still defined).
