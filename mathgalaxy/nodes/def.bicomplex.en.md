## Statement · **draft**
A **bicomplex** in an additive category is a **complex of complexes**: that is, on $(\mathbb{Z}, \preceq)^{2}$ a diagram $(K^{p,q}, d^{p,q}, d'^{p,q})$ such that for all $p, q$

$$d^{p+1,q} \circ d^{p,q} = 0, \qquad d'^{p,q+1} \circ d'^{p,q} = 0, \qquad d'^{p+1,q} \circ d^{p,q} = d^{p,q+1} \circ d'^{p,q}.$$
