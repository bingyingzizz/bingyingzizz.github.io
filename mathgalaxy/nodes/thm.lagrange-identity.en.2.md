# Lagrange’s Identity　`thm.lagrange-identity`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=thm.lagrange-identity)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./thm.lagrange-identity.md)

For $u, w \in \mathbb{R}^{n}$ (or a real inner product space with an orthonormal basis) we have the **Lagrange identity** (⚠ the coordinate expression on the right equals the left side only in an orthonormal basis: with $\langle x,y\rangle = 4x_{1}y_{1} + x_{2}y_{2}$, $u = (1,0)$, $w = (0,1)$, the left side is $= 4$ while the right is $= 1$; in a general basis one corrects by the Gram matrix $G$)

$$\lVert u\rVert^{2}\lVert w\rVert^{2} - \langle u, w\rangle^{2} = \sum_{1 \le i < j \le n}\bigl(u_{i}w_{j} - u_{j}w_{i}\bigr)^{2} .$$
