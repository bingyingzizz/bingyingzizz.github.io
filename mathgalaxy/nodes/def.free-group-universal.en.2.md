## Notes · **draft**
**Meaning**: giving a homomorphism out of the free group is equivalent to giving an arbitrary map from the generating set to the target group; there are no relations among the generators to satisfy.

**Uniqueness**: by $\tilde f(s)=\iota(s)$ and homomorphy, $\tilde f(s^{\varepsilon})=f(s)^{\varepsilon}$, so $\tilde f$ is uniquely determined on the generators; existence is verified directly by multiplication of words.
