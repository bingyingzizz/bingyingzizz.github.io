## Statement · **draft**
Let $\mathcal{A}$ be an abelian category and $I \in \mathcal{A}$. The following are equivalent:

1. $I$ is an **injective object**;
2. every monomorphism $I \rightarrowtail M$ admits a **retraction** $M \to I$;
3. the functor $\operatorname{Hom}_{\mathcal{A}}(-, I)$ is **exact**;
4. every short exact sequence $0 \to I \to M \to M'' \to 0$ **splits**.
