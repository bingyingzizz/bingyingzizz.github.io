## Statement · **draft**
$$\forall A ( A \notin A )$$

More generally, the Axiom of Foundation rules out every finite $\in$-cycle:

$$A_0 \ni  A_1 \ni  \cdots \ni  A_n = A_0$$

## Notes · **draft**
This theorem shows that the set $R = \{ x : x \notin x \}$ from Russell's paradox cannot exist — otherwise $R \in R$ and $R \notin R$ would both hold.
