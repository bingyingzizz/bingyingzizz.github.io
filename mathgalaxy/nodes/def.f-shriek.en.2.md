# compactly supported pushforward　`def.f-shriek`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=def.f-shriek)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./def.f-shriek.md)

Let $f : X \to Y$ be a separated morphism of finite type, with corresponding diagram of adic spaces

$$\begin{array}{ccc} X_{\mathrm{ad}} & \xrightarrow{\ j\ } & X_{\mathrm{ad}/Y} \\ & f_{\mathrm{ad}} \searrow & \downarrow f_{\mathrm{ad}/Y} \\ & & Y_{\mathrm{ad}} \end{array}$$

where $j$ is an open immersion (an isomorphism when $f$ is proper). Define

$$f_! := (f_{\mathrm{ad}/Y})_* \circ j_! : D(\mathcal{O}_{X,\blacksquare}) \longrightarrow D(\mathcal{O}_{Y,\blacksquare}).$$

It preserves all direct sums, hence has a right adjoint $f^!$. The six functors are now complete.
