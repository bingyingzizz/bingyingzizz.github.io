Statement

Let K∙∈C(A)K^{\bullet} \in C(\mathcal{A})K∙∈C(A) and I∙∈C+(I)I^{\bullet} \in C^{+}(\mathcal{I})I∙∈C+(I) (bounded-above injective complexes). Then the map given by the localization functor

Hom⁡K(A)(K∙,I∙)→  ∼  Hom⁡D(A)(K∙,I∙)\operatorname{Hom}_{\mathbf{K}(\mathcal{A})}(K^{\bullet}, I^{\bullet}) \xrightarrow{\;\sim\;} \operatorname{Hom}_{D(\mathcal{A})}(K^{\bullet}, I^{\bullet})HomK(A)​(K∙,I∙)∼​HomD(A)​(K∙,I∙)

is a bijection. Dually: the same holds with projective complexes as source (P∙∈C−(P)P^{\bullet} \in C^{-}(\mathcal{P})P∙∈C−(P)).

Notes

The lemma to be used. If s:K′∙→K∙s : K'^{\bullet} \to K^{\bullet}s:K′∙→K∙ is a quasi-isomorphism and I∙∈C+(I)I^{\bullet} \in C^{+}(\mathcal{I})I∙∈C+(I), then "right multiplication by sss"

Hom⁡K(K∙,I∙)→  ∘s  Hom⁡K(K′∙,I∙)\operatorname{Hom}_{\mathbf{K}}(K^{\bullet}, I^{\bullet}) \xrightarrow{\;\circ s\;} \operatorname{Hom}_{\mathbf{K}}(K'^{\bullet}, I^{\bullet})HomK​(K∙,I∙)∘s​HomK​(K′∙,I∙)

is a bijection.

Proof: complete sss to a triangle K′∙→K∙→M∙→K′∙[1]K'^{\bullet} \to K^{\bullet} \to M^{\bullet} \to K'^{\bullet}[1]K′∙→K∙→M∙→K′∙[1] (M∙M^{\bullet}M∙ acyclic) and take the long exact sequence for the cohomological functor Hom⁡K(−,I∙)\operatorname{Hom}_{\mathbf{K}}(-, I^{\bullet})HomK​(−,I∙), obtaining

Hom⁡K(M∙[−1],I∙)→Hom⁡K(K∙,I∙)→  ∘s  Hom⁡K(K′∙,I∙)→Hom⁡K(M∙,I∙),\operatorname{Hom}_{\mathbf{K}}(M^{\bullet}[-1], I^{\bullet}) \to \operatorname{Hom}_{\mathbf{K}}(K^{\bullet}, I^{\bullet}) \xrightarrow{\;\circ s\;} \operatorname{Hom}_{\mathbf{K}}(K'^{\bullet}, I^{\bullet}) \to \operatorname{Hom}_{\mathbf{K}}(M^{\bullet}, I^{\bullet}),HomK​(M∙[−1],I∙)→HomK​(K∙,I∙)∘s​HomK​(K′∙,I∙)→HomK​(M∙,I∙),

then kill both ends using "a morphism from an acyclic source to a bounded-above injective complex is homotopic to zero" (construct a homotopy by lifting layer by layer, sn+1∘dn=fn−dn−1∘sns^{n+1} \circ d^{n} = f^{n} - d^{n-1}\circ s^{n}sn+1∘dn=fn−dn−1∘sn).

Surjectivity. A morphism in DDD is a roof K∙←  s  K′∙→  q  I∙K^{\bullet} \xleftarrow{\;s\;} K'^{\bullet} \xrightarrow{\;q\;} I^{\bullet}K∙s​K′∙q​I∙ (sss a quasi-isomorphism).

By the lemma "right multiplication by sss" is surjective, so there exists p:K∙→I∙p : K^{\bullet} \to I^{\bullet}p:K∙→I∙ with ps≃qp s \simeq qps≃q — the roof and the direct morphism ppp represent the same morphism in DDD.

Injectivity. If p,p′p, p'p,p′ are equal in DDD, there is a quasi-isomorphism sss with ps≃p′sp s \simeq p' sps≃p′s (the equivalence relation on roofs is by definition "there exists a common refinement"); by the lemma "right multiplication by sss" is injective, hence p≃p′p \simeq p'p≃p′. ∎

⭐ Use: this is the common foundation of "bounded-above injective complexes are the derived category" and "computing RHom⁡R\operatorname{Hom}RHom by injective resolutions" — with I∙I^{\bullet}I∙ as target, homotopy classes and derived morphisms coincide, so one may compute in K\mathbf{K}K without worry.
