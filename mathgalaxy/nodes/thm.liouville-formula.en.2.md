# Liouville's Formula　`thm.liouville-formula`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=thm.liouville-formula)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./thm.liouville-formula.md)

Let $(u,v)$ be an **orthogonal** coordinate net on $S$ ($F = 0$, $I = E\,\mathrm{d}u^{2}+G\,\mathrm{d}v^{2}$). Let $\gamma$ be parametrized by arc length, and let $\theta(s)$ be the angle between $\dot\gamma(s)$ and the $u$ coordinate line (in the direction $\partial_{u}$). Then

$$k_{g}(\gamma) = \frac{\mathrm{d}\theta}{\mathrm{d}s}-\frac{(\ln E)_{v}}{2\sqrt{G}}\cos\theta+\frac{(\ln G)_{u}}{2\sqrt{E}}\sin\theta.$$
