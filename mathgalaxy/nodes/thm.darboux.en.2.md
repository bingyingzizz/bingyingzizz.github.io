# Darboux’s Theorem　`thm.darboux`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=thm.darboux)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./thm.darboux.md)

Let $(M^{2n}, \omega)$ be a symplectic manifold and let $p \in M$. Then there is a coordinate neighborhood of $p$ and coordinates $(q_{1}, \ldots, q_{n}, p_{1}, \ldots, p_{n})$ such that

$$\omega = \sum_{i=1}^{n} dq_{i} \wedge dp_{i}$$

holds on that neighborhood. These are called **Darboux coordinates** (canonical coordinates).

**Contrast with Riemannian geometry**: A Riemannian metric **generally cannot** be put in such a form. Curvature is a local invariant, and only metrics with **identically zero curvature** (flat metrics) can be put locally in the form $\delta_{ij}$. A symplectic form **always** admits its local normal form.
