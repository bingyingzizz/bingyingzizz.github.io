# Jordan–Hölder theorem　`thm.jordan-holder`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=thm.jordan-holder)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./thm.jordan-holder.md)

Example: $\mathbb{Z}/12$ and $\mathbb{Z}/2 \times \mathbb{Z}/6$ both have composition factors $\mathbb{Z}/2, \mathbb{Z}/2, \mathbb{Z}/3$, but the former has an element of order $12$ and the latter does not, so they are not isomorphic.

That is, composition factors are an **invariant, not a complete invariant**.

⭐ The proof proceeds by **refinement**: first show that any two normal series have equivalent refinements (Schreier's refinement theorem), then use the Zassenhaus (butterfly) lemma to make the refinements.
