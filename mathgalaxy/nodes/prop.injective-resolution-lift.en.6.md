# Lifting Maps to Injective Resolutions　`prop.injective-resolution-lift`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=prop.injective-resolution-lift)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./prop.injective-resolution-lift.md)

The difference $F - G$, on $H^{0}$, induces $0$, so the **same layer-by-layer lifting** produces a homotopy $s^{n} : I^{n}\to J^{n-1}$: at each layer the equation $F^{n}-G^{n} = s^{n+1}d^{n}_{I} + d^{n-1}_{J}s^{n}$ is a problem of "lifting along a monomorphism", again solved by the injectivity of $J^{n-1}$;

at the starting point $F^{0}-G^{0}$ vanishes on $M$, so it factors through $I^{0}/M = B^{1}_{I}$ ✓.

Hence $F \sim G$ ✓.
