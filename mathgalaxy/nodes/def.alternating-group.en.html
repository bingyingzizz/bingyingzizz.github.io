Statement

Every σ∈Sn\sigma \in S_nσ∈Sn​ can be written as a product of transpositions (swaps of two points), and the parity of the number of transpositions used does not depend on the decomposition. This defines the sign

sgn⁡(σ):=(−1)k\operatorname{sgn}(\sigma) := (-1)^{k}sgn(σ):=(−1)k

where σ\sigmaσ is a product of kkk transpositions (well-definedness is guaranteed by the parity of σ\sigmaσ).
