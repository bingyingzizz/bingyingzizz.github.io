## Statement · **draft**
1. If $X$ is weakly Hausdorff, then the diagonal $X \subseteq X \times X$ is **$k$-closed**; when $X$ is compactly generated the converse holds too;
2. if $Y$ is weakly Hausdorff and $f, g : X \to Y$ continuous, then $\ker(f, g) \subseteq X$ is **$k$-closed**.
