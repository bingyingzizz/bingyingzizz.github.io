## Notes · **draft**
⭐ This translates the **geometric** condition of convexity into the analytic condition of monotone derivative — together with “the sign of the derivative $\implies$ monotonicity” this yields the complete chain “the sign of the second derivative $\implies$ the direction in which the graph bends”.

⚠ Deciding convexity by the **sign** of the second derivative alone requires twice differentiability;

$|x|$ is convex but has no second derivative, so outside $C^{2}$ one must return to the increasing-derivative or chord criterion.
