## Notes · **draft**
The two equations point in opposite directions: a section is "first $g$, then $f$, back to $Y$", while a retraction is "first $f$, then $g$, back to $X$". In other words, a section is a right inverse of $f$ and a retraction is a left inverse of $f$ — the names differ because composition is written in the opposite order from the maps.
