Whereas $V \cong V^{\ast\ast}$ has the natural isomorphism $v \mapsto (\varphi \mapsto \varphi(v))$; this is "the dual of the dual returns to itself".

⚠ In infinite dimension $V^{\ast}$ is strictly larger than $V$: $\dim V^{\ast} = \#(K)^{\dim V}$ is a cardinality theorem of **Erdős–Kaplansky** (it uses AC; $\#(V^{\ast}) = \#(K)^{\dim V}$ is elementary, but "the dimension equals this cardinal" is not), so the above isomorphism fails.
