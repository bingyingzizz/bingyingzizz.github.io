## Statement · **draft**
The category $\mathrm{CGWH}$ of compactly generated **weak Hausdorff** spaces is a **reflective** subcategory of $\mathrm{CG}$ (compactly generated spaces).

The reflector is

$$h : X \longmapsto X/R,$$

where $R$ is the **smallest closed equivalence relation** on $X$.
