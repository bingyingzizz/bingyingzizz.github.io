## Statement · **draft**
Let $X$ be a topological space. Then $X$ is **normal** $\iff$ for any two disjoint closed sets $A, B \subseteq X$, there exists a continuous function

$$f : X \longrightarrow [0, 1], \qquad f|_{A} \equiv 0, \qquad f|_{B} \equiv 1.$$
