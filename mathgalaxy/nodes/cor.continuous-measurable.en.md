## Statement · **draft**
Let $X, Y$ be topological spaces, each equipped with its **Borel $\sigma$-algebra** $\mathfrak{B}_X, \mathfrak{B}_Y$. Then every **continuous** map is automatically Borel measurable:

$f : X \to Y$ **continuous** $\implies f$ is $(\mathfrak{B}_X, \mathfrak{B}_Y)$-**measurable**
