# Mayer–Vietoris sequence in homology　`thm.mv-homology`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=thm.mv-homology)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./thm.mv-homology.md)

(extending in both directions to give $\cdots$). The two maps that require explanation are

$$\Phi[z] := (i_{*}[z],\ -j_{*}[z]),\qquad \Psi[a, b] := [a + b].$$

On replacing $H$ by reduced homology $\tilde{H}$, the same sequence holds provided $U \cap V \ne \emptyset$ (otherwise exactness fails in the segment $\tilde{H}_{0}$. When $X = \{a, b\}$, $U = \{a\}$, and $V = \{b\}$, we have $\tilde{H}_{0}(U) = \tilde{H}_{0}(V) = 0$ while $\tilde{H}_{0}(X) = \mathbb{Z}$).
