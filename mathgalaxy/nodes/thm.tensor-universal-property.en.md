## Statement · **draft**
For every **bilinear map** $B : V \times W \to U$ (a linear map on the product space need not be bilinear, e.g. $B(v,w) = v$), there is a **unique** linear map

$$\widetilde{B} : V \otimes W \to U, \qquad \widetilde{B}(v \otimes w) = B(v, w)$$
