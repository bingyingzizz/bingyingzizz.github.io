## Statement · **draft**
A **morphism of sites** is a **continuous functor** $f^{-1} := g : \mathcal{C} \to \mathcal{C}'$ such that $f^{-1}$ is **left exact** (preserves finite limits). If $f^{-1}$ has a further right adjoint $f_{*}$, write $f^{-1} \dashv f_{*}$.
