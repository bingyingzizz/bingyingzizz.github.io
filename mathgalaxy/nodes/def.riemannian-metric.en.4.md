# Riemannian Metric　`def.riemannian-metric`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=def.riemannian-metric)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./def.riemannian-metric.md)

**The two-dimensional case is the first fundamental form**: restricting the inner product of $\mathbb{R}^{3}$ to $T_{p}S$ on a surface gives $I$. So Riemannian geometry is the higher-dimensional version of intrinsic surface geometry, and surface theory is its two-dimensional model.

**The metric determines everything else**: $g$ uniquely determines the Levi-Civita connection and the Christoffel symbols (`def.affine-connection`, `def.christoffel-symbol`), hence geodesics, the curvature tensor and Ricci are all tied to $g$ — this is the entire content of "intrinsic geometry".
