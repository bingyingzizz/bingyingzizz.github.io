Statement

In plain words: in the closed immersion case (embedding Spec⁡A\operatorname{Spec} ASpecA into Spec⁡R\operatorname{Spec} RSpecR), the dualizing complex collapses to one line: it is the "determinant of the normal bundle" shifted by the codimension.

"Regular" means that AAA is obtained from RRR by killing a regular sequence (f1,…,fcf_1, \ldots, f_cf1​,…,fc​: each fif_ifi​ is a nonzerodivisor on the quotient by the preceding ones — "clean transverse cuts, no collisions");

in that case the codimension is ccc everywhere.

Let f:R→Af : R \to Af:R→A be a regular closed immersion: fff is surjective and its kernel III is locally generated on Spec⁡R\operatorname{Spec} RSpecR by a regular sequence; and suppose Spec⁡A⊂Spec⁡R\operatorname{Spec} A \subset \operatorname{Spec} RSpecA⊂SpecR has pure codimension ccc everywhere. Then

f!R=RHom⁡R(A,R)  ≅  det⁡A(I/I2)∗[−c].f^!R = \operatorname{RHom}_R(A, R) \;\cong\; \det\nolimits_A(I/I^2)^*[-c].f!R=RHomR​(A,R)≅detA​(I/I2)∗[−c].

Notes

⭐ Locally A=R/(f1,…,fc)A = R/(f_1, \ldots, f_c)A=R/(f1​,…,fc​) (a regular sequence), and the Koszul complex computes directly that RHom⁡R(A,R)≅A[−c]\operatorname{RHom}_R(A,R) \cong A[-c]RHomR​(A,R)≅A[−c];

det⁡(I/I2)\det(I/I^2)det(I/I2) has a basis given by f1∧⋯∧fcf_1 \wedge \cdots \wedge f_cf1​∧⋯∧fc​.

The global statement must be checked to be independent of the choice of generators.

⭐ This proposition together with flat base change are the two bricks for the smooth case: embed Spec⁡A\operatorname{Spec} ASpecA into affine space (a regular closed immersion), then extract Ω\OmegaΩ using the diagonal.
