## Notes · **stale**
The matrix algebra $M_n(D)$ over a division algebra $D$ is a simple algebra and serves as the model case. A finite-dimensional simple algebra is isomorphic to a matrix algebra over a division algebra (Wedderburn). A group algebra $F[G]$ is simple only in very small cases. Simplicity is a two-sided condition and is not the same as having no nontrivial left ideal.
