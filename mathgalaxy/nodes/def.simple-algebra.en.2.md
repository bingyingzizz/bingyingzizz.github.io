## Notes · **draft**
The matrix algebra $M_n(D)$ over a division algebra $D$ is simple, and this is the model case. Every finite-dimensional simple algebra is isomorphic to a matrix algebra over a division algebra (Wedderburn). The group algebra $F[G]$ is simple only in very small cases. Simplicity is a two-sided condition and is not the same as having no nontrivial left ideals.
