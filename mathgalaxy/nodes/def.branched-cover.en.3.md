## Notes · **draft**
⭐ **Why compactness and holomorphicity are needed for this definition**: Compactness ensures a finite number of sheets and a finite $B$. Without compactness, one point can have infinitely many preimages and branch points can accumulate.

Holomorphicity (the open mapping theorem and isolation of zeros) ensures that the only local model is $z \mapsto z^{e}$. For purely topological branched coverings, finitely many sheets and a finite branch locus must be imposed as additional hypotheses.
