# Structure Equations　`thm.structure-equations`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=thm.structure-equations)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./thm.structure-equations.md)

Together they express $\mathrm{d}^{2}=0$ on the frame: applying the exterior derivative to $\mathrm{d}r$ gives the first equation, and to $\mathrm{d}e_{i}$ gives the second.

## Notes · **draft**
**Why "structure"**: these two equations determine all constraints on "how the frame moves". Given the coframe and the connection, the shape of the surface together with its position in $\mathbb{R}^{3}$ is determined; conversely the only compatibility to check is $\mathrm{d}^{2}=0$. The fundamental theorem of surface theory turns this sentence into a theorem.
