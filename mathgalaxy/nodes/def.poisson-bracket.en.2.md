# Poisson Bracket　`def.poisson-bracket`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=def.poisson-bracket)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./def.poisson-bracket.md)

$$\{f, g\} := \omega(X_{f}, X_{g}) = -\iota_{X_{f}}\iota_{X_{g}}\omega = X_{g}(f) ,$$

where $X_{f}, X_{g}$ are the Hamiltonian vector fields of $f$ and $g$. In Darboux coordinates,

$$\{f, g\} = \sum_{i=1}^{n}\Bigl(\frac{\partial f}{\partial q_{i}}\frac{\partial g}{\partial p_{i}} - \frac{\partial f}{\partial p_{i}}\frac{\partial g}{\partial q_{i}}\Bigr) .$$

**Properties**: It is bilinear and antisymmetric, satisfies the **Jacobi identity**, and acts as a **derivation** (the Leibniz rule).
