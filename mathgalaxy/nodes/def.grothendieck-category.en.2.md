# Grothendieck category　`def.grothendieck-category`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=def.grothendieck-category)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./def.grothendieck-category.md)

on a topos $\mathcal{T}$, $\mathbf{Ab}(\mathcal{T})$ is also one (see "abelian groups on a topos form a Grothendieck category").

Two facts that come with the definition: a Grothendieck category **automatically satisfies AB3\*** (it has all limits); and it **has enough injective objects**.

"Filtered colimits exact + a generator" together are enough to run a whole homological algebra — the minimal requirements Grothendieck's generation chose for "doing homological algebra on abstract categories".
