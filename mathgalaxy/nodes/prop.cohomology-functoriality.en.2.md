## Notes · **draft**
**Proof.** By the adjunction $f^{-1} \dashv f_{*}$, global sections satisfy

$$\Gamma\bigl(X',\ M\bigr) = \operatorname{Hom}(X', M) = \operatorname{Hom}\bigl(f^{-1}(X'),\ f^{-1}M\bigr) = \Gamma\bigl(f^{-1}(X'),\ f^{-1}M\bigr) ✓$$

and $f^{-1}$ is exact and preserves injectives ✓, so it sends injective resolutions to injective resolutions; taking derived functors on both sides gives the canonical map ✓. ∎
