Statement

Let (P,⪯)(P, \preceq )(P,⪯) be a poset and let C⊆PC \subseteq PC⊆P.

CCC is a chain (also called a totally ordered subset) if and only if any two elements of CCC are comparable:

∀x,y∈C(x⪯y∨y⪯x)\forall x, y \in C ( x \preceq y \vee y \preceq x )∀x,y∈C(x⪯y∨y⪯x)

CCC is an antichain if and only if any two distinct elements of CCC are incomparable.

Convention: ∅\emptyset∅ and every singleton count as chains.

Notes

Chain is the central notion in Zorn's lemma and the Hausdorff maximal principle — both ask how large a chain can grow.
