# Jordan canonical form　`thm.jordan-canonical-form`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=thm.jordan-canonical-form)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./thm.jordan-canonical-form.md)

⭐ The two halves of the proof: primary decomposition (split by eigenvalue) plus, on each root subspace, taking "the chain $\ker N^{k}$" of the nilpotent operator $N = T - \lambda I$ and extending the basis layer by layer (this step uses `def.quotient-vs` and induction).

⚠ All three hypotheses are needed: **algebraically closed** (otherwise there is no $\lambda$), **finite-dimensional** (otherwise there may be no eigenvalue), and **$K$ a field**.

Over $\mathbb{R}$ one can only use the "real Jordan form" or switch to the rational canonical form (`thm.rational-canonical-form`).
