## Notes · **draft**
**Proof.** Sheafification is **exact** (it preserves both finite limits and colimits) ✓, so it sends the image in presheaves (of the form $\ker(\operatorname{coker})$) termwise; the image in the category of sheaves is likewise given by $\operatorname{im} = \ker(\operatorname{coker})$ ✓, and the two routes arrive at the same place ✓. ∎
