## Notes · **draft**
⭐ In one sentence: **"independent" is never larger than "generating"**.

It is the entire content of the well-definedness of dimension — the two statements ($n \le m$ and exchangeability) are proved together by one induction.

⭐ The proof inducts on $n$: express $v_1$ in terms of $T$ ($T$ generates), and one of the coefficients must be nonzero (otherwise $v_1 = 0$ contradicts independence); replace it by $v_1$ and the set still generates;

then repeat for the rest.

⚠ Finiteness is a hypothesis: this only covers **finite** independent and generating sets.
