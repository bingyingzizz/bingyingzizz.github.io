## Statement · **draft**
Let $M$ and $N$ be two $n$-dimensional **connected** topological manifolds ($n \ge 1$). Choose an open ball in $M$, denoted $B_{M}$, and an open ball in $N$, denoted $B_{N}$. Remove their interiors to obtain two manifolds with spherical boundary:

$$M^{\circ} := M \setminus \operatorname{int} B_{M}, \qquad N^{\circ} := N \setminus \operatorname{int} B_{N},$$
