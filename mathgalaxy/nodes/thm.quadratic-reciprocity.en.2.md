# law of quadratic reciprocity　`thm.quadratic-reciprocity`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=thm.quadratic-reciprocity)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./thm.quadratic-reciprocity.md)

Equivalently, if at least one of $p, q$ is $\equiv 1 \pmod 4$, then $\left(\frac{p}{q}\right) = \left(\frac{q}{p}\right)$. If both are $\equiv 3 \pmod 4$, the two values have opposite signs. The supplementary formulas are

$$\left(\frac{-1}{p}\right) = (-1)^{\frac{p-1}{2}}, \qquad \left(\frac{2}{p}\right) = (-1)^{\frac{p^{2}-1}{8}} .$$
