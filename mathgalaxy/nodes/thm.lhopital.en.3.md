# l'Hôpital's rule　`thm.lhopital`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=thm.lhopital)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./thm.lhopital.md)

The last one is the most dangerous: when $\lim f'/g'$ does not exist, the right side need not equal the left — apart from saying nothing, one can say nothing (a typical example: $f = x + \sin x$, $g = x$).

⚠ The proof for the "$\infty/\infty$ type" is a little more involved than for the "$0/0$ type" (one first truncates using the monotonicity of $g$), but the conclusion is the same.
