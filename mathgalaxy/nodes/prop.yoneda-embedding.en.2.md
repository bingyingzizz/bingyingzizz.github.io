## Abstract · **reviewed**
The assignment $\delta : \mathcal{C} \to \widehat{\mathcal{C}}$, $X \mapsto h_X = \operatorname{Hom}_{\mathcal{C}}(-, X)$, is fully faithful and preserves all limits. Fully faithful means $\operatorname{Hom}_{\widehat{\mathcal{C}}}(h_X, h_Y) \cong \operatorname{Hom}_{\mathcal{C}}(X, Y)$: the arrows between representable functors reproduce the arrows of $\mathcal{C}$ exactly, no more and no fewer. Embedding $\mathcal{C}$ this way moves problems into a category that has all limits and colimits.
