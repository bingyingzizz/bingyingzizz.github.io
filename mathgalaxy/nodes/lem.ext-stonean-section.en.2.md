# Sections of Ext on Stonean Spaces　`lem.ext-stonean-section`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=lem.ext-stonean-section)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./lem.ext-stonean-section.md)

$$\operatorname{Ext}^{n}_{\mathbb{Z}}(M, N)(S) \cong \operatorname{Ext}^{n}_{\mathbb{Z}}(M \cdot S,\ N).$$

## Notes · **stale**
**What it does.** The left side is "the sections of the Ext sheaf over $S$", the right side is "an ordinary Ext computed out". It trades the **sheaf** Ext for a **single** Ext — so "computing an Ext sheaf" becomes "computing one concrete derived Hom".
