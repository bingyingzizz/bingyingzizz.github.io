## Notes · **draft**
**What it does.** The left side is "the sections of the Ext sheaf over $S$", the right side is "an ordinary Ext computed out". It trades the **sheaf** Ext for a **single** Ext — so "computing an Ext sheaf" becomes "computing one concrete derived Hom".

**Why Stonean is enough.** Over a Stonean space $S$, the group $\mathbb{Z}[S]$ is **projective**, hence $\operatorname{Hom}_{\mathbb{Z}}(\mathbb{Z}[S], -)$ is exact. Apply this exact functor to both sides of the natural isomorphism
