## Statement · **draft**
Let $(V,\rho)$ and $(W,\sigma)$ be representations of a group $G$. The tensor product $V\otimes_F W$ carries the action $g(v\otimes w)=\rho(g)v\otimes\sigma(g)w$, called the tensor product representation of $G$. For a general $F$-algebra $A$ one needs an algebra homomorphism $A\to A\otimes A$ for such an action; the group case uses the diagonal.
