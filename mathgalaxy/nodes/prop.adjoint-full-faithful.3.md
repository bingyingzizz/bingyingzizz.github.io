## English abstract · reviewed by bingyingzizz (2026-10-16) · Chinese is authoritative（续）
For an adjunction $F \dashv G$ with unit $\eta$ and counit $\varepsilon$: $F$ is full and faithful iff $\eta$ is an isomorphism, and $G$ is full and faithful iff $\varepsilon$ is an isomorphism. So fullness and faithfulness of the left adjoint is checked on a single natural transformation rather than on every Hom-set. This is also the dividing line between reflections and coreflections: an inclusion of a full subcategory is always fully faithful, so a reflection has an invertible counit.
