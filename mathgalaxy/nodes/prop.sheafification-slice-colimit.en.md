## Statement · **draft**
Let $T$ be a presheaf on a site $\mathcal{C}$. Then its sheafification is the colimit over the slice category

$$T^{\#} \;\cong\; \varinjlim_{X \in \mathcal{C}/T}\ X^{\sharp},$$
