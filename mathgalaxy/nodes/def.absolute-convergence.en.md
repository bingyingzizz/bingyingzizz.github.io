## Statement · **draft**
Let $\{a_n\} \subseteq \mathbb{R}$ (or $\mathbb{C}$). The series $\sum a_n$ is said to **converge absolutely** iff the series of positive terms

$$\sum_{n=1}^{\infty} |a_n|$$

converges.
