Statement

Let TTT be a presheaf on a site C\mathcal{C}C. Then its sheafification is the colimit over the slice category

T#  ≅  lim→⁡X∈C/T X♯,T^{\#} \;\cong\; \varinjlim_{X \in \mathcal{C}/T}\ X^{\sharp},T#≅X∈C/Tlim​​ X♯,

where each XXX is regarded as its associated sheaf X♯=hX#X^{\sharp} = h_{X}^{\#}X♯=hX#​.

Notes

Proof. T=lim→⁡(X→T)hXT = \varinjlim_{(X \to T)} h_{X}T=lim​(X→T)​hX​ ✓ (the colimit given by the category of elements), sheafification preserves colimits ✓, and hX#=X♯h_{X}^{\#} = X^{\sharp}hX#​=X♯ ✓, hence T#=lim→⁡X♯T^{\#} = \varinjlim X^{\sharp}T#=lim​X♯ ✓. ∎

⭐ Reading: sheafification is "splitting TTT according to its elements, sheafifying each associated sheaf, and gluing back".
