## Statement · **draft**
Let $S$ be a **compact Hausdorff** space and $X$ a (semi)metric space (resp. complete). Then the **compact-open topology** on the continuous function space $C(S, X)$ comes from a (semi)metric (resp. and is complete), namely

$$d(f, g) := \sup_{s \in S}\ d\bigl(f(s),\ g(s)\bigr).$$
