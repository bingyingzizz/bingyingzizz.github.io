# Maximum Modulus Principle　`thm.max-modulus`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=thm.max-modulus)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./thm.max-modulus.md)

⚠ There is no analogue for the **minimum** of $|f|$: $f(z) = z$ attains its minimum on the unit disk at $0$ (an interior point).

The correct minimum modulus principle: if $f$ is holomorphic and **nowhere zero**, apply the maximum modulus principle to $1/f$.
