## Statement · **draft**
Let $V$ be a $K$-vector space, and $\mathcal{B}$, $\mathcal{B}'$ two bases of $V$. Then $\mathcal{B}$ and $\mathcal{B}'$ are **equipotent** (there is a bijection).

Hence the **dimension** of $V$ is well defined:

$$\dim_K V := \#\mathcal{B}$$

(by convention $\dim V = 0$ when $V = \{0\}$; when there is no finite basis, $V$ is called **infinite-dimensional**.)
