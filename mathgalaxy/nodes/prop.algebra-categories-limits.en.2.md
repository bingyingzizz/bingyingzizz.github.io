## Statement · **stale**
Let $\mathcal C$ be one of $\mathbf{Mon}$, $\mathbf{Grp}$, $\mathbf{Rng}$, $G\text{-}\mathbf{Set}$, $R\text{-}\mathbf{Mod}$, $R\text{-}\mathbf{Alg}$, $\mathbf{Vect}_K$, $\mathbf{Set}$, $\mathbf{Top}$, $\mathbf{Ab}$. Then $\mathcal C$ is complete: terminal objects, products, equalizers and pullbacks are all constructed pointwise on underlying sets; the forgetful functor $U:\mathcal C\to\mathbf{Set}$ preserves limits. Colimits are treated in `prop.algebra-categories-colimits`.
