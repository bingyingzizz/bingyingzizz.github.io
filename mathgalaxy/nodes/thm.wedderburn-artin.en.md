## Statement · **draft**
Let $A$ be a finite-dimensional semisimple $F$-algebra. Then $A\cong\prod_{i=1}^s M_{n_i}(D_i)$ with division algebras $D_i$; equivalently, every finite-dimensional $A$-module is semisimple.
