In one sentence: the whole of surface theory is "read what happens on the surface through objects on the parameter space UUU" — this is also the starting point of the chapter. The route here turns it into identities of 111-forms (expanding dr\mathrm{d}rdr, dei\mathrm{d}e_{i}dei​ in the frame) rather than differentiating rrr with respect to the parameters.

The tangent map is not the identity on the tangent plane: it is a linear injection from the tangent space of the parameter domain into R3\mathbb{R}^{3}R3, whose image is exactly the tangent plane.
