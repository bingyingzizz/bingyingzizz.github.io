# Implications among the AB Axioms　`prop.ab5-implications`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=prop.ab5-implications)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./prop.ab5-implications.md)

second half — "AB6 and AB4* fail in general" has counterexamples on a general topos (products of abelian sheaves need not be exact, filtered colimits need not commute with products), so these two **cannot be taken for free** ✓.

∎

⭐ So the AB6 / AB4* of $\mathrm{CondAb}$ is an **extra** gain, coming from the concrete shape of the site $\mathbf{CHaus}$ (termwise reduction to $\mathbf{Ab}$), not from any topos.
