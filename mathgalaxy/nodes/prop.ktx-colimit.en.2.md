## Notes · **draft**
⭐ **How to read it**: this translates "$k$-ification" into "assembling from compact Hausdorff pieces". Thus $X$ compactly generated $\iff$ $X$ means that it is the colimit of its own compact Hausdorff pieces — every later argument about $k\mathbf{Top}$ can be made on **pieces** and then lifted along the colimit.
