# structure theorem for locally compact abelian groups　`thm.lc-structure`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=thm.lc-structure)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./thm.lc-structure.md)

$$0 \longrightarrow V \oplus K \longrightarrow M \longrightarrow D \longrightarrow 0,$$

where $D$ is a **discrete** abelian group.

## Notes · **draft**
**How to read the theorem.** Locally compact Hausdorff abelian groups have three basic building blocks: **discrete groups**, **finite-dimensional real vector spaces**, and **compact Hausdorff groups**. The theorem says that every such group is an extension of $V \oplus K$ by a discrete group.
