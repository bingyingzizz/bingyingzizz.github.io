⚠️ But note that $\operatorname{Hom}_{\mathbb{Z}}(M, N)$ is naturally a **compactly generated topological space**, yet **not a topological abelian group** — because compactly generated spaces are not closed under products (of topological spaces). Treating $\mathrm{CondAb}$ as a "topological abelian group" goes wrong at this step.
