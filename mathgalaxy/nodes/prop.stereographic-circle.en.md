## Statement · **draft**
Stereographic projection maps circles on $S^{2}$ to circles on $\widehat{\mathbb{C}}$:

1. a circle not passing through the north pole $N$ $\mapsto$ a genuine circle in the complex plane;
2. a circle passing through the north pole $N$ $\mapsto$ a line in the complex plane (adding $\infty$ makes it a circle through $\infty$).
