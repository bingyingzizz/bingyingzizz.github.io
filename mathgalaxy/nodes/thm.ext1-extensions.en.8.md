# First Ext Classifies Extensions　`thm.ext1-extensions`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=thm.ext1-extensions)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./thm.ext1-extensions.md)

The result for $\operatorname{Ext}^{1}$ is the simplest case of $n = 1$.

⚠️ In the strict definition of equivalence, the isomorphism must **be the identity at both ends**. Allowing arbitrary isomorphisms at the ends gives a coarser classification by orbits of invertible elements in $\operatorname{Hom}$, so the original correspondence is no longer one-to-one.
