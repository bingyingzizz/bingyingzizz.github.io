# Examples of Étale Sheaves　`prop.etale-sheaf-examples`

[in graph](../#n=prop.etale-sheaf-examples)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./prop.etale-sheaf-examples.md)

2. **Finite étale coverings**: if $Y \to X$ is finite, flat, and unramified (i.e. "finite étale"), then it is a cover for the étale topology, and

$$\mathcal{G}_{Y}(U) := \operatorname{Hom}_{X}\bigl(U,\ Y\bigr)$$

is a sheaf on $X_{\text{et}}$ — this is the correct generalization of "constant sheaf".
