陈述

在做什么：把形式沿着光滑映射「搬回去」—— 这是形式特有的能力（向量场只能正着推），也是「d\mathrm{d}d 与坐标无关」的正式说法。

设 F:U→VF : U\to VF:U→V 是开集间的 C∞C^{\infty}C∞ 映射，ω\omegaω 是 VVV 上的 kkk-形式。拉回 F∗ωF^{\ast}\omegaF∗ω 是 UUU 上的 kkk-形式，定义为

(F∗ω)p(v1,…,vk):=ωF(p)(dFp(v1),…,dFp(vk)).(F^{\ast}\omega)_{p}(v_{1},\ldots,v_{k}) := \omega_{F(p)}\bigl(\mathrm{d}F_{p}(v_{1}),\ldots,\mathrm{d}F_{p}(v_{k})\bigr).(F∗ω)p​(v1​,…,vk​):=ωF(p)​(dFp​(v1​),…,dFp​(vk​)).

于是 F∗F^{\ast}F∗ 把 Ωk(V)\Omega^{k}(V)Ωk(V) 送到 Ωk(U)\Omega^{k}(U)Ωk(U)，且满足

  1. 线性：F∗(aω+bη)=a F∗ω+b F∗ηF^{\ast}(a\omega+b\eta) = a\,F^{\ast}\omega+b\,F^{\ast}\etaF∗(aω+bη)=aF∗ω+bF∗η；对函数 F∗f=f∘FF^{\ast}f = f\circ FF∗f=f∘F；
  2. 保楔积：F∗(ω∧η)=F∗ω∧F∗ηF^{\ast}(\omega\wedge\eta) = F^{\ast}\omega\wedge F^{\ast}\etaF∗(ω∧η)=F∗ω∧F∗η；
  3. 与 d\mathrm{d}d 交换：F∗(dω)=d(F∗ω)F^{\ast}(\mathrm{d}\omega) = \mathrm{d}(F^{\ast}\omega)F∗(dω)=d(F∗ω)；
  4. 保复合：(G∘F)∗=F∗∘G∗(G\circ F)^{\ast} = F^{\ast}\circ G^{\ast}(G∘F)∗=F∗∘G∗，(id⁡)∗=id⁡(\operatorname{id})^{\ast} = \operatorname{id}(id)∗=id。

前两条说 F∗F^{\ast}F∗ 是分次代数同态，第三条说它还是链映射；合起来：F∗F^{\ast}F∗ 是微分分次代数的同态。

说明

方向：F:U→VF : U\to VF:U→V 给出的是 F∗:Ωk(V)→Ωk(U)F^{\ast} : \Omega^{k}(V)\to\Omega^{k}(U)F∗:Ωk(V)→Ωk(U)，与 FFF 反向。这就是「形式是协变的」的确切含义；也是它能沿任意光滑映射定义，而向量场的前推一般要求 FFF 是微分同胚（或只在 FFF-相关时能推到像上）的原因。

（3）是核心：它等价于「d\mathrm{d}d 不依赖坐标」。唯一的实质内容是链式法则，剩下的由 (2) 与 d2=0\mathrm{d}^{2}=0d2=0 收尾（证明在边上）。

几何里的用法：曲面参数化 r:U→R3r : U\to\mathbb{R}^{3}r:U→R3 把 R3\mathbb{R}^{3}R3 上的形式拉回 UUU。

第一基本形式与面积元都是拉回的样子：I=(ω1)2+(ω2)2I = (\omega^{1})^{2}+(\omega^{2})^{2}I=(ω1)2+(ω2)2、dA=ω1∧ω2\mathrm{d}A = \omega^{1}\wedge\omega^{2}dA=ω1∧ω2。

曲面论能用形式写，全靠这一条。

⚠️ 拉回不保积分的方向：∫UF∗ω=∫F(U)ω\int_{U}F^{\ast}\omega = \int_{F(U)}\omega∫U​F∗ω=∫F(U)​ω 只在 FFF 是保向微分同胚、且形式的次数等于维数时成立（换元公式，形式的积分）。

⚠️ (4) 的次序不能记反：复合 G∘FG\circ FG∘F 的拉回是 F∗∘G∗F^{\ast}\circ G^{\ast}F∗∘G∗，先拉后面那个。

Statement draft

What this does: it pulls forms back along a smooth map — a capability specific to forms (vector fields can only be pushed forward), and the precise statement that d\mathrm{d}d is coordinate-free.

Let F:U→VF : U\to VF:U→V be a C∞C^{\infty}C∞ map between open sets, and let ω\omegaω be a kkk-form on VVV. The pullback F∗ωF^{\ast}\omegaF∗ω is the kkk-form on UUU defined by

(F∗ω)p(v1,…,vk):=ωF(p)(dFp(v1),…,dFp(vk)).(F^{\ast}\omega)_{p}(v_{1},\ldots,v_{k}) := \omega_{F(p)}\bigl(\mathrm{d}F_{p}(v_{1}),\ldots,\mathrm{d}F_{p}(v_{k})\bigr).(F∗ω)p​(v1​,…,vk​):=ωF(p)​(dFp​(v1​),…,dFp​(vk​)).

Thus F∗F^{\ast}F∗ sends Ωk(V)\Omega^{k}(V)Ωk(V) to Ωk(U)\Omega^{k}(U)Ωk(U), and satisfies

  1. Linearity: F∗(aω+bη)=a F∗ω+b F∗ηF^{\ast}(a\omega+b\eta) = a\,F^{\ast}\omega+b\,F^{\ast}\etaF∗(aω+bη)=aF∗ω+bF∗η; for functions, F∗f=f∘FF^{\ast}f = f\circ FF∗f=f∘F;
  2. Preserves the wedge product: F∗(ω∧η)=F∗ω∧F∗ηF^{\ast}(\omega\wedge\eta) = F^{\ast}\omega\wedge F^{\ast}\etaF∗(ω∧η)=F∗ω∧F∗η;
  3. Commutes with d\mathrm{d}d: F∗(dω)=d(F∗ω)F^{\ast}(\mathrm{d}\omega) = \mathrm{d}(F^{\ast}\omega)F∗(dω)=d(F∗ω);
  4. Preserves composition: (G∘F)∗=F∗∘G∗(G\circ F)^{\ast} = F^{\ast}\circ G^{\ast}(G∘F)∗=F∗∘G∗, (id⁡)∗=id⁡(\operatorname{id})^{\ast} = \operatorname{id}(id)∗=id.

The first two say F∗F^{\ast}F∗ is a graded algebra homomorphism, the third that it is also a chain map; together: F∗F^{\ast}F∗ is a homomorphism of differential graded algebras.

Notes draft

Direction: F:U→VF : U\to VF:U→V induces F∗:Ωk(V)→Ωk(U)F^{\ast} : \Omega^{k}(V)\to\Omega^{k}(U)F∗:Ωk(V)→Ωk(U), opposite to FFF. This is the precise meaning of "forms are covariant"; it is also why pullback is defined along any smooth map, whereas pushing vector fields forward generally requires FFF to be a diffeomorphism (or at least requires the fields to be FFF-related).

(3) is the core: it is equivalent to saying that d\mathrm{d}d does not depend on coordinates. The only substantive input is the chain rule; the rest follows from (2) and d2=0\mathrm{d}^{2}=0d2=0 (the proof is on the edges).

Use in geometry: a surface parametrization r:U→R3r : U\to\mathbb{R}^{3}r:U→R3 pulls forms on R3\mathbb{R}^{3}R3 back to UUU.

The first fundamental form and the area element are both pullbacks: I=(ω1)2+(ω2)2I = (\omega^{1})^{2}+(\omega^{2})^{2}I=(ω1)2+(ω2)2, dA=ω1∧ω2\mathrm{d}A = \omega^{1}\wedge\omega^{2}dA=ω1∧ω2.

Surface theory can be written in the language of forms precisely because of this.

⚠️ Pullback does not preserve the orientation of an integral: ∫UF∗ω=∫F(U)ω\int_{U}F^{\ast}\omega = \int_{F(U)}\omega∫U​F∗ω=∫F(U)​ω holds only when FFF is an orientation-preserving diffeomorphism and the degree of the form equals the dimension (change of variables, 形式的积分).

⚠️ The order in (4) must not be reversed: the pullback of the composite G∘FG\circ FG∘F is F∗∘G∗F^{\ast}\circ G^{\ast}F∗∘G∗, i.e. apply the later map first.

  • 本页 ⟹ 外微分推出
    开集上的「d\mathrm{d}d 与拉回交换」定出流形上的外微分

    流形上的外微分只能在图卡里定义；要证明它与图卡无关，须说明两张卡上的坐标公式在重叠处给出同一个算子。 重叠处两张卡的坐标变换是开集间的微分同胚，拉回（开集间） 已证 F∗d=dF∗F^{\ast}\mathrm{d} = \mathrm{d}F^{\ast}F∗d=dF∗（开集版本）。 于是两边的坐标公式被这条等式拉到同一个形式，图卡无关性成立，外微分良定义。∎

    图卡无关性靠「d\mathrm{d}d 与拉回交换」这条（开集版已证），它才是证明的引擎。
