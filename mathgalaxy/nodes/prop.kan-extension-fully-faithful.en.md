## Statement · **draft**
Let $p : \mathcal{C} \to \mathcal{C}'$ be **fully faithful** and $\mathcal{D}$ cocomplete. Then for every $F : \mathcal{C} \to \mathcal{D}$ the left Kan extension satisfies

$$p_{!}F \circ p \;\cong\; F.$$
