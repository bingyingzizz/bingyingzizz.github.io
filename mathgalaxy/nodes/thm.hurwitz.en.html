Statement

In plain words: if a sequence of holomorphic functions, none of which has a zero, converges uniformly on compact subsets, then the limit function is either identically zero or also has no zeros.

Let Ω\OmegaΩ be a region, let fkf_kfk​ be holomorphic on Ω\OmegaΩ and converge uniformly on every compact subset of Ω\OmegaΩ to fff. If every fkf_kfk​ has no zero on Ω\OmegaΩ, then the limit fff is either identically zero or nowhere zero on Ω\OmegaΩ.
