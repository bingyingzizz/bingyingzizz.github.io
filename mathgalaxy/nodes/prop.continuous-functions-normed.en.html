Statement

Let SSS be a compact Hausdorff space and let MMM be a (semi)normed abelian group (respectively, a Banach abelian group). Then

  1. The continuous maps C(S,M):=Hom⁡top(S,M)C(S, M) := \operatorname{Hom}_{\mathrm{top}}(S, M)C(S,M):=Homtop​(S,M), equipped with

form a (semi)normed abelian group (respectively, a Banach abelian group);

  1. If SSS is also a Stone space, then C(S,Mdisc)C(S, M^{\mathrm{disc}})C(S,Mdisc) is dense in C(S,M)C(S, M)C(S,M).

Notes

⭐ Statement 2 is the precise version of "there are enough discrete-valued functions": on a Stone space every continuous MMM-valued function can be uniformly approximated by locally constant functions. It is a key step in the proof of acyclicity for Banach coefficients (8.2.6 / 8.2.8).
