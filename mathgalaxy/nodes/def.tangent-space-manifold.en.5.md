# Tangent Space　`def.tangent-space-manifold`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=def.tangent-space-manifold)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./def.tangent-space-manifold.md)

**Why the two definitions are equivalent**: curve classes give directional derivatives (Leibniz follows from the product rule); conversely, given a derivation $D$, apply it to an expansion and use Leibniz to kill terms of order at least two, obtaining $D = \sum_{i}D(x^{i})\,\partial_{i}(p)$ — exactly the directional derivative of some curve.

**Coordinate change**: $\partial/\partial x^{i} = \sum_{j}\frac{\partial y^{j}}{\partial x^{i}}\,\partial/\partial y^{j}$, with the Jacobian as coefficients.
