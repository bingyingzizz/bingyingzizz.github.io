## Statement · **draft**
Let $X$ be a **nonempty** compact metric space and $f : X \to \mathbb{R}$ continuous. Then $f$ **attains** its maximum and minimum: there exist $x_{\min}, x_{\max} \in X$ such that for all $x \in X$,

$$f(x_{\min}) \le f(x) \le f(x_{\max})$$
