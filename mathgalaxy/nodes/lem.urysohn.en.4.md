# Urysohn　`lem.urysohn`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=lem.urysohn)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./lem.urysohn.md)

**Use**: it is the prerequisite for the **Tietze extension theorem** (extending a continuous real function on a closed subset to the whole space), and Tietze in turn is the engine behind the statement that a real Banach space is acyclic on a compact Hausdorff space — a chain that eventually leads to condensed cohomology.
