## Notes · **draft**
**It is "the matrix of an inner product"**: the matrix of any inner product in a basis is a positive definite Hermitian matrix; conversely every positive definite matrix defines an inner product. So "positive definite" = "a legitimate inner-product matrix", a correspondence more useful than any criterion.
