If fff is a complete intersection (locally AAA is obtained from RRR by modding out a regular sequence, i.e. "the cut is clean and there are no superfluous relations"), then f!Zf^!\mathbb{Z}f!Z is invertible in D(A)D(A)D(A) (locally it is a line bundle in some degree).

Notes

⭐ This is the "affine model" of Grothendieck's coherent duality: f!f_!f!​ is cohomology with compact support, f!f^!f! is the dualising complex, and both the projection formula and Serre duality follow formally from the adjunction.

⭐ Difference from the classical approach: in classical coherent duality, f!f_!f!​ in the affine case can only be manufactured artificially out of "formal functions";

here f!f_!f!​ is a direct consequence of the adjunction pair (j!⊣j∗j_! \dashv j^*j!​⊣j∗), and it does not preserve discrete objects — exactly the gain of the condensed method.
