## Notes · **draft**
Three examples, three choices of $N$: for $\mathbb{Z}$ take $N(a) = |a|$; for $K[x]$ take $N(f) = \deg f$ (this is `thm.division-algorithm`); for $\mathbb{Z}[i]$ (Gaussian integers) take $N(a+bi) = a^{2}+b^{2}$.

Why it is valuable: all the later arithmetic ($\gcd$, Bézout, unique factorization) uses **only "the remainder decreases"**, so the whole chain holds at once in a Euclidean domain (starting from `imp.ring-euclid-pid`).
