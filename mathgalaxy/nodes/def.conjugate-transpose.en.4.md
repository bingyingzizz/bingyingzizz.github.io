# Conjugate Transpose (Adjugate)　`def.conjugate-transpose`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=def.conjugate-transpose)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./def.conjugate-transpose.md)

**Why the conjugate is necessary**: the standard inner product on $\mathbb{C}^{n}$ is $\langle x, y\rangle = \sum x_i \overline{y_i}$ (conjugate in the second variable), so when moving $A$ from the left side to the right, $\overline{A}$ appears naturally.

With the "transpose only" version, $\langle Ax,y\rangle = \langle x, A^{T}y\rangle$ is false over the complex field.

⚠️ Three notations coexist: $A^{*}$, $A^{\dagger}$, $A^{H}$. This entry uses $A^{*}$ throughout (the same star as for adjoint operators).
