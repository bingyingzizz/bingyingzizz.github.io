# criterion for a subspace　`prop.subspace-criterion`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=prop.subspace-criterion)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./prop.subspace-criterion.md)

**(Spanning form)** Let $\mathcal{U}$ be the family of all subspaces containing $S$. The **span** of an arbitrary subset $S \subseteq V$ is

$$\operatorname{span} S := \bigcap \mathcal{U} = \bigcap \{ U : S \subseteq U \subseteq V,\ U \text{ is a subspace} \}$$

the smallest subspace containing $S$, and it equals the set of **finite** linear combinations of elements of $S$.
