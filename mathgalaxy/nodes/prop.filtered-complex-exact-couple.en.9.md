# a filtered complex gives an exact couple　`prop.filtered-complex-exact-couple`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=prop.filtered-complex-exact-couple)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./prop.filtered-complex-exact-couple.md)

⚠️ Convergence still requires **boundedness on both sides** (or at least stabilization for each $(p,q)$, with $r$ large enough that both $Z_{r}$ and $B_{r}$ stabilize). Boundedness on the $p \ll 0$ side alone is insufficient. The spectral sequence of an unbounded complex may fail to converge to anything, so convergence cannot be inferred from the notation alone.
