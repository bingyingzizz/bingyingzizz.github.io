## Notes · **draft**
⚠ **Convention**: Hausdorffness is **not** built into the definition of "paracompact" here (some books include it). Whenever separation is needed, "paracompact Hausdorff" is written out explicitly.

**Where it sits in the hierarchy**: compact $\implies$ paracompact (a finite subcover is certainly locally finite); paracompact Hausdorff $\implies$ normal $\implies$ regular $\implies$ Hausdorff (theorems of this pack); metric spaces $\implies$ paracompact (Stone's theorem).
