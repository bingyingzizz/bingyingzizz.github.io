## Notes · **draft**
⭐ **This is the construction**: $\mathbb{R}$ is not assumed to exist; it is glued together from **objects of the previous level** — the rationals ($\mathbb{Q}$) $\to$ rational sequences (functions $\omega \to \mathbb{Q}$) $\to$ the Cauchy sequences among them $\to$ passing to the quotient by the equivalence relation.

Every step uses only what is already available; no new object enters from nothing.

- The elements are **equivalence classes**, not sequences themselves;

otherwise different ways of approximating the same number would become different real numbers.
