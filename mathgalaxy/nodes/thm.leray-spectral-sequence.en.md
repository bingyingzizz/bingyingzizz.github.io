## Statement · **draft**
Let $f : \mathcal{T} \to \mathcal{T}'$ be a topos morphism, $X' \in \mathcal{T}'$, and $M^{\bullet}$ a complex of abelian groups in $\mathcal{T}$. Then there is a spectral sequence

$$E^{p,q}_{2} \;=\; H^{p}\bigl(X',\ R^{q}f_{*}M^{\bullet}\bigr) \;\Longrightarrow\; H^{p+q}\bigl(f^{-1}(X'),\ M^{\bullet}\bigr).$$
