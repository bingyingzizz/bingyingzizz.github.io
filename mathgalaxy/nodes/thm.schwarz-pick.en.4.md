# Schwarz–Pick theorem　`thm.schwarz-pick`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=thm.schwarz-pick)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./thm.schwarz-pick.md)

⚠ The inequality holding for all $z_0$ at once is equivalent to "$f$ is a $1$-Lipschitz map of the hyperbolic metric". This is the full form of Schwarz's lemma — Schwarz's lemma is just the special case $z_0 = 0$, $f(z_0) = 0$.

📌 Corollary: the automorphisms of $\mathbb{D}$ are exactly $z \mapsto e^{i\theta}\dfrac{z - a}{1 - \overline{a} z}$ ($|a| < 1$, $\theta \in \mathbb{R}$). Equality forces $f$ to have this form, and conversely these maps clearly attain equality.
