## Statement · **draft**
Let $K$ be compact Hausdorff and $A \subseteq C(K, \mathbb{R})$ a **subalgebra** containing the unit and **separating points**; write $A$ for it. Then $\overline A = C(K, \mathbb{R})$.

## Notes · **draft**
Three steps:

① Take the closure: $\overline A$ still contains the unit, separates points, and is a **lattice** because a closed unital subalgebra is a lattice.
