## Statement · **draft**
For $z = x + iy \in \mathbb{C}$, define by **power series**

$$e^{z} := \sum_{n=0}^{\infty} \frac{z^{n}}{n!},$$

$$\cos z := \frac{e^{iz} + e^{-iz}}{2},\qquad \sin z := \frac{e^{iz} - e^{-iz}}{2i},$$
