## Notes · **draft**
**Proof.** $T = \varinjlim_{(X \to T)} h_{X}$ ✓ (the colimit given by the category of elements), sheafification preserves colimits ✓, and $h_{X}^{\#} = X^{\sharp}$ ✓, hence $T^{\#} = \varinjlim X^{\sharp}$ ✓. ∎

⭐ Reading: **sheafification is "splitting $T$ according to its elements, sheafifying each associated sheaf, and gluing back"**.
