⚠️ The result fails for **products**. The functor $(-)^{\mathrm{disc}}$ is a left adjoint and need not preserve products. The difference goes beyond exchanging products for direct sums: $\prod_i M_i^{\mathrm{disc}}$ is strictly larger than $(\prod_i M_i)^{\mathrm{disc}}$, with the extra families consisting of **individually locally constant coordinates that are jointly non-locally constant**.
