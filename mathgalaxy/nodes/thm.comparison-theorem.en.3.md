# Comparison Theorem　`thm.comparison-theorem`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=thm.comparison-theorem)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./thm.comparison-theorem.md)

$$\varphi_{n} - \psi_{n} = e_{n+1}s_{n} + s_{n-1}d_{n},$$

where we set $s_{-1} = 0$ and interpret $e_{0}$ as $\varepsilon_{Q}$ and $d_{0}$ as $\varepsilon_{P}$.

## Notes · **draft**
⭐ **Significance.** Resolutions **can vary**, but uniqueness up to chain homotopy suffices. Derived functors, including $\operatorname{Ext}$ and $\operatorname{Tor}$, depend only on the homotopy class of the resolution, so their definitions are independent of the choice of resolution. The construction of derived functors relies on this theorem to be well defined.
