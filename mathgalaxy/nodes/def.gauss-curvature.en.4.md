**Relation to the contraction $\frac{1}{2}g^{ik}g^{j\ell}R_{ijk\ell}$**: in dimension two there is only one independent component, and enumerating the four indices gives $K = -\frac{1}{2}g^{ik}g^{j\ell}R_{ijk\ell}$ (the sign under the convention of this package). The two expressions give the same number; the difference is only the sign coming from the index order.
