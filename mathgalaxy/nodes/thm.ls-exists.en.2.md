## Notes · **draft**
The proof simply concatenates the previous two items: $F \mapsto$ predicts a premeasure $\mu _{0}$ (the preceding proposition) $\to$ extended by Carathéodory to $\mathfrak{B}_\mathbb{R}$ (the two theorems before).

“Up to a constant” is because $\mu _F$ only sees increments $F(b) - F(a)$: replacing $F$ by F + c leaves increments, hence the measure, unchanged. So the precise correspondence is “increasing right-continuous functions modulo constants” $\leftrightarrow$ “locally finite Borel measures on $\mathbb{R}$”.
