# Integration over a Chain　`def.integral-on-chain`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=def.integral-on-chain)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./def.integral-on-chain.md)

**Why no orientation is needed**: the coordinate order of the cube already fixes the orientation — $\mathrm{d}x^{1}\wedge\cdots\wedge\mathrm{d}x^{k}$ on $I^{k}$ is the standard orientation.

To make the cube "run backwards", replace some coordinate by $1-x^{i}$;

then $c^{\ast}\omega$ automatically changes sign, with no extra rule.

So "orientation" is absorbed into the parametrization on the chain side.
