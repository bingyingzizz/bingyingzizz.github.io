Statement

For any presheaf, the square

C/T↪C^/T↓jT↓C→ h C^\begin{array}{ccc} \mathcal{C}/T & \hookrightarrow & \widehat{\mathcal{C}}/T \\ \big\downarrow{\scriptstyle{j_{T}}} & & \big\downarrow \\ \mathcal{C} & \xrightarrow{\ h\ } & \widehat{\mathcal{C}} \end{array}C/T↓⏐​jT​C​↪ h ​​C/T↓⏐​C​

is Cartesian. The top arrow is the embedding of the slice category into the category of presheaves, the bottom arrow is the Yoneda embedding of the category C\mathcal{C}C into the category of presheaves hhh (h(X)=hX=Hom⁡(−,X)h(X) = h_{X} = \operatorname{Hom}(-, X)h(X)=hX​=Hom(−,X)), and the two vertical arrows are the forgetful functors.

Notes

In other words, the slice category C/T\mathcal{C}/TC/T is the pullback of the forgetful functor C^/T→C^\widehat{\mathcal{C}}/T \to \widehat{\mathcal{C}}C/T→C along the Yoneda embedding.

A special case illustrates this. When T=hXT = h_{X}T=hX​ (the representable case), the square gives C/X≃C^/hX\mathcal{C}/X \simeq \widehat{\mathcal{C}}/h_{X}C/X≃C/hX​ — taking the slice in C\mathcal{C}C over XXX is the same as taking the slice in the category of presheaves over hXh_{X}hX​.
