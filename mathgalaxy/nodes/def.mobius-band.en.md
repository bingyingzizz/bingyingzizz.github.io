## Statement · **draft**
On the square $[0,1] \times [0,1]$, take the equivalence relation that glues the left and right sides in **opposite directions**, namely

$$(0, t) \sim (1, 1 - t), \qquad 0 \le t \le 1.$$

The quotient space $M := ([0,1]\times[0,1])/\sim$ is called the **Möbius band**.
