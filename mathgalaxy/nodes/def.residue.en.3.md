So $c_{-1}$ is exactly "the number that obstructs the existence of a primitive" — it **remains** (residue).

⭐ Independence of $r$: $f$ is holomorphic on the annulus between two concentric circles, so by the annulus version of Cauchy–Goursat the two integrals agree.

⚠ The definition at infinity carries a **minus sign**, which comes from the change of coordinates $\zeta = 1/w$, namely the Jacobi factor $1/w^2$.

It makes "the sum of residues on the whole plane is zero" hold: the sum of residues at finitely many singularities plus $\operatorname{Res}(f, \infty)$ equals $0$.
