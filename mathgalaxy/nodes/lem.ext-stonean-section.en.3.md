# Sections of Ext on Stonean Spaces　`lem.ext-stonean-section`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=lem.ext-stonean-section)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./lem.ext-stonean-section.md)

$$\operatorname{Hom}_{\mathbb{Z}}(M \cdot S,\ N) \cong \operatorname{Hom}_{\mathbb{Z}}\bigl(\mathbb{Z}[S],\ \operatorname{Hom}_{\mathbb{Z}}(M, N)\bigr)$$

and the conclusion follows: the right side is "sections of an internal Hom", the left side is "tensor first, then Hom".

⚠️ The key hypothesis is **Stonean** (extremally disconnected), not merely compact Hausdorff — extremal disconnectedness is exactly the source of projectivity of $\mathbb{Z}[S]$.
