## Statement · **draft**
**The category of $G$-sets** $G\text{-}\mathbf{Set}$: objects are left $G$-sets and morphisms are equivariant maps. The terminal object is a one-point set (with the trivial action); products carry the diagonal action; equalizers are fixed-point subsets; pullbacks are fiber products.
