Statement

A pre-analytic ring A\mathcal{A}A is called an analytic ring if and only if: for every complex

C:⋯→Ci→⋯→C1→C0→0C : \cdots \to C_i \to \cdots \to C_1 \to C_0 \to 0C:⋯→Ci​→⋯→C1​→C0​→0

(where each CiC_iCi​ is a direct sum of copies of A[T]\mathcal{A}[T]A[T], with TTT extremally disconnected), and for every extremally disconnected set SSS, the natural map

RHom⁡A(A[S], C)⟶RHom⁡A(A[S], C)\operatorname{RHom}_{\mathcal{A}}\bigl(\mathcal{A}[S],\ C\bigr) \longrightarrow \operatorname{RHom}_{A}\bigl(A[S],\ C\bigr)RHomA​(A[S], C)⟶RHomA​(A[S], C)

is an isomorphism. Here "natural map" means one concrete map, not a figure of speech: it goes from the A\mathcal{A}A side to the AAA side and is induced by composing along ιS\iota_SιS​. Three steps make this precise:

  1. First there is a canonical map ιS:A[S]→A[S]\iota_S : A[S] \to \mathcal{A}[S]ιS​:A[S]→A[S] — it sends a formal finite sum ∑ass\sum a_s s∑as​s into the free complete module (the data of a pre-analytic ring includes this family of maps; see 预解析环).
  2. Pull back along it: RHom⁡A(−,C)\operatorname{RHom}_A(-, C)RHomA​(−,C) is a contravariant functor, and ιS\iota_SιS​ induces RHom⁡A(A[S],C)→RHom⁡A(A[S],C)\operatorname{RHom}_A(\mathcal{A}[S], C) \to \operatorname{RHom}_A(A[S], C)RHomA​(A[S],C)→RHomA​(A[S],C), by the rule g↦g∘ιSg \mapsto g \circ \iota_Sg↦g∘ιS​.
  3. Rewrite the left-hand side (this step has a hypothesis): when CCC carries an A\mathcal{A}A-module structure (that is, lies in the image of (ii)), A[S]\mathcal{A}[S]A[S] is a free A\mathcal{A}A-module, so an A\mathcal{A}A-linear map is determined by its values on generators; hence there is an isomorphism
RHom⁡A(A[S],C)≅RHom⁡A(A[S],C),\operatorname{RHom}_{\mathcal{A}}(\mathcal{A}[S], C) \cong \operatorname{RHom}_A(\mathcal{A}[S], C),RHomA​(A[S],C)≅RHomA​(A[S],C),

This is the isomorphism supplied by the adjunction, not "the same set". For a general AAA-module complex CCC, the left-hand side has not even been defined — the "comparison map" is exactly the object to be checked in the definition; identifying the two directly at the derived level would first require a derived category of complete modules, which is not proved here (see the gap at the end of the notes).

Putting 2 and 3 together gives the arrow appearing in the formula, RHom⁡A(A[S],C)→RHom⁡A(A[S],C)\operatorname{RHom}_{\mathcal{A}}(\mathcal{A}[S], C) \to \operatorname{RHom}_A(A[S], C)RHomA​(A[S],C)→RHomA​(A[S],C).

Notes

⭐ Reading: "A[S]\mathcal{A}[S]A[S] and the ordinary free module A[S]A[S]A[S] cannot be distinguished inside RHom⁡\operatorname{RHom}RHom".

This is the relative version of condition (ii) in the definition of solid groups;

taking A=ZA = \mathbb{Z}A=Z and A[S]=Z[S]■\mathcal{A}[S] = Z[S]^{\blacksquare}A[S]=Z[S]■ returns to the solid case.

⚠ Why require all such complexes and not just a single module? Because the conclusion is needed at the level of derived categories (解析环上的模 (ii)); an isomorphism at a single point is not enough — it must hold for complexes as well.

⭐ Deciding "is this an analytic ring" is usually done via concrete theorems such as 解析环的例子 and 有限生成代数的解析环, not by checking the definition item by item.

Which hypothesis each direction uses (analyticity is a derived test over a whole class of complexes):

  • Analyticity   ⟺  \iff⟺ the comparison map for all the listed complexes and extremally disconnected SSS is a quasi-isomorphism: this is the definition unwound, with the pre-analytic ring already given.
  • The canonical map A[S]→A[S]A[S]\to\mathcal A[S]A[S]→A[S]   ⟹  \implies⟹ the contravariant derived Hom comparison map: this uses functoriality of precomposition and does not presuppose analyticity.
  • Testing only ordinary Hom in degree zero   ⟹  \implies⟹ does not make the derived comparison isomorphism free; ordinary Hom cannot see higher Ext and cannot replace the condition on complexes.
  • The original text's explanation identifying RHom⁡A\operatorname{RHom}_{\mathcal A}RHomA​ with RHom⁡A\operatorname{RHom}_ARHomA​ directly carries a circularity risk: for a general pre-analytic ring the derived category of complete modules has not been set up, so this step is unverified and recorded as a gap; the test condition is better formulated first in the derived category of condensed AAA-modules.
