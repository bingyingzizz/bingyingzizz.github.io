# Divisible Group　`def.divisible-group`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=def.divisible-group)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./def.divisible-group.md)

A $\mathbb{Z}$-module is just an abelian group (`prop.zmod-is-ab`), so "divisible group" = "divisible $\mathbb{Z}$-module"; the definition carries over verbatim to modules over any ring (replace $\mathbb{Z}$ by $R$ and $n$ by a non-zero-divisor).
