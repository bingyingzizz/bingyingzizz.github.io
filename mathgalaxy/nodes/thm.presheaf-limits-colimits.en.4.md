# limits and colimits in presheaf categories　`thm.presheaf-limits-colimits`

[in graph](../#n=thm.presheaf-limits-colimits)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./thm.presheaf-limits-colimits.md)

The resulting presheaf, together with the pointwise cone (cocone), satisfies the universal property: a map from a cone to $\varprojlim T_i$ is uniquely determined at each point $X$, and the universal property is stated **pointwise**, so it holds at every point and hence globally ✓.
