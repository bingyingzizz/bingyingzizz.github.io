# Bolzano's theorem　`cor.zero-point`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=cor.zero-point)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./cor.zero-point.md)

⚠ The hypothesis is **opposite signs**, not "does not vanish": when $f(a) = 0$ the conclusion is trivial but does not give a point in $(a, b)$.

⚠ The converse fails: a zero need not come from opposite signs ($f(x) = x^{2}$ on $[-1, 1]$).
