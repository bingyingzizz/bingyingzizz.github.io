# Étale Space　`def.etale-space`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=def.etale-space)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./def.etale-space.md)

The two share a name — Grothendieck did this on purpose, since étale maps of schemes really are the algebraic substitute for "local isomorphism + finite covering" — but they **cannot be interchanged**: fibres of a local homeomorphism are arbitrary discrete sets, while fibres of an étale morphism of schemes are generally discrete **finite** schemes.
