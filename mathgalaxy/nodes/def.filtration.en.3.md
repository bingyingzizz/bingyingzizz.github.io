## Notes · **draft**
The proposition $\mathbf{F}(\mathcal{C}(\mathcal{A})) \cong \mathcal{C}(\mathbf{F}(\mathcal{A}))$ says that **equipping objects with filtrations and forming complexes commute**. Thus one can first filter the complex and then compute cohomology one graded piece at a time.
