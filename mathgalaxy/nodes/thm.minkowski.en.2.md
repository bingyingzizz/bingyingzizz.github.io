where the last step applies Hölder once to $|f| \cdot |f+g|^{p-1}$ and once to $|g| \cdot |f+g|^{p-1}$. Now note

$$\left\| |f+g|^{p-1} \right\|_q^{\,q} = \int |f+g|^{(p-1)q} = \int |f+g|^p$$

Cancel one factor $\|f+g\|_p^{p/q}$ from both sides.

⭐ With this, $\|\cdot\|_p$ is genuinely a **norm** — the prerequisite for "$L^p$ is a Banach space" below.
