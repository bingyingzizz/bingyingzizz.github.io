# Rayleigh Quotient　`def.rayleigh-quotient`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=def.rayleigh-quotient)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./def.rayleigh-quotient.md)

**Its generalization** is thm.courant-fischer: restricting $R_A$ to a $k$-dimensional subspace and taking the extremum gives the $k$-th eigenvalue. So the Rayleigh quotient is the gateway to the "variational characterization of the spectrum", not an isolated notation.

⚠️ $A$ must be Hermitian (or real symmetric) for $R_A$ to be real and to have an extremal meaning; for a general matrix $x^{*}Ax$ is complex, the Rayleigh quotient degenerates into a complex-valued function, and the standard theory does not apply.
