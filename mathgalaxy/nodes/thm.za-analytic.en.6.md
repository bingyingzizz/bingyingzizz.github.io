# analytic rings from finitely generated algebras　`thm.za-analytic`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=thm.za-analytic)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./thm.za-analytic.md)

⑥ General finitely generated $A$: by Noether normalisation find a polynomial ring $R=\mathbb{Z}[X_1,\ldots,X_n]$ and a finite injection $R\to A$, and identify $A_\blacksquare$ with $(A,R)_\blacksquare$ at the derived level.

⑦ The projection formula $j_!j^*M\cong M\otimes_{A_\blacksquare}j_!A$ follows formally from $j_!\dashv j^*$.
