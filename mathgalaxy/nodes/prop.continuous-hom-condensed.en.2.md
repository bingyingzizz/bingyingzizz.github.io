## Notes · **draft**
⭐ The role of the hypothesis "$M$ is compactly generated": only then does $\operatorname{Hom}_{\mathbb{Z}}(\underline{M}, -)$ really measure continuous homomorphisms; on a general topological abelian group the condensed Hom is **larger** than the continuous homs (one can glue a map piecewise that is not continuous globally).
