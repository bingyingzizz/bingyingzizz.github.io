## Statement · **draft**
Let $S$ be a closed surface. Remove two open disks from $S$ to obtain a surface with two boundary circles. Glue the two ends of the cylinder $S^{1}\times[0,1]$ to the respective boundary circles. The resulting closed surface is obtained by **adding a handle** to $S$. Equivalently, adding a handle means taking the connected sum with a torus: $S\mapsto S\#T^{2}$.
