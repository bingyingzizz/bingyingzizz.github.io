**Why complex analysis alone does not suffice**: The construction of $r$ above uses only continuity of $f$. The topological input is $\pi_{1}(S^{1}) \cong \mathbb{Z}$. The proof of `thm.no-retraction-disk` is an immediate application of functoriality: A retraction $r$ would force $r_{*} \circ i_{*} = \operatorname{id}_{\mathbb{Z}}$ to factor through $\pi_{1}(\overline{\mathbb{D}}) = 1$.
