Statement

Let VVV be a finite-dimensional KKK-vector space, T:V→WT : V \to WT:V→W linear. Then

dim⁡V=rank⁡T+null⁡T=dim⁡im⁡T+dim⁡ker⁡T\dim V = \operatorname{rank} T + \operatorname{null} T = \dim \operatorname{im} T + \dim \ker TdimV=rankT+nullT=dimimT+dimkerT

Notes

⭐ This is the central theorem of finite-dimensional linear algebra: the input dimension of a linear map is split exactly between what is "kept" and what is "lost", with nothing left over.

⭐ Corollaries (the three most used):

  • TTT is injective   ⟺  rank⁡T=dim⁡V\iff \operatorname{rank} T = \dim V⟺rankT=dimV;

TTT is surjective   ⟺  rank⁡T=dim⁡W\iff \operatorname{rank} T = \dim W⟺rankT=dimW;

  • when dim⁡V=dim⁡W<∞\dim V = \dim W < \inftydimV=dimW<∞: TTT injective   ⟺  \iff⟺ TTT surjective   ⟺  \iff⟺ TTT invertible (finite dimension only; it fails in infinite dimension: the shift operator T(x1,x2,…)=(0,x1,x2,…)T(x_1, x_2, \ldots) = (0, x_1, x_2, \ldots)T(x1​,x2​,…)=(0,x1​,x2​,…) is injective but not surjective);
  • the solution space of an nnn-variable homogeneous linear system has dimension =n−rank⁡A= n - \operatorname{rank} A=n−rankA.

⚠ The clean identity holds only when dim⁡V<∞\dim V < \inftydimV<∞;

in infinite dimension the right-hand side is a sum of cardinals, and one must write dim⁡V=dim⁡ker⁡T+dim⁡im⁡T\dim V = \dim \ker T + \dim \operatorname{im} TdimV=dimkerT+dimimT (which holds for cardinals).
