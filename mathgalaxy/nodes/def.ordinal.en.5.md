# ordinal　`def.ordinal`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=def.ordinal)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./def.ordinal.md)

Ordinals are not just "a generalization of $\mathbb{N}$". The ordinals after $\omega$ get longer and longer, but their **size** (cardinality) can stay at $\aleph_0$ — for ordinals, "longer" and "larger" are two different things.
