Statement

A subset Y⊆XY \subseteq XY⊆X is called kkk-open (kkk-closed) if for every continuous map f:S→Xf : S \to Xf:S→X (with SSS compact Hausdorff), f−1(Y)f^{-1}(Y)f−1(Y) is open (closed) in SSS.

The topology formed by all kkk-open sets on XXX is called the kkk-topology; write kXkXkX for "the same set with the topology replaced by the kkk-topology".

Notes

The kkk-topology is the final (finest) topology making all continuous maps S→XS \to XS→X (with SSS compact Hausdorff) continuous. It is finer than the original topology — there are more open sets (those originally open are of course still kkk-open).
