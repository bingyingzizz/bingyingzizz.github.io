Statement

Notes

Intuition: approximate fff from below by simple functions and take the supremum of their integrals. This differs from the Jordan measure recipe of “covering from outside and filling from inside” — here we only fill from inside.

Why no “outer cover” is required: the measure already sits on the dual side (μ\muμ itself is defined from outside via an outer measure), so taking an infimum over simple functions would only make the integral too small.

⚠ This definition works only for nonnegative functions. For a general real-valued function, split it into positive and negative parts and subtract (see “integral of a complex function”).
