# Quotients of Divisible Groups　`prop.divisible-quotient`

## Notes · **draft**
**Proof**: write $\pi : D \to D/H$.

Given $\bar{x} \in D/H$ and $n \ne 0$, choose a representative $x$; since $D$ is divisible there is $y$ with $n y = x$; then $n \bar{y} = \overline{n y} = \bar{x}$ ✓.

Hence multiplication by $n \cdot$ is surjective on $D/H$, so $D/H$ is divisible ✓.

∎

⚠️ This holds only for **quotients**: a subgroup of a divisible group need not be divisible ($\mathbb{Z} \subseteq \mathbb{Q}$: $\mathbb{Q}$ is divisible, $\mathbb{Z}$ is not).
