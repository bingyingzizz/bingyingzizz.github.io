Here $\sqrt{\det g}$ is the factor on the "density" side and $\mathrm{d}u\wedge\mathrm{d}v$ the orientation on the "form" side; both signs must be right to avoid errors.

**Why partitions of unity suffice**: $\omega$ is compactly supported, so only finitely many $\rho_{\alpha}\omega$ are non-zero, each supported in an oriented chart, reducing to the case of $\mathbb{R}^{n}$. Without compact support one replaces this by absolute integrability; this library does not elaborate.
