# topological abelian group　`def.topo-ab-group`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=def.topo-ab-group)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./def.topo-ab-group.md)

- The functor in $\underline{M} \mapsto M$ **has no obvious adjoint**, because $X \mapsto X(\bullet)$ (taking the underlying topological space) **does not preserve products**;
- Even if $M$ is a topological abelian group, $kM$ **need not be** a topological abelian group, because compactly generated spaces are not closed under products taken in topological spaces.
