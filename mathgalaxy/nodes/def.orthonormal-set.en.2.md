## Notes · **draft**
⚠️ Completeness is not "spanning $H$" but "the **closure** of the span is the whole space": the algebraic span of $\{e_n\}$ in $\ell^2$ consists of finitely supported sequences, which is dense but not equal to $\ell^2$.

⚠️ In infinite dimension **uncountable orthonormal bases really occur**: the Hermite functions in $L^2(\mathbb{R})$ are countable, but the orthonormal basis of a general $\ell^2(I)$ is $\{e_i\}_{i \in I}$, and $I$ can be arbitrarily large.
