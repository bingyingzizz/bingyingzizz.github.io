# Change of Variables on Manifolds　`thm.change-of-variables-manifold`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=thm.change-of-variables-manifold)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./thm.change-of-variables-manifold.md)

$$\int_{M}f^{\ast}\omega = \int_{N}\omega ;$$

**if $f$ reverses orientation**

$$\int_{M}f^{\ast}\omega = -\int_{N}\omega .$$

Both cases combine as $\int_{M}f^{\ast}\omega = \varepsilon\int_{N}\omega$, where $\varepsilon = +1$ means orientation-preserving and $-1$ orientation-reversing.
