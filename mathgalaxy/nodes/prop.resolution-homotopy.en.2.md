# Homotopy Uniqueness of Projective Resolutions　`prop.resolution-homotopy`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=prop.resolution-homotopy)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./prop.resolution-homotopy.md)

$$P_{\bullet} \xrightarrow{\ \varphi\ } Q_{\bullet}, \qquad Q_{\bullet} \xrightarrow{\ \psi\ } P_{\bullet}$$

such that $\psi\varphi \sim 1_{P_{\bullet}}$, $\varphi\psi \sim 1_{Q_{\bullet}}$. In other words: any two projective resolutions are isomorphic in the **homotopy category**.

In particular, for every $R$-module $N$ and every $n \ge 0$, the groups obtained by applying $\operatorname{Hom}(- , N)$ and taking homology are all isomorphic — hence $\operatorname{Ext}^{n}_{R}(M, N)$ is independent of the choice of resolution.
