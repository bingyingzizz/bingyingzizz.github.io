# orthogonal projection　`def.orthogonal-projection`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=def.orthogonal-projection)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./def.orthogonal-projection.md)

⭐ An idempotent operator that is not self-adjoint is called a **projection**: it projects "parallel to $\ker P$ onto $\operatorname{im} P$", and is in general not an orthogonal projection.

The difference between the two is whether the "shortest distance" is attained.
