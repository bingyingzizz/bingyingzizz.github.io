# cross ratio　`def.cross-ratio`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=def.cross-ratio)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./def.cross-ratio.md)

⭐ **It is the coordinate of "four-point geometry"**: a triangle is described by side lengths and angles, while four points need one complex quantity — the cross-ratio being real or taking a special value corresponds to the two classical facts "concyclic" and "harmonic division".

⚠️ The value depends on the **order** of the points: swapping two positions gives $1/\lambda$, $1-\lambda$, and four more values — six ways of writing the same "non-harmonic ratio". Fix the order before writing a formula.
