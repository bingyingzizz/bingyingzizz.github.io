Statement

Fix a commutative unital ring RRR. The category of RRR-algebras R-AlgR\text{-}\mathbf{Alg}R-Alg: objects are unital associative RRR-algebras (multiplication need not be commutative) and morphisms preserve 111 and are homomorphisms of RRR-algebras.

RRR is the initial object (r↦r1r\mapsto r1r↦r1); zero algebras (1=01=01=0) are allowed, and are the terminal object. Products are constructed pointwise; equalizers are subalgebras; pullbacks are fiber products. Coproducts are free products; coequalizers are quotient algebras (the generated two-sided ideal); pushouts are amalgamated free products.

Notes

Complete and cocomplete; not additive; in general not cartesian closed.

Commutative case: the coproduct of commutative RRR-algebras is ⊗R\otimes_R⊗R​; no separate entry is given for the category of commutative algebras, so this is noted here.

Convention on zero algebras: if zero algebras were disallowed, the category would have no terminal object; here they are allowed, so that a terminal object exists, as recorded in this note.
