# the disk does not retract onto the circle　`thm.no-retraction-disk`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=thm.no-retraction-disk)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./thm.no-retraction-disk.md)

💡 The conclusion looks like a mere "impossible", but it is the source of Brouwer's fixed point theorem, Poincaré–Miranda, and the family of theorems saying "the sphere cannot be deformed continuously to a point" (see the edge to `thm.brouwer-fixed-point`).
