# dual space　`def.dual-space`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=def.dual-space)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./def.dual-space.md)

$$T^{\ast} : W^{\ast} \to V^{\ast}, \qquad \varphi \longmapsto \varphi \circ T$$

It is linear, and $(S \circ T)^{\ast} = T^{\ast} \circ S^{\ast}$ (**contravariance**).

## Notes · **draft**
⭐ Mind the direction: the dual $T^{\ast}$ of $T : V \to W$ is $W^{\ast} \to V^{\ast}$, **the arrow is reversed**.

This is the entire meaning of the word "dual", and it is consistent with the status of $(-)^{\ast}$ as a contravariant functor.

⭐ In finite dimension $V \cong V^{\ast}$ (equal dimensions), but there is **no natural isomorphism** — to find one, a basis must first be chosen.
