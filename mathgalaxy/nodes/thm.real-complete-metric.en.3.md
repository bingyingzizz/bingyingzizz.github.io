# R is a complete metric space　`thm.real-complete-metric`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=thm.real-complete-metric)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./thm.real-complete-metric.md)

⭐ Following this edge outward leads to **completion**: every metric space embeds into a complete metric space (take Cauchy sequences and quotient), and the construction of $\mathbb{R}$ is the prototype — completing $\mathbb{Q}$ gives $\mathbb{R}$.

⚠ In a general metric space "complete" and "compact" are different: $\mathbb{R}$ is complete but not compact.
