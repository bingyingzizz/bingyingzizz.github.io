## Notes · **draft**
The three statements are one statement after taking complements: dense open $\leftrightarrow$ nowhere dense closed, intersection $\leftrightarrow$ union, "dense" $\leftrightarrow$ "empty interior".

⭐ **How category is used (the lecture notes' formulation)**: to prove "there exists a point satisfying property $P$", prove that $\{x : P(x)\}$ is a **dense $G_\delta$**. What this yields is not merely existence but "almost everywhere existence" (genericity).
