## Statement · **draft**
Let $(X, d)$ and $(Y, \rho)$ be metric spaces, $f : X \to Y$, and $x_0 \in X$.

We say $f$ is **continuous at** $x_0$ if

$$\forall \varepsilon > 0\ \exists \delta > 0:\quad d(x, x_0) < \delta \implies \rho(f(x), f(x_0)) < \varepsilon .$$
