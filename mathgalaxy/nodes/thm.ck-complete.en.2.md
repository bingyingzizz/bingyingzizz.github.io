# C(K,E) is complete when E is　`thm.ck-complete`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=thm.ck-complete)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./thm.ck-complete.md)

## Notes · **draft**
Proof: a Cauchy sequence $\implies$ pointwise Cauchy ($d(f_n(x), f_m(x)) \le \Delta(f_n, f_m)$) $\implies$ a pointwise limit $f$ ($E$ complete) $\implies$ the uniform estimate in $\Delta$ gives $f_n \to f$ $\implies$ a uniform limit of continuous functions is continuous.

⭐ Why it matters: the uniform convergence space lets one "keep limits of continuous functions inside continuous functions"; this is the premise of Stone–Weierstrass, Arzelà–Ascoli, and indeed of all of $C^*$-algebra theory.
