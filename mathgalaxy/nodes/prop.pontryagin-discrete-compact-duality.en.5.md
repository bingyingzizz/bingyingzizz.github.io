# duality between discrete and compact Hausdorff groups　`prop.pontryagin-discrete-compact-duality`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=prop.pontryagin-discrete-compact-duality)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./prop.pontryagin-discrete-compact-duality.md)

📌 This is **one half** of the Pontryagin duality dictionary (the other half concerns torsion and connectedness).

**Proof.**
- **$M$ discrete $\implies \widehat M$ compact**: Take a surjection $\mathbb{Z}^{(I)} \twoheadrightarrow M$. Duality turns the surjection into an **injection** (by precomposition), and its image is closed ($\widehat M \hookrightarrow \mathbb{T}^{I}$ is a closed embedding).
