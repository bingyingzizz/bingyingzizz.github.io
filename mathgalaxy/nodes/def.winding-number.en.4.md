# winding number　`def.winding-number`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=def.winding-number)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./def.winding-number.md)

⭐ Relation to the argument: the integral in the exponent is precisely one choice of $\log(\gamma(t) - a_0)$ varying continuously along $\gamma$ (a logarithm can only be chosen branch by branch along a path), and its imaginary part is the change of the argument.

⚠ The winding number depends on **orientation**: traversing the same curve backwards changes its sign.

⚠ "$n(\gamma, a_0) = 0$" does **not** mean "$a_0$ lies outside the curve" — going in and coming back out again also gives $0$. Only for a simple closed curve (Jordan curve) is it a criterion for inside versus outside.
