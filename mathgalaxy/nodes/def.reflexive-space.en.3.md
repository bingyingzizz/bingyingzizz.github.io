# reflexive Banach space　`def.reflexive-space`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=def.reflexive-space)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./def.reflexive-space.md)

⭐ The core use of reflexivity: bounded sequences have weakly convergent subsequences (Eberlein–Šmulian; the graph has no weak-topology node yet), which is the entry point to variational existence and to Milman–Pettis.

📌 Relation to pairing language: $X$ is reflexive $\iff$ both embeddings of the perfect pairing $(X, X^*)$ are surjective (`def.perfect-pairing`, `thm.riesz-perfect-pairing`).
