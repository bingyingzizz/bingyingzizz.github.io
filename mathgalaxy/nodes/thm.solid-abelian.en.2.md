# solid abelian groups form an abelian subcategory　`thm.solid-abelian`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=thm.solid-abelian)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./thm.solid-abelian.md)

Let $\mathbf{Solid} \subset \operatorname{Cond}(\mathbf{Ab})$ be the full subcategory of solid abelian groups. Then:

**(i)** $\mathbf{Solid}$ is an abelian subcategory, stable under all limits, colimits and extensions; the objects $\prod_I \mathbb{Z}$ ($I$ arbitrary) form a family of **compact projective generators**; the inclusion has a left adjoint

$$M \longmapsto M^{\blacksquare} : \operatorname{Cond}(\mathbf{Ab}) \longrightarrow \mathbf{Solid},$$

which is the unique colimit-preserving extension of $\mathbb{Z}[S] \mapsto Z[S]^{\blacksquare}$ (**solidification**).
