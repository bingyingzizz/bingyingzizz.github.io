## Notes · **draft**
The question it answers: **can $F$ be extended to $\mathcal{C}'$?** The left Kan extension is the **best left approximate extension** of $F$ — it does not ask that $F$ actually extend, only that, on $\mathcal{C}$, among functors agreeing with $F$ it fits best.
