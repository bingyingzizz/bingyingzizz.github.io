# termwise operations on power series　`thm.power-series-analytic`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=thm.power-series-analytic)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./thm.power-series-analytic.md)

- on $|x - x_0| < R$ the function $f$ is continuous, and on every $[x_0 - r, x_0 + r]$ ($r < R$) it converges **uniformly**;
- $f$ is differentiable on $|x - x_0| < R$, and **term-by-term differentiation** gives the derivative, with the same radius of convergence $R$:

$$f'(x) = \sum_{n=1}^{\infty} n\, a_n (x - x_0)^{n-1}$$

- likewise one may **integrate term by term**, again with radius of convergence $R$.
