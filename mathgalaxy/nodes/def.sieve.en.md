## Statement · **draft**
Let $X \in \mathcal{C}$.

A **sieve** on $X$ is a subfunctor $R \subseteq h_{X}$ of the presheaf $h_{X} = \operatorname{Hom}_{\mathcal{C}}(-, X)$: for each $X'$ it gives a subset $R(X') \subseteq \operatorname{Hom}_{\mathcal{C}}(X', X)$, such that whenever $f \in R(X')$, every $g : X'' \to X'$ satisfies $f \circ g \in R(X'')$.
