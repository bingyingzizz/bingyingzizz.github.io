**Proof of the corollary (at the chain level)**: Write $\iota_{m} : C_{*}(K^{(m)}) \hookrightarrow S_{*}(|K|)$ and $\iota : C_{*}(K) \hookrightarrow S_{*}(|K|)$ for the inclusions. Apply the theorem to the identity map $\operatorname{id} : |K| \to |K|$ to obtain $m$ and a simplicial map $g : K^{(m)} \to K$ such that $|g| \simeq \operatorname{id}$.

- $\iota\,g_{*} = (|g|)_{*}\,\iota_{m}$ ($g$ is simplicial, and both sides give the same image on each simplex).
