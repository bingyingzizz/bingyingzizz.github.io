## Statement · **draft**
If $X$ is Baire and $\Omega \subseteq X$ is open, then $\implies$ $\Omega$ (with the subspace topology) is a Baire space.

## Notes · **draft**
Proof: let $O_n$ be dense open in $\Omega$ and write $O_n = \Omega \cap O_n'$ with $O_n'$ open in $X$.
