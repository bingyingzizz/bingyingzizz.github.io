[in graph](../#n=def.derived-category)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./def.derived-category.md)

$$K^{\bullet} \xleftarrow{\ \sim\ } K'^{\bullet} \longrightarrow L^{\bullet},$$

The arrow on the left is a **quasi-isomorphism** (now turned into an isomorphism).

## Notes · **draft**
⭐ **Why quotient once more in $K(\mathcal{A})$**: homotopic morphisms are identified in $K(\mathcal{A})$, but **quasi-isomorphisms are not yet isomorphisms** — and what derived functors, $\operatorname{Ext}$ and spectral sequences really want is the category where "quasi-isomorphism = isomorphism".

$D(\mathcal{A})$ was built for exactly this.
