# category of monoids　`def.category-of-monoids`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=def.category-of-monoids)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./def.category-of-monoids.md)

**Relation to $\mathbf{Grp}$**: groups form a full subcategory (monoids in which every element is invertible), and the inclusion has a left adjoint, “groupification”.

**Not additive**: $\hom(M,N)$ has no monoid structure in general; the free monoid is the word monoid. Cartesian closedness is not developed here.
