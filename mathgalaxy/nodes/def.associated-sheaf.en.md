## Statement · **draft**
Let $\mathcal{C}$ be a site and $X \in \mathcal{C}$. The **associated sheaf** is "first Yoneda-embed, then sheafify":

$$X^{\sharp} \;:=\; h_{X}^{\#}, \qquad h_{X} = \operatorname{Hom}_{\mathcal{C}}(-, X).$$

It is characterized by a **universal property**: for every sheaf $F$,

$$\operatorname{Hom}\bigl(X^{\sharp},\ F\bigr) \;\cong\; F(X).$$
