# change of basis and similarity　`thm.change-of-basis`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=thm.change-of-basis)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./thm.change-of-basis.md)

$A$ and $B$ are equivalent (only $B = PAQ$ with $P, Q$ invertible is required) $\iff$ they have equal rank (even the spaces may change).

⚠ Similarity is much stronger than equivalence: similar matrices **have the same rank, trace, determinant and characteristic polynomial** — these are similarity invariants (needed later for the theory of eigenvalues).
