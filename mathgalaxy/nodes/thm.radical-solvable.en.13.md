**Taking closures at each level**: Let $M_{i}$ be the compositum of all conjugates of $E_{i}$ generated under $F^{\prime}$. Then $M_{0}=F^{\prime}$ and $M_{m}=M$, and each $M_{i}/F^{\prime}$ is Galois. The key is that $M_{i}$ is obtained from $M_{i-1}$ by adjoining several conjugates of $\alpha_{i}$, denoted by $\beta$, with $\beta^{n_{i}}\in M_{i-1}$. Write $\beta=\sigma(\alpha_{i})$. Then $\beta^{n_{i}}=\sigma(\alpha_{i}^{n_{i}})\in\sigma(F_{i-1})\subseteq M_{i-1}$.
