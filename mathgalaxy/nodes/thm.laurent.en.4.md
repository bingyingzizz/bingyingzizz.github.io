# Laurent Expansion Theorem　`thm.laurent`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=thm.laurent)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./thm.laurent.md)

⭐ Uniqueness: the coefficients are given by the integral formula, so they do not depend on the way the expansion is obtained — in practice one may use any algebraic manipulation (geometric series, undetermined coefficients), and the result is necessarily the same.

⚠ The annulus must not degenerate into a disc or an exterior domain: when $r = 0$ the principal part may diverge, and when $R = +\infty$ the regular part may diverge.

**The degenerate cases are precisely the entry point to the classification of singularities**: when $r = 0$ the three shapes of the principal part correspond to the three types of isolated singularities.
