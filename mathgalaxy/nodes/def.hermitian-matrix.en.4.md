# Hermitian and Symmetric Matrices　`def.hermitian-matrix`

**Why eigenvectors of distinct eigenvalues are orthogonal**: if $Ax = \lambda x$ and $Ay = \mu y$, then $\lambda\langle x,y\rangle = \langle Ax,y\rangle = \langle x,Ay\rangle = \mu\langle x,y\rangle$, while $\lambda \ne \mu$ $\implies$ $\langle x,y\rangle = 0$.

These two lines are the core computation of the whole chapter; the spectral theorem starts from them.
