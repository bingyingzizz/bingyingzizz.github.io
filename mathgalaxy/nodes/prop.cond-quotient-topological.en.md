## Statement · **draft**
Let $N$ be a **closed** subgroup of a locally compact Hausdorff abelian group $M$. Then the condensed group associated to the topological quotient agrees with the quotient of the associated condensed groups:

$$\underline{M/N} \;\cong\; \underline{M}\big/\underline{N}.$$
