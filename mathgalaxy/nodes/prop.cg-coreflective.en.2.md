# compactly generated spaces form a coreflective subcategory　`prop.cg-coreflective`

## Abstract · **reviewed**
The category $k\mathbf{Top}$ of compactly generated spaces is a coreflective subcategory of $\mathbf{Top}$: the inclusion $i$ has a right adjoint $k$ that keeps the underlying set and replaces the topology by the $k$-topology, $i \dashv k$, i.e. $\operatorname{Hom}_{\mathbf{Top}}(X, Y) \cong \operatorname{Hom}_{k\mathbf{Top}}(X, kY)$ for compactly generated $X$. The counit $kY \to Y$ is the identity map, continuous because the $k$-topology is the finer one.
