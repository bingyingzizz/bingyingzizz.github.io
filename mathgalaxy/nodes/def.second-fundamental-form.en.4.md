# Second Fundamental Form　`def.second-fundamental-form`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=def.second-fundamental-form)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./def.second-fundamental-form.md)

**Independent of parameters**: $II$ is a tensor, and $L, M, N$ are merely its components relative to $(u,v)$. Under $n \mapsto -n$ one has $II \mapsto -II$, while the definition of $W$ already carries a minus sign and changes sign accordingly, so $K$ and $|H|$ are unchanged.

**Geometric meaning**: $II(v,v)$ is "how much one bends in the normal direction while walking along $v$"; together with $E, F, G$, $L, M, N$ determine the two principal curvatures (solve $\det(h-kg) = 0$).
