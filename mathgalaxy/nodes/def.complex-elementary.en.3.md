## Notes · **draft**
**Why define them this way**: the real exponential is $\sum x^{n}/n!$, and the power series still converges on the complex plane — replacing $x$ by $z$ suffices, with no new axioms. The trigonometric definitions are the same: combining $e^{iz}$ and $e^{-iz}$ gives Euler's formula.

**Holomorphy**: a power series may be differentiated term by term inside its disc of convergence, and these three series have radius $\infty$; hence they are holomorphic on $\mathbb{C}$, and $(e^{z})' = e^{z}$, $(\sin z)' = \cos z$, $(\cos z)' = -\sin z$.
