# Mayer–Vietoris sequence in homology　`thm.mv-homology`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=thm.mv-homology)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./thm.mv-homology.md)

**Reduced version.** Apply the same argument to the augmented chain complexes. When $U \cap V \ne \emptyset$, choose a basepoint $x_{0} \in U \cap V$. The augmentation map $\varepsilon$ agrees on all three complexes, and the short exact sequence remains exact in the augmentation degree, giving the reduced long exact sequence. When $U \cap V = \emptyset$, exactness fails in the segment $\tilde{H}_{0}$. See the statement for a counterexample.
