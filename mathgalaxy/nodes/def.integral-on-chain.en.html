Statement

What it does: integration over a chain is not a new kind of integral — pull the form back to coordinates along the cube, and what remains is an ordinary multiple integral. The coordinate order carried by the cube is its orientation, so no separate orientation has to be chosen.

Notes

What the coefficient fff is: f=c∗ω(∂1,…,∂k)f = c^{\ast}\omega(\partial_{1},\ldots,\partial_{k})f=c∗ω(∂1​,…,∂k​), that is, feed the tangent vectors in the coordinate directions of the cube to the pulled-back form. It is a smooth function; its values on the boundary are determined by the smooth extension of ccc, and measure-zero sets do not affect the integral.

Why no orientation is needed: the coordinate order of the cube already fixes the orientation — dx1∧⋯∧dxk\mathrm{d}x^{1}\wedge\cdots\wedge\mathrm{d}x^{k}dx1∧⋯∧dxk on IkI^{k}Ik is the standard orientation.

To make the cube "run backwards", replace some coordinate by 1−xi1-x^{i}1−xi;

then c∗ωc^{\ast}\omegac∗ω automatically changes sign, with no extra rule.

So "orientation" is absorbed into the parametrization on the chain side.

Division of labour with 形式的积分: there, an nnn-form is integrated over an open set, and partitions of unity are needed to glue to the manifold; here, a kkk-form is integrated along one cube, one multiple integral from start to finish. The global meaning is carried by chains (summation), not by partitions of unity — this is the most economical feature of this bookkeeping.

⚠️ Degrees must match: a kkk-form only pairs with a kkk-chain. Pulling back a jjj-form along a kkk-cube (j<kj<kj<k) is still meaningful, but that is a jjj-form on a kkk-dimensional region and cannot be integrated; for j>kj>kj>k the pullback is simply zero. So "pairing of chains and forms" is defined only in equal degrees.

⚠️ Degenerate cubes: if ccc has less than full rank, c∗ωc^{\ast}\omegac∗ω may be zero (for instance if ccc is constant), and the integral is then automatically zero. The definition needs no patch for the degenerate case.
