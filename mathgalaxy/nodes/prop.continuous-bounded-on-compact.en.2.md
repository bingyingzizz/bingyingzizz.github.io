## Notes · **draft**
⭐ **Neither hypothesis** in the conclusion **can be dropped**:
- not compact: $f(x) = 1/x$ is continuous on $(0, 1)$ but unbounded;
- not continuous: $f(x) = 1/x$ on $[0, 1]$ (with $f(0)$ assigned arbitrarily) is unbounded.

⚠ In a general topological space the cleaner formulation is "the continuous image of a compact set is compact", together with "compact subsets of $\mathbb{R}$ are bounded"; the proof here takes the sequence route. Both routes are correct; this one is shorter.
