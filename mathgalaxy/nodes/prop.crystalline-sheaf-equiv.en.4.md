# Crystalline Sheaves Are Sheaves on X　`prop.crystalline-sheaf-equiv`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=prop.crystalline-sheaf-equiv)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./prop.crystalline-sheaf-equiv.md)

ed.
- The reverse construction from sheaves to étale spaces uses the disjoint union of stalks with the germ topology; from étale spaces to sheaves one takes local sections. That they are mutually inverse is the standard theorem for sheaves and local homeomorphisms, not a mere change of terminology.
- The crystalline condition cannot be dropped: transitions of a general compatible system need not be isomorphisms, so the whole system cannot be recovered from the data at the terminal object; crystalline also does not mean arbitrary homotopy invariance.
