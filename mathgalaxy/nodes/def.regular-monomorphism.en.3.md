# regular monomorphism　`def.regular-monomorphism`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=def.regular-monomorphism)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./def.regular-monomorphism.md)

**Relation to monomorphisms**: a regular monomorphism is always a monomorphism (an equalizer is monic), but not conversely. In $\mathbf{Top}$ monomorphisms are injective continuous maps, whereas regular monomorphisms require the subspace topology to be exactly the one given by the equalizer, so not all are regular.

**In $\mathbf{Ab}$**: monomorphisms, regular monomorphisms, and kernels of some map all coincide; this is also the origin of the term "kernel".
