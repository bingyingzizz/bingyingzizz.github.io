# subspace　`def.subspace`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=def.subspace)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./def.subspace.md)

**Inclusion** between subspaces is defined as set inclusion, and the **intersection** of any family of subspaces is again a subspace (verify addition and scalar multiplication term by term).

## Notes · **draft**
⭐ The first of the three conditions can be absorbed into the other two: it suffices that $U \ne \emptyset$ and that the set is closed under subtraction (take $u - u = 0$).

Writing the three conditions separately is useful for **checking them one by one**.

⚠ **A union is generally not a subspace**: the union of the $x$-axis and the $y$-axis does not even contain $(1,0)+(0,1)$.
