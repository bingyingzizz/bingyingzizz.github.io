## Statement · **reviewed**
Let $X$ be a metric space with metric $d$. Then the following three conditions are **equivalent**:

- (a) $X$ is **compact**: every open cover has a finite subcover;
- (b) $X$ is **sequentially compact**: every sequence has a subsequence converging in $X$;
- (c) $X$ is **complete and totally bounded**.
