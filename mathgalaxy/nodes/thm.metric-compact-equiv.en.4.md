## Notes · **draft**
The equivalence of compactness, sequential compactness, and completeness plus total boundedness is proved on the edge `imp.metric-compact-equiv-proof`: compact $\implies$ sequentially compact uses the contradiction that a neighbourhood of every point meets only finitely many terms; sequentially compact $\implies$ complete/totally bounded uses Cauchy subsequences and $\varepsilon$-nets; totally bounded $\implies$ compact uses Lebesgue numbers.
