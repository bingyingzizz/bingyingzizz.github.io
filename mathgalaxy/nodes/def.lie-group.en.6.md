**Why $\operatorname{O}(n)$ is a manifold**: it is the zero set of $F(A) = A^{\mathsf{T}}A - I$, and $\mathrm{d}F_{A}(B) = A^{\mathsf{T}}B + B^{\mathsf{T}}A$ is surjective on $F^{-1}(0)$ (given a symmetric $S$, take $B = AS/2$), so `thm.regular-level-set` gives $\dim = n^{2} - n(n+1)/2 = n(n-1)/2$.

The group structure comes from "the product of orthogonal matrices is again orthogonal".
