# concatenation of paths　`lem.path-product`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=lem.path-product)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./lem.path-product.md)

💡 In one sentence: **multiplication at the level of paths is bad (not associative, no unit), at the level of homotopy classes it is good (a group)** — which is why the fundamental group collects "classes" rather than "paths".
