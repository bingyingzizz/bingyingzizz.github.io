# Pullback: Between Open Sets　`prop.pullback-forms`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=prop.pullback-forms)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./prop.pullback-forms.md)

⚠️ **Pullback does not preserve the orientation of an integral**: $\int_{U}F^{\ast}\omega = \int_{F(U)}\omega$ holds only when $F$ is an orientation-preserving diffeomorphism and the degree of the form equals the dimension (change of variables, `def.integral-form`).

⚠️ The order in (4) must not be reversed: the pullback of the composite $G\circ F$ is $F^{\ast}\circ G^{\ast}$, i.e. apply the later map first.
