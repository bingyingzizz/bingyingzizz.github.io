Moreover, $H^{n}(\operatorname{Hom}(P_{\bullet}, N)) = \operatorname{Hom}_{K}(P_{\bullet}, N[n])$ (the standard identification of degree-$n$ cohomology with homotopy classes) ✓, so the left-hand side also equals $\operatorname{Hom}_{D}(M, N[n])$ ✓.
