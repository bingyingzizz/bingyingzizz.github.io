⚠️ **In infinite dimensions "isometry" $\ne$ "unitary"**: the unilateral shift $S(x_1, x_2, \ldots) = (0, x_1, x_2, \ldots)$ satisfies $\|Sx\| = \|x\|$ (isometry) but is not surjective; $S$ is a partial isometry, not a unitary operator. For the full definition and equivalent conditions for partial isometries see @@def.partial-isometry@@.

⭐ The polar decomposition $u = V|u|$ (with $V$ a partial isometry and $|u| = \sqrt{u^*u}$) is the standard way partial isometries appear. For the finite-dimensional version see @@def.unitary-matrix@@.
