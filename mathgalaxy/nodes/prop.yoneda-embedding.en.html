Abstract

The assignment δ:C→C^\delta : \mathcal{C} \to \widehat{\mathcal{C}}δ:C→C, X↦hX=Hom⁡C(−,X)X \mapsto h_X = \operatorname{Hom}_{\mathcal{C}}(-, X)X↦hX​=HomC​(−,X), is fully faithful and preserves all limits. Fully faithful means Hom⁡C^(hX,hY)≅Hom⁡C(X,Y)\operatorname{Hom}_{\widehat{\mathcal{C}}}(h_X, h_Y) \cong \operatorname{Hom}_{\mathcal{C}}(X, Y)HomC​(hX​,hY​)≅HomC​(X,Y): the arrows between representable functors reproduce the arrows of C\mathcal{C}C exactly, no more and no fewer. Embedding C\mathcal{C}C this way moves problems into a category that has all limits and colimits.
