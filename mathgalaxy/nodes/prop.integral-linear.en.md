## Statement · **draft**
Let $f, g$ be integrable on $[a, b]$ and $c \in \mathbb{R}$. Then $f + g$ and $cf$ are integrable, and

$$\int_a^b (f + g) = \int_a^b f + \int_a^b g, \qquad \int_a^b cf = c\int_a^b f$$
