Then θj(ai)=δijid⁡Vj\theta_j(a_i)=\delta_{ij}\operatorname{id}_{V_j}θj​(ai​)=δij​idVj​​, since eie_iei​ acts as δij\delta_{ij}δij​ times the identity on VjV_jVj​.
