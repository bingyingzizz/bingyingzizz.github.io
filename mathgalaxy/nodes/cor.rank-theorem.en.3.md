# Constant Rank Theorem　`cor.rank-theorem`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=cor.rank-theorem)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./cor.rank-theorem.md)

This is a **classification theorem** in calculus, and the culmination of the "local $\to$ normal form" method.

⭐ Three corollaries at once:
- $r = n = m$: $\psi \circ f \circ \varphi^{-1} = \mathrm{id}$ $\implies$ a local isomorphism, i.e. the **inverse mapping theorem**;
- $r = n < m$: $x \mapsto (x, 0)$, the normal form of an **immersion**;
- $r = m < n$: $(x_1, \ldots, x_n) \mapsto (x_1, \ldots, x_m)$, the normal form of a **submersion** (local projection).
