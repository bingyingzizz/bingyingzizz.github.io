# Adjoints between Additive Categories Are Additive　`prop.adjoint-additive`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=prop.adjoint-additive)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./prop.adjoint-additive.md)

$$\operatorname{Hom}_{\mathcal{D}}(FM, N) \;\cong\; \operatorname{Hom}_{\mathcal{C}}(M, GN)$$

is an isomorphism of **abelian groups** (natural in both $M$ and $N$).

## Notes · **draft**
⭐ In one sentence: **once there is an adjunction, additivity comes for free.** So an adjunction between additive categories can always be treated as an additive adjunction, and $\operatorname{Hom}(FM,N) \cong \operatorname{Hom}(M,GN)$ is automatically upgraded to a group isomorphism.
