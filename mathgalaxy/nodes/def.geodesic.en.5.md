# Geodesic　`def.geodesic`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=def.geodesic)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./def.geodesic.md)

**Why parallel transport preserves length**: $\frac{\mathrm{d}}{\mathrm{d}s}\langle \dot\gamma,\dot\gamma\rangle = 2\langle \nabla_{\dot\gamma}\dot\gamma,\dot\gamma\rangle$, and on a geodesic this expression vanishes, so the speed is constant and the parameter is necessarily an affine function of arc length.

**Every example is worth remembering**: geodesics in the plane are straight lines; on the sphere they are great circles; on a surface of revolution they are governed by Clairaut's relation; on a cylinder one simply rolls a straight line of the development back up.
