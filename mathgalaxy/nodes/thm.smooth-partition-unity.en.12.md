# Smooth Partition of Unity　`thm.smooth-partition-unity`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=thm.smooth-partition-unity)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./thm.smooth-partition-unity.md)

**What it is used to prove**: Existence of Riemannian metrics (`def.riemannian-metric`), existence of fiber metrics on vector bundles (`prop.bundle-metric`), integration of top-degree forms (`def.integral-form`), and characterization of orientability by a nowhere-vanishing $n$-form (`prop.volume-form-orientation`). Each proof constructs the objects locally and glues them with a partition of unity.
