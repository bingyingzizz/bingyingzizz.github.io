# argument principle　`thm.argument-principle`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=thm.argument-principle)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./thm.argument-principle.md)

Let $\gamma$ be a **positively oriented, simple** piecewise smooth closed curve (counterclockwise, no self-intersection), let $\Omega$ contain $\gamma$ and its interior, let $f$ be holomorphic on $\Omega$ except at finitely many poles, and suppose $\gamma$ contains neither zeros nor poles. Then

$$\frac{1}{2\pi i}\oint_{\gamma} \frac{f'(z)}{f(z)}\,\mathrm{d}z = Z - P = n(f \circ \gamma,\, 0),$$

where $Z$ is the number of zeros of $f$ inside $\gamma$ (counted with multiplicity), $P$ is the number of poles (counted with order), and $n$ is the winding number.
