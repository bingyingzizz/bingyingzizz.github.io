## Notes · **draft**
Use the Fitting decomposition $V=\operatorname{Im}(f^\infty)\oplus\operatorname{Ker}(f^\infty)$. Both summands are subrepresentations, so indecomposability forces one of them to be $0$. If $\operatorname{Ker}=0$, then $f$ is injective hence an automorphism; if $\operatorname{Im}=0$, then $f$ is nilpotent. This is the all-or-nothing property used in Krull–Schmidt.
