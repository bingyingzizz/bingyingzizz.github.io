## Notes · **draft**
This is the **standard source** of absolutely continuous measures, and exactly what the Radon–Nikodym theorem proves in reverse: **every** absolutely continuous $\nu$ has this form.

In notation one often writes the differential form:

$$\nu(E) = \int_E f d\mu\quad  \iff\quad  d\nu = f d\mu$$
