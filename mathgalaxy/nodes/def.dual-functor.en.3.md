## Notes · **draft**
**Contravariant is not the same as starting from the opposite category**: this is only a way of writing a contravariant functor as a covariant functor on $\mathbf{Vect}_K^{\mathrm{op}}$, which is why the order of composition is reversed.

**Infinite dimension**: $\eta_V$ is injective but generally not surjective, so $V\to V^{\ast\ast}$ is not an isomorphism; duality does not make all of $\mathbf{Vect}_K$ self-dual.

**Relation to the Hom functor**: $(-)^{\ast}=\operatorname{Hom}_K(-,K)$ is the special case $W=K$ of the contravariant Hom functor (see the Hom functor node).
