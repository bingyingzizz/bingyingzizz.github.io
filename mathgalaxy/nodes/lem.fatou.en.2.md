# Fatou's lemma　`lem.fatou`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=lem.fatou)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./lem.fatou.md)

This is what remains of MCT once monotonicity is dropped — without monotonicity, equality degenerates into an inequality.

A counterexample in the other direction: $f_n = \chi_{[n, n+1]}$ (on $\mathbb{R}$) has $\int f_{n} = 1$ for every index, but $\int \liminf f_{n} = 0$.
