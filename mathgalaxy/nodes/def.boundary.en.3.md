## Notes · **draft**
⭐ **All of $d^{2} = 0$ is this single statement**: $B^{n} \subseteq Z^{n}$. The axiom $d\circ d = 0$ has no other use — its only purpose is to make the quotient "cycles / boundaries" exist. So a complex is "a device that lets homology be defined".

⚠️ **"Boundary" collides with the topological boundary**: the boundary of a manifold $\partial M$ and the boundary operator of a simplicial complex $\partial_{n}$ are, in the language of complexes, the boundary here ($\partial_{n}$ is $d_{n}$).

But a **general category has no notion of "point-set boundary"**, only images.

Always read "boundary" as $\operatorname{im} d$.
