## Statement · **draft**
Fix a site $\mathcal{C}$. Write

$$\widehat{\mathcal{C}}(\mathbf{Ab}) := \operatorname{Hom}(\mathcal{C}^{\mathrm{op}}, \mathbf{Ab})$$

for the category of **presheaves of abelian groups**, and $\widetilde{\mathcal{C}}(\mathbf{Ab})$ for the full subcategory of **sheaves** in it. The latter is called the **abelian sheaves** on $\mathcal{C}$, also the **abelian groups** on $\mathcal{C}$.
