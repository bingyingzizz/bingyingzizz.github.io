## Notes · **draft**
⭐ This is the precise meaning of "rational open set = affine open set": each rational open set is itself an $\operatorname{Spa}$, obtained by inverting $f$ and adjoining $g_i/f$ to $A^+$.

Thus adic spaces have a **basis of affine open sets**, and one can do sheaf theory on them as on schemes.
