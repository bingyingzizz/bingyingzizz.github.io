# best approximation and the projection formula　`thm.projection-formula`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=thm.projection-formula)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./thm.projection-formula.md)

⭐ The explicit formula shows the power of an orthonormal basis: the projection need not solve a linear system, only compute $k$ inner products.

⚠ "$U$ finite-dimensional" or "$U$ closed" is necessary: in infinite dimension, when the subspace is not closed, the closest **point may fail to exist** (only the infimum exists).
