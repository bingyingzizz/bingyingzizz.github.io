# compactly supported pushforward　`def.f-shriek`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=def.f-shriek)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./def.f-shriek.md)

⚠ "Preserves direct sums" is the key to the existence of $f^!$: $j_!$, as a left adjoint, preserves colimits, and $(f^{\mathrm{ad}/Y})_*$ is, locally on affines, a limit of finitely many forgetful functors, hence also preserves direct sums.

⭐ General **existence** is in `thm.f-shriek-existence` (still marked skeleton: conditional on $j_{!}$ for open immersions).
