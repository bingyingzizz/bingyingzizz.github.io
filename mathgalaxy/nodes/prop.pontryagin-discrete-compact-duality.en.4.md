# duality between discrete and compact Hausdorff groups　`prop.pontryagin-discrete-compact-duality`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=prop.pontryagin-discrete-compact-duality)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./prop.pontryagin-discrete-compact-duality.md)

**Two examples**: $\widehat{\mathbb{Z}} \cong \mathbb{T}$ (discrete $\to$ compact), $\widehat{\mathbb{T}} \cong \mathbb{Z}$ (compact $\to$ discrete).

⭐ **This illustrates the origin of the idea of using compact Hausdorff spaces to probe other objects**: A discrete group can be turned into a compact Hausdorff space by duality. Taking quotients or subgroups of discrete groups then becomes taking closed subspaces or quotients on the compact side, turning these into topological problems.
