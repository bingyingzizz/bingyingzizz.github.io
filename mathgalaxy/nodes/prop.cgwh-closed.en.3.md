## Notes · **draft**
Item 2 is exactly the one repeatedly needed in condensed mathematics: assembling "objects built from finitely many pieces" into a larger one along a filtered colimit, without losing the property $\mathrm{CGWH}$.

**Proof.**
- **Open subspaces**: $U \subseteq X$ open.

For a compact Hausdorff $g : S\to U$, its image $g(S)$ is closed in $X$, hence in the subspace topology of $U$ $g(S) = g(S)\cap U$ is closed $\implies$ $U$ is weak Hausdorff ✓;
