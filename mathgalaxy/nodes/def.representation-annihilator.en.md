## Statement · **draft**
For a representation $V$ of $A$, define $\operatorname{Ann}(V)=\{a\in A: aV=0\}$. It is a two-sided ideal of $A$, equal to the kernel of the algebra homomorphism $\theta:A\to\operatorname{End}_F(V)$; $V$ is called faithful if $\operatorname{Ann}(V)=0$.
