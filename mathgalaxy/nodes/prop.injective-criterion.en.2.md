# injective criterion　`prop.injective-criterion`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=prop.injective-criterion)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./prop.injective-criterion.md)

Moreover: if $0 \to M' \to M \to M'' \to 0$ is exact and $M'$ is injective, then

$M$ **injective** $\iff M\prime\prime$ **injective**

## Notes · **draft**
The proof uses a $3 \times 3$ $\operatorname{Hom}$ diagram: stretch the short exact sequence $0 \to A \to B \to C \to 0$ into three rows by the family $\operatorname{Hom}(-, M)$, read the middle row $0 \to \ker f \to \ker g \to \ker h \to \operatorname{coker} f \to \operatorname{coker} g \to 0$ off two applications of the snake lemma, and observe that the first and third rows are exact, so $\ker g = \operatorname{coker} g = 0$.
