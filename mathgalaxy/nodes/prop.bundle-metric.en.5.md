# Fiber Metric on a Vector Bundle　`prop.bundle-metric`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=prop.bundle-metric)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./prop.bundle-metric.md)

**Why metrics face no obstruction while other structures do**: positive-definite symmetric forms form a **convex set**, and convex combinations stay positive definite; complex structures ($J^{2} = -\operatorname{id}$) and symplectic structures ($\omega$ non-degenerate and closed) are not convex conditions, cannot be glued, and have topological obstructions. So "a Riemannian metric on $TM$ always exists, an almost complex structure need not" is not a technical gap but the difference between **convex and non-convex**.
