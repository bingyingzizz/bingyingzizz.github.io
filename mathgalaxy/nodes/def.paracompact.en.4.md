# Paracompact Space　`def.paracompact`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=def.paracompact)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./def.paracompact.md)

**Heredity**: **open** and **closed** subspaces both inherit paracompactness (open subspace $Y$: one **cannot** simply add $X\setminus Y$ to the cover — it need not be open; the correct route is first to take $Y$ open, extend each member openly inside $X$, lift the cover to an open cover of $X$, then take a locally finite open refinement and intersect it with $Y$; closed subspace: intersect the members of the open cover with the closed subspace). A general subspace need not be paracompact.
