# Open, Closed Subspaces and Filtered Colimits in CGWH　`prop.cgwh-closed`

[in graph](../#n=prop.cgwh-closed)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./prop.cgwh-closed.md)

## Statement · **draft**
1. **Open subspaces** and **closed subspaces** of compactly generated weak Hausdorff spaces are still compactly generated and weak Hausdorff.
2. If $X = \varinjlim X_{i}$ is a **filtered** colimit of compactly generated weak Hausdorff spaces along **closed inclusions**, then $X$ is also compactly generated weak Hausdorff, and every $X_{i}$ is closed in $X$.
