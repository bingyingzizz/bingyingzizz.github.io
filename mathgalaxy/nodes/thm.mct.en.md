## Statement · **draft**
Let $\{f_n\} \subseteq L^+$ be a **pointwise increasing** sequence:

$$f_1 \le f_2 \le \cdots, \quad  f_n \uparrow  f$$

Then the integral and the limit can be exchanged:

$$\int f = \lim_{n\to\infty} \int f_n$$

## Notes · **draft**
⭐ This is the **engine** of the whole Lebesgue integration theory: term-by-term integration, Fatou, and the DCT are all corollaries.
