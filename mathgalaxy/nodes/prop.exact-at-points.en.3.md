# Exactness Is Pointwise Exactness　`prop.exact-at-points`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=prop.exact-at-points)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./prop.exact-at-points.md)

**Hypotheses used in each direction** (exactness at each place is a combination of definitions; elementwise judgement has further restrictions):
- Short exact $\iff$ exact at all three places, left exact $\iff$ exact at the first two, right exact $\iff$ exact at the last two: both directions are unfoldings of the definition of an exact sequence, and need no splitting, injective objects, or the axiom of choice.
- Exactness at the left end $\iff\ker i=0\iff i$ is a monomorphism, exactness at the right end $\iff\operatorname{coker}p=0\iff p$ is an epimorphism: the first step uses the zero object, the second the kernel and cok
