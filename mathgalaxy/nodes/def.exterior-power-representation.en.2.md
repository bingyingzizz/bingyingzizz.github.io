## Statement · **draft**
Let $\operatorname{char}F\ne2$ and let $(V,\rho)$ be a representation of a group $G$. The exterior square $\operatorname{Alt}^2V$ (also written $\bigwedge^2V$) is the $-1$ eigenspace of the swap on $V\otimes V$; $G$ acts by $g(v\wedge w)=\rho(g)v\wedge\rho(g)w$. If $e_1,\ldots,e_n$ is a basis, then $\{e_i\wedge e_j:1\le i<j\le n\}$ is a basis.
