## Statement · **draft**
Let $(V,\rho)$ be a representation of a group $G$. The exterior square representation is the quotient representation $\operatorname{Alt}^2V=(V\otimes V)/\langle v\otimes w+w\otimes v\rangle$, with $G$ acting by $g(v\wedge w)=\rho(g)v\wedge\rho(g)w$. If $e_1,\ldots,e_n$ is a basis, then $\{e_i\wedge e_j:1\le i<j\le n\}$ is a basis.
