# abelian sheaf　`def.abelian-sheaf`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=def.abelian-sheaf)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./def.abelian-sheaf.md)

- **Abelian sheaves are detected by their underlying sheaves of sets**: $M$ is an abelian sheaf $\iff$ its underlying presheaf of sets is a sheaf.

Because the forgetful functor preserves all limits and the sheaf condition is a limit diagram — using Yoneda's lemma to replace $\operatorname{Hom}_{\mathbb{Z}}(N, M(-))$ by $M(-)$, the condition becomes the same one.

- **Abelian sheaves = abelian group objects in the sheaf category**: $\widetilde{\mathcal{C}}(\mathbf{Ab}) \simeq \mathbf{Ab}\bigl(\widetilde{\mathcal{C}}\bigr)$.

This is the general version of the definition "condensed abelian groups = abelian groups in the category of condensed sets".
