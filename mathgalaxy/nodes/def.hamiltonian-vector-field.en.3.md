# Hamiltonian Vector Field　`def.hamiltonian-vector-field`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=def.hamiltonian-vector-field)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./def.hamiltonian-vector-field.md)

**Key property**: $\mathcal{L}_{X_{H}}\omega = 0$ (the flow of $H$ preserves $\omega$), so it also preserves the volume form $\omega^{n}/n!$. This is **Liouville's theorem**.

## Notes · **draft**
⚠️ **Sign conventions vary**: $\iota_{X_{H}}\omega = dH$, $= -dH$, and $\omega(X_{H}, \cdot) = dH$ are all used. They differ by an overall sign (equivalently, $H \mapsto -H$).

We use $\iota_{X_{H}}\omega = +dH$. Arnold uses $\sum dp_{i}\wedge dq_{i}$ together with $-dH$. Our $\omega_{0}=\sum dq_{i}\wedge dp_{i}$ differs from his by a sign, so we take $+dH$.
