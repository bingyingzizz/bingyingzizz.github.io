# pre-additive category　`def.pre-additive-category`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=def.pre-additive-category)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./def.pre-additive-category.md)

Hence every $\operatorname{Hom}(M, N)$ has a zero element, namely the **zero morphism** $0 : M \to N$; composing the zero morphism with anything gives the zero morphism.

Examples: $A\text{-}\mathbf{Mod}$, $\mathbf{Ab}$, $K\text{-}\mathbf{Vect}$ are all preadditive. Counterexample: the hom-sets of $\mathbf{Set}$ usually have no group structure.

⚠️ Preadditivity **does not require** a zero object or direct sums; adding those two gives an additive category (see @@def.additive-category@@).
