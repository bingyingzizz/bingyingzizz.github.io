## Statement · **draft**
**What this does**: it moves calculus from $\mathbb{R}^{n}$ to spaces that "look locally like $\mathbb{R}^{n}$" — build a stack of coordinate charts, require that changes of chart be smooth, and only then do "smooth functions" acquire meaning.

Let $M$ be a Hausdorff, second-countable topological space.

An **$n$-dimensional chart** on $M$ is $(U,\varphi)$, where $U\subseteq M$ is open and $\varphi : U\to\varphi(U)\subseteq\mathbb{R}^{n}$ is a homeomorphism.
