# monomorphism, epimorphism and isomorphism　`def.mono`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=def.mono)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./def.mono.md)

Reversing arrows gives the dual statement: $f$ is an isomorphism $\iff$ $f$ is an epimorphism with a **retraction**.

⚠️ "Mono $+$ epi" is **not enough**: in the category of rings $\mathbb{Z} \to \mathbb{Q}$ is both mono and epi, yet not an isomorphism — a section (or retraction) is still missing.
