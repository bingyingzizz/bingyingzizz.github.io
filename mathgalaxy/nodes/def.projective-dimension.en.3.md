# Projective Dimension　`def.projective-dimension`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=def.projective-dimension)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./def.projective-dimension.md)

**Two low-dimensional cases**
$\operatorname{pd}_{R} M = 0 \iff M$ is projective (the resolution $0 \to M \xrightarrow{\ 1\ } M \to 0$ suffices and has length $0$).

$\operatorname{pd}_{R} M \le 1$ means that there is a projective precover $P_{0} \twoheadrightarrow M$ whose kernel is projective.

**Infinite projective dimension occurs**
Localize $R = k[x,y]/(xy)$ at the maximal ideal $(x,y)$. The residue field $k = R/(x,y)$ then has projective dimension $\infty$.
