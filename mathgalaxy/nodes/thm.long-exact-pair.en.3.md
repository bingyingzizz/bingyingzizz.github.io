## Notes · **draft**
**Proof.** Let $i : S_{*}(A) \hookrightarrow S_{*}(X)$ denote the inclusion and $q : S_{*}(X) \twoheadrightarrow S_{*}(X, A) := S_{*}(X)/S_{*}(A)$ the quotient map. Both are chain maps, and for each $p$,

$$0 \to S_{p}(A) \xrightarrow{\ i\ } S_{p}(X) \xrightarrow{\ q\ } S_{p}(X, A) \to 0$$

is a short exact sequence. The long exact sequence in homology follows from this short exact sequence. We give the connecting homomorphism and verify exactness at all three positions below.
