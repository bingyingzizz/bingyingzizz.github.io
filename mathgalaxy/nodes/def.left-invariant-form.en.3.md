# left-invariant form　`def.left-invariant-form`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=def.left-invariant-form)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./def.left-invariant-form.md)

is a linear isomorphism from the space of left-invariant $k$-forms onto $\bigwedge^{k}\mathfrak{g}^{\ast}$, of dimension $\binom{n}{k}$.

**Left-invariant coframe**: take a basis of $\mathfrak{g}$, say $e_{1},\ldots,e_{n}$, and the dual basis $\omega^{1}_{e},\ldots,\omega^{n}_{e}$ ($\omega^{i}_{e}(e_{j}) = \delta^{i}_{\ j}$), and translate them to the left:

$$\omega^{i}_{g} := \mathrm{d}(L_{g^{-1}})^{\ast}_{e}\,\omega^{i}_{e},\qquad \omega^{i}_{g}\bigl((\tilde e_{j})_{g}\bigr) = \delta^{i}_{\ j},$$
