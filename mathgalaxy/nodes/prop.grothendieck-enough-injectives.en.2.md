## Notes · **draft**
⭐ **This is the existence guarantee for "right derived functors"**. The standard definition of a right derived functor $R^{n}F$ is "take an injective resolution, apply $F$, take cohomology" — and this definition makes sense only if **injective resolutions always exist**. This theorem says: as long as the category is Grothendieck, there is nothing to worry about.
