## Notes · **draft**
Direct sum and direct product are the same object in an additive category (a **biproduct**): $M_{1} \oplus M_{2}$ carries both the projections of the product and the inclusions of the coproduct, satisfying the three identities above.

Examples: $A\text{-}\mathbf{Mod}$, $\mathbf{Ab}$, and the category of complexes $\mathcal{C}(\mathcal{C})$ (with termwise direct sums) are all additive.

⚠️ An additive category supplies only "addition" and "direct sums"; to speak of kernels, cokernels, and exact sequences one still needs pre-abelian (see @@def.pre-abelian-category@@).
