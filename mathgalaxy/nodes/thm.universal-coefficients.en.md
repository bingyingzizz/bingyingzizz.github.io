## Statement · **draft**
Let $R$ be a **principal ideal domain**, $C_{\bullet}$ a chain complex **free in every degree**, and $A$ an $R$-module. Then for every $n$ there is a natural short exact sequence

$$0 \to H_{n}(C)\otimes_{R} A \to H_{n}\bigl(C\otimes_{R} A\bigr) \to \operatorname{Tor}_{1}^{R}\bigl(H_{n-1}(C),\ A\bigr) \to 0 .$$
