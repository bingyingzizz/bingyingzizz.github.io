## Statement · **draft**
For a finite-dimensional representation $V$ of $A$, the following are equivalent: (1) $V$ is semisimple; (2) $V$ is a sum of irreducible subrepresentations; (3) every subrepresentation $W\subseteq V$ has a complement $W'$ with $V=W\oplus W'$.
