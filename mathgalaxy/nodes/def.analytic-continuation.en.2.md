# analytical continuation　`def.analytic-continuation`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=def.analytic-continuation)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./def.analytic-continuation.md)

1. $\gamma(t)$ is the centre of $D_{t}$;
2. for every $t$ there is $\delta > 0$ such that when $|s - t| < \delta$ we have $D_{s} \cap D_{t} \ne \emptyset$ and $f_{s} = f_{t}$ on the intersection;
3. $f_{t}$ depends continuously on $t$ (on overlaps).

All elements obtained by continuation along all curves starting at $\gamma(0)$, glued along overlaps, form the **complete analytic function** (generally a multivalued function, lying on the Riemann surface of `def.riemann-surface`).
