⚠️ The entry def.similarity in this graph treats the most general similarity. Do not confuse "$A$ is similar to a diagonal matrix" with "$A$ is orthogonally similar to a diagonal matrix" — the former requires only **enough** eigenvectors, the latter requires them to be **mutually perpendicular** (this is normality, def.normal-matrix).
