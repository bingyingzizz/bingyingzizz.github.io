## Notes · **draft**
⭐ "$K$ is a field" cannot be dropped: over the ring $\mathbb{Z}[x]$ there is no division with remainder ($x^{2}$ divided by $2x + 1$ does not produce a polynomial quotient).

Its role is to guarantee that the **leading coefficient is invertible**, so that the highest-degree term can be eliminated.

⭐ The consequences are far-reaching: division with remainder gives the **Euclidean algorithm** (for the gcd), hence **Bézout's identity** ($af + bg = \gcd$), hence "irreducible $\implies$ prime", hence **unique factorization** (`thm.poly-ufd`).
