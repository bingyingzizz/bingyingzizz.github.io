# Constant Sheaves Are Stable under Pullback　`prop.inverse-image-constant-sheaf`

## Notes · **draft**
**Proof.** The constant sheaf is assembled from colimits: $\overline{E} = \coprod_{e \in E} \mathbf{1}$ ✓; and $f^{-1}$ is a **left adjoint**, hence preserves colimits, and $f^{-1}\mathbf{1} = \mathbf{1}$ ✓, so $f^{-1}\overline{E} = \coprod_{e} f^{-1}\mathbf{1} = \overline{E}$ ✓.

∎
