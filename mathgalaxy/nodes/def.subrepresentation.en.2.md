## Notes · **draft**
The zero subspace and $V$ are always subrepresentations. Sums and intersections of subrepresentations are again subrepresentations; kernels and images of intertwiners are also subrepresentations. For group representations, subrepresentations are exactly $G$-invariant subspaces. Quotient representations are treated in a separate node.
