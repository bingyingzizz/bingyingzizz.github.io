The fiber is then $G$ (right multiplication $g\mapsto hg$ carries it into itself), and so is the structure group $G$. The **frame bundle** gives the most used examples: for a vector bundle $E\to B$ of rank $k$, the bundle of frames is a principal $\operatorname{GL}(k,\mathbb{R})$-bundle — this is the bridge between vector bundles and principal bundles.
