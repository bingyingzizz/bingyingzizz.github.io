## Notes · **draft**
This translates something that looks topological (what counts as a covering) into a purely categorical sentence (whether the coproduct is an epimorphism). From now on "$\{X_{i} \to X\}$ covers $X$" and "$\coprod_{i} X_{i} \to X$ is epimorphic" may be used interchangeably.
