## Statement · **draft**
Let $f, g : \mathbb{R} \to \mathbb{R}$ be continuous at $x_0$ and $c \in \mathbb{R}$. Then the following are continuous at $x_0$:

$$f + g, \quad f - g, \quad cf, \quad fg, \quad |f|;$$

if $g(x_0) \ne 0$, then $f/g$ is continuous at $x_0$. If $g$ is continuous at $f(x_0)$, then the composite $g \circ f$ is continuous at $x_0$.
