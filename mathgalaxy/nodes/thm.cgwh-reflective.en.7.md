# CGWH Is a Reflective Subcategory of CG　`thm.cgwh-reflective`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=thm.cgwh-reflective)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./thm.cgwh-reflective.md)

*larger** the equivalence relation, the **more** is quotiented out and the coarser the space. So the "smallest" closed equivalence relation gives the "most economical" weak Hausdorffification — which is why the reflector first $k$-ifies ($X\rightsquigarrow kX$), then quotients by the smallest closed equivalence relation, both steps functorial.
