# Group Cohomology　`def.group-cohomology`

[in graph](../#n=def.group-cohomology)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./def.group-cohomology.md)

**Two immediate consequences**:
- If $M$ is a $\mathbb{Q}$-vector space and $G$ acts trivially, then $(-)^{G}$ is the identity functor (exact), so $H^{n}(G, M) = 0$ for $n \ge 1$.
- If $G$ acts trivially on $M$, then $H^{1}(G, M) = \operatorname{Hom}(G, M)$; in general one replaces \(\operatorname{Hom}\) by "crossed homomorphisms".
