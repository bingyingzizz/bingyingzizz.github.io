# matrix　`def.matrix`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=def.matrix)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./def.matrix.md)

The $j$-th **column** of $A$ is a column vector in $K^{m}$, and its $i$-th **row** is a row vector in $K^{n}$.

## Notes · **draft**
⭐ A matrix is just a rectangular array of elements of $K$ — **it carries no mathematical structure by itself**; its meaning comes entirely from how it is read (as the ledger of a linear map, as coefficients of a linear system, as the recipe of a bilinear form).

⚠ The two indexing conventions ($a_{ij}$ is the entry in row $i$, column $j$) must be used consistently, or transposition and multiplication go wrong.

The convention throughout is row first, then column.
