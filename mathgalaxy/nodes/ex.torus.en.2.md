## Notes · **draft**
⭐ **Three equivalent descriptions**: (1) $S^{1} \times S^{1}$. (2) A square with opposite sides identified (the quotient of $[0,1]^{2}$ by $(0,t)\sim(1,t)$ and $(s,0)\sim(s,1)$). (3) $\mathbb{R}^{2}/\mathbb{Z}^{2}$. The third is the most convenient for computations. It is the plane modulo integer translations, with fundamental group $\mathbb{Z}^{2}$.

⭐ **Computing $\chi = 0$**: The complex obtained by gluing the square has $V = 1$, $E = 2$, and $F = 1$, so $\chi = 1 - 2 + 1 = 0$, in agreement with $2 - 2g = 0$.
