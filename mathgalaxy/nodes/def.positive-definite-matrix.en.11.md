⚠️ **The three most error-prone points**: (a) forgetting the hypothesis "Hermitian/real symmetric" (in the complex case use $x^{*}$; discussing complex matrices with $x^{T}Ax > 0$ is meaningless, since it may not be real); (b) replacing "all leading principal minors positive" by "all principal minors positive" — the latter is a **stronger** condition (also true for positive definite, but in the semidefinite case only the latter can be used); (c) in the semidefinite case $P$ is not invertible, eigenvalues may be $0$, and the square root is only a seminorm — all three must be relaxed simultaneous
