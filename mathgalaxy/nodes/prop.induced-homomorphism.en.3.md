# induced homomorphism　`prop.induced-homomorphism`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=prop.induced-homomorphism)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./prop.induced-homomorphism.md)

- A homeomorphism $f$ gives an isomorphism $f_{*}$ (the inverse is the inverse homeomorphism), so $\pi_{1}$ is a **topological invariant**;
- More strongly, a homotopy equivalence gives an isomorphism — so $\pi_{1}$ is also a **homotopy invariant**, and a contractible space must have fundamental group $1$;
- By (3), $\pi_{1}$ turns "composition of spaces" into "composition of groups", with the **order not reversed** (unlike $f^{*}$ in cohomology).
