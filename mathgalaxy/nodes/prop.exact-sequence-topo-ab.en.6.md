# transfer of exact sequences of topological abelian groups　`prop.exact-sequence-topo-ab`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=prop.exact-sequence-topo-ab)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./prop.exact-sequence-topo-ab.md)

$y(F)$ is a **compact Hausdorff** subset of $M''$ ($F$ is compact and $M''$ is weak Hausdorff, so $y(F)$ is closed and Hausdorff).

By the **compact lifting condition**, choose a compact Hausdorff $K\subseteq M$ such that $y(F)\subseteq v(K)$.

Choose a preimage at each point to obtain a map $F\to K\to M$.

Continuity follows because $v$ is a quotient map on the compact pieces (in the exact sequence of topological groups, $v$ is a quotient map and remains one when restricted to $K$ and $y(F)$).

Thus $y$ lifts along the map, and $\underline v(F)$ is surjective ✓.
