## Statement · **draft**
Let $X$ be a topological space and $x : \mathbf{1} \to X$ a point. Then the topos morphism it gives satisfies

$$(x_{*}E)(U) = \begin{cases} E & x \in U,\\ \emptyset & x \notin U,\end{cases} \qquad x^{-1}\mathcal{F} = \mathcal{F}_{x}.$$
