## Statement · **draft**
A topological space $X$ is **normal** if it is $T_{1}$ and any two **disjoint closed sets** $A, B$ can be separated by open sets: there are open sets $U \supseteq A$ and $V \supseteq B$ with $U \cap V = \emptyset$.

Replacing "disjoint closed sets" by "a point and a closed set not containing it" gives a **regular** space; replacing it by "two points" gives a **Hausdorff** space.
