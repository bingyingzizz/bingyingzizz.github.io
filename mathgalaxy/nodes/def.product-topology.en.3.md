# product topology　`def.product-topology`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=def.product-topology)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./def.product-topology.md)

**Categorical meaning**: $\prod_{i} X_{i}$ together with the projections is the **product** in $\mathbf{Top}$. So the product topology is not an arbitrary choice: it is the one uniquely determined by the universal property.
