## Statement · **draft**
Let $M$ be an $R$-module and choose a surjection $\pi_{0} : P_{0} \twoheadrightarrow M$ (usually with $P_{0}$ free). Its kernel

$$\Omega M := \ker\bigl(\pi_{0} : P_{0} \to M\bigr)$$

is called the **(first) syzygy** of $M$ and is also denoted $\Omega^{1}M$. Recursively, given $\pi_{n} : P_{n} \twoheadrightarrow \Omega^{n}M$, set

$$\Omega^{n+1}M := \ker\bigl(\pi_{n}\bigr), \qquad \Omega^{0}M := M .$$
