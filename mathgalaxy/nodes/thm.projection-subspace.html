陈述

设 HHH 是 Hilbert 空间，E⊆HE \subseteq HE⊆H 是闭子空间。则

  1. y=PE(x)  ⟺  y∈Ey = P_E(x) \iff y \in Ey=PE​(x)⟺y∈E 且 x−y∈E⊥x - y \in E^\perpx−y∈E⊥；
  2. PEP_EPE​ 线性，且 ∥PE∥≤1\|P_E\| \le 1∥PE​∥≤1（E≠0E \ne 0E=0 时为 111）；
  3. 正交分解 H=E⊕E⊥H = E \oplus E^\perpH=E⊕E⊥，且 ∥x∥2=∥x−PEx∥2+∥PEx∥2\|x\|^2 = \|x - P_E x\|^2 + \|P_E x\|^2∥x∥2=∥x−PE​x∥2+∥PE​x∥2。

说明

证法：由凸闭集投影，(1) 的「  ⟹  \implies⟹」把 zzz 换成 ±z\pm z±z 消掉实部得 Re⁡⟨x−y,z⟩≤0\operatorname{Re}\langle x - y, z\rangle \le 0Re⟨x−y,z⟩≤0 对所有 z∈Ez \in Ez∈E，即 x−y⊥Ex - y \perp Ex−y⊥E；

「⟸\Longleftarrow⟸」用 ∥x−z∥2=∥x−y∥2+∥y−z∥2≥∥x−y∥2\|x - z\|^2 = \|x - y\|^2 + \|y - z\|^2 \ge \|x - y\|^2∥x−z∥2=∥x−y∥2+∥y−z∥2≥∥x−y∥2。

(2) 用 α1x1+α2x2−α1Px1−α2Px2⊥E\alpha_1 x_1 + \alpha_2 x_2 - \alpha_1 P x_1 - \alpha_2 P x_2 \perp Eα1​x1​+α2​x2​−α1​Px1​−α2​Px2​⊥E。

(3) 勾股定理。

⚠️ 「EEE 闭」不能去掉：E=E = E= 有限支撑序列在 ℓ2\ell^2ℓ2 里稠密但不闭，距离为零但取不到，故不存在 EEE 中的最近点，正交分解也失效。

⭐ 这条是 Hilbert 空间与一般 Banach 空间的最大区别之一：一般 Banach 空间里闭子空间不一定有补（c0⊂ℓ∞c_0 \subset \ell^\inftyc0​⊂ℓ∞ 就没有），所以「投影」这个词在 Banach 空间里不能乱用。

各方向用到的假设（正交刻画不靠完备性，全局分解靠投影存在）：

  • 最近点   ⟹  \implies⟹ 正交：用子空间中任意实数倍方向作变分；复空间还须用纯虚数倍方向，才能从实部为零推出内积为零。
  • y∈Ey\in Ey∈E 且 x−y∈E⊥  ⟹  y=PExx-y\in E^\perp\implies y=P_E xx−y∈E⊥⟹y=PE​x：由勾股恒等式直接得唯一最小点，不要求闭性或完备性；正交补本身的条件才是定义展开。
  • 每个 xxx 都有正交分解：调用 凸闭集的最近点，用 Hilbert 完备性与 EEE 闭；线性由分解唯一性推出，范数界由勾股恒等式推出。
  • 闭性不能删：E=c00⊂ℓ2E=c_{00}\subset\ell^2E=c00​⊂ℓ2 时，对 x∈ℓ2∖c00x\in\ell^2\setminus c_{00}x∈ℓ2∖c00​ 距离为零却不取到，故不存在 EEE 中最近点。

📌 「正交投影」的定义在 正交投影；本节点讲的是「投影像恰是闭子空间」这条。

Statement draft

Let HHH be a Hilbert space and E⊆HE \subseteq HE⊆H a closed subspace. Then

  1. y=PE(x)  ⟺  y∈Ey = P_E(x) \iff y \in Ey=PE​(x)⟺y∈E and x−y∈E⊥x - y \in E^\perpx−y∈E⊥;
  2. PEP_EPE​ is linear, and ∥PE∥≤1\|P_E\| \le 1∥PE​∥≤1 (it is 111 when E≠0E \ne 0E=0);
  3. Orthogonal decomposition H=E⊕E⊥H = E \oplus E^\perpH=E⊕E⊥, and ∥x∥2=∥x−PEx∥2+∥PEx∥2\|x\|^2 = \|x - P_E x\|^2 + \|P_E x\|^2∥x∥2=∥x−PE​x∥2+∥PE​x∥2.

Notes draft

Proof: by the projection onto a closed convex set. For (1), the step "  ⟹  \implies⟹" replaces zzz by ±z\pm z±z, kills the real part, and gives Re⁡⟨x−y,z⟩≤0\operatorname{Re}\langle x - y, z\rangle \le 0Re⟨x−y,z⟩≤0 for all z∈Ez \in Ez∈E, i.e. x−y⊥Ex - y \perp Ex−y⊥E;

the step "⟸\Longleftarrow⟸" uses ∥x−z∥2=∥x−y∥2+∥y−z∥2≥∥x−y∥2\|x - z\|^2 = \|x - y\|^2 + \|y - z\|^2 \ge \|x - y\|^2∥x−z∥2=∥x−y∥2+∥y−z∥2≥∥x−y∥2.

(2) uses α1x1+α2x2−α1Px1−α2Px2⊥E\alpha_1 x_1 + \alpha_2 x_2 - \alpha_1 P x_1 - \alpha_2 P x_2 \perp Eα1​x1​+α2​x2​−α1​Px1​−α2​Px2​⊥E.

(3) is Pythagoras.

⚠️ The word "closed" in "EEE is closed" cannot be dropped: for E=E = E= the finitely supported sequences are dense in ℓ2\ell^2ℓ2 but not closed; the distance is zero but not attained, so no nearest point in EEE exists, and the orthogonal decomposition fails as well.

⭐ This is one of the biggest differences between Hilbert spaces and general Banach spaces: in a general Banach space a closed subspace need not be complemented (c0⊂ℓ∞c_0 \subset \ell^\inftyc0​⊂ℓ∞ is not), so the word "projection" cannot be used loosely in Banach spaces.

Which hypothesis each direction uses (the orthogonal characterisation does not need completeness; the global decomposition needs existence of projections):

  • Nearest point   ⟹  \implies⟹ orthogonality: use variations in arbitrary real multiples of directions in the subspace; in the complex case one must also use purely imaginary multiples, so that vanishing of the real part forces the inner product to vanish.
  • y∈Ey\in Ey∈E and x−y∈E⊥  ⟹  y=PExx-y\in E^\perp\implies y=P_E xx−y∈E⊥⟹y=PE​x: the Pythagorean identity gives the unique minimiser directly, with no closedness or completeness required; the condition for the orthogonal complement itself is what the definition expands.
  • Every xxx has an orthogonal decomposition: invoke 凸闭集的最近点, using completeness of the Hilbert space and closedness of EEE; linearity follows from uniqueness of the decomposition, and the norm bound from the Pythagorean identity.
  • Closedness cannot be removed: for E=c00⊂ℓ2E=c_{00}\subset\ell^2E=c00​⊂ℓ2, the distance to x∈ℓ2∖c00x\in\ell^2\setminus c_{00}x∈ℓ2∖c00​ is zero but not attained, so no nearest point in EEE exists.

📌 The definition of "orthogonal projection" is in 正交投影; this entry is about the statement that the image of the projection is exactly the closed subspace.
