**Hypotheses used in each direction** (the universal properties of kernels and cokernels also determine the topology):
- Kernel $\implies$ closed embedding: The kernel carries the subspace topology, and the Hausdorff target ensures that zero is closed. Conversely, a closed embedding is the kernel of the quotient map $M\to M/H$. Calling it the equalizer of itself and zero would be incorrect.
