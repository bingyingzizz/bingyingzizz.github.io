Statement

Let K,LK, LK,L be simplicial complexes, with KKK finite, and let f:∣K∣→∣L∣f : |K| \to |L|f:∣K∣→∣L∣ be continuous. Write K(m)K^{(m)}K(m) for the result of applying barycentric subdivision to KKK a total of mmm times (∣K(m)∣=∣K∣|K^{(m)}| = |K|∣K(m)∣=∣K∣, with simplex diameters shrinking by a fixed factor). Then there exist m≥1m \ge 1m≥1 and a simplicial map

g:K(m)⟶Lg : K^{(m)} \longrightarrow Lg:K(m)⟶L

such that ∣g∣≃f|g| \simeq f∣g∣≃f (∣g∣|g|∣g∣ and fff are homotopic). Such a map ggg is called a simplicial approximation to fff.

Corollary: Let KKK be a finite simplicial complex. The inclusion C∗(K)↪S∗(∣K∣)C_{*}(K) \hookrightarrow S_{*}(|K|)C∗​(K)↪S∗​(∣K∣) induces an isomorphism

Hp(K)  ≅  Hp(∣K∣)∀p.H_{p}(K) \;\cong\; H_{p}(|K|) \qquad \forall p.Hp​(K)≅Hp​(∣K∣)∀p.

Thus the singular homology of a polyhedron can be computed as the simplicial homology of a triangulation, which is a finite matrix calculation.

⚠️ Technical detail: The proof uses two standard constructions, the chain map induced by a simplicial map and the prism construction for chain homotopies (see chain homotopy and homotopy invariance). If C∗C_{*}C∗​ is taken to be an ordered chain group, ggg may collapse a simplex in degenerate cases, and the signs in the chain map must respect the vertex order. Homology here is defined using the quotient of ordered chain groups by degenerate chains. The sign details are omitted.

Limits and counterexamples:

  • The finiteness of KKK is essential. For infinite complexes, repeated barycentric subdivision need not make the mesh tend to zero (an infinite sequence of subdivisions can remain coarse), so the star condition may fail.
  • LLL need not be finite. The theorem only uses that the vertex stars of LLL form an open cover.
  • The open star st⁡(w)\operatorname{st}(w)st(w) is generally not convex, so one cannot justify the homotopy by joining points in st⁡(w)\operatorname{st}(w)st(w). Part (ii) of Lemma 1 uses that f(x),g(x)f(x), g(x)f(x),g(x) lie in a common closed simplex ρ\rhoρ, and ρ\rhoρ is convex.

⭐ Why the corollary matters: It gives three conclusions at once. (i) Simplicial homology is a topological invariant independent of triangulation. (ii) Singular homology can be computed from ranks of finite matrices. (iii) The Euler characteristic formula carries over to singular homology (see the edge for Euler characteristic).

💡 In short: continuous map ≈\approx≈ simplicial map after subdivision. This gives a combinatorial description of the topology.
