# Equivalent Characterizations of Compact Generation　`prop.cg-equivalent-conditions`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=prop.cg-equivalent-conditions)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./prop.cg-equivalent-conditions.md)

1. $X$ is compactly generated;
2. $X$ is a quotient space of a "disjoint union of compact Hausdorff spaces";
3. $X$ is a quotient space of a "locally compact Hausdorff space";
4. $Y \subseteq X$ is open $\iff$ for every continuous $f : S \to X$ from a compact Hausdorff space, $f^{-1}(Y)$ is open;
5. $X \to Y$ is continuous $\iff$ for every continuous $f : S \to X$, the composite $S \to X \to Y$ is continuous;
6. $X$ is the colimit of all continuous maps $S \to X$ ($S$ compact Hausdorff).
