# exact couple　`def.exact-couple`

[in graph](../#n=def.exact-couple)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./def.exact-couple.md)

if $e \in \operatorname{im} d$ (i.e. $e = j k(e_{0})$), then $k e = k j k(e_{0}) = 0$ — independent of representatives.

- $i^{\prime}$: $i(D^{\prime}) = i^{2}(D) \subseteq i(D) = D^{\prime}$.

Checking the three exactness conditions once more shows the derived couple is again an exact couple. Hence one can **iterate indefinitely**: $E_{1} := E$, $E_{r+1} := H(E_{r}, d_{r})$. This is precisely "computing a spectral sequence page by page".

**Bidegrees.** The most common case comes from a **filtered complex**: the bidegree of $i$ is $(-1, 1)$, of $j$ is $(0, 0)$, of $k$ is $(1, 0)$.
