## 陈述（续）
$$R_k(h) = \sum_{|\alpha| = k+1} \frac{D^{\alpha} f(a + \theta h)}{\alpha!}\, h^{\alpha} = O\bigl(\|h\|^{k+1}\bigr)$$

最常用的两档：

$$f(a+h) = f(a) + Df(a)(h) + o(\|h\|)$$

$$f(a+h) = f(a) + Df(a)(h) + \frac{1}{2}\, D^{2}f(a)(h, h) + o\bigl(\|h\|^{2}\bigr)$$
