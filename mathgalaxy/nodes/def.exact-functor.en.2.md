## Notes · **draft**
**Why "finite"**: in general categories the usage of "left exact" comes from abelian categories — there it suffices to preserve $0$, finite products, and kernels, which is equivalent to sending the short exact sequence $0 \to M' \to M \to M'' \to 0$ to $0 \to FM' \to FM \to FM''$ (exact, exact on the left). So "exact" in the name refers to exact sequences.

⭐ **Right adjoints are left exact and left adjoints are right exact** — a direct consequence of "adjoint $\implies$ preserves limits / colimits", and the most commonly used criterion.
