Statement

Let C\mathcal{C}C be a site. Then "abelian sheaves on the site" and "abelian group objects in the topos C~\widetilde{\mathcal{C}}C" form the same category:

C~(Ab)  ≃  Ab(C~).\widetilde{\mathcal{C}}(\mathbf{Ab}) \;\simeq\; \mathbf{Ab}\bigl(\widetilde{\mathcal{C}}\bigr).C(Ab)≃Ab(C).

Notes

Idea of proof. Both directions are "evaluate pointwise":

  • →\to→: an abelian group object MMM in the topos evaluated on X∈CX \in \mathcal{C}X∈C uses that global sections preserve products (the group operations, 000 and inverses are read off pointwise) ✓, so X↦M(X)X \mapsto M(X)X↦M(X) gives an abelian presheaf, and it is a sheaf ✓;
  • ←\leftarrow←: conversely, given an abelian sheaf MMM, its value at each XXX automatically carries an abelian group structure, and the group structure in the category of sheaves is determined pointwise by these values (group objects in a presheaf category are pointwise groups) ✓.

The two directions are mutually inverse, since both "take values at every XXX" ✓. ∎
