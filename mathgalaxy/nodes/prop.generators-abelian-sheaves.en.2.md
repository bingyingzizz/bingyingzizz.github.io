**Proof.** By the universal property of free objects, $\operatorname{Hom}_{\mathbb{Z}}(\mathbb{Z}\cdot X, M) = M(X) = \operatorname{Hom}(X, M)$ ✓ — hence "detecting morphisms using $\mathbb{Z}\cdot X$" and "detecting morphisms using $X$" are the same thing ✓.

That $S$ generates $\mathcal{T}$ shows the latter is sufficient, so the former is sufficient too ✓.
