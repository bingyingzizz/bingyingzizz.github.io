## Statement · **draft**
Let $X$ be a topological space. Then the abelian sheaf category $\mathbf{Ab}(X)$ **has enough injectives**: for each $\mathcal{F} \in \mathbf{Ab}(X)$, embed every stalk into its injective hull in $\mathbf{Ab}$, namely $\mathcal{F}_{x} \rightarrowtail I_{x}$; then the natural map

$$\mathcal{F} \longrightarrow \prod_{x \in X} i_{x,*}\, I_{x}$$
