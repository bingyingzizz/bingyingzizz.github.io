## Notes · **draft**
**Proof.** (1) Given a compact Hausdorff $S$ and a continuous $h : S \to X \times X$, $S$ is compact Hausdorff, so the two components of $h$ send $S$ into compact pieces; weak Hausdorffness says images of compact pieces are closed ✓, so $h^{-1}(X)$ is the preimage of the intersection of two closed sets, hence closed ✓ — that is, $X$ sits in $X\times X$ and is closed in the $k$-topology ✓.

Conversely (when compactly generated), read off weak Hausdorffness from "$k$-closed $\iff$ closed intersection with every compact piece" ✓.
