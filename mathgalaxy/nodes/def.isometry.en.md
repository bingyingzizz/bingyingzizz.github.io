## Statement · **draft**
A linear map $T : V \to V$ is an **isometry** iff it preserves the inner product (equivalently, the norm):

$$\langle Tv, Tw \rangle = \langle v, w \rangle \quad (\forall v, w) \qquad \iff \qquad \|Tv\| = \|v\| \quad (\forall v)$$
