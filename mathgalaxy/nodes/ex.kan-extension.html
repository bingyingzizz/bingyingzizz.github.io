Abstract reviewed

Two examples showing that Kan extensions absorb the earlier notions. (1) A diagram D:I→CD : I \to \mathcal{C}D:I→C has a colimit iff the left Kan extension of DDD along p:I→1p : I \to \mathbf{1}p:I→1 exists, and then (p!D)(∗)=lim→⁡i∈ID(i)(p_!D)(\ast) = \varinjlim_{i \in I} D(i)(p!​D)(∗)=lim​i∈I​D(i). (2) F:C→DF : \mathcal{C} \to \mathcal{D}F:C→D has a right adjoint iff the left Kan extension of 1C1_{\mathcal{C}}1C​ along FFF exists, and then G=F!1CG = F_!1_{\mathcal{C}}G=F!​1C​, the unit being the transpose of 1C  ⟹  G∘F1_{\mathcal{C}} \implies G \circ F1C​⟹G∘F.

Statement reviewed

(1) A diagram D:I→CD : I \to \mathcal{C}D:I→C has a colimit XXX   ⟺  \iff⟺ the left Kan extension p!D:1→Cp_{!}D : \mathbf{1} \to \mathcal{C}p!​D:1→C along the projection p:I→1p : I \to \mathbf{1}p:I→1 exists, and its value is

(p!D)(∗)=lim→⁡i∈ID(i)=X(p_{!}D)(\ast) = \varinjlim_{i \in I} D(i) = X(p!​D)(∗)=i∈Ilim​​D(i)=X

(2) A functor F:C→DF : \mathcal{C} \to \mathcal{D}F:C→D has a right adjoint GGG   ⟺  \iff⟺ the left Kan extension of the identity functor 1C1_{\mathcal{C}}1C​ along FFF exists, and then

G=F!1C,Hom⁡(F!1C, G)≅Hom⁡(1C, G∘F)G = F_{!}1_{\mathcal{C}}, \qquad \operatorname{Hom}(F_{!}1_{\mathcal{C}},\ G) \cong \operatorname{Hom}(1_{\mathcal{C}},\ G \circ F)G=F!​1C​,Hom(F!​1C​, G)≅Hom(1C​, G∘F)

The map 1C  ⟹  G∘F1_{\mathcal{C}} \implies G \circ F1C​⟹G∘F corresponding to the right-hand side is exactly the unit η\etaη.

Notes reviewed

Read the two together: colimits and right adjoints are special cases of Kan extensions. The Kan extension is the unifying exit of this whole cluster — the definitions scattered earlier (limit, adjunction, reflection) each fall into place inside it.

  • Kan 延拓、极限、伴随函子 ⟹ 本页推出
    Kan 延拓   ⟹  \implies⟹ 余极限与右伴随都是它的特例

    (1) 取 p:I→1p : I \to \mathbf{1}p:I→1 为到终范畴的唯一函子。则预复合 p∗:C1→CIp^{*} : \mathcal{C}^{\mathbf{1}} \to \mathcal{C}^{I}p∗:C1→CI 就是常图函子 Δ\DeltaΔ。于是

    Hom⁡(p!D, G)  ≅  Hom⁡(D, G∘p)  =  Hom⁡CI(D, ΔG(∗))\operatorname{Hom}(p_{!}D,\ G) \;\cong\; \operatorname{Hom}(D,\ G \circ p) \;=\; \operatorname{Hom}_{\mathcal{C}^{I}}\bigl(D,\ \Delta G(\ast)\bigr)Hom(p!​D, G)≅Hom(D, G∘p)=HomCI​(D, ΔG(∗))

    右边正是「DDD 到常图 G(∗)G(\ast)G(∗) 的余锥」的集合。由余极限的泛性质（锥函子被 colim⁡D\operatorname{colim} DcolimD 表示），能表示这个函子的只有一个对象：

    (p!D)(∗)  =  lim→⁡i∈ID(i)(p_{!}D)(\ast) \;=\; \varinjlim_{i \in I} D(i)(p!​D)(∗)=i∈Ilim​​D(i)

    所以 p!Dp_{!}Dp!​D 存在   ⟺  D\iff D⟺D 有余极限。∎

    (2) 取 p=F:C→Dp = F : \mathcal{C} \to \mathcal{D}p=F:C→D、FFF 位置的函子取恒等函子 1C1_{\mathcal{C}}1C​。左 Kan 延拓的泛性质给出

    Hom⁡(F!1C, G)  ≅  Hom⁡(1C, G∘F)\operatorname{Hom}(F_{!}1_{\mathcal{C}},\ G) \;\cong\; \operatorname{Hom}(1_{\mathcal{C}},\ G \circ F)Hom(F!​1C​, G)≅Hom(1C​, G∘F)

    右边只有「1C1_{\mathcal{C}}1C​ 到 G∘FG \circ FG∘F 的全部自然变换」一个元素（取 1C1_{\mathcal{C}}1C​ 的像即得），所以左侧 F!1CF_{!}1_{\mathcal{C}}F!​1C​ 到每个 GGG 的态射也恰好只有一个 —— 这正是「F!1CF_{!}1_{\mathcal{C}}F!​1C​ 是 Hom⁡(1C,−∘F)\operatorname{Hom}(1_{\mathcal{C}}, - \circ F)Hom(1C​,−∘F) 的表示对象」的说法，而那个表示对象就是左伴随。记 G=F!1CG = F_{!}1_{\mathcal{C}}G=F!​1C​，同构两侧的形状恰好就是伴随的定义，对应的自然变换 1C  ⟹  G∘F1_{\mathcal{C}} \implies G \circ F1C​⟹G∘F 就是单位 η\etaη。∎

    看完这两条，前面那些定义就串成一条线了：极限与余极限是「沿到终范畴的函子」延拓，伴随是「沿函数子本身」延拓。
