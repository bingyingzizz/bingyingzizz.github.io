## Notes · **draft**
**What it says.** The round trip "dualize, then compute RHom into a target" is turned into a **derived tensor product**, with the degree **lowered by one** ($[-1]$). Thus "computing Ext of a dual" becomes "computing a tensor product" — and the latter can be done on discrete groups by a free resolution.

**Proof.**

Use the short exact sequence $0 \to \mathbb{Z} \to \mathbb{R} \to T \to 0$.

By the two vanishing theorems above, $\operatorname{RHom}_{\mathbb{Z}}(\mathbb{R}, N) = 0$, hence $\operatorname{RHom}_{\mathbb{Z}}(T, N) \cong N[-1]$.
