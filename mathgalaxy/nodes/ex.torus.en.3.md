# torus　`ex.torus`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=ex.torus)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./ex.torus.md)

⭐ **It is the surface in the familiar comparison of a coffee cup and a doughnut**. Both are homeomorphic to $T^{2}$ (each has one hole), so they are topologically indistinguishable. Their **geometric shapes** as embedded in $\mathbb{R}^{3}$ concern parametrized surfaces in $\mathbb{R}^{3}$.

⚠ **$T^{2}$ cannot embed in $\mathbb{R}^{2}$**. It is compact, two-dimensional, and without boundary, so an embedding in the plane would give a compact open subset of the plane. Only the empty set is both. A drawing of the torus on paper is therefore a **projection**.
