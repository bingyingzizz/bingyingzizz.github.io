## Notes · **draft**
**Proof.** A pro-object is just a diagram $\{E_{i}\}$ indexed by a filtering category and taken as a projective limit ($E_i$ finite discrete) ✓, and it evidently gives a profinite space ✓; conversely, a profinite space is a (filtered) projective limit of finite discrete spaces ✓ (this is the definition read backwards). The two directions are mutually inverse ✓.

⭐ So the combinatorial name "profinite set" matches: it can be viewed either as pro-(finite sets) or as a compact totally disconnected space.
