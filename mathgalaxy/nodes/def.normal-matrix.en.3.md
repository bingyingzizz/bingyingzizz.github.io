## Notes · **draft**
**Why normality is the "right" class**: in the proof of the spectral theorem, the only things really used are "eigenvectors are orthogonal + there are enough eigenvectors". Together these two are exactly equivalent to $A^{*}A = AA^{*}$ — so "normal" is not an artificial condition, but the **algebraic translation** of "unitarily diagonalizable".
