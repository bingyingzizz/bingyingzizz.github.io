## Statement · **draft**
Let $\varepsilon : P_{0} \to M$ be the **augmentation** of a projective resolution of the $R$-module $M$. Thus

$$\cdots \to P_{2} \xrightarrow{\ d_{2}\ } P_{1} \xrightarrow{\ d_{1}\ } P_{0} \xrightarrow{\ \varepsilon\ } M \to 0$$

is exact everywhere, and each $P_{n}$ is projective. The **deleted resolution** is the chain complex obtained by **removing the augmentation**:
