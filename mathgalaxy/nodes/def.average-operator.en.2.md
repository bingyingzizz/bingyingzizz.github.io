## Notes · **draft**
This is the operator that "smooths out" a function: $A_r f$ is a continuous version of $f$ (see the next item), and as $r \to 0$ it should return to $f$ itself — that is exactly what the Lebesgue differentiation theorem says.

⚠ The integral is taken with respect to **Lebesgue measure $m$**, and has nothing to do with the signed measure $\nu$ of this section.
