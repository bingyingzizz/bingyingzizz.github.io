**Why $\operatorname{GL}(n,\mathbb{R})$ is a manifold**: $\det : \mathbb{R}^{n^{2}} \to \mathbb{R}$ is continuous, $\mathbb{R}\setminus\{0\}$ is open, so $\det^{-1}(\mathbb{R}\setminus\{0\})$ is open in $\mathbb{R}^{n^{2}}$ — one chart suffices, with $\varphi = \operatorname{id}$.

This is the least effortful example of "an open subset inherits the manifold structure".
