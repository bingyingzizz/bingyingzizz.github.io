# closed graph theorem　`thm.closed-graph`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=thm.closed-graph)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./thm.closed-graph.md)

**Linearity.** A nonlinear map can have a closed graph and be discontinuous: take $u : \mathbb{R} \to \mathbb{R}$ with $u(x) = 1/x$ for $x \ne 0$ and $u(0) = 0$. Its graph is closed in $\mathbb{R}^2$ (a hyperbola together with the origin), but $u$ is discontinuous at $0$.

📌 **TFAE version**: boundedness is equivalent to closedness of the graph (for Banach spaces at both ends). The graph records two typical uses: `thm.lax-milgram` (coercivity gives a bounded two-sided inverse) and the criterion for complemented subspaces (a closed algebraic complement gives a bounded projection).
