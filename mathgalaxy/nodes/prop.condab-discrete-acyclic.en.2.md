# Acyclicity on Discrete and Discrete Times Stonean Spaces　`prop.condab-discrete-acyclic`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=prop.condab-discrete-acyclic)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./prop.condab-discrete-acyclic.md)

1. when $E$ is a **discrete** topological space, $H^{n}_{\mathrm{cond}}(E, M) = 0$ ($n > 0$);
2. more generally, when $E$ is discrete and $S$ is **Stonean**, $H^{n}_{\mathrm{cond}}(E \times S,\ M) = 0$ ($n > 0$).
