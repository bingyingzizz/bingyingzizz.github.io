## Statement · **draft**
A morphism of complexes $f : K \to L$ is called a **quasi-isomorphism** if for every $n$, $H^{n}(f)$ is an isomorphism. A complex $K$ is called **acyclic** if $H^{n}(K) = 0$ for all $n$.
