Statement

Let VVV be a KKK-vector space. VVV's kkk-th exterior power ⋀kV\bigwedge^{k} V⋀kV is the quotient of V⊗kV^{\otimes k}V⊗k by the subspace spanned by

v1⊗⋯⊗vkwith vi=vj (i≠j)v_1 \otimes \cdots \otimes v_k \quad \text{with}\ v_i = v_j\ (i \ne j)v1​⊗⋯⊗vk​with vi​=vj​ (i=j)

and the image is written v1∧⋯∧vkv_1 \wedge \cdots \wedge v_kv1​∧⋯∧vk​. Thus

v∧v=0,v∧w=− w∧vv \wedge v = 0, \qquad v \wedge w = -\, w \wedge vv∧v=0,v∧w=−w∧v

and when n=dim⁡V<∞n = \dim V < \inftyn=dimV<∞ one has dim⁡⋀kV=(nk)\dim \bigwedge^{k} V = \binom{n}{k}dim⋀kV=(kn​); for k>nk > nk>n, ⋀kV=0\bigwedge^{k}V = 0⋀kV=0.

Notes

⭐ Geometric meaning: when dim⁡V=n\dim V = ndimV=n, ⋀nV\bigwedge^{n} V⋀nV is one-dimensional, and v1∧⋯∧vnv_1 \wedge \cdots \wedge v_nv1​∧⋯∧vn​ is exactly "the oriented volume of the parallelepiped spanned by these nnn vectors" — its coefficient is the determinant.

⭐ Alternation is achieved by "quotienting": v∧v=0v \wedge v = 0v∧v=0 (send v⊗vv \otimes vv⊗v into the quotient), which is the "zero when two arguments agree" from 交错型.

⚠ Over a field of characteristic 222, v∧w=−w∧vv \wedge w = -w \wedge vv∧w=−w∧v and v∧w=w∧vv \wedge w = w \wedge vv∧w=w∧v say the same thing (−1=1-1 = 1−1=1), and alternating and symmetric are no longer distinguishable.

The convention here is char⁡K≠2\operatorname{char} K \ne 2charK=2.
