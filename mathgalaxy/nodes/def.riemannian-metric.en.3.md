## Notes · **draft**
**Existence**: locally a metric always exists (take $\delta_{ij}$ in coordinates), and then a partition of unity convexly combines the local metrics into a global one — positive definiteness is closed under convex combinations. Hence **every** smooth manifold admits a Riemannian metric (complex and symplectic structures have topological obstructions; metrics do not).

**It is not part of the manifold**: the same smooth manifold carries infinitely many metrics. The choice of metric is **extra structure**; quantities depending only on $g$ are called intrinsic, those depending on an embedding extrinsic.
