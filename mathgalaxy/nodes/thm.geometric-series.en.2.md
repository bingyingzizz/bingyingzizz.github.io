# geometric series　`thm.geometric-series`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=thm.geometric-series)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./thm.geometric-series.md)

The phrase "the geometric series converges" in `imp.root-test` and `imp.banach-fixed-point` refers to this statement.

⭐ For $|q| < 1$, the proof of $q^{n} \to 0$ uses Bernoulli plus the Archimedean property: write $1/|q| = 1 + h$ ($h > 0$), so $(1+h)^{n} \ge 1 + nh$, and hence $|q|^{n} \le \dfrac{1}{1 + nh} \le \dfrac{1}{n h}$ can be made arbitrarily small.

⚠ For $|q| \ge 1$ the general term does not tend to $0$, so the series must diverge — this half needs no test at all.
