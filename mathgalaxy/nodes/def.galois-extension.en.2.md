## Notes · **draft**
⭐ **Why the three conditions are equivalent**: normal + separable ensures that roots go to roots and that roots are distinct, so automorphisms can spread over all $[K:F]$ conjugates; conversely, enough automorphisms force normality and separability. The full proof of the equivalence is the backbone of the course; it is not expanded here.

⭐ **The invariant is the fixed field**: $K^{G} := \{\, x \in K : \sigma(x) = x\ \ \forall \sigma \in G \,\}$ is a subfield of $K$ and automatically contains $F$; the Galois condition says precisely that it **equals** $F$, no more.
