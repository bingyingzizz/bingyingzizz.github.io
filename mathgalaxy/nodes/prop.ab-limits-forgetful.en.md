## Statement · **draft**
If $\mathcal{C}$ has all limits, then $\mathrm{Ab}(\mathcal{C})$ also has all limits, and the **forgetful functor** $\mathrm{Ab}(\mathcal{C}) \to \mathcal{C}$ preserves them.
