# Riesz representation via perfect pairings　`thm.riesz-perfect-pairing`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=thm.riesz-perfect-pairing)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./thm.riesz-perfect-pairing.md)

⭐ The two Riesz theorems are instances of the same framework: a perfect pairing together with surjectivity. The proofs in (c) use the projection theorem and Radon–Nikodym independently, so there is no circularity.

⚠ **A perfect pairing alone does not imply surjectivity**: In the pairing of $\ell^1$ with $c_0$, $T : X \to Y^*$ is surjective but $S : Y \to X^*$ is not (see the notes to `def.perfect-pairing`). Thus (b) requires reflexivity. Without it, ‘one is surjective $\implies$ the other is surjective’ is false.
