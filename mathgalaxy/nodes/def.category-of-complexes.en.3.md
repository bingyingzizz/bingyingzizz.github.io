# category of complexes　`def.category-of-complexes`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=def.category-of-complexes)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./def.category-of-complexes.md)

Bounded below, bounded above and bounded complexes give **full subcategories** $\mathcal{C}^{+}(\mathcal{C})$, $\mathcal{C}^{-}(\mathcal{C})$, $\mathcal{C}^{b}(\mathcal{C})$; the homotopy category receives $+$, $-$, $b$ similarly.

Limits and colimits are computed **termwise**, and so is the additive structure (see @@prop.complex-additive@@); when $\mathcal{C}$ is abelian, $\mathcal{C}(\mathcal{C})$ is abelian too.
