## Notes · **draft**
⭐ **In one sentence**: the group ring spreads group-theoretic problems into linear algebra — the multiplication of the group is expanded into a bilinear operation on $R$-modules, so the machinery of "modules", "resolutions", and "derived functors" becomes available for groups.
