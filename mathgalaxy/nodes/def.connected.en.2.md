## Notes · **draft**
The connected components form a partition of $X$, and each component is closed. Subsets that are **both open and closed** (called **clopen** sets) are the other face of connectedness: $X$ is connected iff its only clopen subsets are $\emptyset$ and $X$ itself — so "is there a non-trivial clopen set?" is the same question as "can the space be split?".
