# Abel–Dirichlet test　`thm.abel-dirichlet-series`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=thm.abel-dirichlet-series)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./thm.abel-dirichlet-series.md)

⭐ The heart of the proof is **Abel summation (summation by parts)**: rewrite $\sum_{k \le n} a_k b_k$ as $A_n b_n - A_m b_{m+1} - \sum_{k} A_k (b_{k+1} - b_k)$, so that "$A$ is bounded" and "$b$ is monotone" each do their job once, and then finish with the Cauchy criterion.
