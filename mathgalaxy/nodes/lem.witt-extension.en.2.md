## Notes · **draft**
The proof is induction on $\dim A$: choose a non-isotropic $a\in A$, write $A$ as $A_0\perp\operatorname{span}(a)$, extend $\sigma|_{A_0}$ by induction, then use one reflection to move the image point onto $\sigma(a)$, and glue orthogonally.

The proof is on the edge `imp.witt-extension`; it is the direct source of Witt's cancellation theorem.
