## Statement · **draft**
Let $(X, \mathcal{M}, \mu )$ be a measure space. Define

$$\mu_0(E) := \sup \{ \mu(F) : F \subseteq E, F \in \mathcal{M}, \mu(F) < \infty \}$$

Then $\mu _{0}$ is called the **semifinite part** of $\mu$; it is a semifinite measure, and $\mu _{0} \le \mu$.
