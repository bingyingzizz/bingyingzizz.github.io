(The preceding inequalities hold for every $R$-module $M$.) Furthermore, if $R$ is a field, then $\operatorname{gl.dim} R = 0$. Otherwise, $\operatorname{gl.dim} R = 1$. In particular,

$$\operatorname{gl.dim} \mathbb{Z} = 1 .$$
