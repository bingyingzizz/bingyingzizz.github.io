# category of monoids　`def.category-of-monoids`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=def.category-of-monoids)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./def.category-of-monoids.md)

Coproducts are **free products** (juxtapose words, then quotient so that the identity and the operation are respected); the initial object is again the trivial monoid (isomorphic to the terminal object); coequalizers are quotient monoids (the closure of the generated congruence); pushouts are amalgamated free products.

## Notes · **draft**
**Complete and cocomplete**: the constructions above give small limits and small colimits term by term; the forgetful functor $\mathbf{Mon}\to\mathbf{Set}$ preserves limits.
