# Fitting Decomposition　`prop.fitting-decomposition`

[in graph](../#n=prop.fitting-decomposition)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./prop.fitting-decomposition.md)

## Statement · **draft**
Let $f\in\operatorname{End}_A(V)$ with $\dim V<\infty$. Then $V=\operatorname{Im}(f^\infty)\oplus\operatorname{Ker}(f^\infty)$, where $\operatorname{Im}(f^\infty)=\bigcap_n\operatorname{Im}(f^n)$ and $\operatorname{Ker}(f^\infty)=\bigcup_n\operatorname{Ker}(f^n)$; $f$ is an automorphism on $\operatorname{Im}(f^\infty)$ and nilpotent on $\operatorname{Ker}(f^\infty)$.
