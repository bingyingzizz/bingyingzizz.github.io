Statement

A **functor** F:C→DF : \mathcal{C} \to \mathcal{D}F:C→D from a category C\mathcal{C}C to a category D\mathcal{D}D consists of two parts:

  • a map on objects X↦F(X)X \mapsto F(X)X↦F(X);
  • a map on morphisms f↦F(f)f \mapsto F(f)f↦F(f), sending f:X→Yf : X \to Yf:X→Y to F(f):F(X)→F(Y)F(f) : F(X) \to F(Y)F(f):F(X)→F(Y).

It is required to preserve identities** and preserve composition**:

F(1X)=1F(X),F(g∘f)=F(g)∘F(f)F(1_X) = 1_{F(X)}, \qquad F(g \circ f) = F(g) \circ F(f)F(1X​)=1F(X)​,F(g∘f)=F(g)∘F(f)

(whenever g∘fg \circ fg∘f is defined). A functor in the opposite direction F:Cop→DF : \mathcal{C}^{\mathrm{op}} \to \mathcal{D}F:Cop→D is called a **contravariant functor**.

Notes

The two requirements together say one thing: **a functor transports the entire structure of a category verbatim** — identities remain identities, composites remain composites, and even associativity need not be checked again (it follows automatically).

Examples:

  • the **forgetful functor** Top→Set\mathbf{Top} \to \mathbf{Set}Top→Set: (X,τ)↦X(X, \tau) \mapsto X(X,τ)↦X, treating continuous maps as ordinary maps.

The topology is discarded, hence "forgetful".

Likewise Ab→Set\mathbf{Ab} \to \mathbf{Set}Ab→Set, Grp→Mon\mathbf{Grp} \to \mathbf{Mon}Grp→Mon (inclusion).

  • Set→Top\mathbf{Set} \to \mathbf{Top}Set→Top has two: X↦(X,P(X))X \mapsto (X, \mathcal{P}(X))X↦(X,P(X)) (discrete topology) and X↦(X,{∅,X})X \mapsto (X, \{ \emptyset, X \})X↦(X,{∅,X}) (trivial topology). Both work, because maps out of a discrete space and into a trivial space are always continuous.
  • **free constructions** Set→Ab\mathbf{Set} \to \mathbf{Ab}Set→Ab: X↦⨁XZX \mapsto \bigoplus_X \mathbb{Z}X↦⨁X​Z; Set→Grp\mathbf{Set} \to \mathbf{Grp}Set→Grp: X↦⟨x,x−1∣x∈X⟩X \mapsto \langle x, x^{-1} \mid x \in X \rangleX↦⟨x,x−1∣x∈X⟩.
  • **abelianization** Grp→Ab\mathbf{Grp} \to \mathbf{Ab}Grp→Ab: G↦G/[G,G]G \mapsto G / [G, G]G↦G/[G,G].
  • **groupification** Mon→Grp\mathbf{Mon} \to \mathbf{Grp}Mon→Grp: M↦Mgr=⟨xg (g∈M)∣xgxh=xgh⟩M \mapsto M^{gr} = \langle x_g \ (g \in M) \mid x_g x_h = x_{gh} \rangleM↦Mgr=⟨xg​ (g∈M)∣xg​xh​=xgh​⟩.
  • Topop→Cat\mathbf{Top}^{\mathrm{op}} \to \mathbf{Cat}Topop→Cat: (X,τ)↦Open⁡(X)(X, \tau) \mapsto \operatorname{Open}(X)(X,τ)↦Open(X). Contravariant, because fewer open sets means more maps.
