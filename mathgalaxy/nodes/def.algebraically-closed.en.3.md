# algebraically closed field　`def.algebraically-closed`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=def.algebraically-closed)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./def.algebraically-closed.md)

⭐ **Existence of algebraic closures needs choice**: well-order the monic irreducibles over $F$, quotient by them one at a time, build a chain of extensions inductively, and take the union by **Zorn's lemma** (the union is again a field, and algebraic). So “every field has an algebraic closure” sits oddly in the graph: it is one of the **rare existence theorems in field theory that need AC**.
