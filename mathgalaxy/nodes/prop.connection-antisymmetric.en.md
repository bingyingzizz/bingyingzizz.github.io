## Statement · **draft**
Let $(e_{1}, e_{2}, e_{3})$ be a moving frame (an orthonormal frame) and let $\omega^{i}_{\ j}$ be the connection forms. Then for all $i,j$:

$$\omega^{i}_{\ j}+\omega^{j}_{\ i} = 0 ;$$
