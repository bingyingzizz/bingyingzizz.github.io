## Notes · **draft**
"Equivalence relation + compatible with operations" is precisely the general definition of a **congruence relation**; quotienting by it gives the quotient ring $\mathbb{Z}/n\mathbb{Z}$.

For $n = 0$ one sets $a \equiv b \pmod 0 \iff a = b$: congruence modulo $0$ is equality.

Congruence turns "divisibility" into "equality in a finite ring", the most central step of elementary number theory: applying Lagrange's theorem to $\mathbb{Z}/n\mathbb{Z}$ later yields Euler's theorem and Fermat's little theorem.
