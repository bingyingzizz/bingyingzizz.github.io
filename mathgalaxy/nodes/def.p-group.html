陈述

设 ppp 是素数，GGG 是有限群。若 ∣G∣=pn|G| = p^{n}∣G∣=pn（n≥0n \ge 0n≥0），称 GGG 是 ppp-群。 等价地，GGG 的每个元素的阶都是 ppp 的幂：元素的阶整除 ∣G∣|G|∣G∣，故只能是 ppp 的幂；反之若每个元素的阶都是 ppp 的幂，由 Cauchy 定理 ∣G∣|G|∣G∣ 没有其他素因子，故 ∣G∣|G|∣G∣ 是 ppp 的幂。

子群 H≤GH \le GH≤G 若本身是 ppp-群，称 ppp-子群；GGG 的极大 ppp-子群（按包含关系）叫 Sylow ppp-子群，见 Sylow 子群。

说明

⭐ 例：Z/pn\mathbb{Z}/p^{n}Z/pn、V4=Z/2×Z/2V_4 = \mathbb{Z}/2 \times \mathbb{Z}/2V4​=Z/2×Z/2、ppp-群的中心非平凡（类方程的直接推论）。

⚠ ppp-群不必阿贝尔：D8D_8D8​（888 阶二面体群）是 222-群但不交换。

⭐ ppp-群可解，而且是幂零群：它的下中心列每一步都真增长，有限步到 GGG（因为中心非平凡，对 G/ZG/ZG/Z 归纳）。

Statement draft

Let ppp be a prime and GGG a finite group. If ∣G∣=pn|G| = p^{n}∣G∣=pn with n≥0n \ge 0n≥0, then GGG is a ppp-group.

Equivalently, every element of GGG has order a power of ppp: the order of an element divides ∣G∣|G|∣G∣, hence can only be a power of ppp; conversely, if every element has order a power of ppp, then by Cauchy's theorem ∣G∣|G|∣G∣ has no other prime divisor, so ∣G∣|G|∣G∣ is a power of ppp.

A subgroup H≤GH \le GH≤G that is itself a ppp-group is a ppp-subgroup; of GGG, a maximal ppp-subgroup (under inclusion) is called a Sylow ppp-subgroup, see Sylow 子群.

Notes draft

⭐ Examples: Z/pn\mathbb{Z}/p^{n}Z/pn, V4=Z/2×Z/2V_4 = \mathbb{Z}/2 \times \mathbb{Z}/2V4​=Z/2×Z/2; the center of a ppp-group is nontrivial (a direct consequence of the class equation).

⚠ A ppp-group need not be abelian: D8D_8D8​ (the dihedral group of order 888) is a 222-group but is not abelian.

⭐ A ppp-group is solvable, and in fact nilpotent: each step of its lower central series grows strictly and reaches GGG in finitely many steps (the center is nontrivial, then induct on G/ZG/ZG/Z).
