# A Topos Morphism from Cond over X to Sheaves on Opens　`prop.cond-slice-topos-morphism`

where $\mathrm{Open}(X)$ is the topos corresponding to the topological space of open sets of $X$ (open sets ordered by inclusion, with its standard topology).

The **inverse image** sends $Y \in \mathrm{Cond}/X$ to $U \mapsto \operatorname{Hom}_{/X}(\underline{U}, Y)$;
the **direct image** sends $F \in \mathrm{Open}(X)$ to $\varinjlim_{U \subseteq X,\ s \in F(U)} \underline{U}$.
