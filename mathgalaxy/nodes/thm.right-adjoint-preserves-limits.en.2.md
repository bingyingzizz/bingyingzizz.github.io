# Right Adjoints Preserve Limits　`thm.right-adjoint-preserves-limits`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=thm.right-adjoint-preserves-limits)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./thm.right-adjoint-preserves-limits.md)

This is the quickest way to show that a functor cannot be a right adjoint — if it fails to preserve some limit (say a product), it is out at once. Conversely, it explains why forgetful functors can always be right adjoints: they usually preserve limits (the structure is defined pointwise).
