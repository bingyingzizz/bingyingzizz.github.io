## Notes · **draft**
**Why the "smallest closed equivalence relation".** For the quotient $X/R$ to be weak Hausdorff, $R$ must be $k$-closed (previous item); and to quotient out as little as possible, so that the reflector is the "most economical" repair, one takes the smallest such closed equivalence relation. ($R \mapsto X/R$ the larger the relation, the smaller the quotient — do not get the direction backwards.)
