# entire and meromorphic functions　`def.entire-meromorphic`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=def.entire-meromorphic)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./def.entire-meromorphic.md)

Equivalent description: for every $a \in \Omega$ there are a neighbourhood $U$ of $a$ and holomorphic functions $p, q$ on $U$ (with $q \not\equiv 0$), such that $f = p/q$ holds on $U$.

## Notes · **draft**
⭐ The sphere viewpoint is cleanest: replacing $\Omega$ by $\hat{\mathbb{C}}$, meromorphic functions are exactly the holomorphic maps $\hat{\mathbb{C}} \to \hat{\mathbb{C}}$ — poles are the points mapped to $\infty$. The meromorphic functions on $\hat{\mathbb{C}}$ are precisely the rational functions.
