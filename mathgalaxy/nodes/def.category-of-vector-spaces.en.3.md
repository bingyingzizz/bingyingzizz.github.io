## Notes · **draft**
**Why it is listed separately**: the pair "objects + linear maps" of linear algebra is, in categorical language, $\mathbf{Vect}_K$; once it is available, "duality" and "Hom" can be written as functors.

**Forgetting structure**: the forgetful functor $\mathbf{Vect}_K\to\mathbf{Set}$ forgets addition and scalar multiplication, retaining only the underlying sets and functions; it is faithful but not full (not every function is linear).
