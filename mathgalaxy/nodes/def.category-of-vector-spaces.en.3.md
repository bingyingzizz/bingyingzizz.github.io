# category of vector spaces　`def.category-of-vector-spaces`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=def.category-of-vector-spaces)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./def.category-of-vector-spaces.md)

$\mathbf{Vect}_K$ is an **additive category**: $\operatorname{Hom}_K(V,W)$ becomes a $K$-vector space under pointwise addition and pointwise scalar multiplication, composition is linear in each variable, and finite direct sums are simultaneously products and coproducts.

## Notes · **draft**
**Why it is listed separately**: the pair "objects + linear maps" of linear algebra is, in categorical language, $\mathbf{Vect}_K$; once it is available, "duality" and "Hom" can be written as functors.
