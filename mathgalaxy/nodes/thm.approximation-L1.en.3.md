# approximation in L1　`thm.approximation-L1`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=thm.approximation-L1)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./thm.approximation-L1.md)

Proof idea: split $f$ into the four nonnegative parts $\operatorname{Re} f^{+}$, $\operatorname{Re} f^{-}$, $\operatorname{Im} f^{+}$, $\operatorname{Im} f^{-}$ and approximate each by simple functions (simple approximation theorem);

in the case of $\mathbb{R}$, cut the domain into bounded pieces and use continuous functions to dominate the indicators of half-open intervals.

⚠ The continuous-function clause **depends on $\mu$ being Lebesgue–Stieltjes**: for an arbitrary measure the simple-function approximation still holds, but the continuous-function statement fails.
