## Notes · **draft**
**Basic laws.** For $m, n \in \mathbb{Z}$ one has $x^{m+n} = x^{m} x^{n}$, $(x^{m})^{n} = x^{mn}$, $x^{-n} = (x^{n})^{-1}$. The first two are proved by induction on $n \ge 0$, with negative exponents reduced to the inverse; the third follows from $x^{n} x^{-n} = e$ and uniqueness of inverses.
