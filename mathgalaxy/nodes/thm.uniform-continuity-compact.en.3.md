# continuous on a compact set implies uniformly continuous　`thm.uniform-continuity-compact`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=thm.uniform-continuity-compact)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./thm.uniform-continuity-compact.md)

⚠ Replacing the closed interval by an open one fails immediately: $f(x) = 1/x$ is continuous on $(0, 1)$ but **not** uniformly continuous — the closer to $0$ the smaller the $\delta$ needed, and $0$ is not in the domain, so there is nowhere to put that lower bound.

⭐ Direct uses: Riemann integrability (oscillation sums over partitions can be made uniformly small), uniform convergence of function sequences, and integrals depending on a parameter.
