prop.sheaf-mono-epi-iso

Machine-drafted and pending author review. The Chinese text is authoritative.

Statement

Let u:F→Gu : F \to Gu:F→G be a morphism of sheaves. Then:

  1. if uuu is both a monomorphism and an epimorphism, then uuu is an isomorphism;
  2. uuu has a unique epi-mono factorization: there is a unique III with u=F↠I↣Gu = F \twoheadrightarrow I \rightarrowtail Gu=F↠I↣G.

Notes

These two also hold in Set\mathbf{Set}Set, but in a category of sheaves they are not automatic and must be proved. They are precisely the content of the pretopos axioms — so this is exactly the interface from "sheaves" to "toposes".
