# separable Hilbert spaces are l2　`thm.separable-hilbert-is-l2`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=thm.separable-hilbert-is-l2)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./thm.separable-hilbert-is-l2.md)

⭐ Conclusion: **there is only one infinite-dimensional separable Hilbert space up to isometry**. Banach spaces are completely different: $\ell^p$ (for distinct $p$) are pairwise non-isomorphic, and $C[0,1]$, $L^1$, $c_0$ are also all different. This is the precise meaning of "Hilbert spaces are simple".

⚠️ If the target space is fixed to be $\ell^2(\mathbb{N})$, then "separable" cannot be dropped: for uncountable $I$, $\ell^2(I)$ and $\ell^2(\mathbb{N})$ are not isomorphic (compare the cardinalities of orthonormal bases).
