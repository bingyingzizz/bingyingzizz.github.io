# Cauchy in measure implies convergence　`thm.cauchy-in-measure`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=thm.cauchy-in-measure)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./thm.cauchy-in-measure.md)

This is also the half of Riesz's theorem stating “convergence in measure $\implies$ there is a subsequence converging a.e.” (the converse fails: a.e. convergence does not imply convergence in measure unless the space has finite measure).
