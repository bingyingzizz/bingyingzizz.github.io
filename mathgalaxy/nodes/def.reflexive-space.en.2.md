# reflexive Banach space　`def.reflexive-space`

## Notes · **draft**
The natural embedding is always isometric (Hahn–Banach): $\|J_X x\| = \sup_{\|\varphi\| \le 1}|\varphi(x)| = \|x\|$. Hence reflexivity $\iff$ every element of $X^{**}$ comes from $X$.

**Examples**: Hilbert spaces; $L^p$ and $\ell^p$ ($1 < p < \infty$, see `cor.lp-reflexive`); finite-dimensional spaces.

**Counterexamples**: $c_0$ ($c_0^{**} = \ell^\infty \ne c_0$), $\ell^1$ ($\ell^{1**} = (\ell^\infty)^* \supsetneq \ell^1$), $C[0,1]$.
