lem.iso-factorization

Statement

Let V,WV,WV,W be finite-dimensional indecomposable representations and let α:W→V\alpha:W\to Vα:W→V, β:V→W\beta:V\to Wβ:V→W be AAA-linear maps such that α∘β\alpha\circ\betaα∘β is an automorphism of VVV. Then α,β\alpha,\betaα,β are isomorphisms.

Notes

If αβ\alpha\betaαβ is an automorphism, then β\betaβ is injective and α\alphaα is surjective. Write W=im⁡β⊕ker⁡αW=\operatorname{im}\beta\oplus\ker\alphaW=imβ⊕kerα; both summands are subrepresentations, and indecomposability forces ker⁡α=0\ker\alpha=0kerα=0. Thus α\alphaα is injective and surjective, hence an isomorphism, and β\betaβ is an isomorphism as well. This is the cancellation step in Krull–Schmidt.
