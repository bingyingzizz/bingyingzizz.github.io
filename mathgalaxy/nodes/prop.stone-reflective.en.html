Statement

The category of Stone spaces is a reflective subcategory of CHaus\mathbf{CHaus}CHaus.

Notes

Corollary: any limit of Stone spaces is again a Stone space. Put this next to "totally disconnected spaces are closed under limits" and one reads off the double identity of Stone spaces: they are both "the totally disconnected ones among compact Hausdorff spaces" and "what remains after reflecting once more inside that reflective subcategory".

Proof. A Stone space is compact Hausdorff and totally disconnected. Stack the two reflections:

  • the inclusion CHaus↪Top\mathbf{CHaus}\hookrightarrow\mathbf{Top}CHaus↪Top has a left adjoint β\betaβ (Stone–Čech compactification), i.e. CHaus\mathbf{CHaus}CHaus is reflective;
  • inside CHaus\mathbf{CHaus}CHaus take the totally disconnected ones: the inclusion Stone↪CHaus\mathbf{Stone}\hookrightarrow\mathbf{CHaus}Stone↪CHaus has a left adjoint "take the quotient by connected components" S↦S/ ⁣∼S\mapsto S/\!\simS↦S/∼ (crush each connected component to a point), because a continuous map sends connected components of a compact Hausdorff space to connected sets, and in a totally disconnected space the only connected sets are points;
  • hence Stone↪CHaus\mathbf{Stone}\hookrightarrow\mathbf{CHaus}Stone↪CHaus is reflective, with reflector "quotient by connected components" (which again gives a compact Hausdorff space ✓). ∎

⭐ A reflective subcategory's inclusion preserves limits, so limits of Stone spaces (computed in CHaus\mathbf{CHaus}CHaus or Top\mathbf{Top}Top) are again Stone ✓ — this is exactly "Stone spaces are closed under limits".
