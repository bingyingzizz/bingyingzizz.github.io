# C(S,Z) is a free abelian group　`thm.csz-free`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=thm.csz-free)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./thm.csz-free.md)

⭐ It is the pivot of the whole solid theory: without it, $Z[S]^{\blacksquare}$ could not be described as $\prod_I \mathbb{Z}$, and the "compact projective generators" in `thm.solid-abelian` would have no basis.

⚠ Note that $C(S,\mathbb{Z})$ behaves completely differently from its **bounded** version and from $C(S,\mathbb{R})$: the claim here concerns $C(S,\mathbb{Z})$ as an abstract abelian group; $C(S,\mathbb{R})$ as an abstract vector space of course has a basis, but the freeness of the former cannot simply be transferred.
