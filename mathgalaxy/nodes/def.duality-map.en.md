## Statement · **draft**
Let $p, q$ be conjugate, $g \in L^q$. Define the linear functional on $L^p$

$$\varphi_g(f) := \int f g \, d\mu$$

## Notes · **draft**
By **Hölder's inequality** this functional is bounded, and

$$\|\varphi_g\| \le \|g\|_q$$

(here $\|\varphi_g\|$ is the operator norm in $(L^p)^*$, namely $\sup\{|\varphi_g(f)| : \|f\|_p = 1\}$.)
