## Statement · **draft**
Regard all $\mathbb{Z}/n\mathbb{Z}$ as discrete compact spaces and, for $n \mid m$, take the **inverse limit** along the quotient maps $\mathbb{Z}/m \to \mathbb{Z}/n$:

$$\hat{\mathbb{Z}} := \varprojlim_{n \ge 1} \mathbb{Z}/n\mathbb{Z} \;\subseteq\; \prod_{n \ge 1} \mathbb{Z}/n\mathbb{Z}.$$
