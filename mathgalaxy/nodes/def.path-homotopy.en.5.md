## Notes · **draft**
**Why the endpoints must be pinned**: $[0, 1]$ is convex and contractible, so **any** two paths are homotopic (shrink $\alpha$ to a point along a straight line and then expand into $\beta$). A homotopy that does not pin the endpoints collapses all paths into one class and detects nothing; only after pinning the endpoints does the information "how it winds" survive.

⚠️ Path homotopy **makes sense only for paths with common endpoints** — otherwise the conditions $F(0, s)$ and $F(1, s)$ cannot even be written down.
