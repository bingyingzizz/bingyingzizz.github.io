# Change of Variables on Manifolds　`thm.change-of-variables-manifold`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=thm.change-of-variables-manifold)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./thm.change-of-variables-manifold.md)

**Why it holds only for $n$-forms**: a form with $k<n$ transforms by $k\times k$ minors under a diffeomorphism, which does not match the $n$-dimensional volume element, so there is no "change-of-variables formula for $k$-forms on a manifold". To integrate a $k$-form one first chooses a $k$-dimensional submanifold and pulls back (`def.integral-form`, opening sentence).
