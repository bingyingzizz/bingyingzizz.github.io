**Proof (standard approach).**
1. **Connected component**: $M^{0}$ (the connected component of the identity) is a closed subgroup. $M^0$ is a **connected locally compact abelian group**, and such groups are precisely those of the form $\mathbb{R}^{n}\times K$ (with $K$ a compact connected abelian group). This is classical theory (the no-small-subgroups property and Lie theory separate the compact subgroup part of $M^{0}$ from the vector group part).
