# Real Banach Spaces Are Acyclic on Compact Hausdorff Spaces　`thm.real-banach-chaus-acyclic`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=thm.real-banach-chaus-acyclic)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./thm.real-banach-chaus-acyclic.md)

⚠️ **The result fails for complex Banach spaces.** This is one direct motivation for the theory of solid modules and solid quasi-coherent sheaves. In the real case one can use the **order structure** behind Tietze extension (suprema on $\mathbb{R}$ and partitions) to eliminate the discrepancy. The complex case lacks these tools and requires different machinery.
