# interval　`def.interval`

Its **length** (the convention of Lebesgue measure $m$) is $b - a$ (requiring $a \le b$; for $a > b$, $(a, b] = \emptyset$, which needs a separate convention).

## Notes · **draft**
⭐ **The half-open interval $\{ (a, b] : a < b \}$ is the starting point for constructing Lebesgue–Stieltjes measure**: it is only a basic class; finite disjoint unions give a ring, and length is already a premeasure on that ring.

Why half-open rather than open intervals: lengths of disjoint unions are easy to compute — $[a, b) \sqcup [b, c) = [a, c)$ fails for open intervals.
