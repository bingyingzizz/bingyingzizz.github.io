\u26A0 **Two more traps**: in the definition of "additive category", "all direct sums" and "all finite products" agree only **given preadditivity** (in a general category finite products and finite coproducts need not coincide); and "all finite limits and colimits" is the phrasing attached to (2), which does **not** imply abelianness ($\mathbf{Set}$ has finite limits and coproducts yet is far from abelian) \u2014 the difference lies in disjointness, universality and effectiveness.
