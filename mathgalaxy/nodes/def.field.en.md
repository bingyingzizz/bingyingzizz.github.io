## Statement · **draft**
Let $F$ be a **set** and $+$, $\cdot$ two **binary operations** on $F$ (that is, two functions $F \times F \to F$). Then $(F, +, \cdot)$ is a **field** if and only if:

- $(F, +)$ is an **abelian group**;
- $(F \setminus \{0\}, \cdot)$ is an **abelian group** (here $0$ is the identity of the additive group);
- the **distributive law** $a (b + c) = ab + ac$ holds for all $a, b, c \in F$.
