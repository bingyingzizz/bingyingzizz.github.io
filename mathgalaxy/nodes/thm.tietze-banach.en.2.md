# Tietze　`thm.tietze-banach`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=thm.tietze-banach)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./thm.tietze-banach.md)

(1) Finite-dimensional case: $V = \mathbb{R}^{m}$. Apply the classical Tietze extension theorem coordinatewise (extension of continuous real-valued functions on normal spaces, based on Urysohn's lemma).

(2) General separable Banach case: Every separable Banach space is isomorphic to a closed subspace of $\ell^{\infty}$, and $\ell^{\infty}$ is a **Lipschitz retract**. First extend the map to $\ell^{\infty}$ and then retract.

(3) In the nonseparable case, the compact image lies in a separable closed subspace, reducing the problem to (2).
