# Arzelà–Ascoli theorem　`thm.arzela-ascoli`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=thm.arzela-ascoli)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./thm.arzela-ascoli.md)

1. **Uniformly bounded**: there exists $M$ such that $|f(x)| \le M$ for all $f \in \mathcal{F}$, $x \in X$;
2. **Equicontinuous**: for every $\forall \varepsilon > 0$ there exists $\delta > 0$ such that $d(x, y) < \delta$ implies $|f(x) - f(y)| < \varepsilon$ for **all** $f \in \mathcal{F}$ simultaneously.

Then $\mathcal{F}$ is **relatively compact** in $C(X)$: every sequence in $\mathcal{F}$ has a subsequence converging in the sup norm.
