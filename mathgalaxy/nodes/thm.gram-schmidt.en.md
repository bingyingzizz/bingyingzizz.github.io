## Statement · **draft**
Let $(v_1, \ldots, v_k)$ be a linearly independent family in an inner product space $V$. Then there is an orthonormal family $(e_1, \ldots, e_k)$ and an invertible upper-triangular coefficient relation such that

$$\operatorname{span}(e_1, \ldots, e_j) = \operatorname{span}(v_1, \ldots, v_j), \qquad j = 1, \ldots, k$$
