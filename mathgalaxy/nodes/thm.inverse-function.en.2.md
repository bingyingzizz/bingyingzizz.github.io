and its inverse $g := (f\bigl|_{V})^{-1} : W \to V$ is also $C^{1}$, and for every $y \in W$

$$Dg(y) = \bigl( Df(g(y)) \bigr)^{-1}$$

## Notes · **draft**
⭐ The shape of the theorem is worth remembering: **locally invertible $\iff$ $Df(a)$ invertible**, and "the derivative of the inverse is the inverse of the derivative".

The second half is pure linear algebra — once $f$ is viewed locally as "a linear map plus higher-order terms", inversion naturally lands on $Df$.

⚠ "Locally" cannot be dropped.
