## Statement · **draft**
Let $E^{p,q}_{r} \implies H^{p+q}$ be a **first-quadrant** spectral sequence. If for some $r \ge 1$ we have

$$p \ne 0 \implies E^{p,q}_{r} = 0 \qquad (\forall q),$$

then for every $q$ we have $E^{0,q}_{r} \cong H^{q}$.
