# compactly generated　`def.compactly-generated`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=def.compactly-generated)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./def.compactly-generated.md)

Equivalently: the topology of $X$ is completely determined by "continuous maps from compact Hausdorff spaces" — $Y \subseteq X$ is open (closed) $\iff$ for every compact Hausdorff space $S$ and every continuous map $f : S \to X$, $f^{-1}(Y)$ is open (closed) in $S$.

## Notes · **draft**
"The topology is determined by maps from compact Hausdorff spaces" means: **a subset is open as soon as its preimage under every such map is open**.

General topological spaces do not satisfy this — so compact generation excludes the spaces with "too few tests", and the remaining ones behave well under products and function spaces.
