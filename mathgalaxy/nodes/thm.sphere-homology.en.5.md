# homology of spheres　`thm.sphere-homology`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=thm.sphere-homology)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./thm.sphere-homology.md)

This map is continuous and takes values in $S^{n}$. The point $H_{0}(x, t) = (x/\lVert x \rVert, 0)$ lies on the equator $S^{n-1}$, we have $H_{1} = \mathrm{id}$, and $H_{s}$ maps the equator into itself. Thus $S^{n-1}$ is a deformation retract of $U \cap V$, so $\tilde{H}_{p}(U \cap V) \cong \tilde{H}_{p}(S^{n-1})$ by homotopy invariance.

**Reduced Mayer–Vietoris.** Since $U \cup V = S^{n}$ and $U \cap V \ne \emptyset$, we have the long exact sequence

$$\cdots \to \tilde{H}_{p}(U \cap V) \to \tilde{H}_{p}(U) \oplus \tilde{H}_{p}(V) \to \tilde{H}_{p}(S^{n}) \to \tilde{H}_{p-1}(U \cap V) \to \cdots$$
