## Statement · **draft**
Fix $V\in\mathbf{Vect}_K$. The **covariant Hom functor**

$$\operatorname{Hom}_K(V,-):\mathbf{Vect}_K\to\mathbf{Vect}_K$$

sends an object to $U\mapsto\operatorname{Hom}_K(V,U)$ and a morphism $T:U_1\to U_2$ to precomposition $T\circ(-):\operatorname{Hom}_K(V,U_1)\to\operatorname{Hom}_K(V,U_2)$.
