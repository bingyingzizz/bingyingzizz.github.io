# Residue Theorem　`thm.residue`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=thm.residue)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./thm.residue.md)

$$\oint_{\partial\Omega} f\,\mathrm{d}z = \sum_{k=1}^{n} \oint_{|\zeta - z_k| = \varepsilon} f\,\mathrm{d}\zeta = 2\pi i \sum_{k=1}^{n} \operatorname{Res}(f, z_k),$$

The last step is the definition of the residue.

⭐ This is the hardest-working theorem in complex analysis: the whole batch of real integrals in Chapters 23 and 24 ($\int_0^{2\pi} Q(\sin\theta, \cos\theta)\,\mathrm{d}\theta$, $\int_{-\infty}^{\infty} f(x)\,\mathrm{d}x$, $\int_{-\infty}^{\infty} e^{iax}f(x)\,\mathrm{d}x$, $\int_0^{\infty} x^{a-1}f(x)\,\mathrm{d}x$, series sums) rests on it and becomes residue computation.
