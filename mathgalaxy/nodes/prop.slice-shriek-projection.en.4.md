[in graph](../#n=prop.slice-shriek-projection)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./prop.slice-shriek-projection.md)

⚠️ **The $M,N$ in the two formulas must not be mixed up**: $j_{X!}$ is defined only on $\mathrm{Ab}(\mathcal{T}/X)$, so $M$ in the first formula and $N$ in the second must first be in the slice; plugging $N \in \mathrm{Ab}(\mathcal{T})$ into the second formula would make $j_{X!}N$ nonexistent.
