## Statement · **draft**
Let $\mathcal{T}$ be a topos and $X \in \mathcal{T}$. Then the functor

$$j_{X}^{-1} : \mathcal{T}(\mathbf{Ab}) \longrightarrow \mathcal{T}/X(\mathbf{Ab})$$

has a **left adjoint** $j_{X!}$, and $j_{X!}$ is **exact**.
