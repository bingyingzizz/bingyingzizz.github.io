# Lagrangian Submanifold　`def.lagrangian-submanifold`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=def.lagrangian-submanifold)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./def.lagrangian-submanifold.md)

nates, the graph of a closed 1-form $\iff$ a Lagrangian graph, by computing the pullback of the canonical symplectic form. A closed 1-form is locally of the form $dF$ by the Poincaré lemma. Global existence also requires exactness.
- To express a general Lagrangian submanifold as a graph in fixed position coordinates, the projection must also be a local diffeomorphism. A cotangent fiber is itself Lagrangian but cannot be written as $p=dF(q)$ under that position projection.
