is **faithful** and has a right adjoint $X \mapsto X(\cdot)$. Restricted to the full subcategory of **compactly generated spaces**, it becomes **fully faithful**.

## Notes · **draft**
Key sentence: **if $X$ is a topological space, then its underlying space $X(\cdot) \cong kX$**.

So the round trip "topological space $\to$ condensed set $\to$ underlying topological space" is exactly **$k$-ification**: it replaces a general topological space by the compactly generated one, after which it no longer changes.
