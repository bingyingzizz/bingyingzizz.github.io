The full verification is in `prop.boundary-sign-convention` — the conclusion is that these signs agree **face by face** with the induced orientation "outward normal first".

**$2k$ faces**: $i$ ranges over $k$ values and $\alpha$ over the two ends, so there are $2k$ $(k-1)$-cubes. They are not pairwise distinct as maps, but as terms of a chain they are pairwise distinct (they may coincide elsewhere, but not as maps).

**Square zero**: see `prop.boundary-squared-zero`. This is the key to the whole bookkeeping — it guarantees "the boundary has no boundary", so Stokes' theorem does not acquire an extra layer on either side.
