## Notes · **draft**
**Three well-definedness checks** (the whole difficulty of "the derived couple is again an exact couple"):

- $j^{\prime}$: if $i(x) = i(x^{\prime})$, then $x - x^{\prime} \in \ker i = \operatorname{im} k$, hence $j(x - x^{\prime}) \in j(\operatorname{im} k) = 0$ (because $kj = 0$) — independent of representatives.

Also $j(x)$ is a $d$-cocycle: $d\, j(x) = j k j(x) = 0$.
