# Structure Theorem for Finitely Generated Modules over a PID　`thm.pid-fg-module-structure`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=thm.pid-fg-module-structure)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./thm.pid-fg-module-structure.md)

$M\cong R^r\oplus R/(d_1)\oplus\cdots\oplus R/(d_k).$

The free rank $r$ and the ideals $(d_1),\ldots,(d_k)$ (up to units) are uniquely determined by $M$; $k=0$ if and only if $M$ is torsion-free.

## Notes · **draft**
Proof: see the edge @@imp.pid-fg-module-structure@@.

When $R=K[x]$ and $M$ comes from a linear operator $A$, the free part is zero, $d_i$ are exactly the invariant factors of $xI-A$, and the blocks give the rational canonical form (@@thm.rational-canonical-form@@).
