## Statement · **draft**
Let $G$ be a finite group, $\operatorname{char}F=0$, and $F$ algebraically closed. Put $\operatorname{Irr}(G)_F=\{V_1,\ldots,V_s\}$ and let $CL(G)=\{C_1,\ldots,C_r\}$ be the conjugacy classes with representatives $g_j\in C_j$. The character table is the $s\times r$ matrix $T=(\chi_{V_i}(g_j))_{i,j}$.
