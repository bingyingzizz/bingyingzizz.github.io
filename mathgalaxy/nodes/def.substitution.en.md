## Statement · **draft**
Let $x$ be a variable, $t$ a term, $\varphi$ a formula. Replacing, in $\varphi$, every **free occurrence** of $x$ by $t$ gives a formula written

$$\varphi (x/t)$$

called the **substitution** of $t$ for $x$ (bound occurrences are untouched — they are governed by quantifiers).
