Statement

Let MMM be a Banach abelian group and let SSS be a Stone space. Then for every n>0n > 0n>0

Hcondn(S, M)=0.H^{n}_{\mathrm{cond}}(S,\ M) = 0.Hcondn​(S, M)=0.

Notes

Proof in three steps.

(1) Choose a surjection of Stone spaces S0↠SS_{0} \twoheadrightarrow SS0​↠S and use it to compute the Čech complex. (2) The preceding result gives that this complex is 111-bounded acyclic. (3) The implication “KKK-bounded acyclicity   ⟹  \implies⟹ acyclicity” gives ordinary acyclicity. Thus Čech cohomology vanishes, and on a Stone space Čech cohomology agrees with condensed cohomology.

Relation to acyclicity with constant coefficients on Stone spaces. The constant-coefficient result uses discrete abelian groups as coefficients (the extreme case of a discrete topology). This result uses Banach abelian groups, with their complete norm structure. The conclusions have the same form, but the proofs differ. Constant coefficients use profinite inverse limits, while Banach coefficients use KKK-boundedness and Čech complexes.
