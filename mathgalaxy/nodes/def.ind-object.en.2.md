# ind-object　`def.ind-object`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=def.ind-object)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./def.ind-object.md)

$$\operatorname{Hom}_{\operatorname{Ind}(\mathcal{C})}\Bigl(\text{“}\varinjlim_{i \in I} X_{i}\text{”},\ \text{“}\varinjlim_{j \in J} Y_{j}\text{”}\Bigr) \;=\; \varprojlim_{i \in I}\ \varinjlim_{j \in J}\ \operatorname{Hom}_{\mathcal{C}}(X_{i}, Y_{j}).$$

## Notes · **draft**
**Reading**: the $\varinjlim X_{i}$ in quotes is a **formal notation** — the object is the "filtered diagram" itself, not its colimit (the colimit may not exist at all).
