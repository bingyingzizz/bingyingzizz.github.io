Abstract

The volume form of an nnn-manifold cannot simply be pulled back to a kkk-dimensional submanifold: for k<nk<nk<n every nnn-form restricts to the zero form. The volume of a submanifold therefore comes from the induced metric gN:=i∗gg_{N} := i^{*}ggN​:=i∗g, whose volume form in an oriented chart is det⁡(gab) dx1∧⋯∧dxk\sqrt{\det(g_{ab})}\,\mathrm{d}x^{1}\wedge\cdots\wedge\mathrm{d}x^{k}det(gab​)​dx1∧⋯∧dxk with gab:=g(∂ai,∂bi)g_{ab} := g(\partial_{a}i,\partial_{b}i)gab​:=g(∂a​i,∂b​i). For a hypersurface this reduces to the interior product ιν dVg\iota_{\nu}\,\mathrm{d}V_{g}ιν​dVg​ with the unit normal, which is exactly where the surface element in the divergence theorem comes from.
