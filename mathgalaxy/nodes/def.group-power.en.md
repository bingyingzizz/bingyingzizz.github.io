## Statement · **draft**
Let $G$ be a group, $x \in G$, and $e$ the identity. The **integer powers** $x^{n}$ ($n \in \mathbb{Z}$) are defined by

$$x^{0} := e, \qquad x^{n+1} := x^{n} x \ (n \ge 0), \qquad x^{-n} := (x^{-1})^{n} \ (n \ge 1)$$

equivalently $x^{-n} = (x^{n})^{-1}$.
