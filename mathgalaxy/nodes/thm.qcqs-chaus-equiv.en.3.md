## Notes · **draft**
**What this says.** A condensed set is "a sheaf probed by compact Hausdorff spaces". This theorem says: the most regular of the probed objects (quasi-compact quasi-separated) **are the probers themselves**. Thus the category of compact Hausdorff spaces sits **unchanged** inside $\mathrm{Cond}$, as its "compact-piece part".
