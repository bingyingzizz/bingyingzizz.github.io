## Notes · **draft**
**What self-adjoint equivalence means**: $M \mapsto \widehat{M}$ is both left and right adjoint to $M \mapsto \widehat{M}$ (duality is adjoint to itself, with the pairing given by the evaluation map $M \times \widehat{M} \to \mathbb{T}$).

**Duality carries over entire exact sequences**: If $0 \to M' \to M \to M'' \to 0$ is a (strict) exact sequence of locally compact abelian groups, then its dual $0 \to \widehat{M''} \to \widehat{M} \to \widehat{M'} \to 0$ is still exact, with **all arrows reversed**.
