# integer powers　`def.power`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=def.power)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./def.power.md)

⚠ $0^{0} = 1$ is an **algebraic convention** (empty product); in **analysis** $0^{0}$ is an indeterminate form with its own limit convention — the two do not conflict, but do not mix them.

⚠ Powers of the **same element** satisfy $a^{n}a = a\cdot a^{n} = a^{n+1}$ by associativity, with **no** commutativity needed; what needs care is powers of **different elements** (in a noncommutative ring $(ab)^{n} \ne a^{n}b^{n}$).
