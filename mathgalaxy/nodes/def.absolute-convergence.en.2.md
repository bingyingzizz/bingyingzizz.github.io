## Notes · **draft**
⭐ Absolute convergence is the watershed of this pack: **an absolutely convergent series can be treated as a finite sum** — it may be reordered arbitrarily (rearrangement-invariant), multiplied term by term (Cauchy product), and grouped arbitrarily.

A conditionally convergent series has **none** of these properties.

⭐ It **reduces** the problem of a general series to one of positive terms: once $\sum |a_n|$ is known to converge, the whole family of tests (comparison, Kummer, root, integral, condensation) becomes available.
