# Prime and Maximal Ideals　`prop.prime-ideal`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=prop.prime-ideal)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./prop.prime-ideal.md)

⭐ A maximal ideal is "the simplest after quotienting": $\mathbb{Z}/p\mathbb{Z}$ is a field, $\mathbb{Z}/(6)$ is not (it has zero divisors).

⚠ Maximal $\implies$ prime, but **not conversely**: in $k[x,y]$, $(x)$ is prime ($k[x,y]/(x) \cong k[y]$ is an integral domain), yet not maximal ($(x) \subset (x, y) \subset k[x,y]$).

In a one-dimensional integral domain, the **nonzero** prime ideals are all maximal (e.g. $\mathbb{Z}$, $k[x]$); $(0)$ is prime but not maximal ($\mathbb{Z}$ in $(0) \subset (2) \subset \mathbb{Z}$).
