The conclusion also holds for **contractible** open sets: star-shaped implies contractible, and the argument uses only the contraction homotopy, not the specific shape of a star-shaped region.

## Notes · **draft**
**The key is a chain homotopy**: there is a linear map $K : \Omega^{k}(U) \to \Omega^{k-1}(U)$ (the "integral" along $x \mapsto tx$, $t \in [0,1]$) such that

$$\mathrm{d}K + K\mathrm{d} = \operatorname{id}_{\Omega^{k}}\qquad (k \ge 1),$$
