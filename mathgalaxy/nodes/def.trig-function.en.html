Notes

⭐ The analytic definition of the trigonometric functions (rather than the geometric one): starting from the series, every identity and derivative formula can be computed, and they hold automatically for complex numbers.

⭐ The relation to exp⁡\expexp is revealed in the entry on Euler's formula: splitting the series of exp⁡\expexp according to the parity of nnn gives cos⁡\coscos and sin⁡\sinsin.

⚠ The geometric meaning (unit circle, angle, radian) is attached only later: the series first tell us that cos⁡\coscos has zeros and sin⁡\sinsin has a period, and the geometric picture follows.

⭐ The radius of convergence of both series on the whole plane is given by imp.trig-series-radius (Cauchy–Hadamard plus an estimate for the growth of factorials).
