# Group Cohomology　`def.group-cohomology`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=def.group-cohomology)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./def.group-cohomology.md)

**Two algorithms**:
- **Injective resolution** (the definition itself): take an injective $R[G]$-module resolution $M \rightarrowtail I^{\bullet}$ of $M$, take invariants, take homology. Cleanest, but injective $R[G]$-modules are invisible.
- **Standard (bar) resolution** (computable): take the **free** $R[G]$-module resolution $B_{\bullet} \to R$ of $R$, then take $\operatorname{Hom}_{R[G]}(B_{\bullet}, M)$, obtaining an **explicit** cochain complex. This gives a concrete formula for $H^{n}$.
