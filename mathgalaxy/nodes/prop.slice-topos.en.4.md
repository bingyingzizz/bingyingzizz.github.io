- The map from slice sheaves to sheaves on the base: sheafify the data on the category of elements accordingly, obtaining a sheaf over $\widetilde T$; when $T$ is merely a presheaf, this step cannot be described as objects being literally the same.
- Conversely obtain slice sheaves from $F\to\widetilde T$: pull back along sections; the slice uses the covers induced by the base, and only then is the sheaf condition compatible; the inverse property uses descent for sheaves and sheafification.
