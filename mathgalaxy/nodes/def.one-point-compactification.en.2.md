# One-Point Compactification　`def.one-point-compactification`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=def.one-point-compactification)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./def.one-point-compactification.md)

Let $X$ be a topological space. Take $X^{*} := X \sqcup \{\infty\}$, with the topology whose open sets are

1. the open sets of $X$;
2. sets of the form $U \cup \{\infty\}$, where $U \subseteq X$ is open and $X \setminus U$ is **compact** in $X$.

$X^{*}$ is called the **one-point compactification** of $X$ (also the Alexandrov compactification).

**Theorem**:

- $X^{*}$ is **compact** (for arbitrary $X$);
- $X^{*}$ is **Hausdorff** $\iff$ $X$ is a locally compact Hausdorff space.
