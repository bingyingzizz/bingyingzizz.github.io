## Notes · **draft**
"Pro-finite" = projective limit of finite objects. Intuitively it is built from "increasingly fine finite resolutions": $X_{i}$ is what the space looks like at resolution level $i$, and $I$ being directed guarantees that any two levels can be refined simultaneously.
