## Notes · **draft**
The dimension of the representation is $\dim_F V$. The trivial representation has $\rho(g)=\operatorname{id}_V$, and the regular representation is the left multiplication action of $G$ on $F[G]$. The group algebra $F[G]$ consists of formal sums $\sum a_g g$ with convolution multiplication, and $\rho$ extends uniquely to a unital algebra homomorphism $F[G]\to\operatorname{End}_F(V)$. This equivalence transfers all results on algebra representations to groups.
