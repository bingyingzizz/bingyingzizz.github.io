## Notes · **draft**
The representation of $G$ need not be finite-dimensional; most results in this cluster are stated in the finite-dimensional case. The trivial representation has $\rho(g)=\operatorname{id}_V$; the regular representation is left multiplication on $F[G]$. The group algebra $F[G]$ consists of formal sums $\sum a_g g$ with convolution, and $\rho$ extends uniquely to a unital algebra homomorphism $F[G]\to\operatorname{End}_F(V)$. This equivalence transfers all results on algebra representations to groups.
