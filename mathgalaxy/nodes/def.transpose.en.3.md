# Transpose of a Matrix　`def.transpose`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=def.transpose)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./def.transpose.md)

Once the direction is reversed, the order of composition must reverse as well — this is what $(AB)^{T} = B^{T}A^{T}$ says.

**Compatibility with the pairing**: regarding vectors as columns, $y^{T}Ax$ is a scalar, and equals $(A^{T}y)^{T}x$ — moving $A$ from one side to the other rests entirely on this, and it is the starting point of everything about adjoint operators and symmetric matrices that follows.
