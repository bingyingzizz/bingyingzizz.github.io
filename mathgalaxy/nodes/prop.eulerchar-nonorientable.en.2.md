## Notes · **draft**
**This proposition assumes standard form.** Here $S$ is the connected sum of $k$ copies of $\mathbb{RP}^{2}$ ($k\ge1$), so the computation below needs no classification theorem. Reduction of a general closed nonorientable surface to standard form belongs to `thm.classification-surfaces` (still a skeleton) and is outside the scope of the proposition.
