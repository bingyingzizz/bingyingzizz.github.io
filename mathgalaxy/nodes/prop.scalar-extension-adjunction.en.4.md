# Extension–Restriction Adjunction　`prop.scalar-extension-adjunction`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=prop.scalar-extension-adjunction)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./prop.scalar-extension-adjunction.md)

Triangle identities: $\varepsilon_{S \otimes M} \circ (S \otimes \eta_{M}) = \mathrm{id}$ and $f_{\ast}\varepsilon_{P} \circ \eta_{f_{\ast}P} = \mathrm{id}$, checked on pure tensors (or elements of $M$) and extended linearly ✓.

∎

⭐ **The precise meaning of "most free"**: every map that sends $M$ into some $S$-module and is compatible with the $R$-action factors uniquely through $1 \otimes -$. So $S \otimes_{R} M$ is the standard answer to "the image of $M$ in the category of $S$-modules".
