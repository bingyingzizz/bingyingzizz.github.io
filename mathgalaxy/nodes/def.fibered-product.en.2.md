Such a square is called **cartesian**. Dually, the colimit of such a diagram is the **fibred coproduct**, also called the **pushout**.

## Notes · **draft**
Intuition: the pullback is “the product after matching over the base $X_0$” — solving $f_1(x_1) = f_2(x_2)$; the pushout is “gluing $X_1, X_2$ together along their images in $X_0$”.

Example ($\mathbf{Set}$):

$$X_1 \times_{X_0} X_2 = \{(x_1, x_2) \in X_1 \times X_2 : f_1(x_1) = f_2(x_2)\}, \qquad X_1 \cup_{X_0} X_2 = (X_1 \sqcup X_2)/\!\sim$$
