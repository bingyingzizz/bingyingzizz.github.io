# Stokes' Theorem on Chains　`thm.stokes-chain`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=thm.stokes-chain)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./thm.stokes-chain.md)

Each is a special case of the other. Decompose (or subdivide) $M$ into a covering $n$-chain. Summing the chain formula term by term gives the manifold version.

Conversely, restricting the manifold version to a single orientation-preserving parametrized cube gives the single-cube case of the chain version.

When $k = 1$ and $c = \gamma : [0,1]\to\mathbb{R}$, we have $\partial\gamma = \gamma(1)-\gamma(0)$, and the formula becomes $F(\gamma(1))-F(\gamma(0)) = \int_{0}^{1}(F^{\prime}\circ\gamma)\,\gamma^{\prime}\,\mathrm{d}t$, precisely the fundamental theorem of calculus.
