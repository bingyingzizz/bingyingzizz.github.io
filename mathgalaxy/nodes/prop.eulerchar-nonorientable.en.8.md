# Euler characteristic and nonorientable genus　`prop.eulerchar-nonorientable`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=prop.eulerchar-nonorientable)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./prop.eulerchar-nonorientable.md)

⚠ **The term genus does not carry over directly to the nonorientable case**. `def.genus` is defined only for orientable surfaces (division by $2$ relies on the free rank of $H_{1}$ being even). The standard quantity in the nonorientable case is $k$ (nonorientable genus), with the relation $\chi=2-k$.

⭐ Examples: for $\mathbb{RP}^{2}$, $k=1$ and $\chi=1$; for the Klein bottle, $k=2$ and $\chi=0$; for the connected sum of three projective planes, $k=3$ and $\chi=-1$.
