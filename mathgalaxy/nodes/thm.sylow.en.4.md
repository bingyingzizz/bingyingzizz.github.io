Thus $P_3 \trianglelefteq G$ and $P_5 \trianglelefteq G$, with $P_3 \cap P_5 = \{e\}$ (the orders are coprime), so $G \cong P_3 \times P_5 \cong \mathbb{Z}/15$. **Every group of order 15 is cyclic**.

⚠ The counting conditions are only necessary. A count is ruled out only when both $n_p \equiv 1 \pmod p$ and $n_p \mid m$ fail, and satisfying them does not determine how many subgroups exist.
