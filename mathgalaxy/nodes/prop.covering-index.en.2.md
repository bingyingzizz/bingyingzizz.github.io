# index of a covering　`prop.covering-index`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=prop.covering-index)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./prop.covering-index.md)

sending $e \in p^{-1}(b_{0})$ to the right coset of the projection of the path from $e_{0}$ to $e$.

Hence the number of sheets $\#p^{-1}(b_{0})$ equals the index $[\pi_{1}(B) : p_{*}\pi_{1}(E)]$ (when both are finite).
