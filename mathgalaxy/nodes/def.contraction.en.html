The difference between "locally contracting at every point" and "uniformly contracting overall" is the life-or-death line for the theorem.

⭐ A contraction is uniformly continuous, much stronger than continuity; and its Lipschitz constant is exactly qqq.

⭐ Contractions are the only class of maps whose existence proof comes with an algorithm and an error estimate (see 压缩映射原理) — this is why they occupy such a high place in analysis: the inverse mapping theorem, the implicit function theorem, the Picard–Lindelöf theorem for ODEs and the Bellman equation all use them as their engine.
