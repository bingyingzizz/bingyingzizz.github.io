## Statement · **draft**
Let $z_{0}$ be an **essential singularity** of $f$. Then for every punctured neighbourhood $z_{0}$ of $U$, $f(U)$ takes every value in $\mathbb{C}$ except **at most one** point (and the one omitted value is attained at most finitely often).

In particular: the “density” of Casorati–Weierstrass (`thm.casorati-weierstrass`) is upgraded here to “almost surjectivity” —
