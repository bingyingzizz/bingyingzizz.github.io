# The Total Complex Is Still an Injective Resolution　`prop.total-complex-injective-resolution`

## Notes · **draft**
**Proof.** Each term of the total complex is a **finite** direct sum of injective objects (bounded below guarantees only finitely many terms in each degree), and a direct sum of injectives is injective ✓; and the total complex is compatible with the homology of the double complex: $H^{n}(\operatorname{Tot}) \cong H^{n}$ (this uses convergence from "bounded below + every column a resolution") ✓, so $K^{\bullet} \to I^{\bullet}$ is a quasi-isomorphism ✓.

∎
