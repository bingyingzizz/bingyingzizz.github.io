## Statement · **draft**
Let $\operatorname{char}F\nmid|G|$ and $F$ algebraically closed. The regular representation decomposes as $\rho_{\mathrm{reg}}\cong\bigoplus_{V\in\operatorname{Irr}(G)_F}(\dim V)\,V$, hence $\chi_{\mathrm{reg}}=\sum_V(\dim V)\chi_V$. In particular $|G|=\sum_V(\dim V)^2$, and for $g\ne1$ one has $\sum_V(\dim V)\chi_V(g)=0$.
