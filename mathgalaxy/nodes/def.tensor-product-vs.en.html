Statement

Let V,WV, WV,W be KKK-vector spaces. Take bases (ei)(e_i)(ei​), (fj)(f_j)(fj​) and form all formal linear combinations of the symbols ei⊗fje_i \otimes f_jei​⊗fj​:

V⊗KW:={ ∑i,jcij ei⊗fj }V \otimes_K W := \Bigl\{\, \sum_{i, j} c_{ij}\, e_i \otimes f_j \,\Bigr\}V⊗K​W:={i,j∑​cij​ei​⊗fj​}

together with the bilinear map ⊗:V×W→V⊗W\otimes : V \times W \to V \otimes W⊗:V×W→V⊗W, v⊗wv \otimes wv⊗w satisfying

(v+v′)⊗w=v⊗w+v′⊗w,v⊗(w+w′)=v⊗w+v⊗w′,(cv)⊗w=v⊗(cw)(v + v') \otimes w = v \otimes w + v' \otimes w, \qquad v \otimes (w + w') = v \otimes w + v \otimes w', \qquad (cv) \otimes w = v \otimes (cw)(v+v′)⊗w=v⊗w+v′⊗w,v⊗(w+w′)=v⊗w+v⊗w′,(cv)⊗w=v⊗(cw)

Then dim⁡(V⊗W)=(dim⁡V)(dim⁡W)\dim(V \otimes W) = (\dim V)(\dim W)dim(V⊗W)=(dimV)(dimW), and the construction does not depend on the choice of bases (see the universal property).

Notes

⭐ Elements fall into two classes: decomposable tensors v⊗wv \otimes wv⊗w and their finite sums.

Only the latter fill V⊗WV \otimes WV⊗W — “a general tensor is not a single v⊗wv \otimes wv⊗w” is the most common beginner's trap.

⚠ Can v⊗w=0v \otimes w = 0v⊗w=0 happen when v≠0,w≠0v \ne 0, w \ne 0v=0,w=0? No (the tensor product is a free construction, with basis ei⊗fje_i \otimes f_jei​⊗fj​).

This is a different question from “not decomposable into pure tensors”.

⭐ The tensor product is symmetric (V⊗W≅W⊗VV \otimes W \cong W \otimes VV⊗W≅W⊗V) and associative, with unit KKK (V⊗K≅VV \otimes K \cong VV⊗K≅V) — so finite-dimensional vector spaces with ⊗\otimes⊗ form a symmetric monoidal category.
