# Affine Connection　`def.affine-connection`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=def.affine-connection)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./def.affine-connection.md)

**Curvature is the commutator of the connection**: $R(X,Y) = [\nabla_{X},\nabla_{Y}]-\nabla_{[X,Y]}$ (`def.curvature-tensor`). Thus a single "connection" gives rise to parallel transport, geodesics, and curvature at once.

⚠️ **Index conventions**: when torsion-free, $\Gamma^{k}_{ij}$ is symmetric in its two lower indices; $\omega^{k}_{\ j}$ is the same object in an orthonormal frame (`def.connection-form`). The two differ by a Jacobi factor when changing frames, and neither **is a tensor**.
