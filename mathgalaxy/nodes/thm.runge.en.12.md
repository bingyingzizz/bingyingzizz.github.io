# Runge approximation theorem　`thm.runge`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=thm.runge)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./thm.runge.md)

⭐ **Runge's theorem, Mittag-Leffler's theorem, and the Weierstrass product theorem (`thm.weierstrass-product`) belong together**: Runge concerns approximation, Mittag-Leffler constructs functions with prescribed principal parts, and Weierstrass constructs functions with prescribed zeros. All three answer the same question: **What kinds of holomorphic or meromorphic functions can be constructed on a domain?**
