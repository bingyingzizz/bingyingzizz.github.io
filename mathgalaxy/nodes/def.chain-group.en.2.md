where $n(\sigma) = 0$ for all but finitely many $\sigma$ (this is what "finite formal sum" means).

Addition is pointwise ($n + m$ means $(n + m)(\sigma) = n(\sigma) + m(\sigma)$). Elements are written as finite formal sums

$$c = \sum_{\sigma \in K_{p}} n_{\sigma}\,\sigma,\qquad n_{\sigma} \in \mathbb{Z},$$

and $n_{\sigma}$ is called the **multiplicity** of $\sigma$ in $c$. By convention $C_{p}(K) := 0$ when $p < 0$ or $p > \dim K$. $C_{p}(K)$ is free abelian of rank equal to the number $\alpha_{p}$ of $p$-simplices of $K$.
