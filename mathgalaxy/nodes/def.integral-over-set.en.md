## Statement · **draft**
Let $(X, \mathcal{A}, \mu)$ be a measure space, $E \in \mathcal{A}$, and $f$ measurable. If $f \ge 0$, define

$\int_E f\,d\mu := \int f\,\mathbf{1}_E\,d\mu \in [0, \infty].$
