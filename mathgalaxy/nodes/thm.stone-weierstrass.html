设 KKK 紧 Hausdorff，A⊆C(K,R)A \subseteq C(K, \mathbb{R})A⊆C(K,R) 是含单位的子代数，且 AAA 分离点。则 A‾=C(K,R)\overline A = C(K, \mathbb{R})A=C(K,R)。

说明

证明三步：

① 取闭包：A‾\overline AA 仍含单位、分离点，并由「含单位的闭子代数是格」成为格。

② 两点插值：x≠yx \ne yx=y、目标值 α,β∈R\alpha, \beta \in \mathbb{R}α,β∈R。取 g∈Ag \in Ag∈A 使 g(x)≠g(y)g(x) \ne g(y)g(x)=g(y)，解二元一次方程组得 f=a⋅1+bg∈Af = a\cdot 1 + b g \in Af=a⋅1+bg∈A 使 f(x)=αf(x) = \alphaf(x)=α、f(y)=βf(y) = \betaf(y)=β。

③ 上下夹（全程在 A‾\overline{A}A 里做 —— 它已由①②验过是含单位的闭子代数，且对 max⁡,min⁡\max,\minmax,min 封闭）：

  • 两点同时命中：给定 x≠yx \ne yx=y 与 ε>0\varepsilon > 0ε>0。由②取 gxy∈A‾g_{xy} \in \overline{A}gxy​∈A 使 gxy(x)=f(x)g_{xy}(x) = f(x)gxy​(x)=f(x)、gxy(y)=f(y)g_{xy}(y) = f(y)gxy​(y)=f(y)。
  • 固定 xxx、对 yyy 取有限 max⁡\maxmax：对每个 yyy，由 gxy(y)=f(y)<f(y)+εg_{xy}(y) = f(y) < f(y) + \varepsilongxy​(y)=f(y)<f(y)+ε 与连续性，存在 yyy 的开邻域 VyV_{y}Vy​，在其上 gxy<f+εg_{xy} < f + \varepsilongxy​<f+ε。KKK 紧，取有限个 Vy1,…,VynV_{y_1}, \ldots, V_{y_n}Vy1​​,…,Vyn​​ 覆盖 KKK，令 hx:=max⁡jgxyj∈A‾h_{x} := \max_{j} g_{xy_j} \in \overline{A}hx​:=maxj​gxyj​​∈A。则 hx<f+εh_{x} < f + \varepsilonhx​<f+ε 处处成立，且 hx(x)=f(x)>f(x)−εh_{x}(x) = f(x) > f(x) - \varepsilonhx​(x)=f(x)>f(x)−ε。

∎

Statement draft

Let KKK be compact Hausdorff and A⊆C(K,R)A \subseteq C(K, \mathbb{R})A⊆C(K,R) a subalgebra containing the unit and separating points; write AAA for it. Then A‾=C(K,R)\overline A = C(K, \mathbb{R})A=C(K,R).

Notes draft

Three steps:

① Take the closure: A‾\overline AA still contains the unit, separates points, and is a lattice because a closed unital subalgebra is a lattice.

② Two-point interpolation: for x≠yx \ne yx=y and target values α,β∈R\alpha, \beta \in \mathbb{R}α,β∈R, take g∈Ag \in Ag∈A with g(x)≠g(y)g(x) \ne g(y)g(x)=g(y); solving the 2-by-2 linear system gives f=a⋅1+bg∈Af = a\cdot 1 + b g \in Af=a⋅1+bg∈A with f(x)=αf(x) = \alphaf(x)=α and f(y)=βf(y) = \betaf(y)=β.

③ Sandwich from above and below (all performed inside A‾\overline{A}A, which ① and ② have already certified to be a closed unital subalgebra, closed under max⁡,min⁡\max,\minmax,min):

  • Two points hit simultaneously: given x≠yx \ne yx=y and ε>0\varepsilon > 0ε>0. By ② take gxy∈A‾g_{xy} \in \overline{A}gxy​∈A with gxy(x)=f(x)g_{xy}(x) = f(x)gxy​(x)=f(x) and gxy(y)=f(y)g_{xy}(y) = f(y)gxy​(y)=f(y).
  • Fix xxx and, for each yyy, take a finite max⁡\maxmax: for each yyy, since gxy(y)=f(y)<f(y)+εg_{xy}(y) = f(y) < f(y) + \varepsilongxy​(y)=f(y)<f(y)+ε and by continuity, there is an open neighbourhood VyV_{y}Vy​ of yyy on which gxy<f+εg_{xy} < f + \varepsilongxy​<f+ε. Then hx<f+εh_{x} < f + \varepsilonhx​<f+ε holds everywhere, and hx(x)=f(x)>f(x)−εh_{x}(x) = f(x) > f(x) - \varepsilonhx​(x)=f(x)>f(x)−ε.
  • Cover over xxx and take a finite max⁡\maxmax: put Ux:={t∈K:hx(t)>f(t)−ε}U_{x} := \{t \in K : h_{x}(t) > f(t) - \varepsilon\}Ux​:={t∈K:hx​(t)>f(t)−ε}, which is open and contains xxx. Finitely many Ux1,…,UxmU_{x_1}, \ldots, U_{x_m}Ux1​​,…,Uxm​​ cover KKK; put k:=max⁡ihxi∈A‾k := \max_{i} h_{x_i} \in \overline{A}k:=maxi​hxi​​∈A. Lower bound: every point lies in some UxjU_{x_j}Uxj​​, so k≥hxj>f−εk \ge h_{x_j} > f - \varepsilonk≥hxj​​>f−ε. Together f−ε<k<f+εf - \varepsilon < k < f + \varepsilonf−ε<k<f+ε, that is, ∥k−f∥∞<ε\|k - f\|_\infty < \varepsilon∥k−f∥∞​<ε.

Since ε\varepsilonε is arbitrary, f∈A‾f \in \overline{A}f∈A, hence A‾=C(K)\overline{A} = C(K)A=C(K).

∎

⚠️ None of the three hypotheses can be dropped: A={f:f(0)=0}A = \{f : f(0) = 0\}A={f:f(0)=0} lacks the unit; A=A = A= the constant functions lacks separation of points.

⭐ This is the model of trading algebraic properties for approximation power: no smoothness, no countability, no special kernel is needed. The classical Weierstrass theorem (polynomial approximation) is the case K=[a,b]K = [a,b]K=[a,b] and A=A = A= the polynomials.
