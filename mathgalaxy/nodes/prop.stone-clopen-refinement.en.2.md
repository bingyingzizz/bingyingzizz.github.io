## Notes · **draft**
⭐ Use: it replaces an "open cover" by a "**finite disjoint clopen partition**", so one can **define piece by piece and glue piece by piece**. In §8.2 the statement "on a Stone space $C(S, M^{\mathrm{disc}})$ is dense in $C(S, M)$" uses exactly this: choose a constant on each piece and glue to a locally constant function.
