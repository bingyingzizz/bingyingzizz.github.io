## Statement · **draft**
**What this does**: it aligns two "boundaries" — the algebraic boundary $\partial c$ determined on a cube by the coordinate numbering, and the geometric boundary of a manifold with boundary determined by "outward normal first". They differ by exactly the signs $(-1)^{i+\alpha}$.
