# Operations on Characters　`prop.character-operations`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=prop.character-operations)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./prop.character-operations.md)

## Statement · **draft**
Let $V,W$ be finite-dimensional representations of a finite group $G$ with $\operatorname{char}F\nmid|G|$. Then:
(1) $\chi_{V\oplus W}=\chi_V+\chi_W$;
(2) $\chi_{V\otimes W}=\chi_V\chi_W$;
(3) $\chi_{V^*}(g)=\chi_V(g^{-1})$;
(4) if $\operatorname{char}F\ne2$, $\chi_{\operatorname{Sym}^2V}(g)=\frac12(\chi_V(g)^2+\chi_V(g^2))$;
(5) if $\operatorname{char}F\ne2$, $\chi_{\operatorname{Alt}^2V}(g)=\frac12(\chi_V(g)^2-\chi_V(g^2))$.
