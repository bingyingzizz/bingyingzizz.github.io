# Operations on Characters　`prop.character-operations`

## Statement · **draft**
Let $V,W$ be finite-dimensional representations of a finite group $G$ with $\operatorname{char}F\nmid|G|$ and $\operatorname{char}F\ne2$. Then: (1) $\chi_{V\oplus W}=\chi_V+\chi_W$; (2) $\chi_{V\otimes W}=\chi_V\chi_W$; (3) $\chi_{V^*}(g)=\chi_V(g^{-1})$; (4) $\chi_{\operatorname{Sym}^2V}(g)=\frac12(\chi_V(g)^2+\chi_V(g^2))$; (5) $\chi_{\operatorname{Alt}^2V}(g)=\frac12(\chi_V(g)^2-\chi_V(g^2))$. Parts (4) and (5) use the hypothesis that the characteristic is not two.
