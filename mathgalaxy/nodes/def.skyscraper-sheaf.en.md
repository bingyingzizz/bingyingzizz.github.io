## Statement · **draft**
Let $X$ be a topological space, $x \in X$, $A$ an abelian group, and $i_{x} : \{x\} \hookrightarrow X$ the inclusion. The **skyscraper sheaf** at $x$ with value $A$ is the pushforward sheaf

$$i_{x,*}A : \quad U \;\longmapsto\; \begin{cases} A, & x \in U, \\ 0, & x \notin U . \end{cases}$$
