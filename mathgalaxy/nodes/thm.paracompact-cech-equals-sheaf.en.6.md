# Čech Cohomology Equals Sheaf Cohomology on Paracompact Spaces　`thm.paracompact-cech-equals-sheaf`

[in graph](../#n=thm.paracompact-cech-equals-sheaf)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./thm.paracompact-cech-equals-sheaf.md)

**① The Čech complex of sheaves is a resolution.** For a cover $\mathfrak{U} = (U_{i})_{i}$, set $\check{\mathcal{C}}^{p}(\mathfrak{U}, \mathcal{M}) := \prod_{i_{0} < \cdots < i_{p}} j_{*}\bigl(\mathcal{M}|_{U_{i_{0} \cdots i_{p}}}\bigr)$, where $j : U_{i_{0} \cdots i_{p}} \hookrightarrow X$ is the inclusion.
