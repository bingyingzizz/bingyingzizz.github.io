# Adjoint Functors　`def.adjoint`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=def.adjoint)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./def.adjoint.md)

- the forgetful functor $\mathbf{Top} \to \mathbf{Set}$ has **two** adjoints: the left adjoint is the **discrete topology** ($\operatorname{Hom}_{\mathbf{Top}}(S_{d}, X) \cong \operatorname{Hom}_{\mathbf{Set}}(S, F(X))$), the right adjoint is the **trivial topology** ($\operatorname{Hom}_{\mathbf{Top}}(X, S_{t}) \cong \operatorname{Hom}_{\mathbf{Set}}(F(X), S)$).
- the left adjoint of the forgetful functor $\mathbf{Ab} \to \mathbf{Set}$ is the **free abelian group**: $\operatorname{Hom}_{\mathbf{Ab}}(\mathbb{Z}^{(X)}, M) \cong \operatorname{Hom}_{\mathbf{Set}}(X, \operatorname{forget} M)$.

**Free is left adjoint to forgetful**.
