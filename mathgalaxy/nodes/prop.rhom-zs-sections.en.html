Statement

Let SSS be a Stonean space and NNN a condensed abelian group. Then

RHom⁡Z(Z[S], N)  ≅  N(S)\operatorname{RHom}_{\mathbb{Z}}\bigl(\mathbb{Z}[S],\ N\bigr) \;\cong\; N(S)RHomZ​(Z[S], N)≅N(S)

(the right-hand side is understood as the complex concentrated in degree 000, i.e. all higher Ext vanish).

Notes

Proof. When SSS is Stonean, Z[S]\mathbb{Z}[S]Z[S] is a projective object: extremal disconnectedness gives "section-type" projectivity on clopen sets, and Z[S]\mathbb{Z}[S]Z[S] is the colimit of the Z[C]\mathbb{Z}[C]Z[C] over clopen CCC, with projectivity preserved under colimits ✓. Hence

Hom⁡Z(Z[S], −)=Γ(S,−)=(N↦N(S))\operatorname{Hom}_{\mathbb{Z}}\bigl(\mathbb{Z}[S],\ -\bigr) = \Gamma(S, -) = \bigl(N \mapsto N(S)\bigr)HomZ​(Z[S], −)=Γ(S,−)=(N↦N(S))

is an exact functor, its right derived functors are concentrated in degree 000, and R0=Γ(S,−)R^{0} = \Gamma(S,-)R0=Γ(S,−) ✓. Therefore RHom⁡(Z[S],N)\operatorname{RHom}(\mathbb{Z}[S], N)RHom(Z[S],N) equals Hom⁡(Z[S],N)=N(S)\operatorname{Hom}(\mathbb{Z}[S], N) = N(S)Hom(Z[S],N)=N(S) (a complex concentrated in degree 000) ✓. ∎

⭐ This is the same statement as "condensed abelian groups on a Stonean space are acyclic", in two equivalent forms: coefficient acyclicity   ⟺  \iff⟺ the test object Z[S]\mathbb{Z}[S]Z[S] is projective.
