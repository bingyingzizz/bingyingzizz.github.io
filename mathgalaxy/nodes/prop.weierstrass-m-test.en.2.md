## Notes · **draft**
⭐ It is the most useful criterion for uniform convergence: it reduces "a matter of functions" to "a matter of numbers" — one only needs a dominating series independent of $x$.

⚠ It gives a **sufficient** condition: a uniformly convergent series need not admit such a "uniform termwise bound" (examples of the type $\sum \frac{(-1)^{n}}{n}$).

⭐ Usage: a power series converges uniformly on $|x| \le r < R$ precisely by taking $M_n = |a_n| r^{n}$ and applying the root test to $\sum M_n$.

This is the entry point for all the properties of power series in the next section.
