# category of abelian groups　`def.category-of-abelian-groups`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=def.category-of-abelian-groups)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./def.category-of-abelian-groups.md)

**The tensor product** gives a symmetric monoidal structure with unit $\mathbb Z$; $\hom(A,B)$ is again an abelian group, so $\mathbf{Ab}$ is even symmetric monoidal closed.

Relation to $A\text{-}\mathbf{Mod}$: $\mathbb Z\text{-}\mathbf{Mod}\simeq\mathbf{Ab}$, the most basic example of "modules" unifying "abelian groups".
