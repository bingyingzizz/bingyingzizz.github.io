English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=thm.borsuk-ulam)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./thm.borsuk-ulam.md)

⚠ The dimension cannot be lowered: the analogue for $S^{1} \to \mathbb{R}^{1}$ holds (intermediate value theorem), but $S^{1} \to \mathbb{R}^{2}$ fails (the identity map has no antipodal pair with equal values).
