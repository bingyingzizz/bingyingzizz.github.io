Statement

The projective objects in CHaus\mathbf{CHaus}CHaus are exactly the Stonean spaces.

Notes

What it says: among compact Hausdorff spaces, those with the lifting property against surjections (projective objects) are exactly the Stonean (extremally disconnected) spaces.

⭐ Why it matters: in condensed mathematics, "Z[S]\mathbb{Z}[S]Z[S] is projective   ⟺  \iff⟺ SSS is Stonean" is a direct consequence — a topological property (extreme disconnectedness) is translated into a categorical one (projectivity).

⚠ Keep the two separation properties apart: Stonean (extremally disconnected: the closure of an open set is open) is strictly stronger than Stone (totally disconnected); they are different nodes here (全不连通与 Stone 空间 treats the latter).
