# Differential Form: Intrinsic Definition on Manifolds　`def.form-on-manifold`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=def.form-on-manifold)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./def.form-on-manifold.md)

**Conventions**: $\Omega^{0}(M) := C^{\infty}(M)$; $\Omega^{1}(M)$ consists of the 1-forms; and $\Omega^{k}(M) = 0$ for $k > n$ (there is no nonzero alternating $k$-linear form on an $n$-dimensional space). The direct sum $\Omega^{\bullet}(M) = \bigoplus_{k\ge 0}\Omega^{k}(M)$, with the wedge product, is a graded algebra.
