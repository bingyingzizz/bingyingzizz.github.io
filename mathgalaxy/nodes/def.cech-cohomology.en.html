Statement

Let M∙M^{\bullet}M∙ be an abelian presheaf complex on a site C\mathcal{C}C, and let X∈CX \in \mathcal{C}X∈C. Its nnn-th Čech cohomology group is

Hˇn(X,M∙):=Γ(X, Hˇn(M∙)).\check{H}^{n}(X, M^{\bullet}) := \Gamma\bigl(X,\ \check{H}^{n}(M^{\bullet})\bigr).Hˇn(X,M∙):=Γ(X, Hˇn(M∙)).

Notes

Difference from sheaf cohomology: sheaf cohomology requires taking an injective resolution first (expensive); Čech cohomology computes directly from a cover (cheap), at the price that it is in general only an approximation.

Why the name Čech: it is built from Čech's intuition of "computing global information from the overlaps of an open cover" — each layer of overlaps gives a group, inclusions of overlaps give the differential, and taking cohomology gives Čech cohomology.
