# the fundamental group of the circle　`thm.pi1-circle`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=thm.pi1-circle)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./thm.pi1-circle.md)

💡 This is the first **non-trivial** computation in all of algebraic topology. Its value: $\pi_{1}(S^{1}) \ne 1$ immediately gives "$S^{1}$ is not contractible", "the disc cannot retract onto its boundary", and the Brouwer fixed point theorem — one computation, a chain of geometric theorems.
