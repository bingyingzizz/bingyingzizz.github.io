# Explicit Formula for Čech Cochains　`prop.cech-cochain-formula`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=prop.cech-cochain-formula)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./prop.cech-cochain-formula.md)

$$\check{C}^{n}(\mathfrak{X}, M) \;=\; \prod_{i_{0}, \ldots, i_{n}} M\bigl(X_{i_{0}} \times_{X} \cdots \times_{X} X_{i_{n}}\bigr).$$
