## Statement · **draft**
Let $K$ be a **complete ordered field** (for example $\mathbb{R}$). Then:

**(i) Archimedean property**: the natural numbers are unbounded in $K$ —

$$\forall x \in K,\ \exists n \in \mathbb{N} : n > x$$

**(ii) $\mathbb{Q}$ is dense in $K$**: between any two elements there lies a rational —

$$\forall x, y \in K,\ x < y \implies \exists q \in \mathbb{Q} : x < q < y$$
