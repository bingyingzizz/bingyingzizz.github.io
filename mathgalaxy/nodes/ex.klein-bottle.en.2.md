## Notes · **draft**
⭐ **Two equivalent descriptions**: (1) Glue one pair of opposite sides of a square in the **same direction** (as in $T^{2}$) and the other pair in **opposite directions**. (2) Glue two Möbius bands along their boundaries. Both constructions give the same surface (the equivalence is stated here without proof).

⭐ **$\chi = 0$**: $2 - k = 2 - 2 = 0$. The **torus** has the same value, so the information $\chi = 0$ alone does not distinguish the Klein bottle. Orientability is also needed. The two surfaces are standard examples of the same $\chi$ with different topological types.
