 2: the system $\{\check{H}^{n}(\pi_{i}^{-1}(\mathfrak{X}), M)\}$ along $i$ is cofinal with $\{\check{H}^{n}(\mathfrak{X}, M)\}_{\mathfrak{X} \in \mathrm{Cov}(X_{i})}$, and Čech cohomology is the filtered colimit over covers, so $\check{H}^{n}(X, M) = \varinjlim_{i} \check{H}^{n}(X_{i}, M)$.
Finally use "Čech = sheaf cohomology on compact Hausdorff spaces" once at each end to obtain the asserted equality. ∎
