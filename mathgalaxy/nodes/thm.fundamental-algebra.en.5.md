# fundamental theorem of algebra　`thm.fundamental-algebra`

**Hypotheses used in each direction** (the equivalences between the forms are algebra; that complex numbers have roots is the core theorem):
- every nonconstant polynomial has a root $\iff$ the field is algebraically closed: free by unfolding the usual definition; here only univariate polynomials over a field are treated.
- a root $\implies$ factorization into linear factors: use the factor theorem and induction on degree; conversely, a factorization of a nonconstant polynomial immediately gives a root, with no analysis needed.
- all irreducible polynomials are linear $\iff$ complete splitting: use irreducible facto
