## Statement · **draft**
Let $M$ be a $G$-module. For $g \in G$, write $g - 1 : M \to M$ for the $R$-linear map $m \mapsto g \cdot m - m$. The **invariant submodule** of $M$ is

$$M^{G} := \bigcap_{g \in G} \ker\bigl(g - 1\bigr) \;\subseteq\; M .$$

$M \mapsto M^{G}$, $f \mapsto f|_{M^{G}}$ constitute the **invariant functor**
