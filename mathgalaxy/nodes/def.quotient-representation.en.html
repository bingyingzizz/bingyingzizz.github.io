Statement

If W⊆VW\subseteq VW⊆V is a subrepresentation, the quotient space V/WV/WV/W carries a well-defined action a(v+W)=av+Wa(v+W)=av+Wa(v+W)=av+W, and the quotient map V→V/WV\to V/WV→V/W is AAA-linear.

Notes

Well-definedness uses θ(a)W⊆W\theta(a)W\subseteq Wθ(a)W⊆W. The dimension of the quotient is dim⁡V−dim⁡W\dim V-\dim WdimV−dimW. For group representations the action is ρ(g)(v+W)=ρ(g)v+W\rho(g)(v+W)=\rho(g)v+Wρ(g)(v+W)=ρ(g)v+W. Quotients of semisimple representations are again semisimple; see the proposition on subrepresentations and quotients.
