# finitely presented object　`def.finitely-presented`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=def.finitely-presented)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./def.finitely-presented.md)

⭐ **Use in homological algebra.** $X$ is finitely presented $\iff$ $h^{X}$ preserves filtered colimits $\iff$ $X$ is a “small” test object. This is the idea behind the statement in condensed mathematics that “CondAb is generated by finitely presented projective objects” — finitely presented projective objects are generators of $\mathrm{CondAb}$.

⚠️ “Finitely presented” differs from “finitely generated.” Finite generation requires a surjection $A^{n} \twoheadrightarrow M$. Finite presentation also requires its kernel to be finitely generated (that is, $A^{m} \to A^{n} \to M \to 0$ is exact).
