## Notes · **draft**
⭐ This makes $\operatorname{Mod}^{\mathrm{cond}}_{\mathcal{A}}$ functorial in $\mathcal{A}$: changing the analytic ring is base change.

⭐ The proof is short: by the definition of the map, $\mathcal{B}[S]$ already lies in $\operatorname{Mod}^{\mathrm{cond}}_{\mathcal{A}}$, and these are compact projective generators, so all of $\operatorname{Mod}^{\mathrm{cond}}_{\mathcal{B}}$ lands inside.
