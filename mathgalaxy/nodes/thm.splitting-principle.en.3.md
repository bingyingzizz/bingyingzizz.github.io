## Notes · **draft**
**Why this is not "cheating"**: injectivity of $\pi^{*}$ means "a polynomial identity holding on $\tilde B$ can be pushed back to $B$". So the rigorous logic is: **first prove the identity on the flag bundle, then push back using injectivity**. ⚠️ The key word is **injective**, not "a splitting exists" — a splitting without injectivity cannot be pushed back.
