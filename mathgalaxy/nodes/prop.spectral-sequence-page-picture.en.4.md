# pages of a spectral sequence in the (p,q) plane　`prop.spectral-sequence-page-picture`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=prop.spectral-sequence-page-picture)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./prop.spectral-sequence-page-picture.md)

⭐ **Use.** In a **first-quadrant** spectral sequence, the diagram lets you see at a glance which terms survive to $E_{\infty}$. For example, in the first quadrant $E^{0,q}$ has **no** incoming differential because the source $(-r, q+r-1)$ is always zero. It can still be killed by a nonzero **outgoing** differential.
