⚠️ **Boundedness plays no role here**: each $f(\xi_i)$ is a real number and a finite sum is always defined. Boundedness enters only when discussing Riemann **integrability** — that is where the Darboux upper and lower sums require $\sup/\inf$ to be finite.

## Notes · **draft**
⭐ Geometric reading: take the "height" on each small subinterval to be $f(\xi_i)$, obtaining $n$ rectangles; $S$ is their total area. **The choice of tags** is the only freedom in this theory — requiring the integrability condition to hold for every choice is exactly what removes that freedom.
