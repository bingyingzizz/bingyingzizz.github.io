# Differential of a Smooth Map　`def.differential-map`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=def.differential-map)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./def.differential-map.md)

$$\mathrm{d}F_{p} : T_{p}N \longrightarrow T_{F(p)}M,\qquad \mathrm{d}F_{p}([\gamma]) := [F \circ \gamma],$$

i.e. the class of a curve through $p$, say $\gamma$, goes to the class through $F(p)$ of $F \circ \gamma$. $\mathrm{d}F_{p}$ is the **tangent map** (also **differential**) of $F$ at $p$.
