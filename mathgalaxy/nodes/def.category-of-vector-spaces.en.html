Statement

The category of vector spaces VectK\mathbf{Vect}_KVectK​: its objects are the vector spaces over a field KKK, its morphisms are the KKK-linear maps, composition is composition of maps, and the identity is the identity map.

The finite-dimensional vector spaces form the full subcategory VectKfd\mathbf{Vect}_K^{\mathrm{fd}}VectKfd​: the objects are only the finite-dimensional spaces, while the morphisms are still all linear maps.

VectK\mathbf{Vect}_KVectK​ is an additive category: Hom⁡K(V,W)\operatorname{Hom}_K(V,W)HomK​(V,W) becomes a KKK-vector space under pointwise addition and pointwise scalar multiplication, composition is linear in each variable, and finite direct sums are simultaneously products and coproducts.

Notes

Why it is listed separately: the pair "objects + linear maps" of linear algebra is, in categorical language, VectK\mathbf{Vect}_KVectK​; once it is available, "duality" and "Hom" can be written as functors.

Forgetting structure: the forgetful functor VectK→Set\mathbf{Vect}_K\to\mathbf{Set}VectK​→Set forgets addition and scalar multiplication, retaining only the underlying sets and functions; it is faithful but not full (not every function is linear).

Relation to additive categories: each Hom-set is itself a vector space and composition is bilinear; finite direct sums coincide with finite products, so it is an additive category, hence a preabelian category (kernels and cokernels are taken pointwise).
