## Statement · **draft**
Let $a < b$, and suppose $f$ is continuous on $[a, b]$ and differentiable on $(a, b)$. Then there exists $\xi \in (a, b)$ such that

$$f(b) - f(a) = f'(\xi)\,(b - a)$$

Equivalently, $f'(\xi) = \dfrac{f(b) - f(a)}{b - a}$: **the average rate of change is attained at some point as an instantaneous rate**.
