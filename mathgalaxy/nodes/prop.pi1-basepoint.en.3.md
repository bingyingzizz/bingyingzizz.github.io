## Notes · **draft**
⭐ **Conclusion**: when $X$ is path-connected, $\pi_{1}(X, x_{0}) \cong \pi_{1}(X, x_{1})$, but this isomorphism is not canonical. Hence "$\pi_{1}(X) = 1$" (simply connected) is a **well-defined** statement, while "$\pi_{1}(X) \cong \mathbb{Z}$, and which element is the generator" is not.
