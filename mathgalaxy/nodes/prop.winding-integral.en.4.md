# integral formula for the winding number　`prop.winding-integral`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=prop.winding-integral)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./prop.winding-integral.md)

⭐ **Meaning of the formula**: the winding number was originally the geometric phrase "how many times the curve turns around the point"; here it becomes an **integral**, so a "geometric quantity" can enter computations — the argument principle uses it to count zeros.

⚠️ The value of the integral is independent of the parametrisation of $\gamma$ and depends only on orientation: going around the other way changes the sign of the winding number.
