In particular, taking $I = \emptyset$ gives $X(\emptyset) = \{\ast\}$.
- **Surjection**: the cover is a single surjection $S' \twoheadrightarrow S$. The sheaf condition is exactly that $X(S) \to X(S') \rightrightarrows X(S' \times_{S} S')$ is left exact, i.e. (4.2).
Conversely, the two conditions together evidently imply "is a sheaf for all covers" (a cover decomposes into a composite of these two kinds), so the criterion holds. ∎
