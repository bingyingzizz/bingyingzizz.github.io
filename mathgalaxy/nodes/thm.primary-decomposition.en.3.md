# primary decomposition into generalised eigenspaces　`thm.primary-decomposition`

[in graph](../#n=thm.primary-decomposition)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./thm.primary-decomposition.md)

The second half splits, within each block, the "nilpotency degree of $T - \lambda_i I$" into small blocks (those are the Jordan blocks).

⭐ Its algebraic version: $V$ is a $K[x]$-module, and $\chi_T$ is the "annihilating polynomial" on it;

by the unique factorization in $K[x]$, the module splits along the irreducible factors of $\chi_T$ (**Chinese remainder theorem**) — this is where the name "primary decomposition" comes from.
