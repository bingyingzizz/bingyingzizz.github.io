Statement

In plain terms: the frame itself also rotates — after a small step in some direction, (e1,e2,e3)(e_{1},e_{2},e_{3})(e1​,e2​,e3​) turns slightly. The connection form records this "rotation" in nine 111-forms: feed it a direction and it tells you about which axis the frame turns and how fast. It is the same thing as the Christoffel symbols, only recorded in an orthonormal frame.

Expand dei\mathrm{d}e_{i}dei​ in the frame, writing the coefficients as ω ij\omega^{j}_{\ i}ω ij​:

dei=∑j=13ω ij ej,ω ij=⟨dei,ej⟩.\mathrm{d}e_{i} = \sum_{j=1}^{3}\omega^{j}_{\ i}\,e_{j},\qquad \omega^{j}_{\ i} = \langle \mathrm{d}e_{i}, e_{j}\rangle .dei​=j=1∑3​ω ij​ej​,ω ij​=⟨dei​,ej​⟩.

These nine 111-forms are called connection forms (Γ\GammaΓ-forms). The matrix (ω ji)(\omega^{i}_{\ j})(ω ji​) is skew-symmetric (see 联络形式反对称), so only three are independent: ω 21,ω 31,ω 32\omega^{1}_{\ 2}, \omega^{1}_{\ 3}, \omega^{2}_{\ 3}ω 21​,ω 31​,ω 32​.

After taking e3=ne_{3} = ne3​=n, they split into two classes:

  • ω 21\omega^{1}_{\ 2}ω 21​: the intrinsic part — the Levi-Civita connection of the surface, from which the Gauss curvature is computed;
  • ω 31,ω 32\omega^{1}_{\ 3}, \omega^{2}_{\ 3}ω 31​,ω 32​: the extrinsic part — the second fundamental form is computed from them.

Notes

Origin of the name: ω ij\omega^{j}_{\ i}ω ij​ describes "how the frame rotates when one walks in a direction". It is not an artificially invented exterior form: at each point it is a 3×33\times33×3 skew-symmetric matrix over R3\mathbb{R}^{3}R3 (333 degrees of freedom), and evaluating it along directions gives a 111-form.

⭐ This split is surface theory: in R3\mathbb{R}^{3}R3 the curvature form vanishes identically; restricted to a surface, the ω 21\omega^{1}_{\ 2}ω 21​ part is determined by the intrinsic metric (Gauss equation), while the remaining extrinsic part gives IIIIII and the Codazzi equations. The whole of surface theory consists in reading the coefficients of these two classes of forms.

⚠ ω ji\omega^{i}_{\ j}ω ji​ and ω ij\omega^{j}_{\ i}ω ij​ differ by a sign; this is the easiest place in the whole map to make a copying error. There is only one criterion: differentiate ⟨ei,ej⟩=δij\langle e_{i}, e_{j}\rangle = \delta_{ij}⟨ei​,ej​⟩=δij​ and do not rely on memory.

Relation to the Christoffel symbols: expanding it in the non-orthonormal frame {ru,rv,n}\{r_{u}, r_{v}, n\}{ru​,rv​,n}, the coefficients are a mixture of Γ ijk\Gamma^{k}_{\ ij}Γ ijk​ and hijh_{ij}hij​ (compare 第二基本形式 and 克里斯托费尔记号);

only in an orthonormal frame does it become a clean skew-symmetric matrix.
