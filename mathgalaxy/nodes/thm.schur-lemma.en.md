## Statement · **draft**
Let $V_1,V_2$ be irreducible representations of $A$. Every nonzero $A$-linear map $h:V_1\to V_2$ is an isomorphism; in particular, $\operatorname{End}_A(V)$ is a division algebra. See cor.schur-scalar for the scalar conclusion over an algebraically closed $F$.
