# sphere　`ex.sphere`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=ex.sphere)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./ex.sphere.md)

⭐ **A cover by two charts**: Stereographic projection gives two charts, one omitting the north pole and the other the south pole. The transition map is of the type $x \mapsto x/\lVert x\rVert^{2}$, which shows directly that $S^{2}$ is a smooth two-dimensional topological manifold.

⚠ **$S^{2}$ and $\mathbb{R}^{2}$ are not homeomorphic** because only one is compact, although deleting a point gives a homeomorphism. This is one reason compactness is essential in the definition of a closed surface.

📌 $H_{0} = \mathbb{Z}$, $H_{1} = 0$, and $H_{2} = \mathbb{Z}$, so $\chi = 1 - 0 + 1 = 2$, in agreement with $2 - 2g$.
