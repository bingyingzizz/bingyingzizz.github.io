Notes

⭐ Its theoretical meaning exceeds its computational meaning: it gives an explicit expression for the solution in terms of bbb, so one immediately sees that "the solution depends continuously on bbb" (when det⁡A≠0\det A \ne 0detA=0, A−1A^{-1}A−1 exists, x=A−1bx = A^{-1}bx=A−1b).

⚠ Computationally it is a bad deal: computing nnn determinants of order nnn is far more expensive than elimination (unless nnn is very small).

Modern numerical linear algebra never solves systems this way.

⭐ It is the algorithmic embodiment of "det⁡A≠0  ⟺  \det A \ne 0 \iffdetA=0⟺ is invertible": nonzero denominator   ⟺  \iff⟺ unique solution   ⟺  \iff⟺ invertible.
