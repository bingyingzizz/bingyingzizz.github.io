# Spaces Built from Compact Hausdorff Blocks along Closed Inclusions　`prop.closed-inclusion-filtered-limit`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=prop.closed-inclusion-filtered-limit)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./prop.closed-inclusion-filtered-limit.md)

**Why the pieces must be compact Hausdorff.** Compactness is the geometric incarnation of the finiteness condition "finite subcover"; in condensed mathematics, compact Hausdorff spaces are exactly the "probes". Writing a large object as a filtered colimit of compact pieces amounts to writing it as a limit of "larger and larger finite information" — every computation can stop at some piece.
