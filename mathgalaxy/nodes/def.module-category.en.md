## Statement · **draft**
Let $A$ be a ring with unity. The **category of left $A$-modules** $A\text{-}\mathbf{Mod}$ has as objects the left $A$-modules and as morphisms the $A$-linear maps, with composition and identities defined pointwise.

Right $A$-modules form $\mathbf{Mod}\text{-}A$; when $A$ is commutative the two categories are equivalent.
