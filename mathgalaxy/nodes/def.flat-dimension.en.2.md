## Notes · **draft**
**Basic relations**: $\operatorname{fd}M=0$ if and only if $M$ is flat (see `def.flat-module`). Since projective modules are flat, one always has $\operatorname{fd}M\le\operatorname{pd}M$ (`def.projective-dimension`).

**Duality with injective dimension**: $\operatorname{fd}_{R}M=\operatorname{id}_{R^{\mathrm{op}}}\operatorname{Hom}_{\mathbb{Z}}(M,\mathbb{Q}/\mathbb{Z})$, so taking the character dual converts flat dimension into the corresponding injective dimension.
