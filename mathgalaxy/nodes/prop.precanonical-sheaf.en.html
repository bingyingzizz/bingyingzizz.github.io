Statement

Notes

These two write "sheaf" as two identities that are obvious in Set\mathbf{Set}Set and need checking here: finite coproducts become finite products, and quotients become kernels. They are the precise statement that "coproducts and quotients in a pretopos look like those in sets".

Corollary: the precanonical topology is subcanonical — the representable presheaf hXh_{X}hX​ satisfies the two conditions above (Hom⁡(X,−)\operatorname{Hom}(X, -)Hom(X,−) preserves products, and Hom⁡(X/R,−)\operatorname{Hom}(X/R, -)Hom(X/R,−) gives the kernel from the coequalizer), so they are all sheaves.
