## Statement · **draft**
Let $a \ge 0$, $b \ge 0$, $0 < \lambda < 1$. Then

$$a^{\lambda} b^{1-\lambda} \le \lambda a + (1-\lambda) b$$

## Notes · **draft**
This is the concavity of $\log$ (equivalently, the convexity of the exponential function):

$$\log\!\left( \lambda a + (1-\lambda) b \right) \ge \lambda \log a + (1-\lambda) \log b$$

Exponentiating both sides gives the claim.
