## Notes · **draft**
In $\mathbf{Set}$ **every surjection is regular**: take $W = X \times_{Y} X$ with the two projections; their coequalizer is exactly $f$. In a general category this fails — "epi" (right cancellable) is much weaker than "regular epi", and the gap is precisely whether $Y$ is really a quotient of $X$.
