# Stokes' Theorem　`thm.stokes`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=thm.stokes)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./thm.stokes.md)

**List of special cases**: $n = 1$ is the fundamental theorem of calculus; $n = 2$ is Green's formula; the two faces for $n = 3$ are Gauss–Ostrogradsky (flux) and Kelvin–Stokes (circulation). They are not four theorems but one.

**Relation to Gauss–Bonnet**: on a surface $K\,\mathrm{d}A = \mathrm{d}\omega^{1}_{\ 2}$ (Gauss's equation, `thm.gauss-equation`), so the global Gauss–Bonnet formula is Stokes plus the boundary term $\int k_{g}\,\mathrm{d}s$ — the two theorems share the same root at this level.
