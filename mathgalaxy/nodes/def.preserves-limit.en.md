## Statement · **draft**
Let $D : I \to \mathcal{C}$ be a diagram and $F : \mathcal{C} \to \mathcal{D}$ a functor. $F$ induces a comparison map between cones

$$F(\lim D) \longrightarrow \lim (F \circ D).$$

If this comparison map is an isomorphism for **every** diagram $D$ (of the relevant type), one says that $F$ **preserves limits** (written $\lim D \cong \lim F \circ D$). Dually: **preserves colimits**.
