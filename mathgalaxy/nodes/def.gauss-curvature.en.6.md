English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=def.gauss-curvature)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./def.gauss-curvature.md)

📌 Examples: the sphere of radius $R$ has $K = 1/R^{2}$ (see `ex.sphere-curvature`); the cylinder has $K = 0$ ($k_{1} = 1/R$, $k_{2} = 0$, see `ex.cylinder-first-form`); the plane has $K = 0$. The three examples cover the three signs of $K$.
