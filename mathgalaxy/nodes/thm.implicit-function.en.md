## Statement · **draft**
Let $U \subseteq \mathbb{R}^{n} \times \mathbb{R}^{m}$ be open, $F : U \to \mathbb{R}^{m}$ of class $C^{1}$, $(a, b) \in U$ with $F(a, b) = 0$, and $D_y F(a, b)$ **invertible**:

$$F(a, b) = 0, \qquad D_y F(a, b) \in M_m(\mathbb{R})\ \text{invertible}$$
