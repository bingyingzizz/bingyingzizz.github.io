## Notes · **draft**
**In one sentence**: a Stonean space is **invisible** to condensed cohomology — as long as the coefficients are a condensed abelian group, all higher cohomology vanishes.

**Why.**

The section functor $\Gamma(S, -) = \operatorname{Hom}(\mathbb{Z}[S], -)$ is **exact** on a Stonean space: extremal disconnectedness makes $\mathbb{Z}[S]$ projective, so $\operatorname{Hom}(\mathbb{Z}[S], -)$ preserves epimorphisms, and the right derived functor measures exactly "whether epimorphisms are preserved" at $n>0$.
