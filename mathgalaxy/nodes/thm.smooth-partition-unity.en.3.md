# Smooth Partition of Unity　`thm.smooth-partition-unity`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=thm.smooth-partition-unity)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./thm.smooth-partition-unity.md)

We call $\{\rho_{\alpha}\}$ a **smooth partition of unity subordinate to $\mathcal{U}$**. In particular, every open cover of $M$ has a subordinate smooth partition of unity.

The three requirements are exactly those of `def.partition-of-unity`, with smoothness in place of continuity.
