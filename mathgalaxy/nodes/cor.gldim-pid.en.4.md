# Global Dimension of a Principal Ideal Domain　`cor.gldim-pid`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=cor.gldim-pid)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./cor.gldim-pid.md)

If this module were projective, the short exact sequence $0 \to (a) \to R \to R/(a) \to 0$ would split by projectivity of $R/(a)$. Thus $(a)$ would be a direct summand of $R$ cut out by an idempotent.

The only idempotents in an integral domain are $0, 1$. Since $(a) \ne 0$ and $(a) \ne R$, this is a contradiction ✓.

Hence $\operatorname{pd}_{R} M = 1$, with the upper bound supplied by ①, and therefore $\operatorname{gl.dim} R = 1$ ✓.

∎
