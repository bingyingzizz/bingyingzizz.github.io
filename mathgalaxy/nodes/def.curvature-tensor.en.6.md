# Riemann Curvature Tensor　`def.curvature-tensor`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=def.curvature-tensor)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./def.curvature-tensor.md)

**Why it is intrinsic**: the formula for $R$ contains only $\Gamma$ and $\partial\Gamma$, and $\Gamma$ contains only $g$ and $\partial g$. **The second fundamental form does not appear.**

⚠ In dimension two there is only one independent component ($R_{1212}$, the rest being determined by symmetry), so "curvature" on a surface is one number rather than a pile of numbers; only in dimension three and higher does $R$ acquire genuine tensor structure (and then $K$ gives only part of its trace).
