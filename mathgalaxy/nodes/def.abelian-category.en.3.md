# abelian category　`def.abelian-category`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=def.abelian-category)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./def.abelian-category.md)

ian categories are the working environment of homological algebra — exactness needs kernels and cokernels. $A\text{-}\mathbf{Mod}$ and $\mathbf{Ab}(\mathcal{T})$ for a topos are abelian; $\mathbf{AbTop}$ is not.
