cked. Abelian categories are the setting for exact sequences and homological algebra; kernels and cokernels alone do not suffice, since $\mathbf{AbTop}$ is preabelian but not abelian, whereas $A\text{-}\mathbf{Mod}$ and $\mathbf{Ab}(\mathcal{T})$ for a topos are abelian.
