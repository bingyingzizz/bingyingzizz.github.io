## Notes · **draft**
**Proof.** $\mathbf{Set}$: small products and equalizers give all small limits ✓, disjoint unions and coequalizers give all small colimits ✓. $\mathbf{Top}$: the same constructions with the corresponding topologies (product topology, subspace/equalizer topology, disjoint-union topology, quotient topology) ✓. $\mathbf{Ab}$: build them using free abelian groups and quotient groups ✓.
