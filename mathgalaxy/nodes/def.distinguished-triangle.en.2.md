# Distinguished Triangle　`def.distinguished-triangle`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=def.distinguished-triangle)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./def.distinguished-triangle.md)

A **morphism** of triangles is a family of maps $(u, v, w, u[1])$ making all squares commute.

## Notes · **draft**
A distinguished triangle is the **homotopy version** of a "short exact sequence": it does not require $M$ to be a quotient, only that $K \to L \to M \to K[1]$ fits together in shape. After adding the shift, a single morphism can be embedded in a sequence of arbitrary length — precisely the form a triangulated category wants.
