# AB6: Filtered Colimits Commute with Products　`prop.ab6`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=prop.ab6)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./prop.ab6.md)

$$\varinjlim_{(i_{j})_{j} \in \prod_{j} I_{j}} \ \prod_{j \in J} M_{j}(i_{j}) \longrightarrow \prod_{j \in J} \varinjlim_{i_{j} \in I_{j}} M_{j}(i_{j})$$

is an isomorphism.
