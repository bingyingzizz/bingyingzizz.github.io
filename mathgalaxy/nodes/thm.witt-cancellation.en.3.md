## Notes · **draft**
**Why the hypothesis “nondegenerate”**: in the degenerate case cancellation fails (when $\beta = 0$ the left side is always $0$). This is why the whole theory of quadratic forms assumes nondegeneracy first — not pedantry, but the lifeline of cancellation.

⭐ **It is the last brick of “classification”**: Sylvester's law of inertia gives “the number of signs in a diagonal form is unique” over the reals; Witt cancellation gives “the number of blocks in a decomposition is unique over **any** field”. The former works only over $\mathbb{R}$, the latter over any field with char $\ne$ 2.
