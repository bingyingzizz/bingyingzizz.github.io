Statement

A short exact sequence

0⟶M1→  i1  M→  i2  M2⟶00 \longrightarrow M_{1} \xrightarrow{\;i_{1}\;} M \xrightarrow{\;i_{2}\;} M_{2} \longrightarrow 00⟶M1​i1​​Mi2​​M2​⟶0

is called split if it is isomorphic to

0⟶M1→  pr1  M1⊕M2→  pr2  M2⟶00 \longrightarrow M_{1} \xrightarrow{\;\mathrm{pr}_{1}\;} M_{1} \oplus M_{2} \xrightarrow{\;\mathrm{pr}_{2}\;} M_{2} \longrightarrow 00⟶M1​pr1​​M1​⊕M2​pr2​​M2​⟶0

(that is, i1i_{1}i1​ is the inclusion into the first summand and i2i_{2}i2​ is the projection onto the second summand).

Notes

Equivalent criteria (in an abelian category, the three are equivalent):

  • i2i_{2}i2​ has a section s:M2→Ms : M_{2} \to Ms:M2​→M (i2∘s=idi_{2} \circ s = \mathrm{id}i2​∘s=id).
  • i1i_{1}i1​ has a retraction r:M→M1r : M \to M_{1}r:M→M1​ (r∘i1=idr \circ i_{1} = \mathrm{id}r∘i1​=id).
  • M≅M1⊕M2M \cong M_{1} \oplus M_{2}M≅M1​⊕M2​ is compatible with the sequence.

Proof. Write i:=i1i := i_{1}i:=i1​ and p:=i2p := i_{2}p:=i2​. Then iii is monic, ppp is epic, and im⁡i=ker⁡p\operatorname{im} i = \ker pimi=kerp.

  • Has a section   ⟹  \implies⟹ splits: Suppose ps=1M2p s = 1_{M_{2}}ps=1M2​​. Then p(1M−sp)=p−psp=0p(1_{M} - s p) = p - p s p = 0p(1M​−sp)=p−psp=0, so 1M−sp1_{M} - sp1M​−sp lies in ker⁡p=im⁡i\ker p = \operatorname{im} ikerp=imi. Thus there exists r:M→M1r : M \to M_{1}r:M→M1​ such that
ir=1M−sp.i r = 1_{M} - s p.ir=1M​−sp.

Since iii is monic, i(ri)=(1M−sp) i=i−s(pi)=ii(r i) = (1_{M} - sp)\,i = i - s(p i) = ii(ri)=(1M​−sp)i=i−s(pi)=i (using pi=0p i = 0pi=0), so ri=1M1r i = 1_{M_{1}}ri=1M1​​ ✓.

Together with pi=0p i = 0pi=0, the maps p1:=rp_{1} := rp1​:=r, i1:=ii_{1} := ii1​:=i, p2:=pp_{2} := pp2​:=p, and i2:=si_{2} := si2​:=s satisfy the three biproduct identities (ri=1r i = 1ri=1, ps=1p s = 1ps=1, ir+sp=1Mi r + s p = 1_{M}ir+sp=1M​, see ‘The direct sum is a product’). Thus MMM is precisely M1⊕M2M_{1} \oplus M_{2}M1​⊕M2​ and the sequence splits ✓.

  • Splits   ⟹  \implies⟹ has a section: If the sequence splits, it is isomorphic to 0→M1→ pr1 M1⊕M2→ pr2 M2→00 \to M_{1} \xrightarrow{\ \mathrm{pr}_{1}\ } M_{1} \oplus M_{2} \xrightarrow{\ \mathrm{pr}_{2}\ } M_{2} \to 00→M1​ pr1​ ​M1​⊕M2​ pr2​ ​M2​→0, and pr2\mathrm{pr}_{2}pr2​ has an evident section (the inclusion into the second summand). Transport it along the isomorphism to obtain a section of ppp ✓.
  • Duality: Apply the same argument in Cop\mathcal{C}^{\mathrm{op}}Cop to obtain ‘splits   ⟺  \iff⟺ iii has a retraction’ ✓. ∎

⭐ This is the splitting lemma: In an additive category, splitting of a short exact sequence, existence of a section, and a compatible direct-sum decomposition are equivalent. It reduces questions about sequences to questions about direct sums, a standard simplification in homological algebra.

⚠️ Short exact sequences need not split (0→Z→2Z→Z/2→00 \to \mathbb{Z} \xrightarrow{2} \mathbb{Z} \to \mathbb{Z}/2 \to 00→Z2​Z→Z/2→0 is one that does not). Splitting is a special case, and Ext⁡1\operatorname{Ext}^{1}Ext1 addresses whether a given sequence splits.
