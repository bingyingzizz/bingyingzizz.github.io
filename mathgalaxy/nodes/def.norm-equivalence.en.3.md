# equivalent norms　`def.norm-equivalence`

[in graph](../#n=def.norm-equivalence)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./def.norm-equivalence.md)

⚠️ Equivalence preserves completeness and very little else: two equivalent norms give the same completeness (the same Cauchy sequences), but **extremal problems** can have different answers (the balls differ).
