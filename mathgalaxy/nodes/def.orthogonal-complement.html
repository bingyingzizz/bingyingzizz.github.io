又称正交补

陈述

设 U⊆VU \subseteq VU⊆V 是子空间。UUU 的正交补定义为

U⊥:={ v∈V:⟨u,v⟩=0 for all u∈U }U^{\perp} := \{\, v \in V : \langle u, v \rangle = 0 \ \text{for all}\ u \in U \,\}U⊥:={v∈V:⟨u,v⟩=0 for all u∈U}

它是 VVV 的子空间，并且

  • U∩U⊥={0}U \cap U^{\perp} = \{0\}U∩U⊥={0}；
  • U⊆(U⊥)⊥U \subseteq (U^{\perp})^{\perp}U⊆(U⊥)⊥；
  • 有限维时 V=U⊕U⊥V = U \oplus U^{\perp}V=U⊕U⊥，且 (U⊥)⊥=U(U^{\perp})^{\perp} = U(U⊥)⊥=U、dim⁡U⊥=dim⁡V−dim⁡U\dim U^{\perp} = \dim V - \dim UdimU⊥=dimV−dimU。

说明

⭐ «V=U⊕U⊥V = U \oplus U^{\perp}V=U⊕U⊥» 是内积空间比一般向量空间多出来的东西：一般的子空间有补空间但不唯一（见 直和与补空间），而正交补是唯一的补空间。

⭐ 它与对偶里的零化子是一对：U⊥⊆VU^{\perp} \subseteq VU⊥⊆V、U0⊆V∗U^{0} \subseteq V^{\ast}U0⊆V∗，两者的维数公式长得一样（dim⁡V−dim⁡U\dim V - \dim UdimV−dimU），在「V≅V∗V \cong V^{\ast}V≅V∗（靠内积）」之下互相翻译。

⚠ V=U⊕U⊥V = U \oplus U^{\perp}V=U⊕U⊥ 在无限维时对闭子空间才成立，任意子空间不成立（这是 Hilbert 空间理论的一个真定理）。

Statement draft

Let U⊆VU \subseteq VU⊆V be a subspace. The orthogonal complement of UUU is

U⊥:={ v∈V:⟨u,v⟩=0 for all u∈U }U^{\perp} := \{\, v \in V : \langle u, v \rangle = 0 \ \text{for all}\ u \in U \,\}U⊥:={v∈V:⟨u,v⟩=0 for all u∈U}

It is a subspace of VVV, and

  • U∩U⊥={0}U \cap U^{\perp} = \{0\}U∩U⊥={0};
  • U⊆(U⊥)⊥U \subseteq (U^{\perp})^{\perp}U⊆(U⊥)⊥;
  • in finite dimension, V=U⊕U⊥V = U \oplus U^{\perp}V=U⊕U⊥, and moreover (U⊥)⊥=U(U^{\perp})^{\perp} = U(U⊥)⊥=U and dim⁡U⊥=dim⁡V−dim⁡U\dim U^{\perp} = \dim V - \dim UdimU⊥=dimV−dimU.

Notes draft

⭐ «V=U⊕U⊥V = U \oplus U^{\perp}V=U⊕U⊥» is what an inner product space has beyond a general vector space: a general subspace has complements, but they are not unique (直和与补空间), whereas the orthogonal complement is the unique complement.

⭐ It is paired with the annihilator in duality: U⊥⊆VU^{\perp} \subseteq VU⊥⊆V, U0⊆V∗U^{0} \subseteq V^{\ast}U0⊆V∗, and their dimension formulas look the same (dim⁡V−dim⁡U\dim V - \dim UdimV−dimU); under "V≅V∗V \cong V^{\ast}V≅V∗" (via the inner product) they translate into each other.

⚠ V=U⊕U⊥V = U \oplus U^{\perp}V=U⊕U⊥ holds in infinite dimension only for closed subspaces, not for arbitrary subspaces (this is a genuine theorem of Hilbert space theory).
