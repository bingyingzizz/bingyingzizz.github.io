# Flatness and the Vanishing of Tor　`prop.tor-flat-vanishing`

[in graph](../#n=prop.tor-flat-vanishing)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./prop.tor-flat-vanishing.md)

ications hold over any unital ring, with no commutativity, Noetherian, or PID hypothesis. The pairing of $F$ as a right module with $N$ as a left module is essential. Over a noncommutative ring the two inputs cannot be interchanged freely.
- Vanishing is required for every $N$. Testing only $N=R$ gives vanishing for any $F$, including $F=\mathbb Z/2$, which is not flat. Flatness cannot be replaced by projectivity either. For example, $\mathbb Q$ is a flat, nonprojective $\mathbb Z$-module.
