# Hom in the Homotopy and Derived Categories　`prop.hom-k-eq-hom-d`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=prop.hom-k-eq-hom-d)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./prop.hom-k-eq-hom-d.md)

**Injectivity.** If $p, p'$ are equal in $D$, there is a quasi-isomorphism $s$ with $p s \simeq p' s$ (the equivalence relation on roofs is by definition "there exists a common refinement"); by the lemma "right multiplication by $s$" is injective, hence $p \simeq p'$. ∎

⭐ **Use**: this is the common foundation of "bounded-above injective complexes **are** the derived category" and "computing $R\operatorname{Hom}$ by injective resolutions" — with $I^{\bullet}$ as target, homotopy classes and derived morphisms coincide, so one may compute in $\mathbf{K}$ without worry.
