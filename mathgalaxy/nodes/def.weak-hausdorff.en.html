def.weak-hausdorff · CGWH

Machine-drafted and pending author review. The Chinese text is authoritative.

Statement

A topological space XXX is called weakly Hausdorff if for every continuous map f:S→Xf : S \to Xf:S→X (with SSS compact Hausdorff), the image f(S)f(S)f(S) is closed in XXX.

Notes

What it says: it asks that "images of maps from compact Hausdorff spaces are closed" — a separation condition cheaper than Hausdorff but sufficient.

⭐ Why needed: compactly generated spaces are not required to be Hausdorff, and without separation, quotients and colimits leave the good category; weak Hausdorffness exactly guarantees that "images of compact pieces are closed", so that quotients and colimits in kTopk\mathbf{Top}kTop can still be computed.

⚠ Strength: Hausdorff   ⟹  \implies⟹ weakly Hausdorff, but not conversely. ⛔ Do not write it as an equivalence; and do not confuse it with @@def.chaus@@ (compact + Hausdorff), whose condition is stronger.
