# Flatness and Torsion-Freeness over a Domain　`prop.flat-domain-torsionfree`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=prop.flat-domain-torsionfree)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./prop.flat-domain-torsionfree.md)

**Converse over a PID**: finitely generated submodules of a torsion-free module are free (finitely generated torsion-free over a PID is free), and $M$ is the filtered colimit of its finitely generated submodules; free modules are flat, and $(-)\otimes$ preserves filtered colimits, so $M$ is flat ✓.

⚠️ **The converse fails over a general domain**: take $R = k[x, y]$ and $I = (x, y)$.
