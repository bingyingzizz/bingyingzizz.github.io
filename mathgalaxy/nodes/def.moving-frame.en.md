## Statement · **draft**
Let $U_{0} \subseteq S$ be an open set on the surface. A **moving frame** is a triple of $C^{1}$ vector fields $e_{1}, e_{2}, e_{3}$ such that at every point $(e_{1}, e_{2}, e_{3})$ is an orthonormal basis of $\mathbb{R}^{3}$ (it moves with the point, hence "moving"). The **dual coframe** is

$$\omega^{i} := \langle \mathrm{d}r,\, e_{i}\rangle ,\qquad i = 1,2,3,$$
