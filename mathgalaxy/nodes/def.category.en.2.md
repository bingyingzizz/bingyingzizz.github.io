# category　`def.category`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=def.category)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./def.category.md)

$$M \times_{s,t} M = \{ (f, g) \in M \times M : s(f) = t(g) \}$$

this is the set of all pairs of morphisms "whose target of $g$ equals the source of $f$"; $\circ : M \times_{s,t} M \to M$ sends $(f, g)$ to $f \circ g$, and satisfies the **associativity law**

$$h \circ (g \circ f) = (h \circ g) \circ f$$

(whenever both sides are defined).

**Identity** $i : \mathcal{O} \to M$ assigns to each object $X$ a morphism $1_X = i(X)$, satisfying the **identity law**

$$f \circ 1_X = f = 1_Y \circ f \qquad (f : X \to Y)$$
