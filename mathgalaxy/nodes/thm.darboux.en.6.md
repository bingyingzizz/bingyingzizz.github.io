**Step 2: Construct $\omega_{0}$ so that $\omega_{0}(p) = \omega(p)$**. On $T_{p}M$, take the symplectic basis $e_{i}, f_{i}$ from Step 1, so $\omega_{p}(e_{i}, f_{j}) = \delta_{ij}$ and all other pairings vanish. Take a chart centered at $p$, denoted by $(U, x)$ ($x(p) = 0$), let $q_{i}, p_{i}$ be the coordinates given by the dual basis, and set $\omega_{0} = \sum_{i=1}^{n} dq_{i} \wedge dp_{i}$. Then $\omega_{0}$ has constant coefficients, is closed and nondegenerate, and satisfies $\omega_{0}(p) = \omega_{p}$.
