# completion　`thm.completion`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=thm.completion)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./thm.completion.md)

1. $\bar{\mathcal{M}}$ is a $\sigma$-algebra, and $\mathcal{M} \subseteq \bar{\mathcal{M}}$;
2. let $\bar{\mu}(E \cup F) := \mu(E)$; it is a well-defined measure on $\bar{\mathcal{M}}$;
3. $(X, \bar{\mathcal{M}}, \bar{\mu})$ is a **complete** measure space, called the **completion** of $(X, \mathcal{M}, \mu )$.

## Notes · **draft**
The intuition for $\bar{\mathcal{M}}$: "sprinkle a little dust of null sets onto the original measurable sets".

**Well-definedness must be checked**: the representation $E \cup F$ is not unique; if $E_{1} \cup F_{1} = E_{2} \cup F_{2}$, one must prove $\mu (E_{1}) = \mu (E_{2})$.
