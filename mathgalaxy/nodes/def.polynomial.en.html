Statement

Let KKK be a field. A univariate polynomial over KKK is a formal expression

f=a0+a1x+a2x2+⋯+anxn,ai∈Kf = a_0 + a_1 x + a_2 x^{2} + \cdots + a_n x^{n}, \qquad a_i \in Kf=a0​+a1​x+a2​x2+⋯+an​xn,ai​∈K

(only finitely many coefficients are nonzero); xxx is called the indeterminate and aia_iai​ the coefficients.

If the top nonzero coefficient is an≠0a_n \ne 0an​=0, the degree of fff is nnn, written deg⁡f:=n\deg f := ndegf:=n. The zero polynomial has no degree, and a nonzero constant polynomial has degree 000. ana_nan​ is called the leading coefficient; when an=1a_n = 1an​=1, fff is called monic.

Two polynomials are equal iff all coefficients agree — “polynomial” and “polynomial function” are two different things (see the notes).
