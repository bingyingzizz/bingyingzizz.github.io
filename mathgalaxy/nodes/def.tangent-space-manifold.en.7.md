# Tangent Space　`def.tangent-space-manifold`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=def.tangent-space-manifold)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./def.tangent-space-manifold.md)

**Agreement with the tangent plane of a surface**: for $S\subseteq\mathbb{R}^{3}$, the space $T_{p}S$ (spanned by $r_{u},r_{v}$) is the same space as the abstract definition — take the chart $\varphi = r^{-1}$ and compare; the velocity of a curve is exactly the velocity in $\mathbb{R}^{3}$.
