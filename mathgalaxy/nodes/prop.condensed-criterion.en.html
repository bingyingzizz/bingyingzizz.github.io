Statement

On CHaus\mathbf{CHaus}CHaus, a presheaf of sets XXX is a condensed set   ⟺  \iff⟺

X(S)⟶X(S′)⇉X(S′×SS′)X(S) \longrightarrow X(S') \rightrightarrows X(S' \times_{S} S')X(S)⟶X(S′)⇉X(S′×S​S′)

Notes

CHaus\mathbf{CHaus}CHaus is a pretopos, so the prestandard topology on it is generated by exactly two kinds of covers: finite disjoint unions and surjections. Items 1 and 2 above are the sheaf conditions for these two kinds — "finite coproducts become finite products" and "quotients become kernels".

Another way to put it: XXX is a condensed set   ⟺  \iff⟺ it turns finite coproducts into finite products and coequalizers into equalizers. Every later construction uses this sentence.

Proof. It suffices to check the sheaf condition on the generators of the prestandard topology — a general cover is a composite of "finite disjoint union" and "surjection", and these two suffice:

  • Finite disjoint union: the cover is (Si↪S)i∈I(S_{i} \hookrightarrow S)_{i \in I}(Si​↪S)i∈I​ (III finite, ∐iSi→S\coprod_{i} S_{i} \to S∐i​Si​→S a bijection).

The sheaf condition requires X(S)→∏iX(Si)⇉∏i,jX(Si×SSj)X(S) \to \prod_{i} X(S_{i}) \rightrightarrows \prod_{i,j} X(S_{i} \times_{S} S_{j})X(S)→∏i​X(Si​)⇉∏i,j​X(Si​×S​Sj​) to be exact;

by disjointness, when i≠ji \ne ji=j one has Si×SSj=∅S_{i} \times_{S} S_{j} = \emptysetSi​×S​Sj​=∅, and when i=ji = ji=j one has =Si= S_{i}=Si​, while X(∅)={∗}X(\emptyset) = \{\ast\}X(∅)={∗}, so of that pair of maps one is the identity and the other is constant — exactness reduces to X(S)≅∏iX(Si)X(S) \cong \prod_{i} X(S_{i})X(S)≅∏i​X(Si​), i.e. (4.1).

  • Surjection: the cover is a single surjection S′↠SS' \twoheadrightarrow SS′↠S. The sheaf condition is exactly that X(S)→X(S′)⇉X(S′×SS′)X(S) \to X(S') \rightrightarrows X(S' \times_{S} S')X(S)→X(S′)⇉X(S′×S​S′) is left exact, i.e. (4.2).

Conversely, the two conditions together evidently imply "is a sheaf for all covers" (a cover decomposes into a composite of these two kinds), so the criterion holds. ∎

Hypotheses used in each direction (the generating-cover criterion for the finite-union surjection topology):

  • A sheaf   ⟹  \implies⟹ preserves finite coproducts as products and sends surjections to kernel–coequalizer: unfold the sheaf conditions for the two kinds of covers; the empty cover also requires X(∅)X(\emptyset)X(∅) to be a point.
  • The two criteria   ⟹  \implies⟹ sheaf: use that every finite-union surjection cover decomposes into a finite disjoint union and a single surjection, and descent for composite covers; this does not hold for an arbitrary topology.
  • The closed-equivalence-relation quotient condition   ⟺  \iff⟺ the condition for surjections: use that compact Hausdorff surjections are effective quotients and that quotients by closed equivalence relations are again compact Hausdorff, with the cost paid in @@def.chaus-pretopos@@.
  • Descent for a single surjection is not enough: the constant two-element-set presheaf satisfies the kernel–coequalizer condition for every single surjection, yet does not send the empty coproduct to a point and does not preserve binary coproducts, so the finite-coproduct condition cannot be dropped.
