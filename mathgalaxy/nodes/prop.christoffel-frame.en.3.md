## Notes · **draft**
**The real relation to ① and $\Gamma$**: the coordinate frame $\partial_{i}$ is generally neither orthogonal nor normalized; after orthonormalization $e_{i}$ is no longer a coordinate frame ($[e_{1},e_{2}]\ne 0$), and the extra term $\langle [e_{i},e_{j}],e_{k}\rangle$ is precisely the price of this.
