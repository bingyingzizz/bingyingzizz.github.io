# Regular Surface and Gauss Map　`def.regular-surface`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=def.regular-surface)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./def.regular-surface.md)

**Two choices of normal**: both $n$ and $-n$ are unit normals. $II$ and $H$ change sign with this choice, while $K$ and $|H|$ do not — wherever a sign must be fixed, this package always fixes $e_{3} = n$ (the direction of $r_{u}\times r_{v}$).
