# ind-representable iff left exact　`prop.ind-representable-iff-left-exact`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=prop.ind-representable-iff-left-exact)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./prop.ind-representable-iff-left-exact.md)

1. $T$ is **ind-representable** (it can be expressed as a filtered colimit of representable presheaves).
2. The slice category $\mathcal{C}/T$ is **filtered**.
3. $T$ is **left exact** (it preserves finite limits).
