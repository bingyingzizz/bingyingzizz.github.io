## Notes · **draft**
**Preliminary: a cone is acyclic $\iff$ quasi-isomorphism.** A morphism of complexes $s : K^{\bullet} \to L^{\bullet}$ is a quasi-isomorphism if and only if its **mapping cone** $M(s)^{\bullet}$ is acyclic — applying homology to the triangle $K^{\bullet} \to L^{\bullet} \to M(s)^{\bullet} \to K^{\bullet}[1]$ gives a long exact sequence, and $M(s)$ being acyclic is exactly "$H^{n}(s)$ are all isomorphisms".

**Ore condition.** Given a quasi-isomorphism $w : L'^{\bullet} \to L^{\bullet}$ and any $f : K^{\bullet} \to L^{\bullet}$.
