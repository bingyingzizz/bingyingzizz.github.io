# branched cover of Riemann surfaces　`def.branched-cover`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=def.branched-cover)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./def.branched-cover.md)

- There is a finite set $B \subset X$ such that $p$ restricts to a **covering map** on $Y \setminus p^{-1}(B) \to X \setminus B$ (`def.covering-space`);
- The number of sheets $d$ (independent of the point, by the sum identity for ramification indices below) is called the **degree** of $p$;
- $B$ is called the **branch locus**, the points of $p^{-1}(B)$ are called **ramification points**, and $p$ is called a **branched covering** of $X$ of degree $d$.
