# homology of spheres　`thm.sphere-homology`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=thm.sphere-homology)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./thm.sphere-homology.md)

**Return to unreduced homology.** When $n \ge 1$, the space $S^{n}$ is path connected, so $H_{0}(S^{n}) = \mathbb{Z}$ (that is, $\tilde{H}_{0} = 0$). When $p \ge 1$, we have $H_{p} = \tilde{H}_{p}$. Thus $H_{0}(S^{n}) = \mathbb{Z}$ and $H_{n}(S^{n}) = \mathbb{Z}$, with $H_{p}(S^{n}) = 0$ in all other cases. ∎

⭐ **Interpretation.** This makes precise the statement that homology detects a hole of dimension $n$. Compare `cor.pi1-sphere` ($\pi_{1}(S^{n}) = 1$ for $n \ge 2$). The group $\pi_{1}$ detects only degree $n = 1$, while $H_{n}$ detects every degree ($\pi_{n}$ can also detect these holes, through a different theory).
