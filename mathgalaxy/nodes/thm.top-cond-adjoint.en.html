Statement

The functor

Top⟶Cond,X↦X‾,X‾(S):=C(S,X)\mathbf{Top} \longrightarrow \mathrm{Cond}, \qquad X \mapsto \underline{X},\quad \underline{X}(S) := C(S, X)Top⟶Cond,X↦X​,X​(S):=C(S,X)

is faithful and has a right adjoint X↦X(⋅)X \mapsto X(\cdot)X↦X(⋅). Restricted to the full subcategory of compactly generated spaces, it becomes fully faithful.

Notes

Key sentence: if XXX is a topological space, then its underlying space X(⋅)≅kXX(\cdot) \cong kXX(⋅)≅kX.

So the round trip "topological space →\to→ condensed set →\to→ underlying topological space" is exactly kkk-ification: it replaces a general topological space by the compactly generated one, after which it no longer changes.

Thus kTopk\mathbf{Top}kTop is precisely the part of Cond\mathrm{Cond}Cond that "remembers itself completely", while the extra spaces in general Top\mathbf{Top}Top are merged away in Cond\mathrm{Cond}Cond.
