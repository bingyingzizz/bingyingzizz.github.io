## 陈述（续）
$$\int_0^{2\pi} R(\cos\theta, \sin\theta)\,\mathrm{d}\theta = \oint_{|z| = 1} R\left( \frac{z + z^{-1}}{2},\ \frac{z - z^{-1}}{2 i} \right) \frac{\mathrm{d}z}{i z}.$$
