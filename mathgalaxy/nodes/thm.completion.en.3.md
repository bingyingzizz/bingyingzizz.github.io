# completion　`thm.completion`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=thm.completion)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./thm.completion.md)

The method is to use $E_{1} \subseteq E_{2} \cup N_{2}$ ($N_{2}$ a null set) to get $\mu (E_{1}) \le \mu (E_{2})$, and symmetrically for the reverse.

⚠ Completion is "stuffing the subsets of null sets in"; the price is that the $\sigma$-algebra grows while the measure stays the same.
