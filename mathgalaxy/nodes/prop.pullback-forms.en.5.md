# Pullback: Between Open Sets　`prop.pullback-forms`

**(3) is the core**: it is equivalent to saying that $\mathrm{d}$ does not depend on coordinates. The only substantive input is the chain rule; the rest follows from (2) and $\mathrm{d}^{2}=0$ (the proof is on the edges).

**Use in geometry**: a surface parametrization $r : U\to\mathbb{R}^{3}$ pulls forms on $\mathbb{R}^{3}$ back to $U$.

The first fundamental form and the area element are both pullbacks: $I = (\omega^{1})^{2}+(\omega^{2})^{2}$, $\mathrm{d}A = \omega^{1}\wedge\omega^{2}$.

Surface theory can be written in the language of forms precisely because of this.
