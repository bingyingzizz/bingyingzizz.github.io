## Statement · **draft**
Let $K$ be a field, $V$ a vector space over $K$, and $S \subseteq V$.

- **$S$ is linearly independent**: every finite subset of $S$ is linearly independent, i.e. there are no $c_{1}$…$c_{n} \in K$, not all zero, with $c_{1}v_{1} + \cdots + c_{n}v_{n} = 0$.
- **$S$ spans $V$**: every vector in $V$ is a linear combination of finitely many vectors in $S$.
- **$S$ is a basis of $V$**: $S$ is both linearly independent and spans $V$.
