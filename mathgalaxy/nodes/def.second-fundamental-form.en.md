## Statement · **draft**
The **second fundamental form** of a surface is

$$II(v,w) := \langle \mathrm{d}r(v), -\mathrm{d}n(w)\rangle = \langle W(\mathrm{d}r(v)), \mathrm{d}r(w)\rangle .$$
