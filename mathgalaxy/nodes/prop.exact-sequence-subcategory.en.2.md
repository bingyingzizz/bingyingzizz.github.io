# Exact Sequences Form a Subcategory　`prop.exact-sequence-subcategory`

## Statement · **draft**
Let $\mathcal{C}$ be an abelian category and $\mathcal{C}^{3}$ the category of **composable pairs of morphisms** (objects $M' \to M \to M''$, morphisms the compatible comparison maps of the three components). Then left exact, right exact and short exact sequences each form an **additive subcategory** of $\mathcal{C}^{3}$.
