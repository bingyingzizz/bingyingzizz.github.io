[in graph](../#n=prop.ab-axioms-examples)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./prop.ab-axioms-examples.md)

⭐ The $\mathbf{AbCHaus} \simeq \mathbf{Ab}^{\mathrm{op}}$ line in the table is the result of upgrading Pontryagin duality to an equivalence of categories: the category of compact Hausdorff abelian groups is **anti-equivalent** to the category of discrete abelian groups, so its axiom level is the **dual** version of that of $\mathbf{Ab}$.
