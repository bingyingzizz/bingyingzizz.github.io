Statement

(ii) The functor D(Solid)→D(Cond⁡(Ab))D(\mathbf{Solid}) \to D(\operatorname{Cond}(\mathbf{Ab}))D(Solid)→D(Cond(Ab)) is fully faithful, and its essential image consists precisely of the solid complexes in D(Cond⁡(Ab))D(\operatorname{Cond}(\mathbf{Ab}))D(Cond(Ab)).

A complex CCC is solid if and only if all Hi(C)H^i(C)Hi(C) are solid abelian groups.

The inclusion has a left adjoint

C⟼CL■:D(Cond⁡(Ab))⟶D(Solid),C \longmapsto C^{L\blacksquare} : D(\operatorname{Cond}(\mathbf{Ab})) \longrightarrow D(\mathbf{Solid}),C⟼CL■:D(Cond(Ab))⟶D(Solid),

which is the left derived functor of M↦M■M \mapsto M^{\blacksquare}M↦M■.

Notes

⭐ This extends solidity from objects to complexes. It suffices to check cohomology in place of checking each term.

⚠ Together with (i), this is the full statement of Theorem 5.8. It is presented in two nodes because (i) is used for constructions and (ii) for computations.

⭐ Corollary: The derived category of Solid\mathbf{Solid}Solid can be viewed as a reflective full subcategory of D(Cond⁡(Ab))D(\operatorname{Cond}(\mathbf{Ab}))D(Cond(Ab)), with reflector C↦CL■C \mapsto C^{L\blacksquare}C↦CL■.
