## Notes · **draft**
⭐ This theorem explains the three axioms of the determinant at a stroke:
- multilinearity $\Longleftarrow$ $\wedge$ is linear in each variable;
- alternation $\Longleftarrow$ $v \wedge v = 0$ (the quotient construction);
- normalization $\Longleftarrow$ when $v_i = e_i$, $e_1 \wedge \cdots \wedge e_n$ is a basis and the coefficient is normalized to $1$.

⭐ Hence “existence and uniqueness” becomes “a linear map of a one-dimensional space can only be multiplication by a number” — a second proof of the same theorem, and the conceptually cleaner one.
