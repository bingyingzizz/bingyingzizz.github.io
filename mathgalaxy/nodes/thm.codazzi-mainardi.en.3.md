## Notes · **draft**
**What it says**: the rate of change of the bending is symmetric in the tangent directions — the component along $Y$ of the change of $II$ while moving along $X$ equals the component along $X$ while moving along $Y$. Equivalently: the mixed components $R^{i}_{\ 3jk}$ of the curvature of $\mathbb{R}^{3}$ must vanish.
