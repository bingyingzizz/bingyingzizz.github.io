thm.induced-topos-morphism

Machine-drafted and pending author review. The Chinese text is authoritative.

Statement

Let g:C→C′g : \mathcal{C} \to \mathcal{C}'g:C→C′ be any functor. Then precomposition

g^−1:C′^⟶C^,T′⟼T′∘gop\hat{g}^{-1} : \widehat{\mathcal{C}'} \longrightarrow \widehat{\mathcal{C}}, \qquad T' \longmapsto T' \circ g^{\mathrm{op}}g^​−1:C′⟶C,T′⟼T′∘gop

has both a left adjoint g^!\hat{g}_{!}g^​!​ and a right adjoint g^∗\hat{g}_{*}g^​∗​. In particular g^−1\hat{g}^{-1}g^​−1 is exact, so

g^:C^⟶C′^,g^∗:=g^−1 ⊣ g^∗\hat{g} : \widehat{\mathcal{C}} \longrightarrow \widehat{\mathcal{C}'}, \qquad \hat{g}^{*} := \hat{g}^{-1} \ \dashv\ \hat{g}_{*}g^​:C⟶C′,g^​∗:=g^​−1 ⊣ g^​∗​

is a morphism of toposes. That is, any functor induces a morphism between presheaf categories, in the direction opposite to ggg.

Notes

⭐ One-line reading: ggg is just an arrow between categories, but once we pass to presheaf categories it automatically grows adjoints on both sides — g^!\hat{g}_{!}g^​!​ on the left and g^∗\hat{g}_{*}g^​∗​ on the right. Thus "functor" upgrades to "topos morphism" at the presheaf level.

⚠️ The direction is easy to reverse: g:C→C′g : \mathcal{C} \to \mathcal{C}'g:C→C′ while g^:C^→C′^\hat{g} : \widehat{\mathcal{C}} \to \widehat{\mathcal{C}'}g^​:C→C′; pushforward g^∗\hat{g}_{*}g^​∗​ goes along ggg, while pullback g^−1\hat{g}^{-1}g^​−1 goes the other way.

📌 This is the general engine behind "a map between topological spaces / sites gives a topos morphism"; it is used in Chapter 4 to align sheaves on CHaus\mathbf{CHaus}CHaus with sheaves on a smaller site.
