## Statement · **draft**
Suppose $X = U \cup V$ and $U, V \subseteq X$ are **open sets** (more generally, the interiors of $U, V$ cover $X$). Let $i_{*}, j_{*}$ denote the maps induced by the inclusions of $U \cap V \hookrightarrow U, V$. Then there is a long exact sequence

$$H_{p}(U \cap V) \to H_{p}(U) \oplus H_{p}(V) \to H_{p}(X)$$
$$\xrightarrow{\ \partial_{*}\ } H_{p-1}(U \cap V)$$
