Statement

Let NNN be a closed subgroup of a locally compact Hausdorff abelian group MMM. Then the condensed group associated to the topological quotient agrees with the quotient of the associated condensed groups:

M/N‾  ≅  M‾/N‾.\underline{M/N} \;\cong\; \underline{M}\big/\underline{N}.M/N​≅M​/N​.

Notes

📌 Use: when computing condensed abelian groups, one may first compute the quotient on the topological side and then discretise.
