陈述

拓扑阿贝尔群 (M,+,τ)(M, +, \tau)(M,+,τ) 是一个阿贝尔群 MMM 连带一个拓扑 τ\tauτ，使运算连续：

+:M×M⟶M,−:M⟶M.+ : M \times M \longrightarrow M, \qquad - : M \longrightarrow M.+:M×M⟶M,−:M⟶M.

两个拓扑阿贝尔群之间的连续同态记作

CZ(M,N)  ⊆  C(M,N),C_{\mathbb{Z}}(M, N) \;\subseteq\; C(M, N),CZ​(M,N)⊆C(M,N),

右边带紧开拓扑，左边取子空间拓扑。

说明

⭐ 为什么用 CZC_{\mathbb{Z}}CZ​ 这个记号：Z\mathbb{Z}Z 提醒你这些是 Z\mathbb{Z}Z-模（也就是阿贝尔群）的同态，不是随便的连续映射。

与内部 Hom 的关系：MMM 紧生成时 CZ(M,N)=Hom⁡Z(M,N)C_{\mathbb{Z}}(M, N) = \operatorname{Hom}_{\mathbb{Z}}(M, N)CZ​(M,N)=HomZ​(M,N) —— 见下一条命题。（这是「测试够了」的又一个红利。）

⚠️ 两个容易掉进去的坑（专门点出来）：

  • M‾↦M\underline{M} \mapsto MM​↦M 那条通道没有明显的伴随，因为函子 X↦X(∙)X \mapsto X(\bullet)X↦X(∙)（取底拓扑空间）不保积；
  • 就算 MMM 是拓扑阿贝尔群，kMkMkM 一般也不是拓扑阿贝尔群 —— 因为紧生成空间对（拓扑空间的）积不封闭。

Statement draft

A topological abelian group (M,+,τ)(M, +, \tau)(M,+,τ) is an abelian group MMM equipped with a topology τ\tauτ for which the group operations are continuous:

+:M×M⟶M,−:M⟶M.+ : M \times M \longrightarrow M, \qquad - : M \longrightarrow M.+:M×M⟶M,−:M⟶M.

The continuous homomorphisms between two topological abelian groups are denoted by

CZ(M,N)  ⊆  C(M,N),C_{\mathbb{Z}}(M, N) \;\subseteq\; C(M, N),CZ​(M,N)⊆C(M,N),

The right-hand side has the compact-open topology, and the left-hand side has the subspace topology.

Notes draft

⭐ Why use the notation CZC_{\mathbb{Z}}CZ​: Z\mathbb{Z}Z emphasizes that these continuous maps are homomorphisms of Z\mathbb{Z}Z-modules (that is, abelian groups).

Relation to internal Hom: When MMM is compactly generated, CZ(M,N)=Hom⁡Z(M,N)C_{\mathbb{Z}}(M, N) = \operatorname{Hom}_{\mathbb{Z}}(M, N)CZ​(M,N)=HomZ​(M,N). See the next proposition. This is another consequence of having enough test objects.

⚠️ Two common pitfalls:

  • The functor in M‾↦M\underline{M} \mapsto MM​↦M has no obvious adjoint, because X↦X(∙)X \mapsto X(\bullet)X↦X(∙) (taking the underlying topological space) does not preserve products;
  • Even if MMM is a topological abelian group, kMkMkM need not be a topological abelian group, because compactly generated spaces are not closed under products taken in topological spaces.
