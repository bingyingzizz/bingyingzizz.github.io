## Notes · **draft**
Two directions: a sheaf on $\mathbf{CHaus}$ restricts to a sheaf on $\mathbf{FCHaus}$ (there are fewer coverings, so the sheaf condition is only easier to satisfy); conversely, for a sheaf on $\mathbf{FCHaus}$, say $X$, taking the filtered colimit along "the map from free objects to $S$" extends it back to $\mathbf{CHaus}$, and the result is exactly the glued value.
