## Notes · **draft**
⭐ **What filters are for: a clean characterization of compactness**: $X$ is compact $\iff$ every ultrafilter converges (equivalently, every filter has a cluster point). This is shorter than the net version, because the "subnet" step is absorbed by maximality of the ultrafilter.

⭐ **The ultrafilter lemma cannot be dispensed with**: it is not a theorem but **a small piece of the axiom of choice** (strictly weaker than AC, but equivalent to the Boolean prime ideal theorem). So every proof that "takes an ultrafilter" is secretly using choice — in contrast with the family of arguments in `thm.paracompact-hausdorff-normal` that use **only compactness**.
