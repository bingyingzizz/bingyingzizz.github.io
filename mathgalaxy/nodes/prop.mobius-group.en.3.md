# Möbius transformations form a group　`prop.mobius-group`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=prop.mobius-group)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./prop.mobius-group.md)

**Why there are no other automorphisms**: a holomorphic automorphism of $\widehat{\mathbb{C}}$ — look at the image of $\infty$. If it is $\infty$, the map is entire and bijective — by a Liouville-type argument it must be $az + b$. Otherwise — when the image is not $\infty$ — compose first with $z \mapsto 1/(z - z_{0})$ to reduce to the previous case. ∎
