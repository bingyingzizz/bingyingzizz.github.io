 surjective the image is closed, and continuous functions on a compact Hausdorff space separate points from closed sets; surjection $\implies$ regular epimorphism uses that a continuous surjection from a compact space to a Hausdorff space is a closed quotient map.
- Internal equivalence relation $\implies$ effective and universal: the relation is a closed subspace of the product, and the quotient of a closed equivalence relation is again compact Hausdorff; general topological equivalence relations have no such guarantee.
- Finite disjoint unions are again compact, giving the finite-coproduct property; disjoint unions of infinitely many
