## Notes · **draft**
Isomorphisms are exactly bijective intertwiners, and their inverses are again $A$-linear. The kernel and image of an intertwiner are subrepresentations. The space $\operatorname{End}_A(V)=\operatorname{Hom}_A(V,V)$ is an $F$-algebra; Schur's lemma describes it for irreducible $V$. For $A=F[G]$, intertwiners are exactly $G$-equivariant maps.
