又称Cayley–Hamilton 定理·凯莱-哈密顿定理·哈密顿-凯莱定理

陈述

设 T:V→VT : V \to VT:V→V 是有限维 KKK-向量空间上的线性映射，χT\chi_TχT​ 是它的特征多项式。把 χT\chi_TχT​ 里出现的 xxx 换成 TTT（常数项乘 III），得到算子 χT(T)\chi_T(T)χT​(T)。则

χT(T)=0\chi_T(T) = 0χT​(T)=0

（右边是零算子；矩阵形式是零矩阵。）

矩阵形式：对 A∈Mn(K)A \in M_n(K)A∈Mn​(K) 有 χA(A)=0\chi_A(A) = 0χA​(A)=0（零矩阵）。

说明

⭐ 这条定理说的是「每个算子都满足自己的特征方程」—— 于是 χT\chi_TχT​ 是「把 TTT 送进零」的多项式之一，这立刻给出一个把 K[x]K[x]K[x]-模的结构用上的入口（VVV 成了 K[x]/(χT)K[x]/(\chi_T)K[x]/(χT​)-模）。

⚠ 常见错误记法：不能把 χT(T)=det⁡(TI−T)\chi_T(T) = \det(T I - T)χT​(T)=det(TI−T) 这样代进去（那是「矩阵里的矩阵」），要先把 χT\chi_TχT​ 展开成 ∑ckxk\sum c_k x^{k}∑ck​xk，再逐项换成 ∑ckTk\sum c_k T^{k}∑ck​Tk。

⭐ 用途：算矩阵的幂（AnA^{n}An 可以用低次幂线性表出）、证明极小多项式整除特征多项式、求逆矩阵（A−1A^{-1}A−1 由 χA\chi_AχA​ 的系数给出）。

Statement draft

Let T:V→VT : V \to VT:V→V be a linear map on a finite-dimensional KKK-vector space, and χT\chi_TχT​ its characteristic polynomial. Replace xxx appearing in χT\chi_TχT​ by TTT (multiplying the constant term by III), obtaining the operator χT(T)\chi_T(T)χT​(T). Then

χT(T)=0\chi_T(T) = 0χT​(T)=0

(the right-hand side is the zero operator; in matrix form, the zero matrix.)

Matrix form: for A∈Mn(K)A \in M_n(K)A∈Mn​(K), one has χA(A)=0\chi_A(A) = 0χA​(A)=0 (the zero matrix).

Notes draft

⭐ The theorem says "every operator satisfies its own characteristic equation" — so χT\chi_TχT​ is one of the polynomials that send TTT to zero, which immediately gives an entry point for using the structure of the K[x]K[x]K[x]-module (VVV becomes a K[x]/(χT)K[x]/(\chi_T)K[x]/(χT​)-module).

⚠ A common notational error: one cannot substitute χT(T)=det⁡(TI−T)\chi_T(T) = \det(T I - T)χT​(T)=det(TI−T) like that (that would be a "matrix inside a matrix"); one must first expand χT\chi_TχT​ as ∑ckxk\sum c_k x^{k}∑ck​xk, then replace term by term with ∑ckTk\sum c_k T^{k}∑ck​Tk.

⭐ Uses: computing powers of a matrix (AnA^{n}An can be expressed linearly in lower powers), proving that the minimal polynomial divides the characteristic polynomial, and finding inverse matrices (A−1A^{-1}A−1 is given by the coefficients of χA\chi_AχA​).
