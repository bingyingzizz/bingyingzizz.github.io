## Statement · **draft**
Every **compact surface** (a compact, connected, $2$-dimensional topological manifold, possibly with boundary) is triangulable (`def.triangulation`).

A common equivalent formulation is that every compact connected surface is homeomorphic to the geometric realization of a **finite** simplicial complex.
