# the integrally closed subring is determined by the spectrum　`prop.huber-subring-recovery`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=prop.huber-subring-recovery)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./prop.huber-subring-recovery.md)

Write $\operatorname{Spv}(A) := \operatorname{Spa}(A, \widetilde{\mathbb{Z}})$ for all equivalence classes of valuations on $A$ (taking the **smallest** $A^+$, the integral closure of $\mathbb{Z}$ in $A$ — it sits in the unit ball of every valuation; the smaller $A^+$ is, the fewer points are filtered out, so no valuation is discarded). Then

$$A^+ \longmapsto U = \operatorname{Spa}(A, A^+) = \bigcap_{f \in A^+} U_{1,f} \subseteq \operatorname{Spv}(A),$$
