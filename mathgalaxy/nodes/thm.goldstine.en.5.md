But $\xi_0 \in B_{X^{**}}$ gives $\xi_0(x) \le \|\xi_0\|\,\|x\| \le \|x\|$, a contradiction. Therefore $K = B_{X^{**}}$, i.e. $J_X(B_X)$ is weak* dense. $\blacksquare$

⚠ Dense is not closed: in general $J_X(B_X)$ is indeed not weak* closed (it is weak* closed iff $X$ is reflexive), which is exactly the point used in `thm.milman-pettis`.

📌 The geometric separation lemma here uses only `thm.hahn-banach`; together the two turn "$X$ is reflexive" into a purely geometric condition: every point of $B_{X^{**}}$ lies in the weak* closure of $J_X(B_X)$.
