# Unitary and Orthogonal Matrices　`def.unitary-matrix`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=def.unitary-matrix)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./def.unitary-matrix.md)

**Why it suffices that the columns form an orthonormal basis**: for $U^{*}U = I$, its $(j,k)$-th entry is exactly "the inner product of the $j$-th column with the $k$-th column = $\delta_{jk}$". So "unitary" = "sends an orthonormal basis to an orthonormal basis", which is the most convenient criterion.
