Statement

Let T:V→WT : V \to WT:V→W be linear. The nullity of TTT is defined as the dimension of its kernel:

null⁡T:=dim⁡ker⁡T\operatorname{null} T := \dim \ker TnullT:=dimkerT

(also called the defect; English nullity or defect.)

Notes

⭐ Nullity measures "how many dimensions of information TTT throws away". It pairs with the rank: the rank–nullity theorem says the two add up to the dimension of the domain.

⚠ null⁡T=0  ⟺  ker⁡T={0}  ⟺  T\operatorname{null} T = 0 \iff \ker T = \{0\} \iff TnullT=0⟺kerT={0}⟺T is injective — nullity is the cheapest injectivity test.
