# Semidirect Product　`def.semidirect-product`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=def.semidirect-product)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./def.semidirect-product.md)

**Three basic properties**:
- the identity is $(1_{H}, 1_{G})$ and inverses are $(h, g)^{-1} = \bigl(\varphi(g^{-1})(h^{-1}),\ g^{-1}\bigr)$;
- there is a **split** short exact sequence $1 \to H \to H \rtimes_{\varphi} G \xrightarrow{\ \pi\ } G \to 1$, whose section $g \mapsto (1_{H}, g)$ is a **group homomorphism**;
- when $\varphi$ is trivial, $H \rtimes_{\varphi} G = H \times G$ (direct product).
