## Notes · **draft**
⭐ **This is the root of why Stonean spaces are convenient in condensed mathematics**: sheaf cohomology **degenerates automatically** on them, and $\Gamma(S, -)$ is directly an exact functor, so "taking sections" costs no derived correction.
