## Statement · **draft**
Let $K$ be a field. A **univariate polynomial** over $K$ is a formal expression

$$f = a_0 + a_1 x + a_2 x^{2} + \cdots + a_n x^{n}, \qquad a_i \in K$$

(only finitely many coefficients are nonzero); $x$ is called the **indeterminate** and $a_i$ the **coefficients**.
