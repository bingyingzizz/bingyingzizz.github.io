Statement

Let SSS be a compact Hausdorff space and NNN a real Banach space. Then RHom⁡Z(Z⋅S, N)≅C(S,N)\operatorname{RHom}_{\mathbb{Z}}(\mathbb{Z} \cdot S,\ N) \cong C(S, N)RHomZ​(Z⋅S, N)≅C(S,N), concentrated in degree 0.

Notes

Intuition: Z⋅S\mathbb{Z} \cdot SZ⋅S is the free abelian group generated by SSS; a group homomorphism from it to NNN is "assign to each point of SSS an element of NNN". Requiring it to be continuous gives exactly the continuous function space C(S,N)C(S, N)C(S,N).

Why only degree 0: Z⋅S\mathbb{Z} \cdot SZ⋅S is projective when SSS is compact Hausdorff, and projective objects have no higher Ext.

⭐ This is the local computation behind the step "real Banach is acyclic on compact Hausdorff spaces": every cell of the spectral sequence has this shape.
