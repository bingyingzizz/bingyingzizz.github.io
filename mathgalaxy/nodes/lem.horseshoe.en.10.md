# Horseshoe Lemma　`lem.horseshoe`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=lem.horseshoe)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./lem.horseshoe.md)

⭐ **Practical use.** When an explicit resolution of a module $M$ is unavailable, place it in the middle of a short exact sequence and resolve the two end terms. This gives a resolution of $M$ **that can be written explicitly as a termwise direct sum**. Koszul complexes, resolutions of tensor products, and double complexes in spectral sequences are all assembled this way.

**Dual version.** Reverse all the arrows to obtain the horseshoe lemma for injective resolutions. It expresses the same principle as “The Total Complex Is Still an Injective Resolution” (the total complex of the double complex is again a resolution).
