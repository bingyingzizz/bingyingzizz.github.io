## Notes · **draft**
**Weak* topology = restriction of the product topology.** Put $P = \prod_{x \in X} [-\|x\|, \|x\|]$ and $\Phi : B_{X^{*}} \to P$, $\Phi(\varphi) = (\varphi(x))_{x \in X}$. Since $|\varphi(x)| \le \|\varphi\|\,\|x\| \le \|x\|$, the image lies in $P$; the weak* topology is exactly the topology pulled back from the product topology on $P$ (each coordinate is weak* continuous, and the coordinates separate points); $\Phi$ is injective on $B_{X^{*}}$.
