# k-bounded completion　`lem.k-bounded-completion`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=lem.k-bounded-completion)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./lem.k-bounded-completion.md)

Then $M^{\bullet}$ is boundedly exact at $M^{n}$ with bound $K$ $\iff$ $\widehat{M}^{\bullet}$ is boundedly exact at $\widehat{M}^{n}$ with bound $K$.

## Notes · **stale**
**Purpose**: This reduces the general seminormed case **to the Banach case**. Proving subsequent theorems for Banach groups therefore suffices for the general case.
