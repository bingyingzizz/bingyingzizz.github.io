# covering space　`def.covering-space`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=def.covering-space)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./def.covering-space.md)

💡 Picture: the base space is the shadow cast downward, and the covering space is the sheet of paper held above it. The same shadow can be cast by several sheets, and the sheets are related by "go once around, change sheet" — that switching rule is $\pi_{1}$.
