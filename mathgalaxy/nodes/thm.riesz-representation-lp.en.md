## Statement · **draft**
Let $1 < p < \infty$ and let $q$ be the conjugate exponent. Then for every $\Phi \in (L^p)^*$ there is a **unique** (a.e.) $g \in L^q$ such that

$$
\Phi(f) = \int f g \, d\mu \qquad \forall f \in L^p
$$

Hence the map $g \mapsto \varphi_g$ is an **isometric isomorphism** from $L^q$ onto $(L^p)^*$:

$$(L^p)^* \cong L^q$$
