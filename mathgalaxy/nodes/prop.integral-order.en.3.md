# Monotonicity and Estimates for Integrals　`prop.integral-order`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=prop.integral-order)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./prop.integral-order.md)

⭐ Integrability of $|f|$ is a phenomenon specific to Riemann integration (on the Lebesgue side, integrability of $f$ immediately implies integrability of $|f|$);

conversely integrability of $|f|$ does not imply integrability of $f$ — take $f$ alternating between $1$ and $-1$ on rationals/irrationals; then $|f| \equiv 1$ is integrable while $f$ is not.
