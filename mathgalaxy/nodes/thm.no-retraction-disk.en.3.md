# the disk does not retract onto the circle　`thm.no-retraction-disk`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=thm.no-retraction-disk)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./thm.no-retraction-disk.md)

**The whole argument uses one line of group theory**, given that $\pi_{1}(S^{1})$ is known.

**Generalisation**: the same argument works for every $n \ge 1$ (replacing the fundamental group by the homology group $H_{n-1}$): $S^{n-1}$ is not a retract of $D^{n}$.

⚠️ "Convex $\implies$ contractible" is literal in the proof: for any $D^{2}$ in $\sigma$, take $F(t, s) := (1 - s)\,\sigma(t)$, shrinking the whole path along a straight line to $0$. No machinery is needed for this step.
