# Stalks of a Skyscraper Sheaf　`prop.skyscraper-stalks`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=prop.skyscraper-stalks)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./prop.skyscraper-stalks.md)

- If $y \in \overline{\{x\}}$: every open neighbourhood $U \ni y$ of $x$ contains $A$ (this is the characterization of the closure), so every term in the system is $\mathrm{id}_{A}$ and every transition map is $A$ — the colimit is $y \notin \overline{\{x\}}$.
- If $U_{0} \ni y$: there is $x$ not containing $U_{0}$. Take the **cofinal** subsystem of terms contained in $0$; every term there is $0$ — the colimit is $T_{1}$. ∎
