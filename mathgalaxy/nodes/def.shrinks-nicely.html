Abstract reviewed

Notes reviewed

What the two conditions say: it fits inside (ErE_rEr​ lies in the ball) and it is not too flat (its volume is at least alphaalphaalpha times that of the ball).

Typical examples: take Er=B(r,x)E_r = B(r, x)Er​=B(r,x) itself (alpha=1alpha = 1alpha=1); or take ErE_rEr​ to be a fixed shape inside the ball (a cube, say), rescaled proportionally.
