# Cotangent Bundle　`def.cotangent-bundle`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=def.cotangent-bundle)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./def.cotangent-bundle.md)

**Smooth structure**: on a chart $(U,\varphi)$ the differentials $\mathrm{d}x^{i}\big|_{p}$ of the coordinate functions $x^{1},\ldots,x^{n}$ form a basis of $T_{p}^{\ast}M$ (dual to $\partial/\partial x^{i}\big|_{p}$), so

$$\Phi : \pi^{-1}(U)\longrightarrow\varphi(U)\times\mathbb{R}^{n}$$

$$\sum_{i=1}^{n}a_{i}\,\mathrm{d}x^{i}\big|_{p}\longmapsto\bigl(\varphi(p),\,a_{1},\ldots,a_{n}\bigr),$$
