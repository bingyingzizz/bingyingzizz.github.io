## Statement · **draft**
Let $u \in B(E, F)$ and $v \in B(F, G)$. Then $v \circ u \in B(E, G)$ and

$$\|v \circ u\| \le \|v\| \, \|u\| .$$

In particular $B(E)$ is a **normed algebra** (multiplication is continuous); if $E \ne \{0\}$ then $\|\mathrm{id}\| = 1$.
