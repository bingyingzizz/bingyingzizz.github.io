## Notes · **draft**
Example: **ordinary abelian groups are flat $\iff$ torsion-free**. It follows immediately that $\mathbb{Z}\cdot X$ is **always flat** — reduce first to presheaves, then to ordinary abelian groups, and free abelian groups are torsion-free.
