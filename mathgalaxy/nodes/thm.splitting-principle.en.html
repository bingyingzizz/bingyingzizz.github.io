Statement

What is being done: the formula for Chern classes in general rank is hard to write down directly. The splitting principle says: to compute polynomial identities one may pretend the bundle is a direct sum of line bundles — this is not a real decomposition, but it suffices to derive every formula.

Let E→BE \to BE→B be a complex vector bundle of rank kkk. Then there are a smooth manifold π:B~→B\pi : \tilde B \to Bπ:B~→B and a map π∗E≅L1⊕⋯⊕Lk\pi^{*}E \cong L_{1} \oplus \cdots \oplus L_{k}π∗E≅L1​⊕⋯⊕Lk​ (with LjL_{j}Lj​ line bundles), and π∗:H∗(B)→H∗(B~)\pi^{*} : H^{*}(B) \to H^{*}(\tilde B)π∗:H∗(B)→H∗(B~) is injective (with coefficients in Q\mathbb{Q}Q; more concretely one takes B~\tilde BB~ to be the flag bundle over BBB).

Thus one may formally write

c(E)=∏j=1k(1+xj),xj:=c1(Lj),c(E) = \prod_{j=1}^{k}\bigl(1 + x_{j}\bigr), \qquad x_{j} := c_{1}(L_{j}) ,c(E)=j=1∏k​(1+xj​),xj​:=c1​(Lj​),

where xjx_{j}xj​ are called the Chern roots of EEE. Every polynomial identity concerning Chern classes, once verified in the case "EEE is a direct sum of line bundles", holds for a general EEE.

Notes

Why this is not "cheating": injectivity of π∗\pi^{*}π∗ means "a polynomial identity holding on B~\tilde BB~ can be pushed back to BBB". So the rigorous logic is: first prove the identity on the flag bundle, then push back using injectivity. ⚠️ The key word is injective, not "a splitting exists" — a splitting without injectivity cannot be pushed back.

Where the cost is: B~\tilde BB~ is usually much larger than BBB (the fibre of the flag bundle is GL(k)/\mathrm{GL}(k)/GL(k)/ (upper triangular), of real dimension ∼k2\sim k^{2}∼k2), and one has to do fibre integration (pushing the identity back down along π\piπ to BBB). So the splitting principle is a "tool for proof", not a "method of computation": to actually compute a concrete cjc_{j}cj​ one still has to return to the curvature or to line bundles.

⚠️ With Z\mathbb{Z}Z coefficients there is a torsion problem: injectivity is cleanest with Q\mathbb{Q}Q coefficients; with integral coefficients one must be careful about 222-torsion (which is why Stiefel–Whitney classes use Z/2\mathbb{Z}/2Z/2 coefficients).

Typical use: from c(E)=∏(1+xj)c(E) = \prod(1+x_{j})c(E)=∏(1+xj​) one immediately gets c1(E)=∑xj=c1(det⁡E)c_{1}(E) = \sum x_{j} = c_{1}(\det E)c1​(E)=∑xj​=c1​(detE) and ck(E)=∏xj=e(E)c_{k}(E) = \prod x_{j} = e(E)ck​(E)=∏xj​=e(E) (top Chern class = Euler class). These two formulas are obtained at once in general rank via the splitting principle.
