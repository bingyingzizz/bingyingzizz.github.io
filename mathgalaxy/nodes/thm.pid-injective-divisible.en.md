## Statement · **draft**
Let $R$ be a **principal ideal domain** (for example $\mathbb{Z}$ or $k[x]$), and $M$ an $R$-module. Then $M$ is injective iff $M$ is **divisible**, i.e.

$$\forall a \ne 0,\ \forall y \in M,\ \exists x \in M, \qquad a x = y .$$
