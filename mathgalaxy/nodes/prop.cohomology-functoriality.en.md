## Statement · **draft**
Let $f : \mathcal{T} \to \mathcal{T}'$ be a morphism of topoi. Then for $X' \in \mathcal{T}'$ and a complex $M^{\bullet}$ there is a **canonical map**

$$H^{n}\bigl(X',\ M^{\bullet}\bigr) \longrightarrow H^{n}\bigl(f^{-1}(X'),\ f^{-1}M^{\bullet}\bigr).$$
