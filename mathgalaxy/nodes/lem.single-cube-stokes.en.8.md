# Stokes' Formula on a Single Cube　`lem.single-cube-stokes`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=lem.single-cube-stokes)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./lem.single-cube-stokes.md)

**Why this case comes first**: The case of a general cube $c$ follows by pullback ($c^{\ast}\mathrm{d} = \mathrm{d}c^{\ast}$), which reduces it to this lemma. This lemma therefore supplies the **only** substantive calculation in Stokes' theorem for chains. The rest is the passage to general cubes and chains.
