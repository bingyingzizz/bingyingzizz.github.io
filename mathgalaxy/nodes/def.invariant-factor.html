特别地，对 A∈Mn(K)A \in M_n(K)A∈Mn​(K)，把 xI−A∈Mn(K[x])xI - A \in M_n(K[x])xI−A∈Mn​(K[x]) 的非常数不变因子称为线性算子 AAA 的不变因子。

说明

与有理标准形：若 d1∣⋯∣dsd_1 \mid \cdots \mid d_sd1​∣⋯∣ds​ 是 xI−AxI - AxI−A 的非常数不变因子，则 AAA 相似于 diag⁡(Cd1,…,Cds)\operatorname{diag}(C_{d_1}, \ldots, C_{d_s})diag(Cd1​​,…,Cds​​)，其中 CdC_dCd​ 是友矩阵；这正是有理标准形。

与极小/特征多项式：最大的不变因子 dsd_sds​ 是极小多项式，乘积 d1⋯dsd_1 \cdots d_sd1​⋯ds​ 是特征多项式。

与初等因子：把每个 did_idi​ 分解成不可约多项式的幂，得到的素幂因子叫初等因子；Jordan 标准形在域分裂时用初等因子陈述。

矩阵版本 vs 算子版本：Smith 标准形对任意矩阵（可长方形、环上）陈述；算子的不变因子是 xI−AxI - AxI−A 这一特殊矩阵的不变因子。

Statement draft

Let RRR be a principal ideal domain and let the Smith normal form of A∈Mm×n(R)A \in M_{m \times n}(R)A∈Mm×n​(R) be

PAQ=diag⁡(d1,…,dr,0,…,0),d1∣d2∣⋯∣dr.P A Q = \operatorname{diag}(d_1, \ldots, d_r, 0, \ldots, 0), \qquad d_1 \mid d_2 \mid \cdots \mid d_r .PAQ=diag(d1​,…,dr​,0,…,0),d1​∣d2​∣⋯∣dr​.

The nonzero elements did_idi​ (unique up to units; for R=K[x]R = K[x]R=K[x] one takes the monic representative) are the invariant factors of AAA. Equivalently, d1⋯dkd_1 \cdots d_kd1​⋯dk​ is the greatest common divisor of all minors of AAA of size kkk.

In particular, for A∈Mn(K)A \in M_n(K)A∈Mn​(K), the nonconstant invariant factors of xI−A∈Mn(K[x])xI - A \in M_n(K[x])xI−A∈Mn​(K[x]) are called the invariant factors of the linear operator AAA.

Notes draft

Relation to rational canonical form: if d1∣⋯∣dsd_1 \mid \cdots \mid d_sd1​∣⋯∣ds​ are the nonconstant invariant factors of xI−AxI - AxI−A, then AAA is similar to diag⁡(Cd1,…,Cds)\operatorname{diag}(C_{d_1}, \ldots, C_{d_s})diag(Cd1​​,…,Cds​​), where CdC_dCd​ is the companion matrix; this is exactly the rational canonical form.

Relation to minimal and characteristic polynomials: the largest invariant factor dsd_sds​ is the minimal polynomial, and the product d1⋯dsd_1 \cdots d_sd1​⋯ds​ is the characteristic polynomial.

Relation to elementary divisors: factor each did_idi​ into powers of irreducible polynomials; the resulting prime-power factors are the elementary divisors. When the field splits, the Jordan normal form is stated in terms of elementary divisors.

Matrix version vs operator version: the Smith normal form applies to arbitrary matrices (possibly rectangular, over a ring); the invariant factors of an operator are those of the special matrix xI−AxI - AxI−A.
