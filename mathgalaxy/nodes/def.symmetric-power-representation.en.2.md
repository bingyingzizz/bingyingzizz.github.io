## Statement · **draft**
Let $\operatorname{char}F\ne2$ and let $(V,\rho)$ be a representation of a group $G$. The symmetric square $\operatorname{Sym}^2V$ is the $+1$ eigenspace of the swap $\tau(v\otimes w)=w\otimes v$ on $V\otimes V$; $G$ acts by $g(vw)=\rho(g)v\,\rho(g)w$, the symmetric square representation. If $e_1,\ldots,e_n$ is a basis of $V$, then $\{e_ie_j:1\le i\le j\le n\}$ is a basis of $\operatorname{Sym}^2V$.
