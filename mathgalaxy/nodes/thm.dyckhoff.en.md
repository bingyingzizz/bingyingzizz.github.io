## Statement · **draft**
Let $X$ be a **locally compact Hausdorff** space and $M$ a **discrete** abelian group (regarded as a constant condensed abelian group). Then for all $n \ge 0$

$$H^{n}_{\mathrm{cond}}(X,\ M) \cong H^{n}_{\mathrm{sheaf}}(X,\ M).$$
