# open mapping theorem for Banach spaces　`thm.open-mapping-banach`

 we get $z = \bigl(u(x_0)+z\bigr) - u(x_0) \in \overline{u(kB_E)} - u(x_0) \subseteq \overline{u(kB_E) - u(x_0)} = \overline{u(kB_E - x_0)}$, and $x_0 \in kB_E$ gives $kB_E - x_0 \subseteq 2k\,B_E$, so $z \in \overline{u(2k\,B_E)}$. Therefore
$$\overline{u(2k\, B_E)} \supseteq B_F(0, \delta/2) ,$$
and homogeneity ($u(2k\,B_E) = 2k\,u(B_E)$, dividing both sides by $2k$) gives: there exists $r = \delta/(8k) > 0$ such that
$$\overline{u(B_E)} \supseteq 2r\, B_F .$$
