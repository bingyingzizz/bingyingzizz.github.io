## Statement · **draft**
There is a constant $C > 0$ (depending only on the dimension $n$) such that for every $f \in L^1$ and every $\alpha > 0$,

$$m(\{x : Hf(x) > \alpha\}) \le (C/\alpha)\cdot\int |f(x)| dx$$
