## Notes · **draft**
Also $J(A)$ is the intersection of all maximal right ideals, and it annihilates every irreducible left module.
In finite dimension $J(A)=0$ is equivalent to $A$ being semisimple (Jacobson criterion).
When $A$ is finite-dimensional, the quotient $A/J(A)$ is semisimple.
The original notes write $\operatorname{Rad}(A)$, synonymous with $J(A)$.
