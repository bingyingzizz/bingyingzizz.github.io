# universal property of the tensor product of vector spaces　`thm.tensor-universal-property`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=thm.tensor-universal-property)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./thm.tensor-universal-property.md)

Conversely, any linear map $\widetilde B$ composed with $\otimes$ gives a bilinear map. Hence there is a natural isomorphism

$$\operatorname{Hom}_K(V \otimes W,\ U) \;\cong\; \operatorname{Bil}(V, W; U)$$

(natural in $U$; this is "the tensor product is the **representation** of the bilinear functor").
