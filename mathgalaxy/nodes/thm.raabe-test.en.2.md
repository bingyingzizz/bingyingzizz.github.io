# Raabe's test　`thm.raabe-test`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=thm.raabe-test)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./thm.raabe-test.md)

When $R>1$ we have $K_n\ge\delta>0$ and $\sum 1/c_n=\sum 1/n$ diverges, hence the series converges; when $R<1$ we have $K_n\le-\delta$, hence it diverges (see `thm.kummer`).

**Two examples with $R=1$**: $\sum 1/n$ has $R=1$ and diverges; $\sum 1/n^2$ has $n\bigl(((n+1)/n)^2-1\bigr)=2+1/n\to2$, that is $R=2$, and converges.

📌 When the ratio test fails ($q=1$) one may use Raabe; when Raabe still fails, use Bertrand or Gauss (`thm.bertrand-test`, `thm.gauss-test`).
