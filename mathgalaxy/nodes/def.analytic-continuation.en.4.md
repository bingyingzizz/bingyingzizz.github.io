# analytical continuation　`def.analytic-continuation`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=def.analytic-continuation)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./def.analytic-continuation.md)

⭐ **The carrier is the power series**: every element can be expanded at its centre into a convergent power series (`thm.taylor-complex`), so continuation can also be seen as "changing the expansion point along the curve". The radius of convergence is determined by the distance to the nearest singularity — hitting a singularity breaks the continuation.
