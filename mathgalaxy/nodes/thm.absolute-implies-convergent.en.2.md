# absolute convergence implies convergence　`thm.absolute-implies-convergent`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=thm.absolute-implies-convergent)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./thm.absolute-implies-convergent.md)

⚠ **The converse fails**: the alternating harmonic series converges but not absolutely.

⭐ The real use of the theorem is **reduction**: to prove that a general series converges, first prove that $\sum |a_n|$ converges — the latter is a positive-term series, for which far more tools are available.
