# Mayer–Vietoris sequence in homology　`thm.mv-homology`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=thm.mv-homology)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./thm.mv-homology.md)

Proof. For each singular $p$-simplex $\sigma$, $\{\sigma^{-1}(U), \sigma^{-1}(V)\}$ is an open cover of the compact space $\Delta^{p}$ and has a Lebesgue number $\delta_{\sigma} > 0$ (any subset of diameter $< \delta_{\sigma}$ lies in one member of the cover). Choose $m$ so that $(p/(p+1))^{m}\operatorname{diam}(\Delta^{p}) < \delta_{\sigma}$. Then the image of each smaller simplex of $\operatorname{Sd}^{m}(\sigma)$ lies in $U$ or $V$.

Write $T_{M} := \sum_{j=0}^{M-1}\operatorname{Sd}^{j} T$. Then $\partial T_{M} + T_{M}\partial = \operatorname{Sd}^{M} - \operatorname{id}$.
