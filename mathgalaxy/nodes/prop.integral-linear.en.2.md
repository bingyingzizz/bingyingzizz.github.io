# Linearity and Additivity of the Integral　`prop.integral-linear`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=prop.integral-linear)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./prop.integral-linear.md)

Moreover, for $a < c < b$: $f$ is integrable on $[a, b]$ if and only if it is integrable on $[a, c]$ and $[c, b]$, in which case

$$\int_a^b f = \int_a^c f + \int_c^b f$$

## Notes · **draft**
⭐ "Additivity over intervals" is the most practical property of the Riemann integral: it cuts a problem on a large interval into two halves — and it is also the basis for defining improper integrals later.
