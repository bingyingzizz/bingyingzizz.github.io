## Statement · **draft**
**(Sequences)** Suppose there is $N_0$ such that $a_n \le x_n \le b_n$ for all $n \ge N_0$. If $a_n \to L$ and $b_n \to L$, then $x_n \to L$.

**(Functions)** Suppose $g(x) \le f(x) \le h(x)$ holds on some punctured neighbourhood of $x_0$. If

$$\lim_{x \to x_0} g(x) = \lim_{x \to x_0} h(x) = L$$

then $\lim_{x \to x_0} f(x) = L$.
