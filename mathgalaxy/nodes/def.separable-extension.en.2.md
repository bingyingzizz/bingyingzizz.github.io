## Notes · **draft**
⭐ **Why characteristic 0 is automatic**: if the irreducible $m$ shared a factor with $m'$, then, since $\deg m' < \deg m$, that factor would have to be $m' = 0$; but in characteristic 0 the derivative $m' = 0$ can only be zero, contradicting the irreducibility of $m$. **Real trouble appears only in characteristic $p$**: $x^{p} - t$ is irreducible over $\mathbb{F}_{p}(t)$, yet $x^{p} - t = (x - \sqrt[p]{t})^{p}$ in the splitting field — one root, with multiplicity $p$. This is an **inseparable extension**.
