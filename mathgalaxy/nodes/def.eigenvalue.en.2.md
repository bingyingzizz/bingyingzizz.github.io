## Notes · **draft**
⭐ In one sentence: **eigenvectors are the vectors that $T$ only stretches, without changing their direction**, and the eigenvalue is the stretch factor.

Equivalently: $\lambda$ is an eigenvalue $\iff$ $T - \lambda I$ is **not invertible** $\iff$ $\det(\lambda I - T) = 0$.

⭐ The eigenspace $E_\lambda$ is $T$-invariant (and on it $T$ is just "multiplication by $\lambda$"); eigenspaces for distinct eigenvalues are **linearly independent** (their sum is direct).
