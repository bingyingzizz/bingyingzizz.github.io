## Statement · **draft**
Let $X$ be a topological space, $x \in X$, and $A$ an abelian group. If $A$ is **injective** in $\mathbf{Ab}$ (i.e. $A$ is a divisible group), then the skyscraper sheaf $i_{x,*}A$ is **injective** in the category of abelian sheaves $\mathbf{Ab}(X)$.
