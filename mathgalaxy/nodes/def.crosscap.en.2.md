## Notes · **draft**
**Effect**: Adding a crosscap decreases the Euler characteristic by $1$ and makes the surface nonorientable. Adding $k\ge1$ crosscaps to a sphere yields all closed nonorientable surfaces (classification theorem).

**Examples**: $k=1$ is the projective plane. $k=2$ is the Klein bottle.

⚠ Antipodal identification changes the local structure near the boundary and gives a nonorientable surface. Gluing the disk back directly would restore the original surface.
