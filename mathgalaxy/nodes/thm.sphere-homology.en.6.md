# homology of spheres　`thm.sphere-homology`

The middle terms all vanish, so for every $p \ge 1$ we have $\tilde{H}_{p}(S^{n}) \cong \tilde{H}_{p-1}(S^{n-1})$. For $p = 0$, the end of the sequence $\tilde{H}_{0}(U) \oplus \tilde{H}_{0}(V) \to \tilde{H}_{0}(S^{n}) \to 0$ gives $\tilde{H}_{0}(S^{n}) = 0$ (when $n \ge 1$, the space $S^{n}$ is path connected).

**Conclusion of the induction.** The base case and the recurrence give $\tilde{H}_{p}(S^{n}) = \mathbb{Z}$ when $p = n$, and $0$ otherwise.
