## Statement · **draft**
Let $z_{1}, z_{2}, z_{3}$ and $w_{1}, w_{2}, w_{3}$ be three distinct points of $\widehat{\mathbb{C}}$. Then there is a **unique** Möbius transformation $T$ with $T(z_{k}) = w_{k}$ ($k = 1, 2, 3$).

The explicit form is the equality of cross-ratios:

$$(z, z_{1}; z_{2}, z_{3}) = (Tz, w_{1}; w_{2}, w_{3}).$$
