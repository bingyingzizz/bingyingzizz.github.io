## Statement · **draft**
In plain words: **$A^+$ looks like extra data, but it is determined by the spectrum itself** — "integrally closed subring $A^+$" corresponds bijectively to "the region $\bigcap U_{1,f}$ of the spectrum, viewed as a subset $U$". So $(A,A^+)$ and "the ring $A$ together with a region of its spectrum" are the same thing; this is the step that translates ring theory into geometry.
