⚠ **Note that the right side uses the rank (Betti number), not $\#H_{p}$**. Torsion (e.g. $\mathbb{Z}/2$) **does not enter** the alternating sum; the example $\mathbb{RP}^{2}$ above matches precisely because of this.

⭐ **The real value of the theorem** is not the identity itself but that the right side **depends only on homology** — hence $\chi$ is independent of the triangulation (together with `thm.simplicial-approximation`) and is a topological invariant. Conversely, to compute $\chi$ one only counts simplices, without computing homology at all.
