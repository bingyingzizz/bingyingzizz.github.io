## Statement · **draft**
Let $F \subseteq K$ be fields, with the operations of $F$ being the restrictions of the operations of $K$ to $F$. Then $K$ is called an **extension field** of $F$, written $K/F$ (read “$K$ over $F$”).

Regarding $K$ as a **vector space** over $F$ (addition from $K$, scalar multiplication the multiplication in $F$), its dimension is called the **degree**:

$$[K : F] := \dim_{F} K .$$
