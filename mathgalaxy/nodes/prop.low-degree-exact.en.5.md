# Low-Degree Exact Sequence　`prop.low-degree-exact`

[in graph](../#n=prop.low-degree-exact)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./prop.low-degree-exact.md)

⭐ **These two facts are the technical source of the concrete meaning of $H^{1}$ and $H^{2}$**: the first says that $H^{1}$ is the space of "sections / crossed homomorphisms", and the second says that the **obstruction** to splitting a short exact sequence lands exactly in $H^{1}$ — the next two classification theorems add semantics to $Z^{1}$ and $Z^{2}$.

⚠️ Do not confuse this with the **full long exact sequence**: it actually continues $\cdots \to H^{1}(G, C) \to H^{2}(G, A) \to H^{2}(G, B) \to \cdots$, and only the first terms are isolated here.
