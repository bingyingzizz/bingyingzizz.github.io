# Affine Connection　`def.affine-connection`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=def.affine-connection)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./def.affine-connection.md)

**Parallel transport and geodesics**: solving $\nabla_{\dot\gamma}V = 0$ gives the **parallel transport** of $V$ along $\gamma$; the solutions of $\nabla_{\dot\gamma}\dot\gamma = 0$ are **geodesics**. Both use only $\nabla$, with no need for an ambient space.

**Connections are not unique**: a general affine connection has infinitely many choices (any non-symmetric part of $\Gamma$ can be changed). Only adding "torsion-free + metric" makes it unique — this is the existence and uniqueness of the Levi-Civita connection (Koszul formula).
