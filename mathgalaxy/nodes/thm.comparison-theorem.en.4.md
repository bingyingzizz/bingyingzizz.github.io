# Comparison Theorem　`thm.comparison-theorem`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=thm.comparison-theorem)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./thm.comparison-theorem.md)

⚠️ The theorem gives uniqueness **up to homotopy**. Two projective resolutions of the same module can differ by a contractible complex, as when one takes a resolution and adds a contractible summand of the form $0 \to P \xrightarrow{1} P \to 0$. The two resolutions represent isomorphic objects in the derived category.

**Dual version.** Lifts between injective resolutions satisfy the same two conclusions, with the direction of lifting reversed by the extension property of injective objects. See “Lifts Between Injective Resolutions.”
