## Statement · **draft**
If $\mathcal{C}$ is a pretopos (with the precanonical topology), then the functor

$$X \mapsto h_{X}$$

gives an **equivalence** between $\mathcal{C}$ and the category of "**quasi-compact and quasi-separated** sheaves on $\mathcal{C}$".
