# isolated zeros and their order　`prop.zero-isolated`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=prop.zero-isolated)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./prop.zero-isolated.md)

Since $f$ is not identically zero, not all coefficients vanish; take the least $m$ with $a_{m} \ne 0$, then $f(z) = (z - z_{0})^{m}\bigl(a_{m} + a_{m+1}(z - z_{0}) + \cdots\bigr)$; the factor in parentheses takes at $z_{0}$ the value $a_{m} \ne 0$, and this is the required $g$.

**Isolation**: $g(z_{0}) \ne 0$ and $g$ is continuous, so on some neighbourhood of $z_{0}$ one has $g \ne 0$; hence $f$ vanishes there only at $z_{0}$ (on the punctured neighbourhood $(z - z_{0})^{m} \ne 0$).
