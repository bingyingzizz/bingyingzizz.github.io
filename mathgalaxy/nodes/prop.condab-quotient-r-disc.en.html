Statement

Write Q:=R/RdiscQ := \mathbb{R}/\mathbb{R}^{\mathrm{disc}}Q:=R/Rdisc (regard Rdisc\mathbb{R}^{\mathrm{disc}}Rdisc as an additive group with the discrete topology, R\mathbb{R}R with the usual topology, and take the quotient in condensed abelian groups). Let SSS be a Stone space. Then

Q(S)  =  C(S,R) / C(S,Rdisc),Q(S) \;=\; C(S, \mathbb{R})\,\big/\,C(S, \mathbb{R}^{\mathrm{disc}}),Q(S)=C(S,R)/C(S,Rdisc),

that is: continuous functions to R\mathbb{R}R, modulo locally constant functions.

Notes

⚠️ This quotient is not zero: as soon as there is a nonconstant continuous real-valued function on SSS (for example, when S=βIS = \beta IS=βI, take a bounded function that is not locally constant), Q(S)≠0Q(S) \ne 0Q(S)=0. This is two sides of the same fact as "topological abelian groups are not an abelian category": quotients behave badly at the level of topological groups, and only at the condensed level does the correct section formula above appear.

⭐ It is also the general shape of the "Zdisc→Zcoarse\mathbb{Z}^{\mathrm{disc}} \to \mathbb{Z}^{\mathrm{coarse}}Zdisc→Zcoarse-type phenomenon": changing the topology without changing the underlying set really does change sections of a sheaf.
