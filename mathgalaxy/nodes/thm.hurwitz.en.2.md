# Hurwitz theorem　`thm.hurwitz`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=thm.hurwitz)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./thm.hurwitz.md)

Let $\Omega$ be a region, let $f_k$ be holomorphic on $\Omega$ and converge uniformly on every compact subset of $\Omega$ to $f$. If every $f_k$ has **no zero** on $\Omega$, then the limit $f$ is either identically zero or nowhere zero on $\Omega$.

Equivalently: if $f$ is not identically zero and $f(z_0) = 0$, then for every sufficiently large $k$, $f_k$ has a zero in every small neighbourhood of $z_0$.

## Notes · **draft**
⭐ The exception "identically zero" **cannot be removed**: $f_k(z) = 1/k$ has no zeros on the unit disc and converges uniformly on compact subsets to $0$.
