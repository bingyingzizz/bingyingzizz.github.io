# representable functors preserve limits　`prop.representable-preserves-limits`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=prop.representable-preserves-limits)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./prop.representable-preserves-limits.md)

$$\operatorname{Hom}_{\mathcal{C}}\bigl(X,\ \lim D\bigr) \;\cong\; \lim \operatorname{Hom}_{\mathcal{C}}\bigl(X,\ D(-)\bigr).$$

## Notes · **draft**
Now $F \cong \operatorname{Hom}(X,-)$, and naturally isomorphic functors preserve the same limits, so $F$ also preserves all limits.
