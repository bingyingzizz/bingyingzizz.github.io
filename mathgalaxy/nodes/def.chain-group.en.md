## Statement · **draft**
Let $K$ be a simplicial complex and write $K_{p}$ for the set of all its $p$-simplices (when $K$ is **finite** this is a finite set; in general it may be infinite, and the "finite support" condition below is there for exactly this reason). The **$p$-th chain group** $C_{p}(K)$ is the **free abelian group on $K_{p}$**; concretely,

$$C_{p}(K) := \{\, n : K_{p} \to \mathbb{Z} \,\},$$
