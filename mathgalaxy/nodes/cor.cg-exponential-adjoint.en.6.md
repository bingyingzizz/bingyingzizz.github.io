# Exponential Adjunction for Compactly Generated Spaces　`cor.cg-exponential-adjoint`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=cor.cg-exponential-adjoint)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./cor.cg-exponential-adjoint.md)

at the level of topology, when $Z$ is compactly generated "continuity can be tested on compact Hausdorff pieces", and the compact pieces of $kY$ are the compact pieces of $Y$, so the compact-open topologies on $C(Y,Z)$ and $C(kY,Z)$ agree.
Combining the two gives a natural bijection, i.e. $k(X\times Y) \dashv kC(kY, -)$. ∎
**Two corollaries** (general properties of adjunctions): the left adjoint $X\mapsto k(X\times Y)$ **preserves colimits**; the right adjoint $Z\mapsto kC(kY,Z)$ **preserves limits**.
