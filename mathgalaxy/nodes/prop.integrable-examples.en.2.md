## Notes · **draft**
⭐ All three are direct applications of “the oscillation sum can be made arbitrarily small”.

⭐ Why the Dirichlet function is not integrable: every subinterval of any partition contains both **rationals** and **irrationals** (density), so $M_i = 1$, $m_i = 0$, hence $U - L = \sum \Delta x_i = b - a$ holds for every partition, and the oscillation sum never tends to zero.
