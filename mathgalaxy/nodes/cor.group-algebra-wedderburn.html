Statement draft

Let GGG be finite, char⁡F=0\operatorname{char}F=0charF=0, and FFF algebraically closed. Then F[G]≅∏i=1sMni(F)F[G]\cong\prod_{i=1}^s M_{n_i}(F)F[G]≅∏i=1s​Mni​​(F) and, as a left F[G]F[G]F[G]-module, F[G]≅⨁i=1sniViF[G]\cong\bigoplus_{i=1}^s n_iV_iF[G]≅⨁i=1s​ni​Vi​, where the ViV_iVi​ are pairwise non-isomorphic irreducible representations and ni=dim⁡Vin_i=\dim V_ini​=dimVi​.

Notes draft

Maschke's theorem gives that F[G]F[G]F[G] is semisimple. By thm.wedderburn-artin, F[G]≅∏iMni(Di)F[G]\cong\prod_i M_{n_i}(D_i)F[G]≅∏i​Mni​​(Di​). Since char⁡F=0\operatorname{char}F=0charF=0 and FFF is algebraically closed, every finite-dimensional division FFF-algebra satisfies Di=FD_i=FDi​=F: if a∈Dia\in D_ia∈Di​ lies outside FFF, then F(a)F(a)F(a) is a finite extension, contradicting algebraic closedness. As a left F[G]F[G]F[G]-module, the natural module FniF^{n_i}Fni​ of Mni(F)M_{n_i}(F)Mni​​(F) is simple, so F[G]≅⨁iniViF[G]\cong\bigoplus_i n_iV_iF[G]≅⨁i​ni​Vi​ with Vi=FniV_i=F^{n_i}Vi​=Fni​ and ni=dim⁡Vin_i=\dim V_ini​=dimVi​.
