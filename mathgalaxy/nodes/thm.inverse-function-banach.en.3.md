# Inverse Function Theorem in Banach Spaces　`thm.inverse-function-banach`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=thm.inverse-function-banach)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./thm.inverse-function-banach.md)

Then there is a neighborhood of $a$, denoted by $V$, and a neighborhood of $f(a)$, denoted by $W$, such that $f|_{V} : V \to W$ is a homeomorphism, its inverse $g$ is $C^{1}$, and $Dg(y) = (Df(g(y)))^{-1}$.

## Notes · **draft**
⭐ There is **one crucial difference** from the finite-dimensional version (`thm.inverse-function`). In finite dimensions, invertibility of $Df(a)$ automatically gives a bounded inverse (every linear map between finite-dimensional spaces is bounded, including the inverse).
