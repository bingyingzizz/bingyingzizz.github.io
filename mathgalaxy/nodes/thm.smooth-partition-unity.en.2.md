# Smooth Partition of Unity　`thm.smooth-partition-unity`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=thm.smooth-partition-unity)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./thm.smooth-partition-unity.md)

Let $M$ be a smooth manifold and let $\mathcal{U} = \{U_{\alpha}\}_{\alpha \in A}$ be an open cover of $M$. Then there is a family of smooth functions $\{\rho_{\alpha}\}_{\alpha \in A}$ such that

1. $0 \le \rho_{\alpha} \le 1$ and $\rho_{\alpha}$ is smooth.
2. $\operatorname{supp}\rho_{\alpha} \subseteq U_{\alpha}$, and the family of supports $\{\operatorname{supp}\rho_{\alpha}\}$ is **locally finite**.
3. $\sum_{\alpha \in A}\rho_{\alpha} \equiv 1$ (the locally finite sum is well-defined pointwise and is smooth).
