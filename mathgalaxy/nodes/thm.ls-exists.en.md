## Statement · **draft**
For every increasing right-continuous $F : \mathbb{R} \to \mathbb{R}$, there exists on $\mathbb{R}$ a Borel measure $\mu _F$ such that

$$\mu_F( (a, b] ) = F(b) - F(a)\quad  \forall a < b$$

and the correspondence between such $\mu _F$ and $F$ is one-to-one **up to an additive constant**.
