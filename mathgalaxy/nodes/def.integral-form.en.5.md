# Integral of a Differential Form　`def.integral-form`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=def.integral-form)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./def.integral-form.md)

**Orientation cannot be dropped**: replacing $\psi$ by an orientation-reversing map changes the sign of $\det$ and hence of the integral. Without an orientation the only integrable objects are **densities** $\lvert\omega\rvert$ — the area element $\mathrm{d}A$ is a density (`def.area-element`).

**Interface with the area element**: on a surface $\mathrm{d}A = \sqrt{\det g}\,\mathrm{d}u\wedge\mathrm{d}v$, and integrating over an oriented surface gives area.
