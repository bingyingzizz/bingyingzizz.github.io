This identifies the images of $A \to I$ and $A \to B$ in $P$. The map $I \to P$ is still a monomorphism (a pushout of a monomorphism is a monomorphism: to test whether $(x, 0)$ lies in the image, inspect only the $I$ component). By 2, $I \rightarrowtail P$ has a retraction $r$, so $r \circ (B \to P)$ is the required extension of $f$ ✓.

**2 and 3 are equivalent**: By the criterion in `def.split-exact`, a short exact sequence splits if and only if its monomorphism has a retraction ✓. ∎
