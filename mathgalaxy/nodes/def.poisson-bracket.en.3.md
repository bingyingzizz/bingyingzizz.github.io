## Notes · **draft**
**It tests whether $H$ is conserved**: $\frac{d}{dt}f = \{f, H\}$. Thus $f$ is a constant of motion $\iff$ $\{f, H\} = 0$. This algebraic condition characterizes conserved quantities in mechanics. It is also the symplectic version of Noether's theorem (symmetry $\implies$ conserved quantity).

⭐ **$C^{\infty}(M)$ is therefore a Lie algebra**: The bracket supplies the Lie bracket, and the Hamiltonian map $H \mapsto X_{H}$ is a Lie **antihomomorphism** (it is linear and reverses the bracket), that is, $[X_{f}, X_{g}] = -X_{\{f,g\}}$.
