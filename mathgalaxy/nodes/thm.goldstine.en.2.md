## Notes · **draft**
**Preparation: every weak* continuous linear functional is an evaluation.** Let $\ell$ be a continuous linear functional on $(X^{**}, w^{*})$. Continuity gives $x_1,\ldots,x_n \in X$ and $c > 0$ with $|\ell(\xi)| \le c \max_j |\xi(x_j)|$. Thus $\ell$ factors through $\xi \mapsto (\xi(x_1),\ldots,\xi(x_n))$, hence is a linear combination of these coordinates: $\ell = \sum_j c_j\, \mathrm{ev}_{x_j} = \mathrm{ev}_{\,x}$, where $x = \sum_j c_j x_j \in X$. That is, $(X^{**}, w^{*})^{*} = J_X(X)$.
