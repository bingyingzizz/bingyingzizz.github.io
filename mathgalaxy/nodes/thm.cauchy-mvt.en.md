## Statement · **draft**
Let $a < b$, and let $f, g$ be continuous on $[a, b]$ and differentiable on $(a, b)$, with $g'(x) \ne 0$ holding everywhere in $(a, b)$. Then there exists $\xi \in (a, b)$ such that

$$\frac{f(b) - f(a)}{g(b) - g(a)} = \frac{f'(\xi)}{g'(\xi)}$$
