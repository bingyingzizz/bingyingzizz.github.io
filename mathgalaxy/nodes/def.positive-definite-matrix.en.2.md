# Positive Definite Matrix　`def.positive-definite-matrix`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=def.positive-definite-matrix)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./def.positive-definite-matrix.md)

Replacing $>$ by $\ge$ gives **positive semidefinite**. In the real symmetric case replace $x^{*}$ by $x^{T}$.

**Equivalent characterizations** (the first two are the most used):

1. all eigenvalues $> 0$ (semidefinite: $\ge 0$);
2. all **leading principal minors** $> 0$ (Sylvester's criterion, thm.sylvester-criterion);
3. there exists an invertible $P$ with $A = P^{*}P$ (semidefinite: $A = P^{*}P$ need not be invertible);
4. $x \mapsto \sqrt{x^{*}Ax}$ is a norm (a seminorm in the semidefinite case).
