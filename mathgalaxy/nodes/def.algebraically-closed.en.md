## Statement · **draft**
A field $K$ is **algebraically closed** if every **nonconstant** polynomial in $K[x]$ factors into linear factors over $K[x]$ (equivalently: every nonconstant $f \in K[x]$ has a root in $K$).

If $\overline{F}/F$ is algebraic and $\overline{F}$ is algebraically closed, then $\overline{F}$ is called an **algebraic closure** of $F$.
