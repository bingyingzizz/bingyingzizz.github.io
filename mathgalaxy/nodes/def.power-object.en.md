## Statement · **draft**
Let $\mathcal{C}$ have finite products and $X, Y \in \mathcal{C}$. If there are an object $Y^{X}$ and a morphism $\operatorname{ev} : Y^{X} \times X \to Y$ such that for every $Z$ and every $f : Z \times X \to Y$ there is a **unique** $\tilde f : Z \to Y^{X}$ with

$$\operatorname{ev} \circ (\tilde f \times \operatorname{id}_{X}) = f,$$
