# The Total Complex Is Still an Injective Resolution　`prop.total-complex-injective-resolution`

## Statement · **draft**
Let $K^{\bullet,\bullet}$ be a bounded-below double complex with injective terms, whose total complex $I^{\bullet}$ together with the augmentation gives a resolution $K^{\bullet}$. Then $K^{\bullet} \to I^{\bullet}$ is **still an injective resolution**.
