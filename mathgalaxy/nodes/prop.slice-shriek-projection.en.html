Statement

Let T\mathcal{T}T be a topos and X∈TX \in \mathcal{T}X∈T. Then for M∈Ab(T/X)M \in \mathrm{Ab}(\mathcal{T}/X)M∈Ab(T/X), N∈Ab(T)N \in \mathrm{Ab}(\mathcal{T})N∈Ab(T),

jX!(M⊗ZjX−1N)=jX!M⊗ZN,j_{X!}\bigl(M \otimes_{\mathbb{Z}} j_{X}^{-1}N\bigr) = j_{X!}M \otimes_{\mathbb{Z}} N,jX!​(M⊗Z​jX−1​N)=jX!​M⊗Z​N,

Proof. The projection formula is a general phenomenon of "compatibility of a left adjoint with a closed monoidal structure": map the Hom's on both sides to the same place using jX!⊣jX−1j_{X!} \dashv j_{X}^{-1}jX!​⊣jX−1​ and the tensor–internal Hom adjunction, and Yoneda's lemma gives the isomorphism ✓.
