## Statement · **draft**
Let $M$ be a **connected** locally compact Hausdorff abelian group and $N$ a **discrete** abelian group. Then $\operatorname{Ext}^{n}_{\mathbb{Z}}(M, N) = 0$, for all $n \ne 1$.
