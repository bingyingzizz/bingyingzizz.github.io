## Notes · **draft**
**Why colimits fail.** The inclusion functor $i:\mathrm{Cond}\hookrightarrow\widehat{\mathrm{CHaus}}$ is a right adjoint and does not preserve colimits; sheafification $a$ is its left adjoint and only repairs the presheaf colimit into a sheaf, so a colimit must first be taken pointwise in presheaves and then sheafified once.
