又称集合范畴·Set 范畴·集合的范畴·Set

陈述

集合范畴 Set\mathbf{Set}Set：对象是集合，态射是函数，复合是映射复合，单位是恒等映射。 Set\mathbf{Set}Set 中积是笛卡尔积，余积是不交并；它是遗忘函子的标准靶。

说明

完备且余完备：小积与等化子给出所有小极限，小余积与余等化子给出所有小余极限。

不是加性范畴：hom⁡(A,B)\hom(A,B)hom(A,B) 一般没有群结构，不能逐点相加。

与拓扑空间范畴的关系：存在遗忘函子 Top→Set\mathbf{Top}\to\mathbf{Set}Top→Set，它保极限与余极限，因为拓扑都按底层集合逐点构造。

Statement draft

The category of sets Set\mathbf{Set}Set: objects are sets, morphisms are functions, composition is composition of maps, and identities are identity maps.

In Set\mathbf{Set}Set products are cartesian products and coproducts are disjoint unions; it is the standard target of the forgetful functor.

Notes draft

Complete and cocomplete: small products together with equalizers give all small limits; small coproducts together with coequalizers give all small colimits.

Not an additive category: hom⁡(A,B)\hom(A,B)hom(A,B) in general has no group structure, so one cannot add pointwise.

Relation to the category of topological spaces: there is a forgetful functor Top→Set\mathbf{Top}\to\mathbf{Set}Top→Set preserving limits and colimits, because the topologies are built pointwise from the underlying sets.
