Notes

⭐ A power is just a compressed notation for "multiplication written many times"; it has no new content — but it turns "nnn times" into an object one can induct on: 二项式定理, Bernoulli 不等式, geometric series and power series all induct on nnn.

⚠ Only integer exponents are defined here.

a1/2a^{1/2}a1/2 must wait for n 次方根 (n-th roots), and a2a^{\sqrt{2}}a2​ for 有理指数与实数指数幂 — the order cannot be reversed, or "taking roots" would be an undefined notion.

⚠ 00=10^{0} = 100=1 is an algebraic convention (empty product); in analysis 000^{0}00 is an indeterminate form with its own limit convention — the two do not conflict, but do not mix them.
