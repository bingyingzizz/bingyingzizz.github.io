# exact couples give spectral sequences　`thm.exact-couple-spectral`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=thm.exact-couple-spectral)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./thm.exact-couple-spectral.md)

- **Spectral sequence structure**: a derived couple is again an exact couple (the three well-definedness conditions), so one can iterate; and $E_{r+1} = H(E_{r}, d_{r})$ holds by construction.
- **Bidegree of the differential**: $i_{r}$ is always $(-1, 1)$ and $k_{r}$ always $(1, 0)$ (derivation does not change them), while $j_{r}$ gains one $(1, -1)$ at each derivation. Hence the bidegree of $j_{r}$ is $(r - 1,\ 1 - r)$, and multiplying by $k_{r}$ gives

$$d_{r} = j_{r} k_{r} : \quad (p, q) \longmapsto \bigl(p + r,\ q - (r - 1)\bigr) .$$
