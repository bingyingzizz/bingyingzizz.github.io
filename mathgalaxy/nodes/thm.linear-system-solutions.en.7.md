⚠️ **Three hypotheses that cannot be dropped**:
- "$K$ is a **field**": over $\mathbb{Z}$ the equation $2x = 1$ has no solution while $2x = 2$ has one, and the rank criterion must be replaced by the Smith normal form (or by a gcd condition for existence $\iff$).
- "**Finite-dimensional**": in infinite dimension the equality $\dim \ker A = n - \operatorname{rank} A$ fails (there is no $n$ to subtract), and one must speak of codimension.
