# open mapping theorem　`thm.open-mapping`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=thm.open-mapping)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./thm.open-mapping.md)

1. **Another formulation of the maximum modulus principle**: $|f|$ cannot attain a maximum in the interior of $\Omega$, since otherwise the image would fail to be open.
2. A holomorphic **injection** is biholomorphic onto its image (its inverse is also holomorphic).
3. If the image of $f$ is contained in an open set, then “$\operatorname{Im} f$ has empty interior” is equivalent to “$f$ is constant.”

## Notes · **draft**
**Proof**: Take $z_{0} \in \Omega$ and $w_{0} := f(z_{0})$. It suffices to show that $w_{0}$ is an interior point of $f(\Omega)$. By translation, we may assume $w_{0} = 0$.
