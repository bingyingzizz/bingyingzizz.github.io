# Cycles and Cocycles　`def.cycle`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=def.cycle)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./def.cycle.md)

**Cochain case**: the $n$-th **group of cocycles** is

$$Z^{n}(K) := \ker d^{n} = \{\, x \in K^{n} : d^{n}x = 0 \,\} ;$$

its elements are called **cocycles**.

**Chain case** (arrows reversed, $d_{n} : K_{n} \to K_{n-1}$): the $n$-th **group of cycles** is

$$Z_{n}(K) := \ker d_{n} ,$$

its elements are called **cycles**.

⚠️ The two names refer to **the same thing** (the kernel of $d$), only the grading direction of the complex differs: the kernel of a cochain complex is written $Z^{n}$, that of a chain complex $Z_{n}$.
