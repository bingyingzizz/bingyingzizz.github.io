## Notes · **draft**
**The proof is a partition of unity**: take a locally trivializing cover $\{U_{\alpha}\}$, on each $U_{\alpha}$, identify $E$ with $U_{\alpha} \times \mathbb{R}^{k}$, and take the standard inner product $\langle\cdot,\cdot\rangle_{\alpha}$;

then take a smooth partition of unity $\{\rho_{\alpha}\}$ (`thm.smooth-partition-unity`) and set $\langle s,t\rangle := \sum_{\alpha}\rho_{\alpha}\langle s,t\rangle_{\alpha}$.

This is a **convex combination**: symmetry and bilinearity are clear;
