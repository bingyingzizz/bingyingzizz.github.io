# Tensor Product of Abelian Sheaves　`def.tensor-abelian-sheaf`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=def.tensor-abelian-sheaf)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./def.tensor-abelian-sheaf.md)

$$\mathbb{Z}\cdot X \;\otimes_{\mathbb{Z}}\; \mathbb{Z}\cdot Y \;\cong\; \mathbb{Z}\cdot (X \times Y)$$

Writing $M \cdot X := M \otimes_{\mathbb{Z}} \mathbb{Z}\cdot X$, one has $\operatorname{Hom}_{\mathbb{Z}}(M, N)(X) \cong \operatorname{Hom}_{\mathbb{Z}}(M \cdot X,\ N)$ — **"evaluating at a point" = "tensoring at the point, then taking Hom"**.
