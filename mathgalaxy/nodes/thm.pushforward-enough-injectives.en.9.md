# Abelian Sheaves Have Enough Injectives　`thm.pushforward-enough-injectives`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=thm.pushforward-enough-injectives)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./thm.pushforward-enough-injectives.md)

⭐ **The shape of the argument is worth remembering**: it uses only two things, "stalks" and "direct products", so it holds for any site with "enough points": in the abelian sheaf categories of topological spaces and of topoi, **injectivity can be checked pointwise**. This is the most naive source of "sheaf cohomology can be read off stalk by stalk".

⚠️ Choosing $I_{x}$ uses that $\mathbf{Ab}$ has enough injectives (products of $\mathbb{Q}/\mathbb{Z}$ give injective hulls) — that is the Baer criterion for $\mathbf{Ab}$, not redone here.
