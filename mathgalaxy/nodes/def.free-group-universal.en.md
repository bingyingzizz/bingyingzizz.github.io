## Statement · **draft**
Let $S$ be a set, $F(S)$ the free group, and $\iota:S\to F(S)$ the inclusion. For any group $G$ and any map $f:S\to G$ there is a unique group homomorphism $\tilde f:F(S)\to G$ with $\tilde f\circ\iota=f$. Equivalently, $\operatorname{Hom}_{\mathbf{Grp}}(F(S),G)\cong\operatorname{Hom}_{\mathbf{Set}}(S,U(G))$, where $U$ is the forgetful functor.
