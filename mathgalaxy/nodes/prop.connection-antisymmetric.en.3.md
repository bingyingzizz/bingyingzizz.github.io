# Antisymmetry of the Connection Form　`prop.connection-antisymmetric`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=prop.connection-antisymmetric)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./prop.connection-antisymmetric.md)

⭐ **This is the foundation of the orthonormal frame method**: it writes the rotational degrees of freedom of $\mathrm{O}(3)$ ($3$ parameters) as $3$ $1$-forms, which is what makes the pairwise exterior products in the structure equations meaningful.

⚠ If one uses a **non-orthonormal** frame instead (such as $\{r_{u}, r_{v}, n\}$), then $g_{ij}$ is not $\delta_{ij}$, antisymmetry fails, and $\omega$ degenerates into a mixture of $\Gamma$ and $h$ — exactly the shape of the classical formulas.
