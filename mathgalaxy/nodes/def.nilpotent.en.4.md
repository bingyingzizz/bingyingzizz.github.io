# Nilpotent Operator　`def.nilpotent`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=def.nilpotent)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./def.nilpotent.md)

⚠️ Nilpotent must **not** be confused with "rank less than $n$": $\begin{pmatrix} 0 & 1 \\ 0 & 0\end{pmatrix}$ is nilpotent, but $\begin{pmatrix} 0 & 0 \\ 0 & 1\end{pmatrix}$ has rank $1$ and is not nilpotent (its eigenvalues are $1$ and $0$).

The criterion is **all eigenvalues zero**, not rank.

⚠️ A nilpotent matrix has trace and determinant both $0$, but **the converse fails** ($\begin{pmatrix} 1 & 0 \\ 0 & -1\end{pmatrix}$ has both equal to $0$ yet is not nilpotent).
