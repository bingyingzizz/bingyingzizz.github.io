Statement

What it does: it turns "at each point eat kkk tangent vectors and spit out a number, changing sign when two arguments are swapped" into a smooth object — a differential form is the kind of quantity on a manifold that can be pulled back and integrated.

Let U⊆RnU\subseteq\mathbb{R}^{n}U⊆Rn be open. A kkk-form on UUU is a map ω\omegaω assigning to each point p∈Up\in Up∈U an alternating kkk-linear form

Notes

Relation to the exterior algebra: at each point ωp∈⋀k(Rn)∗\omega_{p}\in\bigwedge^{k}(\mathbb{R}^{n})^{\ast}ωp​∈⋀k(Rn)∗, so a kkk-form is "a smooth function with values in ⋀k(Rn)∗\bigwedge^{k}(\mathbb{R}^{n})^{\ast}⋀k(Rn)∗".

The exterior algebra supplies the algebraic structure, smoothness the analytic structure;

only together do they allow the definition of d\mathrm{d}d (外微分（欧氏开集）).

Difference from a general multilinear form: a general kkk-linear form has nkn^{k}nk components; after imposing alternation only (nk)\binom{n}{k}(kn​) remain — exactly why it can be integrated (形式的积分).

The everyday identity of low-degree forms (n=3n = 3n=3): a 000-form is a function; a 111-form P dx+Q dy+R dzP\,\mathrm{d}x+Q\,\mathrm{d}y+R\,\mathrm{d}zPdx+Qdy+Rdz is work and circulation; a 222-form is flux; a 333-form is density. Gradient, curl, and divergence are the same d\mathrm{d}d at different degrees.

⚠️ Direction: forms travel against a map (pullback, 拉回（开集间）), while vector fields travel with it (pushforward: along a diffeomorphism, or when FFF-related). This is the fundamental reason forms are easier to use than vector fields.

On manifolds: replace Rn\mathbb{R}^{n}Rn by an nnn-dimensional manifold MMM; one writes everything in coordinate differentials on a chart, then uses the smoothness of transition maps to show that "being a kkk-form" is chart-independent — the same argument as "tangent vectors are chart-independent" in 切空间.
