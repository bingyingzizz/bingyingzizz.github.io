# Eigenspace　`def.eigenspace`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=def.eigenspace)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./def.eigenspace.md)

⭐ **The generalized eigenspace** is its generalization: iterate $\ker(T - \lambda I)^{k}$ until it stabilizes, and the dimension is exactly the algebraic multiplicity. The blocks of the Jordan form are organized accordingly (see thm.primary-decomposition).

⚠️ Notational conventions differ: some books write $E_{\lambda}$ (eigenspace), others $V(\lambda)$. This graph uses $V_{\lambda}$, aligning with the notation of def.eigenvalue.
