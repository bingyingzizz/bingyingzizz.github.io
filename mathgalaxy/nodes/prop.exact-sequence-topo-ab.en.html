Statement

Suppose

(Compact lifting condition) For every compact Hausdorff K′′⊆M′′K'' \subseteq M''K′′⊆M′′, there exists a compact Hausdorff K⊆MK \subseteq MK⊆M such that K′′⊆v(K)K'' \subseteq v(K)K′′⊆v(K).

Notes

Left exactness is automatic because the functor in the previous proposition preserves all limits. At the right end, v‾\underline{v}v​ must be an epimorphism. Epimorphisms of condensed objects are detected by pointwise surjectivity on free compact Hausdorff spaces, so this reduces to the compact lifting condition.

📌 Local compactness ensures the compact lifting condition. Thus an entire exact sequence of locally compact abelian groups can be transferred.
