$$\operatorname{sd}_{0} := [v_{0}],\qquad \operatorname{sd}_{p} := [b_{p},\ \operatorname{sd}_{p-1}(\partial\Delta^{p})],$$

Here $b_{p}$ is the barycenter of $\Delta^{p}$, $[b, c]$ denotes coning with apex $b$ over the chain $c$, and $\partial\Delta^{p} = \sum_{i}(-1)^{i}\Delta^{p}_{i}$ is the standard boundary. The cone formula $\partial[b, c] = c - [b, \partial c]$ gives

$$\partial\operatorname{sd}_{p} = \operatorname{sd}_{p-1}(\partial\Delta^{p}).$$
