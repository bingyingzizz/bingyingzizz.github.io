## Statement · **draft**
Let $V, W$ be finite-dimensional inner product spaces and $T : V \to W$ linear. Then there is a **unique** linear map $T^{\ast} : W \to V$ such that

$$\langle T v, w \rangle_W = \langle v, T^{\ast} w \rangle_V \qquad (\forall v \in V, w \in W)$$

$T^{\ast}$ is called the **adjoint** of $T$. In an orthonormal basis, $[T^{\ast}] = [T]^{\dagger}$ (conjugate transpose).
