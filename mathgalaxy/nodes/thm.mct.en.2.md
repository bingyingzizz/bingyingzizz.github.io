# monotone convergence theorem　`thm.mct`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=thm.mct)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./thm.mct.md)

Compare with the Riemann integral: there "exchanging integral and limit" needs a strong condition such as uniform convergence; here **monotonicity** alone suffices — that is the entire advantage of the Lebesgue integral.

⚠ Both sides may be $\infty$; the identity is understood in the extended sense.
