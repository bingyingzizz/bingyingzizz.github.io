# solvable by radicals　`thm.radical-solvable`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=thm.radical-solvable)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./thm.radical-solvable.md)

⭐ **Why characteristic zero is required**: Both directions use that $x^{m}-1$ has no repeated roots (so $\sigma$ is diagonalizable), as well as separability. In characteristic $p$, pure radical extensions must be replaced by Artin–Schreier extensions $x^{p}-x-a$, and the conclusion must be reformulated.
