## Notes · **draft**
⭐ **Why normality.** A coset $gH$ is a **set**, and to turn the quotient set into a group via “$gH \cdot g'H := gg'H$” the definition must be **independent of the choice of representatives**. Compute where the difference lies:

$$(g h)(g' h') = g g' \cdot \bigl((g')^{-1} h g'\bigr) h',$$

The element $(g')^{-1} h g'$ in the rightmost bracket must still lie in $H$ — “$H$ is closed under conjugation” is exactly normality. **Every subgroup of an abelian group is normal** (conjugation does nothing), so this step never causes trouble in an abelian group.
