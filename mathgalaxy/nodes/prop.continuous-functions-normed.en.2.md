# Continuous Functions Form a Normed Banach Group　`prop.continuous-functions-normed`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=prop.continuous-functions-normed)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./prop.continuous-functions-normed.md)

1. The continuous maps $C(S, M) := \operatorname{Hom}_{\mathrm{top}}(S, M)$, equipped with
$$\|f\| := \sup_{x \in S}\|f(x)\|$$
form a (semi)normed abelian group (respectively, a Banach abelian group);
2. If $S$ is also a **Stone** space, then $C(S, M^{\mathrm{disc}})$ is **dense** in $C(S, M)$.
