## Statement · **draft**
For pairwise distinct $z_{1}, z_{2}, z_{3}, z_{4} \in \widehat{\mathbb{C}}$, define their **cross-ratio**

$$(z_{1}, z_{2}; z_{3}, z_{4}) := \frac{N}{D},$$

where $N := (z_{3} - z_{1})(z_{4} - z_{2})$, $D := (z_{3} - z_{2})(z_{4} - z_{1})$.

When $\infty$ occurs, interpret the expression by a limit (cancel the factors containing $\infty$).
