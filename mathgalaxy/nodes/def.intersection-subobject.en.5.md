# intersection of subobjects　`def.intersection-subobject`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=def.intersection-subobject)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./def.intersection-subobject.md)

**Existence**: the intersection is a fibred product, so "the intersection exists" iff the fibred product along $X$ exists; in a category without fibred products one cannot speak of intersections. The pullback of a monomorphism along any morphism is still a monomorphism (see @@def.mono@@), so $Y \times_{X} Z \to X$ really is a subobject.
