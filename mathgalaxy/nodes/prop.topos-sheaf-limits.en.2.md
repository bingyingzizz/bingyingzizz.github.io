## Notes · **draft**
The proof is one line: if $R$ is a covering sieve of $X$, then $X \cong \varinjlim_{X' \to X \in R} X'$ — that sentence is precisely the definition of a covering; sending it through $F$ gives $F(X) \cong \varprojlim_{X' \in \mathcal{T}/R} F(X')$, the sheaf condition.

So on a topos, "**sheaf**" and "**presheaf that sends colimits back to limits**" are the same thing.

The two conditions of sheaf theory (separatedness + gluing) here merge into the single word "continuous".
