# Hom in the Homotopy and Derived Categories　`prop.hom-k-eq-hom-d`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=prop.hom-k-eq-hom-d)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./prop.hom-k-eq-hom-d.md)

**Surjectivity.** A morphism in $D$ is a roof $K^{\bullet} \xleftarrow{\;s\;} K'^{\bullet} \xrightarrow{\;q\;} I^{\bullet}$ ($s$ a quasi-isomorphism).

By the lemma "right multiplication by $s$" is surjective, so there exists $p : K^{\bullet} \to I^{\bullet}$ with $p s \simeq q$ — the roof and the direct morphism $p$ represent the same morphism in $D$.
