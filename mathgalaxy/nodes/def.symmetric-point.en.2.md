# symmetric point with respect to a circle　`def.symmetric-point`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=def.symmetric-point)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./def.symmetric-point.md)

(Equivalently: $z^{*}$ lies on the ray $a + t(z - a)$ ($t > 0$), and $|z - a|\,|z^{*} - a| = R^{2}$.)

Symmetry with respect to a **line** $L$ is the usual mirror image; regarding $L$ as a circle through $\infty$, the two cases combine into one statement: "$z, z^{*}$ are symmetric with respect to $C$ $\iff$ every circle through $z, z^{*}$ is orthogonal to $C$."
