# the integrally closed subring is determined by the spectrum　`prop.huber-subring-recovery`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=prop.huber-subring-recovery)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./prop.huber-subring-recovery.md)

📌 The proof uses existence of valuation rings (Zariski's lemma): if $f \notin A^+$, take a prime ideal in $A^+[1/f]$ and then a valuation ring dominating it, producing a point with $|f| > 1$.
