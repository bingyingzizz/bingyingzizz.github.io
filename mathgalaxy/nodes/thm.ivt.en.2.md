# intermediate value theorem　`thm.ivt`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=thm.ivt)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./thm.ivt.md)

⭐ This is the most frequently used existence tool in elementary analysis: existence of roots, of inverse functions, of fixed points, and comparison of intermediate values all follow from it.

It also has **constructive** content: the bisection method used in the proof is itself a root-finding algorithm, and every step is computable.

⚠ It guarantees only **existence**, not uniqueness ($x^{3} - x$ has three zeros on $[-2, 2]$);

uniqueness requires an additional assumption of **strict monotonicity**.
