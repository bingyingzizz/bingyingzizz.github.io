# Weingarten Map (Shape Operator)　`def.weingarten-map`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=def.weingarten-map)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./def.weingarten-map.md)

The notation $W\colon r_{i} \mapsto -n_{i}$ is exactly this convention.

**Third fundamental form**: $III(v,w) := \langle \mathrm{d}n(v), \mathrm{d}n(w)\rangle = \langle W(v), W(w)\rangle$.

The characteristic equation $W^{2}-2HW+K\,\mathrm{id} = 0$ of $W$ translates, as a quadratic-form identity, into $III-2H\,II+K\,I = 0$ (Cayley–Hamilton).
