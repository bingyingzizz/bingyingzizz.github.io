## Statement · **draft**
$\theta(x)=\pi(x)\log x-\int_{2}^{x}\frac{\pi(t)}{t}\,\mathrm{d}t$, $\pi(x)=\frac{\theta(x)}{\log x}+\int_{2}^{x}\frac{\theta(t)}{t\log^{2}t}\,\mathrm{d}t$ ($x\ge2$).

## Notes · **draft**
Obtained from Stieltjes summation by parts (with $f=\log t$ and $f=1/\log t$) (Stieltjes summation by parts).
