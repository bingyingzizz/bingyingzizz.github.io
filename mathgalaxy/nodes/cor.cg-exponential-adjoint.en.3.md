# Exponential Adjunction for Compactly Generated Spaces　`cor.cg-exponential-adjoint`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=cor.cg-exponential-adjoint)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./cor.cg-exponential-adjoint.md)

⭐ **Colimits in $k\mathbf{Top}$ are universal.** Because "multiplying by $Y$" has a right adjoint, it commutes with colimits, and this is exactly what is required for colimits to be universal (pulling a colimit back along any morphism is again a colimit). **This fails in $\mathbf{Top}$** — another reason one "has to work in $k\mathbf{Top}$".
