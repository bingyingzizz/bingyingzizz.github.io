**Hypotheses used in each direction** (the open mapping theorem is an analytic result, and the equivalence between constancy and empty interior is a corollary):
- Being an open map $\iff$ having open image for every open set follows in both directions by definition. The implication nonconstant and holomorphic $\implies$ open uses isolated zeros and Rouché's theorem, comparing $f-f(z_0)$ with $f-f(z_0)-w$.
