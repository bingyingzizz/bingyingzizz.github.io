# simplicial approximation　`thm.simplicial-approximation`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=thm.simplicial-approximation)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./thm.simplicial-approximation.md)

- The **finiteness** of $K$ is essential. For infinite complexes, repeated barycentric subdivision need not make the mesh tend to zero (an infinite sequence of subdivisions can remain coarse), so the star condition may fail.

- $L$ need not be finite. The theorem only uses that the vertex stars of $L$ form an open cover.

- The open star $\operatorname{st}(w)$ is generally not convex, so one cannot justify the homotopy by joining points in $\operatorname{st}(w)$. Part (ii) of Lemma 1 uses that $f(x), g(x)$ lie in a common **closed simplex** $\rho$, and $\rho$ is convex.
