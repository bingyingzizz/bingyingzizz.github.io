# extension group　`def.ext`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=def.ext)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./def.ext.md)

- $\operatorname{Ext}^{n}(M, N) = 0$ ($n < 0$);
- $\operatorname{Ext}^{0}(M, N) = \operatorname{Hom}(M, N)$;
- $\operatorname{Ext}^{1}(M, N)$ **classifies extensions of $N$ by $M$** (up to isomorphism);
- in $\mathbf{Ab}$, $\operatorname{Ext}^{n}(M, N) = 0$ ($n > 1$);
- $\operatorname{Ext}^{1}(\mathbb{Z}/n\mathbb{Z},\ \mathbb{Z}/m\mathbb{Z}) = \mathbb{Z}/d\mathbb{Z}$, $d = \gcd(m, n)$.

⭐ **Two algorithms**: $\operatorname{Ext}^{n} = $ "Hom in the derived category" (this definition) $ = R^{n}\operatorname{Hom}$ (computed by injective resolutions). They agree — see the next item.
