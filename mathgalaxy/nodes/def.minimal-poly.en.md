## Statement · **draft**
Let $\dim V < \infty$ and $T : V \to V$. The set

$$I_T := \{\, p \in K[x] : p(T) = 0 \,\}$$

is a non-zero ideal of $K[x]$ (by Cayley–Hamilton, $\chi_T \in I_T$). Since $K[x]$ is a principal ideal domain, there is a **unique** monic polynomial $\mu_T$ with $I_T = (\mu_T)$.
