取 III 为离散范畴（只有对象，没有非恒等的态射），图 D:I→CD : I \to \mathcal{C}D:I→C 就只是一族对象 (Xi)i∈I(X_i)_{i \in I}(Xi​)i∈I​。它的极限叫积 ∏iXi\prod_{i} X_i∏i​Xi​，余极限叫余积 ∐iXi\coprod_{i} X_i∐i​Xi​。泛性质写成同构集就是

说明

一句话：积就是「一个映射 = 一族映射」。射入积的映射与「分别射入每个分量的映射组成的族」一一对应，投影 pip_ipi​ 就是取出第 iii 个分量。

例：Set\mathbf{Set}Set 里积是笛卡尔积，余积是无交并；Top\mathbf{Top}Top 里积取乘积拓扑（使它成为积的最粗的拓扑），余积取不交并拓扑（最细的那个）；Ab\mathbf{Ab}Ab 里积是直积 ∏iAi\prod_i A_i∏i​Ai​，余积是直和 ⨁iAi\bigoplus_i A_i⨁i​Ai​。

Statement draft

Take III to be a discrete category (objects only, no non-identity morphisms); then the diagram D:I→CD : I \to \mathcal{C}D:I→C is just a family of objects (Xi)i∈I(X_i)_{i \in I}(Xi​)i∈I​. Its limit is called the product ∏iXi\prod_{i} X_i∏i​Xi​, and its colimit the coproduct ∐iXi\coprod_{i} X_i∐i​Xi​. Written as an isomorphism of hom-sets, the universal property is

Hom⁡C(Y,∏iXi)  ≅  ∏iHom⁡C(Y,Xi)\operatorname{Hom}_{\mathcal{C}}\Bigl(Y, \prod_{i} X_i\Bigr) \;\cong\; \prod_{i} \operatorname{Hom}_{\mathcal{C}}(Y, X_i)HomC​(Y,i∏​Xi​)≅i∏​HomC​(Y,Xi​)

Notes draft

In one sentence: a product is "one map = a family of maps". Maps into the product correspond bijectively to families of maps into each factor, and the projection pip_ipi​ extracts the iii-th component.

Examples: in Set\mathbf{Set}Set the product is the Cartesian product and the coproduct the disjoint union; in Top\mathbf{Top}Top the product carries the product topology (the coarsest topology making it a product) and the coproduct the disjoint-union topology (the finest one); in Ab\mathbf{Ab}Ab the product is the direct product ∏iAi\prod_i A_i∏i​Ai​ and the coproduct the direct sum ⨁iAi\bigoplus_i A_i⨁i​Ai​.

  • 本页 ⟹ 代数范畴的余极限推出

    构造。 先看余积：Mon\mathbf{Mon}Mon 的自由积取字并置后对「单位与运算相容」生成同余取商；Grp\mathbf{Grp}Grp 的自由积同理（取正规闭包）；Rng\mathbf{Rng}Rng 的自由积取非交换多项式环；AlgR\mathbf{Alg}_RAlgR​ 的自由积取张量代数再商关系；G-SetG\text{-}\mathbf{Set}G-Set 取不交并；R-ModR\text{-}\mathbf{Mod}R-Mod 取直和。每种的包含映射都满足余积泛性质。

    余等化子：对 f,g:A→Bf,g:A\to Bf,g:A→B，在 Set\mathbf{Set}Set 中取商 B/∼B/\simB/∼（f(a)∼g(a)f(a)\sim g(a)f(a)∼g(a) 生成），再取使商映射成为该范畴态射的最细结构：幺半群取同余闭包、群取正规闭包、环取代数理想、GGG-集取生成等价关系、模取子模、代数取双边理想。商对象满足余等化子的泛性质。
