# Riesz representation via perfect pairings　`thm.riesz-perfect-pairing`

**Proof of (b)**: Suppose $T$ is surjective and $Y$ is reflexive. The map $T$ is an isometric isomorphism $X \to Y^*$, so $T^{*} : Y^{**} \to X^{*}$ is an isometric isomorphism. Also, $S = T^{*} \circ J_Y$ (to check this, use $T^{*} J_Y y (x) = J_Y y(T x) = \langle x, y\rangle = S y(x)$). Reflexivity of $Y$ means that $J_Y$ is surjective (an isometric isomorphism), so $S = T^{*} \circ J_Y$ is surjective. Dualizing gives the symmetric case. $\blacksquare$
