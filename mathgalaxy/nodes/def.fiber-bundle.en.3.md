# Fiber Bundle　`def.fiber-bundle`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=def.fiber-bundle)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./def.fiber-bundle.md)

$$\Phi_{\alpha}\circ\Phi_{\beta}^{-1}\,(b, y) = \bigl(b,\ g_{\alpha\beta}(b)\,y\bigr),$$

where $g_{\alpha\beta} : U_{\alpha}\cap U_{\beta}\to\operatorname{Diff}(F)$ is the **transition function**. They satisfy the cocycle conditions $g_{\alpha\alpha} = \operatorname{id}$ and $g_{\alpha\beta}\,g_{\beta\gamma}\,g_{\gamma\alpha} = \operatorname{id}$ (on overlaps), and they measure how twisted the bundle is.

If a Lie group $G$ acts on $F$ and the trivializations can always be chosen so that all $g_{\alpha\beta}$ take values in $G$, one says the bundle has **structure group** $G$.
