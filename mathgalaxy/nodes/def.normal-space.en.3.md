# normal space　`def.normal-space`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=def.normal-space)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./def.normal-space.md)

The **proof** runs along two steps, "compact $\implies$ paracompact" plus "paracompact $+$ Hausdorff $\implies$ normal", see `thm.paracompact-hausdorff-normal`; ⚠️ **normality is not hereditary**: a subspace of a normal space need not be normal. This differs from both "compact" and "Hausdorff".
