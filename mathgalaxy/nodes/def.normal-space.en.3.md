# normal space　`def.normal-space`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=def.normal-space)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./def.normal-space.md)

**Compact Hausdorff $\implies$ normal** (the most frequently used consequence of compactness: in a compact space two disjoint closed sets can be separated by finitely many pairs of open sets). Hence spaces such as $[0,1]$ and $\beta I$ are normal — so Urysohn's lemma always applies there.

The **full proof** runs along two steps, "compact $\implies$ paracompact" plus "paracompact $+$ Hausdorff $\implies$ normal", see `thm.paracompact-hausdorff-normal`; ⚠️ **normality is not hereditary**: a subspace of a normal space need not be normal. This differs from both "compact" and "Hausdorff".
