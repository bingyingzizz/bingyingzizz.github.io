又称表示的零化子·零化子理想·忠实表示的零化子

陈述

对 AAA 的表示 VVV，定义 Ann⁡(V)={a∈A:aV=0}\operatorname{Ann}(V)=\{a\in A: aV=0\}Ann(V)={a∈A:aV=0}。它是 AAA 的双边理想，等于代数同态 θ:A→End⁡F(V)\theta:A\to\operatorname{End}_F(V)θ:A→EndF​(V) 的核；若 Ann⁡(V)=0\operatorname{Ann}(V)=0Ann(V)=0 则称 VVV 忠实。

说明

Ann⁡(V)\operatorname{Ann}(V)Ann(V) 是结构映射的核，故为理想。 忠实等价于 A→End⁡F(V)A\to\operatorname{End}_F(V)A→EndF​(V) 单射。 单模的零化子是原初理想；有限维半单情形这些恰是极大双边理想。 直和的零化子是各零化子之交。

Statement draft

For a representation VVV of AAA, define Ann⁡(V)={a∈A:aV=0}\operatorname{Ann}(V)=\{a\in A: aV=0\}Ann(V)={a∈A:aV=0}. It is a two-sided ideal of AAA, equal to the kernel of the algebra homomorphism θ:A→End⁡F(V)\theta:A\to\operatorname{End}_F(V)θ:A→EndF​(V); VVV is called faithful if Ann⁡(V)=0\operatorname{Ann}(V)=0Ann(V)=0.

Notes draft

The set Ann⁡(V)\operatorname{Ann}(V)Ann(V) is the kernel of the structure map, hence an ideal. Faithfulness is equivalent to injectivity of A→End⁡F(V)A\to\operatorname{End}_F(V)A→EndF​(V). The annihilator of a simple module is a primitive ideal; in the finite-dimensional semisimple case these are exactly the maximal two-sided ideals. The annihilator of a direct sum is the intersection of the annihilators.
