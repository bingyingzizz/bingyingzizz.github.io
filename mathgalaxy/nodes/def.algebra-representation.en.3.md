## Notes · **stale**
The condition $\theta(1_A)=\operatorname{id}_M$ is part of unitality. Under this equivalence, $A$-linear maps are exactly morphisms of representations, so $\mathrm{Rep}_A^{\mathrm{uni}}$ is equivalent to $A\text{-}\mathrm{Mod}$. The natural action of $M_n(F)$ on $F^n$ is a basic example. An $F[x_1,\ldots,x_n]$-module structure is the same as $n$ commuting endomorphisms $T_i$.
