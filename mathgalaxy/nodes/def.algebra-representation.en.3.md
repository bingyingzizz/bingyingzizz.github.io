## Notes · **draft**
A general representation only requires $\theta$ to preserve addition and multiplication, not the unit. The condition $\theta(1_A)=\operatorname{id}_M$ makes $M$ a left $A$-module; under this equivalence $A$-linear maps are exactly morphisms of representations. The natural action of $M_n(F)$ on $F^n$ is a basic example. An $F[x_1,\ldots,x_n]$-module structure is the same as $n$ commuting endomorphisms $T_i$.
