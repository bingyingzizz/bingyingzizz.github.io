# spectral theorem for real symmetric operators　`thm.spectral-theorem`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=thm.spectral-theorem)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./thm.spectral-theorem.md)

Moreover, all eigenvalues $\lambda_i$ are **real**. Matrix form: for a real symmetric matrix $A$ there is an orthogonal matrix $Q$ such that $Q^{\mathsf{T}} A Q = \operatorname{diag}(\lambda_1, \ldots, \lambda_n)$.

## Notes · **draft**
⭐ Difference from Jordan form: Jordan guarantees only "similarity", while the spectral theorem guarantees "**unitary/orthogonal** similarity", at the price of tightening the hypothesis to self-adjoint (normal).

These are the answers to two different classification problems in finite dimension.
