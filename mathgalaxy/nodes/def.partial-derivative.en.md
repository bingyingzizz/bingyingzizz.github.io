## Statement · **draft**
Let $U \subseteq \mathbb{R}^{n}$ be open, $f : U \to \mathbb{R}^{m}$, $a \in U$. The **partial derivative** of $f$ at $a$ with respect to the $j$-th variable is (if the limit exists)

$$\frac{\partial f}{\partial x_j}(a) := \lim_{h \to 0} \frac{f(a + h e_j) - f(a)}{h}$$
