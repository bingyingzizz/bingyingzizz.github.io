Statement

Let VVV be an inner product space and u,v∈Vu, v \in Vu,v∈V. Then

∣⟨u,v⟩∣≤∥u∥ ∥v∥|\langle u, v \rangle| \le \|u\|\,\|v\|∣⟨u,v⟩∣≤∥u∥∥v∥
