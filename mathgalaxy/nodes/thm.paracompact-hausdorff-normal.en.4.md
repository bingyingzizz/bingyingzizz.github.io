# Paracompact Hausdorff Spaces Are Normal　`thm.paracompact-hausdorff-normal`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=thm.paracompact-hausdorff-normal)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./thm.paracompact-hausdorff-normal.md)

Since the closure of the union of a locally finite family equals the union of the closures, $\overline{W} = \bigcup \{\, \overline{C} : C \cap F \ne \emptyset \,\}$ does not contain $x$.

Thus $W \ni F$ and $X \setminus \overline{W} \ni x$ are disjoint open sets separating $F$ and $x$.
**Step 2: Regular $+$ paracompact $\implies$ normal.** Let $A, B$ be disjoint closed sets.

For every $x \in A$, regularity ($x \notin B$) gives an open set $O_{x} \ni x$ such that $\overline{O_{x}} \cap B = \emptyset$.
