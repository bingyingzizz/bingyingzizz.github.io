Hence one may differentiate repeatedly — a power series is C∞C^{\infty}C∞ on its interval of convergence, and every derivative is determined by the coefficients: an=f(n)(x0)/n!a_n = f^{(n)}(x_0)/n!an​=f(n)(x0​)/n!.
