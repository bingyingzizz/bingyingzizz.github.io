# Rouché's theorem　`thm.rouche`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=thm.rouche)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./thm.rouche.md)

⚠ **"Simple" cannot be dropped**: the assertion is about the number of zeros inside (unweighted). For a general closed curve the correct statement is weighted by the winding number — by the argument principle $\dfrac{1}{2\pi i}\oint f'/f$ is always a weighted sum, and the two sides agree only when the winding number at every interior point is $= 1$ (see the notes to `thm.argument-principle`).
