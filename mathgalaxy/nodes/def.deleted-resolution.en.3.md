# Deleted Resolution　`def.deleted-resolution`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=def.deleted-resolution)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./def.deleted-resolution.md)

$$\operatorname{Ext}^{n}_{R}(M, N) = H^{n}\bigl(\operatorname{Hom}_{R}(P_{\bullet}, N)\bigr), \qquad \operatorname{Tor}_{n}^{R}(M, N) = H_{n}\bigl(P_{\bullet} \otimes_{R} N\bigr).$$

Both occurrences of $H$ on the right-hand sides use the deleted resolution, with the term $M$ omitted. See “Computing Ext in Either Variable” and “Tor.”

**Two details that are easy to get wrong.** ① The deleted resolution **is no longer exact**: $H_{0}(P_{\bullet}) = P_{0}/\operatorname{im} d_{1} \cong M$, and all higher homology vanishes. Its exactness thus differs from that of the original complex only in degree $0$.
