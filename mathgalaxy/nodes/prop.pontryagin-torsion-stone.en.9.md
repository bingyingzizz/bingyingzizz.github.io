**Hypotheses used in each direction** (read Stonean in the original text as Stone. The two terms denote different properties):
- Torsion $\implies$ Stone dual: A discrete torsion abelian group is the filtered union of its finitely generated finite subgroups. Duality turns it into an inverse limit of finite groups, so it is compact Hausdorff and totally disconnected. This uses duality and inverse limit theory.
- Stone dual $\implies$ torsion: A compact totally disconnected group has a neighborhood basis of open subgroups. Every circle-valued character has open kernel by the no-small-subgroups property o
