# pretopology　`def.pretopology`

$$\bigl(\, X_{i} \times_{X} Y \to Y \,\bigr)_{i \in I} \in \operatorname{Cov}(Y)$$

3. **Transitivity**: if $(X_{i} \to X)_{i \in I} \in \operatorname{Cov}(X)$ and for each $i$ one has $(X_{ij} \to X_{i})_{j \in J_{i}} \in \operatorname{Cov}(X_{i})$, then $(X_{ij} \to X)_{i \in I, j \in J_{i}} \in \operatorname{Cov}(X)$.

## Notes · **draft**
The three axioms read as one sentence: **isomorphisms cover, covers pull back, and covers of covers are covers**.

Example: on $\mathbf{Set}$ take $\operatorname{Cov}(X) = \{(X_{i} \to X) : X = \bigcup_{i} X_{i}\}$; all three hold — this is the prototype of the word "cover".
