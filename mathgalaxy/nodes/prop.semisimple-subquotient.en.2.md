## Notes · **draft**
For a subrepresentation $W$ of $V=\oplus V_i$, one shows that $W$ is the sum of its intersections with the $V_i$, hence semisimple. Quotients are handled with complements and the equivalent characterizations. Iterating, all subquotients of a semisimple representation are semisimple. This result is used in the proof of Wedderburn–Artin.
