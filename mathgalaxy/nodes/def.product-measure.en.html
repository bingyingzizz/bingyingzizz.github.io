Abstract

For measure spaces (X,M,μ)(X, \mathcal{M}, \mu)(X,M,μ) and (Y,N,ν)(Y, \mathcal{N}, \nu)(Y,N,ν): rectangles A×BA \times BA×B generate M⊗N\mathcal{M} \otimes \mathcal{N}M⊗N; on finite disjoint unions of rectangles set μ0(⨆jAj×Bj):=∑jμ(Aj)ν(Bj)\mu_0(\bigsqcup_j A_j \times B_j) := \sum_j \mu(A_j)\nu(B_j)μ0​(⨆j​Aj​×Bj​):=∑j​μ(Aj​)ν(Bj​), which is a premeasure; extend it to an outer measure and restrict to M⊗N\mathcal{M} \otimes \mathcal{N}M⊗N. That restriction is the product measure μ×ν\mu \times \nuμ×ν. Disjointness is exactly what makes μ0\mu_0μ0​ well defined. When both factors are σ\sigmaσ-finite so is the product, and Carathéodory uniqueness makes it the only such measure.

Statement

Let (X,M,μ)(X, \mathcal{M}, \mu )(X,M,μ) and (Y,N,ν)(Y, \mathcal{N}, \nu )(Y,N,ν) be measure spaces.

① Rectangles: sets of the form A×BA \times BA×B (with A∈MA \in \mathcal{M}A∈M, B∈NB \in \mathcal{N}B∈N) are called (measurable) rectangles. All rectangles generate M⊗N\mathcal{M} \otimes \mathcal{N}M⊗N.

② Premeasure on rectangles: let A\mathcal{A}A be the collection of all finite disjoint unions of rectangles. For

E=⨆j=1n(Aj×Bj)E = \bigsqcup_{j=1}^{n} (A_j \times B_j)E=j=1⨆n​(Aj​×Bj​)

define

μ0(E):=∑j=1nμ(Aj)ν(Bj)\mu_{0} (E) := \sum_{j=1}^{n} \mu(A_j) \nu(B_j)μ0​(E):=j=1∑n​μ(Aj​)ν(Bj​)

Then μ0\mu_{0}μ0​ is a premeasure on the algebra A\mathcal{A}A.

③ Extension: μ0\mu_{0}μ0​ induces an outer measure on X×YX \times YX×Y, and restricting it to M⊗N\mathcal{M} \otimes \mathcal{N}M⊗N gives a measure, written

μ×ν\mu \times \nuμ×ν

that is, the product measure.

Notes

Well-definedness of μ0\mu_{0} μ0​ has to be checked (the same EEE can be split into different disjoint unions of rectangles) — this is exactly why "rectangles" are replaced by "disjoint unions of rectangles": under disjoint unions, additivity is easy to verify.

Everything goes through when the measures are σ\sigmaσ-finite: if μ\muμ and ν\nuν are both σ\sigmaσ-finite, then μ×ν\mu \times \nuμ×ν is σ\sigmaσ-finite as well, and by Carathéodory uniqueness the measure constructed this way is unique.

⚠ The whole machine of this step (premeasure →\to→ outer measure →\to→ Carathéodory extension →\to→ uniqueness) is exactly the off-the-shelf quartet from the "constructing measures" cluster.
