# split short exact sequence　`def.split-exact`

[in graph](../#n=def.split-exact)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./def.split-exact.md)

Together with $p i = 0$, the maps $p_{1} := r$, $i_{1} := i$, $p_{2} := p$, and $i_{2} := s$ satisfy the three biproduct identities ($r i = 1$, $p s = 1$, $i r + s p = 1_{M}$, see ‘The direct sum is a product’). Thus $M$ is precisely $M_{1} \oplus M_{2}$ and the sequence splits ✓.
- **Splits $\implies$ has a section**: If the sequence splits, it is isomorphic to $0 \to M_{1} \xrightarrow{\ \mathrm{pr}_{1}\ } M_{1} \oplus M_{2} \xrightarrow{\ \mathrm{pr}_{2}\ } M_{2} \to 0$, and $\mathrm{pr}_{2}$ has an evident section (the inclusion into the second summand). Transport it along the isomorphism to obtain a section of $p$ ✓.
