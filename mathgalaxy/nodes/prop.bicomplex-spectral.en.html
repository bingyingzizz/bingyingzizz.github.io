Statement

Let K∙,∙K^{\bullet,\bullet}K∙,∙ be a bicomplex that is bounded below in both directions. Then there is a spectral sequence

Ep,q0  =  Kp,q  ⟹  Hp+q(K∙),E^{0}_{p,q} \;=\; K^{p,q} \;\Longrightarrow\; H^{p+q}(K^{\bullet}),Ep,q0​=Kp,q⟹Hp+q(K∙),

where K∙K^{\bullet}K∙ is the total complex Kn:=⨁p+q=nKp,qK^{n} := \bigoplus_{p+q=n} K^{p,q}Kn:=⨁p+q=n​Kp,q with differential d+d′d + d'd+d′ (with signs).

Notes

⭐ The next page comes from computing cohomology row by row and then column by column. Taking cohomology in the vertical direction gives

Ep,q1  =  Hq(Kp,∙),E^{1}_{p,q} \;=\; H^{q}\bigl(K^{p,\bullet}\bigr),Ep,q1​=Hq(Kp,∙),

and then taking cohomology in the horizontal direction gives the E2E^{2}E2 page. The spectral sequence continues from this page and converges to the cohomology of the total complex Hp+q(K∙)H^{p+q}(K^{\bullet})Hp+q(K∙) ✓.

⭐ A typical application is to take this spectral sequence for the bicomplex naturally obtained from an injective resolution of a complex, yielding the Grothendieck spectral sequence. The other filtration (horizontal first, then vertical) yields the Cartan–Eilenberg decomposition ✓.

⚠️ Boundedness below is essential. In the unbounded case, convergence of the spectral sequence requires a separate analysis (generally using the full machinery of lim←⁡\varprojlimlim​).
