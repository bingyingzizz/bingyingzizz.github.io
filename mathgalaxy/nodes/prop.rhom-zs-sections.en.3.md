# Derived Hom of Free Modules over Stonean Spaces　`prop.rhom-zs-sections`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=prop.rhom-zs-sections)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./prop.rhom-zs-sections.md)

is an **exact** functor, its right derived functors are concentrated in degree $0$, and $R^{0} = \Gamma(S,-)$ ✓. Therefore $\operatorname{RHom}(\mathbb{Z}[S], N)$ equals $\operatorname{Hom}(\mathbb{Z}[S], N) = N(S)$ (a complex concentrated in degree $0$) ✓. ∎

⭐ This is the same statement as "condensed abelian groups on a Stonean space are acyclic", in two equivalent forms: **coefficient acyclicity $\iff$ the test object $\mathbb{Z}[S]$ is projective**.
