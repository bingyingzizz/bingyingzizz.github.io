# Syzygy　`def.syzygy`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=def.syzygy)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./def.syzygy.md)

$$\cdots \to P_{2} \to P_{1} \to P_{0} \to M \to 0$$

and a sequence of syzygies $\Omega M \hookrightarrow P_{0}$, $\Omega^{2}M \hookrightarrow P_{1}$, $\ldots$ encode the same data. Thus asking whether $M$ has a projective resolution of length $n$ is equivalent to asking whether $\Omega^{n}M$ is projective (see “Projective Dimension and Vanishing of Ext”).

⚠️ **Syzygies depend on the resolution**
Changing the resolution can change $\Omega M$. It is unique only up to adding projective direct summands, as stated in “Stability of Syzygies,” which follows from the syzygy comparison lemma.
