## Statement · **draft**
All presheaves on $\mathcal{C}$, with natural transformations as morphisms, form the category

$$\widehat{\mathcal{C}} := \operatorname{Hom}(\mathcal{C}^{\mathrm{op}}, \mathbf{Set})$$

## Notes · **draft**
It is a functor category, so limits and colimits are computed **pointwise** — every later construction involving $\widehat{\mathcal{C}}$ starts here.
