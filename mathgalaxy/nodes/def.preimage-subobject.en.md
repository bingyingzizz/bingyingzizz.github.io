## Statement · **draft**
Let $f : X^{\prime} \to X$ be a morphism and $Y \rightarrowtail X$ a subobject of $X$ (see @@def.subobject@@). The **preimage** of $Y$ along $f$ is the fibred product

$$f^{-1}(Y) := Y \times_{X} X^{\prime} \;\longrightarrow\; X^{\prime} ,$$
