## Statement · **draft**
Let $S$ be a set. The **free group** on $S$, written $F(S)$, consists of all **reduced words** $s_{1}^{\varepsilon_{1}}\cdots s_{n}^{\varepsilon_{n}}$, where $s_{i}\in S$, $\varepsilon_{i}=\pm1$, and adjacent $s_{i}=s_{i+1}$, $\varepsilon_{i}=-\varepsilon_{i+1}$ have been cancelled; the empty word is written $1$. Multiplication is concatenation of words followed by reduction. The inclusion $\iota:S\to F(S)$ sends $s$ to the one-letter word.
