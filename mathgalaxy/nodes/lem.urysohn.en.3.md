# Urysohn　`lem.urysohn`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=lem.urysohn)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./lem.urysohn.md)

This step uses only normality (each step is "disjoint closed sets are separated by open sets"). Then set

$$f(x) := \inf\{\, q : x \in U_{q} \,\}$$

(take $1$ if $x \notin$ any $U_{q}$). The **monotone squeezing** of that family of open sets shows that $f$ is continuous, with $f|_{A} = 0$ and $f|_{B} = 1$. The converse is direct: $f^{-1}[0, 1/2)$ and $f^{-1}(1/2, 1]$ are the open sets separating $A, B$. ∎
