## Statement · **draft**
For a sequence of real numbers $\{x_n\}$, set

$$\limsup_{n \to \infty} x_n := \lim_{n \to \infty} \Bigl( \sup_{k \ge n} x_k \Bigr), \qquad \liminf_{n \to \infty} x_n := \lim_{n \to \infty} \Bigl( \inf_{k \ge n} x_k \Bigr)$$
