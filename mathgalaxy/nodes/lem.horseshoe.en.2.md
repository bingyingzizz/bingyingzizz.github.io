# Horseshoe Lemma　`lem.horseshoe`

Then there is a projective resolution of $M$, denoted by $R_{\bullet} \twoheadrightarrow M$, such that

1. $R_{n} = P_{n} \oplus Q_{n}$ (a termwise direct sum).
2. The inclusions and projections form a **termwise split** short exact sequence of complexes $0 \to P_{\bullet} \to R_{\bullet} \to Q_{\bullet} \to 0$.
3. The augmentation $R_{0} = P_{0}\oplus Q_{0} \to M$ combines $i\varepsilon$ with a lift of $\eta$.

The diagram formed by the three resolutions is a **double complex**. The resolution of $M$ runs horizontally, and the two end terms have their respective resolutions running vertically. The diagram is shaped like a horseshoe.
