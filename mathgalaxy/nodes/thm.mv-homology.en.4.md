**Small chains.** A singular simplex $\sigma : \Delta^{p} \to X$ is **small** with respect to the cover $\{U, V\}$ if $\sigma(\Delta^{p}) \subseteq U$ or $\sigma(\Delta^{p}) \subseteq V$. Finite sums of small simplices form a subgroup $S^{\mathcal{U}}_{p}(X) \subseteq S_{p}(X)$. The boundary operator preserves smallness because the faces still lie in the same set, so $S^{\mathcal{U}}_{*}(X)$ is a subcomplex. Write $\iota : S^{\mathcal{U}}_{*}(X) \hookrightarrow S_{*}(X)$.
