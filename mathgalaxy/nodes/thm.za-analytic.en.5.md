# analytic rings from finitely generated algebras　`thm.za-analytic`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=thm.za-analytic)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./thm.za-analytic.md)

④ Analyticity: the category of solid modules over $A_\blacksquare$ corresponds to the subcategory of complexes satisfying $\operatorname{RHom}_A(A_\infty,C)=0$ (this is the working form of the definition of an analytic ring).

⑤ Existence of $j_!$: use $\operatorname{coker}\bigl(\prod_I \mathbb{Z}\otimes A\to\prod_I A\bigr)$ to construct $j_!A$, then use presentability / the adjoint functor theorem to extend $j_!$ to the whole derived category.
