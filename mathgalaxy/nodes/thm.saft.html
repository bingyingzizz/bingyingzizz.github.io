陈述

设 D\mathcal{D}D 是小且完备的范畴。则函子 G:D→CG : \mathcal{D} \to \mathcal{C}G:D→C 有左伴随   ⟺  \iff⟺ GGG 保持所有极限。

左伴随由一个极限的构造给出：

F(X)  =  lim←⁡(Y, f)∈(X↓G)YF(X) \;=\; \varprojlim_{(Y,\, f) \in (X \downarrow G)} YF(X)=(Y,f)∈(X↓G)lim​​Y

说明

保极限是唯一的障碍。这与「右伴随保极限」合起来就是一条完整的判据：保极限既是必要条件，在完备的小范畴上也是充分条件。

构造的含义：把 XXX 映射进 GGG 的像的所有方式组成一个范畴 X↓GX \downarrow GX↓G，在其中取极限 —— F(X)F(X)F(X) 是 D\mathcal{D}D 中「最接近」XXX 的对象。所有 X→G(Y)X \to G(Y)X→G(Y) 都能被整理成 X→G(F(X))→G(Y)X \to G(F(X)) \to G(Y)X→G(F(X))→G(Y)。

Abstract reviewed

Let D\mathcal{D}D be small and complete. A functor G:D→CG : \mathcal{D} \to \mathcal{C}G:D→C has a left adjoint iff it preserves all limits; when it does, one may take F(X)=lim←⁡(Y,f)∈(X↓G)YF(X) = \varprojlim_{(Y,f) \in (X \downarrow G)} YF(X)=lim​(Y,f)∈(X↓G)​Y. So preservation of limits, which is automatic for right adjoints, is also sufficient under these hypotheses. The construction reads: F(X)F(X)F(X) is the object of D\mathcal{D}D closest to XXX, and every X→G(Y)X \to G(Y)X→G(Y) factors through X→G(FX)→G(Y)X \to G(FX) \to G(Y)X→G(FX)→G(Y).

Statement reviewed

Let D\mathcal{D}D be a small and complete category. Then a functor G:D→CG : \mathcal{D} \to \mathcal{C}G:D→C has a left adjoint   ⟺  \iff⟺ GGG preserves all limits.

The left adjoint is given by a limit construction:

F(X)  =  lim←⁡(Y, f)∈(X↓G)YF(X) \;=\; \varprojlim_{(Y,\, f) \in (X \downarrow G)} YF(X)=(Y,f)∈(X↓G)lim​​Y

Notes reviewed

Preservation of limits is the only obstruction. Together with "a right adjoint preserves limits" this gives a complete criterion: preservation of limits is necessary, and on a complete small category it is also sufficient.

What the construction means: all the ways of mapping XXX into the image of GGG form a category X↓GX \downarrow GX↓G, and one takes a limit in it — F(X)F(X)F(X) is the object of D\mathcal{D}D "closest" to XXX. Every X→G(Y)X \to G(Y)X→G(Y) can be organised as X→G(F(X))→G(Y)X \to G(F(X)) \to G(Y)X→G(F(X))→G(Y).

The proof is the derivation on the edge.
