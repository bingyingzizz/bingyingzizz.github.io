## Notes · **draft**
**Why $LU$ holds**: Each step of Gaussian elimination is left multiplication by an elementary matrix. Composing the elimination steps gives $L^{-1}$. Nonzero leading principal minors ensure that no row swaps are needed. See the edge `imp.lu-decomposition-proof` for the full proof, including Schur complement induction and uniqueness. The permutation version uses partial pivoting, as shown in the final paragraph of that proof.
