# Euler characteristic and nonorientable genus　`prop.eulerchar-nonorientable`

**1. Triangulating $\mathbb{RP}^{2}$.** Identify antipodal points of the regular icosahedron ($12$ vertices, $30$ edges, and $20$ faces) to obtain a triangulation of $\mathbb{RP}^{2}$ with $6$ vertices, $15$ edges, and $10$ faces, so $\chi(\mathbb{RP}^{2})=6-15+10=1=2-1$. The antipodal action is free and simplicial (each simplex is distinct from its antipodal image), so the quotient complex is still a simplicial complex.
