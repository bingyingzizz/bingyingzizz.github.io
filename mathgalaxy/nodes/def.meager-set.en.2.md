## Notes · **draft**
"Category" is a third notion of size, neither cardinality nor measure: $\mathbb{Q}$ is countable and dense yet meager (each singleton is nowhere dense); the Cantor set is uncountable and of measure zero yet meager (it is itself nowhere dense); $[0,1] \setminus \mathbb{Q}$ is of the second category and residual **in the subspace $[0,1]$** (it is not residual in $\mathbb{R}$: its complement contains the interval $(1,2)$, which is not meager).

⚠️ Second category $\ne$ dense: $[0,1] \cup \{2\}$ is also of the second category.
