[in graph](../#n=prop.left-invariant-fields)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./prop.left-invariant-fields.md)

Let $G$ be a Lie group and $\mathfrak{g} := T_{e}G$ ($e$ the identity). A vector field $X \in \mathfrak{X}(G)$ is **left-invariant** if for all $g, h \in G$

$$X_{gh} = \mathrm{d}(L_{g})_{h}\bigl(X_{h}\bigr).$$
