⭐ How to read it: "computing page by page" in the language of exact couples is just "deriving the couple once". DDD is "what is already known", EEE is "the quotient on this page", iii is "forget one layer", jjj is "take the quotient", kkk is "the connecting homomorphism".

⚠ Bidegrees are not unique: the filtration-complex convention (−1,1)/(0,0)/(1,0)(-1,1)/(0,0)/(1,0)(−1,1)/(0,0)/(1,0) is fixed here. In homological notation it is (1,−1)/(0,0)/(1,0)(1,-1)/(0,0)/(1,0)(1,−1)/(0,0)/(1,0); the differential lands one slot differently, and conversion is just an index shift.

⚠ Convergence is not free: the stabilization hypothesis must be checked. An exact couple by itself guarantees only the relation between pages, not that E∞E_{\infty}E∞​ is the associated graded of anything — counterexamples exist in the unbounded case.
