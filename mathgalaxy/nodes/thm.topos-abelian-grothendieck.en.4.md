## Notes · **draft**
One more remark: $\mathbb{Z}\cdot X$ is a **generator** and is **flat**, but it is **not** projective — $\operatorname{Hom}(\mathbb{Z}\cdot X, -) \cong \Gamma(X, -)$ (global sections) is only left exact, so a nonsplit short exact sequence of sheaves $0 \to \mathcal{F} \to \mathcal{G} \to \mathcal{H} \to 0$ shows that it is not right exact. Projectivity holds only in the presheaf category (where $\operatorname{Hom}(\mathbb{Z}\cdot h_{X}, -) \cong (-)(X)$ is exact, see `prop.presheaf-free-projective`).
