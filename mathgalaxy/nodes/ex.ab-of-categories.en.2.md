## Statement · **draft**
- $\mathrm{Ab}(\mathbf{Set}) = \mathbf{Ab}$;
- $\mathrm{Ab}(\mathbf{Top})$ = **topological abelian groups**;
- $\mathrm{Ab}(\mathbf{Manifold})$ = abelian Lie groups;
- $\mathrm{Ab}(\mathbf{Grp}) = \mathbf{Ab}$ (operations between group objects are pointwise);
- $\mathrm{Ab}(\mathrm{Cond}) = \mathrm{CondAb}$;
- $\mathrm{Ab}(\widehat{\mathcal{C}})$ = abelian group objects in the presheaf category $\simeq$ presheaves valued in $\mathbf{Ab}$.
