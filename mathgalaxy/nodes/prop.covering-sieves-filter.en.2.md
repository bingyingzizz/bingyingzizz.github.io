# Covering Sieves Form a Filter　`prop.covering-sieves-filter`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=prop.covering-sieves-filter)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./prop.covering-sieves-filter.md)

1. $J(X) \ne \emptyset$;
2. $R, R' \in J(X) \implies R \cap R' \in J(X)$ (that is, $J(X)$ is closed under lower bounds);
3. $R \in J(X)$ and $R \subseteq R' \implies R' \in J(X)$ (closed under larger sieves).

## Notes · **draft**
**Proof.** All three are direct rewrites of the Grothendieck topology axioms: axiom (T1) gives $h_{X} \in J(X)$ ✓; axiom (T2) (coverings pull back along themselves) gives item 3 ✓; axiom (T3) (coverings refine along coverings) gives item 2 = "a common refinement of two coverings is still a covering" ✓. ∎
