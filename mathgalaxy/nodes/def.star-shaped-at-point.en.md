## Statement · **draft**
Let $V$ be a real vector space, $C \subseteq V$, $x_{0} \in V$. One says $C$ is **star-shaped with respect to $x_{0}$** if $x_{0} \in C$ and for every $y \in C$, $t \in [0,1]$,

$$(1-t)x_{0} + t y \in C.$$

Equivalently: $x_{0} \in C$, and $C$ contains the whole segment from $x_{0}$ to any point of $C$.
