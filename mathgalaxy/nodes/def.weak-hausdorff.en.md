## Statement · **draft**
A topological space $X$ is called **weakly Hausdorff** if for every continuous map $f : S \to X$ (with $S$ compact Hausdorff), the image $f(S)$ is **closed** in $X$.

## Notes · **draft**
**What it says**: it asks that "images of maps from compact Hausdorff spaces are closed" — a separation condition cheaper than Hausdorff but sufficient.
