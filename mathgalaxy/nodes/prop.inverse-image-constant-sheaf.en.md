## Statement · **draft**
Let $f : \mathcal{T} \to \mathcal{T}'$ be a morphism of topoi and $\overline{E}$ a constant sheaf ($E$ a set). Then

$$f^{-1}\bigl(\overline{E}\bigr) \;=\; \overline{E}.$$
