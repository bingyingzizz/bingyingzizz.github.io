## Statement · **draft**
Let $E$ be a set. Then the **constant sheaf** on $\mathbf{Top}$ is the sheaf given by the **discrete space** $E$:

$$\widetilde{E}_{\mathbf{Top}} \;\cong\; \underline{E^{\mathrm{disc}}},$$

and $\widetilde{E}_{\mathbf{Top}}(X) = E$ $\iff$ $X$ is **connected**.
