(a) 若 E∈M⊗NE \in \mathcal{M} \otimes \mathcal{N}E∈M⊗N，则对一切 x∈Xx \in Xx∈X 有 Ex∈NE_x \in \mathcal{N}Ex​∈N，对一切 y∈Yy \in Yy∈Y 有 Ey∈ME^y \in \mathcal{M}Ey∈M。

(b) 若 fff 是 M⊗N\mathcal{M} \otimes \mathcal{N}M⊗N-可测的，则对一切 xxx 有 fxf_xfx​ 是 N\mathcal{N}N-可测的，对一切 yyy 有 fyf^yfy 是 M\mathcal{M}M-可测的。

说明

证明是一个「先看矩形、再看 σ\sigmaσ代数」的标准套路：令

R={E⊆X×Y:Ex∈N ∧ Ey∈M∀(x,y)}\mathcal{R} = \{E \subseteq X \times Y : E_x \in \mathcal{N} \ \wedge\ E^y \in \mathcal{M}\quad \forall(x, y)\}R={E⊆X×Y:Ex​∈N ∧ Ey∈M∀(x,y)}

则 R\mathcal{R}R 包含所有矩形，而且R\mathcal{R}R 是一个 σ\sigmaσ代数（截口运算保持并、交、补）。于是 R⊇M⊗N\mathcal{R} \supseteq \mathcal{M} \otimes \mathcal{N}R⊇M⊗N。

(b) 由 (a) 与两条恒等式得到：

fx−1(B)=(f−1(B))x,fy−1(B)=(f−1(B))yf_x^{-1}(B) = (f^{-1}(B))_x, \quad f^{y-1}(B) = (f^{-1}(B))^yfx−1​(B)=(f−1(B))x​,fy−1(B)=(f−1(B))y

⚠ 注意 (b) 说的是「每一个 xxx 都行」，而 Fubini 定理里只能保证「几乎每一个」—— 那是因为 Fubini 还要它可积。

Abstract reviewed

Sections of measurable sets are measurable: if E∈M⊗NE \in \mathcal{M} \otimes \mathcal{N}E∈M⊗N then Ex∈NE_x \in \mathcal{N}Ex​∈N for every xxx and Ey∈ME^y \in \mathcal{M}Ey∈M for every yyy; likewise the sections of an M⊗N\mathcal{M} \otimes \mathcal{N}M⊗N-measurable fff are measurable, because fx−1(B)=(f−1(B))xf_x^{-1}(B) = (f^{-1}(B))_xfx−1​(B)=(f−1(B))x​ and dually. The proof is the standard one: the class of sets all of whose sections are measurable contains every rectangle and is a σ\sigmaσ-algebra. Note that this holds for every point, whereas Fubini can only promise almost every point once integrability enters.

Statement reviewed

(a) If E∈M⊗NE \in \mathcal{M} \otimes \mathcal{N}E∈M⊗N, then for every x∈Xx \in Xx∈X we have Ex∈NE_x \in \mathcal{N}Ex​∈N, and for every y∈Yy \in Yy∈Y we have Ey∈ME^y \in \mathcal{M}Ey∈M.

(b) If fff is M⊗N\mathcal{M} \otimes \mathcal{N}M⊗N-measurable, then for every xxx the function fxf_xfx​ is N\mathcal{N}N-measurable, and for every yyy the function fyf^yfy is M\mathcal{M}M-measurable.

Notes reviewed

The proof is the standard pattern "rectangles first, then σ\sigmaσ-algebras". Let

R={E⊆X×Y:Ex∈N ∧ Ey∈M∀(x,y)}\mathcal{R} = \{E \subseteq X \times Y : E_x \in \mathcal{N} \ \wedge\ E^y \in \mathcal{M}\quad \forall(x, y)\}R={E⊆X×Y:Ex​∈N ∧ Ey∈M∀(x,y)}

Then R\mathcal{R}R contains all rectangles, and R\mathcal{R}R is a σ\sigmaσ-algebra (the section operations preserve unions, intersections and complements). Hence R⊇M⊗N\mathcal{R} \supseteq \mathcal{M} \otimes \mathcal{N}R⊇M⊗N.

(b) follows from (a) and two identities:

fx−1(B)=(f−1(B))x,fy−1(B)=(f−1(B))yf_x^{-1}(B) = (f^{-1}(B))_x, \quad f^{y-1}(B) = (f^{-1}(B))^yfx−1​(B)=(f−1(B))x​,fy−1(B)=(f−1(B))y

⚠ Note that (b) says "every xxx works", whereas Fubini's theorem can only guarantee "almost every" — because Fubini also needs integrability.
