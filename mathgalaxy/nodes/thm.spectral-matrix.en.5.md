# Spectral Theorem (Matrices)　`thm.spectral-matrix`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=thm.spectral-matrix)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./thm.spectral-matrix.md)

⚠️ A non-normal matrix may still be diagonalizable (`thm.diagonalizable`), but its eigenvectors are **not orthogonal**, and the similarity matrix $P$ is generally ill-conditioned. So "diagonalizable" and "unitarily diagonalizable" are two different things: the latter demands **orthogonality** and is much stronger.
