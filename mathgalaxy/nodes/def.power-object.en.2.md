## Notes · **draft**
**Equivalent description (more useful)**: $Y^{X}$ is the **right adjoint** of the functor $-\times X : \mathcal{C} \to \mathcal{C}$, that is,

$$\operatorname{Hom}(Z \times X,\, Y) \;\cong\; \operatorname{Hom}(Z,\, Y^{X}).$$

So "has power objects" = "the product functor has a right adjoint".
