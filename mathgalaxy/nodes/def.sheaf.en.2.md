## Notes · **draft**
By the Yoneda lemma $\operatorname{Hom}(h_{X}, F) \cong F(X)$, this definition can be read as two sentences: the left-hand side is $F(X)$ (global sections), while the right-hand side is "a compatible family of sections along $R$":

- **Injective = separatedness**: two global sections that agree on $R$ must be equal;
- **Bijective = gluing**: any compatible family of sections glues **uniquely** into a global section.
