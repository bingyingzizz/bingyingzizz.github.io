## Statement · **draft**
For $A, B \in M_n(K)$:

$$\det(AB) = \det A \cdot \det B, \qquad \det A^{\mathsf{T}} = \det A, \qquad \det(cA) = c^{n} \det A$$

Hence if $A$ is invertible then $\det(A^{-1}) = (\det A)^{-1}$; conversely $A$ is invertible $\iff \det A \ne 0$.

(This is the contrapositive of "$\det$ is a multiplicative homomorphism": $\det A = 0$ $\iff$ $A$ is not invertible.)
