⚠ The proof of the MCT below **relies on (d)**: the step asserting $\lim_n \int_{E_n} \varphi = \int \varphi$ uses exactly “$A \mapsto \int _A \varphi$ is a measure” together with continuity from below for measures ($E_n \uparrow  X$).

In (a) one writes $c > 0$ rather than $c \ge 0$: because of the convention $0\cdot \infty = 0$, the left-hand side also holds automatically when $c = 0$, and writing $c > 0$ merely avoids a case distinction.
