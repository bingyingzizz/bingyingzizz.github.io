## Notes · **draft**
**Why a homeomorphism and not merely a bijection**: coordinates must carry the topological notions — continuity, convergence, compactness — otherwise what is computed is only a property of sets. A homeomorphism makes the chart a coordinate chart in the **topological** sense; this is why a topological space is part of the definition of a manifold.
