Statement

Let T\mathcal{T}T be a topos and M,N,PM, N, PM,N,P abelian groups in T\mathcal{T}T. Then there is a natural map

M⊗ZLRHom⁡Z(P, N)⟶RHom⁡Z(RHom⁡Z(M, P), N).M \otimes^{\mathbf{L}}_{\mathbb{Z}} \operatorname{RHom}_{\mathbb{Z}}(P,\ N) \longrightarrow \operatorname{RHom}_{\mathbb{Z}}\bigl(\operatorname{RHom}_{\mathbb{Z}}(M,\ P),\ N\bigr).M⊗ZL​RHomZ​(P, N)⟶RHomZ​(RHomZ​(M, P), N).

Notes

⚠️ In general it is not an isomorphism — making it one requires extra finiteness/duality conditions. What is given here is a natural map, whose use is to replace "the RHom of the dual" by "the RHom of a tensor product", so that free resolutions can be used to compute.
