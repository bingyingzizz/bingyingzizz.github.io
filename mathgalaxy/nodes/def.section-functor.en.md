## Statement · **draft**
Let $\mathcal{C}$ be a site, $X \in \mathcal{C}$, and $\widetilde{\mathcal{C}}$ the sheaf category. The **section functor at $X$** is

$$\Gamma(X, -) : \widetilde{\mathcal{C}} \longrightarrow \mathbf{Set}, \qquad F \longmapsto \Gamma(X, F) := F(X).$$

When $X = \mathbf{1}$ (the terminal object) it is called the **global section functor**, written $\Gamma(F) := F(\mathbf{1})$.
