# Quotients of Divisible Groups　`prop.divisible-quotient`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=prop.divisible-quotient)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./prop.divisible-quotient.md)

📌 Over a general ring be careful: divisibility is always closed under quotients (the elementwise argument), but "injective is closed under quotients" follows from "divisible = injective" only over a PID such as $\mathbb{Z}$, and must not be taken as a general fact.
