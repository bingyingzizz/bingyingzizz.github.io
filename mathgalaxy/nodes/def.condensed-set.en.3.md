**Example 2. Convergent sequences.** Take $X$ to be a topological space; then $\underline{X}(\bar{\mathbb{N}})$ is exactly the set of "**convergent sequences $(x_{n})_{n \ge 1}$ together with a specified limit $x_{\infty}$**" — here $\bar{\mathbb{N}}$ is the **one-point compactification** of $\mathbb{N}$, and "a convergent sequence together with its limit" is precisely a continuous map from $\bar{\mathbb{N}}$ to $X$: the image on $\mathbb{N}$ gives the sequence, and the image at the new point gives the limit.
