## Notes · **draft**
$\omega$ is at the same time the smallest limit ordinal. Its construction needs only the **axiom of infinity + separation schema** (**the power set is not needed**: $\omega$ is the set of those elements that "belong to every inductive subset", which separation extracts directly from an inductive set).
