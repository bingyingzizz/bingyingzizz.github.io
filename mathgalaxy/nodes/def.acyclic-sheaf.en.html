Statement

Let C\mathcal{C}C be a site. An abelian sheaf MMM on it is called acyclic if Hn(M)=0H^{n}(M) = 0Hn(M)=0 for all n≠0n \ne 0n=0.

Notes

⚠️ A special warning: this definition depends on the site C\mathcal{C}C, not only on the topos C~\widetilde{\mathcal{C}}C — changing the site (with the same topos) may change acyclicity. This contrasts with "the notion of a sheaf depends only on the topos".
