# Stokes' Theorem on Chains　`thm.stokes-chain`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=thm.stokes-chain)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./thm.stokes-chain.md)

**⚠️ Degree and dimension**: The degree of $\omega$ must be one less than the dimension of the chain. When $k = 0$, we have $\partial c = 0$ and the right-hand side is zero, so the formula reduces to $0 = 0$. Thus the theorem starts at $k\ge 1$.

**⚠️ Stating the theorem only for closed chains would be too restrictive**: The theorem does not require $c$ to be a closed chain. When $\partial c\ne 0$, the two sides still agree. The vanishing of the pairing between a closed chain and an exact form ($\int_{c}\mathrm{d}\omega = 0$) is a corollary.
