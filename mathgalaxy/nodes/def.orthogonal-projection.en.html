Notes

⭐ In one sentence: orthogonal projection = idempotent + self-adjoint.

Conversely, every idempotent self-adjoint operator is the orthogonal projection onto some subspace — this "operator-language" characterization later becomes, in the spectral theorem, "a self-adjoint operator whose eigenvalues are only 0,10, 10,1".

⭐ An idempotent operator that is not self-adjoint is called a projection: it projects "parallel to ker⁡P\ker PkerP onto im⁡P\operatorname{im} PimP", and is in general not an orthogonal projection.

The difference between the two is whether the "shortest distance" is attained.
