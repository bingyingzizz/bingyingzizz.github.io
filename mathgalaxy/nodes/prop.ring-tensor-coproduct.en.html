prop.ring-tensor-coproduct

Machine-drafted and pending author review. The Chinese text is authoritative.

Statement

In the category of commutative rings, the fiber coproduct (pushout) along A→BA \to BA→B and A→CA \to CA→C is B⊗ACB \otimes_{A} CB⊗A​C.

Notes

⚠️ Commutativity is essential: over a non-commutative ring, B⊗ACB\otimes_{A}CB⊗A​C is in general not a ring (multiplication does not descend to the tensor product) — then the fiber coproduct must be built instead as a free product / coequalizer.
