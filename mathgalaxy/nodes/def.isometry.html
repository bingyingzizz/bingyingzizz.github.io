又称等距与正交变换

陈述

线性映射 T:V→VT : V \to VT:V→V 叫等距，当且仅当它保持内积（等价地保持范数）：

⟨Tv,Tw⟩=⟨v,w⟩(∀v,w)  ⟺  ∥Tv∥=∥v∥(∀v)\langle Tv, Tw \rangle = \langle v, w \rangle \quad (\forall v, w) \qquad \iff \qquad \|Tv\| = \|v\| \quad (\forall v)⟨Tv,Tw⟩=⟨v,w⟩(∀v,w)⟺∥Tv∥=∥v∥(∀v)

实数情形称正交变换，复数情形称酉变换；它们的矩阵分别叫正交矩阵（QTQ=IQ^{\mathsf{T}}Q = IQTQ=I）与酉矩阵（U†U=IU^{\dagger}U = IU†U=I）。

等距的等价刻画：T∗T=IT^{\ast}T = IT∗T=I（  ⟺  T\iff T⟺T 可逆且 T∗=T−1T^{\ast} = T^{-1}T∗=T−1）。全体正交（酉）变换构成群 O(n)O(n)O(n)（U(n)U(n)U(n)）。

说明

⭐ 等距保持全部几何：长度、角度、正交性、体积（∣det⁡∣=1|\det| = 1∣det∣=1）。

所以「两个图形全等」在代数上就是「差一个正交变换」。

⭐ O(n)O(n)O(n) 是紧李群（有界闭），SO(n):={Q:det⁡Q=1}SO(n) := \{Q : \det Q = 1\}SO(n):={Q:detQ=1} 是它的连通分支（含单位元的那个）—— 旋转群。

⚠ 「保持范数   ⟹  \implies⟹ 保持内积」的推导用极化恒等式：内积可以由范数恢复（⟨u,v⟩=14∑k=03ik∥u+ikv∥2\langle u,v\rangle = \frac{1}{4}\sum_{k=0}^{3} i^{k}\|u + i^{k}v\|^{2}⟨u,v⟩=41​∑k=03​ik∥u+ikv∥2）—— 这也是「范数未必来自内积」那一节的另一半。

Statement draft

A linear map T:V→VT : V \to VT:V→V is an isometry iff it preserves the inner product (equivalently, the norm):

⟨Tv,Tw⟩=⟨v,w⟩(∀v,w)  ⟺  ∥Tv∥=∥v∥(∀v)\langle Tv, Tw \rangle = \langle v, w \rangle \quad (\forall v, w) \qquad \iff \qquad \|Tv\| = \|v\| \quad (\forall v)⟨Tv,Tw⟩=⟨v,w⟩(∀v,w)⟺∥Tv∥=∥v∥(∀v)

In the real case it is called an orthogonal transformation, in the complex case a unitary transformation; their matrices are called orthogonal matrices (QTQ=IQ^{\mathsf{T}}Q = IQTQ=I) and unitary matrices (U†U=IU^{\dagger}U = IU†U=I).

Equivalent characterization of an isometry: T∗T=IT^{\ast}T = IT∗T=I (  ⟺  T\iff T⟺T invertible and T∗=T−1T^{\ast} = T^{-1}T∗=T−1). All orthogonal (unitary) transformations form a group O(n)O(n)O(n) (U(n)U(n)U(n)).

Notes draft

⭐ An isometry preserves all of geometry: lengths, angles, orthogonality, volume (∣det⁡∣=1|\det| = 1∣det∣=1).

Thus "two figures are congruent" is, algebraically, "they differ by an orthogonal transformation".

⭐ O(n)O(n)O(n) is a compact Lie group (bounded and closed), and SO(n):={Q:det⁡Q=1}SO(n) := \{Q : \det Q = 1\}SO(n):={Q:detQ=1} is its connected component (the one containing the identity) — the rotation group.

⚠ The derivation "preserving the norm   ⟹  \implies⟹ preserving the inner product" uses the polarization identity: the inner product can be recovered from the norm (⟨u,v⟩=14∑k=03ik∥u+ikv∥2\langle u,v\rangle = \frac{1}{4}\sum_{k=0}^{3} i^{k}\|u + i^{k}v\|^{2}⟨u,v⟩=41​∑k=03​ik∥u+ikv∥2) — this is the other half of the section "norms need not come from inner products".
