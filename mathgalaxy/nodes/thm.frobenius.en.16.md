**VII. The conclusion is local.** The theorem gives foliation coordinates near each point. It does **not** guarantee global coordinates straightening $\mathcal{D}$ into coordinate slices of $\mathbb{R}^{n}$, which would require the leaf space itself to be a product manifold. The irrational-slope foliation on the torus is a counterexample. Its leaves are locally straight lines, yet each winds densely around the torus.
