and every $\varphi \in V^{\ast}$ satisfies $\varphi = \sum_i \varphi(v_i)\, v_i^{\ast}$.

## Notes · **draft**
⭐ The dual basis is the functional “the $i$-th coordinate”: $v_i^{\ast}(v) = [v]_{\mathcal{B}}$ picks out the $i$-th component.

So coordinates themselves are linear functionals.

⭐ This yields the transpose of a matrix: $[T^{\ast}]_{\mathcal{B}^{\ast}}^{\mathcal{C}^{\ast}} = ([T]_{\mathcal{C}}^{\mathcal{B}})^{\mathsf{T}}$ — **duality is transposition**, and that is the origin of the name.

⚠ The dual basis depends on the choice of basis: change the basis and the dual basis changes with it (contravariantly).
