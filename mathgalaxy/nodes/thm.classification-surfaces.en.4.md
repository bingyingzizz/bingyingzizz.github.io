# classification of closed surfaces　`thm.classification-surfaces`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=thm.classification-surfaces)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./thm.classification-surfaces.md)

**The three steps.** (1) Radó's theorem gives a triangulation of every closed surface (`thm.rado`, skeleton). (2) Combinatorial classification reduces any triangulation to standard form in finitely many moves. (3) The invariants computed on the standard forms, orientability and $\chi$, determine $(g)$ or $(k)$ and distinguish the homeomorphism types.
