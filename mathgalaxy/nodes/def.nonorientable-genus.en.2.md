## Notes · **draft**
**Why not use $\chi=2-k$ directly as the definition**: That would presuppose the classification theorem (every closed nonorientable surface is homeomorphic to a standard form). Defining it as the minimum number of projective planes in a connected sum is independent of classification, following the same idea as the homological definition in `def.genus`.

**Small values**: $k=1$ the projective plane. $k=2$ the Klein bottle. $k=3$ is the surface with three crosscaps.
