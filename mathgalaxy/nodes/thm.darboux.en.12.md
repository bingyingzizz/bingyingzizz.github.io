# Darboux’s Theorem　`thm.darboux`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=thm.darboux)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./thm.darboux.md)

📌 **What the proof uses**: Nondegeneracy (to solve $X_{t}$), closedness ($\omega_{t}$ is closed and $\eta$ has primitive $\theta$), contractibility of the chart (`lem.poincare-lemma`), and the local flow theorem for ODEs (`thm.flow-local`). The agreement of $\omega_{0}$ and $\omega$ at $p$ is crucial. Otherwise $\omega_{t}$ might degenerate at $p$, preventing the Moser flow from being defined. The node `thm.symplectic-normal-form` currently provides only a linear algebra proof sketch. The existence of the required symplectic basis is proved again here, so this proof is independent of that node.
