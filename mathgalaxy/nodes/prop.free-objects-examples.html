陈述

「自由」在几个代数范畴里的样子：Mon\mathbf{Mon}Mon 里是以 XXX 为生成元的字幺半群；Gr\mathbf{Gr}Gr 里是字群（允许消去）；Rng\mathbf{Rng}Rng 里是非交换多项式环；

G-SetG\text{-}\mathbf{Set}G-Set 里是 G×XG \times XG×X（左正则作用 g⋅(h,x)=(gh,x)g \cdot (h, x) = (gh, x)g⋅(h,x)=(gh,x)；XXX 为单点时就退化成 GGG 本身）；

A-ModA\text{-}\mathbf{Mod}A-Mod 里是 A(X)A^{(X)}A(X)；

k-Algk\text{-}\mathbf{Alg}k-Alg 里是张量代数。

说明

证明。 逐条核对自由对象的万有性质 Hom⁡(FX,−)≅Hom⁡Set(X,U(−))\operatorname{Hom}(FX, -) \cong \operatorname{Hom}_{\mathbf{Set}}\bigl(X, U(-)\bigr)Hom(FX,−)≅HomSet​(X,U(−)) ✓。∎

Statement draft

"Free" in several algebraic categories: in Mon\mathbf{Mon}Mon it is the free monoid on XXX; in Gr\mathbf{Gr}Gr it is the free group (with cancellation allowed); in Rng\mathbf{Rng}Rng it is the noncommutative polynomial ring;

in G-SetG\text{-}\mathbf{Set}G-Set it is G×XG \times XG×X (left regular action g⋅(h,x)=(gh,x)g \cdot (h, x) = (gh, x)g⋅(h,x)=(gh,x); when XXX is a single point it degenerates to GGG itself);

in A-ModA\text{-}\mathbf{Mod}A-Mod it is A(X)A^{(X)}A(X);

in k-Algk\text{-}\mathbf{Alg}k-Alg it is the tensor algebra.

Notes draft

Proof. Check the universal property of free objects Hom⁡(FX,−)≅Hom⁡Set(X,U(−))\operatorname{Hom}(FX, -) \cong \operatorname{Hom}_{\mathbf{Set}}\bigl(X, U(-)\bigr)Hom(FX,−)≅HomSet​(X,U(−)) in each case ✓. ∎
