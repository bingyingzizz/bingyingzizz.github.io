## Statement · **draft**
Let $V,W$ be finite-dimensional indecomposable representations and let $\alpha:W\to V$, $\beta:V\to W$ be $A$-linear maps such that $\alpha\circ\beta$ is an automorphism of $V$. Then $\alpha,\beta$ are isomorphisms.
