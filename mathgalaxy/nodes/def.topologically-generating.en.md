## Statement · **draft**
Let $\mathcal{C}$ be a site. A set $G \subseteq \mathcal{C}$ is called a **topologically generating set** if every $X \in \mathcal{C}$ admits a covering family consisting of objects **lying in $G$**, say $(G_{i} \to X)_{i}$.
