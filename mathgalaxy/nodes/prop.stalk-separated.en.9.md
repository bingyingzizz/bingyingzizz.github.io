# stalk　`prop.stalk-separated`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=prop.stalk-separated)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./prop.stalk-separated.md)

imply gluing: the constant presheaf on the discrete two-point space taking a set with more than one element, and the point on the empty set, is separated but cannot glue two different local constants.
- The pushforward formula in the original text needs correction: the pushforward of a set-valued sheaf along a point inclusion should take the one-point terminal object on open sets not containing the point, not the empty set; the left adjoint of the stalk functor also does not in general exist automatically, and this step of the original adjunction chain is unverified, recorded as a gap.
