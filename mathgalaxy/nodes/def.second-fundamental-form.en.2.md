# Second Fundamental Form　`def.second-fundamental-form`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=def.second-fundamental-form)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./def.second-fundamental-form.md)

relative to parameters $(u,v)$ it is written $II = L\,\mathrm{d}u^{2}+2M\,\mathrm{d}u\,\mathrm{d}v+N\,\mathrm{d}v^{2}$. $II$ is symmetric, and $II(v,v)$ is the normal bending in the direction $\mathrm{d}r(v)$.

After identifying $T_{p}U$ with the tangent plane $T_{p}S$ via $\mathrm{d}r$, $II$ can also be written directly on tangent vectors: $II(V,W) = \langle W(V), W\rangle$. The discussion of $k_{n}(V)$, Rodrigues' theorem, and Euler's formula below uses this form.
