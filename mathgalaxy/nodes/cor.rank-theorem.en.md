## Statement · **draft**
Let $U \subseteq \mathbb{R}^{n}$ be open and $f : U \to \mathbb{R}^{m}$ of class $C^{1}$, and suppose the rank of the Jacobian is **identically** $r$ on $U$ (that is, $\operatorname{rank} Df(x) = r$ for all $x \in U$).
