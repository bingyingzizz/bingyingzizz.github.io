# snake lemma　`thm.snake-lemma`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=thm.snake-lemma)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./thm.snake-lemma.md)

⭐ **Use**: it is the engine of **long exact sequences** — stringing together each layer (kernel, cokernel) of a short exact sequence $0 \to K \to L \to M \to 0$ uses the $\delta$ of each layer. Wherever one "has a short exact sequence and wants a long one", the snake lemma is running underneath.
