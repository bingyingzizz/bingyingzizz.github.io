# Equivalence Relations in Condensed Sets Are Effective　`prop.cond-equivalence-effective`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=prop.cond-equivalence-effective)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./prop.cond-equivalence-effective.md)

⭐ **Together these three are exactly the input to Giraud's theorem**: a category with all colimits, universal colimits, and effective equivalence relations is a topos. Conversely, that $\mathrm{Cond}$ is a topos can be checked **item by item** from these three, without returning to the definition "it is a category of sheaves on a site".
