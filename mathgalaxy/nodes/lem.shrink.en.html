Statement

Let XXX be a normal space and let U={Ui}i∈I\mathcal{U} = \{ U_{i} \}_{i \in I}U={Ui​}i∈I​ be a locally finite open cover of XXX. Then there is an open cover of XXX, denoted by V={Vi}i∈I\mathcal{V} = \{ V_{i} \}_{i \in I}V={Vi​}i∈I​ (with the same index set), such that

Vi⊆Vi‾⊆Ui(i∈I),V_{i} \subseteq \overline{V_{i}} \subseteq U_{i}\qquad (i \in I),Vi​⊆Vi​​⊆Ui​(i∈I),

and V\mathcal{V}V is still locally finite. Such a family V\mathcal{V}V is called a shrinking of U\mathcal{U}U.

Moreover, x∉Wi+x \notin \mathcal{W}_{i^{+}}x∈/Wi+​ implies x∉Vjx \notin V_{j}x∈/Vj​ (j<ij < ij<i) and x∉Ukx \notin U_{k}x∈/Uk​ (k>ik > ik>i), so the invariant forces xxx to lie in UiU_{i}Ui​. Hence Ai⊆UiA_{i} \subseteq U_{i}Ai​⊆Ui​.

This is the standard reformulation of normality: between a closed set and an open set containing it, there is an open set whose closure still lies in the larger open set.

Thus Wi+\mathcal{W}_{i^{+}}Wi+​ still covers XXX. Limit step (iii is a limit ordinal): Let x∈Xx \in Xx∈X.

If x∈Ukx \in U_{k}x∈Uk​ for some k≥ik \ge ik≥i, the point is already covered.

Otherwise, x∉Ukx \notin U_{k}x∈/Uk​ for every k≥ik \ge ik≥i, and the cover Wi\mathcal{W}_{i}Wi​ gives some j<ij < ij<i such that x∈Ujx \in U_{j}x∈Uj​.

To check coverage, given xxx, point finiteness gives an index iii greater than all indices of members containing xxx among the UjU_{j}Uj​. Applying the covering property to Wi\mathcal{W}_{i}Wi​ then gives x∈Vjx \in V_{j}x∈Vj​ (for some j<ij < ij<i).

∎

Why local finiteness is essential: The limit ordinal step uses the fact that only finitely many members contain xxx. A general open cover need not have this property and may admit no shrinking. This explains why normality does not imply paracompactness.

⭐ There are two uses: turning a locally finite open refinement from paracompactness into a locally finite closed refinement by taking closures, and turning a locally finite open refinement into a partition of unity by using Urysohn's lemma between AiA_{i}Ai​ and X∖UiX \setminus U_{i}X∖Ui​ and then normalizing.

⚠️ After shrinking, the closures also lie in the corresponding original members. This is stronger than refining again, which only preserves coverage and local finiteness and does not guarantee containment of closures in the corresponding members.
