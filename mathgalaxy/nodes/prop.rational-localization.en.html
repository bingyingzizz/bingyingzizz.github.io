Statement

Let (A,A+)→(B,B+)(A,A^+) \to (B,B^+)(A,A+)→(B,B+) be a map of discrete Huber pairs such that Spa⁡(B,B+)→Spa⁡(A,A+)\operatorname{Spa}(B,B^+) \to \operatorname{Spa}(A,A^+)Spa(B,B+)→Spa(A,A+) factors through the rational open set U=U ⁣(g1,…,gnf)U = U\!\left(\frac{g_1,\ldots,g_n}{f}\right)U=U(fg1​,…,gn​​).

Then the map factors uniquely through

(A[1f], A+[g1f,…,gnf]~),\Bigl(A\bigl[\tfrac{1}{f}\bigr],\ \widetilde{A^+\bigl[\tfrac{g_1}{f}, \ldots, \tfrac{g_n}{f}\bigr]}\Bigr),(A[f1​], A+[fg1​​,…,fgn​​]​),

where the tilde denotes integral closure in A[1/f]A[1/f]A[1/f]. Moreover

Spa⁡(A[1f], A+[gif]~)⟶Spa⁡(A,A+)\operatorname{Spa}\Bigl(A\bigl[\tfrac{1}{f}\bigr],\ \widetilde{A^+\bigl[\tfrac{g_i}{f}\bigr]}\Bigr) \longrightarrow \operatorname{Spa}(A,A^+)Spa(A[f1​], A+[fgi​​]​)⟶Spa(A,A+)

is a homeomorphism onto UUU.

Notes

⭐ This is the precise meaning of "rational open set = affine open set": each rational open set is itself an Spa⁡\operatorname{Spa}Spa, obtained by inverting fff and adjoining gi/fg_i/fgi​/f to A+A^+A+.

Thus adic spaces have a basis of affine open sets, and one can do sheaf theory on them as on schemes.
