# Cohomology as Ext　`prop.cohomology-as-ext`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=prop.cohomology-as-ext)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./prop.cohomology-as-ext.md)

📌 Compare with "the section formula on Stonean spaces": that one writes Ext as $\operatorname{Ext}^{n}(M \cdot S, N)$, this one writes Ext as $H^{n}(X, N)$, and together they say exactly that "$H^{n}(X, N)$ can be computed from a resolution of $\mathbb{Z}\cdot X$".
