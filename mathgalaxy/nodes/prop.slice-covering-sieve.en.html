Statement

Let C\mathcal{C}C be a site, T∈C^T \in \widehat{\mathcal{C}}T∈C, s∈T(X)s \in T(X)s∈T(X). Then on sss, a sieve RRR is a covering in C/T\mathcal{C}/TC/T   ⟺  \iff⟺ j^T!R\hat j_{T!}Rj^​T!​R is the covering sieve of XXX in C\mathcal{C}C.

Notes

Proof. Both sides are translations of "can the cocone over RRR cover" ✓: coverings in C/T\mathcal{C}/TC/T are pulled back from coverings of C\mathcal{C}C along jTj_{T}jT​, and jT!j_{T!}jT!​ is precisely the functor carrying sieves in C/T\mathcal{C}/TC/T back to C\mathcal{C}C ✓.

⚠️ But jT!j_{T!}jT!​ is not left exact in general — so this does not produce a morphism of sites (which is exactly why one later uses the cocontinuity of jTj_{T}jT​ to build morphisms of topoi). ∎
