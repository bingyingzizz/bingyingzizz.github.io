# Cond is a topos　`thm.cond-topos`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=thm.cond-topos)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./thm.cond-topos.md)

- **limits and colimits are computed pointwise** ("limits and colimits in Cond"): this guarantees colimits exist and are universal;
- **every epimorphism is regular** ("epimorphisms in Cond are regular"): this guarantees quotient maps are well behaved;
- **every equivalence relation is effective** ("equivalence relations in Cond are effective"): this guarantees that quotienting and pulling back recovers the original.

The three together $\implies$ a topos. So this item is the **conclusion** and those three are the **reasons**; reading this cluster, first look at the conclusion for direction, then at the three for why it holds.
