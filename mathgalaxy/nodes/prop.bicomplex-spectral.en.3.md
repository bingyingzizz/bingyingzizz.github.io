# a bicomplex gives a spectral sequence　`prop.bicomplex-spectral`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=prop.bicomplex-spectral)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./prop.bicomplex-spectral.md)

⭐ A typical application is to take this spectral sequence for the bicomplex naturally obtained from an injective resolution of a complex, yielding the **Grothendieck spectral sequence**. The other filtration (horizontal first, then vertical) yields the Cartan–Eilenberg decomposition ✓.

⚠️ **Boundedness below** is essential. In the unbounded case, convergence of the spectral sequence requires a separate analysis (generally using the full machinery of $\varprojlim$).
