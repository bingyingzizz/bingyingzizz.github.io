# Stability of Syzygies　`prop.syzygy-stability`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=prop.syzygy-stability)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./prop.syzygy-stability.md)

$$\Omega^{n}M \oplus F \;\cong\; \Omega_{1}^{n}M \oplus F_{1} .$$

In particular, $\Omega^{n}M$ is projective $\iff$ $\Omega_{1}^{n}M$ is projective. Thus whether the $n$th syzygy is projective is independent of the choice of resolution, which is why projective dimension is well defined.
