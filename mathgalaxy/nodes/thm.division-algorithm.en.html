Notes

⭐ "KKK is a field" cannot be dropped: over the ring Z[x]\mathbb{Z}[x]Z[x] there is no division with remainder (x2x^{2}x2 divided by 2x+12x + 12x+1 does not produce a polynomial quotient).

Its role is to guarantee that the leading coefficient is invertible, so that the highest-degree term can be eliminated.

⭐ The consequences are far-reaching: division with remainder gives the Euclidean algorithm (for the gcd), hence Bézout's identity (af+bg=gcd⁡af + bg = \gcdaf+bg=gcd), hence "irreducible   ⟹  \implies⟹ prime", hence unique factorization (K[x] 是唯一分解整环).

Euclidean domains are the "everything works" class of rings.

⚠ Uniqueness uses comparison of degrees, not "uniqueness of the division procedure" — the choices at each step of the division are not unique, but the final quotient and remainder are.
