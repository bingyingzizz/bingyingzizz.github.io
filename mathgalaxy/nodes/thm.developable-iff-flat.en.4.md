# criterion for developable surfaces　`thm.developable-iff-flat`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=thm.developable-iff-flat)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./thm.developable-iff-flat.md)

- (3) and (4): if $K \equiv 0$, the local classification of constant-curvature surfaces (`thm.constant-gauss-curvature`, case $K = 0$) gives local isometry to the plane; conversely Gauss's theorema egregium ($K$ is an isometry invariant, and the plane has $K$ zero).

⚠ **With flat points, 1 and 4 decouple**: there are surfaces with $K \equiv 0$ containing flat patches that cannot be written with "tangent plane constant along the rulings". So 4 should be read as "locally isometric to the plane", which always holds.
