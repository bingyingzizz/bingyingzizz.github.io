**Step 6. Obtaining the long exact sequence.** Apply `thm.long-exact-pair` to the short exact sequence above, using the chain complex version proved by diagram chasing, to obtain the long exact sequence. Here $H_{p}(S_{*}(U) \oplus S_{*}(V)) = H_{p}(U) \oplus H_{p}(V)$, and $H_{p}(S_{*}(U) + S_{*}(V)) \cong H_{p}(X)$ follows from Step 4. To identify the two maps, $(a) \mapsto (a, -a)$ gives $\Phi[z] = (i_{*}[z], -j_{*}[z])$, and $(a, b) \mapsto a + b$ gives $\Psi[a, b] = [a + b]$. ∎
