## Statement · **draft**
Let $H$, $G$ be groups and $\varphi : G \to \operatorname{Aut}(H)$ a group homomorphism. The underlying set of the **semidirect product** $H \rtimes_{\varphi} G$ is $H \times G$, with multiplication

$$(h_{1}, g_{1})(h_{2}, g_{2}) := \bigl(h_{1}\, \varphi(g_{1})(h_{2}),\ g_{1} g_{2}\bigr).$$
