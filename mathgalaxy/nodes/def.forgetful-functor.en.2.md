# forgetful functor　`def.forgetful-functor`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=def.forgetful-functor)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./def.forgetful-functor.md)

- $\mathbf{Top}\to\mathbf{Set}$: forgets the open sets, keeping only the underlying sets and functions;
- $\mathbf{Grp}\to\mathbf{Set}$: forgets the group operation and the unit;
- $\mathbf{Vect}_K\to\mathbf{Set}$: forgets addition and scalar multiplication;
- $\mathbf{Vect}_K\to\mathbf{Ab}$: forgets the $K$-scalar multiplication, keeping only the additive group.

Strictly speaking, "forgetful functor" is not a single functor but a standard class of functors from **concrete categories** (categories equipped with a faithful functor to $\mathbf{Set}$) to $\mathbf{Set}$.
