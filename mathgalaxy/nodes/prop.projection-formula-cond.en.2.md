## Notes · **draft**
⭐ **Reading**: the underlying-space functor $X \mapsto X(\cdot)$ commutes with "multiplying by a locally compact space". Thus operations such as "multiplying by a compact piece" can be performed first in $\mathrm{Cond}$ and then brought back to topological spaces, or in the reverse order.
