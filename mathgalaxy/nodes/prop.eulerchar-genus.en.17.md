**9. Independent verification in standard form (complete).** Represent the connected sum of $g$ tori by a polygon with $4g$ sides, paired according to $a_{1}b_{1}a_{1}^{-1}b_{1}^{-1}\cdots$. Take the fan triangulation from the center. The vertex count is $2$ (the center $+$ one boundary vertex class), the edge count is $2g$ (paired boundary edges) $+4g$ (spokes) $=6g$, and the face count is $4g$, so $\chi=2-6g+4g=2-2g$.
