## Statement · **draft**
Let $M^{\bullet}$ be a complex of seminormed abelian groups, and let $\widehat{M}^{\bullet}$ be its Banach completion (the completion after taking the Hausdorff quotient).

Then $M^{\bullet}$ is boundedly exact at $M^{n}$ with bound $K$ $\iff$ $\widehat{M}^{\bullet}$ is boundedly exact at $\widehat{M}^{n}$ with bound $K$.
