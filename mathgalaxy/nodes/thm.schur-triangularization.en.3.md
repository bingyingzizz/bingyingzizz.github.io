# Schur Triangularization　`thm.schur-triangularization`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=thm.schur-triangularization)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./thm.schur-triangularization.md)

**Choose an eigenvector**: The characteristic polynomial $\chi_{A}(t) = \det(tI - A)$ has degree $n$ and complex coefficients. By the **fundamental theorem of algebra**, it has a root $\lambda$. Thus there exists $0 \ne x \in \mathbb{C}^{n}$ such that $Ax = \lambda x$. Normalize $x$, keeping the notation $x$, so that $\lVert x\rVert = 1$.
