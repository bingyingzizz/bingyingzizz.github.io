设 GGG 是有限 ppp-群，H<GH < GH<G 是真子群。则 H<NG(H),H < N_G(H),H<NG​(H), 即 HHH 真包含于它在 GGG 中的正规化子。

特别地，指数 ppp 的子群正规；并且 ppp-群的中心非平凡。

说明

证明见边 imp.p-group-subgroups：对 ∣G∣|G|∣G∣ 归纳，用类方程给出 Z(G)≠{e}Z(G) \ne \{e\}Z(G)={e}，再分 HZ(G)<GHZ(G) < GHZ(G)<G 与 HZ(G)=GHZ(G) = GHZ(G)=G 两种情形。

⭐ 用途：对 iii 归纳可证 ppp-群有所有 pip^{i}pi 阶子群（0≤i≤n0 \le i \le n0≤i≤n）；这是 Sylow 存在性证明中「i<ai < ai<a 部分」的关键一步。

⚠ 结论对一般有限群不成立：S3S_3S3​ 中 H=⟨(12)⟩H = \langle(12)\rangleH=⟨(12)⟩ 的 NS3(H)=HN_{S_3}(H) = HNS3​​(H)=H，但 S3S_3S3​ 不是 ppp-群。

Statement draft

Let GGG be a finite ppp-group and H<GH < GH<G a proper subgroup. Then

H<NG(H),H < N_G(H),H<NG​(H),

that is, HHH is properly contained in its normalizer in GGG.

In particular, a subgroup of index ppp is normal; and the center of a ppp-group is nontrivial.

Notes draft

The proof is on the edge imp.p-group-subgroups: induct on ∣G∣|G|∣G∣, use the class equation to get Z(G)≠{e}Z(G) \ne \{e\}Z(G)={e}, then split into the two cases HZ(G)<GHZ(G) < GHZ(G)<G and HZ(G)=GHZ(G) = GHZ(G)=G.

⭐ Use: induction on iii shows that a ppp-group has subgroups of every order pip^{i}pi (0≤i≤n0 \le i \le n0≤i≤n); this is the key step in the "i<ai < ai<a part" of the Sylow existence proof.

⚠ The conclusion fails for general finite groups: in S3S_3S3​, for H=⟨(12)⟩H = \langle(12)\rangleH=⟨(12)⟩ one has NS3(H)=HN_{S_3}(H) = HNS3​​(H)=H, but S3S_3S3​ is not a ppp-group.
