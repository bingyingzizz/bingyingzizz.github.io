## Statement · **reviewed**
$\mathbf{CHaus}$ is a **reflective subcategory** of $\mathbf{Top}$; the reflection is called the **Stone–Čech compactification**:

$$\beta : \mathbf{Top} \longrightarrow \mathbf{CHaus}, \qquad X \mapsto \beta X$$

that is, for every $X \in \mathbf{Top}$ and every compact Hausdorff space $K$,

$$\operatorname{Hom}_{\mathbf{CHaus}}\bigl(\beta X,\ K\bigr) \;\cong\; \operatorname{Hom}_{\mathbf{Top}}\bigl(X,\ K\bigr)$$

and the inclusion $X \to \beta X$ is an isomorphism whenever $X$ itself is compact Hausdorff.
