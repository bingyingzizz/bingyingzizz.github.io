# product measure　`def.product-measure`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=def.product-measure)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./def.product-measure.md)

**③ Extension**: $\mu_{0}$ induces an outer measure on $X \times Y$, and restricting it to $\mathcal{M} \otimes  \mathcal{N}$ gives a measure, written

$$\mu \times \nu$$

that is, the **product measure**.

## Notes · **reviewed**
Well-definedness of $\mu_{0} $ has to be checked (the same $E$ can be split into different disjoint unions of rectangles) — this is exactly why "rectangles" are replaced by "**disjoint** unions of rectangles": under disjoint unions, additivity is easy to verify.
