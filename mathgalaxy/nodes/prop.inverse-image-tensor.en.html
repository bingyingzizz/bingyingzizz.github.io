Statement

Let f:T→T′f : \mathcal{T} \to \mathcal{T}'f:T→T′ be a morphism of topoi and M,NM, NM,N abelian group sheaves. Then

f∗Hom⁡Z(f−1M, N)=Hom⁡Z(M, f∗N),f−1(M⊗ZN)≅f−1M⊗Zf−1N.f_{*}\operatorname{Hom}_{\mathbb{Z}}\bigl(f^{-1}M,\ N\bigr) = \operatorname{Hom}_{\mathbb{Z}}\bigl(M,\ f_{*}N\bigr), \qquad f^{-1}\bigl(M \otimes_{\mathbb{Z}} N\bigr) \cong f^{-1}M \otimes_{\mathbb{Z}} f^{-1}N.f∗​HomZ​(f−1M, N)=HomZ​(M, f∗​N),f−1(M⊗Z​N)≅f−1M⊗Z​f−1N.

Notes

Proof. The first identity strings together the adjunction f−1⊣f∗f^{-1} \dashv f_{*}f−1⊣f∗​ and the adjunction of internal Hom ✓ (the natural isomorphisms they give on test objects are the same).

The second: f−1f^{-1}f−1 is a left adjoint, hence preserves colimits ✓, and the tensor product is a colimit in the monoidal structure (a cokernel of a quotient) ✓; one may also verify it pointwise ✓. ∎
