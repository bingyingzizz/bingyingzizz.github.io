## Notes · **draft**
In $\mathbf{Set}$ monomorphisms are exactly injections and epimorphisms exactly surjections — the latter **does not need the axiom of choice**: if $f$ is not surjective, choose $b \notin \operatorname{im} f$ and on the two-point set $\{0,1\}$ set $g(b)=0$, $h(b)=1$, while both agree on $\operatorname{im} f$; then $g \circ f = h \circ f$ but $g \ne h$.
