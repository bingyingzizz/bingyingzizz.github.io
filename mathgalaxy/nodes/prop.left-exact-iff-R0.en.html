prop.left-exact-iff-R0

Statement

Let A\mathcal{A}A be an abelian category (with enough injectives), A′\mathcal{A}'A′ an abelian category, and F:A→A′F : \mathcal{A} \to \mathcal{A}'F:A→A′ an additive functor. Then

FFF is left exact   ⟺  \iff⟺ for every M∈AM \in \mathcal{A}M∈A one has R0F(M)≅F(M)R^{0}F(M) \cong F(M)R0F(M)≅F(M).

Dually (with enough projectives): FFF is right exact   ⟺  L0F≅F\iff L_{0}F \cong F⟺L0​F≅F.

Notes

⭐ In one sentence: left exactness can be tested using only the 000-th derived functor. Conversely, this explains why "R0F=FR^{0}F = FR0F=F" is treated as the default property of a left exact functor in the notation of derived functors.

⚠️ The hypothesis "FFF is additive" cannot be dropped: derived functors of non-additive functors make no sense (they are defined on complexes in an additive category).
