## Notes · **draft**
Putting compactness and Hausdorffness together is a good bargain: **a compact Hausdorff space is regular and normal**, and a continuous bijection from a compact space to a Hausdorff space is automatically a homeomorphism. Many things that require extra hypotheses in $\mathbf{Top}$ come for free here.

**Arbitrary limits exist** in $\mathbf{CHaus}$, and they are exactly the result computed in $\mathbf{Top}$ (see the entry "CHaus is a reflective subcategory").
