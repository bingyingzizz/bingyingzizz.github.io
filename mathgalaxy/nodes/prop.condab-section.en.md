## Statement · **draft**
Let $F \in \mathbf{FCHaus}$. Then the **section functor**

$$\Gamma(F, -) : \mathrm{CondAb} \longrightarrow \mathbf{Ab}, \qquad M \mapsto \Gamma(F, M) := M(F)$$

preserves **all limits and all colimits**.
