## Statement · **draft**
**The category of rings** $\mathbf{Rng}$: objects are rings (in the convention of @@def.ring@@: not necessarily unital, not necessarily commutative) and morphisms are ring homomorphisms. **The zero ring is a zero object** (both initial and terminal); products are constructed pointwise; equalizers are subrings; pullbacks are fiber products.
