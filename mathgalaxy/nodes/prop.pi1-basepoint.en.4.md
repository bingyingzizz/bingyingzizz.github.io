# change of basepoint　`prop.pi1-basepoint`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=prop.pi1-basepoint)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./prop.pi1-basepoint.md)

⚠️ If $\pi_{1}$ is non-abelian, an inner automorphism need not be the identity, so **conjugacy classes** are the basepoint-independent data. Another reading of the conjugacy-class space: the conjugation action on $\pi_{1}(X)$ $\leftrightarrow$ the classification of homotopy classes of loops based at $x_{0}$ under "free homotopy".

**Contrast with $\pi_{0}$**: $\pi_{1}$ uses **paths** to connect different basepoints; reordering the basepoints is exactly why path-connectedness is needed here (otherwise $\Pi_{\gamma}$ cannot even be written down).
