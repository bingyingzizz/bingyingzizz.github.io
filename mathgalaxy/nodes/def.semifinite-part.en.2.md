## Notes · **draft**
Its role: to repair a measure that may take very bad infinite values into a semifinite one, while keeping as much of the original finite part as possible.

If $\mu$ is itself semifinite, then $\mu _{0} = \mu$ — so this is the “semifinitization” operation.
