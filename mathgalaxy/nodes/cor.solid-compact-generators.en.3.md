## Notes · **draft**
⭐ (iii) is the most striking feature of "non-Archimedean completion": $\mathbb{R}$ **completes to $0$** in the solid sense.

This is not a bug — solid theory is designed for $p$-adic/analytic geometry, and the Archimedean direction has to be handled separately (Clausen–Scholze's later analytic stacks use liquid/analytic rings).

⚠ Contrast: $\mathbb{Z}_p$ remains itself after solidification (already completed in the $p$-adic sense), whereas $\mathbb{R}$ disappears — "solid" must not be understood as "complete the space".
