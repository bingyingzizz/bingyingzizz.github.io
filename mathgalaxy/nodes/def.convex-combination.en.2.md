## Notes · **draft**
⭐ Characterization of a convex set $C$: $C$ is convex $\iff$ $C$ is closed under arbitrary finite convex combinations; this is the standard way of extending convexity from two points to finitely many points. ⚠ Only **finitely** many points are allowed here; infinite convex combinations generally need topology/convergence and are not part of the elementary definition.
