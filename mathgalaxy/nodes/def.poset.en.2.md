# partial order　`def.poset`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=def.poset)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./def.poset.md)

If in addition **comparability** $\forall x, y \in A ( x \preceq y \vee y \preceq x )$ holds, then $\preceq$ is called a **total order** (linear order) on $A$, and $(A, \preceq )$ is called a totally ordered set.

Call $(A, \preceq )$ a **partially ordered set** (poset). Define the **strict order** from $\preceq$ by: $x \prec y :\iff x \preceq y \wedge  x \ne y$

## Notes · **draft**
Standard examples: $\langle \mathcal{P}(S), \subseteq \rangle$ is a poset but not totally ordered; $\langle \mathbb{R}, \le \rangle$ is totally ordered.
