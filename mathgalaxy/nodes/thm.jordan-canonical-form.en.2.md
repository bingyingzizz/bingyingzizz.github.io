# Jordan canonical form　`thm.jordan-canonical-form`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=thm.jordan-canonical-form)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./thm.jordan-canonical-form.md)

each $J_k$ is a **Jordan block** of the form $\lambda_k I + N_k$ ($N_k$ being the nilpotent "up-shift" matrix).

Equivalent formulation: $T$ is diagonalizable $\iff$ all Jordan blocks are $1 \times 1$.

## Notes · **draft**
⭐ This is **the only complete classification theorem in finite-dimensional linear algebra**: over an algebraically closed field, "similarity" is completely determined by the Jordan type (block sizes and the distribution of eigenvalues).

It also gives a complete list of "similarity invariants".
