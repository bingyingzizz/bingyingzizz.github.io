# Chart and Transition Maps　`def.manifold-chart`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=def.manifold-chart)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./def.manifold-chart.md)

**Example**: on $\mathbb{R}^{n}$ take $U = \mathbb{R}^{n}$ and $\varphi = \operatorname{id}$; this is a one-chart atlas.

For $S^{n} \subseteq \mathbb{R}^{n+1}$ use the two stereographic charts (from the north and south poles to $\mathbb{R}^{n}$); their transition map is $x \mapsto x/\lVert x\rVert^{2}$, which is smooth.
