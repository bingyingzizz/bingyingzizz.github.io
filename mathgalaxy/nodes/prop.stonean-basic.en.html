Statement

(a) If XXX is extremally disconnected, then the closures of any two disjoint open sets U,VU, VU,V are still disjoint: U‾∩V‾=∅\overline{U} \cap \overline{V} = \emptysetU∩V=∅.

(b) An extremally disconnected Hausdorff space is totally disconnected.

Notes

(a) By contradiction: if U‾∩V‾\overline{U} \cap \overline{V}U∩V is non-empty, take a point xxx in it.

Since XXX is extremally disconnected, U‾\overline{U}U and V‾\overline{V}V are both clopen, so their relation to their own interiors forces a contradiction — concretely, every neighbourhood of xxx must meet both UUU and VVV, while these two clopen sets separate xxx from "the other".

(b) Let x≠yx \ne yx=y. Hausdorffness gives open sets U∋xU \ni xU∋x, V∋yV \ni yV∋y separating them; by (a) two disjoint clopen sets separate xxx from yyy, hence C(x)⊆U∌yC(x) \subseteq U \not\ni yC(x)⊆U∋y, y∉C(x)y \notin C(x)y∈/C(x). So every connected component is a point.

Making (a) precise. Let U,VU, VU,V be disjoint open sets. Put W:=X∖V‾W := X\setminus \overline{V}W:=X∖V — it is open, and U⊆WU\subseteq WU⊆W. Since U⊆W=W∘U\subseteq W = W^{\circ}U⊆W=W∘, taking closures gives U‾⊆W∘‾\overline U\subseteq\overline{W^{\circ}}U⊆W∘; and the equivalent characterization of extremal disconnectedness is "the closure of every open set is open", i.e. W∘‾\overline{W^{\circ}}W∘ is open, so W∘‾=W∘\overline{W^{\circ}} = W^{\circ}W∘=W∘, hence U‾⊆W∘=W=X∖V‾\overline U \subseteq W^{\circ} = W = X\setminus\overline VU⊆W∘=W=X∖V, i.e. U‾∩V‾=∅\overline U\cap\overline V = \emptysetU∩V=∅ ✓.

∎ (Equivalent phrasing: extremally disconnected   ⟺  \iff⟺ the closures of disjoint open sets are disjoint.) (b) made precise: by (a), the separating open sets U∋xU\ni xU∋x, V∋yV\ni yV∋y given by Hausdorffness satisfy U‾∩V‾=∅\overline U\cap\overline V = \emptysetU∩V=∅;

and U‾\overline UU, V‾\overline VV are both clopen (extremal disconnectedness), so they form an open-closed separation between xxx and yyy: the connected component of xxx lies in U‾\overline UU, hence does not contain yyy ✓.

Thus every connected component is a point, i.e. the space is totally disconnected.

∎
