## Notes · **draft**
**"Inversion is smooth" can actually be omitted**: assuming only that multiplication is smooth, every $L_{g}$ and $R_{g}$ is smooth; the inverse function theorem gives smoothness of $i$ near $e$, and $i = L_{g^{-1}} \circ i \circ R_{g^{-1}}$ propagates it everywhere. Following convention, the definition here includes both conditions, saving one appeal to the inverse function theorem.
