## Notes · **draft**
Trichotomy lets ordinals serve as **coordinates**: the order types of any two **well-ordered sets** are comparable, and **no Choice is needed**.

This is a completely different statement from "any two **cardinals** are comparable", which does need AC (see "the comparability theorem for cardinals"). The difference is that an ordinal **carries** a well-order — namely $\in$ — whereas a general set has no a priori well-order, and finding one for it is exactly the content of the well-ordering theorem ($\iff$ the Axiom of Choice).
