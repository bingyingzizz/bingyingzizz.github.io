An $(r,s)$-tensor at $p \in M$ is a multilinear map $T : (T^{*}_{p}M)^{r} \times (T_{p}M)^{s} \to \mathbb{R}$, and the tensor bundle $T^{(r,s)}M$ assembles these into a smooth manifold of dimension $n + n^{r+s}$ whose transition maps are products of $r$ copies of the Jacobian and $s$ copies of its inverse transpose. A tensor field is a smooth section of this bundle, written in coordinates with components $T^{i_{1}\cdots i_{r}}_{\ \ j_{1}\cdots j_{s}}$ that transform by one Jacobian factor per index.
