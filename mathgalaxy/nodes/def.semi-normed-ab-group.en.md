## Statement · **draft**
A **seminormed abelian group** is an abelian group $M$ equipped with a function $\|\cdot\| : M \to \mathbb{R}_{\ge 0}$ satisfying

- $\|0\| = 0$;
- $\|s + t\| \le \|s\| + \|t\|$ (triangle inequality);
- $\|-s\| = \|s\|$ (symmetry).
