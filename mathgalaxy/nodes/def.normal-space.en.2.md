## Notes · **draft**
**The hierarchy of separation axioms** (each step strengthens the previous one):

- $T_{1}$: singletons are closed;
- Hausdorff: two points can be separated by open sets;
- regular: a point and a closed set not containing it can be separated $\implies$ Hausdorff;
- **normal**: two disjoint closed sets can be separated $\implies$ regular.

**Compact Hausdorff $\implies$ normal** (the most frequently used consequence of compactness: in a compact space two disjoint closed sets can be separated by finitely many pairs of open sets). Hence spaces such as $[0,1]$ and $\beta I$ are normal — so Urysohn's lemma always applies there.
