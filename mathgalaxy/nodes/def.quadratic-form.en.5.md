# quadratic form　`def.quadratic-form`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=def.quadratic-form)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./def.quadratic-form.md)

- $q(x) = x^{\mathsf{T}}Ax$ (**$A$ symmetric**): the convention "$A$ symmetric" is already in force — a non-symmetric $A$ gives the same $q$ (because $x^{\mathsf{T}}A x$ sees only the symmetric part), so **quadratic forms correspond one-to-one with symmetric matrices**.
- $q \leftrightarrow$ a **symmetric bilinear form** $B$: by the **polarization formula** $B(x,y) = \frac{1}{2}\bigl(q(x+y) - q(x) - q(y)\bigr)$. ⚠️ The $\frac{1}{2}$ requires $\operatorname{char} K \ne 2$; in characteristic $2$ quadratic forms and bilinear forms **no longer correspond** (which is why quadratic form theory in characteristic 2 needs separate treatment).
