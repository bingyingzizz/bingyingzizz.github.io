## Statement · **draft**
Let $p$ be an odd prime with $p \nmid a$. Then

$$\left(\frac{a}{p}\right) \equiv a^{\frac{p-1}{2}} \pmod p .$$

## Notes · **draft**
**Why**: $\mathbb{F}_{p}^{\times}$ is a cyclic group of order $p-1$. Write $a = g^{k}$ ($g$ a primitive root).
