## Notes · **draft**
The two requirements together say one thing: **a functor transports the entire structure of a category verbatim** — identities remain identities, composites remain composites, and even associativity need not be checked again (it follows automatically).

Examples:

- the **forgetful functor** $\mathbf{Top} \to \mathbf{Set}$: $(X, \tau) \mapsto X$, treating continuous maps as ordinary maps.

The topology is discarded, hence "forgetful".
