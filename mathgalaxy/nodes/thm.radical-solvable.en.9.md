**Removing the roots-of-unity hypothesis**: In general, $F$ need not contain $\zeta_{n}$. Choose $N$ to be a common multiple of $1,2,\ldots,n$ (for example, $n!$), and set $F_{1}=F(\zeta_{N})$ and $K_{1}=K(\zeta_{N})$.
