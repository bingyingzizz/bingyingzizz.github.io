# Morera's theorem　`thm.morera`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=thm.morera)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./thm.morera.md)

⚠ The hypothesis "all triangles" **can** be replaced by "all circles": under **continuity**, it already **suffices** that the integral over every circle inside a closed disk vanishes to guarantee holomorphy — convolving with a mollifier keeps the circle condition, the expansion in small circles gives $\partial_{\bar z} f_\varepsilon = 0$, and then one takes a locally uniform limit.

📌 Together with Cauchy–Goursat: when $f$ is continuous, "$f$ holomorphic" and "$f$ has zero integral along every closed triangle" are **equivalent** statements.
