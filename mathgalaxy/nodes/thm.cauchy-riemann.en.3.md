# Cauchy–Riemann Equations　`thm.cauchy-riemann`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=thm.cauchy-riemann)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./thm.cauchy-riemann.md)

$\Delta z$ and $\overline{\Delta z}$ form a basis of $\mathbb{C}$ as a real vector space, and the increment can be "multiplication by a complex number" only if **only** $\Delta z$ appears;

the coefficient of $\Delta z$ is exactly $u_x + i v_x$.

The CR equations are simply "the coefficient of $\overline{\Delta z}$ is $0$", written in components.

⭐ A useful by-product: the Jacobian determinant of $f$ at $z_0$ is $= u_x v_y - u_y v_x = u_x^2 + v_x^2 = |f'(z_0)|^2 \ge 0$.

Hence a holomorphic map is **orientation-preserving** wherever the derivative is nonzero, which is the source of its conformality.
