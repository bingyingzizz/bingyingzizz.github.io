## Statement · **draft**
For an integer $p \ge 0$ define the **elementary factor**

$$E_{p}(z) := \begin{cases} 1 - z, & p = 0,\\ (1 - z)\exp\left(z + \frac{z^{2}}{2} + \cdots + \frac{z^{p}}{p}\right), & p \ge 1.\end{cases}$$

Key properties: $E_{p}$ is entire, $E_{p}(0) = 1$, and its **only zero** is $z = 1$; moreover the larger $p$, the closer $E_{p}(z)$ is to $1$ on $|z| \le 1$.
