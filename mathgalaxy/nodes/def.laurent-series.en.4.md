⚠ The trichotomy below requires $a$ to be an **isolated singularity** (holomorphic on some punctured disc $0 < |z - a| < R$); otherwise the shape of the principal part does not reflect the type of singularity — for example on $|z| > 1$ the Laurent expansion has infinitely many principal terms, while $0$ is holomorphic at $f$.
