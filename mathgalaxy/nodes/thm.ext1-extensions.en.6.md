# First Ext Classifies Extensions　`thm.ext1-extensions`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=thm.ext1-extensions)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./thm.ext1-extensions.md)

Associativity and commutativity follow from the fiber product and quotient constructions.

The zero element is $N\oplus M$ (the split extension).

The inverse of $E$ is obtained by replacing $E\to M$ with the negative of $N\to E$, that is, in $E$ reverse the sign of $N$ ✓.

Thus the equivalence classes form an abelian group ✓.
**Agreement with the group structure on $\operatorname{Ext}^{1}$.** The bijection above sends the Baer sum to addition in $\operatorname{Ext}^{1}$. When adding the classes of two extensions, the chosen splittings of the pullbacks can be combined, so the corresponding $\theta$ add ✓. ∎
