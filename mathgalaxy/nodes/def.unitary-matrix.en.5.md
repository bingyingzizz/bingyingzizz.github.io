# Unitary and Orthogonal Matrices　`def.unitary-matrix`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=def.unitary-matrix)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./def.unitary-matrix.md)

**Relation to def.isometry**: unitary matrices are exactly the matrices of "inner-product-preserving linear maps in an orthonormal basis". ⚠️ Inner-product-preserving operators (isometries) and unitary matrices correspond one-to-one only in an orthonormal basis; after a change of basis the matrix is no longer unitary, but the operator still preserves the inner product.

⭐ **Numerical significance**: unitary transformations do not amplify errors ($\lVert Ux\rVert = \lVert x\rVert$), so "unitary similarity" is the most stable kind of similarity — this is why both Schur triangularization and the SVD choose unitary transformations.
