# First Ext Classifies Extensions　`thm.ext1-extensions`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=thm.ext1-extensions)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./thm.ext1-extensions.md)

⭐ **This gives an interpretation of the elements of $\operatorname{Ext}^{1}$**: The group $\operatorname{Ext}^{1}$ classifies **actual middle terms**. Thus $\operatorname{Ext}^{1}(M, N) = 0$ has a concrete meaning that can be checked by hand: **every extension splits**.

📌 Higher $\operatorname{Ext}$ groups also have an interpretation in terms of their elements. The group $\operatorname{Ext}^{n}(M, N)$ classifies extensions $Y$ of the form $N \to E_{1}\to \cdots \to E_{n}\to M$ and of degree $n$ (homotopy classes of exact sequences of length $n$).
