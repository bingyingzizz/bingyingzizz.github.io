# Sheaves for the Precanonical Topology　`prop.precanonical-sheaf`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=prop.precanonical-sheaf)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./prop.precanonical-sheaf.md)

$$F(X) \;\cong\; \ker\Bigl(\prod_{i} F(X_{i}) \rightrightarrows \prod_{i,j} F(X_{i} \times_{X} X_{j})\Bigr)$$

Equivalently, the following two hold simultaneously:

$$F\Bigl(\coprod_{i \in I} X_{i}\Bigr) \;\cong\; \prod_{i \in I} F(X_{i}),$$

where $R$ is the **equivalence relation** on $X$ (the one corresponding to the cover in the topos).
