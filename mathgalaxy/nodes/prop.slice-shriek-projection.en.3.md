**Proof.** The projection formula is a general phenomenon of "compatibility of a left adjoint with a closed monoidal structure": map the Hom's on both sides to the same place using $j_{X!} \dashv j_{X}^{-1}$ and the tensor–internal Hom adjunction, and Yoneda's lemma gives the isomorphism ✓.
