whereas in $h_{S}(S^{1}) = \mathrm{Hom}(S^{1}, S^{1})$ these two elements are not equal.

The two differ, so the colimit is not computed pointwise.

**What "pointwise" means**: to compute a condensed set one must know its value at every $S$. Limits can be taken all the way down to each $S$; colimits cannot, with one extra sheafification step.

⭐ **This is the technical basis of all later computations**: every limit appearing in $\mathrm{Cond}$ can be computed at each $S$, and limits of sets are easy.
