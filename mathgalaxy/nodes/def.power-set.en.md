## Statement · **draft**
Let $X$ be a set. The **power set** of $X$ is the set whose elements are all subsets of $X$:

$$\mathcal{P}(X) := \{ A : A \subseteq X \}$$

Thus $A \in \mathcal{P}(X) \iff A \subseteq X$, and every element of $\mathcal{P}(X)$ is itself a set.

By the **Axiom of Power Set**, $\mathcal{P}(X)$ is indeed a set; by Extensionality it is unique.
