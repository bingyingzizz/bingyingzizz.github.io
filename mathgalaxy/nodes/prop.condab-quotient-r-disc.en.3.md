## Notes · **draft**
⚠️ **This quotient is not zero**: as soon as there is a nonconstant continuous real-valued function on $S$ (for example, when $S = \beta I$, take a bounded function that is not locally constant), $Q(S) \ne 0$. This is two sides of the same fact as "topological abelian groups are **not** an abelian category": quotients behave badly at the level of topological groups, and only at the condensed level does the correct section formula above appear.
