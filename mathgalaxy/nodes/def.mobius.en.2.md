# Möbius Transformation　`def.mobius`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=def.mobius)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./def.mobius.md)

It extends to a bijection of the Riemann sphere $\hat{\mathbb{C}} = \mathbb{C} \cup \{\infty\}$ onto itself: when $c \ne 0$, $f(-d/c) = \infty$ and $f(\infty) = a/c$;

when $c = 0$, $f(\infty) = \infty$.

The inverse is again a fractional linear transformation:

$$f^{-1}(w) = \frac{dw - b}{-cw + a}.$$

## Notes · **draft**
⭐ $ad - bc \ne 0$ is the **nondegeneracy** condition: when it equals $0$, $f$ degenerates to a constant.

It also guarantees $f'(z) = (ad - bc)/(cz + d)^2 \ne 0$, so $f$ is **conformal everywhere** (angle-preserving; see the entry on conformality).
