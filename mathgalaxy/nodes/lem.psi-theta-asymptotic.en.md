## Statement · **draft**
$\psi(x)-\theta(x)=O(\sqrt{x}\log x)=o(x)$. Hence $\psi(x)\sim x\iff\theta(x)\sim x$.

## Notes · **draft**
Only $\theta(y)\le y\log y$ and $k\le\log_{2}x$ are used (the tail estimate $O(\sqrt{x}\log x)$).
