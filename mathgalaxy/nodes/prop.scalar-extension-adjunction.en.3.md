## Notes · **draft**
**Proof**: an $S$-linear map $g : S \otimes_{R} M \to P$ is first of all $R$-linear, hence determined by $m \mapsto g(1 \otimes m)$ (because $s \otimes m = s(1 \otimes m)$);

conversely, given an $R$-linear map $\varphi : M \to P$, the $S$-balanced formula $(s, m) \mapsto s \varphi(m)$ is well defined, and the universal property gives a unique $S$-linear map $S \otimes_{R} M \to P$.

The two directions are inverse, and nothing is chosen in the formula, so it is natural ✓.
