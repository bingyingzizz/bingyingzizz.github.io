## Statement · **draft**
Let $S$ be a **closed nonorientable surface** already in **standard form**, the connected sum of $k$ copies of $\mathbb{RP}^{2}$ ($k\ge1$, see `thm.classification-surfaces`; $k$ is called the **nonorientable genus**). Then

$$\chi(S) = 2 - k .$$
