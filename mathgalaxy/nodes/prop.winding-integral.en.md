## Statement · **draft**
Let $\gamma : [0,1] \to \mathbb{C}$ be a piecewise $C^{1}$ closed curve and $a \notin \gamma([0,1])$. Then

$$n(\gamma, a) = \frac{1}{2\pi i} \oint_{\gamma} \frac{\mathrm{d}z}{z - a},$$

In particular the right-hand side is **always an integer**.
