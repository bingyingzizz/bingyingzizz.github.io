## Statement · **draft**
Let $V$ be a **finite-dimensional** real Banach space. Then its Pontryagin dual is the usual vector space dual:

$$\widehat{V} \;\cong\; V^{\ast} = \operatorname{Hom}_{\mathbb{R}}(V,\ \mathbb{R}).$$
