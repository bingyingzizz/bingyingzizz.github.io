## Notes · **draft**
**Why $\omega^{3} = 0$**: $\omega^{3}(v) = \langle \mathrm{d}r(v), e_{3}\rangle$, and $\mathrm{d}r(v)$ is a tangent vector of the surface while $e_{3}$ is normal. Equivalently: when $r$ moves along the surface it has no normal component.

⭐ **The system therefore gets shorter**: taking $i=3$ in the first structure equation turns it into the identity $0 = \omega^{1}\wedge\omega^{3}_{\ 1}+\omega^{2}\wedge\omega^{3}_{\ 2}$;

taking $i,j \in \{1,2\}$ in the second structure equation leaves only the $k=3$ term "left over" — and that term is the Gauss curvature.
