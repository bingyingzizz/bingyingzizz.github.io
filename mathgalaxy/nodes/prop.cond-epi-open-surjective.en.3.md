**Proof.** By "the criterion for epimorphisms of condensed sets", it suffices to check, for every **free** compact Hausdorff space $F$, that $C(F, X) \to C(F, Y)$ is surjective ✓.
Given $g : F \to Y$. Since $F$ is compact and $Y$ weak Hausdorff, $g(F)$ is a compact Hausdorff subset ✓, so there is a compact Hausdorff $S \subseteq X$ with $g(F) = f(S)$ ✓.
