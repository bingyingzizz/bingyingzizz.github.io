陈述

设 FFF 是自由紧 Hausdorff 空间（更一般地，Stonean 空间）。则自由凝聚态阿贝尔群 Z⋅F\mathbb{Z}\cdot FZ⋅F 是有限表现的投射凝聚态阿贝尔群。

说明

要证的等价形式是：函子 M↦Hom⁡Z(Z⋅F,M)≅Γ(F,M)M \mapsto \operatorname{Hom}_{\mathbb{Z}}(\mathbb{Z}\cdot F, M) \cong \Gamma(F, M)M↦HomZ​(Z⋅F,M)≅Γ(F,M) 保满态射与滤过余极限。

前半由「自由紧 Hausdorff 空间在 Cond\mathrm{Cond}Cond 里投射」给出，后半由截面函子的构造给出 —— 而截面函子实际上保所有余极限。

证明。 由张量—Hom 伴随，Hom⁡Z(Z⋅F,M)≅M(F)=Γ(F,M)\operatorname{Hom}_{\mathbb{Z}}(\mathbb{Z}\cdot F, M) \cong M(F) = \Gamma(F, M)HomZ​(Z⋅F,M)≅M(F)=Γ(F,M) ✓，所以只需看 Γ(F,−)\Gamma(F,-)Γ(F,−)。

  • 保满态射：上一条说 Γ(F,−)\Gamma(F,-)Γ(F,−) 保所有余极限，特别地保余核；vvv 满   ⟺  \iff⟺ coker⁡v=0\operatorname{coker} v = 0cokerv=0   ⟹  \implies⟹ coker⁡Γ(v)=0\operatorname{coker}\Gamma(v) = 0cokerΓ(v)=0   ⟹  \implies⟹ Γ(v)\Gamma(v)Γ(v) 满 ✓；
  • 保滤过余极限：同样由「保所有余极限」✓。

而标准判据是：PPP 投射   ⟺  \iff⟺ Hom⁡(P,−)\operatorname{Hom}(P,-)Hom(P,−) 保满态射；PPP 有限表现   ⟺  \iff⟺ Hom⁡(P,−)\operatorname{Hom}(P,-)Hom(P,−) 保滤过余极限。两条合起来，Z⋅F\mathbb{Z}\cdot FZ⋅F 就是有限表现的投射对象。∎

Statement draft

Let FFF be a free compact Hausdorff space (or, more generally, a Stonean space). Then the free condensed abelian group Z⋅F\mathbb{Z}\cdot FZ⋅F is a finitely presented projective condensed abelian group.

Notes draft

Equivalently, we must show that the functor M↦Hom⁡Z(Z⋅F,M)≅Γ(F,M)M \mapsto \operatorname{Hom}_{\mathbb{Z}}(\mathbb{Z}\cdot F, M) \cong \Gamma(F, M)M↦HomZ​(Z⋅F,M)≅Γ(F,M) preserves epimorphisms and filtered colimits.

The first assertion follows from the projectivity of free compact Hausdorff spaces in Cond\mathrm{Cond}Cond. The second follows from the construction of the section functor, which in fact preserves all colimits.

Proof. By the tensor–Hom adjunction, Hom⁡Z(Z⋅F,M)≅M(F)=Γ(F,M)\operatorname{Hom}_{\mathbb{Z}}(\mathbb{Z}\cdot F, M) \cong M(F) = \Gamma(F, M)HomZ​(Z⋅F,M)≅M(F)=Γ(F,M) ✓, so it suffices to consider Γ(F,−)\Gamma(F,-)Γ(F,−).

  • Preservation of epimorphisms: The preceding result says that Γ(F,−)\Gamma(F,-)Γ(F,−) preserves all colimits, in particular cokernels. vvv is an epimorphism   ⟺  \iff⟺ coker⁡v=0\operatorname{coker} v = 0cokerv=0   ⟹  \implies⟹ coker⁡Γ(v)=0\operatorname{coker}\Gamma(v) = 0cokerΓ(v)=0   ⟹  \implies⟹ Γ(v)\Gamma(v)Γ(v) is an epimorphism ✓;
  • Preservation of filtered colimits: This also follows from preservation of all colimits ✓.

The standard criteria are: PPP is projective   ⟺  \iff⟺ Hom⁡(P,−)\operatorname{Hom}(P,-)Hom(P,−) preserves epimorphisms, and PPP is finitely presented   ⟺  \iff⟺ Hom⁡(P,−)\operatorname{Hom}(P,-)Hom(P,−) preserves filtered colimits. Together, these give that Z⋅F\mathbb{Z}\cdot FZ⋅F is finitely presented and projective. ∎
