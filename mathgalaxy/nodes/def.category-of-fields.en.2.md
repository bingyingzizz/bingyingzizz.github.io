## Statement · **draft**
**The category of fields** $\mathbf{Field}$: objects are fields and morphisms are field homomorphisms (automatically injective). It has **no terminal object** (the zero ring is not a field), **no initial object** (no field admits homomorphisms to both $\mathbb Q$ and $\mathbb F_p$), and **no binary products** (the product of $\mathbb Q$ and $\mathbb F_p$ does not exist). Hence $\mathbf{Field}$ is neither complete nor cocomplete.
