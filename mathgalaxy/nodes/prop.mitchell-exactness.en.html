prop.mitchell-exactness · Freyd-Mitchell

Machine-drafted and pending author review. The Chinese text is authoritative.

Statement

Let C\mathcal{C}C be a small abelian category and E:C→A-ModE : \mathcal{C} \to A\text{-}\mathbf{Mod}E:C→A-Mod the Freyd–Mitchell embedding (fully faithful, exact). Then

  1. EEE is exact: it preserves finite limits and finite colimits, and carries kernels, cokernels, images, and short exact sequences over verbatim;
  2. EEE reflects projective and injective objects: if EPEPEP is projective in A-ModA\text{-}\mathbf{Mod}A-Mod, then PPP is projective in C\mathcal{C}C (similarly for injectives: EIEIEI injective   ⟹  \implies⟹ III injective);
  3. hence item 2 gives a sufficient condition directly: to establish that PPP is projective in C\mathcal{C}C (or III injective), it suffices to check that EPEPEP is projective in A-ModA\text{-}\mathbf{Mod}A-Mod (EIEIEI injective) — there one has elements, direct summands, and explicit free modules, so it is much easier to decide.

This is the precise form of the most commonly used step in "chasing elements": the difficulty is in C\mathcal{C}C, the verification is done in A-ModA\text{-}\mathbf{Mod}A-Mod.

Notes

Proof (uses only "fully faithful + exact"). ① Exactness. This is part of the statement of the Freyd–Mitchell theorem (exact = preserves finite limits and finite colimits), so kernels, cokernels, images, and short exact sequences are all carried over verbatim ✓. ② Reflection of projectives. Suppose EPEPEP is projective in A-ModA\text{-}\mathbf{Mod}A-Mod, i.e. Hom⁡A-Mod(EP,−)\operatorname{Hom}_{A\text{-}\mathbf{Mod}}(EP, -)HomA-Mod​(EP,−) is exact. Restricting it to the image of EEE is still exact; and full faithfulness of EEE gives a natural isomorphism

Hom⁡C(P,X)  ≅  Hom⁡A-Mod(EP,EX),\operatorname{Hom}_{\mathcal{C}}(P, X) \;\cong\; \operatorname{Hom}_{A\text{-}\mathbf{Mod}}(EP, EX),HomC​(P,X)≅HomA-Mod​(EP,EX),

so Hom⁡C(P,−)\operatorname{Hom}_{\mathcal{C}}(P, -)HomC​(P,−) is exact ✓, i.e. PPP is projective in C\mathcal{C}C ✓. (Only "EEE is fully faithful" is used — an epimorphism is sent by EEE to an epimorphism because EEE is exact and preserves quotients.) ③ Reflection of injectives. Dual: Hom⁡C(−,I)≅Hom⁡(−,EI)\operatorname{Hom}_{\mathcal{C}}(-, I) \cong \operatorname{Hom}(-, EI)HomC​(−,I)≅Hom(−,EI); injectivity of EIEIEI makes the right-hand side send monomorphisms to epimorphisms; and exactness of EEE sends monomorphisms in C\mathcal{C}C to monomorphisms, so the left-hand side does too ✓. ④ Note the direction. What is proved above is "projective in the module category   ⟹  \implies⟹ projective in C\mathcal{C}C", which is exactly what is used: to establish that C\mathcal{C}C is projective in PPP, go to A-ModA\text{-}\mathbf{Mod}A-Mod and check that EPEPEP is projective. ∎

⭐ Why the "reflection" direction is the useful half: in module categories there are ready-made tools for deciding projectivity (direct summands, vanishing of Ext⁡1\operatorname{Ext}^{1}Ext1, Baer-type criteria), whereas in an abstract small abelian category one has almost no handle. Reflection turns "a hard problem in C\mathcal{C}C" into "an easy problem in a module category".

⚠️ The other half (C\mathcal{C}C projective   ⟹  EP\implies EP⟹EP) is not what this item asserts, nor does it follow directly from the two conditions "fully faithful + exact": obtaining it requires the concrete construction of the embedding (Mitchell's construction is a Yoneda-type functor into a module category, and for the standard construction this half does hold). This map does not claim it here — cite the concrete construction when needed.

⚠️ Do not overpromise: EEE in general does not preserve infinite coproducts and infinite products (unless one adds a completeness condition such as AB4). So "constructions built with coproducts" (for example a free resolution of a generator) cannot be transported unconditionally.

📌 Division of labour with Giraud's theorem: Giraud embeds a topos as an exact reflective subcategory of a presheaf category; Freyd–Mitchell embeds a small abelian category as a fully faithful exact subcategory of a module category. Both are "find a concrete large category that contains you", and this item supplies "after embedding, which properties can be transported back".
