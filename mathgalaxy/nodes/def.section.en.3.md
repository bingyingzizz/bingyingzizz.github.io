# Section　`def.section`

[in graph](../#n=def.section)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./def.section.md)

called respectively the **vertical section** of $E$ at $x$ and the **horizontal section** at $y$.

Similarly, for a function $f : X \times Y \to \mathbb{C}$ define

$$f_x(y) := f^y(x) := f(x, y)$$

## Notes · **reviewed**
Intuition: cut a plane figure with a vertical (or horizontal) line; the segment you cut out is the section.

Sections are the language of Fubini's theorem: the double integral $\iint f d(\mu \times \nu)$ is "integrate along the sections first, then integrate the resulting function of the other variable".
