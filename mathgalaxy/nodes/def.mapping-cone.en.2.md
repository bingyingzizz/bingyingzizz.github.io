# mapping cone　`def.mapping-cone`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=def.mapping-cone)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./def.mapping-cone.md)

(the matrix is blocked according to $K^{n+1} \oplus L^{n}$: top left from the differential of $K$, bottom right from the differential of $L$, bottom left the component of $f$; $d^{2} = 0$ holds as soon as $f$ commutes with the differentials). The $k$-**shift** $K[k]$ is defined by $K[k]^{n} := K^{n+k}$, with differential carrying the sign $(-1)^{k} d^{n+k}$.

## Notes · **draft**
Use: it yields a short exact sequence $0 \to L \to M(f) \to K[1] \to 0$ (usually not split). Thus **every morphism embeds into an "almost exact" sequence**, which is the starting point of triangulated categories — all later definitions of distinguished triangles rest on it.
