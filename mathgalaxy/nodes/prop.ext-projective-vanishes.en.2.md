# Ext Vanishes on Projectives　`prop.ext-projective-vanishes`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=prop.ext-projective-vanishes)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./prop.ext-projective-vanishes.md)

$$\operatorname{Ext}^{n}_{\mathcal{A}}(P,\ M) = 0 \qquad (n > 0), \qquad \operatorname{Ext}^{0}_{\mathcal{A}}(P,\ M) = \operatorname{Hom}_{\mathcal{A}}(P,\ M).$$

Note that this does **not** require $\mathcal{A}$ to have enough projectives — $P$ being projective itself gives a projective resolution of $P$ of length $0$.

## Notes · **draft**
⭐ Dually: **injective objects have no higher Ext** ($\operatorname{Ext}^{n}(M, I) = 0$, $n>0$) — together these two are the entire reason "projective / injective objects may replace the terms in a resolution".
