# Rayleigh Quotient　`def.rayleigh-quotient`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=def.rayleigh-quotient)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./def.rayleigh-quotient.md)

(for a unit vector $x$ this is just $x^{*}Ax$, so it is really "the average stretch factor of $A$ in the direction $x$".)

**Core properties**:

1. $R_{A}(x)$ is always **real** and lies in $[\lambda_{\min}, \lambda_{\max}]$;
2. $\lambda_{\min} = \min_{x\ne 0} R_A(x)$, $\lambda_{\max} = \max_{x\ne 0} R_A(x)$, with the extrema attained at the corresponding eigenvectors;
3. more generally, all critical points are exactly the eigenvectors, and the critical values are exactly the eigenvalues.
