# category of algebras　`def.category-of-algebras`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=def.category-of-algebras)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./def.category-of-algebras.md)

$R$ is the initial object ($r\mapsto r1$); **zero algebras** ($1=0$) are allowed, and are the terminal object. Products are constructed pointwise; equalizers are subalgebras; pullbacks are fiber products. Coproducts are free products; coequalizers are quotient algebras (the generated two-sided ideal); pushouts are amalgamated free products.

## Notes · **draft**
**Complete and cocomplete**; **not additive**; in general **not cartesian closed**.
