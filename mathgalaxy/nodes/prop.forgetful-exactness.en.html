prop.forgetful-exactness

Statement

Algebraic forgetful functors preserve limits but not colimits; the inclusion C~↪C^\widetilde{\mathcal{C}} \hookrightarrow \widehat{\mathcal{C}}C↪C preserves limits but not colimits, while its left adjoint (sheafification) is exact.

Notes

Proof. Preservation of limits is the preceding proposition ✓; failure of colimits: the underlying set of M⊕NM \oplus NM⊕N is not M⊔NM \sqcup NM⊔N ✓; sheafification is a left adjoint and left exact, hence exact ✓. ∎
