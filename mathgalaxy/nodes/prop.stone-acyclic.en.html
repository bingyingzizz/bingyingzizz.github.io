Statement

If SSS is a Stone space (= compact Hausdorff totally disconnected), then for every abelian sheaf MMM, Hn(S,M)=0H^{n}(S, M) = 0Hn(S,M)=0 for all n≠0n \ne 0n=0.

Notes

⭐ This is the root of why Stonean spaces are convenient in condensed mathematics: sheaf cohomology degenerates automatically on them, and Γ(S,−)\Gamma(S, -)Γ(S,−) is directly an exact functor, so "taking sections" costs no derived correction.
