⭐ **Where it is used**: to prove "the underlying space of a quasi-separated condensed set is weak Hausdorff", first write the quasi-separated $X$ as a filtered colimit of compact Hausdorff pieces along inclusions, then take underlying spaces of this chain — by this item the colimit is still compactly generated weak Hausdorff.
