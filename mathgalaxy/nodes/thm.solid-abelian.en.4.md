# solid abelian groups form an abelian subcategory　`thm.solid-abelian`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=thm.solid-abelian)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./thm.solid-abelian.md)

$$\operatorname{RHom}(Z[S]^{\blacksquare}, C) \cong R\Gamma(S, C).$$

This is the main computation of Lecture VI (replace $C$ by $C \to C_{\mathbb{R}} \to C_{\mathbb{R}/\mathbb{Z}}$, then use the two-step resolution of compact abelian groups and the contractibility of $\mathbb{R}$-modules).

⭐ **The proof** is on the edge `imp.solid-abelian`: reduce to complexes of $\prod_I\mathbb{Z}$-type $\to$ the core assertion (6.1) $\to$ push the assertion back to $Z[S]^{\blacksquare}$ $\to$ apply the completion criterion.
