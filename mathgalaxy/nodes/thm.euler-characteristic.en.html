Statement

Let KKK be a finite** simplicial complex, αp\alpha_{p}αp​ the number of its ppp-simplices, and βp:=rank⁡Hp(K)\beta_{p} := \operatorname{rank} H_{p}(K)βp​:=rankHp​(K) the ppp-th Betti number** (the rank of the free part). Then

χ(K)=∑p(−1)pαp=∑p(−1)pβp.\chi(K) = \sum_{p} (-1)^{p} \alpha_{p} = \sum_{p} (-1)^{p} \beta_{p}.χ(K)=p∑​(−1)pαp​=p∑​(−1)pβp​.

That is: **the left side merely counts simplices, while the right side depends only on the homology groups**.

Notes

**Two examples**:

  • The tetrahedral subdivision of S2S^{2}S2: α=(4,6,4,0)\alpha = (4, 6, 4, 0)α=(4,6,4,0), χ=4−6+4−0=2\chi = 4 - 6 + 4 - 0 = 2χ=4−6+4−0=2; β=(1,0,1)\beta = (1, 0, 1)β=(1,0,1), ∑=1−0+1=2\sum = 1 - 0 + 1 = 2∑=1−0+1=2 ✓
  • The minimal subdivision of the real projective plane RP2\mathbb{RP}^{2}RP2 (6 vertices / 15 edges / 10 faces): χ=6−15+10=1\chi = 6 - 15 + 10 = 1χ=6−15+10=1; while H1(RP2)=Z/2H_{1}(\mathbb{RP}^{2}) = \mathbb{Z}/2H1​(RP2)=Z/2 **has rank zero**, so ∑(−1)pβp=1−0+0=1\sum(-1)^{p}\beta_{p} = 1 - 0 + 0 = 1∑(−1)pβp​=1−0+0=1 ✓

⚠ Note that the right side uses the rank (Betti number), not #Hp\#H_{p}#Hp​. Torsion (e.g. Z/2\mathbb{Z}/2Z/2) **does not enter** the alternating sum; the example RP2\mathbb{RP}^{2}RP2 above matches precisely because of this.

⭐ The real value of the theorem** is not the identity itself but that the right side depends only on homology** — hence χ\chiχ is independent of the triangulation (together with 单纯逼近) and is a topological invariant. Conversely, to compute χ\chiχ one only counts simplices, without computing homology at all.

**Idea of the proof** (the full computation is on the edge): twice rank–nullity, once on ∂p\partial_{p}∂p​ and once on Zp/Bp≅HpZ_{p}/B_{p} \cong H_{p}Zp​/Bp​≅Hp​; the dimensions of the boundary groups cancel in pairs in the alternating sum (telescoping).
