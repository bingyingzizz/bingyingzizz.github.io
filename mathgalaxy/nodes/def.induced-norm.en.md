## Statement · **draft**
Let $(V, \langle \cdot, \cdot \rangle)$ be an inner product space. Define

$$\|v\| := \sqrt{\langle v, v \rangle}, \qquad d(u, v) := \|u - v\|$$

Then $\|\cdot\|$ is a **norm** on $V$ (positive definite, homogeneous, triangle inequality), $d$ is a **metric**, and

$$\|v\| = 0 \iff v = 0, \qquad \|cv\| = |c|\,\|v\|, \qquad \|u + v\| \le \|u\| + \|v\|$$
