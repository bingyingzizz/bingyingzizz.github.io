Notes

Immediate consequences of the four items:

  • A homeomorphism fff gives an isomorphism f∗f_{*}f∗​ (the inverse is the inverse homeomorphism), so π1\pi_{1}π1​ is a topological invariant;
  • More strongly, a homotopy equivalence gives an isomorphism — so π1\pi_{1}π1​ is also a homotopy invariant, and a contractible space must have fundamental group 111;
  • By (3), π1\pi_{1}π1​ turns "composition of spaces" into "composition of groups", with the order not reversed (unlike f∗f^{*}f∗ in cohomology).
