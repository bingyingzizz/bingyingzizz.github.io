## Statement · **draft**
Let $u : F \to G$ be a morphism of sheaves. Then:

1. if $u$ is both a monomorphism and an epimorphism, then $u$ is an isomorphism;
2. $u$ has a **unique** epi-mono factorization: there is a unique $I$ with $u = F \twoheadrightarrow I \rightarrowtail G$.
