# topos　`def.topos`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=def.topos)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./def.topos.md)

- **① Small set of generators**: **this is the "smallness" condition, which pretopoi lack**. It guarantees that a topos behaves well enough (supports internal language, admits sheafification), and it is the hypothesis for "$\mathcal{T} \simeq \operatorname{Sh}(\mathcal{C}, J)$ with $\mathcal{C}$ small". ⚠️ Without it, the category may be "too large" to be a sheaf category.
- **② Finite limits**: same as pretopos, a purely structural requirement.
- **③ Coproducts exist and are disjoint and universal** (equivalently: **all** colimits exist): **one notch stronger than a pretopos** — a pretopos requires only **finite** coproducts. Together with ② this gives "all colimits can be computed"
