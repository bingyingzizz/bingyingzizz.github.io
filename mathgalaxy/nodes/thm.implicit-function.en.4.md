# Implicit Function Theorem　`thm.implicit-function`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=thm.implicit-function)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./thm.implicit-function.md)

⚠ The invertibility of $D_yF$ is **essential**: for $F(x, y) = x^{2} - y^{2}$ at $(0,0)$, $D_yF = 0$, and the equation $x^{2} = y^{2}$ cannot be solved uniquely for $y = g(x)$ near the origin (there are two branches $y = \pm x$).

⭐ The formula for $Dg$ need not be memorized: differentiate $F(x, g(x)) = 0$ on both sides and use the chain rule — $D_xF + D_yF \cdot Dg = 0$, then solve for $Dg$.

The full proof is on the edge.
