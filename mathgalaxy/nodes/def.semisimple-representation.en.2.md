## Notes · **draft**
The zero representation is semisimple by convention (empty direct sum). In finite dimension there are finitely many summands. Equivalent characterizations (a sum of irreducibles, existence of complements) are given in a separate theorem. Maschke's theorem shows that $F[G]$ is semisimple when $\operatorname{char}F\nmid |G|$.
