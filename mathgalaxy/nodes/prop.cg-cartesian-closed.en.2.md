# Compactly Generated Spaces Are Cartesian Closed　`prop.cg-cartesian-closed`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=prop.cg-cartesian-closed)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./prop.cg-cartesian-closed.md)

$$C\bigl(k(X \times Y),\ Z\bigr) \;\cong\; C\bigl(X,\ C(Y, Z)\bigr).$$

## Notes · **draft**
⭐ **This is the "usable thing" this cluster sets out to build**: in $k\mathbf{Top}$ **there are function spaces, and the function spaces stay inside the category**, so constructions such as "mapping spaces" and "homotopies" can be carried out entirely inside $k\mathbf{Top}$. $\mathbf{Top}$ itself cannot do this.

**How the proof goes.** First verify the set-level bijection by hand for compactly generated spaces
