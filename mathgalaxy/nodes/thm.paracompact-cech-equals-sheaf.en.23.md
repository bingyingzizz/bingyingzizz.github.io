# Čech Cohomology Equals Sheaf Cohomology on Paracompact Spaces　`thm.paracompact-cech-equals-sheaf`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=thm.paracompact-cech-equals-sheaf)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./thm.paracompact-cech-equals-sheaf.md)

\check{H}^{n}(X,\mathcal{M})=\varinjlim_{\mathfrak{U}}\check{H}^{n}(\mathfrak{U},\mathcal{M})\cong H^{n}(X,\mathcal{M})$. The isomorphism holds for an individual **good cover** (one whose finite intersections have vanishing higher cohomology, with contractible finite intersections in the constant sheaf case).
