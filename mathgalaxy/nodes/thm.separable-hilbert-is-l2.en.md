## Statement · **draft**
Let $H$ be a Hilbert space and $\dim H = \#I$ (the Hilbert dimension). Then $H$ is isometrically isomorphic to $\ell^2(I)$. In particular, every infinite-dimensional separable Hilbert space is isometrically isomorphic to $\ell^2(\mathbb{N})$.

## Notes · **draft**
Define $U : H \to \ell^2(I)$ by $U(x) := (\langle x, e_i\rangle)_{i \in I}$.
