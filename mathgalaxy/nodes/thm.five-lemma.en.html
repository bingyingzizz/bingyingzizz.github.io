thm.five-lemma

Machine-drafted and pending author review. The Chinese text is authoritative.

Statement

Let A\mathcal{A}A be an abelian category; both rows of the following commutative diagram are exact:

A1→A2→A3→A4→A5α1↓α2↓α3↓α4↓α5↓B1→B2→B3→B4→B5\begin{array}{ccccccccc} A_{1} & \to & A_{2} & \to & A_{3} & \to & A_{4} & \to & A_{5} \\[2pt] {\scriptstyle \alpha_{1}}\big\downarrow & & {\scriptstyle \alpha_{2}}\big\downarrow & & {\scriptstyle \alpha_{3}}\big\downarrow & & {\scriptstyle \alpha_{4}}\big\downarrow & & {\scriptstyle \alpha_{5}}\big\downarrow \\[2pt] B_{1} & \to & B_{2} & \to & B_{3} & \to & B_{4} & \to & B_{5} \end{array}A1​α1​↓⏐​B1​​→→​A2​α2​↓⏐​B2​​→→​A3​α3​↓⏐​B3​​→→​A4​α4​↓⏐​B4​​→→​A5​α5​↓⏐​B5​​

If α1\alpha_{1}α1​ is an epimorphism, α2,α4\alpha_{2}, \alpha_{4}α2​,α4​ are isomorphisms, and α5\alpha_{5}α5​ is a monomorphism, then the middle α3\alpha_{3}α3​ is also an isomorphism.

Notes

⭐ Corollary (the common form): if in the diagram A1=B1=0A_{1} = B_{1} = 0A1​=B1​=0, A5=B5=0A_{5} = B_{5} = 0A5​=B5​=0, then α3\alpha_{3}α3​ being mono (epi / iso) is determined by α2,α4\alpha_{2}, \alpha_{4}α2​,α4​ being mono (epi / iso) — this is the sentence most often quoted when "comparing long exact sequences": same at both ends, same in the middle.

⚠️ Relation to the snake lemma: neither can be proved by chasing elements; both need generalized elements and universal properties. The difference is that the five lemma only transfers isomorphisms (no connecting homomorphism needed), while the snake lemma must construct δ\deltaδ. Whenever one wants to "compare two spectral sequences / two long exact sequences", use the five lemma.
