# paracompactness and partitions of unity　`prop.paracompact-partition-unity`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=prop.paracompact-partition-unity)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./prop.paracompact-partition-unity.md)

⭐ This result **underlies extension theorems**: A partition of unity combines locally defined data by a weighted sum to produce a globally defined function. Tietze- and Dugundji-type extensions, integration on manifolds, and Stokes' theorem all use this construction.

⚠️ **Local finiteness is essential**: Without local finiteness, $\sum \rho_{i} = 1$ is merely a pointwise assertion, and the pointwise sum need not be continuous.
