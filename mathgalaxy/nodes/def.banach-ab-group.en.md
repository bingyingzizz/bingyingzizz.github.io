## Statement · **draft**
A **Banach abelian group** is a seminormed abelian group $M$ satisfying

- $\|s\| = 0 \implies s = 0$ (**separation**, meaning that the seminorm is a norm);
- $M$ is **complete** in the metric induced by the norm.
