Notes

⭐ The condition “measurable” looks weak, but Lusin says: a measurable function is, up to a small error, a continuous function — provided one is allowed to cut a small piece out of the domain.

The division of labour with Egorov: Egorov says a sequence of functions converges almost uniformly, Lusin says a single function is almost continuous. Both trade a small set for regularity.

⚠ The piece cut out must be allowed to be open, and the remainder must be compact (on [a,b], compact   ⟺  \iff⟺ bounded and closed), not merely measurable.
