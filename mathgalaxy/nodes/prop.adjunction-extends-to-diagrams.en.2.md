# Adjunctions Extend to Diagram Categories　`prop.adjunction-extends-to-diagrams`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=prop.adjunction-extends-to-diagrams)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./prop.adjunction-extends-to-diagrams.md)

$$F_{I} \dashv G_{I}, \qquad F_{I}(D) = F \circ D,\quad G_{I}(E) = G \circ E,$$

that is, $\operatorname{Hom}_{\mathcal{D}^{I}}\bigl(F \circ D,\ E\bigr) \cong \operatorname{Hom}_{\mathcal{C}^{I}}\bigl(D,\ G \circ E\bigr)$.

## Notes · **draft**
**Proof.** Hom-sets in a diagram category are formed **pointwise**: giving a natural transformation is equivalent to giving a compatible family of morphisms ✓.
