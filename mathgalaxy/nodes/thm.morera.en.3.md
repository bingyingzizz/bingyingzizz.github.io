# Morera's theorem　`thm.morera`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=thm.morera)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./thm.morera.md)

⭐ The most-used corollary: **the limit of a locally uniformly convergent sequence of holomorphic functions is holomorphic**. Because $\partial T$ is compact, $\oint_{\partial T} f_k = 0$ can be taken to the limit to get $\oint_{\partial T} f = 0$, which is then fed to Morera. Hurwitz's theorem is the "zero version" of this.

⚠ Continuity cannot be dropped. Without it, the same integral condition does not imply differentiability.
