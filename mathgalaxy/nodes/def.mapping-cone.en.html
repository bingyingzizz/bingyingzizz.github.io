Notes

Use: it yields a short exact sequence 0→L→M(f)→K[1]→00 \to L \to M(f) \to K[1] \to 00→L→M(f)→K[1]→0 (usually not split). Thus every morphism embeds into an "almost exact" sequence, which is the starting point of triangulated categories — all later definitions of distinguished triangles rest on it.
