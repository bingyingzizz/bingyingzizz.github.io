Statement

The tensor product M⊗ZNM \otimes_{\mathbb{Z}} NM⊗Z​N (resp. M⊗kNM \otimes_{k} NM⊗k​N) is universal for Z\mathbb{Z}Z-bilinear (resp. kkk-bilinear) maps:

Bil⁡Z(M×N, P)  ≅  Hom⁡Z(M⊗ZN, P),\operatorname{Bil}_{\mathbb{Z}}(M \times N,\ P) \;\cong\; \operatorname{Hom}_{\mathbb{Z}}\bigl(M \otimes_{\mathbb{Z}} N,\ P\bigr),BilZ​(M×N, P)≅HomZ​(M⊗Z​N, P),

bilinear maps on the left correspond one-to-one to group homomorphisms on the right.

Notes

Proof. First build the tensor product on free modules: take Z⋅(M×N)\mathbb{Z}\cdot(M \times N)Z⋅(M×N) (the free abelian group) and quotient by the "bilinear relations"

(m+m′,n)∼(m,n)+(m′,n),(m,n+n′)∼(m,n)+(m,n′),(am,n)∼a(m,n)∼(m,an)✓(m + m', n) \sim (m, n) + (m', n), \quad (m, n + n') \sim (m, n) + (m, n'), \quad (am, n) \sim a(m,n) \sim (m, an) ✓(m+m′,n)∼(m,n)+(m′,n),(m,n+n′)∼(m,n)+(m,n′),(am,n)∼a(m,n)∼(m,an)✓

A general module is a quotient of a free one (take a presentation) ✓, and the tensor product over the quotient is the quotient ✓. Then M×N→M⊗NM \times N \to M \otimes NM×N→M⊗N is bilinear ✓, and any bilinear map M×N→PM \times N \to PM×N→P factors uniquely through it ✓ — that is exactly the universal property ✓. ∎
