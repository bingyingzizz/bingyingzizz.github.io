## Statement · **draft**
A presheaf morphism $\varphi : T \implies T'$ is a monomorphism (epimorphism) in $\widehat{\mathcal{C}}$ $\iff$ for every $X \in \mathcal{C}$, $\varphi_{X} : T(X) \to T'(X)$ is injective (surjective).
