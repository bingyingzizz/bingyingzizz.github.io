## Notes · **draft**
**This is a pure ODE theorem**: in a chart $(V,x)$, $X = \sum_{i}X^{i}\partial_{i}$, the equation becomes the autonomous system

$$\dot y^{i}(t) = X^{i}\bigl(y(t)\bigr)\qquad (i = 1,\ldots,n),$$

whose right-hand side is smooth ($X^{i}$ is smooth), so Picard–Lindelöf gives local existence and uniqueness, and smooth dependence of solutions on initial data ($C^{\infty}$) gives the smoothness of $\varphi$. The manifold enters in exactly one place: **do it in a chart**.
