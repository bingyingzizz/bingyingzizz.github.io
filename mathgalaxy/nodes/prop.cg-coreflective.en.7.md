# compactly generated spaces form a coreflective subcategory　`prop.cg-coreflective`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=prop.cg-coreflective)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./prop.cg-coreflective.md)

⭐ Keep this side by side with compact Hausdorff: $\mathbf{CHaus}$ is a **reflective** subcategory of $\mathbf{Top}$ (the inclusion has a **left** adjoint $\beta$), whereas $k\mathbf{Top}$ is **coreflective** (the inclusion has a **right** adjoint $k$). One "tightens" a space, the other "loosens" it — hence the former is closed under limits and the latter under **colimits**.
