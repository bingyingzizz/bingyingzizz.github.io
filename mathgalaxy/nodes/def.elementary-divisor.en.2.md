## Statement · **draft**
Let $R$ be a principal ideal domain and let the nonzero invariant factors of $A\in M_{m\times n}(R)$ be $d_1\mid\cdots\mid d_r$. Factor each $d_i$ into a product of prime powers, $d_i=\prod_p p^{e_{i,p}}$; all prime powers whose exponent satisfies $e_{i,p}>0$, namely $p^{e_{i,p}}$ (counted with multiplicity), are the **elementary divisors** of $A$. For $A\in M_n(K)$, the elementary divisors of $xI-A$ are called the elementary divisors of the linear operator $A$.
