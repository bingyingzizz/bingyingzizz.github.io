## Statement · **draft**
Let $K^{\bullet}$ be a **filtered** complex (with descending filtration $F^{p}K^{\bullet}$), and suppose the filtration is **bounded on both sides** (for each $n$, if $p \ll 0$ then $F^{p}K^{n} = K^{n}$, and if $p \gg 0$ then $F^{p}K^{n} = 0$ — the former condition alone is insufficient, since stabilization of $Z^{p,q}_{r}$ requires the latter). Then there is a spectral sequence

$$E^{p,q}_{0} = \operatorname{Gr}^{p}K^{p+q} \;\Longrightarrow\; H^{p+q}(K^{\bullet}).$$
