# Connection Form　`def.connection-form`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=def.connection-form)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./def.connection-form.md)

⭐ **This split is surface theory**: in $\mathbb{R}^{3}$ the curvature form vanishes identically; restricted to a surface, the $\omega^{1}_{\ 2}$ part is determined by the intrinsic metric (Gauss equation), while the remaining extrinsic part gives $II$ and the Codazzi equations. The whole of surface theory consists in reading the coefficients of these two classes of forms.

⚠ $\omega^{i}_{\ j}$ and $\omega^{j}_{\ i}$ differ by a sign; this is the easiest place in the whole map to make a copying error. There is only one criterion: differentiate $\langle e_{i}, e_{j}\rangle = \delta_{ij}$ and do not rely on memory.
