## Notes · **draft**
($\delta$ is the piece obtained from the connecting homomorphism of the triangle by applying $H^{n}$.)

**Proof.** A short exact sequence gives a derived triangle; in $D^{+}(\mathcal{A})$ there is a derived triangle
$$K^{\bullet} \to L^{\bullet} \to M^{\bullet} \to K^{\bullet}[1].$$
By $\mathbf{K}^{+}(\mathcal{I}) \simeq D^{+}(\mathcal{A})$ we may assume that all three complexes are **made of injective objects** and that the morphisms are also taken in $C(\mathcal{A})$ (isomorphisms do not change homology).
