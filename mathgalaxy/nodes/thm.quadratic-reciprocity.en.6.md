# law of quadratic reciprocity　`thm.quadratic-reciprocity`

[in graph](../#n=thm.quadratic-reciprocity)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./thm.quadratic-reciprocity.md)

**Proof (Gauss's lemma and lattice-point counting in a rectangle, elementary).** Write $P = \frac{p-1}{2}$ and $Q = \frac{q-1}{2}$. For $1 \le x \le P$, write $m_{x} := \left\lfloor \frac{qx}{p} \right\rfloor$ and let $r_{x}$ be the least positive residue of $qx$ modulo $p$ ($1 \le r_{x} \le p-1$ because $p \nmid qx$).
