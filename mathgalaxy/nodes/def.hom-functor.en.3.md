## Notes · **draft**
**Functoriality**: $\operatorname{Hom}(V,-)$ preserves identities and composition; $\operatorname{Hom}(-,W)$ is contravariant and reverses the order of composition.

**Relation to duality**: taking $W=K$, $\operatorname{Hom}_K(-,K)=(-)^{\ast}$ is the dual functor; the dual space is the value of the Hom functor at $K$.

**Internal Hom**: in a closed structure the Hom functor is represented by an internal Hom object; the version used here lives in a topos, and the representing relation is "the internal Hom represents the Hom functor".
