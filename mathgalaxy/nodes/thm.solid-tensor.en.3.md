# solid tensor product　`thm.solid-tensor`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=thm.solid-tensor)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./thm.solid-tensor.md)

⚠ Uniqueness follows because colimit-preserving functors are determined by their values on free objects.

For existence, verify that $(M \otimes N)^{\blacksquare} \to (M^{\blacksquare} \otimes N^{\blacksquare})^{\blacksquare}$ is an isomorphism by reducing to $M = \mathbb{Z}[S]$ and $N = \mathbb{Z}[T]$ and using a computation of the kind in part (iv) of `cor.solid-compact-generators`.
