## Statement · **draft**
The units of a few common adjunctions: the unit of **free $\dashv$ forgetful** is “sending $x$ into the generators”; the unit of **sheafification $\dashv$ inclusion** is $T \to T^{\#}$; the unit of **$k$-ification $\dashv$ inclusion** is the identity ($X \to kX$ when $X$ is already compactly generated).

From “counit epimorphic $\iff$ faithful” one can also read faithfulness off the unit and counit.

## Notes · **draft**
**Proof.** Compute each example with $\eta_{X} = \Phi(1_{FX})$, $\varepsilon_{Y} = \Phi^{-1}(1_{GY})$ ✓. ∎
