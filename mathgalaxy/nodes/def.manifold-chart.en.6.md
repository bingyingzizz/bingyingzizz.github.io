# Chart and Transition Maps　`def.manifold-chart`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=def.manifold-chart)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./def.manifold-chart.md)

**The transition map is the only thing to manage**: calculus on $\mathbb{R}^{n}$ knows only $\mathbb{R}^{n}$-coordinates. Every derivative on a manifold descends into some chart, and the chain-rule factor when changing charts is the Jacobian of the transition map. Thus "calculus on a manifold" is **equivalent** to "transition maps are smooth" — this single technical assumption is the whole subject.
