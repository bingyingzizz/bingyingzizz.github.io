## Statement · **draft**
For every continuous map $f : S^{2} \to \mathbb{R}^{2}$ there is $x \in S^{2}$ such that

$$f(x) = f(-x).$$

Equivalently: there is no continuous odd map ($g(-x) = -g(x)$) $g : S^{2} \to S^{1}$.
