Statement

Let p:C→C′p : \mathcal{C} \to \mathcal{C}'p:C→C′ be a functor between small categories and F:C→DF : \mathcal{C} \to \mathcal{D}F:C→D. The left Kan extension of FFF along ppp is a functor p!F:C′→Dp_{!}F : \mathcal{C}' \to \mathcal{D}p!​F:C′→D together with a natural transformation

α:F  ⟹  p!F∘p\alpha : F \implies p_{!}F \circ pα:F⟹p!​F∘p

such that for every G:C′→DG : \mathcal{C}' \to \mathcal{D}G:C′→D and every γ:F  ⟹  G∘p\gamma : F \implies G \circ pγ:F⟹G∘p there is a unique γ~:p!F  ⟹  G\widetilde{\gamma} : p_{!}F \implies Gγ​:p!​F⟹G with

γ=(γ~∘p)∘α\gamma = (\widetilde{\gamma} \circ p) \circ \alphaγ=(γ​∘p)∘α

Equivalently:

Hom⁡(p!F, G)  ≅  Hom⁡(F, G∘p)\operatorname{Hom}(p_{!}F,\ G) \;\cong\; \operatorname{Hom}(F,\ G \circ p)Hom(p!​F, G)≅Hom(F, G∘p)

i.e. p!p_{!}p!​ is the left adjoint of the precomposition functor p∗=(−)∘pp^{*} = (-) \circ pp∗=(−)∘p. Reversing arrows gives the right Kan extension p∗⊣p∗p_{*} \dashv p^{*}p∗​⊣p∗.

Notes

The question it answers: can FFF be extended to C′\mathcal{C}'C′? The left Kan extension is the best left approximate extension of FFF — it does not ask that FFF actually extend, only that, on C\mathcal{C}C, among functors agreeing with FFF it fits best.

Compare with adjunctions line by line:
