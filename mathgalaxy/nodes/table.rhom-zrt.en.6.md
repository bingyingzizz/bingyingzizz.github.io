# Table of Derived Hom for Z, R and T　`table.rhom-zrt`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=table.rhom-zrt)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./table.rhom-zrt.md)

**Why ‘real vector space $\to$ discrete’ vanishes in every degree**: $\mathbb{R}^{n}$ is divisible, so maps from a divisible group into any group have images in which division by $n$ is possible. Thus $\operatorname{Hom}_{\mathbb{Z}}(\mathbb{R}^{n}, \mathbb{Z}) = 0$ (elements of the image of $1/n$ must be divisible by $n$, leaving only $0$).

Together with divisibility $\operatorname{Ext}^{1}(\mathbb{R}^{n}, \mathbb{Z}) = 0$, this gives zero for the entire $\operatorname{RHom}$.
