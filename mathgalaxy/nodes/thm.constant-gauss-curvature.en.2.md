# surfaces of constant Gauss curvature　`thm.constant-gauss-curvature`

On a surface $\Sigma$, let the Gauss curvature $K$ be **constant**. At any point take **geodesic polar coordinates** $(r, \theta)$; then

$$g = \mathrm{d}r^{2} + f(r,\theta)^{2}\,\mathrm{d}\theta^{2} ,$$

and Cartan's structure equation $\mathrm{d}\omega^{2} = K\,\omega^{1}\wedge\omega^{2}$ gives that $f$ satisfies

$$\frac{\partial^{2} f}{\partial r^{2}} + K f = 0 ,$$
