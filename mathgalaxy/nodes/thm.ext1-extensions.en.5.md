**Baer sum.** Given two extensions $E_{1}, E_{2}$, take their fiber product over $M$, denoted $E_{1}\times_{M}E_{2}$ (it combines the two middle terms over the same $M$). Then quotient by the image of the antidiagonal $\{\, (n, -n) : n \in N \,\}$. The resulting middle term, denoted $E_{1} + E_{2}$, gives the Baer sum ✓.
