# Symplectic Manifold　`def.symplectic-manifold`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=def.symplectic-manifold)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./def.symplectic-manifold.md)

⚠️ Compact symplectic manifolds are subject to a strong restriction: $[\omega] \in H^{2}(M)$ is nonzero (otherwise $\omega=d\theta$ would make $\omega^{n}=d(\theta\wedge\omega^{n-1})$ exact, giving integral zero and contradicting the fact that $\omega^{n}$ is a nowhere-vanishing volume form). This is why a symplectic manifold cannot be $S^{2n}$, $n \ge 2$. The cohomological argument is omitted here.
