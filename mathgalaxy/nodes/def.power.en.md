## Statement · **draft**
Let $A$ be a ring with unit (e.g. $\mathbb{Z}$, $\mathbb{R}$), $a \in A$. The **integer powers** are defined recursively ($a^{0} := 1$ is the convention for the **empty product**; when $a = 0$ one also takes $0^{0} := 1$):

$$a^{0} := 1, \qquad a^{n+1} := a^{n} \cdot a \quad (n \in \mathbb{N});$$

If $a$ is invertible (in $\mathbb{R}$ this means $a \ne 0$), define for $n \ge 1$

$$a^{-n} := (a^{-1})^{n} = \frac{1}{a^{n}}.$$
