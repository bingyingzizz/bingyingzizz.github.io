# limit and colimit　`def.limit`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=def.limit)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./def.limit.md)

Dually, the **colimit** $\operatorname{colim} D$ of $D$ satisfies $\operatorname{Hom}_{\mathcal{C}^{I}}(D, \Delta Y) \cong \operatorname{Hom}_{\mathcal{C}}(\operatorname{colim} D, Y)$.

When $I$ is finite it is called a **finite limit**.

## Notes · **draft**
The universal property says just one thing: **every other cone factors through it uniquely**. Hence a limit, if it exists, is unique up to isomorphism — there is no need to choose a particular construction; any one will do.

Two equivalent formulations: "a universal cone" and "$\operatorname{Hom}_{\mathcal{C}^{I}}(\Delta -, D)$ is representable". The latter turns the limit into a computable isomorphism.
