1. $N$ is flat, i.e. $(-) \otimes_{R} N$ is exact;
2. for every injection $M_{1} \rightarrowtail M$, the map $M_{1} \otimes_{R} N \to M \otimes_{R} N$ is injective;
3. for every $R$-module $M$, $\operatorname{Tor}_{1}^{R}(M, N) = 0$;
