# chain homotopy　`def.chain-homotopy`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=def.chain-homotopy)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./def.chain-homotopy.md)

✅ Chain homotopy **is** an equivalence relation: reflexive with $0$, symmetric with $-P$, transitive with $P + Q$ ($\partial(P+Q) + (P+Q)\partial = (g - f) + (h - g) = h - f$). So "chain homotopy classes" are the real morphisms in the homotopy category; formally inverting chain homotopy classes gives the homotopy category.
