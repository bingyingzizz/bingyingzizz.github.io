⭐ **This is the legitimate basis for "resolutions may be chosen freely".** In computations one usually picks the shortest and most explicit one (free modules, two terms, Koszul complex); changing it does not affect the result — but only isomorphism of the result is guaranteed, **not that the families appearing in the middle agree**.

**Example (homotopy equivalent but not isomorphic)**: take the direct sum of $P_{\bullet}$ with a contractible complex of the form

$$\cdots \to 0 \to P_{0} \xrightarrow{\ 1\ } P_{0} \to 0 \to \cdots$$
