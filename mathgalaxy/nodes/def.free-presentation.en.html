Statement

A continuous surjection F↠SF \twoheadrightarrow SF↠S between compact Hausdorff spaces is called a free presentation of SSS if

  1. FFF is a free compact Hausdorff space;
  2. F→SF \to SF→S is an epimorphism;
  3. with R:=F×SFR := F \times_{S} FR:=F×S​F, the map F→RF \to RF→R is an epimorphism.

Then S≅coker⁡(R⇉F)S \cong \operatorname{coker}(R \rightrightarrows F)S≅coker(R⇉F).

Notes

conversely, given an equivalence relation RRR on XXX, set Y:=X/RY := X/RY:=X/R, and then R=X×YXR = X \times_{Y} XR=X×Y​X.

A free presentation carries this picture into CHaus\mathbf{CHaus}CHaus: RRR is the equivalence relation "glue together points with the same image", and the third condition requires that RRR itself be a surjective image of a free compact Hausdorff space — so the whole of SSS is built from free objects by a single coequalizer.

In CHaus\mathbf{CHaus}CHaus coequalizers exist, hence coker⁡(R⇉F)\operatorname{coker}(R \rightrightarrows F)coker(R⇉F) exists; it is the quotient space F/RF/RF/R.
