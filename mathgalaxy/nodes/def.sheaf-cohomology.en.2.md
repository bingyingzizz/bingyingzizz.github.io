## Notes · **draft**
⭐ **In one sentence**: sheaf cohomology is the **right derived functor of the section functor**. $\Gamma(X,-)$ is left exact but not right exact (sheafification interferes), and $R^{n}\Gamma$ is exactly the half that repairs the break.

**Why it is needed**: $H^{0}(X, M) = \Gamma(X, M) = M(X)$ alone sees only global sections; from $H^{1}$ on it records the information that "sheaves fail to glue" ($H^{1}$ classifies principal bundles / torsors of sheaves).
