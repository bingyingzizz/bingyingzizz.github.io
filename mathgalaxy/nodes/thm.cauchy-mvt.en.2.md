## Notes · **draft**
⭐ It is not simply Lagrange's theorem "with $g(x) = x$": expressing the increments of two functions **simultaneously** at the same point $\xi$ is the entire reason l'Hôpital's rule works.

⚠ The condition $g' \ne 0$ cannot be omitted — it also guarantees $g(b) \ne g(a)$ (by Rolle's theorem), so that the denominator makes sense.
