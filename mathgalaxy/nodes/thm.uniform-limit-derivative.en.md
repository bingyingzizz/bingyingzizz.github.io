## Statement · **draft**
Let $f_n$ be differentiable on $[a, b]$ with $f_n'$ continuous, and suppose

- $f_n' \rightrightarrows g$ (**the derivatives** converge uniformly),
- at at least one point $x_0$, the values $f_n(x_0)$ converge.

Then $f_n$ converges uniformly to some $f$, $f$ is differentiable, and

$$f' = g$$
