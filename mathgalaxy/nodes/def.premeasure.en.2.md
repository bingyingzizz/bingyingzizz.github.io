## Notes · **draft**
Why this intermediate notion: on $\mathbb{R}$ the half-open intervals (a, b] form only a basic class; their finite disjoint unions form a ring, not yet a $\sigma$-algebra — but length can already be defined there, and that is the premeasure.

Carathéodory's extension theorem lifts the premeasure from the ring all the way to the generated $\sigma$-algebra. So "**build the premeasure first, then extend**" is the standard two-step construction of a measure.
