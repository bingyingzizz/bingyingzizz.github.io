# Integral of a Differential Form　`def.integral-form`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=def.integral-form)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./def.integral-form.md)

Let $U\subseteq\mathbb{R}^{n}$ be open and let $\omega = f\,\mathrm{d}x^{1}\wedge\cdots\wedge\mathrm{d}x^{n}$ be a compactly supported $n$-form. Define

$$\int_{U}\omega := \int_{U}f(x)\,\mathrm{d}x^{1}\cdots\mathrm{d}x^{n},$$

where the right-hand side is the usual (Lebesgue or Riemann) integral and $\mathrm{d}x^{1}\cdots\mathrm{d}x^{n}$ denotes Lebesgue measure. **Well-definedness**: if $y = \psi(x)$ is an orientation-preserving diffeomorphism, then

$$\omega = (f\circ\psi^{-1})\det\Bigl(\frac{\partial x}{\partial y}\Bigr)\,\mathrm{d}y^{1}\wedge\cdots\wedge\mathrm{d}y^{n},$$
