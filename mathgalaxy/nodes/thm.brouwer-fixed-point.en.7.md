💡 **Equivalent formulation**: There is no continuous retraction of $\overline{\mathbb{D}}$ onto $S^{1}$. Brouwer's theorem is equivalent to saying that $S^{1}$ is not a retract of the disk (see the edge involving `thm.borsuk-ulam` and `thm.no-retraction-disk`).
