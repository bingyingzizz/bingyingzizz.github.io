def.subobject

Machine-drafted and pending author review. The Chinese text is authoritative.

Statement

Regard a monomorphism Y↣XY \rightarrowtail XY↣X as a subobject of XXX, written Y⊆XY \subseteq XY⊆X. The intersection of subobjects and the preimage along a morphism are defined in @@def.intersection-subobject@@ and @@def.preimage-subobject@@ respectively; both constructions are written out below as an overview, with the details deferred to those two nodes.

Y∩Z:=Y×XZ,f−1(Y):=Y×XX′Y \cap Z := Y \times_{X} Z, \qquad f^{-1}(Y) := Y \times_{X} X'Y∩Z:=Y×X​Z,f−1(Y):=Y×X​X′

(for Y,Z⊆XY, Z \subseteq XY,Z⊆X and any f:X′→Xf : X' \to Xf:X′→X; defined only when they exist).

Notes

Replacing "subset" by "monomorphism" has the advantage of no longer depending on elements — so this language transfers verbatim to categories without elements. Both "intersection" and "preimage" are pullbacks (fiber products): Y∩ZY \cap ZY∩Z solves "the part that is both in YYY and in ZZZ", and f−1(Y)f^{-1}(Y)f−1(Y) solves "the part where y=f(x)y = f(x)y=f(x) has a solution"; for details see @@def.intersection-subobject@@ and @@def.preimage-subobject@@.
