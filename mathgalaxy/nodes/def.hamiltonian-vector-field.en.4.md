When switching references, **check the signs in Hamilton's equations**.

⭐ **The correspondence ‘function $\implies$ vector field’ is distinctive to symplectic geometry**: On a general manifold, differentiating a function gives only its differential ($dH$, a 1-form). To obtain a vector field requires an isomorphism from 1-forms to vector fields. This is precisely what $\omega$ provides ($\flat : TM \to T^{*}M$, $v \mapsto \iota_{v}\omega$).

A Riemannian metric also gives such a map (a musical isomorphism), but the resulting flow does not preserve the metric.
