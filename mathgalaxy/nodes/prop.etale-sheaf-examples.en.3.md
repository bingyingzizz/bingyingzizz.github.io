# Examples of Étale Sheaves　`prop.etale-sheaf-examples`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=prop.etale-sheaf-examples)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./prop.etale-sheaf-examples.md)

3. **Constant sheaves**: $\underline{A}$ (regarding $A$ as a discrete group and taking locally constant functions) is an étale sheaf, and the most commonly used coefficient in étale cohomology. ⚠️ The constant **presheaf** (for every $U$ the set $A$, with the identity on every map) is in general **not** a sheaf and must be sheafified.

4. **Concrete examples**: $\operatorname{Spec}\mathbb{C} \to \operatorname{Spec}\mathbb{R}$ is finite étale (of degree $2$);

$t \mapsto t^{n} : \mathbb{G}_{m} \to \mathbb{G}_{m}$ is finite étale when $n$ is invertible on the base (a Kummer covering).
