# product and coproduct　`def.product`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=def.product)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./def.product.md)

Examples: in $\mathbf{Set}$ the product is the Cartesian product and the coproduct the disjoint union; in $\mathbf{Top}$ the product carries the **product topology** (the coarsest topology making it a product) and the coproduct the **disjoint-union topology** (the finest one); in $\mathbf{Ab}$ the product is the direct product $\prod_i A_i$ and the coproduct the direct sum $\bigoplus_i A_i$.
