# limits and colimits in presheaf categories　`thm.presheaf-limits-colimits`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=thm.presheaf-limits-colimits)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./thm.presheaf-limits-colimits.md)

**(i) The pointwise definition really gives limits (colimits).** In $\mathbf{Set}$, for each $X \in \mathcal{C}$ take the limit (or colimit) of that family of sets; for a morphism $f : Y \to X$, the restriction map $\bigl(\varprojlim T_i\bigr)(X) \to \bigl(\varprojlim T_i\bigr)(Y)$ is given by the universal property of the limit (on the colimit side by that of the colimit) ✓.
