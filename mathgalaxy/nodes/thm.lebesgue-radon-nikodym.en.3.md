# Radon-Nikodym theorem　`thm.lebesgue-radon-nikodym`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=thm.lebesgue-radon-nikodym)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./thm.lebesgue-radon-nikodym.md)

⚠ $\sigma$-finiteness cannot be dropped (it is needed for both $\nu$ and $\mu$).

The three steps of the proof (expanded in the next arrow): $I. \nu$, with $\mu$ finite; II. cut into finite pieces using $\sigma$-finiteness; $III. \nu$ a signed measure, treat $\nu^+, \nu^-$ separately.
