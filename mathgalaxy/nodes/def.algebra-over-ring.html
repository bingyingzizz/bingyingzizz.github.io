又称环上的代数·结合代数·含幺代数·R-algebra

陈述

设 RRR 是有单位元的交换环。RRR-代数是一个 RRR-模 AAA 连同 RRR-双线性乘法 A×A→AA\times A\to AA×A→A，(x,y)↦xy(x,y)\mapsto xy(x,y)↦xy。若乘法满足结合律，称 AAA 为结合代数；若存在 1A∈A1_A\in A1A​∈A 使 1Ax=x1A=x1_Ax=x1_A=x1A​x=x1A​=x，称 AAA 为含幺代数。含幺结合 RRR-代数与保乘法、保单位的 RRR-线性映射构成范畴 AlgRuni\mathrm{Alg}_R^{\mathrm{uni}}AlgRuni​。

说明

基环交换，左右 RRR-作用才一致。 含幺代数有结构映射 R→AR\to AR→A，r↦r1Ar\mapsto r1_Ar↦r1A​，其像落在 AAA 的中心。 例：Mn(F)M_n(F)Mn​(F)、多项式代数 F[x1,…,xn]F[x_1,\ldots,x_n]F[x1​,…,xn​]、群代数 F[G]F[G]F[G]。 结合性与含幺性是附加公理，不属于「代数」的裸定义；本团默认含幺结合。

Statement draft

Let RRR be a commutative ring with identity. An RRR-algebra is an RRR-module AAA together with an RRR-bilinear multiplication A×A→AA\times A\to AA×A→A, (x,y)↦xy(x,y)\mapsto xy(x,y)↦xy. If the multiplication is associative, AAA is called an associative algebra; if there is 1A∈A1_A\in A1A​∈A with 1Ax=x1A=x1_Ax=x1_A=x1A​x=x1A​=x, then AAA is called unital. Unital associative RRR-algebras with multiplication- and unit-preserving RRR-linear maps form the category AlgRuni\mathrm{Alg}_R^{\mathrm{uni}}AlgRuni​.

Notes draft

The base ring is commutative so that the left and right RRR-actions agree. A unital algebra has a structure map R→AR\to AR→A, r↦r1Ar\mapsto r1_Ar↦r1A​, whose image lies in the centre of AAA. Examples are Mn(F)M_n(F)Mn​(F), the polynomial algebra F[x1,…,xn]F[x_1,\ldots,x_n]F[x1​,…,xn​], and the group algebra F[G]F[G]F[G]. Associativity and unitality are additional axioms; this cluster works with unital associative algebras.
