# existence of a local flow　`thm.flow-local`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=thm.flow-local)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./thm.flow-local.md)

**"Autonomous" is the source of the one-parameter group property**: solutions of a non-autonomous equation $\dot y = f(t,y)$ do not form a group (going first for $s$ and then for $t$ differs from going directly for $s+t$, because the "environment" changes). The flow of a vector field is autonomous, hence invariant under time translation — this is $\varphi_{s}\circ\varphi_{t} = \varphi_{s+t}$.
