The vanishing of $\operatorname{Ext}^{1}_{R}(K, -)$ in every case forces $K$ to be projective. If $K$ were not projective, a surjection $F \twoheadrightarrow K$ with $F$ free would fail to split. The classification of first extensions then gives $\operatorname{Ext}^{1}_{R}(K, \ker) \ne 0$, a contradiction ✓.
