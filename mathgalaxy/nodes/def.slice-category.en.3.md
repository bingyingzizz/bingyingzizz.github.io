# slice category　`def.slice-category`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=def.slice-category)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./def.slice-category.md)

⭐ The comma-category description makes its identity clear: it is the category obtained by **pulling the presheaf $T$ back along the Yoneda embedding** — on one side $\mathcal{C} \hookrightarrow \widehat{\mathcal{C}}$, on the other $\mathbf{1} \xrightarrow{T} \widehat{\mathcal{C}}$.

It also explains why, when $X \in \mathcal{C}$, $\mathcal{C}/X \simeq \widehat{\mathcal{C}}/h_{X}$ holds.

Its use is to give a presheaf an **index category**: when speaking of "which points glue this presheaf together", the indices run over $\mathcal{C}/T$ (the density theorem is used this way).
