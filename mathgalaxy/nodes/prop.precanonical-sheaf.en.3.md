## Notes · **draft**
These two write "sheaf" as two identities that are obvious in $\mathbf{Set}$ and need checking here: **finite coproducts become finite products**, and **quotients become kernels**. They are the precise statement that "coproducts and quotients in a pretopos look like those in sets".

Corollary: **the precanonical topology is subcanonical** — the representable presheaf $h_{X}$ satisfies the two conditions above ($\operatorname{Hom}(X, -)$ preserves products, and $\operatorname{Hom}(X/R, -)$ gives the kernel from the coequalizer), so they are all sheaves.
