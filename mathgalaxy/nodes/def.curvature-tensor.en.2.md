## Statement · **draft**
In plain terms: **the curvature tensor measures "how much the result changes when one transports around in two directions and swaps their order"**. On a flat plane swapping makes no difference, and the difference is zero; on a sphere swapping the order leaves a small discrepancy, and that discrepancy is curvature. It is intrinsic: the formula contains only $g$ and its derivatives — no normal, no second fundamental form.

The Levi-Civita connection on a surface defines the **curvature operator**

$$R(X,Y)Z = \nabla_{X}\nabla_{Y}Z-\nabla_{Y}\nabla_{X}Z-\nabla_{[X,Y]}Z,$$
