## Notes · **draft**
It is even weaker than a premeasure: a premeasure is countably additive (only its domain is a ring), while a finitely additive measure requires only finite additivity.

⚠ Finitely additive $\ne$ countably additive: on $\mathbb{N}$ define $\mu (E) = 0$ ($E$ finite), $\mu (E) = +\infty$ ($E$ infinite); it is finitely additive, yet fails countable additivity for $\bigcup _{n} \{n\} = \mathbb{N}$.
