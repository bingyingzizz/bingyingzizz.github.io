📌 **Euler characteristic**: $M$ is homotopy equivalent to the cylinder (both are homotopy equivalent to $S^{1}$), so $\chi(M) = \chi(S^{1}) = 0$. Homotopy invariants cannot detect the twist, while orientability can.
