## Statement · **draft**
Let $f$ be differentiable on an interval $I$. Then $f$ is constant on $I$ if and only if for every $x \in I$,

$$f'(x) = 0$$

## Notes · **draft**
⭐ This corollary means more than it seems: **differentiation loses exactly one constant** on an interval.
