## Statement · **draft**
In $\mathcal{C}$, all limits indexed by $I$ exist $\iff$ the constant-diagram functor $\Delta : \mathcal{C} \to \mathcal{C}^{I}$ has a **right adjoint**, given by

$$D \mapsto \lim D$$

Dually, all colimits indexed by $I$ exist $\iff$ $\Delta$ has a left adjoint, given by $D \mapsto \operatorname{colim} D$.
