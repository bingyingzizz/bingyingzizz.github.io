# Lagrangian Submanifold　`def.lagrangian-submanifold`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=def.lagrangian-submanifold)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./def.lagrangian-submanifold.md)

⚠️ **Lagrangian submanifolds need not be closed or embedded**: Physics often uses immersed submanifolds and even multiple covers (such as periodic orbits in Bohr–Sommerfeld quantization). The definition here allows immersions.

⚠️ Terminology: Lagrangian subspaces and Lagrangian submanifolds are named for the same person and express the same concept at two scales. They are **unrelated** to `thm.lagrange-identity` in inner product geometry.
