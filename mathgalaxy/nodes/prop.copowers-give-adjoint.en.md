## Statement · **draft**
If a small category $\mathcal{C}$ has **copowers**, then every representable functor $h_{X}$ has a left adjoint: $\mathbf{Set} \to \mathcal{C},\ I \mapsto X^{(I)}$ **$\dashv$** $h_{X} = \operatorname{Hom}_{\mathcal{C}}(X, -)$.
