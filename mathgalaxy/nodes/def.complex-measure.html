陈述

可测空间 (X,M)(X, \mathcal{M})(X,M) 上的复测度是一个映射

ν:M→C\nu : \mathcal{M} \to \mathbb{C}ν:M→C

满足

ν(∅)=0,\nu(\emptyset) = 0, ν(∅)=0,

且对两两不交的 {Ej}⊆M\{E_j\} \subseteq \mathcal{M}{Ej​}⊆M：

ν(⨆jEj)=∑jν(Ej)\nu( \bigsqcup_j E_j ) = \sum_j \nu(E_j)ν(j⨆​Ej​)=j∑​ν(Ej​)

（右边的级数绝对收敛。）

说明

⚠ 与符号测度相比，复测度自动有限（取值在 C\mathbb{C}C 里，不涉及 ±∞\pm \infty±∞），而且可数可加性里的那个级数自动绝对收敛。

所以在复测度的世界里，「有限性」「重排问题」这些麻烦全部消失 —— 代价是不能再有「无穷质量」的例子。

Abstract reviewed

A complex measure on a measurable space (X,M)(X, \mathcal{M})(X,M) is a map ν:M→C\nu : \mathcal{M} \to \mathbb{C}ν:M→C with ν(∅)=0\nu(\emptyset) = 0ν(∅)=0 and ν(⨆jEj)=∑jν(Ej)\nu(\bigsqcup_j E_j) = \sum_j \nu(E_j)ν(⨆j​Ej​)=∑j​ν(Ej​) for pairwise disjoint families, the series converging absolutely. Unlike signed measures, complex measures are automatically finite and that series is automatically absolutely convergent, so questions of finiteness and rearrangement never arise — at the cost of losing examples with infinite mass.

Statement reviewed

A complex measure on a measurable space (X,M)(X, \mathcal{M})(X,M) is a map

ν:M→C\nu : \mathcal{M} \to \mathbb{C}ν:M→C

such that

ν(∅)=0,\nu(\emptyset) = 0, ν(∅)=0,

and for pairwise disjoint {Ej}⊆M\{E_j\} \subseteq \mathcal{M}{Ej​}⊆M:

ν(⨆jEj)=∑jν(Ej)\nu( \bigsqcup_j E_j ) = \sum_j \nu(E_j)ν(j⨆​Ej​)=j∑​ν(Ej​)

(the series on the right converges absolutely).

Notes reviewed

⚠ Compared with a signed measure, a complex measure is automatically finite (its values lie in C\mathbb{C}C, no ±∞\pm \infty±∞ is involved), and the series in countable additivity converges absolutely automatically.

So in the world of complex measures the headaches of "finiteness" and "rearrangement" disappear entirely — at the cost of no longer having examples with "infinite mass".
