# Residue Theorem　`thm.residue`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=thm.residue)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./thm.residue.md)

⚠ The closed curves must be **positively** oriented (counterclockwise);

reversing the orientation changes the overall sign.

Singularities **outside** the curve contribute nothing to the integral — alternatively one may compute with the residue at infinity.
