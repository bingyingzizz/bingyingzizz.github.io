# Orientation and Volume Forms　`prop.volume-form-orientation`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=prop.volume-form-orientation)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./prop.volume-form-orientation.md)

$(\Longleftarrow)$ given an everywhere nonzero $\omega$, on a chart it equals $f\,\mathrm{d}x^{1}\wedge\cdots\wedge\mathrm{d}x^{n}$ with $f$ everywhere nonzero; replace every chart with $f < 0$ by reversed coordinates — then all transition Jacobians are positive, giving an oriented atlas.
