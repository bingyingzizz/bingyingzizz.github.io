# ring　`def.ring`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=def.ring)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./def.ring.md)

A ring whose multiplication is also commutative is called a **commutative ring**; a ring that moreover has a multiplicative identity $1 \ne 0$ is called a **ring with identity**.

## Notes · **draft**
⭐ **Again "set + operations + axioms"**, and this layer directly **cites** the abelian group below: the additive axioms are not rewritten, just mentioned in one phrase.

**Contrast with a field**: a field requires every non-zero element to be invertible ($(F\setminus\{0\}, \cdot)$ is an abelian group), a ring **does not**.
