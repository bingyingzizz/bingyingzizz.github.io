## Statement · **draft**
Let $G$ be a finite group with $|G| = p^{a} m$, where $p$ is prime and $p \nmid m$. Then

**(1) Existence**: For every $0 \le i \le a$, the group $G$ has a subgroup of order $p^{i}$. In particular, a subgroup of order $p^{a}$ exists and is called a **Sylow $p$-subgroup**.

**(2) Conjugacy**: Any two Sylow subgroups of $G$ for the prime $p$ are conjugate. More generally, every $p$-subgroup is contained in a Sylow $p$-subgroup.
