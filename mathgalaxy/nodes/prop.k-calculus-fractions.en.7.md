and the long exact sequence of the triangle $M(s) \xrightarrow{h'} M^{\bullet} \xrightarrow{t} M(h') \to M(s)[1]$ together with "$M(s)$ acyclic" gives $H^{n}(M^{\bullet}) \cong H^{n}(M(h')^{\bullet})$ — so **$t$ is a quasi-isomorphism**, hence $t f \simeq t g$.

Identity is a quasi-isomorphism and quasi-isomorphisms are closed under composition — both are trivial ($H^{n}$ is a functor). The **left** calculus is obtained by performing the same construction in $\mathcal{A}^{\mathrm{op}}$ (equivalently, by moving the quasi-isomorphism component of $\theta$ to the first side). ∎
