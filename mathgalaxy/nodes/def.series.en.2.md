# series　`def.series`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=def.series)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./def.series.md)

$$\sum_{n=1}^{\infty} a_n = \sup_N s_N \in [0, +\infty]$$

always makes sense (possibly $+\infty$).

## Notes · **draft**
⭐ Countable additivity of measures and term-by-term properties of integrals both rely on this line for nonnegative series: nonnegativity removes any worry about conditional convergence and rearrangement.

⚠ A convergent series **can be rearranged** only when it converges absolutely; rearranging a conditionally convergent series may change its sum (the Riemann rearrangement theorem).
