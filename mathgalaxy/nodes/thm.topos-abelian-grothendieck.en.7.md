# abelian groups in a topos form a Grothendieck category　`thm.topos-abelian-grothendieck`

One more fact along the way: $\mathbb{Z}\cdot X$ is **projective** in $\mathcal{T}(\mathbf{Ab})$ (look at presheaves first: $M \mapsto \operatorname{Hom}_{\mathbb{Z}}(\mathbb{Z}\cdot h_{X}, M) \cong M(X)$ is exact on presheaves).
