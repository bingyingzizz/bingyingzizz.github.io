# a fully faithful functor is essentially injective　`prop.fully-faithful-injective`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=prop.fully-faithful-injective)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./prop.fully-faithful-injective.md)

By full faithfulness, $u$ comes uniquely from some $f : X \to Y$, and $u^{-1}$ uniquely from some $g : Y \to X$ ✓.

Then $F(g f) = u^{-1} u = 1_{F(X)} = F(1_{X})$ ✓, and faithfulness gives $g f = 1_{X}$ ✓;

symmetrically $f g = 1_{Y}$ ✓.

∎

⚠️ The converse fails: an essentially injective functor need not be fully faithful.
