# flat dimension　`def.flat-dimension`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=def.flat-dimension)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./def.flat-dimension.md)

**Global dimension**: The weak global dimension of a ring is the supremum of the flat dimensions of all modules. Over a Noetherian ring, the weak global dimension equals the global dimension.

⚠ Previously, the notes in `def.injective-dimension` only mentioned that flat dimension had no separate entry. This entry supplies the definition. Flat dimension and projective dimension are distinct notions.
