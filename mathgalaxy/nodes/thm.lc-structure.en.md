## Statement · **draft**
Let $M$ be a **locally compact Hausdorff abelian group**. Then $M$ contains an **open** subgroup isomorphic to

$$V \oplus K,$$

where $V$ is a **finite-dimensional real vector space** (viewed as an additive group) and $K$ is a **compact Hausdorff abelian group**.
