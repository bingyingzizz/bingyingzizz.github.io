Statement

Let C\mathcal{C}C be a site with fibre products, X∈CX \in \mathcal{C}X∈C, and MMM an abelian sheaf on C\mathcal{C}C. Then there is a spectral sequence

E2p,q  =  Hˇp(X, Hq(M))  ⟹  Hp+q(X, M),E^{p,q}_{2} \;=\; \check{H}^{p}\bigl(X,\ H^{q}(M)\bigr) \;\Longrightarrow\; H^{p+q}(X,\ M),E2p,q​=Hˇp(X, Hq(M))⟹Hp+q(X, M),

where Hq(M)H^{q}(M)Hq(M) denotes the presheaf U↦Hq(U,M)U \mapsto H^{q}(U, M)U↦Hq(U,M), and Hˇp(X,−)\check{H}^{p}(X, -)Hˇp(X,−) is Čech cohomology, taken as the filtered colimit over covers.

Notes

⭐ Use: this is the general justification for "computing sheaf cohomology with Čech cohomology". To make the two directly equal (rather than merely linked by a spectral sequence), one additionally requires the higher Čech groups to degenerate on the E2E_{2}E2​ page — exactly the step used in the proof of "acyclic   ⟺  \iff⟺ Čech cohomology vanishes" ✓.

⚠ Difference from the Cartan–Leray spectral sequence: that one fixes a single cover X\mathfrak{X}X; this one takes the colimit over all covers, so the left-hand side can be written directly as Hˇp(X,⋅)\check{H}^{p}(X, \cdot)Hˇp(X,⋅).
