## Notes · **draft**
⭐ In one sentence: **the constant presheaf $\Delta$ is a right adjoint and preserves all limits; the constant sheaf $\underline{(-)}=a\circ\Delta$'s left adjoint $a$ preserves only finite limits** — this is the source of "finite limits can be computed pointwise, colimits must be sheafified first".
