# limit superior and limit inferior　`def.limsup-liminf`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=def.limsup-liminf)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./def.limsup-liminf.md)

($n \mapsto \sup_{k \ge n} x_k$ is monotone decreasing and $n \mapsto \inf_{k \ge n} x_k$ is monotone increasing, so both limits in $\overline{\mathbb{R}}$ **always exist**.)

Moreover, the limit of the sequence exists (allowing the value $\pm\infty$) if and only if the upper and lower limits agree, i.e.

$$\liminf_{n \to \infty} x_n = \limsup_{n \to \infty} x_n$$
