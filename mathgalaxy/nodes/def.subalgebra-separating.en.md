## Statement · **draft**
Let $K$ be compact Hausdorff and $A \subseteq C(K, \mathbb{K})$.

- $A$ is a **subalgebra**: it is a vector space and is closed under multiplication;
- $A$ **separates points**: $x \ne y \implies \exists f \in A$, $f(x) \ne f(y)$;
- $A$ is a **lattice**: closed under $\min, \max$.
