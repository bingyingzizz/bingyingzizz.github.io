# Hadamard factorization theorem　`thm.hadamard-factorization`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=thm.hadamard-factorization)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./thm.hadamard-factorization.md)

• For $|a_{n}|\le4r$, we have $|z-a_{n}|\ge\delta$ (when $|a_{n}|\in[r/2,4r]$, this follows from the choice of $R$, and when $|a_{n}|<r/2$, we have $|z-a_{n}|\ge R-r/2\ge r/2$). Thus $\log|1-z/a_{n}|=\log|z-a_{n}|-\log|a_{n}|\ge\log(\delta/(4r))$, and by (i) each term satisfies $\ge\log(\delta/(4r))-C(R/|a_{n}|)^{p}$. Summing over at most $N\le C r^{\rho+\varepsilon}$ terms and using $\sum_{|a_{n}|\le4r}(R/|a_{n}|)^{p}\le C r^{\rho+\varepsilon}$ (by summation by parts) gives $\sum_{|a_{n}|\le4r}\log|E_{p}(z/a_{n})|\ge-C\,r^{\rho+\varepsilon}\log r$.
