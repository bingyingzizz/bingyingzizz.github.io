# category of rings　`def.category-of-rings`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=def.category-of-rings)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./def.category-of-rings.md)

Coproducts are **free products** of rings; coequalizers are quotient rings (taking the closure of the generated two-sided ideal); pushouts are amalgamated free products.

## Notes · **draft**
**Complete and cocomplete**; **not additive** ($\hom(R,S)$ has no abelian group structure in general).

**Why no unit is required**: @@def.ring@@ explicitly does not require a unit. In the category of unital rings the initial object is $\mathbb Z$; that does not conflict with the zero ring being terminal, but it would diverge from the convention of @@def.ring@@, so the category of unital rings is not given a separate entry.
