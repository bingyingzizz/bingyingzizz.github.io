# Quasi-Separated Condensed Sets as Filtered Colimits　`lem.qseparated-ind-inclusions`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=lem.qseparated-ind-inclusions)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./lem.qseparated-ind-inclusions.md)

**Corollary**: the objects of $\operatorname{Ind}(\mathbf{CHaus})$ whose transition maps are injective are exactly the quasi-compact quasi-separated condensed sets.

## Notes · **draft**
⚠️ **Do not read this as $\operatorname{Ind}(\mathbf{CHaus}) \cong \mathrm{Cond}$.** The whole of $\operatorname{Ind}(\mathbf{CHaus})$ is **larger** than condensed sets: for a general condensed set the transition maps of its compact pieces are merely maps, not inclusions, so only the "inclusion transitions" part matches the quasi-compact quasi-separated objects.
