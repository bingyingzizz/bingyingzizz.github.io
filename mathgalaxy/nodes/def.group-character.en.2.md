## Notes · **draft**
Conjugacy invariance follows from the cyclicity of the trace: $\chi(hgh^{-1})=\operatorname{tr}(\rho(h)\rho(g)\rho(h)^{-1})=\chi(g)$. Isomorphic representations have the same character. Characters add on direct sums and multiply on tensor products (see the node on character operations). A character is not a group homomorphism $G\to F^\times$.
