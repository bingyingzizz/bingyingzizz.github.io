Statement

A subspace W⊆VW\subseteq VW⊆V is called a subrepresentation if θ(a)W⊆W\theta(a)W\subseteq Wθ(a)W⊆W for all a∈Aa\in Aa∈A; then θ\thetaθ restricts to a representation on WWW. For a group representation this means ρ(g)W⊆W\rho(g)W\subseteq Wρ(g)W⊆W for all g∈Gg\in Gg∈G.

Notes

The zero subspace and VVV are always subrepresentations. Sums and intersections of subrepresentations are again subrepresentations; kernels and images of intertwiners are also subrepresentations. For group representations, subrepresentations are exactly GGG-invariant subspaces. Quotient representations are treated in a separate node.
