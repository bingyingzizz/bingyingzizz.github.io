By the integral representation N(s)=12πi∮H(s,⋅)dz/(z−a)N(s) = \frac{1}{2\pi i}\oint_{H(s,\cdot)} \mathrm{d}z/(z-a)N(s)=2πi1​∮H(s,⋅)​dz/(z−a), both the integrand and the domain depend continuously on sss, so NNN is continuous; but NNN takes only integer values, and continuous plus integer-valued   ⟹  \implies⟹ constant. ⭐ It is the first hinge between complex analysis and topology: the winding number is a homotopy invariant, so "holomorphic   ⟹  \implies⟹ the contour integral depends only on the homotopy class" — this is why Cauchy's theorem holds for any curve that can be contracted to a point, with no convexity of the region required. The complex-analytic proof of Brouwer's fixed-point theorem (Brouwer 不动点定理（圆盘）) applies this directly to γ(t)=e2πit\gamma(t) = e^{2\pi i t}γ(t)=e2πit.
