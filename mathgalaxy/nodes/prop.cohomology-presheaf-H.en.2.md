# Derived Global Sections Depend Only on the Associated Sheaf　`prop.cohomology-presheaf-H`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=prop.cohomology-presheaf-H)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./prop.cohomology-presheaf-H.md)

$$\Gamma\bigl(X,\ R\mathcal{H}(M^{\bullet})\bigr) \;=\; R\Gamma\bigl(X,\ M^{\bullet}\bigr), \qquad \Gamma\bigl(X,\ \mathcal{H}^{n}(M^{\bullet})\bigr) \;=\; H^{n}\bigl(X,\ M^{\bullet}\bigr).$$

## Notes · **draft**
**Proof.** $\mathcal{H}$ is adjoint to **sheafification**, and sheafification is **exact** ✓, so $\mathcal{H}$ preserves injectives ✓ (the right adjoint of an exact left adjoint preserves injectives).

Conversely, the functor $M \mapsto \Gamma(X, M)$ is exact on **presheaves** ✓.
