## Statement · **draft**
If $X = \bigcup_{i \in I} X_{i}$ is an **open cover** of a topological space $X$ (with $X_i$ open), then, regarding each $X_i$ as a condensed set, $(X_{i} \to X)_{i \in I}$ is a **covering** of $X$ in $\mathrm{Cond}$.
