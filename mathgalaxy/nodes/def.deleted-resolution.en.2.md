# Deleted Resolution　`def.deleted-resolution`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=def.deleted-resolution)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./def.deleted-resolution.md)

$$P_{\bullet} : \quad \cdots \to P_{2} \xrightarrow{\ d_{2}\ } P_{1} \xrightarrow{\ d_{1}\ } P_{0} \to 0 .$$

In other words, in the exact complex with $M$ in degree $-1$, delete the term in degree $-1$ and keep the rest as it is.
