Statement

A finite-dimensional FFF-algebra AAA is semisimple if and only if J(A)=0J(A)=0J(A)=0.

Notes

The proof is on the edge imp.jacobson: first J(A)J(A)J(A) annihilates every irreducible module; then it is a two-sided ideal; finally the description as a finite intersection of maximal left ideals shows that J(A)=0J(A)=0J(A)=0 is equivalent to semisimplicity of the regular module.
