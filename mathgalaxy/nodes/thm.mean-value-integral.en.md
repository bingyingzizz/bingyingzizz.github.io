## Statement · **draft**
Let $f$ be continuous on $[a, b]$, and let $g$ be integrable and of **constant sign** ($g \ge 0$ or $g \le 0$). Then there is $\xi \in [a, b]$ such that

$$\int_a^b f g = f(\xi) \int_a^b g$$
