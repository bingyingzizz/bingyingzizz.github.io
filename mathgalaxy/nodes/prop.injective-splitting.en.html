Statement

Let III be a left RRR-module. The following are equivalent:

  1. III is injective.
  2. Every monomorphism I↣MI \rightarrowtail MI↣M splits, meaning that there is a retraction r:M→Ir : M \to Ir:M→I satisfying r∘i=idIr \circ i = \mathrm{id}_{I}r∘i=idI​.
  3. Every short exact sequence 0→I→M→M2→00 \to I \to M \to M_{2} \to 00→I→M→M2​→0 splits.

Notes

1 implies 2: Take A=IA = IA=I and f=idIf = \mathrm{id}_{I}f=idI​. The extension M→IM \to IM→I supplied by injectivity is the required retraction ✓.

2 implies 1: Given A↣BA \rightarrowtail BA↣B and f:A→If : A \to If:A→I, form the pushout

P:=(I⊕B)/{ (f(a), −a):a∈A },P := (I \oplus B) / \{\, (f(a),\ -a) : a \in A \,\},P:=(I⊕B)/{(f(a), −a):a∈A},

This identifies the images of A→IA \to IA→I and A→BA \to BA→B in PPP. The map I→PI \to PI→P is still a monomorphism (a pushout of a monomorphism is a monomorphism: to test whether (x,0)(x, 0)(x,0) lies in the image, inspect only the III component). By 2, I↣PI \rightarrowtail PI↣P has a retraction rrr, so r∘(B→P)r \circ (B \to P)r∘(B→P) is the required extension of fff ✓.

2 and 3 are equivalent: By the criterion in split short exact sequence, a short exact sequence splits if and only if its monomorphism has a retraction ✓. ∎

📌 This is the same result as injective criterion (four equivalent conditions for injective objects in an abelian category). Items 2 and 4 there are two formulations of this result. The proof carries over verbatim to any abelian category, since it uses only pushouts and kernels.

Among the three conditions, (1)  ⟹  \implies⟹(2) follows from the definition, (2)  ⟹  \implies⟹(3) requires a pushout, and (3)  ⟹  \implies⟹(1) is the substantive criterion:

  • (1)   ⟹  \implies⟹ (2): One line (injectivity gives the retraction directly).
  • (2)   ⟹  \implies⟹ (3): Use a pushout diagram. Given 0→I→M→M2→00 \to I \to M \to M_{2} \to 00→I→M→M2​→0, apply (2) to I↣MI \rightarrowtail MI↣M to obtain a splitting.
  • (3)   ⟹  \implies⟹ (1): This step uses a lift. Given I↣MI \rightarrowtail MI↣M and I→NI \to NI→N, form the pushout M→PM \to PM→P. Then 0→N→P→coker⁡→00 \to N \to P \to \operatorname{coker} \to 00→N→P→coker→0 splits, and composing the section with M→PM \to PM→P gives a lift of M→NM \to NM→N. ⚠️ Pushouts must exist (as they always do in an abelian category). Enough injectives are unnecessary.

⚠️ Stated only for the category of RRR-modules (or an abelian category): Splitting the short exact sequences in (3) presupposes kernels and cokernels, and a retraction in (2) is defined using composition of morphisms. Both conditions need to be reformulated in nonabelian categories.
