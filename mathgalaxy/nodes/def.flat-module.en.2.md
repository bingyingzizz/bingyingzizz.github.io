## Notes · **draft**
**There are many equivalent formulations**; the usual ones are:

- $\operatorname{Tor}_{n}^{R}(F, -) = 0$ for all $n \ge 1$ (see "flat iff all higher Tor vanish");
- for every **finitely generated ideal** $I \subseteq R$, the map $F\otimes I \to F\otimes R$ is injective (**Lambek's criterion** — only finitely generated ideals need be checked);
- $F = \varinjlim F_{i}$ with $F_{i}$ finitely generated free (see "flat modules are filtered colimits of finitely generated free modules").
