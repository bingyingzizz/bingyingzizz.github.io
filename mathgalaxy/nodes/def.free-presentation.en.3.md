# free presentation　`def.free-presentation`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=def.free-presentation)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./def.free-presentation.md)

A free presentation carries this picture into $\mathbf{CHaus}$: $R$ is the equivalence relation "glue together points with the same image", and the third condition requires that $R$ itself be a surjective image of a free compact Hausdorff space — so the whole of $S$ is built from free objects by a single coequalizer.

In $\mathbf{CHaus}$ coequalizers exist, hence $\operatorname{coker}(R \rightrightarrows F)$ exists; it is the quotient space $F/R$.
