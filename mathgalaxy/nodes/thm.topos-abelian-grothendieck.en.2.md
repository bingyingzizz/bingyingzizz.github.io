## Abstract · **reviewed**
For a topos $\mathcal{T}$, the category $\mathcal{T}(\mathbf{Ab})$ of abelian group objects in sheaves is a Grothendieck category: abelian, with a small set of generators, all colimits, and filtered colimits exact. Abelianness is checked componentwise in the presheaf category and transported along the exact sheafification reflection; limits and colimits exist by that reflection; and if $S$ generates $\mathcal{T}$ then $\{\mathbb{Z}\cdot X : X \in S\}$ generates $\mathcal{T}(\mathbf{Ab})$.
