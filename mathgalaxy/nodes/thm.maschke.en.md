## Statement · **draft**
Let $G$ be a finite group and let $F$ be a field with $\operatorname{char}F\nmid|G|$. Then the group algebra $F[G]$ is semisimple; equivalently, every finite-dimensional representation of $G$ is semisimple (completely reducible).
