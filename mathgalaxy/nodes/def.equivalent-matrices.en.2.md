# Equivalent matrices　`def.equivalent-matrices`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=def.equivalent-matrices)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./def.equivalent-matrices.md)

Equivalence is an **equivalence relation** on $M_{m \times n}(K)$, and it allows $m \ne n$. Over a field $K$, the equivalence classes are completely classified by rank: every $A$ is equivalent to the unique normal form

$$\operatorname{diag}(I_r, 0), \qquad r = \operatorname{rank} A.$$

Equivalence is weaker than both **similarity** ($B = P^{-1} A P$) and **congruence** ($B = P^{T} A P$): the latter two are stated only for square matrices of the same order, and each of them preserves more invariants.
