# Theorema Egregium　`thm.theorema-egregium`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=thm.theorema-egregium)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./thm.theorema-egregium.md)

**Two checks**: the cylinder ($K = 0$) is locally isometric to the plane, so the curvatures must agree; the sphere ($K = 1/R^{2}$) is not locally isometric to any surface with $K = 0$, so curvature really is an isometry invariant.

⚠️ **What is not intrinsic**: $k_{1}$, $k_{2}$ and $H$ are all extrinsic (the cylinder versus the plane is the counterexample: $k_{1}$ is $1/R$ and $0$ respectively). The remarkable theorem holds for the **product**, not for the factors. This also explains why $K$ appears everywhere in intrinsic geometry while $H$ hardly ever does.
