# Sheafification and the Sheaf Reflection　`thm.sheafification`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=thm.sheafification)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./thm.sheafification.md)

$$\operatorname{Hom}_{\widehat{\mathcal{C}}}\bigl(T^{\sharp},\ F\bigr) \;\cong\; \operatorname{Hom}_{\widehat{\mathcal{C}}}\bigl(T,\ F\bigr)$$

and it is **idempotent**: $(T^{\sharp})^{\sharp} \cong T^{\sharp}$. Concretely, $T^{\sharp}$ is obtained by "applying the Čech construction to a presheaf twice".

## Notes · **draft**
Corollaries:

1. **Limits in the category of sheaves are computed in the category of presheaves** (a reflective subcategory is closed under limits);
2. $T \mapsto T^{\sharp}$ **preserves all colimits and finite limits**.
