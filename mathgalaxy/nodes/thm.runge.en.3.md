**Lemma A (Cauchy representation)**: Let $K$ be compact, let $V$ be an open neighborhood of $K$, and let $f$ be holomorphic on $V$. Then there is a finite union of closed squares $S$ with positively oriented boundary $\Gamma = \partial S$ such that $K \subset \operatorname{int} S,\qquad S \subset V,\qquad f(z) = \frac{1}{2\pi i}\int_{\Gamma}\frac{f(\zeta)}{\zeta - z}\,\mathrm{d}\zeta\quad(z\in K).$. Proof: $d := \operatorname{dist}(K,\mathbb{C}\setminus V) > 0$. Take a square grid of side length $h$, with $h\sqrt{2} < d$. Let $S$ be the union of all closed grid squares that meet $K$.
