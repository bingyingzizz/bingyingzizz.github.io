# Skyscraper Sheaves Are Injective　`prop.skyscraper-injective`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=prop.skyscraper-injective)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./prop.skyscraper-injective.md)

$i_{x,*}A$ is injective, so $\operatorname{Hom}(i_{x,*}C, i_{x,*}A) \to \operatorname{Hom}(i_{x,*}B, i_{x,*}A)$ is surjective, and under the same adjunction isomorphism this is precisely the lift of $\operatorname{Hom}(C, A) \to \operatorname{Hom}(B, A)$ — hence the latter is surjective and $A$ is injective.

∎
