# Quasi-Separated Condensed Sets as Filtered Colimits　`lem.qseparated-ind-inclusions`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=lem.qseparated-ind-inclusions)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./lem.qseparated-ind-inclusions.md)

**Why this matters.** It replaces the abstract finiteness condition "quasi-separated" by an **operable** description: $X$ is the colimit of a sequence of larger and larger compact Hausdorff pieces $S_{1} \hookrightarrow S_{2} \hookrightarrow \cdots$.

The language of $\operatorname{Ind}$-objects is exactly designed for "things assembled as filtered colimits", so many computations in $\mathrm{Cond}$ can be done piece by piece and then colimit-taken.
