## Notes · **draft**
**Proof**: first translate $a$ to $0$ (translation does not change the integral). Set $\varphi(t) := \displaystyle\int_{0}^{t} \frac{\gamma'(s)}{\gamma(s)}\,\mathrm{d}s$; then $(\gamma e^{-\varphi})' = e^{-\varphi}(\gamma' - \varphi'\gamma) = 0$, so $\gamma(t) e^{-\varphi(t)}$ is constant;

from $\varphi(0) = 0$ we get $e^{\varphi(t)} = \gamma(t)/\gamma(0)$.
