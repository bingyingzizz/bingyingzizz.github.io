# Stability of Syzygies　`prop.syzygy-stability`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=prop.syzygy-stability)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./prop.syzygy-stability.md)

⚠️ In the inductive step, the distinction between $\Omega^{n}M$ and $\Omega_{1}^{n}M$ matters. The induction hypothesis gives an **isomorphism after adding direct summands**, so at every step the precovers must be constructed again after taking direct sums. This is what “stable” means here.
