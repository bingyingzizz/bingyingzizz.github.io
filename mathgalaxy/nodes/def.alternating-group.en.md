## Statement · **draft**
Every $\sigma \in S_n$ can be written as a product of **transpositions** (swaps of two points), and the **parity** of the number of transpositions used does not depend on the decomposition. This defines the **sign**

$$\operatorname{sgn}(\sigma) := (-1)^{k}$$
where $\sigma$ is a product of $k$ transpositions (well-definedness is guaranteed by the parity of $\sigma$).
