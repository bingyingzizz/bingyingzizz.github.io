## Statement · **draft**
The **Bernoulli numbers** $B_n$ are determined by $B_0=1$ and the recursion
$$\sum_{k=0}^{n}\binom{n+1}{k}B_k=0\qquad(n\ge1)$$
equivalently, as a formal power series (`def.formal-power-series`)
$$\frac{t}{e^t-1}=\sum_{n\ge0}B_n\frac{t^n}{n!}.$$
The first few are: $B_0=1$, $B_1=-\tfrac12$, $B_2=\tfrac16$, $B_3=0$, $B_4=-\tfrac1{30}$, $B_6=\tfrac1{42}$; and $B_{2k+1}=0$ for $k\ge1$.
