## Statement · **draft**
Let $f \in L^+$. Define the integral of $f$ as the supremum of the integrals of simple functions not exceeding it:

$\int f d\mu := \sup \{ \int \varphi d\mu : 0 \le \varphi \le f,\ \varphi$ **is a simple function** $\}$
