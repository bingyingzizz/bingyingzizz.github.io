these are the Blaschke factors sending $z_{j}$ to $0$, with modulus exactly $|z| = R$ on $1$. Then $g := f / B$ has no zeros on $|z| \le R$, apply the zero-free case to it, and compute term by term $\log|B(0)| = \sum \log(|z_{j}|/R)$. ∎

⭐ **It is the bridge between "growth $\leftrightarrow$ zeros"**: the right-hand side is controlled by $\log M(R)$, hence

$$n(R)\,\log 2 \le \log M(2R) - \log|f(0)|$$

which pins "how many zeros lie in $R$" below "the maximum modulus on $2R$" — this is the entire driving force behind Weierstrass products and the Hadamard factorization.
