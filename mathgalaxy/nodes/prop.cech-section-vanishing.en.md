## Statement · **draft**
Let $\mathcal{T}$ be a topos and $X_{0} \xrightarrow{\ p\ } X$ a morphism **admitting a section** $s$ ($p s = 1_{X}$). Write

$$X_{n} := X_{0} \times_{X} \cdots \times_{X} X_{0},$$

(with $n+1$ factors.)
