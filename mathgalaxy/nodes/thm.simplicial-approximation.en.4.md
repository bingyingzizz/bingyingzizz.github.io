# simplicial approximation　`thm.simplicial-approximation`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=thm.simplicial-approximation)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./thm.simplicial-approximation.md)

Proof of (i): Suppose $v_{0}, \ldots, v_{p}$ span, in $K$, a simplex $\sigma$. Choose an interior point $x \in \sigma^{\circ}$. For each $i$ we have $x \in \operatorname{st}_{K}(v_{i})$, so $f(x) \in \operatorname{st}_{L}(g(v_{i}))$. Hence

$$f(x) \;\in\; \bigcap_{i=0}^{p} \operatorname{st}_{L}\big(g(v_{i})\big).$$
