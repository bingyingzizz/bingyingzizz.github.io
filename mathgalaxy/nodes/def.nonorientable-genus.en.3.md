# nonorientable genus　`def.nonorientable-genus`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=def.nonorientable-genus)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./def.nonorientable-genus.md)

**Distinction from orientable genus**: In the orientable case, $\chi=2-2g$. In the nonorientable case, $\chi=2-k$. The same $\chi$ gives different counts of holes in the two classes of surfaces, so the two notions must be distinguished.

**Homology**: $H_{1}(M;\mathbb{Z})\cong\mathbb{Z}^{k-1}\oplus\mathbb{Z}/2$ ($k\ge1$), with the torsion recording nonorientability.

⚠ There is no simple conversion between $k$ and $g$. Taking the orientation double cover of a nonorientable surface gives the orientable genus $g=k-1$.
