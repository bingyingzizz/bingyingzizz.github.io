# free compact Hausdorff　`def.free-compact-hausdorff`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=def.free-compact-hausdorff)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./def.free-compact-hausdorff.md)

that is, continuous maps $F \to S$ correspond bijectively to families of points in $S$, say $(t_{i})_{i \in I}$. When this family makes $F \to S$ surjective, it is called a set of **generators** of $F$.

## Notes · **draft**
The word "free" means here exactly what it means for free groups and free modules: **there are no constraints on $I$** ($I$ is discrete, so a map out of $I$ is continuous as soon as one specifies points arbitrarily), hence "maps from $F$ to elsewhere" and "families of points in $I$" are the same thing.
