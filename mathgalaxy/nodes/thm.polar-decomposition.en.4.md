# Polar Decomposition　`thm.polar-decomposition`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=thm.polar-decomposition)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./thm.polar-decomposition.md)

**Division of labour with the SVD**: the polar decomposition emphasizes the **structure** "rotation $\times$ stretch", while the SVD emphasizes the **coordinate expression** "$A = U\Sigma V^{*}$". Both carry the same information; the SVD gives two extra bases.

⚠️ Uniqueness must be stated carefully: $P$ is always unique, but when $A$ is singular, $U$ may be chosen arbitrarily on the kernel of $A$ — so the sentence "the polar decomposition is unique" holds only in the invertible case.
