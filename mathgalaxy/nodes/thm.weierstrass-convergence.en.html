Statement

⭐ Why this is the foundation of the whole chapter: it says that holomorphy is closed under uniform convergence, so one may safely pass to limits — normal families (Montel 定理), the Riemann mapping theorem, and Hurwitz's theorem all rely on it to justify that "the limit function is still holomorphic".
