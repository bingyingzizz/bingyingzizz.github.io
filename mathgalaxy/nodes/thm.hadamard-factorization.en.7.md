**Step 4. The order of $\Phi$ is $\le\rho$.** Let $h$ be an entire logarithm of $\Phi$. Its construction in Step 5 uses only that $\Phi$ is nowhere zero, so we may use it here independently of the estimates in this step. Then $\operatorname{Re}h=\log|\Phi|$. It suffices to show that for every sufficiently large $r$ there is an $R\in[r,2r]$ such that on $|z|=R$ we have $\log|\Phi(z)|\le C\,r^{\rho+\varepsilon}$.
