Statement

Let VVV be a finite-dimensional real Banach space. Then its Pontryagin dual is the usual vector space dual:

V^  ≅  V∗=Hom⁡R(V, R).\widehat{V} \;\cong\; V^{\ast} = \operatorname{Hom}_{\mathbb{R}}(V,\ \mathbb{R}).V≅V∗=HomR​(V, R).

Notes

Proof. Since T=R/Z\mathbb{T} = \mathbb{R}/\mathbb{Z}T=R/Z and VVV is a finite-dimensional real vector space, hence simply connected ✓, every continuous homomorphism V→TV \to \mathbb{T}V→T lifts uniquely to a continuous homomorphism V→RV \to \mathbb{R}V→R ✓.

Now observe that a continuous additive map V→RV \to \mathbb{R}V→R is automatically R\mathbb{R}R-linear. Additivity first gives f(qv)=qf(v)f(qv) = q f(v)f(qv)=qf(v) for rational scalars ✓, and continuity then extends this to real scalars by approximation ✓.

Thus V^≅Hom⁡R(V,R)=V∗\widehat{V} \cong \operatorname{Hom}_{\mathbb{R}}(V, \mathbb{R}) = V^{\ast}V≅HomR​(V,R)=V∗ ✓.

∎

📌 Finite dimensionality is essential: A real Banach space is locally compact if and only if it is finite-dimensional.
