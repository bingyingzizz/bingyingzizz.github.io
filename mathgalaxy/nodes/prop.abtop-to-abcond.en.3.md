# topological abelian groups embed into condensed ones　`prop.abtop-to-abcond`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=prop.abtop-to-abcond)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./prop.abtop-to-abcond.md)

**Preservation of all limits** follows from the theorem that $\mathbf{Top} \to \mathrm{Cond}$ preserves finite products. Limits of condensed objects are computed pointwise, and computing limits of continuous maps in $\mathbf{Top}$ agrees with computing them pointwise.
