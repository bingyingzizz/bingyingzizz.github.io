## Notes · **draft**
**Proof (the two standard steps of the comparison theorem).**
**① Existence (lifting layer by layer).** Degree $0$: $M = Z^{0}_{I} \hookrightarrow I^{0}$ is a monomorphism (because $I^{-1} = 0$), and the composite $M \xrightarrow{\ f\ } N \to J^{0}$, by the **injectivity** of $J^{0}$, lifts along this monomorphism to $F^{0} : I^{0} \to J^{0}$ ✓.
Inductive step: suppose $F^{0}, \ldots, F^{n}$ has been constructed and, for $k \le n$, $F^{k}d^{k-1}_{I} = d^{k-1}_{J}F^{k-1}$ holds.
