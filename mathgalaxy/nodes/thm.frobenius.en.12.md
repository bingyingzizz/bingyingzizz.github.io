# Frobenius theorem　`thm.frobenius`

[in graph](../#n=thm.frobenius)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./thm.frobenius.md)

**IV. (2)$\implies$(4):** Each point has a coordinate chart $N_{q}$. Order all connected integral manifolds by inclusion and apply Zorn's lemma to obtain maximal elements. The union of a chain is still a connected integral manifold, since locally it is part of a coordinate chart and smoothness is preserved along the chain. The maximal elements are pairwise disjoint, since otherwise their union would still be a connected integral manifold, and they cover $M$. We need $M$ to be second countable to ensure that the set of leaves is countable and the construction is well-defined. Second countability is already part of the definition of a manifold.
