Statement draft

Let C\mathcal{C}C be a site, Δ:Set→C^\Delta : \mathbf{Set} \to \widehat{\mathcal{C}}Δ:Set→C the functor sending a set EEE to the constant presheaf, and (−)‾\underline{(-)}(−)​ the corresponding constant sheaf. Then:

  1. if E=lim←⁡iEiE = \varprojlim_{i} E_{i}E=lim​i​Ei​ is a limit in sets, then ΔE=lim←⁡iΔEi\Delta E = \varprojlim_{i}\Delta E_{i}ΔE=lim​i​ΔEi​;
  2. the constant-sheaf functor (−)‾\underline{(-)}(−)​ preserves finite limits: if E=lim←⁡iEiE = \varprojlim_{i} E_{i}E=lim​i​Ei​ is a finite limit, then E‾=lim←⁡iEi‾\underline{E} = \varprojlim_{i}\underline{E_{i}}E​=lim​i​Ei​​.

Notes draft

⭐ In one sentence: the constant presheaf Δ\DeltaΔ is a right adjoint and preserves all limits; the constant sheaf (−)‾=a∘Δ\underline{(-)}=a\circ\Delta(−)​=a∘Δ's left adjoint aaa preserves only finite limits — this is the source of "finite limits can be computed pointwise, colimits must be sheafified first".
