## Notes · **draft**
**Proof.** Projectivity is equivalent to $\operatorname{Hom}_{\mathbb{Z}}(\mathbb{Z}\cdot T, -)$ being **exact** ✓.

By the universal property of free objects, $\operatorname{Hom}_{\mathbb{Z}}(\mathbb{Z}\cdot T, M) \cong M(T)$ — here $M(T)$ means the family of pointwise evaluations "evaluate a presheaf at $T$" ✓;

and exactness in a presheaf category is judged **pointwise**, and pointwise it is just Hom between abelian groups, hence exact ✓.

∎
