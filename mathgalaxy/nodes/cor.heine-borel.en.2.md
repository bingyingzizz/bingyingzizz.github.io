## Notes · **draft**
⭐ This is **a corollary of a corollary**: $\mathbb{R}^{n}$ complete + bounded sets are totally bounded $\implies$ bounded closed sets are sequentially compact $\implies$ compact (see "three equivalent characterizations of compactness in metric spaces").

What is really used is the coordinatewise version of Bolzano–Weierstrass.

⚠ It is **not** a theorem for general metric spaces: in a general metric space "compact $\implies$ bounded closed" always holds, but not conversely (the discrete space is a sufficient example, as is the unit ball of $\ell^{2}$).
