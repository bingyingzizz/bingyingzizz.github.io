⚠ **Checking the CR equations alone is not enough**; one also needs $f$ to be real differentiable.

Counterexample: $f(x + iy) = \sqrt{|xy|}$ has all four partial derivatives equal to $0$ at the origin (CR holds), yet the difference quotient along $h = t(1+i)$ tends to $1/(1+i) \ne 0$, so it is not complex differentiable.

This also shows that "partial derivatives exist" is far weaker than "real differentiable".

📌 Holomorphy therefore has two practical routes: the abstract difference quotient (good geometric intuition), or checking CR concretely (convenient for computation).
