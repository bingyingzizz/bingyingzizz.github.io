# homotopy invariance of the winding number　`thm.winding-homotopy`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=thm.winding-homotopy)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./thm.winding-homotopy.md)

**The Cauchy form**: after rendering $\Omega$ simply connected, $f\,\mathrm{d}z$ can be deformed continuously along the homotopy without changing the integral; differentiating with respect to $s$ gives $\frac{\mathrm{d}}{\mathrm{d}s}\oint_{H(s,\cdot)} f\,\mathrm{d}z = 0$; this is the same route as "$f$ has a primitive", see `prop.single-valued-branch`.
