Statement

The tensor product is a bifunctor ⊗R:Mod-R×R-Mod→Ab\otimes_{R} : \mathbf{Mod}\text{-}R \times R\text{-}\mathbf{Mod} \to \mathbf{Ab}⊗R​:Mod-R×R-Mod→Ab, and:

  1. Additivity: for f:M→M1f : M \to M_{1}f:M→M1​ and g:N→N1g : N \to N_{1}g:N→N1​ there is f⊗gf \otimes gf⊗g; fixing one variable gives an additive functor.
  2. Commutes with direct sums: (⨁iMi)⊗RN≅⨁i(Mi⊗RN)(\bigoplus_{i} M_{i}) \otimes_{R} N \cong \bigoplus_{i} (M_{i} \otimes_{R} N)(⨁i​Mi​)⊗R​N≅⨁i​(Mi​⊗R​N), M⊗R(⨁jNj)≅⨁j(M⊗RNj)M \otimes_{R} (\bigoplus_{j} N_{j}) \cong \bigoplus_{j} (M \otimes_{R} N_{j})M⊗R​(⨁j​Nj​)≅⨁j​(M⊗R​Nj​), naturally.
  3. Unit: R⊗RN≅NR \otimes_{R} N \cong NR⊗R​N≅N (r⊗n↦rnr \otimes n \mapsto r nr⊗n↦rn); more generally R(I)⊗RN≅N(I)R^{(I)} \otimes_{R} N \cong N^{(I)}R(I)⊗R​N≅N(I).
  4. Associativity: if MMM is an (S,R)(S, R)(S,R)-bimodule, NNN an (R,T)(R, T)(R,T)-bimodule and PPP a left TTT-module, then
(M⊗RN)⊗TP  ≅  M⊗R(N⊗TP).(M \otimes_{R} N) \otimes_{T} P \;\cong\; M \otimes_{R} (N \otimes_{T} P).(M⊗R​N)⊗T​P≅M⊗R​(N⊗T​P).
  1. Symmetry (RRR commutative): τ:M⊗RN→N⊗RM\tau : M \otimes_{R} N \to N \otimes_{R} Mτ:M⊗R​N→N⊗R​M, m⊗n↦n⊗mm \otimes n \mapsto n \otimes mm⊗n↦n⊗m, and τ∘τ=id\tau \circ \tau = \mathrm{id}τ∘τ=id.

Notes

⭐ From 2 and 3 it follows at once that (−)⊗RN(-) \otimes_{R} N(−)⊗R​N preserves colimits (direct sums, cokernels, filtered colimits). This explains both "right exact" (preserves cokernels) and "preserves direct sums", and is the common root of 张量函子右正合 and 自由模平坦.

⚠ In general it does not preserve limits: ⊗\otimes⊗ does not preserve kernels.

0→Z→  2  Z0 \to \mathbb{Z} \xrightarrow{\;2\;} \mathbb{Z}0→Z2​Z is exact; after tensoring with N=QN = \mathbb{Q}N=Q it stays exact (multiplication by 222 is still injective on Q\mathbb{Q}Q), but after tensoring with N=Z/2N = \mathbb{Z}/2N=Z/2 multiplication by 222 becomes the zero map and a kernel appears (see 张量函子右正合).

Nor does it preserve infinite products.

📌 On a commutative ring, associativity 4 reads (M⊗RN)⊗RP≅M⊗RN⊗RP(M \otimes_{R} N) \otimes_{R} P \cong M \otimes_{R} N \otimes_{R} P(M⊗R​N)⊗R​P≅M⊗R​N⊗R​P, so multiple tensor products can be written without parentheses.
