Statement

In plain words: a function holomorphic on the whole complex plane and bounded must be constant — the complex plane is "too crowded" to accommodate a nonconstant bounded holomorphic function.

If fff is holomorphic on C\mathbb{C}C (such functions are called entire), and there is a constant MMM with

∣f(z)∣≤M,∀z∈C,|f(z)| \le M, \qquad \forall z \in \mathbb{C},∣f(z)∣≤M,∀z∈C,

then fff is constant.
