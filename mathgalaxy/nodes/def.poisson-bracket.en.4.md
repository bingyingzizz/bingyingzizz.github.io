# Poisson Bracket　`def.poisson-bracket`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=def.poisson-bracket)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./def.poisson-bracket.md)

This connects the algebra of functions with the algebra of vector fields.

⚠️ **Poisson brackets are more general than symplectic structures**: Allowing degenerate Poisson brackets gives **Poisson manifolds** (for example, the Lie–Poisson bracket on the dual $\mathfrak{g}^{*}$ of a Lie algebra). Symplectic manifolds are the nondegenerate special case. Only the symplectic case is covered here.
