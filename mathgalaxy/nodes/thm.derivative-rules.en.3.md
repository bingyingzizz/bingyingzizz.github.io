# rules for differentiation　`thm.derivative-rules`

⚠ The chain rule **cannot** be written directly as $\dfrac{\Delta g}{\Delta f}\cdot\dfrac{\Delta f}{\Delta x}$: $\Delta f$ may be $0$ (when $f$ takes the same value near $x_0$), and the denominator vanishes.

The correct approach writes the increment of $g$ as "linear main part + remainder": $g(y) - g(y_0) = [g'(y_0) + \varepsilon(y)]\,(y - y_0)$, where $\varepsilon(y) \to 0$.

⭐ The quotient rule is obtained from the product rule and $\left(\dfrac{1}{g}\right)' = -\dfrac{g'}{g^{2}}$;
