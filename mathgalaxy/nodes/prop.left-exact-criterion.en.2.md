# Criterion for Left Exactness　`prop.left-exact-criterion`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=prop.left-exact-criterion)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./prop.left-exact-criterion.md)

Dually: $F$ is right exact $\iff$ $F$ is additive and sends right exact sequences to right exact sequences.

## Notes · **draft**
⭐ This splits "left exact" into two **termwise checkable** facts: **additivity** (look at biproducts) and **preservation of left exact sequences** (look at the actual sequences). These are the two rulers used in practice to verify that a functor is left exact.
