## Notes · **draft**
**The proof has two steps**: First prove that the star condition $\Longrightarrow$ gives a simplicial map and a homotopy, then prove that sufficiently fine subdivision makes the star condition hold.

**Lemma 1 (star condition)**: Suppose $g : \operatorname{Vert}(K) \to \operatorname{Vert}(L)$ satisfies

$$f\big(\operatorname{st}_{K}(v)\big) \;\subseteq\; \operatorname{st}_{L}\big(g(v)\big) \qquad \forall v .$$

Then (i) $g$ sends every simplex of $K$ to a simplex of $L$, hence is simplicial, and (ii) $|g| \simeq f$.
