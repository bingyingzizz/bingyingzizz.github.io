# Étale Sheaf　`def.etale-sheaf`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=def.etale-sheaf)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./def.etale-sheaf.md)

- locally of finite presentation;
- **flat**;
- **unramified** (that is, $\Omega^{1}_{U/X} = 0$).

These three are the algebraic counterparts of "local homeomorphism" in topology, but they additionally allow **finite coverings** (for example $\operatorname{Spec}\mathbb{C} \to \operatorname{Spec}\mathbb{R}$: two points covering one).
