## Statement · **draft**
Let $u \in B(H)$. One calls $u$ a **unitary operator** if

$$u\,u^* = u^*\,u = I .$$

Equivalently: $u$ is a surjective isometry preserving the inner product, or $u^* = u^{-1}$.

## Notes · **draft**
A unitary operator is exactly an "invertible isometry": $\langle ux, uy\rangle = \langle x, y\rangle$ and $u$ surjective.
