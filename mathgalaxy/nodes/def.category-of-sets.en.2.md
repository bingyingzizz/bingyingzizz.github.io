# category of sets　`def.category-of-sets`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=def.category-of-sets)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./def.category-of-sets.md)

In $\mathbf{Set}$ products are cartesian products $X\times Y$ (projections are coordinate maps), the terminal object is a singleton, equalizers are $\{x:f(x)=g(x)\}$, and pullbacks are fiber products $X\times_Z Y$; coproducts are disjoint unions $X\sqcup Y$, the initial object is the empty set, coequalizers are quotients $Y/\sim$ (generated by $f(x)\sim g(x)$), and pushouts are gluings. It is the standard target of the forgetful functor.

## Notes · **stale**
**Complete and cocomplete**: small products together with equalizers give all small limits; small coproducts together with coequalizers give all small colimits.
