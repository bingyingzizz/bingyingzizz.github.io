## Statement · **draft**
Let $a_n>0$ and $R=\lim_{n\to\infty} n\Bigl(\frac{a_n}{a_{n+1}}-1\Bigr)$. Then
1. if $R>1$, $\sum a_n$ converges;
2. if $R<1$, $\sum a_n$ diverges;
3. if $R=1$, the test is inconclusive.

## Notes · **draft**
**Relation to Kummer's test**: take $c_n=n$ in Kummer's test; then

$$K_n=c_n\frac{a_n}{a_{n+1}}-c_{n+1}=n\frac{a_n}{a_{n+1}}-(n+1)=n\Bigl(\frac{a_n}{a_{n+1}}-1\Bigr)-1\to R-1 .$$
