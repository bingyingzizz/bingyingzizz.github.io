## Notes · **draft**
The family of open sets is in general only a topology, not a $\sigma$-algebra — one must take the $\sigma$-algebra it generates to obtain all Borel sets.

On $\mathbb{R}$, $\mathfrak{B}_\mathbb{R}$ can also be generated by the half-open intervals $\{ (a, b] : a < b \}$ (or { [a, b) }). These forms are more convenient when constructing measures, because disjoint unions of half-open intervals are easy to compute.
