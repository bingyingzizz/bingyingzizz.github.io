prop.slice-fully-faithful-mono

Statement

A morphism f:Y→Xf : Y \to Xf:Y→X is a monomorphism   ⟺  \iff⟺ the slice functor along fff

C/Y⟶C/X,(A→Y)⟼(A→Y→X)\mathcal{C}/Y \longrightarrow \mathcal{C}/X, \qquad (A \to Y) \longmapsto (A \to Y \to X)C/Y⟶C/X,(A→Y)⟼(A→Y→X)

is fully faithful. Dually: fff is an epimorphism   ⟺  \iff⟺ X\C→Y\CX\backslash\mathcal{C} \to Y\backslash\mathcal{C}X\C→Y\C is fully faithful.

Notes

Proof. On Hom sets the functor is just an inclusion (both sides are subsets of Hom⁡(A,B)\operatorname{Hom}(A, B)Hom(A,B)), so faithfulness is automatic ✓; only fullness needs checking.

(⟹)(\Longrightarrow)(⟹) Given v:A→Bv : A \to Bv:A→B and b∘v=f∘ab \circ v = f \circ ab∘v=f∘a: from fff monic one gets b∘v=ab \circ v = ab∘v=a ✓, that is, vvv is a morphism in /Y/Y/Y ✓.

(⟸)(\Longleftarrow)(⟸) If fg=fhf g = f hfg=fh, take a=ga = ga=g, b=1Yb = 1_{Y}b=1Y​: in /X/X/X both hhh and ggg are morphisms between the same pair of objects, so faithfulness gives g=hg = hg=h ✓. ∎
