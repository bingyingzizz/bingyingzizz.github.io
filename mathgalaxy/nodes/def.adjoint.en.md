## Statement · **draft**
Let $F : \mathcal{C} \to \mathcal{D}$, $G : \mathcal{D} \to \mathcal{C}$ be a pair of functors. $(F, G)$ is called a pair of **adjoint functors** if there is a natural isomorphism

$$\operatorname{Hom}_{\mathcal{D}}\bigl(F(X),\ Y\bigr) \;\cong\; \operatorname{Hom}_{\mathcal{C}}\bigl(X,\ G(Y)\bigr) \qquad (X \in \mathcal{C},\ Y \in \mathcal{D})$$
