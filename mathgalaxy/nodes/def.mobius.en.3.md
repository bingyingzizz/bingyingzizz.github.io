# Möbius Transformation　`def.mobius`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=def.mobius)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./def.mobius.md)

⭐ Three basic building blocks: translation $z \mapsto z + b$, dilation-rotation $z \mapsto az$ ($a \ne 0$), inversion $z \mapsto 1/z$.

Every Möbius transformation is a composite of these, and $ad - bc \ne 0$ guarantees that the composite does not collapse to a constant.

⭐ Geometric properties (Chapter 13): it sends **circles or lines** to circles or lines (collectively "generalized circles", which on the sphere are simply "circles");

and it preserves the **cross-ratio**:

$$(z_1, z_2; z_3, z_4) = \frac{(z_1 - z_3)(z_2 - z_4)}{(z_1 - z_4)(z_2 - z_3)}.$$
