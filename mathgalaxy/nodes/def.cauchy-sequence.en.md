## Statement · **draft**
A real sequence $\{x_n\}$ is a **Cauchy sequence** iff

$$\forall \varepsilon > 0,\ \exists N,\ \forall m, n \ge N :\ |x_m - x_n| < \varepsilon$$

That is: once the index is far enough out, **any two terms** are close to each other.
