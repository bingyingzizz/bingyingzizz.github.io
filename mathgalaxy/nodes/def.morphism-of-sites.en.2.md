## Notes · **draft**
⭐ **A pair of adjoints**: $f^{-1} \dashv f_{*}$ — $f^{-1}$ is the left adjoint (so preserves colimits), $f_{*}$ the right adjoint (so preserves limits). Together with "continuous" ($f^{-1}$ pulls sheaves back to sheaves), this pair constitutes a morphism between topoi (see "morphism of topoi").

**Why left exactness is required**: finite limits in a category of sheaves are computed pointwise in presheaves; for $f^{-1}$ to send sheaves to sheaves **and still preserve finite limits**, it must preserve finite limits already at the level of sites.
