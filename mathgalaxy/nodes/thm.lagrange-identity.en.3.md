# Lagrange’s Identity　`thm.lagrange-identity`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=thm.lagrange-identity)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./thm.lagrange-identity.md)

$$\det\begin{pmatrix} \langle u,u\rangle & \langle u,w\rangle \\ \langle w,u\rangle & \langle w,w\rangle\end{pmatrix} ,$$

So geometrically it says: **Gram determinant = the square of the area of the parallelogram**.
