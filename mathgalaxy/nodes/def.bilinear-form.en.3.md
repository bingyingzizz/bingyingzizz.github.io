# bilinear form　`def.bilinear-form`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=def.bilinear-form)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./def.bilinear-form.md)

⭐ When $\dim V, \dim W < \infty$, fixing a basis gives $\operatorname{Bil}(V, W; K) \cong M_{m \times n}(K)$ (via $B(e_i, f_j)$), consistent with "a matrix is the table of coefficients of a bilinear form".

⭐ $\operatorname{Bil}(V, W; U) \cong \operatorname{Hom}(V \otimes W, U)$ — this is the universal property.
