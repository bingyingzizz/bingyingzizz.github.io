Statement

Every locally compact Hausdorff space is a Baire space.

Notes

The proof is the same as in the complete metric case, replacing "the radii of nested closed balls tend →0\to 0→0" by "nested compact neighbourhoods": non-empty intersection is guaranteed by the finite intersection property of compact sets (no shrinking radius is needed).

⭐ Significance: this frees Baire category from metric spaces, so it applies to C(K)C(K)C(K), compact groups and Stone spaces — objects that carry no metric. Compact Hausdorff spaces in condensed mathematics can therefore also be handled by "generic point" arguments.
