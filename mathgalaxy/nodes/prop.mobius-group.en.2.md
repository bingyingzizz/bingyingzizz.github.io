# Möbius transformations form a group　`prop.mobius-group`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=prop.mobius-group)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./prop.mobius-group.md)

Moreover, it is **exactly** the group of holomorphic automorphisms of the extended plane $\widehat{\mathbb{C}}$: every biholomorphism $\widehat{\mathbb{C}} \to \widehat{\mathbb{C}}$ is fractional linear.

## Notes · **draft**
**Group structure**: matrix multiplication corresponds to composition of transformations; $\begin{pmatrix} a & b\\ c & d\end{pmatrix}$ and $\lambda\begin{pmatrix} a & b\\ c & d\end{pmatrix}$ give the same transformation, so one must quotient by scalars. The matrix of the inverse transformation is the inverse matrix (up to a scalar).
