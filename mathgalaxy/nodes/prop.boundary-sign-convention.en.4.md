# Sign Convention for Boundaries　`prop.boundary-sign-convention`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=prop.boundary-sign-convention)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./prop.boundary-sign-convention.md)

Then for every face $(i,\alpha)$: viewing $c_{(i,\alpha)}$ as a parametrization of a piece of $\partial M$, **it agrees with the induced orientation of $\partial M$ if and only if $(-1)^{i+\alpha} = +1$**.

In other words: after reordering the signs of all faces according to the induced orientation, the resulting chain is

$$\partial c = \sum_{i=1}^{n}\sum_{\alpha=0}^{1}(-1)^{i+\alpha}\,c_{(i,\alpha)} .$$

So the "algebraic boundary" and the "geometric boundary" are not two conventions but one: the signs are already written in the coefficients of $\partial c$.
