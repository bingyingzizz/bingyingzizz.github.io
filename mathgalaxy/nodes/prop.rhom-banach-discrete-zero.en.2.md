# Derived Hom from Finite-Dimensional Banach Spaces to Discrete Groups Vanishes　`prop.rhom-banach-discrete-zero`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=prop.rhom-banach-discrete-zero)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./prop.rhom-banach-discrete-zero.md)

$$\operatorname{RHom}_{\mathbb{Z}}(M,\ N) = 0.$$

More generally: if $V$ is an arbitrary $\mathbb{R}$-module and $N$ is discrete, then $\operatorname{RHom}_{\mathbb{Z}}(V, N) = 0$.
