# Schwarz lemma　`lem.schwarz`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=lem.schwarz)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./lem.schwarz.md)

⚠ $f(0) = 0$ cannot be omitted. $f(z) = (z + 1)/2$ maps the disk to the disk, but $|f(0)| = 1/2 > 0$ — as soon as the origin is moved, the lemma's conclusions all fail.

⚠ Equality in $|f(z)| \le |z|$ at **a single interior point** already suffices; it need not hold everywhere — this is the rigidity of the maximum modulus principle.

📌 The version without the hypothesis $f(0) = 0$ is the Schwarz–Pick theorem. This lemma is also the source of "the automorphism group of the disk is small", and a basic tool in conformal mapping.
