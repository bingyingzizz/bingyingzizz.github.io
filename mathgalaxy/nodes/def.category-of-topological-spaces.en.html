def.category-of-topological-spaces · Top

Translated for an earlier content version and not updated since. The Chinese text is authoritative.

Statement

The category of topological spaces Top\mathbf{Top}Top: objects are topological spaces, morphisms are continuous maps, composition is composition of maps, and identities are identity maps.

Products are products with the product topology, coproducts are disjoint unions, equalizers carry the subspace topology, and coequalizers carry the quotient topology.

Notes

Complete and cocomplete: the constructions above give small limits and small colimits term by term.

The forgetful functor Top→Set\mathbf{Top}\to\mathbf{Set}Top→Set preserves all limits and colimits, but it is faithful and not full: not every function is continuous.

Not an additive category: hom⁡(X,Y)\hom(X,Y)hom(X,Y) in general has no group structure; open maps are not morphisms either (morphisms need only be continuous).
