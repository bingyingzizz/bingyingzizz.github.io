## Statement · **draft**
The functor

$$M \longmapsto \widehat{M} := C_{\mathbb{Z}}(M, \mathbb{T}),$$

is an **autoequivalence** of the category of **locally compact Hausdorff abelian groups** and is a self-adjoint equivalence.

In particular, $\widehat{M}$ is again locally compact Hausdorff, and **taking the dual twice recovers the original group**: $\widehat{\widehat{M}} = M$.
