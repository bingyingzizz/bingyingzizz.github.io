Statement

Let D2:={ z∈C:∣z∣≤1 }D^{2} := \{\, z \in \mathbb{C} : |z| \le 1 \,\}D2:={z∈C:∣z∣≤1}, S1=∂D2S^{1} = \partial D^{2}S1=∂D2. Then there is no continuous map

r:D2⟶S1r : D^{2} \longrightarrow S^{1}r:D2⟶S1

with r(z)=zr(z) = zr(z)=z for all z∈S1z \in S^{1}z∈S1. Such an rrr, if it existed, would be called a retraction of S1S^{1}S1 in D2D^{2}D2, and S1S^{1}S1 a retract of D2D^{2}D2. Equivalently: the inclusion i:S1↪D2i : S^{1} \hookrightarrow D^{2}i:S1↪D2 has no left inverse.
