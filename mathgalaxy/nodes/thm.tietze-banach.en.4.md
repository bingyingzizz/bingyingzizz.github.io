**Proof (Dugundji extension theorem).** Dugundji's theorem is the version of Tietze for maps into topological vector spaces. Suppose $X$ is **paracompact** (a metric space suffices), $A \subseteq X$ is **closed**, and $V$ is a **locally convex** topological vector space. Then there is a **linear** extension operator $C(A, V) \to C(X, V)$, so the restriction map is surjective.
