## Statement · **reviewed**
Let $A$ and $B$ be sets.

A **relation** $R$ consists of ordered pairs; $R \subseteq A \times B$ means that $R$ is a relation from $A$ to $B$.

- **domain** $\operatorname{dom} R = \{ a : \exists b (a, b) \in R \}$;
- **range** $\operatorname{ran} R = \{ b : \exists a (a, b) \in R \}$;
- **inverse** $R^{-1} = \{ (b, a) : (a, b) \in R \}$.
