## Statement · **draft**
Let $X$ be a topological space.

A continuous map $p : E \to X$ is an **étale map** (local homeomorphism) if every $e \in E$ has an open neighbourhood $V \ni e$ such that $p(V)$ is open in $X$ and $p|_{V} : V \to p(V)$ is a homeomorphism.

Then $(E, p)$ is an **étale space** (espace étalé) over $X$.
