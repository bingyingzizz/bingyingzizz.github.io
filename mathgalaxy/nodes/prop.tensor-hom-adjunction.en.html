prop.tensor-hom-adjunction

Machine-drafted and pending author review. The Chinese text is authoritative.

Statement

Fix VVV. Then for all KKK-vector spaces W,UW, UW,U there is a natural isomorphism

Hom⁡K(W⊗V, U)  ≅  Hom⁡K(W, Hom⁡K(V,U))\operatorname{Hom}_K(W \otimes V,\ U) \;\cong\; \operatorname{Hom}_K\bigl( W,\ \operatorname{Hom}_K(V, U) \bigr)HomK​(W⊗V, U)≅HomK​(W, HomK​(V,U))

that is, the functor (−)⊗V(-) \otimes V(−)⊗V is left adjoint to Hom⁡(V,−)\operatorname{Hom}(V, -)Hom(V,−):

(−)⊗KV  ⊣  Hom⁡K(V,−)(-) \otimes_K V \;\dashv\; \operatorname{Hom}_K(V, -)(−)⊗K​V⊣HomK​(V,−)

Notes

⭐ This is the tensor–hom adjunction, the second paradigm besides “free–forgetful”: it matches “linear maps of two variables” with “linear maps valued in a function space” (currying).

⭐ Its categorical status: “⊗\otimes⊗ is closed” (a symmetric monoidal closed category) is exactly the existence of this adjunction — the material around Adjoint Functors applies directly.

⚠ The word “natural” cannot be dropped: the isomorphism itself is easy to write, but only naturality in W,UW, UW,U makes it an adjunction.

This is also what distinguishes an adjunction from “an accidental isomorphism”.
