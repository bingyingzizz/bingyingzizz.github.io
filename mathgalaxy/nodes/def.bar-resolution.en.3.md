**Two points about the notation**:
- **$R[G]$-freeness**: $[g_{0}|\cdots|g_{n}] = g_{0} \cdot [1|g_{1}|\cdots|g_{n}]$, so as an $R[G]$-module $B_{n}$ has basis $\{[1|g_{1}|\cdots|g_{n}]\}$ — it is **free**, and this is the key to using the whole resolution to compute derived functors (free $\implies$ projective).
