Statement

All presheaves on C\mathcal{C}C, with natural transformations as morphisms, form the category

C^:=Hom⁡(Cop,Set)\widehat{\mathcal{C}} := \operatorname{Hom}(\mathcal{C}^{\mathrm{op}}, \mathbf{Set})C:=Hom(Cop,Set)

Notes

It is a functor category, so limits and colimits are computed pointwise — every later construction involving C^\widehat{\mathcal{C}}C starts here.

A few small examples: ∅^=1\widehat{\emptyset} = \mathbf{1}∅​=1 (the unique functor from the empty category to Set\mathbf{Set}Set); 1^≃Set\widehat{\mathbf{1}} \simeq \mathbf{Set}1≃Set (choose an object and a set);

when GGG is a monoid, G^≃G-Set\widehat{G} \simeq G\text{-}\mathbf{Set}G≃G-Set (sets with a GGG-action, with maps compatible with translation by every element of GGG).
