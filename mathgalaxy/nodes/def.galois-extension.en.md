## Statement · **draft**
A finite extension $K/F$ is **Galois** if it is **both** normal and separable.

Equivalent conditions (any one of them):

- $|\operatorname{Gal}(K/F)| = [K:F]$;
- $F = K^{\operatorname{Gal}(K/F)}$ (the elements fixed by the group are exactly $F$ itself);
- $K$ is the splitting field over $F$ of some **separable** polynomial.
