## Notes · **draft**
**Normal form**: a reduced word is the unique representation of each element, so two words are equal iff they are letter-by-letter equal after reduction; this makes the operation on $F(S)$ well defined and leaves no further relations.

**Basic examples**: $F(\emptyset)=1$; $F(\{s\})\cong\mathbb{Z}$ (words $s^{n}$); $F(\{a,b\})$ is non-abelian, and $\pi_{1}(S^{1}\vee S^{1})\cong F(\{a,b\})$.

⚠ **A different object from the free abelian group**: `thm.csz-free` concerns free abelian groups (commutative); this node is the non-abelian free group; the abelianization of $F(S)$ is the free abelian group.
