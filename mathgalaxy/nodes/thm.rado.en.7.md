**Can the nonorientable case use the orientation double cover?** Yes, but **an additional argument is needed**. Let $p:\widetilde{S}\to S$ be the orientation double cover. On $S$ there is a corresponding free $\mathbb{Z}/2$-action, with no fixed points and two points per fiber. Even given a triangulation of $\widetilde{S}$, descending it to $S$ requires an **equivariant** triangulation: there must be a triangulation of $\widetilde{S}$ for which the involution is a simplicial automorphism.
