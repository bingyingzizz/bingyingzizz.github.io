# injective criterion　`prop.injective-criterion`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=prop.injective-criterion)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./prop.injective-criterion.md)

**One supplement uses Zorn (crucial)**: in the category of $R$-modules, the Bolzano–Weierstrass-style argument — "$I$ injective $\iff$ for every **ideal** $J \subseteq R$, every $J \to I$ extends to $R \to I$" (Baer's criterion) — uses the **Zorn lemma** (take a maximal extendable submodule). So the convenience "injectivity can be tested on ideals" **carries the cost of the axiom of choice**; in a general Grothendieck category the analogue is "test on subobjects", which needs no AC.
