## Notes · **draft**
**Proof.** The two universal elements give the respective isomorphisms in $h_{X} \cong F \cong h_{X'}$ ✓. The Yoneda lemma gives the unique isomorphism $X \cong X'$ ✓, and sending $s$ to $s'$ is precisely how this isomorphism is expressed at the level of elements ✓. ∎
