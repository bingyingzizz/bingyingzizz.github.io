## Statement · **draft**
1. **A Grothendieck category automatically satisfies AB3\*** (has all limits) — that is, AB5 together with a generator gives AB3\* for free;
2. apart from the zero category $\{0\}$, **no category satisfies both AB5 and AB5\***;
3. $\mathbf{Ab}(\mathcal{T})$ on a topos satisfies **AB5 and AB3\***, but **in general does not satisfy AB6 or AB4\***.
