## Notes · **draft**
The trace is basis-independent, so $\chi_\theta$ is well defined. Characters are additive on direct sums but generally not multiplicative: $\chi(ab)\ne\chi(a)\chi(b)$. For $A=F[G]$, restricting $\chi_\theta$ to $G$ gives a group character. If $W$ is a subrepresentation, then $\chi_V=\chi_W+\chi_{V/W}$.
