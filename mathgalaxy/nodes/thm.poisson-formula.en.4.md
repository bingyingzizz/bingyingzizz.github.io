# Poisson integral formula　`thm.poisson-formula`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=thm.poisson-formula)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./thm.poisson-formula.md)

⭐ Shape of the kernel: when $z = 0$, $P_r = 1$, and the formula degenerates to the mean-value property; when $|z| \to r$ and the direction points at $e^{i\theta_0}$, the kernel blows up to $\infty$ there and tends to zero elsewhere — hence it behaves like an “approximate identity”, piling the weight onto the corresponding boundary point.

⚠ Both the $\operatorname{Re} f$ and the $f$ versions hold (the kernel is **real-valued**, so the formula for $f$ can simply be real-parted).
