# Injective and Divisible Modules over a PID　`thm.pid-injective-divisible`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=thm.pid-injective-divisible)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./thm.pid-injective-divisible.md)

In particular: a $\mathbb{Z}$-module is injective iff it is a divisible group.

## Notes · **draft**
**Divisible $\implies$ injective** (via Baer's criterion): let $J \subseteq R$ be an ideal and $f : J \to I$.

In a PID $J = (a)$.

If $a = 0$ then $J = 0$, take $x = 0$;

if $a \ne 0$, set $y := f(a)$, and by divisibility choose $x \in I$ with $a x = y$.

Then for $r a \in J$ we have $f(r a) = r f(a) = r a x = (r a) x$ ✓, i.e. $f$ is given by "multiplication by $x$".
