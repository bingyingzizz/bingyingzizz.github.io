# singular value decomposition　`thm.svd`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=thm.svd)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./thm.svd.md)

The $\sigma_i$ are called the **singular values** of $A$; they are the square roots of the eigenvalues of $A^{\dagger}A$ (or $AA^{\dagger}$).

## Notes · **draft**
⭐ Geometric reading of SVD: **every linear map = rotation/reflection $\to$ stretch along the coordinate axes $\to$ another rotation/reflection**.

Thus it reduces an arbitrary matrix to diagonal form "after choosing good bases $U$ and $V$" — this is the complete answer to the **equivalence** classification ($A \mapsto PAQ$) (the diagonal entries are the singular values, the number of nonzero ones is the rank).
