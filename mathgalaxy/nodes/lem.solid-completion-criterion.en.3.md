# criterion for solid completeness　`lem.solid-completion-criterion`

Let $F : \mathcal{A}_0 \to \mathcal{A}$ come with a **natural transformation** $X \to F(X)$, i.e. a family of maps $\eta_X : X \to F(X)$, such that for every map in $\mathcal{A}_0$, say $u : X \to X'$, one has $F(u) \circ \eta_X = \eta_{X'} \circ u$ ("$F$ commutes with $u$"), and suppose:

for every $X \in \mathcal{A}_0$, every map built from direct sums of images of $F$, say $f : Y \to Z$, with kernel $K$,

$$\operatorname{RHom}(F(X), K) \longrightarrow \operatorname{RHom}(X, K)$$
