# power series　`def.power-series`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=def.power-series)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./def.power-series.md)

is called a **power series**. The set of $x$ at which it converges is its **domain of convergence**; the **interior** of that domain is always an interval (or disk) centred at $x_0$, and each $x$ of it is thus an interior point of convergence.

## Notes · **draft**
⭐ A power series is where "series" and "function" meet: it is both a series (every term computable) and a function (on its domain of convergence).

So the tools of the two previous packs converge here.
