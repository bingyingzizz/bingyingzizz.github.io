Statement

A bialgebra is a monoid in the opposite of the category of kkk-algebras. k[t]k[t]k[t] with t↦t⊗1+1⊗tt \mapsto t \otimes 1 + 1 \otimes tt↦t⊗1+1⊗t, and k[t,1/t]k[t, 1/t]k[t,1/t] with t↦t⊗tt \mapsto t \otimes tt↦t⊗t, are both bialgebras.

Notes

Proof. Translating "monoid in the opposite category" back into kkk-algebras gives "the comultiplication Δ:A→A⊗A\Delta : A \to A \otimes AΔ:A→A⊗A is an algebra homomorphism" ✓; both Δ\DeltaΔ above preserve multiplication ✓ (in k[t,1/t]k[t,1/t]k[t,1/t] the element ttt is invertible, so t↦t⊗tt \mapsto t \otimes tt↦t⊗t is the only choice) ✓. ∎
