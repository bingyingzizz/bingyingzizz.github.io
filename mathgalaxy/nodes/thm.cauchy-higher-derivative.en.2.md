# Cauchy Integral Formula for Derivatives　`thm.cauchy-higher-derivative`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=thm.cauchy-higher-derivative)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./thm.cauchy-higher-derivative.md)

The setting is the same as for the Cauchy integral formula ($f$ holomorphic on an open set containing $\overline{\Omega}$, with $\partial\Omega$ a piecewise smooth simple closed curve, positively oriented). Then for $n = 0, 1, 2, \cdots$ and $z \in \Omega$,

$$f^{(n)}(z) = \frac{n!}{2\pi i}\oint_{\partial\Omega} \frac{f(\zeta)}{(\zeta - z)^{n+1}}\,\mathrm{d}\zeta.$$

In particular, $f$ is **infinitely differentiable** on $\Omega$, and every derivative is holomorphic.

## Notes · **draft**
⭐ The proof is simply **differentiation under the integral sign**.
