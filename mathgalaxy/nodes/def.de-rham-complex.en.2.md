# de Rham Complex and Cohomology　`def.de-rham-complex`

Let $M$ be a smooth manifold and $\Omega^{k}(M)$ the space of $k$-forms. The exterior derivative on a manifold gives the **de Rham complex**

$$0 \longrightarrow \Omega^{0}(M) \xrightarrow{\ \mathrm{d}\ } \Omega^{1}(M) \xrightarrow{\ \mathrm{d}\ } \Omega^{2}(M) \xrightarrow{\ \mathrm{d}\ } \cdots$$

From $\mathrm{d}^{2} = 0$ one gets $\operatorname{im}\mathrm{d} \subseteq \ker\mathrm{d}$. Write the spaces of **closed forms** and **exact forms** as

$$Z^{k}(M) := \ker\bigl(\mathrm{d} : \Omega^{k}(M) \to \Omega^{k+1}(M)\bigr),$$
