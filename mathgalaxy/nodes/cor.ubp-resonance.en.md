## Statement · **draft**
Let $E$ be Banach, $F$ normed, $u_n \in B(E, F)$, and suppose $\sup_n \|u_n\| = \infty$. Then

$$R := \Bigl\{ x \in E : \sup_n \|u_n(x)\| = \infty \Bigr\}$$

is a **dense $G_\delta$** (in particular non-empty).
