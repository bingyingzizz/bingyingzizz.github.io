# Left Exactness and the Zeroth Derived Functor　`prop.left-exact-iff-R0`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=prop.left-exact-iff-R0)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./prop.left-exact-iff-R0.md)

$F$ is **left exact** $\iff$ for every $M \in \mathcal{A}$ one has $R^{0}F(M) \cong F(M)$.

Dually (with enough projectives): $F$ is right exact $\iff L_{0}F \cong F$.

## Notes · **draft**
⭐ In one sentence: **left exactness can be tested using only the $0$-th derived functor**. Conversely, this explains why "$R^{0}F = F$" is treated as the default property of a left exact functor in the notation of derived functors.

⚠️ The hypothesis "$F$ is additive" cannot be dropped: derived functors of non-additive functors make no sense (they are defined on complexes in an additive category).
