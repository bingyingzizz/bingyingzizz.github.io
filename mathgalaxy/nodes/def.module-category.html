def.module-category · 模 · 范畴 · 阿贝尔范畴 · 同调代数

又称模范畴·A-Mod

陈述

设 AAA 是含幺环。左 AAA-模范畴 A-ModA\text{-}\mathbf{Mod}A-Mod 的对象是左 AAA-模，态射是 AAA-线性映射，复合与单位逐点定义。

右 AAA-模构成 Mod-A\mathbf{Mod}\text{-}AMod-A；AAA 交换时两者等价。极限：积=直积、终对象=零模、等化子=核、拉回=纤维积；余极限：余积=直和、始对象=零模、余等化子=商模、推出=粘合。它满足 AB3/AB4/AB4*/AB5；本图不写 AB6、不写 AB3*。

说明

同调代数的样板间：A-ModA\text{-}\mathbf{Mod}A-Mod 是阿贝尔范畴，有核、余核、单满态射严格；自由模给出足够投射对象，Baer 判据给出足够内射对象，还是 Grothendieck 范畴。

特例：Z-Mod≃Ab\mathbb Z\text{-}\mathbf{Mod}\simeq\mathbf{Ab}Z-Mod≃Ab；kkk 是域时 k-Modk\text{-}\mathbf{Mod}k-Mod 就是 Vectk\mathbf{Vect}_kVectk​。

Freyd–Mitchell：小阿贝尔范畴可全忠实、正合地嵌入某个 A-ModA\text{-}\mathbf{Mod}A-Mod，所以「在模范畴里追元素」是合法的推广。

Statement stale

Let AAA be a ring with unity. The category of left AAA-modules A-ModA\text{-}\mathbf{Mod}A-Mod has as objects the left AAA-modules and as morphisms the AAA-linear maps, with composition and identities defined pointwise.

Right AAA-modules form Mod-A\mathbf{Mod}\text{-}AMod-A; when AAA is commutative the two categories are equivalent.

Notes draft

The model room of homological algebra: A-ModA\text{-}\mathbf{Mod}A-Mod is abelian, with kernels, cokernels, and strict monomorphisms and epimorphisms; free modules give enough projectives, Baer's criterion gives enough injectives, and it is even a Grothendieck category.

Special cases: Z-Mod≃Ab\mathbb Z\text{-}\mathbf{Mod}\simeq\mathbf{Ab}Z-Mod≃Ab; when kkk is a field, k-Modk\text{-}\mathbf{Mod}k-Mod is just Vectk\mathbf{Vect}_kVectk​.

Freyd–Mitchell: every small abelian category embeds fully faithfully and exactly into some A-ModA\text{-}\mathbf{Mod}A-Mod, so “chasing elements in a module category” is a legitimate generalization.
