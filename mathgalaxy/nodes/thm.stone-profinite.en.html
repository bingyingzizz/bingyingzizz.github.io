thm.stone-profinite · Stone

Machine-drafted and pending author review. The Chinese text is authoritative.

Statement

A topological space is a Stone space   ⟺  \iff⟺ it is a profinite space.

Notes

⭐ This translates a topological condition into a colimit condition: Stone spaces are exactly the "(filtered) colimits of finite discrete spaces", that is, the compact topology carried by a profinite set.

📌 Thus constructions on Stone spaces can be checked on finite sets and then extended by limits — this device is used repeatedly in the @@topo.stone@@ cluster.

⚠ Do not read "profinite" as "projective object + finite": lim←⁡\varprojlimlim​ here means a limit of finite sets, a completely different matter from "projective objects of CHaus\mathbf{CHaus}CHaus" in @@thm.gleason@@ (that is extreme disconnectedness).
