Abstract

The category CHaus\mathbf{CHaus}CHaus is a reflective subcategory of Top\mathbf{Top}Top, the reflection being the Stone–Čech compactification: Hom⁡CHaus(βX,K)≅Hom⁡Top(X,K)\operatorname{Hom}_{\mathbf{CHaus}}(\beta X, K) \cong \operatorname{Hom}_{\mathbf{Top}}(X, K)HomCHaus​(βX,K)≅HomTop​(X,K) for every compact Hausdorff KKK. Construction: embed XXX into the Tychonoff cube [0,1]C(X,[0,1])[0,1]^{C(X,[0,1])}[0,1]C(X,[0,1]) and take the closure, which is compact Hausdorff as a closed subset of a compact Hausdorff space. The embedding is injective iff XXX is Tychonoff. Corollary: any limit of compact Hausdorff spaces is again compact Hausdorff.
