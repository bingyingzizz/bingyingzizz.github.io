## Statement · **draft**
Let $X$ be a normed space, $X^{*}$ its continuous dual, and $B_{X^{*}} = \{\varphi \in X^{*} : \|\varphi\| \le 1\}$. Then $B_{X^{*}}$ is compact in the **weak* topology** $\sigma(X^{*}, X)$ (generated by the seminorms $\varphi \mapsto |\varphi(x)|$, $x \in X$).
