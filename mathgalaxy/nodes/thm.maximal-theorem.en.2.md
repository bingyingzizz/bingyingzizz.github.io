## Notes · **draft**
This is a **weak (1,1) inequality**: it does not ensure $Hf \in L^1$ (in general it is not), but it controls its **distribution function** by $1/\alpha$.

In $\mathbb{R}^{n}$ one has $C = 3^n$ (the dilation factor from the covering lemma).

⭐ Its role: **the exceptional set of averaging is small** — hence every pointwise convergence problem can be reduced to "the measure of the exceptional set $\to 0$".
