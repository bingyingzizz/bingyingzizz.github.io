## Statement · **draft**
Let $(G,\cdot,e,{}^{-1})$ be a group. If for all $a,b\in G$ one has $ab=ba$, then $G$ is called an **abelian group** (or **commutative group**). Equivalently, $G$ is a $\mathbb Z$-module (with scalar multiplication by $n$ regarded as the action).
