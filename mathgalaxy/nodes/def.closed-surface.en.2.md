# closed surface　`def.closed-surface`

Equivalently, $S$ is a compact, connected, Hausdorff, second-countable space without boundary that is locally homeomorphic to $\mathbb{R}^{2}$.

## Notes · **draft**
⭐ **Each of the three conditions has a role, and dropping any one changes the classification theorem**:

- Dropping connectedness, $\implies$ becomes a **disjoint union** of finitely many closed surfaces, and the classification theorem applies to each component.

- Dropping compactness, $\implies$ can have infinite genus or infinitely many holes. The classification becomes much more complicated and is outside the scope of this graph.
