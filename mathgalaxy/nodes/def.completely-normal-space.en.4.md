# Completely Normal Space　`def.completely-normal-space`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=def.completely-normal-space)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./def.completely-normal-space.md)

⚠️ **Normality is not hereditary — this is the sole reason $T_{5}$ exists**: the classical example is the Tychonoff plank, or a variant of the Sorgenfrey plane — a subspace of a normal space need not be normal. So $T_{5}$ is not a rewording of $T_{4}$ but genuinely adds an axiom.

⚠️ **Every metrizable space is $T_{5}$**: every subspace of a metric space is again a metric space and hence normal. This is why analysis rarely needs $T_{5}$: it holds automatically (the stronger property is $T_{6}$, see `def.perfectly-normal-space`).
