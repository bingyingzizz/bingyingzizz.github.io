## Notes · **draft**
⭐ **Having a section $\implies$ implies no higher Čech cohomology**. Hence if a single member of a covering family admits a section, the Čech complex of the whole family contracts — the cases "isomorphism" and "finite disjoint covering" are all automatically acyclic.
