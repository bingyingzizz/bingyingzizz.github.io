## Statement · **draft**
The **homotopy category** $\mathbf{K}(\mathcal{C})$ has the same objects as $\mathcal{C}(\mathcal{C})$, and morphisms

$$\operatorname{Hom}_{\mathbf{K}(\mathcal{C})}(K, L) \;:=\; \operatorname{Hom}_{\mathcal{C}(\mathcal{C})}(K, L) \big/\ \sim,$$
where $\sim$ is generated by the **null-homotopic** maps.
