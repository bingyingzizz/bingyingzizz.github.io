## Statement · **draft**
Let $M$ be a $G$-module. The **$n$-th cohomology group** of the group $G$ with coefficients in $M$ is the right derived functor of the invariant functor:

$$H^{n}(G, M) := R^{n}(-)^{G}(M),$$

equivalently

$$H^{n}(G, M) = \operatorname{Ext}^{n}_{R[G]}\bigl(R,\ M\bigr),$$

where $R$ carries the trivial $G$-action. In particular $H^{0}(G, M) = M^{G}$ and $H^{n}(G, M) = 0$ (for $n < 0$).
