## Statement · **draft**
**(a)** If $X$ is extremally disconnected, then the closures of any two disjoint open sets $U, V$ are still disjoint: $\overline{U} \cap \overline{V} = \emptyset$.

**(b)** An extremally disconnected Hausdorff space is **totally disconnected**.

## Notes · **draft**
**(a)** By contradiction: if $\overline{U} \cap \overline{V}$ is non-empty, take a point $x$ in it.
