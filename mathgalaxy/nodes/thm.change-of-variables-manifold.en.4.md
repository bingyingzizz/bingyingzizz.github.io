# Change of Variables on Manifolds　`thm.change-of-variables-manifold`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=thm.change-of-variables-manifold)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./thm.change-of-variables-manifold.md)

**The classical change of variables is the coordinate version**: take $M,U\subseteq\mathbb{R}^{n}$, $N = g(U)$ and $\omega = F\,\mathrm{d}y^{1}\wedge\cdots\wedge\mathrm{d}y^{n}$; then $f^{\ast}\omega = (F\circ g)\det(\partial g/\partial x)\,\mathrm{d}x^{1}\wedge\cdots\wedge\mathrm{d}x^{n}$, and the formula becomes $\int_{g(U)}F\,\mathrm{d}y = \int_{U}(F\circ g)\det(\partial g/\partial x)\,\mathrm{d}x$ — the only difference from a calculus textbook is the absence of absolute values.
