# Casorati–Weierstrass　`thm.casorati-weierstrass`

[in graph](../#n=thm.casorati-weierstrass)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./thm.casorati-weierstrass.md)

⚠️ Casorati–Weierstrass only says **dense**, not "attains every value". The stronger **Picard great theorem** says that the image of a punctured neighbourhood misses **at most one point** (and that value is attained at most finitely often) — not developed here, only cited.
