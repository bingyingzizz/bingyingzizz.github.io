⭐ It is enough to remember this table: **normal $\iff$ unitarily diagonalizable**; adding further conditions yields the special classes — adding "all eigenvalues real" gives Hermitian, adding "all eigenvalues on the unit circle" gives unitary, and adding both gives the self-adjoint unitary with "eigenvalues only $\pm 1$".
