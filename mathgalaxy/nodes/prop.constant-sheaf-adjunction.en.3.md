# Adjunction for Constant Sheaves　`prop.constant-sheaf-adjunction`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=prop.constant-sheaf-adjunction)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./prop.constant-sheaf-adjunction.md)

$$\operatorname{Hom}\bigl(\widetilde{E},\ F\bigr) \cong \operatorname{Hom}\Bigl(\coprod_{E} \mathbf{1}_{\mathcal{C}},\ F\Bigr) \cong \operatorname{Hom}(\mathbf{1}_{\mathcal{C}}, F)^{E} ✓$$

then replace the left-hand side by $\operatorname{Hom}_{\mathbf{Set}}(E, \operatorname{Hom}(\mathbf{1}, F))$ ✓. ∎
