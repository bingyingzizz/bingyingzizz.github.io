The volume form of an $n$-manifold cannot simply be pulled back to a $k$-dimensional submanifold: for $k<n$ every $n$-form restricts to the zero form. The volume of a submanifold therefore comes from the induced metric $g_{N} := i^{*}g$, whose volume form in an oriented chart is $\sqrt{\det(g_{ab})}\,\mathrm{d}x^{1}\wedge\cdots\wedge\mathrm{d}x^{k}$ with $g_{ab} := g(\partial_{a}i,\partial_{b}i)$.
