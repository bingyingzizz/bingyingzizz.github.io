## Notes · **draft**
Existence rests entirely on Riesz: fix $x \in K$; the functional $y \mapsto \langle x, u(y)\rangle_K$ is continuous on $H$ (bound $\le \|x\|\,\|u\|$), so Riesz representation gives a unique $u^*(x)$.

Then check that $u^*$ is linear (from $\langle x, u(\cdot)\rangle$ being linear in $x$) and bounded;

$\|u^*\| = \|u\|$ follows by exchanging two suprema.
