Statement draft

Let AAA be a ring and I⊆AI \subseteq AI⊆A an ideal. On the additive quotient group A/IA/IA/I define

(a+I)+(b+I):=(a+b)+I,(a+I)(b+I):=ab+I.(a+I)+(b+I) := (a+b)+I, \qquad (a+I)(b+I) := ab+I .(a+I)+(b+I):=(a+b)+I,(a+I)(b+I):=ab+I.

These operations are well defined and make A/IA/IA/I a ring, the quotient ring; the quotient map π:A→A/I\pi : A \to A/Iπ:A→A/I, π(a)=a+I\pi(a) = a+Iπ(a)=a+I, is a ring homomorphism with ker⁡π=I\ker \pi = Ikerπ=I.

Universal property: if f:A→Bf : A \to Bf:A→B is a ring homomorphism with f(I)=0f(I) = 0f(I)=0, then there is a unique ring homomorphism fˉ:A/I→B\bar f : A/I \to Bfˉ​:A/I→B with fˉ∘π=f\bar f \circ \pi = ffˉ​∘π=f.

Notes draft

⭐ Why it must be an ideal: to make (a+x)(b+y)≡ab(modI)(a+x)(b+y) \equiv ab \pmod I(a+x)(b+y)≡ab(modI) hold one needs ay,xb,xy∈Iay, xb, xy \in Iay,xb,xy∈I; now x,y∈Ix, y \in Ix,y∈I while a,ba, ba,b are arbitrary, so III must absorb multiplication from both sides.

⭐ Examples: Z/nZ\mathbb{Z}/n\mathbb{Z}Z/nZ, K[x]/(f)K[x]/(f)K[x]/(f) (a field when fff is irreducible), Fp[x]/(x2+1)\mathbb{F}_p[x]/(x^{2}+1)Fp​[x]/(x2+1).

⭐ If AAA is a commutative ring with unit, then A/IA/IA/I is an integral domain iff III is prime; A/IA/IA/I is a field iff III is maximal (both objects come in later nodes; this pack only gives the construction of the quotient ring). Commutativity cannot be dropped: the ring M2(R)M_2(\mathbb{R})M2​(R) has zero ideal (0)(0)(0), which is a maximal two-sided ideal, but M2(R)/(0)≅M2(R)M_2(\mathbb{R})/(0)\cong M_2(\mathbb{R})M2​(R)/(0)≅M2​(R) is neither a domain nor a field.
