## Statement · **draft**
Let $F, G : [a, b] \to \mathbb{R}$ be differentiable with $F', G'$ integrable. Then

$$\int_a^b F G' = F(b)G(b) - F(a)G(a) - \int_a^b F' G$$

(written in the customary form: $\int_a^b u\,dv = uv\bigr|_a^b - \int_a^b v\,du$.)

## Notes · **draft**
⭐ It is again the **product rule read backwards**: $(FG)' = F'G + FG'$; integrate both sides and use Newton–Leibniz.
