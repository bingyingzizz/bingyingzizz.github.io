## Notes · **draft**
⭐ “Universal property” = **characterizing the quotient as “the most general target that kills $U$”**: every map vanishing on $U$ factors **uniquely** through $V/U$.

Existence comes from the definition, uniqueness from $\pi$ being surjective.

⭐ This is the general recipe for the family “cokernel / quotient”; in category theory it is the universal property of a **coequalizer**.

⚠ The hypothesis is $U \subseteq \ker T$ ($T$ vanishes on $U$), not $U = \ker T$ — one is allowed to kill a little more.
