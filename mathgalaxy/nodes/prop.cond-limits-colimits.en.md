## Statement · **draft**
$\mathrm{Cond}$ has all (small) limits and colimits. **Limits are computed pointwise**: for every compact Hausdorff space $S$,

$$\Bigl(\varprojlim_{i} X_{i}\Bigr)(S) = \varprojlim_{i} X_{i}(S).$$
