# Tensor Product of Abelian Sheaves　`def.tensor-abelian-sheaf`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=def.tensor-abelian-sheaf)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./def.tensor-abelian-sheaf.md)

$$\operatorname{Hom}_{\mathbb{Z}}\bigl(M \otimes_{\mathbb{Z}} N,\ P\bigr) \;\cong\; \operatorname{Hom}_{\mathbb{Z}}\bigl(M,\ \operatorname{Hom}_{\mathbb{Z}}(N, P)\bigr)$$

in other words $\mathcal{T}(\mathbf{Ab})$ is a **closed symmetric monoidal** category: for fixed $N$, $M \mapsto M \otimes_{\mathbb{Z}} N$ is left adjoint to $P \mapsto \operatorname{Hom}_{\mathbb{Z}}(N, P)$.
