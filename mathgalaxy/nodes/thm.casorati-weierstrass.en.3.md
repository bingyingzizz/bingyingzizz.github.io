# Casorati–Weierstrass　`thm.casorati-weierstrass`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=thm.casorati-weierstrass)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./thm.casorati-weierstrass.md)

Hence near $z_{0}$ the behaviour of $f = w_{0} + 1/F$ can only be: $F(z_{0}) \ne 0$ $\implies$ $f$ is holomorphic; $F$ has a zero of order $m$ $\implies$ $f$ has a pole of order $m$;

$F \equiv 0$ $\implies$ $f \equiv \infty$ (impossible).

All three contradict "$z_{0}$ is an essential singularity".

∎

⭐ **How to remember it**: among the three types of singularities, a removable singularity is **bounded** nearby, a pole **tends to infinity**, and at an essential singularity **anything can happen** — it scatters the image of every punctured neighbourhood over all of $\mathbb{C}$.
