## Statement · **draft**
Let $H$ be a Hilbert space and $E \subseteq H$ a **closed** subspace. Then

1. $y = P_E(x) \iff y \in E$ and $x - y \in E^\perp$;
2. $P_E$ is linear, and $\|P_E\| \le 1$ (it is $1$ when $E \ne 0$);
3. Orthogonal decomposition $H = E \oplus E^\perp$, and $\|x\|^2 = \|x - P_E x\|^2 + \|P_E x\|^2$.
