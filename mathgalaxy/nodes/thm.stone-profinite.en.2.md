# Stone duality　`thm.stone-profinite`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=thm.stone-profinite)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./thm.stone-profinite.md)

📌 Thus constructions on Stone spaces can be checked on finite sets and then extended by limits — this device is used repeatedly in the @@topo.stone@@ cluster.

⚠ Do not read "profinite" as "projective object + finite": $\varprojlim$ here means a limit of finite sets, a completely different matter from "projective objects of $\mathbf{CHaus}$" in @@thm.gleason@@ (that is extreme disconnectedness).
