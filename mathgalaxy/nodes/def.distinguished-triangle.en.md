## Statement · **draft**
A **distinguished triangle** in $\mathbf{K}(\mathcal{C})$ is a diagram of the shape

$$K \longrightarrow L \longrightarrow M \longrightarrow K[1]$$

It is called **distinguished** if it is isomorphic in $\mathbf{K}(\mathcal{C})$ to some mapping-cone triangle

$$K \xrightarrow{\ f\ } L \longrightarrow M(f) \longrightarrow K[1]$$
