陈述

设 F:C→SetF : \mathcal{C} \to \mathbf{Set}F:C→Set 是函子，X∈CX \in \mathcal{C}X∈C，并记

hX=Hom⁡C(X,−):C→Seth^{X} = \operatorname{Hom}_{\mathcal{C}}(X, -) : \mathcal{C} \to \mathbf{Set}hX=HomC​(X,−):C→Set

则存在自然双射

Hom⁡(hX,F)  ≅  F(X),α↦αX(1X)\operatorname{Hom}(h^{X}, F) \;\cong\; F(X), \qquad \alpha \mapsto \alpha_{X}(1_{X})Hom(hX,F)≅F(X),α↦αX​(1X​)

即「全体自然变换」与「F(X)F(X)F(X) 的元素」一一对应。

说明

这个双射在两边都自然：对 FFF（自然变换）自然，对 XXX（态射）也自然。两头都自然，才是这条引理真正有分量的地方。

它把「hXh^{X}hX 到 FFF 的所有自然变换」这个原则上无从下手的集合，压成了 F(X)F(X)F(X) 里的一个元素。后面凡是遇到「自然变换不好数」的地方，基本都是靠这一步救场。

对偶形式：TTT 是预层（T:Cop→SetT : \mathcal{C}^{\mathrm{op}} \to \mathbf{Set}T:Cop→Set）、hX=Hom⁡(−,X)h_{X} = \operatorname{Hom}(-, X)hX​=Hom(−,X) 时可表示预层时，同样有 Hom⁡(hX,T)≅T(X)\operatorname{Hom}(h_{X}, T) \cong T(X)Hom(hX​,T)≅T(X)。

两式只是把 C\mathcal{C}C 换成 Cop\mathcal{C}^{\mathrm{op}}Cop，所以后面的推导两套都用。

Statement draft

Let F:C→SetF : \mathcal{C} \to \mathbf{Set}F:C→Set be a functor, X∈CX \in \mathcal{C}X∈C, and write

hX=Hom⁡C(X,−):C→Seth^{X} = \operatorname{Hom}_{\mathcal{C}}(X, -) : \mathcal{C} \to \mathbf{Set}hX=HomC​(X,−):C→Set

Then there is a natural bijection

Hom⁡(hX,F)  ≅  F(X),α↦αX(1X)\operatorname{Hom}(h^{X}, F) \;\cong\; F(X), \qquad \alpha \mapsto \alpha_{X}(1_{X})Hom(hX,F)≅F(X),α↦αX​(1X​)

that is, "all natural transformations" correspond one-to-one with "elements of F(X)F(X)F(X)".

Notes draft

Both sides of this bijection are natural: it is natural in FFF (natural transformations) and also in XXX (morphisms). Naturality in both variables is what gives the lemma its real strength.

It turns the set of "all natural transformations from hXh^{X}hX to FFF", which is a priori hopeless, into a single element of F(X)F(X)F(X). Whenever "natural transformations are hard to count", this is the step that saves the day.

The dual form: when TTT is a presheaf (T:Cop→SetT : \mathcal{C}^{\mathrm{op}} \to \mathbf{Set}T:Cop→Set) and hX=Hom⁡(−,X)h_{X} = \operatorname{Hom}(-, X)hX​=Hom(−,X) is a representable presheaf, one likewise has Hom⁡(hX,T)≅T(X)\operatorname{Hom}(h_{X}, T) \cong T(X)Hom(hX​,T)≅T(X).

The two formulas differ only by replacing C\mathcal{C}C with Cop\mathcal{C}^{\mathrm{op}}Cop, so both versions are used below.
