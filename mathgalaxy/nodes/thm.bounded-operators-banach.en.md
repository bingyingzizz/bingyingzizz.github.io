## Statement · **draft**
Let $E$ be normed and $F$ Banach. Then $(B(E, F), \|\cdot\|)$ is a Banach space. In particular, the dual space $E^* = B(E, \mathbb{K})$ is **always** Banach (regardless of whether $E$ is complete).
