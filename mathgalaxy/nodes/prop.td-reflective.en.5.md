# totally disconnected spaces are a reflective subcategory　`prop.td-reflective`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=prop.td-reflective)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./prop.td-reflective.md)

- **Universal property**: given $f : X \to Y$ ($Y$ totally disconnected). A continuous map sends connected sets to connected sets, and the only connected subsets of $Y$ are points, so $f$ is constant on each connected component, hence $f$ factors uniquely through $X\to\pi_{0}(X)$ ✓.
The two directions are mutually inverse, so $\pi_{0}\dashv i$ ✓, i.e. $\mathbf{Top}_{\mathrm{td}}$ is a reflective subcategory.
**"$X\to\pi_{0}(X)$ is an isomorphism"** $\iff$ each component is a point $\iff$ $X$ totally disconnected ✓. ∎
