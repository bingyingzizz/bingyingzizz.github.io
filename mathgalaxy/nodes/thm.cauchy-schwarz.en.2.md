# Cauchy–Schwarz inequality　`thm.cauchy-schwarz`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=thm.cauchy-schwarz)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./thm.cauchy-schwarz.md)

⭐ Proof trick: for every $t \in K$ set $f(t) := \|u - t v\|^{2} \ge 0$; after expansion this is a quadratic expression in $t$;

taking $t = \langle v, u \rangle / \|v\|^{2}$ gives the conclusion.

⭐ It is one of the most used inequalities in analysis: on $L^{2}$ it is $|\int f\bar{g}| \le \|f\|_{2}\|g\|_{2}$, and in probability theory it is $|\operatorname{Cov}| \le \sigma_X\sigma_Y$.

It is the same inequality as the "Cauchy–Schwarz for sequences" (the case $p = q = 2$ of Hölder in the analysis.lp cluster).
