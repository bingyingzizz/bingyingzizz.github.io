## Notes · **draft**
⭐ **This is exactly "the exponential law holds trivially for a discrete exponent"**: the product topology on $Y^{X}$ is the compact-open topology. This also shows the **information content** of the compact-open topology: on a non-discrete $X$ the product topology is too fine (it is the "pointwise convergence" topology); the compact-open topology is the right one.
