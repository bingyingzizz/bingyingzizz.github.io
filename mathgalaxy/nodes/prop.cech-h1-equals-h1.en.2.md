# First Čech Cohomology Equals First Cohomology　`prop.cech-h1-equals-h1`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=prop.cech-h1-equals-h1)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./prop.cech-h1-equals-h1.md)

$$E^{p,q}_{2} = \check{H}^{p}\bigl(X,\ H^{q}(M)\bigr) \implies H^{p+q}(X, M) ✓$$

By "higher cohomology presheaves have no Čech cohomology", the whole block with $q \ne 0$ vanishes ✓, in particular $E^{0,1}_{2} = 0$.

Then the five-term exact sequence of the first-quadrant spectral sequence gives

$$0 \to E^{1,0}_{2} \to H^{1} \to E^{0,1}_{2} \to \cdots$$

the last term is zero, so $E^{1,0}_{2} \cong H^{1}$ ✓; and $E^{1,0}_{2} = \check{H}^{1}(X, H^{0}(M)) = \check{H}^{1}(X, M)$ ✓ ($H^{0}(M) = M$). ∎
