# Refinement of the Embedding Theorem　`prop.mitchell-exactness`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=prop.mitchell-exactness)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./prop.mitchell-exactness.md)

⚠️ **Do not overpromise**: $E$ in general does **not** preserve infinite coproducts and infinite products (unless one adds a completeness condition such as AB4). So "constructions built with coproducts" (for example a free resolution of a generator) cannot be transported unconditionally.
