# Skyscraper Sheaf　`def.skyscraper-sheaf`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=def.skyscraper-sheaf)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./def.skyscraper-sheaf.md)

**Two structural properties**:
- $i_{x,*}$ is a **pushforward**, and together with $i_{x}^{-1}$ (which in the one-point case is taking the stalk) it forms an adjunction $i_{x}^{-1} \dashv i_{x,*}$;
- $i_{x,*}$ is exact and faithful: on $\{x\}$ there are only two open sets, $\{x\}$ and $\emptyset$, so there is no exactness to break.

**Use**: skyscraper sheaves are the raw material for **building injective sheaves** — the standard proof that "the category of sheaves has enough injectives" is "prop up each stalk by an injective group, then take the direct product", see "enough injective sheaves".
