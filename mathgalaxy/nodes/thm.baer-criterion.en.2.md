# Baer's Criterion　`thm.baer-criterion`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=thm.baer-criterion)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./thm.baer-criterion.md)

In other words: to test injectivity, the case "$A \rightarrowtail B$" may be restricted to "the inclusion of an ideal $J \rightarrowtail R$".

## Notes · **draft**
**Necessity**: $J \rightarrowtail R$ is itself injective, so injectivity directly gives an extension $R \to I$ ✓.

**Sufficiency**: given an injection $A \subseteq B$ and $f : A \to I$.

Consider all extension pairs $(A_{1}, f_{1})$ ($A \subseteq A_{1} \subseteq B$, $f_{1}$ agrees with $f$ on $A$), ordered by "larger domain".

The union of a chain is again an extension, so by **Zorn's lemma** there is a maximal element $(A_{1}, f_{1})$.
