# Projective Dimension and the Vanishing of Ext　`prop.pd-vanishing`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=prop.pd-vanishing)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./prop.pd-vanishing.md)

⚠️ **The case $n = 0$**: $\mathrm{pd} M \le 0$ $\iff$ $M$ is projective $\iff$ $\operatorname{Ext}^{k}(M,-) = 0$ for all $k \ge 1$. The assertion that $\operatorname{Ext}^{1} = 0$ implies projectivity holds for arbitrary $n$: only the case $n = 0$ (that is, $\operatorname{Ext}^{1}$) behaves this way.
