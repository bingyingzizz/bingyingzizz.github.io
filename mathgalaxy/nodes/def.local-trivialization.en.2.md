# Local Trivialization　`def.local-trivialization`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=def.local-trivialization)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./def.local-trivialization.md)

Let $\pi : E\to B$ be a continuous surjection, $F$ a topological space and $U\subseteq B$ an open set. A continuous map

$$\Phi : \pi^{-1}(U)\longrightarrow U\times F$$

is called a **local trivialization** of $\pi$ over $U$ if $\Phi$ is a homeomorphism and commutes with the projection:

$$\operatorname{pr}_{1}\circ\Phi = \pi .$$
