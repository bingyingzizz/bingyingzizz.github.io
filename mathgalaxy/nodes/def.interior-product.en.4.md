# interior product　`def.interior-product`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=def.interior-product)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./def.interior-product.md)

It is called the **interior product**, but it is **not** the same thing as $\langle\cdot,\cdot\rangle$ in an inner product space: the former eats "one vector $+$ and one form" and returns a form of one lower degree, the latter eats "two vectors" and returns a number.

## Notes · **draft**
**Why it is "pointwise" while the Lie derivative is "global"**: the interior product is computed from $X_{p}$ alone, yielding $(\iota_{X}\omega)_{p}$, so it is a tensor operation (linear in $X$ over $C^{\infty}$) and requires solving no equation;
