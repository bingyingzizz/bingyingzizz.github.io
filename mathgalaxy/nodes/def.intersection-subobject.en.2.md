# intersection of subobjects　`def.intersection-subobject`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=def.intersection-subobject)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./def.intersection-subobject.md)

together with the projection to $X$ (if the fibred product exists). It is universal among subobjects "contained in both $Y$ and $Z$": every $T \to X$ that factors through both monomorphisms factors uniquely through $Y \cap Z \to X$.

In $\mathbf{Set}$, the intersection is the set-theoretic intersection $Y \cap Z$; for preimages along a morphism see @@def.preimage-subobject@@.
