## Notes · **draft**
Intuition: approximate $f$ from **below** by simple functions and take the **supremum** of their integrals. This differs from the Jordan measure recipe of “covering from outside and filling from inside” — here we only fill from inside.

Why no “outer cover” is required: the measure already sits on the dual side ($\mu$ itself is defined from outside via an outer measure), so taking an infimum over simple functions would only make the integral too small.
