# Dimension Shift　`prop.dimension-shift`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=prop.dimension-shift)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./prop.dimension-shift.md)

⭐ **Why it is called a shift**: it trades "the invariant of $M$ in degree $n+1$" for "the invariant of its syzygy in degree $n$".

Thus any statement about $\operatorname{Ext}^{n}$ can be **lowered step by step** until it lands on $\operatorname{Ext}^{0} = \operatorname{Hom}$ or $\operatorname{Ext}^{1}$ — the only levels where elements are visible.

⚠️ The hypothesis "$P$ projective" cannot be dropped: the proof of $\operatorname{Ext}^{n+1}(M,N)\cong\operatorname{Ext}^n(K,N)$ uses exactly the vanishing of the $P$ term. For a general short exact sequence there is only a **five-term** long exact sequence, not an isomorphism.
