Statement

  1. If XXX is weakly Hausdorff, then the diagonal X⊆X×XX \subseteq X \times XX⊆X×X is kkk-closed; when XXX is compactly generated the converse holds too;
  2. if YYY is weakly Hausdorff and f,g:X→Yf, g : X \to Yf,g:X→Y continuous, then ker⁡(f,g)⊆X\ker(f, g) \subseteq Xker(f,g)⊆X is kkk-closed.

Notes

Proof. (1) Given a compact Hausdorff SSS and a continuous h:S→X×Xh : S \to X \times Xh:S→X×X, SSS is compact Hausdorff, so the two components of hhh send SSS into compact pieces; weak Hausdorffness says images of compact pieces are closed ✓, so h−1(X)h^{-1}(X)h−1(X) is the preimage of the intersection of two closed sets, hence closed ✓ — that is, XXX sits in X×XX\times XX×X and is closed in the kkk-topology ✓.

Conversely (when compactly generated), read off weak Hausdorffness from "kkk-closed   ⟺  \iff⟺ closed intersection with every compact piece" ✓.

(2) ker⁡(f,g)\ker(f,g)ker(f,g) is the preimage of the diagonal along the continuous map (f,g):X→Y×Y(f,g) : X \to Y \times Y(f,g):X→Y×Y ✓, and kkk-closedness is preserved under preimages ✓.

∎
