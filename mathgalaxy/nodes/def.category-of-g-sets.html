又称G-集范畴·G-Set

陈述

GGG-集范畴 G-SetG\text{-}\mathbf{Set}G-Set：对象是左 GGG-集，态射是等变映射。终对象是单点集（平凡作用）；积取对角作用；等化子是不动点子集；拉回是纤维积。

始对象是空集；余积是不交并；余等化子是商 GGG-集（生成等价关系的闭包）；推出是粘合。

说明

完备且余完备，且是笛卡尔闭的：YXY^XYX 取共轭作用 (g⋅f)(x)=gf(g−1x)(g\cdot f)(x)=g f(g^{-1}x)(g⋅f)(x)=gf(g−1x)，求值与 curry 逐点验证。

是拓扑斯：等价于单对象群胚上的预层范畴，见 拓扑斯。

非加性；自由 GGG-集是 G×XG\times XG×X（左乘作用）。

Statement draft

The category of GGG-sets G-SetG\text{-}\mathbf{Set}G-Set: objects are left GGG-sets and morphisms are equivariant maps. The terminal object is a one-point set (with the trivial action); products carry the diagonal action; equalizers are fixed-point subsets; pullbacks are fiber products.

The initial object is the empty set; coproducts are disjoint unions; coequalizers are quotient GGG-sets (the closure of the generated equivalence relation); pushouts are gluings.

Notes draft

Complete and cocomplete, and cartesian closed: YXY^XYX carries the conjugation action (g⋅f)(x)=gf(g−1x)(g\cdot f)(x)=g f(g^{-1}x)(g⋅f)(x)=gf(g−1x), and evaluation and currying are checked pointwise.

It is a topos: it is equivalent to the category of presheaves on the one-object groupoid, see @@def.topos@@.

Not additive; the free GGG-set is G×XG\times XG×X (with the left-multiplication action).
