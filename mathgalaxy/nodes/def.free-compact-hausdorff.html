又称自由紧 Hausdorff 空间·free compact Hausdorff·FCHaus·Definition 2.2.1·Def 2.2.1·Exercise 2.11

陈述

自由紧 Hausdorff 空间就是某个离散空间 III 的 Stone–Čech 紧化 F≅βIF \cong \beta IF≅βI。称 III 是 FFF 的一组基：此时

Hom⁡CHaus(F,S)  ≅  Hom⁡Top(I,S)  =  SI\operatorname{Hom}_{\mathbf{CHaus}}(F, S) \;\cong\; \operatorname{Hom}_{\mathbf{Top}}(I, S) \;=\; S^{I}HomCHaus​(F,S)≅HomTop​(I,S)=SI

即连续映射 F→SF \to SF→S 与 SSS 中一族点 (ti)i∈I(t_{i})_{i \in I}(ti​)i∈I​ 一一对应。这族点使 F→SF \to SF→S 成为满射时，叫 FFF 的生成元。

说明

「自由」两个字在这里的含义和自由群、自由模完全一样：在 III 上没有任何约束（III 是离散的，所以从 III 出发的映射随便给点就是连续的），于是「FFF 到别处的映射」与「III 上的一族点」是一回事。

Statement draft

The free compact Hausdorff space on a discrete space III is its Stone–Čech compactification F≅βIF \cong \beta IF≅βI. One calls III a set of generators of FFF: in this case

Hom⁡CHaus(F,S)  ≅  Hom⁡Top(I,S)  =  SI\operatorname{Hom}_{\mathbf{CHaus}}(F, S) \;\cong\; \operatorname{Hom}_{\mathbf{Top}}(I, S) \;=\; S^{I}HomCHaus​(F,S)≅HomTop​(I,S)=SI

that is, continuous maps F→SF \to SF→S correspond bijectively to families of points in SSS, say (ti)i∈I(t_{i})_{i \in I}(ti​)i∈I​. When this family makes F→SF \to SF→S surjective, it is called a set of generators of FFF.

Notes draft

The word "free" means here exactly what it means for free groups and free modules: there are no constraints on III (III is discrete, so a map out of III is continuous as soon as one specifies points arbitrarily), hence "maps from FFF to elsewhere" and "families of points in III" are the same thing.
