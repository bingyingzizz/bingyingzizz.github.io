prop.sheafify-equivalence

Machine-drafted and pending author review. The Chinese text is authoritative.

Statement

If RRR is an equivalence relation on a presheaf TTT, then after sheafification R♯R^{\sharp}R♯ is an equivalence relation on T♯T^{\sharp}T♯, and

(T/R)♯  ≅  T♯/R♯\bigl(T/R\bigr)^{\sharp} \;\cong\; T^{\sharp} / R^{\sharp}(T/R)♯≅T♯/R♯

Notes

"Taking the quotient first and then sheafifying" gives the same result as "sheafifying first and then taking the quotient" — this kind of commutativity is the concrete face of "sheafification preserves colimits" (a quotient is a coequalizer, hence a colimit).
