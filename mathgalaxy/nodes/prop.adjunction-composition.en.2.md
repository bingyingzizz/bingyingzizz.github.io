# Composition of Adjunctions　`prop.adjunction-composition`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=prop.adjunction-composition)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./prop.adjunction-composition.md)

$$\operatorname{Hom}_{\mathcal{E}}\bigl(F_{2}F_{1}X,\ Z\bigr) \cong \operatorname{Hom}_{\mathcal{D}}\bigl(F_{1}X,\ G_{2}Z\bigr) \cong \operatorname{Hom}_{\mathcal{C}}\bigl(X,\ G_{1}G_{2}Z\bigr) ✓$$

Both steps use the definition of adjunction, and the family of isomorphisms is natural in $X, Z$ (a composite of two natural isomorphisms is natural) ✓. ∎
