# Symmetry of the Second Fundamental Form　`lem.second-form-symmetric`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=lem.second-form-symmetric)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./lem.second-form-symmetric.md)

$$II = \sum_{i,j=1}^{2}h_{ij}\,\omega^{i}\omega^{j} = h_{11}(\omega^{1})^{2}+2h_{12}\omega^{1}\omega^{2}+h_{22}(\omega^{2})^{2}$$

is a symmetric bilinear form ($\omega^{i}\omega^{j}$ denotes the symmetric product).

## Notes · **draft**
**The proof is one line**: substitute $\mathrm{d}\omega^{3} = 0$ into the first structure equation, obtaining $(c_{12}-c_{21})\,\omega^{1}\wedge\omega^{2} = 0$. See the incoming edge.
