## Statement · **draft**
Let $\mathcal{A}$ be an abelian category. A (**decreasing**) **filtration** on $M \in \mathcal{A}$ is a diagram of subobjects of $M$ indexed by $(\mathbb{Z}, \ge)$

$$\cdots \supseteq F^{n}M \supseteq F^{n+1}M \supseteq \cdots \supseteq M$$
