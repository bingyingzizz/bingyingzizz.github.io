# Schanuel's Lemma　`lem.schanuel`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=lem.schanuel)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./lem.schanuel.md)

⚠️ The conclusion is an **isomorphism of direct sums**. The modules $K$ and $K_{1}$ need not be isomorphic. To remove this ambiguity, work in the **stable category**, where objects that differ by projective direct summands are identified up to isomorphism.

**Name.** This is Schanuel's lemma. It first appeared in Schanuel's unpublished lecture notes and became widely known through texts by Rotman, Cartan–Eilenberg, and others.
