## Notes · **draft**
**1 and 2 are the same sentence** (right exactness is free). **2 and 3**: take a projective resolution of $M$; $\operatorname{Tor}_{1}$ measures exactly the injectivity lost after tensoring.

**3 implies 4**: from $0 \to I \to R \to R/I \to 0$ and $\operatorname{Tor}_{1}^{R}(R, N) = 0$ one gets the long exact sequence

$$0 \longrightarrow \operatorname{Tor}_{1}^{R}(R/I, N) \longrightarrow I \otimes N \longrightarrow N,$$

so $\operatorname{Tor}_{1}^{R}(R/I, N) = 0$ forces $I \otimes N \to N$ injective ✓.
