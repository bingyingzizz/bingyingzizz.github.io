# row rank equals column rank　`thm.rank-row-col`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=thm.rank-row-col)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./thm.rank-row-col.md)

$$\operatorname{rank}_{\text{row}} A = \operatorname{rank}_{\text{col}} A = \operatorname{rank} A$$

where $\operatorname{rank} A$ is the rank of the linear map $x \mapsto Ax$ (see "rank").

## Notes · **draft**
⭐ "Row rank = column rank" is not obvious: it says that in $K^{n}$ the $m$ row vectors and in $K^{m}$ the $n$ column vectors are **exactly equally independent**, and this is precisely why "the rank of a matrix" is a single number.
