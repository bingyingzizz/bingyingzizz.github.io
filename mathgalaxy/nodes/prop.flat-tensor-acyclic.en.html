Statement

Let PPP be a flat abelian group in a topos and MMM an acyclic complex (bounded below, with homology vanishing for n≠0n \ne 0n=0). Then P⊗ZMP \otimes_{\mathbb{Z}} MP⊗Z​M is also acyclic.

Notes

Proof. Flatness says exactly that P⊗Z−P \otimes_{\mathbb{Z}} -P⊗Z​− is exact ✓. A "bounded below + acyclic" complex is an exact sequence ✓, and an exact functor sends exact sequences to exact sequences, so P⊗MP \otimes MP⊗M is also exact, that is, acyclic ✓. ∎
