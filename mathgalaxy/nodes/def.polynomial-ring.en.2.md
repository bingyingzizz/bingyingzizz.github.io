# polynomial ring　`def.polynomial-ring`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=def.polynomial-ring)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./def.polynomial-ring.md)

$$\deg(fg) = \deg f + \deg g \qquad (f, g \ne 0)$$

Hence $K[x]$ is an **integral domain** (no zero divisors): $fg = 0 \implies f = 0$ or $g = 0$.

## Notes · **stale**
⭐ $K[x]$ is the value of the “univariate polynomial functor on $K$”: $K \mapsto K[x]$ is left adjoint to “sending $K$ to $K$-algebras” (the free $K$-algebra).

(In the category of $k$-algebras the free object is the tensor algebra.)algebra-categories` already mentioned $k\text{-}\mathbf{Alg}$.

⭐ $\deg(fg) = \deg f + \deg g$ is the whole source of integrality: integrality of the coefficient field makes degrees add, so there are no zero divisors.
