## Statement · **draft**
Let $p : (E, e_{0}) \to (B, b_{0})$ be a covering map with $E, B$ path-connected and locally path-connected. Then

1. **Injectivity**: $p_{*} : \pi_{1}(E, e_{0}) \to \pi_{1}(B, b_{0})$ is injective;
2. **Fibre = cosets**: there is a bijection

$$p^{-1}(b_{0}) \;\longleftrightarrow\; \pi_{1}(B, b_{0}) \big/ p_{*}\pi_{1}(E, e_{0}),$$
