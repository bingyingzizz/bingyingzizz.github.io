⭐ The three “quotients of V⊗∙V^{\otimes \bullet}V⊗∙” form a complete table:

  • no quotient:   ⟹  \implies⟹ the tensor algebra T∙VT^{\bullet}VT∙V (noncommutative, free);

  • quotient by vw+wvvw + wvvw+wv   ⟹  \implies⟹ the exterior algebra ⋀∙V\bigwedge^{\bullet}V⋀∙V (graded-commutative).

⭐ «Sym⁡∙V≅K[x1,…,xn]\operatorname{Sym}^{\bullet}V \cong K[x_1,\ldots,x_n]Sym∙V≅K[x1​,…,xn​]» shows that the polynomial ring is not conjured from nothing — it is the symmetric algebra of an nnn-dimensional space.

This explains why polynomials and multilinear forms are so tightly linked (and leads back to polynomial ring).

⭐ The classification of bilinear forms (symmetric / alternating) is the algebraic shadow of these two quotients.
