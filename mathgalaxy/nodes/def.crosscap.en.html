def.crosscap

Machine-drafted and pending author review. The Chinese text is authoritative.

Statement

Let SSS be a closed surface. Remove an open disk from SSS and identify antipodal points on the boundary circle (x∼−xx\sim -xx∼−x). The resulting closed surface is obtained by adding a crosscap to SSS. Equivalently, adding a crosscap means taking the connected sum with a projective plane: S↦S#RP2S\mapsto S\#\mathbb{R}P^{2}S↦S#RP2. It is also equivalent to gluing a Möbius band along the boundary of the removed disk.

Notes

Effect: Adding a crosscap decreases the Euler characteristic by 111 and makes the surface nonorientable. Adding k≥1k\ge1k≥1 crosscaps to a sphere yields all closed nonorientable surfaces (classification theorem).

Examples: k=1k=1k=1 is the projective plane. k=2k=2k=2 is the Klein bottle.

⚠ Antipodal identification changes the local structure near the boundary and gives a nonorientable surface. Gluing the disk back directly would restore the original surface.
