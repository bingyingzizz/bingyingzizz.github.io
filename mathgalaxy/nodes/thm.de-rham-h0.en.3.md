# Zeroth de Rham Cohomology　`thm.de-rham-h0`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=thm.de-rham-h0)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./thm.de-rham-h0.md)

**The two steps of the proof**: ① $\mathrm{d}f = 0$ if and only if $f$ is constant on each connected component (a component is connected, any two points can be joined by a chain of charts, and adjacent charts agree on the constant on their connected overlap); ② $B^{0} = 0$, since there are no $-1$-forms, nothing can be quotiented out. Hence $H^{0}$ is isomorphic to the space of locally constant functions, whose dimension is exactly the number of components. Details are on the edge.
