# Liouville's Formula　`thm.liouville-formula`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=thm.liouville-formula)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./thm.liouville-formula.md)

⚠️ A common error is to simplify the formula to $\frac{\mathrm{d}\theta}{\mathrm{d}s}+(k_{1}-k_{2})\sin\theta\cos\theta$ in curvature-line coordinates.

The sphere gives a counterexample. On a circle of latitude, $\theta\equiv\frac{\pi}{2}$ and $\frac{\mathrm{d}\theta}{\mathrm{d}s}\equiv 0$, while $k_{g} = \cot\rho_{0}\ne0$.

On the sphere, $k_{1} = k_{2} = 1$ also makes both terms on the right-hand side vanish.

**This simplified formula is invalid. Do not use it.**
