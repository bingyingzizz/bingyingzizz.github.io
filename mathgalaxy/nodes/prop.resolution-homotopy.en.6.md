# Homotopy Uniqueness of Projective Resolutions　`prop.resolution-homotopy`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=prop.resolution-homotopy)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./prop.resolution-homotopy.md)

This gives another projective resolution of $M$, visibly not isomorphic to $P_{\bullet}$ (every term has doubled), yet homotopy equivalent ✓.

⚠️ **Dual case**: injective resolutions satisfy the same conclusion (apply the comparison theorem on the other side). Together with "lifts between injective resolutions", this is the entire basis for "compute derived functors from resolutions on either side".
