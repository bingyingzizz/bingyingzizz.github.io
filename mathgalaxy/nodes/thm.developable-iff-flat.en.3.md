# criterion for developable surfaces　`thm.developable-iff-flat`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=thm.developable-iff-flat)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./thm.developable-iff-flat.md)

- (1) and (2): $N \propto X_u \times X_v = a'\times b + v\,(b'\times b)$; independence of $v$ means $b'\times b$ is parallel to $a'\times b$, equivalently $\det(a',b,b') = 0$.

- (2) and (3): compute directly from $K = \det(h_{ij})/\det(g_{ij})$; on a ruled surface $\det(h_{ij})$ and $\det(a',b,b')^{2}$ differ by a **sign** (not necessarily a positive factor: $X = (u,v,uv)$ gives $\det h = -1$, while $\det(a',b,b')^{2} > 0$) — but "being zero" is unaffected by the sign, so $K \equiv 0$ is still equivalent to the determinant vanishing identically. Only the second fundamental form is used.
