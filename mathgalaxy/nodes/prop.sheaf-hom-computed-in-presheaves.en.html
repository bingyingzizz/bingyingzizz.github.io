Statement

Let C\mathcal{C}C be a site, TTT a presheaf, and FFF a sheaf. Then

Hom⁡C~(T#, F)  =  Hom⁡C^(T, F).\operatorname{Hom}_{\widetilde{\mathcal{C}}}\bigl(T^{\#},\ F\bigr) \;=\; \operatorname{Hom}_{\widehat{\mathcal{C}}}\bigl(T,\ F\bigr).HomC​(T#, F)=HomC​(T, F).

Notes

Proof. Sheafification (−)#(-)^{\#}(−)# and the inclusion form an adjoint pair ✓, hence

Hom⁡C~(T#,F)=Hom⁡C^(T, F)✓\operatorname{Hom}_{\widetilde{\mathcal{C}}}\bigl(T^{\#}, F\bigr) = \operatorname{Hom}_{\widehat{\mathcal{C}}}\bigl(T,\ F\bigr) ✓HomC​(T#,F)=HomC​(T, F)✓

(the right side regards FFF as a presheaf) ✓. ∎
