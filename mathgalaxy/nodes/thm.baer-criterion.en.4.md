# Baer's Criterion　`thm.baer-criterion`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=thm.baer-criterion)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./thm.baer-criterion.md)

⭐ **Meaning**: it compresses "test against all injections" into "test against ideals of $R$" — and ideals are subsets of $R$, usually far fewer. This step is the key to translating a categorical condition into a condition on the ring; `thm.pid-injective-divisible` depends entirely on it.

⚠ The "ideal" in the criterion cannot be replaced by "finitely generated ideal": flatness has Lambek's theorem allowing only finitely generated ideals to be checked, but injectivity has **no** such dual weakening (unless the ring is Noetherian).
