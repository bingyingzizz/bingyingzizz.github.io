# Splitting Principle　`thm.splitting-principle`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=thm.splitting-principle)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./thm.splitting-principle.md)

**Where the cost is**: $\tilde B$ is usually **much larger than** $B$ (the fibre of the flag bundle is $\mathrm{GL}(k)/$ (upper triangular), of real dimension $\sim k^{2}$), and one has to do **fibre integration** (pushing the identity back down along $\pi$ to $B$). So the splitting principle is a "tool for proof", not a "method of computation": to actually compute a concrete $c_{j}$ one still has to return to the curvature or to line bundles.
