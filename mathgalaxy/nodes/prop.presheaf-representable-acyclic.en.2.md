# Representable Presheaves Are Acyclic　`prop.presheaf-representable-acyclic`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=prop.presheaf-representable-acyclic)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./prop.presheaf-representable-acyclic.md)

$$H^{n}\bigl(h_{X},\ M\bigr) = 0 \qquad (n > 0).$$

## Notes · **draft**
⭐ **Why this deserves its own statement**: because it is the best explanation of **why sheafification is necessary** — at the presheaf level, representable objects have no higher cohomology at all (everything is exact); once one sheafifies, $\mathbb{Z}\cdot X$ is no longer the "evaluation" functor, and higher terms appear. The nontriviality of sheaf cohomology comes entirely from the fact that **exactness is broken in the category of sheaves**.
