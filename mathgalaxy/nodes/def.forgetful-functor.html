陈述

遗忘函子是把结构「忘掉」一部分、只保留底层数据的函子：对象上 (X,structure)↦X(X,\text{structure})\mapsto X(X,structure)↦X，态射上保持同一个底层映射。

典型例子：

  • Top→Set\mathbf{Top}\to\mathbf{Set}Top→Set：忘掉开集，只留底层集合与函数；
  • Grp→Set\mathbf{Grp}\to\mathbf{Set}Grp→Set：忘掉群运算与单位元；
  • VectK→Set\mathbf{Vect}_K\to\mathbf{Set}VectK​→Set：忘掉加法与数乘；
  • VectK→Ab\mathbf{Vect}_K\to\mathbf{Ab}VectK​→Ab：忘掉 KKK-数乘，只留加法群。

严格说，遗忘函子不是单一函子，而是一类具体范畴（带忠实函子到 Set\mathbf{Set}Set 的范畴）到 Set\mathbf{Set}Set 的标准函子。

说明

忠实但通常不满：底层映射相同不保证保持结构；Top→Set\mathbf{Top}\to\mathbf{Set}Top→Set、Ab→Set\mathbf{Ab}\to\mathbf{Set}Ab→Set 都忠实而不满。

左伴随是自由构造：自由群、自由向量空间分别是相应遗忘函子的左伴随；Top→Set\mathbf{Top}\to\mathbf{Set}Top→Set 的左伴随是密着拓扑（indiscrete，只含 ∅\emptyset∅ 与全集），右伴随才是离散拓扑 —— 两侧方向不能对调。

极限与余极限：代数类遗忘函子（Mon\mathbf{Mon}Mon、Gr\mathbf{Gr}Gr、Ab\mathbf{Ab}Ab、A-ModA\text{-}\mathbf{Mod}A-Mod 等）保所有极限（逐点构造），但一般不保余极限；拓扑类行为不同 —— Top→Set\mathbf{Top}\to\mathbf{Set}Top→Set 反而保所有余极限（商拓扑逐点拼出）。所以「保不保」要按具体范畴看，不能一概而论。

Statement stale

A forgetful functor is a functor that "forgets" part of an algebraic structure and keeps only the underlying data: on objects (X,structure)↦X(X,\text{structure})\mapsto X(X,structure)↦X, and on morphisms it keeps the same underlying map.

Typical examples:

  • Top→Set\mathbf{Top}\to\mathbf{Set}Top→Set: forgets the open sets, keeping only the underlying sets and functions;
  • Grp→Set\mathbf{Grp}\to\mathbf{Set}Grp→Set: forgets the group operation and the unit;
  • VectK→Set\mathbf{Vect}_K\to\mathbf{Set}VectK​→Set: forgets addition and scalar multiplication;
  • VectK→Ab\mathbf{Vect}_K\to\mathbf{Ab}VectK​→Ab: forgets the KKK-scalar multiplication, keeping only the additive group.

Strictly speaking, "forgetful functor" is not a single functor but a standard class of functors from concrete categories (categories equipped with a faithful functor to Set\mathbf{Set}Set) to Set\mathbf{Set}Set.

Notes stale

Faithful but usually not full: having the same underlying map does not guarantee preserving structure; Top→Set\mathbf{Top}\to\mathbf{Set}Top→Set and Ab→Set\mathbf{Ab}\to\mathbf{Set}Ab→Set are both faithful and not full.

Left adjoints are free constructions: free groups, free vector spaces, and the discrete topology are the left adjoints of the corresponding forgetful functors.

Preservation of limits: forgetful functors to Set\mathbf{Set}Set usually preserve all limits (constructed pointwise), but in general they do not preserve colimits.
