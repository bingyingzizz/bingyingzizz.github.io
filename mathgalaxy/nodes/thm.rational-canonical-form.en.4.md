# Rational Canonical Form　`thm.rational-canonical-form`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=thm.rational-canonical-form)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./thm.rational-canonical-form.md)

**Division of labor with the Jordan form**: when the characteristic polynomial splits over $K$, both the rational canonical form and the Jordan form are complete invariants; when it does not split, **only** the rational canonical form (over $K$) is available — the Jordan form exists only after passing to an extension field.

## Notes · **draft**
⭐ **Why "rational"**: historically it refers to the case $K = \mathbb{Q}$ — exactly the typical situation in which the characteristic polynomial does not split over $\mathbb{Q}$. The name stuck, and it now means "valid over an arbitrary field".
