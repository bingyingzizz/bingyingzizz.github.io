## Notes · **draft**
⭐ The value of the theorem: it asserts that the limit exists **without knowing who the limit is** — and the proof identifies it along the way (it is the supremum of the sequence).

All of real analysis uses this pattern (series with nonnegative terms, monotone functions, continuity of measure, the prototype of the monotone convergence theorem).

⚠ Neither hypothesis can be dropped: monotone without boundedness diverges ($x_n = n$); bounded without monotonicity may fail to converge ($x_n = (-1)^{n}$).
