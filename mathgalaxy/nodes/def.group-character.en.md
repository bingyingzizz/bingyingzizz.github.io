## Statement · **draft**
Let $(V,\rho)$ be a finite-dimensional representation of a finite group $G$. Its character is the function $\chi_\rho:G\to F$, $\chi_\rho(g)=\operatorname{tr}\rho(g)$. It satisfies $\chi_\rho(1)=\dim V$ and is constant on conjugacy classes.
