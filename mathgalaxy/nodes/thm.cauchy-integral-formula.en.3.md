More generally, if $\gamma$ is a **simple** closed curve in the region and $f$ is holomorphic inside $\gamma$ and on $\gamma$, then the formula holds for every point $z$ inside $\gamma$ (for a general closed curve, write $\operatorname{Ind}(\gamma, z)f(z) = \frac{1}{2\pi i}\oint_\gamma \frac{f(\zeta)}{\zeta - z}\,\mathrm{d}\zeta$).
