## Statement · **draft**
Let $I_n = [a_n, b_n]$ be a sequence of **closed** intervals satisfying

- $I_1 \supseteq I_2 \supseteq \cdots$ (nested),
- $b_n - a_n \to 0$ (the lengths tend to zero).

Then $\bigcap_{n=1}^{\infty} I_n$ contains exactly **one** point.
