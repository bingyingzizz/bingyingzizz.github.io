又称幺半群·monoid

陈述

设 MMM 是集合，⋅\cdot⋅ 是 MMM 上的二元运算，e∈Me\in Me∈M。若结合律 (ab)c=a(bc)(ab)c=a(bc)(ab)c=a(bc) 与单位元 ea=ae=aea=ae=aea=ae=a 对一切 a,b,ca,b,ca,b,c 成立，称 (M,⋅,e)(M,\cdot,e)(M,⋅,e) 为幺半群（monoid）；再满足交换律的称为交换幺半群。同态是保运算与单位的映射。

说明

例：(N,+,0)(\mathbb N,+ ,0)(N,+,0) 与 (N,⋅,1)(\mathbb N,\cdot,1)(N,⋅,1) 都是交换幺半群；字符串在连接下是幺半群；n×nn\times nn×n 矩阵在乘法下是幺半群（n≥2n\ge 2n≥2 时不交换）。

与群的关系：群恰是每个元素都可逆的幺半群；本图把群公理单列在 群，把交换群单列在 阿贝尔群。

与范畴中的幺半群对象分开：幺半群对象 是「对象 + 乘法 + 单位」在一般范畴里的内化，本条只是集合层面。

Statement draft

Let MMM be a set and ⋅\cdot⋅ a binary operation on MMM, with e∈Me\in Me∈M. If associativity (ab)c=a(bc)(ab)c=a(bc)(ab)c=a(bc) and the identity law ea=ae=aea=ae=aea=ae=a hold for all a,b,ca,b,ca,b,c, then (M,⋅,e)(M,\cdot,e)(M,⋅,e) is called a monoid; one that also satisfies the commutative law is a commutative monoid. A homomorphism is a map preserving the operation and the identity.

Notes draft

Examples: (N,+,0)(\mathbb N,+ ,0)(N,+,0) and (N,⋅,1)(\mathbb N,\cdot,1)(N,⋅,1) are commutative monoids; strings form a monoid under concatenation; the n×nn\times nn×n matrices form a monoid under multiplication (non-commutative when n≥2n\ge 2n≥2).

Relation to groups: a group is exactly a monoid in which every element is invertible; the group axioms are given separately in @@def.group@@, and abelian groups in @@def.abelian-group@@.

Kept separate from monoid objects in a category: @@def.monoid-object@@ internalizes “object + multiplication + unit” in a general category; the present entry stays at the level of sets.
