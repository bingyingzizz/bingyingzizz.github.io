# well-formed formula　`def.formula`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=def.formula)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./def.formula.md)

⭐ **Inductive definition = smallest set**: behind this is induction on $\mathbb{N}$ (see "the principle of induction and recursive definitions"). It is also the reason "induction on the complexity of a formula" works — the two are two sides of the same thing.

⚠ **Only some primitive symbols are kept**: $\exists$ is an abbreviation for $\neg \forall \neg$, and $\vee$, $\to$ can also be defined from $\neg, \wedge$. The fewer primitive symbols, the shorter the syntax rules and subsequent proofs, at the cost of more parentheses when writing formulas.
