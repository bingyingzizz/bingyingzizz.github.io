# Completely Regular Space　`def.completely-regular-space`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=def.completely-regular-space)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./def.completely-regular-space.md)

This is the same convention as "$T_{3}$ versus regular space".

⭐ **Why adding $T_{1}$ is not redundant**: without $T_{1}$, $T_{3.5}$ does not even guarantee that points are closed, so $T_{2}$ and uniqueness of limits all fail. Once $T_{1}$ is added, $\{y\}$ is closed, and a continuous function separating $x$ from $\{y\}$ immediately gives disjoint open sets — hence a completely regular space is automatically $T_{2}$.
