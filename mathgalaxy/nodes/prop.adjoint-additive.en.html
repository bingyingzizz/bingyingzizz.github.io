Statement

Let C,D\mathcal{C}, \mathcal{D}C,D be additive categories and F:C→DF : \mathcal{C} \to \mathcal{D}F:C→D left adjoint to G:D→CG : \mathcal{D} \to \mathcal{C}G:D→C (write F⊣GF \dashv GF⊣G). Then FFF and GGG are both additive, and the adjunction bijection

Hom⁡D(FM,N)  ≅  Hom⁡C(M,GN)\operatorname{Hom}_{\mathcal{D}}(FM, N) \;\cong\; \operatorname{Hom}_{\mathcal{C}}(M, GN)HomD​(FM,N)≅HomC​(M,GN)

is an isomorphism of abelian groups (natural in both MMM and NNN).

Notes

⭐ In one sentence: once there is an adjunction, additivity comes for free. So an adjunction between additive categories can always be treated as an additive adjunction, and Hom⁡(FM,N)≅Hom⁡(M,GN)\operatorname{Hom}(FM,N) \cong \operatorname{Hom}(M,GN)Hom(FM,N)≅Hom(M,GN) is automatically upgraded to a group isomorphism.
