# open mapping theorem for Banach spaces　`thm.open-mapping-banach`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=thm.open-mapping-banach)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./thm.open-mapping-banach.md)

**What each hypothesis does.** Completeness of $F$ is used only in Step 1 (for the category theorem); completeness of $E$ only in Step 2 (for convergence of the series). Both are indispensable: with incomplete target $F = (C[0,1], \|\cdot\|_1)$ the identity map $(C[0,1], \|\cdot\|_\infty) \to (C[0,1], \|\cdot\|_1)$ is a continuous bijection but not open (here $E = (C[0,1], \|\cdot\|_\infty)$ is in fact complete) — this identity map is itself the counterexample with $F$ incomplete.
