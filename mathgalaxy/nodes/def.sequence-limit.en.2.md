## Notes · **draft**
⭐ This is the "$\varepsilon$-$N$ language": it turns "getting closer and closer" into a condition that can be checked one step at a time.

Every proof in analysis rests on this format.

Basic properties (all verified directly from the definition):
- the limit is **unique**;

- a convergent sequence is **bounded**;

- limits commute with the four arithmetic operations (when the limit of the denominator is nonzero).

⚠ "Bounded" does not guarantee convergence ($(-1)^n$);

the order "$\varepsilon$ fixed, $N$ pushed later" cannot be reversed.
