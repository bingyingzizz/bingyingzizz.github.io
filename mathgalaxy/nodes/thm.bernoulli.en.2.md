# Bernoulli's inequality　`thm.bernoulli`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=thm.bernoulli)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./thm.bernoulli.md)

In analysis it is used for **lower bounds**: $(1+h)^{n} \ge 1 + nh$, $(1-h)^{n} \ge 1 - nh$ — the second direction of `thm.nth-root` is exactly this.

⚠ The hypothesis $x \ge -1$ is there to make $1+x \ge 0$: the induction step multiplies both sides by $1+x$, and only if it is nonnegative does the inequality not reverse.

For $x < -1$ the statement may fail or lose its induction base.
