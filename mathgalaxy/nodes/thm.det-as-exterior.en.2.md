# the determinant is the action on the top exterior power　`thm.det-as-exterior`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=thm.det-as-exterior)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./thm.det-as-exterior.md)

which must be “multiplication by a number”. That number is exactly the determinant:

$$\bigl( \textstyle\bigwedge^{n} T \bigr)(v_1 \wedge \cdots \wedge v_n) = (\det T)\,(v_1 \wedge \cdots \wedge v_n)$$

Equivalent form: $\det T$ is the eigenvalue of $T$ on the one-dimensional space of the top exterior power.
