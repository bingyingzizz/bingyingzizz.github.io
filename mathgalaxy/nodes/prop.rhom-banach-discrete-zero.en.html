Statement

Let MMM be a finite-dimensional real Banach space and NNN a discrete abelian group. Then

RHom⁡Z(M, N)=0.\operatorname{RHom}_{\mathbb{Z}}(M,\ N) = 0.RHomZ​(M, N)=0.

More generally: if VVV is an arbitrary R\mathbb{R}R-module and NNN is discrete, then RHom⁡Z(V,N)=0\operatorname{RHom}_{\mathbb{Z}}(V, N) = 0RHomZ​(V,N)=0.

Notes

Intuition: R\mathbb{R}R is divisible (R\mathbb{R}R's elements all admit roots of every order, and are divisible by every nnn), while a discrete group has no such divisibility. Divisibility collapses completely in the direction into a discrete group — hence all higher Hom groups (and the zeroth) out of a real vector space vanish.

Proof. Use the spectral sequence above: MsM^{s}Ms (a power of the finite-dimensional MMM) is contractible (all vector spaces are), and Hq(Ms×S,N)≅Hq(S,N)H^{q}(M^{s} \times S, N) \cong H^{q}(S, N)Hq(Ms×S,N)≅Hq(S,N), so E1p,qE^{p,q}_{1}E1p,q​ depends only on SSS; the limit term matches the "constant decomposition" case and the computation vanishes throughout.

⭐ Use: any Ext problem with a real vector space as source and a discrete group as target is immediately zero — one of the key components of the later step "locally compact abelian groups have only Ext⁡0,Ext⁡1\operatorname{Ext}^{0}, \operatorname{Ext}^{1}Ext0,Ext1".

Proof. Use the spectral sequence above (with SSS Stonean); to show the limit vanishes it suffices to show every E1p,qE^{p,q}_{1}E1p,q​ vanishes.

  • Msp,iM^{s_{p,i}}Msp,i​ is a (possibly large) power of a finite-dimensional real vector space, convex   ⟹  \implies⟹ contractible;
  • and Msp,i×S→SM^{s_{p,i}}\times S \to SMsp,i​×S→S is a homotopy equivalence, with Msp,i×SM^{s_{p,i}}\times SMsp,i​×S locally compact Hausdorff and NNN discrete, so Dyckhoff (locally compact with discrete coefficients: condensed = sheaf cohomology) and "sheaf cohomology is a homotopy invariant" give
Hq(Msp,i×S, N)≅Hq(S, N).H^{q}\bigl(M^{s_{p,i}}\times S,\ N\bigr) \cong H^{q}(S,\ N).Hq(Msp,i​×S, N)≅Hq(S, N).
  • The right side involves no MMM at all.

Hence this family of E1E_{1}E1​ terms is independent of MMM: substituting M=0M = 0M=0 gives the same family of E1E_{1}E1​ terms, and RHom⁡Z(0,N)=0\operatorname{RHom}_{\mathbb{Z}}(0, N) = 0RHomZ​(0,N)=0, so the spectral sequence converges to 000;

∎
