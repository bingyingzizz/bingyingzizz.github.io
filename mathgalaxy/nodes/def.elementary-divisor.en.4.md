# Elementary Divisors of a Matrix　`def.elementary-divisor`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=def.elementary-divisor)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./def.elementary-divisor.md)

Example: if the invariant factors are $x-1$ and $(x-1)(x-2)$, the elementary divisors are $x-1,\ x-1,\ x-2$. When $K$ is algebraically closed, the elementary divisors of $xI-A$ are exactly $(x-\lambda)^s$, each corresponding to a Jordan block of size $s$ (@@thm.jordan-canonical-form@@).
