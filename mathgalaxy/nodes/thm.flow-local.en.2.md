# existence of a local flow　`thm.flow-local`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=thm.flow-local)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./thm.flow-local.md)

1. $\varphi_{0} = \operatorname{id}_{U}$;
2. $\dfrac{\partial}{\partial t}\varphi_{t}(q) = X\bigl(\varphi_{t}(q)\bigr)$ for all $t \in (-\varepsilon,\varepsilon)$ and $q \in U$;
3. **Uniqueness**: two integral curves through the same point agree on their common interval of definition;
4. **Local one-parameter group**: whenever $|s|, |t|, |s+t| < \varepsilon$ and all intermediate points lie in $U$, $\varphi_{s+t} = \varphi_{s}\circ\varphi_{t}$.

Moreover, gluing the local flows of the various points along curves yields a unique **maximal flow** (its domain $\mathcal{W} \subseteq \mathbb{R}\times M$ is open; see `def.flow`).
