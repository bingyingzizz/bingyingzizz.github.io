# Banach Groups Are Acyclic on Stone Spaces　`prop.banach-stone-acyclic`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=prop.banach-stone-acyclic)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./prop.banach-stone-acyclic.md)

(1) Choose a surjection of Stone spaces $S_{0} \twoheadrightarrow S$ and use it to compute the Čech complex. (2) The preceding result gives that this complex is $1$-bounded acyclic. (3) The implication “$K$-bounded acyclicity $\implies$ acyclicity” gives ordinary acyclicity. Thus Čech cohomology vanishes, and on a Stone space Čech cohomology agrees with condensed cohomology.
