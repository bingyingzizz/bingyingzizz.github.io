# Injective Dimension　`def.injective-dimension`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=def.injective-dimension)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./def.injective-dimension.md)

⚠ The formula $\operatorname{id}_{R} M = \operatorname{pd}_{R^{\mathrm{op}}} M$ is incorrect. Take $R = \mathbb{Z}$ and $M = \mathbb{Q}$. Then $\mathbb{Q}$ is divisible $\implies$ $\operatorname{id} = 0$, whereas $\operatorname{pd}_{\mathbb{Z}}\mathbb{Q} = 1$ (since $\mathbb{Q}$ is not projective). The two dimensions differ.
