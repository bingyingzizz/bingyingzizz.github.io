# Liouville's Formula　`thm.liouville-formula`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=thm.liouville-formula)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./thm.liouville-formula.md)

**Special case**: If the orthogonal net also uses geodesic coordinates ($E\equiv1$), the formula reduces to $k_{g} = \frac{\mathrm{d}\theta}{\mathrm{d}s}+\frac{(\ln G)_{u}}{2}\sin\theta$. If, in addition, $G$ is independent of $u$, this is the plane polar coordinate case.

⭐ This formula makes it **explicit** that computing $k_{g}$ requires only $I$. The right-hand side involves only $E,G$ and the angle of the curve, with no extrinsic data.
