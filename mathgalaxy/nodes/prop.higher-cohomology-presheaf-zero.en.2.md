# Higher Cohomology Presheaves Sheafify to Zero　`prop.higher-cohomology-presheaf-zero`

[in graph](../#n=prop.higher-cohomology-presheaf-zero)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./prop.higher-cohomology-presheaf-zero.md)

## Notes · **draft**
**Proof.** For $M$ take an injective resolution $I^{\bullet}$ ✓, then $H^{n}(U, M) = H^{n}\Gamma(U, I^{\bullet})$ ✓.

Given a cohomology class $s$, use its representing cocycle $c$ on each piece of some covering: because $I^{\bullet}$, as a complex, is exact at every point, $c$ is **locally a coboundary** ✓ — hence $s$ restricts to $0$ on each piece of the covering ✓, that is, its germ in the associated sheaf is $0$ ✓.

∎
