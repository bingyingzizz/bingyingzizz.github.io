# Klein bottle　`ex.klein-bottle`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=ex.klein-bottle)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./ex.klein-bottle.md)

⚠ **It still cannot embed in $\mathbb{R}^{3}$**. No compact nonorientable surface without boundary embeds in $\mathbb{R}^{3}$. The familiar glass model whose neck passes through the body is an **immersion** with self-intersection.

📌 $H_{1}(K;\mathbb{Z}) \cong \mathbb{Z} \oplus \mathbb{Z}/2$ and $H_{2} = 0$, so $\chi = 1 - 1 + 0 = 0$.

⚠ The Klein bottle is obtained by gluing two **Möbius bands** along their boundaries (`def.mobius-band`).
