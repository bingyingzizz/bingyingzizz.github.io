# quadratic form　`def.quadratic-form`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=def.quadratic-form)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./def.quadratic-form.md)

- **positive definite**: $x \ne 0 \implies q(x) > 0$;
- **positive semidefinite**: $x \ne 0 \implies q(x) \ge 0$;
- **negative definite** / **indefinite** are defined similarly.

By the spectral theorem, $q$ is positive definite $\iff$ every eigenvalue of $A$ is positive $\iff$ there exists an invertible $P$ with $A = P^{\mathsf{T}}P$ (that is, $A$ is congruent to a positive definite matrix).

## Notes · **draft**
⭐ A quadratic form is a **relaxation** of an inner product: an inner product requires positive definiteness, whereas a quadratic form may be indefinite (or even degenerate).
