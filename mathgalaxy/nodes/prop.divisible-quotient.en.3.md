# Quotients of Divisible Groups　`prop.divisible-quotient`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=prop.divisible-quotient)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./prop.divisible-quotient.md)

⭐ Two contrasts: among $\mathbb{Z}$-modules the **quotient** of an injective object is still injective (this item), while its **submodule** need not be ($\mathbb{Z} \subseteq \mathbb{Q}$).

Projective modules behave badly on both sides: both its quotient ($\mathbb{Z}/2$ of $\mathbb{Z}$) and its submodule (the ideal $(x, y) \subseteq k[x, y]$) can fail to be projective.
