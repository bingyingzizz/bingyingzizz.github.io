# Simplicial Method for Sheaf Cohomology　`method.simplicial-cohomology`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=method.simplicial-cohomology)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./method.simplicial-cohomology.md)

**Why it works**: when $F_{n}$ consists of representable objects, the sections $\Gamma(X, F_{n})$ can be written down directly (they are the values of $F_{n}$ on that object). And a map between two resolutions gives a **chain homotopy**, so the resulting homotopy groups do not depend on the choice of resolution.
