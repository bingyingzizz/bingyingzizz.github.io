## Statement · **draft**
A **category** is a sextuple

$$( \mathcal{O}, M, s, t, \circ, i )$$

where $(\mathcal{O}, M, s, t)$ is a **graph**: $\mathcal{O}$ are the **objects**, $M$ the **morphisms**, $s, t : M \to \mathcal{O}$ assigns to each morphism its **source** and **target**, and one writes $f : X \to Y$ to mean $s(f) = X$ and $t(f) = Y$.
