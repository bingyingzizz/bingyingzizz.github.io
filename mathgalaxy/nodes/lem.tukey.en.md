## Statement · **draft**
Let $\mathcal{A}$ be a **non-empty family of sets with finite character**. Then $(\mathcal{A}, \subseteq )$ has a maximal element.

That is, there is $A \in \mathcal{A}$ such that no $B \in \mathcal{A}$ satisfies $A \subset B$.
