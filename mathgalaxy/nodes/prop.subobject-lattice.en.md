## Statement · **draft**
Let $\mathcal{C}$ be a pretopos and $X \in \mathcal{C}$. Then the collection of subobjects $\{X' : X' \rightarrowtail X\}$ forms a **bounded lattice**:

$$X_{1} \cup X_{2} := \operatorname{im}\bigl(X_{1} \coprod X_{2} \to X\bigr)$$
