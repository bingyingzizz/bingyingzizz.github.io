# pre-additive category　`def.pre-additive-category`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=def.pre-additive-category)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./def.pre-additive-category.md)

$$\operatorname{Hom}(M, N) \times \operatorname{Hom}(N, P) \longrightarrow \operatorname{Hom}(M, P)$$

is **bilinear** (additive in one variable when the other is fixed).

## Notes · **draft**
Equivalent description: the functor $\operatorname{Hom}$, with signature $\mathcal{C}^{\mathrm{op}} \times \mathcal{C} \to \mathbf{Ab}$, exists, and after the forgetful functor $\mathbf{Ab} \to \mathbf{Set}$ it is the usual Hom functor.
