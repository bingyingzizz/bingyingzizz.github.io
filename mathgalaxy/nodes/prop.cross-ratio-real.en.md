## Statement · **draft**
Let $z_{1}, z_{2}, z_{3}, z_{4}$ be pairwise distinct. Then

$$(z_{1}, z_{2}; z_{3}, z_{4}) \in \mathbb{R}$$

if and only if $z_{1}, z_{2}, z_{3}, z_{4}$ are **concyclic or collinear**.

(On the extended plane "circles" and "lines" are the same kind of object, so the statement need not split into two cases.)
