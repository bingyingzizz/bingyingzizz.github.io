Notes

⭐ A matrix is just a rectangular array of elements of KKK — it carries no mathematical structure by itself; its meaning comes entirely from how it is read (as the ledger of a linear map, as coefficients of a linear system, as the recipe of a bilinear form).

⚠ The two indexing conventions (aija_{ij}aij​ is the entry in row iii, column jjj) must be used consistently, or transposition and multiplication go wrong.

The convention throughout is row first, then column.

⭐ As a set, Mm×n(K)M_{m \times n}(K)Mm×n​(K) is just KmnK^{mn}Kmn — hence it is certainly a KKK-vector space (operations componentwise).
