# five lemma　`thm.five-lemma`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=thm.five-lemma)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./thm.five-lemma.md)

⚠️ Relation to the snake lemma: **neither can be proved by chasing elements**; both need generalized elements and universal properties. The difference is that the five lemma only transfers isomorphisms (no connecting homomorphism needed), while the snake lemma must **construct** $\delta$. Whenever one wants to "compare two spectral sequences / two long exact sequences", use the five lemma.
