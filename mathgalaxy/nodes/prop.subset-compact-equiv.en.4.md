# characterisation of compact subsets　`prop.subset-compact-equiv`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=prop.subset-compact-equiv)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./prop.subset-compact-equiv.md)

Now **compactness**: total boundedness gives a countable dense subset, so any sequence in $A$ has a Cauchy subsequence, obtained layer by layer from $2^{-n}$-nets and the pigeonhole principle;

completeness makes that subsequence converge ✓.

Hence $A$ is **sequentially compact**;

and in a **metric space** sequential compactness $\iff$ compactness ✓.

∎

⚠️ The step "$X$ complete $\implies$ $A$ complete" is exactly where the hypothesis in the title is used (see the previous remark).
