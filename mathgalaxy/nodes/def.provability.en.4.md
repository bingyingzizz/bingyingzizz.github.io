⚠ **The choice of logical axioms is not unique**: Hilbert-style (the system above), natural deduction, and sequent calculus give the same **provability relation** (under the same completeness theorem). So "the definition of $\vdash$ depends on a particular axiom system" is not a problem: it is extensionally invariant.

⚠ The word "finite" is essential: a proof is a **finite** sequence. The compactness theorem is exactly what upgrades "every finite subset has a model" to "the whole theory has a model", and it relies on this.
