def.semisimple-representation

Machine-drafted and pending author review. The Chinese text is authoritative.

Statement

A representation VVV of AAA is called semisimple (completely reducible) if it is a direct sum of irreducible subrepresentations.

Notes

The zero representation is semisimple by convention (empty direct sum). In finite dimension there are finitely many summands. Equivalent characterizations (a sum of irreducibles, existence of complements) are given in a separate theorem. Maschke's theorem shows that F[G]F[G]F[G] is semisimple when char⁡F∤∣G∣\operatorname{char}F\nmid |G|charF∤∣G∣.
