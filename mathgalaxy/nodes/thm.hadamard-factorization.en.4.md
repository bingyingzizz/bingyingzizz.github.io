# Hadamard factorization theorem　`thm.hadamard-factorization`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=thm.hadamard-factorization)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./thm.hadamard-factorization.md)

**Step 1. Density of zeros.** Apply Jensen's formula (`prop.jensen`) to $f/z^{m}$, whose value at the origin is nonzero, to obtain $n(R)\log 2\le\log M(2R)-\log|f(0)|+O(1)$. Finite order gives $\log M(2R)\le C\,R^{\rho+\varepsilon}$, hence $n(R)\le C\,R^{\rho+\varepsilon}$. Thus for any $\sigma>\rho$, summation by parts gives $\sum_{n}|a_{n}|^{-\sigma}=\sigma\int_{0}^{\infty}n(r)\,r^{-\sigma-1}\,\mathrm{d}r<\infty$ ($n(r)$, when $r<|a_{1}|$, equals $0$, so there is no boundary term). In particular, $\sum_{n}|a_{n}|^{-(p+1)}<\infty$.
