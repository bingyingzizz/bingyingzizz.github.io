**Proof (both items are special cases of theorems for general toposes, applied to $\mathrm{Cond}$).**
- **Item 1**: the topos-theoretic characterization of "quasi-compact" is "there is an epimorphism from a **projective / free** object" — the reverse is clear (an epimorphism $F \twoheadrightarrow X$ gives a cover, and the parametrization by $F$ extracts a finite subcover, because the free object $F = \beta I$ is "generated by a set of points", so finiteness is pointwise);
