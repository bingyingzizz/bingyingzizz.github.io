⚠ Relation to def.normal-operator (normal **operator**): that entry speaks about an operator $T^{*}T = TT^{*}$ and is coordinate-free; this entry speaks about the matrix condition in an **orthonormal basis**. The two translate into each other, but do not mix them (after a non-orthonormal change of basis the matrix of the operator is no longer "normal").
