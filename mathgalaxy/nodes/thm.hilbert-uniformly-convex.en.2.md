## Notes · **draft**
**Proof.** Put $\|x\| = \|y\| = 1$. By the parallelogram law,

$$\|x + y\|^{2} + \|x - y\|^{2} = 2\|x\|^{2} + 2\|y\|^{2} = 4 ,$$

that is, $\left\|\frac{x + y}{2}\right\|^{2} = 1 - \frac{\|x - y\|^{2}}{4}$. When $\|x - y\| \ge \varepsilon$, the right-hand side is $\le 1 - \varepsilon^{2}/4$, and taking square roots gives $\left\|\frac{x + y}{2}\right\| \le \sqrt{1 - \varepsilon^{2}/4} = 1 - \delta$. $\blacksquare$
