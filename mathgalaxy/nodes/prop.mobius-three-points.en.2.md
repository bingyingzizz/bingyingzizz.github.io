Regarding the right-hand side as a fractional linear equation in $w$ and the left-hand side as one in $z$, solve for $w = T(z)$.

## Notes · **draft**
**Existence**: the displayed equation determines $z$ uniquely for each $w$ (unless the denominator vanishes, in which case $w = \infty$); so it defines a map. It is fractional linear in $z$, hence a Möbius transformation. Substituting point by point gives $Tz_{k} = w_{k}$.
