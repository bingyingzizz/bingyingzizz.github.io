# Pontryagin Dual as a Derived Hom　`prop.dual-rhom-characterization`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=prop.dual-rhom-characterization)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./prop.dual-rhom-characterization.md)

- in general $M^{\vee} \cong \operatorname{RHom}_{\mathbb{Z}}(M, T)$;
- if $M$ is **compact**, then $M^{\vee} \cong \operatorname{RHom}_{\mathbb{Z}}(M, \mathbb{Z})[1]$;
- if $M$ is a (finite-dimensional real) **Banach space**, then $M^{\vee} \cong \operatorname{RHom}_{\mathbb{Z}}(M, \mathbb{R})$.

Here $T = \mathbb{R}/\mathbb{Z}$.
