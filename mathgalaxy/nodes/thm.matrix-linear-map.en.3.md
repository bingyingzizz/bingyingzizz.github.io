## Notes · **draft**
⭐ This is the **birth certificate** of all matrix theory: a matrix is not a defined object but the **coordinates** of a linear map in chosen bases (exactly the same mechanism as the entry on coordinates, only with a basis chosen on each side).

⭐ Thus every matrix operation acquires meaning: addition = sum of maps, multiplication = composition, identity matrix = identity map, invertible = isomorphism.

**Every formula about matrices can be translated into the language of maps**, and conversely — this is why the graph puts the two in separate clusters yet connects them by edges.
