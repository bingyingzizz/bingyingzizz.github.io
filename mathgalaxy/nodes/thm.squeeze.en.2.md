## Notes · **draft**
⭐ The squeeze theorem is one of the **few tools that can assert the existence of a limit without knowing its value in advance** (the other two are the monotone bounded theorem and the Cauchy criterion).

Its most typical uses are defining $e$, proving $\lim_{n} q^{n} = 0$ ($|q| < 1$), and $\lim_{n} \sqrt[n]{n} = 1$.

⚠ Both hypotheses are indispensable: the function must be squeezed **on both sides**, and the two outer limits must be **equal**.

Squeezing on one side alone yields no conclusion at all.
