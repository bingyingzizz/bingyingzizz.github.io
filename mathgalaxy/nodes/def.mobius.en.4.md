Thus $f$ is **uniquely** determined by the images of three points — the cross-ratio is a complete invariant of Möbius transformations.

⚠ The point $\infty$ on the sphere is not "a very large number": it is a point adjoined by stereographic projection.

Only on the sphere is the statement "$f$ is a bijection" clean — on $\mathbb{C}$ it always misses one or two points.
