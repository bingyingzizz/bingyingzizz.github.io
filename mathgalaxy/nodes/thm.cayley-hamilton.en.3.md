# Cayley–Hamilton theorem　`thm.cayley-hamilton`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=thm.cayley-hamilton)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./thm.cayley-hamilton.md)

⭐ Uses: computing powers of a matrix ($A^{n}$ can be expressed linearly in lower powers), proving that the minimal polynomial divides the characteristic polynomial, and finding inverse matrices ($A^{-1}$ is given by the coefficients of $\chi_A$).
