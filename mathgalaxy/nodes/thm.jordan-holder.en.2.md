# Jordan–Hölder theorem　`thm.jordan-holder`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=thm.jordan-holder)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./thm.jordan-holder.md)

That is: **the multiset of composition factors is independent of the choice of composition series**.

## Notes · **draft**
⭐ This theorem makes the phrase “the composition factors of $G$” **legitimate** — otherwise it would be a property of one chain.

It occupies in group theory exactly the place of the fundamental theorem of arithmetic in the integers: split an object into indecomposable pieces and guarantee the splitting is unique.

⚠ **The converse fails**: equal composition factors do **not** imply isomorphic.
