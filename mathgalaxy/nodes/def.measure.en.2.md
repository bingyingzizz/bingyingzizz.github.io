## Notes · **draft**
(i) is **independent of** (ii), and (ii) alone **does not imply** it: the constant function $+\infty$ is countably additive yet does not vanish on the empty set (taking $E_j \equiv \emptyset$ yields $\mu(\emptyset) = +\infty \cdot \mu(\emptyset)$, and both sides are legitimate). The axiom is there to exclude the meaningless solution that assigns $\infty$ to everything, and at the same time to ensure that the series in (ii) is never $\infty - \infty$.

**Countable additivity is the only requirement in this definition with real content**: it upgrades "size" from finite additivity to countable additivity, at the price of allowing the value $\infty$.
