# Limits and Colimits of Condensed Sets　`prop.cond-limits-colimits`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=prop.cond-limits-colimits)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./prop.cond-limits-colimits.md)

⚠️ **Finite coproducts are the exception** (they are pointwise): $h_{S_{1}} \coprod h_{S_{2}} = h_{S_{1} \coprod S_{2}}$ — because $\coprod$ is precisely a cover of the prestandard topology, and the sheaf condition "finite coproducts become finite products" gives it directly.
