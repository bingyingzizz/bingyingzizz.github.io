## Notes · **draft**
⭐ Three standard examples: $\mathbb{R}^{n}$ (completeness is the finite-dimensional version of `thm.real-complete-metric`), $L^{p}$ (`thm.lp-banach`, Riesz–Fischer), and $C(S)$ on a compact Hausdorff space with the sup norm.

⚠ Completeness is not automatic: $C[0, 1]$ with $\|f\|_{1} = \int_0^1 |f|$ is **not** complete (one can approximate a discontinuous function).

The same set of functions is complete under one norm and incomplete under another — "complete" is a property of the norm, not of the set.
