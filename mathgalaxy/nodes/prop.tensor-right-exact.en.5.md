# Right Exactness of the Tensor Functor　`prop.tensor-right-exact`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=prop.tensor-right-exact)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./prop.tensor-right-exact.md)

⚠️ **The left end can be lost**: after tensoring $0 \to \mathbb{Z} \xrightarrow{\;2\;} \mathbb{Z} \to \mathbb{Z}/2 \to 0$ with $N = \mathbb{Z}/2$, the map $\mathbb{Z} \otimes \mathbb{Z}/2 \xrightarrow{\;2\;} \mathbb{Z} \otimes \mathbb{Z}/2$ is zero, no longer injective.
