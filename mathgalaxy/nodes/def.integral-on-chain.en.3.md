# Integration over a Chain　`def.integral-on-chain`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=def.integral-on-chain)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./def.integral-on-chain.md)

Convention for $k = 0$: a $0$-cube is a point $p$, a $0$-form is a function, $\int_{p}F := F(p)$, $\int_{\sum n_{l}p_{l}}F := \sum_{l}n_{l}F(p_{l})$.

## Notes · **draft**
**What the coefficient $f$ is**: $f = c^{\ast}\omega(\partial_{1},\ldots,\partial_{k})$, that is, feed the tangent vectors in the coordinate directions of the cube to the pulled-back form. It is a smooth function; its values on the boundary are determined by the smooth extension of $c$, and measure-zero sets do not affect the integral.
