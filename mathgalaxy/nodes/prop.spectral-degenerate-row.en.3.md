# a degenerate row of a spectral sequence　`prop.spectral-degenerate-row`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=prop.spectral-degenerate-row)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./prop.spectral-degenerate-row.md)

In the filtration on the abutment $H^{q}$, we have $E^{\infty}_{0,q} = \operatorname{Gr}^{0}H^{q}$, and the vanishing of all terms with $p < 0$ means that the filtration on $H^{q}$ has no lower step below $0$. Thus $\operatorname{Gr}^{0}H^{q} = H^{q}$ ✓. ∎
