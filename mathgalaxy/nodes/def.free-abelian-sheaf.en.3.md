**Why this is right.** The presheaf layer is pointwise: adjunctions in a functor category are built pointwise, and the forgetful functor $\mathbf{Ab} \to \mathbf{Set}$ has a left adjoint $E \mapsto \mathbb{Z}\cdot E$ (the free abelian group) ✓, so the left adjoint of $\widehat{\mathcal{C}}(\mathbf{Ab}) \to \widehat{\mathcal{C}}$ is pointwise free ✓.
