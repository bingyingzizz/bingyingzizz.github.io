# Mayer–Vietoris sequence in homology　`thm.mv-homology`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=thm.mv-homology)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./thm.mv-homology.md)

⚠️ **Difference from `thm.mayer-vietoris`.** That node gives the **de Rham version** for smooth manifolds with real coefficients and the complex of differential forms (`def.de-rham-complex`). This is the **singular-chain version** for arbitrary spaces with $\mathbb{Z}$ coefficients. The sequences have the same form, but only one retains torsion information. They are distinct results and should not be cited as proofs of each other.
