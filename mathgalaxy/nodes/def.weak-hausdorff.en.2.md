# weak Hausdorff　`def.weak-hausdorff`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=def.weak-hausdorff)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./def.weak-hausdorff.md)

⭐ Why needed: compactly generated spaces are not required to be Hausdorff, and without separation, quotients and colimits leave the good category; weak Hausdorffness exactly guarantees that "images of compact pieces are closed", so that quotients and colimits in $k\mathbf{Top}$ can still be computed.

⚠ Strength: Hausdorff $\implies$ weakly Hausdorff, **but not conversely**. ⛔ Do not write it as an equivalence; and do not confuse it with @@def.chaus@@ (compact + Hausdorff), whose condition is stronger.
