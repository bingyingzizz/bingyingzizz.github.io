# topos　`def.topos`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=def.topos)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./def.topos.md)

Examples: $\mathbf{Set}$, $G\text{-}\mathbf{Set}$ are topoi; the presheaf category $\widehat{\mathcal{C}}$ is a topos; sheaf categories of sites are topoi; sheaves on a topological space and its étalé space also form a topos. **$\mathbf{Top}$ is not a topos.**

⭐ **The category of condensed sets is a topos** — this is the foundation of the Cond section later.
