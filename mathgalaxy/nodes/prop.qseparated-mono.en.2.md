## Notes · **draft**
**Proof.** It suffices to check, for each compact Hausdorff $S$, that $X(S) \to \underline{X(\cdot)}(S) = C(S, X(\cdot))$ is injective ✓ (monomorphisms are checked pointwise).

By the generalization of "countable increasing colimits" (quasi-separated $\iff$ filtered colimit with inclusion transitions), $X = \varinjlim_i S_{i}$ and $X(S) = \varinjlim_i \operatorname{Hom}(S, S_{i})$ ✓.
