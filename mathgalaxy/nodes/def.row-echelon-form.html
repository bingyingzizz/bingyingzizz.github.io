陈述

设 R∈Mm×n(K)R \in M_{m \times n}(K)R∈Mm×n​(K)。若

  • 所有零行都排在非零行之下；
  • 每个非零行的首个非零元（称为主元）所在的列，严格在上一行主元的右侧，

则称 RRR 为行阶梯形（row echelon form）。若进一步每个主元都等于 111、且主元所在列的其他元素全为 000，则称 RRR 为行简化阶梯形（reduced row echelon form，RREF）。

定理（消元法）：任何 A∈Mm×n(K)A \in M_{m \times n}(K)A∈Mm×n​(K) 都能经过有限次初等行变换化为行简化阶梯形，而且这个行简化阶梯形是唯一的。主元的个数等于 rank⁡A\operatorname{rank} ArankA。

说明

唯一性是 RREF 的关键：行阶梯形不唯一（可以继续做行变换），但「主元为 111、主元列其余为零」把自由度全部消掉，只剩一个。

用途：解线性方程组（主元列对应基本变量，其余列自由变量）、求秩（数主元）、求逆（对 [A∣I][A \mid I][A∣I] 化 RREF）、求列空间的一组基（主元列）。

初等行变换对应左乘初等矩阵；RREF 是「行变换下的标准形」，而相抵还允许列变换，标准形是 diag⁡(Ir,0)\operatorname{diag}(I_r, 0)diag(Ir​,0)。

Statement draft

Let R∈Mm×n(K)R \in M_{m \times n}(K)R∈Mm×n​(K). If

  • every zero row lies below every nonzero row;
  • in each nonzero row, the column of the first nonzero entry (called a pivot) lies strictly to the right of the pivot of the row above,

then RRR is in row echelon form. If moreover every pivot equals 111 and every other entry in a pivot column is 000, then RRR is in reduced row echelon form (RREF).

Theorem (elimination): every A∈Mm×n(K)A \in M_{m \times n}(K)A∈Mm×n​(K) can be brought to reduced row echelon form by finitely many elementary row operations, and this reduced row echelon form is unique. The number of pivots equals rank⁡A\operatorname{rank} ArankA.

Notes draft

Uniqueness is the point of RREF: row echelon form is not unique (one may continue performing row operations), but "pivots equal to 111, all other entries in pivot columns zero" removes every degree of freedom and leaves exactly one form.

Uses: solving linear systems (pivot columns correspond to basic variables, the other columns to free variables), computing rank (count the pivots), inverting (reduce [A∣I][A \mid I][A∣I]), and finding a basis of the column space (the pivot columns).

Elementary row operations correspond to left multiplication by elementary matrices; RREF is the "normal form under row operations", while equivalence also allows column operations, with normal form diag⁡(Ir,0)\operatorname{diag}(I_r, 0)diag(Ir​,0).
