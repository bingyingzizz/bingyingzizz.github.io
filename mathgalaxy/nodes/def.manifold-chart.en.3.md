# Chart and Transition Maps　`def.manifold-chart`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=def.manifold-chart)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./def.manifold-chart.md)

$$\psi \circ \varphi^{-1} : \varphi(U \cap V) \longrightarrow \psi(U \cap V),$$

which turns $\varphi$-coordinates into $\psi$-coordinates. The charts are **$C^{\infty}$-compatible** when the transition map is $C^{\infty}$ everywhere (it is defined on an open subset of $\mathbb{R}^{n}$, so partial derivatives test it); if $U \cap V = \emptyset$ they are declared compatible.

Covering $M$, a family of pairwise compatible charts $\mathcal{A} = \{(U_{\alpha},\varphi_{\alpha})\}$ is an **atlas** of $M$ of dimension $n$.
