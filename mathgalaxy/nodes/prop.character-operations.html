Notes draft

(1)-(3) hold in any characteristic; (4)(5) use the ±1\pm1±1 decomposition of the swap and require char⁡F≠2\operatorname{char}F\ne2charF=2. In (3), V∗V^*V∗ is an AopA^{\mathrm{op}}Aop-representation, i.e. g↦ρ(g−1)Tg\mapsto\rho(g^{-1})^Tg↦ρ(g−1)T for groups. When char⁡F≠2\operatorname{char}F\ne2charF=2, the characters of Sym²/Alt² follow from the trace decomposition of V⊗2V^{\otimes2}V⊗2.
