# Symmetry of the Second Fundamental Form　`lem.second-form-symmetric`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=lem.second-form-symmetric)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./lem.second-form-symmetric.md)

**Comparison with the coordinate version**: there symmetry is obtained in $\{r_{u}, r_{v}\}$ from "the matrix $h^{j}_{\ i}$ of $W$ is self-adjoint because mixed partials are equal". $\mathrm{d}^{2}=0$ is the coordinate-free version of the same fact, and it does not require a special parameter net.

⭐ **The payoff of symmetry**: a real symmetric matrix has orthogonal real eigendirections (spectral theorem) — this is the source of principal directions, Euler's formula and Rodrigues' theorem.
