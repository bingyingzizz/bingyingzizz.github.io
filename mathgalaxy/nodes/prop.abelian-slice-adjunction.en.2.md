## Notes · **draft**
**Proof.** In the presheaf case there is an explicit formula

$$j_{S!}M(U) = \bigoplus_{s : U \to S} M(s) ✓$$

which is plainly left adjoint to $j_{S}^{-1}$ ✓, exactness coming from "$\bigoplus$ commutes with pullback" ✓. On a general topos, sheafify both sides ✓ (exactness is preserved by sheafification, since sheafification is exact) ✓. ∎
