Statement

  • Ab(Set)=Ab\mathrm{Ab}(\mathbf{Set}) = \mathbf{Ab}Ab(Set)=Ab;
  • Ab(Top)\mathrm{Ab}(\mathbf{Top})Ab(Top) = topological abelian groups;
  • Ab(Manifold)\mathrm{Ab}(\mathbf{Manifold})Ab(Manifold) = abelian Lie groups;
  • Ab(Grp)=Ab\mathrm{Ab}(\mathbf{Grp}) = \mathbf{Ab}Ab(Grp)=Ab (operations between group objects are pointwise);
  • Ab(Cond)=CondAb\mathrm{Ab}(\mathrm{Cond}) = \mathrm{CondAb}Ab(Cond)=CondAb;
  • Ab(C^)\mathrm{Ab}(\widehat{\mathcal{C}})Ab(C) = abelian group objects in the presheaf category ≃\simeq≃ presheaves valued in Ab\mathbf{Ab}Ab.

Notes

⭐ The most useful thing about this table is reading it backwards: to study topological abelian groups, take C\mathcal{C}C to be Top\mathbf{Top}Top; to study condensed abelian groups, take C\mathcal{C}C to be Cond\mathrm{Cond}Cond.

One definition, one set of theorems (kernels, cokernels, exact sequences, ...) transfers directly.

⚠️ The identity Ab(Grp)=Ab\mathrm{Ab}(\mathbf{Grp}) = \mathbf{Ab}Ab(Grp)=Ab is the one that gives pause: a group object in the category of groups is exactly an abelian group (Eckmann–Hilton argument) — adding the structure "group" on top of the category "groups" forces commutativity.
