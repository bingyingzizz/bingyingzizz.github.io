## Notes · **draft**
⚠ **None of these four is an equality at the level of paths.** $(\alpha * \beta) * \gamma$ and $\alpha * (\beta * \gamma)$ have genuinely different values at $t = 1/4, 1/2$; they are only **homotopic**. Hence multiplication must be defined on **homotopy classes**.
