# The Topos Morphism Induced by a Point　`prop.topos-morphism-point`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=prop.topos-morphism-point)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./prop.topos-morphism-point.md)

$x^{-1}$ is left adjoint to $x_{*}$; a computation of the adjunction formula $\operatorname{Hom}(x^{-1}\mathcal{F}, E) = \operatorname{Hom}(\mathcal{F}, x_{*}E) = \mathcal{F}_{x}$ shows it is the stalk ✓ (one may also use directly "the stalk = filtered colimit over open neighbourhoods of $x$") ✓.

∎
