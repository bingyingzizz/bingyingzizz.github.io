# monodromy theorem　`thm.monodromy`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=thm.monodromy)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./thm.monodromy.md)

Equivalent statement: if $\gamma_{0}, \gamma_{1}$ are two curves in $\Omega$ starting at the same point and ending at the same point, then when they are homotopic in $\Omega$ they give the **same** terminal value of the continuation.

## Notes · **draft**
**Idea of the proof**: regard the homotopy as a continuous family $\gamma_{s}$ ($s \in [0,1]$). For each $s$, continuing along $\gamma_{s}$ gives a function element $\varphi(s)$ at the endpoint.
