## Statement · **draft**
Let $f \in L^1_{loc}$, $x \in \mathbb{R}^n$, $r > 0$. Define

$$A_r f(x) := \frac{1}{m(B(x, r))} \int_{B(x, r)} f(y) dy$$

that is, the **average value** of $f$ over the ball centred at $x$ with radius $r$.
