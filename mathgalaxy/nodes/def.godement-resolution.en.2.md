## Statement · **draft**
Let $\mathcal{F}$ be an abelian sheaf on $X$. The **Godement resolution** is the complex $0\to\mathcal{F}\to\mathcal{C}^{0}(\mathcal{F})\to\mathcal{C}^{1}(\mathcal{F})\to\cdots$, where $\mathcal{C}^{q}(\mathcal{F})(U)=\prod_{x\in U}\mathcal{F}_{x}$ ($q\ge0$), with differential given by the alternating sum of stalks. Each $\mathcal{C}^{q}(\mathcal{F})$ is a flabby sheaf. That it is an acyclic resolution is conditional on flabby acyclicity (flabby sheaves are acyclic, Godement's theorem); this is not proved in this graph, see step 3 of @@thm.soft-sheaf-acyclic@@.
