## Notes · **draft**
⭐ **What it says**: Čech cohomology (the $\check{H}^{p}$ on the left) with coefficients in $H^{q}(M)$ converges to the **genuine** sheaf cohomology. Thus it explains how the "cheap algorithm" approximates the "expensive" one.

**Corollary**: if $H^{q} = 0$ on every layer of the cover ($q \ne 0$), then $\check{H}^{n} \cong H^{n}$ — in that case Čech cohomology directly gives sheaf cohomology.
