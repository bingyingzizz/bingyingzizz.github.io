# category of complexes　`def.category-of-complexes`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=def.category-of-complexes)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./def.category-of-complexes.md)

$$d_{L}^{n} \circ f^{n} = f^{n+1} \circ d_{K}^{n}$$

holds for all $n$. It is a **full** additive subcategory of $\mathcal{C}^{(\mathbb{Z}, \le)}$ — a morphism of sequences is precisely a natural transformation, whose condition is exactly the commutativity above.

## Notes · **draft**
**Fullness**: every morphism of sequences between two complexes automatically commutes with the differentials, so the Hom set of $\mathcal{C}(\mathcal{C})$ is that of the sequence category; no further filtering is needed.
