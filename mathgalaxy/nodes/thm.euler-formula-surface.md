取主方向标架：$e_{1}, e_{2}$ 是单位正交主方向，$W(e_{i}) = k_{i}e_{i}$。设单位向量 $V = \cos\theta\,e_{1}+\sin\theta\,e_{2}$，则

$$k_{n}(V) = k_{1}\cos^{2}\theta+k_{2}\sin^{2}\theta .$$
