## Notes · **draft**
**Checking the three properties one by one**: $P^{2} = P$ (idempotent); $P^{\ast} = P$ (symmetric); $\ker P = \operatorname{span}(e_{3})$, $\operatorname{im} P = W$, and $\mathbb{R}^{3} = \ker P \oplus \operatorname{im} P$ is an **orthogonal** direct sum. Conversely: idempotent + symmetric $\implies$ orthogonal projection (idempotent alone gives an oblique projection, whose image and kernel are not perpendicular).
