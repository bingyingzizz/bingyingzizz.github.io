# Darboux’s Theorem　`thm.darboux`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=thm.darboux)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./thm.darboux.md)

⭐ **This theorem marks a dividing line in symplectic geometry**: Local coordinates cannot distinguish symplectic manifolds, so all symplectic invariants (Gromov–Witten invariants, Floer homology, and capacities) are **global**. Riemannian geometry starts from local data such as curvature, while symplectic geometry relies on global data.

⚠️ The theorem **does not** say that every symplectic manifold is diffeomorphic to $\mathbb{R}^{2n}$. It supplies only local coordinates and imposes no constraint on global topology (as illustrated by the area form on $T^{2}$). This is the most common misreading.
