## Notes · **draft**
⭐ This is **the starting point of every estimate**: linear operations on Cauchy sequences, the algebra of limits, the comparison test for series, and the inequality $d(x, z) \le d(x, y) + d(y, z)$ in metric spaces all reduce to it (the metric-space version takes it as an axiom).

⚠ The proof is only case analysis, with no trick — but the two statements "$a \le |a|$ and $-a \le |a|$" are the whole point: they give $-(|x| + |y|) \le x + y \le |x| + |y|$, and $|t| \le c \iff -c \le t \le c$ (for $c \ge 0$).
