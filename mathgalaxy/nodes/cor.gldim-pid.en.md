## Statement · **draft**
Let $R$ be a **principal ideal domain**. Then every $R$-module $M$ has a **free** resolution of length at most $1$, so

$$\operatorname{pd}_{R} M \le 1 , \qquad \operatorname{gl.dim} R \le 1 .$$
