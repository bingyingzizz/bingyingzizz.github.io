## Notes · **draft**
**Why this multiplication**: for $H \times G$ to be a group while both $H \hookrightarrow H \rtimes_{\varphi} G$ and $G \hookrightarrow H \rtimes_{\varphi} G$ are group homomorphisms, one must let $G$, when passing through $H$, twist $H$ once — that twist is $\varphi(g_{1})$.
