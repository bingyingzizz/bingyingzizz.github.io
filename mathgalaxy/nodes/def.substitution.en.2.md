# substitution　`def.substitution`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=def.substitution)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./def.substitution.md)

⚠ Substitution requires that $t$ be, in $\varphi$, **substitutable for** $x$: variables occurring in $t$ are not "captured" by quantifiers of $\varphi$ after the substitution. This is called **variable capture**.

## Notes · **draft**
⚠ **What capture looks like**: take $\varphi = \exists y\, (y > x)$, $t = y$. Direct substitution gives $\exists y\, (y > y)$ — the meaning is completely changed (it was "there is a number larger than $x$", and now it is a false statement). One then says that $t$, with respect to $\varphi$, is not substitutable for $x$.
