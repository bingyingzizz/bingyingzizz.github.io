## Notes · **draft**
Bilinearity is immediate; symmetry follows from the substitution $g\mapsto g^{-1}$.\nOn class functions $\langle f,f\rangle=\frac1{|G|}\sum_g f(g)f(g^{-1})$; for complex representations ($F\subseteq\mathbb C$) with $f$ complex conjugation, it is a positive definite Hermitian form when restricted to the space of characters; over a general field only nondegeneracy is required.\nThe pairing writes the orthogonality of irreducible characters as $\langle\chi_i,\chi_j\rangle=\delta_{ij}$.
