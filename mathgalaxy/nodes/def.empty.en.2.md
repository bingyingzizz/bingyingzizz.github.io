## Notes · **draft**
Conditions such as "$\emptyset \in \mathcal{A}$" and "$\emptyset \notin F$" are everywhere in measure theory (the Axiom of Choice requires the family to contain no empty set; $\mu(\emptyset) = 0$ is the first axiom of a measure).

The Axiom of Extensionality supplies **at most** one such set;

the half that says one **exists** is a separate result — see "the empty set exists".

Distinguish $\emptyset$ from $\{\emptyset\}$: the latter has one element (namely the empty set), so $\{\emptyset\} \ne \emptyset$.

$\emptyset \subseteq A$ is **vacuously true**: there is no $x \in \emptyset$ to check.
