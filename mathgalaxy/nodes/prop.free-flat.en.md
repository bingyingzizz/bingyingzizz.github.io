## Statement · **draft**
The free module $R^{(I)}$ is **flat**; more generally, **projective modules are flat**.

Concretely, $M \otimes_{R} R^{(I)} \cong M^{(I)}$ naturally ($m \otimes (r_{i})_{i} \mapsto (m r_{i})_{i}$), and "taking the $I$-fold direct sum" detects exactness componentwise.
