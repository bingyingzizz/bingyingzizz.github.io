# Paracompact Hausdorff Spaces Are Normal　`thm.paracompact-hausdorff-normal`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=thm.paracompact-hausdorff-normal)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./thm.paracompact-hausdorff-normal.md)

⭐ This result places paracompactness in the hierarchy of separation axioms: **compact Hausdorff $\implies$ paracompact Hausdorff $\implies$ normal $\implies$ regular $\implies$ Hausdorff**.

This supplies the normality used with Urysohn's lemma in Pack 40. Compact Hausdorff spaces ($[0,1]$, $\beta I$) are paracompact.

⚠️ **Hausdorffness is essential**: The two-point Sierpiński space is paracompact (its open covers are finite and hence locally finite), but it fails to be $T_{1}$ and therefore is not normal.
