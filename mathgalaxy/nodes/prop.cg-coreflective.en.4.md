# compactly generated spaces form a coreflective subcategory　`prop.cg-coreflective`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=prop.cg-coreflective)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./prop.cg-coreflective.md)

$$\operatorname{Hom}_{\mathbf{Top}}(X,\ Y) \;\cong\; \operatorname{Hom}_{k\mathbf{Top}}\bigl(X,\ kY\bigr).$$

**Proof.** The counit is $\varepsilon_{Y} : kY \to Y$ (the identity map, topology from finer to coarser, continuous).
