## Statement · **draft**
Let $\Omega \subset \mathbb{C}$ be a **simply connected** domain, $(f, D)$ a function element at some point of $\Omega$, and suppose $f$ can be analytically continued along every curve in $\Omega$. Then there is on $\Omega$ a **single-valued** holomorphic function $F$ which, on $D$, equals $f$.
