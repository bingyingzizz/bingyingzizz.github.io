# Lie Group　`def.lie-group`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=def.lie-group)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./def.lie-group.md)

such that **multiplication** $m : G \times G \to G$ ($m(g,h) := gh$, with $G \times G$ given the product manifold structure) and **inversion** $i : G \to G$ ($i(g) := g^{-1}$) are both smooth maps.

For $g \in G$ define **left translation** and **right translation**

$$L_{g} : G \to G,\quad L_{g}(h) := gh ;\qquad R_{g} : G \to G,\quad R_{g}(h) := hg .$$

Both are diffeomorphisms (with inverses $L_{g^{-1}}$ and $R_{g^{-1}}$, respectively), and $L_{g} \circ L_{h} = L_{gh}$.

**Examples**: $\operatorname{GL}(n,\mathbb{R}) = \{A : \det A \ne 0\}$ (an open subset of $\mathbb{R}^{n^{2}}$, hence a manifold of dimension $n^{2}$);
