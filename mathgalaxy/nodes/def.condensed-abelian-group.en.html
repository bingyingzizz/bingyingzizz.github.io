Statement

A condensed abelian group is an abelian sheaf on Cond\mathrm{Cond}Cond. Four descriptions give the same category:

Ab(Cond)  ≃  Cond(Ab)  ≃  CHaus^(Ab)  ≃  FCHaus^(Ab)\mathrm{Ab}(\mathrm{Cond}) \;\simeq\; \mathrm{Cond}(\mathrm{Ab}) \;\simeq\; \widehat{\mathbf{CHaus}}(\mathrm{Ab}) \;\simeq\; \widehat{\mathbf{FCHaus}}(\mathrm{Ab})Ab(Cond)≃Cond(Ab)≃CHaus(Ab)≃FCHaus(Ab)

Write this category as CondAb\mathrm{CondAb}CondAb.

Notes

For the general definition, see “abelian sheaf.” Condensed abelian groups are the case of T=Cond\mathcal{T} = \mathrm{Cond}T=Cond.

In the compact Hausdorff setting, the sheaf condition simplifies: MMM is a condensed abelian group   ⟺  \iff⟺ it preserves finite products (a general topos has no such simple criterion).

Notation: Hom⁡Z(M,N):=Hom⁡CondAb(M,N)\operatorname{Hom}_{\mathbb{Z}}(M, N) := \operatorname{Hom}_{\mathrm{CondAb}}(M, N)HomZ​(M,N):=HomCondAb​(M,N).
