## Statement · **draft**
Let $S$ be a **Stonean** space and $N$ a condensed abelian group. Then

$$\operatorname{RHom}_{\mathbb{Z}}\bigl(\mathbb{Z}[S],\ N\bigr) \;\cong\; N(S)$$

(the right-hand side is understood as the complex concentrated in degree $0$, i.e. all higher Ext vanish).
