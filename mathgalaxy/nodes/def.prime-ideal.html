又称素理想·prime ideal of a ring

陈述

设 AAA 是含幺交换环，P⊂AP \subset AP⊂A 是真理想。称 PPP 是素理想，若 ab∈P  ⟹  a∈P or b∈P(a,b∈A).a b \in P \implies a \in P \ \text{or}\ b \in P \qquad (a, b \in A).ab∈P⟹a∈P or b∈P(a,b∈A). 等价地，商环 A/PA/PA/P 是整环（见 商环）。

说明

⭐ 例：Z\mathbb{Z}Z 中 (p)(p)(p)（ppp 素数）与 (0)(0)(0) 都是素理想；K[x]K[x]K[x] 中 (f)(f)(f) 素   ⟺  \iff⟺ fff 不可约，(0)(0)(0) 也素。

⚠ 素理想不必极大：Z[x]\mathbb{Z}[x]Z[x] 中 (x)(x)(x) 素（Z[x]/(x)≅Z\mathbb{Z}[x]/(x) \cong \mathbb{Z}Z[x]/(x)≅Z 是整环）但不极大。

⭐ 素理想是「整除」的抽象：a∈(p)  ⟺  p∣aa \in (p) \iff p \mid aa∈(p)⟺p∣a；ab∈(p)  ⟹  p∣ab  ⟹  p∣aab \in (p) \implies p \mid ab \implies p \mid aab∈(p)⟹p∣ab⟹p∣a 或 p∣bp \mid bp∣b 正是 Euclid 引理。

Statement draft

Let AAA be a commutative ring with identity and P⊂AP \subset AP⊂A a proper ideal. Then PPP is a prime ideal if

ab∈P  ⟹  a∈P or b∈P(a,b∈A).a b \in P \implies a \in P \ \text{or}\ b \in P \qquad (a, b \in A).ab∈P⟹a∈P or b∈P(a,b∈A).

Equivalently, the quotient ring A/PA/PA/P is a domain (see 商环).

Notes draft

⭐ Examples: in Z\mathbb{Z}Z both (p)(p)(p) (ppp prime) and (0)(0)(0) are prime; in K[x]K[x]K[x], (f)(f)(f) is prime   ⟺  \iff⟺ fff is irreducible, and (0)(0)(0) is prime too.

⚠ A prime ideal need not be maximal: in Z[x]\mathbb{Z}[x]Z[x], (x)(x)(x) is prime (Z[x]/(x)≅Z\mathbb{Z}[x]/(x) \cong \mathbb{Z}Z[x]/(x)≅Z is a domain) but not maximal.

⭐ A prime ideal is the abstraction of "divisibility": a∈(p)  ⟺  p∣aa \in (p) \iff p \mid aa∈(p)⟺p∣a; ab∈(p)  ⟹  p∣ab  ⟹  p∣aab \in (p) \implies p \mid ab \implies p \mid aab∈(p)⟹p∣ab⟹p∣a or p∣bp \mid bp∣b is exactly Euclid's lemma.
