# partial isometry　`def.partial-isometry`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=def.partial-isometry)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./def.partial-isometry.md)

**Initial and final projections**: the image of $P = u^*u$ is $(\ker u)^\perp$ (initial space), the image of $Q = u\,u^*$ is $\operatorname{im} u$ (final space), and $u$ maps $P H$ isometrically onto $Q H$.

⚠️ The unilateral shift $S(x_1, x_2, \ldots) = (0, x_1, x_2, \ldots)$ is a partial isometry but not unitary: $S^*S = I$, while $S\,S^* = I - \operatorname{proj}_{\ker(S^*)}$ is a projection (not $I$).

⭐ What appears in polar decomposition and polar-coordinate decomposition is always a partial isometry; a unitary operator is exactly the special case where both the initial and final projections are $I$.
