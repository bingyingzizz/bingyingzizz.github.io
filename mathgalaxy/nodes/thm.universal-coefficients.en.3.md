# Universal Coefficient Theorem　`thm.universal-coefficients`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=thm.universal-coefficients)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./thm.universal-coefficients.md)

"Extending coefficients to a field" merely tensors homology and **produces no new classes**. Conversely, when $A$ has torsion, the extra piece comes from the next lower homology — this is the real content of the universal coefficient theorem.

⚠️ **Two hypotheses**: $R$ a principal ideal domain (ensuring submodules are free) and $C_{\bullet}$ free in every degree. Over a general ring one must replace this by "the coboundaries $B_{n}$ are all projective" (i.e. the complex splits in every degree), otherwise the first step of ① breaks.
