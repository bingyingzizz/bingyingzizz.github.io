## Notes · **draft**
**"There exists a metric", not "a metric is specified"**: the same topology can carry many metrics ($d$ and $\min(d, 1)$ induce the same topology), so metrizability is a purely topological property, invariant under homeomorphism. "Complete", "bounded" and "uniformly continuous" are not topological properties — they change when the metric is replaced.
