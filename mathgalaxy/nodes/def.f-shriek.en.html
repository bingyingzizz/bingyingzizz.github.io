Statement

In plain words: f!f_!f!​ is the composite of "first push into the open part, then proper pushforward along the relative space"; its right adjoint f!f^!f! is the dualizing complex functor. The name "compact support" comes from the intuition that f!f_!f!​ remembers only the cohomology "supported on compact subsets of each fibre", just as an integral is computed only over bounded regions.
