# duality between discrete and compact Hausdorff groups　`prop.pontryagin-discrete-compact-duality`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=prop.pontryagin-discrete-compact-duality)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./prop.pontryagin-discrete-compact-duality.md)

**Hypotheses used in each direction** (the forward compactness proof and the biduality theorem play different roles):
- $M$ discrete $\implies$ $\widehat M$ compact Hausdorff: The characters form a closed subgroup of $\mathbb T^M$, and the compact-open topology equals the topology of pointwise convergence. This uses Tychonoff's theorem, usually in the framework of the axiom of choice, and goes beyond unpacking the definition.
- $M$ compact $\implies$ $\widehat M$ discrete: Take a small arc about the identity
