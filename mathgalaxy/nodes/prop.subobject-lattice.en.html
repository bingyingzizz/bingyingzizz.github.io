Statement

Let C\mathcal{C}C be a pretopos and X∈CX \in \mathcal{C}X∈C. Then the collection of subobjects {X′:X′↣X}\{X' : X' \rightarrowtail X\}{X′:X′↣X} forms a bounded lattice:

X1∪X2:=im⁡(X1∐X2→X)X_{1} \cup X_{2} := \operatorname{im}\bigl(X_{1} \coprod X_{2} \to X\bigr)X1​∪X2​:=im(X1​∐X2​→X)

and pullback is a homomorphism of lattices (preserving meets, joins and the top).

Notes

"Bounded" means the lattice has a least element (the subobject coming from the initial object) and a greatest element (XXX itself). Viewing "subobjects" as a lattice is exactly what will be used later for the internal logic of a topos — its meets and joins are the "and" and "or" of the logic.
