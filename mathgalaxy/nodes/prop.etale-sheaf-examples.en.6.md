# Examples of Étale Sheaves　`prop.etale-sheaf-examples`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=prop.etale-sheaf-examples)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./prop.etale-sheaf-examples.md)

$t \mapsto t^{n}$ is an "$n$-fold Kummer covering", turning the operation of taking the $n$-th root into a linear structure on a sheaf — Kummer theory says "$\mu_{n}$-torsors are classified by $H^{1}(X_{\text{et}}, \mu_{n})$".

⚠️ **Do not read item 3 as a tautology**: the fact that the constant presheaf is not a sheaf already happens for the Zariski topology (one must glue over intersections of two open sets), and the gluing condition becomes stronger for the étale topology. Only the sheafified $\underline{A}$ is the sheaf of "locally constant functions with values in $A$".
