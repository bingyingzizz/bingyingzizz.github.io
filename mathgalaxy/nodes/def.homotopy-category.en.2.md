Its isomorphisms are called **homotopy equivalences**; a complex that in $\mathbf{K}(\mathcal{C})$ becomes $0$ is called **homotopy trivial**.

## Notes · **draft**
- (a) Equivalent formulation: $f : K \to L$ is a homotopy equivalence - (b) there exists $g : L \to K$ with $g \circ f \sim 1_{K}$, $f \circ g \sim 1_{L}$; $K$ is homotopy trivial - (c) $1_{K} \sim 0_{K}$.

$\mathbf{K}(\mathcal{C})$ is an **additive category**, and $\mathcal{C}(\mathcal{C}) \to \mathbf{K}(\mathcal{C})$ is additive;
