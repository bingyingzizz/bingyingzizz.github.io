# connected sum　`def.connected-sum`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=def.connected-sum)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./def.connected-sum.md)

⭐ **The result is a manifold and is independent of the choices**. At the seam, the ring of points in $M^{\circ}$, together with $S^{n-1}$ and the corresponding ring in $N^{\circ}$, has half-neighborhoods on the two sides. Gluing them gives an open set in $\mathbb{R}^{n}$, so every point has a chart. Choosing different balls or a different homeomorphism gives a homeomorphic result (this uses the fact that any two embedded balls inside a ball are isotopic, which is not developed here).
