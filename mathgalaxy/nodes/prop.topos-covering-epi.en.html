Statement

In a topos T\mathcal{T}T (with the standard topology), a family (Xi→X)i∈I(X_{i} \to X)_{i \in I}(Xi​→X)i∈I​ is a covering   ⟺  ∐i∈IXi→X\iff \coprod_{i \in I} X_{i} \to X⟺∐i∈I​Xi​→X is an epimorphism.

Notes

This is also true in a general site, but it is more convenient in a topos: coproducts in a topos are "good" (disjoint and universal), so the word "covering" can here be replaced entirely by the two purely categorical notions of coproduct and epimorphism, with no geometric residue left.
