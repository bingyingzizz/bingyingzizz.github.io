## Statement · **draft**
Let $S$ be a **Stone** space (compact, Hausdorff, totally disconnected) and $A$ an ordinary abelian group (regarded as the constant condensed abelian group $\underline{A}$). Then for all $n > 0$

$$H^{n}_{\mathrm{cond}}(S,\ \underline{A}) = 0.$$
