Statement

Let X,Y,ZX, Y, ZX,Y,Z be compactly generated spaces. Then there is a homeomorphism

kC(k(X×Y), kZ)  ≅  kC(kX, kC(kY,kZ)).kC\bigl(k(X \times Y),\ kZ\bigr) \;\cong\; kC\bigl(kX,\ kC(kY, kZ)\bigr).kC(k(X×Y), kZ)≅kC(kX, kC(kY,kZ)).

Equivalently, currying holds in kTopk\mathbf{Top}kTop:

C(k(X×Y), Z)  ≅  C(X, C(Y,Z)).C\bigl(k(X \times Y),\ Z\bigr) \;\cong\; C\bigl(X,\ C(Y, Z)\bigr).C(k(X×Y), Z)≅C(X, C(Y,Z)).

Notes

⭐ This is the "usable thing" this cluster sets out to build: in kTopk\mathbf{Top}kTop there are function spaces, and the function spaces stay inside the category, so constructions such as "mapping spaces" and "homotopies" can be carried out entirely inside kTopk\mathbf{Top}kTop. Top\mathbf{Top}Top itself cannot do this.

How the proof goes. First verify the set-level bijection by hand for compactly generated spaces

C(k(X×Y), Z)  ≅  C(X, C(Y,Z)),C\bigl(k(X \times Y),\ Z\bigr) \;\cong\; C\bigl(X,\ C(Y, Z)\bigr),C(k(X×Y), Z)≅C(X, C(Y,Z)),

then use Yoneda's lemma to reduce "a homeomorphism over kCkCkC" to "equality of mapping sets for every compactly generated test space TTT":

C(T, kC(k(X×Y),kZ))≅C(k(T×X×Y), Z)≅C(T, kC(kX,kC(kY,kZ))).C\bigl(T,\ kC(k(X\times Y), kZ)\bigr) \cong C\bigl(k(T\times X\times Y),\ Z\bigr) \cong C\bigl(T,\ kC(kX, kC(kY,kZ))\bigr).C(T, kC(k(X×Y),kZ))≅C(k(T×X×Y), Z)≅C(T, kC(kX,kC(kY,kZ))).

Both times only associativity of the product and functoriality of kkk-ification are used — which is also why one must take the kkk-ification first: without it the right-hand C(Y,Z)C(Y,Z)C(Y,Z) need not be compactly generated.

⚠️ An easy trap: C(kX,kY)=C(kX,Y)C(kX, kY) = C(kX, Y)C(kX,kY)=C(kX,Y) are equal as sets, but in general not homeomorphic, even after replacing by the kkk-topology.
