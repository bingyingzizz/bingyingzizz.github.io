# interior product　`def.interior-product`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=def.interior-product)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./def.interior-product.md)

Let $X \in \mathfrak{X}(M)$, $\omega \in \Omega^{k}(M)$ ($k \ge 1$). The **interior product** (also called interior multiplication) $\iota_{X}\omega \in \Omega^{k-1}(M)$ is defined pointwise by

$$(\iota_{X}\omega)_{p}(v_{2},\ldots,v_{k}) := \omega_{p}\bigl(X_{p},v_{2},\ldots,v_{k}\bigr);$$
