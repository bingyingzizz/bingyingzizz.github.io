⭐ **It is equivalent to**: $g$ pulls covering families of $\mathcal{C}'$ back to covering families of $\mathcal{C}$ (the preimage of a covering sieve is again a covering sieve). So "continuous" = "covering relations are preserved".

**Notation**: the name $f^{-1}$ comes from topology: a continuous map $f : X \to Y$ between spaces gives a functor $f^{-1} : \operatorname{Open}(Y) \to \operatorname{Open}(X)$ between the categories of open sets (taking preimages), and sheaves are pulled back along it.
