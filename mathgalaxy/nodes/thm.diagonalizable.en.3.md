## Notes · **draft**
⭐ The most used criteria are **(3)/(4)**: the existence of $n$ linearly independent eigenvectors $\iff$ diagonalizable.

Note that "the number of eigenvalues is $= n$" is not enough (repeated roots must be counted with multiplicity).

⭐ **(5)** is the deepest formulation: it translates "diagonalizable" into "the minimal polynomial has no repeated roots" (see "the minimal polynomial divides the characteristic polynomial") — this is used in the discussion of the spectral theorem and of Jordan form.
