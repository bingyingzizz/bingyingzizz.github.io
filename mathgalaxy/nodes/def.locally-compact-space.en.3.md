# locally compact space　`def.locally-compact-space`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=def.locally-compact-space)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./def.locally-compact-space.md)

**Examples**: every compact space (take $K = X$), $\mathbb{R}^{n}$, and every discrete space is locally compact; $\mathbb{Q}$ is not. A compact space is always locally compact, but not necessarily Hausdorff.

**Use**: when Hausdorffness is needed, combine this with `def.hausdorff`; without it the definition still stands, but the stronger property of having a base of compact neighbourhoods may fail.
