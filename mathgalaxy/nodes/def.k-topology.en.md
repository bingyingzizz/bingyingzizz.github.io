## Statement · **draft**
A subset $Y \subseteq X$ is called **$k$-open** (**$k$-closed**) if for every continuous map $f : S \to X$ (with $S$ compact Hausdorff), $f^{-1}(Y)$ is open (closed) in $S$.

The topology formed by all $k$-open sets on $X$ is called the **$k$-topology**; write $kX$ for "the same set with the topology replaced by the $k$-topology".
