# Symmetries of the Curvature Tensor　`prop.curvature-tensor-symmetries`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=prop.curvature-tensor-symmetries)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./prop.curvature-tensor-symmetries.md)

Without indices: $R(X,Y,Z,W) := \langle R(X,Y)Z,W\rangle$ is a symmetric bilinear form on $\bigwedge^{2}T_{p}M$ (the first two give antisymmetry, the third symmetry), and the fourth cuts out another $\binom{n}{4}$ dimensions.

## Notes · **draft**
**The first two have different sources**: antisymmetry in $X,Y$ is immediate from $[\nabla_{X},\nabla_{Y}]$ in the definition; antisymmetry in $Z,W$ uses **metric compatibility** ($\nabla g = 0$) — first prove $\langle R(X,Y)Z,Z\rangle = 0$ and then polarize.
