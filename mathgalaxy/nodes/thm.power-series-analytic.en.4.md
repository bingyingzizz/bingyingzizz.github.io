# termwise operations on power series　`thm.power-series-analytic`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=thm.power-series-analytic)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./thm.power-series-analytic.md)

⚠ "Uniform convergence" holds only on **closed subintervals** $[x_0 - r, x_0 + r]$, not on the whole open interval — near the endpoints uniform convergence fails.

⭐ This is the other side of Taylor's formula: Taylor's theorem says "$f$ can be approximated by polynomials"; here, if $f$ **itself** is a power series, those polynomials are its partial sums and they really do converge to $f$.
