## Statement · **draft**
Let $\{a_n\} \subseteq \mathbb{R}$, $s_N := \sum_{n=1}^{N} a_n$. Then $\sum a_n$ converges if and only if

$$\forall \varepsilon > 0,\ \exists N,\ \forall n > m \ge N :\ \Bigl| \sum_{k=m+1}^{n} a_k \Bigr| < \varepsilon$$

**Necessary condition**: $\sum a_n$ converges $\implies$ $a_n \to 0$.
