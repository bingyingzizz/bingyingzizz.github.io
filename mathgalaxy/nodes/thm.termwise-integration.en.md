## Statement · **draft**
Let $\{f_n\} \subseteq L^+$. Then

$$\int \sum_{n=1}^{\infty} f_n = \sum_{n=1}^{\infty} \int f_n$$

## Notes · **draft**
(Now $f_n \ge 0$, so both sides may be $\infty$; the conclusion is equivalent to: the anomaly "termwise integrals diverge while the total integral is finite" is impossible.)
