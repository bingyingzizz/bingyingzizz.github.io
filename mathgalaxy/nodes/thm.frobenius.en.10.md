# Frobenius theorem　`thm.frobenius`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=thm.frobenius)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./thm.frobenius.md)

$$\mathcal{D}=\operatorname{span}\{\partial_{1},\ldots,\partial_{k}\}.\qquad\blacksquare$$

**II. (2)$\implies$(3):** In flat coordinates, the tangent spaces of $N=\{x^{k+1}=\cdots=x^{n}=0\}$ are spanned precisely by $\partial_{1},\ldots,\partial_{k}$, so $N$ is an $k$-dimensional integral manifold through $p$. $\square$
