## Notes · **draft**
**Why "weak" Hausdorff.** Hausdorff asks that open neighbourhoods separate any two points; weak Hausdorff only asks that "images of compact pieces are closed" — a much weaker condition, yet exactly enough: the constructions appearing in $\mathrm{CGWH}$ (quotients, function spaces, fibred products) do not need full Hausdorffness.

⚠️ Weak Hausdorff **does not imply** Hausdorff (there are compactly generated weak Hausdorff spaces that are not Hausdorff); but it implies $T_{1}$, which is the second item above.
