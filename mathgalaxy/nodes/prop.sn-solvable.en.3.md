# solvability of symmetric groups　`prop.sn-solvable`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=prop.sn-solvable)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./prop.sn-solvable.md)

⭐ A splitting of $S_4$: $\{e\} \trianglelefteq V_4 \trianglelefteq A_4 \trianglelefteq S_4$, with quotients $\mathbb{Z}/2 \times \mathbb{Z}/2$, $\mathbb{Z}/3$, $\mathbb{Z}/2$, all abelian ✓.

For $S_3$ use $\{e\} \trianglelefteq A_3 \trianglelefteq S_3$, with quotients $\mathbb{Z}/3, \mathbb{Z}/2$ ✓.

⭐ Simplicity of $A_5$ can be proved completely elementarily: the conjugacy classes of $A_5$ have sizes $1, 15, 20, 12, 12$; a normal subgroup is a union of classes and its order divides $60$ — checking the possibilities leaves only $1$ and $60$.

The full proof is on the edge.
