## Notes · **draft**
**What it measures**: $H^{k} = 0$ means "closed implies exact"; $H^{k} \ne 0$ says there is a closed but non-exact form.

On $\mathbb{R}^{2}\setminus\{0\}$ the angle form $\frac{x\,\mathrm{d}y - y\,\mathrm{d}x}{x^{2}+y^{2}}$ is closed but not exact; its class generates $H^{1} \cong \mathbb{R}$ and measures "the number of turns around the origin".
