## Statement · **draft**
Let $(V,\theta)$ be a representation of $A$. On the dual space $V^*=\operatorname{Hom}_F(V,F)$ put $\theta^*(a)\ell=\ell\circ\theta(a)$. Since $\theta^*(ab)=\theta^*(b)\theta^*(a)$, the space $V^*$ is a representation of the opposite algebra $A^{\mathrm{op}}$, not generally of $A$. For a group representation, $\rho^*(g)=\rho(g^{-1})^T$ makes $V^*$ a representation of $G$.
