Statement

Let MMM be an RRR-module. The flat dimension of MMM, denoted fd⁡RM\operatorname{fd}_{R}MfdR​M, is found by asking whether MMM has a flat resolution of length nnn (an exact sequence 0→Fn→⋯→F0→M→00\to F_{n}\to\cdots\to F_{0}\to M\to 00→Fn​→⋯→F0​→M→0 with each FiF_{i}Fi​ flat) and taking the least such integer nnn. If no such integer exists, set fd⁡RM=∞\operatorname{fd}_{R}M=\inftyfdR​M=∞.

Notes

Basic relations: fd⁡M=0\operatorname{fd}M=0fdM=0 if and only if MMM is flat (see Flat Module). Since projective modules are flat, one always has fd⁡M≤pd⁡M\operatorname{fd}M\le\operatorname{pd}MfdM≤pdM (Projective Dimension).

Duality with injective dimension: fd⁡RM=id⁡RopHom⁡Z(M,Q/Z)\operatorname{fd}_{R}M=\operatorname{id}_{R^{\mathrm{op}}}\operatorname{Hom}_{\mathbb{Z}}(M,\mathbb{Q}/\mathbb{Z})fdR​M=idRop​HomZ​(M,Q/Z), so taking the character dual converts flat dimension into the corresponding injective dimension.

Global dimension: The weak global dimension of a ring is the supremum of the flat dimensions of all modules. Over a Noetherian ring, the weak global dimension equals the global dimension.

⚠ Previously, the notes in Injective Dimension only mentioned that flat dimension had no separate entry. This entry supplies the definition. Flat dimension and projective dimension are distinct notions.
