English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=def.sheaf)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./def.sheaf.md)

In the old language of topological spaces ($\mathcal{C} = \operatorname{Open}(X)^{\mathrm{op}}$), these two conditions read: if $s|_{U_{i}} = t|_{U_{i}}$ holds everywhere then $s = t$; and local sections $s_{i}$ that agree on overlaps glue into a global section.
