**Check of the special case.** When $X$ is locally compact each point has a compact neighbourhood $S_x$; $f$ open means $f(S_x)$ is a neighbourhood of $f(x)$, and $f$ surjective means $Y = \bigcup_x f(S_x)$ ✓; in $Y$ any compact set is covered by finitely many $f(S_{x_i})$, and one takes $S := \bigcup_i S_{x_i}$ (a finite union of compact Hausdorff spaces is still compact Hausdorff) ✓.
