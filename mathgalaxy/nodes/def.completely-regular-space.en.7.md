- completely regular $\iff$ $T_{3.5}$ and $T_1$: this is a definitional unfolding of the notion used here, free of charge; it does **not** derive $T_1$ from bare $T_{3.5}$.
- completely regular $\implies$ embedding in $[0,1]^I$: take the evaluation map of all continuous interval-valued functions; $T_1$ (points closed) together with point–closed separation gives injectivity, and point–closed separation recovers the induced topology. The product topology is used, not the product compactness theorem.
