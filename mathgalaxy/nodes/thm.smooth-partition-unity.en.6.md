**Step 2: Take a locally finite refinement**. The manifold $M$ is second countable, Hausdorff, and locally Euclidean, hence paracompact (the hypothesis of `prop.paracompact-partition-unity`). The open cover $\mathcal{U}$ therefore has a locally finite refinement $\{V_{\alpha}\}$, and we can require each $\overline{V_{\alpha}}$ to be compact and contained in a coordinate chart $W_{\alpha} \subseteq U_{\alpha}$. First refine by coordinate balls within $U_{\alpha}$, then refine again and shrink the closures.
