# Laplace expansion　`prop.det-laplace`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=prop.det-laplace)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./prop.det-laplace.md)

⭐ The "off-diagonal expansion equals zero" statement can be rewritten directly as the matrix identity $A \cdot \operatorname{adj} A = (\det A) I_n$ ($\operatorname{adj} A$ being the transpose of the cofactor matrix), that is, **when $\det A \ne 0$**, $A^{-1} = (\det A)^{-1} \operatorname{adj} A$ — Cramer's rule and the inverse matrix formula both come from here (when $A = 0$, $\operatorname{adj} A = 0$, and neither side is defined).
