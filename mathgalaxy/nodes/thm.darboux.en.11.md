# Darboux’s Theorem　`thm.darboux`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=thm.darboux)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./thm.darboux.md)

 By $d\omega_{t} = 0$, $\iota_{X_{t}}\omega_{t} = -\theta$, and $\eta = d\theta$, the parenthesized expression equals $-d\theta + 0 + d\theta = 0$. Thus $\varphi_{t}^{*}\omega_{t}$ is independent of $t$ and equals its value at $t = 0$, namely $\varphi_{0}^{*}\omega_{0} = \omega_{0}$. Taking $t = 1$ gives $\varphi_{1}^{*}\omega = \omega_{0}$. Hence $(q_{i} \circ \varphi_{1}^{-1},\, p_{i} \circ \varphi_{1}^{-1})$ are Darboux coordinates on $\varphi_{1}(U)$. ∎
