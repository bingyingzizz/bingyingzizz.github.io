# Stability of Syzygies　`prop.syzygy-stability`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=prop.syzygy-stability)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./prop.syzygy-stability.md)

⭐ **Making the construction canonical.** Once differences by projective direct summands are quotiented out, $\Omega^{n}$ becomes a genuine functor. The resulting quotient category is the **stable module category**, where syzygies are well defined up to isomorphism. This also explains the role of stability in $K_{0}$, stable homotopy theory, and $K$-theory.

📌 An immediate consequence is that $\operatorname{pd}_{R} M \le n \iff \Omega^{n}M$ is projective. The result “Characterizing Projective Dimension by the Vanishing of Ext Groups” uses this as its main criterion.
