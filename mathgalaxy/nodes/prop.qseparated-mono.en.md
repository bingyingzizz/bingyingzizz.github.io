## Statement · **draft**
If $X$ is a **quasi-separated** condensed set, then the canonical map

$$X \longrightarrow \underline{X(\cdot)}$$

(sending $X$ to the condensed set represented by its underlying topological space $X(\cdot)$) is a **monomorphism**.
