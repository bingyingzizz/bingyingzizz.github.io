# the integrally closed subring is determined by the spectrum　`prop.huber-subring-recovery`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=prop.huber-subring-recovery)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./prop.huber-subring-recovery.md)

gives a **bijection** between "integrally closed subrings $A^+ \subseteq A$" and "regions of the form $\bigcap_{f \in I} U_{1,f}$, i.e. subsets $U$", with inverse

$$U \longmapsto A^+ = \{\, f \in A : \forall x \in U,\ |f(x)| \le 1 \,\}.$$

In particular $A^+ = \{f \in A : \forall x \in \operatorname{Spa}(A,A^+),\ |f(x)| \le 1\}$.

## Notes · **draft**
⭐ This says: **$A^+$ is not extra data but the "unit-ball sections" over the spectrum**.
