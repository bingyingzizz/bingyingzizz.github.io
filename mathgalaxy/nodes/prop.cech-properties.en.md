## Statement · **draft**
1. $\widehat{H}$ is **left exact** (preserves finite limits);
2. $T$ is **separated** as a presheaf $\implies$ $\widehat{H}(T)$; $T$ separated $\implies$ $\widehat{H}(T)$ is a **sheaf**;
3. $T$ is separated (a sheaf) $\iff$ $T \to \widehat{H}(T)$ is a monomorphism (isomorphism).
