## Notes · **draft**
**Proof.** We have $E^{0}_{p,q} = \operatorname{Gr}^{p}K^{p+q}$ ✓, and on page $0$ the differential $d_{0}$ is the differential induced on the filtration quotient (the differential of $K^{\bullet}$ descends to the graded piece) ✓.

Taking cohomology gives $E^{1}_{p,q} = H^{p+q}(\operatorname{Gr}^{p}K^{\bullet})$ ✓.

∎
