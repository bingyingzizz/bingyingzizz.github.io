# Simplicial Method for Sheaf Cohomology　`method.simplicial-cohomology`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=method.simplicial-cohomology)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./method.simplicial-cohomology.md)

⭐ **This is the modern form of the Godement decomposition, and the entry point of the $\infty$-categorical method**: in $\infty$-categorical language, "hypercover" is replaced by "simplicial decomposition by $X$", and cohomology is the homotopy groups of the mapping space of that simplicial object. The whole computation of sheaf cohomology in derived algebraic geometry follows this route.
