# Principal Bundle　`def.principal-bundle`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=def.principal-bundle)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./def.principal-bundle.md)

1. **fibers are preserved**: $\pi(u\cdot g) = \pi(u)$;
2. **the action is free and transitive on each fiber**: $u\cdot g = u \iff g = e$, and any two points of the same fiber $P_{b}$ differ by a right multiplication (so $P_{b}$ is homeomorphic to $G$);
3. **it is locally trivial and $G$-equivariant**: every point has a neighbourhood $U$ and a diffeomorphism $\Phi : \pi^{-1}(U)\to U\times G$, written $\Phi(u) = \bigl(\pi(u),\varphi(u)\bigr)$, satisfying

$$\Phi(u\cdot g) = \bigl(\pi(u),\ \varphi(u)\,g\bigr).$$
