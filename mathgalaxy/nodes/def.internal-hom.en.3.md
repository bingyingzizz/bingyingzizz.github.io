# internal hom　`def.internal-hom`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=def.internal-hom)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./def.internal-hom.md)

The "representable" step uses only one fact: that presheaf **preserves all limits** (hence, by the criterion for representable functors, it has a representing object).

Two corollaries: $\operatorname{Hom}_{\mathbb{Z}}(M, N) = \operatorname{Hom}_{\mathbb{Z}}(M, N)(\mathbf{1})$ — in other words **$\mathcal{T}(\mathbf{Ab})$ is enriched over itself**;

and $\operatorname{Hom}_{\mathbb{Z}}(\mathbb{Z}\cdot X, M) \cong \operatorname{Hom}(X, M)$.
