## Notes · **draft**
The difference between the two formulas is exactly the two levels of the coreflective subcategory: in general $k$ is not closed under products, and one must apply $k$ once more to the product; when $Y$ is locally compact Hausdorff, (by the theorem on quotient maps and products) $k$ commutes with "multiplying by $Y$". **This is why products in $k\mathbf{Top}$ must be taken as $k(X \times Y)$ rather than $X \times Y$.**
