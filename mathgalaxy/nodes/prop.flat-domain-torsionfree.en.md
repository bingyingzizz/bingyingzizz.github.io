## Statement · **draft**
Let $R$ be an **integral domain** and $M$ a flat $R$-module. Then $M$ is **torsion-free**: for $r \ne 0$ and $m \in M$, $r m = 0$ implies $m = 0$. Equivalently, multiplication by $r$, $M \xrightarrow{\;\cdot r\;} M$, is injective.
