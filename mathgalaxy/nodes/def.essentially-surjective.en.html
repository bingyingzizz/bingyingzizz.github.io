def.essentially-surjective

Statement

A functor F:C→C′F : \mathcal{C} \to \mathcal{C}'F:C→C′ is essentially surjective if for every object of C′\mathcal{C}'C′, say X′X'X′, there is an object of C\mathcal{C}C, say XXX, with

F(X)≅X′F(X) \cong X'F(X)≅X′

Notes

Note that this is ≅\cong≅, not ===: the requirement is that the image of FFF be isomorphic to every object, not that the image of FFF be all objects. This single isomorphism is exactly where equivalence is laxer than isomorphism — and precisely what makes it useful, since most interesting equivalences are not isomorphisms.
