[in graph](../#n=def.triangulation)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./def.triangulation.md)

📌 The definition does not require $K$ to be finite. A triangulation of a compact space is automatically finite, while noncompact spaces (such as open surfaces) require **locally finite** triangulations.
