# Contraction Mapping　`def.contraction`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=def.contraction)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./def.contraction.md)

⭐ Contractions are the **only** class of maps whose existence proof comes with an algorithm and an error estimate (see `thm.banach-fixed-point`) — this is why they occupy such a high place in analysis: the inverse mapping theorem, the implicit function theorem, the Picard–Lindelöf theorem for ODEs and the Bellman equation all use them as their engine.
