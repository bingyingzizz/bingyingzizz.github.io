Statement

Let KKK be a field, VVV a vector space over KKK, and S⊆VS \subseteq VS⊆V.

  • SSS is linearly independent: every finite subset of SSS is linearly independent, i.e. there are no c1c_{1}c1​…cn∈Kc_{n} \in Kcn​∈K, not all zero, with c1v1+⋯+cnvn=0c_{1}v_{1} + \cdots + c_{n}v_{n} = 0c1​v1​+⋯+cn​vn​=0.
  • SSS spans VVV: every vector in VVV is a linear combination of finitely many vectors in SSS.
  • SSS is a basis of VVV: SSS is both linearly independent and spans VVV.

Note that linear independence is defined finite-subset by finite-subset — this is exactly why it is “finitary”.
