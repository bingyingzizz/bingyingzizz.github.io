Conversely, every metrizable space has a $\sigma$-locally finite base, so this is a **necessary and sufficient condition**.

## Notes · **draft**
**Why this is stronger than Urysohn's theorem**: A countable base can be split into countably many discrete families by placing each basic open set in a singleton family. Thus second countability is a special case of having a $\sigma$-locally finite base. Here the hypothesis is weakened to $\sigma$-local finiteness, with the same conclusion.
