# Constant Rank Theorem　`cor.rank-theorem`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=cor.rank-theorem)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./cor.rank-theorem.md)

⚠ The hypothesis of **constant rank** cannot be dropped: $f(x) = x^{2}$ ($\mathbb{R} \to \mathbb{R}$) has rank $1$ at points $x \ne 0$ and rank $0$ at $0$, so its local shape is not a standard form (it is a "fold").

The full proof is on the edge (first build coordinates for $r$ that turn the first $r$ components of $f$ into coordinates, then eliminate the remaining components by the implicit function theorem).
