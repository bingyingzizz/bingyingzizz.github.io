Statement

Let ν\nuν be a signed measure on (X,M)(X, \mathcal{M})(X,M). Then there exist P,N∈MP, N \in \mathcal{M}P,N∈M such that

P∪N=X,P∩N=∅,P \cup N = X, \quad P \cap N = \emptyset, P∪N=X,P∩N=∅,

where **PPP is positive and NNN is negative**.

If P′,N′P', N'P′,N′ is another such pair, then P△P′=N△N′P \triangle P' = N \triangle N'P△P′=N△N′ is a ν\nuν-null set.

Notes

This is "cutting the space in two along the sign": ν\nuν is nonnegative on PPP and nonpositive on NNN.

⚠ "P△P′P \triangle P'P△P′ is a null set" only says the decomposition is **unique up to null sets** — strictly speaking a Hahn decomposition is not unique (one may move null sets freely between PPP and NNN), but the ν\nuν values of all measurable sets are exactly the same.

⭐ With it, "signed measure" reduces completely to "two genuine measures" — which is exactly the content of the Jordan decomposition below.
