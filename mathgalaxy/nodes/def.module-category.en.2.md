# category of modules　`def.module-category`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=def.module-category)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./def.module-category.md)

Right $A$-modules form $\mathbf{Mod}\text{-}A$; when $A$ is commutative the two categories are equivalent.

## Notes · **draft**
**The model room of homological algebra**: $A\text{-}\mathbf{Mod}$ is abelian, with kernels, cokernels, and strict monomorphisms and epimorphisms; free modules give enough projectives, Baer's criterion gives enough injectives, and it is even a Grothendieck category.

**Special cases**: $\mathbb Z\text{-}\mathbf{Mod}\simeq\mathbf{Ab}$; when $k$ is a field, $k\text{-}\mathbf{Mod}$ is just $\mathbf{Vect}_k$.
