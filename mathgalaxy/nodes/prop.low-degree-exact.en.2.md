# Low-Degree Exact Sequence　`prop.low-degree-exact`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=prop.low-degree-exact)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./prop.low-degree-exact.md)

- If $G$ acts **trivially** on $M$, then $\delta^{0} = 0$, so $M^{G} = M$ and $H^{1}(G, M) = \operatorname{Hom}(G, M)$;
- For a short exact sequence $0 \to A \to B \to C \to 0$ of $G$-modules, there is an exact sequence

$$0 \to A^{G} \to B^{G} \to C^{G} \xrightarrow{\ \partial\ } H^{1}(G, A) \to H^{1}(G, B) \to H^{1}(G, C).$$
