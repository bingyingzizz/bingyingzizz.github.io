# Improper Integral　`def.improper-integral`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=def.improper-integral)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./def.improper-integral.md)

⚠ Splitting is necessary in the mixed case: defining $\int_{-\infty}^{+\infty} f$ as $\lim_{R \to \infty}\int_{-R}^{R}$ loses phenomena that are not absolutely convergent ($\int_{-R}^{R} x\,dx = 0$ for every $R$, while both $\int_0^{+\infty}$ and $\int_{-\infty}^{0}$ diverge).

This distinction is called the **principal value**.
