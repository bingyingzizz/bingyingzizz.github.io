# Schur Triangularization　`thm.schur-triangularization`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=thm.schur-triangularization)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./thm.schur-triangularization.md)

**A shortcut to the spectral theorem**: If $A$ is normal and $U^{*}AU = T$ is upper triangular, then $T$ is also normal. An upper triangular normal matrix $\implies$ must be diagonal (compare the two sides of $(TT^{*})_{11}$ to see that the off-diagonal entries of the first row are zero, then use induction).

⚠️ In the real case, **one cannot always obtain a real diagonal or triangular form** with only $1\times1$ blocks. The rotation example $90^\circ$ shows why $2\times 2$ blocks are necessary.
