## Statement · **draft**
Let $f:[0,\infty)\to\mathbb{C}$ be bounded and locally integrable, and suppose its Laplace transform

$$g(z)=\int_{0}^{\infty}f(t)\,e^{-zt}\,\mathrm{d}t\qquad(\operatorname{Re}z>0)$$

extends analytically to some open set containing the closed half-plane $\{\operatorname{Re}z\ge0\}$. Then the improper integral $\int_{0}^{\infty}f(t)\,\mathrm{d}t$ converges and equals $g(0)$.
