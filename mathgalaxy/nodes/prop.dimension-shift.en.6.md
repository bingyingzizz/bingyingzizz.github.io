# Dimension Shift　`prop.dimension-shift`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=prop.dimension-shift)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./prop.dimension-shift.md)

**Combined with syzygies**: $\operatorname{Ext}^{n}(M, N) \cong \operatorname{Ext}^{1}(\Omega^{n-1}M, N)$, and with "classification of first-order extensions" this turns higher Ext into "extensions of $N$ by $\Omega^{n-1}M$".
