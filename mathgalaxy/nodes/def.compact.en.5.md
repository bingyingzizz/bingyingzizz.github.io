# compact　`def.compact`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=def.compact)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./def.compact.md)

Examples: $[a, b] \subseteq \mathbb{R}$ is compact (Heine–Borel, `cor.heine-borel`); $\mathbb{R}$ is not compact (the cover $\{(-n, n)\}$ has no finite subcover); $(0, 1]$ is not compact. Standard examples in general topology are in `def.chaus` and `thm.tykhonov` (arbitrary products remain compact).
