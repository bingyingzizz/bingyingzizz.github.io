Statement

Let XXX be a topological space, TTT a presheaf on XXX, and x∈Xx \in Xx∈X. Then sheafification does not change stalks:

(T#)x  =  Tx.\bigl(T^{\#}\bigr)_{x} \;=\; T_{x}.(T#)x​=Tx​.

Notes

Proof. The stalk is the filtered colimit over the open neighbourhoods of xxx ✓, and sheafification preserves filtered colimits (it is a left adjoint, hence colimit-preserving) ✓; both sides reduce to the values of TTT on the same family of neighbourhoods ✓. ∎
