## Abstract · **draft**
For a smooth manifold $M$ and each $k \ge 0$ there is an isomorphism $\Phi^{k} : H^{k}_{\mathrm{dR}}(M) \to H^{k}_{\mathrm{sing}}(M;\mathbb{R})$ sending $[\omega]$ to the functional $[c] \mapsto \int_{c} \omega$ on smooth singular chains. It is compatible with pullbacks, so these maps form a natural isomorphism between de Rham cohomology and singular cohomology with real coefficients, welding analysis, geometry and topology together.
