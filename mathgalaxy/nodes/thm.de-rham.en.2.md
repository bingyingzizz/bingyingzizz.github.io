## Abstract · **draft**
Integrating forms pairs de Rham cohomology, closed forms modulo exact ones, with singular homology, and the de Rham theorem says the pairing is perfect: for every $k$ there is an isomorphism $H^{k}_{\mathrm{dR}}(M)\to H^{k}_{\mathrm{sing}}(M;\mathbb{R})$ sending $[\omega]$ to the functional $[c]\mapsto\int_{c}\omega$. The maps are compatible with pullbacks, so they form a natural isomorphism of graded algebras. The theorem welds analysis, geometry and topology into a single object.
