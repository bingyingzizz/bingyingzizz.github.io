# Regular Surface and Gauss Map　`def.regular-surface`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=def.regular-surface)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./def.regular-surface.md)

**The tangent map is not the identity on the tangent plane**: it is a linear injection from the tangent space of the parameter domain into $\mathbb{R}^{3}$, whose image is exactly the tangent plane. When writing $I$ and $II$, both arguments are first sent to the tangent plane by $\mathrm{d}r$, and then the inner product is taken.

⚠️ A parametrization is not the surface itself: changing $(u,v)$ changes the expressions for $E,F,G$, but not the tensor that is the first fundamental form. So the conclusions in this package are always written "relative to a frame", independent of the parametrization.
