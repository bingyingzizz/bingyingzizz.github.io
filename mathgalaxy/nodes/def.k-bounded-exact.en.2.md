# K-bounded exact　`def.k-bounded-exact`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=def.k-bounded-exact)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./def.k-bounded-exact.md)

If $M^{\bullet}$ is boundedly exact at every $M^{n}$ with bound $K$, it is called **$K$-bounded acyclic**.

## Notes · **draft**
**How this differs from ordinary exactness.** Ordinary exactness says that every cycle is a boundary (an existence statement). In $K$-bounded exactness, the preimage $s'$ must exist and **satisfy a bound**. The error is at most $K\|ds\|$ plus an arbitrary $\varepsilon$.
