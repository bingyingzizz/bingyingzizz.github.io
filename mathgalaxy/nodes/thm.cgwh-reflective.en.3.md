# CGWH Is a Reflective Subcategory of CG　`thm.cgwh-reflective`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=thm.cgwh-reflective)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./thm.cgwh-reflective.md)

⭐ This gives the complete chain for "how to repair a space": $k$-ification ($X \mapsto kX$, producing $\mathrm{CG}$) $\mapsto$ quotienting by the smallest closed equivalence relation ($kX \mapsto kX/R$, producing $\mathrm{CGWH}$).

Both steps are **functorial**, so isomorphisms, limits, and colimits in $\mathrm{CGWH}$ can be transported from $\mathbf{Top}$.

⭐ This is why modern algebraic topology works in $\mathrm{CGWH}$ by default: $\mathrm{CGWH}$ is closed under **finite products**, the function space $kC(X,Y)$ stays inside it, and it is cartesian closed — whereas $\mathbf{Top}$ satisfies none of these.
