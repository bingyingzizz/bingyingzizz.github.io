# Sylow theorems　`thm.sylow`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=thm.sylow)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./thm.sylow.md)

**(3) Counting**: The number of Sylow $p$-subgroups, denoted by $n_p$, satisfies

$$n_p \equiv 1 \pmod{p}, \qquad n_p \mid m, \qquad n_p = [G : N_G(P)]$$

(Here $P$ is any Sylow $p$-subgroup and $N_G(P)$ is its normalizer.) In particular, $n_p = 1 \iff P \trianglelefteq G$.

## Notes · **draft**
⭐ Sylow's theorem is a **partial converse to Lagrange's theorem**. Lagrange says that $|H|$ must divide $|G|$. The claim that $d \mid |G|$ $\implies$ has a subgroup of order $d$ is false. Sylow guarantees **a subgroup of the largest possible $p$-power order, unique up to conjugacy**.
