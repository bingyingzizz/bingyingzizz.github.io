**Where to be careful**: $S$ uses only **compact Hausdorff** spaces (not "arbitrary compact spaces"). For compact Hausdorff spaces are closed under quotients and products (@@thm.tykhonov@@, @@prop.quotient-of-compact@@), so they suffice as probes; replacing them by "all compact spaces" would make the equivalence of (2) and (3) fail.
