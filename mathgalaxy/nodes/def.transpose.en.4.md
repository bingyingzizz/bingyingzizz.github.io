# Transpose of a Matrix　`def.transpose`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=def.transpose)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./def.transpose.md)

⚠️ The transpose depends on the **choice of basis** (it uses the dual basis), whereas the conjugate transpose ($A^{*}$) of the next section equals "the matrix of the adjoint operator" only in an orthonormal basis. Confusing the two is the most common accident for beginners.

⚠️ Do not remember $(AB)^{T} = B^{T}A^{T}$ as $A^{T}B^{T}$: the latter can hold only when $A, B$ are both square and happen to commute, and in general it fails.
