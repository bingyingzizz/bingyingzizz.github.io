# Banach abelian group　`def.banach-ab-group`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=def.banach-ab-group)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./def.banach-ab-group.md)

⭐ Taking the Hausdorff quotient and then completing is a **reflection** from the category of seminormed abelian groups onto the subcategory of Banach abelian groups. Quantitative properties such as $K$-bounded exactness pass through this reflection **with the same bounds**.
