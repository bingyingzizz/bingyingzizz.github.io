# Universal Property of the Tensor Product　`prop.module-tensor-universal`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=prop.module-tensor-universal)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./prop.module-tensor-universal.md)

$$\operatorname{Bil}_{\mathbb{Z}}(M \times N,\ P) \;\cong\; \operatorname{Hom}_{\mathbb{Z}}\bigl(M \otimes_{\mathbb{Z}} N,\ P\bigr),$$

bilinear maps on the left correspond one-to-one to group homomorphisms on the right.

## Notes · **stale**
**Proof.** First build the tensor product on free modules: take $\mathbb{Z}\cdot(M \times N)$ (the free abelian group) and quotient by the "bilinear relations"

$$(m + m', n) \sim (m, n) + (m', n), \quad (m, n + n') \sim (m, n) + (m, n'), \quad (am, n) \sim a(m,n) \sim (m, an) ✓$$
