## Notes · **draft**
- (a) equivalence - (b) the same induced topology - (c) the same Cauchy sequences - (d) the same convergent sequences.

⚠️ The relation is asymmetric, and its direction runs against intuition: a **strong** norm (larger $p$) has smaller balls, **more** open sets, harder convergence, and **fewer** Cauchy sequences. So "passing to a stronger norm" **removes** convergent objects — which is exactly why one chooses the weakest norm that suffices (e.g. the weak topology).
