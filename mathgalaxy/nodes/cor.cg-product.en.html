Statement

If XXX is compactly generated and YYY is locally compact Hausdorff, then X×YX \times YX×Y is compactly generated.

Notes

⚠️ The local compactness of YYY cannot be dropped: products of general topological spaces do not preserve quotient maps, and "−×Y-\times Y−×Y preserves colimits" is precisely the equivalent formulation of this fact.
