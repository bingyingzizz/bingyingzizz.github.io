The first two say $F^{\ast}$ is a graded algebra homomorphism, the third that it is also a chain map; together: **$F^{\ast}$ is a homomorphism of differential graded algebras**.

## Notes · **draft**
**Direction**: $F : U\to V$ induces $F^{\ast} : \Omega^{k}(V)\to\Omega^{k}(U)$, opposite to $F$. This is the precise meaning of "forms are covariant"; it is also why pullback is defined along any smooth map, whereas pushing vector fields forward generally requires $F$ to be a diffeomorphism (or at least requires the fields to be $F$-related).
