## Statement · **draft**
Let $S$ be a closed surface. Remove an open disk from $S$ and identify antipodal points on the boundary circle ($x\sim -x$). The resulting closed surface is obtained by **adding a crosscap** to $S$. Equivalently, adding a crosscap means taking the connected sum with a projective plane: $S\mapsto S\#\mathbb{R}P^{2}$. It is also equivalent to gluing a Möbius band along the boundary of the removed disk.
