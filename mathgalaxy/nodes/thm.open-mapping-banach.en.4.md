Move this point back to the origin, in two small steps: **translation** and **subtraction**. Take $x_0 \in k B_E$ with $\|y_0 - u(x_0)\| < \delta/2$ (possible since $y_0$ lies in the closure). **Translation**: for $\|z\| < \delta/2$ we have $\|z + (u(x_0) - y_0)\| \le \|z\| + \|u(x_0) - y_0\| < \delta/2 + \delta/2 = \delta$, hence $u(x_0) + z = y_0 + \bigl(z + u(x_0) - y_0\bigr) \in B_F(y_0, \delta) \subseteq \overline{u(kB_E)}$, that is, $B_F(u(x_0), \delta/2) \subseteq \overline{u(kB_E)}$. **Subtraction**: from $u(x_0) + z \in \overline{u(kB_E)}$
