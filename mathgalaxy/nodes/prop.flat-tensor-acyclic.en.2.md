## Notes · **draft**
**Proof.** Flatness says exactly that $P \otimes_{\mathbb{Z}} -$ is **exact** ✓. A "bounded below + acyclic" complex is an exact sequence ✓, and an exact functor sends exact sequences to exact sequences, so $P \otimes M$ is also exact, that is, acyclic ✓. ∎
