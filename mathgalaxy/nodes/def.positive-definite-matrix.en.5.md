# Positive Definite Matrix　`def.positive-definite-matrix`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=def.positive-definite-matrix)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./def.positive-definite-matrix.md)

⭐ **Positive definite $\implies$ admits a square root**: there is a unique positive semidefinite $\sqrt{A}$ with $(\sqrt A)^{2} = A$ (take square roots of the eigenvalues in an orthonormal basis). This is precisely where $A = UP$ and the factor $P$ in the polar decomposition come from (thm.polar-decomposition).

⚠️ The sentence "$A^{T}A$ is always positive semidefinite" must be written $A^{*}A$ over the complex field; and it is positive definite exactly when the columns of $A$ are linearly independent (otherwise there is a nonzero kernel vector). This point recurs in regression and least squares.
