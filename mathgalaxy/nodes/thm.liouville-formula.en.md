## Statement · **draft**
In plain terms, **geodesic curvature is the turning rate of the curve minus the turning rate of the frame due to the metric**. The latter is determined solely by the partial derivatives of $E, G$. Thus the metric, that is, $I$, suffices to compute the geodesic curvature of any curve, independently of how the surface is embedded in $\mathbb{R}^{3}$.
