[in graph](../#n=def.ideal)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./def.ideal.md)

⭐ **Comparison table**: to quotient a **group** and get a group, one quotients by a **normal subgroup**; to quotient a **ring** and get a ring, one quotients by an **ideal**. (As an additive subgroup, $I$ is of course automatically normal — the additive group is abelian — so no extra condition is needed on that side.)
