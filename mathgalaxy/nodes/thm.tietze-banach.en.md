## Statement · **draft**
Let $X$ be a **normal** topological space, let $K \subseteq X$ be a **compact** subset, and let $V$ be a **real Banach space**. Then the restriction map

$$C(X,\ V) \twoheadrightarrow C(K,\ V)$$

is **surjective**. Every continuous map $K \to V$ extends to $X$.
