## Notes · **draft**
What generates it is really two kinds of coverings: **finite disjoint unions** ($\coprod_{i \in I} X_{i} \cong X$) and **surjections** $(X_{i} \to X)$. The "pre" in the name is because it is **coarser** than the standard topology — but still subcanonical.
