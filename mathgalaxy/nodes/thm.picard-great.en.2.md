$$\#\left(\mathbb{C} \setminus f(U)\right) \le 1.$$

## Notes · **draft**
**Relation to the great and little theorems**: the little theorem is the special case “singularity at $\infty$”, and the great theorem reduces to it by moving an arbitrary essential singularity to $\infty$ with a Möbius transformation (whence the name).

**Source of the proof**: again Bloch's theorem or the modular function, not expanded here — the node and the edge state explicitly “reduction by the little Picard theorem + transformations, with the deep input recorded as external”.
