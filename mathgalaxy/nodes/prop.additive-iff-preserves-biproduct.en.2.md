# Additive Functors Preserve Zero Objects and Biproducts　`prop.additive-iff-preserves-biproduct`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=prop.additive-iff-preserves-biproduct)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./prop.additive-iff-preserves-biproduct.md)

$F$ is **additive** $\iff$ $F$ **preserves the zero object and all direct sums** $\iff$ $F$ **preserves all finite products** $\iff$ $F$ **preserves all finite coproducts**

## Notes · **draft**
⭐ Practical value: to check that a functor is additive, it suffices to check that it **preserves biproducts**, without touching the group structure on $\operatorname{Hom}$.

**The equivalence of the four relies entirely on "in an additive category, finite product = finite coproduct = biproduct"**:
