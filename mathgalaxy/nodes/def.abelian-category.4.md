## English abstract · **stale** (the Chinese wording changed since this translation) · machine-drafted, pending author review; Chinese is authoritative（续）
The ladder of definitions runs $\text{preadditive} \to \text{zero object} \to \text{direct sums} \to \text{additive} \to \text{preabelian} \to \text{abelian}$, where preadditive means $\mathbf{Ab}$-enriched with bilinear composition, preabelian means that kernels and cokernels exist, and abelian means any one of four equivalent conditions: every monomorphism is regular; $N \cong \ker(M \to \operatorname{coker} i)$ for $i : N \rightarrowtail M$; every map factors uniquely as an epimorphism
