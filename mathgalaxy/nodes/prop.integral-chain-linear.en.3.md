## Notes · **draft**
**Why this deserves its own node**: it is the legal basis for every "split and compute" operation — splitting $\omega$ into components, splitting a chain into cubes, splitting a region into small pieces all use these two linearities. Without it, Stokes' theorem could only be stated for a single cube, never "for a chain".

**Pairing viewpoint**: $\Omega^{k}(M)$ is a vector space over the reals (infinite-dimensional), and $C_{k}(M)$ is a free abelian group; the two pair to $\mathbb{R}$, each linearly.
