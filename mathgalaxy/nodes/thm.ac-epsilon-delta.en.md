## Statement · **draft**
Let $\nu$ be a **finite** signed measure and $\mu$ a measure. Then

$$\nu \ll  \mu \iff \forall\varepsilon > 0, \exists\delta > 0 : \mu(E) < \delta \implies |\nu(E)| \le \varepsilon$$
