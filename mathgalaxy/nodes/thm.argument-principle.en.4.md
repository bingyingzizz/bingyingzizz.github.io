# argument principle　`thm.argument-principle`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=thm.argument-principle)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./thm.argument-principle.md)

⭐ A by-product: $Z - P$ is **invariant** under small deformations of the curve (as long as no zero or pole is swept), because it is an integer and varies continuously.

📌 Every technique for "counting zeros" (Rouché, Hurwitz) starts from this identity; it also explains why zeros and poles can cancel each other at the level of integrals.
