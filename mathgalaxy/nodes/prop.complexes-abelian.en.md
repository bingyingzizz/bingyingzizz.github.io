## Statement · **draft**
Let $\mathcal{A}$ be an **abelian category**.

Then the category of cochain complexes $\mathcal{C}(\mathcal{A})$ is **also abelian** (the same holds for the bounded cases $\mathcal{C}^{+}(\mathcal{A})$, $\mathcal{C}^{-}(\mathcal{A})$, $\mathcal{C}^{b}(\mathcal{A})$).
