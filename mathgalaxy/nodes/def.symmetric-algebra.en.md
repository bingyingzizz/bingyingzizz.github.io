## Statement · **draft**
In parallel with the exterior algebra: quotient the **tensor algebra** $T^{\bullet}V$ by the **homogeneous two-sided ideal** generated by $v \otimes w - w \otimes v$; its **$k$-th piece** is called the **symmetric power** $\operatorname{Sym}^{k}V$ (the image is written $v \cdot w$, and is commutative); the direct sum of the pieces is the **symmetric algebra**
