## Notes · **draft**
Maschke's theorem gives that $F[G]$ is semisimple, so the regular module decomposes as $F[G]\cong\bigoplus_i n_iV_i$.
By cor.character-multiplicity, $n_i=\langle\chi_{\mathrm{reg}},\chi_{V_i}\rangle$; since $\chi_{\mathrm{reg}}(1)=|G|$ and $\chi_{\mathrm{reg}}(g)=0$ for $g\ne1$, we get $n_i=\frac1{|G|}|G|\chi_{V_i}(1)=\dim V_i$.
Evaluating at $g=1$ and at $g\ne1$ gives the two identities.
