Statement

Let M,NM, NM,N be condensed abelian groups and SSS a Stonean space. Then there is a natural spectral sequence

E1p,q=⨁i=1rpHq(Msp,i×S, N)  ⟹  Ext⁡Zp+q(M,N)(S).E^{p,q}_{1} = \bigoplus_{i=1}^{r_{p}} H^{q}\bigl(M^{s_{p,i}} \times S,\ N\bigr) \implies \operatorname{Ext}^{p+q}_{\mathbb{Z}}(M, N)(S).E1p,q​=i=1⨁rp​​Hq(Msp,i​×S, N)⟹ExtZp+q​(M,N)(S).

Notes

Construction.

Base-change the Breen–Deligne decomposition F(M)∙F(M)^{\bullet}F(M)∙ along SSS (tensor with Z[S]\mathbb{Z}[S]Z[S]).

Since Z[S]\mathbb{Z}[S]Z[S] is flat, F(M)∙⋅S→M⋅SF(M)^{\bullet} \cdot S \to M \cdot SF(M)∙⋅S→M⋅S is still a quasi-isomorphism;

then apply the section formula over Stonean spaces to replace Ext⁡(M⋅S,N)\operatorname{Ext}(M \cdot S, N)Ext(M⋅S,N) by Ext⁡(M,N)(S)\operatorname{Ext}(M, N)(S)Ext(M,N)(S).

Take the standard spectral sequence of the "degree filtration on the decomposition"; its E1E_{1}E1​ terms are the Ext of the individual free objects.

How to read the shape of E1E_{1}E1​: Msp,iM^{s_{p,i}}Msp,i​ is "the product of sp,is_{p,i}sp,i​ copies of MMM", Msp,i×SM^{s_{p,i}} \times SMsp,i​×S pairs it with the test space SSS; Hq(−,N)H^{q}(-, N)Hq(−,N) is condensed cohomology on that space.

That is: convert Ext into the cohomology of a batch of spaces, and the cohomology of spaces can be killed by the acyclicity theorems of §8.1–8.2.

⭐ This is the computational path: "to compute Ext, first expand into a spectral sequence, then kill each entry's cohomology according to the type of space (Stone / compact Hausdorff / locally compact) using acyclicity theorems". Every computational theorem after §8.3 is a special case of this spectral sequence.

Proof. Take a Breen–Deligne decomposition of MMM, F(M)∙↠MF(M)^{\bullet} \twoheadrightarrow MF(M)∙↠M, base-change along the Stonean space SSS (tensor with Z[S]\mathbb{Z}[S]Z[S]), and apply to the functor Hom⁡Z(−,N)\operatorname{Hom}_{\mathbb{Z}}(-, N)HomZ​(−,N) the standard spectral sequence of "resolve first, then evaluate on the test space":

E1p,q=Ext⁡Zq(F(M)p⋅S, N)  ⟹  Ext⁡Zp+q(F(M)∙⋅S, N).E^{p,q}_{1} = \operatorname{Ext}^{q}_{\mathbb{Z}}\bigl(F(M)^{p}\cdot S,\ N\bigr) \;\Longrightarrow\; \operatorname{Ext}^{p+q}_{\mathbb{Z}}\bigl(F(M)^{\bullet}\cdot S,\ N\bigr).E1p,q​=ExtZq​(F(M)p⋅S, N)⟹ExtZp+q​(F(M)∙⋅S, N).
  • Left side: F(M)p⋅S=⨁iZ⋅Msp,i⊗Z[S]=⨁iZ⋅(Msp,i×S)F(M)^{p}\cdot S = \bigoplus_{i} \mathbb{Z}\cdot M^{s_{p,i}} \otimes \mathbb{Z}[S] = \bigoplus_{i} \mathbb{Z}\cdot\bigl(M^{s_{p,i}}\times S\bigr)F(M)p⋅S=⨁i​Z⋅Msp,i​⊗Z[S]=⨁i​Z⋅(Msp,i​×S), so "condensed cohomology = Ext of free objects" gives Ext⁡Zq(Z⋅(Msp,i×S),N)=Hq(Msp,i×S, N)\operatorname{Ext}^{q}_{\mathbb{Z}}(\mathbb{Z}\cdot(M^{s_{p,i}}\times S), N) = H^{q}\bigl(M^{s_{p,i}}\times S,\ N\bigr)ExtZq​(Z⋅(Msp,i​×S),N)=Hq(Msp,i​×S, N).
  • Right side: Z[S]\mathbb{Z}[S]Z[S] is flat, so F(M)∙⋅S→M⋅SF(M)^{\bullet}\cdot S \to M\cdot SF(M)∙⋅S→M⋅S is still a quasi-isomorphism; then the section formula over Stonean (Ext⁡n(M⋅S,N)≅Ext⁡n(M,N)(S)\operatorname{Ext}^{n}(M\cdot S, N) \cong \operatorname{Ext}^{n}(M,N)(S)Extn(M⋅S,N)≅Extn(M,N)(S)) gives Ext⁡Zn(F(M)∙⋅S,N)≅Ext⁡Zn(M,N)(S)\operatorname{Ext}^{n}_{\mathbb{Z}}(F(M)^{\bullet}\cdot S, N) \cong \operatorname{Ext}^{n}_{\mathbb{Z}}(M,N)(S)ExtZn​(F(M)∙⋅S,N)≅ExtZn​(M,N)(S).

Combining the two sides gives the desired spectral sequence. ∎
