说明

⚠️ 完备不是「张成 HHH」而是「张成的闭包是整个空间」：{en}\{e_n\}{en​} 在 ℓ2\ell^2ℓ2 里的代数张成是有限支撑序列，稠密但不等于 ℓ2\ell^2ℓ2。

⭐ 有了正交基，Hilbert 空间就变成「不带权重的 ℓ2\ell^2ℓ2」（见 Bessel/Parseval 与「可分 Hilbert ≅ℓ2\cong \ell^2≅ℓ2」）。

Notes draft

⚠️ Completeness is not "spanning HHH" but "the closure of the span is the whole space": the algebraic span of {en}\{e_n\}{en​} in ℓ2\ell^2ℓ2 consists of finitely supported sequences, which is dense but not equal to ℓ2\ell^2ℓ2.

⚠️ In infinite dimension uncountable orthonormal bases really occur: the Hermite functions in L2(R)L^2(\mathbb{R})L2(R) are countable, but the orthonormal basis of a general ℓ2(I)\ell^2(I)ℓ2(I) is {ei}i∈I\{e_i\}_{i \in I}{ei​}i∈I​, and III can be arbitrarily large.

⭐ Once an orthonormal basis is available, a Hilbert space becomes "ℓ2\ell^2ℓ2 without weights" (see Bessel/Parseval and "separable Hilbert ≅ℓ2\cong \ell^2≅ℓ2").
