# Liouville's Formula　`thm.liouville-formula`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=thm.liouville-formula)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./thm.liouville-formula.md)

**Two checks** (both can be done by hand):

- In plane polar coordinates ($E = 1$, $G = \rho^{2}$), a circle of radius $\rho_{0}$ centered at the origin has $\theta\equiv\frac{\pi}{2}$ and $\frac{\mathrm{d}\theta}{\mathrm{d}s} = 0$; the formula gives $k_{g} = \frac{2/\rho_{0}}{2} = \frac{1}{\rho_{0}}$, the curvature of that circle for the polar metric.

- On the sphere ($I = \mathrm{d}\rho^{2}+\sin^{2}\rho\,\mathrm{d}\theta^{2}$), the circle of latitude $\rho = \rho_{0}$ gives $k_{g} = \frac{2\cot\rho_{0}}{2} = \cot\rho_{0}$, in agreement with direct computation.
