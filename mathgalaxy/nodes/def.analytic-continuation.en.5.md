# analytical continuation　`def.analytic-continuation`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=def.analytic-continuation)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./def.analytic-continuation.md)

⚠️ **The result of continuation depends on the path**. The standard example: $\log z$ starting from $1$ and going once counterclockwise around the unit circle returns to $1$ with the value $2\pi i$. When is it path-independent? That is exactly what `thm.monodromy` answers.

📌 Relation to `def.logarithm-branch` and `def.branch`: the "branch" there is the **result** of continuation, whereas here we describe the **process**. One object, two ways of speaking.
