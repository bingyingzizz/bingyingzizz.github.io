Thus every property of the real integral (linearity, additivity over intervals, the triangle inequality, substitution) carries over automatically.

⭐ The real difference from the real integral is **path dependence**: $\int_\gamma f\,\mathrm{d}z$ is in general not a function of the endpoints alone.

Cauchy's theorem is exactly the statement that "when the function is holomorphic it suddenly depends only on the endpoints, and vanishes on closed curves" ($0$).

⭐ The real/imaginary splitting above is the interface to Green's formula: applying Green once to $P = u$, $Q = -v$ and once to $P = v$, $Q = u$ yields the Cauchy–Green identity.
