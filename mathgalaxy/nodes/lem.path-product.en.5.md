# concatenation of paths　`lem.path-product`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=lem.path-product)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./lem.path-product.md)

**Reparametrization is the whole trick**: if $\varphi : [0, 1] \to [0, 1]$ is continuous with $\varphi(0) = 0$ and $\varphi(1) = 1$, then $\alpha \circ \varphi \simeq_{p} \alpha$, with homotopy $F(t, s) := \alpha((1 - s)\,\varphi(t) + s\,t)$. Associativity is just taking $\varphi$ to be the reparametrization "split at $1/4, 1/2, 1/4$".

⭐ The homotopy in the unit law **cannot** be something "stationary" like $F(t, s) = e_{x_{0}} * \alpha$: the unit law really requires a deformation, not an equality.
