# Triangle Inequality for Moduli　`prop.complex-triangle`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=prop.complex-triangle)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./prop.complex-triangle.md)

The second equality is “a nonnegative real equals its own real part”, and the last step is $\operatorname{Re} w \le |w|$.

⭐ The equality condition is read off directly: every step must be an equality, i.e. every $e^{-i\theta} z_k$ is a nonnegative real.

⭐ Two standard corollaries: the reverse triangle inequality $\big| |z| - |w| \big| \le |z - w|$;

and the basic integral inequality $\left| \int_\gamma f\,\mathrm{d}z \right| \le \int_\gamma |f|\,|\mathrm{d}z| \le \max_\gamma |f| \cdot L(\gamma)$ — the Cauchy inequality and Liouville's theorem later depend entirely on it.
