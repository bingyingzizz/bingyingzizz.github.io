## Statement · **draft**
The functor $\mathrm{Cond} \to k\mathbf{Top}$, $X \mapsto X(\cdot)$, **preserves finite products**: for any $X, Y \in \mathrm{Cond}$,

$$(X \times Y)(\cdot) \;\cong\; k\bigl(X(\cdot) \times Y(\cdot)\bigr).$$
