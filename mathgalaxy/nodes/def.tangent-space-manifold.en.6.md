So tangent vectors are **contravariant** (they move opposite to the coordinates), while cotangent vectors such as $\mathrm{d}f$ are **covariant**.

⚠ **Notation trap**: $\partial_{i}(p)$ is a **vector**, while $\mathrm{d}x^{i}(p)$ is its dual **covector** ($\mathrm{d}x^{i}(\partial_{j}) = \delta^{i}_{\ j}$).

On a surface these two are the basis of the tangent plane and the dual coframe (`def.moving-frame`).
