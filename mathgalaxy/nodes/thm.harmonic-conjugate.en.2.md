# harmonic conjugate　`thm.harmonic-conjugate`

Moreover $v$ (the **harmonic conjugate**, satisfying $v_x = -u_y$ and $v_y = u_x$) is unique up to an **additive real** constant — equivalently, $f = u + iv$ is unique up to an **additive purely imaginary** constant.

## Notes · **draft**
⭐ The proof is "a closed 1-form is exact": set $\omega = -u_y\,\mathrm{d}x + u_x\,\mathrm{d}y$, then

$$\mathrm{d}\omega = (u_{xx} + u_{yy})\,\mathrm{d}x \wedge \mathrm{d}y = 0,$$
