## Statement · **draft**
Let $n = p_{1}^{e_{1}} \cdots p_{r}^{e_{r}}$. Then

$$\tau(n) = \prod_{i=1}^{r} (e_{i} + 1), \qquad \sigma(n) = \prod_{i=1}^{r} \frac{p_{i}^{\,e_{i}+1} - 1}{p_{i} - 1} .$$
