# Hamiltonian Vector Field　`def.hamiltonian-vector-field`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=def.hamiltonian-vector-field)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./def.hamiltonian-vector-field.md)

Let $(M, \omega)$ be a symplectic manifold and let $H \in C^{\infty}(M)$. By nondegeneracy of $\omega$, there is a unique vector field $X_{H}$ such that

$$\iota_{X_{H}}\omega = +dH ,$$

We call $X_{H}$ the **Hamiltonian vector field** of $H$ ($\iota$ is the interior product, see `def.interior-product`). Its integral curves satisfy **Hamilton's equations**, which in Darboux coordinates are

$$\dot{q}_{i} = \frac{\partial H}{\partial p_{i}}, \qquad \dot{p}_{i} = -\frac{\partial H}{\partial q_{i}} .$$
