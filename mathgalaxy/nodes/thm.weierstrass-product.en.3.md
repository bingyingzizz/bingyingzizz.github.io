## Notes · **draft**
**Proof.** First remove the terms of the sequence equal to $0$ (they contribute only the factor $z^{m}$). Continue to denote the remaining sequence by $a_{n}\ne 0$ and take the elementary factors to be $E_{p}(z) = (1-z)\exp\Bigl(z + \frac{z^{2}}{2} + \cdots + \frac{z^{p}}{p}\Bigr),\qquad E_{0}(z) = 1-z .$.
