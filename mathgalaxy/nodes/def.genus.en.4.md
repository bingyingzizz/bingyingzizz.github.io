# genus　`def.genus`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=def.genus)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./def.genus.md)

⭐ Three values that can be computed directly from the definition: for the sphere, $g = 0$ ($H_{1} = 0$); for the torus, $g = 1$; and for the connected sum of $g$ tori, $g(M \# N) = g(M) + g(N)$ (the part of $H_{1}$ of the connected sum that is the direct sum of those of the two summands). ⚠ The last assertion is a **theorem** requiring Mayer–Vietoris and does not yet have a node here.
