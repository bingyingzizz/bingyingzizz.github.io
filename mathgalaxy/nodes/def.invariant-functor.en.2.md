# Invariant Functor　`def.invariant-functor`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=def.invariant-functor)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./def.invariant-functor.md)

$$(-)^{G} : R[G]\text{-}\mathbf{Mod} \longrightarrow R\text{-}\mathbf{Mod} .$$

It is naturally isomorphic to $\operatorname{Hom}_{R[G]}(R, -)$ ($R$ with the trivial $G$-action), hence is **left exact**, but in general not right exact.

## Notes · **draft**
⭐ **In one sentence**: $(-)^{G}$ is "taking fixed points". It compresses the information of the group action into $R$-modules, and is the starting point of group cohomology ($H^{0} = (-)^{G}$).
