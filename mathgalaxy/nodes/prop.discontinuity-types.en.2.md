# classification of discontinuities　`prop.discontinuity-types`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=prop.discontinuity-types)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./prop.discontinuity-types.md)

- **removable discontinuity**: the two one-sided limits are equal (denote the common value by $L$). In that case redefining $f(x_0)$ to be $L$ makes it continuous;
- **jump discontinuity**: the two one-sided limits differ; the difference $f(x_0^{+}) - f(x_0^{-})$ is called the **jump**.

**Second kind**: at least one one-sided limit fails to exist, or equals $\pm\infty$.
