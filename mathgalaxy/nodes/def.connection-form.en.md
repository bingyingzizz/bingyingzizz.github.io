## Statement · **draft**
In plain terms: the frame **itself also rotates** — after a small step in some direction, $(e_{1},e_{2},e_{3})$ turns slightly. The **connection form** records this "rotation" in nine $1$-forms: feed it a direction and it tells you about which axis the frame turns and how fast. It is the same thing as the Christoffel symbols, only recorded in an orthonormal frame.
