# Acyclicity on Discrete and Discrete Times Stonean Spaces　`prop.condab-discrete-acyclic`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=prop.condab-discrete-acyclic)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./prop.condab-discrete-acyclic.md)

E \times S = \coprod_{e \in E} S$ is the **disjoint union** of $S$, and again use "disjoint union $\implies$ product":
$$H^{n}(E \times S,\ M) \cong \prod_{e \in E} H^{n}(S,\ M) = 0 \qquad (n > 0),$$
The last step uses "condensed abelian groups are acyclic on Stonean spaces" ✓. ∎
