## Statement · **draft**
Let $\operatorname{char}F\nmid|G|$, let $F$ be algebraically closed, and let $V$ be a finite-dimensional semisimple representation, $V\cong\bigoplus_i n_iV_i$ with the $V_i$ pairwise non-isomorphic irreducible. Then $n_i=\langle\chi_V,\chi_{V_i}\rangle$.
