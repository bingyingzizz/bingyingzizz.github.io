## Statement · **draft**
**In plain words**: **these two equations govern how the frame moves**. The first (zero torsion) says "the change of the coframe is completely determined by the connection forms, with no extra twist"; the second ($\mathbb{R}^{3}$ is flat) says "rotating once in each of two directions gives the same result regardless of order". Together they express $\mathrm{d}^{2} = 0$ in a frame.
