## Statement · **draft**
A subset $K$ of $X$ is **clopen** if it is both open and closed. Equivalently, $K$ and its complement $K^{c}$ are both open, i.e. $X = K \sqcup K^{c}$ is a union of disjoint non-empty open sets (when $K$ is non-trivial).
