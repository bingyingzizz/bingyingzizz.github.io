# orthogonality　`def.orthogonality`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=def.orthogonality)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./def.orthogonality.md)

- $u \perp v$ (one says $u$ and $v$ are **orthogonal**) $\iff \langle u, v \rangle = 0$;
- $S \perp T$: every vector of $S$ is orthogonal to every vector of $T$;
- $\{v_1, \ldots, v_k\}$ is called an **orthonormal set** exactly when $\langle v_i, v_j \rangle = \delta_{ij}$;
- $\{v_i\}$ is an **orthonormal basis** exactly when it is both a basis and an orthonormal set.

**Pythagoras' theorem**: $u \perp v \implies \|u + v\|^{2} = \|u\|^{2} + \|v\|^{2}$.
