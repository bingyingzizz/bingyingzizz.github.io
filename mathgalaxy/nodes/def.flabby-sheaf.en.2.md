## Notes · **draft**
**Difference from soft sheaves**: flabby sheaves require extension from **open** sets; soft sheaves require extension from **closed** sets. Neither generally contains the other, but on a paracompact Hausdorff space flabby sheaves are soft (a theorem, not proved here).

**Why it matters**: flabby sheaves have no higher cohomology (Godement) — **not proved in this graph**; read together with the skeleton status of @@thm.soft-sheaf-acyclic@@; hence flabby resolutions compute sheaf cohomology; every term of the Godement resolution is flabby (see @@def.godement-resolution@@).
