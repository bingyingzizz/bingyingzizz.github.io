[in graph](../#n=def.quadratic-form)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./def.quadratic-form.md)

⚠️ **Do not confuse "quadratic form" with "homogeneous quadratic polynomial"**: every homogeneous quadratic polynomial can be written as $x^{\mathsf{T}}Ax$ (take $A$ symmetric), but in characteristic $2$ the symmetrization of a cross term such as $x_{1}x_{2}$ degenerates.
