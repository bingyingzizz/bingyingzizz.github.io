5. **The image is metrizable**: $\rho(a, b) := \sum_{k \ge 1} 2^{-k} |a_{k} - b_{k}|$ is a metric on $[0, 1]^{\mathbb{N}}$ inducing the product topology; restrict it to the image.
Therefore $X$ is homeomorphic to a subspace of a separable metric space, hence metrizable. ∎

⚠️ **The converse fails**: metrizable does not imply second countable — an uncountable discrete space is metrizable (discrete metric) but not second countable. So this theorem is a **sufficient condition**, not a characterisation.
