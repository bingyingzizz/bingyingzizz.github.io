# Free Abelian Sheaf　`def.free-abelian-sheaf`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=def.free-abelian-sheaf)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./def.free-abelian-sheaf.md)

$$\operatorname{Hom}_{\mathbb{Z}}\bigl(\mathbb{Z}\cdot X,\ M\bigr) = \operatorname{Hom}_{\widehat{\mathcal{C}}(\mathbf{Ab})}\bigl(\mathbb{Z}\cdot X,\ M\bigr) \cong \operatorname{Hom}_{\widehat{\mathcal{C}}}(X,\ M) = M(X) ✓$$

($M$ is itself a sheaf, so the Hom on the presheaf side equals the Hom on the sheaf side). ∎

⭐ **Use**: $\mathbb{Z}\cdot X$ is the device for "regarding a space or sheaf as a free generator".

The Čech complex is $\check{C}^{\bullet}(X, M) = \operatorname{Hom}_{\mathbb{Z}}(\mathbb{Z}\cdot X_{\bullet}, M)$;
