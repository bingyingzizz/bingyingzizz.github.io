## Statement · **draft**
Let $F \dashv G$. Then $G$ **preserves all limits** (that exist in $\mathcal{D}$): for every diagram $D : I \to \mathcal{D}$,

$$G\bigl(\lim D\bigr) \;\cong\; \lim\, (G \circ D)$$

## Notes · **draft**
Dually, **left adjoints preserve all colimits**.
