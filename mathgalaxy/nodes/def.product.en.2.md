$$\operatorname{Hom}_{\mathcal{C}}\Bigl(Y, \prod_{i} X_i\Bigr) \;\cong\; \prod_{i} \operatorname{Hom}_{\mathcal{C}}(Y, X_i)$$

## Notes · **draft**
In one sentence: **a product is "one map = a family of maps"**. Maps into the product correspond bijectively to families of maps into each factor, and the projection $p_i$ extracts the $i$-th component.
