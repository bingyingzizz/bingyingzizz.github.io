Statement

A presheaf morphism φ:T  ⟹  T′\varphi : T \implies T'φ:T⟹T′ is a monomorphism (epimorphism) in C^\widehat{\mathcal{C}}C   ⟺  \iff⟺ for every X∈CX \in \mathcal{C}X∈C, φX:T(X)→T′(X)\varphi_{X} : T(X) \to T'(X)φX​:T(X)→T′(X) is injective (surjective).

Notes

In short, presheaf properties are checked pointwise. Everything in C^\widehat{\mathcal{C}}C is defined pointwise, including monomorphisms and epimorphisms.

The (⟸\Longleftarrow⟸) direction is immediate because a pointwise injective family is left-cancellable. The (⟹\Longrightarrow⟹) direction uses the Yoneda lemma. See the proof on the edge.
