## Notes · **draft**
⚠ Note the difference from **uniform continuity**: uniform continuity controls the oscillation on a **single** small interval, whereas absolute continuity requires that **any number of small intervals together** still be controlled — precisely to rule out the Cantor-function phenomenon of “climbing on a null set”.

Notation: $AC([a, b])$ denotes the set of all absolutely continuous functions on $[a,b]$.
