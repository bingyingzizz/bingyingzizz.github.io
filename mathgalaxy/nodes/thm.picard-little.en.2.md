## Notes · **draft**
⭐ **How much stronger it is than Liouville**: Liouville (`thm.liouville`) says “a bounded entire function is constant” and only rules out a bounded image; Picard says “the image cannot omit even two points” — even when the image is unbounded.

**The proof given in this graph covers only the finite-order case** (this is an honest label): if $f$ has finite order and omits two values $a, b$, then $g := (f - a)/(f - b)$ is an entire function **omitting $0$ and $1$**.
