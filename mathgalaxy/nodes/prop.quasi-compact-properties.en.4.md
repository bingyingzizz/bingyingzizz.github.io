# Properties of Quasi-Compact Objects　`prop.quasi-compact-properties`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=prop.quasi-compact-properties)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./prop.quasi-compact-properties.md)

For each $k$, pull back along $X_{k}\to X$ to obtain a cover of $X_{k}$, namely $(Y_{j}\times_{X}X_{k} \to X_{k})$;

by quasi-compactness of $X_{k}$ extract a finite subfamily $(j \in J_{k})$.
