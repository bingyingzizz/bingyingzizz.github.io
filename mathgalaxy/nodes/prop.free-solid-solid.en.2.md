# the free solid group is solid　`prop.free-solid-solid`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=prop.free-solid-solid)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./prop.free-solid-solid.md)

⭐ The proof reduces to the case $Z[S]^{\blacksquare} = \mathbb{Z}$ (using `cor.free-solid-product` to write the general case as $\prod_J \mathbb{Z}$, then computing termwise); there the two sides are $H^i(T,\mathbb{Z})$ and $\operatorname{RHom}(\prod_J\mathbb{Z},\mathbb{Z})$, computed from $0 \to \prod_J \mathbb{Z} \to \prod_J \mathbb{R} \to \prod_J \mathbb{R}/\mathbb{Z} \to 0$ and the contractibility of $\mathbb{R}$.
