## Notes · **draft**
Corollary: **any limit of Stone spaces is again a Stone space**. Put this next to "totally disconnected spaces are closed under limits" and one reads off the double identity of Stone spaces: they are both "the totally disconnected ones among compact Hausdorff spaces" and "what remains after reflecting once more inside that reflective subcategory".
