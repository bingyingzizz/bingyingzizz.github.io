# Weierstrass product theorem　`thm.weierstrass-product`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=thm.weierstrass-product)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./thm.weierstrass-product.md)

⭐ **This is the factorization “entire function = zeros $\times$ zero-free factor.”** In comparison, Weierstrass handles **zeros** multiplicatively, while Mittag-Leffler handles **principal parts and poles** additively.

⚠️ The product representation **is not unique** ($p_{n}$ can be chosen larger), so the theorem asserts existence rather than uniqueness.

⚠️ The node `def.elementary-factor` contains a stronger estimate for elementary factors. Hurwitz's theorem has no separate node, so Lemma 2 is given here in full.
