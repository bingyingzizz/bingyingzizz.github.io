## Notes · **draft**
**Proof of (1)**: holomorphy is tested by `thm.morera`. Take any closed triangle $T$ in $\Omega$ (with its interior); the integral of each $f_{k}$ along $\partial T$ vanishes (Cauchy–Goursat). Since $\partial T$ is compact, uniform convergence lets the integral pass to the limit, so $\oint_{\partial T} f = \lim_{k} \oint_{\partial T} f_{k} = 0$. By Morera, $f$ is holomorphic.
