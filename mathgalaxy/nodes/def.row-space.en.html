Statement

Let A∈Mm×n(K)A \in M_{m \times n}(K)A∈Mm×n​(K), with mmm row vectors a1,…,am∈Kna_1, \ldots, a_m \in K^{n}a1​,…,am​∈Kn. The subspace they span,

Row⁡(A):=span⁡{a1,…,am}⊆Kn\operatorname{Row}(A) := \operatorname{span}\{a_1, \ldots, a_m\} \subseteq K^{n}Row(A):=span{a1​,…,am​}⊆Kn

is the row space of AAA, and its dimension is the row rank. Equivalently, Row⁡(A)=Col⁡(AT)\operatorname{Row}(A) = \operatorname{Col}(A^{\mathsf T})Row(A)=Col(AT), the column space of ATA^{\mathsf T}AT.

Elementary row operations preserve the row space: left multiplication by an invertible matrix only replaces the row vectors by another basis (row operations replace the rows by invertible linear combinations of them), so row-equivalent matrices have the same row space. Hence the nonzero rows of a row echelon form give a basis of Row⁡(A)\operatorname{Row}(A)Row(A).

Notes

The row space lives in KnK^{n}Kn (a row has length nnn), while the column space lives in KmK^{m}Km — in general they are not the same space, so do not confuse them.

Dimension: dim⁡Row⁡(A)=rank⁡A\dim \operatorname{Row}(A) = \operatorname{rank} AdimRow(A)=rankA (the row rank equals the rank of the matrix).

The row space and the solution space of the homogeneous system are orthogonal: x∈ker⁡Ax \in \ker Ax∈kerA if and only if xxx is orthogonal to every row vector; hence dim⁡Row⁡(A)+dim⁡ker⁡A=n\dim \operatorname{Row}(A) + \dim \ker A = ndimRow(A)+dimkerA=n.
