## Statement · **draft**
The functor $F : \mathcal{C} \to \mathbf{Set}$ is represented by $X \in \mathcal{C}$ $\iff$ $h^{X} \cong F$.

In other words, “there exists a universal element $(X, s)$” and “$F$ is isomorphic to $\operatorname{Hom}_{\mathcal{C}}(X, -)$” are equivalent.
