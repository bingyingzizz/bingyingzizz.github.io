# solid tensor product　`thm.solid-tensor`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=thm.solid-tensor)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./thm.solid-tensor.md)

**(ii)** On $D(\mathbf{Solid})$ there is a unique symmetric monoidal structure $\otimes^{L\blacksquare}$ making $C \mapsto C^{L\blacksquare}$ symmetric monoidal. It is the left-derived functor of $\otimes^{\blacksquare}$.

## Notes · **draft**
⭐ This is **the object the whole theory aims to construct**: a tensor product compatible with completion.

For comparison, the classical $\mathbb{Z}_p \hat\otimes \mathbb{R}$ is not well-defined, whereas $\mathbb{Z}_p \otimes^{L\blacksquare} \mathbb{R} = 0$ (see `ex.solid-tensor-computations`).
