## Statement · **draft**
Given a sequence $\{a_n\} \subseteq \mathbb{R}$ (or $\mathbb{C}$) and a centre $x_0$, an expression of the form

$$\sum_{n=0}^{\infty} a_n (x - x_0)^{n}$$
