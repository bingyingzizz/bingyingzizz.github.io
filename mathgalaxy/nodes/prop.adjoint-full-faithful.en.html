Abstract

For an adjunction F⊣GF \dashv GF⊣G with unit η\etaη and counit ε\varepsilonε: FFF is full and faithful iff η\etaη is an isomorphism, and GGG is full and faithful iff ε\varepsilonε is an isomorphism. So fullness and faithfulness of the left adjoint is checked on a single natural transformation rather than on every Hom-set. This is also the dividing line between reflections and coreflections: an inclusion of a full subcategory is always fully faithful, so a reflection has an invertible counit.

Statement

Let F⊣GF \dashv GF⊣G with unit η\etaη and counit ε\varepsilonε. Then FFF is fully faithful   ⟺  \iff⟺ η\etaη is an isomorphism; GGG is fully faithful   ⟺  \iff⟺ ε\varepsilonε is an isomorphism.

Notes

So "a left adjoint is fully faithful" reduces to checking that every component of a single natural transformation is invertible — far easier than checking Hom-sets pair by pair.

This is also the dividing line between reflective and coreflective: reflective means "the inclusion functor has a left adjoint", and an inclusion functor is always fully faithful (full subcategory), so for a reflection the counit is always an isomorphism.
