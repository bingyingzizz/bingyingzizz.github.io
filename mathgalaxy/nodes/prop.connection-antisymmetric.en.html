Statement

Let (e1,e2,e3)(e_{1}, e_{2}, e_{3})(e1​,e2​,e3​) be a moving frame (an orthonormal frame) and let ω ji\omega^{i}_{\ j}ω ji​ be the connection forms. Then for all i,ji,ji,j:

ω ji+ω ij=0;\omega^{i}_{\ j}+\omega^{j}_{\ i} = 0 ;ω ji​+ω ij​=0;

in particular ω ii=0\omega^{i}_{\ i} = 0ω ii​=0. Thus the nine connection forms have only three independent components, ω 21,ω 31,ω 32\omega^{1}_{\ 2}, \omega^{1}_{\ 3}, \omega^{2}_{\ 3}ω 21​,ω 31​,ω 32​.

Notes

The proof uses "the inner product is constant" and needs no coordinates: take the exterior derivative of ⟨ei,ej⟩=δij\langle e_{i}, e_{j}\rangle = \delta_{ij}⟨ei​,ej​⟩=δij​ (see the incoming edge).

⭐ This is the foundation of the orthonormal frame method: it writes the rotational degrees of freedom of O(3)\mathrm{O}(3)O(3) (333 parameters) as 333 111-forms, which is what makes the pairwise exterior products in the structure equations meaningful.

⚠ If one uses a non-orthonormal frame instead (such as {ru,rv,n}\{r_{u}, r_{v}, n\}{ru​,rv​,n}), then gijg_{ij}gij​ is not δij\delta_{ij}δij​, antisymmetry fails, and ω\omegaω degenerates into a mixture of Γ\GammaΓ and hhh — exactly the shape of the classical formulas.
