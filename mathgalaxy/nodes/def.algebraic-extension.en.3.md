# algebraic extension　`def.algebraic-extension`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=def.algebraic-extension)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./def.algebraic-extension.md)

⭐ **Algebraic extensions are closed under towers**: $F \subseteq K \subseteq L$, if $K/F$ and $L/K$ are both algebraic, then $L/F$ is algebraic. (The proof passes through “finitely generated”; it is not expanded here.)

⚠ The standard transcendental example: $\mathbb{Q}(x)/\mathbb{Q}$ (the field of rational functions) — one cannot even speak of its **degree**.
