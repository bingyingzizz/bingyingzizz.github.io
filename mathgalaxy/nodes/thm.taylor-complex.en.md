## Statement · **draft**
**In plain words**: near any point a holomorphic function is a power series — that is, for complex functions "differentiable" and "expressible as a power series" are the same thing.

Let $f$ be holomorphic on the disk $B(a, R)$. Then for $z \in B(a, R)$,

$$f(z) = \sum_{n=0}^{\infty} \frac{f^{(n)}(a)}{n!}\,(z - a)^n,$$
