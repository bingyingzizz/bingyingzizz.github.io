# Orientation and Volume Forms　`prop.volume-form-orientation`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=prop.volume-form-orientation)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./prop.volume-form-orientation.md)

**Line-bundle language**: $\bigwedge^{n}T^{\ast}M$ is a line bundle of rank $1$ (the **orientation bundle**), and $\omega$ is an everywhere nonzero section of it. Thus "orientable" and "orientation bundle trivial" are two ways of saying the same thing — the same criterion as "an everywhere nonzero section is equivalent to a trivial bundle" in `def.bundle-section`.
