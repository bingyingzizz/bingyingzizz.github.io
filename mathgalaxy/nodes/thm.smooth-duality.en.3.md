# dualizing complex of a smooth map　`thm.smooth-duality`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=thm.smooth-duality)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./thm.smooth-duality.md)

$$f^!R \;\cong\; \det\nolimits_A\bigl(\Omega^1_{A/R}\bigr)[d].$$

Since the isomorphism is natural and everything is defined locally, it globalizes to any smooth map $f : X \to Y$.

## Notes · **draft**
⭐ This is the **dualizing complex**: in the smooth case $f^!\mathbf{1}_Y$ is an invertible sheaf (a line bundle) shifted to degree $d$, given by the determinant of the Kähler differentials $\Omega^1_{A/R}$.
