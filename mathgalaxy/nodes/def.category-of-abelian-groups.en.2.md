## Notes · **draft**
**The prototype of an abelian category**: it has kernels and cokernels, monomorphisms and epimorphisms are strict; $\hom(A,B)$ with pointwise addition is an abelian group.

**Enough projective/injective objects**: free abelian groups are projective; $\mathbb Q/\mathbb Z$ gives an injective cogenerator, and every abelian group embeds into an injective group.

**The tensor product** gives a symmetric monoidal structure with unit $\mathbb Z$; $\hom(A,B)$ is again an abelian group, so $\mathbf{Ab}$ is even symmetric monoidal closed.
