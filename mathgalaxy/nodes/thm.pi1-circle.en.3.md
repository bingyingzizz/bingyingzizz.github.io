# the fundamental group of the circle　`thm.pi1-circle`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=thm.pi1-circle)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./thm.pi1-circle.md)

⭐ **Interface with complex analysis**: the winding number is written in complex analysis as $n(\gamma, 0) = \frac{1}{2\pi i}\oint_{\gamma}\frac{\mathrm{d}z}{z}$ (`prop.winding-integral`). The two are the same integer: lifting a closed curve to polar coordinates gives the lift. This also explains the topological source of the theorem that the winding number is invariant under homotopies of the curve (`thm.winding-homotopy`).
