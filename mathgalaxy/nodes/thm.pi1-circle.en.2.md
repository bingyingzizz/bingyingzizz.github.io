# the fundamental group of the circle　`thm.pi1-circle`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=thm.pi1-circle)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./thm.pi1-circle.md)

$$\deg : \pi_{1}(S^{1}, 1) \xrightarrow{\ \cong\ } \mathbb{Z}.$$

Concretely, the generator class with $\deg[\alpha] = n$ is $\alpha_{n}(t) = e^{2\pi i n t}$; $n > 0$ means "wound $n$ times counterclockwise", and $n < 0$ means "wound $|n|$ times clockwise".

## Notes · **draft**
**Geometric translation**: the fundamental group of $S^{1}$ carries only one piece of information, "how many times it winds", and that is a **complete invariant** — two closed paths are homotopic iff they have the same winding number. Nothing else can hide in $S^{1}$.
