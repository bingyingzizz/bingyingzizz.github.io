## Notes · **draft**
**Example**: in $\mathbf{Set}$, the regular monomorphisms are exactly the injective maps. Given an injection $i : Y \to X$, take $Z = \{0, 1\}$, let $f$ be defined by: on $i(Y)$ it equals $0$, on the complement it equals $1$, and let $g$ be constantly $0$; then $\ker(f, g) = i(Y) \cong Y$.
