But **any** matrix is unitarily similar to an upper triangular one (thm.schur-triangularization) — this is the complete answer to "how far can orthogonal similarity simplify a matrix".

⚠️ Keep the difference between the three notions firmly in mind: **similarity** $P^{-1}AP$ (any invertible $P$, preserves eigenvalues); **congruence** $P^{T}AP$ (any invertible $P$, preserves inertia); **orthogonal similarity** $Q^{T}AQ$ with $Q^{-1} = Q^{T}$ (preserves both). Only in the third is "$P^{-1}$ and $P^{T}$ the same thing".
