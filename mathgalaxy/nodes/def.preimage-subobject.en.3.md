# preimage of a subobject　`def.preimage-subobject`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=def.preimage-subobject)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./def.preimage-subobject.md)

**It is the same construction as intersection**: the intersection $Y \cap Z = Y \times_{X} Z$ is the pullback along two monomorphisms, while the preimage is the pullback along an arbitrary morphism $f$; both are fibred products, only the morphisms pulled back along differ.

**It is still a subobject**: the pullback of a monomorphism along any morphism is still a monomorphism (pullbacks preserve monomorphisms), so $f^{-1}(Y) \to X^{\prime}$ really is a subobject.

⚠ One needs the fibred product along $f$ to exist; in a category without fibred products the preimage may not be defined.
