## Statement · **draft**
Let $T : V \to W$ be a linear map. Then

$$\overline{T} : V / \ker T \longrightarrow \operatorname{im} T, \qquad v + \ker T \longmapsto T(v)$$

is a well-defined **linear isomorphism**. In particular

$$V / \ker T \cong \operatorname{im} T$$
