# Goldstine theorem　`thm.goldstine`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=thm.goldstine)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./thm.goldstine.md)

**Main proof.** Let $K = \overline{J_X(B_X)}^{w^{*}} \subseteq B_{X^{**}}$ (closed convex compact). If $K \ne B_{X^{**}}$, pick $\xi_0 \in B_{X^{**}} \setminus K$. By the separation lemma there is a weak* continuous linear functional $\ell$ with $\ell(\xi_0) > \sup_{K} \ell$. By the preparation, $\ell = \mathrm{ev}_x$ for some $x \in X$. Hence

$\xi_0(x) > \sup_{\eta \in K} \eta(x) \ge \sup_{y \in B_X} (J_X y)(x) = \sup_{y \in B_X} x(y) = \|x\|.$
