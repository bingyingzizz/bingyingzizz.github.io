设 (V,ρ)(V,\rho)(V,ρ)、(W,σ)(W,\sigma)(W,σ) 是群 GGG 的表示。张量积 V⊗FWV\otimes_F WV⊗F​W 上取作用 g(v⊗w)=ρ(g)v⊗σ(g)wg(v\otimes w)=\rho(g)v\otimes\sigma(g)wg(v⊗w)=ρ(g)v⊗σ(g)w，称为 GGG 的张量积表示。对一般 FFF-代数 AAA，需要代数同态 A→A⊗AA\to A\otimes AA→A⊗A 才能这样作用；群情形用的是对角。

Statement draft

Let (V,ρ)(V,\rho)(V,ρ) and (W,σ)(W,\sigma)(W,σ) be representations of a group GGG. The tensor product V⊗FWV\otimes_F WV⊗F​W carries the action g(v⊗w)=ρ(g)v⊗σ(g)wg(v\otimes w)=\rho(g)v\otimes\sigma(g)wg(v⊗w)=ρ(g)v⊗σ(g)w, called the tensor product representation of GGG. For a general FFF-algebra AAA one needs an algebra homomorphism A→A⊗AA\to A\otimes AA→A⊗A for such an action; the group case uses the diagonal.

Notes draft

The action is well defined because ⊗\otimes⊗ is bilinear and ρ(g),σ(g)\rho(g),\sigma(g)ρ(g),σ(g) are linear. Its dimension is (dim⁡V)(dim⁡W)(\dim V)(\dim W)(dimV)(dimW). The character satisfies χV⊗W(g)=χV(g)χW(g)\chi_{V\otimes W}(g)=\chi_V(g)\chi_W(g)χV⊗W​(g)=χV​(g)χW​(g), see the node on character operations. For a general algebra AAA, V⊗WV\otimes WV⊗W is only an A⊗AA\otimes AA⊗A-module unless AAA is a bialgebra; the group algebra F[G]F[G]F[G] is the main case here.
