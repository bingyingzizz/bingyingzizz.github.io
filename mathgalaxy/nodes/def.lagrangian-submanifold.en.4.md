Thus a Lagrangian submanifold is locally such a graph, and $F$ is called a **generating function**.

This connects geometry with analysis and underlies Hamilton–Jacobi theory.

⚠️ **$\dim L = n$ does not follow from isotropy**: An isotropic submanifold can have dimension far below $n$ (even a line in a symplectic vector space is isotropic). One must **also** require half dimension or, equivalently, maximality ($T_pL = (T_pL)^{\perp}$). This is exactly as in the linear case (see the notes to `def.lagrangian-subspace`).
