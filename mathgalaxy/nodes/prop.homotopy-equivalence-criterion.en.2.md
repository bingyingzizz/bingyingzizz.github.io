# Criterion for Homotopy Equivalence　`prop.homotopy-equivalence-criterion`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=prop.homotopy-equivalence-criterion)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./prop.homotopy-equivalence-criterion.md)

1. $f$ is a **homotopy equivalence** (that is, an isomorphism in $\mathbf{K}(\mathcal{C})$) $\iff$ there exists $g : L^{\bullet} \to K^{\bullet}$ with $g f \sim 1_{K}$, $f g \sim 1_{L}$;
2. a complex $K^{\bullet}$ is **homotopy trivial** $\iff$ $1_{K} \sim 0_{K}$.
