lem.weak-hausdorff-qseparated

Machine-drafted and pending author review. The Chinese text is authoritative.

Statement

Let XXX be a weakly Hausdorff space (with XXX compactly generated) and X1,X2↪XX_{1}, X_{2} \hookrightarrow XX1​,X2​↪X a quasi-compact subspace. Then the fiber product

X1×XX2X_{1} \times_{X} X_{2}X1​×X​X2​

is compact Hausdorff. In particular, the intersection of any two quasi-compact open subspaces is quasi-compact, that is, XXX is quasi-separated.

Notes

Intuition: quasi-separatedness asks that "the intersection of two compact pieces is still a compact piece". Weak Hausdorffness guarantees exactly this — it says images of compact pieces are closed, so the pullback of two compact pieces along XXX lands in a compact piece.

⚠️ Here the compactness of X1×XX2X_{1} \times_{X} X_{2}X1​×X​X2​ is a direct application of the "fiber product of compact Hausdorff along weakly Hausdorff" item in the CGWH\mathrm{CGWH}CGWH family.
