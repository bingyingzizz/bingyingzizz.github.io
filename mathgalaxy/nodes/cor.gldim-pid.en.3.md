$$0 \to R^{(J)} \to R^{(I)} \to M \to 0 ,$$

which is a free, hence projective, resolution of length $1$ ✓. Thus $\operatorname{pd}_{R} M \le 1$ for every $M$ ✓.
**② The case $\operatorname{gl.dim} = 0$.** If $R$ is a field, every module is free because every vector space has a basis. Every module is therefore projective, so $\operatorname{pd} = 0$ and $\operatorname{gl.dim} R = 0$ ✓.
**③ If the ring is not a field, then $\operatorname{gl.dim} = 1$.** Choose a nonzero nonunit $a \in R$ and consider $M := R/(a)$.
