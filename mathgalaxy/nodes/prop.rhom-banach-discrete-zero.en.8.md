# Derived Hom from Finite-Dimensional Banach Spaces to Discrete Groups Vanishes　`prop.rhom-banach-discrete-zero`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=prop.rhom-banach-discrete-zero)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./prop.rhom-banach-discrete-zero.md)

**The general $\mathbb{R}$-module case**: $\operatorname{RHom}_{\mathbb{Z}}(V, N) = \operatorname{RHom}_{\mathbb{R}}(V, \operatorname{RHom}_{\mathbb{Z}}(\mathbb{R}, N)) = \operatorname{RHom}_{\mathbb{R}}(V, 0) = 0$ (with $\operatorname{RHom}_{\mathbb{Z}}(\mathbb{R}, N) = 0$ obtained from the finite-dimensional case by taking a colimit).
