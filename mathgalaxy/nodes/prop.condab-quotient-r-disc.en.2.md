## Statement · **draft**
Write $Q := \mathbb{R}/\mathbb{R}^{\mathrm{disc}}$ (regard $\mathbb{R}^{\mathrm{disc}}$ as an additive group with the discrete topology, $\mathbb{R}$ with the usual topology, and take the quotient in condensed abelian groups). Let $S$ be a **Stone** space. Then

$$Q(S) \;=\; C(S, \mathbb{R})\,\big/\,C(S, \mathbb{R}^{\mathrm{disc}}),$$

that is: continuous functions to $\mathbb{R}$, modulo **locally constant** functions.
