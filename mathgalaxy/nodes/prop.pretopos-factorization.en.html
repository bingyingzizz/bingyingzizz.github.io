prop.pretopos-factorization

Machine-drafted and pending author review. The Chinese text is authoritative.

Statement

Let C\mathcal{C}C be a pretopos. Then

  1. C\mathcal{C}C is balanced: a morphism that is both monic and epic is an isomorphism;
  2. every morphism is strict: im⁡f≅coim⁡f\operatorname{im} f \cong \operatorname{coim} fimf≅coimf;
  3. every morphism has a unique epi-mono factorization
f:X↠X‾↣Y,X‾:=X/R,R:=X×YXf : X \twoheadrightarrow \overline{X} \rightarrowtail Y, \qquad \overline{X} := X / R,\quad R := X \times_{Y} Xf:X↠X↣Y,X:=X/R,R:=X×Y​X

Notes

These three are obvious in Set\mathbf{Set}Set, but in a general category they must be proved. Together they say: "the image in a pretopos = the quotient", exactly as in sets, where "the image is the quotient by the kernel pair".
