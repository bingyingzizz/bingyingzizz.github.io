## Notes · **draft**
**Proof.** Quasi-compactness means "an epimorphism out of a coproduct admits a finite subfamily that is still epimorphic" ✓; in the concrete world of sheaves on $X$, "$\coprod_{i} \mathcal{F}_{i} \to \mathcal{G}$ is epimorphic" is exactly the map of espace étalé being surjective on each fiber, so "a finite subfamily is already epimorphic" $\iff$ "the corresponding compact space is covered by finitely many pieces" ✓ — both sides hold simultaneously ✓.

∎

⭐ Reading: **a "quasi-compact sheaf" on a topological space is exactly that "compact étale space" of geometry**.
