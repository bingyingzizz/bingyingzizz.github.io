# quadratic form　`def.quadratic-form`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=def.quadratic-form)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./def.quadratic-form.md)

- **Positive definite $\iff$ all eigenvalues positive $\iff$ congruent to $I$**: the first uses the spectral theorem (real symmetric); the third uses the **law of inertia** (`thm.sylvester-law-inertia`), which requires only invertible congruence, not orthogonality.
