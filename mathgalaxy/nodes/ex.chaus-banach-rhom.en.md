## Statement · **draft**
Let $S$ be a compact Hausdorff space and $N$ a real Banach space. Then $\operatorname{RHom}_{\mathbb{Z}}(\mathbb{Z} \cdot S,\ N) \cong C(S, N)$, concentrated in **degree 0**.
