# Sheafification and the Sheaf Reflection　`thm.sheafification`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=thm.sheafification)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./thm.sheafification.md)

- For the **coarsest** topology ($J(X) = \{h_{X}\}$) every presheaf is a sheaf and $\sharp$ is the identity; for the **finest** topology ($J(X) = $ all sieves) the category of sheaves has only one object, the constant presheaf $\mathbf{1}$.
- The sheafification of a constant presheaf (the same set $E$ on every open set) is called the **constant sheaf**.
- Taking $X$ and $\mathcal{C} = \operatorname{Open}(X)$ on a topological space gives the usual category of sheaves, and $\sharp$ is the usual sheafification functor.
