## Statement · **draft**
The symmetric group $S_n$ is solvable (`def.solvable-group`) if and only if $n \le 4$.

Equivalently: $S_5$ and all $S_n$ with $n \ge 5$ are **not** solvable, while $S_1, S_2, S_3, S_4$ are solvable.
