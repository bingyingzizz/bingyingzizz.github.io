# Real Banach Spaces Are Acyclic on Compact Hausdorff Spaces　`thm.real-banach-chaus-acyclic`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=thm.real-banach-chaus-acyclic)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./thm.real-banach-chaus-acyclic.md)

(1) Map a Stonean space $S_{0}$ onto $S$ (using filtered colimits of compact Hausdorff spaces along closed inclusions and Stonean covers). (2) On $S_{0}$, acyclicity for Banach coefficients on Stone spaces gives acyclicity of the Čech complex;

(3) Compare the Čech complex of $S_{0} \to S$ with the cohomology of $S$ itself. The discrepancy term lies in $C(K, V)$;

(4) Use **Banach-valued Tietze extension** to eliminate the discrepancy, obtaining **bounded** acyclicity on $S$;
