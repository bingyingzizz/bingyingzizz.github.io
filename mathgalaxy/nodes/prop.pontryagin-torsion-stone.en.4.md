# torsion Stonean　`prop.pontryagin-torsion-stone`

[in graph](../#n=prop.pontryagin-torsion-stone)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./prop.pontryagin-torsion-stone.md)

**Why torsion-freeness corresponds to connectedness.** Conversely, if $M$ is torsion-free (like $\mathbb{Z}$), its dual $\widehat{M}$ is connected. For example, $\widehat{\mathbb{Z}} = \mathbb{T}$ is connected, whereas $\widehat{\mathbb{Z}/n} = \mathbb{Z}/n$ is finite, discrete, and extremally disconnected.
