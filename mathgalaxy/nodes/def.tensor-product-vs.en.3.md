⚠ Can $v \otimes w = 0$ happen when $v \ne 0, w \ne 0$? No (the tensor product is a free construction, with basis $e_i \otimes f_j$).

This is a different question from “not decomposable into pure tensors”.

⭐ The tensor product is **symmetric** ($V \otimes W \cong W \otimes V$) and associative, with unit $K$ ($V \otimes K \cong V$) — so finite-dimensional vector spaces with $\otimes$ form a symmetric monoidal category.
