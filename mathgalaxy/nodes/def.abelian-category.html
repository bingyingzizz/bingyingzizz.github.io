陈述

阿贝尔范畴是满足下列等价条件之一的预阿贝尔范畴（预加法、加法、预阿贝尔的定义见 预加法范畴、加法范畴、预阿贝尔范畴）：

  1. 每个单态射都是正则的（对偶地每个满态射都是正则的）；
  2. 若 i:N↣Mi : N \rightarrowtail Mi:N↣M 是单态射，则 N≅ker⁡(M↠coker⁡(i))N \cong \ker\bigl(M \twoheadrightarrow \operatorname{coker}(i)\bigr)N≅ker(M↠coker(i))（对偶同理）；
  3. 每个态射都能（在相差一个同构的意义下唯一地）分解成满态射接单态射；
  4. 对每个 f:M→Nf : M \to Nf:M→N 有 ker⁡(N→coker⁡f)≅coker⁡(ker⁡f→M)\ker\bigl(N \to \operatorname{coker} f\bigr) \cong \operatorname{coker}\bigl(\ker f \to M\bigr)ker(N→cokerf)≅coker(kerf→M)。

说明

在阿贝尔范畴里，人们习惯说单射 / 满射而不说单态射 / 满态射 —— 因为第 1 条保证了「正则的」与「可消的」是一回事。

例：A-ModA\text{-}\mathbf{Mod}A-Mod 是阿贝尔范畴；拓扑斯上的 Ab(T)\mathbf{Ab}(\mathcal{T})Ab(T) 也是；AbCHaus\mathbf{AbCHaus}AbCHaus 也是。

而 AbTop\mathbf{AbTop}AbTop 不是（Zdisc→Zcoarse\mathbb{Z}^{\mathrm{disc}} \to \mathbb{Z}^{\mathrm{coarse}}Zdisc→Zcoarse 这个恒等映射不严格），AbHaus\mathbf{AbHaus}AbHaus 也不是。

阿贝尔范畴是整套同调代数的工作环境：有核有余核，「正合列」才说得出口；而加法范畴只提供零对象与直和这两样加法结构，还不足以谈「像」。

四条里哪条是“真公理”、哪条是它的推论（阿贝尔范畴的这组等价常被并列，其实第 4 条最弱也最像“公理”，前三条各有代价）：

  • (1)   ⟹  \implies⟹ (2)：一次取核 + 余核的图追踪。i:N↣Mi : N \rightarrowtail Mi:N↣M 把 NNN 等同到 ker⁡(M→coker⁡i)\ker(M \to \operatorname{coker} i)ker(M→cokeri)；这一步用「单态射 = 自身的核」这一条预阿贝尔性质，不用加法结构之外的额外假设。
  • (2)   ⟹  \implies⟹ (1)：反向几乎免费（NNN 同构于余核的核 就是说 iii 是某个态的核，即正则）。
  • (1)   ⟺  \iff⟺ (3)：这一步要「有核有余核」（即预阿贝尔）。f=M→Nf = M \to Nf=M→N 分解成 M↠im⁡f↣NM \twoheadrightarrow \operatorname{im} f \rightarrowtail NM↠imf↣N；「满射段正则」正是 (1)。⚠️ 唯一性在只差一个同构的意义下成立 —— 「唯一」这个词在这里是本质的：分解不是严格的，因为同一个像可以有不同的代表。
  • (1)   ⟺  \iff⟺ (4)：这是最“代数”的一条（Abel 的原始形式）：ker⁡(N→coker⁡f)≅coker⁡(ker⁡f→M)\ker(N \to \operatorname{coker} f) \cong \operatorname{coker}(\ker f \to M)ker(N→cokerf)≅coker(kerf→M)。它等价于「第一同构定理在范畴里成立」，需要核与余核都存在且单/满态射的正则性。

⚠️ 预阿贝尔 ≠\ne= 阿贝尔的关键：预阿贝尔只要求核与余核存在；(1)–(4) 里任何一条都可以失败。最标准的反例是 PreAb\mathbf{PreAb}PreAb（预阿贝尔群范畴）或「带滤过的向量空间」范畴 —— 里面有满态射不是余核、单态射不是核的情形。所以阿贝尔 = 预阿贝尔 + （任一上述等价条件），这个“加一条”不能省。

⚠️ 另两处易错：

  • 「加法范畴」的定义里「所有直和」与「所有有限积」等价，但要预加法为前提（一般范畴里有限积与有限余积不必重合）。
  • 「有所有有限极限与余极限」是 (2) 那一行的说法，它不蕴含阿贝尔性（例如 Set\mathbf{Set}Set 有有限极限与余积，却远非阿贝尔）—— 差别正在「不交、万有、有效」这些附加条件。

📌 实践里怎么用：最常验证的是 (3)。一旦每个映射都有「满射接单射」的像分解，就可以定义正合列、作蛇引理、取导出函子 —— 同调代数整套机器从这里开动。

The ladder of definitions runs preadditive→zero object→direct sums→additive→preabelian→abelian\text{preadditive} \to \text{zero object} \to \text{direct sums} \to \text{additive} \to \text{preabelian} \to \text{abelian}preadditive→zero object→direct sums→additive→preabelian→abelian, where preadditive means Ab\mathbf{Ab}Ab-enriched with bilinear composition, preabelian means that kernels and cokernels exist, and abelian means any one of four equivalent conditions: every monomorphism is regular; N≅ker⁡(M→coker⁡i)N \cong \ker(M \to \operatorname{coker} i)N≅ker(M→cokeri) for i:N↣Mi : N \rightarrowtail Mi:N↣M; every map factors uniquely as an epimorphism followed by a monomorphism; ker⁡(N→coker⁡f)≅coker⁡(ker⁡f→M)\ker(N \to \operatorname{coker} f) \cong \operatorname{coker}(\ker f \to M)ker(N→cokerf)≅coker(kerf→M). Abelian categories are the working environment of homological algebra — exactness needs kernels and cokernels. A-ModA\text{-}\mathbf{Mod}A-Mod and Ab(T)\mathbf{Ab}(\mathcal{T})Ab(T) for a topos are abelian; AbTop\mathbf{AbTop}AbTop is not.

Statement stale

An abelian category is a preabelian category satisfying one of the following equivalent conditions (for preadditive, additive and preabelian categories see 预加法范畴, 加法范畴, 预阿贝尔范畴):

  1. every monomorphism is regular (dually, every epimorphism is regular);
  2. if i:N↣Mi : N \rightarrowtail Mi:N↣M is a monomorphism, then N≅ker⁡(M↠coker⁡(i))N \cong \ker\bigl(M \twoheadrightarrow \operatorname{coker}(i)\bigr)N≅ker(M↠coker(i)) (and dually);
  3. every morphism factors (uniquely up to isomorphism) as an epimorphism followed by a monomorphism;
  4. for every f:M→Nf : M \to Nf:M→N, ker⁡(N→coker⁡f)≅coker⁡(ker⁡f→M)\ker\bigl(N \to \operatorname{coker} f\bigr) \cong \operatorname{coker}\bigl(\ker f \to M\bigr)ker(N→cokerf)≅coker(kerf→M).

Notes stale

In an abelian category one says injective / surjective rather than monomorphism / epimorphism — because condition 1 guarantees that "regular" and "cancellable" are the same thing.

Examples: A-ModA\text{-}\mathbf{Mod}A-Mod is abelian; so is Ab(T)\mathbf{Ab}(\mathcal{T})Ab(T) for a topos; so is AbCHaus\mathbf{AbCHaus}AbCHaus. But AbTop\mathbf{AbTop}AbTop is not (the identity map Zdisc→Zcoarse\mathbb{Z}^{\mathrm{disc}} \to \mathbb{Z}^{\mathrm{coarse}}Zdisc→Zcoarse is not strict), and neither is AbHaus\mathbf{AbHaus}AbHaus.

An abelian category is the working environment of homological algebra as a whole: with kernels and cokernels one can even state "exact sequence"; an additive category only supplies the additive skeleton (zero object and direct sums).Which of the four is the real axiom, and which are its consequences (they are usually listed side by side, but condition 4 is the weakest and behaves most like an axiom, while the first three each cost something):

  • (1)   ⟹  \implies⟹ (2): one kernel-plus-cokernel diagram chase. i:N↣Mi : N \rightarrowtail Mi:N↣M identifies NNN with ker⁡(M→coker⁡i)\ker(M \to \operatorname{coker} i)ker(M→cokeri); this uses only the preabelian fact that a monomorphism is its own kernel — no assumption beyond the additive structure.
  • (2)   ⟹  \implies⟹ (1): almost free in reverse (NNN being isomorphic to the kernel of a cokernel simply says iii is a kernel, i.e. regular).
  • (1)   ⟺  \iff⟺ (3): this step needs kernels and cokernels (i.e. preabelian). Factor f=M→Nf = M \to Nf=M→N as M↠im⁡f↣NM \twoheadrightarrow \operatorname{im} f \rightarrowtail NM↠imf↣N; "the epimorphism half is regular" is exactly (1). ⚠ Uniqueness holds up to isomorphism — the word matters: the factorisation is not strict, since one image can have different representatives.
  • (1)   ⟺  \iff⟺ (4): the most "algebraic" one (Abel's original form): ker⁡(N→coker⁡f)≅coker⁡(ker⁡f→M)\ker(N \to \operatorname{coker} f) \cong \operatorname{coker}(\ker f \to M)ker(N→cokerf)≅coker(kerf→M). It is equivalent to "the first isomorphism theorem holds in the category", and needs kernels and cokernels to exist plus regularity of mono/epi.

⚠ Preabelian ≠\ne= abelian — the point: preabelian asks only that kernels and cokernels exist; any one of (1)–(4) can fail. The standard counterexamples are PreAb\mathbf{PreAb}PreAb (preabelian groups) or filtered vector spaces, where an epimorphism need not be a cokernel and a monomorphism need not be a kernel. So abelian = preabelian + (any one of the equivalent conditions), and that last clause cannot be dropped.

⚠ Two more traps: in the definition of "additive category", "all direct sums" and "all finite products" agree only given preadditivity (in a general category finite products and finite coproducts need not coincide); and "all finite limits and colimits" is the phrasing attached to (2), which does not imply abelianness (Set\mathbf{Set}Set has finite limits and coproducts yet is far from abelian) — the difference lies in disjointness, universality and effectiveness.
