# Schur Triangularization　`thm.schur-triangularization`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=thm.schur-triangularization)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./thm.schur-triangularization.md)

**The diagonal entries are the eigenvalues**: $U^{*}AU$ and $A$ are similar, so they have the same characteristic polynomial. The characteristic polynomial of the block upper triangular matrix (upper left $\lambda$, lower right $A_{1}$, lower left $0$) is $(t - \lambda)\chi_{A_{1}}(t)$. Therefore the multiset of diagonal entries of $U^{*}AU$ is exactly $\operatorname{spec}(A)$. Here the diagonal entries of $T_{1}$ are the eigenvalues of $A_{1}$ by induction, and $\operatorname{spec}(A) = \{\lambda\} \cup \operatorname{spec}(A_{1})$.
