# a filtered complex gives an exact couple　`prop.filtered-complex-exact-couple`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=prop.filtered-complex-exact-couple)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./prop.filtered-complex-exact-couple.md)

$$E_{r}^{p,q} = Z_{r}^{p,q} \big/ B_{r}^{p,q}, \qquad Z_{r}^{p,q} := F^{p}K^{p+q} \cap d^{-1}\bigl(F^{p+r}K^{p+q+1}\bigr), \qquad B_{r}^{p,q} := F^{p}K^{p+q} \cap d\bigl(F^{p-r+1}K^{p+q-1}\bigr),$$

so it **agrees with the spectral sequence obtained by the direct construction**. If the filtration is bounded on both sides, it converges to $E_{\infty}^{p,q} = \operatorname{Gr}^{p} H^{p+q}(K)$.
