# Euler characteristic and genus　`prop.eulerchar-genus`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=prop.eulerchar-genus)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./prop.eulerchar-genus.md)

⭐ Three examples: for $S^{2}$, $g=0$ and $\chi=2$; for $T^{2}$, $g=1$ and $\chi=0$; for the double torus, $g=2$ and $\chi=-2$.

📌 Connection with geometry: Gauss–Bonnet gives $\int_{M}K\,\mathrm{d}A=2\pi\chi(M)$. Combining it with this proposition gives $\int K=4\pi(1-g)$, so the total curvature depends only on the genus.
