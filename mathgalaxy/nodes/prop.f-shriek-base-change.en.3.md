# base change for the exceptional inverse image　`prop.f-shriek-base-change`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=prop.f-shriek-base-change)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./prop.f-shriek-base-change.md)

⭐ The proof reduces to two cases: $f$ surjective (then $f_! = f_*$ is the forgetful functor and $f^! = \operatorname{RHom}_R(A,-)$, where base change is evident) and $A = R[T]$ (then $f_!M = M \otimes_{(A,R)_\blacksquare} R((T^{-1}))/R[T][-1]$, $f^!R = R[T][1]$, and base change reduces to that of $R((T^{-1}))/R[T]$).

⚠ Finite Tor dimension is necessary: otherwise $f^!R$ is not a finite complex and the twisting formula fails.
