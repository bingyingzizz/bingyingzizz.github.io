## Statement · **draft**
Let $F : J \to I$ be a functor. For $i \in I$, write $i \downarrow F$ for the comma category whose objects are $\{\, f : i \to F(j) \,\}$ ($j \in J$).

One says that $F$ is **cofinal** (also written **final**) if for every $i \in I$:

$i \downarrow F$ is **non-empty and connected**.
