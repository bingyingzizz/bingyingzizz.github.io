# Flatness and the Vanishing of Tor　`prop.tor-flat-vanishing`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=prop.tor-flat-vanishing)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./prop.tor-flat-vanishing.md)

- (1) $\iff$ (2): **A long exact sequence is needed** (flatness means that $(-)\otimes F$ is exact. Its equivalence to the universal vanishing of $\operatorname{Tor}_{1}$ follows from the long exact sequence for $\operatorname{Tor}$).
- (2) $\implies$ (3): **This step uses dimension shifting**. If $\operatorname{Tor}_{1}(F, N) = 0$ for all $N$, take the short exact sequence for $N$ and use the long exact sequence to obtain $\operatorname{Tor}_{n} = 0$ by induction. ⚠️ This requires the long exact sequence (the Tor version of `thm.long-exact`).
- (3) $\implies$ (2): **A special case** ($n = 1$).
