Verify the evaluation map $M \to \widehat{\widehat M}$ by elementary computation for finite and finitely generated groups. Then use that $M$ is the filtered colimit of its finitely generated subgroups, duality turns colimits into inverse limits, and both sides are exact, to obtain the isomorphism by passing to the limit ✓.
