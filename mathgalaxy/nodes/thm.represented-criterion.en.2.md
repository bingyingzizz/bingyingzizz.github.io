## Notes · **draft**
This connects two ways of describing representability. A universal element describes it **at the level of elements**, and $h^{X} \cong F$ describes it **at the level of functors**. To verify representability, one often uses the first description by finding the element $s$. To use representability, one often uses the second description to transport calculations directly along the isomorphism.
