Statement

Let f:Vn→Kf : V^{n} \to Kf:Vn→K be a multilinear form.

  • fff is called symmetric if and only if for every permutation σ\sigmaσ one has f(vσ(1),…,vσ(n))=f(v1,…,vn)f(v_{\sigma(1)}, \ldots, v_{\sigma(n)}) = f(v_1, \ldots, v_n)f(vσ(1)​,…,vσ(n)​)=f(v1​,…,vn​);
  • fff is called alternating if and only if f=0f = 0f=0 whenever two of its arguments are equal;
  • fff is called skew-symmetric if and only if f(vσ(1),…)=sgn⁡(σ) f(v1,…)f(v_{\sigma(1)}, \ldots) = \operatorname{sgn}(\sigma)\, f(v_1, \ldots)f(vσ(1)​,…)=sgn(σ)f(v1​,…).

Over a field of characteristic ≠2\ne 2=2, alternating   ⟺  \iff⟺ skew-symmetric.

Notes

⭐ The criterion for "alternating" (zero when two arguments coincide) is the easiest to use, while the formula for "skew-symmetric" (with signs) is the easiest to compute with — the two agree when char⁡K≠2\operatorname{char} K \ne 2charK=2.

⚠ They differ when char⁡K=2\operatorname{char} K = 2charK=2: then −1=1-1 = 1−1=1, so every skew-symmetric form is automatically symmetric, and alternation is the stronger condition.

The convention here is char⁡K=0\operatorname{char} K = 0charK=0, or at least ≠2\ne 2=2 (all of R,C,Q\mathbb{R}, \mathbb{C}, \mathbb{Q}R,C,Q qualify).

⭐ Alternation is the core property that makes a determinant a determinant: swapping two rows changes the sign (sgn⁡(σ)\operatorname{sgn}(\sigma)sgn(σ)), and two equal rows give zero.

Which hypothesis each direction uses (alternating implies skew-symmetric without dividing by two; the converse needs it):

  • Alternating   ⟺  \iff⟺ the value is zero when two arguments coincide; this is just unfolding the definition and holds in any characteristic.
  • Alternating   ⟹  \implies⟹ swapping two arguments changes the sign: substitute u+vu+vu+v into the two slots at once and expand by multilinearity; then decompose the permutation to get skew-symmetry, with no assumption on char⁡K≠2\operatorname{char}K\ne2charK=2.
  • Skew-symmetric   ⟹  \implies⟹ alternating: swapping two equal arguments gives 2f=02f=02f=0, and one needs char⁡K≠2\operatorname{char}K\ne2charK=2 to cancel 222; this is the genuine field condition in the equivalence.
  • A counterexample in characteristic 222: over K=F2K=\mathbb F_2K=F2​ the bilinear form f(x,y)=xyf(x,y)=xyf(x,y)=xy is skew-symmetric but f(1,1)=1f(1,1)=1f(1,1)=1, so it is not alternating.
