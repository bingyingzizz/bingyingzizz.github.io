## Statement · **draft**
A morphism $f : X \to Y$ is called **strict** if the middle step of its factorization through the **coimage**, $X \twoheadrightarrow \operatorname{coim} f \to \operatorname{im} f \hookrightarrow Y$, is an **isomorphism** — that is, the natural map "from coimage to image" is invertible.
