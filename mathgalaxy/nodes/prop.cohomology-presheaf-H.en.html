prop.cohomology-presheaf-H

Statement

Let X∈CX \in \mathcal{C}X∈C and M∙M^{\bullet}M∙ be a complex of abelian sheaves, and let H\mathcal{H}H denote the inclusion functor "regard an abelian sheaf as a presheaf". Then

Γ(X, RH(M∙))  =  RΓ(X, M∙),Γ(X, Hn(M∙))  =  Hn(X, M∙).\Gamma\bigl(X,\ R\mathcal{H}(M^{\bullet})\bigr) \;=\; R\Gamma\bigl(X,\ M^{\bullet}\bigr), \qquad \Gamma\bigl(X,\ \mathcal{H}^{n}(M^{\bullet})\bigr) \;=\; H^{n}\bigl(X,\ M^{\bullet}\bigr).Γ(X, RH(M∙))=RΓ(X, M∙),Γ(X, Hn(M∙))=Hn(X, M∙).

Notes

Proof. H\mathcal{H}H is adjoint to sheafification, and sheafification is exact ✓, so H\mathcal{H}H preserves injectives ✓ (the right adjoint of an exact left adjoint preserves injectives).

Conversely, the functor M↦Γ(X,M)M \mapsto \Gamma(X, M)M↦Γ(X,M) is exact on presheaves ✓.

Applying "composition of derived functors with an exact factor" to the two gives the first identity ✓;

the second is obtained by taking nnn-th cohomology and reading off values on the sheaf side ✓.

∎

⭐ Use: the value of the cohomology presheaf Hn(M)H^{n}(M)Hn(M) is cohomology — this sentence is used repeatedly when relating Čech cohomology to sheaf cohomology.
