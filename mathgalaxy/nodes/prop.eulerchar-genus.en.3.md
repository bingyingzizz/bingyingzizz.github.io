**0. Conventions and notation.** $S$ is a closed surface (`def.closed-surface`), and $g=\tfrac12\operatorname{rank}H_{1}(S;\mathbb{Z})$ (`def.genus`, used directly as the definition). Compute $\chi(S)$ from any triangulation: `thm.euler-characteristic` gives $\chi(K)=\sum_{p}(-1)^{p}\alpha_{p}=\sum_{p}(-1)^{p}\beta_{p}(K)$, and `thm.simplicial-approximation` gives $\beta_{p}(K)=\beta_{p}(S)$. Hence $\sum_{p}(-1)^{p}\beta_{p}(S)$ is independent of the triangulation, and we denote it by $\chi(S)$. Here, orientability of $S$ is understood in the topological sense.
