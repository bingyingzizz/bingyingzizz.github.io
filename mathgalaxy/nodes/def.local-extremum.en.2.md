Both are called **local extremum points**. Replacing $\le$, $\ge$ by $<$, $>$ (for $x \ne x_0$) gives **strict** local extrema.

If $x_0$ lies in the **interior** of the domain and is a local extremum point, it is called an **interior extremum point**.

## Notes · **draft**
⭐ The word "local" is the whole point: the definition compares only values near $x_0$, not values far away.

So there can be many local extremum points ($\sin x$ has infinitely many), while a **global** extremum (the one supplied by the extreme value theorem) is at most two.
