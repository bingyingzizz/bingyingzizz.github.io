⚠ **What does not follow from it**: the comparison test (more basic), the root test (which goes through $\limsup$ and comparison with a geometric series, and is not mutually derivable with Kummer; rather, "existence of the ratio limit $\implies$ existence of the root limit, and they are equal", so the root test is stronger), the integral test and the condensation test (one replaces the sum by an improper integral, the other groups terms by $2^k$).

Each of these four gets its own node.
