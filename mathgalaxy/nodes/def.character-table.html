Statement draft

Let GGG be a finite group, char⁡F=0\operatorname{char}F=0charF=0, and FFF algebraically closed. Put Irr⁡(G)F={V1,…,Vs}\operatorname{Irr}(G)_F=\{V_1,\ldots,V_s\}Irr(G)F​={V1​,…,Vs​} and let CL(G)={C1,…,Cr}CL(G)=\{C_1,\ldots,C_r\}CL(G)={C1​,…,Cr​} be the conjugacy classes with representatives gj∈Cjg_j\in C_jgj​∈Cj​. The character table is the s×rs\times rs×r matrix T=(χVi(gj))i,jT=(\chi_{V_i}(g_j))_{i,j}T=(χVi​​(gj​))i,j​.

Notes draft

The rows are the irreducible characters; char⁡F=0\operatorname{char}F=0charF=0 guarantees that χi=χj\chi_i=\chi_jχi​=χj​ iff Vi≅VjV_i\cong V_jVi​≅Vj​ (thm.character-determines-semisimple). The rows are orthonormal for the character inner product (thm.character-orthogonality). Characters are class functions, so the columns depend only on the conjugacy class; s≤rs\le rs≤r, with equality requiring the additional fact that the characters form a basis of the class-function space.
