Statement

Let RRR be a ring and FFF a right RRR-module. FFF is flat if the functor

F⊗R−:R-Mod⟶AbF \otimes_{R} - : R\text{-}\mathbf{Mod} \longrightarrow \mathbf{Ab}F⊗R​−:R-Mod⟶Ab

is exact. Concretely: for every short exact sequence 0→M1→M→M2→00 \to M_{1} \to M \to M_{2} \to 00→M1​→M→M2​→0, after tensoring

0→F⊗RM1→F⊗RM→F⊗RM2→00 \to F\otimes_{R} M_{1} \to F\otimes_{R} M \to F\otimes_{R} M_{2} \to 00→F⊗R​M1​→F⊗R​M→F⊗R​M2​→0

is still exact. (The right end is automatically surjective because ⊗\otimes⊗ is right exact; so the real requirement is only "preserves injectivity".)

Notes

There are many equivalent formulations; the usual ones are:

  • Tor⁡nR(F,−)=0\operatorname{Tor}_{n}^{R}(F, -) = 0TornR​(F,−)=0 for all n≥1n \ge 1n≥1 (see "flat iff all higher Tor vanish");
  • for every finitely generated ideal I⊆RI \subseteq RI⊆R, the map F⊗I→F⊗RF\otimes I \to F\otimes RF⊗I→F⊗R is injective (Lambek's criterion — only finitely generated ideals need be checked);
  • F=lim→⁡FiF = \varinjlim F_{i}F=lim​Fi​ with FiF_{i}Fi​ finitely generated free (see "flat modules are filtered colimits of finitely generated free modules").

Which modules are flat: free modules, projective modules (a projective is a direct summand of a free module, and direct summands preserve flatness), localizations S−1RS^{-1}RS−1R (tensoring commutes with localization, and ⊗\otimes⊗ is exact over RRR). Over an integral domain a flat module is necessarily torsion-free; over a PID (more generally a Bézout domain) the converse also holds, so there "flat = torsion-free" ✓.

⚠️ Flat and projective are not equivalent: Q\mathbb{Q}Q is flat over Z\mathbb{Z}Z (Q\mathbb{Q}Q is torsion-free) but not projective (Q\mathbb{Q}Q is not free, nor a direct summand of a free module). So "flat" is indeed one level weaker than "projective".

⚠️ Not the same as flatness in a topos: "flat abelian group" in a category of sheaves is a different definition (a double condition using filtered colimits / exactness of tensoring), see "flat abelian sheaves". This entry refers only to module categories.
