## Statement · **draft**
Let $X \subseteq \mathbb{R}$, $f : X \to \mathbb{R}$. Then $f$ is **uniformly continuous** if and only if

$$\forall \varepsilon > 0,\ \exists \delta > 0,\ \forall x, x' \in X :\ |x - x'| < \delta \implies |f(x) - f(x')| < \varepsilon$$
