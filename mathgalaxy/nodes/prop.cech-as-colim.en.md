## Statement · **draft**
If $X \in \mathcal{C}$ and $M$ is an abelian presheaf, then $\check{H}^{n}(X, M) = \varinjlim_{R \in J(X)} H^{n}(R, M)$ (on $X$, the filtered colimit over all covering sieves $R$).
