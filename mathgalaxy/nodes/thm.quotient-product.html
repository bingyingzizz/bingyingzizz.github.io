设 q:X→Yq : X \to Yq:X→Y 是商映射，ZZZ 是局部紧 Hausdorff 空间。则

q×idZ:X×Z⟶Y×Zq \times \mathrm{id}_{Z} : X \times Z \longrightarrow Y \times Zq×idZ​:X×Z⟶Y×Z

也是商映射。

⭐ 这是「紧生成空间对积封闭」那条的引擎，也是搜「商映射」「局部紧」时该落到的地方。

V×K  ⊆  U,y∈V,    z∈K,V \times K \;\subseteq\; U, \qquad y \in V,\;\; z \in K,V×K⊆U,y∈V,z∈K,

其中 VVV 开、KKK 是 zzz 的紧邻域（局部紧 Hausdorff   ⟹  \implies⟹ 每点有紧邻域基，可以要求 KKK 落在任意给定的邻域里）。由 qqq 是商映射，q−1(V)q^{-1}(V)q−1(V) 是 XXX 中的开集，于是

q−1(V)×K  ⊆  Wq^{-1}(V) \times K \;\subseteq\; Wq−1(V)×K⊆W

⚠️ 局部紧这个条件不能省。 一般情形下「商映射乘 id」不是商映射：取 qqq 是某条坏商、ZZZ 是一个既不局部紧又不紧生成的空间，乘积的商拓扑会严格细于商空间应有的拓扑。

Abstract reviewed

If q:X→Yq : X \to Yq:X→Y is a quotient map and ZZZ is locally compact Hausdorff, then q×idZ:X×Z→Y×Zq \times \mathrm{id}_Z : X \times Z \to Y \times Zq×idZ​:X×Z→Y×Z is again a quotient map. For a point (x,z)(x,z)(x,z) of the preimage of an open set one picks a basic open V×K⊆UV \times K \subseteq UV×K⊆U with KKK a compact neighbourhood of zzz; then q−1(V)×Kq^{-1}(V) \times Kq−1(V)×K is an open neighbourhood of (x,z)(x,z)(x,z) inside that preimage. Local compactness cannot be dropped. This is the engine behind "compactly generated spaces are closed under products".

Statement reviewed

Let q:X→Yq : X \to Yq:X→Y be a quotient map and ZZZ a locally compact Hausdorff space. Then

q×idZ:X×Z⟶Y×Zq \times \mathrm{id}_{Z} : X \times Z \longrightarrow Y \times Zq×idZ​:X×Z⟶Y×Z

is again a quotient map.

Notes reviewed

⭐ This is the engine behind "compactly generated spaces are closed under products", and it is where a search for "quotient map" or "locally compact" should land.

How the proof goes (continuity of the inverse). Write W:=(q×idZ)−1(U)W := (q \times \mathrm{id}_{Z})^{-1}(U)W:=(q×idZ​)−1(U) and show that WWW is open. Take a point (x,z)∈W(x, z) \in W(x,z)∈W and put y=q(x)y = q(x)y=q(x). Since UUU is open in Y×ZY \times ZY×Z, one can pick a basic open set

V×K  ⊆  U,y∈V,    z∈K,V \times K \;\subseteq\; U, \qquad y \in V,\;\; z \in K,V×K⊆U,y∈V,z∈K,

where VVV is open and KKK is a compact neighbourhood of zzz (a locally compact Hausdorff space has a base of compact neighbourhoods at every point, and KKK may be required to lie inside any prescribed neighbourhood). Since qqq is a quotient map, q−1(V)q^{-1}(V)q−1(V) is open in XXX, so

q−1(V)×K  ⊆  Wq^{-1}(V) \times K \;\subseteq\; Wq−1(V)×K⊆W

is an open neighbourhood of (x,z)(x, z)(x,z) — hence (x,z)(x, z)(x,z) is an interior point of WWW, so WWW is open. ∎

⚠ The locally compact hypothesis cannot be dropped. In general "a quotient map times the identity" is not a quotient map: take qqq to be some badly behaved quotient and ZZZ a space that is neither locally compact nor compactly generated; the product topology is then strictly finer than the topology the quotient space should have.

Terminology: some write this as "ZZZ locally compact Hausdorff   ⟹  \implies⟹ the functor −×Z- \times Z−×Z preserves quotient maps".
