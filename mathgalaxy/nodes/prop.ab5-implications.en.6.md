# Implications among the AB Axioms　`prop.ab5-implications`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=prop.ab5-implications)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./prop.ab5-implications.md)

Idea: AB5 makes **co**limits of shape $\mathbb{N}$ well behaved, AB5* makes **inverse** limits of shape $\mathbb{N}$ well behaved;

if both held, one would be forced into a Milnor-type exact sequence (comparing $\prod$ and $\bigoplus$), which in an abelian category can hold only for the zero object ✓.
- **Item 3**: first half — $\mathbf{Ab}(\mathcal{T})$ is a sheaf category on a topos, hence a Grothendieck category, so AB5 + AB3* (item 1) ✓;
