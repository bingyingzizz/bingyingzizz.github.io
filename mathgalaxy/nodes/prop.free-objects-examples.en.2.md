# examples of free objects　`prop.free-objects-examples`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=prop.free-objects-examples)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./prop.free-objects-examples.md)

in $G\text{-}\mathbf{Set}$ it is $G \times X$ (**left** regular action $g \cdot (h, x) = (gh, x)$; when $X$ is a single point it degenerates to $G$ itself);

in $A\text{-}\mathbf{Mod}$ it is $A^{(X)}$;

in $k\text{-}\mathbf{Alg}$ it is the **tensor algebra**.

## Notes · **draft**
**Proof.** Check the universal property of free objects $\operatorname{Hom}(FX, -) \cong \operatorname{Hom}_{\mathbf{Set}}\bigl(X, U(-)\bigr)$ in each case ✓. ∎
