## Statement · **draft**
Let $\mu(X) < \infty$, and $f_n \to f$ a.e. Then $f_n \to f$ **almost uniformly**:

$$\forall\varepsilon > 0,\ \exists E \subseteq X :\ \mu(E) < \varepsilon$$

and $f_n \rightrightarrows  f$ **converges uniformly** on $X \setminus E$.
