**Note on the real case**: Nonreal eigenvalues occur in conjugate pairs. Taking real and imaginary parts gives an invariant two-dimensional real subspace. The same induction gives the real Schur form (with diagonal block sizes $1\times 1$ or $2\times 2$). The details are omitted. ∎
