That is, $L = -P^{-1}$. The matrices are inverses **up to a sign** (when $n = 1$, we have $L = P = \begin{pmatrix}0 & 1\cr -1 & 0\end{pmatrix}$ and $LP = -I$, and the general formula is $\sum_{k} [u_{i},u_{k}]\{u_{k},u_{j}\} = -\delta_{ij}$).

**Criterion for a canonical transformation**: The transformation $(q, p) \mapsto (Q, P)$ is a **canonical transformation** (symplectomorphism) $\iff$ for every $i, j$, both $[Q_{i}, Q_{j}] = [P_{i}, P_{j}] = 0$ and $[Q_{i}, P_{j}] = \delta_{ij}$ hold.
