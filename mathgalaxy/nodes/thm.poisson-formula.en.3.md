## Notes · **draft**
⭐ Use: to solve the **Dirichlet problem** on the disc — given a continuous boundary function $b$, replace $u(r e^{i\theta})$ in the formula above by $b$; the resulting function is harmonic in the disc and tends to $b$ as one approaches the boundary value from inside. That the values of a harmonic function inside are “completely determined by the boundary” is the whole content.
