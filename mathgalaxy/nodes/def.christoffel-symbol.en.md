## Statement · **draft**
In plain words: moving a vector along a curve on a surface needs a rule, and that rule is called a **connection**; once local coordinates are chosen, the connection degenerates into $2^{3}$ functions $\Gamma^{k}_{ij}$, which say "how much of a $\partial_{k}$ component appears when $\partial_{j}$ is transported a little in the $\partial_{i}$ direction".
