⚠ Geometric multiplicity $\le$ algebraic multiplicity;

the standard example where they differ is $\begin{pmatrix} 1 & 1 \\ 0 & 1 \end{pmatrix}$ ($\lambda = 1$: algebraic multiplicity 2, geometric multiplicity 1) — which is exactly why it is not diagonalizable.
