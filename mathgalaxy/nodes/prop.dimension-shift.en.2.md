# Dimension Shift　`prop.dimension-shift`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=prop.dimension-shift)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./prop.dimension-shift.md)

Iterating: for $k \ge 0$, $\operatorname{Ext}^{n+k}_{R}(M, N) \cong \operatorname{Ext}^{n}_{R}(\Omega^{k}M, N)$ ($n \ge 1$).

## Notes · **draft**
**Proof (Ext side: resolve $N$ injectively).** Take an injective resolution of $N$: a quasi-isomorphism $N \to I^{\bullet}$. Since $I^{k}$ is injective, $\operatorname{Hom}(-, I^{k})$ is exact, so applying it termwise to the short exact sequence yields a **short exact sequence of complexes** (in the opposite direction)

$$0 \to \operatorname{Hom}(M, I^{\bullet}) \to \operatorname{Hom}(P, I^{\bullet}) \to \operatorname{Hom}(K, I^{\bullet}) \to 0 .$$
