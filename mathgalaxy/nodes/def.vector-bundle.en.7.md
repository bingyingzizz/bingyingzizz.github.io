# Vector Bundle　`def.vector-bundle`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=def.vector-bundle)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./def.vector-bundle.md)

**Rank $1$ is a line bundle**: the Möbius line bundle over $S^{1}$, the tautological line bundle over $\mathbb{RP}^{1}$, and the Hopf line bundle over $S^{2}$ are the three standard examples. Isomorphism classes of **real** line bundles are detected by $w_{1} \in H^{1}(M; \mathbb{Z}/2)$ (this separates the trivial and Möbius bundles over $S^{1}$, while $H^{2}(S^{1}; \mathbb{Z}) = 0$ cannot); **complex** line bundles use the first Chern class $c_{1} \in H^{2}$. This is the entry point of topology into geometry.
