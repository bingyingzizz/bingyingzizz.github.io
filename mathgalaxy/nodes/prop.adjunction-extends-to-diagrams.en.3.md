# Adjunctions Extend to Diagram Categories　`prop.adjunction-extends-to-diagrams`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=prop.adjunction-extends-to-diagrams)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./prop.adjunction-extends-to-diagrams.md)

At each $i \in I$, apply $F \dashv G$ to get $\operatorname{Hom}(FD_{i}, E_{i}) \cong \operatorname{Hom}(D_{i}, GE_{i})$ ✓;

this family is compatible with the morphisms of $I$ (because the original isomorphism is natural in $F,G$) ✓, so the isomorphisms combine into one between the two Hom-sets ✓.

∎
