# arithmetic of continuous functions　`prop.continuous-arithmetic`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=prop.continuous-arithmetic)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./prop.continuous-arithmetic.md)

rational functions are continuous where the denominator does not vanish;

by `thm.elementary-derivatives` and power series, $\exp$, $\sin$, $\cos$ are continuous too — so "elementary functions are continuous on their domains" is **proved**, not stipulated.
