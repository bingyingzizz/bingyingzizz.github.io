# absolutely continuous implies bounded variation　`lem.ac-subset-bv`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=lem.ac-subset-bv)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./lem.ac-subset-bv.md)

⚠ The converse fails: **the Cantor function is of bounded variation on [0,1] but not absolutely continuous**. So the inclusion is strict.

⭐ This is the preparation for the “fundamental theorem of calculus TFAE” below: it guarantees $(a) \implies BV$, hence one may speak of $F'$.
