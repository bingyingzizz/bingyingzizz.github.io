## Statement · **draft**
Let $f$ be entire and **not constant**. Then $f$ takes every value in $\mathbb{C}$ except **at most one** point.

That is: there is at most one $w_{0} \in \mathbb{C}$ such that the equation $f(z) = w$ has a solution for every $w \ne w_{0}$.

Example: $e^{z}$ omits $0$ (and indeed $0$ is not attained), exactly one.
