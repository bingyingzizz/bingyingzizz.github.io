## Notes · **draft**
⚠️ **There is no requirement that $\|s\| = 0 \implies s = 0$**. This is exactly what the prefix “semi” means. The elements of norm $0$ form a subgroup $M_{0} = \{\, s : \|s\| = 0 \,\}$.

**Why start with seminorms.** Completion starts from a seminormed space. Before completing it, one first quotients out $M_{0}$. The definition of $K$-bounded exactness is most natural for **seminormed** groups, while the Banach setting gives the cleanest formulation. This is why both notions are retained.
