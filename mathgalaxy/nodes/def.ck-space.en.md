## Statement · **draft**
Let $K$ be compact Hausdorff and $(E, d)$ a metric space. On $C(K, E)$ define

$$\Delta(f, g) := \sup_{x \in K} d\bigl(f(x), g(x)\bigr) .$$

When $E$ is normed this is the sup norm $\|f\| = \sup_x \|f(x)\|$.
