## Statement · **draft**

(a) $x \mapsto \langle x, \cdot\rangle$ is the image of the isometric isomorphism $X \to Y^*$, and $y \mapsto \langle \cdot, y\rangle$ is an isometric embedding of $Y \to X^*$.
