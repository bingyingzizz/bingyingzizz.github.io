## Statement · **draft**
Let $\mathcal{C}$ be an additive category. A **(co)chain complex** is a (long) sequence in $\mathcal{C}$ (see @@def.sequence-of-morphisms@@) $K$ such that $d^{n} \circ d^{n-1} = 0$ holds for every $n$; $d$ is called the **differential**.

Reversing the arrows (equivalently, working in $\mathcal{C}^{\mathrm{op}}$) gives a **chain complex**, written $K_{n} := K^{-n}$, $d_{n} := d^{-n}$.
