# isometry and orthogonal transformation　`def.isometry`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=def.isometry)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./def.isometry.md)

⭐ $O(n)$ is a compact Lie group (bounded and closed), and $SO(n) := \{Q : \det Q = 1\}$ is its connected component (the one containing the identity) — the rotation group.

⚠ The derivation "preserving the norm $\implies$ preserving the inner product" uses the **polarization identity**: the inner product can be recovered from the norm ($\langle u,v\rangle = \frac{1}{4}\sum_{k=0}^{3} i^{k}\|u + i^{k}v\|^{2}$) — this is the other half of the section "norms need not come from inner products".
