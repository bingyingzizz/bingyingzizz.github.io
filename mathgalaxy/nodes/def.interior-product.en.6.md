# interior product　`def.interior-product`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=def.interior-product)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./def.interior-product.md)

**In physics notation it is "contracting one index"**: the electromagnetic field $F = \frac{1}{2}F_{ij}\,\mathrm{d}x^{i}\wedge\mathrm{d}x^{j}$ and $\iota_{E}F$ give quantities such as "the magnetic flux in the direction of the electric field";

in Maxwell's equations $\mathrm{d}F = 0$, $\mathrm{d}\star F = J$, where $\star$ depends on the metric while $\iota$ and $\mathrm{d}$ are purely combinatorial.

**Relation to contraction**: $\iota_{X}\omega$ is exactly putting $X$ into the first slot and contracting — a special case of tensor contraction on alternating tensors, except that the inserted vector need not be a basis vector.
