## Notes · **draft**
**Four equivalent formulations; any one may serve as the definition**: $A$ is normal; $A$ has an orthonormal basis of eigenvectors; $A = U\Lambda U^{*}$; the spectral measure of $A$ takes only atomic values. In matrix language the first is the most economical, the second the most intuitive (the whole content of `thm.spectral-matrix` is "the two are equivalent").

**How to read the spectral decomposition**: $A = \sum_i \lambda_i P_i$, where $P_i = u_i u_i^{*}$ is the orthogonal projection onto the eigen-direction, satisfying $P_i^2 = P_i$, $P_iP_j = 0$ ($i \ne j$), $\sum_i P_i = I$.
