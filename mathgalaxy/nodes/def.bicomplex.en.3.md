# bicomplex　`def.bicomplex`

[in graph](../#n=def.bicomplex)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./def.bicomplex.md)

⭐ **How to turn it into an ordinary complex**: take the **total complex** $K^{n} := \bigoplus_{p+q=n} K^{p,q}$ (assuming the direct sum exists: in general $\mathcal{A}$ need not have countable direct sums — in the category of finite-dimensional $k$-vector spaces every diagonal can have infinitely many nonzero terms, and then the total complex does not exist; alternatively use the version "each diagonal has only finitely many nonzero terms"), with differential $d + d'$ (with signs). Then the relation between the homology of the bicomplex and "compute row by row, then column by column" is exactly what produces spectral sequences.
