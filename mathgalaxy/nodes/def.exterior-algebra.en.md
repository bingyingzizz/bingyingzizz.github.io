## Statement · **draft**
Take the direct sum of all exterior powers:

$$\bigwedge^{\bullet} V := \bigoplus_{k \ge 0} \bigwedge^{k} V \qquad (\bigwedge^{0} V := K,\ \bigwedge^{1} V := V)$$

With the multiplication $\wedge$ given by "tensor first, then take the image", it becomes a **graded-commutative algebra**:

$$(u \wedge v) \wedge w = u \wedge (v \wedge w), \qquad v \wedge w = (-1)^{|v| |w|}\ w \wedge v$$
