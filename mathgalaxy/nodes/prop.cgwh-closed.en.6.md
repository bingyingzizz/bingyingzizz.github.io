By the lemma above and **compactness** of $S$, $g(S)$ lands in **some single** $X_{i}$ (the standard "a compact set lands in one piece" lemma for filtered colimits);

moreover $X_{i}$ is closed in $X$, and the image of $g$ through $X_{i}$ is closed in $X_{i}$ $\implies$ $g(S)$ is closed in $X$ ✓.
- **$X$ is compactly generated**: from the description of closed sets of the colimit (decided piecewise) and the compact generation of each piece, checking piecewise gives it ✓. ∎
