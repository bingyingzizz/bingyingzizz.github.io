## Statement · **draft**
Let $\preceq$ be a binary relation on a set $A$. Then $\preceq$ is a **partial order on $A$** if and only if:

- **Reflexive**: $\forall x \in A, x \preceq x$;
- **Antisymmetric**: $\forall x, y \in A ( x \preceq y \wedge y \preceq x \to x = y )$;
- **Transitive**: $\forall x, y, z \in A ( x \preceq y \wedge y \preceq z \to x \preceq z )$.
