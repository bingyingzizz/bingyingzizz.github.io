# Equivalence Relations in Condensed Sets Are Effective　`prop.cond-equivalence-effective`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=prop.cond-equivalence-effective)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./prop.cond-equivalence-effective.md)

$$R \cong X \times_{Y} X,\qquad Y := \operatorname{coeq}\bigl(R \rightrightarrows X\bigr).$$

That is: the quotient by the equivalence relation exists, and $R$ is exactly the kernel pair of that quotient.
