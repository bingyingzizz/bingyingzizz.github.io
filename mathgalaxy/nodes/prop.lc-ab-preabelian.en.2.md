# pre-abelian　`prop.lc-ab-preabelian`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=prop.lc-ab-preabelian)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./prop.lc-ab-preabelian.md)

Explicitly, the kernel is the usual kernel with the subspace topology. The cokernel is the quotient group $N/\overline{f(M)}$ with the quotient topology (one must quotient by the **closure** to obtain a Hausdorff quotient). Morphisms **need not be strict**. For example,

$$\mathrm{id} : \mathbb{R}^{\mathrm{disc}} \longrightarrow \mathbb{R}$$

is not strict, with the discrete topology on one copy of the group and the usual topology on the other.
