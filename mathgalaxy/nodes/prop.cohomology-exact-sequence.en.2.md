## Notes · **draft**
That is, $0 \to Z^{k}/B^{k} \to K^{k}/B^{k} \to Z^{k+1} \to Z^{k+1}/B^{k+1} \to 0$. This splits "cohomology" into two steps: **first take the cokernel to remove the image of the previous differential, then take the kernel to pick out cocycles, and finally take the quotient** — thus the information in the complex is peeled off layer by layer.

Each of the three exactness assertions uses only "$d^{2} = 0$" and the universal properties of kernel/cokernel.
