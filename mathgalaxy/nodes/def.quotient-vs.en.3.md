# quotient vector space　`def.quotient-vs`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=def.quotient-vs)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./def.quotient-vs.md)

⭐ "Every subspace is the kernel of some linear map" follows: take $\pi$.

This is completely parallel to the group case (every normal subgroup is the kernel of some homomorphism).

⚠ What is quotiented is a **subspace** (not an arbitrary subset): otherwise the sum of cosets is not closed.

On the group side the counterpart is "normal subgroup"; here every subspace is automatically normal (because $K$-vector spaces are abelian groups).
