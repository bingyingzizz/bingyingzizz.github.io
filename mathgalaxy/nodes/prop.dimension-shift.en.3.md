# Dimension Shift　`prop.dimension-shift`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=prop.dimension-shift)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./prop.dimension-shift.md)

Take the long exact cohomology sequence

$$\cdots \to \operatorname{Ext}^{n}(M, N) \to \operatorname{Ext}^{n}(P, N) \to \operatorname{Ext}^{n}(K, N) \xrightarrow{\ \delta\ } \operatorname{Ext}^{n+1}(M, N) \to \operatorname{Ext}^{n+1}(P, N) \to \cdots$$

Since $P$ is projective, $\operatorname{Ext}^{k}(P, N) = 0$ for $k \ge 1$, so every segment degenerates into an isomorphism: $\delta$ is bijective, i.e. $\operatorname{Ext}^{n+1}(M, N) \cong \operatorname{Ext}^{n}(K, N)$ ✓.
