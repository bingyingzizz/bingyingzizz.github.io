## Statement · **draft**
The Klein bottle $K$ is $\mathbb{RP}^{2} \# \mathbb{RP}^{2}$ (the connected sum of two projective planes). It is also the quotient of a square obtained by gluing one pair of opposite sides in the same direction and the other pair in opposite directions. It is a **closed nonorientable surface** with nonorientable genus $k = 2$ and $\chi(K) = 0$.
