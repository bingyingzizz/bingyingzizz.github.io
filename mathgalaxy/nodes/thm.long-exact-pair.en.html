Why this is simpler in homology than in homotopy. The long exact sequence of relative homotopy groups holds for every pair (X,A)(X, A)(X,A) and requires no fibration. The delicate step is replacing the relative groups by the absolute groups of the quotient space X/AX/AX/A; it requires a good pair hypothesis (NDR pair or cofibration) on (X,A)(X, A)(X,A). The homology sequence applies whenever A⊆XA \subseteq XA⊆X is a subspace. This is one of the main reasons chain complexes are easier to work with than homotopy groups.

💡 Application. Break the homology of a large space into a smaller space +++ a relative part, and use the known groups to compute the unknown ones (Mayer–Vietoris sequence in homology is obtained this way).
