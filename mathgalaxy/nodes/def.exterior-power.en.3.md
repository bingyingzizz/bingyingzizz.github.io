# exterior power　`def.exterior-power`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=def.exterior-power)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./def.exterior-power.md)

⚠ Over a field of characteristic $2$, $v \wedge w = -w \wedge v$ and $v \wedge w = w \wedge v$ say the same thing ($-1 = 1$), and alternating and symmetric are no longer distinguishable.

The convention here is $\operatorname{char} K \ne 2$.
