Statement

Let fff be bounded on [a,b][a, b][a,b].

  • fff continuous   ⟹  \implies⟹ fff integrable;
  • fff monotone   ⟹  \implies⟹ fff integrable;
  • fff bounded with only finitely many discontinuities   ⟹  \implies⟹ fff integrable.

The converses fail. The Dirichlet function (xxx rational gives 111, irrational gives 000) is bounded but not integrable.

⭐ All three are direct applications of “the oscillation sum can be made arbitrarily small”.
