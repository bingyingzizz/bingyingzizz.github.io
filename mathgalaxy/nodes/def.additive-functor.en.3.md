# additive functor　`def.additive-functor`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=def.additive-functor)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./def.additive-functor.md)

⭐ **Why homological algebra speaks only of additive functors**: derived functors, $\operatorname{Ext}$, $H^{n}$ are all additive. Every operation on complexes ($d^{2}=0$, taking homology, mapping cones) is **addition and subtraction** at each step; a non-additive functor cannot even send complexes to complexes.

**Examples**: $\operatorname{Hom}(X, -)$ is additive ($\operatorname{Hom}$ is itself an abelian group); $M \otimes_{A} -$ is additive; the forgetful functor $A\text{-}\mathbf{Mod} \to \mathbf{Ab}$ is additive.
