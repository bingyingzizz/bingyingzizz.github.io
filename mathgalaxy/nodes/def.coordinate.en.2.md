# coordinates　`def.coordinate`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=def.coordinate)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./def.coordinate.md)

called the **coordinate vector** of $v$ in the basis $\mathcal{B}$. Thus

$$v \mapsto [v]_{\mathcal{B}}$$

is a bijection $V \to K^{n}$, and it is linear (it preserves addition and scalar multiplication).

## Notes · **draft**
⭐ This entry translates "an abstract vector space" into "column vectors in $K^{n}$" — **this is the entire justification of matrix theory**: from now on, matrices are just what this looks like with respect to two different bases.

⚠ "Uniqueness" comes from the definition of a basis (both linearly independent and spanning);
