## Statement · **draft**
Let $\sum a_n$ be **absolutely convergent** and let $\sigma : \mathbb{N} \to \mathbb{N}$ be any bijection. Then $\sum a_{\sigma(n)}$ is also absolutely convergent, and

$$\sum_{n=1}^{\infty} a_{\sigma(n)} = \sum_{n=1}^{\infty} a_n$$
