## Notes · **draft**
**Comparison with the cylinder**: The cylinder glues the two sides in the **same direction**, $(0, t) \sim (1, t)$. The Möbius band reverses one end before gluing ($t \mapsto 1 - t$). This single change gives very different results.

⭐ **Nonorientability**: After one circuit along the centerline $t = 1/2$, the normal vector reverses direction, so the Möbius band **admits no orientation**. It is also a standard building block for nonorientable surfaces: $M \cup_{\partial} M \cong$ the Klein bottle (see `ex.klein-bottle`).
