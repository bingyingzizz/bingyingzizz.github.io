# law of quadratic reciprocity　`thm.quadratic-reciprocity`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=thm.quadratic-reciprocity)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./thm.quadratic-reciprocity.md)

**Application.** To evaluate $\left(\frac{a}{p}\right)$, factor it into primes, use reciprocity to swap the arguments for each prime factor, and recurse. This takes on the order of $O(\log p)$ divisions. The two supplementary formulas above handle $-1$ and $2$, the two cases where the arguments cannot be swapped.
