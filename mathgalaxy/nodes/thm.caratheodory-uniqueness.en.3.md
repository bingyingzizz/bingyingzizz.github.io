## Notes · **draft**
Key point: **the Carathéodory extension is the "largest" one**.

The reason is hidden in the definition of $\mu^{*}$ — it is built by "taking infima over coverings", and any extension $\nu$ must be subadditive, so $\nu (A) \le \sum \nu (E_{j}) = \sum \mu _{0}(E_{j})$; taking the infimum over all coverings gives $\nu \le \mu^{*}$.
