# Biproducts Are Products and Coproducts　`prop.biproduct-is-product`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=prop.biproduct-is-product)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./prop.biproduct-is-product.md)

**Why an additive category must have biproducts**: precisely because the two coincide, "addition" ($f + g$) and "the inclusions of the coproduct" can be written in one notation — this is the foundation of all notation in homological algebra.
