# Splitting Principle　`thm.splitting-principle`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=thm.splitting-principle)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./thm.splitting-principle.md)

Let $E \to B$ be a complex vector bundle of rank $k$. Then there are a smooth manifold $\pi : \tilde B \to B$ and a map $\pi^{*}E \cong L_{1} \oplus \cdots \oplus L_{k}$ (with $L_{j}$ line bundles), and $\pi^{*} : H^{*}(B) \to H^{*}(\tilde B)$ is **injective** (with coefficients in $\mathbb{Q}$; more concretely one takes $\tilde B$ to be the flag bundle over $B$).

Thus one may formally write

$$c(E) = \prod_{j=1}^{k}\bigl(1 + x_{j}\bigr), \qquad x_{j} := c_{1}(L_{j}) ,$$
