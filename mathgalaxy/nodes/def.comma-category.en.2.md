# comma category　`def.comma-category`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=def.comma-category)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./def.comma-category.md)

$$\begin{array}{ccc} F(A) & \xrightarrow{\;f\;} & G(B) \\[2pt] {\scriptstyle F(a)}\big\downarrow & & \big\downarrow{\scriptstyle G(b)} \\[2pt] F(A') & \xrightarrow[\;f'\;]{} & G(B') \end{array}$$

**Two special cases**: taking $\mathcal{A} = \mathbf{1}$ (the one-object category), $F$ is just "selecting an object of $\mathcal{C}$, say $X$", and $F \downarrow G$ is written $X \downarrow G$; taking $\mathcal{B} = \mathbf{1}$ gives $F \downarrow Y$.
