## Statement · **draft**
Let $M$ be a **closed** (compact and without boundary) **orientable** surface. Its **genus** is defined to be

$$g(M) := \tfrac{1}{2} \operatorname{rank}_{\mathbb{Z}} H_{1}(M; \mathbb{Z}).$$

that is, half the free rank of its first integral homology group.

## Notes · **draft**
⭐ **Why divide by 2**: For a closed orientable surface, $H_{1}$ is a free abelian group, and

$$H_{1}(M; \mathbb{Z}) \cong \mathbb{Z}^{2g},$$
