Statement

Let C\mathcal{C}C be a site and u:T→T′u : T \to T'u:T→T′ a morphism of presheaves. Then

im⁡(T#→T′#)  =  (im⁡(T→T′))#.\operatorname{im}\bigl(T^{\#} \to T'^{\#}\bigr) \;=\; \Bigl(\operatorname{im}\bigl(T \to T'\bigr)\Bigr)^{\#}.im(T#→T′#)=(im(T→T′))#.

Notes

Proof. Sheafification is exact (it preserves both finite limits and colimits) ✓, so it sends the image in presheaves (of the form ker⁡(coker⁡)\ker(\operatorname{coker})ker(coker)) termwise; the image in the category of sheaves is likewise given by im⁡=ker⁡(coker⁡)\operatorname{im} = \ker(\operatorname{coker})im=ker(coker) ✓, and the two routes arrive at the same place ✓. ∎
