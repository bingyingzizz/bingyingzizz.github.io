## Statement · **draft**
Let $M$ be an abelian sheaf on a site, $n \ne 0$, and write $H^{n}(M)$ for the presheaf $U \mapsto H^{n}(U, M)$. Then

$$\check{H}^{p}\bigl(H^{n}(M)\bigr) = 0 \qquad (\forall p \ge 0).$$
