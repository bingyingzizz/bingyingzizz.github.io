⚠ All of this **depends on the choice of bases**: change the basis and the matrix changes. **The same space** $V = W$ with the same basis used on both sides gives **similarity** $P^{-1}AP$; for a general $T : V \to W$ the two bases change independently and one gets **equivalence** $Q^{-1}AP$ (e.g. $V = W = \mathbb{R}$, $T = \mathrm{id}$: changing only the basis of the domain to $(2)$ gives $[2]$, which is not similar to $[1]$).

The linear map itself, however, does not change.
