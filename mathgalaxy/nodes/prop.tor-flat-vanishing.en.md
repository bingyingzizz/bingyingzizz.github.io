## Statement · **draft**
Let $R$ be a ring and $F$ a right $R$-module. The following conditions are equivalent:

1. $F$ is **flat**;
2. For every left $R$-module $N$, $\operatorname{Tor}_{1}^{R}(F, N) = 0$;
3. For every left $R$-module $N$ and every $n \ge 1$, $\operatorname{Tor}_{n}^{R}(F, N) = 0$.
