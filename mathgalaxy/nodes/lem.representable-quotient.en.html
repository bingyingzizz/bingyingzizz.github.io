Statement

Let T\mathcal{T}T be a topos (with the standard topology). If in an epimorphism between sheaves

∐i∈IFi⟶F\coprod_{i \in I} F_{i} \longrightarrow Fi∈I∐​Fi​⟶F

every FiF_{i}Fi​ and every Fi×FFjF_{i} \times_{F} F_{j}Fi​×F​Fj​ is representable, then FFF is also representable.

Notes

Writing Fi≅XiF_{i} \cong X_{i}Fi​≅Xi​, Fi×FFj≅Xi,jF_{i} \times_{F} F_{j} \cong X_{i,j}Fi​×F​Fj​≅Xi,j​, the object R:=∐i,jXi,jR := \coprod_{i,j} X_{i,j}R:=∐i,j​Xi,j​ defines an equivalence relation on X:=∐iXiX := \coprod_{i} X_{i}X:=∐i​Xi​, so F≅X/RF \cong X/RF≅X/R — quotients exist in a topos (equivalence relations are effective), hence FFF is isomorphic to a representable sheaf.
