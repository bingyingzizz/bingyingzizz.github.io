## Statement · **draft**
Let $K$ be a **finite** simplicial complex, $\alpha_{p}$ the number of its $p$-simplices, and $\beta_{p} := \operatorname{rank} H_{p}(K)$ the $p$-th **Betti number** (the rank of the free part). Then

$$\chi(K) = \sum_{p} (-1)^{p} \alpha_{p} = \sum_{p} (-1)^{p} \beta_{p}.$$

That is: **the left side merely counts simplices, while the right side depends only on the homology groups**.
