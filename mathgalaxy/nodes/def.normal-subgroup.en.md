## Statement · **draft**
Let $H \le G$ and $g \in G$. The **left coset** and **right coset** are

$$gH := \{\, gh : h \in H \,\}, \qquad Hg := \{\, hg : h \in H \,\}.$$

We call $H$ a **normal subgroup** of $G$ (written $H \trianglelefteq G$) if $gH = Hg$ for all $g \in G$, equivalently $gHg^{-1} = H$.
