## Notes · **draft**
The proof uses that $\ker h$ and $\operatorname{im}h$ are subrepresentations; irreducibility forces $\ker h=0$ and $\operatorname{im}h=V_2$.
This is the main tool for decomposing semisimple representations.
The scalar statement over an algebraically closed field is a separate corollary, cor.schur-scalar.
