**Area reading**: the Gauss map multiplies the area element by $\det(\mathrm{d}n) = \det W = K$ (with sign), so $K = \pm|n_{u}\times n_{v}|/|r_{u}\times r_{v}|$. This is the same quantity as $\det h$.
