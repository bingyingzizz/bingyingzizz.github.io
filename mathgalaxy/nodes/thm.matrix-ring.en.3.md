## Notes · **draft**
⭐ Every sentence of this theorem is the result of reading $M_n(K)$ as $\operatorname{End}(K^{n})$: addition of matrices is addition of maps, multiplication is composition, and the unit is the identity map (all proved in the entry "linear map $\leftrightarrow$ matrix").

⭐ Thus $M_n(K)$ becomes a **ring**, and one can ask about its ideals, its invertible elements ($\operatorname{GL}_n(K)$), one-sided inverses, and so on — the tools of abstract algebra transfer directly.
