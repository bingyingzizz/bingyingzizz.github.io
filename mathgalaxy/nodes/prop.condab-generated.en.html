Statement

CondAb\mathrm{CondAb}CondAb is generated by finitely presented projective condensed abelian groups. In particular, it has enough projectives.

Notes

Proof.

  • Generation: Cond\mathrm{Cond}Cond is generated by {hF}\{h_{F}\}{hF​} (with FFF free compact Hausdorff). In general, if {Xi}\{X_{i}\}{Xi​} generates C\mathcal{C}C, then {Z⋅Xi}\{\mathbb{Z}\cdot X_{i}\}{Z⋅Xi​} generates Ab(C)\mathrm{Ab}(\mathcal{C})Ab(C) (use the universal property of free abelian group objects: two morphisms agreeing on all Z⋅Xi\mathbb{Z}\cdot X_{i}Z⋅Xi​ are equal).

Thus {Z⋅F}\{\mathbb{Z}\cdot F\}{Z⋅F} generates CondAb\mathrm{CondAb}CondAb ✓;

  • Each Z⋅F\mathbb{Z}\cdot FZ⋅F is finitely presented and projective by the preceding result ✓;
  • Enough projectives: Given MMM, generation gives an epimorphism ⨁iZ⋅Fi↠M\bigoplus_{i} \mathbb{Z}\cdot F_{i} \twoheadrightarrow M⨁i​Z⋅Fi​↠M. A coproduct of projective objects is projective ✓, so every MMM is a quotient of a projective object. ∎

⭐ This is one of the two facts that allow derived functors to be computed in CondAb\mathrm{CondAb}CondAb (the other is that it has enough injectives, since it is a Grothendieck category). Having enough projectives allows left derived functors to be computed directly.
