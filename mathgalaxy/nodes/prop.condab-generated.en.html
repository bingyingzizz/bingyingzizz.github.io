Statement

CondAb\mathrm{CondAb}CondAb is generated by finitely presented projective condensed abelian groups. In particular, it has enough projectives.

Notes

⭐ This is the other half of the justification that "one can do derived algebra in CondAb\mathrm{CondAb}CondAb" (the first half being "enough injectives", supplied by the fact that it is a Grothendieck category): enough projective objects means left derived functors can also be computed directly.
