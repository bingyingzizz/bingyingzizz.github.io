# Table of Derived Hom for Z, R and T　`table.rhom-zrt`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=table.rhom-zrt)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./table.rhom-zrt.md)

$$\begin{array}{c|ccc} & \mathbb{Z} & \mathbb{R} & T \\ \hline \mathbb{Z} & \mathbb{Z} & \mathbb{R} & T \\ \mathbb{R} & 0 & \mathbb{R} & \mathbb{R} \\ T & \mathbb{Z}[-1] & 0 & \mathbb{Z} \end{array}$$
**Computing the entries: Two facts suffice for the calculation**

1. **$\mathbb{Z}$ is free (projective)**: A resolution of $\mathbb{Z}$ can be taken to be the one-dimensional $[\mathbb{Z}]$. Thus $\operatorname{RHom}_{\mathbb{Z}}(\mathbb{Z}, N) = \operatorname{Hom}_{\mathbb{Z}}(\mathbb{Z}, N) = N$, with **no higher terms**.

This gives the first row directly.
