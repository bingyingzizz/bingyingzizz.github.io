# Maschke theorem　`thm.maschke`

## Notes · **draft**
Given a subrepresentation $W\subseteq V$, take any linear projection $p:V\to W$ and average over $G$: $\bar p(v)=\frac1{|G|}\sum_g g\,p(g^{-1}v)$. The condition $\operatorname{char}F\nmid|G|$ makes $\frac1{|G|}$ meaningful; $\bar p$ is a $G$-equivariant projection, so $V=W\oplus\ker\bar p$. Splitting every subrepresentation this way gives complete reducibility; the group-algebra version follows from the equivalence between representations and $F[G]$-modules.
