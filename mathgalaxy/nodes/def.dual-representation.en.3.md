## Notes · **draft**
The transpose convention is forced by contravariance: $\theta^*$ is an anti-homomorphism. In the group case the inverse restores a genuine action. In a basis, the matrix of $\rho^*(g)$ is the inverse transpose of that of $\rho(g)$. In finite dimension the double dual is naturally isomorphic to $V$.
