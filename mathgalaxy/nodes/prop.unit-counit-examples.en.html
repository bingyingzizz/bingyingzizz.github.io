Statement

The units of a few common adjunctions: the unit of free ⊣\dashv⊣ forgetful is “sending xxx into the generators”; the unit of sheafification ⊣\dashv⊣ inclusion is T→T#T \to T^{\#}T→T#; the unit of kkk-ification ⊣\dashv⊣ inclusion is the identity (X→kXX \to kXX→kX when XXX is already compactly generated).

From “counit epimorphic   ⟺  \iff⟺ faithful” one can also read faithfulness off the unit and counit.
