## Notes · **draft**
**Why it deserves its own entry**: it translates a **metric condition** ($K$ constant) into three concrete metric forms using only the first fundamental form — how the surface sits in $\mathbb{R}^{3}$ plays no role. This is the first big victory of intrinsic geometry after Gauss's theorema egregium.
