## Statement · **draft**
Let $A, B \in M_{m \times n}(K)$. If there exist invertible matrices $P \in GL_m(K)$ and $Q \in GL_n(K)$ such that

$$B = P A Q,$$

then $A$ and $B$ are called **equivalent**. This is the same as saying that $A$ can be transformed into $B$ by finitely many elementary row and column operations.
