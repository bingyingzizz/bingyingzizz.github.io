# Mayer–Vietoris sequence in homology　`thm.mv-homology`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=thm.mv-homology)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./thm.mv-homology.md)

**Step 2. Naturality.** For a singular simplex $\sigma$, set $\operatorname{Sd}(\sigma) := \sigma_{\#}\operatorname{sd}_{p}$ and $T(\sigma) := \sigma_{\#} t_{p}$, and extend linearly. Since $\sigma_{\#}$ is a chain map and is compatible with continuous maps,

$$\partial\operatorname{Sd} = \operatorname{Sd}\partial,\qquad \partial T + T\partial = \operatorname{Sd} - \operatorname{id}.$$

(This follows by applying the preceding identity term by term. $T$ may produce degenerate simplices, which do not affect the chain identity.) A single $\operatorname{Sd}(\sigma)$ may fail to be small. Iteration ensures smallness.
