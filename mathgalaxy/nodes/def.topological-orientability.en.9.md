**4. The surface case (the main application here).** For a closed surface $S$, $S$ is orientable $\iff$ $S$ contains no Möbius band as a subspace $\iff$ $S$ admits a triangulation in which every $2$-simplex can be **coherently oriented** (the two sides of each interior edge induce opposite orientations on it, as used in Lemma D of `prop.eulerchar-genus`) $\iff$ $H_{2}(S;\mathbb{Z})\cong\mathbb{Z}$.
