# pretopos　`def.pretopos`

[in graph](../#n=def.pretopos)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./def.pretopos.md)

- **① Finite limits exist**: a **purely structural requirement**, guaranteeing only that one can speak of fibred products and equalizers (hence of "relations"). It involves neither colimits nor epi/mono.
- **② Finite coproducts are disjoint and universal**: **this is a real condition and cannot be dropped**. "Disjoint" = the pullback $A \times_{A\sqcup B} B \simeq \emptyset$ is empty (one requires an initial object and that it is "really empty"); "universal" = coproducts are stable under pullback. **Merely requiring "finite coproducts exist" is far from enough** — coproducts in $\mathbf{Set}$ are disjoint and universal, but coproducts in $\mathbf{Grp}$ (free products) are
