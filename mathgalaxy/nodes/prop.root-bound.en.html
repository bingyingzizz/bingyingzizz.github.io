Notes

⭐ The proof is repeated use of the factor theorem: each root lets one divide by (x−a)(x - a)(x−a), lowering the degree by 1; the degree cannot go negative, so there are at most nnn roots.

⭐ The usefulness of this "small" proposition is surprisingly large: testing polynomial identities over finite fields (two polynomials of degree <n< n<n that agree at nnn points are identical) — interpolation and error-correcting codes (Reed–Solomon) rely on it.
