Statement

Let F:C→DF : \mathcal{C} \to \mathcal{D}F:C→D, G:D→CG : \mathcal{D} \to \mathcal{C}G:D→C be a pair of functors. (F,G)(F, G)(F,G) is called a pair of adjoint functors if there is a natural isomorphism

Hom⁡D(F(X), Y)  ≅  Hom⁡C(X, G(Y))(X∈C, Y∈D)\operatorname{Hom}_{\mathcal{D}}\bigl(F(X),\ Y\bigr) \;\cong\; \operatorname{Hom}_{\mathcal{C}}\bigl(X,\ G(Y)\bigr) \qquad (X \in \mathcal{C},\ Y \in \mathcal{D})HomD​(F(X), Y)≅HomC​(X, G(Y))(X∈C, Y∈D)

In that case write F⊣GF \dashv GF⊣G: FFF is called the left adjoint of GGG, and GGG the right adjoint of FFF.

Notes

In one sentence: translations between the two directions are mutually optimal. Going around the left (apply FFF, then map out) and going around the right (map out, then apply GGG) give the same amount — and naturally so.

Examples:

  • the forgetful functor Top→Set\mathbf{Top} \to \mathbf{Set}Top→Set has two adjoints: the left adjoint is the discrete topology (Hom⁡Top(Sd,X)≅Hom⁡Set(S,F(X))\operatorname{Hom}_{\mathbf{Top}}(S_{d}, X) \cong \operatorname{Hom}_{\mathbf{Set}}(S, F(X))HomTop​(Sd​,X)≅HomSet​(S,F(X))), the right adjoint is the trivial topology (Hom⁡Top(X,St)≅Hom⁡Set(F(X),S)\operatorname{Hom}_{\mathbf{Top}}(X, S_{t}) \cong \operatorname{Hom}_{\mathbf{Set}}(F(X), S)HomTop​(X,St​)≅HomSet​(F(X),S)).
  • the left adjoint of the forgetful functor Ab→Set\mathbf{Ab} \to \mathbf{Set}Ab→Set is the free abelian group: Hom⁡Ab(Z(X),M)≅Hom⁡Set(X,forget⁡M)\operatorname{Hom}_{\mathbf{Ab}}(\mathbb{Z}^{(X)}, M) \cong \operatorname{Hom}_{\mathbf{Set}}(X, \operatorname{forget} M)HomAb​(Z(X),M)≅HomSet​(X,forgetM).

Free is left adjoint to forgetful.

Two rules of computation:

  • duality: F⊣GF \dashv GF⊣G   ⟺  \iff⟺ Gop⊣FopG^{\mathrm{op}} \dashv F^{\mathrm{op}}Gop⊣Fop (on Cop→Dop\mathcal{C}^{\mathrm{op}} \to \mathcal{D}^{\mathrm{op}}Cop→Dop).
  • composition: F⊣GF \dashv GF⊣G and F′⊣G′F' \dashv G'F′⊣G′   ⟹  \implies⟹ F′∘F⊣G∘G′F' \circ F \dashv G \circ G'F′∘F⊣G∘G′, because
Hom⁡(F′F(X),Y)≅Hom⁡(F(X),G′(Y))≅Hom⁡(X,GG′(Y))\operatorname{Hom}(F'F(X), Y) \cong \operatorname{Hom}(F(X), G'(Y)) \cong \operatorname{Hom}(X, GG'(Y))Hom(F′F(X),Y)≅Hom(F(X),G′(Y))≅Hom(X,GG′(Y))
