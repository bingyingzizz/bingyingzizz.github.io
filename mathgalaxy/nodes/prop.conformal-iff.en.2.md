# Nonvanishing Derivative and Conformality　`prop.conformal-iff`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=prop.conformal-iff)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./prop.conformal-iff.md)

**(i)** Let $f$ be holomorphic near $z_0$ and $f'(z_0) \ne 0$. Then $f$ is **conformal** at $z_0$: for any two smooth curves through $z_0$ with **nonzero velocity at $z_0$**, the angle between their images at $f(z_0)$ has the **same size and the same orientation**. (⚠ "nonzero velocity" cannot be dropped: $\gamma_{1}(t) = t^{2}$ and $\gamma_{2}(t) = i t^{2}$ have no tangent at $t = 0$, so there is no angle to speak of.)
