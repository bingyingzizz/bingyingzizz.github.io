# Continuous Functions Form a Normed Banach Group　`prop.continuous-functions-normed`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=prop.continuous-functions-normed)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./prop.continuous-functions-normed.md)

⭐ Part 2 makes precise the claim that there are **enough discrete-valued functions**. Every continuous $M$-valued function on a Stone space can be uniformly approximated by locally constant functions. This is a key step in the acyclicity proofs with Banach coefficients (8.2.6 and 8.2.8).
