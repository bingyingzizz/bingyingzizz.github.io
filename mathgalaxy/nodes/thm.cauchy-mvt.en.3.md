[in graph](../#n=thm.cauchy-mvt)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./thm.cauchy-mvt.md)

⚠ Parametric-curve reading: $t \mapsto (g(t), f(t))$ is a curve, and the conclusion says that the tangent at some interior point is **parallel to** the chord joining the endpoints — geometrically the same statement as Lagrange's "parallel to the chord", except that the chord need not be the graph of a function.
