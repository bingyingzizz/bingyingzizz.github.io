## Statement · **draft**
Let $E, F$ be Banach spaces and $u \in B(E, F)$ **surjective**. Then $u$ is an open map: the image of every open set is open.

By linearity of $u$, this is equivalent to "the image of some neighbourhood of $0$ is a neighbourhood of $0$", that is, there exists $r > 0$ such that

$$u(B_E) \supseteq r\, B_F ,$$
