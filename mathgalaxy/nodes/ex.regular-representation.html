Statement draft

Let GGG be a finite group. The regular representation is the left multiplication action of GGG on F[G]F[G]F[G], ρreg(g)(h)=gh\rho_{\mathrm{reg}}(g)(h)=ghρreg​(g)(h)=gh, viewed as a permutation representation on FGF^GFG. Its character is χreg(1)=∣G∣\chi_{\mathrm{reg}}(1)=|G|χreg​(1)=∣G∣ and χreg(g)=0\chi_{\mathrm{reg}}(g)=0χreg​(g)=0 for g≠1g\ne1g=1.

Notes draft

The dimension of the regular representation is ∣G∣|G|∣G∣. For the character formula: the diagonal entry of the permutation matrix of ggg in the basis GGG is 111 exactly when gh=hgh=hgh=h, that is, g=1g=1g=1. When char⁡F∤∣G∣\operatorname{char}F\nmid|G|charF∤∣G∣ and FFF is algebraically closed, ρreg≅⨁V(dim⁡V)V\rho_{\mathrm{reg}}\cong\bigoplus_V(\dim V)Vρreg​≅⨁V​(dimV)V with VVV running over the irreducible representations. Comparing dimensions gives ∣G∣=∑V(dim⁡V)2|G|=\sum_V(\dim V)^2∣G∣=∑V​(dimV)2.
