# closure properties of positive sets　`prop.positive-set-closure`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=prop.positive-set-closure)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./prop.positive-set-closure.md)

The second is the technical step used repeatedly in the proof of the Hahn decomposition: make countably many positive sets $P_1, P_2, \ldots $ disjoint ($P_j' = P_j \setminus \bigcup_{i<j} P_i$; the difference of positive sets is again positive), then verify block by block using countable additivity.
