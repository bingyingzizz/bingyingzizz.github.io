**6. Relation to the smooth definition.** Orientability for smooth manifolds is the smooth version, with transition maps of positive Jacobian determinant. Smooth orientability implies topological orientability because the smooth atlas is an oriented atlas. Conversely, for a topologically orientable smooth manifold, a partition of unity glues local $n$-forms into a nowhere-zero $n$-form (see the node on volume forms and orientability in this cluster).
