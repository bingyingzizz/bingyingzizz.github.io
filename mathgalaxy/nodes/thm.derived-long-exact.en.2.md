## Statement · **draft**
Let $\mathcal{A},\mathcal{B}$ be abelian categories, let $\mathcal{A}$ have **enough injectives**, and let $F : \mathcal{A}\to\mathcal{B}$ be additive and left exact (so $RF$ is defined by injective resolutions in $C^{+}(\mathcal{A})$ and $R^{n}F$ exists). Then every short exact sequence $0 \to K^{\bullet} \to L^{\bullet} \to M^{\bullet} \to 0$ (in $C^{+}(\mathcal{A})$) gives a long exact sequence

$$\cdots \to R^{n}FK^{\bullet} \to R^{n}FL^{\bullet} \to R^{n}FM^{\bullet} \xrightarrow{\ \delta\ } R^{n+1}FK^{\bullet} \to \cdots$$
