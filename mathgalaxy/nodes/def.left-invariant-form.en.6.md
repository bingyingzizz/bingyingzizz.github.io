# left-invariant form　`def.left-invariant-form`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=def.left-invariant-form)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./def.left-invariant-form.md)

**Dual to left-invariant vector fields**: `prop.left-invariant-fields` says left-invariant vector fields are isomorphic to $\mathfrak{g}$; here left-invariant $k$-forms are isomorphic to $\bigwedge^{k}\mathfrak{g}^{\ast}$.

For $k = 1$ the two are dual bases — this is the version on a group of the method of moving frames (`def.moving-frame`): the frame itself does not move, only the point does.
