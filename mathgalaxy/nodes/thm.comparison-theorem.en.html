Notes

⭐ Significance. Resolutions can vary, but uniqueness up to chain homotopy suffices. Derived functors, including Ext⁡\operatorname{Ext}Ext and Tor⁡\operatorname{Tor}Tor, depend only on the homotopy class of the resolution, so their definitions are independent of the choice of resolution. The construction of derived functors relies on this theorem to be well defined.

⚠️ The theorem gives uniqueness up to homotopy. Two projective resolutions of the same module can differ by a contractible complex, as when one takes a resolution and adds a contractible summand of the form 0→P→1P→00 \to P \xrightarrow{1} P \to 00→P1​P→0. The two resolutions represent isomorphic objects in the derived category.

Dual version. Lifts between injective resolutions satisfy the same two conclusions, with the direction of lifting reversed by the extension property of injective objects. See “Lifts Between Injective Resolutions.”

⚠️ The argument uses termwise projectivity and exactness of the two resolutions. It requires no choice principle beyond the axiom of choice and applies to noncommutative rings as well.
