# Cauchy–Goursat Theorem　`thm.cauchy-goursat`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=thm.cauchy-goursat)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./thm.cauchy-goursat.md)

The multiply connected case: let $\gamma_0, \gamma_1, \cdots, \gamma_n$ be piecewise smooth simple closed curves in $\Omega$, with $\gamma_1, \cdots, \gamma_n$ lying inside $\gamma_0$ and pairwise disjoint, **all oriented counterclockwise**, and $f$ holomorphic on some open set containing the **closed region** they bound (holomorphy "on the region" alone is not enough: the curves themselves must lie in the domain). Then

$$\oint_{\gamma_0} f\,\mathrm{d}z = \sum_{k=1}^{n} \oint_{\gamma_k} f\,\mathrm{d}z.$$
