# category of G-sets　`def.category-of-g-sets`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=def.category-of-g-sets)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./def.category-of-g-sets.md)

The initial object is the empty set; coproducts are disjoint unions; coequalizers are quotient $G$-sets (the closure of the generated equivalence relation); pushouts are gluings.

## Notes · **draft**
**Complete and cocomplete**, and **cartesian closed**: $Y^X$ carries the conjugation action $(g\cdot f)(x)=g f(g^{-1}x)$, and evaluation and currying are checked pointwise.

**It is a topos**: it is equivalent to the category of presheaves on the one-object groupoid, see @@def.topos@@.

**Not additive**; the free $G$-set is $G\times X$ (with the left-multiplication action).
