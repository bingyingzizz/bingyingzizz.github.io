## Notes · **draft**
Why these two conditions: on $\mathbb{R}$, the next theorem establishes a one-to-one correspondence with **complex Borel measures $\leftrightarrow NBV$**, and the correspondence is

$$F(x) = \mu((-\infty, x])$$

(the function on the right is automatically right continuous and vanishes at $-\infty$). Restricting the left side to NBV makes the correspondence a **bijection** rather than a surjection.
