# Exactness Is Pointwise Exactness　`prop.exact-at-points`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=prop.exact-at-points)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./prop.exact-at-points.md)

ernel criteria of an abelian category.
- Exactness in the middle $\iff\operatorname{im}i=\ker p$ is by definition; "pointwise" means the positions of the objects in the sequence, and an abstract abelian category cannot use elements or set-theoretic surjections without interpretation.
- Short exact does not imply split: $0\to\mathbb Z\xrightarrow{2}\mathbb Z\to\mathbb Z/2\to0$ is a counterexample; checking only the middle is also not enough, since $0\to\mathbb Z\to0\to0\to0$ is exact in the middle but not at the left end.
