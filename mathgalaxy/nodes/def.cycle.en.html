Statement

What is being made precise: the numerator in the homology formula Hn=Zn/BnH^{n} = Z^{n}/B^{n}Hn=Zn/Bn. A cycle is an element killed by the differential — all of homological algebra asks "of what is killed, how much really came from somewhere, and how much went the long way round".

Let KKK be a complex in an additive category C\mathcal{C}C, with differential dn:Kn→Kn+1d^{n} : K^{n} \to K^{n+1}dn:Kn→Kn+1 (@@def.cochain-complex@@).

Cochain case: the nnn-th group of cocycles is

Zn(K):=ker⁡dn={ x∈Kn:dnx=0 };Z^{n}(K) := \ker d^{n} = \{\, x \in K^{n} : d^{n}x = 0 \,\} ;Zn(K):=kerdn={x∈Kn:dnx=0};

its elements are called cocycles.

Chain case (arrows reversed, dn:Kn→Kn−1d_{n} : K_{n} \to K_{n-1}dn​:Kn​→Kn−1​): the nnn-th group of cycles is

Zn(K):=ker⁡dn,Z_{n}(K) := \ker d_{n} ,Zn​(K):=kerdn​,

its elements are called cycles.

⚠️ The two names refer to the same thing (the kernel of ddd), only the grading direction of the complex differs: the kernel of a cochain complex is written ZnZ^{n}Zn, that of a chain complex ZnZ_{n}Zn​.

Notes

⭐ The word "closed" comes from topology: in a simplicial complex a chain with ∂z=0\partial z = 0∂z=0 is a "chain without boundary" — intuitively a bunch of simplices closed up (a circle, a sphere). In the de Rham complex a form with dω=0d\omega = 0dω=0 is called closed, with the very same definition. So "cycle / closed form / cocycle" are three ways of saying one thing.

⚠️ Origin of the letter ZZZ: German Zyklus (cycle). BBB comes from boundary (edge) — see @@def.boundary@@. Older literature also writes CnC^{n}Cn (cocycle) and BnB^{n}Bn; check the notation when reading.

⚠️ ZnZ^{n}Zn is a subobject of KnK^{n}Kn (a kernel), but it is not a subcomplex: restricting ddd to ZnZ^{n}Zn gives the zero map, so ZZZ carries a complex structure that is "zero everywhere". Be careful whether you mean the subobject or the subcomplex.

⭐ Homology measures "how much of the cycles is not a boundary": Hn=Zn/BnH^{n} = Z^{n}/B^{n}Hn=Zn/Bn (@@def.cohomology@@). Hence Hn=0  ⟺  H^{n} = 0 \iffHn=0⟺ every cycle is a boundary   ⟺  \iff⟺ the complex is exact in degree nnn. This is the only bridge translating "homology vanishes" into "closed = boundary".
