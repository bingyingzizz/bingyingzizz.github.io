## Notes · **draft**
⭐ This is the rule table for all **inequality estimates**.

Part (iii) is the easiest to get wrong: multiplying by a negative number **reverses** the inequality, so when making an $\varepsilon$ estimate always first check whether the quantity being multiplied is positive.

⭐ Part (iv) (reciprocal reverses order) is used daily in places like "$|g(x)| > |B|/2 \implies 1/|g(x)| < 2/|B|$" — the reciprocal rule for limits relies on it to amplify $1/|g|$.
