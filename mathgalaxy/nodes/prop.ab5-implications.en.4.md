# Implications among the AB Axioms　`prop.ab5-implications`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=prop.ab5-implications)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./prop.ab5-implications.md)

**Item 3 is the answer to "why condensed mathematics uses CondAb rather than an arbitrary topos"**: $\mathbf{Ab}(\mathcal{T})$ has only AB5 and AB3*, which suffices for general homological algebra;

but $\mathrm{CondAb}$ additionally satisfies **AB6 and AB4\*** — that is, "filtered colimits commute with products" and "products are exact".

Only with these two can one freely interchange limits and colimits as in $\mathbf{Ab}$.

**The reason for each of the three.**
- **Item 1**: use **Freyd's special adjoint functor theorem**.
