# Rational Canonical Form　`thm.rational-canonical-form`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=thm.rational-canonical-form)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./thm.rational-canonical-form.md)

$$\operatorname{diag}\bigl(C_{d_{1}}, C_{d_{2}}, \ldots, C_{d_{s}}\bigr) ,$$

where each $C_{d}$ is a **companion matrix**: $d(x) = x^{m} + a_{m-1}x^{m-1} + \cdots + a_{0}$ is monic,

$$C_{d} := \begin{pmatrix} 0 & & & & -a_{0} \\ 1 & 0 & & & -a_{1} \\ & \ddots & \ddots & & \vdots \\ & & 1 & 0 & -a_{m-2} \\ & & & 1 & -a_{m-1} \end{pmatrix} ,$$
