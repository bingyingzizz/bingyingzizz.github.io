# Injective Objects Are Exactly the Acyclic Ones　`prop.injective-iff-acyclic`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=prop.injective-iff-acyclic)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./prop.injective-iff-acyclic.md)

And by the **injective criterion**, $\operatorname{Ext}^{1}_{\mathcal{A}}(-, I) = 0$ is exactly the equivalent characterization of "$I$ injective" (in an abelian category it is equivalent to "every monomorphism $I \rightarrowtail N$ has a retraction") ✓.

Hence $I$ is injective ✓.

∎

⭐ Use: to verify "injective" one may start from the side of **any** left exact functor — and in the condensed / sheaf setting the most convenient left exact functor is taking sections $\Gamma(S, -)$.
