Statement draft

Let a group GGG act on a set XXX and let x∈Xx \in Xx∈X. The set

Gx:=Stab⁡(x):={ g∈G:g⋅x=x }≤GG_x := \operatorname{Stab}(x) := \{\, g \in G : g \cdot x = x \,\} \le GGx​:=Stab(x):={g∈G:g⋅x=x}≤G

is the stabilizer of xxx (or isotropy subgroup).

GxG_xGx​ is a subgroup of GGG; the kernel of the action is ⋂x∈XGx\bigcap_{x \in X} G_x⋂x∈X​Gx​. Orbit–stabilizer gives a bijection G/Gx→GxG/G_x \to GxG/Gx​→Gx, gGx↦g⋅xgG_x \mapsto g \cdot xgGx​↦g⋅x. Conjugate points have conjugate stabilizers: Gg⋅x=gGxg−1G_{g \cdot x} = g G_x g^{-1}Gg⋅x​=gGx​g−1.

Notes draft

⭐ When GGG is finite, the larger the stabilizer the smaller the orbit; the two are balanced exactly by ∣G∣=∣Gx∣⋅∣Gx∣|G| = |Gx| \cdot |G_x|∣G∣=∣Gx∣⋅∣Gx​∣.

⭐ The action is free iff all stabilizers are trivial (e.g. left multiplication); it is faithful iff the kernel is trivial.

⭐ Examples: in the natural action of SnS_nSn​, Gn≅Sn−1G_n \cong S_{n-1}Gn​≅Sn−1​; for the conjugation action, Gx=CG(x)G_x = C_G(x)Gx​=CG​(x) is the centralizer, see 中心化子.
