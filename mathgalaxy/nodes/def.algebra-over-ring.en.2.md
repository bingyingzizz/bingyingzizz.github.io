## Statement · **draft**
Let $R$ be a commutative ring with identity. An $R$-algebra is an $R$-module $A$ together with an $R$-bilinear multiplication $A\times A\to A$, $(x,y)\mapsto xy$. If the multiplication is associative, $A$ is called an associative algebra; if there is $1_A\in A$ with $1_Ax=x1_A=x$, then $A$ is called unital. Unital associative $R$-algebras with multiplication- and unit-preserving $R$-linear maps form the category $\mathrm{Alg}_R^{\mathrm{uni}}$.
