# snake lemma　`thm.snake-lemma`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=thm.snake-lemma)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./thm.snake-lemma.md)

⚠️ **One cannot chase elements.** $\mathcal{A}$ is a general abelian category; its objects have no "elements" to pick, so a phrase like $x \in \ker c$ is meaningless here. The legitimate substitute is **generalized elements**: "$x \in X$" is always read as "given any morphism $t : T \to X$" (Yoneda's element language), and everything else rests on the universal properties of kernels and cokernels.
