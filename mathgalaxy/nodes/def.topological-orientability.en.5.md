**2. The orientation double cover.** Let $\widehat{M}$ be the set of all pairs $(x,\mathfrak{o}_{x})$, where $x\in M$ and $\mathfrak{o}_{x}$ is a local orientation of $T_{x}M$, obtained by pulling back the standard orientation given by a chart around $x$ from $\mathbb{R}^{n}$. Two charts give the same $\mathfrak{o}_{x}$ if and only if the transition map preserves orientation at $x$. The charts identify $\widehat{M}$ locally with $U\times\{\pm1\}$, giving its topology. The projection $p:\widehat{M}\to M$ is a two-sheeted covering called the **orientation double cover**. $M$
