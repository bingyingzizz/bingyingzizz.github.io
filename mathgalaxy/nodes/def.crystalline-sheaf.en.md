## Statement · **draft**
On $\mathbf{Top}_{/X}$, giving a sheaf $\mathcal{F}$ is equivalent to giving all $\mathcal{F}_{Y}$ ($Y \to X$) together with compatible morphisms

$$\alpha_{f} : f^{-1}\mathcal{F}_{Y} \longrightarrow \mathcal{F}_{Z} \qquad (f : Z \to Y).$$

If all $\alpha_{f}$ are **isomorphisms**, $\mathcal{F}$ is called a **crystalline sheaf**.
