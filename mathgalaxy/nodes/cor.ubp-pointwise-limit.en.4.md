# pointwise limit of uniformly bounded operators　`cor.ubp-pointwise-limit`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=cor.ubp-pointwise-limit)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./cor.ubp-pointwise-limit.md)

⭐ This upgrades "pointwise convergence" to "a norm estimate for the limit operator": one need not know in advance that $\sup_n \|u_n\|$ is finite — the uniform boundedness principle supplies it. It is the usual tool in numerical analysis for deciding that "the limit of a sequence of operators is again an operator".

⚠️ Only a bound on $\liminf$ is available; it cannot be replaced by $\limsup$: $\|u\|$ may be strictly smaller than $\liminf \|u_n\|$ (for instance, take a sequence of operators that "almost loses" its norm in some direction; the pointwise limit drops that direction).
