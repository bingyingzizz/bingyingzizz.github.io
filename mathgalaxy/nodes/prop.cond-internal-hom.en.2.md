where the left-hand side is the condensed set determined by "the test object $S \mapsto C\bigl(X(S), Y\bigr)$" (the space of continuous maps with the compact-open topology), and the right-hand side is the **internal Hom** of $\mathrm{Cond}$.

In particular, if $X$ and $Y$ are **both compactly generated**, then $kC(X, Y) \cong \operatorname{Hom}(X, Y)$.
