An occurrence of a variable inside the scope of $\forall v_{i}$ or $\exists v_{i}$ is called **bound**, otherwise **free**; a formula with no free variables is a **sentence** (closed formula).

The **complexity** of a formula (the number of connectives and quantifiers) supports induction — this is the basis for "induction on formulas".
