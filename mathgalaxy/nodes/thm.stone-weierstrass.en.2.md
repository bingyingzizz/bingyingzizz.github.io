# Stone-Weierstrass theorem　`thm.stone-weierstrass`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=thm.stone-weierstrass)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./thm.stone-weierstrass.md)

② **Two-point interpolation**: for $x \ne y$ and target values $\alpha, \beta \in \mathbb{R}$, take $g \in A$ with $g(x) \ne g(y)$; solving the 2-by-2 linear system gives $f = a\cdot 1 + b g \in A$ with $f(x) = \alpha$ and $f(y) = \beta$.

③ **Sandwich from above and below** (all performed inside $\overline{A}$, which ① and ② have already certified to be a closed unital subalgebra, closed under $\max,\min$):

- **Two points hit simultaneously**: given $x \ne y$ and $\varepsilon > 0$. By ② take $g_{xy} \in \overline{A}$ with $g_{xy}(x) = f(x)$ and $g_{xy}(y) = f(y)$.
