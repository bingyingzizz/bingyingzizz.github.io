## Statement · **draft**
An integral domain $A$ is a **principal ideal domain** (PID) if every ideal is principal: for every ideal $I$ there is $a$ such that $I = (a) = aA$.

## Notes · **draft**
Examples: $\mathbb{Z}$ (every ideal is $(n)$), $K[x]$ (ideals are generated by the polynomial of least degree), $\mathbb{Z}[i]$.
