## Notes · **draft**
Every one-dimensional representation is irreducible. Irreducible implies indecomposable, but not conversely: over $F[x]$ the matrix $T=\begin{pmatrix}1&1\\0&1\end{pmatrix}$ is indecomposable yet has an invariant line, so it is reducible. In module language, irreducible representations are simple $A$-modules. Over an algebraically closed field, Schur's lemma forces $\operatorname{End}_A(V)$ to consist of scalars.
