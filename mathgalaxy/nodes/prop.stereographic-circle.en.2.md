## Notes · **draft**
**The algorithm for circle preservation**: a circle on the sphere is “the intersection of a plane with the sphere”, i.e. $ax + by + cw = d$. Substitute the stereographic projection formula and rearrange into a linear expression in $u, v, |z|^{2}$:

$$(d - c)|z|^{2} - 2au - 2bv = (d + c),$$

When $c \ne d$ this is the equation of a circle; when $c = d$ it degenerates into a line — exactly matching “whether the circle passes through the north pole”. ∎
