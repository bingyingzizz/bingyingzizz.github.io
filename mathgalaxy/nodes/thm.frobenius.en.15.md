**VI. The condition is sharp.** A contact distribution is a counterexample (see the notes to `def.involutive-distribution`). It is smooth, has rank $2$, and is nowhere zero, yet fails to be involutive, so no surface can be an integral manifold for it. Involutivity is therefore necessary as well as sufficient.
