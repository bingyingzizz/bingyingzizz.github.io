# simplicial approximation　`thm.simplicial-approximation`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=thm.simplicial-approximation)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./thm.simplicial-approximation.md)

such that $|g| \simeq f$ ($|g|$ and $f$ are homotopic). Such a map $g$ is called a **simplicial approximation** to $f$.

**Corollary**: Let $K$ be a finite simplicial complex. The inclusion $C_{*}(K) \hookrightarrow S_{*}(|K|)$ induces an isomorphism

$$H_{p}(K) \;\cong\; H_{p}(|K|) \qquad \forall p.$$

Thus the singular homology of a polyhedron can be computed as the simplicial homology of a triangulation, which is a **finite matrix** calculation.
