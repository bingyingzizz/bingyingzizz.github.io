# polygonal representation of a surface　`def.polygon-representation`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=def.polygon-representation)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./def.polygon-representation.md)

**Why use it**: A polygonal representation encodes a surface by finite combinatorial data, namely the edge pairings. These data are the input to the classification theorem. Gluing the edges is the inverse step that reconstructs the surface.

⚠ Gluing need not produce a manifold. If a vertex link is not a circle, the quotient may be a pseudomanifold (for example, when the two endpoints of the $2$-gon $aa$ are identified, this needs a separate check). The definition of a polygonal representation gives the quotient space. Whether it is a surface requires verification.
