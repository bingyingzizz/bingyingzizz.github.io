## English abstract · reviewed by bingyingzizz (2026-10-16) · Chinese is authoritative（续）
For $E \subseteq X \times Y$ the vertical and horizontal sections are $E_x := \{y \in Y : (x,y) \in E\}$ and $E^y := \{x \in X : (x,y) \in E\}$; for a function $f : X \times Y \to \mathbb{C}$ one sets $f_x(y) = f^y(x) = f(x,y)$. Geometrically, cut the picture with a line and look at what remains. Sections are the language in which Fubini is stated: integrate along the sections first, then integrate the resulting function against the other variable.
