# Linearity of the Integral on Chains　`prop.integral-chain-linear`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=prop.integral-chain-linear)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./prop.integral-chain-linear.md)

The dual directions are "$\mathrm{d}$ raises degree, $\partial$ lowers degree", and $\int$ is precisely the bracket that couples this pair of duals (`thm.stokes-chain`).

**⚠️ Antisymmetry is not "antisymmetry"**: $\int_{-c} = -\int_{c}$ says the coefficients of the chain are negative, not that "swapping the two arguments changes the sign". There is no commutativity between chains and forms — they have different types.
