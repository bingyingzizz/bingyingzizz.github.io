## Statement · **draft**
Let $f \in K[x]$, $a \in K$. Then

$$f(a) = 0 \iff (x - a) \text{ divides } f \ \text{ in } K[x]$$

That is: there exists $h \in K[x]$ with $f = (x - a)\,h$. In this case $a$ is called a **root** (zero) of $f$.
