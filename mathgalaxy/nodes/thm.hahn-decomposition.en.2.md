## Notes · **draft**
This is "cutting the space in two along the sign": $\nu$ is nonnegative on $P$ and nonpositive on $N$.

⚠ "$P \triangle P'$ is a null set" only says the decomposition is **unique up to null sets** — strictly speaking a Hahn decomposition is not unique (one may move null sets freely between $P$ and $N$), but the $\nu$ values of all measurable sets are exactly the same.

⭐ With it, "signed measure" reduces completely to "two genuine measures" — which is exactly the content of the Jordan decomposition below.
