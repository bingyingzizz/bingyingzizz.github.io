## Notes · **draft**
On $K[x]/(d)$, multiplication by $x$ has, in the basis $1,x,\ldots,x^{m-1}$, the matrix described above: the relation $x^m\equiv-(a_0+a_1x+\cdots+a_{m-1}x^{m-1})$ produces the last column.
