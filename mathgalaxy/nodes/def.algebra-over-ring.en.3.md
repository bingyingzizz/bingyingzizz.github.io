## Notes · **stale**
The base ring is commutative so that the left and right $R$-actions agree. A unital algebra has a structure map $R\to A$, $r\mapsto r1_A$, whose image lies in the centre of $A$. Examples are $M_n(F)$, the polynomial algebra $F[x_1,\ldots,x_n]$, and the group algebra $F[G]$. Associativity and unitality are additional axioms; this cluster works with unital associative algebras.
