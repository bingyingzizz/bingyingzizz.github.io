where $I$ runs over all $i_{1}<\cdots<i_{k}$ and the coefficients are given by $a_{I} := \omega\bigl(\partial_{i_{1}},\ldots,\partial_{i_{k}}\bigr)$. On the overlap of two charts the two sets of coefficients are converted into one another by the $k$-minors of the Jacobian matrix — and minors are smooth, so "the coefficients are smooth" does not depend on the chart chosen. This step is what makes the definition work at all: **it welds a local expression into a global object**.
