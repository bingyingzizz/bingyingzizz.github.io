By prop.character-inner-product-hom the inner product equals dim⁡Hom⁡G(V1,V2)\dim\operatorname{Hom}_G(V_1,V_2)dimHomG​(V1​,V2​). Schur's lemma: there are no nonzero homomorphisms between non-isomorphic irreducible representations; when isomorphic, End⁡G(V1)=F\operatorname{End}_G(V_1)=FEndG​(V1​)=F (algebraically closed, see cor.schur-scalar), of dimension 1.
