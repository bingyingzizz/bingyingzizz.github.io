## Statement · **draft**
A subspace $W\subseteq V$ is called a subrepresentation if $\theta(a)W\subseteq W$ for all $a\in A$; then $\theta$ restricts to a representation on $W$. For a group representation this means $\rho(g)W\subseteq W$ for all $g\in G$.
