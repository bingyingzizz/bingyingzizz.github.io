## Notes · **draft**
⭐ **Why this matters so much for condensed mathematics**: $\mathrm{Cond}$ has enough **projective** objects (unlike a general topos, which only has enough injectives), so "left derived functors" in $\mathrm{Cond}$ can be computed directly using free compact Hausdorff pieces — this is half of the basis for doing derived algebra in $\mathrm{CondAb}$.
