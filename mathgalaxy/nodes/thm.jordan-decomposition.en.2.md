# Jordan decomposition　`thm.jordan-decomposition`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=thm.jordan-decomposition)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./thm.jordan-decomposition.md)

$$\nu^+ := \nu(\cdot \cap P), \quad  \nu^- := -\nu(\cdot \cap N)$$

Then $\nu = \nu^+ - \nu^-$ and $\nu^+ \perp  \nu^-$.

## Notes · **draft**
The **positive variation** $\nu^+$ and the **negative variation** $\nu^-$ are both genuine measures; their sum is called the **total variation**:

$$|\nu| := \nu^+ + \nu^-$$

⭐ "Signed measure" is thereby fully dissected: every signed measure is the difference of a pair of mutually singular measures.

⚠ "At least one finite" corresponds to the clause "takes at most one of $\pm \infty$" in the definition of a signed measure.
