def.field-extension · 定义 · 代数 · 域论

又称域扩张·扩域·次数·K over F

陈述

设 F⊆KF \subseteq KF⊆K 都是域，且 FFF 的运算就是 KKK 的运算在 FFF 上的限制。这时称 KKK 是 FFF 的一个扩域，记作 K/FK/FK/F（读作「KKK over FFF」）。

把 KKK 看成 FFF 上的向量空间（加法来自 KKK，纯量乘法就是 FFF 中的乘法），它的维数叫次数：

[K:F]:=dim⁡FK.[K : F] := \dim_{F} K .[K:F]:=dimF​K.

[K:F]<∞[K:F] < \infty[K:F]<∞ 时叫有限扩张。

说明

⭐ 「扩张」不是新对象，是换视角：同一个域 KKK，一旦指定子域 FFF，就能把 KKK 当 FFF-向量空间看。于是「域论」立刻借到线性代数的整套机器（基、维数、线性无关）—— 这是整个 Galois 理论的起手式。

⭐ 次数怎么算：[C:R]=2[\mathbb{C}:\mathbb{R}] = 2[C:R]=2（基 {1,i}\{1, i\}{1,i}）；[Q(2):Q]=2[\mathbb{Q}(\sqrt{2}):\mathbb{Q}] = 2[Q(2​):Q]=2；[Fpn:Fp]=n[\mathbb{F}_{p^{n}}:\mathbb{F}_{p}] = n[Fpn​:Fp​]=n。

⭐ 塔公式（本图未成节点）：有限扩张 F⊆K⊆LF \subseteq K \subseteq LF⊆K⊆L 满足

[L:F]=[L:K] [K:F],[L:F] = [L:K]\,[K:F],[L:F]=[L:K][K:F],

因为 LLL 是自由 KKK-模、KKK 是自由 FFF-模，取基作积。后面所有「次数整除」的结论都靠它。

⚠ 次数有限不等于「能算」：[Q(α):Q][\mathbb{Q}(\alpha):\mathbb{Q}][Q(α):Q] 可能很大，而 α\alphaα 是某个具体多项式的根 ——「写得出来」与「有限」是两件事（这正是五次方程那条线的主题）。

Statement draft

Let F⊆KF \subseteq KF⊆K be fields, with the operations of FFF being the restrictions of the operations of KKK to FFF. Then KKK is called an extension field of FFF, written K/FK/FK/F (read “KKK over FFF”).

Regarding KKK as a vector space over FFF (addition from KKK, scalar multiplication the multiplication in FFF), its dimension is called the degree:

[K:F]:=dim⁡FK.[K : F] := \dim_{F} K .[K:F]:=dimF​K.

It is a finite extension when [K:F]<∞[K:F] < \infty[K:F]<∞.

Notes draft

⭐ “Extension” is not a new object but a change of viewpoint: the same field KKK, once a subfield FFF is specified, can be regarded as a vector space KKK over FFF. Field theory thus immediately borrows the whole linear-algebra machine (basis, dimension, linear independence) — this is the opening move of all Galois theory.

⭐ How to compute degrees: [C:R]=2[\mathbb{C}:\mathbb{R}] = 2[C:R]=2 (basis {1,i}\{1, i\}{1,i}); [Q(2):Q]=2[\mathbb{Q}(\sqrt{2}):\mathbb{Q}] = 2[Q(2​):Q]=2; [Fpn:Fp]=n[\mathbb{F}_{p^{n}}:\mathbb{F}_{p}] = n[Fpn​:Fp​]=n.

⭐ Tower formula (not a node in this graph): a finite extension F⊆K⊆LF \subseteq K \subseteq LF⊆K⊆L satisfies

[L:F]=[L:K] [K:F],[L:F] = [L:K]\,[K:F],[L:F]=[L:K][K:F],

because LLL is a free KKK-module and KKK is a free FFF-module; multiply bases. Every later “degree divides” statement relies on it.

⚠ Finite degree does not mean computable: [Q(α):Q][\mathbb{Q}(\alpha):\mathbb{Q}][Q(α):Q] can be huge while α\alphaα is a root of a concrete polynomial — “writable down” and “finite” are two different things (the theme of the quintic story).
