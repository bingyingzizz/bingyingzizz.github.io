# Multiplicity Formula for Characters　`cor.character-multiplicity`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=cor.character-multiplicity)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./cor.character-multiplicity.md)

## Statement · **stale**
Let $\operatorname{char}F\nmid|G|$, let $F$ be algebraically closed, and let $V$ be a finite-dimensional semisimple representation, $V\cong\bigoplus_i n_iV_i$ with the $V_i$ pairwise non-isomorphic irreducible. Then $n_i=\langle\chi_V,\chi_{V_i}\rangle$.

## Notes · **stale**
By prop.character-operations (1), $\chi_V=\sum_j n_j\chi_{V_j}$.
Taking the inner product with $\chi_{V_i}$ and using orthogonality $\langle\chi_{V_j},\chi_{V_i}\rangle=\delta_{ji}$ gives $\langle\chi_V,\chi_{V_i}\rangle=n_i$.
