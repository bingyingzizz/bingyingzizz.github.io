## Notes · **draft**
By prop.character-operations (1), $\chi_V=\sum_j n_j\chi_{V_j}$.
Taking the inner product with $\chi_{V_i}$ and using orthogonality $\langle\chi_{V_j},\chi_{V_i}\rangle=\delta_{ji}$ gives $\langle\chi_V,\chi_{V_i}\rangle=n_i$.
