# Lie Group　`def.lie-group`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=def.lie-group)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./def.lie-group.md)

**Topological restrictions**: not every group can be a Lie group (second countability and local Euclideanity are required). Cartan's closed-subgroup theorem says that a closed subgroup of a Lie group is automatically a submanifold — this generalizes "$\operatorname{SL}$ and $\operatorname{O}$ are Lie groups"; this library only mentions it.

**Why it matters**: Lie groups turn "symmetry" into a differentiable object, so differentiating at the identity gives a Lie algebra (`def.lie-algebra`, `prop.left-invariant-fields`) — linearizing a nonlinear problem, the technical core of the whole theory.
