# infinity　`ax.inf`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=ax.inf)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./ax.inf.md)

The clause $\emptyset \in I$ **cannot be dropped**: $I = \omega \setminus \{0\}$ is non-empty and closed under successor ($x \ne 0 \implies S(x) \ne 0$), yet it does not contain $\emptyset$ — "non-empty + closed under successor" does not force $\emptyset \in I$. ($\emptyset$ is a primitive object, see "the empty set"). This axiom asserts the existence of an infinite set, and is the only source from which the set of natural numbers $\omega$ is built.
