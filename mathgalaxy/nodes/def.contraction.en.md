## Statement · **draft**
Let $(X, d)$ be a metric space and $T : X \to X$. The map $T$ is called a **contraction** if and only if there is a constant $q \in [0, 1)$ such that

$$d(Tx, Ty) \le q\, d(x, y) \qquad (x, y \in X)$$

The smallest such $q$ is called the **contraction constant** of $T$. (For $q = 1$ the map is called nonexpansive; the conclusions then change.)
