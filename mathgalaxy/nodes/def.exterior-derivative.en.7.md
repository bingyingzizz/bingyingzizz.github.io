# Exterior Derivative: Coordinate Formula on Euclidean Open Sets　`def.exterior-derivative`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=def.exterior-derivative)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./def.exterior-derivative.md)

**Unification of three classical operators** ($n=3$): $\mathrm{d}$ on $0$-forms gives the gradient (as a $1$-form), on $1$-forms the curl (as a $2$-form), and on $2$-forms the divergence (as a $3$-form). Hence $\mathrm{d}^{2}=0$ is exactly "the divergence of a curl is zero" and "the curl of a gradient is zero".

⚠️ **One cannot apply $\mathrm{d}$ to a vector field**: $\mathrm{d}$ acts only on forms. Differentiating vector fields requires extra structure (a connection, `def.affine-connection`), and the result is a covariant derivative rather than an exterior derivative.
