Statement

Let C\mathcal{C}C be a site. A set G⊆CG \subseteq \mathcal{C}G⊆C is called a topologically generating set if every X∈CX \in \mathcal{C}X∈C admits a covering family consisting of objects lying in GGG, say (Gi→X)i(G_{i} \to X)_{i}(Gi​→X)i​.

Notes

What it says: GGG is a topological generating set means every object XXX admits a covering family all of whose objects lie in GGG.

⭐ Use: the sheaf condition need only be checked on GGG (coverings generated by GGG); equivalently, "{ hG }G∈G\{\, h_{G} \,\}_{G \in G}{hG​}G∈G​ forms a generating family".

⚠ Easily confused with the generator of category theory (the one requiring "Hom is faithful", in the two category-theory clusters): what is meant here is only coverings of a site, not faithfulness of representable functors.
