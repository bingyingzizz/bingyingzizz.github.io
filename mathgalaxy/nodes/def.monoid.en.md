## Statement · **draft**
Let $M$ be a set and $\cdot$ a binary operation on $M$, with $e\in M$. If **associativity** $(ab)c=a(bc)$ and the **identity law** $ea=ae=a$ hold for all $a,b,c$, then $(M,\cdot,e)$ is called a **monoid**; one that also satisfies the **commutative law** is a **commutative monoid**. A homomorphism is a map preserving the operation and the identity.
