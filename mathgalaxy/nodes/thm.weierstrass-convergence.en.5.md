# Weierstrass convergence theorem　`thm.weierstrass-convergence`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=thm.weierstrass-convergence)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./thm.weierstrass-convergence.md)

⚠️ The hypothesis must be **uniform on compact subsets** (uniform on every compact set). With pointwise convergence alone the limit need not be holomorphic and the derivatives need not converge. (Note: $f_k(z) = z^{k}$ is **not** a counterexample — it converges uniformly on compact subsets of $|z| < 1$, and its derivatives converge uniformly to $0$. A genuine counterexample needs compact sets that touch the boundary of convergence.)
