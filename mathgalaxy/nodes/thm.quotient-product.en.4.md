# quotient map product　`thm.quotient-product`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=thm.quotient-product)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./thm.quotient-product.md)

**How the proof goes (continuity of the inverse).** Write $W := (q \times \mathrm{id}_{Z})^{-1}(U)$ and show that $W$ is open. Take a point $(x, z) \in W$ and put $y = q(x)$. Since $U$ is open in $Y \times Z$, one can pick a basic open set

$$V \times K \;\subseteq\; U, \qquad y \in V,\;\; z \in K,$$

where $V$ is open and $K$ is a **compact neighbourhood** of $z$ (a locally compact Hausdorff space has a base of compact neighbourhoods at every point, and $K$ may be required to lie inside any prescribed neighbourhood). Since $q$ is a quotient map, $q^{-1}(V)$ is open in $X$, so

$$q^{-1}(V) \times K \;\subseteq\; W$$
