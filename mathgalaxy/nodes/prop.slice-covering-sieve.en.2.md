## Notes · **draft**
**Proof.** Both sides are translations of "can the cocone over $R$ cover" ✓: coverings in $\mathcal{C}/T$ are pulled back from coverings of $\mathcal{C}$ along $j_{T}$, and $j_{T!}$ is precisely the functor carrying sieves in $\mathcal{C}/T$ back to $\mathcal{C}$ ✓.

⚠️ But $j_{T!}$ is **not left exact in general** — so this does not produce a morphism of sites (which is exactly why one later uses the **cocontinuity** of $j_{T}$ to build morphisms of topoi). ∎
