prop.functor-preserves-sections

Machine-drafted and pending author review. The Chinese text is authoritative.

Statement

A functor sends sections (resp. retractions, isomorphisms) to sections (resp. retractions, isomorphisms); in particular a functor sends isomorphisms to isomorphisms.

Notes

Proof. f∘g=1f \circ g = 1f∘g=1 and g∘f=1g \circ f = 1g∘f=1 remain the same equalities under a functor (functors preserve identities and composition) ✓. ∎
