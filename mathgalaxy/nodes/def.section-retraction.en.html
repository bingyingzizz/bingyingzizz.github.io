Statement

Let f:X→Yf : X \to Yf:X→Y be a morphism in a category.

  • A section of fff is a morphism g:Y→Xg : Y \to Xg:Y→X such that
f∘g=1Yf \circ g = 1_Yf∘g=1Y​
  • A retraction of fff is a morphism g:Y→Xg : Y \to Xg:Y→X such that
g∘f=1Xg \circ f = 1_Xg∘f=1X​

Notes

The two equations point in opposite directions: a section is "first ggg, then fff, back to YYY", while a retraction is "first fff, then ggg, back to XXX". In other words, a section is a right inverse of fff and a retraction is a left inverse of fff — the names differ because composition is written in the opposite order from the maps.
