# symmetric algebra　`def.symmetric-algebra`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=def.symmetric-algebra)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./def.symmetric-algebra.md)

- no quotient: $\implies$ the **tensor algebra** $T^{\bullet}V$ (noncommutative, free);
- quotient by $vw - wv$ $\implies$ the **symmetric algebra** $\operatorname{Sym}^{\bullet}V \cong K[x_1, \ldots, x_n]$ (commutative);
- quotient by $vw + wv$ $\implies$ the **exterior algebra** $\bigwedge^{\bullet}V$ (graded-commutative).

⭐ «$\operatorname{Sym}^{\bullet}V \cong K[x_1,\ldots,x_n]$» shows that the polynomial ring is not conjured from nothing — it is the symmetric algebra of an $n$-dimensional space.
