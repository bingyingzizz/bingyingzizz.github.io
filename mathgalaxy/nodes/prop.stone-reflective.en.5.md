# Stone spaces are a reflective subcategory of compact Hausdorff spaces　`prop.stone-reflective`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=prop.stone-reflective)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./prop.stone-reflective.md)

- hence $\mathbf{Stone}\hookrightarrow\mathbf{CHaus}$ is reflective, with reflector "quotient by connected components" (which again gives a compact Hausdorff space ✓). ∎

⭐ A reflective subcategory's **inclusion preserves limits**, so limits of Stone spaces (computed in $\mathbf{CHaus}$ or $\mathbf{Top}$) are again Stone ✓ — this is exactly "Stone spaces are closed under limits".
