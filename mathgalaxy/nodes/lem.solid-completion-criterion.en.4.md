is an isomorphism. Let $\mathcal{A}_F$ be the full subcategory of objects for which "$\operatorname{Hom}(F(X), Y) \to \operatorname{Hom}(X, Y)$ is an isomorphism for all $X \in \mathcal{A}_0$"; its objects are the $Y$ with this property. Then:

**(i)** $\mathcal{A}_F \subset \mathcal{A}$ is an abelian subcategory stable under all limits, colimits and extensions; the $F(X)$ ($X \in \mathcal{A}_0$) form a family of compact projective generators of $\mathcal{A}_F$; the inclusion has a left adjoint $L$, which is the unique colimit-preserving extension of $F$.
