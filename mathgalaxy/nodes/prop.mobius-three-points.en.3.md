# three points determine a Möbius transformation　`prop.mobius-three-points`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=prop.mobius-three-points)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./prop.mobius-three-points.md)

**Uniqueness**: if both $T_{1}, T_{2}$ work, then $S := T_{1}^{-1}T_{2}$ fixes three points. A Möbius transformation fixing three points is the identity (by invariance of the cross-ratio: $Sz$ and $z$ have the same cross-ratio with respect to the same three points, hence $Sz = z$). ∎

⭐ **Practical value**: for problems of the form "construct a transformation sending a given disk to the upper half-plane", it suffices to match three boundary points — the transformation is then uniquely determined, with no coefficients to guess.
