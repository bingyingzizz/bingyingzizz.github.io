# right derived functor　`def.right-derived-functor`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=def.right-derived-functor)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./def.right-derived-functor.md)

**Component form**: $R^{n}F(M) := H^{n}(RF(M))$, where $M$ is regarded as a complex concentrated in degree $0$. $R^{0}F = F$ (for a left exact functor), $R^{n}F = 0$ ($n < 0$).

⭐ **Motivation**: when $F$ is left exact, a short exact sequence $0 \to M' \to M \to M'' \to 0$ yields only $0 \to FM' \to FM \to FM''$ — **the right end breaks off**. The right derived functor $R^{1}F(M'')$ repairs exactly that break and splices the long exact sequence back together.
