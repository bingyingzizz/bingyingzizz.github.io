## Notes · **draft**
**Only open sets are used**: the definition mentions no metric and no $\mathbb{R}^{n}$ — compactness is a purely topological property, so it is preserved by continuous maps and invariant under homeomorphisms (`def.continuous-map` / `def.homeomorphism`).

**Equivalent form (closed sets)**: every family of closed sets with the finite intersection property has non-empty intersection — that is, "$\{F_{i}\}$ closed and every finite subfamily has non-empty intersection $\implies$ the whole family has non-empty intersection". This form is the one most often used in proofs by contradiction.
