## Statement · **draft**
Let $f, g \in L^1(\mu)$. Then the following three are equivalent:

$$\int_E f d\mu = \int_E g d\mu\quad  \forall E \in \mathcal{M}; $$
$$\int |f - g| d\mu = 0; $$
$$f = g\quad  \mu-\text{a.e.}$$

## Notes · **draft**
Purpose: statements of this kind **identify a function** by its integrals over all measurable sets.
