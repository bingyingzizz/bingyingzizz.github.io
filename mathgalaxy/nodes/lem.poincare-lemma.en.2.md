# Poincaré Lemma　`lem.poincare-lemma`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=lem.poincare-lemma)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./lem.poincare-lemma.md)

Let $U \subseteq \mathbb{R}^{n}$ be a **star-shaped** open set: there is $p_{0} \in U$ such that for every $x \in U$, the segment $\{p_{0} + t(x - p_{0}) : t \in [0,1]\}$ lies entirely in $U$. Then for $k \ge 1$,

$$\mathrm{d}\omega = 0 \implies \exists\ \eta \in \Omega^{k-1}(U)\ \text{such that}\ \omega = \mathrm{d}\eta .$$

Equivalently: $H^{k}_{\mathrm{dR}}(U) = 0$ for all $k \ge 1$, while $H^{0}_{\mathrm{dR}}(U) = \mathbb{R}$ (a star-shaped region is connected).
