## Statement · **draft**
Let $(X_{i}, \mathcal{T}_{i})_{i \in I}$ be a family of topological spaces. The **product topology** on $\prod_{i \in I} X_{i}$ is the topology with basis the sets

$$\prod_{i \in I} U_{i} \qquad (U_{i} \in \mathcal{T}_{i},$$
where $U_{i} = X_{i}$ for all but finitely many indices $i$)
