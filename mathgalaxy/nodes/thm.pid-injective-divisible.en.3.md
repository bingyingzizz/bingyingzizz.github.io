# Injective and Divisible Modules over a PID　`thm.pid-injective-divisible`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=thm.pid-injective-divisible)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./thm.pid-injective-divisible.md)

**Injective $\implies$ divisible**: let $a \ne 0$ and $y \in I$. Apply Baer's criterion to the ideal $(a)$ and the map $a \mapsto y$ to obtain $x$ with $a x = y$ ✓.

The case $\mathbb{Z}$ is just $R = \mathbb{Z}$ (a PID); then $R$-modules are abelian groups (`prop.zmod-is-ab`) ✓.

⭐ One-line mnemonic: **"divide by $a$" is "extend along $(a) \rightarrowtail R$"**. On a PID the ideals have the simplest possible structure, so Baer's criterion degenerates into a division problem.
