Statement

Let C\mathcal{C}C be a site with fiber products. Then the family of all "covering sieves generating XXX", namely (Xi→X)i∈I(X_{i} \to X)_{i \in I}(Xi​→X)i∈I​, forms a pretopology on C\mathcal{C}C, and it generates the original topology of C\mathcal{C}C — this pretopology is called the maximal pretopology.

⚠️ Note: a family generating a covering sieve is not necessarily a covering family under the given pretopology — "maximal" means "with respect to the given topology", not "with respect to the given pretopology".

Notes

Proof. Check the pretopology axioms one by one ✓: identity isomorphisms are coverings ✓; coverings pull back along arbitrary morphisms to coverings (using (T2) and fiber products) ✓; a common refinement of two coverings is a covering (using (T3)) ✓. ∎

⭐ Reading: a site and a pretopology (on a category with fiber products) are two ways of writing the same datum — sieves make it convenient to talk about sheaves, families make it convenient to compute.
