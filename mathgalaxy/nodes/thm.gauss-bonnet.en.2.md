## Abstract · **draft**
For a compact orientable surface $M \subseteq \mathbb{R}^{3}$, possibly with piecewise smooth boundary, $\int_{M} K\,\mathrm{d}A + \int_{\partial M} k_{g}\,\mathrm{d}s + \sum_{i}(\pi - \alpha_{i}) = 2\pi\chi(M)$, where $\alpha_{i}$ are the interior angles at the corners. For a closed surface the corner term disappears and $\int_{M} K\,\mathrm{d}A = 2\pi\chi(M)$, so total curvature is a topological quantity: $4\pi$ for the sphere, $0$ for the torus, and $4\pi(1-g)$ in genus $g$.
