[in graph](../#n=def.sigma-algebra)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./def.sigma-algebra.md)

Why it is needed: countable additivity of a measure $\mu (\bigcup A_{n}) = \sum \mu (A_{n})$ requires a domain closed under **countable** operations, otherwise the two sides of the identity do not even live in the same family of sets. The whole construction of measure starts here.
