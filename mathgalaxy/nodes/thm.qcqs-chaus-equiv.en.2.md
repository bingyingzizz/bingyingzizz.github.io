# Quasi-Compact and Quasi-Separated Condensed Sets　`thm.qcqs-chaus-equiv`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=thm.qcqs-chaus-equiv)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./thm.qcqs-chaus-equiv.md)

2. the functor $S \mapsto \underline{S}$ (regarding a compact Hausdorff space as the condensed set it represents) gives an equivalence of categories

$$\mathbf{CHaus}\ \simeq\ \mathrm{CHaus}\ \longrightarrow \mathrm{CondSet}_{\mathrm{qcqs}}.$$
that is: **compact Hausdorff spaces** and **quasi-compact quasi-separated condensed sets** are equivalent.
