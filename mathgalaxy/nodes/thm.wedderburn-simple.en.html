Statement

Let AAA be a finite-dimensional simple FFF-algebra. Then A≅Mn(Dop)A\cong M_n(D^{\mathrm{op}})A≅Mn​(Dop), where DDD is a division algebra, n≥1n\ge1n≥1, and for a simple left ideal VVV one has D≅End⁡A(V)D\cong\operatorname{End}_A(V)D≅EndA​(V). If FFF is algebraically closed, then D=FD=FD=F and A≅Mn(F)A\cong M_n(F)A≅Mn​(F).

Notes

Take a minimal nonzero left ideal VVV; it is a simple AAA-module, so VVV is semisimple, and by Schur's lemma D=End⁡A(V)D=\operatorname{End}_A(V)D=EndA​(V) is a division algebra. The natural map A→End⁡D(V)A\to\operatorname{End}_D(V)A→EndD​(V) is injective because AAA is simple: if aV=0aV=0aV=0, then Ann⁡(V)\operatorname{Ann}(V)Ann(V) is a nonzero two-sided ideal, hence equals AAA, contradicting AV=VAV=VAV=V. Burnside's theorem makes it surjective, so A≅End⁡D(V)≅Mn(Dop)A\cong\operatorname{End}_D(V)\cong M_n(D^{\mathrm{op}})A≅EndD​(V)≅Mn​(Dop). If the field is algebraically closed, Schur's lemma gives D=FD=FD=F. Note the direction: AAA is a matrix algebra over DopD^{\mathrm{op}}Dop.
