Statement

Let AAA and BBB be sets.\n\n- AAA is a subset of BBB (written A⊆BA \subseteq BA⊆B): ∀x(x∈A→x∈B)\forall x ( x \in A \to x \in B )∀x(x∈A→x∈B)\n- AAA is a proper subset of BBB (written A⊂BA \subset BA⊂B): A⊆BA \subseteq BA⊆B and A≠BA \ne BA=B.\n\nBasic properties: ⊆\subseteq⊆ is reflexive and transitive; and by the axiom of extensionality, two sets are equal exactly when they contain each other:\n\nA=B  ⟺  A⊆B∧B⊆AA = B \iff A \subseteq B \wedge B \subseteq AA=B⟺A⊆B∧B⊆A\n\nThis is the most common way to prove two sets equal: containment both ways.
