## Notes · **draft**
**Hypotheses used in each direction** (the crystalline condition makes all data determined by the terminal object):
- Crystalline sheaves $\mathcal F$ to $\mathcal F_X$: restricting to the identity map is free; only the crystalline transition isomorphisms for $f:Y\to X$ yield $f^{-1}\mathcal F_X\cong\mathcal F_Y$.
- Sheaves on $X$ to crystalline sheaves: take $\mathcal F_Y=f^{-1}\mathcal F$, and use the composite compatibility isomorphism of inverse images together with descent along open covers; no Hausdorff or local connectedness hypothesis is need
