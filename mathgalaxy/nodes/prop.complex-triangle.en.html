Statement

In plain words: the modulus of a sum does not exceed the sum of the moduli; equality holds only when these complex numbers “point in the same direction”.

For arbitrary z1,⋯ ,zn∈Cz_1, \cdots, z_n \in \mathbb{C}z1​,⋯,zn​∈C,

∣∑k=1nzk∣≤∑k=1n∣zk∣.\left| \sum_{k=1}^{n} z_k \right| \le \sum_{k=1}^{n} |z_k|.​k=1∑n​zk​​≤k=1∑n​∣zk​∣.

Equality holds iff: the nonzero zkz_kzk​ all have the same argument.
