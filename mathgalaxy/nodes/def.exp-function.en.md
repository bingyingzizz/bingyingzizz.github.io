## Statement · **draft**
For $z \in \mathbb{C}$, define

$$\exp z := \sum_{n=0}^{\infty} \frac{z^{n}}{n!}$$

This series converges absolutely for every $z$ (radius of convergence $+\infty$), and satisfies

$$\exp(z + w) = \exp z \cdot \exp w, \qquad \exp 0 = 1, \qquad \frac{d}{dz}\exp z = \exp z$$
