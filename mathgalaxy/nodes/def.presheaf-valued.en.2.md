# sheaf with values in a category　`def.presheaf-valued`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=def.presheaf-valued)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./def.presheaf-valued.md)

$$X \longmapsto \operatorname{Hom}_{\mathcal{D}}\bigl(Y,\ F(X)\bigr)$$

is a sheaf.

## Notes · **draft**
Taking $\mathcal{D}$ to be $\mathbf{Set}$ returns the original notion of sheaf; taking it to be $\mathbf{Ab}$ gives **abelian sheaves**.

The trick in the definition is: **use "maps into $Y$" to reduce the sheaf condition in $\mathcal{D}$ to sheaves of sets**. Hence every result about sheaves of sets whose statement only uses constructions also available in $\mathcal{D}$ (limits, products, …) carries over item by item — this is the whole secret of "sheaves with general values".
