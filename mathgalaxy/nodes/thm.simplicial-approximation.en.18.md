# simplicial approximation　`thm.simplicial-approximation`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=thm.simplicial-approximation)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./thm.simplicial-approximation.md)

⭐ **Why the corollary matters**: It gives three conclusions at once. (i) Simplicial homology is a topological invariant independent of triangulation. (ii) Singular homology can be computed from ranks of finite matrices. (iii) The Euler characteristic formula carries over to singular homology (see the edge for `thm.euler-characteristic`).

💡 In short: **continuous map $\approx$ simplicial map after subdivision**. This gives a combinatorial description of the topology.
