# injective criterion　`prop.injective-criterion`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=prop.injective-criterion)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./prop.injective-criterion.md)

⚠️ **The hypothesis of the second assertion**: when $M'$ is injective, $M$ injective $\iff M''$ injective — this is "extensions of injectives are injective", which uses only short exact sequences and (4), and **does not need** $\mathcal{A}$ to have enough injectives. Do not confuse it with the category-level hypothesis "$\mathcal{A}$ has enough injectives".
