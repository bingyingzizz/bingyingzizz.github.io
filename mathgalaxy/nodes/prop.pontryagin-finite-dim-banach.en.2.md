## Notes · **draft**
**Proof.** Since $\mathbb{T} = \mathbb{R}/\mathbb{Z}$ and $V$ is a finite-dimensional real vector space, hence **simply connected** ✓, every continuous homomorphism $V \to \mathbb{T}$ lifts uniquely to a continuous homomorphism $V \to \mathbb{R}$ ✓.

Now observe that **a continuous additive map $V \to \mathbb{R}$ is automatically $\mathbb{R}$-linear**. Additivity first gives $f(qv) = q f(v)$ for rational scalars ✓, and continuity then extends this to real scalars by approximation ✓.
