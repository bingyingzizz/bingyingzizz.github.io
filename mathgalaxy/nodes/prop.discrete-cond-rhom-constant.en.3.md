## Notes · **draft**
⭐ In one sentence: **discrete groups "cannot feel" the condensed layer** — on them, condensed homological algebra and abstract homological algebra give the same answer (up to a constant sheaf). So to really use the condensed structure one must test it on non-discrete objects such as $\mathbb{R}$, $\mathbb{Z}[S]$.
