## Statement · **draft**
A topological space $X$ is a **Hausdorff space** (or a **$T_{2}$ space**) if any two distinct points **can be separated by open sets**:

$$\forall x \ne y \in X,\ \exists U \ni x,\ V \ni y\ \text{open}:\quad U \cap V = \emptyset$$

## Notes · **draft**
In one phrase: **limits are unique**. A sequence that converges can converge to only one point — this is the kind of space analysis tacitly assumes.
