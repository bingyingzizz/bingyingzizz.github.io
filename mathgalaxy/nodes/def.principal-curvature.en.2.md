# Principal Curvatures　`def.principal-curvature`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=def.principal-curvature)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./def.principal-curvature.md)

In an orthonormal frame $g_{ij} = \delta_{ij}$, the characteristic polynomial is $k^{2}-2Hk+K$, where $H = \tfrac{1}{2}\operatorname{tr}W$ and $K = \det W$.

## Notes · **draft**
**Why eigenvalues**: $k_{n}$ is the Rayleigh quotient of $W$; extremal points on the unit circle satisfy $II(v,\cdot) = k\,I(v,\cdot)$, i.e. $W(\mathrm{d}r(v)) = k\,\mathrm{d}r(v)$ (Lagrange multipliers), and the extremal values are the eigenvalues — this is exactly the statement that "the directions of extremal value are eigen-directions of $W$".
