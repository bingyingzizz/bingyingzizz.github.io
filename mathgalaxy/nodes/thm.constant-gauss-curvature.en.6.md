# surfaces of constant Gauss curvature　`thm.constant-gauss-curvature`

[in graph](../#n=thm.constant-gauss-curvature)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./thm.constant-gauss-curvature.md)

⭐ The row $K = 0$ is exactly the developable case (`def.developable-surface`): $\mathrm{d}r^{2} + r^{2}\mathrm{d}\theta^{2}$ is the polar-coordinate metric of the plane, so a surface with $K \equiv 0$ is locally isometric to the plane — this is the analytic basis of "developable".

⚠ **Locally only three, globally many**: flat tori, cylinders and hyperbolic surfaces all have constant curvature. The corollary's "simply connected + complete" excludes them, and both hypotheses are needed.
