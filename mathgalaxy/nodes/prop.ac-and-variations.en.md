## Statement · **draft**
Let $\mu$ be a measure and $\nu$ a signed measure. Then

$$\nu \ll  \mu \iff |\nu| \ll  \mu \iff \nu^+ \ll  \mu\ \wedge \ \nu^- \ll  \mu$$

Moreover: if both $\nu \perp  \mu$ and $\nu \ll  \mu$ hold, then $\nu = 0$.
