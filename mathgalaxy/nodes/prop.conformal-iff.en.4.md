# Nonvanishing Derivative and Conformality　`prop.conformal-iff`

[in graph](../#n=prop.conformal-iff)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./prop.conformal-iff.md)

⚠ (ii) is not a tautology: write the real differential as $\Delta z \mapsto \Delta f$, and being a similarity forces $\Delta z$ and $\overline{\Delta z}$ to have the same length but be orthogonal; equivalently $\partial f/\partial \bar z(z_0) = 0$, the Cauchy–Riemann equation in conjugate form.

⚠ "Nowhere zero" is essential: $f(z) = z^2$ doubles angles at $0$ ($0$), and $f(z) = \bar z$ is angle-preserving but not holomorphic.

The fractional linear map $f(z) = (az+b)/(cz+d)$ has derivative $(ad-bc)/(cz+d)^2$, and $ad - bc \ne 0$ guarantees it is conformal everywhere.
