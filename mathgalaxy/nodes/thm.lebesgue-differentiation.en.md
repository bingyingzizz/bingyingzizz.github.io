## Statement · **draft**
Let $f \in L^1_{loc}$. Then

$$\lim_{r\to0} A_r f(x) = f(x)\quad \text{for a.e. } x \in \mathbb{R}^n$$

## Notes · **draft**
⭐ In one sentence: **an integrable function equals the limit of its local averages almost everywhere**.
