# Kernel and Cokernel　`def.kernel-cokernel`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=def.kernel-cokernel)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./def.kernel-cokernel.md)

- a sequence $0 \to M' \xrightarrow{\varphi} M \xrightarrow{\psi} M'' \to 0$ that is both left exact and right exact is called a **short exact sequence**; then $M$ is called an **extension** of $M'$ by $M''$.

## Notes · **draft**
**Why the kernel is an "equalizer" and not a "preimage"**: the equalizer of $f$ and $0_{M,N}$ is the **universal object** among "all morphisms killed by $f$" — it is far more basic than "$f^{-1}(0)$", because in a general category there are no elements to take preimages of. (In $\mathbf{Ab}$ the two agree: $\ker f = f^{-1}(0)$.)
