Statement

Let VVV be a finite-dimensional indecomposable representation and r,η∈End⁡A(V)r,\eta\in\operatorname{End}_A(V)r,η∈EndA​(V) nilpotent. Then r+ηr+\etar+η is nilpotent.

Notes

By lem.endomorphism-local, End⁡A(V)\operatorname{End}_A(V)EndA​(V) is a local ring whose maximal ideal consists of the nilpotent endomorphisms; a maximal ideal is closed under addition, so r+ηr+\etar+η is nilpotent. Indecomposability cannot be dropped: for V=F2V=F^2V=F2 with r=E12r=E_{12}r=E12​ and η=E21\eta=E_{21}η=E21​ both are nilpotent but r+ηr+\etar+η is invertible. No commutativity of r,ηr,\etar,η is needed.
