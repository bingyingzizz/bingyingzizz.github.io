# Semidirect Product　`def.semidirect-product`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=def.semidirect-product)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./def.semidirect-product.md)

⚠️ **Careful in the converse direction**: a short exact sequence $1 \to H \to E \xrightarrow{\pi} G \to 1$ with a homomorphic section only tells you that $E$ is a **semidirect product** of $H$ and $G$ (when $H$ is normal in $E$ one has $E \cong H \rtimes_{\varphi} G$, with $\varphi$ given by conjugation);

but **once the $G$-module structure is given, the split extension is unique up to isomorphism**.

$H^{1}$ classifies the **choice of section**, not the extension itself — see the warning in "one-dimensional cohomology and split extensions".
