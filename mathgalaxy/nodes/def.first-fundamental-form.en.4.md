## Notes · **draft**
$I$ is the **pullback** of the inner product of $\mathbb{R}^{3}$ along $r$: $\mathrm{d}r$ sends tangent vectors of the parameter domain into the tangent plane, and then one measures with the inner product of $\mathbb{R}^{3}$. Thus $I$ sees only "lengths on the surface" and not "how the surface bends" — the bending is in $II$.

**Why an orthonormal coframe gives a sum of two squares**: $\mathrm{d}r = \sum_{i}\omega^{i}e_{i}$ with $e_{i}$ orthonormal, hence $\langle \mathrm{d}r, \mathrm{d}r\rangle = \sum_{i}(\omega^{i})^{2}$;
