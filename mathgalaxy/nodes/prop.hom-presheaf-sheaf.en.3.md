Applying this to the cover above: $Y(U) \to \prod_i Y(U_{i}) \rightrightarrows \prod_{i,j} Y(U_{i} \cap U_{j})$ is an equalizer, which is exactly the sheaf condition for $U \mapsto Y(U)$ ✓. ∎

⭐ In one sentence: **"sections over the open sets of $X$" form a sheaf**, because open covers are already covers in $\mathrm{Cond}/X$.
