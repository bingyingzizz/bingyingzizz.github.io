# closed graph theorem　`thm.closed-graph`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=thm.closed-graph)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./thm.closed-graph.md)

is bounded as a composite of two bounded maps. $\square$

**The practical value is that "closed" is easier to verify than "bounded".** To prove $u$ bounded directly one must estimate $\|u x\|$; to see that the graph is closed one only needs: take convergent $x_n \to x$ and $u(x_n) \to y$, and prove $y = u(x)$ — no norm estimates in between. This is the route by which distribution theory proves that differential operators and the Fourier transform are continuous.
