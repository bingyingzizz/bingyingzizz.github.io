# Lifting Maps to Injective Resolutions　`prop.injective-resolution-lift`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=prop.injective-resolution-lift)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./prop.injective-resolution-lift.md)

Then for every morphism $f : M \to N$:

1. **there exists** a morphism of complexes $F : I^{\bullet} \to J^{\bullet}$ lifting $f$ (that is, inducing $H^{0}(F) = f$);
2. any two such lifts are **homotopic**.
