## Statement · **draft**
Let $K$ be a field, $f, g \in K[x]$, $g \ne 0$. Then there exist **unique** $q, r \in K[x]$ such that

$$f = q\,g + r, \qquad \deg r < \deg g$$

($r$ is called the **remainder**, $q$ the **quotient**; $r = 0$ is allowed.)

Consequently $K[x]$ is a **Euclidean domain**: there is a Euclidean function "degree" satisfying $\deg r < \deg g$.
