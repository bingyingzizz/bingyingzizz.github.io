## Statement · **draft**
Let $F$ be a **left exact** additive functor with enough injectives. An object $M \in \mathcal{A}$ is called (right) **$F$-acyclic** if

$$FM = RFM,$$

that is, $FM = R^{0}FM$ and $R^{n}FM = 0$ ($n \ne 0$).
