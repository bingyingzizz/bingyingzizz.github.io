## Notes · **draft**
⭐ This is the **interface** of two lines: on one side "$\mathbb{R}$ is a complete ordered field" (supremum principle), on the other "a metric space $X$ is complete" (every Cauchy sequence converges).

On $\mathbb{R}$ the two coincide, but they **come from different sources**: the first is an order property, the second a metric property.
