## Statement · **draft**
If $\sum |a_n|$ converges, then $\sum a_n$ converges, and

$$\Bigl| \sum_{n=1}^{\infty} a_n \Bigr| \le \sum_{n=1}^{\infty} |a_n|$$

## Notes · **draft**
⭐ The proof uses the triangle inequality once (note $|\sum_{k=m+1}^{n} a_k| \le \sum_{k=m+1}^{n} |a_k|$) plus the Cauchy criterion for series.
