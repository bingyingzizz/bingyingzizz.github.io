# Chern class of a line bundle　`prop.chern-class-line-bundle`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=prop.chern-class-line-bundle)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./prop.chern-class-line-bundle.md)

⚠️ **One cannot drop $c_{j}(L)=0$ ($j\ge2$)**: it guarantees that the characteristic class of a line bundle has only one degree of freedom. ⚠️ But do not wrongly infer "$c_1=0$ implies trivial": $c_1=0$ only says the bundle is invisible at the level of $H^2$; the flat line bundle is an example ($c_1=0$ yet possibly non-trivial).
