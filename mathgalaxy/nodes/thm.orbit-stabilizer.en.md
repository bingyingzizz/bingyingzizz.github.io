## Statement · **draft**
Let a finite group $G$ act on a set $X$, and let $x \in X$. Then $\operatorname{Stab}(x) \le G$ and

$$\bigl| \operatorname{Orb}(x) \bigr| = [G : \operatorname{Stab}(x)] = \frac{|G|}{|\operatorname{Stab}(x)|}$$

In particular $|\operatorname{Orb}(x)|$ divides $|G|$, and

$$|G| = \bigl| \operatorname{Orb}(x) \bigr| \cdot \bigl| \operatorname{Stab}(x) \bigr|$$
