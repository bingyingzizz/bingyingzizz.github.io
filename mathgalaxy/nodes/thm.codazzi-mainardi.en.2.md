# Codazzi–Mainardi Equations　`thm.codazzi-mainardi`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=thm.codazzi-mainardi)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./thm.codazzi-mainardi.md)

Equivalent form: $(\nabla_{X}II)(Y,Z) = (\nabla_{Y}II)(X,Z)$, i.e. $\nabla II$ is symmetric in its first two variables. In coefficients:

$$\nabla_{i}h_{jk} = \nabla_{j}h_{ik},\qquad \nabla_{i}h_{jk} = \partial_{i}h_{jk}-\Gamma^{l}_{\ ij}h_{lk}-\Gamma^{l}_{\ ik}h_{jl}.$$

In dimension two there are only two independent equations ($i = 1$ and $i = 2$; the remaining index is determined by symmetry).
