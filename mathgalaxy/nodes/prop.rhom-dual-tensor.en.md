## Statement · **draft**
Let $M, N$ be **discrete** abelian groups and $T = \mathbb{R}/\mathbb{Z}$. Then

$$\operatorname{RHom}_{\mathbb{Z}}\bigl(\operatorname{RHom}_{\mathbb{Z}}(M, T),\ N\bigr) \cong M \otimes^{\mathbf{L}}_{\mathbb{Z}} N[-1].$$
