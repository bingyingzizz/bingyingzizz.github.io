**⚠️ The most common sign error**: When $\eta$ is written as $\sum_{j}(-1)^{j-1}f_{j}\mathrm{d}x^{1}\wedge\cdots\widehat{\mathrm{d}x^{j}}\cdots\wedge\mathrm{d}x^{k}$ (a hat indicates that the factor is omitted), there is **no** additional sign in $\mathrm{d}\eta = \sum_{j}\partial_{j}f_{j}\mathrm{d}x^{1}\wedge\cdots\wedge\mathrm{d}x^{k}$. Inserting an extra $(-1)^{j-1}$ here throws off the matching of the face terms on the right-hand side.
