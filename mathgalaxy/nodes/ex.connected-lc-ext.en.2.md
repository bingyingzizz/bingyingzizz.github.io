## Notes · **draft**
**Difference from the main theorem**: the main theorem (only $\operatorname{Ext}^{0}, \operatorname{Ext}^{1}$ remain between locally compact groups) requires both source and target to be locally compact; this item narrows the hypotheses to "source connected + target discrete", and even $\operatorname{Ext}^{0}$ disappears from the conclusion — because a continuous homomorphism from a connected group to a discrete group must be trivial (a connected image inside a discrete topology is a single point).
