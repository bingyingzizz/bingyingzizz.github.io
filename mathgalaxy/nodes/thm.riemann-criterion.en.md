## Statement · **draft**
Let $f$ be bounded on $[a, b]$. Then $f$ is integrable if and only if for every $\varepsilon > 0$ there exists a partition $P$ such that

$$U(f, P) - L(f, P) = \sum_{i} \omega_i\,\Delta x_i < \varepsilon$$
