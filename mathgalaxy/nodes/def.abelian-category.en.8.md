# additive and abelian categories　`def.abelian-category`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=def.abelian-category)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./def.abelian-category.md)

An abelian category is the **working environment** of homological algebra as a whole: with kernels and cokernels one can even state "exact sequence"; an additive category only supplies the additive skeleton (zero object and direct sums).
