设 X=Spa⁡(A,A+)X = \operatorname{Spa}(A,A^+)X=Spa(A,A+)。在有理开集上定义

说明

⭐ 证明的关键是一个「借道概形」的技巧：有映射 ψ:Spec⁡A→Spa⁡(A,A+)\psi : \operatorname{Spec} A \to \operatorname{Spa}(A,A^+)ψ:SpecA→Spa(A,A+)，把素理想 p\mathfrak{p}p 送到 A→Frac⁡(A/p)→{0,1}A \to \operatorname{Frac}(A/\mathfrak{p}) \to \{0,1\}A→Frac(A/p)→{0,1}。

有理开集在 ψ\psiψ 下的原像正是 D(f)D(f)D(f)，于是 OSpa⁡(A,A+)\mathcal{O}_{\operatorname{Spa}(A,A^+)}OSpa(A,A+)​ 是 OSpec⁡A\mathcal{O}_{\operatorname{Spec} A}OSpecA​ 在 ψ\psiψ 下的推前；

推前保层。

⭐ (iii) 说明 O+\mathcal{O}^+O+ 不是额外结构，而是 O\mathcal{O}O 里「逐点 ≤1\le 1≤1」的那部分 —— 与 A⁺ 由谱决定 是同一句话的局部版本。

Statement draft

Let X=Spa⁡(A,A+)X = \operatorname{Spa}(A,A^+)X=Spa(A,A+). On rational open sets define

OX(U):=A[1f],OX+(U):=A+[g1f,…,gnf]~\mathcal{O}_X(U) := A\bigl[\tfrac{1}{f}\bigr], \qquad \mathcal{O}_X^+(U) := \widetilde{A^+\bigl[\tfrac{g_1}{f}, \ldots, \tfrac{g_n}{f}\bigr]}OX​(U):=A[f1​],OX+​(U):=A+[fg1​​,…,fgn​​]​

(U=U ⁣(g1,…,gnf)U = U\!\left(\frac{g_1,\ldots,g_n}{f}\right)U=U(fg1​,…,gn​​)). Then:

(i) OX\mathcal{O}_XOX​ and OX+\mathcal{O}_X^+OX+​ are both sheaves;

(ii) for every point x∈Xx \in Xx∈X, the valuation f↦∣f(x)∣f \mapsto |f(x)|f↦∣f(x)∣ extends uniquely to the stalk OX,x\mathcal{O}_{X,x}OX,x​;

(iii) OX+(U)={f∈OX(U):∀x∈U, ∣f(x)∣≤1}\mathcal{O}_X^+(U) = \{f \in \mathcal{O}_X(U) : \forall x \in U,\ |f(x)| \le 1\}OX+​(U)={f∈OX​(U):∀x∈U, ∣f(x)∣≤1}.

Notes draft

⭐ The key to the proof is a "detour through schemes" trick: there is a map ψ:Spec⁡A→Spa⁡(A,A+)\psi : \operatorname{Spec} A \to \operatorname{Spa}(A,A^+)ψ:SpecA→Spa(A,A+) sending a prime ideal p\mathfrak{p}p to A→Frac⁡(A/p)→{0,1}A \to \operatorname{Frac}(A/\mathfrak{p}) \to \{0,1\}A→Frac(A/p)→{0,1}.

The preimage of a rational open set under ψ\psiψ is exactly D(f)D(f)D(f), so OSpa⁡(A,A+)\mathcal{O}_{\operatorname{Spa}(A,A^+)}OSpa(A,A+)​ is the pushforward of OSpec⁡A\mathcal{O}_{\operatorname{Spec} A}OSpecA​ along ψ\psiψ; pushforwards preserve sheaves.

⭐ (iii) says that O+\mathcal{O}^+O+ is not extra structure but the part of O\mathcal{O}O consisting of functions that are pointwise ≤1\le 1≤1 — the local version of the same statement as A⁺ 由谱决定.
