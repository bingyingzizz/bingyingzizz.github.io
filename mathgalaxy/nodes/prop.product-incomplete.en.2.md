## Notes · **draft**
This is why one needs a separate "$F$–$T$" for the complete case: in practice (for instance Lebesgue measure on $\mathbb{R}^{2}$) the measure at hand is usually the completion of $\mu \times \nu$, not the raw product.

⚠ A common trap: even with $\mu$, $\nu$ complete, $\mu \times \nu$ can still be **incomplete**.
