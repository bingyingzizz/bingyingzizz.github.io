## Statement · **draft**
Let $\mathcal{A} \xrightarrow{F} \mathcal{A}' \xrightarrow{G} \mathcal{A}''$ be **left exact** additive functors, with $\mathcal{A}, \mathcal{A}'$ having enough injectives and $F$ sending injectives to $G$-acyclic objects. Then there is a spectral sequence (⚠ left exactness cannot be dropped: for $F = \operatorname{Hom}(\mathbb{Z}/2, -)$ and $G = -\otimes \mathbb{Z}/2$, $G$ kills all injectives and $E_{2}$ vanishes, while $GF \cong F$ gives $R^{0}(GF)(\mathbb{Z}/2) = \mathbb{Z}/2$)
