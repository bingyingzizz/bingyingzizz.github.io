## Notes · **draft**
**Proof.** By "every functor induces a topos morphism", $X : \mathbf{1} \to \mathcal{C}$ induces $\widehat{X}_{!} \dashv \widehat{X}^{-1} \dashv \widehat{X}_{*}$ ✓.

The three formulas are: pullback is evaluation ✓;

the left Kan extension along $\mathbf{1} \to \mathcal{C}$ is given by a colimit over the comma category, and "$\exists Y \to X$" is exactly the condition for that colimit to be nonempty ✓;

the right adjoint is dual ✓.

∎
