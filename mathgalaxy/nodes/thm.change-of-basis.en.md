## Statement · **draft**
Let $T : V \to V$ be linear and let $\mathcal{B}, \mathcal{B}'$ be two ordered bases of $V$.

Write $P := [\mathrm{id}]_{\mathcal{B}'}^{\mathcal{B}}$ (**transition matrix**; its $j$-th column is the coordinate vector of the $\mathcal{B}'$-th vector of $j$ in the basis $\mathcal{B}$).

Then $P$ is invertible, and

$$[T]_{\mathcal{B}'} = P^{-1}\,[T]_{\mathcal{B}}\,P$$
