# flabby sheaf　`def.flabby-sheaf`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=def.flabby-sheaf)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./def.flabby-sheaf.md)

**Examples**: $\mathcal{C}^{q}(\mathcal{F})$ in the Godement resolution; the sheaf of discontinuous sections $U\mapsto\prod_{x\in U}\mathcal{F}_{x}$. Constant sheaves are in general not flabby.

⚠ Flabby sheaves differ from injective sheaves: injective sheaves are always flabby, but not conversely.
