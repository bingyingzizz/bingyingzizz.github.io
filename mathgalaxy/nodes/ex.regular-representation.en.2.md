## Notes · **draft**
The dimension of the regular representation is $|G|$. For the character formula: the diagonal entry of the permutation matrix of $g$ in the basis $G$ is $1$ exactly when $gh=h$, that is, $g=1$. When $\operatorname{char}F\nmid|G|$ and $F$ is algebraically closed, $\rho_{\mathrm{reg}}\cong\bigoplus_V(\dim V)V$ with $V$ running over the irreducible representations. Comparing dimensions gives $|G|=\sum_V(\dim V)^2$.
