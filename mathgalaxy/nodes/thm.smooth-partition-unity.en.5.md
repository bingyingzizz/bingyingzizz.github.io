# Smooth Partition of Unity　`thm.smooth-partition-unity`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=thm.smooth-partition-unity)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./thm.smooth-partition-unity.md)

 All derivatives of $b$ at $t = 0$ are $0$ ($e^{-1/t}$ tends to $0$ faster than any polynomial), so $b$ is smooth. The two denominator terms never vanish simultaneously, and their sum is positive (for $\lVert x\rVert \in [\tfrac{1}{2}, 1]$ exactly one term is positive), so $\beta$ is smooth. Also, $\beta \equiv 1$ when $\lVert x\rVert \le \tfrac{1}{2}$ and $\beta \equiv 0$ when $\lVert x\rVert \ge 1$. Translating and rescaling $\beta$ produces a bump function on any ball.
