## Notes · **draft**
"Bounded" means the lattice has a least element (the subobject coming from the initial object) and a greatest element ($X$ itself). Viewing "subobjects" as a lattice is exactly what will be used later for the internal logic of a topos — its meets and joins are the "and" and "or" of the logic.
