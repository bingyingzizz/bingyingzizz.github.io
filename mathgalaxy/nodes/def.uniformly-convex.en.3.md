# uniformly convex space　`def.uniformly-convex`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=def.uniformly-convex)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./def.uniformly-convex.md)

⭐ Uniform convexity $\implies$ strict convexity (`def.strictly-convex`): if $\|x\| = \|y\| = 1$ and $x \ne y$, take $\varepsilon = \|x - y\| > 0$ and the midpoint has norm $< 1$.

📌 Uniform convexity is "the norm bends uniformly": it upgrades the pointwise, pair-by-pair conclusion of strict convexity to an estimate uniform over all unit vectors — exactly the quantitative form needed for Milman–Pettis (`thm.milman-pettis`) and Clarkson's inequality.
