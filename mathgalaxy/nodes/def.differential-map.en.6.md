# Differential of a Smooth Map　`def.differential-map`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=def.differential-map)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./def.differential-map.md)

It is first-order information; under a change of coordinates one multiplies by a Jacobian on each side (one forward, one backward), so "$\mathrm{d}F$ is a tensor" is right while "$\partial_{i}F$ is a tensor" is not.

**Meaning of the chain rule**: $M \mapsto T_{p}M$ and $F \mapsto \mathrm{d}F_{p}$ form a **functor** (the tangent functor). Thus on the layer of manifolds and smooth maps, tangent spaces automatically inherit all composition structure — which is also why $\mathrm{d}^{2} = 0$ holds formally.
