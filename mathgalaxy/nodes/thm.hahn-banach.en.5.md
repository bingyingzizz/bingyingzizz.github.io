# Hahn–Banach　`thm.hahn-banach`

**Step 3: norm-preserving extension.** Take $p(x) = \|\varphi\| \cdot \|x\|$ (sublinear) and $f = \varphi$; then $\varphi \le p$ on $M$. This gives a linear $\Phi$ with $\Phi|_M = \varphi$ and $\Phi \le p$ on $X$. Applying $\Phi \le p$ to $x$ and to $-x$ gives $|\Phi(x)| \le \|\varphi\|\,\|x\|$, hence $\|\Phi\| \le \|\varphi\|$; the reverse inequality comes from the extension, so equality holds. $\blacksquare$

**The complex case**: regard $\varphi$ as a real-linear functional, extend it, then set $\Phi_{\mathbb C}(x) = \Psi(x) - i\,\Psi(ix)$; one checks that it is complex-linear and norm-preserving.
