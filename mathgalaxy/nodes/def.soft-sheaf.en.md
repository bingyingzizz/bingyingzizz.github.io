## Statement · **draft**
Let $X$ be a topological space and $\mathcal{F}$ an abelian sheaf on $X$. $\mathcal{F}$ is called a **soft sheaf** if for every closed set $A\subseteq X$ the restriction map $\Gamma(X,\mathcal{F})\to\Gamma(A,\mathcal{F})$ is surjective: that is, every section over a closed set extends to a section over the whole space.
