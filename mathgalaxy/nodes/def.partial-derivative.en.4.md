# Partial Derivative　`def.partial-derivative`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=def.partial-derivative)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./def.partial-derivative.md)

No number of one-dimensional conditions can add up to an $n$-dimensional conclusion.

⭐ Conversely, $C^{1}$ (partial derivatives exist **and are continuous**) suffices for differentiability — the most used sufficient condition in practice (see `def.total-differential`).

⭐ On $\mathbb{R}^n$, a space "grown from coordinates", partial derivatives are free;

on a **manifold** or a **Banach space** the coordinates themselves must first be built, and only then can one discuss directional derivatives and differentiability.
