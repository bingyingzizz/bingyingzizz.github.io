# preimage of a subobject　`def.preimage-subobject`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=def.preimage-subobject)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./def.preimage-subobject.md)

together with the projection to $X^{\prime}$ (if the fibred product exists). It satisfies the universal property: for any $g : T \to X^{\prime}$, if $f \circ g$ factors through $Y \rightarrowtail X$, then $g$ factors uniquely through $f^{-1}(Y) \to X^{\prime}$.

In $\mathbf{Set}$, $f^{-1}(Y) = \{\, x \in X^{\prime} : f(x) \in Y \,\}$, that is, the usual set-theoretic preimage.

## Notes · **draft**
**Intuition**: the preimage is "pulling $Y$ along $f$ back to $X^{\prime}$" — it solves $f(x) \in Y$.
