Abstract

Integrating forms pairs de Rham cohomology, closed forms modulo exact ones, with singular homology, and the de Rham theorem says the pairing is perfect: for every kkk there is an isomorphism HdRk(M)→Hsingk(M;R)H^{k}_{\mathrm{dR}}(M)\to H^{k}_{\mathrm{sing}}(M;\mathbb{R})HdRk​(M)→Hsingk​(M;R) sending [ω][\omega][ω] to the functional [c]↦∫cω[c]\mapsto\int_{c}\omega[c]↦∫c​ω. The maps are compatible with pullbacks, so they form a natural isomorphism of graded algebras. The theorem welds analysis, geometry and topology into a single object.
