# diagram and commutative diagram　`def.commutative-diagram`
English entry · root `../`
[in graph](../#n=def.commutative-diagram)　·　[Chinese page (authoritative)](./def.commutative-diagram.md)

**Reading it on paper.** A diagram drawn on paper is only a **presentation**: objects are assigned to the vertices of a directed graph $G$ and morphisms to its arrows. Saying that it **commutes** means that any two paths with the same source and target have **equal** composites under the labelling — this is a **condition to be checked**, equivalent to the labelling descending to a functor on the quotient of the free category on $G$.

$I$ is called the **indexing category**: it prescribes only the shape of the diagram, not its content.
