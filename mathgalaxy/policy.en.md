# MathGalaxy · Content policy

> English version: **draft** · Chinese source is authoritative · machine-drafted, pending author review.

## 1. Every layer is, as far as possible, *constructed* from the layer below

Objects should be built out of things that already exist (subsets, power sets,
Cartesian products, quotient sets, function spaces, …), and every concept that is
substantively used needs a node and a definition reference.
