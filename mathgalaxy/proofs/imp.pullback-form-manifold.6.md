`imp.pullback-form-manifold` · 推出 · strong 边 · 根 `../`

`def.form-on-manifold` 微分形式 + `def.differential-map` 切映射 + `def.smooth-map` 光滑映射 → `prop.pullback-form-manifold` 拉回

\mathrm{d}F^{j}\wedge\mathrm{d}F^{I} = F^{\ast}\Bigl(\sum_{j}\partial_{j}a\,\mathrm{d}y^{j}\wedge\mathrm{d}y^{I}\Bigr) = F^{\ast}(\mathrm{d}a\wedge\mathrm{d}y^{I}) = F^{\ast}(\mathrm{d}\omega),$ 末步用了 $\mathrm{d}\omega = \mathrm{d}a\wedge\mathrm{d}y^{I} + a\,\mathrm{d}(\mathrm{d}y^{I}) = \mathrm{d}a\wedge\mathrm{d}y^{I}$。
